# Abohar citizen charter for 2025–26

## Service standards

Section 2.3 of the charter gives these selected targets in working days. They describe the 2025–26 edition and are not a guarantee of present turnaround. Check the precise service entry before relying on a deadline.

| Service | Target | Responsible designations in the charter |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| New water connection | 7 days | Junior Engineer, A.C.E., C.E. |
| Complaint about a street light not working | 10 days | Junior Engineer, A.C.E., C.E. |
| Current-year birth or death certificate | 21 days | Local or district registrar |
| Assign a unique property ID | 12 days | Clerk, Inspector, Superintendent |

## Complaints

Section 3.1 lists a complaint centre at the Abohar corporation office and gives office complaint hours as 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., except public holidays. It lists **75085-09000** for sanitation and **14420** for water supply and sewerage. Section 3.2 says the complainant receives a grievance number and is told what action was taken and how to pursue the matter further if dissatisfied.

The separate toll-free operating paragraph mentions Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar and gives different opening hours. The contacts above come from the Abohar-specific section; the copied paragraph is not used to establish local hours or coverage.

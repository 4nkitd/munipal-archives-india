# Abohar municipal corporation election results, May 2026

## Declared seats

The result sheet identifies the local body as "M Corp Abohar" in Fazilka district. It gives the poll date as **26 May 2026** and reports results declared for **50 of 50 wards**. The commission's index dates the sheet **29 May 2026**.

| Party or category | Seats won |

| Aam Aadmi Party | 20 |
| Indian National Congress | 1 |

| Bahujan Samaj Party | 0 |
| Shiromani Akali Dal | 0 |

## Which local body the result covers

The [13 May 2026 election gazette](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Gazette_Notification_of_Election.pdf), No. SEC/ME/SAM (CME)/2026/832, lists Abohar in Annexure I, printed page 2333, under municipal corporations with 50 wards. It lists Fazilka separately under councils and nagar panchayats. The [August 2023 SEC ward list](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/CamScanner_08-01-2023_13.08.52_3.pdf), page 3, row 108, also gives Abohar corporation 50 wards. The identical totals do not establish that ward boundaries stayed the same.

The [5 June 2026 district gazette](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Fazilka.pdf), No. SEC/ME/SAM(CME-RN)/2026/874, gives elected members by constituency. It is a record of that election, not a municipal boundary notification or a register of later changes to membership.

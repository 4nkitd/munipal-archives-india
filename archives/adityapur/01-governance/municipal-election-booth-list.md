# Adityapur booth list and district gazette for the 2026 municipal election

## Election documents

The notice ran from 6 January to 30 April 2026. Adityapur voters should use the attachment labelled “Adityapur Booth list 2026_0001”. The district also links a gazette form for Adityapur and Seraikella and the returning officer and assistant returning officer notification 1197 dated 19 December 2025.

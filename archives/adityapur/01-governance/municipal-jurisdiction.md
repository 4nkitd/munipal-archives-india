# Adityapur Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and office

## Municipal area

The state profile dates the establishment of Adityapur Municipal Council to 1963. It gives the urban local body an area of 49 square kilometres and lists 35 wards. It does not give the date of conversion from council to corporation.

# Agartala municipal government and the 1994 Act

## Municipal government

A royal proclamation established Agartala Municipality in 1871 over three square miles with 875 residents. The city introduced municipal taxation regulations in 1874. Its early responsibilities included roads, drains, bridges, markets and sanitation.

AMC dates passage of the Tripura Municipal Act, 1994 to 26 October 1994. The December 1995 municipal election returned a council of 17 elected members, joined by three nominated members. The council area expanded from 16.012 to 62.02 square kilometres in 2004. The city's history distinguishes that area from the 92-square-kilometre Greater Agartala Planning Area. These are historical figures.

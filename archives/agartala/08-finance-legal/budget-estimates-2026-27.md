# Agartala budget estimates for 2026-27

## Budget at a glance

The “Budget at a Glance” table on PDF page 6 separates revenue from capital and gives both actuals and estimates. All figures below are in ₹ lakh.

| Account | 2025-26 actual | 2026-27 estimate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Revenue receipts | 15,714.41 | 16,992.25 |
| Capital receipts | 15,724.57 | 40,940.10 |
| Total receipts | 31,438.98 | 57,932.35 |
| Revenue expenditure | 10,313.37 | 13,945.59 |
| Capital expenditure | 22,422.45 | 44,084.43 |
| Total expenditure | 32,735.82 | 58,030.02 |
| Overall deficit | 1,296.84 | 97.67 |

The 2026-27 capital estimate includes ₹10,000 lakh in municipal bond receipts, against zero actual receipts under that head in 2025-26. The estimate does not establish that bonds were issued.

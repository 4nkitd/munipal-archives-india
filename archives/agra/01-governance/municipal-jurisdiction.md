# Who handles what in Agra?

The [corporation](https://nagarnigamagra.com/corporation/) identifies itself as both Agra Municipal Corporation and Agra Nagar Nigam. It lists sanitation, public health and parks among its responsibilities. Its [public office](https://nagarnigamagra.com/) is near Soorsadan on M.G. Road, Sanjay Place, Agra 282002. The homepage names Hemalata Diwakar as mayor and Santosh Kumar Vaishya as municipal commissioner.

The [Agra Development Authority](https://www.adaagra.org.in/) publishes master and zonal plans, handles building-map submissions and lists ADA-developed and ADA-approved colonies. Its development area includes Fatehpur Sikri. Inclusion in that area does not establish that a property lies in an Agra municipal ward.

The Directorate General Defence Estates [lists Agra Cantonment separately](https://dgde.gov.in/en/cantonments/). For an address inside the cantonment, consult the cantonment board rather than assuming the Nagar Nigam is the local body. This profile covers the municipal area, not Agra district as a whole.

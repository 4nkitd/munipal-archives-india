# Agra zone offices and ward delimitation

| Zone in the municipal directory | Office location |
|---|---|
| Chatta | Water Works Compound at Water Works Churaha; Dhuliya Ganj near the Torrent office |
| Hari Parvat | Room 101, Nagar Nigam, near Sur Sadan |
| Loha Mandi | Near Pani ki Tanki, Loha Mandi; camp office in Sector 9, Awas Vikas, Kargil Road |
| Taj Ganj | Torrent Power office, Mughal ki Pulia, Fatehabad Road |

The [corporation's directory](https://nagarnigamagra.com/zones-wards/) groups entries headed "Corporate Wards" under these offices. It prints `100-NAI KI MANDI` under Chatta and `101-SHASTRI PURAM` under Loha Mandi. It gives no election year, notification number, effective date or boundary descriptions.

The [Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Department's order register](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/GO_main_menu-2017.html) lists orders 625 and 646, dated 26 and 28 April 2022, about delimiting wards in newly created or expanded urban local bodies using the 2011 population. Neither register entry gives Agra's final boundaries. Use the municipal table to locate a zone office; it does not establish the current number of elected seats or the ward for a particular street.

## Which numbering does the archive use?

The [State Election Commission's 2023 winner list](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/NewWinnerList2023.aspx), filtered to **नगर निगम पार्षद** and district **आगरा**, gives 100 Agra corporation ward numbers, 1 through 100. For example, it lists `9-ईदगाह` and `72-नगला पदी`. The municipal directory instead lists `8-EDGAH` and `71-NAGLA PADI`. The archive's ward list uses the election return's Hindi names and numbers without attaching the municipal directory's zone assignments. These are 2023 electoral units; the return does not describe their boundaries.

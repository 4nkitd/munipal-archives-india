# Agra municipal service routes

On the [corporation homepage](https://nagarnigamagra.com/), use the **ANN Online Services** menu for three different property tasks: "Self Assessment of Your House Tax" for assessment, "Pay Your House Tax" for payment, and "Apply for Mutation" to request a change in the municipal property record.

The same menu sends trade licence applicants to Uttar Pradesh's E-Nagar Sewa portal. For grievances, the homepage lists "Complaints / IGRS" for Jansunwai and a separate street-light complaint link. It lists **14420** specifically for septic and sewer issues. General correspondence goes to the published office mailbox, `amcagra1@gmail.com`.

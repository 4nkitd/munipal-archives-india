# Finding Agra's municipal budgets

The [corporation budget index](https://nagarnigamagra.com/budget/) lists fiscal years from 2011-12 through 2018-19, then from 2020-21 through 2026-27. Its newest entry is [Mool Budget 2026-27](https://nagarnigamagra.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Budget-2026-27.pdf). The year identifies the budget period; the index gives no adoption date.

Two pairs need care: the 2011-12 and 2013-14 labels link to the same file, and the 2022-23 label points to the same `MOOL-BUDGET-2021-22-SADAN-PASS-.pdf` as 2021-22. The 2012-13 label has no download link. These labels alone do not establish separate budgets for those years. The site links audited balance sheets separately; a budget entry does not show actual receipts or spending.

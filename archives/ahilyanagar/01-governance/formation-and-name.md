# Ahilyanagar Municipal Corporation: institutional history and name

## Corporation history and name

The corporation's history says it has served the city since 2003. That page provides no day, month or constituting notification number. The [district administration](https://ahilyanagar.maharashtra.gov.in/en/) and the [Maharashtra Government Gazette of 20 October 2025](https://amc.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/GazetteAhilyanagar.pdf#page=11) both use Ahilyanagar Municipal Corporation. Ahmednagar Municipal Corporation is the name on older material, including the earlier final ward composition documents still listed on the [municipal election page](https://amc.gov.in/en/election/). The 2025 ward gazette confirms the name in use by that date; it is not the renaming order.

## Head office

The [municipal contact page](https://amc.gov.in/en/contact-us-amc/) gives the head office address as Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Road, Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra 414001. Its office telephone table lists 0241-2343622 and 0241-2340522. The head office card lists `it.nagar@amc.gov.in`.

# Ahilyanagar final delimitation: 17 electoral wards

## Gazette and seat allocation

The Maharashtra Government Gazette Extraordinary, Part Two, extraordinary number 191, published the final schedule on 20 October 2025. It invokes section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949 and cites draft notification 127 dated 3 September 2025. It fixes 17 four-member electoral wards and 68 seats for the next general election. The Maharashtra State Election Commission's [2025–26 results table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) reports results for all 68 seats.

## How to use the schedule

The English schedule starts on [gazette page 11](https://amc.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/GazetteAhilyanagar.pdf#page=11). Each ward entry gives its schedule population, seat identifiers and a boundary description. Ward 1 includes Nagapur village gaothan and the Bhistabagh Mahal area; its schedule population is 22,234. Ward 17 includes Kinetic Chowk and Kedgaon Devi premises; its schedule population is 19,729. Neither figure is a current population estimate.

Use the boundary description and the [municipal election page's ward maps](https://amc.gov.in/en/election/), not a landmark alone, to place an address. The gazette says the mapped boundary controls any discrepancy between the Marathi and English descriptions, and that maps are available at the municipal office during office hours.

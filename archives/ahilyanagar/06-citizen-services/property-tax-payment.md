# Ahilyanagar property tax lookup and online payment

## Look up and pay a bill

The corporation's [property tax portal](https://propertytax.ahilyanagarcorporation.in/) offers search by property number or owner name. The portal's FAQ describes entering the property number, opening the bill and selecting **Pay Now**. It lists net banking, debit and credit cards, and UPI as payment methods. The payment step requires an email address and a mobile number. The FAQ says a successful payment generates a mobile confirmation and directs residents to the Nagar MSEVA app for online copies of receipts from payments made at a municipal office.

## Questions about assessment

The corporation's [tax department page](https://amc.gov.in/en/property-tax/) lists assessment of newly constructed buildings at zone-wise rates, collection of building taxes, and investigation of collection complaints among its responsibilities. It describes the administrative route for new assessment reports: Ward Committee offices 1–4 send reports through the Assistant Valuation Officer to the Deputy Commissioner for Tax. These committee offices do not correspond to the 17 electoral wards.

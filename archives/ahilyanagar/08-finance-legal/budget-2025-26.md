# Ahilyanagar budget estimates for 2025–26

## Revenue estimates

The [corporation's budget index](https://amc.gov.in/en/budget/) labels this volume Budget 2025–2026. Its revenue summary compares the revised 2024–25 provision with the administrator and commissioner's proposed 2025–26 provision. The document reports the figures in lakh rupees.

| Revenue item | Revised 2024–25 (₹ lakh) | Proposed 2025–26 (₹ lakh) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| General tax | 8,380.48 | 10,380.48 |
| Road tax | 242.00 | 442.01 |
| Solid waste management service tax | 600.00 | 1,332.00 |
| Collection department total | 9,900.49 | 12,922.53 |

These are citywide estimates in the revenue section, not tax rates, property bills or audited collections. Use the headings in the source to distinguish historical receipts from revised provisions and the proposed budget.

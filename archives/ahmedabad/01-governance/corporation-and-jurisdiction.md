# Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation: formation, powers and jurisdiction

## Formation and legal basis

AMC's [corporation history](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/Home/AboutTheCorporation) gives July 1950 as the month the corporation was established under the then Bombay provincial municipal corporations legislation. The page gives no day in July. It traces earlier civic administration to a Town Wall Fund Committee convened on 21 April 1831 and to the Republic Municipality of 1 April 1915. Those are predecessor milestones, not dates of formation for AMC. The age of the historic city likewise does not date the municipal corporation.

The [Gujarat government's published consolidation](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/gpmcact.pdf) gives the current title as the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949, Bombay Act No. LIX of 1949. That copy states that it incorporates changes through 30 November 2017. Section 63 sets out obligatory municipal functions, including street cleaning, sewage and rubbish disposal, drainage works, fire protection, public hospitals and dispensaries, and lighting of public streets. Section 66 covers discretionary provision. Consult amendments and the relevant gazette for a legal question that turns on later text.

## Which authority and which geography?

| Authority | What its own source describes |
|---|---|
| [Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/Home/AboutTheCorporation) | City civic infrastructure and administration. The corporation's overview describes seven administrative zones, subdivided into 48 wards, with four corporators per ward. |
| [Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority](https://auda.org.in/) | AUDA dates its establishment to 1 February 1978 and describes its founding purpose as long-term development schemes outside the then municipal corporation limits. Its [Revised Development Plan 2021](https://auda.org.in/RDP/) is a planning source, not an AMC ward list. |
| [Ahmedabad District Collectorate](https://ahmedabad.nic.in/about-district/) | The state government's district administration. Its site explains the Collectorate's district-level coordination, delivery of services and handling of land matters. Its jurisdiction is not a municipal ward boundary. |

AMC records that 17 municipalities and 30 gram panchayats were added to its jurisdiction in 2006, when the reported area became 449.343 km². AMC also describes the inclusion of Chandkheda and Motera in 2008 and gives 464.16 km² in that historical narrative. The archive does not use either area as a verified current figure. AMC's separate [demographics page](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/SP/Demographics) labels 5,577,940 people and 1,179,823 households as 2011 Census figures for the AMC area. They are not estimates for present city limits, AUDA or the district.

The published office number is 079-25391811 and the office email is `mc@ahmedabadcity.gov.in`, also listed in the [Gujarat urban development department's corporation directory](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/corporations.php). AMC's central address is Sardar Patel Bhavan, Danapith - Khamasa, Ahmedabad 380001. The office contact is distinct from the civic complaint line, 155303.

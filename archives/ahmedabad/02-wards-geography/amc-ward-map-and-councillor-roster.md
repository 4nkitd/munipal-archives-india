## Choosing a source for a ward question

The Secretary's Office links a [Gujarati councillor list for the 2026-31 term](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/ViewFile/ViewFile?TYPE=FileRepository,2638). It is arranged by ward and zone, with separate entries for each councillor. The sequence in its first column numbers councillors, not wards. For example, Gota has four entries at the start of the roster, while the map gives Gota ward number 1. The roster's shorter Gujarati labels need not reproduce each part of the map's English label. Use the roster for representation, not as a numbered boundary schedule. This archive does not reduce four seats to a single listed councillor or reproduce councillors' residential details and mobile numbers.

The [City Civic Centers directory](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/StaticPage/CityCivicCenters) serves a different purpose. It lists service counters and displays its own ward codes. For example, the Gota counter has code `026` in that directory, while Gota is ward 1 on the map. Do not convert civic-centre codes into electoral ward numbers. Likewise, AMC's [2011 Census demographics page](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/SP/Demographics) labels its ward data by census year; it is not the source for today's ward numbering.

Use the map as a public lookup aid. For a legal boundary, reservation or election dispute, consult the applicable official notification and delimitation schedule. Section 5 of the [Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/gpmcact.pdf) distinguishes the State Government's ward and seat notification from the State Election Commission's boundary determination under the circumstances set out there. This record does not establish the gazette text or a parcel's legal placement.

# Ahmedabad heat alert press note of 21 May 2026

## Seven-day warning and its limits

AMC's Health Department published a Gujarati press note dated **21 May 2026**. Its website links the file under **Heat Action Plan**, but the document itself is a time-specific press note, not the full city heat action plan or a standing order. It reported the India Meteorological Department's orange and yellow alerts for the coming week. The attached forecast table lists daytime maxima of 42–44°C and a minimum of 28°C for each day. Its highest forecast maximum, 44°C, applies to the window from 08:30 IST on 23 May to 08:30 IST on 24 May. The final window ends at 08:30 IST on 28 May 2026. These are forecasts published at the time, not observed temperatures or a current warning.

## The three-day ward annex

The department also described a finer-grained forecast of possible hotspots for the coming three days, separate from the seven-day weather table. The annex lists **51 locations across 16 wards and six zones**. It uses zone and ward names, a nearby landmark, and dimensions for each listed location. The ward numbers in this archive's metadata are cross-references from the separate [AMC City Map](https://inhouse-amcmodules.ahmedabadcity.gov.in:8023/); the annex itself does not number the wards.

| Zone | Wards named in the annex |
|---|---|
| South | Lambha, Behrampura, Vatva, Maninagar |
| East | Ramol Hathijan, Odhav, Vastral |
| North West | Gota, Thaltej, Bodakdev |
| North | Naroda, India Colony, Sardar Nagar |
| South West | Sarkhej, Maktampura |
| West | Chandkheda, shown as Chandkheda Motera on the city map |

Examples of the landmarks in the note are Chandola Lake in Behrampura, Ognaj Lake in Gota, the airport in Sardar Nagar and the Ramol police station in Ramol Hathijan. The list identifies individual forecast locations; it does not classify whole wards as hotspots or establish that a zone absent from the annex had no risk. The notice advised residents to limit afternoon exposure where possible and listed `108` for emergencies.

# Ahmedabad citizen services: property tax, complaints and certificates

## Property tax and civic complaints

| Task | AMC route |
|---|---|
| Look up and pay property tax | [Pay Property Tax](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/PTAX/DuesSearch). The form asks for a 15-character tenement number. AMC's example ends in a letter. |
| View a bill or recover a receipt | [Tax Bill Display](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/PTAX/Bill) and [Property Tax Receipt](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/PTAX/PropertyTaxReceipt) are separate pages. The receipt form also asks for the tenement number. |
| Apply for a change or track an application | In AMC's Online Services → Property Tax menu, use **Apply Online for Property Tax** or **Know your Application Status**. The menu also separates name-transfer fees and receipts from regular property tax. |
| File a civic complaint | Use AMC's linked [CCRS registration page](https://www.amccrs.com/AMCPortal/Complaint/Register), call `155303`, or message the official [WhatsApp bot](https://wa.me/+917567855303). |
| Follow up on a complaint | CCRS has a separate [Complaint Status page](https://www.amccrs.com/AMCPortal/Complaint/TrackProgress) for entries already submitted. Its menu also distinguishes tracking and reopening a complaint. |

AMC publishes the corporation's main phone as `079-25391811` and website feedback email as `feedback@ahmedabadcity.gov.in`. Neither replaces the dedicated complaint line.

## Birth, death and marriage registration

AMC's [Birth & Death Registration department page](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/StaticPage/birth_death_reg) says events occurring within AMC jurisdiction are registered through ward units. It also lists units at V.S. Hospital and Civil Hospital for events at those hospitals. The same page lists registration centres and describes certificate issuance and applications to correct errors. It describes a 21-day ordinary registration period for births and deaths. Check the current rules or the registration office before relying on the page's undated late-registration fee and procedure text.

AMC's Online Services menu links the [new marriage registration portal](https://marriageregistration.ahmedabadcity.gov.in/) with an effective date of 1 August 2026. This date is for the portal, not the start of marriage registration in AMC. The portal describes application, status checking and certificate download. AMC's department page describes its marriage registration work as applying to marriages taking place within its limits.

## Service counters, RTI and charters

The [City Civic Centers directory](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/StaticPage/CityCivicCenters) gives zone, address and counter timings. For example, it lists Relief Road opposite Roopam Cinema, 9:00 a.m. to 7:30 p.m., and the Gota Ward Office near ADB Water Tank on Science City Road, Sola, 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Those hours are the published entries for these specific counters, not citywide hours or a holiday schedule.

AMC's [RTI page](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/StaticPage/RTI) lists public information and appellate officer directories, English and Gujarati application forms, and fees. Its separate [Citizen Charter](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/SP/CitizenCharter) index links charters for birth and death, public health and the central laboratory, drainage engineering, and property tax and related charges. The [Right of Citizen to Public Services page](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/StaticPage/RCPS) publishes further service documents, including one for birth and death certificates. Check the applicable document for the particular transaction rather than treating a charter, an RTI request and a grievance as the same process.

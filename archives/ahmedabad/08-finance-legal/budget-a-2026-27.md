# Ahmedabad Budget A 2026-27: Standing Committee estimates

## Which budget stage is this?

AMC's [Budget index](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/SP/Budget) publishes the Gujarati book as **Budget 2026-27 - A**. The book opens its Standing Committee section with resolution no. **2078**, dated **10 February 2026**. The resolution cites the Municipal Commissioner's letter **CA/MIS/SC/06** dated **4 February 2026**, which presented the income and expenditure estimates under section 95 of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. The date on this archive record is the date printed on the committee resolution, not a website upload date.

On printed page 47 of the Standing Committee section, AMC compares the Municipal Commissioner's proposal, the increase and the estimate prepared by the Standing Committee. All amounts in the table are in crore rupees.

| Budget item | Commissioner's proposal | Increase | Standing Committee estimate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue expenditure | 7,150.00 | 285.1456 | 7,435.1456 |
| Development works | 9,868.00 | 1,214.8544 | 11,082.8544 |
| Total budget | 17,018.00 | 1,500.00 | 18,518.00 |

## What the book does and does not show

The index separates the Standing Committee resolution from the Commissioner's note. It also points to summaries and detailed tables for revenue receipts, revenue expenditure, capital expenditure and capital receipts. On printed page 46 of the Standing Committee section, the grant-works schedule gives, among other lines, ₹651.06 crore for AMRUT-related grant works, ₹550 crore for Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana and ₹200 crore for Swachh Bharat Mission works; those lines are the same in the Commissioner's and Standing Committee's columns. They are estimates in the development expenditure schedule, not a record of payments or works completed.

This record does not assign the Standing Committee date or figures to an independently verified General Board sanction. For comparison, AMC's [English 2023-24 budget book](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/ViewFile/ViewFile?TYPE=FileRepository,2203) separately opens with municipal corporation sanction resolution no. 769 and then reproduces the Standing Committee material. AMC's [Balance Sheet index](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in/SP/BalanceSheet) is where the corporation lists final accounts and available audit reports. Those are separate evidence from Budget A estimates.

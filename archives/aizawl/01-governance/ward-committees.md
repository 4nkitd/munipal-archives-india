# Aizawl ward committees and local councils

## Representation

Aizawl elects one representative from each of its 19 wards. The municipal profile also identifies members appointed by the Governor of Mizoram and gives the corporation a five-year term. The Mayor, Deputy Mayor and Commissioner administer the municipal office.

Each ward's elected councillor chairs its ward committee. Every local council in the ward sends two members to the committee. A local council is therefore a smaller unit than the municipal ward; its name is not interchangeable with the ward number.

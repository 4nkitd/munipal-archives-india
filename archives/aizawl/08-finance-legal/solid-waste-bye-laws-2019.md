# Aizawl solid waste management bye-laws of 2019

## Application and authority

Mizoram Gazette Extraordinary issue 480 published notification M.12011/6/2014-AMC on 17 July 2019. The Municipal Commissioner dated the notification 16 July. AMC made the bye-laws under section 371 of the Mizoram Municipalities Act, 2007, after making the draft available for free public inspection for more than one month.

Clauses 1 and 2 bring the bye-laws into force on publication and apply them throughout AMC's area. They cover storage, collection, transport, processing and disposal. Clause 3.3 defines an authorised agent to include an entity appointed by AMC for street sweeping, waste collection or collection of charges and fines.

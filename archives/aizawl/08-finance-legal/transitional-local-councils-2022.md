# Transitional local councils under the 2022 amendment

## Rule 30 and commencement

Mizoram Gazette Extraordinary issue 346 of 16 June 2022 published notification B. 13017/21/2022-UD&PA, dated 6 June. The Governor made the amendment under section 369 of the Mizoram Municipalities Act, 2007 after approval by circulation on 2 June. The amendment took effect on gazette publication.

The amendment replaces rule 30 of the 2010 Ward Committee and Local Council Rules. During the transitional period, existing village councils within the municipal area function as local councils until local councils are constituted under rule 15. The amendment does not redraw Aizawl's wards.

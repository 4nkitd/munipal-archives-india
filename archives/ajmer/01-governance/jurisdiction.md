# Ajmer city corporation and other urban bodies in Ajmer district

## Which municipal office covers the city?

Row 1 of the state urban local body directory lists district **Ajmer**, urban local body **Ajmer**, category **Nagar Nigam**, and 80 wards. Its Raj-mail address is `ceoajm.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in`. The directory links a ward boundary schedule from this same row.

A district address alone does not identify the municipal office. For example, the directory gives Kishangarh, Kekri and Pushkar separate Nagar Parishad entries, and Nasirabad its own Nagar Palika entry. They are not wards of Ajmer Nagar Nigam. The directory gives no municipal incorporation date or parcel-level jurisdiction test.

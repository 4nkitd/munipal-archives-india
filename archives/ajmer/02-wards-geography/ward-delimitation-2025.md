# Ajmer ward boundaries in the September 2025 Rajasthan Gazette

## What the state approved

Part 6(क) of the Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary dated 15 September 2025 publishes notification DLB/25/13441. The state considered objections to the draft submitted through the Ajmer Collector and approved the proposed ward formation under sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994.

## How to read the schedule

Form A prints a ward number, a Hindi boundary description and a population. Ward 1 begins on PDF page 1 and shows 7,134 people. Ward 80 begins on page 36 and shows 5,904. Its boundary walk starts at the Balaji temple at Nausar Ghati on Pushkar Road. These are schedule figures, not current population estimates. Use the full boundary walk for a street address: the schedule can include only part of a locality or one side of a road.

# Rajasthan election notice on electronic voting in municipal wards

## Which contests the direction covers

The Commission's notice of 19 January 2026, file ending **14/635**, covers wards of Nagar Palikas, Nagar Parishads and Nagar Nigams across Rajasthan. For future general elections or by-elections to fill vacant seats, it directs that votes be cast and recorded using electronic voting machines in the prescribed manner until the Commission directs otherwise.

The Commission cites section 26(3) of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 24-क(2) of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994. The notice supersedes order 3913 dated 12 September 2014. It applies to Ajmer as a Nagar Nigam; it does not announce an Ajmer vacancy, polling date or result.

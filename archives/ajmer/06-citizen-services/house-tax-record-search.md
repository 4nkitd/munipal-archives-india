# Search the Ajmer municipal house-tax records

## Search fields

Open the Municipal Corporation Ajmer guest search on Rajasthan's records portal. Under “Search By Meta Keywords”, select a **Ward No** or enter an **Owner Name**. The form also offers a **Location** field. Choosing a ward loads the associated location choices. Select **Search** to request matching records or **Reset** to clear the form.

The ward selector is an index to house-tax records. It does not say which electoral delimitation its ward numbers follow. For present electoral boundaries, consult the 2025 Gazette schedule. The guest form does not quote a tax bill or offer a payment action.

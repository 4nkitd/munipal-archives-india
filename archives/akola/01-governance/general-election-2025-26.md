# Akola municipal election 2025-26: wards, seats and results

## City result

Row 25 of the Maharashtra State Election Commission's final party-position statement covers Akola Municipal Corporation. Its 20 wards are electoral areas; 80 is the number of council seats returned from those areas. The statement counts 469 contesting candidates. It gives a city total rather than the boundaries, names or seat allocation of individual wards.

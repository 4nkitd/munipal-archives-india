# Akola corporation office and district directory

## Corporation office

The district administration lists Akola Municipal Corporation at PIN 444001. Its published office telephone is **0724-2434412**.

## Which local body to contact

This entry is for the city corporation. The [district municipalities directory](https://akola.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/municipalaties/) lists Akot, Murtizapur, Patur and Telhara separately under municipal councils. For a property or municipal service in one of those council areas, use that council's entry. A district address alone does not place a property in the corporation's jurisdiction.

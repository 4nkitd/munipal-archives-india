# Akola property tax, water tax and licence services

## Choose the service on the city portal

| Task | Destination in the Online Services menu |
|---|---|
| Pay property tax | [Akola property tax](https://akolamc.in/pages/onlinepayment.aspx) |
| Pay water tax | [Akola water bill](https://amcwaterbill.org) |
| Apply for a trade licence | [Trade licence service](https://tradeamc.org/SHEL/ShelHome) |
| Register a birth or death | [Civil Registration System](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/) |
| Marriage registration or fire NOC | [MahaULB citizen services](https://mahaulb.in/MahaULB/citizenservicepage) |

The menu marks these entries RTS. It also lists a separate MahaULB entry for a mandap or hall NOC. Choose the permission that matches the proposed activity; the fire NOC and mandap/hall entries lead to different service selections.

# Aligarh city and the district's municipal bodies

## City versus district

The district administration's municipal bodies list distinguishes Aligarh Municipal Corporation from separately listed bodies, including Khair, Atrauli, Harduaganj, Iglas and Tappal. This archive covers the corporation, not those bodies or the rest of the district.

The district's [geography page](https://aligarh.nic.in/about-district/) identifies Aligarh, also called Koil there, as the district headquarters and places the city about 140 km southeast of New Delhi. The [corporation entry in the district directory](https://aligarh.nic.in/public-utility/aligarh-municipal-corporation/) gives the office address as Sewa Bhavan near Ghanta Ghar, Aligarh 202001.

## What the source does not establish

The municipal bodies list is an administrative directory. It does not define the legal city boundary or show which parcels, colonies or villages fall within a ward. The 2023 ward results and reservation notice are separate records and are not boundary schedules either.

# Aligarh corporation ward results, 2023

## What the commission published

On the SEC's declared winner list, select Nagar Nigam councillor (नगर निगम पार्षद) and Aligarh district (अलीगढ़). The Aligarh corporation results contain one winning candidate for each ward numbered 1 through 90. The archive's [structured ward register](../wards.json) transcribes the ward names and winning candidates in Hindi. It omits personal contact information. These are election results, not a check of who currently holds each seat.

| Party at the 2023 election | Wards won |
| --- | ---: |
| Bharatiya Janata Party | 45 |
| Samajwadi Party | 33 |
| Bahujan Samaj Party | 7 |
| Independent | 4 |
| Indian National Congress | 1 |

Under the mayoral filter (नगर निगम महापौर), the same SEC portal lists Prashant Singhal (प्रशांत सिंघल) as the Aligarh winner with 193,889 valid votes, or 47.18%.

## Reading the ward register

For example, the results label ward 1 नगला मानसिंह, ward 49 बेगपुर and ward 90 नगला पटवारी. A ward label does not establish its full street, village or colony coverage. The result screen identifies the election year, but a publication date for this result page was not verified.

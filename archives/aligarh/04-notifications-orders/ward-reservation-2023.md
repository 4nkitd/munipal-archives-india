# Aligarh corporation ward reservation notification, 2023

## Notification and earlier objections

The Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Department's [election orders index](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/GO-Nirvachan-2022.html) lists notification 947/नौ-1-2023-18आर/22 dated 9 April 2023 for reservation of Aligarh Municipal Corporation wards. The same index lists a separate notice, 776/नौ-1-2023-18आर/22 dated 30 March 2023, inviting objections to the proposed reservation. The April document cites that March notice.

## How it relates to the election record

The April notification concerns which seats were reserved. It is separate from the SEC's declared winners and does not establish ward boundaries. The notification is a scanned document; its full schedule was not transcribed from the scan. In the separate SEC result table, the corporation's winning rows are labelled as 37 unreserved seats, 18 women, 13 Other Backward Classes, 10 Scheduled Castes, seven Other Backward Classes women and five Scheduled Castes women. Those counts come from the SEC results, not a transcription of the gazette schedule.

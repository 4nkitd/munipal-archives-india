# Aligarh property records, payments and municipal complaints

## Property search and bills

Select **Aligarh** in the eNagarSewa property search. The form accepts an old or new property ID or lets residents search using fields such as house number, owner name, address, zone, ward or mohalla. The portal menu links to [house and water tax payment](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/onlinepay) and [payment tracking](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/trackpayment). The public search form and city option were checked; no individual property lookup or payment was made.

## Complaints and civil registration

The [UP Directorate of Local Bodies](https://localbodies.up.nic.in/) links to grievance tracking and birth and death certificate verification. The [eNagarSewa home page](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/home) lists 1533 to register grievances. Its [birth and death registration notice](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/birthDeathDisclaimer) says eNagarSewa stopped offering registration and directs applicants to the [Civil Registration System](https://crsorgi.gov.in/). Verification of an existing certificate and registering a new event are separate services.

The district directory lists Sewa Bhavan near Ghanta Ghar and `nnaligarh@gmail.com` for the corporation. That directory does not give a municipal helpdesk number.

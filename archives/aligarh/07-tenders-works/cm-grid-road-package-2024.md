# Aligarh CM Grid road package ALG/CMG-1, 2024 tender

## Package and estimates

The English tender on page 3 of the scanned notice names package ALG/CMG-1 and invites eligible bidders to submit percentage-rate bids on the UP e-tender portal. Its estimates below are in lakh rupees, excluding GST.

| Road segment | Length | Construction | Five-year maintenance | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Ramghat Road, Meenakshi Bridge to Quarshi Thana, wards 33, 49, 53, 74 and 82 | 2.65 km | 1,304.77 | 16.23 | 1,321.00 |
| Begpur Marris Road, Abdul Chauraha to Kelanagar Chauraha, ward 49 | 0.72 km | 760.59 | 5.80 | 766.39 |
| Swarn Jayanti Nagar to Ramesh Vihar, wards 33 and 82 | 1.56 km | 1,424.61 | 16.31 | 1,440.92 |

The schedule gives a package earnest money deposit of ₹52.92 lakh, a construction period of 12 months and a tender fee of ₹41,300.

## Notice and bid dates

The Chief Engineer's signed tender is dated 28 August 2024. The [district notice page](https://aligarh.nic.in/notice/e-tender-notice-for-works-approved-under-cm-grid-scheme-nagar-nigam-aligarh/) lists 29 August to 30 September 2024 as its start and end dates. Those are separate from the signed schedule: documents available 31 August at noon, pre-bid meeting 5 September at 3 p.m., bids due 17 September at 5 p.m., technical bids opening 18 September at 11 a.m. All times are local. The separate publication date, award and any later corrigendum were not verified.

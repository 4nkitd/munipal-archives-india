# Aligarh CM-GRIDS first-instalment sanction, 27 March 2025

## Sanction for one road work

The Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Department's [2024–25 financial sanctions index](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/Financial_SenctionNewIF24-25.html) lists order 39/2025/1414/001-9-7099-302-2024 dated 27 March 2025. It records a first-instalment sanction of ₹2,954.63 lakh for one road development work under the Chief Minister Green Road Infrastructure Development Scheme (Urban). The scanned order lists wards 5, 9, 12, 17, 22, 36, 57, 58 and 62 and puts the assessed project cost at ₹9,379.78 lakh.

## What the amount means

This is a state sanction for a single listed work. The first instalment is not the total project cost, the municipal corporation's annual budget or proof that the money was spent. This order is not identified as an award for the separately archived ALG/CMG-1 road package.

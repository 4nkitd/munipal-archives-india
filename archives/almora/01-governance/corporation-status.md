# Almora’s change from municipal council to municipal corporation

The state register identifies **Nagar Nigam Almora** as the first urban local body in Almora district. Ranikhet municipality and the town panchayats of Dwarahat, Chaukhutia and Bhikiyasain have separate entries.

The [district utility directory](https://almora.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) retains the older “Nagar Palika Parisad Almora” label. For the 2024–25 local-body election, however, the [district election office](https://almora.nic.in/panchsthaneey-election/) lists candidates under **Nagar Nigam Almora**. The [state elected-member roll](https://udd.uk.gov.in/document-category/newly-elected-public-representatives/) records Mayor Ajay Verma and 40 ward members, with **7 February 2025** as the oath and first-board date. The legal effective date of the upgrade is not established by those records.

**Municipal email:** eonagarpalikaalmora@gmail.com, listed against Nagar Nigam Almora in the state’s [ULBs Email ID List](https://udd.uk.gov.in/document-category/list-of-ulb-eo/) dated 5 August 2026. The address retains the former body’s name.

# Almora balance sheet and budget, FY 2024–25

In the department’s **Balance Sheet and Budget 2024–25** register, select the row headed **Almora**. The row identifies an 8 MB filing and gives **20 July 2026** as its date. This is the register date, not the start of FY 2024–25 or a council adoption date.

A separate [state infrastructure order entry](https://udd.uk.gov.in/document/avsthapna-almora-15-62-lac-go/) dated **30 October 2026** identifies ₹15.62 lakh for Almora. This figure belongs to the named order, not to the city’s annual budget.

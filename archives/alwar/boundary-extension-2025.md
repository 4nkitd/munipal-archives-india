# Alwar corporation boundary addition notified in January 2025

## Areas listed in the notice

Rajasthan's Local Self Government Department dated DLB/24/307 on 8 January 2025. Its register description uses Nagar Parishad, but the [signed two-page attachment](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files//Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/LSG/2026/Notification/O_200126_aab5d804-a821-4e92-88be-d0d3736e8ab9.pdf) names Nagar Nigam Alwar throughout. Page 1 sets out areas to be included in the existing corporation boundary. The table names the gram panchayat and the revenue village or villages, which are not interchangeable descriptions.

| Gram panchayat as printed | Revenue village area listed |
|---|---|
| नंगला रायसिस | नंगला समवादी (Nangla Samwadi) |
| दिवाकरी | दिवाकरी (Diwakari) |
| बेलाका | बेलाका (Belaka) and बेरका (Berka) |
| भाखेड़ा | लिवारी (Liwari) and भाखेड़ा (Bhakhera) |

The population of 21,815 printed at the end of the table refers to the listed areas together. The notice does not break it down by village or give a corporation-wide population. It cites section 3(1)(a)(i) and section 3(8)(c), read with section 329 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009.

## Boundary and implementation

On page 2 the department asks Rural Development and Panchayati Raj to take the prescribed steps to separate the included areas from their panchayat boundaries and to send copies to the relevant panchayat officials. It asks the Alwar Collector to take the prescribed action for the corporation and revenue-village boundaries. The notice is an addition to the existing limits, not a complete description of those limits, and does not identify polling stations or municipal tax accounts. For the later ward layout, see [the 2025 ward notification](ward-delimitation-2025.md).

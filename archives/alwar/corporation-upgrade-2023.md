# Alwar Municipal Council upgrade to Municipal Corporation

## What the state notified

The Rajasthan Local Self Government Department issued notification DLB/23/3121 on 1 August 2023. The signed order names Nagar Parishad Alwar, district Alwar, and upgrades it to Nagar Nigam. Its final sentence says the notification takes effect immediately: “यह अधिसूचना तुरन्त प्रभाव से प्रभावशील होगी।” The date comes from the signed instrument and its departmental register, not from the attachment filename.

The state cites section 329 read with section 337 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009. It refers to Alwar's location in the National Capital Region and growing industrialisation when relaxing the criteria set by departmental notification 2428 of 17 July 2023. This is an operative upgrade order. The text does not enumerate the corporation's perimeter, redraw electoral wards or give a timetable for transferring individual services. [Read the signed instrument](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files//Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/LSG/2023/Notification/O_040823_2233372c-d9df-4642-a3e3-c745be9121a9.pdf).

## Which municipal body to look up

The department's [local-body directory](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) lists Alwar as a Nagar Nigam with 65 wards in row 10. It lists Bahadurpur, Baroda Mev, Govindgarh, Kathumar, Kherli and other towns in Alwar district separately. The state hosts a [dedicated Alwar corporation portal](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/ulbalwar/). The Raj-mail address in the directory still contains `nagarparishad`; the same row expressly classifies the body as a corporation.

For changes to the outer municipal area, see [the January 2025 boundary notice](boundary-extension-2025.md). For the way the state's published schedule divides that area into wards, see [the September 2025 ward notification](ward-delimitation-2025.md). Neither the district name nor the upgrade order alone establishes that a given plot falls within corporation limits.

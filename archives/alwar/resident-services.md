# Alwar municipal contacts and online services

## Corporation contact and application routes

The corporation portal footer publishes `0144-2700440` and `mcalwar.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in`. The state's [local-body directory](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) also lists `nagarparishad.alwar@rajasthan.gov.in` for Alwar. These are published contacts, not separately tested phone or email lines. For a property near the city edge, check which local body serves it before starting a municipal application.

The Rajasthan Local Self Government Department's [online services dashboard](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371475/2390) gives the state entry points. It lists lease deed or patta, section 90A land conversion, mutation or name transfer, building-plan approval, trade licence and renewal, and fire NOC. For property revenue it lists property ID creation, urban development tax and lease money deposit as different services. Follow the manual and checklist for the selected service; a property ID entry is not a tax receipt. This is the state service catalogue, not an Alwar fee or assessment schedule.

## Civil registration and grievances

The state's [Pehchan portal](https://pehchan.rajasthan.gov.in/) offers public application, registration search and certificate download for birth, death and marriage records. Its urban instructions place the birth and death registrar at the municipal headquarters. For a birth or death that occurred in the jurisdiction, the site directs households to report within 21 days and identifies Form 1 for a birth and Form 2 for a death. It also lists e-Mitra kiosks as an application route and publishes `1800-180-6785` as its own help line.

[Rajasthan Sampark](https://sampark.rajasthan.gov.in/) has options to register and track a grievance and gives the state toll-free number `181`. No personal record, complaint or payment was submitted to check these routes.

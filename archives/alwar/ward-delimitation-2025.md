# Alwar ward delimitation gazette for 65 wards

## Approval and publication

The Local Self Government Department dated its notification DLB/25/15541 on 1 September 2025. The Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary, Part 6(क), published it on 16 September 2025. The Alwar Collector forwarded the draft ward formation order, objections and his opinion. The state considered the objections, agreed with his opinion and approved the proposed order. The notification cites sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009, and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994.

The attached Form A gives a Hindi text description and a population figure for each of wards 1 through 65. Ward numbers in the [department's demarcation directory](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) refer to this schedule. The PDF runs 21 pages; the final ward's description continues onto printed folio 1744.

These are examples, not abbreviated legal boundaries. The source uses side-of-road instructions and exceptions at plot level. Follow the complete entry before using a landmark to identify a ward. The Gazette provides neither live population estimates nor a councillor list or polling-place notice.

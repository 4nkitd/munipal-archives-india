# Municipal Corporation Ambala: city and governance profile

## Formation, boundaries and wards

Haryana formed Municipal Corporation Ambala on 17 March 2010 by merging the municipal councils of Ambala City and Ambala Sadar. The combined corporation had 20 wards, 11 in Ambala City and nine in Ambala Sadar. Its first election was held in June 2013.

The state separated the Sadar zone in 2019 and constituted Ambala Sadar Municipal Council on 11 September 2019. The military cantonment remained outside the new council and is administered separately by Ambala Cantonment Board. In 2020, Haryana notified 20 wards for the post-split Ambala City corporation, and the State Election Commission held its general election on 27 December 2020. Haryana issued a new final delimitation notification on 24 December 2025, No. 04/51/2025-4CI. The May 2026 general election covered all 20 wards.

The Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department lists the corporation's area as **67.56 sq km** and population as **264,576**. Its key-figures table does not date the population count. These figures refer to the corporation, not Ambala district, Ambala Sadar Municipal Council or the cantonment.

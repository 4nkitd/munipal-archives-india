# Elected council of Municipal Corporation Ambala

## May 2026 general election

Ambala voted for its mayor and 20 ward councillors on 10 May 2026. Votes were counted on 13 May. The State Election Commission (SEC), Haryana, published [notification SEC/1ME/2026/2368](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2026/05/20260521432943498.pdf) on 21 May.

Akshita Saini of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won the mayoral seat, reserved for Backward Class B women. She received 52,794 votes and defeated Kulwinder Kaur Saini of the Indian National Congress (INC) by 21,358 votes. The BJP won 16 ward seats, the INC won three and an independent won one. The [municipal homepage](https://mcambala.gov.in/index.aspx) lists Akshita Saini as mayor. The [municipal contact page](https://www.mcambala.gov.in/contact_us.aspx) still shows the previous council and was not used for current ward contacts.

## December 2020 general election and March 2025 by-election

The general election was held on 27 December 2020, with results announced on 30 December. Shakti Rani Sharma of the HJCP won the mayoralty by 8,084 votes over Vandana Sharma of the BJP. The SEC notified the mayor and councillors on 1 January 2021. Its 10 February correction changed the listed party for wards 4 and 14 from Independent to HDF. The corrected ward totals were BJP 8, HJCP 7, INC 3 and HDF 2.

The mayoral seat fell vacant after Shakti Rani Sharma was elected as the BJP MLA for Kalka in 2024. In the by-election held on 2 March 2025, Shailja Sachdeva of the BJP received 40,620 votes against 20,133 for Amisha Chawla of the INC, a margin of 20,487. Counting took place on 12 March. The SEC notified Sachdeva's election on 20 March for the remainder of the preceding mayor's term. The Ambala by-election was for mayor only.

## Senior Deputy Mayor and Deputy Mayor

The 2020 council elected Meena Dhingra (BJP, Ward 8) as Senior Deputy Mayor and Rajesh Mehta (then HJCP, Ward 5) as Deputy Mayor on 8 December 2022. Mehta had won Ward 5 on an INC ticket in 2020 and joined the BJP in February 2025. In May 2026, Mehta was returned as a BJP councillor for Ward 5 and Dhingra lost Ward 8 to Gunjeet Kaur.

The deputy posts are elected by the council, separately from the public vote. Reporting on 16 June 2026 said both elections were still pending after the new council took office. No later election result for either post was verified as of 22 November 2026. The previous officeholders should not be treated as holding those posts in the 2026 council.

# Ambala City wards, boundaries and locality index

## Jurisdiction and source date

This record concerns Municipal Corporation Ambala, also described by the district administration as Municipal Corporation, Ambala City. It does not describe Municipal Council Ambala Sadar or Ambala Cantonment Board. The district's [urban local bodies directory](https://ambala.gov.in/urban-local-bodies/) lists Ambala City corporation and Ambala Sadar council separately. The Cantonment Board is a defence cantonment authority, not another name for either Haryana urban local body. An address saying only "Ambala" or "Ambala Cantt" is insufficient to select a ward register.

The primary source is Haryana Urban Local Bodies notification No. 04/51/2025-4CI dated 24 December 2025. It publishes boundaries under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994, with reference to the preliminary notification dated 12 December 2025. The English schedule specifies Wards 1 through 20. It must be read with drawing No. 1173/LFA dated 28 November 2025.

The companion `../wards.json` contains 20 numbered records based on that final schedule. Its boundary summaries are abbreviated finding aids, not legal descriptions or polygons. Village, colony and sector arrays index names encountered on boundaries. They do not certify that an entire named area lies inside that ward. No administrative zone or engineering division allocation was verified in the notification, so those JSON fields are `null`.

## HSVP sectors and named urban localities

The requested HSVP sector names are retained as normalized sector numbers. The notification uses individual houses, roads, institutions and sector edges to draw boundaries. It does not support a simple one-sector-to-one-ward lookup.

## How to identify a property's ward

Use the December 2025 notification and attached drawing together. Start with the actual street and house number, then compare the relevant boundary segment. An address in Sector 8, Model Town or Kanwala can be near more than one ward. A boundary landmark can also be outside a ward whose edge it describes.

For electoral use, confirm the polling part and ward in the applicable final electoral roll. For a municipal complaint or property-tax correction, confirm the corporation property record rather than relying on a commercial locality directory. This archive does not infer councillors, reservation categories, population counts, engineering divisions or service zones from locality names.

There are differences between the English and Hindi text in some coordinate and landmark sequences. For example, Ward 20's English schedule gives the municipal-limit point as 30.34615, 76.75450, while the extracted Hindi text contains a differently printed longitude. Ward 9 also has differences in the Yuvraj Palace route. This record follows the English schedule for its abbreviated summaries and does not attempt to repair or digitize disputed geometry.

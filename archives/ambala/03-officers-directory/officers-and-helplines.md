This record concerns Ambala City Municipal Corporation, not Municipal Council Ambala Sadar or Ambala Cantonment.

## Citizen-service routes

The municipal website links to [Haryana property tax](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/), [ULB public grievances](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/), [SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) for birth and death certificates and [marriage registration](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/). These are online routes, not telephone helplines. No separate verified sanitation or property-tax helpline was found in the current directory; named branch contacts and the municipal landline are provided above.

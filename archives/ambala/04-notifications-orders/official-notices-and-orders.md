# Ambala City official notices, orders and colony notification sources

## Scope and dates

This record covers Municipal Corporation Ambala, whose office is at Jagadhari Gate, Ambala City. The municipal website separately links Municipal Council Ambala Sadar. A district-level reference to "Ambala" does not establish that a colony falls within Ambala City.

The frontmatter date is the latest dated notice observed on the municipal notification index, 18 November 2026. Dates below are the dates displayed by the portal unless expressly identified as dates printed in a notification. Attachment upload folders are not issue dates. This is an index of official publications, not a transcription of each attached order.

## Regularized colony notification register

The state [regularized colony register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) links notification batches dated 25 July 2023, 6 October 2023, 15 December 2023, 1 February 2024, 6 March 2024 and later dates. The register's 26 September 2025 upload date for several documents is not their notification date. Batch totals are statewide, not counts for Ambala City.

The [civic amenities and infrastructure deficient areas register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure) records colony names, revenue estates, drawing numbers, areas, notified khasra numbers, layout links and colony codes. For example, its Ambala-labelled entries under notification 09/45/2024-4C-II dated 6 March 2024 include Nirmal Colony, D.N. 103/2022, 3.890 acres, code AMB145, revenue estate Babyal, and Satnam Colony, D.N. 106/2022, 8.820 acres, code AMB146, also Babyal.

These examples identify the official register's fields. They are not assigned here to Ambala City because the register's broad "Ambala" label needs reconciliation with the separate Ambala Sadar jurisdiction. No verified complete Ambala City colony schedule has been transcribed in this record.

For a particular property, retrieve the notification and layout, compare the revenue estate and exact khasra entry, and confirm municipal jurisdiction. A matching colony name alone is insufficient. A property ID, tax receipt or general newspaper list should not substitute for the notified boundary schedule.

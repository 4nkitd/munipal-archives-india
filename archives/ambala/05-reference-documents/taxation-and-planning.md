# Ambala City property tax, NDC, building code and Lal Dora reference

## Property tax policy and assessment

The [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) calls itself the Property Tax Dues Payment and No Dues Certificate Management System. Municipal Corporation Ambala links this portal from its [official homepage](https://mcambala.in/). Select the municipality matching the property, not another local body in Ambala district.

The portal explicitly links a [tax calculator](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/taxScreen.html) for the new policy dated 30 July 2026. That policy date supplies the frontmatter date. The state [property tax notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists "Property tax notification 2026 Corporation" with publication date 31 July 2026 and a separate notification for councils and committees. Ambala City's corporation status makes the corporation document the relevant starting reference.

The [2026 corporation attachment](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf) is linked for authoritative consultation. Its rate tables have not been extracted here. Older 2020 notifications and historical rebates remain in official indexes, but this record does not present them as the current rate schedule. Confirm the applicable assessment year, property category and current demand before paying.

## Property ID, correction and NDC

The official [portal FAQ](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/FAQ) describes the following workflow:

1. Register with the required personal details and verify the mobile OTP.
2. Under "Search Property", select the municipality. Search using the eight-character property ID, old ID, plot number, owner or occupier name, mobile number, colony or property category. A GIS map search is also available.
3. Open "View Details". Self-certify only correct information using "Yes" and "Verify Details".
4. For incorrect data, choose "No", then "Update Details". Submit corrections and supporting documents using the stated draft workflow. The FAQ says the municipality addresses the resulting objection before self-certification.
5. Under "Make Payment/Generate NDC", enter the property ID, choose "Pay All Dues and Generate NDC", verify the dues and proceed through OTP and payment.

The FAQ lists property and fire tax, development charges and garbage collection charges within the all-dues workflow. Paying only one charge category is not the same as clearing all dues.

| Request | Published FAQ information |
| --- | --- |
| New property ID, normal mode | No charge; 10 working days |
| Priority new/split ID | 2 working days from successful payment |
| Priority fee | English FAQ says INR 1,000; Hindi FAQ says INR 2,500 |

The bilingual fee conflict is unresolved in the source. Confirm the current payable fee with the municipal office or live application before payment. Split-ID requests can concern plot subdivision, floor subdivision or correction of wrongly merged properties. The FAQ's availability of this tax-record service should not be read as planning permission for a subdivision.

## Haryana Building Code 2017 and planning sources

The state [town planning notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/TownPlanningNotifications) lists "Final Haryana Building Code 2017 with letter" and "Adoption of amendments in the Haryana Building Code 2017". Both display 4 January 2021 as index dates. That date does not establish when the original 2017 code was issued. The final-code row has no attachment in the retrieved index; the [amendment-adoption document](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F455f005a-8818-4499-bca7-e903adace365.pdf) is linked.

Ambala's [rules and bye-laws library](https://www.mcambala.in/library/rules-and-bye-laws) also lists Building Bye Laws 1982 and Haryana Municipal Corporation Building Bye-laws 2014. These are distinct dated instruments, not alternative names for the 2017 code.

For a proposed building, consult the applicable code and subsequent amendments alongside the plot's approved layout and relevant planning conditions. No plot-specific FAR, setbacks, height allowance or building-plan exemption has been verified here. Neither tax self-certification nor issuance of an NDC establishes building-plan approval.

## Lal Dora and Abadi Deh property certificate rules

The state property-tax index lists the municipal Abadi Deh/Lal Dora certificate policy on 15 March 2024. An [official policy and SOP copy](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2F9bb08af2-072a-4fff-a20f-a8e54dd3e36e.pdf) carries a covering memo dated 12 March 2024. It invokes section 250 of the Haryana Municipal Act 1973 and section 398 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act 1994.

The policy concerns old inhabited village areas later brought into municipalities where revenue records of rights were not traditionally maintained. Its definition excludes specific khasra-number land. Eligibility rests on acceptable ownership documents or documents indicating possession for the last 10 years.

The process includes boundary identification, publication of property and possession details, claims and objections, hearing, final publication, appeal, municipal resolution and update of the property-tax/NDC records. Objections may be filed within 30 days of notice publication. An appeal against the final possession list goes to the concerned ward committee within 30 days of final publication.

The SOP requires a claimant affidavit attested by the revenue authority and any two listed possession documents establishing the last 10 years, such as electricity bills, water bills, government address documents, property-tax receipts or proof of a constructed structure. Available title documents should also be supplied. A legal-heir certificate is required where the original owner has died.

Public streets, wells, common grounds, burial grounds and drains must remain recorded under municipal ownership. Certificates remain subject to the Transfer of Property Act 1882 and court decisions or decrees. The policy is not a blanket building-rule exemption.

Ambala's [local notice index](https://mcambala.in/services/notification?page=1) lists a certificate-issued list dated 23 June 2025 and final Lal Dora list publication dated 14 August 2025. Check the particular property entry and applicable local notice rather than assuming every property in an old settlement qualifies.

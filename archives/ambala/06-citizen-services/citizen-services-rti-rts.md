# Ambala citizen services, RTI and Right to Service

## Scope and service portals

This guide covers Municipal Corporation Ambala, based at Jagadhari Gate, Ambala City, Haryana 134003. It does not treat Municipal Council Ambala Sadar or the cantonment administration as the same authority. The district administration lists Ambala Corporation and Ambala Cantt separately. Select the correct local body when applying or paying.

The corporation's homepage links the following services. These links were checked as published routes, not by submitting an application or making a payment.

| Requirement | Official route | Citizen action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax and property records | [Haryana ULB property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Locate the Ambala property record, check the demand and pay through the portal; use the relevant correction or ownership-change service if the record is wrong. |
| Birth and death certificates | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Choose the applicable certificate, correction or delayed-registration service. |
| Dog registration and renewal | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Select the ULB dog-registration service or renewal. The notified service names refer to dogs, not all pet species. |
| Marriage registration | [Haryana marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) | Follow the applicable marriage-registration process and retain the application reference. |
| Civic complaint | [ULB grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Record the problem, exact location and supporting evidence; retain the complaint number. |

The [citizen corner](https://www.mcambala.in/services/citizen-corner) lists birth-report forms, Hindi and English death-report forms and marriage-file documents. A reporting form is not itself a certificate. Check the selected service's current documents and fees before applying.

## Property tax, civil registration and marriage

For property matters, preserve the Property ID, demand details, payment receipt and any objection acknowledgement. A tax-register ownership change is a distinct service from payment. The current ULB Right to Service list also separates new Property IDs, property-data objections, ordinary ownership changes and death-related ownership changes.

SARAL lists separate applications for birth-certificate issuance, death-certificate issuance, corrections in birth or death records, and delayed birth or death registration. Choose the service that matches the record's status. Do not assume a duplicate certificate, a correction and registration of an unregistered event have the same procedure or deadline. The current ULB schedule cited below does not provide a general birth/death deadline, so this record does not substitute an older municipal schedule for the relevant current notification.

SARAL issues an Application Reference ID for tracking. Its homepage provides application/appeal tracking and certificate download. Assisted access is available through Antyodaya Kendras, SARAL Kendras and Antyodaya-SARAL Kendras.

The ULB marriage guidance states that registration in Urban Local Bodies is carried out under the Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Act, 2008 and Rules, 2008. Ambala's homepage directs applicants to the Shaadi portal. SARAL also lists urban marriage services under different legal categories. Use the route for the applicable Act rather than treating every marriage service as interchangeable. No single marriage deadline or fee is asserted here.

Rejection must carry written reasons. These are service-delivery limits, not promises that an incomplete application will be approved.

## RTI Act, 2005

Use RTI to request existing municipal records, such as assessment records, expenditure statements, work orders, inspection reports or copies of decisions. Ask for identifiable records and a date range. A request for information is different from asking the corporation to repair a street or approve an application.

Under section 6, submit the request to the concerned State Public Information Officer in writing or electronically, with the prescribed fee where applicable. An applicant need not give reasons for seeking information. No currently verified Ambala SPIO name or Haryana fee amount is asserted in this guide. Confirm the current municipal designation and state fee rules before filing.

| RTI provision | Statutory rule |
| --- | --- |
| Section 6(3) | Transfer to another public authority, where required, as soon as practicable and no later than 5 days; inform the applicant. |
| Section 7(1) | Ordinary response within 30 days of receipt; information concerning life or liberty within 48 hours. |
| Section 5(2) proviso | Add 5 days when the request is submitted through an Assistant Public Information Officer. |
| Section 7(3) | The period between dispatch of an additional-fee notice and payment is excluded from the ordinary response calculation. |
| Section 7(6) | Information is supplied free of charge when the authority fails to comply with the statutory time limits. |
| Section 19(1) | First appeal to the public authority's senior appellate officer within 30 days of the decision or expiry of the response period, subject to admission of delayed appeals for sufficient cause. |
| Section 19(6) | First appeal decided within 30 days, extendable to a total of 45 days with written reasons. |
| Section 19(3) | Second appeal to the State Information Commission within 90 days of the date the decision should have been made or was received, subject to admission for sufficient cause. |

Ambala's homepage service card sends RTI applicants to SARAL, but its footer RTI link points to a Manesar page. That footer is not evidence of Ambala's SPIO or appellate authority. Confirm that any online form actually accepts Municipal Corporation Ambala before paying or submitting. A written request to the corporation's current SPIO remains a separate route under the Act.

## Grievances and Samadhan camps

The current Ambala homepage directs public complaints to the ULB grievance portal. The older [municipal grievance form](https://www.mcambala.gov.in/Grievances_master.aspx) asks for a brief description and specific location, up to 2,000 characters, an address up to 256 characters, and optional PDF or image evidence. Use the current homepage route first; this archive does not establish that the older form still accepts submissions.

For follow-up, keep the complaint number, submission date, ward or locality, photographs where useful, and earlier correspondence. Contact the municipal office at 0171-2441077 or cmc-ambala@ulbharyana.gov.in. The ULB department lists 1800-8900-929 for IT support and grievance registration, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Samadhan Shivirs provide an in-person grievance channel at district and subdivision levels. A Tribune report dated 13 January 2026 records an Ambala camp at the Deputy Commissioner's office and directions to upload complaints on the portal. A Jagran report dated 26 February 2026 describes camps on Mondays and Thursdays from 10 AM to noon. An older report dated 1 January 2025 described every-working-day camps. These are dated reports, not a verified standing schedule for November 2026. Confirm the current venue, days and hours with the administration before travelling. Bring the application or complaint reference and supporting documents, and request an acknowledgement.

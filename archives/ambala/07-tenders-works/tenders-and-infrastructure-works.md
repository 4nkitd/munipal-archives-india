# Ambala tenders and infrastructure works, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and tender verification

This record covers Municipal Corporation Ambala City and identifies adjoining Ambala Sadar or district flood-protection works separately. An announcement, tender estimate or foundation stone is not evidence of expenditure or completion. News-reported figures below retain their publication dates and attribution.

The [Haryana Engineering Works Portal live-tender page](https://works.haryana.gov.in/LiveTender.aspx) states that HEWP tenders are published on the [NIC Haryana eProcurement portal](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app) for bidder participation. They appear in HEWP's live list on the next day after NIC publication. Search by tender ID or reference number and check the organisation chain, BOQ, corrigenda and award record. Ambala City notices can use the organisation label `Urban Local Bodies > MC Amballa`.

The [MC Ambala tender register](https://www.mcambala.in/tender) supplies tender IDs, descriptions, notice dates, expiry dates and linked documents. Its dates are register metadata, not proof that a contract was awarded. The following examples were visible on verification.

## Patvi solid-waste facility and processing procurement

The Tribune's [22 January 2025 report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/pollution-board-warns-ambala-mc-over-solid-waste-plant-violations/) records an HSPCB show-cause notice concerning the Patvi municipal solid-waste processing facility. Reported deficiencies included missing statutory permissions, inadequate waste records, unmanaged leachate and inadequate green-belt development. The board directed preparation of an action plan for fresh and legacy waste and installation of an adequately sized effluent treatment plant. This is a compliance report, not confirmation that remedial works were completed.

The [29 June 2025 follow-up](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/ambala-mc-fined-rs-62-lakh-for-violating-waste-rules/) reported interim environmental compensation of Rs 62 lakh, calculated at Rs 1 lakh per month for 1 April 2020 to 31 May 2025, with payment required within 15 days. It reported approximately 45,000 metric tonnes of dumped waste. Payment or closure of the proceedings is not established here.

An [official MC processing RFP](https://mcambala.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/tender1_7a23dc.pdf), notice `MCA/CSI/2026/5228` dated 21 September 2026, invited an agency to process daily generated municipal solid waste of all ULBs in Ambala district for 10 years. The indexed notice lists an estimated project cost of Rs 9.97 crore, total daily waste generation of 297.25 tonnes, a document fee of Rs 25,000 and bid submission from 22 September to 13 October 2026 at 3 pm. These are procurement figures, not measured Patvi operating performance or a verified award. The PDF also carries a revised-model-RFP date of 10 June 2025; that date should not be confused with the local invitation date.

The November 2026 collection-and-processing RFP in the register is a separate tender entry. Its relationship to the September invitation, including any cancellation or retendering, is not established by the register alone.

## City stormwater drainage and regional flood mitigation

The Haryana Government's [13 August 2026 release](https://prms.prharyana.gov.in/press-release/4109) confirms administrative sanction of Rs 12.42 crore for a stormwater drain along Old Delhi Road, from Jandli Flyover to Polytechnic Chowk in Ambala City. Technical approval had already been received. The release does not establish completion. Its separate Rs 80 crore allocation for urban drainage and flood control in 2026-27 is statewide, not an Ambala project total.

The Tribune [reported on 30 April 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/4-new-pump-houses-in-ambala-city-to-help-in-preventing-waterlogging/) that PHED was establishing four pump houses at Jaraut Road, Jalbera Road, Dashmesh Market and Inderpuri for around Rs 18 crore. The same report attributed a purchase cost of around Rs 2.45 crore to an MC super-sucker-cum-jetting machine deployed for drain cleaning. The pump-house report describes construction activity, not certified commissioning.

Regional works must not be charged to the City corporation without project-level evidence:

## Roads, streets and lighting

An [official NIC tender record](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app?component=%24DirectLink&page=FrontEndLatestActiveTenders&service=direct&sp=SgteCrRAw%2FQxuU4lSkL27eA%3D%3D) identifies `2025_HRY_439853_1`, published 10 April 2025, for raising the street and drain from house 2578 Chopra to NBM International in Kaziwara, ward 6. The estimate is Rs 7,56,143, EMD Rs 15,120 and stipulated work period 210 days. Submission closed on 17 April 2025 at noon. A corrigendum records retendering because minimum bids were not received. This notice therefore does not establish an awarded or completed street project.

The Tribune's [23 August 2025 lighting report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/over-24000-new-led-lights-to-illuminate-ambalas-dark-spots/) states that installation of over 24,000 remaining LED lights had begun. An MC official said the procurement work order was issued in October 2023, lights arrived in February 2024 and over 3,000 had already been installed. The reported total project cost was around Rs 35 crore.

The report describes approximately 18,000 replacement locations, 6,000 new locations, 95 new high-mast lights and a proposed central monitoring system at the MC office. Completion before Diwali was a target, not a verified outcome. These figures concern the reported overall lighting programme and must not be presented as the value or completion status of any single installation tender.

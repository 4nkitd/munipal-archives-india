# Ambala municipal budgets and civic governance, FY 2024-25 to FY 2026-27

## Scope and financial-year coverage

This record concerns Municipal Corporation Ambala City. It does not combine the corporation's finances with Municipal Council Ambala Sadar, the cantonment board, district expenditure, or Haryana government project announcements. FY 2024-25 runs from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025. The next two financial years end on 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2027.

The figures below come from dated reporting of municipal meetings, not an extracted, signed annual budget book. Estimated income, approved expenditure, expenditure reported for an earlier period, and tender values are different measures. None should be treated as audited actual expenditure without the underlying accounts.

| Financial year | Meeting or evidence date | Reported amount | Evidence qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-25 | 11 March 2024 | Estimated income nearly Rs 144 crore; expenditure Rs 162 crore | The Tribune reported both figures. Hindustan Times independently reported a Rs 162 crore approved budget. |
| 2025-26 | 7 April 2025 | Rs 181.19 crore in Amar Ujala's meeting report | A later Amar Ujala report also described the budget as Rs 181 crore. Haribhoomi instead reported Rs 178 crore. Signed estimates and meeting minutes are needed to reconcile this difference. |
| 2026-27 | Research checked 22 November 2026 | Not verified | No reliable annual income/expenditure total or signed budget statement was located. D Plan 2026-27 tender labels do not establish the corporation's total annual budget. |

The frontmatter date is the FY 2025-26 meeting date, derived from the report published on 8 April 2025 describing Monday's meeting. It is not the date of a verified official budget notification.

## FY 2024-25 budget and House proceedings

The Tribune's report dated 12 March 2024 states that the House passed the FY 2024-25 budget at Ambala Club on 11 March. Estimated income was nearly Rs 144 crore against expenditure of Rs 162 crore. The approximate difference is Rs 18 crore, but the report does not explain opening balances, grants, financing, or the treatment of carried-forward funds. It is therefore not sufficient evidence of a cash deficit or borrowing requirement.

Mayor Shakti Rani Sharma said amendments had increased allocations under several heads, with sanitation, cleanliness and hygiene among the priorities. Both The Tribune and Hindustan Times described arguments during the meeting. These are reports of proceedings, not findings of financial misconduct.

## FY 2025-26 allocations and unresolved total

Amar Ujala's 8 April 2025 report gives an approved total of approximately Rs 181 crore 19 lakh and describes a budget meeting followed by an ordinary meeting with 14 agenda items. It reports that Congress councillors sought discussion, while Mayor Shailja Sachdeva maintained that the agenda had passed unanimously.

Haribhoomi's report of the same meeting gives Rs 178 crore and states that each ward would receive Rs 1 crore for roads, split between Rs 75 lakh for construction and Rs 25 lakh for repairs. Preserve this as an attributed report, not a reconciled official allocation.

Amar Ujala's follow-up published on 10 April 2025 supplies these selected FY 2025-26 provisions:

| Spending head | Reported provision |
| --- | --- |
| Door-to-door collection, transport and waste disposal | Rs 13.30 crore |
| Corporation office building maintenance and repairs | Rs 1 crore |
| Public parking and stadium maintenance and repairs | Rs 20 lakh |
| Park maintenance and repairs | Rs 3 crore |
| Vehicle maintenance and repairs | Rs 20 lakh |
| Cremation-ground maintenance and repairs | Rs 50 lakh |
| Toilet maintenance and repairs | Rs 30 lakh |
| Street lights, traffic signals and CCTV maintenance | Rs 3 crore |
| Roads and lanes maintenance | Rs 5 crore |
| Stray-dog sterilisation | Rs 1 crore |

These are selected newspaper-transcribed heads, not the complete budget. Do not add them to infer the annual total. The report's figures for expenditure in an earlier period lack sufficient accounting context to reproduce here as audited actuals.

## Official records needed for legal or financial reliance

The official corporation tender page separately identifies current procurement, including the waste collection and processing RFP numbered `2026_HRY_559513_2`, dated 10 November 2026. A tender listing proves publication, not award, payment or completion.

For reconciliation, request the FY 2024-25, FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27 signed annual estimates, revised estimates, budget adoption resolutions and minutes, grant schedules, opening balances, and annual accounts from the corporation. Ask specifically whether Rs 178 crore and Rs 181.19 crore refer to different proposal stages or different accounting totals. This archive does not assume an answer.

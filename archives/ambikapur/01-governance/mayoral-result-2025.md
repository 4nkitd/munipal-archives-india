# Ambikapur mayoral election result, 2025

## Vote by party

The Chhattisgarh State Election Commission marks Manjusha Bhagat (मंजूषा भगत) as elected mayor for Ambikapur Municipal Corporation, Surguja. The corporation-wide tally lists six candidates and a separate line for none of the above (NOTA).

| Party or ballot option | Votes |
| --- | ---: |
| Bharatiya Janata Party | 42,836 |
| Indian National Congress | 31,773 |
| Hamar Raj Party | 361 |
| Bahujan Samaj Party | 340 |
| Aam Aadmi Party | 251 |
| Gondwana Gantantra Party | 236 |
| NOTA | 431 |

The winning margin was 11,063 votes. The seven lines add to 76,228 votes including NOTA. These are calculations from the tally, not turnout figures. The [separate list of elected mayors and presidents](https://cgsec.gov.in/Notification2025/election/Mayor-president%202025.pdf#page=1) also names Bhagat.

## Election dates and scope

The [commission's 15 February 2025 release](https://cgsec.gov.in/15022025_2.html) dates the election schedule to 20 January, polling to 11 February, and counting and release of results to 15 February 2025. This record reports the mayoral result at that election. For the separately elected ward councillors, use the [2025 ward return](../02-wards-geography/ward-results-2025.md).

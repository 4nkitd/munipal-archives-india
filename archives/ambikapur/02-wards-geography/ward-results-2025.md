# Ambikapur ward and councillor returns, 2025

## Finding a ward in the election return

The Chhattisgarh State Election Commission's list of elected councillors gives Ambikapur Municipal Corporation 48 numbered wards in the 2025 return. The Ambikapur entries are statewide serials 1259 to 1306. [Page 22](https://cgsec.gov.in/Notification2025/election/Councillor%202025.pdf#page=22) contains wards 1 to 29; [page 23](https://cgsec.gov.in/Notification2025/election/Councillor%202025.pdf#page=23) contains wards 30 to 48. Search by the ward number column, not the statewide serial column.

In all 48 rows, 31 winners are listed for the Bharatiya Janata Party, 15 for the Indian National Congress and two as independents. The table above is a selection, with names transliterated from the commission's Hindi list. The commission [reported counting and release of results on 15 February 2025](https://cgsec.gov.in/15022025_2.html). The return does not give street boundaries, and this record does not treat the election winners as a current contact directory.

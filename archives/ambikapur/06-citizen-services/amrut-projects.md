# Ambikapur water, sewerage and pond projects under AMRUT 2.0

## Approved projects and reported milestones

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs lists three approved projects for Ambikapur under AMRUT 2.0. The city's [aggregate entry on the state list](https://amrut.mohua.gov.in/approvedProjects/ulb/UnpjT2xQTEgxbzZQdGFSWndoQ1RhZz09) gives a combined project cost of ₹126.95 crore.

| Project | Type | Cost (₹ crore) | Milestone shown |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Rejuvenation of Mahamaya Talab | Water body rejuvenation | 5.30 | DPR Stage |
| Ambikapur 24x7 Water Supply Project | Water supply | 17.25 | DPR Approved |
| Ambikapur Sewage Master Plan | Sewerage and septage | 104.40 | Contract Awarded |

DPR means detailed project report. The 24x7 wording is part of the water project title; its approval does not establish that continuous supply is available at any address. The list does not show ward coverage, contract dates, completion dates or utility tariffs.

## Separate AMRUT programme snapshot

The ministry's [older AMRUT programme table, as of 2 March 2026](https://amrut.mohua.gov.in/assets/City-wise-Projects-grounded-under-AMRUT-as-updated-by-States-as-on-02.03.2026.pdf), lists five grounded water projects at ₹101.67 crore, two sewerage and septage projects at ₹0.43 crore, and 27 parks projects at ₹3.26 crore for Ambikapur. Those are counts and costs for the separate AMRUT table. They are not part of the three-project AMRUT 2.0 total above.

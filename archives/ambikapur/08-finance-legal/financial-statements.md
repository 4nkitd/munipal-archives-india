# Ambikapur municipal financial statements and 2021–22 snapshot

## Provisional figures for 2021–22

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' City Finance dashboard attributes its headline figures to Ambikapur's provisional financial statements for 2021–22. The dashboard shows 26 August 2024 as its last update and displays headline amounts in whole crore.

| Indicator | Displayed value (₹ crore) |
| --- | ---: |
| Total tax revenue | 7 |
| Total own revenue | 19 |
| Total grant | 25 |
| Total revenue | 47 |
| Total expenditure | 48 |
| Total balance sheet size | 407 |

The figures are neither budget estimates nor a statement of current finances. Because the headline figures are rounded, the ₹1 crore difference between displayed revenue and expenditure is not a precise deficit figure. Tax revenue is not a separate amount to add to total own revenue.

## Financial years listed

City Finance lists 2021–22, 2020–21, 2019–20, 2017–18 and 2016–17. The selector skips 2018–19. This inventory describes the years available on the dashboard, not whether Ambikapur kept accounts in omitted years. For line items, open the dashboard's Balance Sheet & Income Statement tab and select the relevant year.

# Amravati municipal election 2025-26: wards, seats and results

## City result

Row 24 of the Maharashtra State Election Commission's final party-position statement covers Amravati Municipal Corporation. Its 22 wards are electoral areas; 87 is the number of council seats returned from those areas. The statement counts 661 contesting candidates. It gives a city total rather than the boundaries, names or seat allocation of individual wards.

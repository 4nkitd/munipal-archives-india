# Amravati corporation office and district directory

## Corporation office

The district administration lists Amravati Municipal Corporation at PIN 444601. Its published office telephone is **0721-2561569**.

## Which local body to contact

This entry is for the city corporation. The [district municipalities directory](https://amravati.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists Achalpur separately under municipal councils. For a property or municipal service in one of those council areas, use that council's entry. A district address alone does not place a property in the corporation's jurisdiction.

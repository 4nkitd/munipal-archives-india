# Amravati complaints, property tax and Right to Service applications

## Report and track a complaint

The city gives **0721-2672300** for sanitation complaints. Its [grievance portal](https://crm.amravaticorporation.in/) is the online route for registering a complaint and tracking it. For disaster management, the city lists a separate control room at 0721-2576426 and toll-free 101.

## Applications and bills

The [property tax service](https://ptaxservices.amravaticorporation.in/) is for city property tax. The city directs market and licence applications to its [Right to Service portal](https://rts.amravaticorporation.in/app/FrmDeptServices.aspx). For water bills it links separately to [Maharashtra Jeevan Pradhikaran](https://mjpwms.in/viewpay.aspx). A complaint filed with the grievance portal is distinct from an application for a licence.

## Revenue receipts, part 1, page 1

| Revenue head | Account | Revised estimate 2024-25 (Rs) | Recommended 2025-26 (Rs) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tax on buildings | 1 | 72,00,00,000 | 74,76,00,000 |

The two columns compare estimates. For example, the original building-tax estimate for 2024-25 was Rs 123 crore; the revised estimate was Rs 72 crore. Neither Rs 72 crore nor the 2025-26 recommendation is a statement of audited collections.

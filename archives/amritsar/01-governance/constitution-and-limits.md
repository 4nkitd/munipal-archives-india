# Constitution of Amritsar Municipal Corporation and alteration of city limits

The [corporation history](https://amritsarcorp.com/about.htm) traces the Town Committee to 1858 and the Municipal Committee to 1868. On 29 March 1977 Amritsar was declared a city under the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, and the corporation replaced the committee. The first corporation election took place in 1991 with 50 wards; the 1997 election used 60 and the 2007 election used 65.

The corporation reproduces [section 3 of the Act](https://amritsarcorp.com/punjact/act01.htm) as substituted by Act 2 of 1994. Section 3(5) carried pre-amendment cities and their corporations forward: it deemed those cities to be larger urban areas and their corporations duly constituted under the amended Act. The source for Amritsar’s 1977 formation date is the municipal history; the original notification was not verified.

Under the 1994 text, sections 3(1) and (2) provide for the state government to specify a larger urban area and constitute its corporation by Gazette notification. Section 3(1) excludes military cantonments. Section 3(6) permits the government to alter limits after consultation with the corporation and previous publication in the Gazette. The section does not identify Amritsar’s street or village boundaries.

The [district public-utility directory](https://amritsar.nic.in/public-utility/mcamritsar/) identifies the corporation at C Block, Ranjit Avenue, PIN 143001 and links its website. The [municipal contact page](https://amritsarcorp.com/contact.htm) gives the office email as cmcasr@punjab.gov.in.

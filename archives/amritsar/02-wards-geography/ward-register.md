# Wards 1–85: polling booth lists and councillor directory

## 2024 polling records

The [district election page](https://amritsar.nic.in/municipal-corporation-elections-2024/) groups corporation polling booths by ward: [1–15](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c3992e9a68c5ae12bd18488bc579b30d/uploads/2024/12/2024121947.pdf); [16–29](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c3992e9a68c5ae12bd18488bc579b30d/uploads/2024/12/2024121962.pdf); [30–43](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c3992e9a68c5ae12bd18488bc579b30d/uploads/2024/12/2024121974.pdf); [44–57](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c3992e9a68c5ae12bd18488bc579b30d/uploads/2024/12/2024121989.pdf); [58–71](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c3992e9a68c5ae12bd18488bc579b30d/uploads/2024/12/2024121934.pdf); [72–85](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c3992e9a68c5ae12bd18488bc579b30d/uploads/2024/12/2024121942.pdf). It publishes a separate [list of changed polling booths](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c3992e9a68c5ae12bd18488bc579b30d/uploads/2024/12/2024122037.pdf). Majitha, Ajnala, Baba Bakala Sahib, Rayya and Raja Sansi have separate entries on the same page; their ward numbers are not Amritsar corporation wards.

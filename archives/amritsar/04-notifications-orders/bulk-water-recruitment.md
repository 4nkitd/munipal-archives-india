# Bulk water supply project: three contractual PIU posts

The [municipal recruitment notice](https://amritsarcorp.com/jobs.htm) invited applications on a contractual basis for the project implementation unit of the Amritsar Bulk Water Supply Scheme. It listed one post for each role:

- [Site Engineer for OHSRs (Junior Engineer)](https://amritsarcorp.com/job101.htm)
- [Site Engineer (Assistant Engineer)](https://amritsarcorp.com/job102.htm)
- [Health & Safety Specialist](https://amritsarcorp.com/job103.htm)

The [corporation homepage announcement](https://amritsarcorp.com/) gave the application deadline as 23 October 2026 at 5:00 p.m. The vacancy listing does not identify a publication day or report any selections.

# Property tax, water bills and the municipal complaint cell

The [corporation homepage](https://amritsarcorp.com/) directs residents to [Punjab mSeva](https://mseva.lgpunjab.gov.in) for property tax and water/sewerage payments. Its [public water and sewerage connection search](https://mseva.lgpunjab.gov.in/citizen/withoutAuth/wns/public-search) opens without a login. The corporation’s [Online Complaints link](https://mseva.lgpunjab.gov.in/citizen/user/login) opened a location-selection page when checked. The [mSeva landing page](https://mseva.lgpunjab.gov.in/common) lists Public Grievance and Citizen Login. Complaint submission and payment were not tested.

The [municipal officers directory](https://amritsarcorp.com/officers.htm) gives the complaint cell landline as 0183-2822222, toll-free number as 1800 180 2103, WhatsApp as +91 99644 94000 and email as complaintsmca@yahoo.com.

The same undated directory lists Bikramjit Singh Shergill as Commissioner (office: 0183-2505450) and Jai Inder Singh as Additional Commissioner (office: 0183-2822226). These are directory entries, not dated appointment orders. The [municipal contact page](https://amritsarcorp.com/contact.htm) lists cmcasr@punjab.gov.in at C Block, Ranjit Avenue.

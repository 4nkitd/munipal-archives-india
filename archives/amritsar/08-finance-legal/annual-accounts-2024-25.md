# Amritsar annual accounts: financial year 2024–25

The [corporation accounts index](https://amritsarcorp.com/accounts.php) groups disclosures by financial year. For [2024–25](https://amritsarcorp.com/fy2024-25), it lists:

- [Audit report](https://amritsarcorp.com/AccountsData/fy2024-25/AUDIT_REPORT_2024-25.pdf)
- [Balance sheet](https://amritsarcorp.com/AccountsData/fy2024-25/Balance_Sheet_2024-25.pdf)
- [Cash flow](https://amritsarcorp.com/AccountsData/fy2024-25/CASH_FLOW_2024-25.pdf)
- [Income and expenditure](https://amritsarcorp.com/AccountsData/fy2024-25/income_exp_2024-25.pdf)
- [Balance sheet annexure](https://amritsarcorp.com/AccountsData/fy2024-25/Balance_Sheet_Annex_2024-25.pdf)
- [Liabilities as on 31 March 2025](https://amritsarcorp.com/AccountsData/fy2024-25/LIABILITIES_31-03-2025.pdf)

For income and spending during the year, use the income and expenditure statement. For the year-end position, use the balance sheet, annexure and liabilities schedule. The date on the liabilities schedule describes the balance date; the index gives no publication day.

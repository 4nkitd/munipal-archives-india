# Anand's larger urban area

Gujarat Gazette Part IV-A, Extra No. 5, notification GV-13 specified the larger urban area under Article 243Q(2) of the Constitution. Schedule I lists the municipalities of Anand, Vallabh Vidhyanagar and Karamsad. Schedule II lists the gram panchayats of Mogri, Jitodiya, Gamdi and Lambhvel, using the Gazette spellings.

These are constituent local bodies at formation. Neither schedule assigns electoral ward numbers or establishes today's ward boundaries.

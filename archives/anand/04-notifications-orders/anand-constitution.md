# Constitution of the Municipal Corporation of the City of Anand

In Gujarat Gazette Part I-A, Extra No. 9, notification KV-14 of 2025 constituted the corporation with effect from 1 January. It applied the remaining provisions of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949 to the new body on the same day.

Clause (iii) appointed Praveen Chaudhary as administrator for one year or until the first councillors were elected, whichever came first. The order excluded government land in the constituent areas from vesting in the corporation. The initial appointment does not identify a current officeholder.

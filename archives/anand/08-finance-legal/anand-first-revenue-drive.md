# Anand shop rent and tax collections

The district's 6 March release covers collections from 1 January through 3 March 2025. It reports ₹17,11,754 in shop rent, ₹4.41 crore in property tax and ₹1.85 crore in business tax. These are collections reported during the drive, not audited annual receipts.

The release gives 7,708 as the total number of residents sent notices. Its figures for Anand (3,600), Vidyanagar (3,900) and Karamsad (308) add up to 7,808. The release does not resolve the 100-notice difference. Its extended counter hours applied only through 31 March 2025.

# Ananthapuramu Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## Which local body this is

The district administration lists **Anantapuramu Municipal Corporation** separately from the local bodies for Guntakal, Kalyandurg, Rayadurg, Tadipatri and Gooty. The state municipal portal uses **Anantapur**, while the State Election Commission's 2026 ward results use **Ananthapuramu** for both the district and the urban local body. The archive uses `anantapur` for the corporation. It does not cover all of Ananthapuramu district or treat the other municipal bodies as parts of the corporation.

## Office and boundary records

The [district public utility entry](https://ananthapuramu.ap.gov.in/public-utility/anantapuramu-municipal-corporation-2/) publishes the office address as **Near Sapthagiri Circle, Anantapur 515001** and the office phone as **08554-230234**. For online services use the [Anantapur municipal portal](https://anantapur.cdma.ap.gov.in/), rather than selecting a municipality just because it is in Ananthapuramu district.

The [2026 ward election record](../02-wards-geography/2026-ward-election-record.md) documents the corporation's electoral ward numbers. The district directory and election report do not describe the legal municipal perimeter. This archive has not verified a formation or extension notification or a legal boundary schedule for an individual address.

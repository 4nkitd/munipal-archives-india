# Ananthapuramu electoral wards in the November 2026 corporation election

## Ward numbers and reservations

The Andhra Pradesh State Election Commission's Proforma X, dated 4 November 2026, lists **62 wards numbered 1 through 62** under Ananthapuramu as both district and urban local body. Its city entries start on PDF page 125 and continue through page 134. This table groups the ward numbers by the report's reservation labels for that election.

## Poll dates and map limits

The report gives 1 November 2026 as the poll date and shows both 1 and 2 November for ward 30. The separate [repoll order](../04-notifications-orders/2026-ward-30-repoll.md) applies to polling station 30/3 only.

Proforma X records electoral results and reservation categories, not the streets or villages making up each ward. The [city map](https://anantapur.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/anantapur/gis-map) is a separate portal. Polling station numbers and Sachivalayam names are not substitutes for an official delimitation schedule.

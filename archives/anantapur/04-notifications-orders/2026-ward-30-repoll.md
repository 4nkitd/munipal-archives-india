# Ananthapuramu ward 30 repoll order for polling station 30/3

## What the order changed

In order **1487/SEC-F1/2026-1**, dated **1 November 2026**, the Andhra Pradesh State Election Commission declared the poll held that day at **polling station 30/3, ward 30**, void. The order cites the Ananthapuramu District Election Authority report **Rc.No.Ele/3571/2026**, also dated 1 November, which reported booth capturing at that station.

The Commission directed a fresh poll for both the mayor and the ward member at that station on **2 November 2026, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.** It did not void the poll at other stations in ward 30. The order invoked Article 243ZA of the Constitution and Section 59A(2)(a) of the Municipal Corporations Act, 1955, and directed that the fresh poll follow the 2026 rules for the election of mayor and ward members.

## Notice and later return

The Commission directed the District Election Authority and Municipal Commissioner, acting as Additional Election Authority, to inform contesting candidates in writing and publicize the repoll in the polling area and in print and electronic media. It directed polling staff to mark the left middle finger with indelible ink. The [ward return](../02-wards-geography/2026-ward-election-record.md) later records both dates for ward 30.

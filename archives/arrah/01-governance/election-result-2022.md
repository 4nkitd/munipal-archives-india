# Ara Nagar Nigam election result of 30 December 2022

## Declaration under rule 87

The returning officer recorded the result at Ara on 30 December 2022 using Form 26, Appendix 9, under rule 87 of the Bihar Municipal Election Rules, 2007. The two corporation-wide entries precede the numbered ward entries. Indu Devi (इन्दु देवी) was elected chief councillor and Poonam Devi (पूनम देवी) deputy chief councillor.

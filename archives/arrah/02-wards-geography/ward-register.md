# Arrah municipal wards: boundaries and census population

## Municipal jurisdiction

The district lists Ara Nagar Nigam at Old Police Line, Ara, PIN 802301. The State Election Commission lists it as an unchanged municipal corporation in its final ward register. The [commission’s municipal register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0) lists 45 wards. Form 6, prescribed under rule 29(1), describes each ward separately. The population column uses the 2011 Census.

The Hindi boundary descriptions follow the published entries. The directional labels पू, प, उ and द indicate east, west, north and south.

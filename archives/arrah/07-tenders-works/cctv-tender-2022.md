# Ara municipal CCTV supply and maintenance tender

## Equipment and records

Ara Nagar Nigam sought an agency to supply, install and maintain cameras, poles, solar panels, solar lighting and backup power. The central server was to retain footage for at least 30 days. The notice required the agency to release recordings only to officials authorised by the head of the administration concerned.

## Bid schedule for 14 November 2022

| Stage | Time and place |
|---|---|
| Last submission | 3:00 pm, Municipal Commissioner's office, Ara |
| Technical opening | 4:00 pm, Collectorate, Ara |
| Financial opening for technically qualified bidders | From 4:30 pm, Collectorate, Ara |

The notice required an English-language bid and a ₹1,00,000 earnest money deposit by demand draft in favour of the Municipal Commissioner, Ara. The listed dates concern the invitation; the notice does not identify a winning bidder.

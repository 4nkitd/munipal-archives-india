# Depopara Kali Madir to Railway Boundary: Ward 30 road works

## Location and scope

Memo 475/WS/Eng/2026, dated 4 November 2026, describes strengthening the bituminous road from Depopara Kali Madir to Railway Boundary in Ward 30, Borough III. The document labels this “3rd Call (Part-2)” and retains the earlier e-tender reference 69/WS/Eng/2026 dated 16 January 2026. The scheme moved from serial 2 in the second call to serial 1 in the third call. These references distinguish a reissued scheme from a new road project.

## Scheme terms

| Item | Tender schedule |
|---|---:|
| Estimated amount | ₹14,84,117 |
| Earnest money | ₹29,682 |
| Price shown for bid documents and annexures | ₹1,000 |
| Completion period | 180 days |
| Defect liability | 5 years |

The note below the schedule says bidders need not pay the document cost to participate; the successful lowest bidder pays for two sets when purchasing documents for the agreement. The named endpoints locate the project, not the electoral boundary of Ward 30.

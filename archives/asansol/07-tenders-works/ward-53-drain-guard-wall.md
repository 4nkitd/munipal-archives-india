# Ward 53 high-drain guard wall: sealed tender

## Estimate and submission

The notice describes a masonry guard wall for the high drain near Dr. P. K. Roy Chamber, Ward 53, Borough V. The estimate was ₹97,047, earnest money ₹1,941, paper cost ₹250 and completion period 20 days. The state tender index posted the notice on 20 November 2026. The tender number is 13/PW/ENG/2026, dated 19 November on the notice.

| Stage | Date and time in the notice |
|---|---|
| Applications | 20 to 27 November 2026, up to 2 pm |
| Issue of tender papers by cash section | 1 December 2026, up to 2 pm |
| Receipt of completed papers | 3 December 2026, up to 2 pm |
| Scheduled opening | 3 December 2026, 3 pm |

The earnest money had to accompany the sealed tender as a bank draft in favour of Asansol Municipal Corporation. Applicants were asked for copies of a valid trade licence and GST documentation. These are invitation terms; the notice records neither an award price nor completion of the work.

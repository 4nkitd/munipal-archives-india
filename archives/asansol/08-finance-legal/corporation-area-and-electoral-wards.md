# Asansol: corporation limits and electoral wards

## Municipal territory

The state directory gives Asansol a municipal area of 326.48 km², 106 wards and ten boroughs. A borough number in a works notice identifies an administrative grouping; the tender’s road endpoints describe the work site, not the whole ward. See the [state corporation directory](https://udma.wb.gov.in/modal-content/1) and [local authority record](https://paschimbardhaman.gov.in/public-utility/asansol-municipal-corporation/).

## Which record defines the boundary?

Section 403(d) expressly names the Asansol Municipal Corporation Act, 1990. It preserves earlier assessments, sanctioned plans and permissions that were in force immediately before commencement, for their existing term unless later altered or withdrawn. For an older Asansol property file, the original assessment or sanction may therefore matter even when it cites the 1990 Act.

The Kolkata Gazette Extraordinary of 29 May 2008 published West Bengal Act XXXIX of 2006 under notification 957-L. Chapter II distinguishes the declaration of intention, its publication, consideration of objections and constitution of the corporation area (sections 3–6). Section 7 concerns the number of wards; section 8 deals with abolishing or altering the limits of a corporation area. The publication date is that of the Act, not a redrawing of Asansol’s wards.

The [SEC Municipal Election Manual 2015](https://wbsec.wb.gov.in/writereaddata/Upload/Portal/Publication/Municipal_Election_Manual_201520241203083924.pdf), printed page 19, reproduces section 3 of the West Bengal Municipal Elections Act, 1994. The State Government determines the ward count; the Commission divides the area by notification, considering population, dwelling pattern, geography and economic conditions. For an existing municipality it considers the municipality’s views. Each ward is a single-member constituency. To establish which side of a street was in a ward at a particular election, use the delimitation notification for that election. Neither the ward count nor this Act supplies that street schedule.

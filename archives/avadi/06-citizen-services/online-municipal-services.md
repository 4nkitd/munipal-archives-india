# Avadi online municipal services

## Payments, receipts and registration

Avadi's E-Governance page lists property tax, water supply, profession tax, non-tax items, underground drainage, trade licensing, and birth and death registration among the Urban Tree Information System modules. The [corporation home page](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/avadi/) points to the [state municipal ePay portal](https://tnurbanepay.tn.gov.in/) for online payments and birth certificates.

The state portal shows Quick Payment, Payment Verification Status, Service Request Status Verification and Download Certificate. Use the assessment for Avadi, not a Chennai corporation assessment. The corporation lists its Commissioner Office at N.M. Road, Avadi 600054. The public portal and office details were checked; no payment, certificate download or service request was submitted.

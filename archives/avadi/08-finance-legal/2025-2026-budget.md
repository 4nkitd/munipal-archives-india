# Avadi corporation budget, 2025-2026

## Publication and estimates

Avadi's [Budget Details register](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/avadi/budget-details-2/) lists **27 February 2025** as the council approval and publication date. The budget compares the FY 2024-2025 revised estimate with the FY 2025-2026 budget estimate.

The FY 2025-2026 budget estimate on PDF page 23 shows the following amounts in ₹ crore.

| Fund | Receipts | Outlays | Balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue and capital | 386.11 | 393.88 | -7.77 |
| Water supply and drainage | 375.94 | 380.60 | -4.66 |
| Elementary education | 11.86 | 8.02 | 3.84 |
| Total | 773.91 | 782.50 | -8.59 |

For comparison, PDF page 22 gives a FY 2024-2025 revised estimate of ₹492.94 crore in receipts, ₹505.26 crore in outlays and a ₹12.32 crore deficit. Neither table reports audited actuals or ward-level amounts.

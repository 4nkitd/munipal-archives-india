# Formation and original limits of Municipal Corporation Ayodhya

## What the state notified

Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Section 7 issued notification `1820(1)/9-7-17-22(Ja)/2017` at Lucknow on 11 May 2017. Acting under Article 243-Q(2) of the Constitution and section 3(1) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959, the state specified the scheduled smaller urban areas of Municipal Council Faizabad and Municipal Council Ayodhya as a single larger urban area for Chapter IX-A of the Constitution. The notification named the new body Municipal Corporation Ayodhya.

The [state order index](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/GO_main_menu-2017.html) lists the Hindi notification and the English translation. The date here is the one printed on the notification. The same index separately lists the administrator appointment order dated 13 May 2017, no. `2272/9-7-17-22(Ja)/2017`.

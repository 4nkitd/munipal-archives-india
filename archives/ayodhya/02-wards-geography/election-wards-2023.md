# Ayodhya ward names and municipal election results, 2023

## What the results show

On the commission's winner list, select `नगर निगम पार्षद` (municipal corporation councillor) and district `अयोध्या`. The 60 rows for the body also named `अयोध्या` cover wards 1 to 60. The party labels on those winning rows give 27 Bharatiya Janata Party seats, 17 Samajwadi Party, 10 independents, three Bahujan Samaj Party, and one each for Aam Aadmi Party, Peace Party and Rashtriya Lok Dal.

Switch the post to `नगर निगम महापौर` (mayor) and select the same district. The commission lists Girish Pati Tripathi (`गिरीश पति त्रिपाठी`) of the Bharatiya Janata Party as the winner of the unreserved mayoral seat, with 77,494 valid votes, 48.67% of votes and 47.92% turnout. These are election results, not a present-day roster.

The commission's [older ward export](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ULBWard2017.aspx) also contains 60 Ayodhya corporation rows, but its numbering differs. For example, it shows `जनौरा` as ward 9, whereas the 2023 results show it as ward 26. The [district summary](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/summary.aspx) separately lists one corporation and 60 corporation wards in Ayodhya district. It counts other urban bodies in separate columns. None of these pages defines ward boundaries, and the 2023 results page does not show a publication date.

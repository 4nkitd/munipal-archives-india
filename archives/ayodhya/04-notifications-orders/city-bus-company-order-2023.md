# State approval for an Ayodhya city bus company

## What the order authorizes

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

On 18 September 2023, Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Section 7 issued order `558/नौ-7-2023-15(ज)/2023` to the Director of Urban Transport. It approved separate special-purpose vehicles for electric city bus operations in Ayodhya, Firozabad and Saharanpur. The Ayodhya entity in the order is Ayodhya City Transport Services Ltd., to be registered at the municipal corporation office address.

| Ayodhya provision | Figure in the order |
|---|---:|
| Buses | 25 |
| Paid-up share capital | ₹1,00,000 |
| Authorized share capital | ₹10,00,000 |
| Value per share | ₹100 |

## Who would run it

The order puts the Divisional Commissioner in the chair and lists the Municipal Commissioner as a director with one share. It calls for the board to appoint a chief executive as managing director. The regional manager of the Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation would carry out the CEO's duties until the appointment.

The company could determine routes, but operation on those routes required the directorate's approval. The board could set fares. The order contains no adopted fare table, route list or launch date. The fleet figure is an approval provision, not a count of buses delivered or in service.

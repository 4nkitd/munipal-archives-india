# Ayodhya online property tax, ledger and receipts

## Find a property record or a past payment

The [Nagar Nigam Ayodhya homepage](https://nnayodhya.in/) links directly to its Property Tax Management System. Use the municipal system's **Pay Tax** page to start with a property search. **View Ledger** and **View Receipt** are separate forms; they do not start a new payment.

| Task | Municipal page | What the public form shows |
|---|---|---|
| Find a property to review dues | [Pay Tax](https://tax.nnayodhya.in/SearchHouseDetailsFO.aspx) | Search options include mohalla, registered mobile number, property ID, name and house number. The form shows a CAPTCHA. |
| View a property ledger | [View Ledger](https://tax.nnayodhya.in/LedgerO.aspx) | Enter the property ID. A help link points users back to property lookup. |
| Find a receipt | [View Receipt](https://tax.nnayodhya.in/SearchPaymentDepositeO.aspx) | Enter either the property ID or the ANN receipt number. The form is headed “View Deposit Amount.” |

The [tax portal's contact page](https://tax.nnayodhya.in/content/cu.html) gives Lajpat Nagar, Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh 224001. This is the address as published on the tax site, not a verified counter location or set of office hours.

## Self-assessment and mutation

The homepage and tax menu link a [self-assessment form](https://tax.nnayodhya.in/SelfAssessmentO.aspx?tmp=@qOD#98GH) citing Rule 7 under section 207A. It is labelled “UAT (Only for testing purpose).” The form loaded, but it should not be treated as a confirmed production filing route or a current tax rate schedule. The municipal homepage's mutation entry points to an under-maintenance message rather than an application form. No property lookup, payment, filing or receipt retrieval was performed.

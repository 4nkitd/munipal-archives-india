# Baddi's quashed December 2024 corporation declaration

## Original declaration and High Court judgment

The Governor first declared Municipal Council Baddi, District Solan, to be Municipal Corporation Baddi under section 3(2) of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. Notification No. UD-A(1)-2/2024 was dated 23 December 2024 and took effect on publication on 26 December 2024, on printed pages 10814–10815 of the Rajpatra.

The Himachal Pradesh High Court quashed both the declaration and the companion notification adding areas on 18 December 2025. See [*Gram Panchayat Haripur Sandholi v. State of Himachal Pradesh*, CWP No. 462 of 2025](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/170291827/), paragraphs 9–12. The court directed the state to reconsider the objections and give reasons for its decision.

## Current corporation

The operative corporation date is **27 February 2026**, when the state published the [fresh section 3(2) declaration](https://rajpatra.hp.gov.in/OPENFILE1.aspx?ID=261/GAZETTE/2026-27/02/2026&etype=SPECIAL#page=49). Do not use the quashed December 2024 documents to determine current boundaries or parcel eligibility.

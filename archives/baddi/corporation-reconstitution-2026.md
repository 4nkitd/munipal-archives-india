# Baddi corporation reconstitution, 27 February 2026

## Operative date

The Governor declared Municipal Council Baddi, District Solan, to be Municipal Corporation Baddi under section 3(2) of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. The fresh Notification No. UD-A(1)-2/2024 is dated 27 February 2026 and took effect on Rajpatra publication that same day. Its authoritative English text is on printed page 12177 of issue 261.

The gazette records that the Himachal Pradesh High Court quashed both 23 December 2024 instruments, the corporation declaration and the companion inclusion notification, on 18 December 2025 in [CWP No. 462 of 2025](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/170291827/). It also records that the state reconsidered the objections, provided a personal hearing and issued a reasoned order on 9 February 2026 before the fresh declaration.

## Later changes to municipal limits

In [*Deepinder Kumar Singla v. State of Himachal Pradesh*, 2026:HHC:7673](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/152286854/), the High Court recorded that a separate 16 March 2026 notification included previously omitted areas, with khasra numbers in its schedule. That later boundary change is not the corporation formation date. The [March re-delimitation programme](https://rajpatra.hp.gov.in/OPENFILE1.aspx?ID=276/GAZETTE/2026-19/03/2026&etype=SPECIAL) and the April final ward notification followed the boundary change.

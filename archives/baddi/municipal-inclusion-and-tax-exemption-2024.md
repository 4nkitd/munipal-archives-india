# Baddi's quashed 2024 boundary and property-tax notification

## Legal status

The [Himachal Pradesh High Court's judgment of 18 December 2025](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/170291827/) set aside both Baddi notifications dated 23 December 2024. That includes the boundary and tax instrument beginning at printed page 10843 of the 26 December 2024 Rajpatra, as the [fresh corporation declaration](https://rajpatra.hp.gov.in/OPENFILE1.aspx?ID=261/GAZETTE/2026-27/02/2026&etype=SPECIAL#page=49) expressly confirms. Do not use this historical schedule to establish a current boundary or claim a current exemption.

## What the quashed instrument said

Notification No. UD-A(1)-2/2024 cited section 5(3) of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Act, 1994. Its schedule identified areas by patwar circle, revenue village and khasra number. It marked Kunjhal, Judi Khurd and Judi Kalan as complete, but Dharampur and Suraj Majara Gujra as partial.

Under section 5(4) read with section 80(1), the instrument provided for a three-year property-tax exemption for scheduled land and buildings from publication and for retention of customary rights as provided in the Wazib-Ul-Urj record of rights. These provisions describe what the quashed instrument said, not present tax treatment.

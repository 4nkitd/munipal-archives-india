# Baddi trade licence bye-laws for use of premises and storage

## Which activities require a licence

Municipal Corporation Baddi's final notification, No. M.Corp./Baddi/Bye Laws (Trade License)/2026-(2), is dated 18 April 2026. The bye-laws took effect when published on 25 April 2026, beginning on printed page 1318 of the Rajpatra, after a draft was published on 10 March.

Anyone operating a trade or business specified in Part I, or storing articles specified in Part II, within the corporation area must apply through the [state citizen services portal](https://citizenseva.hp.gov.in/hp-udd/). The lists in the bye-laws determine whether a particular trade or storage activity is covered.

## Duration, fees and renewal

The corporation issues licences for one or five years. There is no late fee if the holder applies for renewal within one month after expiry. The Commissioner sets the licence fee, and the fee is calculated by multiplying the annual fee by the number of years requested. Transfer is not allowed except to legal heirs after the original holder's death, subject to supporting documents and the Commissioner's decision.

# Baddi corporation ward delimitation, April 2026

## Notification and scope

After a boundary change, the State Election Commission issued a fresh delimitation programme on 18 March 2026. That programme scheduled a draft and objections; it did not set final boundaries. The Commission then issued Notification No. SEC-HP-F002(01)/1/2021-3070-3106 on 25 April after receiving the Deputy Commissioner's final order. Published in the Rajpatra on 27 April 2026, beginning on printed page 1425, it formally notifies the final 15-ward delimitation in Annexure A. It is not a proposal or an election result.

# Municipal Council Bahadurgarh: profile and elected council

## Municipal profile

Bahadurgarh is a municipal council in Jhajjar district, Haryana, near Delhi's Tikri border in the National Capital Region. It has 31 electoral wards. The [Haryana Department of Urban Local Bodies' municipal key figures](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) list an area of 29.29 sq km and a population of 210,473 without giving a reference year. The [2011 Primary Census Abstract for Jhajjar](https://censusindia.mha.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/447) counted 170,767 residents and 34,910 households in Bahadurgarh Municipal Council, census town code 800418. The census counts refer to the municipal limits as recorded in 2011.

Bahadurgarh's industrial areas include the [Modern Industrial Estates](https://dcmsme.gov.in/dips/jhajjar.pdf) and the [Footwear Park in Sector 17](https://msme.haryana.gov.in/footwear-park-bahadurgarh/). Delhi Metro's Green Line extension from Mundka began commercial service on 24 June 2018. The [Haryana Mass Rapid Transport Corporation](https://hmrtc.org.in/Bahadurgarh.aspx) lists three Haryana stations on the extension: Pandit Shree Ram Sharma, Bahadurgarh City and Brigadier Hoshiar Singh. The [Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway](https://api.sci.gov.in/jonew/judis/43639.pdf), also called the Western Peripheral Expressway, crosses the Delhi-Rohtak highway west of Bahadurgarh and provides a road link to Manesar without entering Delhi.

The [Jhajjar district directory](https://jhajjar.nic.in/public-utility/market-committee-bahadurgarh/) lists the municipal office on Delhi Rohtak Road near the City Police Station, Bahadurgarh, Haryana 124507, and gives `mcbgarh2016@gmail.com` as its email. The state ULB helpline, `1800-8900-929`, is for IT support and grievance registration, not the local council switchboard.

## Chairperson and vice-president

Saroj Rathi of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won the separately elected chairperson post in the 19 June 2022 municipal election. The State Election Commission lists her as "Saroj" and calls the office "President"; the seat was reserved for women. [The Tribune's result report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/lotus-blooms-in-hoodas-bastion-bjp-wins-both-seats-in-jhajjar-dist-406159/) gives her 27,415 votes to Indian National Lok Dal (INLD) candidate Monika Rathi's 24,852, a margin of 2,563. [Dainik Tribune identified Saroj Rathi as chairperson in November 2026](https://www.dainiktribuneonline.com/news/gurugram/rs-6-50-crore-will-be-spent-on-construction-of-roads-in-sector-2-saroj-rathi/).

On 15 May 2026, [26 councillors voted to remove Ward 4 councillor Rajpal Sharma, also known as Pale Ram Sharma, from the vice-presidency](https://www.dainiktribuneonline.com/news/gurugram/bahadurgarh-municipal-council-faces-major-upset-no-confidence-motion-passed-against-vice-chairman-rajpal-sharma/). The vote concerned the vice-presidency, not his ward seat. On 18 November 2026, [Punjab Kesari reported](https://haryana.punjabkesari.in/haryana/news/path-cleared-for-vice-president-election-in-bahadurgarh-municipal-council-2400761) that Haryana told the Punjab and Haryana High Court it would convene a meeting to elect a replacement within four weeks. This record does not confirm a successor.

The Bahadurgarh entries appear on pages 2160 to 2162 of [gazette notification SEC/1ME/2022/6120](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2023/11/20231109536788481.pdf), dated 4 July 2022.

# Bahadurgarh wards, sectors and industrial areas

## Municipal wards and boundary evidence

The Department of Urban Local Bodies' Bahadurgarh profile states that the municipal council comprises 31 wards. The accompanying `wards.json` records Ward 1 through Ward 31. It deliberately leaves zone, division and locality assignments unpopulated because a verified final wardbandi schedule was not obtained. Empty arrays mean unverified, not that a ward contains no colonies or sectors.

The Local Government Directory-derived [Bahadurgarh directory](https://bharatsurvey.org/urban-local-bodies/haryana/bahadurgarh) lists all 31 numbered wards and local body code 248560. It is a secondary index, not a substitute for the notified boundary schedule.

Ward numbers, development-plan sector numbers, revenue estates and industrial plot numbers describe different geographic units. Ward 17 must not be equated with Sector 17. A colony can cross a ward boundary, and a planned sector can extend beyond an existing municipal limit. Use the [DULB GIS portal](https://gis.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for the municipal, ward and colony layers, then check the applicable notification before making a parcel-level claim.

The date in this record is the compilation date. It is not the date of a new delimitation or boundary extension.

## Line Par colonies

Line Par, also written Line Paar, is a locality descriptor used in addresses rather than one of the 31 numbered wards. The [Bar Council of Punjab and Haryana's Bahadurgarh address directory](https://bcph.co.in/wp-content/uploads/haryana_elections/Bahadurgarh.pdf) records Johri Nagar, Shankar Garden, Netaji Nagar, Ashok Nagar, Vikas Nagar, Subhash Nagar and New Patel Park with Line Par or Line Paar addresses.

Those address records establish local usage only. They do not prove a current ward assignment, municipal boundary or regularization status. Historic house-number prefixes and ward numbers in professional directories should not be converted into current electoral boundaries.

For a Line Par property, retain the colony name, street, property identifier and revenue-estate particulars. Match these against the municipal GIS and wardbandi schedule. Colony names in a regularization notification may cover only a specified part, extension or cadastral polygon, even where the broader neighbourhood uses the same name.

## MIE and HSIIDC industrial areas

Modern Industrial Estate, commonly abbreviated MIE, is distinct from HSIIDC's numbered industrial sectors. Haryana's [Central Inspection System industry register](https://cisharyana.in/home/industries_list?dept_id=&distt_id=7&per_page=420) separately records MIE Part A, MIE Part B and Sector 17 HSIIDC addresses. Examples include Kakkar Footwears at Plot 85, Part A, MIE, and Ajiveena Exports at Plot 2383, Part B, MIE. These are address examples, not a complete industrial-estate register.

The Directorate of MSME's [Footwear Park, Bahadurgarh profile](https://msme.haryana.gov.in/footwear-park-bahadurgarh/) identifies the park as Sector 17, on the Delhi-Rohtak-Hisar National Highway 10 corridor. Its page, last updated 14 August 2020, describes 316 acres as planned and developed. It lists 6 plots above 4,050 square metres, 76 plots of 4,050 square metres, 69 of 1,800 square metres, 97 of 1,012.50 square metres and 120 of 450 square metres. These are historical planning figures, not a current vacancy statement.

A [22 November 2012 Economic Times report](https://m.economictimes.com/north/bahadurgarh-industrial-estate-expansion-to-create-business-opportunities-worth-rs-3000-crore/articleshow/17319549.cms), quoting HSIIDC officials, distinguishes Sector 16 general industry from Sector 17 Footwear Park. Current government inspection entries also record industrial addresses in Sector 16. Neither an industrial allotment nor a postal address alone establishes which municipal ward contains a site or which agency currently maintains its road, drain or water connection.

The actual statutory declaration concerns specified deficient areas and should not be read as blanket approval of every plot, building or similarly named colony extension. Duplicate-looking colony identifiers are reproduced from the source, not silently corrected. Later notifications may add areas; this table is not claimed as an exhaustive current citywide inventory.

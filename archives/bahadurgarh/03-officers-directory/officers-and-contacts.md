The district listing is older than the officer reports. It verifies that the address and mailbox were officially published, but does not establish that the address is the present visiting location or that the mailbox is monitored. No telephone call, delivery test or in-person verification was conducted. Older municipal numbers found in historical publications have not been republished as current contacts.

For property tax billing and correction, ask the council office for its current taxation branch and responsible officer. Do not confuse municipal property tax with the separate state Excise and Taxation Department. The reviewed Haryana property portal contact table did not contain a Bahadurgarh row, so another council's helpdesk number must not be substituted.

## RTI responsibility and escalation

Haryana ULB's PIO list identifies the Executive Officer, Municipal Council Bahadurgarh, as the State Public Information Officer. It identifies the Sub-Division Magistrate, Bahadurgarh, as the first appellate authority. These are designation-based assignments, not a separate confirmation of the current Executive Officer's name.

The official district entry for the Sub Divisional Officer (civil), Bahadurgarh, publishes telephone **01276-297306** and email **sdocivilbahadurgarh@gmail.com**. The telephone is for the SDM office, not a municipal council general helpline. The district telephone directory gives its address as SDM Office, Bahadurgarh. Use the municipal Executive Officer for the initial municipal RTI request and the designated appellate authority for the applicable first appeal.

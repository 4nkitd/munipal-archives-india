# Bahadurgarh official notices, notified colonies and GRAP orders

## Bahadurgarh colony notification and source limits

The official [DULB civic amenities and infrastructure deficient areas register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure) lists **12 Bahadurgarh entries**, at serial numbers **255 to 266**, under district Jhajjar. Each cites **notification No. 09/121/2023-4CII dated 15 December 2023**. Their individual codes are **BDG71 to BDG82**.

The portal places these records within its regularized-colony information system. The statutory description is a declaration of civic amenities and infrastructure deficient areas. For a specific property, consult the notification schedule, revenue estate, khasra numbers and linked layout plan. A colony name or UC ID alone is not a plot-level boundary determination. The register repeats UC references 13060 and 13061 across different entries, which makes the separate BDG code and layout plan important.

## Statewide notification index and publication dates

The [regularized-colonies index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) links the gazette collection and status reports. Its entry "15-12-2023 90 notified colonies" links [4c8daa7c-53a2-4086-89a0-01ac2245b88d.pdf](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2F4c8daa7c-53a2-4086-89a0-01ac2245b88d.pdf), listed as 712 KB and uploaded on **26 September 2025**. The document date in its title is **15 December 2023**. The upload date is not a new legal notification date.

The same index lists "Municipalities Status of 836 Colonies Data Notified 2025", dated **13 October 2025**, as well as gazette batches dated 25 July 2023, 6 October 2023, 1 February 2024, 6 March 2024, 14 November 2024, 18 March 2025 and 22 May 2025. These are statewide batch or report titles. Their counts are not Bahadurgarh colony totals, and their appearance on an index does not establish that every batch contains a Bahadurgarh entry.

The [DULB notifications index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Notification) also lists the Haryana Management of Civic Amenities and Infrastructure Deficient Municipal Areas Amendment Act, 2025, published **15 December 2025**, and the final Haryana Municipal Account Code 2025, notified **20 May 2025**, with uploads published **6 June 2025**. These are state-level records. They should not be labeled orders issued by Municipal Council Bahadurgarh.

## GRAP framework relevant to Bahadurgarh

Bahadurgarh is an NCR town. The [CAQM GRAP schedule revised 21 November 2025](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Schedule/GRAP%20Schedule7e3bd726-7308-484f-995f-7cb3fa705b8d.pdf) applies its NCR-wide provisions to NCR towns and identifies urban local bodies among implementing agencies. The stages use **Delhi AQI**, rather than a town's AQI alone.

| Stage | Delhi AQI category in the cited schedule |
| --- | --- |
| I | Poor, 201 to 300 |
| II | Very poor, 301 to 400 |
| III | Severe, 401 to 450 |
| IV | Severe+, above 450 |

The schedule allows advance invocation based on forecasts. Actions at a higher stage are additional to earlier-stage actions. Municipal duties include road-dust control, mechanical or vacuum sweeping, water sprinkling and enforcement of construction-site dust measures. Stage III restricts listed dust-generating construction and demolition activities across NCR, with specified exceptions and conditions. It is not accurately summarized as an unconditional ban on every construction-related task.

Some school, office and vehicle provisions specify named districts rather than the whole NCR. A Delhi, Gurugram or Faridabad-specific clause should not automatically be applied to Bahadurgarh. Read the geographical scope of each action and any supplementary Haryana or Jhajjar order.

## GRAP orders and current-status caution

The [official CAQM GRAP orders index](https://caqm.nic.in/landingpage/pages/grap/grap-orders) is the source for invocation and revocation orders. An example is **No. 120017/27/GRAP/2021/CAQM dated 17 January 2026**, [invoking Stage IV](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Home/News/Invocation%20of%20GRAP%20order-%20Stage-%20IV%2017012026eb1a5169-52ec-4ebb-8381-3ee680d6f70f.pdf) with immediate effect, in addition to Stages I, II and III. It also modified the Stage IV truck-entry action and referred to separate Direction No. 88. This illustrates why the invocation order must be read alongside the schedule.

The [Haryana State Pollution Control Board GRAP archive](https://www.hspcb.org.in/page/grap-orders) is search-indexed with implementation orders dated **14 October 2024**, **21 October 2024** and **25 November 2024**. Direct retrieval failed during this research. Those indexed titles are historical references, not proof of today's active stage.

This compilation does **not** certify which GRAP stage was active on 22 November 2026. It does not present a newspaper report as an official municipal order. Before construction, demolition or a regulated vehicle movement, check the latest CAQM invocation or revocation and applicable state or district instructions. No Bahadurgarh-specific signed GRAP enforcement order was verified for inclusion.

# Bahadurgarh citizen services: property tax, certificates, RTI and grievances

## Property tax, property ID and no dues certificate

The Haryana Property Tax Dues Payment & No Dues Certificate Management System is the official citizen portal at [property.ulbharyana.gov.in](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). Its public page provides citizen registration, NRI or foreign-national registration, login by mobile number, email ID or PID, a municipality selector, application-status lookup, document lists, help manuals and municipal support contacts. Select Bahadurgarh for the relevant municipal property record.

Use the portal for the property-tax and NDC workflow. Where a record is wrong, distinguish a correction or objection from payment of an assessed demand. The DULB Right to Service list separately identifies objections concerning dues, size, floor details, authorized or unauthorized status and property category, new property IDs, and changes of owner or occupier. Retain the application number and payment receipt for follow-up. A receipt for payment should not be substituted for the NDC required by a particular transaction.

The public portal links a tax calculator explicitly described as applying the new policy dated **30 July 2026**. The [DULB property-tax instructions index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists "Property tax notification 2026 Councils and Committees" with publication date **31 July 2026**. This is the council category relevant to Bahadurgarh, rather than the separate corporation notification. This guide does not reproduce unverified tax slabs or promise a rebate. The index also lists an interest-waiver notification published 18 May 2026 for arrears from 2010-11 to 2024-2025; its title alone does not establish a currently open payment window.

## Birth and death registration and certificates

The [Jhajjar district e-services directory](https://jhajjar.nic.in/service/e-services/) identifies the Civil Registration System for birth and death certificates. The [national CRS portal](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/home) includes Haryana among the states for which online birth and death registration is available. The district's [Antyodaya SARAL page](https://jhajjar.nic.in/service/antyodaya-saral-services/) provides the state service-delivery and tracking route at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in/).

The official [CRS FAQ](https://censusindia.mha.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/43475/download/47178/CRS_FAQ.pdf) explains that events are registered at the **place of occurrence**, not simply at the applicant's present residence. The normal reporting period is **21 days**. Registration reported within that period carries no registration fee. Delayed registration follows the statutory delayed-registration procedure and prescribed fees. Do not treat the normal reporting period as a certificate-delivery deadline.

For an event in Bahadurgarh, identify the registrar responsible for the place or institution where it occurred. Government hospitals can have their own registration units. For an existing record, use the certificate service; corrections, delayed registration, inclusion of a child's name and a non-availability certificate are distinct requests. The older Bahadurgarh birth/death service URL remains search-indexed but returned a 404 during this verification, so it is not presented as a functioning application route.

## Marriage registration

DULB's [marriage-registration information page](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/WebCMS/Start/?DynamicTypeIdView=11676&Id=1449) identifies the Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Act, 2008 and its 2008 Rules. Later [official CRID instructions hosted by DULB](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/d856bc3a-0f07-4ae1-8b9f-c2e09d4925c3.pdf) explain the revised registration establishment and supersede instructions dated 1 December 2016.

Under those instructions, urban registrars within municipal limits include municipal Executive Officers and Secretaries, Tehsildars and Naib Tehsildars, as well as Joint Commissioners in corporations. The Additional Deputy Commissioner is the District Registrar and first appellate authority. The Deputy Commissioner is the Additional Chief Registrar and second appellate authority. Sub-Divisional Magistrates are Additional District Registrars for their subdivisions.

The instructions require permission of the Additional District Registrar for registration after 90 days but before 365 days, and Deputy Commissioner approval beyond 365 days. These periods concern delayed registration, not a guaranteed processing time. For a Bahadurgarh application, confirm the appropriate registrar and current online application route through the municipal office or SARAL support. The Jhajjar e-services directory contains a generic urban link to Gurugram's website; that link is not evidence that Gurugram processes Bahadurgarh marriages.

Preserve the submission acknowledgement when escalating a delayed notified service. The water/sewer connection and leakage rows on this ULB list expressly apply only to specified corporations, not Bahadurgarh. They should not be copied as Bahadurgarh municipal timelines.

## RTI and general grievances

The [DULB PIO directory](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList), municipal-council row 14, lists **E.O. M.C. Bahadurgarh** as State Public Information Officer and **Sub-Division Magistrate, Bahadurgarh** as First Appellate Authority. It supplies designations rather than a verified current officer name or individual telephone number. Some other municipality classifications on the directory are historical, so confirm the receiving office before filing.

Address municipal record requests to the designated SPIO. Identify the subject, property ID or work reference if relevant, the period covered, and the records sought. RTI is a route to existing information, such as an assessment record, order or file action; it is not the same as an application for correction, tax payment, certificate issuance or a sanitation complaint. This record does not infer a local RTI fee from another state's rules.

For service support, DULB publishes **1800-8900-929**, Monday to Saturday, **9 AM to 6 PM**, for IT support and grievance registration. Jhajjar's SARAL page publishes **0172-3968400**, Monday to Saturday, **8 AM to 8 PM**. These are state support lines, not personal numbers for Bahadurgarh officers. For a notified service, use the service-specific grievance authorities above rather than assuming an RTI appeal will resolve the service request.

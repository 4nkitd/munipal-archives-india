# Bahadurgarh tenders, budgets and infrastructure, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and procurement portals

This record covers Municipal Council Bahadurgarh and separately identified water and sewerage works of the Public Health Engineering Department, PHED, and Haryana Shehri Vikas Pradhikaran, HSVP. Works in the same town are not necessarily municipal council contracts.

The official [HEWP live-tender page](https://works.haryana.gov.in/LiveTender.aspx) states that tenders floated through HEWP are published on [Haryana NIC eProcurement](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app) for bidder participation. HEWP says these entries become available on its live-tender page the following day. The [town-wise works search](https://works.haryana.gov.in/HEWP_Login/Works_Under_Village.aspx) provides town, department and approval-year filters.

Search the NIC portal using the exact tender IDs below and inspect notices, corrigenda, technical and financial results, and award documents. Direct external tender links retrieved during this research returned only the NIC page shell. Entries relying on tender mirrors are labelled accordingly. A scheduled opening date is not evidence that bids opened, and a tender estimate is not an award amount or expenditure. These historical entries are not a current invitation to bid.

## Drainage and waterlogging mitigation

Amar Ujala reported on 1 February 2024 that the council started stormwater-drain construction and park improvements in Sector 2, together with drain repair work in Sector 6. The reported combined allocation was ₹2.84 crore, approximately ₹2 crore in Sector 2 and ₹84 lakh in Sector 6. The report describes commencement, not completion. [Source](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/jhajjar-bahadurgarh/development-work-will-be-done-in-sector-2-and-6-at-a-cost-of-rs-284-crores-jhjhar-news-c-200-1-bgh1002-105210-2024-02-01).

Dainik Bhaskar's 27 May 2025 report quoted council Executive Officer Sanjay Rohilla on a drain-cleaning programme covering 36 drains, a reported combined length of 42 kilometres, and an estimated ₹56 lakh cost. The report named Rohtak's DSP Labour Corporation Society as the agency and quoted a 20-day completion target. Neither the estimate nor the target establishes final payment or completion. [Source](https://www.bhaskar.com/local/haryana/jhajjar/news/campaign-for-cleaning-of-drains-started-jhajjar-bahadurgarh-135112234.html).

For 2026, a TenderShark mirror records council tender `2026_HRY_516949_1`, reference `2026B9BE6B97 326D 4849 A415 82E23064B400293ULB`, for desilting stormwater drains and nalas over one year. It gives an estimated value of ₹1,63,58,987, publication on 22 April 2026, submission deadline 4 May 2026 and scheduled opening 5 May 2026. The work period is 365 days. No award or completion is established here. [Tender mirror](https://www.tendershark.com/details/haryana-tender/haryana-government/4d73bea2-22e4-4444-b9ce-869f1d596c79).

## Water supply and sewerage

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' [AMRUT 2.0 approved-project listing](https://amrut.mohua.gov.in/approvedProjects/list/MVNReTQzNzZUeVNkSG9OTlIwc0RkZz09/VldBeHlVY3dqaDN6WXBHVE9MTDN5Zz09) records upgradation and commissioning of Bahadurgarh's existing 18.0 MLD sewage treatment plant based on UASB technology. Project cost is ₹19.41 crore and the displayed milestone is "Contract Awarded". The listing does not establish that the upgraded plant is commissioned or compliant.

Separately, the ministry's [AMRUT grounded-project statement as updated on 2 March 2026](https://amrut.mohua.gov.in/assets/City-wise-Projects-grounded-under-AMRUT-as-updated-by-States-as-on-02.03.2026.pdf), row 112, records three Bahadurgarh water-supply projects costing ₹29.22 crore, three sewerage and septage-management projects costing ₹49.51 crore, and one park project costing ₹0.82 crore. These are cumulative programme figures, not projects necessarily initiated during 2026. Do not add them to the separate AMRUT 2.0 project without reconciling project lists.

## Roads, streets and associated works

Sigma Tenders records `2025_HRY_472500_1` for construction of a street from H/O Shri Niwas to H/O Sahabudin in Ward 8, Municipal Council Bahadurgarh, under D Plan. Publication is listed as 15 September 2025 and submission deadline as 23 September 2025. No construction cost or completion is verified here. [Mirror](https://sigmatenders.com/tender/oxGQ3eR568AmA6mP98S5uy); [official tender locator](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app?page=FrontEndTenderDetailsExternal&service=page&tnid=550402).

On 6 September 2025, Dainik Bhaskar reported that the council had hired a consultant for drainage planning under Divya Nagar and intended to modernise seven main roads. Named routes included Railway Road, Main Bazaar Road, Najafgarh Road, Mela Ground Road, the Sector 9 and 9A dividing road, and West Jua Drain Road. The report said a DPR would follow the survey and government funding would then be sought. This is a planning-stage report, not a verified sanctioned road budget or completed drainage system. [Source](https://www.bhaskar.com/local/haryana/jhajjar/bahadurgarh/news/jhajjar-bahadurgarh-scientific-drainage-modernization-project-update-135848135.html).

Amar Ujala reported on 29 January 2026 that the council had begun tendering ward-level development works, with ₹50 lakh per ward and approximately ₹15.50 crore overall. Reported scope included streets, drains, damaged-drain repairs, new stormwater drains and waterlogging improvements. The report anticipated work starting within a month. It does not verify that all tenders were awarded or works completed. [Source](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/jhajjar-bahadurgarh/development-works-worth-rs-50-lakh-have-been-initiated-in-every-ward-of-the-city-the-municipal-council-has-issued-tenders-bahadurgarh-news-c-200-1-bgh1005-120667-2026-01-29).

The frontmatter ward array is empty because this is a municipality-wide compilation. The individual Ward 8 tender is identified in the body. No expenditure total is calculated across these records because budgets, grants, tender estimates and programme costs are different accounting measures.

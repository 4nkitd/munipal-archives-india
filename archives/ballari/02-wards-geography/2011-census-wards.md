# Ballari corporation wards in the 2011 Census

## Reading the ward codes

The [Census workbook](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/6756/download/9833/DDW_PCA2911_2011_MDDS%20with%20UI.xlsx) identifies the town as Bellary (M Corp.), code 803114. Its town row gives 86,493 households and 410,445 residents, including 206,149 males and 204,296 females. The catalogue entry dates from 19 January 2021; the figures describe the 2011 Census.

The Bellary town row stands on its own in this extract. City Finance separately reports 39 wards; the two sources describe different records. Ward numbers below identify census units. They do not describe street boundaries or establish today's electoral roll.

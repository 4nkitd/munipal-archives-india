# Ballari audited municipal finances, 2022-23

## Audited statement summary

City Finance identifies the source as the audited financial statements for 2022-23. The dashboard gives amounts rounded to whole crores and shows a last update of 14 May 2025. It does not identify that update as the date of the audit.

| Indicator | ₹ crore |
|---|---:|
| Tax revenue | 26 |
| Own revenue | 56 |
| Grants | 69 |
| Total revenue | 124 |
| Total expenditure | 128 |
| Balance sheet size | 674 |

Tax revenue forms part of own revenue; the two rows cannot be added to calculate total receipts. Balance sheet size is a separate measure from the year's spending. City Finance also lists statements for 2021-22, 2020-21, 2016-17 and 2015-16. The figures above are for 2022-23 only.

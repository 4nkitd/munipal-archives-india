# Bareilly Nagar Nigam: jurisdiction and municipal leadership

## Jurisdiction and administration

The [corporation's profile](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/about-nagarnigam-bareilly.php) describes four zones and 80 wards, each with a ward councillor. Bareilly city is the district and divisional headquarters, but the corporation's wards cover the municipal area, not the entire district. The [district administration's directory](https://bareilly.nic.in/public-utility-category/nagar-nigam/) links to the corporation's site.

The [municipal homepage](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/) lists Dr. Umesh Gautam as mayor and Sanjeev Kumar Maurya, IAS, as municipal commissioner. The [organisation chart](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/organization-structure.php) lists departments including public health, Jal Kal and accounts. The homepage publishes the toll-free helpline 1800-180-3817 and the landline 0581-6612200.

## Limits

The corporation profile is undated. The names are those displayed on the website when checked, not independently verified appointment dates or terms of office. No founding notification, headquarters address or legal boundary schedule was verified for this record.

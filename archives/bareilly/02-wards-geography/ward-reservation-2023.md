# 2023 councillor seat reservation notification for Bareilly wards

## Authority and scope

The Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Department issued notification **955/9-1-2023-12आर/22** on **9 April 2023**. It cites section 7(1), (3) and (4) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959 and the amended 1994 rules on reservation and allocation of seats and posts. It follows the objections and suggestions process under notification 784/नौ-1-2023-12आर/22 of 30 March 2023. The [department's election index](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/GO-Nirvachan-2022.html) lists both documents. Its separate Bareilly district notice 915 of 9 April 2023 is not the corporation schedule.

## Maps and limits

The corporation publishes a separate [ward map index](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/ward-map.php) and a [ward/councillor list](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/mohalla-wards-covered-by-npp-bareilly.php) marked as uploaded on 16 December 2024. An upload date does not establish the meeting or election date of the linked list. This reservation notification is not a boundary order, election result or current councillor directory. No street-level boundary or later reservation update was verified.

# March 2024 notice on inspection of Bareilly's proposed property tax list

## What the notice says

The [municipal notice](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/Download/public-info-3-4-2024.pdf), reference 421 / Property Tax Department / 2023-24, is dated **7 March 2024**. The date is printed on the scan; the filename contains a different date-like string. The notice says Bareilly Nagar Nigam prepared a proposed assessment list for buildings within the corporation limits under section 207 of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959 and rule 5 of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation (Property Tax) Rules, 2000.

It says the list was available immediately at the zone offices during office hours and from **15 March 2024** on the municipal website. It cites section 209 and rule 5क for inspection. Owners or occupiers could object to carpet area, covered area, land-area calculations or other entries online or in the prescribed written format at the zone offices. The notice set the objection period at one month from publication of the notice.

## Limits

The printed date is the notice's issue date. The publication date is unknown, so the archive does not calculate a calendar deadline. This is a historical notice about a proposed list. It does not establish the final assessment, current rates or a live objections window.

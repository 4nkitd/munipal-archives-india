# File or track a Bareilly municipal grievance

## File and track a complaint

The [corporation's grievance form](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/addcomplaint.php) lists cleanliness, drinking water and sewerage, repair of roads, drains and culverts, street lighting, tax, encroachment, animals, health and other grievances. The form shows ward options 1 to 80 and asks for the location and a description of the issue.

Use an active email address and mobile number and select the relevant ward. The form instructions allow supporting documents in PDF, DOC or DOCX format up to 5 MB. If the same issue was reported before, the instructions say to keep the earlier registration number handy. Keep the new complaint number for the corporation's [status lookup](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/search-complaint.php).

## Limits

The form and lookup page were checked, but no complaint was submitted and no status lookup was run. The pages are undated. The instructions do not establish a resolution deadline.

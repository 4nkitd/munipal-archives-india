# Bareilly property tax: official online links

## Where to look up a bill

The [municipal homepage](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/) links its Pay Property Tax button to the Uttar Pradesh [e-NagarSewa citizen login](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/onlinepay/citlogin). The [Property Tax Department page](https://nagarnigambareilly.com/property-tax.php) also links to [Bareilly's PTMS citizen portal](https://ptms-citizen.nagarnigambareilly.com/).

For a missing FY 2026-27 property tax bill, the municipal homepage tells owners to contact the corporation headquarters with a clear photo of the property, its GPS location and a copy of a previous property tax bill. This is the instruction shown for that fiscal year, not a general document list for tax payments or mutations.

## Limits

The state login page loaded. The Bareilly PTMS link was verified as a link published by the corporation, but the destination did not pass the archive's certificate check. No property was searched and no bill, payment, receipt or entitlement was verified. The municipal department page is undated and this record does not quote rates or rebates.

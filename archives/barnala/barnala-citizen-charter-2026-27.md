# Barnala citizen charter for 2026–27

## Service standards

Section 2.3 gives targets in working days. These are selected entries, not measured turnaround or a guarantee of service.

| Service | Target | Responsible designations in the charter |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Complaint about a street light not working | 10 days | Junior Engineer, A.C.E., C.E. |
| Sanction a residential or commercial building plan | 30 days | Building Inspector, Draftsman, A.T.P., M.T.P., Commissioner |
| Current-year birth or death certificate | 2 days | Local or district registrar |
| Assign a unique property ID | 12 days | Clerk, Inspector, Superintendent |

## Complaint centre

The charter cover gives the municipal office number **01679-230048** and email **cmc.barnala@gmail.com**. Section 3.1 puts the complaint centre on **Anaj Mandi Road, Barnala** and states that complaints can be lodged from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., except on public holidays. It also lists the Punjab Local Government toll-free number **1800-1800-0172**. The charter calls for an acknowledgement receipt and lists separate escalation roles by service.

The cover names the Municipal Corporation, though the mission paragraph still uses "Municipal Council Barnala." That wording does not establish the body's legal status or the date of the upgrade.

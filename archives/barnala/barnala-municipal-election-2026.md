# Barnala municipal corporation election results, May 2026

## Declared seats

The result sheet identifies the local body and its district as Barnala. It gives the poll date as **26 May 2026** and reports results declared for **50 of 50 wards**. The commission's index dates the sheet **29 May 2026**.

| Party or category | Seats won |
| --- | ---: |
| Aam Aadmi Party | 36 |
| Bharatiya Janata Party | 7 |
| Independent | 5 |
| Indian National Congress | 2 |
| Bahujan Samaj Party | 0 |
| Shiromani Akali Dal | 0 |

## Council and corporation editions

The [August 2023 SEC ward list](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/CamScanner_08-01-2023_13.08.52_3.pdf), page 3, row 130, gives **31 wards** for "MC Barnala", the former municipal council. The [13 May 2026 election gazette](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Gazette_Notification_of_Election.pdf), No. SEC/ME/SAM (CME)/2026/832, lists Barnala under **municipal corporations** with **50 wards** in Annexure I, printed page 2333. Bhadaur, Dhanaula and Tapa appear separately under councils and nagar panchayats.

The [8 June 2026 district gazette](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Barnala.pdf), No. SEC/ME/SAM(CME-RN)/2026/902, expressly names Municipal Corporation Barnala and notifies members elected from its ward constituencies. These election records establish corporation status by May 2026, not the effective date of the upgrade, the new perimeter, or a mapping between the two ward schemes.

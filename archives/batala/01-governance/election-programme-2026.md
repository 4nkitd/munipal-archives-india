# Batala municipal election programme, May 2026

## Corporation and timetable

Annexure I of Punjab Gazette Extraordinary notification `SEC/ME/SAM (CME)/2026/832`, published on 13 May 2026, lists Batala among the municipal corporations and gives it 50 wards. The Commission issued the programme under section 35 of the Punjab State Election Commission Act, 1994, citing the Department of Local Government's 11 May election notifications. This is an election notice, not a constitution or delimitation order.

| Event | Scheduled date and time |
| --- | --- |
| Filing of nominations | 13 to 15 May, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m.; last date 16 May, up to 3 p.m. |
| Scrutiny | 18 May |
| Withdrawal deadline | 19 May, 3 p.m. |
| Poll, where necessary | 26 May, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. |
| Counting | 29 May |
| Completion of the election process | By 1 June |

The Commission invoked section 57 to fix polling hours. The Model Code of Conduct took effect immediately in the municipalities holding elections, until completion of the poll. See printed pages 2331 to 2333 for the programme and annexure.

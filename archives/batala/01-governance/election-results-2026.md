# Batala municipal election results, May 2026

## Results for Batala

Row 4 of the State Election Commission's Municipal Corporation Final Result Sheet identifies Batala in Gurdaspur district. The sheet gives the poll date as 26 May 2026. The [SEC results index](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/en/result-municipal-election-2026/) dates the sheet to 29 May 2026. Results were declared for all 50 wards.

| Party or affiliation | Wards won |
| --- | ---: |
| Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) | 30 |
| Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) | 2 |
| Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP) | 0 |
| Indian National Congress (INC) | 18 |
| Independent (IND) | 0 |
| Shiromani Akali Dal (SAD) | 0 |
| Total | 50 |

The sheet counts seats won. It does not give Batala's ward-by-ward margins, vote shares or the election of the mayor. For the elected members by ward, see the separate [24 June district gazette](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/032.pdf).

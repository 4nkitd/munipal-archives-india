# Batala elected ward members, June 2026 gazette

## Finding a ward result in the gazette

The State Election Commission issued notification `SEC/ME/SAM(CME-RN)/2026/1032` on 24 June 2026 under section 71 of the Punjab State Election Commission Act, 1994. Find Batala corporation wards 1 to 50 on PDF pages 1 to 4. At page 5 the table switches to Municipal Council Dhariwal, followed by Fatehgarh Churian and Shri Hargobindpur. The continued serial numbers are not additional Batala wards.

| Batala ward | Elected member | Party | Category printed in gazette |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bhupinder Singh | INC | General |
| 2 | Rajwant Kaur | AAP | SC Women |
| 3 | Ayadwinder Singh | AAP | General |
| 4 | Amanpreet Kaur | AAP | Women |
| 5 | Bhupinder Singh | AAP | General |
| 6 | Suman | INC | SC Women |

These examples come from the first page of the notification. Wards 1 and 5 have distinct entries with the same displayed name. The table records who was elected in 2026, not subsequent changes in office. Its address column is not a ward boundary schedule.

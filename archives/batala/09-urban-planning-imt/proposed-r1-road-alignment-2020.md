# Batala master plan proposal for R-1 road near Mubarak, 2020

## Proposed change

Item 6 of the Directorate of Town and Country Planning's public notice proposes changing the alignment of the R-1 road near village Mubarak in Batala's 2010–2031 master plan. It would follow the existing highway alignment and earmark land uses in the areas left over. The notice calls the built alignment NH-15, while [sketch DTP(G) 12/2020](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s330bb3825e8f631cc6075c0f87bb4978c/uploads/2020/12/2020120358.pdf), dated 10 November 2020, labels the line “Actual road of NH-54” and draws it separately from the master plan road. Both files appear on the [district document listing](https://gurdaspur.nic.in/document/batala-local-planning-area/), dated 3 December 2020.

## Consultation at the time

The Punjab Regional and Town Planning and Development Board agreed to invite comments at its 40th meeting on 23 October 2020. The notice allowed objections and suggestions in writing within 30 days of publication, to the Chief Town Planner, Punjab, Room 507, fifth floor, PUDA Bhawan, SAS Nagar. It listed offices where the sketches could be inspected, including the relevant municipal, deputy commissioner and district town planner offices.

This is evidence of a proposal and consultation. It does not demonstrate that the amendment took effect or that the planning area coincides with today's municipal limits.

# Bathinda corporation and the district’s other local bodies

## Which body covers the city?

Bathinda district lists Bhucho Mandi, Goniana, Maur, Rampura Phul and Sangat in its [municipalities directory](https://bathinda.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/). The e-Nigam portal lists Bathinda under Municipal Corporation and each of these towns under Municipal Council. Select Bathinda for the city corporation’s records.

The [May 2026 gazette](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Gazette_Notification_of_Election.pdf) names Bathinda in the municipal corporation column of Annexure I, printed page 2333. The election schedule identifies the body but does not describe its perimeter.

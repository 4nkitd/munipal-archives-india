# Bathinda property tax, water, sewerage and garbage payments

## Property tax and water or sewerage charges

Open Online Services under Utility Services on the [city website](https://mcbathinda.punjab.gov.in/Home). The payment page asks for the UID and shows `Z200000-000-000` as its example. Enter the UID without the `MCB` prefix.

## Garbage charges

Choose Pay Now under Pay Garbage Charges. The [garbage utility page](https://eservices.mcbathinda.com/garbage/Default.aspx) has its own UID field and a Show button. This field accepts up to seven characters; the longer example on the main page belongs to the property and water/sewerage lookup.

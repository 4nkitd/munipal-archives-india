# Begusarai municipal ward map, 2010 edition

## Map and commission listing

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The one-page, sideways scan is titled "Map: Begusarai Municipal Corporation 2010." Its legend distinguishes the corporation boundary, ward boundaries, several types of roads, the railway line and the embankment. The year is part of the map title; the scan does not establish a separate publication date.

The [State Election Commission's unchanged-ward list](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC) links this scan at serial 37. It identifies Begusarai Municipal Corporation in Begusarai subdivision and records 45 wards as unchanged. The same row gives a total population of 252,008: 31,017 Scheduled Caste, 279 Scheduled Tribe and 220,712 other residents. These are the commission schedule's figures, not a current population estimate. This entry does not assign addresses to wards.

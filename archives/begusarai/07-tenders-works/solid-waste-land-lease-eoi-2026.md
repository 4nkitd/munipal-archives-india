# Begusarai EOI 05/2026-27: land lease for solid waste management

## Site and proposal requirements

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The Municipal Commissioner invited expressions of interest for a perpetual lease of about 5 to 5.5 acres within a 15 km radius of the city. The proposal table specifies a material recovery facility and compost plant. The notice also refers to implementation of the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016.

The notice required an access road at least 16 feet wide, clear and undisputed ownership, no waterlogging, no high-tension line overhead and no habitation within 500 metres. The corporation would give preference to land closer to the municipal boundary and review the documents and site before making a decision. The listed documents include a current land revenue receipt, title documents and declarations about disputes and encumbrances.

Sealed proposals had to reach the Municipal Corporation Office by 3 p.m. on 9 June 2026. The [district listing](https://begusarai.nic.in/notice/municipal-corporation-office-2/) gives 31 December 2026 as its display end date, not the submission deadline. The notice does not name a selected site or establish that a lease was signed.

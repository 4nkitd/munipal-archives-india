# Bihar Municipal (Amendment) Act, 2022: Begusarai corporation and elections

## Statutory scope

The Bihar Municipal (Amendment) Act, 2022 is Bihar Act 06 of 2022. It amends the Bihar Municipal Act, 2007 (Bihar Act 11 of 2007). The Governor assented on 1 April 2022, and the Act took effect on publication in the Bihar Gazette Extraordinary, Patna No. 176, on 2 April. The authorized English translation starts on page 4.

Amended section 11(1) provides for an elected chief councillor and deputy chief councillor in each municipality and for as many elected councillors as there are wards determined under section 13. Amended section 23(1) assigns direct election of both leadership posts to the municipality's enrolled voters under the direction, control and supervision of the State Election Commission.

## Application to Begusarai

The [Commission's unchanged-ward schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC) identifies Begusarai as a Nagar Nigam (municipal corporation) in Begusarai subdivision at serial 37. The statewide amendment does not give the corporation's formation date, define its territory or reserve any particular ward.

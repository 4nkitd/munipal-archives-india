# Belagavi City Corporation jurisdiction and office

## City and district are different jurisdictions

Belagavi City Corporation is the urban local body for Belagavi city, also known as Belgaum. The Local Government Directory identifies it as municipal corporation **251824**. The [district municipal directory](https://belagavi.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists City Corporation Belagavi separately from entries for Athani, Bailhongal, Chikkodi and other towns. The district directory is not a legal schedule of city limits.

## Office and streetlight service

The corporation's contact page puts the office at CTS No. 4821/27A, RS No. 1005, Subhash Nagar, Belagavi 590016. It lists 0831-2405316 and 0831-2405337 for the office and `ka.belagavi.cc@gmail.com` as a corporation email. For LED streetlight issues it publishes a separate help line, including the landline 0831-2003125. The PIN on the district's service pages is not used here as the headquarters PIN.

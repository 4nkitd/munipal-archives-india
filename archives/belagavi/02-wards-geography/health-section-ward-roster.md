# Belagavi Health Section roster for wards 1 to 58

## What the ward table records

The corporation's Health Section table covers every integer from 1 to 58. It separates the serial and ward numbers, then lists the senior or junior health inspector, supervisor and a column headed `PKs`. The corporation's [Ward Information selector](https://belagavicitycorp.org/WebSiteBGM/WardInfromation.aspx) independently offers wards 1 through 58. The roster is undated and records staff assignments, not council seats, polling stations or legal boundaries.

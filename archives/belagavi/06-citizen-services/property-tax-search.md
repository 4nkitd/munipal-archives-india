# Belagavi property tax lookup and payment acknowledgement

## Use the city corporation's form

Belagavi district's **Pay Property Tax** page publishes the [Belagavi City Corporation property portal](https://belagavicitycorp.org/WebSiteBGM/PropertySearch1.aspx). The option **View Property Details & Pay Online** shows an [embedded search form](https://belagavicitycorp.org/WebSiteBGM/PropertySearch.aspx) with two modes: **Search by PId** and **Search by Criteria**.

The criteria instructions list ward number, old assessment number, new assessment number, owner name and mobile number. The form instructs users to download or print the acknowledgement after a successful online payment. This record describes the form and does not establish a tax rate, rebate or due date.

The city's separate MRC citizen-services page contains a **Search Property** link pointing to Ballari. Use the Belagavi-specific address published by the district for this service. No personal property lookup or payment was submitted to verify the form.

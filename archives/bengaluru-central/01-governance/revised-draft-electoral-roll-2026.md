# Bengaluru Central City Corporation revised draft electoral-roll counts

## Corporation totals

The Karnataka State Election Commission's [9 March 2026 ward-wise revised draft](https://karsec.karnataka.gov.in/Document/Files/GBA%20Ward%20wise%20total%20count%2009.03.2026%20updated#page=1), pages 1–2, records 1,425,676 voters across 1,303 polling stations in Bengaluru Central City Corporation. Its breakdown is 726,445 male, 698,928 female and 303 in the source's "Others" column. The table covers 63 ward rows. It is a dated draft, not the final roll, an election result, a current polling-place assignment, or a census count.

# Bengaluru Central City Corporation final ward delimitation

## Final ward edition

The [GBA final notification map portal](https://gba.karnataka.gov.in/gba-corporations-delimitation-2025/index.html) groups Central's 63 numbered wards by Assembly constituency. The [State Election Commission circular of 24 November 2025](https://karsec.karnataka.gov.in/Document/Files/GBA%20Revised%20circular%20for%20%20Preparation%20of%20%20Voter%20List#page=1) cites the five final delimitation notifications dated 19 November 2025. The date on this record is the date of that final notified edition, not a publication date printed on the web portal. The ward names and population figures below are transcribed from the corresponding GBA sheets.

## Ward directory

The numbers are local to Central. A group contains only the wards listed for this corporation; it does not establish that the whole Assembly constituency lies within this city. Population figures are the sheets' `TOT_P` column, not registered voter counts or a new census estimate. Use the linked GBA map portal for geometry; this directory does not redraw ward boundaries.

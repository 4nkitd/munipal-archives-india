# Bengaluru East City Corporation revised draft electoral-roll counts

## Corporation totals

The Karnataka State Election Commission's [9 March 2026 ward-wise revised draft](https://karsec.karnataka.gov.in/Document/Files/GBA%20Ward%20wise%20total%20count%2009.03.2026%20updated#page=7), pages 7–8, records 1,041,396 voters across 919 polling stations in Bengaluru East City Corporation. Its breakdown is 549,170 male, 491,968 female and 258 in the source's "Others" column. The table covers 50 ward rows. It is a dated draft, not the final roll, an election result, a current polling-place assignment, or a census count.

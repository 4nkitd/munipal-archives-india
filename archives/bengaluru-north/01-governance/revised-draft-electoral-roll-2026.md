# Bengaluru North City Corporation revised draft electoral-roll counts

## Corporation totals

The Karnataka State Election Commission's [9 March 2026 ward-wise revised draft](https://karsec.karnataka.gov.in/Document/Files/GBA%20Ward%20wise%20total%20count%2009.03.2026%20updated#page=3), pages 3–4, records 1,954,272 voters across 1,706 polling stations in Bengaluru North City Corporation. Its breakdown is 994,319 male, 959,572 female and 381 in the source's "Others" column. The table covers 72 ward rows. It is a dated draft, not the final roll, an election result, a current polling-place assignment, or a census count.

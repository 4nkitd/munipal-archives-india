# Bengaluru South City Corporation revised draft electoral-roll counts

## Corporation totals

The Karnataka State Election Commission's [9 March 2026 ward-wise revised draft](https://karsec.karnataka.gov.in/Document/Files/GBA%20Ward%20wise%20total%20count%2009.03.2026%20updated#page=5), pages 5–6, records 1,744,613 voters across 1,614 polling stations in Bengaluru South City Corporation. Its breakdown is 903,348 male, 841,040 female and 225 in the source's "Others" column. The table covers 72 ward rows. It is a dated draft, not the final roll, an election result, a current polling-place assignment, or a census count.

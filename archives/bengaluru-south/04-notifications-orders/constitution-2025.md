# Bengaluru South City Corporation constitution notification

## Operative notification

[Karnataka Gazette Extraordinary, Part III, No. 522, dated 2 September 2025](https://udd.karnataka.gov.in/uploads/media_to_upload1756807571.pdf) publishes Notification No. UDD 170 BBS 2025. The State Government constituted Bengaluru South City Corporation under sections 5(3) and 7(3) of the Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024, Karnataka Act No. 36 of 2025. This is the final notification, not the proposal published on 19 July 2025 in Gazette No. 403. The notice records that the Government considered objections and suggestions. It was issued by order and in the name of the Governor by Nandakumar B., Under Secretary to Government, Urban Development Department. Its closing provision gives effect from final Gazette publication.

## Municipal limit in Schedule-I

Item 2, pages 5–11, sets out South's municipal boundary. The table is a locator for the text of the schedule, not a substitute for its routes or measurements. The label BDA, where used, refers to the Bangalore Development Authority.

| Adjoining corporation or area as headed | Gazette pages | Landmarks in the description |
|---|---|---|
| West City Corporation | 5–6 | Shri Honnappa Swamy Temple; Bangalore-Mysore expressway link road; Ittamadu Main Road |
| Central City Corporation | 6–7 | Madhavan Park; Mountain Road; 1st Cross Road |
| East City Corporation | 7 | Neptune Car Zone; Belandur tank |
| BDA | 7–11 | St. Stephens Mar Thoma Church near Sarjapur Road; the last stretch joins NH 44 and returns toward Sarjapur Road |

These are the corporation limits, not the internal ward limits.

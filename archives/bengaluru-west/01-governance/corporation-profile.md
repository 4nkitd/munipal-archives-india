# Bengaluru West City Corporation profile

## Municipal jurisdiction

Bengaluru West City Corporation is a municipal corporation in Karnataka's Greater Bengaluru Area. The Government of Karnataka constituted it on 2 September 2025. Schedule-I item 1 of [Notification No. UDD 170 BBS 2025](https://udd.karnataka.gov.in/uploads/media_to_upload1756807571.pdf#page=2) defines its municipal limits. It is not a zone of the former Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP). Under section 7(4) of the [Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024](https://udd.karnataka.gov.in/uploads/media_to_upload1747205958.pdf), BBMP ceased to exist on issuance of the final notification. The Act gives the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) its own role coordinating the city corporations and other agencies.

## Finding the right office

The [corporation website](https://bengaluruwest.karnataka.gov.in/home) is separate from the [GBA website](https://gba.karnataka.gov.in/). The GBA's final ward material lists 112 wards for this corporation. The [Know Your New City Corporation and Ward tool](https://gba.karnataka.gov.in/KnowYourNewCorporation/index.html) is the official starting point for an address lookup. Ward numbers are local to each corporation, so use the corporation name with the ward number when checking a record or contacting an office.

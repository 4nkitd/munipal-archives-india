# Bengaluru West City Corporation revised draft electoral-roll counts

## Corporation totals

The Karnataka State Election Commission's [9 March 2026 ward-wise revised draft](https://karsec.karnataka.gov.in/Document/Files/GBA%20Ward%20wise%20total%20count%2009.03.2026%20updated#page=9), pages 9–11, records 2,726,573 voters across 2,482 polling stations in Bengaluru West City Corporation. Its breakdown is 1,396,470 male, 1,329,635 female and 468 in the source's "Others" column. The table covers 112 ward rows. It is a dated draft, not the final roll, an election result, a current polling-place assignment, or a census count.

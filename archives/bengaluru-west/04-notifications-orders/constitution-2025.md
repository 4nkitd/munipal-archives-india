# Bengaluru West City Corporation constitution notification

## Operative notification

[Karnataka Gazette Extraordinary, Part III, No. 522, dated 2 September 2025](https://udd.karnataka.gov.in/uploads/media_to_upload1756807571.pdf) publishes Notification No. UDD 170 BBS 2025. The State Government constituted Bengaluru West City Corporation under sections 5(3) and 7(3) of the Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024, Karnataka Act No. 36 of 2025. This is the final notification, not the proposal published on 19 July 2025 in Gazette No. 403. The notice records that the Government considered objections and suggestions. It was issued by order and in the name of the Governor by Nandakumar B., Under Secretary to Government, Urban Development Department. Its closing provision gives effect from final Gazette publication.

## Municipal limit in Schedule-I

Item 1, pages 2–5, sets out West's municipal boundary. The table is a locator for the text of the schedule, not a substitute for its routes or measurements. The label BDA, where used, refers to the Bangalore Development Authority.

| Adjoining corporation or area as headed | Gazette pages | Landmarks in the description |
|---|---|---|
| North City Corporation | 2 | Durga saw mill on Tumkur Road, NH 75; Mathikere Main Road; New Bel Road |
| Central City Corporation | 2–3 | K C Palace Club; Palace Road; Sheshadripuram Main Road |
| South City Corporation | 3–4 | Aashirwad Ladies PG; 10th Main Road; Aurobindo Marg; Krishna Rajendra Road |
| BDA | 4–5 | Erayaa VN Serenity; H.Golahalli lake; Vrishabawathi River |

These are the corporation limits, not the internal ward limits.

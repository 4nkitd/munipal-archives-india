# Brahmapur Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and civic responsibilities

## Municipal area and office

Brahmapur Municipal Corporation administers civic services within corporation limits in Ganjam district, Odisha. The corporation uses **Brahmapur** in its English name; the State Election Commission's 2022 results use **Berhampur** for the same body. The [state urban department's local-body register](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/en/node/190939) lists 42 wards and a population of 356,598 under its Census 2011 series. The corporation serves the city, not all of Ganjam district. The register does not identify individual boundary parcels.

The corporation publishes its office address as Corporation Office, Brahmapur, Ganjam, Odisha 760001. Its toll-free helpline, 1800-3456-796, is listed as open 24 hours.

## Who does what

The Mayor and elected council lead the corporation. The Municipal Commissioner exercises executive functions with the department heads. The corporation lists sanitation, roads, street lighting, markets, licensing, public health and water-supply coordination among its responsibilities. Its portal also publishes tenders, corrigenda and awards and accepts civic grievances.

# Berhampur ward seats in the 2022 municipal election

## Coverage and reservation

The Odisha State Election Commission's [2022 election information book](https://sec.odisha.gov.in/Landing/ULBGEto2022) dates the poll to 24 March 2022. Its election statistics list 42 wards for Berhampur Municipal Corporation, up from the 40 shown for the previous general election, with 250 polling booths and 255,326 electors for the 2022 round.

The elected-corporator return lists wards 1 through 42 at serial numbers 60 through 101. These are the 2022 seat reservations, grouped by the commission's labels:

| Reservation | Wards |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled Tribe Woman | 1 |
| Scheduled Caste Woman | 19, 23 |
| Scheduled Caste | 9, 25 |
| Reserved For Woman | 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 20, 28, 30, 31, 33, 36, 37, 38, 39 |
| Unreserved | 2, 7, 8, 11, 12, 17, 18, 21, 22, 24, 26, 27, 29, 32, 34, 35, 40, 41, 42 |

## Ward 42

The SEC return records E Vivek Reddy as elected for the unreserved ward 42. The [corporation's standing-committee page](https://www.berhampur.gov.in/corporators.php) lists wards 1 through 41 in its table and identifies E. Vivek Reddy separately as Deputy Mayor. Neither list describes ward boundaries. Consult the applicable delimitation schedule to determine the ward for an address.

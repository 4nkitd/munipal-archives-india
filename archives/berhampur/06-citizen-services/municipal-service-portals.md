# Brahmapur holding tax, certificates, licences and grievances

## Choose the service

| Service | Where the corporation directs residents |
| --- | --- |
| Property or holding tax | [Brahmapur tax portal](https://www.tax.berhampur.gov.in/) to view demand and pay holding tax. |
| Birth and death registration and certificates | [Odisha Birth and Death Registration System](https://www.birthdeath.odisha.gov.in/) to register an event or download a certificate. |
| Marriage registration and trade licences | [Odisha SUJOG](https://sujog.odisha.gov.in/) for marriage certificates or to apply for or renew a trade licence. |
| Town Hall or Kalyan Mandap booking | [Brahmapur e-Seva](https://eseva.berhampur.gov.in/). |
| Right to Information | [Corporation RTI page](https://www.berhampur.gov.in/rti.php) for the Public Information Officer route. |

## Report a civic problem

The corporation's [file-a-grievance link](https://www.berhampur.gov.in/grievance.php) redirected to Odisha's Jana Sunani public grievance portal when checked. The corporation also publishes a separate [tracking page](https://www.berhampur.gov.in/track.php) that asks for a tracking code; it does not say whether it accepts Jana Sunani codes. Jana Sunani has its own [complaint status page](https://janasunani.odisha.gov.in/complaint-status).

For forms and offline services, the service page directs residents to the corporation office and its [downloads page](https://www.berhampur.gov.in/downloads.php). The service directory does not set fees or disposal deadlines.

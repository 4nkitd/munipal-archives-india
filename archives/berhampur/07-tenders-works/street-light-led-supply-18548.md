# Brahmapur tender BeMC/18548 for 60-watt streetlight LED components

## Notice and timetable

The corporation's procurement page lists BeMC/18548 as a supply tender for 60-watt streetlight LED drivers and modules. The public page gives the following schedule:

| Event | Date and time shown |
| --- | --- |
| Published | 17 November 2026 |
| Bid closing | 25 November 2026, 1:02 p.m. |
| Bid opening | 26 November 2026, 1:02 p.m. |

The listing shows dashes for estimated cost, earnest money deposit and tender fee. Those marks are not a statement that fees or deposits are waived. The public summary does not specify installations or assign the supply to individual wards. It links the [tender attachment](https://www.berhampur.gov.in/download.php?doc=212) for the bid documents. The listing showed no award at the time of verification.

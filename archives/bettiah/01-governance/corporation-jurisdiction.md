# Bettiah Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and classification

## Municipal jurisdiction

In its final Phase II ward-formation register, the Bihar State Election Commission lists Bettiah at serial 35 as नगर निगम, in Bettiah subdivision of West Champaran district. The register marks the body as upgraded (उत्क्रमित). The 46 wards are those of the corporation, not the whole subdivision or district.

## District evidence

West Champaran district maintains a public-utility entry explicitly headed “Bettiah Nagar Nigam”. It lists the Bettiah office in PIN 845438. [District record](https://westchamparan.nic.in/public-utility/bettiah-nagar-nigam/).

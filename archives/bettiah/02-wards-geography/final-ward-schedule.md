# Bettiah final ward schedule: boundaries and census population

## Reading Form 6

The SEC’s Form 6 lists wards 1–46, their four boundaries and their populations on the 2011 Census basis. Ward 1 uses the Bettiah–Lauriya main road on its northern side and the road between the southwest corner of Hazari Tola jail and Manuapul on its western side. The full Hindi boundary descriptions are transcribed in `wards.json`; the SEC links a map alongside each ward. These figures are residents counted on the stated census basis, not electoral-roll totals.

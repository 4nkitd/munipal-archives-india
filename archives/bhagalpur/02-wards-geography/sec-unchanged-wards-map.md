# Bhagalpur municipal ward map and 2022 unchanged-ward listing

## Commission listing

Serial 40 of the State Election Commission's [unchanged-ward list](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC) identifies Bhagalpur Municipal Corporation in Bhagalpur Sadar subdivision. It records 51 wards and marks the entry अपरिवर्तित (unchanged). The schedule gives a total population of 400,146 for that entry, with 32,681 Scheduled Caste, 1,061 Scheduled Tribe and 366,404 other residents. These are figures in the schedule, not a current population estimate.

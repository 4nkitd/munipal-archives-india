# Bhagalpur quotation notice for submersible pumps and water tanks

## Equipment and submission

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The Municipal Commissioner circulated memo 1186 on 3 June 2026 for publication. The [district notice page](https://bhagalpur.nic.in/notice/municipal-corporation-officebhagalpurshort-term-quotation-invitation-notice/) shows 5 June 2026 as the start date. It carries two attachments; this record covers the one for 1.5 HP pumps and tanks.

| Selected item | Quantity |
| --- | ---: |
| 1.5 HP submersible pump sets with one-year warranty | 5 |
| 1.5 HP starter panels | 10 |
| Three-core copper wire, 2.5 mm | 100 metres |
| 2,000-litre water tanks | 5 |

Quotations had to reach the corporation by 3 p.m. on 9 June 2026. Opening was scheduled after 3:30 p.m. that day. The notice required the bidder's registration number, separate envelopes for quoted items, and rates that included taxes and delivery to the waterworks store in Barari. Delivery was due within three days of the work order. The notice does not document an award or identify installation sites.

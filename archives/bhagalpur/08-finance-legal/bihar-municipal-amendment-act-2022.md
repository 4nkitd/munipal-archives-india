# Bihar Municipal (Amendment) Act, 2022: Bhagalpur corporation and elections

## Statutory scope

The Bihar Gazette Extraordinary, Patna No. 176, published Bihar Act 06 of 2022 on 2 April 2022 after the Governor's assent on 1 April. It amends the Bihar Municipal Act, 2007 (Bihar Act 11 of 2007), applies throughout Bihar and came into force on publication. The authorized English translation starts on page 4 of the gazette.

Amended section 11(1) provides for one elected chief councillor and one elected deputy chief councillor and ties the number of elected councillors to the number of wards determined under section 13. Amended section 23(1) provides for the municipality's enrolled voters to elect the two leadership posts directly under the direction, control and supervision of the State Election Commission.

## Application to Bhagalpur

The [Commission's unchanged-ward schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC) lists Bhagalpur at serial 40 as a Nagar Nigam (municipal corporation) in Bhagalpur Sadar. The gazette sets statewide rules. It does not establish Bhagalpur's formation date, territorial limits or individual ward lines.

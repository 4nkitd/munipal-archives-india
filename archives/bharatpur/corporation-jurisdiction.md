# Bharatpur corporation designation and municipal jurisdiction

## Municipal status and office

The corporation's official history records the establishment of a municipality at Bharatpur headquarters in 1905 and its designation as a municipal council in 1973. It attributes the grant of Municipal Corporation status to a Rajasthan Local Self Government Department notification dated 13 June 2014. The history does not identify the notification number, and the original 2014 instrument was not recovered for this archive. The date is the notification date reported by the corporation, not an independently established commencement date.

An [official Bharatpur ward notice dated 12 July 2019](ward-amendment-2019.md) was issued from the office of the Nagar Nigam. The subsequent [2025 Gazette](ward-delimitation-2025.md) expressly identifies the local body as Nagar Nigam Bharatpur. Row 46 of the [state local-body directory](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) lists it as a Nagar Nigam in Bharatpur district and gives `ceobtp.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in` as its Raj-mail address. The municipal history locates its main office at Bijlighar Chauraha near Mathura Gate.

## City, wards and district

The corporate jurisdiction is the notified urban local body, not the district. The state's directory lists separate municipalities for Bayana, Bhusawar, Nadbai, Rupwas, Uchchain and Weir. The municipal history quotes 56.14 square kilometres and gives a 50-ward figure. Those figures are historical page content: the published 2025 schedule has 65 wards and includes urban street descriptions as well as certain named revenue villages. The 50-ward figure is not carried into the current archive profile, and the historical area is not used to certify the current perimeter.

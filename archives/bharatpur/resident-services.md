# Bharatpur municipal services and application routes

## Match the service to its portal

The corporation's [About page](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/mcbharatpur/sm/jankalyan-category-and-entry-type/121462/200/4/1) lists street lighting and maintenance, street and sewer cleaning, public parks and registration of births and deaths among its duties. It locates the main office at Bijlighar Chauraha near Mathura Gate. The [state ULB directory](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) gives `ceobtp.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in` for corporation correspondence.

Use the Rajasthan Local Self Government Department's online services dashboard for the application links and instructions it publishes. The dashboard gives sewer connection a separate checklist, fee and timeline entry. It gives building-plan approval its own applicant manual, checklist or procedure, fee and supporting orders. For trade licence and renewal it lists a Rajasthan SSO link, RajNivesh instructions and a mandatory-documents link. The catalogue does not settle which authority serves a particular property or guarantee that an application is complete.

## Certificates and complaints

For birth, death and marriage registration, the state's [Pehchan](https://pehchan.rajasthan.gov.in/) portal provides search, public applications and certificate downloads. It says the urban birth and death registrar is based at the urban local body's headquarters. It instructs households to report an event within 21 days, using Form 1 for a birth and Form 2 for a death. The portal also lists e-Mitra kiosks and the state civil-registration help line `1800-180-6785`.

[Rajasthan Sampark](https://sampark.rajasthan.gov.in/) publishes separate grievance registration and status links and a toll-free number, `181`. No login, complaint, payment or certificate retrieval was attempted. These listings describe the official access points, not the result of a transaction.

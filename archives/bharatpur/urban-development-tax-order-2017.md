# Urban development tax relief order archived by Bharatpur

## Scope and end date

Bharatpur's municipal portal holds a copy of the Rajasthan Local Self Government Department's DLB/17/34576. The [signed scan](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files//Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/MCBPR/2023/Order/O_190123_241608d4-7a93-4085-9ab4-953fc7d5ab28.pdf) bears the date 29 September 2017. The portal title also mentions 24 October 2017; that is not the date written on the order. This was a statewide order circulated to municipal corporations, councils and boards. It was not a Bharatpur council resolution.

The order cites section 107(4) of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009, and refers to departmental order 6839–7305 of 14 March 2017. That earlier order granted 100 percent relief on penalty and interest when paying outstanding urban development tax from 1 April through 30 September 2017. The September order extends that relief through 31 December 2017. It does not waive the principal tax, give property assessment rates or state the revenue Bharatpur collected under the extension.

## Current applications

The [state online services dashboard](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371475/2390) lists Urban Development Tax, Property ID Creation and Lease Money Deposit separately. Follow the dashboard for the current service rather than applying the expired waiver to a new bill. This archived order is not a budget, payment record or statement of current liability.

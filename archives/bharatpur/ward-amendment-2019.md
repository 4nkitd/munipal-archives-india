# Bharatpur's July 2019 revised ward boundary notice

## Which wards changed in 2019

The municipal office's revised delimitation notice, न.नि.भ./2019-20/4030, is dated 12 July 2019. The [signed notice](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files//Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/MCBPR/2023/Notification/O_190123_45900a33-aac3-41a3-b495-941040ef3c5c.pdf) says that the state's ward redetermination used the 2011 census and assigned Bharatpur Corporation 65 wards. It refers to state notification DLB/19/1523–2194 dated 10 June 2019 and municipal public notice no. 3620. The local notice does not provide new descriptions for every ward. It lists partial changes to wards 8, 9, 43, 44, 49, 55, 56, 58 and 59.

## Reading the historical descriptions

The first sheet gives an idea of the detail needed to locate a boundary. Ward 8 uses the Helak and Murwara railway gates, Jaipur–Mathura highway and the mustard research junction. The text includes Malipura and Sewar Fort. Ward 9 starts at Om Cloth Store in Sewar Main Bazaar and uses the Panchayat Samiti junction, the highway, the old railway crossing and the Helak railway gate. The opening of the Ward 43 entry follows the culvert at Binarayan Gate and the boundary of a cemetery, then refers to Saluja Hospital and 80 Feet Road.

This is a historical notice revising specific ward descriptions, not the corporation's founding order or the current ward schedule. The [September 2025 Gazette](ward-delimitation-2025.md) also contains 65 wards, which does not establish that every boundary remained the same.

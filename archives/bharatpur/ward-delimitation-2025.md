# Bharatpur ward delimitation gazette for 65 wards

## Approval after objections

The Rajasthan Local Self Government Department dated DLB/25/16981 on 1 September 2025. It appeared in the Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary, Part 6(क), on 13 September 2025. The Bharatpur Collector forwarded the draft ward order, objections and his opinion. After considering the objections, the state agreed with his opinion and approved the proposed order under sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009, and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994.

Form A assigns a written boundary and population figure to each ward numbered 1 through 65. All 65 are present across the 33 PDF pages. The [state demarcation directory](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) links this schedule in row 46. The final entries on PDF page 33 appear on printed folio 678.

A whole-village entry and a street or railway landmark serve different purposes. For a street-based ward, read the direction of travel and the included or excluded side in the Hindi text. A number used in the [2019 notice](ward-amendment-2019.md) does not identify the same territory by default. None of these population entries is a current headcount or an electoral roll.

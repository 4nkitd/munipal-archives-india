# Bhavnagar city jurisdiction and head office

Gujarat's [corporation directory](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/corporations.php) lists Bhavnagar at entry 5. BMC describes its remit as providing basic infrastructure to people in the city. Its [demographics page](https://bmcgujarat.com/en/the-city/demographics/) reports a city area of 119 square kilometres. The page gives no survey year or boundary schedule, so the figure cannot establish whether an individual plot falls inside the corporation.

BMC gives its head office as Bhavnagar Mahanagar Seva Sadan, Sir Mangal Sinhji Road, near Kalanala, Bhavnagar 364001. It lists 0278-2424801 and the commissioner's office mailbox, `comm-bmc@gujarat.gov.in`.

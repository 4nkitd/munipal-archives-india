# Bhavnagar's 13 electoral ward names

BMC's elected-body directory lets readers select councillors by ward. Its selector uses the following names:

| Ward | Name | Ward | Name |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Chitra - Fulsar - Nari | 8 | Vadva-A |
| 2 | Kumbharvada | 9 | Bortalav |
| 3 | Vadva-B | 10 | Kaliyabid - Sidsar - Adhewada |
| 4 | Karchaliyapara | 11 | South Sardarnagar - Adhewada |
| 5 | North Krushnanagar Ruva | 12 | North Sardarnagar - Tarsamiya |
| 6 | Pirchhalla | 13 | Ghogha Circle - Akwada |
| 7 | Takhteshwar | | |

Adhewada appears in the names of both wards 10 and 11. A locality name alone does not settle the ward for a particular address.

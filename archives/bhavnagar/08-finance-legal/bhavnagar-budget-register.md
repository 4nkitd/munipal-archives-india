# Bhavnagar municipal budget register

BMC files its budget documents by financial year. On the first page, entries 1 through 4 cover 2026-27, 2025-26, 2024-25 and 2023-24. Older entries are out of sequence: entry 5 is 2014-15, entry 9 is 2021-22 and entry 10 is 2022-23.

Use the year beside the budget link rather than the serial number to locate a document. The register identifies these as budgets; it does not give actual expenditure or a date of adoption for each year.

# Municipal Corporation Bhilai-Charoda profile

## Corporation and jurisdiction

The municipal portal says the Bhilai-Charoda municipal council was constituted in 2000 after the dissolution of SADA and acquired corporation status on 8 June 2015. It gives 40 wards following the new delimitation. Both the [State Election Commission register](https://cgsec.gov.in/BootStrap/PDF/sankhyiki/nagarpalik_nigam.pdf) and the [Durg district register](https://durg.gov.in/en/urban-local-body/) list Bhilai-Charoda and Bhilai separately. The municipal site's historical reference to "old Bhilai" does not make this corporation an alias for Bhilai Municipal Corporation.

## Office

The [district directory](https://durg.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) places the office near the government hospital at Bhilai-3. It lists 0788-2283410 and `nagarnigambhilaicharoda@gmail.com`. The corporation site separately publishes `cmobhilaicharoda@gmail.com` for the Commissioner office.

# Bhilai-Charoda ward scope in the 2021 municipal election

## Separate municipal result

Bhilai-Charoda is municipal body entry 9 on PDF pages 4–5 of the State Election Commission's 2021 elected-councillor list. The table starts at ward 1 Indira Nagar Hathkhoj; the next entries include Hathkhoj Basti, Akoladih, Jarway, Dadar Patharra and Umda. At the end of the list are Sirsa Kala Basti and Sirsa Kala Bhatapara at 36 and 37, Somni at 38, Morid at 39 and Ganiyari at 40. These are election labels, not a legal description of the boundaries.

## Reservation proceedings

The [Collector's signed reservation order](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3851ddf5058cf22df63d3344ad89919cf/uploads/2021/01/2021011894.pdf) of 15 January 2021 scheduled Bhilai-Charoda's 40-ward exercise for 2 p.m. on 22 January in the Collectorate meeting room, Durg. It listed Risali in a different session on 23 January. The commission's election list likewise identifies Bhilai-Charoda separately from Bhilai and Risali.

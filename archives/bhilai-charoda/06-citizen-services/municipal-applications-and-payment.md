# Bhilai-Charoda municipal applications and tax payment links

## Locate the correct municipal service

| Service | Where the official page points |
| --- | --- |
| Property and water tax payment | The [municipal tender page header](https://nagarnigambhilaicharoda.com/tender-820/) links the corporation's [payment page](https://bhilaicharoda.urbanecg.gov.in/BCHD/CitizenHome.html). |
| Building permission | The [state BPMS](https://bpms.sudacg.in/) directs Bhilai-Charoda to [NiwasPass](https://www.niwaspass.com/). |
| Birth and death registration and certificates | The corporation lists the Civil Registration System in its e-governance menu. |
| Marriage, shop establishment and trade registrations | The corporation routes these to [Chhattisgarh e-District](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in/Workflow/MisReportList.html?lang=hi). |

The Public Works menu distinguishes building permission from a new water connection. The tax link did not respond during verification, and no payment or application was submitted. The district lists 0788-2283410 for the municipal office near the government hospital at Bhilai-3.

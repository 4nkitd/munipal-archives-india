# Bhilai-Charoda 2016–17 budget proposal and 2015–16 revised figures

## Corporation-wide figures

| Item | 2015–16 revised | 2016–17 proposed |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Receipts | ₹56,16,07,138 | ₹1,27,62,81,320 |
| Expenditure | ₹57,02,57,217 | ₹1,31,23,17,688 |
| Opening balance | ₹4,51,37,079 | ₹3,64,87,000 |
| Closing balance | ₹3,64,87,000 | ₹4,50,632 |

The first page identifies the two columns of figures as the revised 2015–16 estimates and the 2016–17 budget proposal. The proposed opening balance plus proposed receipts, less proposed expenditure, gives the stated closing balance. These amounts are not audited results or ward-wise allocations.

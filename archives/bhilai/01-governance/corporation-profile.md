# Municipal Corporation Bhilai profile

## Corporation and jurisdiction

Bhilai is one of four municipal corporations in Durg district. The Chhattisgarh State Election Commission lists its formation date as 8 June 1998 and gives Bhilai, Bhilai-Charoda, Risali and Durg separate entries. The [district urban local body register](https://durg.gov.in/en/urban-local-body/) lists 70 wards for Bhilai. The commission's 2021 results separately enumerate Bhilai's wards and those of the two 40-ward corporations. Use the corporation named on the record, not a wider reference to the Bhilai urban area.

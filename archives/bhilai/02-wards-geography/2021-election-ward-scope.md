# Bhilai ward scope in the 2021 municipal election

## The Bhilai entry

Bhilai is municipal body entry 7 in the 2021 elected-councillor results, on PDF pages 2–3. Each row gives the ward number and name, elected councillor and political affiliation. Ward 1 is Junwani, 2 Smriti Nagar, 3 Model Town, 4 Nehru Nagar and 9 Rajiv Nagar (Supela). The end of the block includes 67 Sector 7 West, 68 Sector 8, 69 Sector 9 Hospital Sector and 70 Shaheed Kaushal Yadav Ward.

## Separate ward numbering

The commission starts new blocks and restarts ward numbering for Risali and Bhilai-Charoda. Thus a number such as ward 1 is only meaningful with its corporation name. The [Durg district register](https://durg.gov.in/en/urban-local-body/) agrees with the Bhilai count of 70. The election table does not give legal ward boundaries or establish current officeholders. Its edition identifies the election as 2021; the exact publication date is not given.

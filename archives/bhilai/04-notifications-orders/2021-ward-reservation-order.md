# Bhilai ward reservation hearing order of 24 February 2021

## Proceedings announced

The Collector and District Election Officer's order of 24 February 2021 set the ward reservation proceedings for Municipal Corporation Bhilai at 11 a.m. on 2 March 2021 in the Collectorate meeting room, Durg. It explicitly gives 70 wards and says elected representatives could attend the proceedings. The document refers to the Urban Administration and Development Department letter F 1–212/2019/18 of 17 February 2021, section 10 of the municipal statute of 1956 and the 1994 ward reservation rules.

## What the order establishes

The signed order is a schedule for the reservation exercise, not a ward-by-ward list of reservations or a statement of ward boundaries. It is about Bhilai alone. The Collector's January 2021 order had already listed separate reservation sessions for Bhilai-Charoda and Risali.

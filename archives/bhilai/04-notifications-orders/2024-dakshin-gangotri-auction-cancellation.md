# Bhilai cancellation of three Dakshin Gangotri plot auctions

## Auctions affected

The Bhilai municipal commissioner's notice no. 512/2024, dated 16 August 2024, cancels auction IDs **157430, 157431 and 157432** in Block 30 of the Dakshin Gangotri commercial scheme. It cites an error in plot size and states that the other tenders remained unchanged. The notice cites earlier invitations no. 463/2024 of 8 August and no. 498/2024 of 14 August.

The district listing begins on 23 August 2024, after the date written on the signed notice. Its attachment contains the earlier invitation and the cancellation. The invitation proposed 30-year leases; the figures or dates attached to the three cancelled IDs are no longer valid for that call. The cancellation does not establish a subsequent auction or award.

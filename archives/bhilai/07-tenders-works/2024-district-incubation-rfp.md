# District incubation hub development partner RFP 754

## Issuer and scope

RFP 754, signed 4 July 2024, was issued by the Secretary of the Durg District Urban Public Service Society. The district notice page identifies the listing with Municipal Corporation Bhilai, but the signed letter describes a district incubation hub and names the society as client. This is not a Bhilai-only appropriation or evidence of a change in municipal jurisdiction.

## Published competition terms

Selection was to use combined quality and cost based selection. Sealed proposals had to arrive by speed or registered post by 5 p.m. on 19 July 2024, with opening listed for 11 a.m. on 22 July. The notice required a non-refundable ₹1,000 processing fee and refundable ₹1,00,000 earnest money deposit, payable to the Secretary, DDUPSS, at Durg by demand draft or banker's cheque. The invitation does not identify the selected partner.

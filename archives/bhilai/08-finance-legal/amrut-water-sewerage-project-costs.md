The portal labels the local body "Bhilai Nagar" and lists Bhilai-Charoda separately in the state project index. The amounts above are the detail-page project costs, not audited expenditures or a corporation-wide budget. The water entry's "DPR prepared" milestone means the detailed project report was prepared. The entries do not identify wards or certify that the works are complete.

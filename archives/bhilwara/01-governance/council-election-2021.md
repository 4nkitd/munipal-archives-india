# Historical election record: Bhilwara Municipal Council in 2021

## Council electorate

In the State Election Commission's statistical book for the 2021 general election in 91 urban local bodies, the electorate table lists Bhilwara city as **Nagar Parishad Bhilwara**. Row 8 gives 273,300 electors across 70 wards. The same table counts Asind, Gangapur, Gulabpura, Jahazpur, Mandalgarh and Shahpura separately from the city.

The council figures predate Bhilwara's 2024 upgrade. Although the corporation's 2025 schedule also lists 70 wards, the election report does not establish that the boundaries are the same.

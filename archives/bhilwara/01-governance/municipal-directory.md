# Bhilwara city corporation in the state local bodies directory

## Finding the city entry

Choose the row with **Bhilwara** in both the district and urban local body columns. Row 54 identifies the city as a Nagar Nigam, gives its 70 wards and lists `mcbhilwara.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in`. The link at the end of the row opens the city's ward schedule.

The district also includes separate bodies: Baneda is a Nagar Palika in row 53, Bigod a Nagar Palika in row 55, and Shahpura a Nagar Parishad in row 62. For addresses in those towns use their own municipal rows.

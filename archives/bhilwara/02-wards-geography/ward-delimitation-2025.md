# Bhilwara ward boundaries approved in September 2025

## Approval and schedule

The 17 September 2025 Extraordinary Gazette, Part 6(क), publishes notification DLB/25/14241. The state considered objections to the draft forwarded by the Bhilwara Collector and approved the ward formation under sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994.

Form A describes each ward by a boundary walk. Ward 1 begins on PDF page 1 at Chauth Mata temple and has a listed population of 4,918. Ward 70 begins on page 28 at the western corporation boundary on NH 48 near Hathi Bhata Ashram and lists 5,483. These figures belong to the 2025 schedule. Follow the full walk for an address: the description specifies which side of a road it includes.

# Bhilwara Municipal Council upgraded to a municipal corporation

## Change in legal status

The department lists notification **2781**, issued on **2 September 2024**, for the upgrade of Nagar Parishad Bhilwara in Bhilwara district to Nagar Nigam Bhilwara. Municipal Council and Nagar Parishad are therefore appropriate names when describing the predecessor body.

[Scanned notification](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files//Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/LSG/2026/Notification/O_160126_a2f63e17-233d-4c2d-9583-d74892aeff25.pdf). The later ward approval in the Rajasthan Gazette of 17 September 2025 names Nagar Nigam Bhilwara as the local body.

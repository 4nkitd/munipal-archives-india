# Municipal Council Bhiwadi: profile and elected council

## Municipality and jurisdiction

Municipal Council Bhiwadi, also called Nagar Parishad Bhiwadi (नगर परिषद भिवाड़ी), is a 60-ward urban local body in Khairthal-Tijara district, Rajasthan. The [state Local Self Government directory](https://department.rajasthan.gov.in/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) lists it as a Nagar Parishad at row 204. The council's own portal retains the older Alwar label. Its [history page](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/ulbbhiwadi/sm/jankalyan-category-and-entry-type/132924/935/4/16) dates the establishment of the municipality to 2009 and describes the original 35-member board and the 45 wards at the 2014 election. The state lists 104,921 residents in its [2011 Census-based municipal classification](https://lsg.urban.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/lsgs/lsg-jaipur/pdf/Download%20form/Categorisation%20of%20Municipalities%20in%20Rajasthan.pdf). That is not a current population estimate.

Bhiwadi sits on the Rajasthan side of the NCR industrial corridor, next to Dharuhera in Haryana and linked to Manesar by NH 48. Dharuhera is the immediate cross-border neighbour; Manesar is farther along the highway, not a Bhiwadi municipal boundary. The [Greater Bhiwadi planning report](https://bida.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/doitassets/bida/documents/plaining/BKT%20Report.pdf) describes RIICO's industrial estates and the wider Bhiwadi-Tapookara-Khushkhera planning area. The [Bhiwadi Integrated Development Authority](https://bida.rajasthan.gov.in/content/raj/bida/en/about-bida/aboutb.html) covers a wider notified investment region. Neither that region nor the RIICO estate network defines the council's 60 wards.

## Legal framework and municipal services

The [Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009](https://lsg.urban.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/lsgs/lsg-jaipur/pdf/Rajasthan%20Municipal%20AcT%20English.pdf) provides for municipal councils in smaller urban areas under section 5 and lists core municipal duties under section 45. These include sanitation, solid waste, drainage, public street lighting, and birth and death registration. For a service request, give the council the ward number, location and landmark. If the issue is on an industrial estate or concerns infrastructure managed by another agency, confirm who maintains that asset. The [5 October 2026 council meeting report](https://ncrkhabar.com/archives/27793), for example, distinguishes sanitation in sectors already handed over to the council from work in areas still managed by RIICO or the Rajasthan Housing Board.

The [council portal](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/ulbbhiwadi/) publishes `01493-298027` and `bhiwadi.nagarparishad@gmail.com`. The state directory separately lists `mcbhiwari.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in`. These are published office contacts, not councillor numbers.

## September 2026 election and leadership

The ward results declared on [14 September 2026](https://ncrkhabar.com/archives/27714) gave the BJP 30 seats, independents 20, the Indian National Congress 9 and the Bahujan Samaj Party 1. A majority of the 60 elected seats takes 31. The roster in [`council.json`](../council.json) records ward winners and their affiliation at that election, not any later party changes. The names, ward numbers and seat reservations follow the [Neta Kaun ward table](https://netakaun.com/en/rajasthan/khairthal-tijara/bhiwadi-nagar-parishad) and [myCorporator results](https://www.mycorporator.in/rj/2026/result/bhiwadi-nagar-parishad/), which cite Rajasthan State Election Commission data. The [Commission's reports portal](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/ULB_Election_Reports.aspx) is the official reference for certified returns.

Sheeshram Tanwar was chairperson after the November 2019 election. He won Ward 35 on a BJP ticket in 2026 but withdrew his nomination for chairperson. Gagan Tanwar also withdrew from the chairperson race, leaving Khatana as the sole candidate. The [5 October council meeting report](https://ncrkhabar.com/archives/27793) identifies Khatana as chairperson and Subhiksha as deputy chairperson.

The [14 August 2019 ward formation order](https://lsg.urban.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/lsgs/lsg-jaipur/pdf/New%20Order%20PDF/Demarcation/Bhiwadi.pdf), No. F.10 (Elec) LSG/19/7372, approved a 60-ward arrangement. The 2026 results sources report that ward lines changed before the new election. Use the current delimitation or voter roll to identify an address's ward rather than assuming the 2019 ward map still applies.

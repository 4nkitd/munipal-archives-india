# Bhiwadi wards, RIICO industrial areas and residential sectors

## What the 60 wards cover

Rajasthan's Local Self Government Department approved Bhiwadi Municipal Council's ward formation on 14 August 2019 by order F.10 (Elec) LSG/19/7372. The English order cites sections 6 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipality Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipal Election Rules, 1994. Joint Secretary Ujjwal Rathore signed the order. Its accompanying Hindi schedule records 60 wards and a total delimitation population of 104,921. That is the population used in this historical schedule, not a current population estimate.

The official [ward schedule][1] and separate [2019 municipal ward map][2] are the primary boundary references. The numbering runs from Ward 1 to Ward 60. A secondary LGD-derived directory also reports 60 wards in September 2026, but continuity of the count does not prove that every boundary remains unchanged. This archive has not verified a later replacement boundary notification.

The accompanying `archives/bhiwadi/wards.json` contains eight representative records, not all 60 wards. Each points to the 2019 schedule. Null zones and divisions mean that a municipal administrative assignment was not verified. They do not mean that the ward has no administration.

The schedule contains legacy-font Hindi whose indexed text is partly garbled. The summaries below retain identifiable locality names and avoid inventing house-by-house boundary translations. They are not cadastral polygons, electoral-roll certificates or substitutes for the notification.

## RIICO industrial areas and the Phase V question

RIICO's official allotment register uses the estate name **Bhiwadi I to IV Ph.Ghatal, Samtal**. Its unit-office document likewise identifies Bhiwadi Phases I to IV, Samtel Zone, Ghatal and Rampur Mundana under Bhiwadi Unit I. The [land-availability directory][5] lists Industrial Area Bhiwadi and Kaharani under Bhiwadi I.

The requested phrase "Phases I to V" is not adopted as a verified official estate classification here. The consulted official sources establish Phases I to IV. No separate Bhiwadi Phase V layout or notification was verified. A developer's Phase 5, such as Ashiana Surbhi's residential construction phase, must not be treated as RIICO Industrial Phase V.

| Industrial node | Official source identification | Ward caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bhiwadi Phases I to IV | RIICO allotment register, Bhiwadi Unit I, Ghatal and Samtal estate | Industrial phases are not electoral wards |
| Kaharani | RIICO land-availability list under Bhiwadi I | Kaharani's presence in Ward 49 does not map the whole industrial area |
| Chopanki | Bhiwadi II layout entry, spelled "Chopahki" on the directory; master plan also uses Chaupanki | Preserve spelling variants when searching documents |
| Khushkhera | Bhiwadi II directory | Separate from IID Khushkhera, which has its own directory entry |
| Tapukara | Bhiwadi II directory; master plan also uses Tapookara | Greater Bhiwadi planning coverage is not proof of Municipal Council coverage |

The historical Greater Bhiwadi master-plan report describes the industrial sequence as Bhiwadi in 1976, Khushkhera in 1995, Chaupanki in 1996, Tapookara in 2007 and Kaharani in 2009. Its industrial land and infrastructure tables describe 2009 conditions. They must not be presented as current vacant-plot, water-supply or operating-factory counts. Use RIICO's layout and allotment records for industrial plot identity, and the municipal schedule for electoral identity.

## UIT and BIDA residential planning geography

BIDA's [approved sector-plan index][3] publishes UIT Sectors 1, 2 and 2A, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9 and Sector 9 Extension. It also lists Sector 3 Court and Residence, commercial plans for Sectors 2A and 3A, Sector 6 Main Road, group housing in Sectors 5 and 6, Bhagat Singh Colony, commercial Bhagat Singh Colony Blocks A and B, and Panchsheel Scheme's road network.

These are planning layouts, not a municipal ward list. The same number can identify different schemes. Ward 19's UIT Sector 2 and Ward 31's RHB Sector 2 illustrate why an address should retain the authority prefix. Likewise, RIICO Unit I and Unit II are industrial administrative offices, not Ward 1 and Ward 2.

The [Greater Bhiwadi report][4] records nine UIT water-service sectors, numbered 1, 2, 2A, 3, 3A, 5, 6, 8 and 9. It distinguishes them from old Housing Board Sectors 1, 2 and 3 and Housing Board Sectors 4 and 7. These historical service arrangements explain the overlapping authority names; they do not verify today's maintenance responsibility.

### Vasundhara Nagar, Ashiana and Alwar Bypass

The master-plan report names Vasundhra Colony in its historical water-supply discussion. Ashiana Housing's [Surbhi brochure][8] gives its branch-office address as Ashiana Village, Vasundhra Nagar, Bhiwadi. This supports a residential locality reference, not a complete official boundary for every property marketed as Vasundhara Nagar.

"Ashiana" is a developer and a family of projects, not a single municipal sector. The same brochure identifies Surbhi's site as Sector 77, village Rampura, and its location map distinguishes Ashiana Town, Aangan, Gardens, Greens and other projects. The ward schedule names Ashiana Surbhi in Ward 60. That does not put all Ashiana developments in Ward 60.

Ashiana's own [Aangan page][9] identifies its Bhiwadi location on Alwar Road, while its [Advik page][10] explicitly identifies the site near Thada village on Alwar Bypass Road. "Alwar Bypass" is a road-corridor address used by multiple projects. It is not one ward or a unique numbered UIT sector. Record the project, revenue village, scheme sector and ward separately.

### Uday Vihar remains unverified

No Bhiwadi Uday Vihar scheme entry was verified in the consulted BIDA sector-plan index or official planning report. It is therefore not assigned a sector number, ward number or BIDA approval status in this archive. Do not confuse an unverified Bhiwadi reference with Jaipur Development Authority's similarly named Udai Vihar scheme at Udaipuriya, Chaksu. A Bhiwadi allotment letter, approved layout or khasra-based approval is needed before adding a canonical mapping.

## Dharuhera border and the Manesar connection

The Greater Bhiwadi report places the complex at Rajasthan's northeastern edge and describes Bhiwadi as approximately 5 km from Dharuhera on the road then numbered NH-8. This is historical regional context, not a measured distance from every municipal boundary point. The Rajasthan-Haryana state line is legally different from a ward line, an industrial estate limit or a master-plan boundary.

Dharuhera is the nearby Haryana-side settlement in the Bhiwadi road connection. Manesar lies farther along the Delhi-Jaipur corridor in Haryana's Gurugram district. It should not be described as a verified adjoining Bhiwadi municipal boundary. Manesar's [formation notification][11] gives its own revenue-village boundary schedule; it does not identify Bhiwadi as a boundary counterpart. Its [amended municipal map index][12] is the appropriate starting point for Manesar-side limits.

Cross-border travel, shared industrial supply chains and developer references to "Delhi NCR" do not transfer municipal jurisdiction. A Haryana property should not receive a Bhiwadi ward number merely because its postal or marketing address mentions Bhiwadi. For a boundary-side property, establish the state and revenue village first, then the local body and ward.

## Scope of Greater Bhiwadi versus the Municipal Council

The historical Greater Bhiwadi Master Plan 2031 covers a 99-village complex of approximately 250 square kilometres. Its named industrial nodes include Bhiwadi, Tapookara, Khushkhera, Chaupanki and Kaharani. Those figures describe the planning complex in that report, not the area or village count of the 60-ward Municipal Council.

BIDA's [master-plan landing page][6] labels its Bhiwadi-Khushkhera-Tapukara entry "2041", whereas the linked report indexed and consulted for the historical details above is titled Greater Bhiwadi Master Plan 2031. This archive preserves that distinction and does not relabel the older report as a verified 2041 statutory plan.

For an individual property, use the municipal ward notification for electoral geography, BIDA's approved sector layout for planning references, RIICO's estate layout for an industrial plot, and the revenue record for the village and khasra. None alone establishes all four.

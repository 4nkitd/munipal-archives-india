## Rajasthan Sampark 181

The [official Sampark contact page](https://sampark.rajasthan.gov.in/ContactUs) publishes **181** as its toll-free number and **rajsampark@rajasthan.gov.in** as its email. Citizens can register and track grievances through the [Rajasthan Sampark portal](https://sampark.rajasthan.gov.in/). This is a state grievance channel, not a direct line to a Bhiwadi engineer or sanitation officer.

For a complaint, state the location, subject, responsible agency if known, earlier representation details and supporting evidence. Keep the complaint reference number for follow-up. The [official grievance form instructions](https://sampark.rajasthan.gov.in/grievanceForm) ask users to provide contact details and refer to any earlier complaint. They also say not to register RTI applications through this grievance form and to avoid matters pending before a court.

## Source and verification limits

This record checks published official directory information as of 22 November 2026. No telephone call, email-delivery test or confirmation of an officer's physical presence was performed. Names may change after transfers. Third-party directories with conflicting municipal or BIDA mobile numbers were not used to populate contact fields. Unverified municipal engineering, revenue and sanitation names remain blank rather than being substituted with staff from another agency or city.

# Bhiwadi environmental orders: NCR GRAP and CETP effluent compliance

## Record scope and issuing authorities

This record summarises separate air-quality and water-pollution instruments relevant to Bhiwadi. It is not a consolidated municipal order. The frontmatter date refers to CAQM's revised GRAP direction of 29 September 2026. The CETP inlet-quality order is dated 21 November 2024 in RSPCB's official order index.

Bhiwadi is within the NCR planning context. The official [Greater Bhiwadi Master Plan 2031](https://www.lsg.urban.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/organizations/ctp/pdf/1new16march23greater%20bhiwadi%20report%20and%20notifications.pdf) identifies the Bhiwadi-Tapookara-Khushkhera complex as a priority town in the NCR Regional Plan 2021.

CAQM regulates air-quality measures under its statutory directions. RSPCB prescribes and enforces the cited CETP requirements. Municipal bodies, development authorities and industrial regulators have different responsibilities. A GRAP relaxation does not cancel a CETP or industrial consent condition.

## CAQM revised GRAP, 29 September 2026

CAQM direction **F. No. A-110018/01/2021-CAQM**, dated **29.09.2026**, issued the revised GRAP schedule under section 12 of the CAQM Act, 2021 with immediate effect. Paragraphs 12 and 13 say that stage measures apply when the respective stage is invoked by an appropriate order and require implementation by the concerned governments, pollution-control boards, urban local bodies, development authorities and other agencies.

| Stage | Delhi AQI band | Classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 201 to 300 | Poor |
| II | 301 to 400 | Very Poor |
| III | 401 to 450 | Severe |
| IV | Above 450 | Severe + |

The thresholds refer to **Delhi AQI**, not a separate automatic Bhiwadi trigger. The schedule permits advance invocation based on forecasts and requires lower-stage restrictions to continue alongside higher-stage restrictions.

This archive does not certify which stage was in force on 22 November 2026. Consult the latest CAQM invocation or revocation order before deciding whether a particular activity is restricted. Use the [2026 schedule](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Schedule/01fcdebf-83e1-4cfe-bc0c-d0b1470a4d4e.pdf), rather than treating the earlier 21 November 2025 revision as current.

## GRAP implications for Bhiwadi municipal works

The 2026 Stage II schedule assigns daily mechanical or vacuum sweeping with water sprinkling, proper disposal of collected dust and intensified dust suppression to NCR urban local bodies and road-owning agencies. It also calls for targeted hotspot action and stronger construction-site dust inspections.

When Stage III is invoked, specified dust-generating construction and demolition activities face restrictions throughout NCR. The list includes excavation, piling, demolition, open-trench utility laying, masonry, RMC batching, major welding, road construction and major road repairs. Pothole repair is expressly permitted in the 2026 schedule. Less-polluting activities and specified project categories have conditional permissions, not a general exemption from dust-control rules. Stage IV extends the Stage III construction restrictions to listed linear public projects.

Do not apply every Delhi-specific transport or school measure automatically to Bhiwadi. For example, the Stage III light-motor-vehicle restriction names Delhi and specified high-vehicle-density districts. The geographic wording of each measure and any Rajasthan implementing order must be checked separately.

## SCADA monitoring and consented effluent quantities

A separate [RSPCB SCADA office order](https://environment.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/environment/RPCB/Office%20Order/textile%202581.pdf), signed by **B. Praveen, Member Secretary**, covers CETPs at Balotra, Bhiwadi, Jodhpur and Pali. It appoints the concerned Regional Officer as nodal officer for monitoring CETP SCADA data.

The Regional Officer must regularly examine the captured data and report non-compliances and anomalies. The order also requires the KLD allocation entered for each unit to match the State Board's consent, and monitoring of trade-effluent discharge against the consented quantity. CETP trusts are directed to share the necessary access with the Regional Officers.

The handwritten date in the searchable copy is unclear, so no exact date is assigned here. This is an independent monitoring instrument, not an annexure assumed to form part of the 2024 inlet order. Neither document establishes that a named factory or the CETP is currently compliant. That requires the applicable consent, inspection and sampling records.

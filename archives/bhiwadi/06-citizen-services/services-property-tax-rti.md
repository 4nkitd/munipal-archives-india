# Bhiwadi citizen services: urban development tax, civil registration, grievances and RTI

## Scope and date

This is a compiled service guide, not a municipal notification. The frontmatter date is a schema placeholder for the compilation year, not an issue date. Sources were checked on 22 November 2026. The Rajasthan urban portal lists the council under the label "Nagar-Parishad-Bhilwadi". State service routes below must not be read as proof that every transaction is currently enabled for Bhiwadi.

## Urban development tax and e-Mitra

The Local Self Government portal's official municipal online-services listing describes property tax as "Property Tax (UD TAX)" and sends applicants to [Rajasthan SSO](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/). Its departmental support form also identifies UD Tax, Property ID, mutation and lease deposit as separate online-service subjects.

For a Bhiwadi property, select the relevant municipal body and check that the property identifier, owner, address, assessment period and demand correspond to the council's record before paying. Retain the application number, payment reference and receipt. An e-Mitra kiosk can assist with supported services, but confirm that the requested UD Tax service and Bhiwadi body are available in its live catalogue. Kiosk assistance does not determine tax liability.

No Bhiwadi-specific rate schedule, exemption threshold, rebate deadline or penalty percentage was verified for this record. Obtain the applicable assessment and demand from the council's tax branch. Do not substitute another Rajasthan municipality's rates or confuse lease rent with UD Tax.

The cited [LSG support request form](https://lsg.urban.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/lsgs/lsg-jaipur/FormsToDownload/Online%20services%20%20form.pdf) is an official-to-official escalation form. It asks the ULB to supply the application/file number, issue description and supporting screenshot or letter. It is not a citizen tax-return form.

## Birth, death and marriage through Pehchan

[Pehchan](https://pehchan.rajasthan.gov.in/) is Rajasthan's civil registration portal. Citizens can apply directly, through its mobile application or through e-Mitra. The portal provides registration search, certificate download, child-name addition, legacy certificate computerisation and application-status functions.

For urban areas, Pehchan identifies the municipal headquarters as the location of the birth and death registrar. Its published guidance says to report a birth or death within 21 days for free registration and certification. Form 1 is the birth report and Form 2 is the death report. Use the registrar responsible for the event location; a Bhiwadi residence alone does not establish jurisdiction over an event elsewhere. Delayed registration and corrections require the applicable registrar process.

The [NIC Pehchan project description](https://www.nic.gov.in/project/pehchan/) confirms that marriage applications can be submitted through the portal, mobile application or e-Mitra. Its process includes an appearance date for the couple, document verification, a joint photograph and registrar e-signature before certificate download. Use the current portal checklist rather than assuming that online submission completes registration.

## Municipal complaints through Rajasthan Sampark 181

Use [Rajasthan Sampark](https://sampark.rajasthan.gov.in/) or call **181** for a service complaint. Give the precise location, a short factual description, contact details and any previous complaint number. Keep the new grievance number for tracking. The official form asks citizens to provide pointwise information and discourages complaints about matters pending judicial consideration.

Sampark expressly says not to register RTI requests as grievances. A sanitation or drainage complaint asks the authority to act. An RTI application asks for existing records. If the matter concerns an industrial discharge rather than a municipal drain, identify the industry and location so that the complaint can be directed to the pollution-control authority.

## RTI requests for council records

The [Rajasthan RTI portal](https://rti.rajasthan.gov.in/home) supports online applications, first appeals, online payment and application-status checks for Rajasthan public authorities. It offers SSO and mobile-number login and links to an e-Mitra fee-deposit guide.

For council-held records, select Municipal Council Bhiwadi if available in the live authority list. Otherwise confirm the designated State Public Information Officer with the council before filing through an accepted offline route. This record does not verify the incumbent PIO or first appellate authority and does not assign that role to the portal helpdesk.

Specify existing records and a date range, for example a certified tax assessment, demand calculation, payment ledger, sanctioned work estimate or complaint action-taken record. State whether copies or inspection are requested. Check the portal's current fee and exemption instructions before payment. Use its first-appeal facility where applicable; a Sampark complaint is not an RTI appeal.

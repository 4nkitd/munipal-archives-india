# Bhiwadi tenders, budgets and infrastructure works, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and procurement records

This is a compiled research record, not a consolidated municipal budget or a register of completed contracts. The date in the frontmatter is the compilation date. Each entry below retains its own source date and status. Municipal Council Bhiwadi, Bhiwadi Integrated Development Authority, RIICO and Rajasthan Housing Board appear separately as procuring or implementing bodies.

The [Rajasthan e-procurement tender-status page](https://eproc.rajasthan.gov.in/nicgep/app?page=WebTenderStatusLists&service=page) allows an exact tender-ID search. Organisation or reference-number searches are also available. Search each ID below for the original notice, amendments and any award record. Some older external tender links returned only the portal header during verification. Those entries are explicitly attributed to the named secondary tender index, rather than represented as directly inspected official notices.

A tender's estimated value is not its awarded contract value, payment total or annual departmental budget. A bid deadline does not establish that a contract was awarded. No project in this record is classified as operational solely because a tender, proposal or target completion date exists.

### Officially recorded lighting completion dates

BIDA's official **Detail of Works Falling Under Defect Liability Period** provides stronger completion evidence than a tender listing. Its first entry records M/s M.M Brothers, actual completion on 15 May 2024, and DLP expiry on 14 May 2025. The technical description includes underground 33 kV and 11 kV lines and 176 street lights. Entry 5 records M/s Jyoti Electricals, 38 street lights, completion on 19 February 2024 and DLP expiry on 18 February 2026. [S9]

These are dates reported in the authority's DLP register. They do not establish the present condition of every light or independent verification of uninterrupted service.

## Sewage treatment and CETP upgrades

Rajasthan's monthly progress reports hosted by the National Mission for Clean Ganga record a 34 MLD STP near the existing STP at Keshav Marg, Bhiwadi. MLD means million litres per day. The reports give the following historical construction positions. [S10, S11]

| Reporting date | Capacity | Physical progress | Completion timeline then recorded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 28 February 2025 | 34 MLD | 11% | June 2026 |
| 30 April 2025 | 34 MLD | 24% | June 2026 |

The April report also records the Khanpura 2 MLD proposal at 0% progress. Its explanation says a revised DPR proposed a 2.5 MLD sewage pumping station and transfer of the load to the new 34 MLD STP elsewhere, with a work order issued. This should not be counted as a completed additional 2 MLD treatment plant.

The same report lists existing Bhiwadi plants at RHB, 1.50 MLD; Mundena Mev, 2 MLD; Santhalka, 3 MLD; near the existing STP, 3 MLD; and Vasundhra Nagar, 4 MLD. These are reported plant capacities, not evidence that all sewage was being treated to standard.

On 7 January 2026, The Times of India reported that an interstate meeting described the 6 MLD CETP as recently upgraded to zero liquid discharge standards. It reported a March 2026 operational target for the 34 MLD STP and a plan to convey treated STP water to Sarai Khurd dam for reuse. This later target differs from the June 2026 timeline in the 2025 official progress reports. Neither target alone establishes commissioning or compliant operation. [S12]

## Bhiwadi-Dharuhera flooding and drainage proposals

The HSPCB status report dated 26 July 2024 in NGT OA 617/2023 records a joint inspection on 7 March 2024. It states that approximately 12 MLD of effluent entered Dharuhera from Bhiwadi during non-rainy days, based on the pumps installed near NH-8. It also reports that monitored effluent parameters exceeded prescribed limits. The inspection did not find wastewater being released onto the particular village lands identified in that application. These observations concern that inspection and report, not every location or subsequent date. [S13]

Distinct drainage proposals were reported at different times:

| Report date | Proposal or direction | Status supported by source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 27 November 2024 | BIDA CEO Atul Prakash described a Rs 350 crore drainage-system project, with its DPR sent to IIT Delhi for review | Proposal under review, not an established sanction or award. [S14] |
| 10 July 2025 | Tijara MLA Mahant Balaknath said a proposal for a 3 km pipeline from Bhiwadi bus stand, estimated at Rs 36 crore, had been submitted to the state government | Statement of a submitted proposal, not a completed pipeline. [S15] |
| 7 January 2026 | A meeting chaired by Nitin Gadkari directed a study for an approximately 6 km drain estimated at Rs 150 crore; Rajasthan and Haryana would contribute Rs 25 crore each, with the balance borne by NHAI | Reported study direction and funding arrangement, not a verified construction contract. [S12] |

These figures describe different reported plans. They must not be added together as expenditure or treated as successive sanctioned versions of one project without further documentation.

## Budget evidence and limits

Patrika reported on 8 June 2025 that Bhiwadi had provided Rs 15 crore annually for sanitation in 2025-26. It said the council had sought DLB permission to tender a larger seven-year arrangement at Rs 35.51 crore annually, approximately Rs 248 crore in total. The source describes a recommendation and request for permission, not proof that the proposed amount was approved, awarded or spent. [S16]

For a separate capital-work example, the official Rajasthan procurement record for BIDA stadium phase II, `2026_BIDA_544634_1`, reference `ONLINE NIT-37/2025-26 WORK 1`, lists a tender value of Rs 20 crore and a 545-day work period. It was published on 10 March 2026. Crucially, the same record lists a **Cancellation of Tender** corrigendum. This cannot be recorded as an awarded Rs 20 crore project. [S17]

Rajasthan's Finance Department maintains state-budget documents for 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27. Those state documents are not a substitute for a signed Bhiwadi council budget. No authenticated consolidated council or BIDA annual budget total is established in this record. [S18]

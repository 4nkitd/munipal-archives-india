# Bhiwadi master plan and the Dharuhera-Manesar industrial corridor

## Record scope and plan identification

This is a research compilation dated 22 November 2026, not a new planning notification. The principal source is the Town Planning Department's *Greater Bhiwadi Master Plan-2031*, prepared under the Rajasthan Urban Improvement Trust Act, 1959. Its Greater Bhiwadi area is the Bhiwadi-Tapookara-Khushkhera Complex, comprising 99 revenue villages over approximately 250 square kilometres. The report identifies Bhiwadi, Tapookara, Chaupanki, Kahrani and Khushkhera as industrial nodes. These are planning-area descriptions, not proof that every settlement falls within Nagar Parishad Bhiwadi's municipal boundary. [1]

There is a material catalogue discrepancy. BIDA's master-plan webpage labels its Bhiwadi entry "Bhiwadi-Khushkhera-Tapukara 2041", but the linked `BKT Report.pdf` identifies itself as *Greater Bhiwadi Master Plan-2031*. This archive preserves the report's own horizon. The webpage label alone does not establish a notified replacement plan for 2041. Obtain the relevant sanction notification and amendments before using the plan for a land transaction or development application. [1][2]

The report uses the historical Alwar district geography and the name NH-8. Those descriptions should not be mistaken for current district boundaries or highway numbering.

## Master Plan 2031 and corridor proposals

The report places Bhiwadi about five kilometres from Dharuhera on the Delhi-Jaipur highway, historically NH-8 and now NH-48 on that corridor. It links industrial growth to RIICO's development activity from 1975 and to Bhiwadi's designation as a regional centre in NCR Regional Plan 2021. The original planning exercise considered a 2021 horizon, then extended it to 2031 following a request from the National Capital Region Planning Board. [1]

The plan addresses housing for industrial employees, infrastructure gaps and coordinated growth beyond the earlier urbanisable limit. Its regional transport discussion includes the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor, Dedicated Freight Corridor, a proposed Kasaula-Khushkhera road connection, a proposed RRTS and station-related transit-oriented development, a Kasaula Chowk-Tapookara link proposed by RIICO, and a Neemrana-Bhiwadi link via Azarka. The last is described as approximately 60 kilometres long with a proposed 90-metre right of way. [1]

These are proposals recorded in the plan. They are not evidence that the roads, railway service, stations or reserved development areas have been built or opened. A particular plot requires the applicable land-use map, sector or zonal plan, sanctioned layout, road reservation and subsequent amendments. Proximity to Manesar or the highway does not itself permit industrial or residential development.

## BIDA, Nagar Parishad and RIICO jurisdiction matrix

BIDA's official institutional description identifies Bhiwadi Integrated Township as a special investment region declared under section 3 of the Rajasthan Special Investment Region Act, 2016. The current description covers 370 villages across eight tehsils in Khairthal-Tijara and Kotputli-Behror districts. That wider territory is not interchangeable with the 99-village Greater Bhiwadi plan area. [3]

The matrix below is a routing guide, not a parcel-level allocation of statutory powers. Ownership, transfer of infrastructure and specific notifications can change the responsible office.

| Authority | Documented role or territorial basis | Practical routing and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BIDA | Development authority for Bhiwadi Integrated Township. Its portal publishes master and sector plans and services for section 90A conversion, land-use change, layouts, building plans and occupancy certificates. | Start with BIDA for regional planning and BIDA-administered development permissions. Check whether the plot belongs to a RIICO estate, another scheme authority or a separately administered planning area. [2][3] |
| Nagar Parishad Bhiwadi | Urban local body identified on Rajasthan's official municipal portal. Pollution proceedings also identify the council as an agency responsible for domestic sewage infrastructure. | Route municipal sanitation and council sewerage complaints to the council within its jurisdiction. Do not assume its boundary covers the entire BIT or every industrial estate. [4][6] |
| RIICO | Industrial land and estate administration. Its official land-availability index groups Bhiwadi and Kaharani under Bhiwadi I, and estates including Khushkhera, Tapukara, Chopanki and Karoli under Bhiwadi II. | Use the relevant RIICO unit for industrial-plot and estate-infrastructure matters. RIICO's 29 September 2023 sanction for RCC drains and approaches in Bhiwadi industrial area's Zones 2 and 3 documents an estate drainage role, not sole responsibility for all city sewage. [5] |
| RSPCB and HSPCB | State pollution regulators. HSPCB's filed report records joint sampling with RSPCB across the Bhiwadi-Dharuhera interface. | Report discharge and water-quality concerns to the relevant board, with location and evidence. A pollution consent does not replace land-use or building permission. [6] |
| Haryana local and estate agencies | MC Dharuhera, HSIIDC and HSVP appear in the HSPCB interstate-drainage report. | Haryana-side pumps, estate drains and roads require the relevant Haryana asset owner. BIDA and RIICO are not the estate authorities for IMT Manesar. [6][8] |

For an unresolved drain complaint, request the drain's asset owner, maintenance agency, receiving outfall and any transfer order. A road or drain crossing an administrative boundary needs coordinated action rather than an assumption that one authority controls the whole catchment.

## DMIC and the Khushkhera-Bhiwadi-Neemrana Investment Region

The August 2014 *Final Development Plan for KBNIR*, prepared for Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor Development Corporation Ltd., describes KBNIR as Rajasthan's first investment region under DMIC. It places the proposed industrial hub within the Shahjahanpur-Neemrana-Behror Urban Complex, not simply inside Bhiwadi municipal limits. The report describes a 165.61-square-kilometre region covering 42 villages. It records notification of the SNB Urban Complex Master Plan 2041 on 22 May 2013. [7]

KBNIR's development plan provides a framework for industrial, residential, commercial, knowledge-related and community uses, transport and utilities. It expressly anticipates more detailed zoning, sector, industrial-area and infrastructure plans. Its name therefore does not mean all land between the three named industrial centres is one industrial estate or available for allotment.

A later official SNB zonal-development report dated 9 December 2025 gives KBNIR an area of 161.13 square kilometres and states that Zone D is under KBNIR-RDA jurisdiction. It says that zone's proposed zonal plan was not published for objections and suggestions because KBNIR-RDA would plan and develop it. These figures and institutional arrangements belong to different source versions. They should not be merged into a single undated boundary claim. For current KBNIR permissions, obtain the applicable RDA delineation and operative plan rather than relying only on BIDA's wider institutional description. [9]

## Interstate drainage and NGT proceedings

HSPCB's status report dated 26 July 2024 in OA 617/2023, *Prakash Yadav v. State of Haryana*, is an official filed account of the Dharuhera interface. It records a joint site visit on 7 March 2024 by HSPCB, HSIIDC and HSVP officers. The complaint alleged discharge onto village land through an abandoned HSVP sewage treatment plant. The inspection instead found no HSVP STP at the location, only a non-working pumping unit, and no wastewater then being released onto the named village lands or into the Sahibi River. [6]

The same report records approximately 12 MLD entering Dharuhera from Bhiwadi, inferred from municipal pumping on non-rainy days. It describes flow through Dharuhera towards NH-8 and the industrial area, and states that heavy-rain flows could reach adjacent villages because of the natural gradient. It also records joint HSPCB-RSPCB monitoring and exceedances of prescribed effluent limits. The 12 MLD figure is a dated report finding, not a verified 2026 measurement or proof that all incoming liquid was industrial trade effluent. [6]

The report traces earlier proceedings to OA 124/2015, directions dated 12 December 2017, EA 42/2019 and compensation imposed on 29 October 2021. It states that Supreme Court appeals stayed the impugned order and annexes an order dated 22 March 2022. This record does not establish the appeals' final outcome or a currently recoverable compensation amount. [6]

A later reproduced NGT judgment in OA 258/2024(CZ), uploaded on 11 September 2025, directs joint action by the Municipal Council, BIDA and RIICO on domestic sewage assessment and household sewer connections to reduce sewage entering RIICO stormwater drains. It also directs acceleration of the STP work and reuse of treated water. This secondary reproduction is useful for the responsibility interface but is not proof of compliance, completion of the reported 34 MLD STP, or resolution of the interstate dispute. [10]

## Industrial linkage with IMT Manesar

The Bhiwadi-Dharuhera-Manesar corridor is an economic and transport relationship across Rajasthan and Haryana, not a single municipal or planning jurisdiction. Bhiwadi's industrial nodes connect through Dharuhera to the Delhi-Jaipur highway corridor. IMT Manesar is a separate Haryana industrial township associated with HSIIDC.

A Haryana environmental clearance dated 3 January 2025, reproduced in Maruti Suzuki's December 2025 compliance submission, identifies its automobile-manufacturing site at Plot 1, Sector 3A, IMT Manesar, Gurugram. The clearance cites HSIIDC allotment letters dated 5 April 2004 and 4 January 2005. This establishes a documented automotive anchor at the Haryana end of the corridor. [8]

The shared industrial geography matters for freight access, employee housing and public infrastructure. It does not establish a specific supplier contract, common drainage system or unified building-control authority. Factory permissions and estate services remain location-specific. A Bhiwadi industrial allotment cannot substitute for a Manesar permission, and the Dharuhera pollution findings should not be read as evidence that IMT Manesar receives the same wastewater.

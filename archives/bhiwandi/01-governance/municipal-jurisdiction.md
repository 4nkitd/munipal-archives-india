# Bhiwandi municipal jurisdiction and office

## District register

Entry 4 in Thane district’s Municipal Corporations register links the [Bhiwandi Nizampur City Municipal Corporation](https://bncmc.gov.in/). The corporation gives its office address as the New Administrative Building, Dharmaveer Anand Dighe Chowk, opposite Rajiv Gandhi flyover, near Kap Ali, Bhiwandi 421303.

## Correspondence

The district [telephone directory](https://thane.nic.in/en/telephone-directory/) lists `commissioner@bncmc.gov.in` for the corporation under Corporations & Councils.

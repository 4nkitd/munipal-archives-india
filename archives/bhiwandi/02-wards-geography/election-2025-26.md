# Bhiwandi wards and seats in the 2025-26 general election

## Declared results

Row 6, labelled “Bhivandi-Nizampur”, records 23 electoral wards, 438 contesting candidates and results declared for all 90 seats. The [commission’s publication register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the final party position to 16 January 2026.

| Party or SEC category | Seats won |
|---|---:|
| INC | 30 |
| BJP | 22 |
| SS | 12 |
| NCP-SP | 12 |
| Parties registered with SEC and recognized as a state party outside Maharashtra | 6 |
| Unrecognized parties registered with SEC | 7 |
| Independent | 1 |
| **Total** | **90** |

The table reports corporation-wide totals. It does not identify which ward covers a particular address.

# Bhiwandi municipal disaster control room

## Municipal control room

For a disaster-management incident in Bhiwandi, Thane district lists **02522-250049** against the corporation in row 12 of its control-room directory.

| Level | Office | Control room |
|---|---|---|
| Municipal | Bhiwandi Nizampur City Municipal Corporation | 02522-250049 |
| Divisional | Commissioner Office, Konkan Division | 022-27571516 |
| National | National Disaster Management Authority | 1078 |

The divisional and national numbers are separate entries in the same directory.

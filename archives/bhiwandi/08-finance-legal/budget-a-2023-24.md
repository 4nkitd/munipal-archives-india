# Bhiwandi Budget A estimates for 2023-24

## Budget A summary

The budget book compares revised 2022-23 figures with the original estimates for 2023-24. The amounts below are in ₹ lakh and refer to Budget A (अ अर्थसंकल्प), one of the accounts in the book.

| Budget A line | Revised 2022-23 | Estimated 2023-24 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Opening balance | 24,489.70 | 17,016.85 |
| Revenue receipts | 41,752.93 | 45,351.71 |
| Capital receipts | 4,610.14 | 5,500.00 |
| Extraordinary receipts | 1,009.33 | 3,391.02 |
| Total including opening balance | 71,862.10 | 71,259.58 |
| Total expenditure | 54,845.25 | 71,247.96 |
| Closing balance | 17,016.85 | 11.62 |

The expenditure total includes transfers to other municipal budget accounts. It is not the consolidated expenditure of the corporation.

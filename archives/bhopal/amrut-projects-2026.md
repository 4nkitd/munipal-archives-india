# Grounded AMRUT projects in Bhopal as of 2 March 2026

## State-reported projects

Serial 182 of the ministry's city-wise AMRUT return gives the following counts and costs for Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. The reporting cutoff printed on the schedule is 2 March 2026.

| Sector | Grounded projects | Cost (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Water supply | 3 | 455.13 |
| Sewerage and septage management | 3 | 495.01 |
| Parks | 35 | 20.09 |
| Drainage | 15 | 146.08 |
| Urban transport | 4 | 93.5 |

## Reading the figures

Adding the populated sector entries gives 60 projects and ₹1209.81 crore. The schedule reports project cost, not money disbursed or expenditure in a financial year. It gives no contract numbers, completion dates or ward allocations.

# Bhopal municipal wards in the 2011 Primary Census Abstract

## Edition and coverage

Census table `PC11_PCA-TV-2327` identifies Bhopal by town code `802312`. The catalog record was created on 19 January 2021; the figures refer to 2011. The [City Finance register](https://www.cityfinance.in/api/v1/ulb) separately reports 85 wards for LGD `250946`. Numbering in the census is not evidence of present electoral boundaries.

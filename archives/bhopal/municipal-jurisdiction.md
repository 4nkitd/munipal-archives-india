# Bhopal Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and office

## Which local body to contact

The district lists Berasia Nagar Palika separately. Its office is in PIN 473335 and its telephone is 07565-282231. A Berasia municipal enquiry belongs with that office rather than the Bhopal corporation.

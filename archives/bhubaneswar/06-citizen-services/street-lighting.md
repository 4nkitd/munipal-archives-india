# Street lighting within BMC limits

## Who maintains the lights

BMC assigns maintenance to the regions. The streetlight department at headquarters coordinates their work. Its duties include providing lights on roads within BMC limits, installing high masts at major traffic junctions and maintaining poles and luminaires. The department reports approximately 77,000 operational streetlight points; it gives no inventory date.

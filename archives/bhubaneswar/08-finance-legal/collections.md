# Municipal revenue collections, 2020-21 to 2024-25

## Collection details

Figures are in crore rupees and come from the department's “Collection Details” table. These are reported collections, not budget targets.

| Financial year | Parking fees | Trade licences | Total across all categories |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2020-21 | 1.23 | 4.22 | 118.19 |
| 2021-22 | 1.63 | 4.55 | 151.21 |
| 2022-23 | 1.36 | 5.18 | 163.25 |
| 2023-24 | 1.77 | 4.96 | 193.75 |
| 2024-25 | 1.97 | 5.40 | 223.40 |

The table includes holding tax, property rent, advertising fees and interest earned on deposits in the total.

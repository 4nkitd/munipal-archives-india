# Constitution of the Corporation of the City of Bidar

## Final notification

Karnataka Gazette Extraordinary, Part III, No. 234, dated 15 April 2025, publishes notification UDD 261 MLR 2022(e). Exercising powers under sections 3, 4, 500, 501 and 503 of the Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976, the Governor notified the Bidar city municipal area and 16 villages from six gram panchayats as a larger urban area and specified it as “The Corporation of the City of Bidar”. Schedule A gives the included areas and Schedule B describes the outer boundary.

The earlier notice, UDD 261 MLR 2022, appeared in Part III No. 114 on 21 February 2025. It invited objections and suggestions within 30 days. The April notification records that the government considered the objections received within that period. It is the final instrument.

## Criteria cited in the notification

The notification cites a population of at least 300,000, density of at least 3,000 inhabitants per square kilometre and at least 50% of employment in non-agricultural activities. For annual local tax and non-tax revenue in the year of the last preceding census, the threshold is the higher of ₹6 crore and ₹200 per resident. These are the statutory thresholds recited in the notification, not audited figures for Bidar.

# Bidar City Municipal Council wards in the 2011 Census

## Council and outgrowths

The [2011 Primary Census Abstract workbook](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/6749/download/9826/DDW_PCA2904_2011_MDDS%20with%20UI.xlsx) uses town code 803060. The row for Bidar (CMC) counts 214,373 residents. The larger row for Bidar (CMC + OG) counts 216,020. Bag - E - Karanja (outgrowth 36) contributed 45 people and Aliabad (outgrowth 37) contributed 1,602. Neither belongs in the former council ward subtotal. The catalogue entry was created on 19 January 2021; all household and population figures refer to 2011.

These are historical census units. They do not identify electoral wards of the enlarged corporation constituted in April 2025.

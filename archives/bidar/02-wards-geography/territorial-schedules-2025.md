# Bidar corporation limits: villages and survey boundary

## Schedule A: territory included

The first entry is the existing Bidar City Municipal Council. The remaining entries list villages by their former gram panchayat. The total of 91.96 square kilometres includes the former council; it is not the area of the village additions alone.

| Gram panchayat in Schedule A | Villages included |
|---|---|
| Ashtoor | Odawad, Tajalapur |
| Amalapur | Amalapur, Goranalli(B) |
| Aliyabad | Aliyabad, Chondi, Chowli |
| Gaadagi | Sippalageri, Shamarajpur, Kabirwada, Mamankeri |
| Kolar | Kolar(K), Nijampur, Hajjaragi, Kamalpur |
| Marakhal | Chikpet |

## Schedule B: how to locate the perimeter

The eastern description begins at survey number 86 in Odawala and gives a later segment from survey number 112 to 113 and 114 in Haladakeri. The western description starts at Chondi survey number 16 and follows a segment south to number 78. The northern description starts at Chondi survey number 17 and runs east. The southern description starts at Nizampur survey number 32 and also references Chidri and Mailura in the old municipal limits. These are reference points, not the complete survey sequence. Consult Schedule B for the intervening parcels and directions.

The schedules define the corporation perimeter. They neither number electoral wards nor allocate any of the villages to a ward. Schedule B uses some different English spellings, including Odawala and Nizampur; the table follows Schedule A.

# Bidar City Municipal Council audited finances, 2022-23

## Accounts of the predecessor council

The dashboard attributes its 2022-23 summary to audited financial statements. This reporting year ended before the April 2025 corporation notification. The dashboard carries a 23 May 2025 update date; that is neither the audit date nor evidence that these amounts include the newly added villages.

| Audited indicator | ₹ crore, rounded as displayed |
|---|---:|
| Tax revenue | 11 |
| Own revenue | 22 |
| Grants | 32 |
| Total revenue | 55 |
| Total expenditure | 52 |
| Balance sheet size | 165 |

Tax revenue is part of own revenue. Balance sheet size is not an annual receipt or expense. The same portal lists statements for 2021-22, 2020-21, 2019-20, 2016-17 and 2015-16. Its profile displays 43 square kilometres and 35 wards under the council name. For the expanded corporation's area, use Schedule A of the 2025 notification.

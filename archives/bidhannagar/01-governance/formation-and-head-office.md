# Bidhannagar Municipal Corporation formation and head office

## Municipal jurisdiction

The corporation formed on 18 June 2015 under section 3 of the West Bengal Municipal Corporation Act, 2006. The corporation identifies the former Bidhannagar and Rajarhat-Gopalpur municipal areas and the specified Mahishbathan-II Gram Panchayat areas as its predecessors. It cites the proposal as Notification 244/MA/O/C-4/1A-12/2012 dated 6 May 2015, published in the Kolkata Gazette Extraordinary on 7 May. The proposal date is distinct from the formation date. The corporation dates the constitution of the first board of councillors to 16 October 2015.

The corporation is in Bidhannagar subdivision, North 24 Parganas district. Its jurisdiction is the municipal area, not the entire district or metropolitan area.

# Bidhannagar's 41 wards and municipal census tables

## Ward and borough counts

The municipal profile lists one head office, six borough offices and 41 ward offices. The [state corporation directory](https://udma.wb.gov.in/modal-content/2) also records six boroughs, 41 wards and an area of 55.51 sq km. The [municipal map index](https://www.bmcwbgov.in/index.php/about-us/bmc-map) is a separate source. Office counts and census tables do not define electoral boundaries.

## Selected rows from the 2011 census table

| Ward | Population | Households |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 12,653 | 2,956 |
| 9 | 16,854 | 4,512 |
| 41 | 18,617 | 5,329 |

These rows come from the profile table headed "Ward-Wise Population Detail (as per 2011 census)", not the separate table of ward area, population, road and drainage lengths. The two tables do not agree for all wards. For example, the infrastructure table lists 16,192 for Ward 9 and 14,587 for Ward 41. The archive does not reconcile the figures or treat them as current population counts.

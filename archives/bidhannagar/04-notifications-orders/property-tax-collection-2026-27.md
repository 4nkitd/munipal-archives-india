# Bidhannagar property tax collection notice for 2026-27

## Payment and printed notices

The corporation posted the FY 2026-27 notice on its home page under "Notice on start of collection of payment property tax." The demand notice became available and online collection began on 13 April 2026. The website does not give a separate publication date for the notice.

| Wards named in the notice | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 28 | Counter payment began 16 April 2026, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., at designated HDFC Bank branches within the corporation area and tax collection offices. The notice names cash, cheque and demand draft. |
| 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 37, 39, 40 and 41 | Printed demand notices available from the fourth week of April 2026. Online payment was available from 13 April. |

The notice does not specify special arrangements for wards 35, 36 and 38. The [municipal property tax portal](https://proptax.bmcwbgov.in/PropertyTaxOnline/PropertyTax/Online.aspx) is linked from the same home page.

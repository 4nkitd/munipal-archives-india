# Bidhannagar online tanker and ferrule services

## Online requests

The corporation home page names 9 March 2026 as the start of online access to paid water tanker bookings and water tank refills. The same portal handles new ferrule connections and requests to change, clean or shift an existing ferrule. The corporation says citizens can request and pay for these services without a visit to Poura Bhawan.

## Responsible department

The [water supply department page](https://www.bmcwbgov.in/index.php/services-operations/water-supply-services) assigns the Public Health Engineering department new water, sewerage and drainage lines and the maintenance of existing lines. It also assigns the department water ferrule, sewerage and drainage connections for newly constructed buildings. The department description and the online launch notice do not specify the same set of transactions; use the [dedicated water services portal](https://ftb.bmcwbgov.in/) for the online requests named above.

# Biharsharif electoral wards in Bihar SEC Form 6

## The phase II list for the expanded corporation

Row 20 of the [Bihar SEC phase II final ward-formation register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=2) links the Biharsharif Form 6 under rule 29(1). The form identifies Nalanda district, Biharsharif subdivision and Biharsharif Nagar Nigam. It lists wards 1–51 with Hindi directional boundaries, ward map links and population columns headed 2011 Census. The total is 337,819, including 34,610 Scheduled Castes, 89 Scheduled Tribes and 303,120 in the Other column. Those are the figures for this ward-formation edition, not projections or voter counts.

## Checking a boundary

The excerpts are search clues, not full boundary descriptions. The form often describes a side as a sequence of landmarks and adjoining roads rather than one continuous road. Check the original row and its map together, especially for a property at a junction or on a river bank. Road numbers are retained as printed in the form. The register is not a current set of polling station assignments or a substitute for the state notification establishing the corporation's outer limits.

# Municipal jurisdiction and expansion of Biharsharif Nagar Nigam

## What the expansion entry establishes

Row 20 of the Bihar State Election Commission's phase II final ward-formation register lists Biharsharif as a Nagar Nigam in Biharsharif subdivision, Nalanda district. It marks the body `विस्तारित` (expanded) and lists 51 territorial wards. This is the status recorded in the 2022 ward-formation edition; it is not the date or operative wording of the state order that altered the municipal limits.

The register lists Harnaut, Asthawan and Rahui as separate Nagar Panchayats in Biharsharif subdivision. The [Nalanda public utility directory](https://nalanda.nic.in/en/public-utility/nagar-nigam-biharsharif/) names the Municipal Development Commissioner, Nagar Nigam Biharsharif, and gives PIN 803101. The subdivision and the municipal corporation are different administrative areas, and the directory's PIN is a contact detail, not a jurisdiction boundary.

## The legal source for the outer boundary

Section 8 of the [2007 Bihar municipal text published by the SEC](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/old-sec/download/The%20Bihar%20Municipal%20Act-2007%28%20English%29.pdf) describes changing municipal limits by state notification, including adding contiguous local areas. Section 12 distinguishes a municipal area from the territorial wards within it. The SEC's English copy is headed “Bill”, although the portal labels it the Act; consult the enacted Gazette and amendments for authoritative text. The state notification is the source to seek for the inclusion of a village or parcel. The corporation's Form 6 gives four-sided ward descriptions, but the expansion label alone does not establish which land was added or when.

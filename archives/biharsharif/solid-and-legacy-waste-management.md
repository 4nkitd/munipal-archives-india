# Biharsharif waste collection, segregation and legacy waste

## Sorting and collection

The Nalanda district page on the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 describes the corporation's four streams for segregation at the point of generation: wet or biodegradable waste, dry or recyclable waste, sanitary waste and special-care or hazardous waste. It describes door-to-door collection and transport to processing facilities, with dry waste going to material recovery facilities and wet waste to compost plants. Residual waste goes to sanitary landfill under the model described. The page does not give a pickup calendar or addresses of facilities by ward.

The district page says authorities prohibit open dumping, burning garbage and public littering. For bulk waste generators it identifies hotels, marriage halls and large institutions, and describes on-site processing or local clearances for major events. Consult the rules linked by the district for thresholds and operative requirements rather than treating the page as a full statement of the law.

| Legacy waste bioremediated | About 26,000 metric tonnes |

The page distinguishes waste already treated from waste surveyed for future work. It does not specify whether the quantities overlap, so they should not be added or used to calculate a completion rate. The site shows a last-updated date of 29 September 2026 but gives no separate measurement date for either number. Its nationwide remediation figures are not part of the city totals.

# Bikaner's municipal administration and elected chair

## Municipal administration

The corporation's historical account says municipal administration began as a government department in 1882. The Bikaner Municipal Act of 1923 defined municipal powers and duties. The Act was amended in 1928. The corporation dates its municipal building to 1931.

## Elected chair

The corporation names Badridas Daga as the nominated non-official chair in 1945–46 and Dhanpat Rai as the first chair elected by the public. Rai served from 1947 to 1950. The historical account is undated and does not give the day on which the present corporation was constituted.

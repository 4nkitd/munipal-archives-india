# Bikaner ward boundaries in the September 2025 Rajasthan Gazette

## State approval

Part 6(a) of the Rajasthan Extraordinary Gazette dated 15 September 2025 publishes the state's approval of the ward formation proposed by the Bikaner District Collector after objections to the draft. The notification cites sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994.

## Reading the schedule

The schedule runs from Ward 1 to Ward 80. It uses roads, junctions, buildings and other landmarks to describe which side of a boundary falls within a ward. Two examples:

| Ward | Landmarks in the schedule | Population printed in schedule |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | FCI godowns and Pugal Road vegetable market; the locality list includes Islam Nagar and Vishwakarma Colony. | 7,566 |
| 80 | The route begins and ends at Teliwara Chowk. The locality list includes Naya Kuan Chowk and City Kotwali. | 8,426 |

For an address near the boundary, consult the full Hindi description. The population column is not an elector count.

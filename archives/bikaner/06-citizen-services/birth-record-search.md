# How to search Bikaner's municipal birth records

## Search options

On the corporation's Birth & Death page, choose **Birth Search Detail**. The page lists three ways to search:

1. Enter the registration number.
2. Enter the date of birth and one of the following: father's name, mother's name, hospital or gender.
3. Without a registration number or date of birth, supply any three of those four details.

The form says birth records were updated through **21 May 2026**. This is a cutoff for the searchable records, not the publication date or a deadline for registering a birth.

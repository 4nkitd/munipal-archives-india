# Where to file and track a Bikaner municipal complaint

## Filing and follow-up

The municipal complaint page has two links: **Post Complaint** for a new issue and **Complaint Status** for follow-up. When posting a complaint, first choose the municipal section that handles the issue. Keep the complaint number for later enquiries.

The corporation lists **0151-2226901** as its helpline and **nagarnigambikaner@gmail.com** as its office email. The complaint page gives no resolution deadline.

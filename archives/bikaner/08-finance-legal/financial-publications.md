# Bikaner municipal budgets, balance sheets and income statements

## Financial years in the downloads index

Under **Services → Information & Downloads**, select the document series first, then a financial year.

| Portal heading | First financial year listed | Last financial year listed | Annual links |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Budget | 2006–07 | 2018–19 | 13 |
| Audited Balance Sheet | 2012–13 | 2015–16 | 4 |
| Income & Expenditure | 2012–13 | 2017–18 | 6 |

For 2017–18, the index lists both a budget and an income and expenditure statement. It has no balance sheet link for that year under the audited series. These dates describe the holdings of this municipal webpage; they do not establish when any document was adopted or published.

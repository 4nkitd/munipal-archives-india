# Bilaspur notice inviting claims and objections to proposed inclusion

## Proposed inclusion in the corporation

The Collector invited claims and objections concerning the inclusion of a municipality, Nagar Panchayats and Gram Panchayats in Bilaspur Municipal Corporation. The district notice lists 3 August 2019 as its start date and 15 August 2019 as its end date. It records the consultation, not the final limits of the corporation.

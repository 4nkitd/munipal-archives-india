# Bilaspur Bhagirathi Nal-Jal Yojana application documents

## Documents listed by the department

Use the prescribed Bhagirathi scheme application form. The municipal department lists three supporting items:

- A passport-size photograph of the applicant.
- A photocopy of the below-poverty-line ration card.
- A photocopy of the Aadhaar card.

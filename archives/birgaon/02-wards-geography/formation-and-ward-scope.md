# Birgaon corporation limits and ward history

## Constituent settlements

The municipal history identifies six villages in the original council: Urla (including its dependent village Borjhara), Achholi, Rawanbhatha, Urkura, Birgaon and Sarora. Birgaon adjoins Raipur Municipal Corporation on the Raipur–Bilaspur road. The profile reports an area of 36.85256 km² and a 2011 population of 96,294.

## Changes in status and representation

| Date | Change reported by the corporation |
|---|---|
| 17 January 2003 | Birgaon received municipal status. |
| 11 September 2009 | Following boundary expansion and delimitation, the earlier 30 wards became 35. |
| 2014 | A further delimitation produced 40 wards. |
| 22 July 2014 | The state Urban Administration and Development Department notified the municipal corporation in place of the council, retaining the council limits. |

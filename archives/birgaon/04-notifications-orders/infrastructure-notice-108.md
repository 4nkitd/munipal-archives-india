# Birgaon infrastructure fund notice for 108 works

## Scope of the notice

The notice dated 24 December 2024 lists buildings, cement-concrete roads, stages, boundary walls and reinforced-concrete drains. The figure of 108 is the total across the package; the notice heading does not break it down by work type or ward. The city lists this separately from its 10 November 2024 Fifteenth Finance Commission notice for electricity pole and cable extension.

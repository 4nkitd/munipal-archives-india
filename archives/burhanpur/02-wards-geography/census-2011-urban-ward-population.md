# Burhanpur Municipal Corporation ward population in Census 2011

The Urban Primary Census Abstract in the Burhanpur District Census Handbook, Part XII-B, lists municipal corporation town code `802439`, area 12.67 km², 38,118 households and 210,886 persons, including 28,930 children aged 0–6. The total includes institutional and houseless population. The town and ward rows below are on printed page 100 (PDF page 109). The next town row, Shahpur, is a separate census entry and is excluded.

## How to use these figures

The source gives ward numbers and counts for the 2011 enumeration. It does not assign neighbourhood boundaries, councillors or reservation status. Current electoral ward extent must be read from the relevant state gazette notification under section 10 of the Municipal Corporation Act, not from the census enumeration rows.

# Burhanpur municipal bills, service complaints and help numbers

## Start with the local body on the bill

The [district municipal directory](https://burhanpur.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) gives the corporation office as near Stadium Ground, Burhanpur 450331, with phone 07325-255270 and email commburhanpur@mpurban.gov.in. It lists Nepanagar and Shahpur separately. For a property or water account, check that the bill identifies Burhanpur Municipal Corporation before using the corporation's contact or choosing a local body online.

The [state Urban Development and Housing Department](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/) links to [e-Nagar Palika](https://enagarpalika.mp.gov.in/home). The portal's [English service labels](https://enagarpalika.mp.gov.in/assets/i18n/en.json) identify property tax and water bill payment as separate services. The property payment form asks for a Property ID and water quick pay asks for a Water Connection ID. Applying for a new water connection is a separate service from paying charges for an existing connection. If a bill cannot be matched to the correct account or local body, contact the corporation office to check the identifier. The state portal is not evidence of Burhanpur-specific charges or that a given account can be paid online.

## Report or track a service complaint

The [district helpline directory](https://burhanpur.nic.in/en/helpline/) lists **181** for the CM Helpline and **101** for the Fire Brigade. Use the corporation's office contact for a local account or service enquiry; the state's grievance channel takes complaints, requests and suggestions.

The [CM Helpline homepage](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/) links to a [form for submitting a complaint, request or suggestion](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/Public/VerifyOTPBeforeOnlineComplaint.aspx) and a [separate complaint status page](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/grievance-status.aspx). The status page asks for the complaint number. When reporting a municipal service problem, identify the local body and location and keep the complaint number for follow-up. The published links do not establish a resolution timeline, and no complaint or payment was submitted for this archive.

# Burhanpur Municipal Corporation: legal scope and municipal duties

## City authority and district limits

The [Burhanpur district municipal directory](https://burhanpur.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) distinguishes Burhanpur Municipal Corporation from the Nagar Parishads of Nepanagar and Shahpur. It places the corporation office near Stadium Ground, Burhanpur 450331. The [District Election Office](https://burhanpur.nic.in/en/districtelectionoffice/) also links to that directory for the district's three urban local bodies. A district name or postal PIN alone does not establish which of the three bodies serves a particular address.

Under sections 1(3) and 7 of the Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956, the Act applies to an area from the date it is notified as a larger urban area. Section 7 provides for the Governor to specify such an area by public notification and gives the corporation perpetual succession and a common seal. Section 10 is a separate notification requirement: the State Government determines the number and extent of wards in the official gazette. It provides for one councillor per ward and sets compactness and approximate population equality as ward formation criteria. The district directory is not the Burhanpur constitution or ward notification and does not define its boundary.

## Municipal responsibilities and the fund

Under section 6, the Corporation, Mayor-in-Council, Mayor and Commissioner are the municipal authorities that carry out the Act. Section 66 lists matters for which the corporation must make adequate provision, including street lighting and cleaning, refuse disposal, fire brigade maintenance, public streets and drains, waterworks, and registration of births, marriages and deaths. Under section 69, municipal government generally vests in the corporation. Executive power rests with the Commissioner subject to the Act's requirements for approvals and other restrictions.

Section 86 provides for one Municipal Fund to be held in trust by the corporation. Sections 87 and 88 deal with money credited to the fund and the order in which it is applied. Section 94 sets the general rule that expenditure from the fund must be covered by a budget grant, with exceptions set out in the Act. These are statewide accounting provisions, not Burhanpur's budget or audited financial figures.

The department's [rules and acts index](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=NOWfDSxXXWnABPjhYKFMkQ%3D%3D) lists the 1956 Act compilation and a separate 2024 corporation amendment. The 1956 year does not date the online compilation or Burhanpur's formation.

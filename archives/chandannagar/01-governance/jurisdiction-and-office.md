# Chandernagore Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## Municipal and subdivision limits

Chandernagore Municipal Corporation is the only municipal corporation in Chandannagar subdivision, Hooghly district. The district lists Tarakeswar, Bhadreswar and Champdani as separate municipalities and Singur, Haripal and Tarakeswar as community development blocks. The subdivision name does not make those local bodies part of the corporation.

The corporation's own site uses "Chandernagore." The subdivision is "Chandannagar" on the district site, and the [state corporation directory](https://udma.wb.gov.in/modal-content/3) uses "Chandannagore Municipal Corporation."

## Head office

The [Hooghly district office listing](https://hooghly.nic.in/public-utility/chandernagore-municipal-corporation/) gives Marie Park, P.O. Chandernagore, Hooghly 712136. The state directory adds Swami Vivekanda Sarani to the postal address. The [corporation's contact page](https://chandernagoremunicipalcorporation.org.in/contact.php) lists 033-2683-5297 and `chandernagorecorporation@yahoo.co.in`.

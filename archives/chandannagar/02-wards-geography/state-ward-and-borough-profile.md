# Chandernagore ward and borough counts in the state directory

## Corporation totals

| Measure | State directory figure |
| --- | ---: |
| Municipal wards | 33 |
| Municipal boroughs | 5 |
| Area | 22.03 sq km |
| Population, 2011 Census | 166,771 |

The [West Bengal urban affairs department home page](https://udma.wb.gov.in/) displays this entry under Municipal Corporation. The state entry has no publication date. Its 2011 label applies to population, not to the publication date of the directory or the vintage of a delimitation order.

## Geographic context

The [corporation home page](https://chandernagoremunicipalcorporation.org.in/) also gives 22.03 sq km. It describes the Hooghly River to the east, Chinsura to the north, Bhadreswar to the south and Dhaniakhali to the west. These are general geographic references. The state entry does not give individual ward boundaries or a ward-to-borough roster.

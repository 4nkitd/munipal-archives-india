# Chandernagore online tax, mutation and permit services

## Where to apply or pay

| Municipal service | Portal linked by the corporation |
| --- | --- |
| Property or holding tax payment | [Property Tax Payment Portal](https://holdingtax.co.in/) |
| Municipal property mutation | [West Bengal Urban Services](https://wburbanservices.org/) |
| Building plan applications | [Online Building Plan Sanction](https://obpsudma.wb.gov.in/) |
| Trade or calling enlistment | [West Bengal e-District](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/portal/home) |
| Birth and death registration | [Janma-Mrityu Tatthya](https://janma-mrityutathya.wb.gov.in/) |
| No-dues certificate and film shooting permission | [West Bengal Urban Services](https://wburbanservices.org/) |

The directory links different portals for holding tax payments and mutation applications. It also links to [Bangla Sahayata Kendra](https://bsk.wb.gov.in/), and the [corporation's BSK page](https://chandernagoremunicipalcorporation.org.in/find_bsk.htm) lists local assistance centres. The eServices directory does not state municipal processing times or confirm completion of any transaction.

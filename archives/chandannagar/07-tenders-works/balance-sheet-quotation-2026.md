# Chandernagore quotation notice for four years of balance sheets

## Notice of 20 November 2026

[Scanned notice linked at source_url.]

The corporation's notice No. IA/Tend/2026-27/178 invites experienced firms to submit sealed quotations for preparing its Accounts Department balance sheets for financial years 2022-23 through 2025-26. It asks for a rate per year inclusive of taxes and any incidental charges, and makes a trade enlistment certificate mandatory.

The signed notice sets submission by 3 p.m. on 30 November 2026 and quotation opening at 3:30 p.m. that day. The [online tender index](https://chandernagoremunicipalcorporation.org.in/Tender/nbdp_live_tender.php) displays 20 November 2026 and 30 November 2026 in its opening and closing date columns. The signed notice provides the deadlines for receipt and opening of quotations. The notice is an invitation, not an award or a balance sheet.

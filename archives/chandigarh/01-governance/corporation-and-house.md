# Chandigarh's Municipal Corporation and General House

## Constitution and representation

An ordinance established the Municipal Corporation on 24 May 1994. Act 45 of 1994 extended the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976 to the Union Territory of Chandigarh. The reference to Punjab identifies the legislation extended to Chandigarh; MCC is a local body in the Union Territory. MCC gives its area as 114 square kilometres.

The Mayor heads the Corporation. The General House includes members directly elected from wards, nine nominated members without voting rights and the Lok Sabha member for the area. The Chandigarh Administration appoints the Municipal Commissioner, in whom executive power is vested. The [MCC officials page](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/team.aspx) lists Amit Kumar as Commissioner and Harpreet Singh Sudan as Special Commissioner.

The five archived officers are a selection from the [office directory](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/Directory.aspx), not the entire executive roster. The [365th House minutes, page 4](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/upload/Minutes%20of%20365th%20Meeting%20of%20General%20House%20%2822.09.2026%29.pdf#page=4) record Dr. Inder Jeet, HCS, attending as Joint Commissioner II on 22 September 2026. That attendance establishes his role on the meeting date; the November directory check does not establish who held that post in November.

## Municipal functions and service contacts

The introduction to the [municipal budget](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/upload/Budget%202024-2025.pdf) identifies functions entrusted by the Chandigarh Administration: water supply, sewers, liquid waste, municipal roads and buildings, sanitation, solid waste, street lighting, property tax, vendor welfare, stray animals, and fire and rescue. The budget separates MCC's own receipts from grant-in-aid from the Administration.

The [MCC homepage](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/) lists the New Deluxe Building, Jan Marg, Sector 17, Chandigarh 160017 as its office. It gives 0172-2787200 for MCC service complaints and advertises 14420 as a toll-free complaints line operating from 8 am to 8 pm.

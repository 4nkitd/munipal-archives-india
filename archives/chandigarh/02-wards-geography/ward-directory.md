# Chandigarh's 35 municipal wards: councillors and localities in the MCC directory

## Reading the two municipal lists

The [ward-wise roll](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/upload/mediafiles/638921443964871175Name%20Address%20E-mails%20of%20Councillors.pdf) identifies the councillor against each ward number. The [2025 council list](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/upload/mediafiles/638918191393328003All%20Councillors%20List%202025.pdf) gives the area represented, but its first column is a serial number, not a ward number. For example, Harpreet Kaur Babla appears first in the area list and represents ward 10 in the ward-wise roll. Two entries are named Jasbir Singh; the matching entries in the two lists distinguish wards 21 and 24.

The table records the representation in those lists. The third column gives locality descriptions for orientation, not legal boundary lines or a complete street list. In particular, Burail and the rest of Sector 45 appear in different wards, as do parts of Sector 38 West. Nominated councillors have no ward entry in the roll.

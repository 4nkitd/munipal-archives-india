# Seven-day notice to remove unauthorised advertisements in Chandigarh

## What triggered the notice

The Municipal Commissioner reported unauthorised advertisements on auto-rickshaws and other vehicles in Chandigarh. The record date is the newspaper publication date: [attachment page 3](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/upload/Public%20Notice-3.pdf#page=3) reproduces the Hindi notice in the Chandigarh edition of *Dainik Bhaskar* dated 8 November 2026.

The [undated English notice on page 2](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/upload/Public%20Notice-3.pdf#page=2) directs those concerned to remove such advertisements within seven days of publication. MCC's website lists 18 November as the online start date and 25 November as the end of the listing. The register does not identify 25 November as an enforcement deadline or announce an extension.

## Where permission is required

MCC cites the Chandigarh Advertisement Control Order, 1954. Its notice addresses hoardings, banners, posters, flex displays and other advertisements or election-related publicity material. It says the requisite permission must be obtained for displays on **private property as well as public property and vehicles** within Chandigarh's jurisdiction.

The notice names removal and penalties among the possible consequences of non-compliance. It also reserves the Corporation's power to initiate appropriate criminal proceedings under applicable law. It gives no fixed penalty amount and does not record any individual enforcement decision.

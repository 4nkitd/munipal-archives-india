# Documents for a regular water connection in Chandigarh

## Which documents depend on the premises?

MCC distinguishes temporary from regular connections. Its published checklist for a regular connection calls for the prescribed form in triplicate. The following differences matter before an applicant obtains signatures or pays charges:

| Premises | What the MCC checklist calls for |
| --- | --- |
| Government house | Self-attested copies of the allotment and possession letters. |
| Chandigarh Housing Board allotment | Form signed and stamped by the Housing Board SDE; self-attested allotment and possession letters. |
| Society flat | Society president, secretary or administrator signs and stamps the form. Include allotment and possession papers, a no-dues certificate for construction water charges, the sewerage-charge receipt and a licensed plumber's certificate. MCC specifies a penal rate if the Estate Office has not granted the occupation certificate. |
| Rehabilitation colony house | Allotment letter, roads NOC, road-cut restoration receipt if applicable, and plumber's certificate. MCC states that these connections must be metered. |
| Village house | Ownership proof, roads NOC, applicable road-cut restoration receipt, plumber's certificate and sewerage-charge receipt. The checklist distinguishes the Chandigarh and Manimajra offices for the NOC and deposit. |
| Private house in a sector | Ownership proof, Estate Office occupation certificate, construction-water no-dues certificate, sewerage-charge receipt and final-payment NOC for the temporary connection from the SDE in charge. |
| Second connection for an upper floor | Ownership proof, a licensed plumber's certification of separate plumbing, and any applicable road-cut receipt. For a house held on a percentage-share basis MCC also asks for a magistrate-attested affidavit clarifying the floor. |
| Commercial site (including SCOs, SCFs, booths and schools) | Submit the prescribed form in triplicate, self-attested copies of ownership proof and the Estate Office occupation certificate, the sewerage-charge receipt, and the SDE in charge's NOC confirming final payment for the temporary connection. |

For Housing Board allotments, society flats, rehabilitation colony houses and village houses, applicants acting under a general power of attorney (GPA) must include a copy attested by a notary public or magistrate. For a commercial site's sewerage-charge deposit, MCC names the EE, MCPH-4 office for Chandigarh and the SDE, MCPH-8 office for Manimajra.

## Where to apply

The [MCC homepage](https://mcchandigarh.gov.in/) links “New Water Connection” to its citizen login. The portal also lists a separate application for tertiary treated water. The premises checklist above concerns regular supply; it does not set a fee or a processing deadline.

# Chandigarh municipal budget estimates for 2024–25

## What MCC expected to receive and spend

MCC published the 2024–25 approved estimates alongside the 2023–24 revised estimates. The “Brief Position” table gives figures in lakh rupees. The grant-in-aid line for 2024–25 is a **demand** on the Chandigarh Administration. It should not be read as a grant actually released to the Corporation.

| Municipal budget head (₹ lakh) | Revised estimate 2023–24 | Budget estimate 2024–25 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Grant-in-aid | 56,000 | 165,175 |
| Own receipts | 53,000 | 63,346 |
| Smart City share for 24×7 water supply | 6,077 | 0 |
| Total receipts (B) | 115,077 | 232,521 |
| Capital expenditure | 23,614 | 42,065 |
| Revenue expenditure | 124,095 | 146,469 |
| Total expenditure (C) | 147,709 | 188,534 |

## Reading the own-receipts schedule

The separate schedule “Highlights of the MCC Own Receipts 2024–25” puts water receipts at ₹16,000 lakh, commercial property tax at ₹6,600 lakh and residential property tax at ₹1,900 lakh. It estimates garbage collection charges at ₹3,300 lakh, parking fees at ₹2,500 lakh and advertisement fees at ₹700 lakh. These are estimates of income to MCC, not rates payable by a household or business.

The document's index points to the receipts schedules on printed pages 9–19, capital expenditure on pages 20–31, revenue expenditure on pages 32–43, ward development funds on page 44 and calculation of grant-in-aid demanded on page 45. The reporting period on the cover identifies the budget year, not its publication date.

# Chandrapur municipal election 2025-26: wards, seats and results

## City result

Row 27 of the Maharashtra State Election Commission's final party-position statement covers Chandrapur Municipal Corporation. Its 17 wards are electoral areas; 66 is the number of council seats returned from those areas. The statement counts 451 contesting candidates. It gives a city total rather than the boundaries, names or seat allocation of individual wards.

The [commission notice index](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the final statement to 16 January 2026. The figures describe the election result on that date.

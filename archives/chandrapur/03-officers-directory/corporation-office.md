# Chandrapur corporation office and district directory

## Corporation office

The district administration lists Chandrapur Municipal Corporation at PIN 442401. Its published office telephone is **07172-250220**. The same entry lists com.chandrapur@gmail.com for the office.

## Which local body to contact

This entry is for the city corporation. The [district municipalities directory](https://chanda.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists Ballarpur, Bhadravati and Chimur separately under municipal councils. For a property or municipal service in one of those council areas, use that council's entry. A district address alone does not place a property in the corporation's jurisdiction.

# Chandrapur Right to Service application and status tracking

## Register and submit an application

The instructions under नागरिकांकरिता सूचना direct a first-time applicant to select **Register**, fill in the registration form and submit it. Keep the username and password. Return to **User Login** and select the required municipal service after signing in.

Complete the service-specific information, attach the required documents and submit the application. Record the **application number**. The corporation tells applicants to sign back in to check status. The public instructions do not give a single deadline or document list for every service; choose the service before preparing the attachments.

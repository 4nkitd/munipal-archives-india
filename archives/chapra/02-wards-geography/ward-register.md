# Chapra municipal wards: boundaries and census population

## Municipal jurisdiction

Saran district lists the city as Municipal Corporation, Chhapra, PIN 841301. The State Election Commission uses Chapra and places the corporation in Chapra Sadar subdivision. The [commission’s municipal register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0) lists 45 wards. Form 6, prescribed under rule 29(1), describes each ward separately. The population column uses the 2011 Census. The municipal register gives a total of 202,352; the detailed Form 6 totals 202,452. The ward figures below follow Form 6.

The Hindi boundary descriptions follow the published entries. The directional labels पू, प, उ and द indicate east, west, north and south.

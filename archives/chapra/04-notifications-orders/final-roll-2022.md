# Final electoral roll for Chapra municipal wards 1 to 45

## Publication of the final roll

The municipal registration officer reported final publication under the State Election Commission schedule, citing commission letters 2037 of 28 May and 2466 of 24 June 2022. Form घ on page 3 explicitly covers wards 01 to 45 of Chapra Nagar Nigam.

## Where the public could inspect the roll

The notice listed the offices of the District Election Officer, the Municipal Registration Officer at Chapra Sadar, the revising authority at the Sadar block development office, the municipal corporation, and the Sadar circle office. It also listed Bhagwan Bazar and Chapra Nagar police stations, Bhagwan Bazar post office, Chapra head post office, and all libraries and reading rooms within the corporation. These are inspection locations for the 2022 edition.

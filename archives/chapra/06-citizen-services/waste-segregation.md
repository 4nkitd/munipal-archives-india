# Chapra household waste segregation guidance

## Sort waste at the point of generation

Chapra Municipal Corporation promotes four separate streams: wet biodegradable waste, dry recyclable waste, sanitary waste and special-care or hazardous waste. Sanitary waste is a separate stream in this guidance; it does not belong with ordinary dry recyclables.

## Collection and processing

The district identifies shortages of separate collection vehicles, material recovery facilities and sanitary landfill capacity. It reports that inadequate processing facilities lead to dumping of untreated waste. Its Waste Watch page links the Solid Waste Management Rules 2026 and has sections for district dumping and collection zones and photographs of collections. It gives no ward pickup timetable or user charge.

# Chas deputy mayor election result: district gazette notice

## Offices covered

The District Panchayat Raj Office listed the gazette from 20 March to 20 April 2026. It covers two separate offices: deputy mayor of Chas Municipal Corporation (Class B) and vice president of Phusro Municipal Council (Class B). The HTML notice identifies the offices but does not name the winners.

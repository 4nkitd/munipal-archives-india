# Chas Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and office

## Municipal area

Jharkhand dates the establishment of Chas Municipality to 21 January 1977 and its conversion to a corporation to 9 February 2015. The state profile describes a broader Chas-Bokaro area under notification 707 of 28 December 2005, then says the department reduced the boundary to exclude Bokaro Steel City. The 2005 description is historical.

# Chas-Bokaro homelessness survey expression of interest

## Scope and closing date

The corporation lists the subject as “Expression of Interest for survey of homeless/beggars of Chas-Bokaro”. It gives 9 January 2026 as the publication date and 27 January 2026 as the last date. “Chas-Bokaro” describes the survey area in the procurement title; it does not define the municipal boundary.

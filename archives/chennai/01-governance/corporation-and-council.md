# Greater Chennai Corporation: council, administration and municipal area

## Council and executive

The Mayor heads the council of 200 councillors. The council ordinarily meets once a month. The Commissioner heads the executive, with Deputy Commissioners, department heads and 15 Zonal Officers. The corporation's brief note puts the municipal area at about 426 square kilometres. These figures describe the corporation, not the Chennai Metropolitan Area.

## Municipal history

The corporation dates its inauguration to 29 September 1688. Nathaniel Higginson was the first Mayor, with 12 aldermen and 60 burgesses. The corporation could levy rates for schools, a town hall and a jail. The note identifies the Parliamentary Act of 1792 as the source of its power to levy municipal taxes in the city.

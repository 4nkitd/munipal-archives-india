# Chennai territorial wards: delimitation notification of 14 December 2018

## Authority and geographic scope

Part VI, Section 2, notification VI(2)/160(a-3)/2018, file LE10/1795/2017, superseded the earlier delimitation notification. The Delimitation Commission published draft proposals based on the 2011 Census and invited objections and suggestions. The government directed the Commissioner to verify the figures. After consulting the council, the Commissioner divided the Chennai City Municipal Corporation into the 200 wards in the schedule under section 45 of the Chennai City Municipal Corporation Act, 1919.

## How to check a boundary

The schedule begins on printed page 2. Each entry identifies the ward and describes its boundary by direction. For a map, use the [GCC ward lookup](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/delimited_ward/), select one of the 15 zones, then the division. GCC directs readers to the gazette if a map differs from the notification. Its separate "Reservation - 2022" link identifies electoral seat categories, not a change in ward boundaries.

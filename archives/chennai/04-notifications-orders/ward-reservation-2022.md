# Chennai ward seat reservations: gazette schedule of 17 January 2022

## Notification and ward numbers

Part VI, Section 2, notification VI(2)/4(d)/2022, file R.D.C.L.B.(Elections) C.No.6820/2021, replaced notification VI(2)/46(d)/2019 of 24 May 2019. The Commissioner issued the schedule under section 5(3), (4) and (5) of the Chennai City Municipal Corporation Act, 1919. It applies to the 2022 notification for Chennai; it does not establish reservations for a later election.

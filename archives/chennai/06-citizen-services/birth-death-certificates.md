# Chennai birth and death certificates: copies, name inclusion and corrections

## Downloading an existing record

On the GCC birth and death services page, choose "Birth Certificate Download" or "Death Certificate Download". The Public Health Department dates the free online download service to 7 September 2008. Hospitals have a separate registration channel to report births, stillbirths and deaths. The department describes records dating back to 1930 in the earlier corporation area and a separate zonal digitisation process for the extended areas. It does not give a completion date for that work.

## Adding or correcting a name

To add a child's name, use "Child Name Inclusion". The department asks for a self-declaration and photocopies showing the date of birth, the individual's name and the parent's name before issuing a fresh certificate. For spelling changes in a parent's name or the addition or removal of a surname, provide supporting documents. To correct the spelling of a deceased person's name, the legal heirs must provide documentary evidence for the Registrar to examine.

# Applying for or modifying a Chennai property tax assessment

## Application and supporting records

For a new assessment, submit Form 6. For a modification, use Form 7. GCC accepts requests at TACTV counters on corporation premises, the relevant zonal office or headquarters. Include a request letter with the property address and contact number, copies of registered documents in the applicant's favour, and the completed form. Include the last property or vacant land tax receipt if an earlier building was demolished and rebuilt or the land was previously assessed for vacant land tax.

Online applicants register on the citizen portal with a username and mobile number or email address. The application asks for the division, assessee's name, property location, area, street, door number and contact number. GCC also asks for hard copies of the supporting documents.

## From inspection to order

The Assessor checks the application, informs the applicant before measuring the building and submits a proposal. The approving officer may carry out a field check. The Assessment Committee selects proposals for verification and approves assessments after scrutiny. GCC issues the assessment order as Notice 6 and informs the assessee by SMS. Notice 6 is the order; Form 6 is the application.

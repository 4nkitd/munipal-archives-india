# Greater Chennai Corporation budget at a glance, 2025-26

## Revenue and capital accounts

Page 1 gives all figures in ₹ crore. Negative balances denote deficits. Only the 2023-24 column reports actuals.

## Reading the net figure

The 2025-26 "Net surplus / deficit (revenue and capital)" line on page 1 is minus ₹501.08 crore. That line includes the negative opening balance of ₹363.55 crore as well as the two estimated deficits. The statement lists advances separately on page 2: for 2025-26 it estimates ₹270.45 crore in revenue advance recoveries against ₹233.24 crore in outgoings, and ₹125.00 crore in capital advance recoveries against ₹16.00 crore in outgoings.

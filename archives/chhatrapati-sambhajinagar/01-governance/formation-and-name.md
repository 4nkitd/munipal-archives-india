# Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Municipal Corporation: institutional history and name

## Council, corporation and current name

The corporation's introduction dates the municipal council to 1936 and the upgrade to a municipal corporation to 1982. Its historical [commissioners' list](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/list-of-honble-commissioner) starts with a term beginning 8 December 1982; the list gives a term date, not the date of the constituting order.

The introduction identifies Aurangabad Municipal Corporation as the former name. The [3 October 2025 ward gazette](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/assets/300/2025/10/mediafiles/Gazette.pdf#page=25) and the corporation's [2026 elected-member page](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/honble-corporators-list) use Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. Some municipal service links still use the older Aurangabad domain. The introduction's administrator reference describes an earlier period; for the elected body after the 2025–26 election, use the 2026 elected-member page.

## Municipal headquarters

The [corporation's contact page](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/municipal-contact) lists the Main Building at Town Hall, behind the Head Post Office, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra 431001. Its key contact table gives 0240-2333536 and lists `contact@chhsambhajinagarmc.org` alongside `contact@aurangabadmahapalika.org`.

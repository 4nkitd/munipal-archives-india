# Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar final delimitation: 29 electoral wards

## Approval and seat distribution

The municipal election portal records State Election Commission approval on 1 October 2025. The Maharashtra Government Gazette Extraordinary, Part One-A, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Division, issue 23, published the final notification on 3 October 2025. The English version starts on [page 25](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/assets/300/2025/10/mediafiles/Gazette.pdf#page=25). It cites draft notification 15 dated 23 August 2025 and section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. The schedule fixes 28 four-member wards and one three-member ward for a total of 115 seats in the next general election.

## Boundary schedule

Ward 1 is a four-member ward. Its schedule lists a population of 41,080 and names Harsul village and Phule Nagar among the localities covered. Ward 29 is the three-member ward. Its schedule lists 34,534 people and names Satara village and Kanchanwadi among the localities covered. These are figures from the election schedule, not new population estimates.

The [election portal](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/corporation-election-2025-26) links the final maps and the Marathi and English extent schedules. The gazette says the map controls if the two language versions differ and is available to inspect at the municipal office during office hours. The corporation's ten administrative zones and 120 administrative blocks, described in its overview, are not electoral ward counts.

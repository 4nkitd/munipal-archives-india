# Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar property tax lookup and payment

## Find the tax account

Use **Know & Pay Property Tax** on the [corporation's property page](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/property-pay). It opens the [tax collection form](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/TaxCollection/pg/property/getPropertyPgWebApi), which has fields labelled **Property No** and **Old Property No**. Bring the number printed on a municipal bill if you do not know which identifier to enter. Verify the property and the amount due before proceeding. The form takes payment for one consumer number at a time.

## Legacy address and office enquiries

The corporation also links the form on its legacy `aurangabadmahapalika.org` domain. The old domain name does not indicate a different municipality. For a record you cannot identify, the [municipal office contact page](https://chhsambhajinagarmc.org/municipal-contact) lists 0240-2333536.

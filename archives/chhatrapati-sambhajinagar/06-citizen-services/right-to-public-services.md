# Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar right to public services and civil certificates

## Civil certificate applications

The corporation provides separate forms for [birth certificates](https://rts.chhsambhajinagarmc.org/links/birth-certificate) and [death certificates](https://rts.chhsambhajinagarmc.org/links/death-certificate). The birth form collects the birth details and includes upload fields for a hospital certificate and parents' identity documents. The death form asks for the deceased person's details, the date and place of death and the applicant's relationship to the deceased. Check the live form for mandatory fields and use [Know Your Application Status](https://rts.chhsambhajinagarmc.org/links/get-application) to follow a submitted application.

## Appeals for delayed or refused notified services

The corporation's Right to Public Services page says the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 came into force on 28 April 2015. An eligible person may file first and second appeals with the competent authorities if a notified service is not delivered within the prescribed period or is refused without proper reasons. A third appeal goes to the Maharashtra State Right to Public Services Commission. The corporation's page gives a maximum penalty of ₹5,000 per case for a defaulting officer and links the service notifications and municipal order. The deadline depends on the notified service; there is no single deadline for all requests.

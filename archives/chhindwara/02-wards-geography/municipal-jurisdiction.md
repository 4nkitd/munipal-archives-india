# Chhindwara municipal jurisdiction and ward count

## Identifying the corporation

The [City Finance profile](https://www.cityfinance.in/municipal-data/city/chhindwara-municipality) reports 48 wards and an area of 47.2 km². The underlying directory identifies the corporation by LGD code 251026 and census code 802386. It attributes the ward count to the urban local body. The population figure of 138,291 is from Census 2011. These figures do not identify the ward for a particular address.

## Local office and jurisdiction

The [district municipal directory](https://chhindwara.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) gives the office address as Near Indira Gandhi Cricket Stadium, Chhindwara 480001. The district names the city body Nagar Palik Nigam, with office telephone 07162-222346 and email comm-cdw@mp.gov.in. Amarwara, Chourai and Parasia appear as separate municipal councils. City Finance uses the display name “Chhindwara Municipality” but classifies the body as a Municipal Corporation in its profile.

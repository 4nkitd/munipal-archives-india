# Chhindwara GIS for infrastructure and land use

## Infrastructure layers

Chhindwara district lists road networks, drinking water supply systems, sewer lines and land use on its GIS platform. The same map brings together government assets, schools and hospitals. The district describes these layers as planning and monitoring information. They cover district administration; they are not a municipal ward delimitation order.

## Field records

The district also describes PARAKH, a web and mobile system developed by the Madhya Pradesh State Electronics Development Corporation. Officials use its field inspection forms to record site visits and its asset surveys to track government buildings, open plots and other property. The district page links the [Chhindwara GIS portal](https://chhindwaragis.mp.gov.in/).

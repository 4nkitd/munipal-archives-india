# Chirimiri mayoral election result, 2025

## Vote by candidate

The Chhattisgarh State Election Commission marks Ramnaresh Rai (रामनरेश राय) as elected mayor for Chirimiri Municipal Corporation. The tally appears under Manendragarh-Chirmiri-Bharatpur district and is separate from the result for president of Manendragarh Municipal Council.

| Candidate or ballot option | Party | Votes |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Ramnaresh Rai | Bharatiya Janata Party | 18,891 |
| Dr. Vinay Jaiswal | Indian National Congress | 13,199 |
| Rukmani Manikpuri | Gondwana Gantantra Party | 259 |
| Baldevdas | Independent | 1,976 |
| Prem Shankar Soni | Independent | 1,133 |
| None of the above (NOTA) | | 315 |

The winning margin was 5,692 votes. The six lines add to 35,773 votes including NOTA. These are calculations from the tally, not turnout figures. The [list of elected mayors and presidents](https://cgsec.gov.in/Notification2025/election/Mayor-president%202025.pdf#page=1) also identifies Rai as the winner.

## Election dates and scope

The [commission's 15 February 2025 release](https://cgsec.gov.in/15022025_2.html) dates the election schedule to 20 January, polling to 11 February, and counting and release of results to 15 February 2025. This record reports the mayoral result at that election. The [ward return](../02-wards-geography/ward-results-2025.md) lists the separately elected councillors.

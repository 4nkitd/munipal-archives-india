# Chirimiri ward and councillor returns, 2025

## Finding a ward in the election return

The Chhattisgarh State Election Commission's list of elected councillors identifies 40 numbered wards for Chirimiri Municipal Corporation. Its entries are statewide serials 984 to 1023. [Page 17](https://cgsec.gov.in/Notification2025/election/Councillor%202025.pdf#page=17) covers wards 1 to 14; [page 18](https://cgsec.gov.in/Notification2025/election/Councillor%202025.pdf#page=18) covers wards 15 to 40. The next entries, beginning with serial 1024 and with ward numbering restarting at 1, belong to Manendragarh Municipal Council.

In all 40 rows, 27 winners are listed for the Bharatiya Janata Party, 11 for the Indian National Congress and two as independents. The table above is a selection, with names transliterated from the commission's Hindi list. The commission [reported counting and release of results on 15 February 2025](https://cgsec.gov.in/15022025_2.html). The return does not give ward boundaries, and it does not establish who holds office after the election edition.

# Chirimiri municipal financial statements and 2021–22 snapshot

## Provisional figures for 2021–22

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' City Finance dashboard lists the corporation under "Chirmiri Municipal Corporation." It attributes the headline values to provisional financial statements for 2021–22 and shows a dashboard update of 22 March 2025.

| Indicator | Displayed value (₹ crore) |
| --- | ---: |
| Total tax revenue | 3 |
| Total own revenue | 5 |
| Total grant | 8 |
| Total revenue | 17 |
| Total expenditure | 17 |
| Total balance sheet size | 121 |

The figures are displayed in whole crore. Identical displayed revenue and expenditure do not establish that the underlying amounts were equal, and the values are not audited budget estimates or a report of current finances.

## Financial years listed

The dashboard lists six years of financial data: 2021–22, 2020–21, 2019–20, 2017–18, 2016–17 and 2015–16. The selector skips 2018–19. Use the Balance Sheet & Income Statement tab and its year selector to look for the underlying line items rather than treating the rounded headline figures as exact accounts.

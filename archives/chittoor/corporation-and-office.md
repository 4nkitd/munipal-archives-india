# Chittoor Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## Municipal jurisdiction

The [state directory](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/open/ulbs) classifies Chittoor as a corporation, ULB ID 13, in Andhra Pradesh. The municipal profile dates the municipality to 1917 and the corporation to 2012. The profile traces the change from a third-grade municipality through special grade and selection grade. It identifies sanitation, water supply and urban planning as functions of the Chittoor civic body. District-wide or Kuppam municipal records require a different jurisdiction.

## Where to address a municipal enquiry

The corporation contact record lists 2-11, Officers Line, Kongareddypalli, Chittoor. The address belongs to the city corporation. The district public-utility entry with the URL ending in chittoor-municipal-corporation currently describes Kuppam Municipality and its Netaji Road office. The [municipal contact entry](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbcontacts/13) lists ctrmpl@gmail.com. The [district directory entry](https://chittoor.ap.gov.in/public-utility/chittoor-municipal-corporation/) provides a separate check on the location.

# Chittoor election wards, revenue wards and sachivalayams

## Counts in the municipal profile

The Andhra Pradesh municipal profile for Chittoor carries an update timestamp of 14 October 2026. Its area field gives 95.97 square kilometres. The following counts refer to different units in that record.

| Profile field | Value |
|---|---:|
| Election wards | 60 |
| Revenue wards | 43 |
| Ward sachivalayams | 51 |

## Which figure to use

The historical background on the same page says “around 50 elected wards”. The structured field gives 60. Neither entry names an election or cites a delimitation order. Use 60 when quoting this profile field and identify its edition; use the relevant election notification for an election-specific count. The [municipal About page](https://chittoor.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/chittoor/about) draws on this profile. It does not provide street limits, a ward-by-ward schedule or parcel assignments.

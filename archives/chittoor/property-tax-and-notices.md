# Chittoor property tax search and demand notices

## Find an assessment in Chittoor

The Chittoor property-tax form accepts any one of four search terms: assessment number, old assessment number, owner name or door number. An owner who has an older bill can use the old assessment number directly. The form says the online information is updated and a physical visit is not required for this search.

## Retrieve a bill or proceeding

The [municipal notice search](https://chittoor.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/reports/citizen-searchnotices.action) asks for either the assessment number or the application number. It lists proceedings, notices, certificates, endorsements and demand bills as downloadable records. Owner name and door number belong on the assessment search, not on the notice form.

The [calculator](https://chittoor.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/calculatepropertytax) is a separate service. Its inputs distinguish vacant land, private property and government property. A calculation does not retrieve an issued demand bill. Use the Chittoor notice search for that document.

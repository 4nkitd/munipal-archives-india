# Coimbatore corporation formation and municipal responsibilities

## Formation and scope

Section 3 of the corporation's English Citizen's Charter dates the earlier Coimbatore municipality to **1866** and says that it became a City Municipal Corporation on **1 May 1981**. The formation date in the archive refers to the corporation, not the predecessor municipality. The [district local bodies register](https://coimbatore.nic.in/local-bodies/) lists Coimbatore Municipal Corporation under the corporation heading and lists municipalities and town panchayats separately. The municipal body does not cover the whole district.

## Council and executive wing

The charter describes 100 wards grouped into zonal ward committees. It puts the Mayor at the head of the council and the Commissioner at the head of the executive wing. It lists a Deputy Commissioner, City Health Officer, City Engineer, planning and education officers, and zonal Assistant Commissioners in the executive structure. See the [corporation website](https://ccmc.gov.in/) for the current administration and council directories rather than relying on the charter's account of officeholders or election procedures.

The charter assigns the corporation water supply, sewerage, waste management, roads, drains, street lighting and municipal health facilities. It separately lists tax administration, building and planning permission, trade licensing, and birth and death registration as regulatory functions. The charter is a historical description of those functions, not a source for current fee rates or office hours.

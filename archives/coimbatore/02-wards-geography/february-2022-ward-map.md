# Coimbatore ward map and five municipal zones

## Zone and ward lookup

CCMC's "Know your Zone & Ward" map loads the corporation's [February 2022 ward boundary layer](https://ccmc.gov.in/images/Ward_Boundary_Feb_2022.geojson). It contains 100 ward features. The map descriptions give the zone, new ward number, area name and old ward reference. The table uses the new ward number. Ward numbers are not continuous within each zone.

| Zone | Wards in the map layer |
| --- | --- |
| Central | 31, 32, 46–49, 62–70, 80–84 |
| East | 5–9, 22–24, 50–61 |
| North | 1–4, 10–15, 18–21, 25–30 |
| South | 76–79, 85–100 |
| West | 16, 17, 33–45, 71–75 |

The layer spells North as `NORHT` in the descriptions for wards 19 and 30. The table combines those entries with the other North Zone wards.

## Boundary documents

The corporation's [delimitation index](https://ccmc.gov.in/index.php/createpage/delimitation-of-ward) lists the ward delimitation Form 2D document, a zone map and a 2018 delimitation gazette. Use the map to orient a search and consult the official boundary documents for the applicable edition or a parcel-level question. The layer name identifies its February 2022 edition; it does not establish when the web map was published.

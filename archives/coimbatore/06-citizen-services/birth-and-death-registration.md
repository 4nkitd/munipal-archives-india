# Coimbatore birth and death registration

## Where a birth is reported

CCMC's Public Health page identifies the Commissioner as Corporation Birth and Death Registrar, the Corporation Health Officer as Additional Registrar, and the Sanitary Inspector of the ward concerned as Ward Registrar. It distinguishes three reporting situations. For a birth at a hospital, the hospital reports it on Form 1 to the Sanitary Inspector for the ward where the hospital is located. For a birth at home, the head of the household submits Form 1 to the Sanitary Inspector for the area. For a birth in a moving vehicle, the corporation identifies the first halt as the registration area.

## Reporting forms and certificates

| Use | Form listed by CCMC |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting a birth, death or stillbirth | Form 1, 2 or 3 respectively |
| Medical certification of cause of death in a hospital or at home | Form 4 or Form 4A respectively |
| Birth or death certificate | Form 5 or Form 6 respectively |
| Non-availability certificate | Form 10 |

The department page has separate sections for late registration, adding a child's name and requesting corrections. These are not the same as downloading a certificate for an existing record. Check the department page for the relevant process and current requirements before submitting an application.

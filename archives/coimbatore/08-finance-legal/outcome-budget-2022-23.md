# Coimbatore outcome budget for 2022–23

## General Fund estimates

The English budget's "Abstract of General Fund" compares actuals for 2020–21, the original and revised 2021–22 budget, and the 2022–23 budget. The figures below are from the **2022–23 budget** column, in crore rupees. They are estimates, not audited amounts received or paid.

| Line | 2022–23 estimate (₹ crore) |
| --- | ---: |
| Revenue receipts | 571.36 |
| Capital receipts | 494.65 |
| Total receipts | 1,066.01 |
| Revenue payments | 528.08 |
| Capital payments | 560.17 |
| Total payments | 1,088.25 |
| Deficit | 22.24 |

The General Fund abstract includes ₹178.03 crore of tax revenue, ₹70.70 crore of fees and user charges, and ₹440.97 crore of capital grants and contributions for specific purposes. The preceding summary gives higher, combined receipts of ₹2,317.97 crore and combined payments of ₹2,337.28 crore, with a ₹19.31 crore deficit. The combined total and the General Fund are different scopes within the same budget.

# Establishment and original limits of Cuddalore City Municipal Corporation

## What the founding Act covered

The Cuddalore City Municipal Corporation Act, 2022 received the Governor's assent on 21 January 2022. The Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary published it that day in issue 56, Part IV, Section 2, pages 10–16. Section 1 deemed the Act in force from 21 October 2021. Sections 2 and 3 put the former Cuddalore municipality's local area in the city and established the corporation over that area. The former municipality was deemed abolished on commencement. The Act allowed later changes to the limits by state notification, after consultation with the corporation and subject to previous publication.

Section 4 identified the mayor, council, standing committee, wards committee and commissioner as municipal authorities. Section 5 capped elected councillors at 72 and left the actual number to government notification. That ceiling is not the number of wards used in an election.

## Governing law after April 2023

The [Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies (Amendment) Act, 2022](https://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2022/352_Ex_IV_2-E.pdf) carried Cuddalore into the statewide framework under amended section 4(1-A)(xvi) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998 and added the city-specific Act to the repeal list in section 200(1). [G.O. Ms. No. 44](https://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2023/123_Ex_II_2_2023.pdf) brought the amendment into force on 13 April 2023. The founding Act records the original extent. It is not a current boundary schedule.

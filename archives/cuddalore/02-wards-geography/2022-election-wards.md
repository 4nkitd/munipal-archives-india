# Cuddalore corporation wards in the 2022 urban local election

## Ward numbers and seat categories

The Tamil Nadu State Election Commission records 45 ward-member races for the corporation in the 2022 urban local election. Each section gives the ward number and seat category, followed by elector and ballot counts and candidate results. The seat categories below reproduce the election edition, in English. They do not describe reservation in a later election.

| Seat category | Wards |
| --- | --- |
| General | 3, 9–14, 16, 23, 24, 27, 29, 32, 35, 37, 39–41, 43 |
| General (women) | 1, 2, 5–8, 15, 17–22, 26, 30, 31, 38, 42, 44, 45 |
| Scheduled Castes (general) | 28, 33, 34 |
| Scheduled Castes (women) | 4, 25, 36 |

## How to use the return

On the [state results page](https://tnsec.tn.gov.in/tnsec_static/election_results/ulbe2022/ulbe2022.php), select Cuddalore district and the corporation ward-member results. The municipality and town panchayat results are separate. The corporation return has no street schedules or ward polygons. It cannot locate an address in a ward or establish a revised ward count after the election.

# Cuddalore corporation boundary expansion proposal, December 2024

## What the gazette proposes

The state published G.O. Ms. No. 201 on 31 December 2024 in Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary issue 429, Part II, Section 2. Notification II(2)/MAWS/1223(c-1)/2024 declares the Governor's intention to include the listed areas under section 3(2)(a)(i) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998.

Row 2 on page 2 lists 13 village panchayats, all in Cuddalore Panchayat Union. The proposed extent is marked "Full" for each: Arisipperiyankuppam, Karaiyeravittakuppam, Kudikkadu, Pachayankuppam, Tiruvandipuram, Cuddalore O.T., Gunduuppalavadi, Kondur, Marudadu, Nathapattu, Padirikuppam, Periyakanganankuppam and Thottapattu.

## What the notice does not settle

The notice declares an intention. It does not itself make the inclusion effective or assign the named areas to corporation wards. Use a subsequent operative notification to establish inclusion and a delimitation schedule to determine any new ward assignment.

# Cuddalore municipal tax payments and the 2026–27 first-half incentive

## Payment channels and the dated incentive

The corporation directs residents to [Tamil Nadu Urban ePay](https://tnurbanepay.tn.gov.in/) for municipal payments. Its Tamil notice lists property tax, profession tax and non-tax dues, and identifies online payment by credit or debit card, UPI and net banking. It also names Bharat Bill Payment System and the TN Urban eSevai app as payment channels.

The corporation's first-half notice for financial year 2026–27 cited section 84(1) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998. It asked property owners to pay by 30 April 2026 for an incentive of 5 percent of the half-year's property tax, up to Rs. 5,000. That deadline applies to the period named in the notice and has passed. The page does not give a notice publication date.

## Office channel

The [Cuddalore district directory](https://cuddalore.nic.in/public-utility/cuddalore-municipality/) lists the Municipal Commissioner's office on Bharathi Road, Cuddalore 607 001. The office phone is 04142-230021 and its email is `commr.cuddalore@tn.gov.in`.

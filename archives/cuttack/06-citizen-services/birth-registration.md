# Who reports a birth in Cuttack

## Reporting the birth

CMC directs applicants to the Registrar (Birth and Death) cum Health Officer for the corporation. Its guidance gives a normal reporting period of 21 days from birth. For a home birth, the head of the household, nearest relative of the head, or oldest family member reports the event. For a hospital, nursing home or clinic birth, the institution reports to the registrar for its jurisdiction.

## Application details

The guide lists the child's name, date and order of birth, institution name, parents’ names and permanent address. A hospital or nursing-home discharge slip may accompany the application.

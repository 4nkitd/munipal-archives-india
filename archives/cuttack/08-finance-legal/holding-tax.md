# Residential holding-tax rates published by CMC

## Residential schedule

| Type of building | Rate labelled “Present” (Rs per sq ft) | Rate labelled “Revised” (Rs per sq ft) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Thatched, tile, asbestos or tin house | 2 | 2 |
| Building up to two storeys | 2 | 3 |
| Building of three storeys or more | 2 | 3.5 |
| Apartment building | 2 | 3.5 |

The service page gives no effective date or calculation period for this schedule. These figures alone do not establish the amount payable on a particular holding.

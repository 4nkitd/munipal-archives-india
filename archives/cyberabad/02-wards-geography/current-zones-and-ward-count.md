# Cyberabad municipal zones, circles and wards

## Current jurisdiction

Cyberabad Municipal Corporation covers Serilingampally, Kukatpally and Quthbullapur zones, with 16 circles and 76 wards. The constituting [G.O.Ms.No. 55 dated 11 February 2026](../../hyderabad/01-governance/2026-reorganisation-order.md) spells the third zone Qutbullapur. It defines the corporation by the areas constituted by those three zones in the December 2025 reorganisation.

The corporation publishes a [zone, circle and ward map](https://cmc.telangana.gov.in/img_NewDesign/CMCMap.jpeg). This record preserves the published totals and map link, not a ward-number range or boundary transcription. The 300 wards mentioned in the corporation's history belonged to the former expanded GHMC.

# Cyberabad online tax, licence and grievance services

## Public entry points

| Task | Link published by Cyberabad |
|---|---|
| Pay property tax | [Online payments](https://onlinepayments.ghmc.gov.in/) |
| Apply for property tax self-assessment | [Self-assessment](https://sa.ghmc.gov.in/) |
| Apply for a new trade licence | [Trade application](https://sa.ghmc.gov.in/TradeNewApplicationEntry.aspx) |
| Lodge a civic complaint | [Grievance portal](https://igs.ghmc.gov.in/operator/send_otp_mobile) |
| Submit a building application | [BuildNow](https://buildnow.telangana.gov.in/) |

For local office routing, use the [Cyberabad contact directory](https://cmc.telangana.gov.in/KeyContacts.aspx). The shared `ghmc.gov.in` services are linked by Cyberabad itself. Their hostnames do not indicate that an address falls under the present GHMC.

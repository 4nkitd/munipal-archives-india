# Cyberabad tender notices register

## What the register shows

The public register lists tender type, work name, downloads, start and end dates, submission deadline and bid-opening date fields. The November 2026 entries identify these works:

| Work | Known location | Listed bid submission deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Repairs and annual maintenance of a foot overbridge at Shapoor Nagar | Ward 290, Subhash Nagar, Kompally Circle 56, Quthbullapur Zone | 14 November 2026, noon |
| Topographic, utilities and traffic surveys for Gandi Maisamma Junction | Dundigal Circle 59, Quthbullapur Zone | 17 November 2026, 2 pm |
| Construction, installation and five years of operation and maintenance of smart toilets | Various Cyberabad locations | 26 November 2026, 11 am |

Ward 290 is the number attached to the first work in the register, not a boundary description. The dates are bid deadlines shown by the portal, not publication dates or award dates.

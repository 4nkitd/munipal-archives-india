# Darbhanga Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and official records

## Corporation, subdivision and district

The District Administration's [Administrative Setup](https://darbhanga.nic.in/administrative-setup/) identifies Darbhanga Municipal Corporation by name and lists Benipur Municipal Council separately. It also distinguishes Darbhanga Sadar, Benipur and Biraul as district subdivisions. Its date of 1 January 1875 refers to the establishment of the district, not the incorporation of the city government.

The State Election Commission (SEC), Bihar, lists Darbhanga in Darbhanga Sadar subdivision as a नगर निगम (Nagar Nigam, or municipal corporation). In the [final ward formation register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0), serial 61 shows 48 wards and describes the body as अपरिवर्तित (unchanged) for that ward formation exercise. The register's population of 296,039 is based on the 2011 Census figures assigned to those wards. It is neither a district total nor a current population estimate.

## Which record to use for an address

Use the [SEC Form 6 ward schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=13&SID=1&SN=268) for the four-sided descriptions of the wards in that edition. The district also posted a [Form 2 gazette publication notice](https://darbhanga.nic.in/notice/in-connection-with-the-publication-in-the-district-gazette-of-prapatr-2-there-is-a-connection-between-the-expansion-of-the-area-of-the-municipal-corporations-and-the-renaming-of-the/) with a display window of 24 July to 31 August 2023, concerning area expansion and panchayat changes. Its English landing page does not identify the affected parcels or provide a new ward count. The SEC edition and the later district notice should not be substituted for the latest applicable gazette when deciding whether a particular parcel falls inside the city.

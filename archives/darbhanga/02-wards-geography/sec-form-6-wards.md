# Darbhanga wards in the Bihar SEC final Form 6

## Electoral areas and population

The [Bihar State Election Commission Form 6](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=13&SID=1&SN=268) names Darbhanga district, Darbhanga Sadar subdivision and Darbhanga Nagar Nigam. It gives each of wards 1 through 48 a row with Census 2011 population, four boundary descriptions in Hindi and a map link. Darbhanga also appears in the commission's [list of wards unchanged in the delimitation exercise](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC). Neither table is a list of councillors or registered electors.

## Reading the limits

The Form 6 headings use पूo for east, पo for west, उo for north and दo for south. Ward 2's northern boundary refers to the stretch from the bypass railway crossing to Dulle Bridge near Maharani Pokhar. Its eastern text uses the PWD road and Bela railway crossing. Ward 3's western text refers to Polytechnic Chowk and Bela Parmeshwar Chowk. Ward 48's eastern boundary follows the railway line from near plot 1762 to railway crossing 19. These are reference points, not complete boundary descriptions. Consult all four limits in the corresponding row of Form 6 and the SEC map when checking a property near one of these landmarks.

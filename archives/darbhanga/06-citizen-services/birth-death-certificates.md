# Darbhanga birth and death certificate service routes

## Published service entries

Darbhanga District Administration lists separate pages for a birth certificate and a death certificate. Both point to the same Bihar Urban Development and Housing Department Nagar Seva landing page rather than to separate form URLs. The death certificate page explicitly directs applicants to Right to Public Service, Darbhanga Municipal Corporation, Darbhanga city, PIN 846004. The birth certificate page gives the same PIN.

| Service | District entry | What the entry identifies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birth certificate | [Birth Certificate](https://darbhanga.nic.in/service/birth-certificate/) | State Nagar Seva link and PIN 846004 |
| Death certificate | [Apply for Death Certificate](https://darbhanga.nic.in/service/death-certificate-apply/) | State Nagar Seva link and Right to Public Service at Darbhanga Municipal Corporation, PIN 846004 |

The [Nagar Seva URL](https://nagarseva.bihar.gov.in/udhd/Home.html) is the destination published by the district. The live transaction could not be verified during this check. If that link is unavailable, use the district service entries to confirm where to ask for assistance. The phone fields on those pages repeat the adjacent mobile fields and are not reproduced as office numbers.

## Registration and certificate issue

The district pages do not explain whether the event has already been registered, how to correct an entry or how to apply for delayed registration. When contacting the office, make clear whether the request is for a certificate from an existing record, first-time registration or a correction. The district pages do not provide a fee schedule, document checklist or statutory turnaround for either service.

# Davanagere city jurisdiction and corporation office

## Municipal area and responsibilities

The corporation's [City Summary](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/city-summary) reports an area of 68.63 km², 45 wards and a 2011 Census population of 434,971. These are city figures, not figures for Davanagere district or taluk. The summary gives an area but does not describe the legal boundary street by street or parcel by parcel.

The corporation's [Functions page](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/functions) lists street cleaning and lighting, sewage and waste disposal, drainage, maintenance of public streets and management of municipal waterworks among the obligatory functions. These are municipal responsibilities, not a list of individual service connections or service guarantees.

## Office

The [corporation contact page](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/contact-us) places the office opposite the railway station on PB Road, Davanagere 577002. It lists 08192-252899 and `ka.davanagere.cc@gmail.com` for the corporation, as well as `commissioner_davanagere@yahoo.com` for the commissioner's office. The [district municipal directory](https://davanagere.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists the same location and commissioner's office email.

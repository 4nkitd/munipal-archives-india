## Numbering cross-check

The [City Summary](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/city-summary) reports 45 wards. The separate [revenue property table](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/wardwise-properties) shows ward number 13 against serial row 23, but the Ward Details table and [Census Information table](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/census_info) both show ward 23 for Nijalingappa Badavane and S.S. Badavane "A" Block. This transcription follows Ward Details and does not reconcile the portal's property totals.

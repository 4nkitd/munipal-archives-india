# Davanagere online property tax and citizen services

## Property tax lookup

The corporation's [Citizen Services page](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/citizen-services) links to the tax portal, which has a [property search form](https://davanagerecitycorp.org/WebsiteDavanagere/PropertySearch1.aspx). The form allows search by property ID. Its instructions say that residents who do not know their ID can search by ward number and old or new assessment number instead. The tax portal home page still displays a rebate deadline of 30 April 2023; that is not a current payment deadline.

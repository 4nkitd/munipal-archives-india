# Davanagere municipal budget and audit index

## Budget plans and audit reports

The corporation's [Budget page](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/budget) links seven budget plans, one each for 2020-21, 2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27. The last row is labelled "Budget Plan for the year 2026-27". The index gives fiscal years and download links, not adoption dates or budget totals.

The separate [FSA Audit page](http://www.davanagerecity.mrc.gov.in/en/fsa-audit) lists FSA Audit Reports (CA) beginning in 2013-14. At the last row, the Year column says 2023-24 but the description says 2024-25. The index alone does not establish which year that attachment covers. The linked attachments were not used to extract figures or audit findings.

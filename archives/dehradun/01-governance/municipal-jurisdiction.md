# Dehradun municipal office and jurisdiction

## City corporation

The municipal office is on New Road near Doon Hospital, Dehradun 248001. The corporation lists Saurabh Thapliyal as mayor and its [officer directory](https://nagarnigamdehradun.com/whos.php) lists Alok Kumar Pandey, IAS, as municipal commissioner, with office number 0135-2714074. The corporation publishes [100 municipal ward maps](https://nagarnigamdehradun.com/ward-detail-parasiman2024.php). Its departments include property tax, public works, sanitation and street lighting.

## Which authority holds the record?

The [district administration](https://dehradun.nic.in/public-utility/nagar-nigam-dehradun/) lists the corporation as a municipal public utility. The district's tehsils and blocks are separate administrative units; a Dehradun district address alone does not identify a city ward.

The [Mussoorie Dehradun Development Authority](https://mddaonline.in/) publishes master and zonal plans and handles building-map approval. Its planning maps are distinct from the corporation's ward maps. The [Directorate General Defence Estates](https://dgde.gov.in/en/cantonments/) lists Dehradun and Clement Town as separate cantonments under Central Command. Their boards administer municipal matters within the notified cantonment areas.

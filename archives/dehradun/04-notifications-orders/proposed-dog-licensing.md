# Dehradun public notice proposing dog licensing bylaws

## What the notice proposes

The notice names the proposed instrument the Nagar Nigam Dehradun Dog Licence Bylaws 2025. Clause 2 says it would take effect on publication in the Uttarakhand government gazette. The municipal notice alone does not establish that publication.

Clause 4 would require registration at the municipal office for a pet dog aged three months or older kept within corporation limits. Registration would last one year from registration or renewal. Under the same clause, the licence would lapse if the dog's anti-rabies vaccination expired during that year. Clause 5 calls for an anti-rabies vaccination certificate from a registered veterinarian at registration and a sterilisation certificate for dogs in the category the notice calls “अति आक्रामक”.

## Municipal dispatch

[Page 3](https://nagarnigamdehradun.com/images/tendernotice/dog-fee.pdf#page=3) gives dispatch no. 304(VA), dated 12 December 2025. It asks newspaper editors to print the notice and directs municipal staff to post it on the corporation website and notice board. The dispatch does not establish when a newspaper printed the notice or whether the state gazette published the bylaws.

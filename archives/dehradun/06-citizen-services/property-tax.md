# Property tax, mutation and tax complaints in Dehradun

## Choose a property tax service

The [corporation website](https://nagarnigamdehradun.com/) links “Pay Online Tax” to the [NND service menu](https://nndonline.in/). Choose the entry that matches the request:

| Task | Entry on the portal |
|---|---|
| Pay municipal property tax | [Pay your Property Tax](https://tax.nndonline.in) |
| Apply to change the municipal property record | [Apply for Mutation](https://mutation.nndonline.in) |
| File a self-assessment | Select “Self Assesment” on the service menu. |
| Raise an issue with the tax department | [Register Tax Complaint](https://tax.nndonline.in/cmp_form.aspx) |

The menu does not state a payment deadline, mutation fee or processing time.

# Municipal Corporation of Delhi: statutory charter, unification and jurisdiction

## How the corporation became one body again

The [Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/SEC/generic_multiple_files/delhi_municipal_corporation-act-1957.pdf), Act 66 of 1957, consolidated the law for Delhi's municipal government. It received presidential assent on 28 December 1957. Before that Act, local bodies such as the Delhi and Delhi-Shahdara municipal committees, the Delhi District Board and notified area committees handled civic functions separately. The 1957 text is useful for the original statutory structure and territorial definitions; read it with later amendments.

The [State Election Commission's history](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sec/about-sec-delhi) dates the move to three corporations to December 2011. North Delhi and South Delhi had 104 wards each, and East Delhi had 64. The Commission held the next general municipal election in April 2012.

The [Delhi Municipal Corporation (Amendment) Act, 2022](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-10/amendment_in_dmc_act_1957.pdf), received presidential assent and was published in the Gazette on **18 April 2022**. The SEC history page gives 15 April for the amendment; the Gazette is the source for the assent and publication date. The amendment restored a single corporation under section 3. Section 514AA subsumed the North, South and East Delhi municipal corporations into MCD and transferred their property, rights and liabilities. It also carried over pending legal proceedings and treated references to the old corporations in contracts or other instruments as references to the unified MCD. Section 90A transferred officers and employees, and the new clause in section 42 made establishing an e-governance system for citizen services a municipal duty.

The Ministry of Home Affairs issued [S.O. 2303(E) and S.O. 2304(E)](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-10/enforcement_of_dmc_amendment_act.pdf) on 18 May 2022. The first constituted the unified MCD on **22 May 2022**. The second brought the amendment into force on the same date. April is the assent and publication date; May is the date of the reunified corporation.

## Which civic body covers an address?

- **Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD).** Section 2(10) of the 1957 Act defines "Delhi" for that Act as the territory excluding New Delhi and Delhi Cantonment. MCD reports an area of 1,397.3 square kilometres and 12 zones on its [official portal](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/). It estimates that it serves about 20 million people, without stating a reference year for that figure.
- **New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC).** This is a separate civic authority for the statutory New Delhi area. Its [official history](https://www.ndmc.gov.in/ndmc/history.aspx) records that the New Delhi Municipal Council Act, 1994, renamed the former New Delhi Municipal Committee. Some MCD historical material also uses "NDMC" for the former **North Delhi Municipal Corporation**, one of the three bodies merged in 2022. The full name resolves the ambiguity.
- **Delhi Cantonment Board.** The [Ministry of Defence's board](https://delhi.cantt.gov.in/) administers Delhi Cantonment separately. It did not join the unified MCD.

"New Delhi" in a postal address is not a municipal boundary test. MCD's own headquarters at Dr. S.P. Mukherjee Civic Centre uses New Delhi 110002. To decide which office serves a property, check the relevant civic jurisdiction and ward map rather than relying on the postal city or an old North, South or East corporation label.

# MCD Final Delimitation Order 2022: the 250 electoral wards

The Ministry of Home Affairs issued the final order on **17 October 2022**, file no. **14011/04/2022-Delhi-II**. A separate [10 September 2022 notification](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-10/determination_of_250_seats_and_scheduled_caste_seat_42.pdf) had set 250 councillor seats and 42 seats reserved for Scheduled Castes. That notification sets the number of reserved seats; it does not identify which wards are reserved. The draft boundaries went out on 12 September, with objections and suggestions invited through 3 October. The final order records that the government considered them before fixing ward extents.

The SEC's [28 October 2025 press note](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sec/press-note-election-schedule-bye-elections-12-wards-municipal-corporation-delhi-2025) announced by-elections in 12 existing MCD wards. The [3 December 2025 Gazette results](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/SEC/generic_multiple_files/list_of_elected_councillors_bye_election_2025.pdf) name the councillors elected in those wards after polling on 30 November. The by-elections did not add wards to the 250.

## How to read the final schedule

The order uses Census 2011 enumeration blocks (EBs), with codes and locality labels listed by ward. [Annexure 1, Wards Summary of Population](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/SCERT/circulars-orders/wardwise_population_summary.pdf), gives the ward number and name, the Assembly constituency (AC) and the census-based population figures. Its printed aggregate is **16,419,787**, including **2,746,205** Scheduled Caste residents. Those are delimitation figures based on the 2011 Census, not a current population estimate.

An AC is an election constituency, not an MCD administrative zone. For example, AC 1 Narela contains wards 1 Narela, 2 Bankner, 3 Holambi Kalan, 4 Alipur and 5 Bakhtawarpur. AC 6 Rithala contains wards 21 Rohini-A, 22 Rohini-B, 23 Rithala, 24 Vijay Vihar and 25 Budh Vihar. AC 70 Karawal Nagar contains wards 246 Sri Ram Colony, 247 Sadatpur, 248 Karawal Nagar-West, 249 Sonia Vihar and 250 Sabapur. A ward name by itself is not a full address boundary.

## Checking a locality

1. Locate the ward number in [the archive's ward index](../wards.json) or the SEC population summary.
2. Check the corresponding enumeration blocks and locality descriptions in [Annexure 2](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/SCERT/circulars-orders/fdo_final.pdf). The order itself cautions that the boundary maps use tentative Census 2011 EB maps and that census figures prevail in case of clerical error.
3. Confirm the area on the SEC's individual maps for [wards 1–240](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sec/annexure-4-final-individual-maps-newly-delimited-wards-1-240-maps-0) or [wards 241–250](https://sec.delhi.gov.in/sec/final-individual-maps-newly-delimited-wards-241-250-maps). Do not mix the unified order with the earlier 2016 schedule for the former corporations.

# MCD civic contacts: citizen call centre, complaints and Commissioner's Office

## Citizen enquiries and complaints

The [MCD contact page](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/contactUs) lists **155305** as the Citizen's Call Centre and **mcd-ithelpdesk@mcd.nic.in** as the IT Department helpdesk. For a civic complaint, use the **MCD311** app or the MCD homepage's **Grievances** menu. That menu has separate entries to lodge and track a complaint, as well as a link to the national [Public Grievances portal](https://pgportal.gov.in/). The IT helpdesk address is not a substitute for the designated complaint channel.

For an issue tied to a place, keep the complaint reference and record the exact locality and nearby landmark. MCD lists its 12 administrative areas on the [Zones page](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/zones): Central, City-SP, Civil Lines, Karol Bagh, Keshav Puram, Najafgarh, Narela, North Shahdara, Rohini, South Shahdara, South and West. A zone covers more than one electoral ward. Use the zone's official listing for local office details rather than assuming the ward name is the zone name.

## Headquarters and the Commissioner's Office

MCD's headquarters is **Dr. S.P. Mukherjee Civic Centre, Minto Road, New Delhi 110002**. The [Commissioner's Office directory](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/showContent?menuguide=52457d1a-cdfe-41a7-80aa-03771444c99b), under **About Us**, lists Sanjeev Khirwar, IAS, as Commissioner and Sumit Kumar, IOFS, as Secretary to Commissioner. Both rows give the same office numbers as `23225901,02`: **011-23225901** and **011-23225902**. The structured officer entries keep the first number because each phone field generates a single dial link. These are headquarters roles, not ward assignments. The source does not date these listings, so recheck the directory for personnel changes.

Use the [New Delhi Municipal Council](https://www.ndmc.gov.in/) or the [Delhi Cantonment Board](https://delhi.cantt.gov.in/) for an address under those authorities. MCD's postal address does not change that division of responsibility.

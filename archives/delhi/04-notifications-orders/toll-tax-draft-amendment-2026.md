# MCD public notice inviting comments on draft toll tax bye-laws, November 2026

The MCD Toll Tax Department issued a notice on **20 November 2026** inviting objections and suggestions on proposed changes to the Delhi Municipal Corporation (Toll Tax) Bye-Laws, 2007. The [municipal homepage](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/) listed the notice. The department gave **5 p.m. on 30 November 2026** as the deadline and named the office email addresses **adc.tolltax@mcd.nic.in** and **ao.tolltax@mcd.nic.in** for submissions.

## What the notice proposes

The consultation text proposes a multi-lane free-flow system for identifying toll-liable commercial vehicles entering Delhi without requiring them to stop at a barrier. It defines automatic number plate recognition (ANPR), electronic toll collection infrastructure and the transaction records used to document vehicle passage and payment status. It also proposes a designated digital interface for paying tolls, viewing notices or warrants and tracking unpaid toll tax.

The draft distinguishes a notice of unpaid toll and penalty from a proposed electronic warrant that could follow if the notice were not complied with. It describes information the notice would contain, including vehicle particulars, time and place, unpaid toll, penalty and time allowed for payment.

## Status of this record

The department sought comments before implementation. The notice attaches a **draft notification** with no publication date filled in; its commencement clause ties commencement to publication in the Official Gazette. This archive entry records what MCD put out for consultation. It does not establish that the draft was adopted, that the proposed system was operating, or that any proposed deadline for paying a toll applied.

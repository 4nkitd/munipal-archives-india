# MCD Citizen Charter: property tax, vital records, trade licences and bookings

The Citizen Charter describes service routes, supporting documents and expected delivery times for MCD applicants. Use it with the [Online Services list](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/) to reach the live application. Check the authority first: MCD's charter does not govern services administered by the New Delhi Municipal Council or Delhi Cantonment Board.

## Property tax and change of name

The [Property Tax Return portal](https://mcdonline.nic.in/ptrmcd) covers residential and commercial properties within MCD. The charter lists applicant ID and a property document containing the address, and gives an instant receipt as its service benchmark. Change of name in the property tax record is a separate service. Use the live portal's **e-Mutation Property Tax** link and select the applicable transfer category; the charter lists different supporting documents for different cases.

## Birth and death certificates

Use the [Registration of Birth and Death portal](https://rbd.mcdonline.nic.in/rbd) for MCD records. The charter separates events in hospitals and institutions, where the health unit handles certificate processing, from home births, where parents can apply through MCD with the required documents. A new registration, correction and addition of a child's name are different requests. The charter lists different supporting documents and timeframes depending on the event and request. Confirm the current process before submitting a delayed registration.

## Licensing and bookings

- **General trade and storage.** Start with [General Trade / Storage Licences](https://mcdonline.nic.in/gtlmcd). This is a different route from a Health Trade Licence.
- **Health trade.** The Public Health chapter separates categories A and B from category C uses such as eating, lodging and boarding establishments, guest houses and cinemas. It lists premises occupancy proof, applicant ID and a plan of the premises among the documents. The charter says category C requires officer approval.
- **MCD spaces and pets.** The [Booking Management System](https://mcdonline.nic.in/bms) handles community facilities and park bookings. The portal also links to a separate [pet dog registration module](https://mcdonline.nic.in/petmcd).

The charter is undated. Treat the fees, turnaround times and document lists on the active service screen as the ones to check before paying or filing.

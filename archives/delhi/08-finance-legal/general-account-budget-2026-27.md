# MCD General Account budget: revised estimates 2025–26 and budget estimates 2026–27

The cover identifies this as the **Final Edition**, General Wing, Volume I, of the unified MCD's General Account Income and Expenditure Budget. The [Finance Department's Budget Documents list](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/showContent?menuguide=1b4066c6-73ae-417b-bc62-796f9f0b7c7e) records **5 May 2026** under **Date Uploaded**. The adoption date was not verified.

## What the income table records

The General Account income schedules use **rupees in lakh**. The approved 2026–27 budget estimates (BE) sit alongside the approved 2025–26 revised budget estimates (RBE), original 2025–26 budget estimates and 2024–25 actuals. Selected figures from the printed income schedules on pages 11 and 18:

| Income line | 2024–25 actual | 2025–26 BE | 2025–26 RBE | 2026–27 BE |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Tax share from GNCTD | 298,387.19 | 328,200.00 | 328,226.00 | 361,000.00 |
| Current private property tax receipts | 126,588.72 | 160,000.00 | 116,500.00 | 162,400.00 |
| Duty on property transfer | 274,894.13 | 360,000.00 | 378,954.00 | 420,000.00 |
| Toll tax | 85,886.42 | 90,000.00 | 86,418.00 | 90,000.00 |
| Grand total, General Account income schedule | 1,240,675.07 | 1,670,104.70 | 1,567,972.29 | 1,718,406.50 |

GNCTD is the Government of the National Capital Territory of Delhi. The revised 2025–26 tax share of 328,226.00 lakh is one line; the government grants subtotal of 388,226.00 lakh also includes a separate 60,000.00 lakh grant. The budget places toll tax under discretionary taxes and current private property tax receipts under obligatory taxes. It lists property tax arrears and government property receipts separately.

Use the actuals column for income already recorded for 2024–25 and the estimate columns for projected income. The grand total shown here is the income schedule total, not a total for cash actually collected or a combined municipal budget across other accounts. The Finance Department lists earlier estimates separately, including documents for the former South Delhi Municipal Corporation that do not cover today's unified MCD.

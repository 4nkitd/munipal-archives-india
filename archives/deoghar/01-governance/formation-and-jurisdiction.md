# Formation and jurisdiction of Deoghar Municipal Corporation

## Deoghar and Jasidih

According to the corporation's state-hosted overview, it was established on 26 June 2010 by merging Jasidih Notified Area Committee and Deoghar Municipality. The overview reports 119.70 square kilometres, 36 wards and a 2011 census population of 203,123. These are figures from the overview, not a legal schedule that defines the municipal limits.

## Corporation and district

Deoghar Nagar Nigam is not the same authority as Deoghar district. The [district's 2026 election page](https://deoghar.nic.in/deoghar-nagar-nigam-and-madhupur-nagar-parishad-general-election-2025/) lists the corporation and Madhupur Nagar Parishad separately, with distinct booth, reservation and voter list materials. The [district public utility directory](https://deoghar.nic.in/public-utility/deoghar-municipal-corporation/) locates the corporation office on Court Road, Deoghar 814112.

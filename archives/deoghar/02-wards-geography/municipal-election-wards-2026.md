# Deoghar municipal election rolls and wards, 2026

## Corporation ward series

Schedule II of Jharkhand Extraordinary Gazette No. 22, dated 27 January 2026, lists Deoghar Nagar Nigam wards 1 through 36 at serial numbers 108 through 143. It set polling for 23 February 2026. Schedule I lists the mayoral seat as unreserved for that election. Ward reservations appear separately in Schedule II.

The [district's corporation roll index](https://deoghar.nic.in/draft-voter-list-of-deoghar-nagar-nigam/) labels its contents the updated 2026 municipal election voter list without photographs. The table covers ward numbers 01 through 36. The visible title says updated even though the URL contains the word “draft.”

## Booth lists and district gazettes

The district publishes [two separate booth-list links](https://deoghar.nic.in/regarding-booth-list-for-deoghar-nagar-nigam-and-madhupur-nagar-parishad-upcoming-municipal-election2026/), one for Deoghar Nagar Nigam and one for Madhupur Nagar Parishad. Its [2026 election index](https://deoghar.nic.in/deoghar-nagar-nigam-and-madhupur-nagar-parishad-general-election-2025/) also separates the reservation gazettes and voter rolls for the two authorities. The roll and booth indexes do not identify legally surveyed ward boundaries. This record preserves the ward numbers, not voter details.

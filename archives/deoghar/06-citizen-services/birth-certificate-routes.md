# Deoghar birth certificates through Pragya Kendras and civil registration

## Two published application routes

The district birth certificate page directs residents to the nearest Pragya Kendra for assisted service or to the [Civil Registration System](https://crsorgi.gov.in/) to register and apply online. The [corporation's certificates page](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/ulb/deoghar/BirthDeath.aspx) links the same system under “Apply for Birth and Death Certificate.”

## Office questions

The [district directory](https://deoghar.nic.in/public-utility/deoghar-municipal-corporation/) gives the municipal office address as Court Road, Deoghar 814112. The district service page does not set out a municipal fee, processing time, documents to bring or a late-registration procedure. Contact the office to confirm the route for an event that falls within the municipal area. No certificate application or personal record lookup was submitted.

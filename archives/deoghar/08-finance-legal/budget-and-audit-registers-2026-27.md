# Deoghar municipal budget estimates and audit registers

## How to read the estimates

The [corporation budget register](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/ulb/deoghar/Budget.aspx) lists FY 2026-27 and earlier years. The FY 2026-27 attachment opens with a budget summary cover. The Budget Estimation Sheets that follow separate FY 2024-25 actuals, FY 2025-26 actuals through December 2025, FY 2025-26 budget estimates and FY 2026-27 budget estimates. The sheets start with headings including holding tax, water user charges and rent from municipal property. A partial-year actual is not a full-year collection total, and the FY 2026-27 column is an estimate.

## Where the audit is filed

The separate [Audited Annual Financial Statement register](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/ulb/deoghar/AuditedAFS.aspx) labels its FY 2024-25 entry “AUDIT REPORT DEOGHAR MUNICIPAL CORPORATION 2024-25” and also lists audited annual financial statements for FY 2023-24. Use that register for audited records rather than treating the budget's comparison columns as audit findings. No publication date is given for the budget in the register, and this record does not transcribe figures from the scanned tables.

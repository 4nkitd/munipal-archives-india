# Dewas city and its 2011 census profile

## City and district

Dewas city lies on the Malwa plateau northeast of Indore and southeast of Ujjain. Dewas was the common capital of the former Senior and Junior princely states. The corporation's account of the 1948 merger describes the formation of the district; its list of constituent villages is not a description of the municipal boundary.

## Population recorded in 2011

| City measure | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Population | 289,550 |
| Male population | 150,081 |
| Female population | 139,469 |
| Children aged 0 to 6 | 35,437 |
| Households | 57,397 |

The municipal profile reports 215,088 literate residents and a literacy rate of 84.6% among people aged seven and over. These are census figures for 2011, not population estimates for the year of publication.

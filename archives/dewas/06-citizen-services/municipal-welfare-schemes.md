# Dewas municipal welfare schemes and office enquiries

## Assistance recorded by the district

The district page shows a last update of 20 July 2020. The amounts below describe that listing; the page does not establish today's entitlement.

| Scheme or event | Amount listed |
| --- | --- |
| Indira Gandhi National Old Age, Widow and Disability Pension schemes | ₹600 per month under each scheme |
| National Family Support Scheme | ₹20,000 lump sum for an eligible family unable to support itself after the death of its primary breadwinner |
| Registered construction worker's daughter marries | ₹51,000 per daughter, for up to two daughters |
| Death of registered construction worker | ₹2 lakh for normal death or ₹4 lakh for accidental death; ₹6,000 funeral assistance |

## Municipal office

The district lists the department at A.B. Road, Nagar Palik Nigam, Dewas 455001, Madhya Pradesh. For enquiries it gives 07272-220333 and commdewas@mpurban.gov.in.

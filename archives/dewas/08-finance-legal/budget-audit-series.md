# Dewas municipal budgets and audit report series

## Financial years covered

| Years | Budget menu | Audit Report menu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2015-16 and 2016-17 | No corresponding entries | One annual report for each year |
| 2017-18 | Annual budget | Annual audit report |
| 2018-19 | Annual budget | Both an audit report and a separately labelled projected audit report |
| 2019-20 and 2020-21 | One budget for each year | One audit report for each year |
| 2021-22 | Annual budget | No corresponding entry |

For 2018-19, the municipality labels [the audit report](https://www.dmcdewas.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/DMC_Audit-Report-2018-19.pdf) separately from [the projected audit report](https://www.dmcdewas.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/New_Doc-2019-04-25.pdf). The DMC Revenue Report menu is a third series with PDF and spreadsheet links. The menus identify documents but do not give their adoption dates or state whether the posted series is complete.

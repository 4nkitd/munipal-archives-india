# Dhamtari birth certificate application points

## Where to apply

For a birth certificate, the district directs applicants to the Lok Sewa Kendra at their tehsil or to the Chhattisgarh e-District portal. For Dhamtari city, the listed centre is at Tehsil Dhamtari, PIN 493773. Kurud, Magarlod and Nagri are other tehsil centres in the same district listing. The service page identifies CHIPS at the Office of the Collector; the municipal office at Itwari Bazar is a separate contact.

## Municipal office enquiries

The [district municipal directory](https://dhamtari.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists Municipal Office, Itwari Bazar, Dhamtari. Office telephone: 07722-2241603. Office email: commissionerdhamtari@gmail.com.

# Formation and jurisdiction of Dhanbad Municipal Corporation

## What was combined in 2006

The corporation cites Urban Development Department notification 101/5334, dated 1 February 2006. Its account lists 32 towns and 258 villages in the formation. The towns included the former Dhanbad Municipality, the notified area committees of Jharia, Sindri, Chhatandh and Katras, and 27 census towns as recorded in the 2001 census.

The same page distinguishes the original plan, which covered about 355.77 square kilometres and used a 2001 census population of 1,333,719, from a snapshot table that lists 275 square kilometres and 55 wards. The page does not supply a legal schedule that reconciles the two area figures. Neither figure should be used to decide if an individual settlement or property falls inside current city limits.

## Corporation office and district distinction

The [district directory](https://dhanbad.nic.in/public-utility/dhanbad-municipal-corporation/) places the corporation office on Luby Circular Road, Dhanbad 826001. The [district's 2026 election index](https://dhanbad.nic.in/nagarpalika-election-2026/) publishes separate records for Dhanbad Nagar Nigam and Chirkunda Nagar Parishad. The corporation does not cover the whole district.

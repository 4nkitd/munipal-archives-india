# Dhanbad municipal election booth lists and wards, 2026

## Ward and booth lookup

Schedule II of Jharkhand Extraordinary Gazette No. 22, dated 27 January 2026, lists Dhanbad Nagar Nigam wards 1 through 55 at serial numbers 144 through 198. It set polling for 23 February 2026. Schedule I lists the mayoral seat as unreserved for that election. That does not describe the ward seats, which have their own reservation entries in Schedule II.

The [Dhanbad district booth index](https://dhanbad.nic.in/ward-wise-booth-list/) lists Prapatra-D and separate links for wards 1 through 55. It marks the entries for wards 8, 23 and 24 as amended. The [district election index](https://dhanbad.nic.in/nagarpalika-election-2026/) separately links a notice amending the names of polling stations in wards 1 and 4. A reader checking the 2026 polling record for these wards should consult both the ward entry and any applicable district notice.

## Which record to use

The district's [ward-wise voter-list index](https://dhanbad.nic.in/ward-wise-voter-list/) also lists 1 through 55, without photos. Chirkunda Nagar Parishad has separate documents and its wards are not part of the corporation's series. The district election page lists Form 3 for reservation and allotment, Form 5 for the election, and district gazette entries for ward councillors (Form 23-K) and mayor and deputy mayor (Form 23-KH). The electoral lists identify wards and polling arrangements, not legally surveyed municipal or ward boundaries.

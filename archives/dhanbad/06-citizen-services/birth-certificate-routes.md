# Dhanbad birth certificate portal and office references

## Portal and office are different references

Dhanbad district's birth certificate page links the [Civil Registration System](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/home) and lists DC Office, Dhanbad 826010 under Location. The [district municipal directory](https://dhanbad.nic.in/public-utility/dhanbad-municipal-corporation/) gives a separate corporation office address, Luby Circular Road, Dhanbad 826001. Use the corporation's office contact above to ask which registration office handles a specific case in the municipal area.

## Limits of the published guidance

The corporation's state-hosted birth and death page links the Civil Registration System but has an internal heading naming Adityapur. The district's Dhanbad-specific listing is the source for the route here. It does not give Dhanbad corporation fee schedules, supporting documents, processing times or procedures for a late registration. No application was submitted.

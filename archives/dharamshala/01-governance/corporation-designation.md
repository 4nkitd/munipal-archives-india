# Dharamshala municipal corporation designation

## State roster

Annexure A lists Dharamshala under Kangra district at serial number 17. The roster is expressly as of 31 March 2026. That is the roster's reference date, not its publication date.

| Field | State roster entry |
| --- | --- |
| Local body type | Municipal corporation |
| Year of existence | 1857 |
| Corporation upgrade date | 5 October 2015 |
| Area | 27.60 km² |
| Population column labelled 2011 | 53,543 |

## District directory

The [Kangra district municipalities directory](https://hpkangra.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists Municipal Corporation Dharamshala and identifies its office by the title Municipal Commissioner. The district directory does not establish the current elected officeholders or ward boundaries.

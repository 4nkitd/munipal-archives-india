# Dharamshala public holiday notice for 7 April 2021

## District notice

| Field | Published detail |
| --- | --- |
| Date in the district document register | 31 March 2021 |
| Public holiday named in the notice title | 7 April 2021 |
| Municipal corporations named | Dharamshala and Palampur |

The [state department's 2021 municipal election index](https://ud.hp.gov.in/page/municipal-election-2021) separately lists an election programme and a ward reservation notification for Dharamshala, Palampur, Solan and Mandi. The holiday notice is historical and does not establish current councillors or ward boundaries.

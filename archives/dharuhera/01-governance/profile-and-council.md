# Municipal Committee Dharuhera profile and elected council

## Municipality and jurisdiction

Dharuhera is a municipal committee in Rewari district, Haryana. It lies on NH-48, the Delhi to Jaipur highway, southwest of IMT Manesar and next to Bhiwadi across the Rajasthan border. The Haryana enterprise register lists Hero MotoCorp's Dharuhera plant on the highway. Bhiwadi is outside the committee's jurisdiction.

Haryana notified the municipal area and constituted the committee on 20 July 2007. The State Election Commission's 2008 polling programme cites the original notification as No. 18/2/2007-3C1. Haryana's Urban Local Bodies department lists a municipal area of 11.51 sq km and a population of 32,819 without a reference year. The committee's filing before the National Green Tribunal gives the 2011 Census population as 30,344. The district directory places the committee office on Sohna Road, Dharuhera, Haryana 123106. The state's property tax portal lists 01274-297849 as the municipal helpline.

## From 17 wards to 18

The December 2020 election covered 17 wards and a separate seat for president. Kanwar Singh, also reported as Kanwar Singh Yadav, won the presidency. The State Election Commission set aside his election in March 2021, and the Punjab and Haryana High Court quashed that order in September 2021. The Commission withdrew the scheduled by-election and re-notified him as president on 1 February 2022, subject to the outcome of its appeal.

The Urban Local Bodies department's final ward delimitation order of 9 December 2025 and seat allotment notification of 24 December 2025, both No. 9/119/2025-4C-II, established the 18-ward arrangement used in 2026. The committee's Area Sabha order of 17 January 2026 also lists Wards 1 through 18. The older count of 17 applies to the 2020 election, not the 2026 committee.

## May 2026 election and leadership

Polling took place on 10 May 2026 and counting on 13 May. Satyanarayan alias Ajay Jangra of the BJP won the directly elected presidency with 9,392 votes. Independent Babulal Lamba received 3,156, a margin of 6,236 votes. Kanwar Singh ran as an independent and received 1,246 votes. Haryana Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 92-2026/Ext. of 12 June 2026 notified the winners. Dainik Bhaskar reported that Jangra and the 18 ward members took their oaths on 6 July. Amar Ujala reported that Ward 8 member Aman Rao was elected vice-president unopposed on 31 August 2026.

All 18 ward winners were elected as independents. Ward 5 was unopposed. In Ward 16, Manisha Saini won 650 votes to Deepa's 647. Dainik Bhaskar reported a challenge to that result and a June interim order allowing Saini to take the oath. This roster records the notified election result and does not assert a final ruling on the challenge.

# Dharuhera wards, villages, sectors and colonies

## Scope and date warning

The Rewari district administration publishes a Dharuhera final voter-list archive with numbered ward documents dated 15 April 2019. This record preserves that historical 17-ward framework. The date in the frontmatter is the voter-list date, not a claim that the boundaries remained unchanged through 2026.

The Haryana Department of Urban Local Bodies now lists a "Final order of wardbandi of Municipal Committee, Dharuhera" dated 10 December 2025 and a ward reservation document dated 29 December 2025. Their schedules have not been transcribed here. A 24 November 2025 local report describes a proposed increase from 17 to 18 wards, including rearrangement of the Sector 6 area. Do not treat the historical 17 entries in `../wards.json` as a certified current electoral map.

No authenticated historical boundary schedule was recovered for assigning each sector, village or colony to a numbered ward. Empty locality arrays mean unverified, not uninhabited. Zone and division fields are null because no such municipal grouping was verified. Current ward identification requires the final order, its attached map and the relevant electoral roll.

## HSVP Sector 4A and Sector 6

HSVP residential sectors are not municipal electoral wards. Sector numbers must not be substituted for ward numbers.

A CPCB environmental compensation report filed before the National Green Tribunal identifies a 5 MLD HSVP sewage treatment plant serving Sector 4A and Sector 6, Parts I and II. It records commissioning on 1 July 2019 and discusses drainage near NH-48 and the Bhiwadi boundary. The report also records HSVP's response concerning green belts in sectors 4A, 6 and 8. These statements establish service geography, not electoral boundaries.

An official Haryana procurement entry, tender reference `26/2020/EE/HSVP/Rewari`, describes maintenance of sewerage in Sector 4A, Sector 6 Parts I and II, and Old and New Housing Board areas. Its organisation chain is HUDA, Division Rewari. This is historical evidence of HSVP infrastructure responsibility. It does not establish the current transfer status of every road, drain or water connection.

## Villages and the industrial estate

The Supreme Court judgment dated 3 April 2025, neutral citation `2025 INSC 441`, supplies a documented distinction between the town and adjoining revenue estates.

| Place | Sourced geographical context | Boundary caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dharuhera | Judgment records municipal notification on 20 July 2007; land involved in the Institutional Sector 5A acquisition | Revenue estate and municipal boundary are different concepts |
| Maheshwari | Named with Dharuhera and Garhi Alawalpur in the Sector 5A acquisition discussion | Whole-village municipal inclusion not established here |
| Garhi Alawalpur | Named in the same institutional-sector acquisition | Spelling variants should be checked against revenue records |
| Malpura | Acquisition for Dharuhera industrial sectors 15, 16 and 17 discussed in the judgment | Industrial planning does not prove ward inclusion |
| Kapriwas | Named with Malpura in the industrial-sector acquisition | No ward number assigned here |
| Ghatal Mahaniawas | Named in quoted geographical evidence near Industrial Estate Dharuhera, Maheshwari and Aakera | Proximity alone does not establish municipal inclusion |

The judgment discusses land on both sides of the Delhi-Jaipur highway, historically NH-8 and now NH-48. Industrial Estate Dharuhera and the planned industrial sectors must also be distinguished from RIICO's Bhiwadi industrial area across the state boundary. A Dharuhera postal address, development-plan label or nearby factory is insufficient evidence of Municipal Committee jurisdiction.

## NH-48 residential townships and colony names

The same Supreme Court judgment records surrounding development including HUDA Sector 4 residential colony, Bajrang Nagar, Parshavnath residential flats, M2K City and Bhagat Singh residential colony. It also mentions Bestech Mall adjoining the relevant land. These names are useful for searching historical land and planning records. The judgment is not a colony regularization certificate or a current project approval register.

NH-48 connects this development corridor, but a project advertised as being in Dharuhera may lie in a neighbouring revenue estate. Match its khasra numbers, approved layout and licence with the municipal-limit notification before assigning a ward. Sector 4 and Sector 4A should remain separate search terms unless the source explicitly equates them.

## Regularized colonies and verification limits

The DULB regularized-colony index publishes batches for 2023 and 2024 and later notifications, including a municipality-wise status document for 836 colonies listed on 13 October 2025. This research did not recover an authenticated Dharuhera-specific schedule with colony names, notification numbers and polygon limits. No colony above is labelled regularized on the strength of its name, street services or appearance in a court judgment.

For a colony status check, obtain the applicable gazette schedule and map from DULB or the Municipal Committee. Match the locality spelling and revenue parcels, then distinguish notification for civic amenities from a DTCP developer licence, building-plan sanction and title verification. The ward register intentionally leaves its colony arrays empty until a ward-specific source supports the assignment.

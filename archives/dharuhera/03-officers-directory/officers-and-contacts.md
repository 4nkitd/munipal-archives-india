## Sanitation enquiries and RTI

The Haryana Right to Service table names the Sanitary Inspector as the designated officer for removal of solid waste from streets, roads, green belts and open spaces in a Municipal Committee. It specifies two working days, with the Secretary as first grievance-redressal authority and District Municipal Commissioner as second authority. Ask the municipal office for the current Dharuhera Sanitary Inspector's name and contact.

The state PIO list identifies the Secretary, Municipal Committee Dharuhera as State Public Information Officer and Sub Division Magistrate, Dharuhera as first appellate authority. These are designation-based entries and do not verify an incumbent's name.

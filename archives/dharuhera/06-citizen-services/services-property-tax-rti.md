# Dharuhera citizen services, property tax, RTI and service guarantees

## Scope and municipal office

This guide combines Dharuhera-specific official contact entries with Haryana-wide service routes applicable to municipal committees. The frontmatter date is the stated cutoff of DULB's consolidated Right to Service list, updated up to 4 November 2025. It is not the publication date of every service or the date of a Dharuhera municipal resolution.

The [Rewari district municipal directory](https://rewari.gov.in/public-utility/municipal-committee-2/) locates Municipal Committee Dharuhera at Sohna Road, Dharuhera, Haryana 123106. Use the municipality holding the property or civil registration record, not merely the municipality nearest the applicant's current address.

## Property tax, Property ID and NDC

The [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) is officially titled the Property Tax Dues Payment and No Dues Certificate portal. It provides new-user registration, NRI or foreign-national registration, application-status checking and a tax calculator identified as following the policy dated 30 July 2026. Select Dharuhera as the urban local body and check the property's recorded owner, use, area, floors, dues and authorization category before paying or seeking correction. Retain the Property ID, application reference and receipt.

The [DULB property-tax instructions index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists separate 2026 notifications for corporations and for councils and committees, both published on 31 July 2026. Dharuhera is a committee, so the corporation notification should not be substituted for the committee schedule. The index also lists self-certification instructions published on 27 March 2024, property-management and split-ID SOPs published on 26 September 2023, and an interest-waiver notification published on 18 May 2026 for arrears from 2010-11 to 2024-25. A listing does not establish that a waiver remains open on the application date. Check the notification's eligibility and deadline before claiming relief.

The [property helpdesk directory](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Contact) lists Dharuhera's deployed official at **8059386394** and a landline entered as **1274297849**. The latter is preserved as published, without adding an unverified dialing prefix.

The schedule links property services to the property portal. It links several other applications to SARAL and sanitation or streetlight complaints to the Haryana municipal citizen mobile app. Keep the acknowledgment and identify the notified service when escalating delay.

The schedule's seven-day water or sewer connection service and two-day water-leakage or sewer-overflow service expressly name Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat and Karnal corporations. They are not Dharuhera committee guarantees and are not represented as such here.

## Birth and death records

The [DULB homepage](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/) identifies four service groups: birth or death certificates, correction of a birth or death record, delayed registration, and inclusion of a child's name in a birth record. The [Rewari district death-certificate service page](https://rewari.gov.in/service/apply-for-death-certificate/) directs residents to [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) and explains registration at the place of occurrence within 21 days.

The Haryana Health Services [official application guide](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31177967c7957072da3dc1db4ceb30e7a/uploads/2023/01/2023012363.pdf) distinguishes urban home events and private-hospital events, for which the municipality is a registrar option, from government-institution events, for which the corresponding government institution may hold the record. Do not assume every event involving a Dharuhera resident is registered by the municipal committee.

On SARAL, select the service matching the record problem. An existing record needing correction is different from an unregistered event needing delayed registration. The application guide describes entering event and year details, selecting the registrar, uploading applicant identity proof, completing the payment step and retaining the acknowledgment. Use the current service checklist for additional evidence, fees and delayed-registration permissions. This archive does not assert an unverified Dharuhera-specific certificate fee or processing guarantee.

## Marriage registration

The DULB homepage's Marriage Registration card links to the [Haryana marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) for applying and tracking an application. Use that official entry point and the current portal checklist for jurisdiction, age and identity evidence, marriage evidence, witnesses, fee and appointment requirements. Save the application reference and any appointment acknowledgment.

This is marriage registration, not permission for a marriage palace or a municipal venue booking. Those are separate processes. No Dharuhera-specific marriage registrar's personal name, local fee schedule or marriage-registration RTS deadline was established from the official sources used here.

## RTI requests and appeals

The [DULB PIO list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList), municipal-committee row 56, names **Secretary, Municipal Committee Dharuhera** as State Public Information Officer and **Sub Division Magistrate, Dharuhera** as First Appellant Authority. These are the designations printed on that list. The page includes older municipal classifications elsewhere, so confirm the current appellate office before dispatch rather than silently replacing its published designation with a guessed one.

For a municipal RTI, identify the records sought, their period, and the branch or subject, such as a property assessment, sanctioned work, colony file, council resolution or complaint action report. Address the request to the State Public Information Officer at the municipal office and retain delivery proof. Ask for records or inspection, not an order to resolve a grievance. RTI and Right to Service appeals are separate routes with different authorities.

The [Dharuhera Acts index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Dharuhera/9181) lists an RTI Act document with a portal date of 11 September 2019 and Right to Service material. That portal date is not the enactment date of the RTI Act. Current application fees, payment mode and statutory exceptions should be checked against the applicable Haryana RTI rules and current official instructions.

## Grievances and assistance

DULB links its [Grievance Redressal System](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for complaints concerning departmental services. Its support number is **1800-8900-929**, available **9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday**, for IT support and grievance registration. The [district SARAL entry](https://rewari.gov.in/service/saral/) lists **1800-2000-023** for SARAL assistance.

For a complaint, record the locality, street or landmark, Property ID where relevant, date, photographs if appropriate, and earlier complaint references. For Bhiwadi-origin polluted water, distinguish municipal pumping or local drain maintenance from industrial discharge enforcement by pollution-control authorities. The associated notices record preserves the official NGT and HSPCB case references. A portal link in this guide confirms an official published route, not a completed end-to-end test of application submission.

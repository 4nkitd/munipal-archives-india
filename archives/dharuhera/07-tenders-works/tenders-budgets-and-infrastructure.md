# Dharuhera tenders, budgets and infrastructure, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and procurement portals

This is a source index and project-status record, not a complete municipal works register. The frontmatter date is the publication date of the latest water-supply tender indexed below. Financial values retain the precision supplied by each source.

The Haryana Engineering Works Portal states that all tenders floated through HEWP are published on the NIC Haryana eTendering website for bidder participation. It also states that such notices appear on HEWP's live-tenders page the next day. Search using the tender ID and reference, then check corrigenda, cancellation or retender notices and award records. A tender estimate is not a payment, expenditure statement or completion certificate. [S1]

Municipal street works, PHED town water and sewer schemes, HSVP infrastructure and Rajasthan's Bhiwadi treatment projects have different authorities. Their costs must not be added together as Dharuhera Municipal Committee's budget.

## Bhiwadi runoff and cross-border waterlogging

The Tribune reported on 15 February 2024 that Rajasthan Chief Secretary Sudhansh Pant directed Bhiwadi Municipal Council to examine decentralised STPs, complete sewer lines and domestic connections in the industrial area, and route contaminated industrial water into the common effluent treatment plant conduit. The report describes water flowing towards lower-lying Dharuhera and affecting NH-48 and residential colonies. These were directions and proposals, not evidence that the works had been completed. [S5]

On 7 January 2026, The Times of India reported decisions from a meeting chaired by Union minister Nitin Gadkari concerning waterlogging on NH-919 at the Bhiwadi-Dharuhera border. [S6]

| Component | Figure reported on 7 January 2026 | Status limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bhiwadi domestic sewage treatment | 34 MLD STP, targeted to operate by March 2026 | A reported target, not verified commissioning |
| Industrial effluent treatment | 6 MLD CETP, reported as recently upgraded to zero liquid discharge standards | The article is not an independent performance audit |
| Permanent drainage proposal | Approximately 6 km drain with an estimated cost of about ₹150 crore | Gadkari directed a comprehensive study; this is not a verified tender award |
| Proposed financing | ₹25 crore each from Rajasthan and Haryana, with the balance from NHAI | Reported financing arrangement, not proof of release or expenditure |

The same report describes routing treated STP water to Sarai Khurd dam for reuse and joint water-quality monitoring before discharge to Masani Barrage. These cross-border proposals are separate from Dharuhera's ward-level drain repairs. No later completion certificate for the proposed drain or commissioning record for the Bhiwadi STP was verified for this entry.

## Dharuhera sewage treatment and upgrade estimates

An HSVP rejoinder hosted by the National Green Tribunal in OA 627/2022, *Prakash Yadav v. State of Haryana*, contains an upgrade estimate and a pollution-control consent. The documents concern the HSVP installation in Dharuhera, not the proposed Bhiwadi STP. [S7]

| Document within the filing | Recorded facts | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annexure C, HSVP estimate correspondence referencing Executive Engineer letter 322404 dated 6 October 2025 | Renovation and upgrade of an existing 15 MLD main pumping station and 5 MLD STP, with proposed tertiary treatment; checked estimate ₹11.95 crore plus 18% GST, total ₹14.11 crore | Recommended for technical and administrative approval. The correspondence explicitly says there was no provision in the 2025-26 budget and funds had been sought through reappropriation. It does not establish sanction or award. |
| Annexure D, HSPCB consent dated 16 January 2026 | Consent number `12074135026REWCTOW52807340` for "STP 5 MLD HUDA, Sec-6, Dharuhera"; validity 16 January to 30 September 2026; domestic effluent 5,000 KL/day | A time-limited regulatory consent. No renewal after 30 September 2026 was verified. |

The consent specifies BOD 10 mg/l, COD 50 mg/l, TSS 20 mg/l and faecal coliform 100 MPN/100 ml as effluent limits. These are permitted limits, not measurements proving continuous compliance. [S7]

## Water supply and sewer-network procurement in 2026

WooTenders indexes PHED tender `2026_HRY_561504_1`, reference `20265B66B127 114C 438B B65D F3A73AF9C80F580PUH`, for augmentation of Dharuhera town's water-supply scheme, water works at Kharkhara and distribution-system laying. Its record gives publication on 19 November 2026, an estimated ₹13.81 crore, a 730-day work period, bid closing on 14 December 2026 at noon and opening at 2 pm. The buyer chain is PHED Panchkula, Rewari Circle, Bawal PHED. These are attributed aggregator particulars; the underlying official DNIT and award were not verified. Do not treat this as completed infrastructure or a municipal budget allocation. [S8]

Amar Ujala reported on 16 November 2026 that PHED had issued an approximately ₹3.93 crore sewer-extension tender for remaining approved areas of Dharuhera. It quoted SDO Jaiprakash saying that some pipelines would be replaced and two main pumping stations constructed. The article did not establish the complete ward list, sewer length or receiving STP. Those details are therefore not supplied here. [S9]

## Municipal budget evidence and gaps

Mera Haryana's report dated 31 October 2025 quotes Dharuhera municipal secretary Sumit stating that the expenditure budget was revised from ₹18 crore to ₹24 crore for development works following a reconvened special meeting. This is a reported council decision. The signed resolution, detailed account heads, state approval and audited expenditure were not verified. The reported amount must not be presented as an audited annual outturn. [S10]

No primary municipal budget schedules for 2024-25, 2025-26 or 2026-27 were verified for this record. The HSVP ₹14.11 crore upgrade estimate, PHED tender estimates and proposed interstate drainage financing are not substitutes for those schedules.

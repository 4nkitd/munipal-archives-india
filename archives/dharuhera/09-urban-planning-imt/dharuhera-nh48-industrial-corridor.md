# Dharuhera development plan and NH-48 industrial corridor

## Plan status and the 2031/2041 distinction

This is a research compilation dated 22 November 2026, not a new development-plan notification. The verified Haryana Gazette instrument is the amendment dated 27 February 2019, notification No. CCP(NCR)/RE(DHA)/FDP-2021/2019/579. It expressly amends the Final Development Plan 2021 AD, Dharuhera, originally notified through No. CCP(NCR)/RE(DHA)/FDP/2007/4496 dated 28 December 2007, and refers to the preceding notification dated 7 December 2016. [1]

The 2019 amendment earmarks 725 hectares for residential use. Residential sectors are to follow the density shown on the drawing, with 20 percent variation on either side, together with applicable housing-policy provisions. Plot coverage, FAR and height depend on the prescribed policy, building code or site zoning plan. These provisions should not be replaced by figures from property advertisements. [1]

A separately notified Dharuhera Final Development Plan 2031 or 2041 was not established by the sources reviewed. Rewari's 2031 planning and the NCR's regional 2041 proposals are different instruments. Neither title should be substituted for Dharuhera's verified 2021 plan.

The NCR Planning Board's December 2021 document is explicitly a **Draft Regional Plan 2041**. Section 3.2.6 records the Gurugram-Manesar-Bawal-Dharuhera-Rewari highway corridor as brought under controlled-area regulation by Haryana. Section 3.4.1.3 proposes highway-corridor and transit-oriented development, requiring state identification, notification and detailed plans. The Board's November 2024 planning-committee agenda still described final regional-plan approval as pending; its January 2025 consultancy document likewise described the plan as being finalised. These dated sources do not establish its final notification as of November 2026. [2][3]

For a parcel, obtain the applicable notified land-use drawing, subsequent amendments, zoning plan and any CLU or colony licence from the competent Haryana authority. A regional corridor proposal does not itself grant development permission.

## NH-48 industrial geography and HSIIDC

NH-48, called NH-8 in older records, connects the Manesar, Dharuhera and Bawal industrial belt. Bilaspur is an intermediate highway node between Manesar and Dharuhera. Bhiwadi adjoins Dharuhera across the Rajasthan border and is a lateral connection to this belt, not a stop beyond Bawal in a single linear NH-48 sequence.

| Node | Industrial or planning relationship | Jurisdiction distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IMT Manesar | HSIIDC industrial township serving the Gurugram-Manesar manufacturing belt | Haryana; not part of Dharuhera municipality |
| Bilaspur | Intermediate highway and proposed RRTS node between Panchgaon and Dharuhera | Haryana; no separate HSIIDC IMT status is asserted here |
| Dharuhera | Established industrial estate and automotive supplier location | Rewari district, Haryana |
| Bawal | HSIIDC IMT with automotive-component and other manufacturing units | Rewari district, Haryana; separate from Dharuhera |
| Bhiwadi | Adjoining Rajasthan industrial area linked through the border road network | Rajasthan authorities, including RIICO, BIDA and the municipal body |

The Haryana Investors' Handbook lists Manesar IMT, Bawal IMT phases and IE Dharuhera as separate estates. Its land-availability and allotment-rate figures relate to FY 2017-18 and are not current prices or inventories. [4]

The Ministry of MSME's Rewari district industrial profile for 2015-16 lists Dharuhera firms including Hero MotoCorp, Omax Autos, Rico Auto, GKN Drivelines and Lumax Industries, alongside numerous Bawal component manufacturers. This supports the historical automotive-supplier relationship, not a claim that every listed plant remains operational today. [5]

The Manesar-Bawal Investment Region is also a separate planning concept. HMRTC's historical Gurugram-Bawal MRTS project page describes a revised DPR submitted on 21 January 2016, with an 82 km first phase divided into Gurugram-Manesar, Manesar-Dharuhera and Dharuhera-MBIR sections. That MRTS proposal must not be confused with NCRTC's Delhi-SNB RRTS or treated as an operating service. [6]

## Proposed Delhi-SNB RRTS stations

SNB means Shahjahanpur-Neemrana-Behror. The official forest-clearance proposal FP/HR/RAIL/120165/2021 records a Delhi-SNB scheme of 106.5 km and 16 stations. Its station list is a dated project configuration, not an opening announcement. [7]

| Sequence | Proposed station in the 2021 application |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 4 | Sarai Kale Khan, INA, Munirka, Aerocity |
| 5 to 9 | Udyog Vihar, Sector 17, Rajiv Chowk, Kherki Daula, Manesar |
| 10 to 12 | Panchgaon, Bilaspur, Dharuhera |
| 13 to 16 | MBIR, Rewari, Bawal, SNB |

NCRTC tender DA/DD/COR-OF/017, with NIT publication dated 9 January 2020, covers design of seven elevated stations, Panchgaon, Bilaspur, Dharuhera, MBIR, Rewari, Bawal and SNB. It also includes a Dharuhera depot, Manesar stabling lines, a 62 km elevated viaduct section and detailed planning of five underground stations. This is evidence of design procurement, not evidence that stations or the depot have been constructed. [8]

Older NCRPB Delhi-Alwar feasibility documents include BTK, meaning Bhiwadi-Tapukara-Khushkhera, in a different station sequence. Do not merge that alignment with the 2021 sixteen-station list. NCRTC's project-details page expressly labels its wider Delhi-Gurugram-SNB-Alwar map and information tentative and subject to final approval. No Dharuhera service commencement date is verified here. [9][10]

## Interstate wastewater and drainage coordination

HSPCB's status report dated 26 July 2024 in OA No. 617/2023, Prakash Yadav versus State of Haryana, describes effluent travelling from Bhiwadi through Dharuhera towards NH-8 and the industrial area. It estimates approximately 12 MLD entering Haryana, based on pumping observations during non-rainy days. The estimate is not a measured industrial-only discharge inventory. [11]

The report records a joint site inspection by HSPCB, HSIIDC and HSVP on 7 March 2024. Inspectors found no wastewater release onto the specified village lands or into the Sahibi River at that inspection. They found no HSVP STP in the Dharuhera industrial area, only a pumping unit that was not working. The report nevertheless states that heavy rain could carry the incoming effluent towards the villages because of the natural gradient. Its annexed HSIIDC letter contains a conflicting inspection-year reference; the main report identifies the inspection as 7 March 2024. [11]

HSPCB records joint monitoring with Rajasthan State Pollution Control Board and repeated correspondence about samples exceeding prescribed limits. Coordination therefore involves both pollution-control boards, Dharuhera and Bhiwadi civic authorities, district administrations and industrial-infrastructure agencies. Municipal pumping is not equivalent to sewage treatment or compliance by upstream dischargers.

| Proceeding or event | Verified record and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| OA No. 124/2015 | Earlier Dharuhera wastewater case; HSPCB records directions to Rajasthan agencies on 12 December 2017 |
| EA No. 42/2019 | Enforcement proceeding; NGT imposed environmental compensation on 29 October 2021 |
| Supreme Court appeals | HSPCB reports a stay dated 22 March 2022 in appeals including Nos. 1722/2022, 1377/2022, 1234/2022 and 1228/2022 |
| Interstate meeting, 30 July 2023 | HSPCB records a Haryana Chief Minister meeting with Alwar and Rewari deputy commissioners concerning incoming polluted effluent |
| OA No. 617/2023, 4 December 2023 | NGT required HSPCB's Member Secretary to consider the grievance and take lawful remedial measures |
| MA No. 67/2025, 22 May 2025 | Published order text records that the appeals remained pending and the NGT order stayed; the MA was disposed of with the HSPCB report taken on record |

The May 2025 disposal is not a finding that drainage was repaired or pollution stopped. The appeal position in that order is dated 22 May 2025, not an independently checked November 2026 Supreme Court docket status. [11][12]

For a drainage complaint or industrial-site assessment, distinguish stormwater, domestic sewage and trade effluent. Record the location, receiving drain, inspection date, sample results, pumping arrangements and responsible asset owner. The border location requires a shared drainage solution; neither a highway barrier nor a proposed rail station establishes that wastewater disposal has been resolved.

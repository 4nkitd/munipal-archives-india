# Dhule corporation formation and the 2018 boundary expansion

## Establishment and extent

Dhule Municipal Corporation dates its establishment to **30 June 2003**. The [Dhule district administration](https://dhule.gov.in/en/muncipal-corporation/) lists it separately as the city corporation. The municipal history names water supply, sanitation, health, roads, street lights, building permissions, and birth and death registration among its functions.

The corporation says it added 11 adjoining villages on **5 January 2018**: Valvadi (वळवाडी), Bhokar (भोकर), Mahindale (महिंदळे), Nakane (नकाणे), Avdhan (अवधान), Chitod (चितोड), Navale (नवाळे), Tarwade (तरवडे), Balapur (बाळापूर), Morane (मोराणे) and Pimpri (पिंपरी). This is the corporation's account of the change, not the original boundary order or a survey-number schedule.

| Measure | Before expansion | Added footprint | Expanded total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Area (square kilometres) | 46.44 | 54.62 | 101.06 |
| Population figures reported in the municipal account | 375,514 | 70,580 | 446,094 |

The page identifies the first population figure as the 2011 Census count and separately gives an undated estimate of about 515,000. The expanded total is the sum reported for the old area and added villages; it is not a later census count. The village list does not show which 2025 electoral ward contains any address.

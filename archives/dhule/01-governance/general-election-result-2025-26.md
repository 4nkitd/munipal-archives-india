# Dhule municipal general election result, 2025–26

## State Election Commission return

Row 12 of Maharashtra's final party-wise return records **19 electoral wards**, **74 councillor seats**, and **320 contesting candidates** for Dhule. The commission reports results for all 74 seats. Its PDF identifies the election cycle but does not print a publication date.

| SEC party or reporting category | Seats won |
| --- | ---: |
| Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) | 50 |
| Nationalist Congress Party (NCP) | 8 |
| Shiv Sena (SS) | 5 |
| Parties registered with the SEC and recognized as a state party outside Maharashtra | 10 |
| Independent | 1 |
| Total | 74 |

The return does not identify the party or parties behind the 10-seat category in the Dhule row. The category is distinct from the SEC's column for unrecognized registered parties. This is an aggregate seat result, not a ward-by-ward result or a list of names. The [municipal election department](https://dhulecorporation.org/dmc/election-department) publishes the delimitation and reservation material separately; neither set of documents gives a later council composition after any changes in membership.

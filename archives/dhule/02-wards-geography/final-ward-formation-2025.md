# Dhule final electoral ward formation for the 2025–26 general election

## Final notification and scope

The [municipal election department](https://dhulecorporation.org/dmc/election-department) lists the final Marathi Annexure 14 and English Annexure 15 separately from the draft and from the reservation publications. The English annexure bears **7 October 2025** and cites Section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949. The Dhule notice on the first pages of the [2 December 2025 Maharashtra Government Gazette, Nashik divisional supplement, extraordinary issue 25](https://malegaoncorporation.org/file/electiongazette25.pdf#page=1) identifies the final ward notification published as No. रानिआ/मनपा-2025/प्र.क्र.20/का-5/2025 on **9 October 2025**. The archive date follows that gazette reference, not the date printed on the English annexure.

| Electoral wards | Ward count | Councillors per ward | Seats |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1–17 | 17 | 4 | 68 |
| 18–19 | 2 | 3 | 6 |
| Total | 19 | | 74 |

Issue 25 confirms the seat split by listing A, B and C for wards 18 and 19, and A through D for the other wards. The letters label seats within a ward, not additional geographic wards.

## Using the schedule

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The schedule gives each ward's population, seat count and boundaries by direction. For example, Ward 1 lists a population of 23,237, including 1,551 Scheduled Caste and 1,655 Scheduled Tribe residents, and four seats. Its northern description references Nagaon Gut Nos. 335 and 312 and Agra Road. Use the full directional lines in the annexure for an address; a landmark excerpt does not define a boundary. The later reservation notice assigns categories to seats and does not replace the boundary schedule.

# Dhule regional service offices and the wards they cover

## Office coverage

The corporation publishes the following ward-to-office assignments. Its service description includes sanitation, encroachment action, permits, birth and death registration, and marriage registration. The population figures are as listed, without a stated reference date on that page.

| Regional office | Location | Electoral wards served | Listed population |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | Navrang water tank building, Devpur | 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 | 134,108 |
| 2 | Municipal School No. 2 building, near the Subhash statue | 3, 12, 13, 15 | 102,994 |
| 3 | Municipal School No. 5 building, near Manohar Talkies, Agra Road | 11, 14, 16, 18, 19 | 110,387 |
| 4 | Staff quarters at the Ashok Nagar water tank, Lenin Chowk | 8, 9, 10, 17 | 99,605 |

The four offices are service areas. Their numbers are not the electoral ward numbers, and their locations do not define ward boundaries. For example, a resident in electoral Ward 3 is assigned to regional office 2. The office figures sum to 447,094, which is 1,000 more than the expanded-footprint total on the [municipal history page](https://dhulecorporation.org/dmc/about-corporation). The two pages do not explain the difference.

## Headquarters and complaints

The [municipal contact page](https://dhulecorporation.org/contact) gives the headquarters address as Sakri Road, Lalbag, Dhule 424001. It lists 02562-288301, 02562-288310 and 02562-288381 as office lines, 1800-233-3010 as a toll-free number, and +91-7263006699 as the complaint helpline. It also lists 02562-288381 for smart street-light complaints. These are published office and service lines, not the individual contacts shown in the ward staffing table.

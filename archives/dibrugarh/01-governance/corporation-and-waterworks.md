# Dibrugarh corporation formation and waterworks handover

## From board to corporation

Dibrugarh Municipality dates to 1873. DMC records 7 March 2024 as the announcement of the corporation’s formation and the dissolution of the board. Its office is on A.T. Road beside DTP Dyke, Dibrugarh 786001.

## Water supply transferred to DMC

The corporation reports that the AMRUT 1.0 water supply project was handed over to DMC on 20 November 2025. It gives an investment of ₹193 crore, a 150 km distribution network and 7,676 household connections. The connection figure describes the project at handover, not the number of properties in the municipal district.

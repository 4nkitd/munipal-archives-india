# Dibrugarh Municipal Board jurisdiction before corporation status

## Former board area

The municipal history describes the jurisdiction of Dibrugarh Municipal Board as 15.50 km² divided into 22 wards. This figure describes the predecessor body. It is not a notified area or ward count for the successor corporation.

## Finding a ward representative

The [corporation directory](https://dmcdibrugarh.in/councillors) lists Goutam Dutta for ward 1, Darshan Das for ward 2, Ujjal Phukan for ward 11 and Saikat Patra for ward 14. The directory omits ward 3; that omission does not establish a vacancy. The city also links a [town map with ward boundaries](https://dmcdibrugarh.in/ulb-map). The complaint form's ward selector is not a delimitation order.

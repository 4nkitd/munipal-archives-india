# Filing and tracking a Dibrugarh municipal complaint

## Filing a complaint

Select the nature of the complaint and then the subtype. The list distinguishes drainage from storm water drainage and water bodies from water supply. Other choices include footpaths, markets, public buildings, roads and toilets. Enter complainant details and specify the location and ward.

## Evidence and follow-up

The form accepts up to three files in JPG, JPEG or PNG format. Each file must be no larger than 3 MB. Keep the reference ID: the separate [Track Your Complaint form](https://dmcdibrugarh.in/track-my-complaint) asks for that ID. For correspondence, DMC lists municipalitydmb@gmail.com at its A.T. Road office.

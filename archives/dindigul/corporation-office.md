# Dindigul Municipal Corporation office

## Office details

The Dindigul District public-utilities entry places the office at 15/9, Trichy Road, Dindigul 624001. It lists the landline `0451-2422019` and office mailbox `commr.dindigul@tn.gov.in`. The district's [Commissioner directory entry](https://dindigul.nic.in/directory/commissioner-dindigul-corporation/) repeats both contacts under the designation rather than the name of an incumbent.

The public-utilities entry links to the [state-hosted Dindigul corporation website](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/dindigul/), which timed out during verification. The address and contacts above come from the district pages, not from the unavailable site.

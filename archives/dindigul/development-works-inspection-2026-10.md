# Dindigul district press release on municipal works, October 2026

## Inspection and project estimates

The district page was published on 13 October 2026. The linked [Tamil press release 23/2026](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3f74909ace68e51891440e4da0b65a70c/uploads/2026/10/17918892231539.pdf) is dated 9 October 2026, the date of the inspection. The page reports an estimated ₹130.18 crore for the development works under review.

| Selected work in the press release | Estimate |
| --- | ---: |
| Digital library building on Market Road | ₹2.70 crore |
| Modern fish market | ₹1.00 crore |
| New sewage treatment plant on corporation-owned land | ₹47.86 crore |
| Renovation of the existing sewage treatment plant | ₹1.50 crore |
| New municipal bus station | ₹74.89 crore |

The release also describes drainage desilting and construction of a sewage pumping station at Burma Colony. It reports estimates, not payments, contract awards or completion certificates.

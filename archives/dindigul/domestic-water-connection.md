# Domestic water connection procedure for Dindigul applicants

## Application and work order

The state municipal portal publishes this procedure for corporations and municipalities. Its domestic connection guide tells applicants to clear pending property tax on the building and submit Form A with the registration fee and supporting documents to the municipal commissioner. The form includes a line for the caution deposit and lists a copy of the property tax payment receipt through the previous half-year as an enclosure.

The guide says the office gives notice of the caution deposit and estimated cost within three days of receiving the application. It tells the applicant to pay these amounts within two days and obtain the work order before starting work. The guide cites G.O. No. 97 of 12 July 2007 for a seven-day connection provision based on registration seniority.

## Local figures

The guide is statewide. It does not establish the current deposit or tariff for a Dindigul address, or independently confirm the cited timetable is still in force. For the estimate and current requirements, use the [Dindigul corporation office](corporation-office.md) or the application route listed under New Water Supply Connection in [Urban ePay Help](https://tnurbanepay.tn.gov.in/WithOutLogin_Help.aspx).

# Dindigul Municipal Corporation ward election results, 2022

## Ward coverage and reservation

The Tamil Nadu State Election Commission's Dindigul corporation return lists wards 1 through 48. Counting the labels printed next to the ward numbers gives 22 general, 22 general (women), two Scheduled Caste (general), and two Scheduled Caste (women) wards for this election. The district's municipal council and town panchayat returns are separate files.

## Poll totals as reported

| Ward | Electors | Votes polled | Turnout | Valid votes | Invalid votes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 3,822 | 2,820 | 73.78% | 2,820 | 0 |
| 2 | 3,798 | 2,329 | 61.32% | 2,329 | 0 |

The PDF includes candidate-level returns. The commission says the applicable entries in Forms 23, 25 and 26 and the result declared in Form 27 by the Returning Officer are final. This return does not identify ward boundaries or establish who holds office today. The year in the title identifies the election, not a publication date.

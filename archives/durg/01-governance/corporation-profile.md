# Municipal Corporation Durg profile

## Corporation and jurisdiction

The Chhattisgarh State Election Commission lists Durg as a municipal corporation in Durg district and records its formation date as 1 November 1981. The [Durg district urban local body register](https://durg.gov.in/en/urban-local-body/) lists 60 wards for Durg and separate entries for Bhilai, Bhilai-Charoda and Risali. The name of the municipal corporation does not designate the whole district.

## Office

The [district municipal directory](https://durg.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists the office opposite the Labour Court on Gaurav Path, Durg, with phone 0788-2322148 and email `durgmc@gmail.com`. The [corporation's own contact page](https://municipalcorporationdurg.in/contact_us.html) gives the same address and phone and lists `durgmc2017@gmail.com` as its office email.

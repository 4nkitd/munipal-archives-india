# Durg ward scope in the 2025 municipal election

## Where to find Durg in the results

PDF pages 37–38 of the Chhattisgarh State Election Commission's elected-councillor list contain the Durg Municipal Corporation block. It runs from serial 2124 to 2183, corresponding to municipal wards 1–60. The table gives the district and local body, ward number and name, elected councillor and party. Selected names locate the relevant rows:

| 59 | Katulbod East |
| 60 | Katulbod West |

## Limits of the list

This statewide election result shows the ward labels used in the 2025 Durg corporation election. It is not a boundary schedule, a directory of current councillors or a record for all municipalities in Durg district. The [district urban local body register](https://durg.gov.in/en/urban-local-body/) independently lists Durg as a 60-ward corporation. The result PDF and its index identify the election year but do not establish an exact publication date.

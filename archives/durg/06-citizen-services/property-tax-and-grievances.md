# Durg property-tax records and municipal complaints

## Property tax

The corporation's property-tax page links files for wards 1, 2, 4, 10, 12, 13, 14, 30 and 35. There are two links for ward 13. This is a partial document index, not a tax rate schedule or an indication that only these wards pay tax. The [district property-tax page](https://durg.gov.in/en/service/property-tax/) also points residents to the state municipal payment route. The archive does not certify that the older payment link still processes transactions.

## Complaint form

The [municipal complaint form](https://municipalcorporationdurg.in/shikayat.html) displays fields for the property index number, address, contact numbers, ward, department, email and complaint description. The ward selector spans 1–60. The form lists Water Supply, Public Works and Market among its routing options. No complaint was submitted during verification. The [municipal contact page](https://municipalcorporationdurg.in/contact_us.html) provides the office channel if the form cannot be used.

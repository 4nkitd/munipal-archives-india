# Durg tender 166913 for an old-age home and DDRC building in Pulgaon

## Tender terms

Municipal Corporation Durg issued NIT 1246/PWD/NND/2025 on 9 April 2025. The second call sought construction of an old-age home and DDRC building in Pulgaon. The notice identifies the locality but does not give a ward number in the tender description.

| Item | Published requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Probable contract amount | ₹4,69,18,000 |
| Earnest money deposit | ₹2,35,000 |
| Contractor class | B and above, Chhattisgarh PWD |
| Completion period | 12 months excluding the rainy season |
| Online bid deadline | 24 April 2025, 17:30 |
| Physical EMD envelope deadline | 25 April 2025, 17:00 |

## Submission and status

Bidders had to enroll in the [Chhattisgarh e-Procurement portal](https://eproc.cgstate.gov.in/). The notice directed physical documents to the Executive Engineer, Municipal Corporation, opposite the Labour Court, Gaurav Path, Durg. The published estimate and deadlines are historical procurement terms, not evidence of an award, final spending or completion.

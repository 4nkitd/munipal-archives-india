# Durg AMRUT 2.0 water and sewerage project costs

## Project entries

| Project | Cost | Portal milestone when checked |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Durg 24x7 Water Supply Project | ₹11.50 crore | DPR approved |
| Durg Sewage Master Plan | ₹134.50 crore | Contract awarded |

The national AMRUT 2.0 approved-project list assigns these entries to Durg. It does not give ward coverage, works completion dates or spending to date. "DPR" means detailed project report; approval of a DPR is not a service launch. Likewise, a contract award is not proof of completion. The two project costs total ₹146 crore and are not the corporation's annual budget.

# Street-light distribution works in Durgapur wards 28 and 29

## Scheme and bid conditions

Memo DMC/PW/E/266 of 30 October 2026 identifies the fourth call as WBDMC/PW/ELEC/NIT-11/26-27. It covers extension of the low-tension overhead distribution conductor and pole-mounted street-light panel box at ITI Gosai Tala Basti and Sramik Pally. The work is listed jointly under wards 28 and 29; the schedule does not assign each locality to a separate ward.

| Procurement item | Published terms |
|---|---|
| Estimate | ₹1,41,679 |
| Earnest money | ₹2,834 |
| Completion | 30 days from issue of work order |
| Online publication and bid start | 3 November 2026; bidding from 6:55 pm |
| Submission close | 14 November 2026, 5 pm |
| Technical opening | 16 November 2026, after 5 pm |

Technical and financial bids were to be submitted together with digital signatures. The evaluation committee would consider the financial offer only after technical qualification. The notice called for an updated trade licence, electrical contractor and supervisory licences, PAN, GSTIN and professional-tax payment receipt. The schedule is a solicitation, not a completion certificate.

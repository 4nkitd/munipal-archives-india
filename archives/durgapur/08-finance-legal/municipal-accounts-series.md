# Durgapur budgets, audited statements and internal audit reports

## Financial records by series

The corporation publishes three tables on its Budgetary and Financial page. Fiscal years identify the period covered, not the date of upload. The index itself has no publication date.

| Series as listed | Financial years on the page |
|---|---|
| Budget expenditure | 2013–14, 2014–15 |
| Budget | 2016–17, 2017–18, 2018–19, 2019–20 |
| Audited annual financial statement (AFS) | 2010–11, 2011–12, 2013–14, 2014–15, 2015–16 |
| Internal audit report | 2015–16, 2016–17, 2017–18 |
| Unaudited income and expenditure | 2018–19 |

## Choosing a document

For 2015–16 the index supplies both an internal audit report and an audited AFS. For 2018–19 it lists a budget and an **unaudited** income and expenditure document. These are different stages of financial reporting; an estimate should not be cited as audited expenditure. The last budget listed here is [Budget 2019–20](https://durgapurmunicipalcorporation.org/pdf/BUDGET%202019-20.pdf). The series has gaps, so an absent year is not evidence that DMC did not prepare accounts.

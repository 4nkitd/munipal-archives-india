# Eluru Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## City and district

The [CDMA city profile data](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/89) identifies Eluru Municipal Corporation in Eluru district, Andhra Pradesh. It gives 2005 as its establishment year and reports an area of 62.09 square kilometres and a population of 271,651. The profile does not date that population figure. The [district profile](https://eluru.ap.gov.in/about-district/) separately says that Eluru district came into existence in 2022 with its headquarters at Eluru. The district and corporation are different jurisdictions.

The [municipal contact data](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbcontacts/89) lists the office at 22c-11-28, Chunduri Vari Choultry Street, Main Bazar, Agraharam, Canal Road, Paidichintapadu, Eluru 534001, with email `elurumc@gmail.com` and contact line `08812-232101`. The [district municipality directory](https://eluru.ap.gov.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists a separate corporation landline, `08812-230026`.

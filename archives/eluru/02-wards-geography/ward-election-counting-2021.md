# Eluru ward election counting notification, July 2021

## Counting timetable

The State Election Commission's Notification No. 578/SEC-F1/2021-5, issued on 22 July 2021, directed that counting for Eluru's ward-member election start at 8 am on 25 July and that results be declared immediately after counting. Voting had taken place on 10 March 2021. The notification says that counting originally scheduled for 14 March did not go ahead under the High Court's interim directions and that the ballot boxes were kept in strong rooms.

The notice gave candidates until 5 pm on 24 July to submit appointment or revocation notices for counting agents to the returning officer. The accompanying Order No. 578/SEC-F1/2021-6 set COVID-19 precautions for counting centres. The document names the West Godavari district election authority as a recipient in 2021.

## Ward count in the later city profile

The [CDMA city profile](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/89) for the corporation in present-day Eluru district lists 62 election wards, seven revenue wards and 79 ward secretariats as separate categories. The 2021 notification does not enumerate divisions or supply their boundaries, and the later CDMA figures do not establish the number of divisions in the 2021 poll.

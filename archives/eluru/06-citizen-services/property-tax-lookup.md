# Eluru property tax lookup and assessment services

## Look up dues

Eluru's [Know Your Dues](https://eluru.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/citizen/search/search-searchForm.action) page says that a property can be found using any one of four fields: Assessment Number, Old Assessment Number, Owner Name or Door Number. Use the search for dues on an existing assessment. The [property tax calculator](https://eluru.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/calculatepropertytax) is a separate link and does not look up assessed dues.

## Apply or find proceedings

The corporation lists [Self Assessment](https://eluru.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/create/createProperty-newForm.action?applicationSource=online), revisions, mutation and vacancy remission as separate services. Its [proceedings search](https://eluru.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/reports/citizen-searchnotices.action) is also separate from the dues form. The [district municipality directory](https://eluru.ap.gov.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) gives the corporation's office line as `08812-230026`.

# Erode corporation formation and municipal jurisdiction

## Formation and scale

The corporation's homepage dates the municipality to **1871**, its elevation to Special Grade to **1980**, and its upgrade to corporation status to **1 January 2008**. It reports an area of **109.52 sq km**, **60 electoral wards**, and **15 sanitary divisions**. Sanitary divisions and electoral wards count different units. The homepage gives **498,121** as the *provisional* 2011 population; the archive preserves that label rather than treating the figure as a final census result or a current estimate.

## Which local body?

The [district local bodies register](https://erode.nic.in/about-district/administrative-setup/local-bodies-administration/) lists Erode as the district's one corporation. It lists Bhavani, Gobichettipalayam, Satyamangalam, Punjai Puliampatty and Perundurai separately as municipalities. Those five bodies are outside the scope of this corporation profile. For the boundary shown by the corporation, use the [ward delimitation map entry](https://erode.nic.in/document/erode-corporation-map/), rather than the district list or the historical locality prose on the homepage.

The homepage locates the main corporation office at New No. 246, Old No. 894, Brough Road, Erode 638001.

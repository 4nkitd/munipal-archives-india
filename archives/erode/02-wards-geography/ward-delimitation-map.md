# Erode corporation ward delimitation map

## Reading the map

The district document register lists "Erode Corporation Map" on **3 December 2020**. The [map PDF](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3bca82e41ee7b0833588399b1fcd177c7/uploads/2020/12/2020120380.pdf) identifies itself as the Erode City Municipal Corporation Ward Delimitation Map. It states **60 wards**, **109.52 sq km**, and a scale of **1:20,000**. Its legend shows the corporation boundary in green, ward boundaries in red, and the corporation office as a green dot. Ward numbers appear in blue circles. The map shows the Cauvery beside the eastern edge and labels Namakkal District across the river.

## What the date establishes

The date is the district register's date for the map, not an effective date for a delimitation order. The corporation also has a [Ward Map page](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/erode/ward-map/) with a separate image link. Use the map to locate a ward generally. Neither this index nor the map alone establishes the current legal boundary of a specific property.

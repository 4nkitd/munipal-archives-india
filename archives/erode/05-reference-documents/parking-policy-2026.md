# Erode parking policy, strategy and action plan 2026

## Planning and street design

The corporation's 2026 plan distinguishes changes to statutory planning documents from street-level parking management. It calls for area-level plans of about **3 to 5 sq km**, drawn with reference to land use, road hierarchy, the public transport network and parking demand. Each street stretch between intersections may be designated paid, free or no parking. Where on-street parking is provided, the policy uses a **12 m minimum right of way** and calls for rules to be set for each 100 m segment. It discourages marking spaces on designated bus routes unless the design can preserve bus movement.

## Pricing, enforcement and revenue

The pricing section sets a target of **15% vacant on-street spaces** at any given time. The reinvestment section calls for parking revenue, including fines, to go to an escrow account or Urban Transport Fund and for the surplus after operating costs to support local improvements such as footpaths, cycle facilities, public spaces or public transport. The implementation chapter sets out a pilot before wider rollout. The policy's publication does not establish where parking charges have started or what a particular street currently costs.

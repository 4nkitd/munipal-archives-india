# Faridabad city profile and municipal formation

## Formation and municipal figures

Faridabad was Haryana's first municipal corporation. Section 3(1) of the [Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/2d98f87d-c6f6-44c2-920e-8fb13fede849.pdf) deems the Municipal Corporation of Faridabad declared with effect from 31 May 1994 for the area in the Act's First Schedule. The Act received presidential assent on 20 October 1994 and was first published in the Haryana Government Gazette on 22 October 1994. The [archived MCF city profile](https://web.archive.org/web/20150927074038/https://www.mcfbd.com/) identifies the Faridabad Complex Administration as the corporation's predecessor.

The Haryana Department of Urban Local Bodies [municipal figures table](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists the corporation's area as **285.89 square kilometres** and its population as **19,53,124**. The table does not give a population reference year. These are municipal figures, not a count for the whole Faridabad district. The table's separate figure of 7,41,883 counts properties, not households.

## Wards and zones

The [final ward delimitation notice of 11 January 2024](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/b96e6474-bde2-4ae1-bdc0-071954236882.pdf), Gazette No. 8-2024/Ext., defines wards 1 through 46. The previous layout had [40 wards](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/6-new-wards-added-to-faridabad-mc-581960/). The [State Election Commission's 2025 results notification](https://secharyana.gov.in/result-notification-of-municipalities-2025/) records the election under the new layout.

The archived MCF profile describes three administrative zones: NIT, Old Faridabad and Ballabgarh. That is the historical arrangement, not the full list in MCF's [11 November 2025 National Green Tribunal filing](https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/Reply%20by%20MC,%20Faridabad%20in%20MA%20No.%20122%20of%202025%20IN%20OA%20No.%2034%20of%202025%20%28Sunil%20Sharma%20Vs.%20State%20of%20Haryana%20%26%20Ors.%29.pdf). The filing includes reports for Ballabgarh, Greater Faridabad, Old Faridabad, and a combined report for NIT and Badkhal that names them as separate zones. Ward numbers identify electoral constituencies and should not be read as zone numbers.

## Headquarters and contacts

MCF's head office is at **B.K. Chowk, NIT, Faridabad, Haryana 121001**. The [district municipal office listing](https://faridabad.nic.in/public-utility/municipal-corporation-faridabad-2/) gives the main office number as **0129-2416464**. Haryana Urban Local Bodies lists **0129-2415549** and **0129-2411664** for MCF IT assistance. Its indexed officers directory lists the Commissioner's email as **cmc-faridabad@ulbharyana.gov.in** and the municipal toll-free number as **1800-419-0219**, also shown on the archived MCF homepage. The number **1800-180-1817** appears for Gurugram in the [state property portal's helpdesk directory](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Contact) and on the [Manesar contact page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Manesar/10669), not as an MCF line in those sources.

The [Haryana Urban Local Bodies portal](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Faridabad) lists `mcfaridabad.in` as the corporation's website. The standalone site returned an error when checked; use the state portal or [district MCF page](https://faridabad.nic.in/municipal-corporation-faridabad/) for government information. A [2019 municipal directory](https://amdaindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Details-of-Municipal-Corporation-in-India-2019.pdf) lists `mcfbd.org`, but it no longer serves as the corporation's website.

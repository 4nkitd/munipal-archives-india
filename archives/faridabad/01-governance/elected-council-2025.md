# Faridabad Municipal Corporation: March 2025 elected council

## Mayor and ward results

Faridabad voted on 2 March 2025. On 12 March, Praveen Joshi (BJP) won the mayoral election with 416,927 votes against Congress candidate Lata Rani's 100,075, a margin of 316,852. The State Election Commission spells the mayor's name **Parveen Joshi** in its [20 March result notification, No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/202507081506973353.pdf).

Of the 46 ward seats, BJP won **39**, Congress won **1** (ward 20), and independents won **6** (wards 24, 28, 39, 40, 42 and 43). The mayor was elected separately and is excluded from these totals.

# Mayor and leadership of Municipal Corporation Faridabad

## Elected mayor

Faridabad voters directly elected Praveen Joshi of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) on 2 March 2025. The results were declared on 12 March. Joshi received 4,16,927 votes and the runner-up, Lata Rani of the Indian National Congress, received 1,00,075. Joshi's margin of 3,16,852 votes was reported as a record for an Indian mayoral election at the time. In the separate contests for the corporation's 46 ward seats, the BJP won 39, independents won six and Congress won one.

The [State Election Commission Haryana gazette notification SEC/1ME/2025/4272, dated 20 March 2025](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/20250704337894322.pdf), records her as **Parveen Joshi**. [Akashvani's results report](https://newsonair.gov.in/bjp-sweeps-haryana-local-bodies-elections-by-winning-9-of-10-mayoral-posts/) uses **Praveen Joshi**. [NDTV India](https://ndtv.in/haryana/mayor-election-record-bjp-candidate-praveen-batra-joshi-won-by-3-16-lakh-votes-in-faridabad-7906857) uses **Praveen Batra Joshi** and reports the record margin. [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/bjps-parveen-joshi-wins-faridabad-mayor-poll-by-over-3-lakh-votes/articleshow/118948540.cms) gives the full vote totals and ward-seat split.

Joshi [assumed office on 31 March 2025](https://impressivetimes.com/ncr/news-4304/). [Dainik Tribune](https://www.dainiktribuneonline.com/news/gurugram/faridabad-municipal-corporations-new-website-launched-citizens-will-get-all-the-services-on-a-single-platform/) reported that she and Commissioner Yashendra Singh attended the launch of the corporation's new website on 2 November 2026. The Senior Deputy Mayor and Deputy Mayor posts were [reported vacant on 26 October 2026](https://www.livehindustan.com/ncr/faridabad/story-faridabad-municipal-corporation-bjp-shortlists-names-for-senior-deputy-mayor-and-deputy-mayor-201793037062468.html).

## Municipal commissioner

The mayor is elected. The municipal commissioner is appointed by the state and heads the corporation's administration. The [Faridabad district administrative directory](https://faridabad.nic.in/department/administrative-officers/) lists **Yashendra Singh, IAS**, as Municipal Commissioner. It gives the office address as MCF Office, near B.K. Chowk, Faridabad, and the telephone number as **0129-2416464**. This is separate from the offices of the Deputy Commissioner and the Divisional Commissioner.

| Officer | Dated record |
| --- | --- |
| A. Mona Sreenivas, IAS | Held the municipal commissioner's charge in early 2025. The [Haryana Personnel Department order of 5 May 2025](https://csharyana.gov.in/WriteReadData/Transfers-and-Promotions/Services-II/15193.pdf) relieved her of that charge. |
| Dhirendra Khadgata, IAS | Posted as Municipal Commissioner in the 5 May 2025 order. [The Impressive Times](https://impressivetimes.com/ncr/news-4641/) reported that he took charge on 7 May. He remained in post into 2026 and was transferred in August. |

# Faridabad wardbandi: boundaries of the 46 municipal wards

## Which boundary schedule applies?

Haryana's Urban Local Bodies Department published notification **6/73/2023-5CI** on **11 January 2024**, under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994. The final schedule refers to the draft dated 7 November 2023. The [state notification register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/WardBandiNewNotification) lists the final PDF under 23 April 2024; that is the register date, not the date printed in the gazette. The increase from 40 to 46 wards followed the inclusion of 24 villages in 2021-22, according to [The Tribune, 17 January 2024](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/6-new-wards-added-to-faridabad-mc-581960/).

The [ward index](../wards.json) has one entry for each numbered paragraph of the gazette. Its locality fields index places in or along a ward; a sector dividing road, village revenue line or colony wall can occur in two records. An empty list means no locality of that type was indexed. For a house on a shared boundary, check the direction and side of the road in the PDF, then look for the address in the district's [final and supplementary voter lists](https://faridabad.nic.in/municipal-corporation-faridabad-election/).

## Finding a ward by landmark

| Area | Wards to compare | How to distinguish them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Industrial Town (NIT) | 3, 12, 13 | Ward 3 goes east from Hardware Chowk towards Bata station. Ward 13 reaches Hardware Chowk from below Bata flyover, then turns north. Ward 12 follows the northern wall of CISF Colony from the NIT 1 and 2 junction. |
| Old Faridabad Main Bazar | 31, 32 | Both routes start where the bazar road meets the Sector 28/19 dividing road. Ward 31 goes south towards Sheetla Mata Temple; Ward 32 goes east towards Rasoi Dhaba. |
| Ballabgarh | 40, 41, 42, 43 | Ward 40 follows the canals from Sector 3. Wards 41 and 42 start at Ambedkar Chowk but head in opposite directions: towards the highway and railway station in 41, towards the girls school on Tigaon Road in 42. Ward 43 starts at the Sector 3 disposal works and reaches the IMT bypass bridge. |

## Greater Faridabad and the Neharpar villages

[Amar Ujala's account of the final schedule](https://www.amarujala.com/delhi-ncr/faridabad/ward-closure-fixed-in-faridabad-6-new-wards-created-faridabad-news-c-26-1-gnd1001-19426-2024-01-15) identifies wards 34, 38 and 44 with the village expansion. The gazette describes them by different stretches of the municipal limit:

| Ward | Gazette landmarks |
| --- | --- |
| 34 | The Tilpat shooting range wall, the well at Bhupani and the embankment at Mahavatpur lead to the eastern limit. The return route follows the Kheri Kalan revenue boundary and passes gate 4 of Amrita Hospital. |
| 38 | From BPTP bridge on the east bank of the Agra Canal, the line passes through Bhataula and reaches the BPL flats at Sapera Basti in Sector 76. It goes through Mirzapur and returns to the canal via the Sector 75/76 Resort Chowk. |
| 44 | The line crosses the Agra Canal towards Bairagi Chowk, touches the Lal Dora at Mirzapur and reaches the municipal perimeter. The Chandawali-Bukharpur and Malerna-Jajru revenue lines are boundary markers, not declarations that both villages of each pair lie wholly in Ward 44. |

Other wards also reach across the canal. Ward 35, for example, follows the Budhena revenue line to BPTP Park Elite Floors in Sector 85. A Greater Faridabad postal address alone does not determine a ward.

## What the zone and division fields establish

The gazette defines electoral wards. It does not assign all 46 to administrative zones. The index records Ballabgarh for the Ward 1 site at Ranhera Khera because the [school works tender](https://tender.zoltwic.com/t/740abe0f-7d79-43c4-8272-0f133c726a36) explicitly says “Ballabgarh Zone, Ward No. 02 new ward 01”. A [2026 Sector 25 works listing](https://asiantender.com/location/haryana/faridabad-tenders) identifies the Ward 2 site as NIT. These are site-level references. Other zone entries read “Not verified”.

Division IV entries refer specifically to [MCF's February 2026 waste-lifting contract](https://tender.zoltwic.com/t/2830e216-60c3-4563-ac27-769ad77b7f49), which names wards 34, 40, 41, 42, 43, 45 and 46. They do not establish tax or engineering jurisdiction. Supplemental locality references come from [2026 works listings for wards 19 and 20](https://tender.zoltwic.com/company/SVN%20ASSOCIATES) and the [Faridabad annual action plan](https://caqm.nic.in/WriteReadData/LINKS/Faridabad%20action%20plan3d93066f-2b30-4297-9eff-41f6b7935aae.pdf), which places Pragati Vihar and Bapu Nagar in Ward 46.

# Faridabad wards 16 to 30: boundary landmarks and Old Faridabad area cross-reference

## Source date and numbering

This record uses Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department notification No. 6/73/2023-5CI dated 11 January 2024, published in the Haryana Government Gazette Extraordinary. The Hindi notification renders the suffix as 5क1. It publishes Faridabad ward boundaries under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994, following the preliminary notification dated 7 November 2023. The schedule extends to ward 46.

The official DULB index lists the linked PDF as "Final Notification of Wardbandi of Municipal Corporation Faridabad", with the portal date 23 April 2024 and size 867 KB. The PDF itself is headed 11 January 2024. The record date follows the document, not its later portal listing. English boundary descriptions for wards 16 to 30 appear on printed gazette pages 85 to 92.

The filename's Old Faridabad label is a discovery aid, not an official name for all fifteen wards. In this schedule, wards 16 to 19 concern SGM Nagar, NIT and Badkhal; wards 20 to 23 concern the western railway-side and Surajkund area; wards 24 to 30 concern the northern border, canal corridor and adjoining neighbourhoods. Much of Old Faridabad and the central sector belt appears in wards 31 onward. Do not transfer locality mappings from a former 40-ward map to this 46-ward schedule.

Greenfield, Green Field and Greenfields are common spelling variants; the schedule uses Green Field Colony. The boundary walls of Green Field, Omaxe, NHPC Colony and Green Valley matter because the notification follows their edges and turns. A neighbourhood name alone is not enough to assign a property.

Charmwood is a useful search term for the Surajkund residential corridor, but these ward entries do not expressly name Charmwood Village. This record therefore does not assert that every Charmwood address belongs to ward 22 or 23. Check the particular society, access road and plot against the notified route or the official electoral roll.

The Badarpur-border search area is divided by major physical boundaries. Ward 24 starts on Delhi-Mathura Road and reaches the canal corridor. Wards 25 to 28 use east-of-canal roads, drains, village lanes and the Yamuna edge. "Badarpur border", "Palla" or "Sehatpur" without a full address cannot establish the ward.

For ward 24, the English schedule calls the Ashoka Enclave approach 65 feet wide, while the Hindi text says 60 feet. This record does not resolve that discrepancy. Use house 54, the bridge and adjoining road junctions to identify the segment, and ask the election branch for clarification if road width affects an address decision.

This table retains landmarks rather than choosing a disputed compass direction. The linked gazette is necessary for a boundary dispute.

## Neharpar and Greater Faridabad Sectors 75 to 89

Neharpar is not one ward. Ward 30 crosses both canals toward Mawai and the hospital gate 4 area, but that does not place the entire Greater Faridabad sector belt in ward 30.

The same notification places several named landmarks beyond this file's ward range. Ward 33 uses the Sector 86/87 dividing road, SRS Residency and Sai Society. Ward 34 uses Kheri Kalan revenue limits, SRS International School, RPS Auraiya and the hospital gate 4 approach. Ward 35 uses Sector 87 Sai Park apartments, SRS Pearl Heights, the Sector 86/87 road and BPTP Park Elite Floors in Sector 85.

Ward 38 uses World Street Sector 79, BPTP Parkland, Bhataula, the Sector 78/77 dividing road, Sector 76 and the Sector 75/76 Resort Chowk. Ward 44 also uses the Sector 74/75, 76/73, 79/76 and 77/78 roads and Mirzapur landmarks. These are source-based cross-references, not a claim that each named sector is wholly assigned to that ward. No verified whole-sector allocation for every sector from 75 through 89 is supplied here.

## Address checks and source access

For an individual address, retain the house or plot number, colony block, road, nearby bridge or drain and the ward-numbering year. Compare these details with the full gazette route and the district's final municipal voter lists. A property-tax locality, development-plan sector or assembly constituency is not a substitute for the municipal electoral boundary.

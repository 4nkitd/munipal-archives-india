# Faridabad wards 31 to 46: Old Faridabad, Greater Faridabad and Ballabgarh

## Legal source and numbering

Haryana Urban Local Bodies notification No. 6/73/2023-5CI, dated 11 January 2024, publishes Faridabad's ward delimitation under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Delimitation of Wards Rules, 1994. The gazette is No. 8-2024/Ext. The notification refers to the earlier notification dated 7 November 2023. Commissioner and Secretary Vikas Gupta signed it. The English descriptions for wards 31 through 46 appear on printed gazette pages 92 through 97. [1]

The official DULB index lists the linked PDF as "Final Notification of Wardbandi of Municipal Corporation Faridabad", with a portal date of 23 April 2024. That listing date is not the date printed in the notification. The retrieved PDF itself carries the 11 January 2024 gazette heading. [1][2]

This ward band is not exclusively Ballabgarh. Wards 31 and 32 describe Old Faridabad streets. Wards 33 through 38 include central sectors and land east of the canals. The later wards reach Ballabgarh and its southern and eastern fringe. Do not apply a pre-delimitation 40-ward number to this 46-ward schedule.

The following are boundary landmarks, not declarations that every named locality lies wholly inside one ward. The original schedule describes a closed route using roads, buildings, canals and revenue boundaries. A property on either side of a boundary needs the complete route and election records. The Hindi and English schedules differ in some spelling and canal terminology. For example, the English opening of ward 33 calls the bridge opposite Sector 17 an Agra Canal bridge, while its next segment follows the western edge of Gurgaon Canal. This record does not resolve that wording into a surveyed coordinate.

Sectors 9, 10, 11 and 12 are useful search terms for the central-sector corridor, but the schedule is not a simple sector-to-ward list. Ward 37's route is particularly relevant to that corridor. YMCA Road is explicitly the Sector 7 and 10 dividing road in ward 39. Sector 12 appears on the northern approach of ward 37 and on adjoining ward boundaries. No whole-sector allocation for Sectors 9, 10 or 11 is certified here. Ward 38 also has differing sector-pair wording between the Hindi and English schedules near the municipal-limit segment. Check the published document rather than treating this summary as a polygon. [1, pp. 93 to 94]

Sector 62 and the Sector 64 and 65 dividing road are explicit boundary references. Sector 63 is part of the requested southern-sector search area, but no separate Sector 63 assignment has been verified from these ward descriptions. Likewise, mentioning an adjoining village's revenue boundary does not establish that its entire revenue estate is inside ward 44. [1, pp. 96 to 97]

## Tigaon, added villages and the Sikri route

The Tribune reported on 17 January 2024 that the corporation increased from 40 to 46 wards after inclusion of 24 villages in 2021-22, with three additional wards in Greater Faridabad. That is contextual reporting, not a substitute for the boundary schedule. [3]

Tigaon Road is explicitly part of wards 40 through 43. It must not be read as proof that Tigaon village is included in one of those wards. The IMT bypass bridge is a ward 43 landmark, while ward 44 follows a long outer boundary adjoining the IMT approach villages. Neither reference assigns every HSIIDC industrial plot to MCF.

For the NH-19 route south towards Sikri and Palwal, ward 46 is the relevant verified highway-edge description in this band. The gazette calls the road Delhi-Mathura Road and describes an intersection with the municipal boundary after crossing the railway and Delhi-Mumbai Highway. It does not name Sikri or equate the corporation boundary with the Palwal district boundary. No claim that Sikri village is wholly in ward 46 is made here. Obtain the municipal-limit notification, ward map and parcel record before assigning a property in that corridor.

## Checking an individual address

Use the district administration's municipal-election page to reach ward-wise electoral material. Match the house, colony, road and booth against the 2024 schedule. For Sector 7A, Arya Nagar, Bhatia Colony, Sector 64C and Sector 64D, the notification's individual house and street turns matter more than the broad locality name. [1][4]

This record is a boundary-reference index. It does not certify a cadastral boundary, property-tax jurisdiction, current engineering responsibility or councillor contact. A sector dividing road can be the edge of two wards, and a landmark on that road does not establish which side contains a particular property.

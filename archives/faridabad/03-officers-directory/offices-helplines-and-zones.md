# Municipal Corporation Faridabad offices, helplines and zones

The Municipal Corporation Faridabad head office is at B.K. Chowk, NIT Faridabad, Haryana 121001. The district administration's municipal public-utility entry confirms B.K. Chowk and telephone **0129-2416464**. The ULB Faridabad directory also lists **0129-2416465** for the Commissioner office.

## Toll-free and online grievance channels

The live Haryana ULB grievance portal accepts complaints about sanitation, street lights and other municipal services. Citizens sign in using a mobile number and OTP, submit the problem, retain the grievance ID and use the status facility to track the response. The portal also links the **eSamadhan DULB Haryana Citizen** mobile application.

| Number | Attribution and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| 1800-8900-929 | Current live ULB portal support and grievance number, available 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday |
| 1800-419-0219 | MCF toll-free number in the indexed ULB Faridabad officers directory; direct page retrieval failed, so current operation is unverified |
| 1800-102-5953 | Ecogreen waste-complaint number in secondary Faridabad contact listings; do not treat it as the general municipal switchboard |
| 1800-180-1817 | Gurugram municipal helpline, not established as an MCF Faridabad number |

The requested number **1800-180-1817** must not be labelled as Faridabad's confirmed toll-free helpline. Haryana's official property-tax contact table assigns it to **Corporation Gurugram**, and an official MCG monsoon-preparedness document also identifies it as the MCG helpline.

## District emergency helplines

Municipal grievance channels are not emergency dispatch services. For an immediate emergency use **112**, the integrated emergency number. The district administration's helpline page separately publishes the following legacy and specialist numbers.

| Service | District-listed number |
| --- | --- |
| Police | 100 |
| Child helpline | 1098 |
| Women helpline | 1091 |
| Crime Stopper | 1090 |

The district page displays a last-updated date of 21 August 2019. Its labels are reproduced as published, particularly the local designation of 1090 as Crime Stopper. Faridabad reporting on the July 2021 Haryana 112 launch describes 112 as integrating police, fire and ambulance assistance, formerly reached through 100, 101 and 108.

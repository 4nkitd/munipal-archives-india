# Faridabad official notices and GRAP enforcement, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and date context

This is a consolidated reference, not a single MCF notification. The frontmatter date is the revision date of the CAQM GRAP schedule used below, 21 November 2025. Individual instruments retain their own dates. CAQM orders apply across the National Capital Region, including Faridabad, but some transport and school measures expressly cover only Delhi or specified adjoining districts.

The [Haryana ULB portal](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Faridabad) identifies `https://mcfaridabad.in/` as the corporation website. That website returned an HTTP 522 error during retrieval. This record therefore uses accessible CAQM and Haryana government sources and clearly labels secondary reporting. It does not claim to reproduce every MCF notice issued in 2024 to 2026 or establish which GRAP stages were in force on the verification date.

## GRAP Stages I to IV in Faridabad

The [CAQM schedule revised on 21 November 2025](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Home/News/GRAP%20Schedule7e3bd726-7308-484f-995f-7cb3fa705b8d.pdf) classifies stages by Delhi AQI. Invocation depends on CAQM orders and forecasts, not on a reader independently applying Faridabad's local AQI. Higher stages add to lower-stage actions. Forecasts can justify advance invocation before a nominal threshold is reached.

| Stage | Delhi AQI band in the November 2025 schedule | Faridabad relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I, Poor | 201 to 300 | Dust mitigation and C&D waste controls. Construction projects on plots of at least 500 square metres must meet applicable web-portal registration and monitoring requirements. Municipal commissioners and chief engineers are among implementing agencies. |
| II, Very poor | 301 to 400 | Intensified road cleaning, dust suppression and other pollution-control measures under the schedule. Read diesel-generator restrictions with the applicable CAQM directions and exceptions. |
| III, Severe | 401 to 450 | Restrictions on listed dust-generating construction and demolition activities across NCR. BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel four-wheel LMV restrictions expressly include Faridabad. Specified school classes operate in hybrid mode under the schedule. |
| IV, Severe plus | Above 450 | Adds restrictions on linear public construction projects such as roads, highways, flyovers and pipelines. Hybrid teaching extends to classes VI to IX and XI in Faridabad and the other expressly named districts. Truck-entry restrictions concern entry into Delhi, not a universal ban on trucks moving within Faridabad. |

Stage III lists excavation, piling, demolition, open-trench utility work, masonry, RMC batching, major welding, painting, plastering and other specified activities. The schedule permits certain less-polluting work and enumerated project exemptions subject to dust-control compliance. A blanket statement that every construction activity is prohibited would be inaccurate. Check the actual invocation order, schedule version and exemption before commencing work.

December 2024 releases explain the Supreme Court-directed precautionary thresholds then applicable, above 350 for Stage III and above 400 for Stage IV. Those historical triggers should not be confused with the nominal bands in the November 2025 schedule.

The January 2026 order names the Deputy Commissioner, Faridabad, and Commissioner, Municipal Corporation Faridabad, among recipients. It modifies the Stage IV Delhi-entry provision to stop BS-IV trucks except essential-commodity or essential-service trucks, while permitting LNG, CNG, electric and BS-VI diesel trucks. It separately states that BS-III and below diesel commercial goods vehicles registered outside Delhi were already barred from Delhi entry from 1 November 2025, even when carrying essential commodities.

## Property tax relief and sealing notices

The [ULB property-tax notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists the following official records. Publication dates are portal dates, not independently verified gazette execution dates.

| Portal publication date | Official title or subject | Archival qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 27 March 2024 | Regarding Self-Certification of Property Information | Relevant to correcting and certifying property records before payment. |
| 25 October 2024 | Compendium of Instructions | Reference compilation, not a standalone Faridabad rebate announcement. |
| 18 May 2026 | Notification for one time waiver of interest on the arrears of property tax pending since year 2010-11 to 2024-2025 | The index confirms the title and arrears period. It does not itself establish a percentage, payment deadline or eligibility conditions. |
| 31 July 2026 | Property tax notification 2026 Corporation | Corporation instrument. Do not substitute the separately listed councils-and-committees instrument. |

The [official indicative calculator](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/taxScreen.html), referring to the corporation policy dated 30 July 2026, displays a 75% property-tax rebate for residential properties within Lal Dora of existing villages in municipalities. It also displays a maximum 25% rebate per property among listed categories, including 10% current-year rebate for clearing all dues by 30 April, 5% for qualifying good taxpayers, 10% for qualifying zero-waste housing arrangements, 25% for women-owned residential property with proportionate treatment of joint ownership, and 10% for qualifying residential owners with at least 40% disability. The policy and assessed bill govern eligibility. These displayed 2026 conditions are not evidence of the terms applicable in 2024 or 2025.

Local sealing is documented in secondary reporting, not an accessible signed MCF notice. [The Tribune, 2 January 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/faridabad-civic-body-to-intensify-property-tax-recovery-drive/), reported notices issued or being issued to defaulters owing at least Rs 50,000 and proposed sealing for non-payment. [Dainik Bhaskar, 25 March 2025](https://www.bhaskar.com/local/haryana/faridabad/news/faridabad-municipal-corporation-seals-11-properties-tax-default-134707544.html), reported 11 properties sealed in Ballabhgarh zone with aggregate arrears exceeding Rs 85 lakh after prior notices. These are reported enforcement episodes, not a verified universal statutory sealing threshold or a current list of sealed premises.

## Area Sabha notification coverage

No signed Faridabad Area Sabha notification for 2024 to 2026 was retrieved from an accessible official portal. Two reports dated 28 January 2026 attribute the meeting programme to Additional Municipal Commissioner and corporation secretary Dr Vijaypal Yadav.

[Faridabad Darshan's report](https://faridabaddarshan.com/area-sabha-meeting-nagar-nigam-faridabad/) describes meetings on 29 January 2026 and a Ward 44 meeting on 31 January 2026 at 11 a.m., Panchayat Vatika, Mohna Road, Chandawali, Greater Faridabad. It reports verification of old-age pension, widow pension and Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana beneficiaries, plus other municipal matters with the Sabha's permission. It attributes a minimum 10% voter-attendance requirement to the corporation.

[Hint News](https://hintnews.com/faridabad-municipal-corporation-to-begin-area-committee-meetings-from-january-29th-to-be-held-in-20-wards/) describes 20 wards but enumerates 21 ward numbers for 29 January. That inconsistency prevents treating its list as a verified official schedule. Meeting occurrence, quorum compliance, resolutions and the full ward programme remain unverified. The reported programme is not a ward-delimitation notification.

## Single-use plastic compliance

The [Union government's 25 July 2024 statement on plastic waste management](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2037061&lang=2&reg=3) confirms continued enforcement of the identified single-use plastic ban effective from 1 July 2022 and restrictions on plastic carry bags below 120 microns. It records Plastic Waste Management amendments dated 14 March 2024 and enforcement by CPCB and state pollution-control boards.

The [official list of banned articles](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1843398&lang=2&reg=48) includes plastic plates, cups, glasses, cutlery, straws, trays and stirrers; specified plastic sticks; thermocol decoration; specified wrapping films; and plastic or PVC banners below 100 microns. A thicker bag does not make an independently banned article lawful. The national rules apply in Faridabad but are not a newly issued MCF order from 2024.

A specific MCF circular number, local penalty schedule or signed plastic-enforcement notice for 2024 to 2026 was not verified. Do not infer those details from awareness campaigns or from another municipality's challan schedule.

## Construction dust and industrial emissions

[CAQM Direction No. 79, 13 February 2024](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/Direction%2079%20Prevention%20and%20mitigation%20of%20Air%20Pollution%20from%20C%26D%20activities%20130220244a15f94e-cad9-44c6-a62a-b025fe0a1410.pdf) prohibits issuing completion or occupancy certificates to projects with pending CAQM closure directions. It expressly names MCF and other Faridabad authorities. Expiry or revocation of a general GRAP restriction does not itself prove that an individual closure direction has been lifted.

The [3 August 2026 amendment to Direction No. 86](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/477c43d6-48f6-4d00-9ef1-d3d54d23281d.pdf) records the original direction dated 2 January 2025 and amendment dated 24 June 2025. It authorises specified municipal and development-authority officers, including in Faridabad, to file complaints before the jurisdictional judicial magistrate for gross C&D dust-control violations, alongside closure and environmental-compensation measures.

For industrial stack emissions, [Direction No. 98, 20 February 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/Direction%20No-9866cc9162-5c19-49c5-a836-be302e9053af.pdf) prescribes 50 mg/Nm3 for identified categories across Delhi-NCR. Coverage includes CPCB's 17 highly polluting categories, medium and large red-category air-polluting industries, food-processing and textile industries with boilers or thermic-fluid heaters, and metal industries with furnaces. Existing stricter limits below 50 mg/Nm3 remain applicable.

The [amendment dated 30 July 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/a65b5659-e1ca-4919-9803-cebf41c56204.pdf) replaces the original staggered implementation dates with **1 October 2026 for all covered industries**. [PIB's 31 July 2026 explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2292690&lang=1&reg=1) confirms this change. This is a targeted emission standard, not an assertion that every Faridabad industrial unit has the same consent conditions or has achieved compliance.

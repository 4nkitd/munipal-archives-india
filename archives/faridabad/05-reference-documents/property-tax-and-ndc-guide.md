# Faridabad property tax and No Dues Certificate guide

## Find and self-certify a property ID

Municipal Corporation Faridabad (MCF) uses Haryana's [Property Tax Dues Payment and No Dues Certificate Management System](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). Choose **Citizen** and sign in by one-time password (OTP) using a registered mobile number, email or property ID (PID). New users can register with their own mobile number. The portal has a separate registration option for NRIs and foreign nationals.

1. In **Search Property**, select **Faridabad**. Search by the eight-character PID, old PID, plot or unit number, owner or occupier name, or mobile number. If needed, select the colony and use **Search on Map** to check the location.
2. Open **View Details/Self Certify** and check the property details, mobile number, owner details and outstanding dues. Check the area, use, floors, property and fire tax, development charges, and the recorded authorised or unauthorised colony status.
3. If all sections are correct, select **Yes** in each and choose **Verify Details**. Follow the OTP prompts. The [self-certification manual](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Content/UserManuals/How_to_self_certify_08.07.2024.pdf) describes verification through Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID), Aadhaar, or the mobile number linked to the property if neither ID is available.
4. If a section is wrong, choose **No**, then **Update Details**. Upload the evidence requested in the [document checklist](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments), such as a deed for an area correction or a receipt for an uncredited payment. Save the draft, then complete **Final Preview and Submit Objection**. Track the reference number in [Check Application Status](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ApplicationStatus) and self-certify after MCF corrects the record.

The [19 November 2026 objection manual](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Content/UserManuals/Verify_Property_Details_and_Submit_Objection_19.11.2026.pdf) lists normal processing at no fee within 10 working days and Tatkaal at ₹1,000 within two working days. Use the [municipal support directory](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/CustomerSupport/MCSupportListView) for Faridabad contacts. The [DULB tax-notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists state IT support at 1800-8900-929, 9 am to 6 pm, Monday to Saturday.

## Pay dues and verify the No Dues Certificate

In **Make Payment/Generate NDC**, search by PID and choose **Pay All Dues and Generate NDC**. Review property and fire tax, development charges and garbage collection charges where applicable. Select **Pay Online**, complete the OTP step and pay through the gateway. MCF also accepts payment at its offices. Paying only the property tax or garbage charge does not clear any other balance on the PID. See the [portal FAQ](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/FAQ) and [payment instructions](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Content/UserManuals/How_to_Make_Payment_of_Property_Tax_Dues_08.07.2024.pdf).

Keep the transaction reference and the payment receipt. Once the balance is nil, download the NDC from the signed-in portal. To verify a copy supplied by someone else, compare the PID, municipality, property details and issue date with a fresh copy from the portal or have MCF confirm it. If the certificate includes a QR code, check that it resolves to an official Haryana government page and that the returned details match. If a debit has not posted, use **Verify Payment** or give MCF the transaction reference before paying again. A receipt proves payment of the charges listed on it; an NDC shows that the municipal dues covered by the certificate were cleared for that PID as of issue. Neither establishes legal title, freedom from encumbrances or building approval.

## Which assessment rules apply in 2026?

The [30 July 2026 corporation notification, S.O. 39/H.A.16/1994/S.87/2026](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf), classifies Faridabad as A1. The new system applies from **1 August 2026**. The [2 November 2026 Gazette amendment, No. 4/51/2026-4CI](https://egazette.servantsofknowledge.in/details/in.gazette.haryana.2026-11-02.Extra-Ordinary_4-51-2026-4CI/), extends the option to pay at the 2013 rates through **31 December 2026**. After that, payment under the revised system is mandatory. The amendment preserves payments already made through FY 2026-27 and does not recalculate arrears or interest through FY 2025-26. This payment window is separate from the interest-waiver deadlines below.

### Revised A1 rates under the 30 July 2026 notification

Annual tax is **capital value × applicable rate × usage factor**. The residential floor rate is **0.030%** and the non-residential floor rate is **0.10%**. The respective ceilings are **0.090%** and **0.30%**. The floor rates apply unless the corporation fixes a rate within those limits.

For most properties, capital value is plot area multiplied by the applicable collector rate and a floor factor. A ground-floor-only building has a factor of 1, with 0.25 added for each additional built floor, including a basement. Flats and apartments use carpet area multiplied by the collector rate. Independent floors use plot area multiplied by the collector rate. Mixed-use premises are assessed by area for each use. A rented portion attracts an additional 25% of the tax otherwise applicable to that portion.

| Category | Usage factor under paragraph 4 |
| --- | --- |
| Residential house or independent floor, by plot area | Up to 50 sq m: 0.25; over 50 through 100: 0.50; over 100 through 200: 0.75; above 200: 1.00. |
| Residential flat, by carpet area | Up to 100 sq m: 0.75; above 100: 1.00. |
| Shop, by plot size | Up to 30 sq m: 0.25; over 30 through 50: 0.50; above 50: 1.00. These bands cover stand-alone shops and shops in shopping complexes with plot area up to 1,000 sq m. |
| Other commercial uses | Shops in malls on plots over 1,000 sq m: 1.25, on shop carpet area with common areas apportioned; paid parking: 0.25; markets: 0.50; warehouses and other commercial establishments: 1.00. |
| Industrial, including dairies and poultry | Up to 500 sq m: 0.25; over 500 through 2,000: 0.50; over 2,000 through 5,000: 0.75; above 5,000: 1.00. |

If the revised tax is higher, paragraph 5 caps the annual amount until it reaches the full revised assessment. The caps are 1.25 times for houses up to 250 sq m and flats up to 100 sq m, 1.5 times for houses over 250 through 350 sq m, and 2 times for other residential properties. For industrial properties, the four size bands above have caps of 1.5, 2, 2.5 and 3 times, respectively. Commercial and other properties are capped at 3 times. The [indicative calculator](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/taxScreen.html) needs the PID or old annual demand to estimate the capped amount. Use the assessed demand on the PID for payment.

Under paragraphs 7 and 9 of the 2026 rules, eligible residential property within the Lal Dora of an existing municipal village gets a 75% property-tax rebate. A separate group of rebates is subject to a 25% maximum per property: 10% on current-year tax when all dues including arrears are cleared by 30 April of the assessment year; 5% for paying by 30 April in each of the preceding three years; 10% for an operational zero-waste group housing society or outside cluster of at least 50 properties; 25% on a woman's ownership share in a residential property; and 10% for residential property owned by a person with at least 40% disability. The notification ties rebates to self-certification and full payment of due taxes, not part payment. Late payment attracts simple interest of 1.5% per month or part of a month. Do not treat a past waiver, or the December 2026 transition deadline, as an active interest concession.

## Lal Dora property IDs and possession certificates

Lal Dora or old Abadi Deh is the historically inhabited part of an erstwhile village. The [March 2024 ULB policy and procedure](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2F9bb08af2-072a-4fff-a20f-a8e54dd3e36e.pdf) covers old Abadi Deh within municipal limits where rights records were not traditionally maintained. It excludes land with its own specific khasra number. A PID identifies a parcel in the tax database. A Lal Dora Property Certificate is issued through a separate possession-record process, subject to applicable transfer law and court decisions.

- **Find or correct the PID.** Check the [Lal Dora possession-notice search](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/PropertyList/SearchProperty) by district **Faridabad**, town **Faridabad (070)** and the relevant locality. Match the result against the regular property search, including the map. If a PID exists, use the objection route for incorrect tax-record details. Do not create a duplicate.
- **Apply for a missing or split PID.** If the ordinary search and map show no record, use the new PID link under **Search Property**. Select the locality, mark the parcel on the map, enter owner and floor details, and upload ID, ownership evidence, a site plan and a property photo. The [portal checklist](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments) also asks for Lal Dora evidence such as a registry or sale deed, a court decree registered with revenue authorities, or a competent authority's legal-heir certificate in a death case. For a subdivision, apply against the parent PID as a split. Keep the SMS application ID.
- **Claim the possession certificate.** Check the MCF draft list and file any claim or objection at the relevant municipal zonal office within **30 days of publication**. The SOP calls for a revenue-attested affidavit and two records showing possession over the previous 10 years, such as electricity or water bills, government address records, property-tax receipts or proof of construction. Take any deed or decree available and, if the original owner has died, a legal-heir certificate. Include the PID, boundaries and dimensions. An appeal against the final published list goes to the ward committee within **30 days** of that publication. After resolution and approval, MCF updates the existing PID and distributes the certificate. If no conventional ownership document or PID exists, ask the zone office how to lodge the possession claim rather than creating a second or misclassified record.

Under paragraph 6(i) of the 2026 notification, qualifying Lal Dora residential property within a newly included village has a five-year exemption from the date of inclusion in municipal limits, limited to three floors, unless Haryana extends the period. This provision and the 75% rebate for Lal Dora residential property in existing municipal villages depend on the recorded boundary and use. Neither is an exemption for all property in the village.

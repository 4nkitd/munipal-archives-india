# Faridabad citizen services: grievances, certificates and bookings

## Scope and service routes

This guide was compiled on 22 November 2026. The frontmatter date is the compilation date, not a notification date. The Haryana Urban Local Bodies Right to Service page identifies its consolidated list as updated through 4 November 2025. Its time limits are in working days.

| Need | Official starting point | Important distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Municipal grievance | [ULB grievance redressal system](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Retain the grievance ID |
| Sanitation complaint | [Swachhata platform](https://www.swachh.city/) | Photograph and location identify the site |
| Birth or death certificate | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) or the local registrar through e-Disha | The Faridabad district page also links Civil Registration System |
| Marriage registration | [Shaadi Haryana](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) and SARAL marriage service listings | Choose the applicable Act and urban jurisdiction |
| Municipal community centre booking | SARAL | Availability and written approval are separate from filing |
| Dog registration or renewal | SARAL | The published municipal service is specifically for dogs |
| Municipal water or sewerage connection | SARAL | This service expressly includes Faridabad |

The ULB website now links Faridabad's municipal website at [mcfaridabad.in](https://mcfaridabad.in/). That site returned an access error during research. This guide therefore uses the state ULB service schedule and district administration pages rather than assuming that individual MCF web forms are available.

## Public grievances and helpline correction

The [ULB grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) accepts reports about sanitation, street lights and other municipal services. Its citizen sign-in uses a mobile number and a six-digit SMS code. The public page provides account registration, grievance-status lookup and links to the eSamadhan DULB Haryana Citizen mobile apps.

Describe the problem and its exact location. Keep the grievance ID so that the response and action taken can be checked. The portal publishes **1800-8900-929**, available **9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday**, for toll-free assistance. The same number appears on the ULB department's Right to Service page.

**1800-180-1817 should not be presented as a verified Faridabad grievance number.** An official Municipal Corporation Gurugram monsoon-preparedness document identifies it as the MCG helpline. The Faridabad district [telephone directory](https://faridabad.nic.in/telephone-directory/) instead lists **0129-2416464** for the MCF Commissioner office near B.K. Chowk. This is an office contact, not evidence of a dedicated round-the-clock complaint centre.

For a delayed notified municipal service, retain the application receipt and use the designated officer and grievance authorities in the service table below. A general complaint does not replace the original service application.

## Samadhan Shivirs and Swachhata complaints

Samadhan Shivirs provide an in-person route for public grievances involving municipal and other departments. District administration announcements describe district-level camps at the **Mini Secretariat, Sector 12, Faridabad**. A DC Faridabad announcement reproduced in the source list states **Monday and Thursday, 10 AM to 12 noon**. This is the district camp schedule, not a verified schedule for every MCF zonal office. Check the current district announcement before travelling because camp timings can change.

Bring a written account of the issue, the site address and copies of earlier complaint or application acknowledgements. Ask for a receipt or reference so that a later follow-up can identify the case. Attendance is not a guarantee of immediate disposal.

The [Swachhata citizen platform](https://www.swachh.city/) is a Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs sanitation-grievance route. Its published process is to photograph the issue, identify the location and submit the complaint. The platform forwards the complaint to the concerned municipal corporation and provides status notifications. Citizens can comment on the response and reopen an unsatisfactory resolution.

Use the relevant sanitation category, such as garbage dumping or an unclean public toilet, rather than treating Swachhata as a certificate or booking portal. The national platform's published capabilities do not establish that an individual Faridabad complaint has been received or resolved.

## Birth and death certificates

Start with [SARAL Haryana](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) or an e-Disha centre and select the relevant birth or death service. SARAL provides application tracking and certificate-download controls. Certificate issuance, correction of an existing record, delayed registration and inclusion of a child's name are different requests. Select the request that matches the record problem.

The Faridabad district's [birth/death certificates page](https://faridabad.nic.in/service/birth-death-certificates/) explains that registrars are appointed for local areas under section 7 of the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969. Jurisdiction follows the place where the event occurred. It directs citizens to inform the competent registrar within **21 days**. For an institutional birth or death, the institution is responsible for reporting the event.

For a death, the district page asks for the doctor's original certificate with burial or cremation information. It lists e-Disha centres in Faridabad, PIN **121007**, and links the Civil Registration System at `crsorgi.gov.in`. This district-specific route should not be omitted merely because SARAL is the state's common service portal.

Keep the event date, place, hospital or institution details and existing registration number ready when available. Confirm the current service-specific document list with the portal or registrar. The sources reviewed do not establish a single Faridabad fee or processing limit covering fresh certificates, corrections and delayed registration, so none is asserted here.

## Marriage registration

The requested marriage-registration portal is [shaadi.haryana.gov.in](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/). It returned no readable page content during research, so its current screens and fee schedule were not verified. SARAL's public service catalogue separately lists urban and rural marriage-registration services under the 1954 and 2008 Acts and an entry labelled 1995. Select the applicable service rather than assuming all marriage registrations follow one process.

The Government of India's [Haryana marriage-registration service entry](https://services.india.gov.in/service/detail/apply-for-marriage-registration-haryana) states that registration in urban local bodies is carried out under the **Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Act, 2008** and the **2008 Rules**. It lists Form I, information about the bride and bridegroom, six affidavits, address and date-of-birth proofs, challan, marriage photographs, marriage card and a joint photograph. Treat this as the published service description and check the current selected form before preparing affidavits or paying.

The Haryana Home Department's **1 December 2016** instructions, effective **31 January 2017**, authorize Common Service Centres and e-Disha centres to carry out the application process. The instructions describe registrar jurisdiction by the place of solemnisation or the spouses' qualifying residence. A completed online form is not itself the marriage certificate. Follow any verification or appearance directions issued by the competent registrar.

No current Faridabad-specific marriage fee, witness count or disposal deadline was verified for this guide.

## Community centre bookings and dog registration

Apply through [SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) for **Booking of Community Center**. The state schedule gives **15 working days** and names the **Zonal Taxation Officer** as the designated officer for municipal corporations. The **Joint Municipal Commissioner** is the first grievance redressal authority and the **Commissioner** is the second.

Identify the centre and event date in the application. Before making arrangements, obtain confirmation of availability, the current rent and security deposit, permitted use, cancellation terms and the booking approval from the responsible office. The schedule does not publish a Faridabad centre inventory or a uniform tariff. It also does not mean that all community centres in Faridabad are controlled by MCF.

SARAL is also the published route for **Issue Certificate of Dog Registration/brought for show purpose** and its renewal. The service limits are **15 working days for issue** and **7 working days for renewal**. The designated officer and two grievance authorities are the same as for community centre bookings.

The official schedule names dogs, not every species of pet. It should not be read as proof of a general registration service for cats or other animals. Confirm the selected application's current owner, animal and veterinary-document requirements and fee with SARAL or MCF. A pet registration application is distinct from a complaint about stray animals.

## Water and sewerage connections and service limits

The ULB Right to Service schedule expressly includes **Municipal Corporation Faridabad** in the water supply and sewerage connection service. Apply through [SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/), select the municipal connection service and retain the application reference and payment receipt. Confirm that the property is served by MCF before selecting the authority.

The schedule names the **Assistant Engineer** as the designated officer, the **Executive Engineer** as first grievance redressal authority and the **Superintending Engineer/Chief Engineer** as second grievance redressal authority. The published connection limit is **7 working days**.

| Service | Published limit | Designated officer | First grievance authority | Second grievance authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water supply and sewerage connection | 7 working days | Assistant Engineer | Executive Engineer | Superintending Engineer/Chief Engineer |
| Water leakage, sewer blockage or overflow | 2 working days | Assistant Engineer | Executive Engineer | Superintending Engineer/Chief Engineer |
| Community centre booking | 15 working days | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Dog registration certificate | 15 working days | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Dog registration renewal | 7 working days | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |

For leakage, blockage or overflow, the schedule says to submit an application to the concerned municipality. This is a repair complaint, not a new-connection request. The schedule does not establish one connection fee for every property, pipe size or road-cutting situation. Obtain the applicable demand and approval rather than treating payment of a water bill as connection sanction.

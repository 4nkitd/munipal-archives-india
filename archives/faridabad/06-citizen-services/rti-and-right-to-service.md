# Faridabad municipal RTI and Right to Service timelines

Use the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI) to request records that Municipal Corporation Faridabad (MCF) holds, such as a copy of a sanctioned plan or a work order. Use the Haryana Right to Service Act, 2014 (RTS) when you want the authority to deliver a notified service, such as sanctioning an eligible plan or issuing a new Property ID. The two Acts have different deadlines and appeal routes.

## RTI filing, fees and statutory deadlines

File a request or first appeal through the [Haryana RTI portal](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/). The hostname `rtionline.haryana.gov.in` did not resolve when checked. Use the verified state portal address above, not the Union Government's `rtionline.gov.in`. If the portal does not offer the appropriate municipal authority, submit a written request to the MCF State Public Information Officer (SPIO) at the corporation office near B.K. Chowk, NIT Faridabad. Identify the records, date range, locality and municipal branch if known. Keep the online registration number or proof of delivery.

The Haryana application fee is **Rs 10**. Applicants below the poverty line are exempt and should attach proof. The portal's first appeal has no fee. If further charges apply for providing information, section 7(3) requires the SPIO to notify the applicant of the amount and calculation. The time between dispatch of that notice and payment is excluded from the response period.

| Step | Statutory time |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary response under section 7(1) | Within 30 days of receipt. No decision within the applicable period counts as a refusal under section 7(2). |
| Information concerning a person's life or liberty | Within 48 hours. |
| Transfer to another public authority under section 6(3) | As soon as practicable and no later than five days from receipt. The applicant must be informed. |
| First appeal under section 19(1) | Within 30 days of the response deadline or receipt of the SPIO's decision. The FAA normally decides within 30 days, extendable to 45 days with reasons recorded in writing. |
| Second appeal under section 19(3) | Within 90 days from when the first appeal decision should have been made or was received. The Haryana portal directs applicants to file with the State Information Commission, Haryana by post or by hand in triplicate. |

If the authority misses the statutory response deadline, section 7(6) requires it to provide the information free of charge. See the [RTI Act on India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2065?locale=en) and the [Haryana portal citizen manual](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/pdffiles/UserManualforCitizen.pdf).

## Notified municipal service deadlines

The selected services below come from the [ULB consolidated schedule](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService), updated through 4 November 2025. The schedule links the notifications dated 16 August and 16 November 2022 and the amendments dated 11 March and 27 October 2025. The time limits are **working days** from receipt of the complete application by the Designated Officer or an authorised recipient. The officer must acknowledge the application and provide the service or give written reasons for rejection within the notified period.

Use [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) for water and sewerage connections, conveyance deeds, community centre booking and the notified copy-of-documents service. Use the [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for Property IDs, property objections and property tax register changes. The ULB schedule gives separate application routes and eligibility rules for building plans and occupation certificates. The 20-day building plan rows do not apply to every commercial or institutional project. A [7 January 2026 Right to Service Commission order](https://haryana-rtsc.gov.in/storage/app/notice/cmBVmnOLtHfrJJrkAOdkOzQ9IvEwfhVOAJlGXTcL.pdf) confirms the 10-day Property ID limit and the MCF appeal designations in a Ballabgarh case.

Birth and death records have a separate schedule. The [Haryana Right to Service Commission register](https://haryana-rtsc.gov.in/notify-serviceslist) lists certified copies for already registered events at 14 working days for the current year, 30 for previous years back to 1972, and 90 for records before 1972. These are service limits, not the reporting period. The [Faridabad district guide](https://faridabad.nic.in/service/birth-death-certificates/) directs residents to report events to the registrar for the area where they occurred within 21 days.

## How to appeal an RTS delay or rejection

Keep the application acknowledgement, service name, due date and written rejection, if any. Under sections 6, 7 and 10 of the [Haryana Right to Service Act](https://haryana-rtsc.gov.in/act):

1. Appeal to the service's First Grievance Redressal Authority within 30 days of rejection or expiry of the notified time limit.
2. Appeal to the Second Grievance Redressal Authority within 60 days of the first appeal's rejection or expiry of the time allowed in an order directing delivery.
3. Apply for revision to the Haryana Right to Service Commission within 90 days of the second authority's order.

For services covered by the Auto Appeal System, missed deadlines trigger escalation and applicants can track appeals on SARAL. If a service is not covered, use the [Commission's appeal guidance](https://haryana-rtsc.gov.in/faq). Neither route replaces an RTI appeal to the FAA or the State Information Commission.

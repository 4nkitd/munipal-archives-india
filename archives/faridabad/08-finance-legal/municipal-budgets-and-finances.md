# Municipal Corporation Faridabad budgets and finances, FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27

## Annual budget and spending

Amounts are in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise. This record covers Municipal Corporation Faridabad (MCF), not the Faridabad Metropolitan Development Authority (FMDA). The record date is the FY 2026-27 House vote. Contemporary reporting supplies the budget totals below; audited figures and state-budget transfers are identified separately.

## Property tax and stamp duty

The [10 April 2025 House budget](https://impressivetimes.com/ncr/faridabad-municipal-corporation-budget-2025-praveen-joshi/) estimated about ₹163 crore from property tax and ₹220 crore from stamp duty in FY 2025-26. [Amar Ujala](https://www.amarujala.com/delhi-ncr/faridabad/faridabad-municipal-corporation-ranks-second-in-state-in-property-tax-recovery-faridabad-news-c-26-1-fbd1020-66424-2026-04-02) and [Dainik Jagran](https://www.jagran.com/haryana/faridabad-faridabad-mc-achieves-record-9758-cr-property-tax-recovery-40195478.html) reported ₹97.58 crore in FY 2025-26 property-tax collections after year-end. That is a reported collection figure, not audited revenue, and the reports describe recovery of arrears as well.

For FY 2024-25, [The Tribune's March 2025 report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/faridabad-mc-steps-up-property-tax-recovery-drive-puts-8000-on-notice/) cited a ₹140 crore budget target and about ₹48.5 crore collected by 11 March. This is an interim snapshot, not a verified year-end total. For FY 2026-27, the [House budget coverage](https://www.dainiktribuneonline.com/news/karnal/faridabad-municipal-corporation-budget-passed-development-proposal-worth-rs-2236-crore-emphasis-on-public-amenities/) reports a ₹184 crore aggregate tax revenue target, not a property-tax line. No signed MCF schedule was verified for the FY 2023-24 or FY 2026-27 property-tax estimates or for stamp duty estimates outside FY 2025-26.

Page 3 of [Haryana's FY 2026-27 Budgetary Transfers to Local Bodies](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s386e78499eeb33fb9cac16b7555b50767/uploads/2026/03/20260320389990259.pdf) describes the Faridabad split as 1% for MCF and 1% for FMDA. Its appropriations table aggregates municipal corporations and does not verify MCF's annual stamp duty receipts.

[FY 2026-27 budget coverage](https://www.livehindustan.com/ncr/faridabad/story-faridabad-municipal-corporation-passes-budget-of-2236-crores-for-development-201774710541873.html) reported more than ₹1,000 crore expected from both governments combined. Neither report gives a Haryana-only amount or confirms disbursement.

## Haryana Municipal Account Code, 2025

The [Directorate of Urban Local Bodies](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/HaryanaMunicipalAccountCode2025) lists the final notification dated 20 May 2025. Part I, sections 1.2 and 1.3, apply the Code to all Haryana municipalities from publication in the Official Gazette. The Code sets an accrual-based double-entry framework, with separate chart-of-accounts heads for revenue and capital, budget preparation and monitoring in Chapter 25, annual financial statements and audit in Chapter 28, and reconciliation in Chapter 29. The FY 2025-26 House vote on 10 April preceded the final notification.

The Directorate's [published technical evaluation](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2Fc482eaeb-2dbe-4b9a-bc88-32c715e5aa97.pdf) identifies Faridabad cluster tender `DULB/2024/6004/1` for support with accrual-based double-entry implementation. That procurement is evidence of implementation work, not a finding of compliance. No MCF audit opinion confirming compliance with the 2025 Code or full FY 2026-27 budget schedules in the Code's prescribed format were verified.

# Faridabad civic jurisdiction: municipal services, metropolitan networks and development estates

## How to read the jurisdiction split

Faridabad's ward boundary, FMDA's notified metropolitan area and a development estate boundary answer different questions. An address may have an MCF property ID, an HSVP allotment letter and a water supply fed by an FMDA main. None of those documents alone identifies who repairs the road or drain outside the property.

The date in this record is the gazette publication date of the Faridabad Metropolitan Development Authority Act, 2018, Haryana Act No. 9 of 2019. The Act distinguishes infrastructure that serves the notified area or connects at least two sectors, colonies or villages from internal development works within them, under sections 2(i) and 2(j). Section 16 ties FMDA maintenance to assets vested in or under its control. Sections 46 and 47 provide for notified transfer schemes for assets of HUDA, now HSVP, and HSIIDC. Section 24 allows a local authority to take on maintenance of an FMDA work under agreed terms.

At its first meeting on 10 April 2021, FMDA approved the proposed transfer scheme in agenda item 1.3. The minutes identify master roads, water supply, sewerage and drainage as city-level services and say that the respective agencies would continue maintaining internal sector works. The agenda identifies proposed transfers by GIS asset ID. Schedule A lists roads with a right of way of 30 m or more, Schedule C lists sewers 600 mm or larger with treatment plants, and Schedule D lists stormwater box drains and stormwater lines 600 mm or larger. These are screening criteria for the listed assets, not rules that assign any road or pipe of that size to FMDA. Check the effective handover and the current maintenance assignment.

## Jurisdiction matrix

| Service or asset | First agency to contact | Limits and checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Municipal property tax, property ID and municipal no-dues certificate | Municipal Corporation Faridabad, MCF, through the [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | An HSVP or HSIIDC allotment, transfer fee or estate due is a separate matter. |
| Waste collection, street sweeping, local sanitation, municipal internal streets, neighbourhood parks and municipal streetlights | MCF ward sanitation, engineering or horticulture branch. Use [DULB grievances](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/). | Confirm the maintainer for unhanded-over estate or developer assets. Some MCF parks have a residents' welfare association as maintainer. Lights on a master road or in an industrial estate may go to the relevant road or estate agency. |
| Municipal household water and sewer connections, local leaks, branch sewer blockages and street drains | MCF engineering for its local network. [DULB's Right to Service schedule](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService) lists Faridabad municipal connections and complaints. | For an estate or licensed colony, check the bill and handover record. For a problem upstream of a receiving booster or on an FMDA master main, start with FMDA. |
| Master water supply, Ranney wells on the Yamuna floodplain, trunk pipelines and associated boosting stations | FMDA Infrastructure II for the master system, including its [bulk and industrial connections](http://fmda.haryana.gov.in/onlineservices/water-connection.html). | The 9 December 2024 Right to Service notification limits FMDA's notified new water, sewerage and drainage connection services to bulk and industrial connections. The Ministry of Jal Shakti identifies Ranney wells as a substantial source of Faridabad's supply. |
| Master stormwater drains, trunk sewers, major pumping stations and sewage treatment plants, or STPs | FMDA Infrastructure II for assigned assets. FMDA tender records include the 30 MLD Badshahpur STP and the 7.5 MLD Sector 77 STP. | Separate stormwater drains from sanitary sewers. FMDA's April 2026 minutes record proposals for faecal sludge treatment at Mirzapur, Badshahpur and Pratapgarh STPs. The project record does not by itself establish the operator of every plant or connecting sewer. Check the current maintenance assignment by reach. |
| Master roads, inter-sector links and civil works on those roads | FMDA for roads on its asset list. Its tender for the YMCA Chowk to Bypass master road identifies asset 1071. | A sector-dividing road, service lane, verge or junction can have a different maintainer from the adjacent carriageway. Use the asset ID and endpoints, not road width alone. |
| Metropolitan mobility planning and city CCTV or adaptive traffic management | FMDA for its mobility work and the Phase 2 citywide CCTV procurement, tender 2024_HRY_368454_1. | Check the project phase. Faridabad Smart City Limited was involved in the Phase 3 discussion at the April 2026 FMDA meeting. Report a challan or enforcement issue to Traffic Police, not to the road or camera maintenance contractor. |
| HSVP urban estate development and plot allotment, possession, estate dues and allottee transfers | Haryana Shehri Vikas Pradhikaran, HSVP, through the issuing Estate Office in Faridabad. | Older records say HUDA. HSVP's December 2021 inventory records 32 Faridabad sectors transferred for municipal maintenance. The original allotting agency and the current maintainer can differ. |
| HSIIDC industrial plot allotment, possession and estate services | Haryana State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation, HSIIDC, for its IMT allotments and assigned internal roads, lighting, water and sewerage works. | HSIIDC and the gazetted Final Development Plan identify the developed IMT east of the Agra Canal as Sectors 66, 67, 68 and 69. **Do not classify all of Sector 70 as HSIIDC IMT.** DTCP records separate licensed group housing sites there. Check the approved layout and allotment or licence for the plot. For the common effluent treatment plant, or CETP, and other major assets, check the current operator against the transfer schedule. |
| Development plan, zoning, private colony licence and change of land use, or CLU | Haryana Department of Town and Country Planning, DTCP, for the development plan and private colony licence record. Confirm the competent planning authority for the parcel before applying. | The ULB and Town and Country Planning departments jointly notified the Final Development Plan 2031 A.D. on 14 March 2018, No. CCP(NCR)/FDB/FDP-31)/2018/694. FMDA's notified services cover eligible CLU cases in its area except those reserved to Government, and associated building plans and occupation certificates. MCF and DULB also have notified building and occupation services. Use the parcel location and existing sanction, not just the sector number. |
| Delhi to Agra national highway, old NH-2, and the DND to Faridabad to KMP spur | National Highways Authority of India, NHAI, or its assigned operator, for works and defects within the relevant national highway package. | Official Delhi to Agra work records use NH-19 and NH-44 for different records on the corridor. Identify the package by chainage and nearest junction. NH-148NA is the separate access-controlled spur to the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway, not the old Delhi to Agra highway. |

For a sewer complaint, identify the manhole or street rather than assuming that every trunk line has the same maintainer. A December 2024 report quoted MCF's chief engineer on a proposed return of major-line maintenance to MCF. FMDA's September 2026 Government of Haryana tender 2026_HRY_544877_1 still names FMDA as the agency procuring a master sewer repair near NHPC Chowk at the Sector 34/35 dividing road. Neither record establishes a blanket assignment for the rest of Faridabad.

## Faridabad to Gurugram and Manesar

The Faridabad-Gurugram Road is the direct cross-Aravalli road through the Bandhwari area to Gurugram. For the state road stretch at Bandhwari, start with Haryana PWD (Buildings and Roads), which administered the road concession. A junction or approach may have a separate maintainer. On the Gurugram side, GMDA tender 2026_HRY_538583_1 names the master-road stretch from Ghata red light to Faridabad T-point. That tender does not assign the whole Faridabad-Gurugram corridor to GMDA.

The road reaches Gurugram, not Manesar directly. Continue on Gurugram's network to NH-48 toward Manesar. A separate regional route uses the Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway, or KMP. NHAI's 59.063 km NH-148NA spur runs via the Faridabad-Ballabhgarh Bypass to the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway interchange by KMP. NHAI Package II runs from Jaitpur-Pushta Road to the Sector 62/65 dividing-road junction, at km 9.000 to 33.000. Package III continues from km 33.000 to 59.063. KMP itself is a separate Haryana expressway with HSIIDC project records. A local access road, the spur and KMP may meet at a junction without sharing a maintenance authority.

For repairs or access requests, give the two nearest junctions, direction of travel and, where available, the road asset ID or highway chainage. At an interchange, say whether the issue is on the main carriageway, a ramp, a service road or a local approach. The highway authority handles permission for work in its right of way even when the asset crossing it belongs to another agency, as illustrated by FMDA's water pipeline proposal to cross the Delhi to Agra highway at km 34.500.

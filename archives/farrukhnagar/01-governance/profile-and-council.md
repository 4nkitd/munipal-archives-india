# Farrukhnagar municipal profile and elected council

## Municipal profile and boundaries

The [Gurugram district administration](https://gurugram.gov.in/about-district/administrative-setup/) lists Farrukh Nagar as a municipal committee, separate from the tehsil and development block of the same name. The [Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department's record of municipal changes](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F84b74408-f266-4ae0-af57-a1d6cc199328.pdf) records that the committee was abolished on 2 March 2000 and reconstituted on 28 March 2006. The 1732 date in the [town history](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/about-us/about-farukhnagar-city) refers to the founding of the town, once a salt-trading centre, not the formation of the present committee.

The [state municipal directory](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists 20.21 square kilometres, a population of 21,206 and 19,606 properties. The directory does not specify a census year for the population figure. Property records are not a household count. The state department's notification index dates the [final Farrukh Nagar ward delimitation](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F6435f845-3b84-4a1e-b6ba-fbf9b72de187.pdf) to 22 September 2023. The district's [2025 ward and booth index](https://gurugram.gov.in/voter-list-for-mc-election-farrukh-nagar-2025/) lists wards 1 through 16.

[Sultanpur National Park](https://gurugram.gov.in/tourist-place/sultanpur-national-bird-sanctuary/) lies on the Gurugram-Farrukhnagar road. The district lists Garhi Harsaru Junction and Farrukhnagar among the nearby railway stations. The [Manesar formation notification](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/Municipal%20Corporation%20Manesar%20Formation%20Notification.pdf) traces parts of the corporation's western limit along the revenue boundaries of Garhi Harsaru, Dhorka and Wazirpur. Manesar lists all three in [Zone 1](https://mcmanesar.in/zones). These sources do not establish that Farrukhnagar's municipal limits touch those villages. To check a property or ward boundary, consult the committee's [ward delimitation records](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/library/ward-bandi), rather than using the block name or proximity to the park.

The [municipal office](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/contact-us) is in Farrukhnagar, Gurugram, Haryana 122506. The committee publishes `secymcfarrukhnagar@gmail.com`, and the [state property-tax portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Contact) lists `9812509323` for the local property-tax helpdesk.

## March 2025 election

Polling took place on 2 March 2025 and the results were declared on 12 March. The State Election Commission, Haryana, notified the results on 20 March in [notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4313](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/202507081951815499.pdf), under section 24(2) of the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973. **Birbal Saini**, an independent, won the separately elected **president (chairperson)** post, which was unreserved. [The Public World, reporting the State Election Commissioner's counting announcement](https://thepublicworld.com/archives/105461), gave Saini 3,364 votes and runner-up Dharmendra Kumar, also an independent, 2,207 votes. The margin was 1,157.

Independents won all 16 ward seats. The table uses the commission's English spellings, seat categories and affiliations at the time of the election. The vote totals in the last column come from [Amar Ujala's ward results](https://www.amarujala.com/delhi-ncr/gurgaon/birbal-saini-won-from-farrukhnagar-nagarpalika-gurgaon-news-c-24-1-gur1002-52769-2025-03-13), not the notification. The municipal site's elected-members page names MC Kaithal and lists 31 wards, so it was not used to identify Farrukhnagar's elected members.

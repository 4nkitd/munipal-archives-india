# Farrukhnagar wards, historic mohallas and colony geography

## Ward records and their limits

Farrukhnagar Municipal Committee has 16 numbered wards in the Local Government Directory-derived listing checked for this record. The Haryana Urban Local Bodies notification dated 26 August 2023, No. 9/82/2023-4C2, proposes boundaries for Wards 1 through 16 under the Haryana Municipal Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1977. It is explicitly a draft, with a ten-day objection period, not a final boundary instrument.

The state notification index separately lists "Regarding Final Notification of Wardbandi of Municipal Committee Farrukh Nagar" with the portal date 22 September 2023. That date is an index date and is not treated here as the notification's execution date. The municipal wardbandi page links English and Hindi PDFs. Their contents could not be retrieved during this check. Consequently, `wards.json` preserves the accessible August draft as historical boundary summaries, with a draft status on every record. It does not claim that those descriptions are the current final boundaries.

No verified ward-to-colony, ward-to-village, zone or engineering-division crosswalk was recovered. Empty arrays and null values mean unknown, not that a ward has no settlements. The municipal elected-members page contains an apparent copied reference to MC Kaithal and a Ward 17 entry. It is not used to override the 16-ward record or assign councillors.

## Historic walled town and mohallas

The historic core is a different geographic layer from modern electoral wards. The municipal city profile dates the town's establishment by Faujdar Khan to 1732. Haryana's Directorate of Archaeology and Museums identifies Shish Mahal, also written Sheesh Mahal, as his residential palace and dates its construction to 1733. Its sandstone Diwan-e-Aam and mirror decoration explain the palace name. Use that departmental date rather than conflicting dates on general travel pages.

Delhi Gate, also written Dilli Darwaza, and Jhajjar Gate, also written Jhajjari Darwaza, are historic gateways associated with the walled town. Patli Gate is another surviving gateway described in the secondary town history. The old bazaar and palace precinct should not be assigned to a numbered ward solely because an address uses a gate or monument name. "Sheesh Mahal mohalla" and "Jhajjar Gate mohalla" are not verified here as formal notified colony names.

The official Lal Dora property list records locality labels including Lal Dora Dehli Gate-1, Bahali Basti, Jain Mohalla and Balmiki Mohalla 1. Delhi Gate, main bazaar, Government Girls School Road and Jhajjar Road also occur in property addresses. These are evidence of address usage, not a current ward delimitation. Older ward numbers embedded in those addresses may predate the 2023 exercise. No property owners' names or individual property identifiers are reproduced here.

## Sultanpur, Manesar and the logistics corridor

Sultanpur-Gurugram Road is an explicit boundary reference in draft Wards 2 and 3. Wazirpur Road and Fazilpur Road identify other outward approaches. Sultanpur National Park lies on the Gurgaon-Farukhnagar Road according to the district tourist directory. A place being in Farrukhnagar tehsil or sharing PIN 122506 does not prove that it falls inside Municipal Committee Farrukhnagar.

The boundary with the Manesar municipal area requires the notified municipal-limit maps and revenue-estate boundaries. This record does not establish a direct common municipal boundary or include Sultanpur, Wazirpur, Fazilpur or Manesar as Farrukhnagar ward villages merely because roads lead toward them. Manesar's ward system must be consulted separately for land on that side of the wider region.

Warehousing operators use "Farrukhnagar" for a market extending along SH-15A and toward the Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway. Horizon's Farukhnagar I page identifies SH-15A access and a stated distance of 7 km to KMP. FM Logistic also identifies KMP proximity for its Farrukhnagar facility. These operator statements establish regional transport relationships, not municipal jurisdiction.

The distinction matters across the Jhajjar border. IndoSpace's Farukhnagar park gives its address as village Dadri Toe, tehsil Badli, district Jhajjar. Mapletree's Farukhnagar Logistics Park gives village Khalikpur, tehsil Badli, district Jhajjar. Neither park should be inserted into a Farrukhnagar municipal ward just because its commercial name includes Farukhnagar. Confirm district, revenue estate, khasra and municipal-limit notification for each warehouse.

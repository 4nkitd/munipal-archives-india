# Farrukhnagar public notices, regularized-colony documents and GRAP orders

## Scope and source dates

This record separates municipal website listings, state colony-notification indexes and regional air-quality orders. They are not one combined legal notification. The frontmatter date is the common municipal footer date, 28 March 2026. Individual document years, publication dates and order dates appear below. Research was checked on 22 November 2026.

The [municipal notifications page](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/services/notification) displayed "No Data Found". That is a statement about that page at retrieval, not proof that the municipality has issued no orders. Its separate library and news pages contain document links and notice titles.

No individual property is classified as regularized by this record.

For state-level cross-checking, the [DULB list of regularized colonies](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) includes entries titled "18-03-2025 29 notified Polygons" and "18-03-2025 45 colonies", both with portal publication dates of 26 September 2025. It also lists "Municipalities Status of 836 Colonies Data Notified 2025", published 13 October 2025. These are statewide index entries. Their totals are not Farrukhnagar colony counts, and the municipal 2025 PDF has not been matched to either 18 March state entry.

Before relying on an approval for a transaction or building proposal, obtain the actual notification and its map or polygon annexure and match the property to its boundary. A colony-list title or the existence of a Property ID does not by itself identify which parcel the notification covers.

## GRAP orders relevant to Farrukhnagar

Farrukhnagar is in Gurugram district. CAQM orders applying to the entire National Capital Region, and measures expressly naming Gurugram district, are relevant regional orders rather than orders issued by the municipal committee. The following official-source chronology preserves the dates and scope without claiming a live GRAP status on 22 November 2026.

The 17 November order states that construction and demolition sites subject to specific closure orders must not resume without a specific authorizing order. It records Delhi's AQI as 204 that day and refers to the revised GRAP schedule of 29 September 2026. The [official CAQM press release dated 17 November](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Connect/Press%20Releases/916bdfe5-1f3f-44d4-9a6e-284f3769a9ff.pdf) corroborates the Stage III revocation.

The separate 30 October measures expressly covered 5 to 20 November 2026. This archive does not extend that period to 22 November or assume no later order exists. Consult the [current CAQM GRAP orders index](https://caqm.nic.in/landingpage/pages/grap/grap-orders) before deciding whether construction, vehicle or office restrictions apply on a particular day.

## Municipal air-quality duties and reporting

The schedule attached to the 2 November order assigns urban local bodies duties including lifting municipal solid waste and construction waste, preventing illegal dumping and open burning, road sweeping, water sprinkling and enforcing construction-dust controls. Stage II includes daily mechanical or vacuum sweeping of identified roads, water sprinkling with dust suppressants, intensified construction-site inspections and targeted action at pollution hotspots.

These are regional duties assigned to municipal bodies and other agencies. The sources reviewed do not establish a Farrukhnagar-specific deployment roster, machine count or enforcement result. The [Gurugram district website](https://gurugram.gov.in/) publishes GRAP helpline 0124-2322877. Municipal complaints can also be routed through the [ULB grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) linked by the municipality.

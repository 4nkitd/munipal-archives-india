# Farrukhnagar citizen services, property tax, RTI and service timelines

## Scope and municipal contact

This guide records links and information published by Municipal Committee Farrukhnagar. The municipal pages display a common footer reading "Last updated: 28/3/2026". The frontmatter date records that displayed website date, not the enactment date of every service or a notification date. Sources were checked on 22 November 2026.

The office address is Municipal Committee Office, Farrukhnagar, Gurugram, Haryana, PIN 122506. The published email is secymcfarrukhnagar@gmail.com. The pages alternate between "Municipal Committee" and "Municipal Council"; this record follows the website heading and does not infer a change in legal status from footer wording.

## Official service entry points

The [municipal homepage](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/) links the following services. These are published routing links, not evidence that an application was submitted or that every downstream portal transaction was tested.

| Service | Official destination | Published purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax and property details | [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Property details and online payment; the state portal also identifies itself as the property-tax dues and No Dues Certificate portal |
| Birth and death registration | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Registration status and certificate download |
| Marriage registration | [Haryana marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) | Marriage registration status and certificate access |
| Public grievance | [ULB grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | File a municipal complaint |
| RTI application | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | The destination advertised by the municipal website for RTI applications |
| All online services | [ULB online portal](https://online.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Municipal website's broader service link |

The [state ULB citizen-service page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/desc_gri.aspx) describes birth/death services as certificate applications, record correction, delayed registration and inclusion of a child's name in a birth record. The Farrukhnagar pages reviewed do not publish a local document checklist, fee schedule or birth/death and marriage disposal timeline. Obtain those details from the selected portal service or municipal office rather than treating the homepage's certificate-download wording as an eligibility rule.

## Property tax, NDC and property records

Use the state property portal for property-tax dues and NDC-related work. The [municipal Right to Service table](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/library/right-service) specifically covers objections about property dues, size, floor details and property status on the NDC portal, new Property IDs and changes of owner or occupier. Its service periods and escalation authorities are transcribed below.

The [municipal property-tax page](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/library/property-tax) displays the following residential schedule.

| Residential area | Published rate per square yard |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 300 square yards | Rs 0.50 |
| 301 to 500 square yards | Rs 2.00 |
| 501 to 1,000 square yards | Rs 3.00 |
| 1,001 square yards to 2 acres | Rs 3.50 |
| More than 2 acres | Rs 5.00 |

For commercial office spaces and multiplexes, it displays Rs 6.00 per square foot up to 1,000 square feet and Rs 7.50 per square foot above 1,000 square feet. It says the rented or leased commercial area attracts 1.25 times those rates.

These figures preserve the municipal webpage, not a verified current assessment. The [state property-tax notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists "Property tax notification 2026 Councils and Committees", published 31 July 2026, and the [payment portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) advertises a calculator under the new policy dated 30 July 2026. Those later sources mean the municipal webpage should not be used alone to calculate a current bill. Check the applicable notification, assessment year, property category and portal demand before paying.

Retain the application acknowledgement and any deficiency communication when raising a delay. The municipal table does not state that every listed period means working days, so this record preserves "days" without adding that qualification.

The same page links the [Haryana Right to Service Act](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/RTS%201.pdf) and [ULB services under RTS](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/RTS%202%20%281%29.pdf). Both rows display 26 March 2014. That is the page's document metadata, not independently verified notification dates for each table entry.

## RTI references and limits of the published disclosure

The [municipal RTI page](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/library/right-information-rti) explains access to information held by a public authority and directs citizens denied information to the State Information Commission Haryana complaint mechanism. It links:

- [Right to Information Act, 2005](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/RTI%201%20%281%29.pdf).
- [Haryana Right to Information Rules](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/RTI2%20%281%29.pdf).
- [RTI fees](https://mcfarukhnagar.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/RTI%203%20%281%29.pdf).

The reviewed municipal page does not name a local State Public Information Officer or first appellate authority, give their contact details, or reproduce the fee amounts. Direct retrieval of the fee PDF returned HTTP 403 during research. No fee or local officer identity is inferred here. Ask the municipal office for the current SPIO designation and applicable payment method before submitting a paper application. The homepage's SARAL link is recorded as published; RTI service availability within the authenticated portal was not tested.

For an unresolved civic problem, use the municipal grievance route. For copies of existing records, orders, file notings or other held information, use RTI. A service-delay grievance under the published RTS table is separate from an RTI request.

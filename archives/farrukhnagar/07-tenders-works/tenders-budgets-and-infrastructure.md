# Farrukhnagar tenders, budgets and infrastructure, 2024 to 2026

## Coverage and procurement portals

This is a selected register, not a complete municipal works ledger. The municipal website links its Tenders service to Haryana eProcurement. The organisation name used in the official tender records is `Haryana Government||Urban Local Bodies||MC Farukh Nagar`. PHED procurements appear under a different organisation chain and are identified separately below. [S1]

Search by the exact tender ID when a linked detail page expires or loses its session. The official portal provides Tenders in Archive, Tenders Status, Results of Tenders, and Cancelled/Retendered searches. A tender value is the published procurement estimate, not the accepted contract price or the amount spent. A stated work period is not a verified completion date.

No Farrukhnagar-specific HEWP work ID, payment ledger or completion certificate was verified for this record. The eProcurement references below must not be described as HEWP payment records. No contractor, award value or completed-work claim is inferred from a notice.

## Sewerage operating evidence

Haryana State Pollution Control Board's September 2025 monthly progress report lists Farrukhnagar under "Other STPs" with 3 MLD existing capacity, 3 MLD capacity being utilized, operational status, and "Non Complying" compliance status. This is a dated regulatory observation, not a finding about performance in November 2026. It is also distinct from the 2026 PHED maintenance estimate. [S9]

MLD means million litres per day. Neither the STP table nor the storm-water tender establishes full household sewer connectivity.

## Municipal budgets, water supply and proposed roads

An adopted Farrukhnagar municipal budget, revised estimate, audited expenditure statement or municipality-specific water-supply tender value for 2024-25, 2025-26 or 2026-27 was not verified in the sources retrieved. No annual municipal income, expenditure, surplus or water-supply allocation is therefore stated. Adding selected tender estimates would not produce a municipal budget.

The Haryana budget speech dated 2 March 2026 proposes a six-lane road from Harsaru Bypass on the Gurugram-Pataudi Road to Jhajjar via Wazirpur, along with construction of the Farrukhnagar Bypass. That passage does not give a separate Farrukhnagar bypass cost. It is a state budget proposal, not a Municipal Committee tender award. [S10]

A Hindustan Times report published on 12 January 2026 describes proposed bypass, bus-stand, sewerage, drinking-water and electricity works. It explicitly reports that officials had not disclosed individual project budgets and that technical clearances and funding remained conditions. These proposals are not recorded here as sanctioned municipal expenditure or completed water-supply works. [S11]

For missing records, request the financial-year budget and council approval, administrative and technical sanctions, HEWP work ID, DNIT, corrigenda, award or work order, measurement book, paid bills and completion certificate from the relevant municipal or PHED office. The municipal website publishes the office email in the frontmatter. [S1]

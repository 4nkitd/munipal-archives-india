# Farrukhnagar development planning, KMP logistics and Sultanpur eco-sensitive zone

## Development plan 2031 and the unverified 2041 claim

The GMDA official urban-planning index lists Farukhnagar's Draft Development Plan 2031 under notification **CCP(NCR)/GGN-FGR/DDP-2031/2012/2660 dated 21 August 2012**. It links the draft map and explanatory notification directly to the Town and Country Planning Department. The same index lists the separate Gurugram-Manesar Urban Complex Final Development Plan 2031 under notification **CCP(NCR)/FDP-2031/GGN/2012/3541 dated 15 November 2012**, with an amendment dated 24 January 2017. These are different planning areas. [1]

A later TCP-hosted file is named `FDP-2031 AD Farrukhnagar_Plan.pdf`. Its PDF returned no usable text during this research. [Scanned map/PDF, visual source linked in source 2.] The filename is not sufficient evidence for its notification date, map schedule or subsequent amendments. The older GMDA index must not be taken as proof that no later final plan exists. Obtain the final gazette notification and its zoning regulations from TCP before deciding a parcel's permitted use.

No official Farrukhnagar-specific Development Plan 2041 notification was verified in this research. NCRPB's December 2021 document is explicitly titled *Draft Regional Plan 2041 for the National Capital Region*. It is a regional planning document, not a Farrukhnagar plot-level development permission. Claims about a local "2041 master plan" require a separate official notification and map. [3]

The frontmatter date belongs to the verified Sultanpur zonal master plan notification, not to an assumed Farrukhnagar final-plan date.

## KMP logistics corridor and the Manesar connection

The Haryana Enterprises and Employment Policy 2020, notified on **29 December 2020** as **25/05/2020-4IB-I**, describes the completed 135 km Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway and proposals for a Global Economic Corridor and Panchgram cities. It states that a belt of 2 to 6 km on either side had been designated a controlled area. These are state policy statements, not evidence that every roadside parcel is available for warehousing. [4]

The policy proposes earmarking a logistics zone along KMP, with CLU exemption for enterprises in that zone. A proposal to earmark a zone does not establish that a particular Farrukhnagar khasra has an exemption. Require the operative zone notification, its map and applicable conditions. The policy originally specified a five-year term from 1 January 2021, so its incentives should not be represented as automatically current in November 2026.

There is direct operator evidence of actual logistics activity. FM Logistic's facilities page reports its Farrukhnagar multi-client facility near KMP, with phase 1 operational since May 2022 and phase 2 live since January 2025. These are the operator's statements, not municipal occupancy certificates. [5]

HRIDC's February 2025 orbital rail environmental assessment describes proposed connectivity for Manesar and Farukh Nagar, connection to the Dedicated Freight Corridor at Prithla, and development of multimodal logistics hubs. This establishes the project's planning rationale, not that the railway or a particular freight terminal is operational. [6]

## Sultanpur ESZ legal basis and mapped extent

Haryana Forest and Wildlife Department notification **1012-F-4-2021/2521 dated 18 June 2021**, published in the **Haryana Government Gazette on 22 June 2021**, publishes the Sultanpur National Park ESZ Zonal Master Plan. Its English text occupies gazette pages 459 onward. It cites central notification **S.O. 191(E), 27 January 2010**, and amendment **S.O. 740(E), 17 February 2021**. [7]

Annexure A defines five rings measured from the national park boundary, at 300 metres, 500 metres, 1 km, 3 km and the outer ESZ limit up to 5 km. It describes boundaries along killa/murabba lines. For this master plan, the eastern boundary follows the Gurugram-Pataudi railway line and the southern boundary uses KMP, but the text expressly says some notified ESZ land lies beyond KMP. Crossing the expressway therefore does not establish exemption.

The notification's preamble names settlements including Sultanpur, Patli, Chandu, Budhera and Garhi Harsaru. A village name alone does not establish which ring contains a plot. Use the mapped boundary and authenticated revenue coordinates, with distance measured from the park boundary rather than its entrance or lake centre.

## Building and industrial restrictions

The following is a selective digest of Annexure C of the 2021 zonal master plan, not a replacement for the central notification, amendments or current court orders. [7]

| Location or activity | Verified provision in the 2021 English text |
| --- | --- |
| Within 300 metres | Any construction prohibited except tube-well chambers not exceeding 1,000 cubic feet. Existing structures must not be re-erected after their life cycle. |
| Between 300 and 500 metres | Buildings above two storeys, 30 feet, prohibited. Repair or reconstruction of existing buildings is regulated, with no more than 20% increase in plinth area and the stated height limit. |
| Within 500 metres | New high-tension transmission wires prohibited. |
| Within 1 km | New wood-based industries prohibited. Air-pressure horns and public-address systems or loudspeakers prohibited. |
| Within 3 km | New commercial construction prohibited, with stated exceptions for local residential needs and commercial construction of no more than 100 square metres inside existing village abadi deh. |
| Within 3 km | Post-ESZ red- and orange-category polluting industries and brick kilns prohibited. New mobile towers and glazed/glass-facade buildings also prohibited in the English schedule. |
| Up to 5 km | Commercial mining, stone quarrying and crushing units prohibited. |
| ESZ generally | New hot-mix and ready-mix-concrete plants prohibited. Untreated effluent and solid-waste discharge to land or natural water bodies prohibited. |

The gazette's Hindi and English glass-facade entries differ, stating 5 km and 3 km respectively. Seek written clarification rather than treating the English entry as an unconditional entitlement in the outer ring.

Groundwater extraction is limited to bona fide agriculture and local drinking-water consumption. Sale requires prior approval of the State Ground Water Authority. A warehouse approval does not itself authorize commercial groundwater extraction.

## Warehouses in the outer ESZ ring

Annexure C, regulated construction item B.4(c), permits consideration of grain godowns and warehouses with ancillary handling, storage and packaging in the agricultural zone **beyond 3 km and up to the ESZ limit**, with a maximum height of **15 metres**. It requires compliance with the applicable final GMUC or Farukhnagar development-plan zoning regulations and **prior NOC from the Chief Wildlife Warden**. It is not a blanket warehouse exemption. [7]

Item B.4(b) makes the urbanizable land uses of the notified GMUC and Farukhnagar plans subject to the prohibited-activity conditions. The same provision addresses white-, green- and orange-category industries in the agricultural zone beyond 3 km, subject to approval by the government or competent authority. Do not extend that clause to red-category industry.

Road widening, strengthening and new roads require NOC from the Forest and Wildlife Department and District Monitoring Committee under item B.5. Proximity to a planned road or KMP interchange does not remove that requirement.

## Municipal and metropolitan responsibilities

Farrukhnagar, Municipal Corporation Manesar and Gurugram are linked through freight movement and the GMUC planning interface, but a transport or economic linkage is not a municipal boundary notification. The 2021 ESZ schedule expressly names TCP, GMDA, DULB, Forest/Wildlife and concerned panchayats for different activities. It does not give MC Manesar blanket approval authority over Farrukhnagar. [7]

For a proposed warehouse or building:

1. Establish the revenue estate, khasra numbers and current notified local-body boundary. Do not infer jurisdiction from a Gurugram postal address.
2. Obtain the applicable final development-plan map, zoning regulations and amendments from TCP or the competent planning authority.
3. Establish the ESZ boundary and ring through the Wildlife Department. Check whether land beyond KMP remains notified ESZ.
4. Obtain the wildlife NOC where required, plus the applicable CLU or licence, building approval, pollution permissions, fire clearance and lawful road access.
5. Confirm infrastructure availability separately. A proposed land use or corridor project is not evidence of an installed sewer, water supply or operating freight terminal.

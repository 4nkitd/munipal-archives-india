# Firozabad Nagar Nigam: municipal area and wards

## Which local body?

The [district's administrative list](https://firozabad.nic.in/municipal-corporation/) identifies Firozabad as a Nagar Nigam. Shikohabad, Tundla and Sirsaganj have their own Nagar Palika Parishads. Jasrana, Fariha and Eka appear separately as Nagar Panchayats. A Firozabad district address alone does not establish that a property falls within the corporation.

## Ward count and identifiers

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' [City Finance profile](https://www.cityfinance.in/municipal-data/city/firozabad) gives the municipal area as 35.49 km² and the ward count as 70. Its [local-body dataset](https://www.cityfinance.in/api/v1/ulb) attributes both figures to the urban local body. The Firozabad Municipal Corporation entry has LGD code `249079` and census code `800816`. Neither source gives a boundary schedule or an individual ward map.

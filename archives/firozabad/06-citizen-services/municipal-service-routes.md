# Property tax queries and public grievances in Firozabad

## Property and payment records

The district's [Urban Development Department page](https://firozabad.nic.in/urban-development-department/) names e-NagarSewa. In the [state portal](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/home), open **Citizen Services → Property Tax**. Choose **Search Property** for a property record, **Track Online Payment** for a payment already made, or **Search Receipt** for a receipt. The menu also lists **View and Pay House/Water Tax**. These are state portal routes; availability for a particular Firozabad property was not verified.

## Filing a grievance

The district's [Public Grievance service](https://firozabad.nic.in/service/public-grievance/) directs Firozabad residents to Jansunwai, the Uttar Pradesh Integrated Grievance Redressal System. It accepts complaints, lets the complainant track a lodged grievance and provides the department's response. The district lists the receiving location as “Concerned Offices”; it does not identify a municipal ward counter or promise a resolution time.

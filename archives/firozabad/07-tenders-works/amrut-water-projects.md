# AMRUT 2.0 projects for Suresh Nagar water supply and Dholpura Pond

## Firozabad project register

The [ministry's approved-project register](https://amrut.mohua.gov.in/approvedProjects/state) lists these works under **Uttar Pradesh → Firozabad**:

| Project title as listed | Project cost (₹ crore) | Milestone |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 24X7 Water Supply Scheme in Suresh Nagar Operating Zone of Firozabad City | 11.1743 | Contract Awarded |
| Rejuvenation of 164 Dholpura Pond | 2.3 | Contract Awarded |

The first scheme covers the named operating zone; the entry does not say that all of Firozabad receives round-the-clock water. The register gives project costs, not payments, and does not report commissioning dates for either work.

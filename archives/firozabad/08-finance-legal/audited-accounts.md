# Firozabad audited accounts: FY 2023-24

## What the corporation reported

The [City Finance dashboard](https://www.cityfinance.in/municipal-data/city/firozabad) attributes these figures to the audited financial statements for 2023-24. It displays whole crores of rupees.

| Dashboard measure | ₹ crore |
| --- | ---: |
| Total tax revenue | 8 |
| Total own revenue | 16 |
| Total grant | 149 |
| Total revenue | 165 |
| Total expenditure | 144 |
| Total balance sheet size | 483 |

The figures describe accounts for the financial year. They do not show what the council approved in its budget. The dashboard marks 30 March 2026 as its last update; it gives no publication date for the underlying statements.

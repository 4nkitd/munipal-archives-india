# Constitution of Gandhidham Municipal Corporation

## Effective date and legal basis

Notification `KV-05 of 2025-UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/6445/P Section`, published in the Gujarat Government Gazette, Extraordinary, Part I-A, Extra No. 3, constituted the Municipal Corporation of the City of Gandhidham on 1 January 2025. It cites Article 243Q of the Constitution and section 5(1) of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. It brought the remaining provisions of that Act into operation in the corporation on the same date under section 1(3).

## Transition from the municipality

The order identifies Gandhidham Municipality and the gram panchayats listed in companion notification `GV-04` as the predecessor areas. It states that state government land within those areas would not vest in the corporation. The [1951 Census handbook for Kutch](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/29295) already lists Gandhidham as a municipality in Table A-V. The original municipal founding date is unverified. The corporation's [city history](https://gandhidhamcity.in/history) describes the development of the township in the early 1950s, not a legal act constituting the municipality.

The state appointed Amit Arora, IAS, as administrator from 1 January 2025 for one year or until councillors were elected in the first corporation election, whichever came first. This is a historical appointment, not a current contact listing. The department lists the [Gandhidham Development Authority](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/GandhidhamDevelopment.php) separately.

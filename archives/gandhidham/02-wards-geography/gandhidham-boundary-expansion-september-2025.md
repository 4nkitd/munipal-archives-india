# September 2025 expansion of Gandhidham corporation limits

## Notification and schedule

The Gujarat Urban Development and Urban Housing Department signed notification `GV-351 of 2025 UDUHD/MSM/e-file/18/2025/5509/P Section` on 25 September 2025. The Gujarat Government Gazette published it on 29 September 2025 in Extraordinary Part IV-A, Extra No. 134. The order adds the following scheduled villages and/or gram panchayats to the existing local limits under Article 243Q(2) of the Constitution:

| Schedule entry | Area |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Padana |
| 2 | Chudva |
| 3 | Mithi Rohar |
| 4 | Khari Rohar |
| 5 | Bharapar |

The notification states that government land in the stated areas would not vest in the corporation. It does not assign ward numbers. The same Gazette file also carries `GV-352` for Mahesana; that schedule is separate.

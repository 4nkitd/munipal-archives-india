# Initial larger urban area of Gandhidham

## January 2025 schedule

Notification `GV-04 of 2025-UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/6445/P Section` specified the area of Gandhidham Municipality and six gram panchayats as a larger urban area under Article 243Q(2) of the Constitution. The Gujarat Government Gazette published the schedule on 1 January 2025 in Extraordinary Part IV-A, Extra No. 2.

| Schedule entry | Gram panchayat |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Kidana |
| 2 | Gadpadar |
| 3 | Antarjal |
| 4 | Shinay |
| 5 | Meghpar-Borichi |
| 6 | Meghpar-Kumbhardi |

These are the entries in the founding schedule, not electoral wards. For the five subsequent additions see [notification GV-351](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/P/Notification/MAHESANA_GANDHIDHAM_MC_AREA_INCLUDE.pdf).

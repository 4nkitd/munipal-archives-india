# July 2025 determination of Gandhidham wards and seats

## July determination

Notification `KV-236 of 2025 UDUHD/MSM/e-file/18/2025/0418/P Section`, dated 9 July 2025, cites section 5 of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949 and the 2011 Census. It sets 13 wards and 52 councillor seats.

| Reservation | Seats | Of which for women in that category |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled Castes | 10 | 5 |
| Scheduled Tribes | 1 | Not stated |
| Backward Classes | 14 | 7 |
| Women overall | 26 | Includes the women already counted in the Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes rows |

[Scanned PDF, visually checked.] The order does not number individual wards, draw boundaries or allocate seats to specific wards. It predates the [September 2025 expansion](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/P/Notification/MAHESANA_GANDHIDHAM_MC_AREA_INCLUDE.pdf). The [corporation homepage](https://gandhidhamcity.in/) mentions 15 wards without identifying the edition of the ward scheme, so the July figures are kept as a dated record, not a current ward count.

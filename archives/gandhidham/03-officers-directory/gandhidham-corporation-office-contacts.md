# Gandhidham Municipal Corporation office contacts

## Municipal office

The corporation lists its office at Plot No. 35, Sector 9, Gandhidham, Kachchh 370201, with phone `02836-231610` and email `gmckachchh@gmail.com`. The [Kachchh district directory](https://kachchh.nic.in/public-utility/gmc/) gives the same address and email, and lists `02836-226573` in its phone field.

The Gujarat Urban Development and Urban Housing Department's [corporations directory](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/corporations.php) lists `mc-gmc-kut@gujarat.gov.in` for the commissioner's office. These sources do not assign a ward number to the headquarters or publish counter hours in their contact entries.

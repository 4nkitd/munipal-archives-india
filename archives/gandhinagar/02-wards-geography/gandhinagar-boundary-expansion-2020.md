# Gandhinagar municipal boundary expansion, June 2020

## Authority and scope

The Gujarat Urban Development and Urban Housing Department issued this notification on 18 June 2020 under Article 243Q(2) of the Constitution of India. It cites the state's 16 March 2010 notification constituting the City of Gandhinagar under section 3(2) of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. The 2020 notification specifies the scheduled areas as larger urban areas and adds them to Gandhinagar Municipal Corporation. It states that government land in the scheduled areas does not vest in the corporation.

| Schedule | Area included |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Pethapur Municipality |
| 2 | Kudasan, Raisan, Randesan, Sargasan, Koba, Vasana Hadmatiya, Vavol, Kolavada, Por, Ambapur, Amiyapur, Bhat, Sughad, Zundal, Khoraj, Koteshwar, Nabhoi and Randheja |
| 3 | Specified survey and block numbers in Tarapur, Uvarsad, Dholakuva, Indroda, Lavarpur and Shahpur, and all survey numbers of Basan Gram Panchayat in the GUDA area |

## How to read the schedules

Schedule 3 lists land by survey or block number. A village name in that schedule does not mean the notification includes the whole village. Use pages 2 and 3 of the [official notification](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/P/Notification/GMC.pdf) to check a parcel. The schedule uses Gandhinagar Urban Development Authority planning-scheme references to identify some parcels. The annexation instrument itself is a Gujarat government notification, not a development-authority act. It does not assign electoral wards.

# Gandhinagar ward and seat composition, July 2020

## Number of wards and seats

On 8 July 2020, the Gujarat Urban Development and Urban Housing Department issued notification No. KV-86 of 2020-PRC-102020-2666-P under section 5 of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. The notification cites the June 2020 expansion of the corporation limits and uses Census 2011 figures as the basis for determining the ward and seat totals. It sets 11 wards and 44 elected councillors, superseding the 22 January 2016 determination for Gandhinagar.

| Reservation in the July 2020 notification | Seats | Included seats for women |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Scheduled Castes | 5 | 3 |
| Scheduled Tribes | 1 | None specified |
| Backward Classes | 4 | 2 |
| Women across all categories | 22 | Includes the women's reservations listed above |

The notification preserves actions and pending proceedings under the previous determination and allows sitting councillors to complete their elected terms. These are citywide figures for this edition. The notice does not identify ward boundaries or allocate particular reserved seats to numbered wards, and it does not establish the current election roster.

# Gandhinagar Municipal Corporation office directory

## Office contact

Gandhinagar District Administration lists the corporation at Gandhinagar Mahanagar Seva Sadan, First Floor, M. S. Building, near Family Court, Sector 11, Gandhinagar 382011. The listed office telephone is 079-23220440 and the email is `gmc8gandhinagar@gmail.com`. The [Gujarat corporation directory](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/corporations.php), row 7, separately publishes the same email and links to the [corporation website](https://gandhinagarmunicipal.com/).

The district entry does not identify this number as a service-specific helpline or publish counter hours. Its printed update date is 28 September 2018; call ahead to confirm the office location and the counter you need.

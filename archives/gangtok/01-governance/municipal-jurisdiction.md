# Gangtok municipal jurisdiction and office

## Municipal area

The Urban Development Department's [outer boundary description](https://www.sikkim.gov.in/DepartmentsMenu/urban-dev-housing-department/Municipality/gangtok-municipal-corporation) uses Tashi View Point, Eastern Bye Pass Road, Maney Jhora and the Hanuman Tok ridge on the eastern side. The southern description follows parts of the Rongyek Road and Syari area, then the Tibetology and Defence boundaries towards Ranipool. The western stretch follows Rani Khola to Takste Jhora; the northern stretch reaches Tashi View Point via the Taktse link road and North Sikkim Highway.

These are reference landmarks, not a test for whether a property is inside a municipal ward. The same state department page shows older ward numbering. For a ward boundary use the [December 2025 determination](../02-wards-geography/ward-reorganization-2025.md), not the older ward table. The State Election Commission's [municipality list](https://www.sikkim.gov.in/departments/state-election-commission/municipalities) distinguishes the 21-ward Gangtok corporation from the other urban local bodies listed in Gangtok district.

## Municipal office

The corporation's [About Us page](https://gmc.sikkim.gov.in/about-us) lists its office near Deorali Stand on Deorali Road and publishes office hours of Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 4 pm. The [district directory](https://gangtokdistrict.nic.in/public-utility/25643/) also locates the corporation at Deorali. The corporation page displays different PIN codes in its office section and its footer, so confirm the postal PIN with the office before mailing documents.

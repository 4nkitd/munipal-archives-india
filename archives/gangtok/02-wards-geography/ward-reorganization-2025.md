# Gangtok ward determination for the 2026 municipal election

## Gazette and electoral roll

Notification No. `30/UDD/2025`, dated 12 December 2025, was published on 13 December 2025 in Sikkim Extraordinary Gazette No. 460. It superseded Notification No. `34/UDD/2020` of 31 December 2020 under sections 8 and 9 of the Sikkim Municipalities Act, 2007. Schedule I starts on page 12 of the State Election Commission's compiled PDF. The schedule gives the east, south, west and north limits for each ward and identifies the assembly constituencies involved. The filename's `20260210` is not the Gazette date.

The district's municipal roll notice dated 19 March 2026 lists polling parts, polling areas and polling stations by ward. Its first ward is Bojoghari-2nd Mile, with polling parts at Bojoghari and Enchey schools. Upper Burtuk is Ward 2; the older Urban Development Department web table calls it Ward 1. The district page also lists Singtam separately. Do not apply the Singtam entries to the Gangtok corporation.

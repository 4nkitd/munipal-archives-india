# Garbage user charges for Gangtok under the March 2025 state notice

## Notification No. 11/UDD/2025

The Urban Development Department issued Notification No. `11/UDD/2025` on 26 March 2025. It revises the fees for removing offensive material and rubbish from residential buildings, commercial buildings and government offices in Sikkim's urban local bodies. It cites section 7(e) of the Sikkim (Repeal and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1985 and rule 3 of the Sanitation Rules for Towns of Sikkim, 2000. It supersedes earlier notifications on the subject and says the revised fees take effect immediately.

## Selected monthly charges

Use the Municipal Corporation column for Gangtok. The notice has separate columns for municipal councils and nagar panchayats.

| Consumer category | Municipal Corporation charge per month |
| --- | ---: |
| Household | ₹250 |
| Small shop | ₹100 |
| Big shop | ₹200 |
| Garage | ₹1,000 |
| Hotel with up to 5 rooms | ₹350 |
| Hotel with 6 to 10 rooms | ₹750 |
| Hotel with 11 to 15 rooms | ₹1,000 |
| Hotel with 16 to 20 rooms | ₹1,500 |
| Hotel with more than 20 rooms | ₹2,000 |

These are selected rows from page 1, not the full schedule. The notice contains separate categories, such as luxury hotel classes, that should be checked before using a rate from this table. See the corporation's [circulars page](https://gmc.sikkim.gov.in/circulars) for the notice and the [solid waste service page](https://gmc.sikkim.gov.in/solid-waste-management) for collection and segregation information.

# Gangtok online trade licence applications and renewals

## Where to apply

On the [corporation's Trade License page](https://gmc.sikkim.gov.in/trade-license), choose **Get Started** under **New Application** for a new business or commercial establishment. For an existing individual licence, use **Proceed to Renewal** under **License Renewal**. The portal says renewals for partners and organizations are not yet available. This records what the portal displayed on 22 November 2026; it does not establish a deadline for those renewals.

The [district service page](https://gangtokdistrict.nic.in/service/e-trade-licence/) still points to the former trade licence site. The old site displays a notice directing new applications, renewals and municipal enquiries to `gmc.sikkim.gov.in`. Start at the corporation portal, not the old link. No application was submitted to verify a fee, processing time or approval.

For the rules rather than the filing route, the [municipal circulars](https://gmc.sikkim.gov.in/circulars) list Notification No. `08/UDD/2025`, dated 26 March 2025, for the Sikkim Trade License and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) Rules, 2025.

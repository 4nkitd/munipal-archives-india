# Gaya electoral wards in Bihar SEC Form 6

## The unchanged-wards entry

The Bihar State Election Commission's [unchanged-wards register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC) marks Gaya Nagar Nigam `अपरिवर्तित` (unchanged) at row 15. Form 6 under rule 29(1) identifies the municipality as Nagar Nigam Gaya, subdivision Gaya Sadar. It gives a separate row for every ward from 1 to 53, including a Hindi boundary description and a map link. The population columns are headed 2011 Census: 474,093 in total, including 47,395 Scheduled Castes, 583 Scheduled Tribes and 426,115 in the Other column.

## Reading the boundaries

The railway line and the Falgu appear in multiple ward rows. The river is an eastern boundary for ward 5 and a western boundary for ward 53, so its name alone cannot identify a ward. Use the complete directional description and the map for the selected ward in Form 6. “Unchanged” refers to the SEC's classification in this ward-formation edition; it is not a claim that no boundary or polling arrangement changed afterward.

# Gaya holding tax and municipal user-charge services

## Holding tax

Gaya Ji Municipal Corporation groups its revenue services under Property / Holding Tax. Search Assessment and Self Assessment are distinct from Search Property, which looks up a holding, and from View Payment Details and Print Receipt, which deal with past payments. The site also lists an online payment route, a tax calculator and Know Your Tax Collector.

The [self-assessment entry](https://gayamunicipal.net/pmc/public/property/ulb_selection.html?prop=5) opens a lookup that accepts a PID or old PID, or a SAS number. The citizen page links a downloadable self-assessment form and separate road classification documents for 2024–25 and 2025–26. Those year labels identify the published lists; they do not establish a filing or payment deadline.

## Water, waste and assistance

The portal lists separate Water User Charge and Waste User Charge menus, with consumer lookup, bills and payments. The waste services panel also lists a receipt lookup. These are separate tracks from a holding tax assessment. The portal gives 1800-121-8545 as its public helpline and links a feedback or complaint form.

The service page and self-assessment lookup were reachable on verification. The advertised holding-tax payment link returned a server error at the lookup stage when checked; no payment or receipt issuance was attempted. Use the public helpline if the payment lookup does not open, rather than relying on the menu link as proof that payments are available.

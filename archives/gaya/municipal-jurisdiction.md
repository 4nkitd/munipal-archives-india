# Municipal jurisdiction and functions of Gaya Nagar Nigam

## The corporation is not the district

The district public utility directory lists Gaya Municipal Corporation as a municipal office. The Bihar State Election Commission identifies Gaya as a Nagar Nigam in Gaya Sadar in its [ward-formation register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0). That register separately lists Bodhgaya as a Nagar Parishad in the same subdivision. A district or subdivision name is therefore not enough to establish which municipal body covers a property. The corporation's own citizen portal uses the name [Gaya Ji Municipal Corporation](https://gayamunicipal.net/pmc/public/index.html).

## State powers and city functions

Sections 7–8 of the [2007 Bihar municipal text published by the State Election Commission](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/old-sec/download/The%20Bihar%20Municipal%20Act-2007%28%20English%29.pdf) describe state classification of urban areas and alteration of municipal limits by notification. Sections 12 and 14 describe territorial wards and the Election Commission's role in councillor elections. The English copy is listed as the Act on the commission portal but headed “Bill”; use the enacted Gazette and amendments for legal reliance.

Chapter VIII of that text describes core functions including water supply, drainage and sewerage, solid waste management, road maintenance and street lighting. The text allows the municipality to provide core services itself or arrange them through another agency. That distinction matters when looking for the body operating a particular service. The SEC ward register documents electoral geography; it does not identify the operator for every road, drain or water connection or establish the corporation's original constitution date.

# Ghaziabad Nagar Nigam and its jurisdiction

## Corporation and district

Ghaziabad Nagar Nigam (GNN) is the municipal corporation for Ghaziabad city, Uttar Pradesh. The corporation dates its upgrade from a Municipal Board to 31 August 1994, after the 74th Constitutional Amendment and state conformity legislation. The record date is the upgrade date, not the publication date of the profile page.

The corporation lists City, Kavi Nagar, Vijay Nagar, Mohan Nagar and Vasundhara as its five administrative zones and reports 100 municipal wards. Its head office is in Navyug Market, opposite Old Bus Stand, Ghaziabad 201001. The [district directory](https://ghaziabad.nic.in/en/public-utility/nagar-nigam-ghaziabad/) confirms the Navyug Market address and links to the corporation's property services.

## Other authorities

The Uttar Pradesh e-NagarSewa directory lists Ghaziabad under [Nagar Nigam](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/showUlbList?type=NN). It lists Khora Makanpur, Loni, Modinagar and Muradnagar as separate [Nagar Palika Parishads](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/showUlbList?type=NPP), and Dasna, Faridnagar, Niwari and Patla as separate [Nagar Panchayats](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/showUlbList?type=NP) in the district. Their wards and records are outside this archive.

[Ghaziabad Development Authority](https://gdaghaziabad.in/) is a separate authority with its own development-area, plans, housing and property allotment records. GDA planning zones and budgets are not Nagar Nigam ward assignments or budgets. This profile does not establish a corporation boundary for any individual parcel.

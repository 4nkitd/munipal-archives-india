# Ghaziabad municipal wards by zone

## Ward numbers in the corporation roster

The corporation's [Parshad List page](https://ghaziabadnagarnigam.in/parshad-list.aspx) links a three-page zone-wise and ward-wise list of newly elected councillors. Each ward number from 1 to 100 appears once. The roster has no printed issue date. The PDF's July 2023 modification metadata does not establish a publication date or confirm who holds a seat now.

## Limits of the sources

The corporation's separate [zone and mohalla webpage](https://ghaziabadnagarnigam.in/zones-and-wards.aspx) repeats some ward numbers in more than one zone and includes an entry numbered `880`. For example, it lists Ward 46 in three zones, while the councillor roster and the corporation's [GIS assessment notice](../04-notifications-orders/gis-property-assessment-notice.md) both put it in Kavi Nagar. This archive uses the roster for zone assignments and does not treat the mohalla webpage as a delimitation schedule.

For election results, use the [Uttar Pradesh State Election Commission's 2023 urban local body portal](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ULBElection2023.html) and look for the municipal corporation councillor category, not municipal council or town panchayat members. No current councillor directory, complete locality allocations, or legal ward perimeters are asserted here.

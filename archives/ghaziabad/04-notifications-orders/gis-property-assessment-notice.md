# GIS property assessment list notice for 17 Ghaziabad wards

## Assessment and publication

The corporation published this Hindi notice under section 207 of the municipal corporation act of 1959. It concerns a GIS-based survey of residential and non-residential buildings within the municipal limits. The notice says 96 of 100 wards had been surveyed, four were in progress and 44 had completed joint matching and verification by the corporation and its survey firm. Those were counts at the time of the notice.

| Zone | Wards whose verified assessment lists were announced |
| --- | --- |
| City | 11, 17, 49, 50 |
| Kavi Nagar | 24, 30, 42, 46 |
| Vijay Nagar | 14, 27, 48 |
| Mohan Nagar | 29, 34, 90 |
| Vasundhara | 21, 57, 98 |

## Date and objections

The printed effective date is 1 April 2022. The notice does not identify a separate publication date; the scanner filename is not evidence of one. The period for objections was 30 days from publication, not from the effective date. The notice directed building owners, occupants and taxpayers to the respective zone office or room 07 of the Chief Tax Assessment Officer's office at corporation headquarters. It does not establish a current objection window.

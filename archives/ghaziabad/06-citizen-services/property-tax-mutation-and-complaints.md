# Ghaziabad property tax, mutation and civic complaints

## Property services

| Service | Official entry point | What the portal shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax and property lookup | [Online GNN](https://onlinegnn.com/) | The [property search](https://onlinegnn.com/SearchHouseDetailsF.aspx) accepts a property PIN, name, house number or latest receipt number, with a zone selector. The portal also links to online payment and receipts. |
| Self-assessment | [Online GNN](https://onlinegnn.com/) | Select Self Assessment on the property services landing page. |
| Mutation | [GNN mutation portal](https://mutation.onlinegnn.com/) | Select Apply For Mutation. The separate Pay Mutation Fees page is for payments against an application and property PIN. |
| Municipal user charges | [GNN user charges portal](https://uc.onlinegnn.com/SearchHouseDetailsF.aspx) | Use the property search for the user-charges account, separate from the property tax search. |

## Public helpdesk and charter

The [GNN contact page](https://ghaziabadnagarnigam.in/contact-us.aspx) lists the Navyug Market head office, zonal offices, `gzb.nagar.nigam@gmail.com` and `1800-1803-012`. The corporation also publishes `1533` for municipal issues and `14420` for sewer and septic tank cleaning. Its main site links the [Ghaziabad 311 app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.everythingcivic.ghaziabad&hl=en_IN) for public grievances.

The [citizen charter](https://ghaziabadnagarnigam.in/citizen-charter.aspx) gives targets of one day for drain cleaning and missed garbage collection, four days for a pothole, one day for sewer choking or overflow, and one day for street-light failure. These are published targets. No phone, app submission, payment or resolution was tested.

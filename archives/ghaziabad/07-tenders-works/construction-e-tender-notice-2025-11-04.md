# Ghaziabad municipal construction e-tender notice of 4 November 2025

## Notice and deadlines

The corporation issued notice 098/Nirman/2025-26 on 4 November 2025. It directs bidders to Uttar Pradesh's e-procurement portal for bid documents and for payment of the listed earnest money and nonrefundable tender fee. The work schedule has items numbered 1–59. Some entries name a ward; others name a zone or work site only.

| Works in notice | Bid documents available from | Submission deadline | Opening scheduled |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Items 1–26 | 7 November 2025 | 14 November 2025, 3:00 p.m. | 14 November 2025, 3:30 p.m. |
| Items 27 onward | 10 November 2025 | 25 November 2025, 3:00 p.m. | 25 November 2025, 3:30 p.m. |

## Examples from the schedule

| Item | Work | Estimated cost | Completion period |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 3 | Boundary wall for the GNN headquarters in Mohan Nagar Zone | ₹24,300,000 | 6 months |
| 5 | Beautification of the Chiranjeev Vihar community centre in Ward 56 | ₹4,885,585 | 3 months |
| 6 | Shopping complex in Mohan Nagar Zone | ₹67,245,391 | 24 months |
| 26 | Culvert over a drain in Shastri Nagar, Ward 47 | ₹1,034,967 | 3 months |

These are tender estimates and proposed periods, not contract awards or completion records. Both submission deadlines have passed. No award or corrigendum is asserted here.

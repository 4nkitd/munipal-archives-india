# Ghaziabad Nagar Nigam revised budget for 2025–26

## Income and expenditure at a glance

The corporation's [budget index](https://ghaziabadnagarnigam.in/BudgetBalanceNoties.aspx) lists both a budget and a revised budget for 2025–26. The table below transcribes the comparative summary on page 1 of the revised budget scan. All amounts are in rupees. The August 2025 column is a separate comparison in that statement, not a publication date.

| Item | 2024–25 actual | 2025–26 proposed | 2025–26 August 2025 column | 2025–26 revised |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Opening balance | 6,277,788,327 | 9,867,848,872 | 9,867,848,872 | 11,746,117,676 |
| Income | 13,114,921,952 | 27,566,122,000 | 5,225,825,686 | 25,101,122,000 |
| Opening balance plus income | 19,392,710,279 | 37,433,970,872 | 15,093,674,557 | 36,847,239,676 |
| Expenditure | 9,524,861,408 | 30,448,096,000 | 3,347,556,881 | 28,488,096,000 |
| Closing balance | 9,867,848,872 | 6,985,874,872 | 11,746,117,676 | 8,359,143,676 |

Figures follow the printed statement. Some comparative columns differ by one rupee from arithmetic on the displayed inputs. The proposed column above comes from the revised statement, not a transcription of the separately posted original budget.

Page 2 lists revised income from government grants and other heads at ₹19,721,100,000, revenue at ₹4,094,132,000 and fees at ₹627,690,000. Revised estimates are not audited year-end accounts. The fiscal year is verified, but the document's issue and adoption dates were not.

# Giridih Municipal Corporation formation and revenue villages

## Formation order and correction

Jharkhand Urban Development & Housing Department notification 6156, dated 25 September 2017, constituted Giridih Nagar Nigam from the former Class B Giridih Nagar Parishad and 26 surrounding revenue villages under the Jharkhand Municipal Act, 2011. It says the change took effect on publication. The [department's notice register](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/Other/Notifications.aspx) dates the notification 25 September; the archived scan does not identify a separate gazette publication date.

[Corrigendum 7475, dated 4 December 2017](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/Handlers/NoticesHdler.ashx?id=04122017041205PM.pdf), replaced Appendix I and left the other provisions in force. The corrected appendix records 41.33 km² and 114,533 people for the former council, and 31.99 km² and 67,099 people for the added villages across 13 former panchayat areas. The combined figures are 73.32 km² and 181,632 people, using 2011 Census population data.

# Giridih Form A polling stations for the 2026 municipal election

## Ward and station identifiers

In its 20 January 2026 posting, the district election office links Form A for Giridih Nagar Nigam (Class B) separately from Dhanwar and Badki Sariya Nagar Panchayats. The corporation's 36-page schedule identifies the civic body as VII Giridih / 03 Giridih Nagar Nigam. The station identifier appends the ward and booth number, such as 01/1 or 36/4. Each ward sheet records the building, the streets or localities served, the number of assigned voters, the maximum distance to the station and the room area.

| Ward | Voters on sheet | Proposed stations | Buildings | Examples |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 4,233 | 5 | 3 | 01/1, Upgraded Middle School, Pethiyatand (1,000 voters); 01/2 and 01/3, west and east sections of Sharda Girls Middle School, Pachamba |
| 36 | 3,684 | 4 | 3 | 36/1, Ambedkar Bhavan, Bhorandiha (1,176 voters); 36/3 and 36/4, west and east sections of the primary school at Harsinghraydih |

The number of wards also appears in [notification 6205](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/Handlers/NoticesHdler.ashx?id=17102017121022PM.pdf), issued 26 September 2017 and published in Jharkhand Gazette extraordinary issue 739 on 4 October 2017. The department calculated 35 base wards and one additional ward. Its [18 October 2017 corrigendum 6516](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/Handlers/NoticesHdler.ashx?id=24102017021005PM.pdf) corrected the Census base to 181,632, the population above the 150,000 base to 31,632 and the printed ratio to 0.632, leaving the ward count unchanged. Form A records electoral arrangements for this cycle; it is not a legal boundary schedule.

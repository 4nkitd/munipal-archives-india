The budget estimates ₹11,00,00,000 in tax revenue and ₹2,34,65,75,000 in grants and contributions for specific purposes. The same scan shows a 2025–26 budget estimate of ₹1,20,81,47,901 for total expenditure, which matches the index Amount for that year. Compare equivalent lines in the budgets rather than treating the index Amount column as a single measure across years.

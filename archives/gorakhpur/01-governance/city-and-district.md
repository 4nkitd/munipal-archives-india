# Gorakhpur city corporation and the district's town areas

## Which municipal office serves the town?

The district administration lists the Nagar Nigam at Town Hall, Gorakhpur. Bansgaon, Barhalganj, Gola Bazar, Kasba Sangrampur Unwal, MunderaBazar, Pipiganj, Pipraich and Sahjanwa have separate town-area entries. For example, the directory places Barhalganj in Gola tehsil and Pipiganj in Campierganj tehsil. A Gorakhpur district address alone does not identify a city ward.

## City administration and office

The [city corporation](https://gorakhpurnagarnigam.up.gov.in/) describes an elected body headed by a mayor. Its portal lists the Municipal Commissioner separately and directs residents to departments including JalKal, street lighting and construction. The corporation gives its address as 1, Town Hall, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh 273001, its office telephone as 0551-2333015 and its toll-free helpline as 1533. Neither the district directory nor the municipal overview gives the legal description of the city limits.

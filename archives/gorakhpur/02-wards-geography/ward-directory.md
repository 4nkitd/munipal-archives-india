# Gorakhpur's 80 municipal ward names

## Reading the municipal roster

The city's Parshad List gives a serial number and a ward number in separate columns. The table below uses the ward number and the Hindi spelling in the ward-name column. Similar names appear under distinct numbers: Civil Line Second is ward 72, while Civil Line First is ward 79. The two Mahadev Jharkhandi entries are wards 1 and 7.

The corporation labels the source a list of newly elected councillors but does not date the edition. It identifies wards by name, without street limits or a notification defining which properties fall inside them.

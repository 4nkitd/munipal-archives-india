# Gorakhpur property tax settlement scheme for 2026

## Application window and payment terms

The corporation's Hindi Events Notice announces a one-time settlement scheme (एकमुश्त समाधान योजना) for outstanding property tax. The scheme runs from 15 August to 15 December 2026. The notice sets an earlier deadline for applications: residents could apply online or offline from 15 August through 14 November 2026. The published application window had closed by 22 November.

| Payment condition | Terms in the notice |
|---|---|
| Interest waiver | All interest waived after the applicant pays the full outstanding amount. |
| First instalment | One third of the outstanding property tax within 30 days of the scheme taking effect. |
| Balance | The remaining two thirds in two monthly instalments. |
| Overall limit | No more than three instalments; payment must be complete within three months. |

The notice states the first deadline relative to the start of the scheme, not the date of an individual application. It warns of action under the applicable rules against defaulters who do not apply. The banner gives no order number or publication date.

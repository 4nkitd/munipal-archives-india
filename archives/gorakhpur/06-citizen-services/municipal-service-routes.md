# Gorakhpur bill payments, civil registration and sanitation requests

## Select the service

| Request | Route published by the corporation |
|---|---|
| View a bill or pay property tax or shop rent | Use the [Gorakhpur municipal payment portal](https://gorakhpur.upmunicipal.com/#/gorakhpur-home-page). “View Bill” and “Pay Taxes” point to the same city landing page. |
| Register a birth or death | The municipal footer directs residents to the [Civil Registration System](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/home). |
| Look up an older birth record | Use [Birth Records Detail (old)](https://gorakhpurnagarnigam.up.gov.in/BR.aspx). The municipal form displays fields for the registration number and date of birth, with the date format `dd/MMM/yyyy`. This is a search of existing records. |
| Request sewer or septic tank cleaning | Call **14420**, the toll-free number printed beside the cleaning service on the city homepage. |
| Other municipal enquiries | Call **1533**, the general municipal helpline, or contact the Town Hall office at 0551-2333015. |

For property assessment questions, the city's Important Information section distinguishes the final mohalla rate list from the old rate list and links a separate property tax calculation document. The bill payment link itself does not specify a uniform rate.

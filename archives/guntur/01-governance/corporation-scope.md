# Status and scope of Guntur Municipal Corporation

## Municipal jurisdiction

The corporation's official About page records Guntur's establishment as a municipality in 1866 and its elevation to a municipal corporation in 1994. It identifies urban infrastructure, sanitation and delivery of civic services as corporation functions. The corporation is the urban local body for Guntur city within its notified limits. Its jurisdiction is not the same as that of the Guntur district administration.

The Andhra Pradesh Municipal Corporations Act, 1994 (Act 25 of 1994) uses the notified larger urban area to define the territory of a corporation. Section 4 identifies the corporation, standing committee, commissioner and wards committee as municipal authorities. See the [AP State Election Commission manual](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/MEL/Manual%20of%20Election%20Law%20-%20MC%20-%2024%20September,%202026.pdf), printed page 77. The civic profile does not provide the original Guntur notification or a boundary schedule, so it should not be used to settle whether a parcel lies within the corporation.

The [municipal contact page](https://guntur.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/guntur/contact) lists the main office opposite Gandhi Park, near PVK Naidu Market Main Road. The [district directory](https://guntur.ap.gov.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) confirms the office location and PIN 522003.

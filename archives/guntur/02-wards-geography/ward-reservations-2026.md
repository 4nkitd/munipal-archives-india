# Guntur ward reservations for the 2026 municipal election

## Seat reservation schedule

The Guntur District Collector published the ward reservations in District Extraordinary Gazette No. 176 on 13 October 2026. The [APSEC election returns](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/Rsls/Results%20-%20Ward%20Members%20Municpal%20Corporations-2026.pdf#page=60) also enumerate Guntur wards 1 through 76, with the poll dated 1 November 2026.

| Category in the gazette | Seats | Example ward |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Scheduled Tribes, women and general combined | 2 | 21 |
| Scheduled Castes, women and general combined | 9 | 76 |
| Backward Classes, women and general combined | 27 | 2 |
| Women, general | 20 | 4 |
| Unreserved | 18 | 1 |

The schedule identifies electoral seats and their reservations for this edition. It does not provide street-level boundaries. The [Guntur City Map](https://guntur.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/guntur/gis-map) labels ward secretariat offices with their own codes; those codes are not electoral ward numbers.

# Municipal Corporation of Gurugram: City profile and formation

## Formation and municipal limits

On 2 June 2008, the Haryana government constituted the Municipal Corporation of Gurgaon, now the Municipal Corporation of Gurugram (MCG). It replaced the former Municipal Council and included additional villages. The Punjab and Haryana High Court's judgment in [*Kulraj Kataria v. State of Haryana*](https://www.casemine.com/judgement/in/56099212e4b0149711397dd4) records the notification date and the state's use of section 3(2) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. The state certified a population of 3,80,003 for the area notified in 2008, based on the 2001 Census.

On 16 December 2022, Haryana amended the boundary between Gurugram and the separate Municipal Corporation Manesar. The [amended area notification](https://mcmanesar.in/general-documents) uses a stretch of Dwarka Expressway as the dividing line. [Manesar's amended zone list](https://mcmanesar.in/zones) places Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A and parts of the Harsaru and Sihi revenue estates on the Manesar side. Use the notified boundary schedule and map when determining jurisdiction for a particular property.

## Area and population

Haryana's [Municipalities Key Figures](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists MCG at **297.99 sq km**, using the department's GIS data dated 9 September 2026. The same table lists **10,65,872 residents** from Haryana's Citizen Resource Information Department as of 11 November 2026. This is an administrative population count for the corporation, not a Census figure for Gurugram district or the urban agglomeration. The roughly 232 sq km figure appears in [accounts of the earlier municipal extent](https://theprint.in/ground-reports/for-salaried-class-owning-a-home-in-gurugram-and-noida-are-distant-dream/3101751/). It is not the area in the current state GIS table.

## Wards and administrative zones

MCG has **36 elected wards**. Haryana's Urban Local Bodies Department lists the [final ward delimitation order No. 19/45/2023-6CI](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/b694b244-657e-47a9-af1a-64b76dd6ce64.pdf) under 15 December 2023. The State Election Commission amended the [voter-list programme](https://secharyana.gov.in/orders-notifications-related-to-municipal-elections-4/orders-notifications-related-to-municipal-elections/) in January 2024. The wards were used for the [March 2025 municipal election](https://secharyana.gov.in/result-notification-of-municipalities-2025/).

Wards are electoral constituencies; zones group them for municipal administration. MCG previously used four zones, then reorganised them into [eight zones in June 2026](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/notifications/mcg-administrative-clusters-and-zones.pdf). An order effective 8 September 2026 [reduced the number to six](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcg-reduces-gurugram-zones-from-8-to-6-creates-two-clusters-101788933662424.html), with Zones I to III in the first administrative cluster and IV to VI in the second. The [MCG office directory](https://www.mcg.gov.in/contact/office-contacts) lists six zonal joint commissioner offices.

## Headquarters and public contacts

The [MCG website](https://www.mcg.gov.in/) and office directory list the headquarters at **Plot No. C-1, Info City, Sector 34, Gurugram, Haryana 122001**. [Civil Lines housed zonal offices](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/additional-charges-give-mcg-chance-to-avoid-missteps-in-manesar-101610904006089.html) under the earlier setup. The current directory lists the old MCG office near Sadar Bazar separately from headquarters.

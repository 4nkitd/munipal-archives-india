# Municipal Corporation of Gurugram: March 2025 elected council

## Mayor and council majority

Gurugram polled on 2 March 2025. Votes were counted on 12 March. Raj Rani Malhotra (BJP), listed as **Raj Rani** in the SEC notification, defeated Congress candidate Seema Pahuja by 179,485 votes for mayor. The mayor was elected separately and is excluded from the ward tally.

| Election-day affiliation | Ward seats |
| --- | ---: |
| Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) | 24 |
| Independent | 10 |
| Indian National Congress (Congress) | 1 |
| Jannayak Janta Party (JJP) | 1 |
| **Total** | **36** |

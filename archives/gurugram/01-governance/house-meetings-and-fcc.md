# MCG House meetings and Finance and Contract Committee approvals, 2025 to 2026

## Scope and evidence limits

This report indexes selected meetings and approvals, not the complete agenda or certified proceedings of every MCG meeting. Meeting-specific evidence below is contemporary reporting, including a PTI report quoting an official statement. Signed House minutes, resolution numbers, full F&CC agendas and work-completion certificates were not established from these sources. An approval or sanction is not evidence of tender award, expenditure or completed construction.

The House and the Finance and Contract Committee are distinct decision-making forums. The 11 August 2025 House meeting elected councillors Sundar Singh of Ward 1 and Anup Singh of Ward 25 to the F&CC. Contemporary reporting described both elections as unanimous and unopposed. [1][2]

## House meetings in 2025 and 2026

| Meeting or reporting date | Verified proceedings or status |
|---|---|
| 8 April 2025 | Special budget meeting. Subsequent reporting identified this separately from the first regular House meeting. Councillors raised Bandhwari waste management; reporting put the sanitation allocation at Rs 350 crore, compared with Rs 300 crore in the previous fiscal year. This was a budget allocation, not a specific F&CC contract approval. [3][4] |
| 11 August 2025 | House meeting at Haryana Institute of Public Administration, Sector 18. Sachin Devtwal, Vikrant Yadav and Krishan Swami took oath as nominated members. The 2023-24 audit report was presented and two F&CC members were elected. [1] |
| 14 March 2026 report | Reported only three House meetings since the March 2025 election: the April budget session and meetings in August and November 2025. The report described state directions to hold meetings regularly. It does not supply the November meeting's exact date or a verified agenda. [5] |
| 11 September 2026 | Councillors raised irregular road sweeping, gaps in doorstep collection and delayed lifting from secondary collection points. Officials said two agencies approved in August would deploy 857 collection vehicles across two clusters, with services expected in November or December. These were reported implementation plans, not proof of deployment. [6] |

The August 2025 House approved expansion of rainwater harvesting, councillor name boards, a streetlight rate contract, transfer of land for an urban primary health centre, reductions in certain development charges, special camps for water-connection meters and one compressed biogas plant in each of the four municipal zones then described. The source does not give item-wise sanctions or plant commissioning dates. Commissioner Pradeep Dahiya told the House that the Sewerage Monitoring Cell had identified 40 critical drainage points, with phased resolution planned by April 2026. That date was a target, not a verified completion date. [1]

### 27 January 2026

The F&CC cleared 25 agenda items worth Rs 141.7 crore covering roads, stormwater drainage, sewerage, water supply, streetlights and public infrastructure. Reported water-pipeline approvals included Dhankot at Rs 5.5 crore and Daultabad at Rs 4.1 crore. Infrastructure augmentation in Dhumaspur and Maidawas exceeded Rs 9 crore each. [7]

The committee also cleared strengthening of old 600 mm sewers in Civil Lines, Sadar Bazaar and Jacobpura, and a stormwater drainage and disposal network for Sectors 9, 9A and other waterlogging-affected areas. The cited report does not give separate amounts for these sewer and drainage items.

Other approvals included Atal Park and Atal Smriti Kendra in Dharampur at Rs 8 crore, and secondary waste-collection-point works at Daultabad, Rs 5.2 crore; Carterpuri, Rs 3.9 crore; Beri Bagh, Rs 3.8 crore; and Khandsa, Rs 3.9 crore. [7]

## Bandhwari decisions and sanitation context

In April 2025 councillors questioned continuing waste accumulation at Bandhwari. Ward 20 councillor Narayan Bhadana called for action on the village's association with the landfill. Officials described plans for simultaneous fresh-waste processing and legacy-waste remediation. A proposed processing arrangement is not a verified House resolution authorising a contract. [3]

Separate administrative and technical approvals dated 17 July 2025 covered a Rs 2.45 crore, 15-metre-wide, 400-metre-long cement-concrete road beside the landfill view cutters. Reporting also described an RCC drain, a proposed additional weighbridge, and a Rs 63 lakh leachate-transport proposal for which tendering was still to begin. Works on view cutters, boundary wall and drainage had started on 14 July. These are documented as administrative works and proposals, not attributed here to an unidentified House or F&CC resolution. [9]

## Citizen participation and access to records

Residents can submit a written ward-specific representation to their councillor and MCG, identifying the location, issue, existing complaint number and requested work or agenda discussion. This is a practical representation route, not a verified entitlement to speak during a House or F&CC meeting. The sources reviewed do not establish a public speaking slot, attendance registration process or right to move an agenda item.

MCG's official website provides a civic grievance route and lists the helpdesk at 18001801817 and info@mcg.gov.in. Its head office is in Sector 34, Gurugram. Retain the acknowledgement when asking for action on a pending project. [10]

For a documented participation opportunity, MCG announced an RWA-representative meeting with Commissioner Dr Pankaj for noon on 25 November 2026 at the commissioner’s Sector 34 office. Representatives were invited to raise sanitation, collection, roads, sewerage, waterlogging, streetlights and pending development works. As of verification on 22 November, this was a forthcoming meeting, not a completed consultation or a House sitting. [11]

For authoritative verification of a particular approval, request the meeting notice, agenda item and estimate, signed minutes, resolution or sanction, tender reference, work order and action-taken report. The National Portal of India lists an online RTI service for MCG. An RTI request should identify the meeting date and project rather than asking the authority to explain a newspaper report. [12]

# Mayor and leadership of the Municipal Corporation of Gurugram, 2024 to 2026

## Elected leadership

Raj Rani Malhotra of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) is the mayor. Gurugram voted on 2 March 2025 in its first direct mayoral election, and the result was declared on 12 March. Malhotra won 270,781 votes to 91,296 for Seema Pahuja of the Indian National Congress, a margin of 179,485 votes, according to the [final count reported by Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/haryana-civic-body-polls-bjp-wins-nine-of-10-centres-manesar-gets-independent-mayor-101741801812322.html). The [State Election Commission's notification SEC/1ME/2025/4272 of 20 March 2025](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/20250704337894322.pdf) lists the winner as Raj Rani. She [took the oath on 25 March](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/gurgaon-mayor-to-take-office-this-week-sets-interaction-hours-for-public/articleshow/119497810.cms) and [assumed office on 28 March](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/new-mayor-charts-course-swachh-city-transparent-mcg-top-priority/articleshow/119671956.cms).

The previous House, led by Mayor Madhu Azad, completed its term in November 2022. [MCG had no elected House in 2024](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/delhi/2024/Sep/12/gurgram-battles-civic-woes-as-municipal-polls-pending-for-two-years). The 2025 election filled the mayor's seat and returned councillors for 36 wards. On 14 September 2026 the House [elected Dharmbir Bhangrola as Senior Deputy Mayor and Anoop Singh as Deputy Mayor, both unopposed](https://hindustanera.in/dharmbir-bhangrola-elected-senior-deputy-mayor-anoop-singh-becomes-deputy-mayor-unopposed-in-gurugram/). The two deputy posts are filled by election within the House, not by a separate citywide vote.

## Who decides and who administers

The [Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/2d98f87d-c6f6-44c2-920e-8fb13fede849.pdf) divides authority between the Corporation and the commissioner. The [2018 Second Amendment](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=CONTENTFILES%5C8518c8e7-68f1-4efd-98e0-51e60340e19e.pdf) amended section 4(2) to provide for direct mayoral election, retained the House's election of the two deputies under section 36(2), and moved the mayor's oversight powers to section 36-A(2).

| Authority | Role under the Act |
| --- | --- |
| Corporation House | Under section 41, municipal administration vests in the Corporation, subject to the Act and subordinate rules. The House reviews receipts, expenditure and progress reports and passes resolutions. Section 50 lets it call for records, estimates and reports from the commissioner. |
| Mayor | Presides at House meetings under section 56 and has a casting vote in a tie. The Senior Deputy Mayor and then the Deputy Mayor preside in the mayor's absence. Section 36-A(2) gives the mayor access to Corporation records and allows the mayor to issue directions or call for reports to ensure the Corporation's decisions are implemented. |
| Municipal Commissioner | The state appoints the commissioner under section 45. Section 49 vests executive power in the commissioner, subject to the Act and the Corporation's general supervision and control. The commissioner assigns duties to municipal officers, supervises staff and may act in specified emergencies, with prompt reporting to the Corporation. |

The [Nathupur street-closure notice of 31 October 2026](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/notifications/INVITING%20OBJECTIONS%20SUGGESTIONS%20ON%20THE%20PROPOSED%20PERMANENT%20CLOSURE.pdf) shows the split in practice. The House passed a resolution on 11 September. Commissioner Pradeep Dahiya then issued a notice under section 217(2) on the Corporation's behalf to invite public objections and suggestions. The notice did not itself close the street.

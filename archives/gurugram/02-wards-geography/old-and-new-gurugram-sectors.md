# Old and new Gurugram sectors and municipal handover atlas

## Scope and how to read this record

This is a locality index, not a cadastral boundary certificate or a complete register of every licensed project. "Old Gurugram", "New Gurgaon", "Golf Course Extension" and "Dwarka Expressway" are locality descriptions, not municipal jurisdictions. A sector can contain HSVP plots, licensed colonies, group-housing sites, village land and public facilities with different maintenance arrangements.

Four questions must be kept separate:

1. Is the property inside the notified MCG boundary?
2. Who approved or allotted its layout, plot or licence?
3. Has a particular road, park, drain, market or water network been handed over?
4. Who operates the asset today?

HUDA is the former name used in the older transfer reports. The successor authority is HSVP. Municipal maintenance does not automatically transfer plot-allotment powers, land title, licence enforcement, private apartment facilities or every vacant parcel.

The date above is the compilation date. Historical status statements below retain their source dates. No ward numbers are assigned because old election wards, current wards and engineering zones are different systems. The GMDA sector layer includes Manesar sectors despite appearing under the group named "MCG LIMIT". Its layer name alone is not proof of municipal inclusion. [1]

## Old Gurugram HSVP residential sectors

The contemporaneous report of the completed first-phase handover identifies **30 April 2016** as the maintenance-transfer date. The second phase was reported complete on **19 May 2016**. These are sector-maintenance events, not evidence that every market or later-developed pocket transferred on the same date. [4]

| Sector or pocket | Recorded handover position |
| --- | --- |
| 4, 5, 7 and 7 Extension | In the 30 April 2016 first-phase list |
| 9 and 9A | In the first-phase list; additional residential pockets were transferred in August 2025 |
| 10 and 10A | In the first-phase list; additional Sector 10 pockets transferred in August 2025 |
| 12 and 12A | The completed April transfer report explicitly names 12A. A March 2016 announcement used "12-12A". Do not treat that announcement alone as a separate completion record for all of Sector 12 |
| 14 | In the first-phase list |
| 15 Part I and Part II | Both parts explicitly included in the first-phase list |
| 17, 21, 22 and 23 | In the first-phase list; additional Sector 22 residential pockets transferred in August 2025 |
| 23A | In the 19 May 2016 second-phase list, rather than the completed April list |
| Other first-phase entries | 3, 6, 18 and 37 Parts I and II |

The March announcement and the completed May report do not use identical sector schedules. The completed-transfer report is the stronger source for dates of actual takeover. For Sector 12, obtain the asset schedule or individual handover memorandum before stating that a particular market or road transferred. [4, 5]

MCG became responsible for civic maintenance in transferred areas, including sanitation and the local services covered by the transfer. HUDA retained plot-related recoveries such as extension and transfer fees. Later disputes about revenue sharing do not mean the 2016 maintenance handover never occurred. [4]

## Licensed colonies and builder handovers

Colonies do not map one-to-one to sectors. The following are colony-level maintenance histories, not a declaration that every apartment society or commercial building inside them is municipally operated.

| Colony | Best established position in the cited evidence |
| --- | --- |
| DLF Phases I, II and III | Transfer announced in 2016, but still not completed according to the 19 December 2024 report on MCG's High Court submission. No later completed handover instrument was established for this record |
| Sushant Lok I and Palam Vihar | DTCP immediate-transfer order reported on 18 September 2018. Actual MCG takeover reported as February 2019. Distinguish the order from implementation |
| South City I and South City II | Reported taken over in the subsequent review of the 2016 colony-transfer programme |
| Nirvana Country | Reported under MCG maintenance by the December 2024 retrospective. Earlier transfer planning treated it with South City II |
| Suncity | Reported taken over in the retrospective accounts; do not extend this to all independently licensed projects nearby |
| Sushant Lok II, Sushant Lok III, Ardee City, Malibu Town, Vipul World, Uppal Southend and Rosewood City | Seven colonies reported taken over in June 2022 |
| Greenwood City | Reported taken over in August 2022 |
| Mayfield Garden | Included in the May 2022 nine-colony direction. Later reporting describes one further recent takeover without naming it in that sentence. A precise completed-transfer date is not established here |

Sources distinguish an order to transfer from actual maintenance takeover. The September 2018 Sushant Lok I and Palam Vihar order expressly did not constitute a completion certificate. [9, 10, 11]

The DLF entry deliberately does not follow the premise that all DLF phases have already been handed over. Municipal sanitation or streetlight work inside a colony is not proof that its complete water, sewer, road and park network has transferred. DLF Phases IV and V are also not covered by an order about Phases I to III. [12]

## Golf Course Extension, SPR and Sectors 58 to 72

The sector index for this belt includes 58, 59, 60, 61, 62, 63, 63A, 64, 65, 66, 67, 67A, 68, 69, 70, 70A, 71, 72 and 72A. These are planning-sector identifiers, not one uniform municipal maintenance estate. Golf Course Extension generally describes the eastern corridor; the higher-numbered sectors also extend toward Badshahpur and Southern Peripheral Road.

| Sector group | Treatment for municipal and maintenance enquiries |
| --- | --- |
| 58 to 64, including 63A | Use the actual licensed-colony or HSVP site name and the road's asset owner. The old 2016 HSVP transfer list does not establish a blanket transfer for this belt |
| 65 and 66 | Newer residential and commercial development. MCG's official June 2025 waterlogging schedule explicitly includes a Sector 65 location. Municipal response there does not certify handover of every project |
| 67, 67A and 68 | Residential, mixed-use and reserved institutional land require parcel-level layout checks. Do not classify the whole sector from a housing project's postal address |
| 69, 70 and 70A | SPR and Badshahpur-side development. Distinguish municipal streets from developer internal roads and GMDA master infrastructure |
| 71, 72 and 72A | Southern city sectors. Use the municipal boundary overlay and project licence before assigning a particular parcel or adjoining road |

This belt belongs in the MCG locality enquiry index, but this record does not claim complete builder-to-MCG handover of every sector. The official Manesar zone schedule instead starts its listed southern residential sector group at 76 to 80. That negative comparison does not replace a legal boundary check for an edge parcel. [1, 2, 13]

## Dwarka Expressway, Sectors 99 to 115 and the Manesar side

MC Manesar's official city profile records a municipal boundary amendment dated **16 December 2022**. It describes Dwarka Expressway as the divider and specifically identifies Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A, with Harsaru and Sihi, as additions to Manesar. However, "one side of the expressway" is not a safe substitute for the notified map. [2, 3]

| Sector or group | Jurisdiction reading |
| --- | --- |
| 99 | MCG-side locality, with approach roads affected by the adjoining MCM boundary. Confirm each road segment |
| 99A | Explicitly listed in Manesar Zone 1. Do not fold it into MCG merely because it adjoins Sector 99 |
| 100, 101, 102 and 102A | Include in the MCG-side Dwarka corridor enquiry index, subject to the notified boundary and revenue-estate edge |
| 103, 104, 105, 106 and 107 | MCG-side Dwarka corridor sector index. Sector numbers do not establish completed builder maintenance handover |
| 108, 109, 110 and 110A | Northern Dwarka corridor sector index. Check the actual village, property ID and asset schedule |
| 111, 112, 113, 114 and 115 | Delhi-border end of the Gurugram sector grid. Delhi land beyond the state boundary is not MCG |
| 81, 81A, 82, 82A, 83, 84, 85, 86, 88, 88A, 88B and 89 | Explicitly listed in Manesar Zone 2 |
| 89A, 89B, 90, 91, 92, 93, 94, 95, 95A and 95B | Explicitly listed in Manesar Zone 1, together with 99A |

The boundary problem is concrete. On 5 June 2026, reporting described a proposed transfer of about **1.1 km** of a revenue road from MCM to MCG after demarcation near Sectors 99 and 99A. MCM's engineer said its jurisdiction ended at GMDA's 75-metre road, while the remaining segment fell inside MCG. This was a proposed road transfer, not a notification moving the whole of Sector 99A into MCG. [14]

## Later HSVP handovers and the citizen's asset check

On **7 August 2025**, HSVP formally transferred maintenance of 20 developed markets, one institutional area and 20 residential pockets, according to the report published the next day. The market schedule included 4, 7, 7 Extension, 10A, 14, 15 Parts I and II, 17, 21, 22, 23, 31, 32, 38, 39, 40, 45, 46, 56 and Old Jail Land. The institutional entry named Sector 44 plots 6 to 12. Residential entries included pockets in 9A, 10, 22, 40, 43, 49, 50, 52 and 57. [7]

A parallel report gave totals of 1,485 residential plots and 16 institutional plots and also named a Sector 9 pocket. These summaries differ in detail. Use the signed plot schedule for an individual property instead of combining their totals into an invented comprehensive register. [8]

For a service dispute, supply the sector, colony, block, plot or tower, property ID, nearest junction and the exact failed asset. Ask for the handover memorandum and asset schedule. MCG generally handles transferred local civic assets; GMDA handles its assigned master roads and trunk systems; HSVP retains responsibilities attached to its unsurrendered assets and allotments; the developer or association may retain private internal facilities. DHBVN electricity supply is separate from municipal streetlight maintenance.

This record establishes dated transfer histories, not present-day service performance. An executed transfer does not establish that repairs are complete, and an infrastructure deficiency does not by itself cancel municipal jurisdiction.

# Regularized colonies within MCG limits: notification phases and civic services

## Scope and evidence

This record transcribes the Haryana ULB public register entries whose municipality is Gurugram, not every colony in Gurugram district. The register supplies notification references, colony IDs, revenue estates, acreage, regularized khasra numbers and linked layout plans. Its MCG entries are serial numbers 166 to 203. The tables below retain its identifiers and distinguish similarly named pockets. They are an index, not a substitute for the notified layout and land schedule. [1]

The document date is the latest notification date displayed against these entries, 11 December 2025. No separate 2026 MCG regularization notification was verified in the consulted register. This does not establish that no later order exists. The ULB notification library separately lists the October 2023, March 2024, November 2024 and March 2025 gazettes. Library upload dates, often 26 September 2025, are not notification dates. [2]

Regularization of a named pocket must not be read as approval of every property using that locality name. For example, Laxman Vihar Extension is a specified 10.330-acre entry, while Saraswati Enclave Part I and Part II cover different estates and areas. The source has spelling variations, including Choma/Chauma, Rajendera/Rajendra and Sarswati/Saraswati.

## Development charges and property transactions

Contemporaneous reporting dated 12 March 2024 quotes MCG assistant town planner Siddharth Khandelwal on mandatory development-charge payment in newly regularized colonies. It reports a charge of 5% of the applicable colony or area's collector rate and says the development-charge receipt was required for building-plan approval and the municipal no-dues process before registration. The same report contrasts earlier residential charges of Rs 120 per square metre with the newer collector-rate basis. These are reported implementation details, not a certified current fee schedule. [3]

Obtain the property-specific demand from MCG before payment. Confirm the chargeable area, applicable collector-rate year, land-use category, previous payments and any later concessions. A quoted rupee amount for a sample plot is not a universal rate. This archive does not verify a later 2026 fee revision.

Do not import the separate DTCP policy for colonies outside municipal limits into an MCG fee calculation. A district-wide news total can combine MCG, Manesar, Sohna, Pataudi and Farrukh Nagar. The official register's municipality column is the scope check. Payment of property tax or creation of a property ID is not a substitute for locating the plot in the notified layout.

## Water, sewers and road rollout

Regularization permits civic infrastructure planning, but does not certify that water, sewers or paved roads already function at each property. An August 2024 report described approximately Rs 25 crore of proposed works for 19 MCG colonies, estimates prepared for 75% of works, and road and sewer works started in seven colonies. Those figures describe reported planning and progress at that date, not completed coverage. The article mixes some district-level names into its list, so its narrative list is not used to expand the official MCG register. [4]

The reported programme included water mains and household connections, sewer lines, streets, streetlights and parks. Separate estimates, technical and administrative approvals, tenders, allotments, construction and functioning connections are distinct stages. Residents should request the specific street's work order, contractor, scope, completion date and water/sewer connection procedure from MCG's engineering branch.

No colony-by-colony completion certificate or operational water/sewer test was verified for these phases. The February 2026 Sheetla Colony report also records continuing sewage and road problems, with a councillor reporting resumed repairs on a 100-metre stretch. It is evidence of a service issue, not proof that the whole colony was included in this regularization programme. [6]

## Building plans after regularization

Colony notification is separate from sanction of an individual building. Haryana ULB's approval/revision service directs applicants to the Haryana Online Building Plan Approval System and requires compliance with Haryana Building Code 2017 and other applicable policies. Its published checklist requires ownership records, building drawings, Forms BR-I and BR-II, property-tax receipt, site plan/report and CLU permission where applicable. [5]

The service page lists scrutiny charges of Rs 10 per square metre of covered area, prevailing development charges for non-CLU cases, and composition charges under the applicable policy at occupation-certificate stage. These are separate charge heads. Verify the current demand rather than treating the reported 5% development charge as the entire approval fee.

ULB's verification checklist includes road width, unauthorized construction, approved-scheme violations, land-use permissions, municipal jurisdiction and availability of underground water and sewer services. Depending on the site, access, fire, airport, environmental and other NOCs may be needed. The page provides self-certification for eligible low-risk cases, subject to compliant documents and drawings.

Before construction, have the architect match the plot's khasra and dimensions to the notified layout and obtain the applicable plan sanction. Regularization does not by itself authorize extra floors, a change to commercial use or existing deviations. Separate building and occupation requirements still apply.

# Urban villages, Lal Dora property records and civic services in Gurugram

## Scope and boundary cautions

This record covers village settlements within the Municipal Corporation Gurugram service and property-record context. A revenue village, its historic inhabited settlement, its Lal Dora polygon and its electoral ward are different geographic units. A village name alone does not establish that every parcel in its revenue estate lies within MCG, inside Lal Dora or within a particular ward.

The official combined MCG and Municipal Corporation Manesar map identifies village locations and a boundary labelled "MCG LIMIT BOUNDARY 2020". It is a dated geographic reference, not proof of a current parcel boundary. The Haryana ULB public possession-certificate search also exposes Lal Dora colony names and codes. Its unfiltered page includes municipalities across Haryana, so select district GURUGRAM and town GURUGRAM, municipal code 012, before treating a search result as an MCG record. Manesar has a separate municipal code, 095. [S2, S5]

No current ward numbers are assigned here. Older MCG works entries use historical ward numbers, which should not be copied into a current electoral directory. For an individual house, match the PID location, municipal boundary and applicable ward-delimitation map. This index is not a certified village roster or a complete list of Lal Dora properties.

## What the municipal Lal Dora certificate covers

The controlling source for this guide is the Haryana ULB policy and SOP circulated on 12 March 2024. The department's index publishes it under 15 March 2024. It was issued as directions under section 250 of the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973 and section 398 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. [S1, S6]

The policy concerns the old Abadi Deh of erstwhile villages subsequently included in municipalities, where revenue authorities traditionally did not maintain individual records of rights. It describes inhabited land represented by a common khasra number and generally surrounded by Phirni or Lal Dora, excluding specific khasra-number land. Eligibility depends on the actual historic boundary, not simply a village address or a portal colony label.

The policy provides for certification using acceptable ownership documents or documents establishing possession for the last 10 years. The certificate supports identification and recording of ownership or occupation and transfers in municipal records against existing property IDs. Certificates remain subject to the Transfer of Properties Act, 1882, as named in the source, and court decisions or decrees. A PID, tax receipt or NDC should therefore not be presented as a conclusive adjudication of ownership.

The SOP specifically requires public streets, wells, common grounds, burial grounds and drains to remain recorded under the municipality. Vacant land and properties without an owner or occupier name require particular scrutiny. Do not infer private ownership of shamlat, municipal land or Aravalli land from this programme. This is especially relevant when checking disputed parcels around Gwal Pahari and Bandhwari. [S1]

## Certificate process, objections and appeal

The 2024 SOP assigns the Municipal Commissioner responsibility for constituting ward committees. Their work includes verifying Lal Dora boundaries, identifying properties and possessors, publishing lists and hearing appeals. An officer designated by the Commissioner hears initial claims and objections. The municipal officer acting as committee member secretary must be different from that initial hearing officer. [S1]

| Stage | Procedure or period in the 2024 SOP |
| --- | --- |
| Committee formation | Within 15 days of the instructions |
| Boundary and property-list preparation | Committee ascertains boundaries within 15 days of formation, then extracts property details within the next 15 days |
| Draft publication | Publish possession details on the municipal website within the next 30 days; advertise in one national and two local newspapers and at prominent places; distribute individual details |
| Claims and objections | Any person may object within 30 days of publication; submit to the respective municipality |
| Initial disposal | SOP calls for recording and resolving objections within 30 days of receipt; its hearing section also requires a speaking order within 15 days after hearing |
| Final list | Publish the final possession list on the municipal website within 30 days under the final-list section |
| Appeal | File with the ward committee within 30 days of publication of the final list |
| Appeal decision | Ward committee passes a speaking order within 15 days of the filed appeal |
| Approval and issue | House resolution, or Administrator resolution if no House exists; update the property portal, then print and distribute certificates |

These are the SOP's stage-specific periods, not a promise that an individual certificate will be issued within their sum. No live MCG village-specific objection deadline was established for this record. Obtain the applicable publication notice before calculating a deadline.

The prescribed objection format asks for PID, district, municipality, Lal Dora name and whether the objection concerns possession, area, subdivision or another issue. Give length and width in metres and surrounding boundaries. Include supporting documents, identity details, address and Family ID if available. Retain an acknowledged copy and request the speaking order. [S1]

## Documents for possession certification

The municipal certificate SOP requires the following evidence. This checklist is different from the ordinary new-PID checklist. [S1]

| Requirement | Evidence specified in the SOP |
| --- | --- |
| Mandatory affidavit | Claimant's affidavit, attested by the Revenue Authority, clearly stating ownership or possession in Abadi Deh or Lal Dora |
| Any two supporting categories establishing the last 10 years of possession | Electricity bills; water bills; government-issued address documents such as EPIC, driving licence, passport or GST registration; property-tax receipts; proof of a constructed structure |
| Preferred documents, if available | Sale or conveyance deed; transfer, relinquishment or release deed; Jamabandi or Fard; court decree registered with revenue authorities; registry or sale deed |
| Original owner deceased | Legal-heir certificate issued by the competent revenue authority or civil court, along with the above documents |

Evidence must relate to the property and claimed period. A recent electricity connection alone does not establish 10 years of possession. The publicly accessible policy does not set a universal certificate fee. Confirm any charge with MCG against a written order and official receipt.

## Property ID search, correction and new ID

Use the Haryana ULB Property Tax Dues Payment and NDC Management System for municipal property data. The separate public Lal Dora notice search is useful for published possession entries; it is not itself proof that a final certificate has been issued. [S2, S3]

1. Register or log in at the property portal. Select Gurugram municipality and search by PID, old PID, owner or occupier name, mobile number, plot number or colony. The FAQ also provides a GIS "Search on Map" option.
2. Open "View Details". Compare the recorded owner, area, use, address and construction details with evidence. Self-certify only correct information through "Yes" and "Verify Details".
3. For wrong data, select "No", choose "Update Details", enter the correction, attach evidence and select "Save Draft". The official FAQ describes this as raising an objection for the municipality. Self-certify after disposal.
4. If no property is found after name, plot and map searches, use the new-PID option. Mark the location, enter property and owner details, add floor-wise construction and upload the documents. Record the application ID sent by SMS.

The FAQ states that normal new-PID requests are free and have a 10-working-day timeline. Its English and Hindi text conflict on the priority-service charge, showing ₹1,000 and ₹2,500 respectively. Confirm the current payable amount before selecting priority service. These PID timelines do not replace the Lal Dora certificate and appeal procedure. Consult the official document checklist for the specific request type. [S3, S4]

## Municipal civic services in village settlements

MCG's public site lists its head office at Sector 34, Gurugram 122001, toll-free number 18001801817 and email info@mcg.gov.in. Use its grievance route or helpdesk for local municipal service complaints, with the village name, street or landmark, precise location and photographs where useful. A property-record application and a civic complaint need separate tracking references. [S7]

| Issue in an urban village | First municipal route and information to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Missed waste collection, garbage accumulation or sweeping | MCG sanitation grievance; give collection point, street and dates |
| Local sewer blockage, overflow, drain choking or waterlogging | MCG engineering grievance; identify the affected lane, manhole or drain and whether sewage or rainwater is involved |
| Water-supply interruption or leakage | Ask MCG to identify the local network operator; give connection and location details |
| Village street damage or streetlight fault | MCG engineering or electrical complaint; specify road location or pole identification |
| Community centre, chaupal, cremation-ground or pond maintenance | MCG helpdesk and the responsible engineering division; identify the municipal asset |
| Wrong PID, recorded owner, area or property demand | Haryana ULB property portal and MCG property-tax branch; retain application ID and evidence |
| Historic Lal Dora boundary, possession claim or certificate dispute | Designated municipal hearing officer or ward committee under the published SOP notice |

Do not assume every road, sewer or water main is maintained by MCG. Trunk infrastructure, sector assets and developer networks can involve GMDA, HSVP or a developer. Ask for the asset's responsible agency if a complaint is transferred. The MCG works portal contains historical village community-centre, chaupal and sanitation works, but its old progress entries do not establish present completion or current responsibility. [S8]

## Rural SVAMITVA is a separate route

Reports dated 17 and 18 November 2026 describe a Haryana rural Lal Dora ownership-record campaign with a 90-day claims window under the Haryana Abadi Deh legislation of 2025. The Gurugram district report describes map display, village-level scrutiny and revenue-officer decisions. These reports concern rural SVAMITVA records and are secondary sources. They do not establish that the same deadline applies to an MCG municipal possession notice. [S9, S10]

For a property now within MCG, first establish whether the applicable notice is the municipal 2024 SOP process or a revenue-department process under later law. Do not substitute a rural portal announcement for a dated municipal notice. This record does not certify the legal effect of later legislation on any individual property or establish a current application window.

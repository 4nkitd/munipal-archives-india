# Gurugram's 36 municipal wards: boundaries and locality index

## Which order defines the wards?

Haryana's Urban Local Bodies Department fixed the boundaries of Wards 1 to 36 by [order No. 19/45/2023-6CI dated 14 December 2023](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/b694b244-657e-47a9-af1a-64b76dd6ce64.pdf). The department's [notification register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Notification) lists the final order on 15 December 2023. The order cites rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994 and the preliminary notification of 26 August 2023. The final schedule increased the ward count from 35 to 36. The [district's 2025 election index](https://gurugram.gov.in/voter-list-for-mc-election-gurugram-2025/) lists booths 1 to 21 under Ward 1 and booths 878 to 905 under Ward 36.

The change to the outer municipal limit came first. [MC Manesar's city profile](https://mcmanesar.in/manesar-city-info) dates that amendment to 16 December 2022 and identifies Dwarka Expressway as the dividing line where the two corporations meet. It names Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A among the additions to Manesar. A revenue estate can straddle the municipal limit: Manesar lists part of Sihi in its [Zone 2](https://mcmanesar.in/zones), while the Gurugram delimitation names Sihi in Ward 17.

## Locality index

Use these names to find the relevant paragraph in the scanned order, then check the road, block or village boundary. Sector 109 appears in both Wards 5 and 6, Sector 50 in Wards 14 and 15, Sector 57 in Wards 14 and 21, and Sector 12A in Wards 25 and 26. A sector number alone cannot distinguish those wards. The [ward register](../wards.json) gives more localities and a boundary summary for each entry. Its `colonies` lists also include named industrial estates, institutions and housing complexes.

## How to read the historical zone and division fields

The `zone` field in `wards.json` records the four-zone arrangement [reported in April 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/four-gurugram-mc-zones-restructured-for-easy-execution-of-works-better-administrative-control/). The `division` field records the separate [March 2025 engineering divisions](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/mcg-realigns-ward-division-boundaries-after-delimitation/articleshow/118768038.cms). For example, Wards 1 and 2 were both in Zone 3 in April, but the March allocation placed them in Divisions 8 and 1 respectively.

| April 2025 zone | Wards |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 9, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 |
| 2 | 5, 6, 7, 8, 28, 29, 30, 32, 33, 34 |
| 3 | 1, 2, 3, 4, 12, 13, 27, 31, 35, 36 |
| 4 | 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26 |

For later service enquiries, the [MCG map dated 11 June 2026](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/notifications/mcg-administrative-clusters-and-zones.pdf) shows eight administrative zones. A [further order effective 8 September 2026](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcg-reduces-gurugram-zones-from-8-to-6-creates-two-clusters-101788933662424.html) regrouped the same 36 wards into six zones. Those changes affected municipal administration; the ward numbers in the election index remained the reference for the 2025 poll.

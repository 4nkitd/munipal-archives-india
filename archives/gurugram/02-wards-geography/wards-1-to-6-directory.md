# Gurugram wards 1 to 6: locality and zone directory

## Scope and source dates

This directory covers the 36-ward arrangement used during 2024 and in the March 2025 municipal elections. The official final boundary order linked as source [1] is dated December 2023, not 2024. It bears number 19/45/2023-6CI and refers to the preliminary notification of 26 August 2023. The day in the scanned order's handwritten date is not reliably legible in the indexed transcription. The frontmatter date is this directory's compilation date, not the notification date.

The first six wards are not all in Old Gurugram. In this numbering Ward 1 covers the Nathupur and DLF Phase 3 belt, Ward 2 includes Cyber Hub and Udyog Vihar, and Wards 3 to 6 extend through Carterpuri, Palam Vihar and the western sectors. Older 35-ward lists assign these places different numbers and must not be substituted.

[Scanned map/PDF: visual source linked at source_url]

The official PDF did not yield usable text with `pdftotext`. Search-indexed text supplied the Ward 1 and Ward 2 locality lists and the opening boundary description for Ward 3. Locality lists for Wards 3 to 6 below are attributed to the detailed 7 March 2025 report [2], not represented as a complete transcription of the scanned schedule. For a disputed property, use the notified boundary and electoral-roll entry rather than a sector name alone.

## Ward 1

| Field | Verified locality reference |
| --- | --- |
| Villages | Nathupur; Sikanderpur Ghosi |
| Residential areas | Nathupur Part; DLF Phase 3; National Media Centre; Ambience Island; Garden Estate; Dhanchiri Camp |
| Commercial and landscape references | DLF Corporate Park; Global Business Park; Aravali Hills Sikanderpur Ghosi |
| April 2025 zone | Zone 3 |
| June 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 2, Zone 5 |

The locality list comes directly from the indexed official boundary schedule [1]. Its boundary starts at 28.514, 77.0931 on NH-48 at the Delhi-Haryana border. Other references include the revenue estates of Nathupur and Sikanderpur Ghosi, DLF Phase 1 Block G, Silver Oak Apartment, Kusum Marg, Gurugram-Faridabad Road and Shankar Chowk. These are boundary landmarks, not a declaration that all DLF Phase 1 belongs to Ward 1.

The March 2025 engineering arrangement placed Ward 1 in Division 8 [2]. That assignment predates the April zonal engineering revision and the June 2026 administrative restructuring. It is retained only as a dated historical reference, not a current officer-routing instruction. Zone changes are documented separately below [3][4].

## Ward 2

| Field | Verified locality reference |
| --- | --- |
| Villages | Sarhaul, also written Sarhol in the boundary description; Dundahera |
| Residential areas | DLF City Phase 2 within the notified boundary; Mall Road area; Beverly Park 1 condominium complex; Surya Vihar |
| Commercial and industrial areas | DLF Cyber Hub; Maruti Udyog; Udyog Vihar Phases 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 |
| April 2025 zone | Zone 3 |
| June 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 2, Zone 5 |

The official schedule explicitly names the above places [1]. The March 2025 report describes the DLF Phase 2 coverage as a part of that development [2]. Do not infer whole-development coverage where the notified boundary divides an area.

Boundary references include NH-48, Shankar Chowk, Raghavendra Marg, the southern revenue boundaries of Sikanderpur Ghosi and Sarhol, MG Road Metro Station, the Sector 17/18 dividing road, Old Delhi Road and Hanuman Mandir Chowk. Sector 21 occurs as a boundary corner beside Surya Vihar, not as an included locality in Ward 2. Dundahera's phirni separates this ward from the neighbouring Ward 3 description. The historical March 2025 engineering assignment was Division 1 [2].

## Ward 3

| Field | Verified locality reference |
| --- | --- |
| Village | Carterpuri |
| Colonies and housing | Noble Enclave; Gurgaon One Apartment; Palam Vihar Extension |
| Sectors | Sector 21; Sector 22, Pockets A and B |
| April 2025 zone | Zone 3 |
| June 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 2, Zone 5 |

The locality and pocket list is reported in the March 2025 account of MCG's ward and division realignment [2]. It distinguishes Palam Vihar Extension in Ward 3 from Palam Vihar sectors 1 to 3 in Ward 4. Sector 22 should be read with its named pockets, rather than converted into an unqualified whole-sector assignment.

The indexed official Ward 3 description starts at Shakti Mandir More, 28.503, 77.0574. It follows the Carterpuri revenue boundary along the Haryana-Delhi border, the eastern corner of Sector 21 and western corner of Surya Vihar, Dundahera's phirni, Old Delhi Road and Maruti Company Gate 2 [1]. Inayatpur appears in this boundary narrative; the available excerpt does not establish it as a separately included village. The historical March 2025 engineering assignment was Division 1 [2].

## Ward 4

| Field | Verified locality reference |
| --- | --- |
| Village | Choma Khera, also written Chauma Khera or Choma |
| Residential development | Palam Vihar |
| Sectors | Sector 23; Sector 23A; Palam Vihar sectors 1, 2 and 3 |
| April 2025 zone | Zone 3 |
| June 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 2, Zone 5 |

The March 2025 ward realignment report explicitly lists Sector 23, Sector 23A, Palam Vihar sectors 1 to 3 and Choma Khera village in Ward 4 [2]. The municipal sector labels 1, 2 and 3 here refer to the Palam Vihar locality context. They should not be confused with IMT Manesar sectors bearing the same numbers.

Chauma, Choma and Chauma Khera are spelling variants used in locality references. They are indexed here together, not treated as three separate villages. Palam Vihar Extension is recorded under Ward 3, while New Palam Vihar phases 1 to 3 are recorded under Ward 5. A postal address containing only “Palam Vihar” is therefore insufficient to determine an electoral ward. The historical March 2025 engineering assignment was Division 1 [2].

## Ward 5

| Field | Verified locality reference |
| --- | --- |
| Villages | Bajghera; Sarai Alawardi |
| Colonies | New Palam Vihar phases 1, 2 and 3; Nihal Colony |
| Sectors named in the March 2025 report | Sector 109; Sector 110; Sector 110A; Sectors 111, 112, 113, 114 and 115 |
| April 2025 zone | Zone 2 |
| June 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 1, Zone 3 |

The locality list is from the March 2025 account [2]. “New Palam Vihar 1, 2, 3” refers to its phases, not to Palam Vihar sectors 1, 2 and 3 listed under Ward 4. Sarai Alawardi also appears as Sarai Alwardi in other government locality records.

Sector 109 is listed for both Ward 5 and Ward 6 in source [2]. This directory preserves that overlap instead of assigning the entire sector exclusively to either ward. The article does not provide the dividing alignment within Sector 109. Neither a society's sector number nor proximity to Bajghera establishes its ward without checking the final schedule or electoral roll.

The historical March 2025 engineering assignment was Division 1. The April 2025 zone was Zone 2, whereas the June 2026 administrative zone is Zone 3. These labels refer to different dated arrangements [2][3][4].

## Ward 6

| Field | Verified locality reference |
| --- | --- |
| Villages explicitly named in the March 2025 report | Daulatabad, printed Daultabad; Kherki Majra, printed Kherli Majra |
| Sectors named in that report | Sector 102; Sector 102A; Sector 103; Sector 106; Sector 107; Sector 108; Sector 109 |
| April 2025 zone | Zone 2 |
| June 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 1, Zone 3 |

Source [2] names these sectors and villages. Its list is a reported locality summary, not an exhaustive legal boundary transcription. The Kherki Majra spelling is supported by later indexed municipal works references, which also use Khedki Mazra. No khasra-level boundary or society-by-society coverage is asserted here.

Later tender-index records identify Dhankot and Dharampur works as Ward 6 and a Ward 6 water-supply maintenance package naming Dharampur, Babupur, Pawala Khusurpur and New Palam Vihar T Block [6]. Those are supplementary service-work references. They do not establish the exact extent of each village or colony inside the original notified ward, so they are not silently added to the March 2025 locality list.

Sector 109 overlaps the Ward 5 summary in the same report. Check the boundary schedule for a specific address. The historical March 2025 engineering assignment was Division 2 [2].

## Zone mapping chronology

| Wards | April 2025 four-zone arrangement | Order of 11 June 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1, 2, 3, 4 | Zone 3 | Cluster 2, Zone 5 |
| 5, 6 | Zone 2 | Cluster 1, Zone 3 |

The April 2025 mapping is corroborated by two reports describing the revised four-zone arrangement [3]. It should not be presented as the zone structure notified in 2024.

MCG's official 2026 administrative map cites Order MCG/CMC/2026/16598 dated 11 June 2026 [4]. It assigns wards 1, 2, 3, 4 and 36 to Zone 5, and wards 5, 6, 7, 33 and 34 to Zone 3. A 13 June 2026 report states implementation was to begin on 15 June and gives the cluster allocations. The Haryana ULB solid-waste portal independently lists later operational contracts using these same ward-zone groups [5]. Older contracts on that portal still retain four-zone labels; contract scope dates must therefore be checked before routing a complaint.

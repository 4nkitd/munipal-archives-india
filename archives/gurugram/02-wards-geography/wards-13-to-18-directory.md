# Gurugram wards 13 to 18: notified localities and zone directory

## Scope, dates and source status

The controlling source is Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department order No. 19/45/2023-6CI, dated 14 December 2023, under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994. It refers to the draft notification dated 26 August 2023. This is the final 36-ward scheme used for the 2024 electoral-roll preparations and March 2025 municipal election, not a newly dated 2024 order.

The official PDF is scanned. Text extraction returned no usable text; the ward schedules on PDF pages 8 to 11 were read visually. [Scanned map/PDF: visual source linked at source_url.] Locality lists below reproduce the schedule's express inclusions with normalized spelling and punctuation. They are not parcel-level boundary certificates. A road, village or sector mentioned only as a boundary does not establish inclusion of its whole area.

Administrative zones are dated separately because they changed after delimitation. September 2026 zone assignments below are corroborated reporting of an MCG office order, not a copy of that signed order.

The official schedule on PDF page 8 starts at Rajiv Chowk, 28.4456, 77.0336. It follows Civil Lines Road towards the new PWD Rest House, the Gurugram and Badshahpur assembly-constituency boundary and Bandh Road, then the Sector 15 Part II boundary towards NH-48 near Unitech Signature Tower II. Other boundary references include the Sector 30/31 dividing road, Shrimati Santosh Yadav Road, Netaji Subhash Marg, the Sector 39/40 dividing road, Mohyal Colony's boundary and the Medicity tri-junction.

Civil Lines Road and Mohyal Colony are boundary references here. Do not read them as an assignment of all Civil Lines or Mohyal Colony to Ward 13. The express inclusion list says "Prem Puri Jharsa", not all of Jharsa village.

PDF page 9 expressly includes Dhumaspur, which is omitted in some secondary locality lists. The schedule spells Nirvana Country as "Nirwana Country" and Malibu Towne as "Malibu Town".

The boundary starts at 28.4172, 77.0404, follows the southeastern Malibu Towne boundary and western Mayfield Garden boundary, then approaches Golf Course Road and the Ghata, Tigra and Nangli Umarpur tri-junction. It follows the revenue estates of Nangli Umarpur, Maidawas and Dhumaspur towards IREO Corridors Tower A-9 at 28.3835, 77.0666. The return route follows Badshahpur's northern boundary, Sohna Road, Vatika Chowk, Southern Peripheral Road and the Uppal Southend boundary.

Only part of Malibu Towne is included. Uppal Southend and Badshahpur in this boundary description should not be substituted for the express Ward 15 inclusion list.

The schedule spans PDF pages 10 and 11. The notification spells Narsinghpur as "Narsingpur". It starts at 28.4244, 77.0005, near NH-48 and the 30-metre road towards Begumpur Khatola. It follows the habitation boundary, the eastern Sector 35 boundary, Southern Peripheral Road, Maharana Partap Chowk and the Sector 75/70 dividing road.

The southern boundary uses the Palra and Darbaripur revenue estates, the eastern Sector 76 boundary and Kherki Daula's northern boundary. The return route follows NH-48, Northern Peripheral Road, Gurugram-Pataudi Road, Gadauli Khurd's phirni, the Badshahpur drain, Pace City and Khandsa's southern boundary.

Khandsa is a boundary landmark, not an express Ward 17 village inclusion. Do not assign Khandsa village or the whole Khandsa industrial belt to this ward merely from proximity.

| Villages and settlements | Badshahpur; Ramgarh ki Dhani; Noorpur |
| September 2026 administration | Zone I, Cluster 1 |

PDF page 11 starts Ward 18 at Vatika Chowk, 28.4013, 77.0449. The boundary follows Gurgaon-Sohna Road and Badshahpur's northern revenue boundary towards Hanuman Mandir and IREO Corridors Tower A-9. It then follows the Badshahpur revenue estate towards Bhoop Nagar Colony, 28.3744, 77.0757, and the southern boundary of Badshahpur and Noorpur Jharsa.

The return boundary references the Palra, Noorpur Jharsa and Aklimpur revenue boundaries, GPL Eden Heights, the Sector 75/70 dividing road and Southern Peripheral Road.

The express locality list says "Noorpur"; the boundary description identifies the revenue estate as Noorpur Jharsa. Palra belongs to Ward 17's express inclusion list and is a boundary reference for Ward 18. Bhoop Nagar Colony is a boundary landmark here, not a separately certified whole-colony assignment.

## Zone chronology and old-numbering warning

| Ward | April 2025 four-zone arrangement | September 2026 six-zone arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | Zone III | Zone V, Cluster 2 |
| 14 | Zone I | Zone I, Cluster 1 |
| 15 | Zone I | Zone I, Cluster 1 |
| 16 | Zone I | Zone I, Cluster 1 |
| 17 | Zone I | Zone I, Cluster 1 |
| 18 | Zone I | Zone I, Cluster 1 |

The April 2025 arrangement was followed by an eight-zone system in June 2026 and a six-zone system effective 8 September 2026. Engineering division numbers from March 2025 are historical assignments, not an assertion that those divisions remain the current service-routing structure.

Old Gurugram ward numbers are not interchangeable with these 36-ward numbers. Older lists place Sector 14, Sector 17 and Sukhrali in Ward 17, and Civil Lines and Patel Nagar in Ward 18. The new directory instead places those areas in the later-numbered Old Gurugram wards. The secondary 2025 list records Sector 14, Sector 16, Sector 17 and Sukhrali under Ward 25; Rajeev Nagar under Ward 26; Civil Lines and Patel Nagar under Ward 27; and Shivaji Nagar under Ward 29. These cross-references explain the numbering mismatch, not certified address assignments for those other wards.

No unqualified Sadar Bazar or Sector 12 assignment to Wards 13 to 18 is supported by the six official schedules examined. Sector 12A is also distinct from Sector 12. Consult the complete final notification or ward electoral roll for those addresses.

# Gurugram Wards 19 to 24 locality directory for the 36-ward delimitation

## Scope and source status

The 36-ward arrangement often described as the 2024 delimitation has an earlier final-order date. Haryana's Urban Local Bodies Department lists the final Gurugram wardbandi notification on **15 December 2023**. Contemporary reporting identifies government approval on **14 December 2023**. Order No. **19/45/2023-6CI** refers to the preliminary notification dated **26 August 2023**. The frontmatter date records the reported order date, not a separately verified 2024 notification. [1][2][3]

The official PDF is scanned. Direct `pdftotext` extraction produced no substantive text. [Scanned map/PDF. Visual source linked at source_url.] The locality schedules below are transcribed from the contemporary 17 December 2023 account and checked against the later Newzpapr 36-ward directory. They are not a certified transcription of every boundary coordinate in the official scan. The March 2025 engineering realignment independently corroborates the Ward 21 schedule. [3][4][5]

Locality names are search aids, not parcel-level boundary determinations. A sector, revenue estate or developer colony can cross a ward boundary. References to Wazirabad in both Wards 21 and 22, Sushant Lok 2 in Wards 20 and 21, and Sushant Lok 1 in Wards 22 and 24 must not be read as whole-area exclusive assignments.

## Ward 19

Ward 19 is the Bhondsi and Naya Gaon belt in the final 36-ward locality schedule. It is not the DLF or Silokhera ward. [3][4]

| Type | Recorded areas |
| --- | --- |
| Villages | Bhondsi; Naya Gaon |
| Colonies and neighbourhoods | Mohan Nagar; Vatika Kunj; Maruti Kunj; Defence Colony; Shri Ram Colony; Rayan Colony; Shyam Kunj; Ekta Enclave; Mayur Enclave; Nirmala Enclave; Shanti Kunj |
| Sectors | No numbered sector expressly listed in the cross-checked locality summary |
| Engineering division, March 2025 | Division 6 |
| Administrative zone, April 2025 | Zone 4 |
| June 2026 official zone schedule | Zone VII |

The source spelling of Rayan Colony varies in transliteration. Mohan Nagar appears twice in the later directory and is listed once here. Colony names do not establish regularisation status, ownership or eligibility for a municipal service.

Do not import the older Ward 19 description covering sectors 15, 30, 31 and 40 into this record. A September 2024 news report used that older ward geography. The applicable ward numbering must therefore be checked before comparing a complaint, tender or historical councillor record. The engineering division and zones above have separate effective dates. [5][6][7]

## Ward 20

Ward 20 covers the Kadarpur, Ullawas, Ghata and Gwal Pahari belt, with several Golf Course Extension Road sectors and part of Sushant Lok 2 in the locality schedule. [3][4]

| Type | Recorded areas |
| --- | --- |
| Villages and named settlements | Kadarpur; Ullawas; Behrampur; Bandhwari; Balola; Gwal Pahari; Ghata |
| Sectors | Sector 55; Sector 58; Sector 61; Sector 62 |
| Colonies | Sushant Lok 2 |
| Engineering division, March 2025 | Division 7 |
| Administrative zone, April 2025 | Zone 4 |
| June 2026 official zone schedule | Zone VII |

The Hindi contemporary schedule writes the settlement name as Berampur, while the later directory uses Behrampur. This record preserves the reported Ward 20 entry but does not equate it automatically with every property described as Behrampur elsewhere in Gurugram. The March 2025 engineering report also mentions Behrampur under Ward 16, so a property-specific check is necessary. [3][4][5]

Bandhwari also appears as Bhandwari, and Ullawas as Ullahwas. Sushant Lok 2 is listed under both Wards 20 and 21. Neither the complete colony nor all its blocks should be assigned to Ward 20 from this summary alone.

## Ward 21

Ward 21 is the Wazirabad, Ardee City, Suncity and eastern-sector grouping in the final locality schedule. The March 2025 report on MCG's engineering realignment repeats Ardee City, sectors 52, 57, 56, 53, 52A and 54, Sushant Lok II, Suncity and Sushant Lok E Block. [3][4][5]

| Type | Recorded areas |
| --- | --- |
| Village | Wazirabad |
| Sectors | Sector 52; Sector 57; Sector 56; Sector 53; Sector 52A; Sector 54 |
| Colonies and neighbourhoods | Ardee City; Sushant Lok 2; Suncity; Sushant Lok E Block |
| Engineering division, March 2025 | Division 8 |
| Administrative zone, April 2025 | Zone 4 |
| June 2026 official zone schedule | Zone VII |

The source says "Sushant Lok E Block" without identifying a phase in that phrase. This directory does not supply one. Wazirabad is also mentioned in the Ward 22 summary, and Sushant Lok 2 occurs under Ward 20. Use the official boundary schedule for an individual plot or block.

A later citizen directory assigns the Silokhera and South City 1 grouping to Ward 21. That conflicts with the contemporary schedule and March 2025 corroboration used here. This record does not adopt that reassignment without a supporting official amendment. [8]

## Ward 22

Ward 22 contains the DLF Phase 4 and Phase 5 grouping, Sushant Lok 1 Blocks A and B, and sectors 27, 42 and 43 in the cross-checked locality schedule. [3][4]

| Type | Recorded areas |
| --- | --- |
| Sectors | Sector 27; Sector 42; Sector 43 |
| Developer colonies | DLF Phase 4; DLF Phase 5; Sushant Lok 1 Blocks A and B |
| Other recorded locality references | Harijan Colony; Wazirabad |
| Engineering division, March 2025 | Division 8 |
| Administrative zone, April 2025 | Zone 4 |
| June 2026 official zone schedule | Zone VII |

"Harijan Colony" is retained solely as the locality label printed in the contemporary source. It is not a description of residents. Wazirabad's appearance here does not establish that its whole revenue estate lies in Ward 22.

The Sushant Lok 1 reference is block-specific. The separate Ward 24 entry also names Sushant Lok 1, so a phase name alone cannot resolve a ward. Later tender indexing corroborates Ward 22 for Sushant Lok 1 Blocks A and B and sectors 27, 42 and 43, but this directory retains the dated March 2025 engineering assignment rather than treating tender division labels as permanent. [9]

## Ward 23

Ward 23 is the Chakkarpur, Maruti Vihar, Saraswati Vihar and DLF Phase 1 grouping. The contemporary final-approval report includes Sector 28 and Aravali Hills. [3][4]

| Type | Recorded areas |
| --- | --- |
| Village | Chakkarpur |
| Sector | Sector 28 |
| Colonies | Saraswati Vihar Colony; Maruti Vihar; DLF City Phase 1 |
| Other geographical reference | Aravali Hills |
| Engineering division, March 2025 | Division 8 |
| Administrative zone, April 2025 | Zone 4 |
| June 2026 official zone schedule | Zone VIII |

The later Newzpapr summary also inserts Maruti Kunj in Ward 23. The contemporary final-approval schedule puts Maruti Kunj in Ward 19 and does not include it in Ward 23. This record therefore does not treat the extra Ward 23 entry as verified. [3][4]

DLF Phase 1 is not interchangeable with DLF Phases 2 or 3. The official notification's indexed Ward 1 text includes Nathupur, Sikanderpur Ghosi and the Sikanderpur Ghosi Aravali Hills. It also describes a boundary beside DLF Phase 1 Block G and Silver Oak Apartment. Consequently, the broad "Aravali Hills" and "DLF Phase 1" labels here cannot replace the actual boundary description for edge properties. [1]

## Ward 24

Ward 24 is the Kanhai, Silokhera and South City 1 grouping in the contemporary final locality schedule. Its listed sectors are 44, 45, 29 and 30. [3][4]

| Type | Recorded areas |
| --- | --- |
| Villages and settlements | Kanhai; Saini Khera; Silokhera |
| Sectors | Sector 44; Sector 45; Sector 29; Sector 30 |
| Colonies and complexes | Sushant Lok 1; Essel Towers; Beverly Park; Moti Vihar; South City 1; Vijay Vihar; Jal Vayu Vihar |
| Engineering division, March 2025 | Division 8 |
| Administrative zone, April 2025 | Zone 4 |
| June 2026 official zone schedule | Zone VIII |

Silokhera also appears as Silokhra or Silokhara. Jal Vayu Vihar appears as Jalvayu Vihar. These are retained as search variants, not separate settlements. The contemporary report additionally prints "South City-30", an ambiguous label not adopted as a separate verified colony.

South City 1 is not wholly assignable from its name. The later directory expressly lists several South City 1 blocks under Ward 12 as well. Similarly, the notification's indexed Ward 2 schedule specifies Beverly Park 1, while the Ward 24 summary says only Beverly Park. Do not merge the two entries or infer a tower's ward without the official boundary and electoral-roll check. [1][4]

## Administrative zone chronology

Electoral wards and administrative zones are different geographical units. Do not backdate a later zone number into the 2023 final wardbandi order.

| Source date | Wards 19 to 22 | Wards 23 and 24 | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 April 2025 report | Zone 4 | Zone 4 | Four-zone restructuring report [6] |
| 11 June 2026 official map | Zone VII | Zone VIII | MCG order No. MCG/CMC/2026/16598 [7] |
| 8 September 2026 order reported by Newzpapr | Zone 6, Cluster 2 | Zone 6, Cluster 2 | Later secondary report only [4] |

The September report describes a further six-zone structure. Its underlying signed order was not independently retrieved for this record. The June map is therefore a verified dated official reference, not a claim that its eight-zone structure remained current on the verification date. Confirm the latest MCG office order before routing a grievance or identifying a present zonal officer.

# Gurugram Wards 25 to 30 locality and zone directory

## Scope, notification and evidence

The final 36-ward delimitation is associated with Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department order No. 19/45/2023-6CI dated 14 December 2023, referring to the draft notification of 26 August 2023. The department's notification register lists the final notification on 15 December 2023. This is the scheme relevant to the requested 2024 period, not a separately verified 2024 delimitation order. [1][2][3]

[Scanned map/PDF: visual source linked at source_url]

The downloaded official PDF yielded no text through `pdftotext`. Accordingly, the locality lists below are attributed to the contemporary 17 December 2023 report of the approved schedule, cross-checked against a later published ward directory. They are not presented as a fresh verbatim transcription of the scanned legal boundaries. The official schedule and map remain necessary for an individual property's boundary determination. [1][3][4]

The requested Wazirabad, Ardee City, Kanhai, Jharsa, Tigra, Ghata, Nirvana Country and Mayfield Garden belt is not Ward 25 to Ward 30 in this scheme. A correction table follows the six ward records.

## Ward 25

| Geography field | Recorded coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Village | Sukhrali |
| Sectors | Sector 14, Sector 16, Sector 17 and Sector 12A |
| Colonies and residential areas | Canal Colony, Old DLF Colony, Chander Nagar, Anamika Enclave, HVPNL Colony and Prem Nagar 2 |
| Institutional and employment areas | Air Force Station, MDI, IDC and Police Lines |
| Zone from 8 September 2026 | Zone V, Cluster 2 |
| Earlier engineering division, March 2025 | Division 5 |

The approved-schedule report explicitly places Sukhrali and this central sector and institutional belt in Ward 25. The later directory uses "Anamika Colony" rather than "Anamika Enclave" and abbreviates Prem Nagar without the suffix. These are source spelling or naming differences, not grounds to add another colony. [3][4]

Sector 12A also appears in Ward 26's locality list. Do not infer that the whole sector belongs exclusively to Ward 25. Wazirabad, Sector 45 and Ardee City are not listed as Ward 25 localities in the cited 36-ward coverage. [3]

## Ward 26

| Geography field | Recorded coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Sectors | Sector 12A |
| Colonies and residential areas | Sanjay Gram, Rajiv Nagar, Rajiv Nagar West, Acharya Puri and Sheetla Colony Blocks A and B |
| Village | No village separately named in the cited locality summary |
| Zone from 8 September 2026 | Zone V, Cluster 2 |
| Earlier engineering division, March 2025 | Division 5 |

The later directory separates "Rajeev Nagar West" and "Acharya Puri". The contemporary Hindi report compresses these names in its line for Ward 26. Both sources place Sanjay Gram, Sector 12A and Sheetla Colony Blocks A and B here. Rajiv and Rajeev are spelling variants in the sources. [3][4]

This record does not assign all of Sheetla Colony or all of Sector 12A to Ward 26. The contemporary schedule summary separately names Sheetla Colony under Ward 36 and Sector 12A under Ward 25. Block and street boundaries therefore matter. Nirvana Country and South City 2 are not Ward 26 entries under this scheme. [3]

Rajiv Colony is distinct from Rajiv Nagar, which appears in Ward 26. The contemporary report names the above colony and market belt for Ward 28, and the later directory substantially repeats it. [3][4]

Amar Colony appears in both Ward 28 and Ward 29 summaries. The locality name alone cannot resolve which side of the notified boundary contains a particular house. The older Ward 28 list containing Jharsa, Prem Puri and Sectors 31, 32, 32A and 39 should not replace this 36-ward record. [3][4]

The contemporary report specifies Arjun Nagar and the eastern part of Jyoti Park. The later English directory instead says "Arjun Vihar" and drops the east qualifier from Jyoti Park. The older Ward 30 entry containing Wazirabad, Samaspur, Tigra, Mayfield Garden and Sectors 46, 47, 51 and 57 is not the current 36-ward entry. Those names must not be copied into Ward 30 merely because an older list uses that number. [3][4]

## Zone and division dates

Ward delimitation and administrative zone allocation are separate records. In March 2025, Division 5 covered Wards 25, 26 and 27, while Division 4 covered Wards 28, 29 and 30. [5]

The April 2025 four-zone arrangement put Wards 25 and 26 in Zone IV, Ward 27 in Zone III, and Wards 28 to 30 in Zone II. [6] The official June 2026 eight-zone map, order No. MCG/CMC/2026/16598 dated 11 June 2026, subsequently put Wards 25 and 26 in Zone VIII, Ward 27 in Zone VI, and Wards 28 to 30 in Zone IV. [7]

Reports of the commissioner’s 8 September 2026 order state that the eight-zone setup was replaced by six zones with immediate effect. Wards 25, 26 and 27 moved to Zone V, Cluster 2; Wards 28, 29 and 30 to Zone II, Cluster 1. Those are the latest mappings corroborated in the sources checked for this record. The September order itself was not independently retrieved, so the latest mapping is supported by two named reports, not by the superseded June map. [8][9]

# Municipal Corporation Gurugram Wards 31 to 36 locality directory

## Scope, notification and numbering correction

The Haryana Urban Local Bodies notification index lists the final Gurugram ward-delimitation notification on **15 December 2023**. Its order number is **19/45/2023-6CI**, referring to the preliminary notification dated 26 August 2023. The frontmatter date records the official index publication date, not a separately deciphered signature date. This is the 36-ward arrangement used for the March 2025 municipal election and often described in resident material as the new wardbandi. [1][2]

The premise that Wards 31 to 36 cover Badshahpur, Sohna Road, Sectors 47 to 50 or Sectors 65 to 72 is incorrect for this numbering. The locality sources instead place these six wards in older Gurugram. Badshahpur is in Ward 18; Fazilpur Jharsa and Behrampur are listed in Ward 16; Mohammadpur Jharsa, Narsinghpur and Kherki Daula are listed in Ward 17. Islampur is listed in Ward 12. Those references do not establish a parcel-level MCG boundary with Municipal Corporation Manesar. [3][4]

The downloaded notification returned no usable text through `pdftotext`. Consequently, this record does not present secondary locality lists as a verbatim transcription of its legal boundary schedule. Locality membership below comes from the named cross-references. No coordinates, street-side boundaries or complete revenue-estate inclusions have been inferred.

Gurugram Vision Forum independently lists the four core localities. Newzpapr provides the longer colony list. Its spelling "Bhim Garden" is retained rather than silently changing it to Bhim Nagar. The additional colonies remain secondary-source entries pending a readable transcription of the notified schedule. [4][5]

An indexed MCG tender, **2026_HRY_505612_1**, published 27 February 2026, describes two tubewells in Dayanand Colony in Ward 31, Division 3B. This corroborates Dayanand Colony, but neither that works division nor an older tender's zone label should be treated as the current administrative zone. The September 2026 restructuring places Ward 31 in Zone V. [8][9]

## Ward 32

| Field | Directory entry |
| --- | --- |
| Localities corroborated by March 2025 reporting | Sector 7; Shivpuri; New Colony; Krishna Colony; Jyoti Park; Sector 7 Extension; Housing Board Colony |
| Additional entries in the Newzpapr new-ward list | Feroz Shah Colony 2; Surya Vihar |
| Numbered sector | Sector 7, including its named extension and housing-board locality |
| September 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 1, Zone II |

The Times of India report of 7 March 2025 explicitly lists the seven corroborated entries while describing MCG's post-delimitation engineering reorganisation. Jyoti Park also appears in the Ward 30 list, so the name alone cannot establish which ward contains an individual house. [3][4]

Newzpapr's spelling "Feroz Shah Colony 2" differs from older references to Feroz Gandhi Colony Phase 2. This record does not assume that the two names are interchangeable. Surya Vihar is also a name used elsewhere in Gurugram, including the Dundahera area. Confirm the address against the notified boundary and electoral roll before relying on either additional entry. Whole-sector inclusion beyond the named source list is not established here.

## Ward 33

| Field | Directory entry |
| --- | --- |
| Residential localities | Laxman Vihar Phase 1; Laxman Vihar Phase 2; Cancon Enclave |
| Numbered sector | Sector 4 |
| Village inclusion | No separate village inclusion confirmed in the reviewed locality lists |
| September 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 1, Zone III |

The Times of India post-delimitation report and Newzpapr's new-ward directory both list the two Laxman Vihar phases, Cancon Enclave and Sector 4 in Ward 33. "Cancon Enclave" is the spelling in those references. It has not been replaced with a guessed estate or colony name. [3][4]

Ward 33 is not the Jharsa ward merely because an older directory or legacy administrative code attaches Jharsa to the same number. Ward numbers must be read with their delimitation version. In particular, a map with only 35 wards cannot validate the present 36-ward assignment.

The April 2025 four-zone arrangement put Ward 33 in Zone II. The June 2026 eight-zone arrangement moved it to Zone III, and the September 2026 six-zone restructuring retained it in Zone III. These are changes in municipal administration, not evidence that the ward's electoral boundary changed on each occasion. [6][7][8]

## Ward 34

| Field | Directory entry |
| --- | --- |
| Colonies and residential localities | Rajendra Park; Tek Chand Nagar; Surat Nagar Phase 1 |
| Industrial locality | Daulatabad Industrial Area, also written Daultabad Industrial Area |
| Numbered sectors | None confirmed by the reviewed Ward 34 locality lists |
| September 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 1, Zone III |

The Times of India report of 7 March 2025 and Newzpapr's new-ward directory agree on these four entries. Neither reference justifies assigning the entire revenue estate of Daulatabad to Ward 34. The named entry is its industrial area. Newzpapr separately lists Daulatabad village in Ward 6. [3][4]

Surat Nagar requires the phase number. Phase 1 is listed in Ward 34, while the new-ward directory places Phase 2 in Ward 7. A generic address saying only "Surat Nagar" therefore needs a street-level check.

Rajendra Park and the Daulatabad corridor are associated with western Gurugram, but that geographic description does not turn Ward 34 into a Kherki Daula or Manesar-boundary ward. The notified municipal limit and ward schedule remain necessary for any disputed parcel. Ward 34's current zone assignment is based on the September 2026 restructuring reported by both Hindustan Times and The Times of India. [8]

## Ward 35

| Field | Directory entry |
| --- | --- |
| Localities | Bhimgarh Kheri; Ashok Vihar; Ashok Vihar Phase 2 |
| Phases named by Newzpapr | Bhim Kheri Phases 1, 2 and 3 |
| Numbered sectors | Sector 5; Sector 6 |
| Conflicting locality spelling | Apna Enclave in Gurugram Vision Forum; Apna Vihar in Newzpapr |
| September 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 2, Zone IV |

Gurugram Vision Forum lists Bhim Garh Kheri, Ashok Vihar, Sectors 5 and 6, and Apna Enclave. Newzpapr lists the Bhim Kheri phases, Ashok Vihar Phase 2, the same sectors, and Apna Vihar. The last two Apna names are recorded as a source discrepancy, not certified aliases. [4][10]

MCG works-title corroboration is available for Ashok Vihar Phase 1. Tender **2025_HRY_469952_1** describes sewer and road work at Sitaram Mandir Road in Ashok Vihar Phase 1, Ward 35. Its historical Zone III label reflects the then-used system. [11]

Do not extend this entry to every Ashok Vihar phase. MCG tender descriptions place Ashok Vihar Phase 3 works in Ward 36. The September 2026 assignment for Ward 35 is Zone IV, superseding its June 2026 Zone VI placement. [7][8][12]

## Ward 36

| Field | Directory entry |
| --- | --- |
| Localities in the Newzpapr new-ward list | Ashok Vihar Phase 3; Sheetla Colony; Gurgaon Colony; Amanpura |
| Village corroborated by MCG works titles | Gurugram village, also commonly called Gurgaon village |
| Numbered sectors | None confirmed by the reviewed Ward 36 locality sources |
| September 2026 administrative assignment | Cluster 2, Zone IV |

MCG tender **2026_HRY_500176_1**, published 2 February 2026, describes sewer connections on Sheetla Colony Road and Ashok Vihar Phase 3 Road, also called Saraye Rasta, in Ward 36. Tender **2025_HRY_459850_1** names Sheetla Colony and Amanpura in Ward 36. Tender **2026_HRY_515166_1** names Mouji Ram chaupal in Gurugram village in Ward 36. These are locality corroborations from indexed works descriptions, not a full boundary survey. [12][13][14]

Sheetla Colony must not be assigned wholesale without checking its block. Newzpapr also lists Sheetla Colony Blocks A and B under Ward 26. Its "Gurgaon Colony" entry should not automatically be equated with the Gurugram village revenue estate. [4]

An isolated news report places a Phase 3 event in Ward 35, contradicting the works-title evidence. This directory follows the more specific MCG tender descriptions for Phase 3 and records the conflict rather than treating all Ashok Vihar as one ward.

## Administrative zone chronology

Administrative zones are not ward numbers or permanent engineering-division identifiers.

| Ward | April 2025 four-zone arrangement [6] | June 2026 eight-zone arrangement [7] | September 2026 six-zone arrangement [8] |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 31 | III | VI | V, Cluster 2 |
| 32 | II | IV | II, Cluster 1 |
| 33 | II | III | III, Cluster 1 |
| 34 | II | III | III, Cluster 1 |
| 35 | III | VI | IV, Cluster 2 |
| 36 | III | V | IV, Cluster 2 |

The June arrangement is documented by the official MCG map bearing order **MCG/CMC/2026/16598 dated 11 June 2026**. The later six-zone structure took effect on **8 September 2026**, according to two independent reports of the commissioner's office order. The September order itself was not retrieved for this record. Current zone entries therefore have corroborated reporting support, while the linked official June map is historical. [7][8]

## Southern belt and Manesar boundary caution

The reviewed new-ward lists place Sector 47 in Ward 11; Sectors 49 and 50 and Sectors 65 and 66 in Ward 15; Sectors 67 to 70 and Badshahpur in Ward 18; and Sector 71 and Sector 72A with Fazilpur Jharsa and Behrampur in Ward 16. Sector 50 also appears in the Ward 14 Mayfield Garden entry. Sector 48 and Sector 72 without the A suffix were not conclusively assigned by the reviewed current lists. "Tikri", "Teekri" and "Teekli" must not be merged without checking the revenue village. [3][4]

Kherki Daula, Mohammadpur Jharsa and Narsinghpur appear in the Ward 17 list, not Wards 31 to 36. A locality label or the phrase "Dwarka Expressway" cannot establish the MCG boundary adjoining MC Manesar. No surveyed inter-corporation line, transferred khasra list or complete sector-side allocation has been verified here. Consult the official municipal-limit notification and final ward schedule for those questions.

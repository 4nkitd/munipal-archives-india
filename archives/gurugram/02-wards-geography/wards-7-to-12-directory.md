# Gurugram Wards 7 to 12: locality directory and administrative zones

## Notification and numbering

The 36-ward scheme used for the 2024 electoral preparations comes from Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department order No. 19/45/2023-6CI, dated 14 December 2023. The department's notification index lists its publication on 15 December 2023. The order refers to the preliminary notification dated 26 August 2023. It should not be described as a newly issued 2024 boundary notification. [1][2]

The locality lists below transcribe the final order's express inclusion clauses on PDF pages 4 to 8. Sector names are normalized, but the schedule's named South City blocks are preserved. These are locality indexes, not substitutes for the notification's coordinate-based boundaries. A boundary description mentioning an adjoining colony does not establish that the whole colony belongs to that ward.

The PDF is scanned. Standard text extraction returned no substantive text; its pages were read using optical character recognition. The official source is linked for boundary verification. [Scanned map/PDF: visual source linked at source_url]

## Ward 7

| Geography type | Localities expressly included |
| --- | --- |
| Villages | Dhanwapur; Basai; Gadauli Khurd; Gadauli Kalan |
| Colonies | Surat Nagar Phase II; Ram Vihar |
| Sectors | Sector 9B; Sector 37B; Sector 37D; Sector 100; Sector 101; Sector 104 |
| Administrative zone, effective 8 September 2026 | Zone III, Cluster 1 |
| Engineering division reported 7 March 2025 | Division 3 |

The boundary starts at the southern border of Surat Nagar Phase I, at 28.4830, 77.0056. It uses the railway line, the Sector 9/9B dividing road, Basai village phirni, the Sector 9B/37C and Sector 37C/37D roads, Pataudi Road, Badshahpur Drain and Dwarka Expressway. It returns through the northern revenue boundary of Dhanwapur and Dhanwapur-Bajghera Road. Surat Nagar Phase I is a boundary reference, while the express inclusion list names Phase II. [1, pp. 4-5]

Basai village is in this list; Basai Enclave I and II are expressly listed in Ward 9. Do not merge the village and both enclaves into one ward record. A 2025 tender independently identifies Dhanwapur village pond as Ward 7, then Zone II, illustrating that zone numbers have changed without changing the ward number. [6]

## Ward 8

| Geography type | Localities expressly included |
| --- | --- |
| Sectors | Sector 9; Sector 9A |
| Colonies | Bhawani Enclave; Ambedkar Nagar; Devilal Colony; Ravi Nagar; Vishwakarma Colony |
| Villages | No village separately named in the express inclusion clause |
| Administrative zone, effective 8 September 2026 | Zone II, Cluster 1 |
| Engineering division reported 7 March 2025 | Division 3 |

The boundary begins at Basai Chowk, 28.4575, 76.9904, and follows Shaheed Sukhbir Yadav Marg to the Gurugram-Rewari railway line. It continues along Sector 9A, the boundary with Surya Vihar and the Sector 9/9A dividing road. Its eastern and southern route refers to Firoz Gandhi Colony II, Sector 7 Housing Board, Jyoti Park, Devilal Colony, Ravi Nagar and the Enkay Factory point. The closing route uses Basai Road, Government Hospital Sector 10 and the northern boundary of Alpine Convent School. [1, p. 5]

Sector 7 Housing Board and Jyoti Park appear in the boundary narrative, not in Ward 8's express inclusion clause. This directory therefore does not assign those entire localities to Ward 8. Sector 7's principal locality grouping is under Ward 32 in the 36-ward directory. [3][5]

## Ward 9

| Geography type | Localities expressly included |
| --- | --- |
| Village | Khandsa |
| Sectors | Sector 10; Sector 37C |
| Colonies and enclaves | Krishna Nagar; Vikas Nagar; Basai Enclave I; Basai Enclave II; Kadipur Enclave; Saraswati Enclave |
| Industrial areas | Kadipur Industrial Area; Pace City II; Udyog Vihar Phase VI |
| Administrative zone, effective 8 September 2026 | Zone III, Cluster 1 |
| Engineering division reported 7 March 2025 | Division 3 |

The boundary starts near Enkay Factory at 28.457, 77.0079. It follows the Krishna Nagar/Sector 10 edge to Pataudi Road near Rotary Club Blood Bank, then passes Captain Umang Bhardwaj Chowk and Hero Honda Chowk. Other defining edges include NH-48, Khandsa's southern revenue boundary, Saraswati Enclave, the Sector 37C/37D dividing road and Basai Enclave I's boundary. [1, p. 6]

Kadipur Enclave and Kadipur Industrial Area belong in this inclusion list, while Kadipur village is expressly included in Ward 10. Likewise, Pace City II is listed here and Pace City I in Ward 10. The final order includes Vikas Nagar and Kadipur Enclave even though some shorter online summaries omit them. [1][5]

## Ward 10

| Geography type | Localities expressly included |
| --- | --- |
| Village | Kadipur |
| Sector | Sector 10A |
| Colonies | Shakti Park; Hari Nagar |
| Industrial areas | Pace City I; Old Industrial Area Khandsa Road |
| Administrative zone, effective 8 September 2026 | Zone II, Cluster 1 |
| Engineering division reported 7 March 2025 | Division 3 |

The notification begins the ward at Pataudi Road near Rotary Club Blood Bank, 28.4518, 77.0066. It follows the southwestern boundary of Gandhi Nagar and northeastern boundaries of Shakti Park and Hari Nagar to the highway. It then runs toward Hero Honda Chowk, along Shaheed Sukhbir Singh Yadav Marg to Captain Umang Bhardwaj Chowk, and returns by Kadipur village phirni and Pataudi Road. [1, p. 6]

The official inclusion clause says "Shakti Park". The spelling "Shaktipur" in a secondary online directory is not adopted here. Laxman Vihar and Sector 4 are not Ward 10 under this scheme; they are in Ward 33. Older municipal ward tables that place them in Ward 10 refer to a different numbering system. [1][3][5]

| Sectors | Sector 47; Sector 33 |

The boundary starts at Rajiv Chowk, 28.4456, 77.0336. It follows Sohna Road to Subhash Chowk, then the Sector 38/47 and Sector 46/47 dividing roads toward Bakhtawar Chowk and Vikas Marg. The route follows the Mayfield Garden and Malibu Towne edges, passes the Teekri revenue estate and Badshahpur Drain, and returns by Netaji Subhash Marg, Hero Honda Chowk, NH-48 and Hans Enclave. [1, p. 7]

The official order names village Teekri, not "Teekli". It also expressly includes Infocity II, which is absent from some abridged lists. Ward 15 separately includes part of Malibu Town, so the Ward 11 locality entry must not be interpreted as assigning the entire development without consulting its boundary. Sector 7, Shivpuri and Krishna Colony belong to the Ward 32 grouping, not this Ward 11. [1][3]

| Geography type | Localities expressly included |

| Colonies and developments | Mohyal Colony; Greenwood City; Medicity |
| South City I blocks | I; J; K; L; M; N; O; P; W; Q, as printed in the order |
| Sectors | Sector 38; Sector 39 |
| Administrative zone, effective 8 September 2026 | Zone V, Cluster 2 |

The boundary begins at Rajiv Chowk and passes Medicity Tri-junction, Ch. Bakhtawar Singh Road and the Sector 39/32A dividing road. It continues along the Sector 31/40 road, Mohyal Colony, Jharsa Road, Netaji Subhash Marg and the Sector 40/41 dividing road. South City I, Arya Samaj Marg and Greenwood City's boundary lead toward Unitech Cyber Park and Subhash Chowk; Sohna Road closes the boundary. [1, pp. 7-8]

Sector 32A, Sector 40 and Sector 41 occur as boundary references, but are not separately named in this ward's express inclusion clause. The block letter W is retained because it appears in that clause; it has not been silently replaced or expanded. South City I as a whole must not be assigned to Ward 12, since other wards include parts of it. [1][5]

## Zone history and corrections to the requested locality belt

Ward boundaries, engineering divisions and administrative zones are separate classifications. The March 2025 division allocations above come from reporting of MCG's engineering reorganisation. The latest zone allocations shown here come from reporting of the order effective 8 September 2026. That order replaced the eight-zone setup introduced in June 2026. The underlying September order was not independently retrieved, so those latest zone entries are attributed to the published report rather than presented as direct transcriptions of an official PDF. [3][4]

For historical use, the April 2025 four-zone arrangement placed Wards 7 and 8 in Zone II, Wards 9, 10 and 11 in Zone I, and Ward 12 in Zone III. Do not apply the September 2026 zone numbers retrospectively to 2024 or 2025 documents. [7]

Several localities named in the research brief are outside Wards 7 to 12 under the final 36-ward numbering:

| Locality belt | 36-ward directory assignment |
| --- | --- |
| Sector 7, Shivpuri, New Colony, Krishna Colony, Sector 7 Extension and Housing Board Colony | Ward 32 |
| Laxman Vihar Phases I and II, Sector 4, Cancon Enclave | Ward 33 |
| Rajendra Park, Daulatabad Industrial Area, Surat Nagar Phase I, Tek Chand Nagar | Ward 34 |
| Ashok Vihar and Ashok Vihar Phase II, Sectors 5 and 6, Bhim Kheri Phases I to III | Ward 35 |
| Ashok Vihar Phase III, Sheetla Colony, Gurgaon Colony, Amanpura | Ward 36 |

These cross-references prevent old ward numbers from being carried into this record. "Sheetla Mata" is not treated as an interchangeable name for all of Sheetla Colony or as proof that a particular temple parcel lies in one ward. Confirm individual addresses against the official boundary and electoral roll. [3][5]

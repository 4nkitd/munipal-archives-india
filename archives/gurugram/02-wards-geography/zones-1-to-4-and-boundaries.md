# MCG Zones 1 to 4 and municipal boundaries

## Scope and change of administrative system

This record preserves MCG's four-zone arrangement, including the redistribution reported on 17 April 2025 after delimitation into 36 wards. It is not a current four-zone directory. MCG subsequently adopted eight zones effective 15 June 2026, then six zones under two clusters effective 8 September 2026. Zone numbers must therefore be read with their date. An officer or locality assigned to Zone 3 in 2024 cannot automatically be assigned to Zone 3 in 2025 or 2026. [1][4][5]

Administrative zones group municipal wards for management. They are not separate municipalities, electoral constituencies or extensions of the corporation's external boundary. MCM has its own zone numbering. MCM Zone 2 and MCG Zone 2 refer to different jurisdictions.

The frontmatter date identifies the published four-zone ward schedule below. The 2022 boundary amendment and later administrative changes have their own dates. The frontmatter ward array is empty because this is a municipality-wide historical reference; the complete 36-ward schedule is retained in the body.

## Four-zone ward coverage reported in April 2025

The 17 April 2025 report states that MCG redrew all four zones to stop individual wards falling under more than one zone. This schedule uses the post-delimitation ward numbers, not the former 35-ward numbering. [1]

| Administrative zone | Ward numbers | Ward count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone 1 | 9, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 | 8 |
| Zone 2 | 5, 6, 7, 8, 28, 29, 30, 32, 33, 34 | 10 |
| Zone 3 | 1, 2, 3, 4, 12, 13, 27, 31, 35, 36 | 10 |
| Zone 4 | 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26 | 8 |

Each zone had a Joint Commissioner, senior sanitary inspectors and zonal taxation officers. Engineering work was allocated through eight divisions with executive, assistant and junior engineers. An engineering division or taxation-office number should not be treated as an interchangeable electoral ward or administrative-zone number. [1]

## Boundary amendment of 16 December 2022

MCM's official city profile expressly dates the amendment of the MCG and MCM boundaries to 16 December 2022. It states that Dwarka Expressway now divides the corporations and identifies Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A, together with Harsaru and Sihi, as additions to MCM's limits. [6]

Contemporary reporting describes the relevant western side of the expressway as MCM and the eastern side as MCG. Earlier jurisdiction followed village revenue boundaries, which split some developed sectors and even housing societies between the two corporations. The revised physical dividing line addressed that confusion. [7]

| Area named in official city profile | Jurisdiction after the amendment | Qualification from MCM's official zone list |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sector 83 | MCM | Listed under MCM Zone 2 |
| Sector 84 | MCM | Listed under MCM Zone 2 |
| Sector 88 | MCM | Listed under MCM Zone 2 |
| Sector 88A | MCM | Listed under MCM Zone 2 |
| Harsaru | Added territory within MCM | Zone list says part of the revenue estate of Harsaru |
| Sihi | Added territory within MCM | Zone list says part of the revenue estate of Sihi |

The qualification matters. The city profile's shorthand "Village Harsaru, Sihi" is not sufficient evidence that every parcel of both revenue estates transferred. The more specific official zone schedule explicitly refers to partial revenue estates. Garhi Harsaru is separately listed in MCM Zone 1 and should not be substituted for Harsaru. [8]

The east-west description applies to the revised shared boundary. It must not be extrapolated into a claim that every property anywhere west of the entire expressway is in MCM. The complete 2022 gazette boundary schedule, notification number and authenticated drawing were not recovered for this record. No centreline, right-of-way edge, coordinates or exhaustive parcel list is asserted here. The official MCM area map is a useful visual reference but explicitly says it is for viewing and not for a court dispute. [9]

## Northern Delhi border and southern Sohna-facing limits

The 28 December 2020 Haryana Gazette notification, No. S.O. 59/H.A. 16/1994/S. 3/2020, specifies alterations to MCG's limits under section 3 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. Its schedule must be read with drawing MCG/TP/2020/62 dated 1 December 2020. The following are identifiable boundary anchors, not a substitute for the complete municipal perimeter or later amendments. [10]

| Boundary segment | Gazette description |
| --- | --- |
| Northern Delhi interface, points A to B | Meeting of Delhi State's southern boundary, existing northern MCG boundary and Bajghera revenue boundary; follows Delhi's southern boundary and Bajghera revenue boundary, then closes along the existing MCG boundary |
| Northwestern extension, points C to E | Starts where Babupur revenue boundary and Delhi State boundary meet existing MCG boundary; follows Delhi's southern boundary and western revenue boundaries of Babupur, Dharampur and Daultabad to the Budhera/Kherki Majra junction, then Dhankot's western revenue boundary to existing MCG limits |
| Southern Palra extension, points F to G | Starts at the Darbaripur, Hasanpur and Palra revenue-boundary trijunction and follows Palra's southern revenue boundary to existing MCG limits |
| Southern and southeastern extension, points H to I | Starts where Bhondsi and Aklimpur revenue boundaries meet existing MCG limits; the English schedule proceeds south and southeast along the revenue boundaries of Bhondsi, Kadarpur and Behrampur to the existing municipal boundary |

The southern locality references identify the Sohna-facing and Aravalli-side fringe. "Sohna/Aravalli limits" is a geographical description, not a legal boundary clause. It does not mean that all of Sohna, its controlled area, or the Aravalli hills fall within MCG. GMDA's metropolitan boundary is also a different legal geography and must not be substituted for MCG's municipal boundary.

For a property-level jurisdiction dispute, obtain the applicable gazette schedule and drawing, locate the property's revenue estate and parcel, and check subsequent boundary amendments. A postal address saying "Gurugram", a sector number or the nearest expressway does not settle municipal jurisdiction.

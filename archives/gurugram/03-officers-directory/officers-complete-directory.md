# Municipal Corporation of Gurugram officers directory

The [MCG Office Directory](https://www.mcg.gov.in/contact/office-contacts) lists six administrative zones covering wards 1 to 36. For ward matters, use the Joint Commissioner listed for the ward. For property tax, use the taxation table: its zones have different boundaries. For offices without a direct contact, call the MCG helpline at **18001801817** or email **info@mcg.gov.in**.

The date above is the archive check date. MCG gives no effective date for the directory. Blank cells indicate unpublished or unverified details, not vacancies.

## Source notes and offices without a verified listing

- MCG's [leadership page](https://mcg.gov.in/) and Office Directory name Dr. Pankaj as Commissioner. Its [short contact directory](https://mcg.gov.in/contact/contact-directory) names Pradeep Dahiya at the same office number. The roster follows the Office Directory.
- The directory lists the headquarters post as Additional Commissioner, not Joint Commissioner. It lists Deputy Town Planner rather than District Town Planner, and Zonal Taxation Officers rather than a Joint Commissioner Taxation. No separate incumbent or direct contact was verified for those three requested titles. Electrical is the only Superintending Engineer entry in the directory.
- The [10 April 2026 MCG selection result](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/notifications/Final_Result_10.04.2026.pdf) identifies Dr. Ashish Singla as CMO on that date. The [January recruitment notice](https://www.mcg.gov.in/PDF/NOTIFICATION_Application_Form_08-01-2026.pdf) publishes the CMO mailbox. The directory separately lists him as Joint Commissioner V; his current medical charge was not confirmed.
- MCG's [Nathupur public notice](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/notifications/INVITING%20OBJECTIONS%20SUGGESTIONS%20ON%20THE%20PROPOSED%20PERMANENT%20CLOSURE.pdf) names the Public Relations Officer and Legal Branch as recipients. It does not identify an individual PRO or Law Officer or give their direct contacts.

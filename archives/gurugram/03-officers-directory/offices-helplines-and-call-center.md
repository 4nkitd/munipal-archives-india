# MCG offices, helplines and call center

## Scope and verification

This directory records published contact information reviewed on 22 November 2026. The frontmatter date is the compilation date, not the date of an office relocation or helpline launch. Telephone connections, WhatsApp responses and mailbox delivery were not tested.

MCG's two official contact pages disagree on some incumbent names and office allocations. This record therefore emphasizes office contacts rather than presenting an officer roster. Confirm the relevant office and counter before travelling. Historical zone numbers should not be treated as current service boundaries.

| Sector 42 zonal office | Community Center, Sector 42, Gurugram | Official office-contacts page lists Joint Commissioner-VI here: 0124-2392111; jc6@mcg.gov.in. Older reports call the same locality the Zone-III office. |
| Civil Lines old municipal office | Opposite Civil Hospital, Civil Lines, Gurugram | Hindustan Times, 25 September 2021, documents the former headquarters and planned reconstruction. It is not evidence that every former counter still operates here. |

The Civil Lines building was headquarters until departments moved to Sector 34 in 2015, according to the 2021 report. A January 2025 report also refers to a Civil Lines camp office. These descriptions do not establish the present location of a specific public-service counter.

Sector 15 and Old Judicial Complex are nearby locality descriptions found in local listings. The reviewed official MCG directory does not establish a separate current office address named "Old Judicial Complex". Do not substitute that locality for the confirmed Sector 34 headquarters.

For Sector 39, HLL's Hindlabs directory separately identifies the MCG Engineering Building at Jharsa, near the community centre, as the location of Shri Mata Sheetla Devi Swarna Jayanti Diagnostic Centre. This corroborates the municipal building location, not the present allocation of all engineering or health counters.

An exact current MCG public-office address on Old Delhi Road was not established from the reviewed official contact pages. Old Delhi Road is not another name for the Sector 39 Jharsa office. Ask the call center for the branch, building and landmark before visiting an office described only as "Old Delhi Road".

## Call center and civic complaints

| Channel | Number or address | Published purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MCG toll-free call center | 1800-180-1817 | Municipal complaints; official MCG directory describes it as 24x7. |
| Integrated civic complaint WhatsApp | +91 7840001817 | Water, sewerage, sanitation, waste, roads, street lighting and other civic complaints, with photographs and location details. |
| Integrated call center additional landline | 0124-4753555 | GMDA's official ICCC document lists it alongside 1800-180-1817 for public grievances. |
| Missed-call complaint callback | +91 9873353224 | Reported in July 2024 and July 2026 coverage of the integrated GMDA, MCG and MCM complaint system. |
| MCG general email | info@mcg.gov.in | General municipal correspondence, as published in the MCG website header and footer. |

Share the exact location, nearest landmark, issue description and a photograph where safe. Keep any acknowledgement or complaint number for follow-up. The integrated call center can route complaints across civic agencies; its use does not mean every road, sewer or water-supply asset belongs to MCG.

The Tribune reported on 26 April 2026 that residents can save 7840001817 and send "Hi" on WhatsApp for MCG civic-service information, reminders and complaint-status updates. This record does not promise a fixed resolution time.

## Emergency and protective helplines

These are police, state emergency or protective-service numbers, not MCG sanitation complaint lines. For immediate danger, call 112 rather than waiting for a municipal complaint response.

| Number | Service | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 112 | Police assistance and emergency response | Gurugram Police helpline page |
| 101 | Fire and rescue | Haryana Government helpline page |
| 108 | Ambulance helpline | Haryana Government helpline page |
| 1091 | Women helpline | Gurugram district, Gurugram Police and Haryana Government pages |
| 1098 | Child helpline | Gurugram district and Haryana Government pages |
| 1064 | Haryana State Vigilance and Anti Corruption Bureau complaints | Bureau helpline page |

The Bureau additionally publishes 1800-180-2022, WhatsApp +91 9417891064, email acb@hry.nic.in and headquarters telephone 0172-2970057. Corruption complaints belong with that agency, not the ordinary MCG service call center.

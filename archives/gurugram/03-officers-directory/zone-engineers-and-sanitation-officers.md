# MCG zone engineers and sanitation officers

## Scope and dating

This record separates the official office directory accessed on 22 November 2026 from an older public directory dated 24 October 2024. The verification date records source checking, not telephone testing or confirmation that an officer remains in post. The official directory has no visible appointment date.

The four-zone structure and engineering Divisions I to VIII belong to an older administrative arrangement. A report dated 16 June 2026 describes eight zones replacing four. The official office directory accessed for this record instead lists six Joint Commissioner offices and six SSI offices. These sources are not a single consistent establishment list. An engineering division number must not be assumed to equal a zone number, and the older contacts must not be treated as current appointments.

For a live water, sewerage, civil works, streetlighting, horticulture or sanitation complaint, contact MCG at **18001801817** and request the officer currently responsible for the location. The source does not establish a complete current ward-by-division allocation.

## Water and sewerage responsibility

Water supply, drainage and sewerage work cannot be assigned to a separate named Water EE or Sewerage EE from the official contact page reviewed. An official Haryana ULB tender for sewerage operation and maintenance in Ward 13, with bidding dates in April 2022, identifies **Executive Engineer-I, Municipal Corporation Gurgaon** as the issuing engineering office [5]. Another official 2022 tender identifies EE-I for sewerage work in Mohammad Heri, Zone 1 [6]. These establish historical engineering responsibility for particular works, not a city-wide or current jurisdiction.

The December 2024 reshuffle report says Executive Engineer Nijesh Kumar was responsible for the water and sewerage billing system, construction and demolition waste management, the Swachh Bharat Mission and the new MCG office building [4]. It does not publish a telephone number. Billing responsibility must not be conflated with responsibility for every sewer blockage or water-supply repair.

## Reported sanitation allocation after the June 2026 reorganization

The Times of India report published on 16 June 2026 [7] describes an MCG order assigning sanitation officers across eight newly created zones. Its entries for Zones 1 to 4 are below. These are the new zone numbers, not necessarily the former four zones.

| New zone | Officer | Designation reported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Harish | Senior Sanitary Inspector |
| 2 | Pradeep Kumar | Sanitation expert |
| 3 | Bhupender | Sanitary Inspector |
| 4 | Deepak | Sanitation expert |

The report assigns Bhupender, sanitation expert, to Zone 5; Naresh, sanitation expert, to Zone 6; Devender Bishnoi, expert, to Zone 7; and Deepak Dagar, Sanitary Inspector, to Zone 8. It supplies no telephone numbers. No number from an older or differently titled entry has been attached to these appointments.

The official website also advertises administrative cluster and zone notices dated 11 June 2026 [8]. The conflicting six-office contact page and eight-zone reporting mean that citizens should confirm current jurisdiction with MCG before relying on a zone label.

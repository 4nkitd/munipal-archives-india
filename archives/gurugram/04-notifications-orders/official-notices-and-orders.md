## Water and sewer connection regularisation

[Haryana ULB notification No. 04/36/2025-4C1](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2Facf6fe57-6647-45e6-ac2b-4adc47ce6f80.pdf), gazetted on 25 September 2025 and effective from 1 October 2025, covers new metered connections and regularisation of unauthorised water and sewer connections in municipal areas, including villages within municipal limits. Consumers can pay ₹1,000 for a water connection and ₹500 for a sewer connection upfront, bearing their own material, labour and meter costs. Alternatively, they can opt for ₹10 per month in lieu of the connection fee for 15 years and, if the authority supplies the meter, ₹25 per month for six years for the meter. Normal tariffs apply under both options. The notification waives road-cut charges for five years from the notification date, except where the consumer does not install a domestic water meter.

Local drives preceded and followed the state notification. [Reporting on 8 March 2024](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/others/mcg-begins-process-to-regularise-50-000-illegal-water-connections-101709835698918.html) described a planned MCG survey of an estimated 50,000 unauthorised water connections, not 50,000 completed regularisations. [Reporting on 25 July 2025](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/others/mcgidentifies-over-17k-illegal-water-connections-in-gurugram-101753382759878.html) cited MCG officials saying that 17,373 unauthorised connections had been identified and recipients were given 15 days to pay dues and apply for a legal metered connection. This was a period given to notice recipients, not a fixed citywide deadline.

[Reporting on 13 March 2026](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcg-cracks-down-on-illegal-water-and-sewer-connections-in-gurugram-101773345351540.html) described an MCG water and sewer disconnection drive in Daulatabad Industrial Area and Surat Nagar, as well as a municipal application module. On [20 May 2026](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcg-launches-drive-against-illegal-water-use-and-faulty-meters-101779243069031.html), the same newspaper described an MCG public notice on unauthorised connections, defective or missing meters and misuse of potable water. It reported a ₹1,000 fine for defective meters and an initial ₹2,000 fine for potable-water misuse. The original MCG notices were not located. The [Haryana Right to Service list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService) lists the standard water and sewer connection service for Gurugram at seven working days and directs applicants to [SARAL Haryana](https://saralharyana.gov.in/).

## Bulk Waste Generator compliance

[MCG Special Advisory for Bulk Waste Generators](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/notifications/Bulk-Waste-Generator-Advisory.pdf), memo No. MCG/JC-SBM/2026/1006 dated 26 May 2026, applies the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026. It identifies qualifying institutions, commercial establishments and residential societies by any one of three thresholds: at least 20,000 square metres of floor area, water consumption of 40,000 litres per day, or solid waste generation of 100 kg per day.

The advisory directs eligible generators to register on the [MCG BWG portal](https://onemapdepts.gmda.gov.in/BWG/) and the Central Pollution Control Board portal. It calls for segregation at source into wet, dry, sanitary and special care waste, and for handover of the relevant streams to authorised collectors. New BWGs must provide wet-waste processing capacity. Existing BWGs unable to provide decentralised processing need a municipal exemption and must obtain Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility certificates. Annual returns are due on the central portal by 30 June each year. MCG states that contracting an agency does not transfer the generator's responsibility for final disposal.

The earlier [4 April 2024 report on MCG registration directions](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/register-on-portal-mcg-tells-bwgs/articleshow/109016078.cms) recorded a 30 April 2024 registration deadline under the 2016 rules. That deadline is historical. For authorised service providers, see MCG's [list of 25 empanelled agencies](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/notifications/25%20Empanelled%20Agencies%20for%20BWGs.pdf), listed on the municipal portal on 15 October 2026.

## Area Sabha constitution and meeting notices

MCG office order No. MCG/CMC/2026/2087, dated 20 January 2026, constituted Area Sabhas under the Haryana Municipal Citizens' Participation Act, 2008. [Bharat Sarathi's 21 January report](https://bharatsarathi.com/?p=234158) reproduces the order reference from the municipal public announcement. [The Indian Express on 25 January](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/gurgaon-civic-body-sabhas-exclusion-rwa-10493466/) reported 117 Area Sabhas in MCG's 36 wards, each based on polling booth electoral rolls, with a municipal employee appointed as member secretary. The signed MCG order and polling booth annexure were not located for this index.

The [Haryana ULB copy of the 2008 Act](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/1d68dad6-c254-4732-96f8-91106bdec6a5.pdf) defines membership by registration on the relevant electoral rolls. Section 5 requires at least two general body meetings each year at a public place in the area, one between 15 May and 15 June and one between 15 November and 15 December, with at least 10% of registered voters present. These are statutory windows, not confirmed MCG dates or venues. Section 8 lists functions such as proposing local works, identifying service deficiencies and verifying potential beneficiaries for government schemes.

## Street vendors and vending zones

Haryana ULB's [notified street vending scheme of 12 June 2020, No. 3/2/2020 R-II](https://www.pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/Default/DownloadFile/?id=UT%5CHaryana+Scheme.pdf), sets out vendor surveys, registration and Certificates of Vending. The certificate specifies the activity, days, zone and hours. Sections 21 and 23 of the [Street Vendors Act, 2014](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2124?view_type=browse) distinguish the notified vending plan from Town Vending Committee decisions, which must themselves be notified with reasons.

At a 2 February 2025 meeting, MCG reportedly approved 33,198 Letters of Recommendation, discussed relocation for 41 Sector 14 vendors and formed a committee to identify additional vending zones. These actions are recorded in [Hindustan Times, 3 February 2025](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcgtakes-steps-to-resolve-problems-of-gurugram-street-vendors-101738517910005.html). After a 26 June 2025 Town Vending Committee meeting, [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/dont-encroach-on-roads-place-waste-bins-gurgaon-civic-body-tells-vendors/articleshow/122097738.cms) reported that Additional Commissioner Yash Jaluka directed vendors to keep roads and footpaths clear, place two bins by each cart and use municipal secondary waste collection points. Officials also announced a survey to identify new zones.

No signed 2024 to 2026 MCG vending-zone designation or final zone schedule was located for this index. Neither a Letter of Recommendation nor a reported survey proposal establishes that a particular site was notified or allotted as a vending zone.

# Gurugram property tax and No Dues Certificate guide

## Which assessment rules apply?

The Municipal Corporation of Gurugram (MCG) collects annual property tax on buildings and land within its limits under section 87 of the [Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/2d98f87d-c6f6-44c2-920e-8fb13fede849.pdf). Check that the municipality on the bill is Gurugram, not Manesar or another local body.

Haryana's [30 July 2026 municipal corporation notification, S.O. 39/H.A.16/1994/S.87/2026](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf), introduces a capital-value assessment for FY 2026-27 and classifies Gurugram as A1. The notification states an effective date of 1 August 2026. The [2 November 2026 amendment, No. 4/51/2026-4CI](https://egazette.servantsofknowledge.in/details/in.gazette.haryana.2026-11-02.Extra-Ordinary_4-51-2026-4CI/), replaces paragraph 9(d) and gives owners and occupiers **through 31 December 2026** to opt to pay under the 11 October 2013 notification. From 1 January 2027 only the new payment rules apply under that amendment. The amendment protects tax already paid for FY 2026-27 and leaves arrears and interest through FY 2025-26 unchanged. The December transition deadline is not an extension of any interest waiver.

## 2026 assessment: capital value and usage slabs

Annual property tax before adjustments equals **capital value × applicable annual rate × usage factor**. The A1 floor and ceiling rates are 0.030% and 0.090% for residential property, and 0.10% and 0.30% for non-residential property. The floor applies until the corporation fixes a rate within those limits.

Capital value uses the applicable collector rate for the property category. For flats and apartments, multiply carpet area by the collector rate. For independent floors, the gazette specifies plot area times collector rate as the capital-value basis. For other properties, multiply plot area by collector rate and the floor factor. A ground floor alone has a factor of 1. Add 0.25 for each additional built floor, including a basement. Use matching area and collector-rate units. The [official calculator](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/taxScreen.html) is indicative; use the PID demand for the actual bill.

| Property and size basis | Usage factor |
| --- | --- |
| Residential house or independent floor, plot up to 50 m² | 0.25 |
| Residential house or independent floor, plot above 50 through 100 m² | 0.50 |
| Residential house or independent floor, plot above 100 through 200 m² | 0.75 |
| Flat, carpet area up to 100 m² | 0.75 |
| Other residential | 1.00 |
| Shop plot up to 30 m²; above 30 through 50 m²; above 50 m² | 0.25; 0.50; 1.00, respectively |
| Shop in a mall on a plot above 1,000 m² | 1.25, on shop carpet area with a share of common areas |
| Paid parking; markets; warehouses and other commercial establishments | 0.25; 0.50; 1.00, respectively |
| Industrial, including dairies and poultry, up to 500 m²; above 500 through 2,000 m²; above 2,000 through 5,000 m²; above 5,000 m² | 0.25; 0.50; 0.75; 1.00, respectively |
| Institutional | 0.25 |
| Vacant land | 0.25, according to the land type |
| Mixed use | Apply each relevant factor to the area under that use |

The shop plot slabs cover stand-alone shops and shops in shopping complexes with plot area up to 1,000 m². The rented portion attracts an additional 25% of the tax rate that would otherwise apply. Shared building areas are apportioned to the individual properties.

If the revised annual tax is higher, the annual cap is measured against the existing annual tax until the revised assessment is reached. Houses up to 250 m² and flats up to 100 m² are capped at 1.25 times; houses above 250 through 350 m² at 1.5 times; other residential property at 2 times. The industrial caps in order of the four size bands above are 1.5, 2, 2.5 and 3 times. Other properties are capped at 3 times. Late payment attracts simple interest of 1.5% per month or part of a month under paragraph 8.

## 2013 schedule: old-rate payments and arrears

These are selected annual A1 rates from the [11 October 2013 municipal corporation notification, S.O. 85/H.A.16/1994/S.87/2013](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F0fae26fc-15f7-49f7-ad96-2d48ba5d77b1.pdf), before category-specific amendments, concessions and surcharges. Use them to read an old bill, not to estimate new-regime tax. In particular, the old office and mall schedule uses square feet, while the ground-floor shop schedule uses square yards.

| Property | Old annual A1 rate |
| --- | --- |
| Residential house, ground floor | ₹1/sq yd up to 300 sq yd; ₹4 for 301 to 500; ₹6 for 501 to 1,000; ₹7 for 1,001 sq yd through 2 acres; ₹10 above 2 acres |
| Residential flat | ₹1/sq ft carpet area up to 2,000 sq ft; ₹1.20 for 2,001 to 5,000; ₹1.50 above 5,000 |
| Shop, ground floor | ₹24/sq yd up to 50 sq yd; ₹36 for 51 to 100; ₹48 for 101 to 500; ₹60 for 501 to 1,000. Above 1,000 sq yd of plot, assess as commercial space. |
| Commercial space, including malls, multiplexes and offices | ₹12/sq ft of carpet area up to 1,000 sq ft; ₹15/sq ft above 1,000 |
| Industrial plot | ₹5/sq yd up to 2,500 sq yd; ₹6/sq yd from 2,501 sq yd through 2 acres; above 2 through 50 acres, ₹6/sq yd for the first 2 acres plus ₹2/sq yd on the excess; above 50 acres, the first 50 acres as before plus ₹1/sq yd on the excess |
| Vacant residential plot | Exempt through 100 sq yd; ₹0.50/sq yd for 101 to 500; ₹1/sq yd for 501 and above |
| Vacant commercial plot | Exempt through 100 sq yd; ₹5/sq yd for 101 and above |
| Vacant industrial or institutional plot | Exempt through 500 sq yd; ₹2/sq yd for 501 and above |

Paragraph 4(iv) of the old notification used **31 July** for the 10% early-payment rebate. Do not carry that date over to the 2026 schedule. For earlier-year disputes, ask MCG for the year-wise demand and the category, rate and amendments used for each year.

## 2026 rebates and Lal Dora treatment

Under paragraphs 7 and 9 of the July 2026 notification, rebates apply to self-certified properties on full payment of dues, including arrears, interest and penalties where owed. Part payment does not qualify.

| 2026 provision | Eligibility and amount |
| --- | --- |
| Early payment | 10% of current-year property tax if all dues, including arrears, are cleared by 30 April of the current assessment year |
| Good taxpayer | 5% of current-year tax if tax was paid by 30 April in each of the three preceding consecutive years |
| Zero-waste management | 10% for properties in a group housing society, or a qualifying cluster of at least 50 properties outside a society, with a fully functioning zero-waste system |
| Residential property owned by women | 25%; in joint ownership, applies proportionately to the share owned by women |
| Residential property owned by persons with disabilities | 10% for owners with at least 40% disability |
| Residential property in the Lal Dora of an existing village within municipal limits | 75% under paragraph 7(a), separate from the combined 25% cap on the other rebates listed above |
| Residential property in the Lal Dora of a newly included village | Separate paragraph 6(i) exemption for five years from inclusion in municipal limits, for up to three floors; Haryana may extend the period |

Other residential exemptions under paragraph 6 include a qualifying self-occupied home of up to 250 m² for serving or former defence and paramilitary personnel, spouses or eligible families of deceased personnel. Qualifying self-occupied homes of freedom fighters, their spouses and war widows are also exempt. The relevant household must have no other residential house in Haryana and must not have let out any portion. A Lal Dora tax rebate, a [Lal Dora property certificate](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2Feb519b93-6e15-485c-8707-59ad443c6fc9.pdf) and an NDC are different things. If the PID misstates the Lal Dora boundary or the residential use, raise a correction before certifying.

These windows have closed. A waiver of interest is not a cancellation of the underlying tax. For a payment made during a relief window that was not credited correctly, attach the dated receipt and ask the Taxation Branch for a year-wise reconciliation against that PID.

## Find, self-certify and pay against a Property ID

1. Open the [Haryana Property Tax Dues Payment and NDC Management System](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). Register if needed and sign in with a one-time password (OTP). Under **Search Property**, select **Gurugram**. Search by eight-character Property ID (PID), old PID, plot number, name, mobile number or GIS map. Check the address and unit before using the result. If the record is genuinely missing after checking the map and other identifiers, use the new PID application. Do not create a second PID to fix an existing one.
2. Open **View Details/Self Certify**. Check the property details, registered mobile number, owner details and dues, including the authorised or unauthorised status shown with development charges. Check area, floor count, use and address against your documents. If every section is correct, select **Yes** for each and follow the OTP prompts. The [official self-certification manual](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Content/UserManuals/How_to_self_certify_08.07.2024.pdf) describes using Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID), then Aadhaar if there is no Family ID, or the mobile registered against the property if neither ID is held. If any field is wrong, submit an objection instead, then self-certify the corrected record.
3. Under **Make Payment/Generate NDC**, search by PID and select **Pay All Dues and Generate NDC**. Review the amounts, including property and fire tax, development charges and garbage charges where applicable. Use **Pay Online** and complete the OTP and payment gateway steps. Save the receipt and download the NDC after the applicable dues clear. A property-tax-only payment can leave other amounts outstanding and is not itself an NDC. See the [portal FAQ](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/FAQ) and [payment manual](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Content/UserManuals/How_to_Make_Payment_of_Property_Tax_Dues_08.07.2024.pdf).

The PID identifies a municipal assessment, and the NDC records clearance of municipal dues. Neither document proves ownership, transfers title or regularises unauthorised construction.

## Objections and MCG Taxation Branch escalation

For an incorrect owner entry, mobile, plot size, floor count, use, authorisation status or demand, open the relevant section in **View Details/Self Certify**, select **No**, and use **Update Details**. Enter the correction and upload the [documents required by the portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments), such as a registered deed for ownership details or a dated receipt for an uncredited payment. For tax already paid but still on the bill, select the payment-not-reflected option rather than treating it only as a rate dispute. Click **Save Draft**, then **Final Preview and Submit Objection** and complete the OTP step. Saving a draft is not the final submission. Keep the application number and use [Check Application Status](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ApplicationStatus). If MCG returns the application for documents or clarification, read the reason and respond on the portal.

The [July 2024 objection manual](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Content/UserManuals/Verify_Property_Details_and_Submit_Objection_08.07.2024.pdf) lists normal filing at no charge and 10 working days, and Tatkaal at ₹2,500 and two working days where offered. The [Haryana Right to Service schedule](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService), updated through 4 November 2025, lists the following routes for municipal corporations:

| Service | Working days | Designated officer | First grievance authority | Second grievance authority |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Objections on dues, size, floors, authorisation status or category | 10 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Deputy Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| New PID | 10 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Deputy Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Change of owner or occupier, other than a death case | 15 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Change of owner or occupier in a death case | 45 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Commissioner | Commissioner |

For a stalled or rejected case, give the relevant Zonal Taxation Officer the PID, application number, filing date, portal status or rejection reason, and supporting documents. Ask for a written response or demand reconciliation. Use the matching Right to Service escalation if applicable. Call **1800-180-1817** to confirm the correct taxation zone or nearest assistance counter. The [MCG office directory](https://www.mcg.gov.in/contact/office-contacts) lists Taxation Branch contacts and headquarters at Plot C-1, Info City, Sector 34; the [NDC helpdesk directory](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Contact) lists Gurugram portal assistance. A portal objection or service-delay grievance is not the same as a statutory appeal against a levy or assessment. For a formal demand notice, check the notice and sections 138 to 140 of the Act promptly; do not assume a portal ticket pauses an appeal deadline.

## Yashi property survey and correction camps

The Directorate's [4 October 2018 Gurugram cluster work order](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/8d257271-23f7-4bfb-ae67-0fe5d78147c0.pdf) awarded the geo-enabled property tax and licence survey to Yashi Consulting Services Pvt. Ltd. MCG used the survey data to issue new PIDs. Residents reported incorrect property numbers, names and other details. In September 2022, MCG directed that new PID applications and correction objections go through the state NDC portal, rather than the old MCG or private grievance site. See [The Times of India, 11 September 2022](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/haryana-error-in-property-tax-id-submit-complaints-via-ndc/articleshow/94125832.cms).

MCG ran weekend correction camps in 17 residential areas in [March 2023](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcg-to-hold-weekend-camps-to-rectify-errors-in-property-ids-of-residents-101679077986109.html). Its Taxation Department ran [6 to 10 April 2024 camps](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/from-today-you-can-self-certify-property-tax-data-in-mcg-camps/articleshow/109076478.cms) for objections, self-certification and payment, and [19 and 27 September 2026 camps](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcg-to-hold-special-camps-to-resolve-property-tax-issues-101789669556860.html) at M3M Woodshire in Sector 107 and Brics Lumbini in Sector 109. Those dates are historical, not a standing timetable. Check the current MCG announcement or call the helpline before travelling. Bring the old and new PID if available, any application number, the bill or receipts, and document copies for the disputed field. The camps help with the municipal process; the survey contractor is not the grievance authority.

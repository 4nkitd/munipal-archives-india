# Gurugram certificates, community centre bookings and pet dog registration

## Scope and portal directory

This guide covers Municipal Corporation Gurugram, not Municipal Corporation Manesar or every settlement in Gurugram district. Select the authority responsible for the event or property, rather than relying on the district name alone. The frontmatter date is the date attached to MCG's pet-control order in its official website index. It is not a common commencement date for all services.

| Service | Official entry point | Important distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birth or death certificate, correction, child-name inclusion and delayed registration | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | A certificate copy, correction and first registration are different applications |
| Registration of an already solemnized marriage | [Shaadi Haryana](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) | MCG and SARAL link to this state portal |
| Community centre | [MCG online booking](https://online.mcgworks.in/) | Published inventory and terms do not prove a date is available or payment is functioning |
| Pet dog registration or renewal | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Choose Urban Local Bodies and the relevant dog-registration service |
| Water tanker filling booking | [GMDA water tanker service](https://gmda.gov.in/onlineservices/water-tanker.html) | GMDA service, not a verified MCG doorstep-delivery booking |
| Crematorium arrangements | MCG helpline and the relevant crematorium | No current public MCG slot-booking workflow was verified |

No application, payment or reservation was submitted during research. Portal availability and published instructions were checked, not end-to-end service delivery.

## Birth and death registration and certificates

The [Gurugram district e-services directory](https://gurugram.gov.in/service/e-services/) lists Birth/Death Certificates under Health/MC/MCG and points to the Civil Registration System and SARAL. Haryana's official annual report on registration for 2020 identifies four SARAL services: issuance of birth/death/non-availability certificates, correction of birth or death records, inclusion of a child's name, and delayed birth/death registration.

Use the registrar for the place where the birth or death occurred. Residence in Gurugram does not by itself make MCG responsible for an event elsewhere. The normal reporting period is 21 days. Do not treat a late-registration application as a request for another copy of an existing certificate.

1. Register or sign in to SARAL. Open "Apply for Services" and then "View all available services".
2. Search for the required birth/death service. Check the selected department and local registration authority.
3. Enter the event date, place, names and existing registration particulars, where available. Keep the hospital report or other event evidence and applicant identity details ready. The selected service's current checklist controls the required uploads.
4. For corrections, name inclusion or delayed registration, use that specific service and its supporting-document requirements. A non-availability certificate is not itself registration of the event.
5. Submit, pay any displayed charge and keep the application reference and receipt. Track the application through SARAL and use its certificate-download link after approval.

The older annual report includes historical late-fee and permission schedules. They are not reproduced as current fees because subsequent statutory and procedural changes need checking. Ask the registrar for the current delayed-registration requirements. A cremation slip or hospital cause-of-death document is not the municipal death certificate.

## Marriage registration through Shaadi Haryana

MCG's current service directory and SARAL's useful links direct applicants to [shaadi.haryana.gov.in](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/). The portal did not return readable page content to the research fetch, so the present screen labels, fee calculator and appointment availability were not verified.

The [National Government Services Portal](https://services.india.gov.in/service/detail/apply-for-marriage-registration-haryana) confirms registration in urban local bodies under the Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Act, 2008 and Rules, 2008. Its checklist names Form I, bride and groom particulars, six affidavits, address and date-of-birth proof, challan, marriage photographs, invitation card and a joint photograph. Treat this as an official preparation checklist, not proof that every item remains an upload requirement on the current portal.

The [Home Department's instructions dated 1 December 2016](https://homeharyana.gov.in/pdfs/Orders/Instruction%20Marriage%20Registration.pdf) authorize CSC and e-Disha assistance from 31 January 2017. They describe registrar jurisdiction by place of solemnization, either spouse's residence at marriage, or both spouses' present residence.

Prepare identity, age and address records, marriage evidence, photographs and witness details. Apply through the state portal, retain the application and payment references, and follow its verification appointment instructions. Bring originals and the persons required by the appointment notice. SARAL also links to [marriage appointment rescheduling](https://edisha.gov.in/eForms/MarriageScheduleAppointment).

This service registers a marriage already solemnized. It should not be confused with a separate solemnization procedure under the Special Marriage Act. Current marriage fees and processing times were not established from the readable official material. Conflicting third-party fee tables should not be used for payment.

## MCG community centres and booking conditions

The [MCG booking page](https://online.mcgworks.in/) contains sector entries including Sector 4, 5, 7, 9, 9A, 10, 15 II, 22, 23, 23A, 27, 40, 45, 46, 52 and 56. It also lists village and colony venues. "Sector 15 II" is the portal's specific label, not every venue in Sector 15.

Sector 10A was requested for this guide but was not a separate entry in the retrieved inventory. Do not assume that "Sector 10" books Sector 10A. Ask MCG to identify the venue and booking authority. The fetched page markup hides the area and date controls, so a usable fresh-reservation flow was not verified. No centre-specific tariff or security-deposit schedule was established.

The published [terms and conditions](https://online.mcgworks.in/Termandcondition.aspx) state:

| Subject | Published condition |
| --- | --- |
| Advance booking | Opens 120 days before the function, first come first served |
| Eligibility | Clause 1 refers to residents of MCG area; clause 19 says Haryana residents only. Confirm eligibility if outside MCG |
| Occupancy | 9:00 AM on the booked day until 9:00 AM the next day |
| Applicant | Full name must match the account holder's name; booking is non-transferable |
| Responsibilities | Applicant arranges security, traffic control and parking, and pays for damage |
| Alcohol | Excise licence required if alcoholic drinks are served |
| Noise | No loud noise between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM; outside loudspeakers/DJ prohibited |
| Other restrictions | No weapons, crackers, poster pasting, commercial activity or digging in cemented areas |

Choose the exact venue and function date, inspect the charges and terms, and retain the successful booking receipt before making arrangements. Listing a venue is not reservation confirmation.

### Cancellation and security refund

The [Manage Booking page](https://online.mcgworks.in/userorderdetail.aspx) instructs applicants to submit an application with bank-account details to the MCG IT Cell at the Sector 34 office, either in person or by email to support@mcg.gov.in, to initiate security refund or cancellation.

| Time before function | Published cancellation deduction |
| --- | --- |
| 30 days or more | 5% on booking amount and tax |
| 20 to 30 days | 10% on booking amount and tax |
| 10 to 20 days | 15% on booking amount and tax |
| Up to 10 days | 25% on booking amount and tax |

The portal's bands overlap at their endpoints. Obtain confirmation of the applicable deduction at exactly 10, 20 or 30 days. MCG reserves the right to cancel bookings, with full booking-charge and security refund in that case.

## Pet dog registration, vaccination and fees

The official MCG website index lists a Public Health order dated 23 June 2026, identified there as MCG/POL/2026/001, titled "Regarding registration of Proper control and Regulation of Pet Dogs within the jurisdiction of Municipal Corporation Gurugram". The [official PDF](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/officeorderDocuments/Regarding%20registration%20of%20Proper%20control%20and%20Regulation%20of%20Pet%20Dogs%20within%20%20the%20jurisdiction%20of%20Municipal%20Corporation%20Gurugram..pdf) yielded no extractable text. Its index metadata was verified; its scanned clauses were not independently transcribed.

The official SARAL [initial-registration service sheet](https://kms.saralharyana.nic.in/ViewDoc.aspx?Id=5Ni2wUjSFVndq50B%2B4R0rA%3D%3D) and [renewal sheet](https://kms.saralharyana.nic.in/ViewDoc.aspx?Id=uHQg6Yxc7MA%2BPu2pIwmPPg%3D%3D) provide the following concrete requirements:

| Item | SARAL requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Any one of Aadhaar, passport, voter card, driving licence or ration card, mandatory |
| Dog photograph | Mandatory |
| Rabies inoculation certificate | From a veterinary doctor, mandatory |
| Other records | Immunization certificate; insurance details and government-scheme-related documents are also listed |
| Sterilization certificate | Optional in these service sheets |
| Previous licence | Required for renewal |
| Municipal notice or clearance | If applicable |
| Dog-show permission | Only for a dog brought for show purposes |
| Government fee for MCG | Latest notification of the concerned municipal corporation |
| Assisted submission | Rs 100 Kendra service charge or Rs 100 CSC service charge |
| RTS time limit | 15 days for issue; 7 days for renewal |

Sign in to SARAL, select Urban Local Bodies, search for dog registration, choose MCG, upload the applicable records and pay the displayed amount. Keep the acknowledgement and follow instructions for obtaining the certificate and registration token.

[Hindustan Times, 25 June 2026](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/register-pet-dogs-or-face-action-mcg-tells-owners-in-gurugram-101782324279517.html), reports mandatory registration, a Rs 500 fee for unregistered pets and continued validity of existing registrations. Earlier reports quoted Rs 250 renewal and differing validity periods. The official SARAL sheet does not fix MCG's fee or renewal interval. Its Rs 500 annual registration and Rs 250 renewal schedule is explicitly for councils and committees, not corporations. Confirm MCG's current notification and the expiry on the issued licence before renewing.

## Pet-control, microchip and breed advisories

June 2026 reporting attributes these duties to MCG: keep pets on a leash outside private premises, display the registration token, maintain rabies vaccination, prevent roaming or attacks, carry a waste bag and clean up immediately. Reports describe additional muzzle and restraint requirements for breeds identified as dangerous or ferocious.

[Times of India, 25 June 2026](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/mcg-warns-pet-owners-of-criminal-action-under-bns-for-dog-attacks-negligence/articleshow/131977013.cms), names breeds including Pitbull Terrier, Rottweiler, Dogo Argentino, Cane Corso, Akita, mastiffs and wolf-dog crosses. It also reports that the Punjab and Haryana High Court stayed a 2022 consumer-forum order banning 11 breeds. Do not convert an old "banned breeds" headline or a breeding/import/sale advisory into a verified current ban on ownership. Ask MCG for the operative order and applicable court directions for the particular breed.

[Times of India, 29 November 2023](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/now-register-pets-at-your-doorstep-get-microchips-and-track-vaccinations-too/articleshow/105576867.cms), reported agency-assisted doorstep registration and microchipping, but expressly said mandatory microchipping was unclear. Microchip proof is not listed in the two SARAL service sheets examined. This guide therefore does not classify implantation as a verified mandatory registration condition. Confirm agency authorization, any chip charge and receipt arrangements with MCG before using a doorstep provider.

## Crematorium arrangements and death documentation

No current official public MCG crematorium slot-booking page, rate card or online payment procedure was verified. Contact MCG on 18001801817 or the chosen crematorium to confirm availability, operating arrangements, required death documentation and charges before transport. Do not send money to an unofficial booking intermediary based on this guide.

A [Hindustan Times report of 28 August 2012](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india/now-can-get-death-certificates-at-crematoriums/story-mQa1SRPZn45X1BaShyxhMM.html) described assistance with online death registration at Sukhrali and Madanpuri. This is historical evidence of registration assistance, not proof of a current cremation-slot reservation service or immediate certificate issuance.

Keep the facility's cremation receipt and the medical or other death-reporting documents. Apply to the appropriate registrar through the birth/death route above for the registered death certificate. Crematorium management and civil registration are separate functions.

## Water tanker booking through GMDA

GMDA's [water tanker page](https://gmda.gov.in/onlineservices/water-tanker.html) requires registered users and states that the filling-station process is online, with no manual transaction allowed at the station.

| Water category | Published filling source | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Potable | Basai water works; Sector 16 and Sector 51 boosting stations | Drinking |
| Raw/canal | Chandu Bhudera water works | Construction or landscape irrigation |
| Recycled | Dhanwapur and Beharampur sewage treatment plants | Construction or landscape irrigation |
| CETP-treated | Manesar common effluent treatment plant | Construction or landscape irrigation |

The official [booking manual](https://www.gmda.gov.in/static/docs/water_tanker_handbook.pdf) describes account registration with email/mobile verification, login, selection of "Book", applicant details, water type and filling station, and automatic fee calculation by tanker size and number. Pay online, check the dashboard and download the QR-code document for presentation at the site.

This is a tanker-filling authorization workflow. It does not establish that GMDA will send a vehicle to a household. Confirm transport and delivery separately. For interruption of municipal piped supply, contact MCG rather than treating the GMDA filling booking as an emergency municipal tanker dispatch. No fixed current tanker rate was verified.

## Assistance and verification limits

SARAL's helpline is 0172-3968400, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM Monday to Saturday excluding government holidays. Its portal provides application tracking, certificate download, tickets and appeal links. The [district SARAL directory](https://gurugram.gov.in/service/antyodaya-saral/) lists the Gurugram SARAL Kendra on the ground floor of the Mini Secretariat for assisted applications.

All linked sources were consulted on 22 November 2026. Current fees, live booking capacity, authentication, payments and actual certificate issuance were not tested. Primary service sheets and booking conditions take precedence over historical press reports. Reported pet fees, microchip arrangements and breed restrictions are labelled separately because the scanned MCG order and a current corporation fee notification were not fully verified.

# Municipal complaints, escalation, Swachhata and Sameer in Gurugram

## Choose the complaint channel

| Channel | Use | Tracking or follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MCG toll-free **1800-180-1817** | Municipal sanitation, waterlogging, streetlights and routing to the responsible department | Ask for a complaint number, assigned department and expected response date. MCG advertises this helpline as 24x7. |
| [Haryana ULB GRS](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Municipal service complaints, including sanitation and streetlights | Keep the grievance ID. Use "Check Grievance Status" on the portal. |
| eSamadhan DULB Haryana Citizen app | Mobile alternative to Haryana ULB GRS | Use "My Grievances". The app was previously called Swachh Haryana. |
| Swachhata-MoHUA app | Photographed sanitation and cleanliness complaints | Check status and the resolution photograph. Reopen an unsatisfactorily resolved complaint. |
| CM Window Haryana | Unresolved public grievances requiring state-level monitoring | Register at an authorised counter and retain the SMS registration number. |
| Samadhan Shivir | Presenting unresolved grievances directly to officials, including cases involving multiple departments | Bring the original complaint IDs and obtain an acknowledgement or recorded reference. Confirm the current camp schedule first. |
| CPCB Sameer app | AQI information and specific air-pollution complaints | Register and track the pollution complaint separately from any municipal cleaning request. |

These systems do not necessarily share complaint numbers or synchronise status. When using a second channel, quote the first complaint ID and explain the unresolved issue rather than assuming that the second authority can see the earlier record. Source details appear below.

## File and track an MCG or Haryana ULB complaint

Prepare a short factual description, the exact address or map location, a nearby landmark, the ward if known, the date the issue began, and photographs. For a streetlight, include the pole number if visible. For waterlogging, state the road stretch, whether sewage is mixed with rainwater, and whether homes or access routes are affected. Do not enter a flooded road or approach exposed electrical wiring to take a photograph.

Call **1800-180-1817** and request municipal complaint registration and routing. Ask the operator which agency owns the affected asset if this is unclear. MCG, GMDA, HSVP, road agencies and private colony maintenance bodies can have different responsibilities.

Alternatively, open [GRS](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/), create an account if needed, and use the citizen sign-in. The inspected login page uses mobile verification through a six-digit SMS code. Select the correct municipality and available complaint category, enter the location and description, attach evidence where requested, and retain the grievance ID after submission.

The official [GRS FAQ](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/Login/faq) says any affected citizen or entity may complain, without a fee. The system forwards the complaint to the designated resolver in the relevant ULB. It sends an SMS when a complaint is resolved or rejected. Check status using the grievance ID on the portal, "My Grievances" in eSamadhan, or **1800-8900-929**, available **9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday**. The telephone agent can look up a grievance using its ID or registered mobile number.

Do not assume every app category is enabled for Gurugram. The department's eSamadhan app description restricts certain water-supply, sewerage and storm-water network categories to named municipalities that do not include Gurugram. If the relevant category is unavailable, ask MCG to route the case rather than selecting a misleading category.

## Sanitation complaints through Swachhata-MoHUA

Swachhata-MoHUA is the national Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs sanitation complaint app. It is distinct from Haryana's eSamadhan DULB app and CPCB's Sameer app.

Take a clear photograph, choose the matching category and check the captured location. Add a landmark so field staff can find the spot. Standard categories include garbage dumps, missed garbage collection, unswept roads, uncleared dustbins, dead animals and public-toilet problems. The complaint goes to the relevant municipality and sanitary inspector or engineer.

The official app description says residents receive status notifications and a photograph uploaded as proof of resolution. Inspect the location if safe. If the photograph shows another spot or the problem remains, use the reopening facility and explain the mismatch.

The Swachhata platform's August 2025 administrator FAQ lists category-specific service levels, including **12 hours** for garbage dumps, missed collection and unswept roads, and **48 hours** for dead animals. These are platform service levels, not proof that a particular complaint has been resolved or a universal statutory deadline.

Haryana's separate published procedure for removal of solid waste from streets and roads names the **Senior Sanitary Inspector** as the designated officer for municipal corporations and gives a **two-day** service timeline. That procedure, written under the older Swachh Haryana app name, allows reopening within **24 hours after closure**. Do not apply that Haryana-specific reopening period to every Swachhata-MoHUA complaint.

## Escalation for sanitation, waterlogging and streetlights

The following is a practical departmental follow-up route, not a verified automatic escalation ladder or statutory appeal schedule. Ask the helpline for the currently assigned officer and jurisdiction. Officer postings and ward-to-zone allocations change.

| Problem | First departmental follow-up | Senior follow-up if unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Garbage, missed collection, road sweeping or sanitation | Assigned sanitation resolver or sanitary inspector, then the area's Senior Sanitary Inspector | Zonal Joint Commissioner or Joint Commissioner responsible for Swachh Bharat Mission, then Municipal Commissioner |
| Waterlogging, blocked municipal drains or sewer overflow | Assigned engineering resolver or field engineer, then the responsible Executive Engineer | Supervising engineering officer or Chief Engineer and zonal Joint Commissioner, then Municipal Commissioner |
| Failed municipal streetlight | Assigned electrical resolver or maintenance team, then responsible electrical engineering officer | Supervising electrical engineer and zonal Joint Commissioner, then Municipal Commissioner |

Send the original complaint ID, submission date, exact location, fresh photographs, earlier replies and the specific work still required. Request a written reason for rejection or closure. If the asset belongs to another agency, ask for the agency name and referral details.

MCG's [office directory](https://mcg.gov.in/contact/office-contacts) lists sanitation inspectors, Executive Engineers, senior engineering offices and zonal Joint Commissioners. Its Commissioner Office contact is **0124-2370911**, **cmc@mcg.gov.in**, at **Plot No. C-1, Info City, Sector 34, Gurugram**. MCG directory pages show inconsistent officeholder names, so this guide uses office titles rather than treating a person's name as settled.

Streetlight failure is not the same as a neighbourhood electricity-supply outage. Ask for referral to the electricity distribution utility when the underlying fault is outside municipal lighting infrastructure. Treat live wires, electrocution risk and rapidly rising water as immediate safety hazards, not routine app follow-up.

## CM Window and Samadhan Shivirs

Haryana's official [CM Window page](https://www.haryana.gov.in/cm-window/) states that the grievance monitoring system began on **25 December 2014**. Residents can submit grievances through CM Window counters at district **e-Disha Kendras and sub-divisional offices**. Counter staff register the grievance online, and the resident receives an SMS registration number for online tracking. Use the official state page to reach the current tracking system. Attach the municipal complaint history and explain why the response was inadequate. CM Window is a monitored grievance route, not a substitute for a statutory appeal where one applies.

Samadhan Shivirs bring officials together to hear citizen grievances. They began as daily grievance camps in 2024, and MCG also conducted municipal camps. Do not assume "daily" remains the current schedule. A Gurugram report published on **10 October 2025** described district and sub-divisional camps on **Monday and Thursday, 10 AM to noon**. District Administration Gurugram's November 2026 public posts repeat that schedule.

Confirm the next date, venue and whether an MCG-specific camp has a different timetable before travelling. The district contact directory lists **0124-2321144** for the Deputy Commissioner office. Bring a concise written application, copies of complaint acknowledgements, supporting photographs and relevant service records. Keep the originals and obtain a receipt or reference for follow-up.

## Sameer AQI and air-pollution complaints

The **Central Pollution Control Board's Sameer app** provides hourly National Air Quality Index updates and lets residents register and track air-pollution complaints. Gurugram is in Delhi NCR, where CPCB also provides an [air-pollution complaint form](https://cpcb.nic.in/query-form1.php).

Report an identifiable source, such as open garbage burning, construction dust, road dust, generator emissions or industrial emissions. Include the exact location, observation time, source description and a photograph taken safely. Follow the app's current verification prompts and retain the reference number.

An AQI reading describes air quality at a monitoring location. It does not establish which nearby activity caused the pollution. Identify the observed source rather than submitting only an AQI screenshot.

For burning garbage, a Sameer complaint can address the pollution while an MCG or Swachhata complaint requests removal of the waste. Cross-reference the two records. Sameer is not the general channel for a broken streetlight, an ordinary missed collection or waterlogging without an air-pollution issue.

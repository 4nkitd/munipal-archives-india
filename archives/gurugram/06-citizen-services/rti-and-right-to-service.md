# Right to Information and Right to Service in Gurugram

Use the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI) to obtain records MCG holds, such as a works order, inspection report or file noting. Use the Haryana Right to Service Act, 2014 (RTS) to seek delivery of a notified service, such as a property record correction or removal of solid waste. The two Acts have different deadlines and appeal authorities. The frontmatter date is the update date stated on the Urban Local Bodies (ULB) consolidated RTS schedule.

## RTI officers and record routing

The [Haryana ULB PIO list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList), municipal corporation entry 8 under the former name Gurgaon, lists the MCG Joint Commissioner as State Public Information Officer (SPIO) and the MCG Commissioner as First Appellate Authority (FAA). The SPIO answers requests, can seek help from the branch holding the record, and must transfer the relevant part to another public authority within five days when section 6(3) applies. A designated State Assistant Public Information Officer (APIO) receives and forwards applications and appeals under section 5(2); the APIO does not decide the request.

The ULB list does not assign numbered Joint Commissioners or give separate SPIO, APIO or FAA appointments by MCG zone or branch. Use the following as a routing guide, not an appointment roster. For paper filing, confirm the designated officer, receiving office and fee payee with MCG. Its head office is at Plot C-1, Info City, Sector 34, Gurugram, Haryana 122001.

| Area or branch | What to specify in the RTI request |
| --- | --- |
| Zone I | Under the June 2026 administrative map: wards 9, 10, 16 and 17. Give the property or site address and ward. |
| Zone II | Under that map: wards 11, 14, 15 and 18. Give the property or site address and ward. |
| Zone III | Under that map: wards 5, 6, 7, 33 and 34. Give the property or site address and ward. |
| Zone IV | Under that map: wards 8, 28, 29, 30 and 32. Give the property or site address and ward. |
| Town Planning | Identify the plan, plot or application number. Ask for the sanctioned plan, approval order or relevant file noting. |
| Engineering | Identify the works division if known, street, asset or tender number. Ask for estimates, work orders, measurement book extracts or inspection records. |
| Taxation | Give the Property ID and assessment period. Ask for the assessment entry, demand calculation or decision on a named objection. |
| Health and Sanitation | Give the location, date range and complaint or register reference. Ask for municipal sanitation inspection or clearance records. Apply to the civil registrar for a birth or death certificate itself. |

The [MCG administrative zone map](https://www.mcg.gov.in/assets/notifications/mcg-administrative-clusters-and-zones.pdf) cites order MCG/CMC/2026/16598 dated 11 June 2026 and shows eight zones, not just I to IV. The [MCG office directory](https://www.mcg.gov.in/contact/office-contacts) displays different ward assignments under some Joint Commissioner headings. Give the ward and exact location rather than inferring RTI jurisdiction from the zone number alone.

## Filing an RTI and appealing

1. File with the MCG SPIO in English or Hindi. State the records, date range and identifiers. You need not explain why you want the records. Use Haryana's [RTI portal](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/) for an application or first appeal where the appropriate public authority is available. `rtionline.haryana.gov.in` could not be verified as a working filing address. If online routing is unclear, submit on paper to the designated SPIO and retain the acknowledgment or postal receipt.
2. Haryana's application fee is Rs 10. Applicants below the poverty line are exempt and should attach proof. Standard A4 or A3 copies cost Rs 2 per page. For paper requests, Haryana's amended Model Form A calls for identity proof. Keep the application number and payment receipt.
3. The SPIO normally has 30 days from receipt to provide information or issue a reasoned refusal. Section 7(1) gives 48 hours where the information concerns a person's life or liberty. Filing through a designated APIO adds five days to the ordinary response calculation. Time between a valid additional-fee notice and payment is excluded; where the authority misses the applicable statutory time limit, section 7(6) requires disclosure free of charge.
4. For refusal, an unsatisfactory decision or no reply, file a first appeal under section 19(1) within 30 days of receiving the decision or of the response deadline expiring. The FAA should decide within 30 days, or at most 45 days with written reasons. A second appeal lies to the Haryana State Information Commission within 90 days of when the FAA's decision was due or was received. The Haryana portal directs applicants to file second appeals by post or in person, in triplicate.

Sources: [RTI Act, sections 5, 6, 7 and 19](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2065), [Haryana Institute of Public Administration fee guide](https://hipaco.in/public/hipa/pdf/Course/RTI_Handbook.pdf), [Haryana's amended Model Form A](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3f80ff32e08a25270b5f252ce39522f72/uploads/2021/05/2021050724.pdf).

## RTS timelines for municipal services

The [department's consolidated schedule](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService) gives limits in working days. Under sections 4 and 5 of the [Haryana Right to Service Act](https://haryana-rtsc.gov.in/storage/app/act/2021071652.pdf), the clock starts when the designated officer or authorized recipient receives a complete application. Keep the dated acknowledgment and application or complaint number. If the officer rejects the application, the Act requires written reasons.

| Notified service | Working days | Designated officer | First grievance authority | Second grievance authority |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Property ID correction through an NDC portal objection on dues, size, floor details, authorized or unauthorized status, or property category | 10 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Deputy Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner, MCG |
| New Property ID | 10 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Deputy Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner, MCG |
| Change of owner or occupier in property tax register | 15 normally; 45 in a death case | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Commissioner | Commissioner, MCG |
| Municipal water supply and sewerage connection | 7 | Assistant Engineer | Executive Engineer | Superintending Engineer or Chief Engineer |
| Replacement of street lights | 10 | Assistant Engineer | Executive Engineer | Superintending Engineer or Chief Engineer |
| Removal of solid waste from streets, roads, green belts and open spaces | 2 | Senior Sanitary Inspector | Joint Commissioner | Commissioner, MCG |

Use the [property tax and No Dues Certificate portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for Property ID applications and corrections. The 10-day limit applies to notified objections, not to routine NDC download. For a property with correct details, use the portal's [Pay All Dues and Generate NDC](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/FAQ) workflow and download the certificate after clearing the dues. The schedule does not give ordinary NDC generation a separate processing period.

Apply for a municipal water or sewer connection on [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/). The ULB schedule directs residents to its [citizen grievance app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.ulbharyana.cms.citizen) for street-light replacement and waste removal. The 10-day street-light entry is for replacement, not a separately notified MCG repair standard. The two-day waste entry concerns public-space clearance, not the frequency of household collection.

## RTS timelines for birth and death certificates

The [23 January 2023 notification, No. 7/31/2014-3AR](https://haryana-rtsc.gov.in/storage/app/notified-service/2023062845.pdf), covers Health, Urban Local Bodies and Medical Education and Research. Apply to the registrar for the place where the event occurred, or use the applicable civil registration service on [SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/). A certified copy of an existing entry is different from the first certificate after registration. Haryana's [Civil Registration System guidance](https://haryanahealth.gov.in/service/civil-registration-system/) says to report births and deaths within 21 days of occurrence and that a free copy is available at registration.

## Appealing a service delay or refusal

Under sections 6, 7 and 10 of the Haryana RTS Act, file a first appeal within 30 working days of rejection or expiry of the notified service limit. If the first authority rejects the appeal or the service is not delivered by the deadline in its order, a second appeal is available within 60 working days of that rejection or deadline. An applicant aggrieved by the second authority's order may seek revision before the [Haryana Right to Service Commission](https://haryana-rtsc.gov.in/) within 90 working days of the order.

For services integrated with Haryana's [Auto Appeal System](https://aas.saralharyana.nic.in/), a missed service deadline generates a first appeal. If there is no final decision after 30 working days at each grievance level, the system escalates to the second authority and then to the Commission. Check the appeal status rather than assuming the service uses automatic escalation.

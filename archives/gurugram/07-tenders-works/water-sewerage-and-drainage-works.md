# Gurugram water supply, sewerage and stormwater drainage works, 2024 to 2026

This is a selection of tenders and works, not a schedule of current bids. The frontmatter date is the date on the Municipal Corporation of Gurugram (MCG) hotspot sheet. Each tender below has its own publication date. Haryana eProcurement lists MCG as **Haryana Government > Urban Local Bodies > MC Gurgaon**. The Gurugram Metropolitan Development Authority (GMDA) issues separate tenders for master assets. Ward and zone labels are as published for each work, not a current ward crosswalk.

## Sewer cleaning and super-sucker contracts

## Coordination with GMDA water supply and sewage treatment

MCG's local pipes and boosters connect neighbourhoods to supply, while GMDA's water treatment plants (WTPs), master pipelines and master boosters move bulk water. A shutdown on the master side can interrupt local supply even if the local pumps are working. GMDA's [public notices](https://www.gmda.gov.in/news.html) record a Chandu shutdown on 7 to 8 Jan 2026 for master network works at the Sector 51 booster and a Basai and Sector 16 master shutdown on 23 to 24 Oct 2026 for an upgrade at the Sector 16 booster.

Not all treatment projects are GMDA master works. The [31 Jan 2024 MCG notice 2024_HRY_349369_1](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app?component=%24DirectLink&page=FrontEndViewTender&service=direct&sp=SRXLPWsvRaPdtih1S%2B1rTCQ%3D%3D) describes a 6 MLD sequencing batch reactor STP and village sewerage in Bhondsi, at an estimate of ₹24,90,51,043 and a 720-day work period.

The April 2025 minutes also direct MCG and GMDA to examine linking surrounding villages' local water and sewer networks to the GMDA master lines. A local connection, the receiving main and treatment capacity are separate checks. A storm drain is not a sewer connection, even where GMDA's desilting contract also requires it to disconnect illegal sewage outlets.

## Checking a record

Search the [Haryana eProcurement portal](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app) by Tender ID, including under tender status or results for closed bids. Compare the notice inviting tender, bill of quantities and corrigenda with the award or work order. Use the [GMDA tender index](https://gmda.gov.in/tenders.html) and [tender archive](https://gmda.gov.in/tendersArchive.html) for master assets. Mirror figures above are attributed to the mirror; amounts shown as lakh or crore are rounded as displayed there. A notice, award or notice to proceed does not establish completion. For that, look for the completion certificate, or the commissioning record where the work includes pumps or treatment equipment.

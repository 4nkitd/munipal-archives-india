# Gurugram civic jurisdiction and the Manesar boundary

A municipal boundary tells you which corporation covers a property. It does not tell you who maintains the highway, master sewer, streetlight or private lane beside it. Check the notified municipal limits for taxes and ward services, then the asset register or handover schedule for repairs. Gurugram district also includes other municipalities and rural areas; an address outside Municipal Corporation Manesar (MCM) is not necessarily inside Municipal Corporation of Gurugram (MCG).

## Where Dwarka Expressway divides MCG and MCM

Haryana Urban Local Bodies notification **20/57/2022-6CI, dated 16 December 2022**, altered Manesar's limits under section 3(3) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994, superseding its 24 December 2020 formation notification. Read the written schedule with points A to M on drawing **MCM/TP/2022/01, dated 30 November 2022**, attached to the [official notification](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/Municipal%20Corporation%20Manesar%20Amended%20Area%20Notification.pdf).

| Points | Notified alignment |
| --- | --- |
| A to B | From the intersection of Dhankot's northern revenue boundary and the Gurugram-Farrukhnagar-Jhajjar Road, follow that road east to the Sector 99/99A dividing road. |
| B to C | Go south on the Sector 99/99A divider to its junction with the existing Northern Peripheral Road (NPR). |
| **C to D** | **Follow the NPR south to the Delhi-Jaipur Road (NH-48). This is the scheduled Dwarka Expressway segment. MCM is on the west side and MCG on the east side of this common segment.** |
| D to E | Leave the NPR and follow NH-48 northeast to the Sector 75A/76 dividing road. |
| E to F to H | Follow the Sector 75A/76 divider southeast to the Sector 75/75A divider, then southwest to Shikohpur's revenue boundary at G and southeast to the Shikohpur/Kherki Daula revenue boundary junction at H. The remaining legs close the Manesar perimeter using revenue and district boundaries. |

The [MCM city profile](https://mcmanesar.in/manesar-city-info) names Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A among the additions; the [zone register](https://mcmanesar.in/zones) lists those sectors in Zone 2 and specifies **parts** of the Harsaru and Sihi revenue estates. Garhi Harsaru is a different revenue estate. [Contemporaneous reporting](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/corporation-boundaries-redrawn-with-dwarka-expressway-as-dividing-line/articleshow/96311558.cms) confirms the west/east split. The [final Manesar ward schedule and map of 3 November 2023](https://mcmanesar.in/wards) help identify a ward; for example, Ward 2 describes the stretch south from the Gurugram-Rewari railway crossing along the expressway to the Harsaru/Bamroli revenue boundary junction at Central Peripheral Road (CPR). That ward description is not a substitute for the C-to-D corporation boundary.

Do not extend the west/east rule to the whole expressway or infer a parcel line from a ramp, service road, postal address or sector label. Use the notification drawing, the [MCM area map](https://mcmanesar.in/area-map) and the applicable ward map. For an edge plot, give the corporation's Town Planning branch its geolocation, revenue estate and khasra number and request written confirmation. Check the municipal entry for its property ID separately, especially if a pre-2022 record names the other corporation.

## Agency jurisdiction matrix

| Agency | What it handles | What does not automatically follow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MCG | Municipal tax and property IDs in MCG limits; public sanitation, sweeping and waste services; internal public roads, local drains, municipal water and sewer distribution, streetlights and parks on its inventory. This includes accepted assets in former HSVP sectors and builder colonies and works in notified regularized areas. | A tax bill, regularization notification or location within MCG limits does not transfer each private or state asset. |
| MCM | The corresponding municipal functions in Manesar limits, including tax, sanitation and municipal internal roads, lighting, parks and local water and drainage assets. | GMDA trunk networks, NHAI highway assets and industrial-estate assets can remain under separate agencies. In IMT Manesar Sectors 1 to 8, MCM and Haryana State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation (HSIIDC) agreed that HSIIDC would handle estate roads, water, sewerage, lights and green belts. |
| GMDA | Gurugram Metropolitan Development Authority maintains designated master sector-dividing roads, master water mains and water treatment plants (WTPs), master sewers, stormwater drains and sewage treatment plants (STPs). Examples are Basai and Chandu Budhera WTPs and Dhanwapur and Behrampur STPs. It handles metropolitan mobility planning and its own signals and CCTV through the Integrated Command and Control Centre (ICCC). Gurugram Metropolitan City Bus Limited (GMCBL) operates Gurugaman city buses. | A GMDA road can carry another agency's streetlights or be swept by a corporation. GMDA's master network is not the same as the distribution pipes inside a colony. Police handle traffic enforcement and crime reports. |
| HSVP, formerly HUDA | Haryana Shehri Vikas Pradhikaran develops its urban estates and handles internal civic assets it has not transferred. Its estate offices handle HSVP plot allotment, possession, re-allotment, plot dues, conveyance and transfer records. | A maintenance transfer to MCG does not send HSVP allotment files to MCG. An old HUDA label does not mean HSVP still maintains the asset. |
| DTCP and private licensee | Haryana's Department of Town and Country Planning (DTCP) regulates colony licences, approved layouts, completion and enforcement under the Haryana Development and Regulation of Urban Areas Act, 1975. Until transfer, report internal upkeep to the licensee or designated facilities operator and licence breaches to DTCP. | DTCP regulates the licensee; it is not the road repair or garbage contractor. Change of land use (CLU) and statutory plan cases have separate routing below. |
| NHAI | National Highways Authority of India or its designated operator handles entrusted highway assets, including NH-48 (old NH-8), Dwarka Expressway (NH-248BB) and the Gurugram-Sohna highway including the Sohna Elevated Road (NH-248A, Rajiv Chowk towards Badshahpur and Sohna). | A road next to an NH may be a municipal, GMDA, Haryana PWD or private link. Check whether the service lane, underpass or access ramp is within the highway package. The municipal border does not split highway maintenance between MCG and MCM. |

Under [section 45 of the GMDA Act, 2017](https://gmda.gov.in/aboutus/gmda-act.html), [transfer scheme S.O. 23/H.A.34/2017/S.45/2018 of 11 April 2018](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/12920906/) transferred scheduled HSVP external infrastructure to GMDA, not internal development works. Check current ownership in the [GMDA road inventory](https://onemapdepts.gmda.gov.in/server/rest/services/GMDA_ROAD/GMDA_30days_road/MapServer/2) or [water, sewer and drainage layers](https://onemapdepts.gmda.gov.in/server/rest/services/Wet_Infrastructure_caching/MapServer/layers). The November 2026 [streetlight reporting](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/multiple-agencies-in-gurgaon-yet-roads-remain-in-dark-in-new-sectors-newer-sectors-along-e-way-bear-brunt-of-its-them-not-us-phenomenon/articleshow/135780794.cms) identifies HSVP as the maintainer of existing lights on newer-sector stretches in Sectors 58 to 67, 81 to 95 and 99 to 115, with GMDA handling its installations in Sectors 68 to 80. The proposed municipal transfer was still preliminary in that report. Use the pole or feeder ID to confirm an individual light rather than routing by the carriageway alone.

## How to check a sector or colony handover

- **Former HUDA sectors.** The [2016 rounds](https://www.hindustantimes.com/gurgaon/remaining-huda-sectors-transferred-to-mcg/story-J22k48XBtHaaysYTHnb9iN.html) moved upkeep in developed areas such as Sectors 4, 9A, 23, 40 and 56 to MCG. Separate transfers followed: on 7 August 2025, HSVP handed over upkeep of [20 sector markets, one institutional area and 20 residential pockets](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/hsvp-hands-over-maintenance-of-20-sector-markets-to-mcg-101754589675858.html), including pockets in Sector 57 Parts 1 and 2 and plots 6 to 12 in the Sector 44 institutional area. Do not treat a pocket or market transfer as a transfer of every asset in that sector. For water and sewer service, check the current bill issuer and the line operator.
- **Sushant Lok and other private colonies.** MCG took over maintenance in Sushant Lok I in an earlier round, alongside colonies including Palam Vihar, Suncity, South City I and II, and Nirvana Country. The [DTCP order of 9 May 2022, as reported](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/gurugram-civic-body-takes-over-nine-private-developer-colonies-101652122116042.html), used section 23A of the 1975 Act for nine colonies. The [June municipal rollout](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/gurugram-civic-body-to-take-over-nine-privately-developed-colonies-today-101655232009422.html) covered Sushant Lok II and III, Ardee City, Malibu Towne, Rosewood City, Greenwood City, Mayfield Garden, Uppal Southend and Vipul World (called Vipul Gardens in some May coverage). The handover covers listed public roads, open spaces, parks and public health services, not private apartment common areas. Under section 3(3)(a)(iii), the ordinary licensee upkeep obligation runs for five years from the colony completion certificate unless relieved earlier. Check any special transfer order and signed asset schedule; an apartment occupation certificate alone is not proof of handover.
- **DLF phases.** Do not mark DLF I to V as handed over based on an announcement. MCG's reported High Court submission in [December 2024](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/8-yrs-counting-mcg-yet-to-take-over-dlf-1-3-in-gurgaon/articleshow/116448993.cms) said maintenance takeover of Phases I to III remained pending, and [April 2026 reporting](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/after-mcg-says-not-our-job-dlf-begins-debris-cleanup/articleshow/130522591.cms) still identified DLF Utilities doing internal road clearance in I and II. No later signed blanket handover for I to III, or signed general handover for IV or V, was verified for this record. Ask DLF facilities or the residents' welfare association (RWA) and MCG Engineering for the phase, block, service and signed asset list; start with DLF for untransferred internal works and MCG for a confirmed municipal service. The [reported September 2026 High Court ruling](https://www.barandbench.com/news/dtcp-can-act-against-building-violations-in-gurugram-municipality-punjab-haryana-hc-in-dlf-case) distinguishes municipal maintenance from DTCP's enforcement powers under the 1975 Act.
- **Regularized colonies.** Look up the colony, municipality, approved layout and khasra in the [Haryana civic amenities register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure). For example, [notification 09/46/2024-4C-II of 6 March 2024](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2F134b8c15-f28f-48b9-9f62-9b76c0cd9afc.pdf) identifies specified parts of New Palam Vihar Phases I and II under MCG. A listing is a basis to seek civic works, not evidence that a particular service already exists or that a plot in an excluded strip is covered.

## Which agency handles my issue?

| Issue | Start here | Include or verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax, duplicate or wrong property ID, or an old MCG bill for a transferred Manesar address | [Haryana property tax portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) and the relevant MCG or MCM tax branch | Existing property ID, deed or revenue particulars and previous bill. Ask for a jurisdiction correction before opening another ID. |
| Pothole on an internal public lane, overflowing local drain, broken ward streetlight or municipal park upkeep | [MCG grievance and directory](https://www.mcg.gov.in/contact/office-contacts) or [MCM helpdesk](https://mcmanesar.in/contact-us); [Haryana ULB grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Ward or zone, landmark, map pin and photo; add the pole number for lighting. On an untransferred HSVP or colony asset, use the estate or facilities office instead. |
| Missed public waste pickup, street sweeping or dumping | MCG or MCM sanitation branch for the municipal service; society or contracted facility operator for collection inside a private compound | Date, collection point and map pin. For debris on an NH carriageway use NHAI. |
| No tap water, low pressure, disputed bill or overflowing neighbourhood sewer | Local distribution operator: MCG, MCM, [HSVP utilities](https://www.hsvphry.org.in/Home/OnlineServices) or the developer/society | Consumer number or bill issuer, affected plots and whether the colony's inlet is receiving water. If several areas have no supply at the bulk inlet, ask the operator to raise a GMDA ticket. |
| Burst master main, bulk connection, overflowing master sewer, blocked master storm drain or GMDA plant | [GMDA grievance portal](https://services.gmda.gov.in/), 0124-2653908 | Asset ID, junction and map pin. The [GMDA Right to Service notice](https://www.gmda.gov.in/static/docs/RTS_Notification.pdf) covers bulk and industrial water, sewer and drainage connections. |
| Pothole or flooding on a master sector divider; defective city signal or GMDA public CCTV | [GMDA](https://services.gmda.gov.in/) for its asset; Traffic Police for traffic enforcement or footage needed for an investigation | Both sector numbers, exact segment and carriageway, drain or pole ID if visible, and timestamp. |
| Bus route, stop, timetable, pass or service complaint | [GMCBL routes](https://gmcbl.in/commuter/bus-routes.html) and [GMCBL customer contact](https://www.gmcbl.in/contact-us.html), 73034-22556 | Route number, stop, direction and time. |
| HUDA/HSVP plot papers, possession, transfer or dues; fault on an untransferred estate asset | [HSVP Estate Office or Engineering Wing](https://www.hsvphry.org.in/Information/Wings/EstateOffice), 1800-180-3030 | Urban estate, sector, pocket, plot number and allotment letter; for faults ask which office maintains the exact asset. |
| Roads, lights, drains or water in IMT Manesar Sectors 1 to 8 | [HSIIDC](https://hsiidc.org.in/) estate or engineering office for the listed estate amenities; MCM for its municipal functions | IMT sector, plot, landmark and asset. See the [November 2025 MCM/HSIIDC agreement reporting](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/hsiidc-to-take-over-maintenance-of-public-amenities-in-imt-manesar/). |
| Builder colony has unfinished internal roads, missing amenities, unclear handover or a licence breach | Licensee or facilities office for repairs; [DTCP Haryana](https://tcpharyana.gov.in/) and District Town Planner, Gurugram, for completion records, licence conditions or enforcement | Licence number, phase and block, approved layout, colony completion certificate and any transfer order or inventory. |
| Land use, CLU or GMUC-2031 zoning | [DTCP development plans](https://tcpharyana.gov.in/DevelopmentPlan.htm) for the notified plan; [ULB CLU portal](https://ulbcluhry.org/) for its municipal cases; [GMDA e-CLU](https://www.gmda.gov.in/onlineservices/clu.html) for eligible GMDA controlled-area cases; [DTCP online filing](https://ofa.tcpharyana.gov.in/) where DTCP retains competence | Revenue estate, khasra, present and proposed use, sector plan and municipal status. Confirm the competent authority before filing. |
| Pothole, debris, roadside breakdown or unsafe ramp on NH-48, Dwarka Expressway or Sohna Elevated | NHAI or its highway operator: [national highway helpline 1033](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2014580&lang=2&reg=48) | Highway number, kilometre marker or exit, direction, and whether the fault is on the main deck, slip road or service lane. |

Save the complaint number. If redirected, ask the responding office to name the current maintenance division and quote the asset ID or handover order. At an interface, such as a municipal drain discharging to a GMDA master drain, send both offices the same pin and the linked complaint numbers.

## GMUC-2031 and change of land use

DTCP notified the [Final Development Plan 2031 AD for the Gurgaon-Manesar Urban Complex (GMUC)](https://tcpharyana.gov.in/Development_Plan/Gurgaon/Gurgaon/FDP_2031/FDP_2031_GMUC.pdf) on **15 November 2012**, No. **CCP(NCR)/FDP-2031/GGN/2012/3541**. Use the notified plan and its [24 January 2017 amendment](https://tcpharyana.gov.in/Development_Plan/Gurgaon/Gurgaon/FDP_2031/Amendment_Notification%20Gurgaon_2412017.PDF) with the applicable sector plan. The [13 June 2025 publication](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2F114ab102-cdc4-4333-8c15-cad53c3265e1.pdf) was a draft amendment, not by itself a change in permitted use. The plan assigns land use and reservations; it neither fixes the municipal boundary nor identifies the current repair agency.

For CLU under the 1963 controlled-area law, the [ULB procedure](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=CONTENTFILES%5C6fb1bbc8-4b27-48c0-a4a0-224951f80450.pdf) identifies the Director, Urban Local Bodies, for applicable cases inside municipal limits under section 350D of the Municipal Corporation Act, subject to specific delegations. GMDA's official e-CLU route handles eligible controlled-area cases within its planning jurisdiction, subject to reserved State Government powers. Check with DTCP for cases it retains. A CLU permission is distinct from a colony licence, building plan approval or a municipal maintenance transfer.

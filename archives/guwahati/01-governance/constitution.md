# Constitution of Guwahati Municipal Corporation

## Act and first council

GMC dates its constitution to the first meeting of the elected councillors in 1974 under section 45 of the Guwahati Municipal Corporation Act, 1971. The year in the Act's title is therefore not the year the council first sat.

## Scope of municipal work

GMC's [description of its functions](https://gmc.assam.gov.in/about-us/about-us) distinguishes its six engineering divisions for conservancy and public works from its six revenue zones for property tax and trade licences. It lists road and drain maintenance, property mutation, building permission and lighting among the corporation’s responsibilities.

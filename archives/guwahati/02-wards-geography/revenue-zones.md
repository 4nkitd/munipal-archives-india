# Guwahati revenue zones by ward

## New ward numbers by zone

Use the “New wards Covered” column in the municipal directory for these assignments. Each zone has a deputy commissioner. These are revenue jurisdictions, not street boundaries or engineering divisions.

| Revenue zone | Ward numbers |
| --- | --- |
| Central | 16, 18, 19, 28, 29, 30 |
| East | 37, 38, 39, 40, 50, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 57, 58 |
| South | 17, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, 36 |
| West | 1–9, 11–15 |
| Dispur | 41–49, 51, 59, 60 |
| Lokhra | 10, 20–27 |

The directory also has an old-number column. For example, new ward 10 falls under Lokhra, whereas old ward 10 appears in the South zone.

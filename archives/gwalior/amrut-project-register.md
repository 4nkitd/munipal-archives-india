# Gwalior AMRUT projects reported as grounded, March 2026

## Reported counts and costs

Serial 191 of the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' city table is the Madhya Pradesh row for Gwalior. The heading describes the table as projects grounded under AMRUT, using information updated by the states as of 2 March 2026.

| Sector | Projects | Cost, ₹ crore |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Water supply | 2 | 345.84 |
| Sewerage and septage management | 2 | 388.97 |
| Parks | 6 | 15.31 |
| Drainage | 1 | 21.44 |
| Urban transport | 1 | 4.48 |
| Total of reported cells | 12 | 776.04 |

The sewerage and septage line has the highest reported cost. Parks has the most projects. The total sums the five sector entries; it is not a separate figure printed in the row. The table does not name the works or state their tender numbers, ward coverage, payments or completion dates. The amounts are project costs, not the corporation's capital expenditure for a fiscal year.

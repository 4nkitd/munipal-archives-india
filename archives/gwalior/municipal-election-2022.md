# Gwalior municipal general election and corporation term, 2022 to 2027

## Official calendar

The Madhya Pradesh State Election Commission's urban programme index records the statewide general-election programme under letter `F-53NN-01/2022/5/445`, dated 1 June 2022. The commission's [term calendar](https://www.mplocalelection.mp.gov.in/Data/rightpanel/UploadCalendar_2024.pdf#page=3) lists Gwalior Municipal Corporation at serial 69. The [urban election overview](https://www.mplocalelection.mp.gov.in/Data/rightpanel/EkNagarUpdated2025.pdf#page=1) supplies the counting and result-announcement date for that poll phase.

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| General-election poll, first phase | 6 July 2022 |
| First-phase counting and result announcement | 17 July 2022 |
| First corporation meeting | 5 August 2022 |
| Projected end of term | 4 August 2027 |

## Offices elected

Sections 9 and 14 of the [Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/Uploaded%20Document/act%20%26%20rules/MunicipalCorporationACT1956%2024.pdf) distinguish direct election of the mayor by the municipal area from direct election of councillors by ward, and put both elections under the State Election Commission. Section 10 gives the state government responsibility for publishing the number and extent of wards in the Official Gazette. This calendar records dates, not candidate results, individual ward boundaries or later vacancies.

# Gwalior municipal office and jurisdiction

## Which authority is listed?

The district's public-utility directory places Gwalior Municipal Corporation at City Center, Gwalior 474001. Its separate [Commissioner entry](https://gwalior.nic.in/en/directory/gwalior-municipal-corporation/) lists the same landline. The [state telephone and email directory](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=7HRZoU6F4ZQQb0k3xdV0HA%3D%3D) lists a second office contact, `0751-2438250` and `commgwalior@mpurban.gov.in`. The state and district entries do not say that either contact supersedes the other.

The [state division-wise local-body list](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=hxIqP77vI9ypoSI%2B5%2FYrMg%3D%3D) identifies Gwalior as a Nagar Nigam. The same list has separate rows for Morar Cantonment, Dabra and other urban bodies in the district. It attributes a Census 2011 population of 1,054,420 to the Gwalior corporation row, not to the district or the cantonment. The [Local Government Directory](https://lgdirectory.gov.in/) identifies the corporation as `250694` and links it to Census town code `802100`.

## What the corporation is responsible for

Section 7 of the [Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/Uploaded%20Document/act%20%26%20rules/MunicipalCorporationACT1956%2024.pdf) connects the corporation to a larger urban area specified by public notification. Section 66 lists duties including public street lighting and cleaning, management of municipal waterworks, and birth, marriage and death registration. These statutory duties do not identify the jurisdiction of any particular property. Consult the corporation or the applicable Gazette notice for a parcel at the edge of the city.

# Nagar Nigam Haldwani-Kathgodam: municipal status and jurisdiction

## Name and municipal history

Nagar Nigam Haldwani-Kathgodam is the corporation for the Haldwani-Kathgodam municipal area in Nainital district, Uttarakhand. The [district election portal](https://nainital.nic.in/haldwani-kathgodam/) uses the full name. The state [urban local body roster](https://udd.uk.gov.in/list-of-ulb/) uses the shorter name Nagar Nigam Haldwani, at entry 46.

The municipal history records a notified area in 1904, followed by an upgrade to a third-class municipality in 1942, a second-class municipality in 1956 and a first-class municipality in 1966. It dates the change from Nagar Palika Parishad to Nagar Nigam to 21 May 2011. The same page gives 127,603 residents for the 2001 census and 171,353 for 2011. These are historical figures and do not describe the population under today's ward arrangement.

## Municipal office and separate bodies

The [corporation contact page](https://haldwaninagarnigam.com/Contact) lists the Municipal Commissioner's Office in the Nagar Nigam Main Building on Nainital Road. The same page lists the ground-floor help desk at 8882610000. The state roster separately lists Nagar Palika Parishad Nainital, the municipal council for Nainital town. Being in Nainital district does not by itself put an address under Nagar Nigam Haldwani-Kathgodam.

# 2018 Haldwani-Kathgodam municipal boundary expansion

## Notification and schedule

The state Urban Development Section issued notification no. 911/IV(3)/2018-01(13)/2012 on 5 April 2018. It identifies the corporation as Nagar Nigam Haldwani-Kathgodam, Nainital district. The notification recounts cancellation of the 8 December 2017 expansion notice after the Uttarakhand High Court's 9 March 2018 order, followed by a fresh provisional notification on 10 March 2018 for objections and suggestions.

Schedule One on PDF pages 4–5 identifies territory to include in the corporation, with 36 serial entries. It gives 2,842.922 hectares and a 2011 population of 109,253 for the listed areas. Examples are given below using the names printed in the schedule. The totals are for the listed additions, not the whole corporation.

The scanned [state compilation](https://udd.uk.gov.in/document-category/nikay-adhisuchna/) preserves the issued notification, which directs publication in the extraordinary gazette; the gazette publication date itself was not verified. The schedule's serial numbers are not ward numbers. Use the schedule for the historical expansion, not for assigning individual streets to wards.

# Haldwani-Kathgodam ward numbering and sanitation coverage

## What the municipal source shows

The Health and Sanitation Section describes two groups covering all 60 wards. It attributes daily street and drain cleaning in wards 1 through 33 to municipal sanitation staff. It attributes the work in wards 34 through 60 to sanitation committee staff. The page also distinguishes contracted door-to-door collection in wards 1 through 33 from municipal collection in wards 34 through 60.

| Ward numbers | Collection arrangement as described |
| --- | --- |
| 1–33 | Municipal contractor, with 33 hoppers and 5 compactors |
| 34–60 | Municipal crews, with 35 hoppers and 3 compactors |

The [municipal property tax search](https://ptaxnnh.com/) independently offers each number from 1 through 60 in its ward selector. The state [Baini Sena case study](https://udd.uk.gov.in/scheme/haldwani-baini-sena-shg-solid-waste-management/) gives 60 as the total ward count and reports an improvement in sanitation in 57 of those wards. The figure 57 describes programme coverage, not a smaller ward total.

## Delimitation limits

The sources establish the ward count and numbering, but the sanitation groups are not legal ward or zone boundaries. Neither the selectors nor the sanitation figures establish which ward contains a particular street. The state's [2018 territorial expansion notification](territorial-expansion-2018.md) identifies areas added to the corporation, but does not divide that area into wards. The latest legal ward delimitation schedule remains unverified.

# Dharampura building tax mutation objection notice, September 2026

## Application and objections

The Tax Section's scanned notice no. 1723, dated 29 September 2026, concerns an application to change the name in the corporation's building tax records for part of a building in Dharampura, Haldwani. The notice describes a claim based on a registered will. It records that the applicant submitted supporting material including a copy of the will, a death certificate, a survivor certificate, an affidavit and a building tax receipt.

The notice asks interested parties to file written objections at the municipal office within 30 days of issue, with an affidavit and supporting evidence. It says late objections would not be considered. The notice records an application and an opportunity to object; it does not establish title or show that the corporation approved the requested mutation.

The [corporation's public notices index](https://haldwaninagarnigam.com/Public-Notice) also gives 29 September 2026. This was a property-specific notice, not a general rule for all mutation applications.

# Haldwani-Kathgodam property tax, self-assessment and mutation

## Online services

The [corporation homepage](https://haldwaninagarnigam.com/) links to the property tax portal under Online Service. The search form accepts identifiers including PTIN, PT-ID or property ID, receipt number and house number. It also shows name, zone and ward fields. Separate menu items offer new property self-assessment, assessment status, a mutation application and mutation status.

The portal warns against paying a second time if the amount was debited but a receipt was not generated. The archive checked the public menu and form; it did not make a payment or submit an application.

## How the corporation describes self-assessment

The [Tax Section](https://haldwaninagarnigam.com/Tax-Department) cites board resolution no. 1 from 17 June 2016 for the self-assessment system. It describes calculating building tax using the property's measured area and a prescribed monthly rental value per square foot for the ward. The page discusses residential and commercial buildings but does not provide a current rate table. The resolution date is part of the service history, not the publication date of the current web page.

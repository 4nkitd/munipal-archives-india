# Scheduled areas added to Hamirpur and their property-tax exemption

The proposal dated 23 November 2024 was published on 24 November. After considering objections, the Governor included the scheduled areas in Municipal Council Hamirpur under section 5(3) of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Act, 1994. The inclusion notification, **UD-A(1)-15/2023** dated 23 December, was published on **26 December 2024**. Although it shares a file number and signature date with the corporation declaration, it is a different instrument.

## Which properties qualify?

Printed page 10959 exempts **land and buildings in the schedule** from property tax for **three years from publication**. Residents retain their customary rights recorded in the Wazib-Ul-Urj. This provision does not exempt the whole city. The schedule identifies revenue estates, patwar circles, whether inclusion is complete or partial, and khasra numbers. Selected entries:

| Schedule row | Patwar circle | Muhal/Mouja | Inclusion |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dhalot | Bhira | Complete |
| 91 | Sarhak | Sarhakar | Partial |
| 92 | Lohari | Ropri | Partial |
| 94 | Sarhak | Kharoh | Complete |

For Sarhakar or Ropri, the village name alone cannot establish eligibility. Match the property's khasra number to the relevant schedule row.

# Constitution of Municipal Corporation Hamirpur

The Governor declared Municipal Council, Hamirpur, District Hamirpur, a corporation under section 3(2) of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 (Act 12 of 1994). Notification **UD-A(1)-15/2023**, signed on **23 December 2024**, took effect “from the date of publication of this notification in the Rajpatra”. That publication was **30 December 2024**, issue 208, printed pages 11017–11018. The authoritative English text appears on page 11018. The declaration applies to the city municipality, not to the whole district.

## Area and population

Row 11 of the state's [district-wise ULB roster as at 31 March 2026](https://ud.hp.gov.in/storage/media/Municipalities%20at%20a%20glance/idt8VksfK499MixaXlK0D4jadbpoA7tCKJRqoGAw.pdf) records 40.77 km² and a 2011 population of 41,752. It gives 1974 for the predecessor body and 30 December 2024 for upgradation. The [municipal history](https://mchamirpur.com/Web/history) instead reports 42.04 km² and about 43,055 people. The profile uses the dated state roster; neither population figure is a 2026 headcount.

# Hamirpur Town Hall and municipal service routes

| Request | Route published by the corporation |
|---|---|
| Property-tax collection or garbage-bill payment | [Citizen Seva, Himachal Pradesh Urban Development](https://citizenseva.hp.gov.in/hp-udd/) |
| Trade licence, pet dog registration or municipal asset booking | The city's utility links point to the same Citizen Seva portal. |
| Building planning | The city links to [Himachal Pradesh Town and Country Planning](https://hptcp.hp.gov.in/). |

The [city service list](https://mchamirpur.com/Web/ServicesWeDeliver) also names birth, death and marriage certificates, water and electricity NOCs, NOCs for sale or exchange of land, land subdivision and Parivar Nakl. It does not publish application fees or processing times there.

For a municipal enquiry, use **Town Hall, Kohta, Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh 177001** or **m.corphamirpur@gmail.com**. The [district's municipal utility directory](https://hphamirpur.nic.in/public-utility/municipal-council-hamirpur/) confirms the email address and links to the city's own site.

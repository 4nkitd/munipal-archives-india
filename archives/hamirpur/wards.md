# Hamirpur's 15 corporation wards and their electoral status

Deputy Commissioner order **DCH/UDB//Delimitation-2025/459** is dated **3 July 2025**. The [district notice](https://hphamirpur.nic.in/notice/municipal-corporation-hamirpur-final-delimitation-order/) went online on 5 July. The draft was published on 2 June, claims and objections were received through 9 June, and a speaking order disposed of objections on 17 June.

The schedule also gives locality and boundary descriptions. Ward 13, for example, lists Bohni, Tropka, Baroha and Panjahali; its eastern edge follows the Duhga Khurd boundary from Sai hospital and then the national highway to Kohli bridge. Use the final annexure for properties near a ward line.

## Delimitation is not an election result

The State Election Commission's [General Election Report 2026](https://sechimachal.hp.gov.in/assets/files/GeneralElectionReportToLocalBodies2026.pdf) explicitly says elections were not held for Hamirpur, Una, Baddi or Shimla municipal corporations. The Hamirpur **district** figures in its urban election tables include other municipalities. The [SEC corporation results for 31 May 2026](https://sechimachal.hp.gov.in/DPMIS/PollingResultDashBoardPublicMC.aspx) cover 64 wards: Kangra 32, Mandi 15 and Solan 17. The [city's own history](https://mchamirpur.com/Web/history) describes administration by a commissioner pending elections. No subsequent official return establishing a Hamirpur mayor or councillors was verified.

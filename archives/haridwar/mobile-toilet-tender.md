# Haridwar short-term mobile toilet tender no. 3698

## Notice and listing dates

Nagar Nigam Haridwar dated its short-term mobile toilet tender notice no. 3698 on 13 March 2023. The district tender register gives 14 March as the start date and 22 March as the end date, and links the original notice. The register does not name a successful bidder.

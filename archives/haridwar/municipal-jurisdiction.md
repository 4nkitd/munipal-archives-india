# Haridwar municipal corporation and contact

## Corporation and jurisdiction

The state lists Nagar Nigam Haridwar at serial 32 under Haridwar district. Shivalik Nagar is a separate Nagar Palika Parishad. Haridwar and Roorkee have separate corporations.

# Upgrade of Hassan City Municipal Council to Hassan City Corporation

## The two gazette dates

| Extraordinary issue | Entry under Urban Development |
|---|---|
| 21 January 2025 | Earlier notification concerning the change from Hassan city municipal council area to Hassan city corporation |
| 28 April 2025 | Notification upgrading Hassan City Municipal Council to Hassan City Corporation |

The April issue is indexed under “ಹಾಸನ ನಗರಸಭೆಯನ್ನು ಹಾಸನ ಮಹಾನಗರ ಪಾಲಿಕೆ ಎಂದು ಮೇಲ್ದರ್ಜೆಗೇರಿಸುವ ಬಗ್ಗೆ.” In the e-Rajyapatra department search, select Urban Development and find the 28-Apr-2025 entry under Municipal Administration (One and Three). The register labels the file 6088.79 Kb. The January entry is a separate issue; it should not be used as the date of the completed upgrade.

The distinction matters for Hassan's land records. The same department's register contains a separate 11 January 2024 notice concerning the Hassan **local planning area** under town and country planning law. Its village count describes the planning area, not the boundaries or ward count of the new corporation.

## Declaration and extent

Notification **UDD 307 MLR 2024(e)**, Extraordinary Part III No. 245, declares the larger urban area “The Corporation of the City of Hassan” under sections 3, 4, 500, 501 and 503 of the Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976. Page 6 records that the state considered objections. It calls 21 January 2025 the date of the earlier notification but gives 21 February 2025 as its gazette publication date; the portal indexes the earlier entry under January.

Schedule A on page 7 gives a total of **86.01 km²**. It includes the existing council area, refers to **previous** wards 1 to 35 and identifies villages already added on 31 December 2024. It then lists additions from six gram panchayats:

| Gram panchayat | Places included, as printed |
|---|---|
| Thattekere | Hullenahalli, Narihalli, Thattekere, JI Thattekere |
| Hanumanthapura | Kokkanagatta |
| Bhoovanahalli | Busthenahalli |
| Doddagenigere | Kasthuravalli, Kenchattahalli, Gekaravalli, Samudravalli (including the entire industrial area) |
| B Kaatihalli | Doddapura |
| Hoovinahalli Kaval | Uddooru |

Schedule B describes the perimeter by landmarks and survey numbers, starting on the Hassan Belur State Highway.

# Preliminary ward delimitation for Hassan City Corporation

## Identifying the ward notice

In e-Rajyapatra's department search, select Urban Development and find the Extraordinary issue for 03-Feb-2026. The issuing branch is Municipal Administration (One and Three). The Kannada subject reads “ಹಾಸನ ಮಹಾನಗರ ಪಾಲಿಕೆಗೆ ವಾರ್ಡುವಾರು ಕ್ಷೇತ್ರ ಪುನರ್ ವಿಂಗಡಣೆ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಪ್ರಾಥಮಿಕ ಅಧಿಸೂಚನೆ.” The word “ಪ್ರಾಥಮಿಕ” identifies the notification as preliminary. The attachment is listed at 11617.94 Kb.

This is a proposed division of the corporation into electoral wards. Its date and issuing body distinguish it from the 28 April 2025 notification that upgraded the council, and from the 4 March 2026 local planning area entry issued by Hassan Urban Development Authority. Those two records concern different boundaries. A property’s final electoral ward should be established from the final municipal delimitation, rather than from this preliminary issue or an older council ward number.

The linked 13-page issue is **Part III No. 131**. The ward schedules are not legible in the extracted text. [Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

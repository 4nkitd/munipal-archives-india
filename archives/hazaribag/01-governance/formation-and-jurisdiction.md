# Hazaribag Municipal Corporation formation and revenue villages

## Formation under notification 167

The Urban Development & Housing Department issued notification 167 on 11 January 2016. Jharkhand Gazette extraordinary issue 35 published it on 18 January 2016, the effective date given in the notification. The order formed Hazaribag Nagar Nigam from the existing Class A Hazaribag Nagar Parishad and 19 revenue villages or village parts under the Jharkhand Municipal Act, 2011. It followed draft notification 2071 of 11 June 2015, for which the state had invited objections and suggestions within 30 days.

The formation order gives a 2011 Census population of 142,489 for the former council and 54,977 for the places added, for a combined population of 197,466. Appendix I gives 27.594 km² for the added area alone, not the whole corporation.

## Appendix I additions

The schedule lists 13 entries in Sadar and six in Katkamdag. The numbers below are *thana* identifiers, not wards. Where the annexure says only part of a village was included, that qualifier is retained.

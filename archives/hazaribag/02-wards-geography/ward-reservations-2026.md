# Hazaribag ward seat reservations for the 2026 municipal election

## Form 3 schedule

The District Magistrate-cum-Deputy Commissioner's office published final Form 3 through notification 838 in extraordinary District Gazette issue 50 on 17 December 2025. The schedule identifies the body as V Hazaribag / 02 Hazaribag Nagar Nigam (Class B) for this election and lists councillor seats for wards 1 to 36 under Rule 9 of the Jharkhand Municipal Election and Election Petition Rules, 2012. The woman/other column is separate from the category column.

| Seat category | Women (महिला) | Other (अन्य) |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled Caste | 33, 35 | 9, 25 |
| Scheduled Tribe | None | 11 |
| Extremely Backward Class I | 1, 8, 24, 30, 31 | 2, 16, 17, 29, 32 |
| Backward Class II | 15 | 3, 28 |
| Unreserved | 5, 10, 12, 14, 18, 19, 20, 22, 27 | 4, 6, 7, 13, 21, 23, 26, 34, 36 |

The schedule marks 17 wards in the women column. It lists eligibility categories for councillor seats, not mayoral reservation, results, officeholders or geographic boundaries. The [district's separate polling-station and map posting](https://hazaribag.nic.in/notice/list-of-polling-stations-under-hazaribagh-municipal-corporation-area-along-with-prescribed-form-form-a-form-b-form-c-and-map/) is dated 17 February 2025 and should be read as its own edition.

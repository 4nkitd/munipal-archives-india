# Hazaribag Municipal Corporation budget estimates for 2026–27

## Estimated receipts and payments

The budget's BUD-3 summary and BUD-4 major account head schedule put the 2026–27 estimates alongside prior-year actuals, 2025–26 actuals through December, and original and revised 2025–26 estimates. The figures below are from the 2026–27 column. They are not audited outturns.

| Budget line | 2026–27 estimate (₹) |
|---|---:|
| Opening balance | 549,359,025 |
| Revenue receipts | 255,525,078 |
| Capital receipts | 2,351,372,433 |
| Total funds available, including opening balance | 3,156,256,536 |
| Revenue expenditure | 589,886,751 |
| Capital expenditure | 2,563,082,853 |
| Total expenditure | 3,152,969,604 |
| Closing balance | 3,286,932 |

BUD-4 estimates ₹149,098,950 in tax revenue, ₹2,332,972,984 in grants and contributions for specific purposes, and ₹2,563,067,935 for fixed assets. The portal's Amount field matches the expenditure total, not total funds available. The scanned summary bears signatures dated 25 March 2026, but the portal's budget entry does not give the document's publication date.

# Municipal Corporation Hisar: city profile and governance

## Formation and municipal figures

The Haryana Government upgraded the Hisar Municipal Council to a municipal corporation on 17 March 2010 under the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. The [State Election Commission's 2010 election report](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2023/05/2023050249.pdf) cites the constitution notification as No. 18/1-7/2010-3CI.

The [Haryana Urban Local Bodies key figures table](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists Hisar's municipal area as 92.92 sq km and its population as 3,76,362. The table does not give a reference year for that population. Both figures refer to the corporation, not Hisar district. The corporation has 20 electoral wards.

## Elected and administrative leadership

Praveen Popli of the Bharatiya Janata Party won the mayoral election held on 2 March 2025. Counting took place on 12 March, when [the result was reported under the name Praveen Popli](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/hisar-mayoral-poll-bjp-candidate-praveen-poplis-record-breaking-win/articleshow/118944998.cms). The State Election Commission's result notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272, dated 20 March 2025 and linked above, records him as Parveen Kumar and lists the elected members for wards 1 through 20.

The [Hisar district officials directory](https://hisar.gov.in/about-district/whos-who/) lists C Jayasharadha, IAS, as Commissioner of Municipal Corporation Hisar. This is the corporation's administrative post, separate from the elected mayor and from the Commissioner of Hisar Division.

## Headquarters and public contacts

The [district public utility directory](https://hisar.gov.in/public-utility/municipal-corporation-hisar/) lists the corporation at Balsamand Road, Hisar, Haryana 125001. The corporation's online services are available through the [Haryana Urban Local Bodies Hisar portal](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Hisar/).

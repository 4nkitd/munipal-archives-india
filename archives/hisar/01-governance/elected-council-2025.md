# Hisar Municipal Corporation elected council, March 2025

## Election result

Hisar voted on 2 March 2025. The results were declared on 12 March 2025. The State Election Commission, Haryana, notified the elected mayor and ward members on 20 March 2025 under section 14(1) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994.

Praveen Popli of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), listed as Parveen Kumar in the SEC notification, won the mayoral election with 96,329 votes. His nearest rival, Krishna Titu Singla of the Indian National Congress (INC), received 31,873 votes. Popli's margin was 64,456 votes.

## Ward winners

The BJP won 17 of the council's 20 ward seats. Independents won wards 6 and 8, and the INC won ward 19. The table records affiliation at the election, not any later change. Names follow the SEC English table except for ward 20, where the Hindi table gives Naveen Kumar and the English table prints "Naveen Kuamr." The Tribune reports the ward 19 winner as Satyawan Pannu; the SEC lists him as Satyavan.

# Hisar wards, sectors and locality geography

## Delimitation and reading this index

The Haryana Government Gazette dated 14 December 2024, Extraordinary No. 201-2024/Ext., publishes notification No. 4/8/2024-4C1 under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994. It references the notification of 26 February 2024 and directs readers to drawing No. 6629/MCH/MAP/2024 dated 20 March 2024. The schedule describes Wards 1 through 20 through roads, railway lines, canals, property identifiers and individual buildings.

The companion `../wards.json` contains abbreviated boundary circuits for all 20 wards. These are an index, not a substitute for the notified schedule or drawing. Its `sectors`, `colonies` and `villages` arrays remain empty because a place appearing on a boundary does not establish that the entire place falls inside that ward. `zone` and `division` are null because the source does not provide an administrative zone or engineering-division allocation. HSVP's regional "Hisar Zone" is not a municipal ward zone.

The source is an OCR transcription of a published government gazette hosted by an independent archive. The Hindi and English schedules contain differences in individual landmarks and apparent coordinate errors. This record does not silently turn those coordinates into mapped polygons.

## HSVP sectors and Urban Estate II

Sector numbers belong to planning and estate records, not to the municipal ward numbering system. Sector 13 is not Ward 13, for example. The requested sector index covers Sectors 1, 2, 3 and 4; Sectors 9, 10 and 11; Sector 13; Sector 14; Sector 15; Sectors 16 and 17; and Sector 33. It is not a certified list of every HSVP sector in Hisar.

| Sector or estate | Evidence and ward-mapping limit |
| --- | --- |
| Sectors 1 and 4 | The delimitation names the Sector 1/4 road in Wards 12 and 13. It does not assign both complete sectors to either ward. |
| Sector 2 | Included as a requested locality search term. A reliable ward allocation was not established in the reviewed sources. |
| Sector 3 | Boundary and corner references occur in Wards 8 and 9. Ward 15 also names the road toward Sectors 3/5. |
| Sectors 9 and 11 | Their main road is a boundary reference for Wards 12 and 14. |
| Sector 10 | Do not expand the gazette's "9&11" reference into a confirmed Sector 10 ward allocation. |
| Sector 13 | HSVP's October 2023 layout-plan index lists Sector 13 Part II with Sector 15 Part and Sectors 16/17 Phase II under Hisar urban estate. |
| Sector 14 | The Ward 1/2 boundary references Sector 14. HSVP's September 2024 layout index separately lists Sector 14 Part II, Hisar. |
| Sector 15 | Present in HSVP's combined layout-plan entry. HSVP also separately advertises Sector 15A/15AP; those labels must not be collapsed into Sector 15. |
| Sectors 16 and 17 | HSVP's layout index uses a combined sector label. Its notices also include a 2 January 2025 notice concerning a social/charitable/religious/Dharamshala site in Sector 16-17, Hisar. |
| Sector 33 | HSVP's May 2025 residential auction schedule identifies Sector 33 Part I, Hisar. This establishes the estate designation, not municipal ward membership. |
| Urban Estate II | HSVP's notices of 24 January and 4 February 2025 name Urban Estate II alongside Sector 15A/15AP. Neither notice establishes a whole-estate municipal ward assignment. |

HSVP's layout-plan index asks readers to check the latest plan with the concerned Estate Officer or District Town Planner before relying on it. Apply that check to sector parts, extensions and estate names as well as to plot boundaries.

## Model Town, Auto Market, Mill Gate and the old city

Model Town and Model Town Extension should remain separate search labels. The gazette expressly names house 287 in Model Town Extension and adjoining house/property landmarks on the Ward 12/13 boundary. It does not certify that all of Model Town lies in one ward.

New Auto Market Phase III forms part of the Ward 2/6 boundary description near GJU and Gujjar Dharamshala. "Auto Market" alone is too broad for a property-level ward assignment. Retain the phase and the approach road when recording an address.

Mill Gate is likewise a corridor rather than a single ward. Ward 6 names Tibba Dana Sher and Mill Gate Road; Wards 7 and 9 use Vinod Nagar on Mill Gate Road and HTM police station. The adjoining 12 Quarter Road appears in several wards.

The old city's identifiable boundary landmarks include Nagori Gate, Talaki Gate, Gulab Singh Chowk, Khajanchiyan Bazaar, Gandhi Chowk, Bhagat Singh Chowk, Multani Chowk, Parijat Chowk and Gyan Jyoti Chowk. Wards 1, 2 and 3 share several of these references; Ward 20 also reaches Parijat Chowk and CAV School street. "Old City" is therefore not a legal ward name or a safe single-ward address.

## Regularized colonies and notified polygons

The DULB regularized-colony index and its civic-amenities-deficient-area register are the official starting points. A proposal, survey or council resolution is not the same as a state notification. The statutory schedule identifies land through revenue estates and khasra numbers, so inclusion of a colony name does not establish that every extension or plot is covered.

One official DULB notification PDF available through that index lists the following Hisar entries under the Haryana Management of Civic Amenities and Infrastructure Deficient Municipal Areas Special Provisions Act, 2016.

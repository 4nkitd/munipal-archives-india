## Town planning, accounts and taxation

District Town and Country Planning, HSVP and Municipal Corporation offices are distinct bodies. A District Town Planner listed for another department must not be presented as the corporation's planning officer without an allocation or posting record. No such municipal assignment was verified here.

For a property-tax assessment, property ID correction, payment reconciliation, municipal accounts matter or building-plan file, contact the published municipal office and request the relevant branch and current dealing officer. Do not infer a named tax officer from a website nodal-officer footer. Older Hisar ULB service pages appeared in search results but returned a 404 when retrieved, so they are not used as a current officer directory.

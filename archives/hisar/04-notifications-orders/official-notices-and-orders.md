# Hisar official notices, orders and colony notifications

## Source scope and dates

The District Hisar administration identifies [the ULB Hisar page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Hisar/) as the Municipal Corporation website. On inspection, that address displayed the department's shared statewide notices and services. A notice displayed there is not necessarily a Hisar-specific order. This record separates documents that name Hisar from statewide instruments relevant to the corporation.

The primary source is the department's [regularized colonies index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024). It includes a document titled "01-02-2024 notified colonies", alongside later notification batches and a municipality-wise status compilation published on 13 October 2025. Upload dates on the index are not necessarily notification dates. The February instrument has an endorsement dated 1 February 2024, No. 09/19/2024-4C-II. Its separate upload notice appeared on 12 February 2024.

The [Town Planning Notifications index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/TownPlanningNotifications) also retains "Unauthorized Colonies of Hisar", dated 7 January 2021, but provides no document link in that row. That index entry alone does not establish any colony's present status.

## Conditions on colony coverage

The notification says boundaries are those in approved layout plans submitted by the municipalities and recommended by the concerned Divisional Commissioners and District Municipal Commissioners. It excludes plots affected by high-tension lines, gas or petroleum pipelines, proposed road alignments, green belts or restricted belts shown in the layout and Development Plan, subject to the conditions stated in the notification. It also restricts coverage of commercial, industrial, institutional and other non-residential uses pending the stipulated permission.

The department's label "regularized colonies" should therefore be read with the actual deficient-area declaration and its exclusions. It is not evidence that every plot using a colony's name has clear title, approved land use, a sanctioned building plan or an occupation certificate. Match the colony ID, revenue estate, khasra numbers and plot position to the approved layout through the [Civic Amenities index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure).

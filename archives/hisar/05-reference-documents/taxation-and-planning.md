# Hisar property tax, NDC, building code and Lal Dora references

## Current tax instrument and Hisar classification

The [corporation notification dated 30 July 2026](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf), No. S.O. 39/H.A 16/1994/S. 87/2026, expressly places Municipal Corporation Hisar in category A2. It supersedes notification No. S.O. 85/H.A.16/1994/S.87/2013 dated 11 October 2013. The English text is in the Haryana Government Gazette Extraordinary, pages 291 to 296.

The new system applies from 1 August 2026. The notification allowed one month from that date to pay under the old notification. It preserves arrears and interest through financial year 2025-2026 and protects owners or occupiers who had already paid from an additional impact through financial year 2026-2027, as specified in paragraph 9. Historical 2013 area-based slabs must not be presented as the current general schedule.

| A2 property class | Annual floor rate on capital value | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residential | 0.0290% | Three times the floor rate |
| Non-residential, including special categories | 0.09% | Three times the floor rate |

Municipalities may set rates within these limits. Floor rates apply until the municipality determines its rates. No separate Hisar rate-setting resolution was verified for this record.

## Calculation, use factors and payment conditions

The notification defines property tax as capital value multiplied by the applicable rate and the usage-based multiplication factor. For properties other than apartments, flats and independent floors, capital value is plot area multiplied by the category-specific collector rate and floor factor. The floor factor is 1 for ground-floor-only construction, with 0.25 added for each additional built floor, including the basement. Flats and apartments use carpet area multiplied by collector rate. Independent floors use plot area multiplied by collector rate.

| Selected use category | Usage factor |
| --- | --- |
| Residential houses or independent floors on plots up to 50 square metres | 0.25 |
| Same, above 50 and up to 100 square metres | 0.50 |
| Same, above 100 and up to 200 square metres | 0.75 |
| Flats with carpet area up to 100 square metres | 0.75 |
| Other residential properties | 1.00 |
| Shops on plots up to 30 square metres | 0.25 |
| Shops above 30 and up to 50 square metres | 0.50 |
| Shops above 50 square metres | 1.00 |
| Industrial properties, including dairies and poultry, up to 500 square metres | 0.25 |
| All institutional properties | 0.25 |
| Vacant land, according to land type | 0.25 |

This is a selected table, not the full schedule. Mixed-use properties are assessed by area under each use. A rented portion attracts additional tax at 0.25 times the otherwise applicable rate. Paragraph 5 caps annual increases while the revised assessed amount is reached. For example, the cap is 1.25 times existing annual tax for houses up to 250 square metres and flats up to 100 square metres.

Late payment attracts simple interest of 1.5% per month or part of a month. Rebates require self-certification and full payment of dues, including arrears, interest and penalties. The notification also prescribes penalties for wrong declarations, unauthorized structures and unauthorized use. Paying tax does not regularize development.

## Property ID, corrections and NDC

The current [Property Tax Dues Payment and No Dues Certificate portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) replaces the old ulbhryndc.org address. The official [FAQ](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/FAQ) describes registration, property search, correction and payment.

1. Register or log in, select Hisar as municipality and search by PID, old PID, owner or occupier name, plot number, mobile number or map.
2. Open "View Details/Self Certify". Check property details, mobile details, owner details and dues. Dues can include property and fire tax, development charges and garbage collection charges.
3. Confirm correct sections. For incorrect sections, choose "No", use "Update Details" and supply supporting records. Saving a draft is not the final submission.
4. Complete "Final Preview and Submit Objection", then retain the application reference and check its status.
5. After correction and self-certification, use "Make Payment/Generate NDC", search the PID and select "Pay All Dues and Generate NDC". Retain the payment receipt and generated certificate.

The [official objection manual dated 8 July 2024](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Content/UserManuals/Verify_Property_Details_and_Submit_Objection_08.07.2024.pdf) states 10 working days with no fee for normal correction applications, and two working days with a fee of Rs 2,500 for Tatkaal applications. These are the manual's published service terms, not an assurance that a particular application has been approved.

The [27 March 2024 self-certification instructions](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2F27491324-64a7-4908-866b-cb09e5fe8c17.pdf) expressly state that property tax information is not ownership proof. NDC, title, colony authorization and building permission are separate matters.

## Haryana Building Code 2017 in Hisar

The [official government-hosted code](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s365d2ea03425887a717c435081cfc5dbb/uploads/2024/09/202409161779639739.pdf) states in clause 1.1 that the Haryana Building Code 2017 applies throughout Haryana from 6 January 2017. Hisar is within that statewide extent. The [ULB Town Planning Notifications index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/TownPlanningNotifications) separately lists adoption of amendments and Haryana Online Building Plan Approval System instructions.

Clause 2.1 requires an application to the competent authority for erection, re-erection, alteration or demolition, with the prescribed ownership documents, site and building plans, specifications, structural documents, relevant fire and public-health plans and fees. The cited edition states a non-refundable scrutiny fee of Rs 10 per square metre of covered area. Verify later fee amendments before payment.

The code defines "core area" to include thickly built-up old-town areas and Lal Dora or phirni of villages included in municipalities. Clause 6.1 links permitted use to the Development Plan, approved layout, sector or zoning plan. Clause 6.2 permits subdivision in core areas only with prior approval and independent public-road access. Lal Dora status is not a blanket exemption from building control.

Apply the code together with later amendments and the site's approved controls. In particular, the ULB portal lists an instruction published 29 July 2026 regarding keeping S+4 approvals on hold pending online approval and self-certification modalities. This record does not establish the present outcome of a particular Hisar building application or reproduce an unverified current height entitlement.

## Lal Dora tax relief and property certificates

The July 2026 tax notification contains two distinct provisions. Paragraph 6 grants exemption for residential properties within village Lal Dora for five years after inclusion within municipal limits, limited to a maximum of three floors, subject to government extension. Paragraph 7 grants a 75% property-tax rebate to residential properties within Lal Dora of existing villages falling in municipalities. Do not infer eligibility from a postal address. Check the municipal inclusion date, mapped Lal Dora boundary, residential use and applicable assessment period.

The [statewide property certificate policy](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2F9bb08af2-072a-4fff-a20f-a8e54dd3e36e.pdf), issued through instructions dated 12 March 2024 and listed on the tax index on 15 March 2024, addresses old Abadi Deh of former villages where revenue records of rights were not traditionally maintained. It excludes specific-khasra-number land from that stated scope.

The policy requires municipal publication of property and possession details, a 30-day claims and objections period, hearings and speaking orders. An appeal against the final list lies to the concerned ward committee within 30 days of publication. The policy provides for a speaking order within 15 days of the filed appeal, followed by a House or Administrator resolution and updates on the tax and NDC portal.

Claimants must provide a revenue-authority-attested affidavit and any two prescribed forms of evidence establishing possession for the last 10 years. Examples include electricity or water bills, government address documents, property tax receipts and proof of an existing structure. Available deeds and registered court decrees should also be supplied. A legal-heir certificate is required where the original owner has died.

Certificates remain subject to the Transfer of Property Act, 1882 and court decisions. No Hisar-specific final Lal Dora possession list, village-wide certification completion or individual certificate was verified in this research.

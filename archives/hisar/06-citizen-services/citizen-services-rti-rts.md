# Hisar citizen services, RTI and Right to Service

## Scope and source date

This guide covers municipality-wide services for Municipal Corporation Hisar. The record date is the update date printed on DULB's consolidated Right to Service list, 4 November 2025, not the launch date of these services. That list cites the notification of 16 August 2022 and amendments of 11 March 2025 and 27 October 2025, alongside a notification of 16 November 2022.

The portals and official published instructions establish service routes. No application, payment or authenticated service delivery was tested for this record. Check the selected service's current checklist, fee and jurisdiction before submission.

## Property tax, property ID and corrections

Use the [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for property tax and property data services. Select Hisar and identify the correct property before paying. Retain the Property ID, assessment details, transaction reference and payment receipt. Hisar's official service page separately lists [Property tax and Fire tax](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Hisar/2168?DynamicTypeIdView=4695).

The portal's [official document checklist](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments) distinguishes owner-name changes, death cases, area and category corrections, dues adjustments, new Property IDs and split Property IDs. For dues already paid but not adjusted, it asks for the payment receipt. A new Property ID requires identity and ownership evidence, a site plan showing location and a building photograph, with additional evidence for specified Lal Dora cases.

The checklist states there is no fee for name updating. Death cases have additional succession and notice requirements, including a 30-day notice in two national newspapers. Follow the full current checklist rather than treating a tax receipt as sufficient ownership evidence. A property tax register entry is not a substitute for a registered conveyance or a decision on disputed title.

## Birth, death and pet registration through SARAL

Use [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) to find the relevant service, submit the required enclosures and retain the application reference number. SARAL provides application or appeal tracking and certificate download controls.

| Requirement | Service route and jurisdiction |
| --- | --- |
| Birth, death or non-availability certificate | Search for “Application For Issuance of Birth / Death / Non Availability Certificate”. Select the registrar for the event and place of occurrence. |
| Correction, delayed registration or child's name inclusion | Select the separate service matching the request. A correction is not the same as a fresh certificate request. |
| Pet registration | Search for dog registration, renewal or a dog brought for show purpose. Choose the service for municipal corporations, not councils or committees. |

Hisar's [birth and death service listing](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Hisar/2172?DynamicTypeIdView=6791) confirms the local service category. Haryana Health Department's [civil registration guidance](https://haryanahealth.gov.in/service/civil-registration-system/) states that births and deaths must be reported within 21 days and registered at the place of occurrence. That reporting period is not a certificate-processing deadline.

The Health Department's SARAL instructions distinguish urban home events and private-hospital events, which use the municipality registrar, from government institutional events, which may use the relevant government institution registrar. Hisar residence alone does not determine the registrar. The verified municipal pet service is dog registration; this record does not establish a registration scheme for every species of pet.

## Marriage registration

Use [Haryana's marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) for marriage registration and application tracking. Hisar's official [Application for Marriage Registration listing](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Hisar/2172?DynamicTypeIdView=6793) identifies this municipal service.

DULB's [marriage registration guidance](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/WebCMS/Start/?DynamicTypeIdView=11676&Id=1449) states that registration in urban local bodies is carried out under the Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Act, 2008 and the Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Rules, 2008. Follow the portal's current jurisdiction, identity, marriage evidence, witness and appearance requirements. This record does not verify a current marriage fee or processing deadline.

## RTI Act, 2005

For access to existing municipal records, address the application to the State Public Information Officer, Municipal Corporation Hisar. DULB's [PIO list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList) names the Joint Commissioner as Hisar's State Public Information Officer and the Commissioner as first appellate authority. These are designation-based entries; this guide does not assign an unverified officer name.

Identify the records and period sought, such as a certified assessment order, complaint action-taken record, work estimate or payment entry. Under section 6 of the [Right to Information Act, 2005](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2065?locale=en), a citizen need not explain why the information is wanted. Include contact details and the prescribed Haryana fee or applicable exemption evidence. No current Haryana fee amount is asserted here.

| RTI step | Statutory period |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary decision on request, section 7 | Within 30 days of receipt, subject to statutory qualifications |
| Information concerning life or liberty | Within 48 hours |
| Transfer to another public authority, section 6 | As soon as practicable, no later than 5 days |
| Receipt through an Assistant PIO | 5 days added to the section 7 response calculation |
| Third-party procedure, section 11 | Decision within 40 days where that procedure applies |
| First appeal, section 19 | Within 30 days after the response period expires or the decision is received |
| First appeal disposal | Within 30 days, extendable to a total of 45 days with recorded reasons |
| Second appeal to State Information Commission, Haryana | Within 90 days of when the first appellate decision should have been made or was received |

Section 7 provides information free of charge when the authority fails to comply with the applicable section 7 time limit. Exemptions and statutory procedures still apply. RTI obtains records; it does not itself order tax correction, sanitation work or certificate approval. A service-delay complaint and an RTI request about its handling are separate processes.

## Grievances and Samadhan camps

Submit municipal complaints through the [DULB Grievance Redressal System](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/), the “eSAMADHAN DULB HARYANA” mobile app, or toll-free 1800-8900-929. The official portal gives telephone hours as 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday. Include the exact location, Property ID where relevant, earlier application or complaint references, and supporting receipts or photographs. Retain the complaint number.

The district administration's [municipal office directory](https://hisar.gov.in/public-utility/municipal-corporation-hisar/) lists Municipal Corporation Hisar at Balsamand Road, Hisar 125001, telephone 01662-237884 and email secymc.hisar@hry.nic.in. An email or visit should not be assumed to create a tracked grievance unless an acknowledgement is issued.

Samadhan Shivirs are in-person grievance hearings, separate from the eSAMADHAN app and from statutory RTI or RTS appeals. A [Haryana Government advisory dated 18 July 2024](http://csharyana.gov.in/WriteReadData/Circular-&-Instructions/Monitoring-and-Coordination/14626.pdf) directs district and subdivision camps to be held diligently, requires designated officers' presence and calls for prompt grievance handling.

For local historical context, [Amar Ujala reported on 26 October 2024](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/hisar/39-complaints-received-in-municipal-corporation-6-resolved-on-the-spot-hisar-news-c-21-hsr1005-492648-2024-10-26) that Hisar's municipal camp on 25 October ran from 9 AM to 11 AM and received 39 complaints, with six resolved on the spot. Subjects included property tax, sanitation, tehbazari, birth and death records, and technical-branch matters. Those hours describe that historical camp, not a verified current timetable. Confirm the next municipal or district camp with the office before travelling and bring previous acknowledgements and supporting records.

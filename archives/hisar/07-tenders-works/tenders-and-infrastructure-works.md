# Hisar tenders and infrastructure works, 2024 to 2026

## Procurement portals and evidence

The Haryana Engineering Works Portal states that tenders floated through HEWP are published on NIC's Haryana eTendering website for bidder participation. Its live-tender list displays them on the day after publication on NIC. The list includes the tender reference, eTender ID, department, submission deadline, estimated amount, earnest money deposit and links to the notice and bills of quantities. [S1]

For municipal contracts, search the organisation chain `Haryana Government > Urban Local Bodies > MC Hissar`. PHED, HSVP and PWD procure separate works in Hisar. Their projects should not be described as Municipal Corporation contracts merely because they serve the city.

This record uses official portal metadata and official airport-review minutes where available. Newspaper reports identify announced projects and statements by named officials. Commercial tender indexes are labelled as secondary discovery records. A tender notice does not establish an award, expenditure or completion. Historical deadlines below are not invitations to submit a current bid.

## Dhandoor solid-waste processing and collection

The Tribune reported on 20 February 2024 that door-to-door collection for wards 11 to 20 began on 19 February. Wards 1 to 10 were already covered. Municipal Commissioner Pradeep Dahiya stated that two processing plants near Dhandoor served all 20 wards. [S2]

| Item | Figure reported in February 2024 | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vehicles deployed for wards 11 to 20 | About 40 | Reported deployment |
| Contractor | Green Concrete Construction | Commissioner quoted in report |
| Contract period | One year | Reported contract term |
| Expected collection from these ten wards | 116 tonnes per day | Contract scope stated by commissioner, not measured plant throughput |
| Collection rate | Rs 1,840 per tonne | Reported contract rate |
| Annual spending | Approximately Rs 7.80 crore | Commissioner's estimate, not audited expenditure |

Separate procurement records identify the Satrod material recovery facility to Dhandoor processing-plant transport route. Sigma Tenders lists tender `2025_HRY_434559_1`, reference `MCH/Lfting of garbage`, for lifting garbage between those locations, with a March 2025 closing date. Its original-notice link is preserved in S3. A later secondary listing records `2025_HRY_453179_1`, published 17 June 2025, for transportation from Satrod MRF to the Dhandoor plant, with a 150-day work period. No award amount is adopted from that listing. [S3, S4]

## Water supply and sewerage

An official PHED tender, `2025_HRY_421650_1`, published on 2 January 2025, records new sewerline construction under D-Plan in Hisar town. The portal gives an estimated tender value of Rs 20,75,770, a 90-day work period and a submission deadline of 9 January 2025 at noon. The issuing office is Hisar PHED No. 3. The work-description text reads "Ward No 56 and 7". This appears ambiguous, so it is preserved rather than silently converted into a ward list. [S5]

ETV Bharat's report of the 6 January 2025 project launch attributes an estimated Rs 1,932.38 lakh to an AMRUT drinking-water pipeline project at Satrod Khurd in ward 11. This is a reported project estimate, not a verified paid bill or completion certificate. [S6]

For the later period, Amar Ujala reported on 28 October 2026 that PHED Executive Engineer Adarsh Singla told the municipal House that drinking-water pipelines worth Rs 151 crore would be laid, with an expected project duration of about one and a half years. The same meeting passed a proposal to send the state government a request for transfer of water-supply and sewerage work and funding to the corporation. Passage of that proposal does not establish that the transfer occurred. [S7]

## Stormwater drainage and roads

The Tribune's 18 July 2025 report describes the launch of an Urban Estate-2 stormwater scheme at a stated cost of Rs 9.48 crore. Mayor Praveen Popli said it was expected to finish within a year and would carry three years of contractor maintenance after completion. The same report assigns Rs 11.68 crore to proposed interlocking-paver-block roads in Tara Nagar and Jai Shri Shyam Vihar Colony. These are reported launch-stage costs, not certified expenditure. [S8]

There is a later qualification to the drainage timetable. Amar Ujala reported on 16 October 2026 that the Delhi Road section was awaiting PWD permission. The planned alignment ran through Dabda Chowk toward Jindal Chowk. Municipal Executive Engineer Amit Kaushik said two of the three zones were complete and five months remained in the project period. These are attributed progress statements, not independent completion certification. [S9]

A 6 March 2026 report of a commissioner-led coordination meeting records the following assigned works. [S10]

| Agency | Recorded work or direction |
| --- | --- |
| HSVP | Install a flowmeter at Airport Chowk stormwater disposal; connect its drain toward the PHED disposal at Barwala Chungi and provide a sluice gate |
| HSVP | Clean approximately 6 km of drainage from Barwala Chungi toward the buffalo research centre |
| PHED | Lay a 1,400 mm RCC pipeline from Lahoria Chowk toward the GJU drain in Auto Market |
| PHED | Increase Mini Secretariat stormwater-disposal capacity from 16 to 60 cusecs |
| Municipal Corporation | Prepare estimates after joint inspection for new stormwater lines on identified city routes and add pipes at South Bypass drain crossings |
| All participating departments | Complete drain cleaning by 30 May 2026 and gully-trap cleaning by 30 June 2026 |

These were meeting directions. The report does not prove that the deadlines were met.

## LED streetlights

Sigma Tenders indexes Municipal Corporation tender `2025_HRY_454375_1`, reference `2025E9FD4063 9442 4AF3 B170 69827CEC1167289ULB`, for decorative poles and LED lights on Southern Bypass Road from Aadhar Hospital to Bagla Mod. It records publication on 25 June 2025 and a closing date of 16 July 2025. The indexed original-notice link is included in S11. No installed-light count, energy-saving claim or completion date is established here. [S11]

Lighting procurement by HSVP is separate. Tender `2025_HBC_456087_1` concerns an LT line and 72-watt LED streetlights at convenient shops in Sector 4P-II, Hisar. Its indexed scope includes one year of maintenance and defect liability. This is a tender specification, not evidence of installation. [S12]

## Airport-corridor civic works and agency boundaries

Official minutes of the 5 August 2025 airport-development review distinguish the airport's water, drainage and access works from ordinary municipal street contracts. [S13]

| Civic interface | Recorded position on 5 August 2025 |
| --- | --- |
| Four-lane road from NH-9 to NH-52 via Mirzapur | An 8 km proposal transferred to NHAI; PWD confirmed selection of Option 2 and NHAI was to submit the case to the Chief Minister's office |
| Airport raw-water arrangement | Irrigation Department deposit-work tender recalled |
| Phase-II drinking-water arrangement for IMC and airport | PHED DNIT approved; tender to be floated |
| Operational-area waterlogging | Earlier unlined drain had collapsed in rainfall; a permanent drain was proposed up to the boundary by PHED and beyond it by Irrigation |
| Fresh-water pipeline | PHED reported difficulty obtaining airside access; the chair directed AAI to allow pipeline installation through the airside |

The minutes identify interdepartmental delays. They do not certify completion of the access road, raw-water scheme or permanent drainage. Airport runway, terminal and aviation-lighting expenditure is not treated here as municipal infrastructure spending.

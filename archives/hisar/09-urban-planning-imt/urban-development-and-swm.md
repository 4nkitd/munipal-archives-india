# Hisar airport, manufacturing cluster, development plans and urban sanitation

## Maharaja Agrasen Airport and the aviation hub

The Prime Minister's Office records the laying of the foundation stone for a new terminal at Maharaja Agrasen Airport, Hisar, on 14 April 2025. The announced project cost was over Rs 410 crore, covering a passenger terminal, cargo terminal and air traffic control building. The same release records the start of the Hisar to Ayodhya connection. Other services were announced in that release, but this archive does not treat a 2025 announcement as proof of the operating airline timetable in November 2026.

The new terminal foundation ceremony is distinct from completion of that terminal, the wider Integrated Aviation Hub and the adjoining manufacturing cluster. NICDC's project descriptions place the IMC beside the airport at the intersection of NH-9 and NH-52. References to an international aviation hub describe the development concept and do not establish that scheduled international passenger services are operating.

Source: [PMO release, 14 April 2025](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pm-lays-foundation-stone-of-new-terminal-building-of-hisar-airport-worth-over-rs-410-crore/).

| Road access | Adjacent to NH-9 and NH-52 |
| Focus sectors on NICDC project page | Aerospace and defence including maintenance, repair and overhaul; engineering and fabrication; food processing; readymade garments |

Investment and employment figures are projections, not realised investment or jobs. The PIB release of 20 August 2025 identifies the State Support Agreement and Shareholder Agreement parties as NICDIT, the Government of Haryana and Haryana Airports Development Corporation, HADC. It identifies the signatories as NICDC CEO and MD Rajat Kumar Saini, Civil Aviation Commissioner and Secretary Amneet P. Kumar, and HADC MD Narhari Singh Banger.

Although the research topic is sometimes described as NICDC/HSIIDC development, these primary project agreements specifically name HADC. No reviewed primary source establishes HSIIDC as the Hisar IMC agreement signatory or current plot-allotment authority. Do not infer an HSIIDC allotment route from its role in other Haryana industrial estates.

NICDC's online Delivery Monitoring Unit report records central approval on 28 August 2024, execution of the agreements on 20 August 2025 and incorporation of "NICDC Haryana Integrated Manufacturing Cluster Hisar Project Limited" on 25 September 2025. It further records a PMNC letter of award to M/s Assystem India on 2 June 2026, environmental clearance on 14 July 2026, technical bid opening on 17 August 2026 and financial bid opening on 31 August 2026. The report says EPC award issuance was underway and L-1 credential verification was in process. Its URL is a replaceable report endpoint, so these statements are a retrieval-time record, not a claim of the status after that report.

## Infrastructure procurement and planning horizons

The May 2026 RFQ-cum-RFP, reference `NICDC/IMCSPV/EPC/T06/2025`, names the project SPV as procuring authority. The notice is dated 24 May 2026. It covers design, construction, testing, commissioning and five years of operation and maintenance of trunk infrastructure on an EPC basis.

The scope includes roads, stormwater drains, culverts, water supply, sewerage, a water treatment plant, sewage treatment plant, common effluent treatment plant, power systems and utility ducts, reuse-water lines and avenue plantation. These are procurement requirements, not evidence that the facilities are commissioned.

An older NICDC description refers to first-phase development in 2027, an airport freight rail terminal by 2032 and a final phase in 2037. These are planning horizons. NICDC's newer project page displays a 1,605-acre land-use table alongside the overall 2,988-acre project area. It should not be read as a complete land-use schedule for all 2,988 acres. The published table also contains arithmetic inconsistencies, so this archive does not reproduce it as an approved cadastral allocation.

## Hisar development plans, 2021, 2031 and 2041

The district government's verified document register lists **Development Plan Controlled Area 2021 AD Hisar**, dated 1 March 2018, with an 839 KB map PDF. The date is the portal's document date, not independently verified here as the original gazette notification date.

No authenticated Hisar-city development-plan notification with perspective year 2031 was located in the reviewed official sources. Plans for other towns in Hisar district must not be labelled as Hisar city's plan. The IMC master plan and its 2027-2037 implementation horizons are also separate from the city's statutory development plan.

A 31 January 2026 Naya Haryana report describes a proposed Hisar Master Plan 2041, a controlled area of 38,721.59 hectares, residential expansion toward Tosham Road and remaining objections. It refers to a 21 January 2026 departmental letter identifying 11 errors. This is secondary reporting of a plan under preparation, not a verified final gazette. No final 2041 notification number, authenticated final map or statutory commencement date was established in this research.

For a land transaction or building application, obtain the current gazette, zoning regulations, controlled-area notifications and parcel-specific status from the competent planning authority. A news map or airport project announcement does not establish development permission.

Official map index: [district development-plan register](https://hisar.gov.in/document-category/development-plan-controlled-area-2021-ad-hisar/). Linked map: [2018022826.pdf](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3e56954b4f6347e897f954495eab16a88/uploads/2018/02/2018022826.pdf). Map geometry and individual sector boundaries were not transcribed.

## Urban sanitation and solid-waste document register

| Record | Verified portal metadata | Archival qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft bylaws under Solid Waste Management Rules 2016 | District document page dated 31 January 2024; linked PDF 402 KB | The portal expressly calls this a draft. This record does not present its charges or penalties as an enacted local schedule. |
| Municipal toilet and urinal cleaning and maintenance terms | Hindi district document index lists notices dated 15 January and 31 January 2025, each with a 2 MB attachment | The titles invite registered market associations, RWAs, NGOs and religious, social and cultural institutions. Contract terms and payments require the attachment and award record. |
| Solid Waste Management departmental page | Last updated 17 July 2026 | Links a document labelled "GAZETTE Notification dated 27th January 2026", an "Agenda and HRMS Report", activity photographs and videos. |

The linked 2026 gazette's substantive provisions were not extracted for this record. Its presence does not by itself establish which provisions replaced the 2024 local draft, current user charges or locally applicable penalty amounts. The exact text should be checked before citing a duty or issuing a demand.

The 5 November 2026 Dainik Bhaskar report describes a meeting chaired by Commissioner C. Jayashraddha with RWAs, NGOs and park committees. It reports a proposed initial selection of 50 to 100 homes for wet/dry segregation, composting of suitable waste in designated park or green-belt locations, anti-dumping work, single-use-plastic awareness and reuse of clothing. These are reported action-plan measures, not verified citywide segregation or processing performance.

The reviewed sources do not establish current waste generation tonnage, processing capacity, collection coverage, landfill compliance or a Swachh Survekshan rank. Sewerage treatment and industrial effluent treatment are separate from municipal solid-waste collection; the IMC's proposed STP and CETP must not be counted as operating city sanitation assets.

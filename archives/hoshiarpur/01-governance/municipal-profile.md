# Hoshiarpur municipal jurisdiction and leadership

The [corporation’s city profile](https://mchoshiarpur.punjab.gov.in/Home) gives a 2011 population of 168,653, including 88,304 men and 80,349 women, across 36,627 households. The figures describe Hoshiarpur city, the district headquarters, in that census year.

The municipal leadership page identifies Parveen Saini as mayor and Preetinder Singh Bains, PCS, as commissioner. The Digital-MC House directory labels the house “Third” and lists 50 councillors.

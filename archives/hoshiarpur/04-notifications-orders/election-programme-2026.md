# Hoshiarpur corporation election programme, June–July 2026

The [Punjab Gazette Extraordinary, pp. 2893–94](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/election_22.6.2026.pdf), notification SEC/ME/SAM (CME)/2026/1031, names Hoshiarpur as the municipal corporation in a programme that also covered three other local bodies. Nominations ran from 22 to 25 June; scrutiny was set for 26 June and withdrawal closed at 3 p.m. on 27 June.

The Commission fixed polling, if required, for 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. on 5 July. It directed that EVM votes be counted at the polling booth immediately after the poll, and set 7 July as the deadline for completing the election process. The notification gives the timetable, not the results.

# Hosur Corporation and the urban local bodies of Krishnagiri district

## District register and office address

The [district local bodies register](https://krishnagiri.nic.in/about-district/administrative-setup/local-bodies/) lists Hosur Corporation separately from Krishnagiri Municipality. It also identifies six separate town panchayats: Uthangarai, Kaveripattinam, Kelamangalam, Bargur, Denkanikottai and Nagojanahalli.

For enquiries to Hosur itself, the [district office directory](https://krishnagiri.nic.in/public-utility/hosur-municipality/) gives the corporation's address as Bagalur Road, opposite Thillai Nagar, Hosur 635109, telephone 04344-242241 and email commr.hosur@tn.gov.in.

# District inspection of Hosur bridge and access road works

## Bathalapalli market and connecting roads

The Collector inspected the flyover works near Bathalapalli market on 20 May 2026. The inspection covered the service road, drainage and access for traffic during construction. The [district's release 34](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s37eacb532570ff6858afd2723755ff790/uploads/2026/05/17792757137685.pdf) describes two stretches of new tar road, approximately 620 metres and 350 metres, costing ₹49.94 lakh together. That figure concerns the connecting roads, not the flyover budget.

The [district inspection announcement](https://krishnagiri.nic.in/hosur-corporation-inspection-3/) also covers the railway overbridge works and proposed relocation of the sub-registrar's office.

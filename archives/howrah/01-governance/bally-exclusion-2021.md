# Exclusion of Bally areas from Howrah Municipal Corporation in 2021

## Inclusion and exclusion

The state included areas of the former Bally Municipality in Howrah Municipal Corporation by Notification 428/MA/O/C-4/1M-36/2014 dated 26 June 2015. The [11 February 2021 preliminary gazette](https://udma.wb.gov.in/uploads/circular_pdf/SAR_73_Department_of_Urban_Development_Municipal_Affairs_No__222_MA.pdf) recites that inclusion and records the state's intention to exclude the areas.

After the objection period and Assembly approval, Notification 1445/UDMA-15011(19)/1/2021-LS-MA SEC of 12 November 2021 excluded the scheduled land under section 219(3) of the Howrah Municipal Corporation Act, 1980. [Notification 1446 of the same date](https://udma.wb.gov.in/uploads/circular_pdf/1446_udma_12112021.pdf) constituted that land as Bally Municipal Area under section 6 of the West Bengal Municipal Act, 1993. [Notification 1449](https://udma.wb.gov.in/uploads/circular_pdf/1449_udma_12112021.pdf) separately set Bally's ward count at 35. The [state directory](https://udma.wb.gov.in/municipal/modal-content/122) lists Bally as a separate municipality. These 2021 boundary notifications are distinct from later amendments to Howrah's councillor count.

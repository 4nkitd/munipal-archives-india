# Howrah ward delimitation gazette, September 2026

## Statutory schedule

Notification 1727/UDMA-15011(22)/57/2026-LS-MA SEC was published in the Kolkata Gazette, Extraordinary, Part I, No. WB(Part-I)/2026/SAR-575, on 11 September 2026. The notice cites West Bengal Act XV of 2026 for the increase from 50 to 68 wards. Under section 219(1) of the Howrah Municipal Corporation Act, 1980, it substitutes Schedule II, the written boundaries of the corporation's wards.

| Ward | Selected boundary references in the gazette |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Girish Ghosh Bye Lane, Joya Bibi Road and Lane, an imaginary line through New Central Jute Mill, the river up to Bandha Ghat ferry service, Kissen Lal Burman Road and S.N. Temple Road |
| 67 | The river, Lower Foreshore Road, the southern boundary of Botanical Garden Road, College Road and Shalimar Road |
| 68 | An imaginary line from Andul Road along the Podra Gram Panchayat boundary to the river, then the river and Satyen Bose Road back to Andul Road |

The gazette contains the complete boundary walks. The table is a finding aid, not a substitute for them.

## Earlier ward references

The [8 December 2022 Schedule II](https://udma.wb.gov.in/uploads/circular_pdf/1172_udma_sar-1287.pdf), [state corporation directory](https://udma.wb.gov.in/modal-content/6), and [municipal borough office directory](https://www.myhmc.in/boroughs/) describe the earlier 50-ward arrangement. The [corporation's merger-era profile](https://www.myhmc.in/about-hmc/) and [Know Your Ward selector](https://www.myhmc.in/know-your-ward/) still show 66. Those entries do not establish boundaries or office assignments for the 2026 ward numbers. The 2026 gazette establishes the published Schedule II edition, not a poll date or a list of elected councillors.

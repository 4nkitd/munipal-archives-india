# Howrah municipal complaints and citizen care

## Submit or track a complaint

The corporation links to [online complaint submission](https://www.myhmc.in/grs/) and a separate [complaint status page](https://www.myhmc.in/grs/viewgrsticket.php). Its contact page lists 1800-121-500000 for the Grievance Redressal Cell during office hours and `citizen.care@myhmc.in` for citizen care.

## Head office

The head office is at 4, Mahatma Gandhi Road, Howrah 711101. The corporation lists telephone numbers 033-26383211, 033-26383212 and 033-26383213. Posted hours are 10:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday through Saturday, with the second and fourth Saturdays closed. The [Howrah district directory](https://howrah.gov.in/public-utility/howrah-municipal-corporation/) also lists the address and main telephone number.

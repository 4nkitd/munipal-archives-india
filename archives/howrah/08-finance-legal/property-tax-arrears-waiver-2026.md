# Howrah property tax arrears waiver scheme, 2026

## Bills and amounts covered

Notification 1831/UDMA-15011(24)/7/2026-LS-MA SEC, dated 30 September 2026, introduces the Howrah Municipal Corporation Incentive (Waiver of Interest & Penalty to the Property Tax Payers) Scheme 2026. It applies within Howrah Municipal Corporation to outstanding property tax bills presented through 31 March 2026 under section 117 of the Howrah Municipal Corporation Act, 1980. Residential, commercial and mixed-use premises are covered.

The scheme waives 100% of qualifying interest and penalty. It also provides a 10% waiver on principal property tax for residential buildings, inclusive of Howrah Bridge Tax and surcharge. To qualify, the ratepayer must pay the full amount due after applicable waivers within the scheme period. Part payment during that period does not qualify. Payments made before commencement, including unadjusted ad hoc payments, are handled under existing provisions rather than included in the scheme.

## Applications

Apply to the Commissioner by the deadline notified by the corporation, within the scheme's period of validity. Give the liable person's name, premises number and assessee number. The notification says the Commissioner ordinarily makes the statement of outstanding and payable amounts available within 30 days of application. The scheme runs through 31 March 2027; this end date does not replace any application deadline the Commissioner publishes. The [Howrah Municipal Corporation home page](https://www.myhmc.in/) links to the waiver portal.

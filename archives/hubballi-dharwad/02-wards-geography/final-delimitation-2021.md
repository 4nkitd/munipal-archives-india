# Hubballi-Dharwad final delimitation of 82 wards

## Draft and final dates

The final notification, No. NAE 31 MLR 2021, is dated 3 April 2021. Karnataka published it on 5 April 2021 in Extraordinary Gazette Part III, No. 398. It cites sections 7(2)(a) and 21 of the Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976 and uses 2011 Census figures. The [corporation's gazette register](http://www.hdmc.mrc.gov.in/en/gazette-notification) lists 24 February 2021, the draft notification's date. The final text identifies the draft as published in Gazette No. 209 on 25 February 2021, with a 15-day period for comments or objections.

## Reading the schedule

The final notification specifies 82 wards. Its Kannada annex starts on page 2 and ends with wards 80, 81 and 82 on page 44. Column 3 describes the areas included and column 4 gives ward boundaries. Use those columns together when checking an address. This archive does not transcribe the boundary text.

The [property payment homepage](https://hdmc.in/WebsiteHDMC/Home.aspx) still describes 67 wards or blocks. That older figure is not the 2021 delimitation count, and the notification does not establish which numbers a property database uses for older assessments.

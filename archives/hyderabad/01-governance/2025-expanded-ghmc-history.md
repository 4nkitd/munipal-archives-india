# Historical GHMC jurisdiction: December 2025 territorial extension and merger

## Which GHMC the merger refers to

Telangana Gazette Part I Extraordinary No. 209-G, published 3 December 2025, prints G.O.Ms.No. 264 under the name of K. Ramakrishna Rao, Chief Secretary to Government and Secretary to Government (MA&UD). Under section 3(1) of the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955, it extends the existing corporation's limits to the entire City of the Telangana Core Urban Region as defined under section 3(3B). The order says the extension is deemed in force from **2 December 2025**, one day before publication.

The [8 December gazette, Rules Supplement No. 24](https://tggazette.cgg.gov.in/viewDocument/1769835086550), identifies the merger as **20 municipalities and seven municipal corporations** within the Telangana Core Urban Region. It prints G.O.Ms.No. 267, which amends the ward delimitation rules. It is not the order extending the territory. Neither gazette is a name-by-name list of the absorbed bodies.

[GHMC's history](https://www.ghmc.gov.in/CorporationHistory.aspx) confirms the merger and cites G.O.Ms.No. 292 of 24 December 2025 for the ensuing reorganisation. The [February order](2026-reorganisation-order.md) confirms No. 292 approved 12 zones and 60 circles.

The former expanded GHMC and its absorbed urban local bodies are historical jurisdictions. The surviving name, Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation, applies to the six-zone successor jurisdiction as of 11 February 2026. The surviving GHMC service menu for the "27 erstwhile ULBs" does not make those bodies independent authorities.

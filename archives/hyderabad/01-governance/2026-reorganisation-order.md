# G.O.Ms.No. 55: Reorganisation of the former Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation

## What took effect

The order took immediate effect on 11 February 2026 under section 3(1) of the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955 (Act II of 1956). It names three separate bodies corporate. [Telangana Gazette Part I Extraordinary No. 34](https://tggazette.cgg.gov.in/viewDocument/1771840080633) prints the notification on that date.

| Corporation | Zones named in clause 3 |
|---|---|
| Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation | Shamshabad, Rajendernagar, Charminar, Golconda, Khairatabad and Secunderabad |
| Cyberabad Municipal Corporation | Serilingampally, Kukatpally and Qutbullapur (spelling in the order; the corporation uses Quthbullapur) |
| Malkajgiri Municipal Corporation | Malkajgiri, Uppal and L.B. Nagar |

The order cites G.O.Ms.No. 264 dated 3 December 2025 and Nos. 291 and 292 dated 24 December 2025. It states that No. 292 approved the former GHMC's twelve-zone, sixty-circle arrangement and uses those zones to define the three corporations. The former expanded GHMC is historical. The present GHMC is the continuation in the six remaining zones, not a fourth corporation.

## Retrieving the order

On Telangana's Government Orders Issue Register, choose Municipal Administration and Urban Development, type MS, year 2026 and number 55, then use Download. The retrieved copy carries the printed authorization in the Governor's name naming Jayesh Ranjan, Special Chief Secretary to Government. It does not contain an embedded digital signature.

The Gazette issue gives the same designations, zones, immediate effect and issuing officer as the register copy, but its heading prints `G.O.Ms.No.00`. The register entry and downloaded order give `55`, which is also the number cited by the corporation.

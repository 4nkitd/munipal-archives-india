# Current GHMC ward count and zone map

## Current jurisdiction

GHMC reports 6 zones, 30 circles and 150 wards for the reconstituted corporation. G.O.Ms.No. 55 lists Shamshabad, Rajendernagar, Charminar, Golconda, Khairatabad and Secunderabad as its zones.

The homepage also links a December 2025 delimitation notification for **300 wards**. That number belongs to the historical expanded GHMC before the February split, not to current GHMC. The current total does not imply that GHMC's wards run from 1 through 150. The [municipal zone and circle map](https://www.ghmc.gov.in/Documents/NEW%20ZONES,CIRCLE%20MAP.jpg) is an orientation aid, not a substituted legal boundary schedule.

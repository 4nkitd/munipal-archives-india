# GHMC online property tax payment

## Finding the account

Use the [public property tax payment portal](https://onlinepayments.ghmc.gov.in/) with the 10-digit Property Tax Identification Number (PTIN) shown on the assessment, or the registered mobile number. If the portal reports that no mobile number is registered for the PTIN, it directs taxpayers to the [mobile registration and update page](https://onlinepayments.ghmc.gov.in/PTax/PT_MobileUpdate).

The current GHMC homepage links the payment site in both its main property tax menu and its "27 erstwhile ULBs" menu. Cyberabad and Malkajgiri also link it. The hostname does not determine which corporation has jurisdiction over a property.

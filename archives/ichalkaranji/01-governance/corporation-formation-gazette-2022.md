# Gazette notifications constituting Ichalkaranji Municipal Corporation

## Final notifications and effective date

The Maharashtra Urban Development Department issued three notifications on 29 June 2022. The Maharashtra Government Gazette published them on 30 June 2022 in Part One-A, Central Subdivision, Extraordinary No. 72, Year 8, Issue 42.

| Notification | English page | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| MNC-2022/C.R.No.96(2)/UD-18 | 3 | Declares that the whole local area of the Ichalkaranji Municipal Council in Kolhapur district ceases to be a municipal area under section 6(1)(d) of the Maharashtra Municipal Councils, Nagar Panchayats and Industrial Townships Act, 1965. |
| MNC-2022/C.R.No.96(1)/UD-18 | 5 | Specifies the same area as a larger urban area and constitutes the Municipal Corporation of City of Ichalkaranji under sections 3(2) and 3(2A) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. |
| MNC-2022/C.R.No.96(3)/UD-18 | 7 | States that the larger-urban-area designation took effect on 29 June 2022. It also appoints a government officer from that date to exercise corporation powers and duties under section 452A(1). |

The schedule specifies the whole local area of the former council, not the whole of Hatkanangale taluka or Kolhapur district. The 5 May 2022 notification and proclamation were the proposal stage. The draft Gazette issue, Extraordinary No. 43 dated 6 May 2022, invited objections and said the government would consider the draft on or after 5 June 2022.

## Finding the original issue

On the Maharashtra Gazette search page, choose Central Section, Part -1 A, and Extra - Ordinary. Search 29 June to 2 July 2022 and select **View** for Extraordinary No. 72, dated 30 June 2022. The official site serves the PDF through the search form rather than a separate public document link.

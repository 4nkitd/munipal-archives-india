# Polling and counting dates for the 2025-26 corporation election

## Election timetable

The State Election Commission's press release dated 14 January 2026 lists Ichalkaranji by name among the 29 municipal corporations in the general election. The commission stated that the programme was announced on 15 December 2025.

| Event | Date and local time |
| --- | --- |
| State Election Commission announces the programme | 15 December 2025 |
| Polling | 15 January 2026, 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. |
| Counting starts | 16 January 2026, 10:00 a.m. |

The corporation's [General Election 2025 page](https://ichalkaranjimnp.in/general-election-2025/) covers the same 2025-26 cycle. It links to the local election programme, polling-station list, final ward notification and final result. Its menu title is not the year in which polling took place.

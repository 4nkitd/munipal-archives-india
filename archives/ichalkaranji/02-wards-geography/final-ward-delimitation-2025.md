# Final 16-ward delimitation for the Ichalkaranji corporation election

## What the notification sets

Appendix 15, signed 9 October 2025, fixes ward numbers and boundaries under section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949. The notification cites draft notification no. 12 of 4 September 2025 and says the final delimitation applies to the next general election.

| Wards | Councillors per ward | Total councillors |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 to 15 | 4 | 60 |
| 16 | 5 | 5 |
| Total | | 65 |

## Which boundary source to use

The schedule describes each ward by its northern, eastern, southern and western boundaries. Its closing note says that if the Marathi and English descriptions differ, the boundary shown on the map is valid. The corporation makes the map available for inspection at its office during office hours. Its [election index](https://ichalkaranjimnp.in/general-election-2025/) also links to the Marathi schedule, the consolidated city map and maps for Wards 1 to 16. These are electoral wards, not a statement about service zones or an extension of the corporation's outer limits.

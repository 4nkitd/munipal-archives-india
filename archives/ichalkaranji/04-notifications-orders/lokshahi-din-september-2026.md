# Public notice for Lokshahi Din on 7 September 2026

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

## September session

The Deputy Municipal Commissioner's notice announced Lokshahi Din for Monday, 7 September 2026, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. in the municipal corporation hall. The notice, dated 1 September, cites instructions in a Maharashtra General Administration Department circular dated 26 September 2012. The 7 September date on the homepage is the session date, not the date of the notice. Consult the homepage's Public Notice section for a later session; this record covers only the September 2026 notice.

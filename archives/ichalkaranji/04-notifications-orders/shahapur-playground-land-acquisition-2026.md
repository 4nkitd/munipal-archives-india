# Shahapur playground land acquisition notice LAQ/SPE/500

## Parcel and publication

The Kolhapur district portal lists this notice under reference LAQ/SPE/500 for reservation IV/30, "Playground," in the approved Ichalkaranji development plan. The property is in Mouje Shahapur, Survey No. 19524 (part), Gut No. 391 (part). The notice describes an area of approximately 2,132 square metres.

The district page gives a posting window of 1 to 30 April 2026. It refers to section 126(4) of the Maharashtra Regional and Town Planning Act, 1966, section 19 of the 2013 land acquisition Act, and the declaration notice under section 21(1) and (2). The district's [linked notice PDF](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s33d2d8ccb37df977cb6d9da15b76c3f3a/uploads/2026/04/17751297587197.pdf) is available through the official notice page. The posting window has ended; this record does not establish that acquisition was completed.

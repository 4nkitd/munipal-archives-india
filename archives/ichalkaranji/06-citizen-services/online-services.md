# Online tax, certificate and complaint services in Ichalkaranji

## Where to start

| Task | Route linked by the corporation |
| --- | --- |
| Look up a property tax account | [Property Tax search](https://onlinenagarkaryavali.com/PropSearch.aspx?@=D3D5CA9A85AD7583070F12209B586A7183874422FE771E7E304D4E983003598F) |
| Pay property tax | [Online Property Tax Payment](https://propertytax.nagarkaryavali.com/?@=D3D5CA9A85AD7583070F12209B586A7183874422FE771E7E304D4E983003598F) |
| Water tax | [Water Tax portal](https://watertax.nagarkaryavali.com/?@=D3D5CA9A85AD7583070F12209B586A7183874422FE771E7E304D4E983003598F) |
| No-dues certificate, assessment certificate or application status | [Municipal RTS login](https://nagarkaryavali.com/ANCL_RTS/App/frmLoginUser.aspx?@=18B5C57B27BF5AC0B0F59B48F0EB2CF0) |
| Register a complaint | [Corporation complaint page](https://ichalkaranjimnp.in/complaint-registration-link/), which links to municipal CRM and the state Aaple Sarkar portal |
| Check which services are notified under the Right to Public Services Act | [RTS Services](https://ichalkaranjimnp.in/rts-new-page/), which links to the Act, the rules Gazette and the notified-services list |

The [municipal contact page](https://ichalkaranjimnp.in/contact-us-ichalkaranji/) lists the office on Station Road, beside Guru Talkies, Vikas Nagar, Ichalkaranji 416115. It also lists toll-free number `1800-233-1217`. The [Kolhapur district directory](https://kolhapur.gov.in/en/public-utility/ichalkaranji/) confirms the main landline, office email and municipal website. The links above were identified from the public pages; no payment, complaint or application was submitted.

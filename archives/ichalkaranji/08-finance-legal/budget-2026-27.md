## Annual estimates and revised estimates

The cover identifies the volume as the revised budget for 2025-26 and the annual budget for 2026-27. PDF page 2 gives the 2026-27 summary. Both sides total Rs 9,999,999,999, but these are budget totals, not audited receipts and expenditure.

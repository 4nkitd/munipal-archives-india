# Imphal and the state urban administration

## State and city responsibilities

The Manipur Municipalities Act, 1994 provides the framework for urban local government. MAHUD supervises the corporation, municipal councils, nagar panchayats and small-town committees. Its [urban local body list](https://mahud.mn.gov.in/ulb/) distinguishes Imphal Municipal Corporation from Mayang Imphal Municipal Council.

MAHUD coordinates housing, sanitation and infrastructure schemes. The corporation is one implementing body; the Planning and Development Authorities, Manipur Urban Development Agency, Public Works Department and Public Health Engineering Department also carry out urban projects. A project in Imphal is therefore not necessarily a corporation work.

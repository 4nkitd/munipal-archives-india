# Imphal administrative wards and municipal services

## Ward coverage and office

IMC reports 27 active administrative wards. Its public description gives the total without naming the wards or defining their boundaries.

## Municipal services

The corporation lists sanitation and public health among its duties. Its trade licence service covers applications and renewals; its property tax service covers viewing and paying tax. The municipal office is on Kangla Pat Road, Imphal 795001. Posted office hours are Monday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 4:00 pm.

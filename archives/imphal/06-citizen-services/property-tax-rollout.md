# Imphal and the state property tax rollout

## Which tax service covers Imphal?

The Manipur Municipality Property Tax Board launched its online service on 24 April 2025 for 26 municipal councils. MAHUD expressly excludes Imphal Municipal Corporation from that rollout. The state site's Services menu lists **Imphal Municipal Property Tax** separately from **Manipur Municipality Property Tax Board**.

The board describes online registration, self-assessment, payment, amalgamation, separation and mutation for its council platform. Those features describe the 26-council service; they do not establish the services available on Imphal's separate system.

# Imphal Smart City projects in the 2023-24 administrative report

## Funding and progress

On pages 3 and 4, MAHUD reports a revised Imphal Smart City project cost of ₹550 crore, down from ₹1,053 crore. The report gives a Centre to state funding ratio of 90:10 for the northeast, with a ₹500 crore ceiling on the central share. These are mission figures, not the corporation's annual budget.

| Status in the report | Projects | Value (₹ crore) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Completed | 14 | 73.76 |
| Nearing completion | 5 | 313.58 |
| In various stages of work | 5 | 126.04 |

The report dates the inauguration of seven command-centre modules, the first phase of the western riverfront and the Thangal Bazar mall road to 4 January 2022. Nongpok Thong at Kangla was inaugurated on 6 January 2023.

# Grounded AMRUT projects in Indore as of 2 March 2026

## State-reported projects

Serial 193 of the ministry's city-wise AMRUT return gives the following counts and costs for Indore, Madhya Pradesh. The reporting cutoff printed on the schedule is 2 March 2026.

| Sector | Grounded projects | Cost (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Water supply | 3 | 613.89 |
| Sewerage and septage management | 2 | 300.93 |
| Parks | 4 | 25 |
| Drainage | Not listed | Not listed |
| Urban transport | 2 | 73.76 |

## Reading the figures

Adding the populated sector entries gives 11 projects and ₹1013.58 crore. The schedule reports project cost, not money disbursed or expenditure in a financial year. It gives no contract numbers, completion dates or ward allocations. “Not listed” represents an empty pair of cells in the city row.

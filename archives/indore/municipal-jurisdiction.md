# Indore Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and office

## Which local body to contact

The district lists eight Nagar Parishads alongside the corporation: Betma, Depalpur, Goutampura, Hatod, Manpur, Mhowgaon, Rau and Sanwer. For example, the Rau entry has PIN 453331 and its own office mailbox, cmorao@mpurban.gov.in.

## Corporation office

| Directory field | District entry |
|---|---|
| Office | Indore Municipal Corporation |
| Postal address | Jail Road, Nagar Nigam, Netaji Subash Marg, Indore 452007 |
| Office email | nn.indore@mpurban.gov.in |

For a municipal record search, the [City Finance local-body register](https://www.cityfinance.in/api/v1/ulb) identifies this corporation by LGD code `250901` and Census town code `802273`. These identify the body across datasets; neither code identifies a particular ward or parcel.

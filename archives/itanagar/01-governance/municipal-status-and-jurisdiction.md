# Itanagar Municipal Corporation status and jurisdiction

## From municipal council to corporation

The corporation's [About Us page](https://www.imc.arunachal.gov.in/aboutus.html) says the Itanagar Municipal Council was inaugurated on 14 August 2013 with 30 wards and records the corporation upgrade and 20-ward reorganization in 2020. The final ward gazette cites the underlying declaration, Notification No. `DTP/MUN-COR-01/2018-19` dated 15 October 2019. Under section 3(1) and (2) of the Arunachal Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 2019, that declaration made the area within the limits of the former council the Itanagar Municipal Corporation. The October 2019 declaration and the March 2020 publication of the final ward determination are separate events.

## Municipal area and district administration

The gazetted schedule covers localities in Itanagar, Naharlagun, Nirjuli, Karsingsa and Banderdewa. The [Itanagar Capital Complex district administration](https://itanagar.nic.in/about-district/) separately describes the Deputy Commissioner (Capital)'s jurisdiction as including Itanagar, Naharlagun and Banderdewa. That description is not the gazetted municipal boundary or a ward map. Use the [ward determination](../02-wards-geography/final-ward-delimitation-2020.md) for address-level boundary questions.

The [district's municipal-office listing](https://itanagar.nic.in/public-utility/itanagar-muncipal-council/) gives Kime Pakka, opposite Energy Park, Itanagar 791111. For enquiries, use the email and Help Desk number on the corporation's [contact page](https://www.imc.arunachal.gov.in/contactus.html).

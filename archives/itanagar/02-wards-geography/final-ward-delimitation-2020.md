# Final delimitation of Itanagar's 20 municipal wards

## Gazette and schedules

Notification No. `DULB/MUN-01/2019-20/5367-78`, signed on 21 February 2020, was published in the Arunachal Pradesh Extraordinary Gazette No. 77 on 3 March 2020. It follows the proposed-ward notification of 19 November 2019, which allowed 30 days for claims and objections. The final notification says the Deputy Commissioner, Itanagar Capital Complex, considered and disposed of nine claims or objections, as reported in letter No. `DC/ICC/ELN-07/2019` of 17 February 2020.

Schedule I lists localities and population. Schedule II gives each ward's north, east, south and west boundaries and its area in square kilometres. For example, Schedule II gives Ward 1 an area of 2.84 sq km. Its description uses the Itanagar–Poma Road junction, Zoo Road, the Senki and Chimpu rivers, and the Dokoso Nallah. Use the full directional description for a property or street on a ward edge.

# Itanagar birth and death registration portal notice

## Where to apply

The public notice in the Birth and Death service menus on the [IMC website](https://www.imc.arunachal.gov.in/) says the municipal website no longer accepts new birth or death registrations. It directs both new registrations and corrections to existing birth or death records to the [Central Civil Registration System](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/home). Applicants may also visit the IMC office for help with new registrations and certificate corrections. The notice does not give a publication date, fee, or processing time.

The [district certificate page](https://itanagar.nic.in/service/apply-for-death-certificate/) still describes the earlier municipal submission route. For new registrations and corrections, follow the corporation's own notice rather than the older wording on that page. The notice does not say that every existing certificate download has moved or specify how to handle legacy applications.

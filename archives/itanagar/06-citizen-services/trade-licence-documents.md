# Documents for Itanagar trade licence applications and renewals

## First application

The corporation's [new licence checklist](https://www.imc.arunachal.gov.in/doclist.html?sid=15) lists a passport-size photo, Aadhaar or other government ID, an ST certificate, land allotment papers or a lease agreement from the magistrate if the applicant does not have land, and a self-declaration from the magistrate that the applicant does not hold a government job. It also lists a Town Planning Department building permit and a no-objection certificate from the DC, EAC or administrative officer. Ask the corporation which checklist entries apply to the business and premises.

The checklist names three business-specific documents: a Health Department food safety document for food businesses, a Tax and Excise Department permit for bars and wine shops, and a Health Department drug permit for pharmacies or medicine sellers.

## Renewal

The [renewal checklist](https://www.imc.arunachal.gov.in/doclist.html?sid=16) lists the original older licence, a passport-size photo, Aadhaar or another government ID, the receipt for the garbage collection charge, and an ST certificate. It also lists the Tax and Excise permit for bars and wine shops. The corporation's service menu links forms, document lists and process flows separately for new applications and renewals. The document checklists do not establish a fee or a processing deadline.

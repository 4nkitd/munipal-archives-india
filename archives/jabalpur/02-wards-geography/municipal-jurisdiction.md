# Jabalpur municipal jurisdiction and ward count

## Identifying the corporation

The [City Finance profile](https://www.cityfinance.in/municipal-data/city/jabalpur) reports 79 wards and an area of 53 km². The underlying directory identifies the corporation by LGD code 250995 and census code 802361. It attributes the ward count to the urban local body. The population figure of 1,055,525 is from Census 2011. These figures do not identify the ward for a particular address.

## Local office and jurisdiction

The [district municipal directory](https://jabalpur.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) gives the office address as Near Teen Patti Chowk, Wright Town, Jabalpur 482001. City Finance lists Jabalpur Cantt separately as a Cantonment Board (LGD 277265, eight wards). Those eight do not form part of the corporation count.

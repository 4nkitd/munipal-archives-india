# PM POSHAN volunteer and NGO selection in Jabalpur

## Scope and notice period

Jabalpur district posted an advertisement for the selection of volunteers and non-governmental organizations to implement the PM POSHAN Shakti Nirman Scheme under Jabalpur Municipal Corporation. The district listing gives 19 November 2026 as the start date and 15 December 2026 as the end date.

The district links an 8 MB advertisement under “View”. The HTML listing does not specify a submission time, delivery address or list of selected organizations. It records a call for applicants, not the award of work.

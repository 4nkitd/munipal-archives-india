# Jagdalpur birth certificate application points

## Where to apply

Bastar district lists Lok Seva Kendra and tehsil offices as birth-certificate application points in Jagdalpur, PIN 494001. For a hospital birth the district identifies the person in charge of the institution as the informant. For a birth in a moving vehicle it identifies the person in charge of the vehicle. The page distinguishes the municipal corporation commissioner from the district registrar for smaller municipalities in its delayed-registration table. Its quoted fee waiver ran for five years from 5 November 2014; it does not establish a current fee.

## Municipal office enquiries

The [district municipal directory](https://bastar.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists Nagar Palik Nigam, near Indira Priyadarshani Stadium, Jagdalpur, Bastar 494001. Office telephone: 07782-221055. Office email: jagdalpurnagarnigam@yahoo.com.

# Reconstitution of Nagar Nigam Jaipur and the April 2025 ward consultation

## When the reconstitution took effect

Rajasthan's Local Self Government Department signed [notification DLB/25/9461](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files/Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/LSG/2026/Notification/O_230126_64303132-1388-433f-89d9-ca65f4ca7194.pdf) on **27 March 2025**. It identifies DLB/19/10476–10740 of 18 October 2019 as the notification that divided Jaipur into Heritage and Greater. Under sections 3, 5, 6 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009, the 2025 notification brings the two territories together under the name Nagar Nigam Jaipur.

The final sentence on page 1 says the notification takes effect **when the then-current boards of Jaipur Greater and Jaipur Heritage complete their terms**. It gives no calendar date for that event. The handwritten 27 March date is the notification date, not the effective date.

## What residents could object to

On 7 April 2025, the Jaipur District Collector and District Election Officer issued notice no. 1005, citing the March notification. The Collector proposed ward boundaries using the 2011 Census under departmental instructions dated 13 February and 18 March 2025. Residents could submit objections or suggestions in person, by post or by email. The notice named the Jaipur I, Jaipur II (Sanganer) and Amer subdivision offices, the Greater and Heritage municipal offices and the issuing office. The deadline was **6 p.m. on 17 April 2025**.

The schedule attached to this notice is a draft. For the boundaries subsequently approved by the state, consult the [September Gazette](https://jaipurmc.org/PDF/Others/FinalParisimanGazette2025.pdf).

# Jaipur's 150-ward schedule in the Rajasthan Gazette

## Order and publication dates

The Local Self Government Department dated notification DLB/25/19631 **16 September 2025**. The Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary published it in Part 6(क) on **20 September 2025**. It cites sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994. The Collector forwarded the objections to the draft and the Collector's opinion. The state accepted that opinion and approved the proposed ward formation order.

## Reading Form A

The heading names *Nagar Nigam Jaipur*. Each row gives a ward number, a written boundary description and a population. Ward 1 starts at Kanji Restaurant at Neendar Mor and follows, among other features, the Vidhyadhar Nagar assembly constituency boundary. Its population column reads 18,932. Ward 150, the last entry, has a population of 14,388 and its boundary description continues onto printed page 3962. These are the schedule's figures, not present-day population estimates.

For an address on a boundary road, read the direction of travel and the side included in the Hindi description. A road name alone does not settle which properties fall inside. This gazette does not give a conversion table from old Greater or Heritage ward numbers.

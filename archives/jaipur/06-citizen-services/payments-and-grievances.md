# Jaipur municipal payments and public grievance registration

## Choose the payment by the demand you received

The corporation's online payment page has distinct entries for charges that a resident or business might owe on property. For an urban development tax demand, select [Urban Development Tax](https://www.rajmunicipal.com/public/search_property/). For lease dues, the entry is [Online Sahari Jamabandi (Lease) Payment](https://lease.jaipurmc.org/DueDemandDetail/OnlineLeaseDemandMultiSearch). A municipal dairy booth has a separate [rent lookup](https://dairy.jaipurmc.org/searchDataWebSite/GetDairyInfo). The tender fee and RISL fee link is for tender payments. These are the destinations published by the corporation; the directory gives no common fee or payment deadline.

## Register a municipal grievance

The [municipal homepage](https://jaipurmc.org/) directs residents to [Jaipur Samadhan](https://samadhan.jaipurmc.org) to register a complaint. It gives the JMC Call Centre number as **0141-2747400**, with hours of 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. The [office contact list](https://jaipurmc.org/Presentation/Others/ConctactUs.aspx) confirms that number for public grievance registration and lists **0141-2742900** separately for the control room. The municipal office is at Pandit Dindayal Uppadhyay Bhawan, Lal Kothi, Tonk Road, Jaipur.

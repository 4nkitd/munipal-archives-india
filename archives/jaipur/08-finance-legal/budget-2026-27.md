# Jaipur municipal budget estimates for 2026-27

## Main budget summary

Page 2 of the corporation's 37-page budget gives the following amounts in **₹ lakh**. The cover identifies 2025-26 as the revised budget and 2026-27 as the estimated budget. The source gives financial years; no publication day is established here.

| Budget head | 2025-26 revised estimate | 2026-27 budget estimate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Opening balance | 71,237.01 | 71,237.01 |
| Revenue receipts | 143,795.00 | 158,856.00 |
| Capital receipts | 98,554.00 | 117,229.00 |
| Revenue expenditure | 133,924.00 | 136,150.00 |
| Capital expenditure | 108,425.00 | 139,935.00 |
| Closing balance | 71,237.01 | 71,237.01 |

The 2026-27 expenditure heads together amount to ₹276,085 lakh (₹2,760.85 crore). Opening and closing balances are separate from expenditure. The 2026-27 column is an estimate; it does not report how much the corporation actually spent.

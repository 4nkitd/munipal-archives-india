# Jalandhar city corporation: establishment and jurisdiction

The corporation’s [City at a Glance](https://mcjalandhar.in/city-at-a-glance.html) identifies the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976 and gives 5 July 1977 as its establishment date. It dates the first municipal election after formation to 1991 and the first mayor’s term, that of Jai Kishan Saini, to 18 June 1991–11 June 1996.

MCJ reports approximately 115 km² and 85 administrative wards. These figures describe the corporation. The [district administration](https://jalandhar.nic.in/administrative-setup/) lists tehsils including Nakodar, Phillaur and Shahkot; district subdivisions are not corporation wards.

The [Commissioner’s Office](https://mcjalandhar.in/contact-us.html) is on the first floor of the Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Admin Complex, Nehru Garden, Jalandhar 144001. MCJ lists 0181 224 2411 as its general helpline and 0181 222 7015 separately as the office number.

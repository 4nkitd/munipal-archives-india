# Jalandhar approved budget estimates, FY 2026–27

The date on this record, **13 October 2026**, is the dispatch date of Jalandhar’s approved budget (dispatch no. **20262710491**). The report does not give the date of the council’s approval. The summary labels its figures “BE By HOD” for 2026–2027. They are budget estimates, not audited collections or payments.

| Budget head | Estimate (rupees) |
| --- | ---: |
| Revenue receipt | 4,923,200,000 |
| Revenue expenses | 3,964,748,000 |
| Capital receipt | 0 |
| Capital expenditure | 141,100,000 |
| Extraordinary receipt | 0 |
| Extraordinary payment | 0 |

The receipt schedule includes ₹70 crore for property tax (110-01-00), ₹20 crore for water tax/charges (110-02-01), ₹280 crore for the Punjab Municipal Fund (120-20-02) and ₹50 crore for plan sanction fees (140-12-01). These are components of the revenue estimate, not additional receipts. The [budget register](https://mcjalandhar.in/mcjln/budget-3/) also links earlier annual budgets back to FY 2010–11.

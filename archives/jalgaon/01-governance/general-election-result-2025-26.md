# Jalgaon municipal general election result, 2025–26

## Final party-wise return

Row 13 of the Maharashtra State Election Commission's final return records **19 electoral wards**, **75 councillor seats**, and **333 contesting candidates** for Jalgaon. The commission reports results for all 75 seats. Its table identifies the election cycle but has no printed publication date.

| SEC party or reporting category | Seats won |
| --- | ---: |
| Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) | 46 |
| Shiv Sena (SS) | 22 |
| Shiv Sena (Uddhav Balasaheb Thackeray), listed as SS - UBT | 5 |
| Nationalist Congress Party (NCP) | 1 |
| Independent | 1 |
| Total | 75 |

## Checking a seat or ward

The [corporation's home page](https://jcmc.gov.in/) separately links the [elected-member gazette](https://jcmc.gov.in/pdf/Election2025-26/Members/CORPORATER%20GAZZETTE-2026%20Jalgaon.pdf), which it describes as the return of 75 members across 19 multi-member wards. Use that source for the member returned for a particular seat, and the [final formation annexures](https://jcmc.gov.in/election-ward-formation-final-2025-26.htm) to locate an address. The party totals above do not show who won a given ward's seats or who holds a seat after later changes.

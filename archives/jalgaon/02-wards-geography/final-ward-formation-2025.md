# Jalgaon final electoral ward formation for the 2025–26 general election

## Notification and representation

The [corporation's final ward page](https://jcmc.gov.in/election-ward-formation-final-2025-26.htm) lists both language annexures under **9 October 2025**. The English Annexure 15 bears **7 October 2025** as the date of notice No. SEC/MC-2025/CR.21/Desk-5. It cites Section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949 and says it applies to the next general election after the notification. The district's [June 2025 listing for Class D corporation ward-formation orders](https://jalgaon.gov.in/en/notice/orders-regarding-ward-formation-of-class-d-municipal-corporations-2025/) is an earlier process notice, not the final Jalgaon schedule.

| Type of electoral ward | Wards | Seats |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Four councillors | 18 | 72 |
| Three councillors | 1 | 3 |
| Total | 19 | 75 |

## Finding a ward boundary

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The final page links the Marathi Annexure 14, the English Annexure 15, a combined map for wards 1 through 19 and a separate map for each ward. The English schedule gives directional boundary descriptions as well as ward population. Ward 1, for example, lists 26,168 residents, including 4,713 Scheduled Caste and 732 Scheduled Tribe residents. Its northern line follows the municipal limits from the northwest corner of Jalgaon Shivar Survey No. 380 to the northeast corner of Survey No. 387. Consult the full annexure and the relevant ward map when checking a particular location, rather than relying on this excerpt or the election result.

# Jalgaon notification of 28 time-bound public services

## Notice and service deadlines

The corporation's [Right to Public Services page](https://jcmc.gov.in/RTS.htm) links two parts of its notice covering 28 notified services under Section 3 of the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015. The notice is dated **17 March 2025**, reference जशमनपा/प्र.का./३८६३/२०२५; the newspaper pages carrying it are dated **18 March 2025**. The examples below are from the first part.

| Service | Time limit | Listed fee | Documents listed in the notice |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Issue a part map | 7 days | ₹700 for A4, ₹1,100 for A3, ₹1,500 for A2, ₹2,500 for A1, ₹6,800 for A0; separate rates apply to specified town-planning scheme part maps | Prescribed application, recent ownership extract, site measurement map and City Survey measurement map |
| Sewer connection | 15 days | ₹1,000 | Prescribed application, ownership papers and no-dues certificate |
| Complaint about unauthorized water connection | 7 days | If confirmed, two years of municipal water charges based on connection diameter plus the applicable connection fee | Prescribed application |
| Water non-availability certificate | 30 days | ₹50 | Prescribed application, municipal no-dues certificate, undertaking on obtaining the occupancy certificate and providing rainwater recharge after the no-objection certificate, and any other prescribed documents |

## Who handles applications and appeals

For sewer connections and the water non-availability certificate, the notice lists the junior engineer as the designated officer, the deputy engineer for first appeal and the executive engineer for second appeal. For a part map it lists the assistant town planner, town planner and assistant director of town planning, respectively. If a notified service is delayed or refused, consult the roles in the relevant row. The municipal portal gives the [Nashik divisional Right to Public Services Commission procedure](https://jcmc.gov.in/RTS.htm) for a third appeal, including Form V and a 60-day window. These fees and deadlines are those published in March 2025, not a guarantee against later revision.

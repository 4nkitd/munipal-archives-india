# Jalna City Municipal Corporation: upgrade from municipal council

## Status and date of transition

The corporation's history says the Government of Maharashtra announced the upgrade of Jalna Municipal Council in June 2023 and that it took effect on 7 August 2023. The announcement and effective date are different. This record cites the municipal history for the effective date; the original constituting gazette was not located.

The [district department directory](https://jalna.gov.in/en/departments/municipal-corporation/) uses the shorter label Jalna Municipal Corporation and links to the corporation's website, which styles itself Jalna City Municipal Corporation. The [Maharashtra State Election Commission's 2025–26 final results](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) report 16 wards and results for all 65 seats under municipal corporation general elections. These sources confirm the present corporation status independently of the municipal history.

## Administration and office

The corporation's profile reports an area of 81.6 square kilometres. It identifies the commissioner as head of its administration and describes municipal responsibilities such as water supply, sewerage, street lighting, roads and solid waste. The [office contact page](https://jcmcjalna.org/contact) gives Administrative Building, Railway Station Road, Jalna, Maharashtra 431203, telephone 02482-230350 and toll-free line 1800-233-1793.

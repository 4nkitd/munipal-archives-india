# Jalna final ward maps for the 2025 general election

## Municipal map series

The corporation's General Election 2025 page labels the series अंतिम प्रभाग रचना, or final ward composition. It links a [citywide final map](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PtZki4kC9WaOG-DjaNpLCMviWJCOipbA/view?usp=sharing), [appendices 1 to 6](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TJ_sU1sg1MXaWJdh_GtLW1-Ia6xMnA8Z/view?usp=sharing) and individual maps numbered 1 to 16.

| Municipal source | What to use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Citywide final map | Locate the likely electoral ward on the city map. |
| Ward maps 1–16 on the municipal page | Check the line of the official boundary for a specific address. |
| Appendices 1–6 | Consult the election documents alongside the maps. [Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url] |

## Ward count and seat count

The [State Election Commission's 2025–26 final results](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) list Jalna with 16 electoral wards, 65 seats and results declared for all 65. The seat total is not a count of geographic wards. The results table does not establish any boundary. The municipal index has no stated publication date; the citywide map filename alone does not establish a notification date.

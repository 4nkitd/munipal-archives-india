# Jalna property tax lookup, payment and receipts

## Find the property and pay

The [corporation's website](https://jcmcjalna.org/) links **Property Tax - Pay Online** to the Jalna municipal payment page. The search field lists UPIC ID, mobile number and old property number as inputs. If a search returns multiple properties, verify the property number and address details before proceeding. The property details screen includes a **Previous Receipts** option.

## Payment and assessment are separate

The website lists payment separately from assessment, self-assessment, title transfer and property tax NOC, although these links open the same portal. Paying a bill does not itself update the property record. The [municipal contact page](https://jcmcjalna.org/contact) lists the toll-free line 1800-233-1793 and the office line 02482-230350 for municipal enquiries.

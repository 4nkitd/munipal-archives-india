# Jalna budget estimates for 2025–26

## Budget summary

The corporation links this budget book in its website's Budget menu. Its Marathi cover identifies revised 2024–25 estimates and original 2025–26 estimates. The 2025–26 summary submitted for approval gives the following figures.

| Item | 2025–26 estimate |
| --- | ---: |
| Municipal rates and taxes | ₹69,87,00,000 |
| Revenue receipts including water supply and sewerage | ₹227,68,85,000 |
| Capital receipts from government grants | ₹195,48,24,400 |
| Revenue and capital receipts combined | ₹423,17,09,400 |
| Revenue expenditure including water supply and sewerage | ₹155,30,45,000 |
| Capital expenditure | ₹267,85,74,400 |
| Revenue and capital expenditure combined | ₹423,16,19,400 |
| Opening balance | ₹1,35,000 |
| Closing balance | ₹2,25,000 |

Including the opening balance, the resources shown total ₹423,18,44,400. The same figure on the spending side includes the closing balance; it is not the expenditure excluding that balance. These are estimates, not audited financial results.

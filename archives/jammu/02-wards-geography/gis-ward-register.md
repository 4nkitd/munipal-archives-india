# Jammu Municipal Corporation GIS ward register: wards 1 to 75

## What the register verifies

The MyJammu GIS page publishes one ward label and one map download control per ward. This transcription preserves the listed wording, including both uses of **Channi** at wards 50 and 51 and the spelling **Lawer Muthi** at ward 67. Use the number to distinguish labels that are repeated. The page does not give a publication date specific to this ward register, so the date field is blank; a portal-wide footer is not a date for the list.

Some labels differ from the location picker on JMC's [sanitation payment form](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/SanitationFee.aspx). For example, the GIS labels ward 15 "Ragunath Bazar" and both wards 50 and 51 "Channi"; the payment form offers longer or different labels. The form is not used to edit the map register. The corporation's [Ex Municipal Councillors page](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/councillor.aspx) independently lists ward numbers 1 to 75, but its residential addresses are not boundary evidence and its former councillors are not attached as current representatives.

The ward table and structured rows do not set legal boundaries, identify every locality inside a ward, or establish administrative zones or engineering assignments. The GIS index is the starting point for locating a map; this archive does not verify each attachment or a current ward-delimitation order.

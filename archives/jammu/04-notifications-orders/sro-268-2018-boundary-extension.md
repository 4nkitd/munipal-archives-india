# SRO 268 of 2018: local areas added to Jammu municipal limits

## The municipal limit change

The Jammu and Kashmir Housing and Urban Development Department issued SRO 268 on 11 June 2018. The government invoked section 3(3) of the Jammu and Kashmir Municipal Corporation Act, 2000 and said it had consulted JMC. The operative text includes the specified local areas within the limits of Municipal Corporation, Jammu. The departmental reference is `HUD/LSG/JMC/15/2017`.

## How to read the land schedule

The notification groups village entries by niabat, a revenue subdivision, then lists the khasra numbers included. These short rows illustrate the schedule. Spellings follow the notification.

The three-page scan also includes longer schedules for Sunjwan, Chowaddi, Dharmal and Muthi, among others. Check the source for the complete ranges, subdivisions and partial parcel notations. A listed village is not necessarily included in its entirety. These are municipal-limit additions, not electoral ward boundaries, and the record does not treat a similar name in JMC's ward register as proof of a parcel's ward. The date is the one written on the signed notification and corroborated by [JMC's Orders & Circulars listing](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/notices.aspx?noticetype=Circulars).

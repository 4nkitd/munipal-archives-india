# SRO 83 of 2018: Hazuri Bagh parcels added to Jammu municipal limits

## Inclusion of specified land

On 9 February 2018, the Jammu and Kashmir Housing and Urban Development Department issued SRO 83 under section 3(3) of the Jammu and Kashmir Municipal Corporation Act, 2000. The notification says the government consulted Jammu Municipal Corporation before including the area of village Hazuri Bagh from Khasra Nos. 01 to 144, as shown in its annexure, within the corporation's municipal limits. The departmental reference is `HUD/LSG/JMC/33/2015`.

The annexure identifies Hazuri Bagh in Jammu tehsil and Jammu district and lists khasra entries with area columns in kanals and marlas. A khasra number identifies land in the revenue record; it is not a ward number. The notification does not define a ward or name a ward representative. For a land check, use the annexure and confirm the exact parcel identifier and area rather than relying on the village name alone.

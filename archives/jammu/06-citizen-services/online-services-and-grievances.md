# Jammu Municipal Corporation service links, payments and complaints

## Choosing an application or payment route

| Need | Where JMC directs residents |
| --- | --- |
| Birth or death certificate, NOC/licence, rehri licence or pet dog registration | The [JMC e-governance directory](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/egov.html) links to [ServicePlus Jammu](https://serviceonline.gov.in/jammu/). The destination returned HTTP 503 during the archival check; no application was submitted. |
| Building permission or status, construction intimation, occupancy-cum-completion certificate | JMC's [main menu](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/header.html) links these functions to the [J&K Online Building Permission System](https://obps.jk.gov.in/). The start page redirected to the BPAMS home page. JMC also posts a [building checklist and fee document index](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/checklistbuilding.html). |
| Sanitation user charges | Use JMC's [Pay Sanitation Fee form](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/SanitationFee.aspx). It includes a locality/ward picker. Do not treat its options as complete ward coverage or an electoral register. |
| Municipal shop or flat rent | JMC's e-governance directory links to [Other Fee](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/OtherFee.aspx), which has department and fee-type selectors. The [online payment menu](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/online-payment.html) also lists separate building, sanitation and tender fee links. |

The archive checked links and public landing pages, not payment completion, certificate issuance, statutory deadlines or individual eligibility. The JMC directory does not give a publication date.

## Complaints and office contacts

JMC's [complaint link](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/OnlineGrievances.aspx) leads to the MyJammu grievance department page; JMC also lists it under both "Register Complaint" and "Check Complaint Status". The page groups complaints by service, including sanitation, garbage hotspots, building, civil works, street lights, sewerage and cleaning of deep drains or nallahs. It also has a separate category for construction and repair of deep drains or nallahs. No complaint was filed during verification.

The [JMC contact directory](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/contactus.aspx?type=Officers) gives the office address as Town Hall, Jammu, Jammu and Kashmir 180001. The general toll-free line is 1800-180-7207, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days. The directory separately lists local office number `2578503` for help with online birth and death applications. The structured office directory records selected corporation staff and role mailboxes, not mobile numbers or ward assignments.

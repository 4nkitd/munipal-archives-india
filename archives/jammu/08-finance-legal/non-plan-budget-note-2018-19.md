# Jammu Municipal Corporation non-plan budget note for 2018–19

## Purpose and fiscal coverage

The [JMC section 4 RTI disclosure](https://jmc.jk.gov.in/sec4rti.html) links this PDF under item (xi), budget allocation and expenditure disclosures. The document is titled "Budget Note". It describes JMC's non-plan budget for obtaining grant-in-aid and distinguishes revised estimates for 2017–18 from proposals for 2018–19. It also cites spending to the end of September 2017 for some heads. The estimates are not audited expenditure, nor is the note a statement of the grant actually released.

## Selected expenditure heads

Figures are in ₹ lakh. A dash means the note does not state September spending in the passage for that head; it does not mean zero.

| Head | Spending to end of Sep 2017 | 2017–18 revised estimate | 2018–19 proposed estimate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Salary | 2,878.07 | 6,680.60 | 6,830.09 |
| Leave encashment | 95.74 | 186.60 | 136.85 |
| Telephone | 5.15 | 15.00 | 20.00 |
| Petrol, oil and lubricants | 286.28 | 700.00 | 800.00 |
| Stationery | 7.93 | 20.00 | 25.00 |
| Repair and maintenance works | 1,502.61 | 6,200.00 | 6,400.00 |
| Pension liability | — | 2,000.00 | 2,500.00 |
| Ex gratia | — | 5.00 | 6.00 |

The note attributes the upward 2017–18 salary revision to an increase in dearness allowance from 125% to 136%. For 2018–19 it attributes the lower proposed leave encashment to fewer expected retirements. Its accompanying sanitation section records a then-reported fleet of 182 vehicles and says door-to-door collection and sanitation in 15 extended wards had been outsourced. These descriptions concern the budget period, not the current service arrangement.

## Date and limits

The date field is blank because the note does not supply a verified issue date. Its fiscal years do not stand in for one. Some other passages have internally inconsistent fee or year wording and are not used as a rate schedule. This record does not establish a current budget or current charges.

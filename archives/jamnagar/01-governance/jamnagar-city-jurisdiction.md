# Jamnagar city corporation: formation and municipal duties

JMC dates its predecessor city council to 30 December 1875 and the municipality to 2 October 1949. The municipal corporation itself began on 6 October 1981. Gujarat's [corporation directory](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/corporations.php) lists Jamnagar at entry 6.

Within the city, JMC maintains public roads, bridges, street lights, drains and public toilets. It also registers births and deaths, maintains primary education facilities and provides fire protection. Its public office is at Jubilee Garden, Jamnagar.

The corporation's historical account gives an area of about 26.40 square kilometres alongside a 2001 census population. It provides no current boundary schedule for applying that area to present-day addresses.

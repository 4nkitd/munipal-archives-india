# Jamnagar ward representation on the General Board

JMC's political structure page describes a General Board of 64 councillors elected from 16 wards. Each ward elects four members for a five-year term. The page says half the seats are reserved for women. It assigns financial powers, including sanctioning and awarding major works, to the Standing Committee.

The page gives no election year, ward names or delimitation map. Separately, JMC's [2023-2027 assessment-book notices](https://www.mcjamnagar.com/) group ward numbers 13, 15 and 19 under one application link and numbers 5 and 10 under another. Those notices concern property assessments; they do not establish the number or boundaries of electoral wards for a particular election.

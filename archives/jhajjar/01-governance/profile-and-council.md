# Municipal Council Jhajjar: profile and elected council

## Municipal profile

Jhajjar is a municipal council in Jhajjar district, Haryana, with 19 wards. The Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department's [list of municipalities as of 2 May 2022](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2F84b74408-f266-4ae0-af57-a1d6cc199328.pdf) records the upgrade from municipal committee to municipal council on 7 August 2020. The [state key-figures table](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists a municipal area of 35.78 square kilometres, a population of 53,976 and 37,006 properties. The table does not identify a reference year for those figures. Properties are not the same as households.

Jhajjar town lies northwest of Farrukhnagar and Manesar. The wider corridor includes the Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway and [Reliance MET City](https://www.metcity.in/), a separate township project in the district with direct expressway access. This geographic link does not establish that MET City is within the council's municipal limits.

## Elected ward roster

The State Election Commission's [notification SEC/1ME/2022/6120 of 4 July 2022](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/b7a90171-1b60-4c3d-8617-5137e031b016.pdf) names the president and ward members elected at the 19 June 2022 general election. Saini won 10,711 votes against Rao Nahar Singh's 4,587, a margin of 6,124, according to [The Tribune's election report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/lotus-blooms-in-hoodas-bastion-bjp-wins-both-seats-in-jhajjar-dist-406159/).

# Jhajjar wards, old-town mohallas, sectors and colonies

## Ward count and limits of this record

Jhajjar Municipal Council has 19 numbered wards in the [LGD-derived directory](https://bharatsurvey.org/urban-local-bodies/haryana/jhajjar), which identifies local body code 248562 and dates its underlying directory data to 25 September 2026. A [6 November 2026 municipal works report](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/jhajjar-bahadurgarh/the-municipal-council-will-award-a-contract-for-filling-potholes-and-carrying-out-repairs-across-19-wards-jhajjar-bahadurgarh-news-c-195-1-nnl1001-142138-2026-11-06) also refers to all 19 wards.

A [16 July 2026 report](https://www.amarujala.com/city-and-states/jhajjar-municipal-councils-jurisdiction-likely-to-expand-to-have-20-wards-by-next-year-jhajjar-news-c-195-1-nnl1001-137511-2026-07-16) describes a possible increase to 20 wards for the next election and a proposal to include Rampura village. Neither proposal is treated here as an enacted boundary change. This record does not supply a twentieth ward.

The companion `../wards.json` lists Ward 1 through Ward 19. Its locality arrays contain address evidence, not legally delimited polygons or complete ward membership. Empty arrays mean that coverage was not verified. Null zones and divisions mean no supported allocation was found. No sector-to-ward allocation is inferred from matching numbers.

For a property-specific answer, obtain the operative wardbandi notification and map from Municipal Council Jhajjar and check the property ID against that map. The [district administration directory](https://jhajjar.nic.in/directory/mc-jjr/) confirms the council's official directory entry.

## HSVP Sector 6 and Sector 9

HSVP publishes separate layout-cum-demarcation sheets for Jhajjar's planned urban estate. Sector numbers are planning identifiers, not ward numbers.

| Planning area | Official sheet and concrete details |
| --- | --- |
| Sector 6, Jhajjar | [HSVP revised layout-cum-demarcation plan](https://www.hsvphry.org.in/documents/eauction/13_08_2024/Sector6Jhajjar.pdf), drawing DTP JH 212/2006 dated 4 October 2006. The area schedule states 73.36 hectares, including 11.50 hectares of green belt and 61.86 hectares net planned area. The sheet records later amendments. |
| Sector 9 Part A, Jhajjar | [HSVP revised layout-cum-demarcation plan](https://www.hsvphry.org.in/documents/eauction/18_07_2024/Sector9PartAJhajjar.pdf), drawing DTP JH 340/2009 dated 11 September 2009. Approval note cites memo CTP/STP N/RS/10833 dated 17 November 2009. |
| Sector 9 Part B, Jhajjar | The Part A sheet identifies Part B. HSVP's [layout index](https://www.hsvphry.org.in/documents/home/news_updates/newsUpdate_202409051505_638611455568364144.pdf) separately lists Sector 9 Part B, Jhajjar. |

The Sector 9 area schedule is explicitly for Parts A and B together. It states 252.80 acres acquired and 187.29 acres net planned. Those totals must not be attributed to Part A alone. The map distinguishes released land and land affected by stay, litigation or encroachment. A plan label does not establish that a reserved facility has been built or that a particular parcel has clear title.

The same HSVP index also contains Bahadurgarh sectors. Check the urban-estate name as well as the sector number. No official wardbandi overlay was verified for Jhajjar Sector 6 or Sector 9, so the JSON leaves ward-level `sectors` arrays empty.

The compilation repeats identifier 13067 for Kuldeep Colony and Yadav Colony, and 13065 for two Subhash Nagar-related entries. These source values are retained, not silently corrected. Similar names and repeated IDs do not justify merging polygons or adding their acreage.

The compilation does not provide a ward crosswalk or individual notification dates in these rows. For regularization enquiries, ask the council for the notification and map matching the exact colony pocket. Inclusion does not itself establish ownership, building-plan approval or completion of municipal services.

## MET City, Badli and the Farrukhnagar-Manesar corridor

MET City uses "Jhajjar" in its project address because it lies in Jhajjar district. That does not make it part of Jhajjar Municipal Council's 19 wards. The official [Haryana RERA project record for MET City Pocket L](https://haryanarera.gov.in/view_project/project_preview_open/1654) places the site in Sector 8, Yakubpur and Sondhi, tehsil Badli, district Jhajjar. It is a different planning geography from HSVP Sector 6 and Sector 9 in Jhajjar town.

The location is relevant to the Gurugram-facing side of the district, near Farrukhnagar and the road connections onward to Manesar. A [project-location listing](https://files.propertywala.com/pdf/ee/reliance-met-city-jhajjar.J599711925.pdf) places Farrukhnagar about 6 km from its listed Yakubpur project location. This is a third-party proximity estimate, not a municipal boundary measurement. Individual MET City pockets are spread across different sites, so a single distance should not be used for the entire township.

No surveyed common boundary between Jhajjar Municipal Council and Municipal Corporation Manesar, or between Jhajjar Municipal Council and Farrukhnagar, was established by the cited records. "Bordering Farrukhnagar and Manesar" should therefore be read only as a regional-corridor description unless supported by an operative municipal-limit map. Do not assign Yakubpur, Sondhi, Badli or MET City to a Jhajjar town ward on that basis. Check the relevant revenue village, tehsil, planning licence, RERA registration and notified local-body boundary for each parcel.

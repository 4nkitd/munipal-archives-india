# Municipal Council Jhajjar officers and contacts

## Source dates and verification limits

The officer roster below comes from search-indexed official Haryana Urban Local Bodies pages, which display a last-updated date of 18 December 2024. Both the [employees page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Jhajjar/11120) and [contact page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Jhajjar/1972) returned an error page during direct retrieval on 22 November 2026. These are archived published contacts, not a confirmed current staffing list. No telephone calls or email-delivery tests were performed.

The frontmatter date records the source roster's displayed update date. The verification date records this research check, not an appointment date. Blank JSON contact fields mean that no verified contact was found, not that the office has no telephone.

## District Municipal Commissioner

The current DMC incumbent and a direct DMC telephone or email could not be confirmed. The [Haryana Chief Secretary order dated 25 March 2026](https://csharyana.gov.in/WriteReadData/Transfers-and-Promotions/Services-II/15847.pdf) assigns Abhinav Siwach, IAS, Sub Divisional Officer Civil, Bahadurgarh, additional charge as District Municipal Commissioner, Jhajjar.

This appointment must not be treated as current. [The Tribune's 6 July 2026 report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/haryana-diary-speculation-over-cong-mlas-presence-at-bjp-meets/) records that he was relieved of the DMC charge. A replacement was not established by the sources reviewed. The JSON therefore retains the office with an empty name and empty direct-contact fields rather than naming a historical incumbent as current.

# Jhajjar official notices, notified colonies and GRAP orders

## Source scope and date

The [DULB regularized-colony index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) retains the heading "2023 & 2024" but also lists 2025 material. Its first entry is "Municipalities Status of 836 Colonies Data Notified 2025", published 13 October 2025, 271 KB. The table below transcribes rows 267 through 300 under Jhajjar in that official status PDF.

The preceding rows 255 through 266 are under Municipal Council Bahadurgarh, also in Jhajjar district. They are not included as Jhajjar city colonies. Colony codes are reproduced as listed and are not ward numbers. No parcel boundaries or ward allocation are inferred from colony names.

The frontmatter date is the 17 November 2026 CAQM revocation order discussed below. It is not the notification date of all colonies. The status PDF is a compiled list, not a replacement for each gazette schedule and boundary plan.

## Gazette index and town planning notices

The state index lists notification batches dated 17 April, 25 July, 17 August, 6 October and 15 December 2023; 1 February, 6 March and 14 November 2024; and 18 March and 22 May 2025. The entries uploaded on 26 September 2025 retain earlier dates in their titles. An upload date must not be substituted for a gazette's issue date.

The [Jhajjar town planning notifications page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Jhajjar/8974), last updated 18 December 2024, links lists of 12, 81, 251 and 543 civic-amenity or infrastructure-deficient municipal areas. The first title expressly identifies Palwal. These are linked state compilations, not evidence that every listed area is within Jhajjar city.

The [Jhajjar landing page](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Jhajjar/) displays shared state notices, including the draft marriage-palace regularization policy published 15 July 2024, the Lal Dora possession-certificate notice published 6 July 2024, and the preliminary Municipal Account Code 2024 draft published 10 December 2024. The same page links the final Haryana Municipal Account Code 2025 notified on 20 May 2025, with English and Hindi parts published 6 June 2025. A draft consultation is not a final order.

## GRAP orders addressed to Jhajjar

CAQM's official orders distribute directions to the Deputy Commissioner, Jhajjar, Mini Secretariat, PIN 124103, and the Executive Officer, Municipal Council Jhajjar, Chhuchhakwas Road, PIN 124103. The relevant order sequence is:

| Order date | Verified action | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 21 October 2026 | Invoked Stage I across NCR under the revised GRAP schedule dated 29 September 2026 | [CAQM Stage I order](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/320b067c-7942-4be6-a708-cf04f5321392.pdf) |
| 2 November 2026 | Invoked Stage II across NCR in addition to Stage I | [CAQM Stage II order](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/011a2816-839f-43e5-99df-e5a4019b6bb2.pdf) |
| 6 November 2026 | Invoked Stage III across NCR in addition to Stages I and II | [CAQM Stage III order](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/e93ab81c-af66-4c41-a6b1-41bdd4a8ff28.pdf) |
| 17 November 2026 | Revoked the 6 November Stage III invocation immediately; retained Stages I and II | [CAQM revocation order](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/b91570de-8cc1-4735-8879-28f2bc1550f2.pdf) |

The Stage II schedule assigns urban local bodies and road-owning agencies mechanical/vacuum sweeping, disposal of collected dust at designated sites, and water sprinkling on identified roads. It also calls for construction-site dust-control inspections and targeted action at pollution hotspots.

The 17 November order separately preserved the restrictions imposed by CAQM's 30 October order for 5 through 20 November 2026. Those included restrictions on dust-generating demolition and outdoor construction across NCR, subject to listed exemptions. The office-attendance provision named Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat, Ghaziabad and Gautam Buddha Nagar. Jhajjar was not named in that office-attendance geography.

Revocation of a GRAP stage did not authorize a site closed by a specific violation order to resume without the required express permission. This is an archival sequence, not a certification of the live GRAP stage on 22 November 2026. Check the [CAQM GRAP orders index](https://caqm.nic.in/landingpage/pages/grap/grap-orders) for subsequent orders before starting restricted activity.

These are district administration records, not orders issued by the municipal council.

Only the district index titles and dates are recorded here. Their PDF conditions, permitted hours and enforcement clauses were not extracted, so this record does not assert them. Colony entries above come from extracted PDF text; GRAP findings come from official CAQM order text. No unsupported local order number or parcel boundary has been added.

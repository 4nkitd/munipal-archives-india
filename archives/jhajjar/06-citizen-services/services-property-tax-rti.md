# Jhajjar citizen services: property tax, certificates, RTI and grievances

## Property tax, NDC and property records

The [Jhajjar landing page](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Jhajjar/) links the Haryana [Property Tax and NDC portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for payment of property tax, generation of a No Dues Certificate, objections seeking correction of property details or dues, and creation of a new Property ID. Select the Jhajjar municipality and check that the property record belongs to the intended parcel before submitting an application.

DULB's [claim and objection portal announcement](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/WebCMS/Start/?DynamicTypeIdView=12868&Id=165) expressly includes Municipal Council Jhajjar. The local [property tax calculator entry](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Jhajjar/2504?DynamicTypeIdView=5861) is an older directory entry, last updated 18 December 2024. Use the current property portal for the assessed record and payment.

The [property tax instructions index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists the council and committee property tax notification published 31 July 2026, an interest-waiver notification published 18 May 2026 covering arrears for 2010-11 through 2024-25, and self-certification instructions published 27 March 2024. These are publication dates. This guide does not reproduce unverified tax slabs, waiver percentages or expiry dates. Read the applicable notification before relying on a concession.

## Birth and death certificates

The Jhajjar landing page routes birth and death services to [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/). Its service description includes certificate applications, correction of birth or death records, delayed registration, and inclusion of a child's name in the birth record. These are separate application types, not interchangeable requests.

The [District Jhajjar e-services directory](https://jhajjar.nic.in/service/e-services/) also lists birth/death certificates under Health/MC/MCG and points to the Civil Registration System and e-Disha forms. For the current application route, follow the municipality's SARAL service link and the checklist for the selected service. The directory identifies SARAL/Antyodaya SARAL Kendras in the district as assistance locations.

The current DULB RTS table inspected for this record does not display a birth/death certificate row. No certificate deadline or fee is therefore asserted here from an older table or another municipality's page.

## Marriage registration

The [Jhajjar landing page](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Jhajjar/) links [shaadi.haryana.gov.in](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) to apply for registration of marriage or track an application. Use that link rather than treating the district directory's older reference to Gurugram's municipal site as a Jhajjar application route.

The portal's application category and checklist govern supporting documents, attendance requirements and charges. No Jhajjar-specific marriage registration fee, appointment period or guaranteed delivery deadline was verified in the inspected sources.

## RTI and access to records

DULB's [RTI citizen information entry](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/WebCMS/Start/?DynamicTypeIdView=49&Id=1449) explains that citizens can seek information held by a public authority, take notes or extracts, obtain certified copies and seek certified samples of material. It identifies the State Information Commission, Haryana as the appeal/complaint body for denied access.

For records held by Municipal Council Jhajjar, identify the council as the record-holding public authority and check the current [DULB PIO list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList) or obtain the current designation from the council before addressing an RTI application. The inspected local contact page does not establish a current named Jhajjar PIO or appellate officer. It would be unsafe to assume that an older Executive Officer contact is the current PIO.

An RTS request for a copy of documents is a notified service route and is not a substitute for every RTI request. A civic complaint asks the authority to act; an RTI application asks for existing information or records.

## Civic grievances and contact provenance

The Jhajjar landing page links the [DULB grievance redressal system](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for departmental service complaints. The state helpdesk number is 1800-8900-929, available 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday. The landing page also carries a notice published 13 December 2024 that Harpath is available through the Jan Sahayak mobile application.

The [local contact page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Jhajjar/1972), last updated 18 December 2024, lists Executive Officer Sh. Krishan Kumar Yadav, mobile 9467175401 and eo-jhajjar@ulbharyana.gov.in. This is a dated directory entry, not confirmation of the officer's tenure in November 2026. The role-based email is retained above for council correspondence.

These links and published descriptions were researched on 22 November 2026. No application, payment, login or grievance submission was performed.

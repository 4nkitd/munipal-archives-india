# Jhajjar tenders, budgets and infrastructure, 2024-2026

## Scope and procurement portals

This record covers Municipal Council Jhajjar, not every project within Jhajjar district. Water supply and sewerage works require particular care because the Public Health Engineering Department has a separate role. Projects by HSVP, market committees or other agencies must not automatically be counted as municipal expenditure.

The official [HEWP live-tenders page](https://works.haryana.gov.in/LiveTender.aspx) states that tenders floated through HEWP are published on the [NIC Haryana eTendering portal](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app) for bidder participation. It says these tenders appear in HEWP's live listing on the day after publication on NIC. The listing provides tender references, eTender IDs, department and division, submission deadlines, estimates, earnest money, tender notices and BOQs.

Historical tender details below were verified against publicly accessible tender-index records. They are secondary transcriptions, not authenticated copies of the signed notices. A direct historical NIC link returned an "Unauthorized Page" during verification. Search NIC's archive using the recorded tender ID and check corrigenda before relying on a deadline, estimate or work period. A notice does not establish an award, expenditure or completion.

These are budget estimates reported by the newspaper, not audited actuals. Its headline rounds the surplus to Rs 53 lakh. No signed municipal budget schedule or audited reconciliation was obtained.

| Revised estimates, 2024-25 | Rs 2,58,00,000 |

This is one grant stream, not the council's complete annual budget. No complete municipal annual-budget total for 2024-25 or 2026-27 was verified for this record.

## Street lighting and the 2026 works programme

A [Global Tenders notice published 10 September 2024](https://www.globaltenders.com/tender-detail/installation-of-street-light-poles-cable-92823171), reference 92823171, identifies the procuring organisation as Haryana Government, Urban Local Bodies, MC Jhajjar. The title is "Installation Of Street Light, Poles, Cable, Pannel Etc. In Mc Jhajjar (Re-Call)". Its description gives an estimate of Rs 1,09,94,775, a 120-day contract duration and a deadline of 24 September 2024. This is a secondary listing of a tender, not evidence that lights were installed.

[The Tribune, 22 May 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/33-development-works-worth-over-rs-19-crore-approved-for-jhajjar/), reports financial and administrative approval by the council's Finance and Contracts Committee for 33 development works worth approximately Rs 19.73 crore. The report says construction would begin after tendering. It names streets, sanitation, lighting, intersection improvements, a welcome gate on Gurugram Road and beautification near the Sampla Road highway intersection. Approval must not be described as completion.

In a [Dainik Jagran interview published 30 August 2026](https://www.jagran.com/haryana/jhajjar-major-project-municipal-council-city-streets-to-be-constructed-at-a-cost-of-53-crore-40356653.html), the council's Executive Officer reported:

| Subject | Reported figure and status |
| --- | --- |
| Overall development programme | Approximately Rs 63 crore for 102 works |
| Streets across 19 wards | Approximately Rs 53 crore for 57 works; 26 reported complete, 12 more than halfway complete, and 11 between 10 and 25 percent complete |
| New poles, decorative poles and lights | Approximately Rs 3 crore, described as a plan |
| Lights for 32 approved colonies | Approximately Rs 50 lakh, described as a proposed purchase |
| Slabs to cover drains | Approximately Rs 50 lakh, described as a proposed purchase |
| Door-to-door waste collection | Approximately Rs 10 crore tender reported separately |

These are attributed interview statements, not an independently checked works register. The May approval package and August programme may overlap and must not be added together.

The underlying Assembly reply was not obtained, so these figures remain attributed to the newspaper's account of that reply.

## Water supply, sewerage and evidence gaps

In the 30 August 2026 Jagran interview cited above, the Executive Officer stated that drinking-water and sewerage arrangements fall under the Public Health Department and that the department has the principal responsibility for them. This record therefore does not assign all water or sewer works within the town to the Municipal Council.

No verified 2024-2026 municipal water-supply or sewerage project cost, pipeline length, treatment capacity or completion certificate was obtained. No such figure is inferred from district-wide listings. For a specific project, retrieve the PHED or municipal tender notice, administrative sanction, BOQ, award and completion record separately. Storm-water drain repair and cleaning notices in the table above are not evidence of a sewerage-system upgrade.

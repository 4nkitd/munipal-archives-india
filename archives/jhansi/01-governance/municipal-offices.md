# Jhansi Nagar Nigam leadership and municipal offices

## Corporation and district

The [Jhansi district urban bodies register](https://jhansi.nic.in/urban-bodies/) lists Jhansi as a Nagar Nigam. It lists Chirgaon, Baruasagar, Gursarai, Samthar and Mauranipur separately as Nagar Palika Parishads. Erich, Badagaon, Tori Fatehpur, Moth, Katera, Ranipur and Garautha are listed separately as Nagar Panchayats. The district list does not place those councils and panchayats under Jhansi Nagar Nigam.

The [municipal homepage](https://jnnjhansi.com/) displays Bihari Lal Arya as Mayor and Anna Sudhan (IAS) as Municipal Commissioner. The corporation's elected council roster also names Arya as Mayor for its 2023–24 edition. The homepage and administration page do not give an appointment date for the commissioner or a roster effective date; the names below describe the municipal website listing when checked.

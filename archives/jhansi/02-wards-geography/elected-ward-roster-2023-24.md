# Jhansi elected ward roster for 2023–24

## What the roster records

Jhansi Nagar Nigam labels this document its list of the newly elected mayor and councillors for 2023–24. It names Bihari Lal Arya as Mayor and lists one councillor entry for every ward numbered 1 through 60. The first three pages cover wards 1–20, 21–40 and 41–60, respectively. The edition heading identifies the elected council; it does not give the publication date.

The ward labels matter where neighbourhoods recur. Talpura is split across wards 2 and 18 in this roster, while Nandanpura first, second and third are numbered 35, 17 and 41. Nai Basti first and second are 8 and 34. The roster records the names as electoral labels and does not assign every address or street to a ward.

# Gazette notification specifying the city limits of Jhansi Municipal Corporation

## Designation and effective date

The Uttar Pradesh Extraordinary Gazette published notification No. `5092/IX-7-2001-6 J-2000` at Lucknow on 7 February 2002. The Governor acted under Article 243-Q(2) of the Constitution of India, read with section 3 of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959 (U.P. Act No. 2 of 1959). The notification designated the area in its schedule as a larger urban area for Part IX-A of the Constitution, to be known as the city of Jhansi Municipal Corporation from the date of Gazette publication.

The notification cites population, population density, revenue generated for local administration, non-agricultural employment and economic importance as factors in that designation. The official scan has the Hindi notification on pages 1–2 and the English translation, ordered under Article 348(3), on pages 3–4.

## Coverage described in the schedule

The schedule lists named places including Civil Line, Nai Basti, Sagar Gate, Prem Ganj and Nandanpura before describing the eastern, northern, western and southern limits. The following are reference points in that text, not a complete boundary transcription. Place names follow the Gazette's English translation.

| Side | Selected reference points |
| --- | --- |
| East | Pichhore, Kargavan, Kochhabhanwar to the Digara village boundary, and Bhagwantpura up to the Madhya Pradesh border. The description also names Singarra, Talpura–Bhattagaon–Khirakpatti with the cantonment area excluded, and Simraha, including Srinagar and reserved forest area. |
| North | Budha up to the nala separating it from Bhojla, Nagariya Kuan (Jhansi Khas rural, in Budha gram sabha), Simardha and Karari, with the Ambawai border and Karari–Parwai boundary nala among the limits. |
| West | The Jhansi–Shivpuri National Highway near the end of the railway boundary, the Nagar Palika pillar, Lehargird, Pali Pahari, Nayagaon and Dadri, with the Pahuj river as a reference point. |
| South | Garhiagaon, the crossing of the Jhansi–Bhopal railway line, Hansarigird with the cantonment area excluded, and Bijoli. |

This schedule defines the corporation's outer limits in the 2002 notification. It does not divide the area into wards and does not establish whether later notifications changed the limits.

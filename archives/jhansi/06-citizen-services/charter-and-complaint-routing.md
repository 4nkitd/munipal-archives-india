# Jhansi citizen charter and municipal complaint routing

## Municipal complaint channels

Jhansi Nagar Nigam publishes its [Citizen Charter](https://jnnjhansi.com/citizen_charter.php) as a Hindi booklet. The introduction calls it Citizen Charter 2013, but the municipal page does not date the posting of the linked copy. The booklet refers to the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959, the 74th Constitutional Amendment and the Twelfth Schedule.

The charter's first-stage complaint form asks for the complainant's name, address, phone number, ward number and complaint details. It says a complaint lodged on `0510-3-500-700` receives a registration number by SMS. The second-stage form asks for the ward, zone and details of the complaint, together with the first-stage registration date and number. Several department sections say to bring unresolved complaints to the Additional Municipal Commissioner or Municipal Commissioner.

The [municipal homepage](https://jnnjhansi.com/) lists `0510-3-500-700` as a 24-hour general helpline. It separately advertises `1533` for cleanliness complaints and suggestions and `14420` for septic tank emptying.

## Selected response targets in the charter

| Report or request | Target stated in the relevant service section |
| --- | --- |
| Street-light point not working | Normally within 24 hours, according to the area roster |
| Street-light supply interrupted | Notify the electricity department the same day |
| Broken street-light wire or damaged pole | Have the agency attend within two days |
| Pothole repair | Ten days |
| Road patch repair | Two weeks |
| Remove an obstruction from a road | One working day |
| Have the party responsible remove dumped debris from public land | Within 48 hours after notice |
| Decide a road-cutting application | One week |
| Remove a drainage obstruction | One day |
| Pump out waterlogging | Same day |
| Remove silt and debris from drains | April to June |

These are the targets printed in the charter, not verified response times. A target to notify another department or have an agency attend does not promise that the repair will be completed in that interval.

## State grievance tracking

The corporation links to the Uttar Pradesh [Jansunwai-Samadhan portal](https://jansunwai.up.nic.in/) for public grievances. The portal describes registration by mobile OTP, complaint tracking, reminders if the prescribed time has elapsed and feedback on the manner of disposal. Its intake policy excludes Right to Information requests, matters pending before a court, general suggestions and demands for financial assistance or employment. The state portal's eligibility rules are separate from what the municipal cleanliness line accepts.

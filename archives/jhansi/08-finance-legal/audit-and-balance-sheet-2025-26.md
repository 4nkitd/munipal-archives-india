## Audit observations

Annexure A says the corporation did not keep special funds and grants in separate bank accounts. The auditors recommended separate accounts and crediting interest earned to the grant funds. They also recommended a register for contractor withholdings and securities, with dates, amounts, refunds and year-end balances reconciled contractor by contractor. They asked the corporation to reconcile receivables against ledgers, house-tax registers and rent registers.

The annexure calls for asset records for land, buildings, lakes and ponds, roads and bridges, sewerage and drainage, public lighting, plant and machinery, office equipment, and furniture and fixtures. It recommends showing the value of land separately from buildings. It records written-down-value depreciation rates of 10% for buildings and furniture, 15% for urban infrastructure, plant and machinery and vehicles, and 40% for computer hardware and software. The auditors recommended obtaining a registered valuer's assessment and charging depreciation according to the life of the asset.

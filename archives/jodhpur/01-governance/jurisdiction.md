# Which Jodhpur corporation does a record belong to?

Rajasthan's Local Self Government Department signed [notification DLB/25/9561](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files/Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/LSG/2026/Notification/O_230126_ff0d76f0-91fc-47c6-baac-f910f67ed79a.pdf) on **27 March 2025** under sections 3, 5, 6 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009. It unites the territories of Jodhpur North and Jodhpur South under the name **नगर निगम जोधपुर (Nagar Nigam Jodhpur)**. Its final sentence makes the notification effective **when the two then-serving boards complete their terms**. The notice gives no calendar date for that event.

The notification traces the two corporations to split order DLB/19/10741–11000 of **18 October 2019**. In the [State Election Commission's 2020 statistical report](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/StatisticsArchiveReports.aspx), each had 80 wards. Identify a ward from that election by its number **and** its North or South corporation.

The [ward order DLB/25/17621](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files/Content/UploadFolder/DepartmentMaster/215/2026/Apr/30409/2156e398105-3193-44fc-a07d-0ca9f74c86bd.pdf), dated **1 September 2025** and gazetted **15 September**, approves wards 1–100. The [zone order DLB/2025/20671](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files/Content/UploadFolder/OrderEntry/LSG/2026/Notification/O_230126_a719d275-36ec-4a86-8a40-57ec6904028f.pdf) of **10 November 2025** cites 9561 and groups those wards into eight zones of the reconstituted corporation. Neither order supplies a board-term completion date. In November 2026 the [Commission's election allotment](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/12031.pdf) has one Jodhpur entry.

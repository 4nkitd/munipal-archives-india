# Reading the Jodhpur ward schedule

[Order DLB/25/17621, Form A](https://archive.org/details/in.gazette.rajasthan.2025-09-15.extraordinary-5111597) gives each ward a boundary description and a population column. The state cites sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994. The schedule runs from 01 to 100.

| Ward | Places named in Form A | Population in the schedule |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | The description starts at Soorsagar Chaupar towards Balsamand, includes Gandhi Nagar Awasiya Yojana and names Kaliberi. It excludes Bhil Basti Chaupar on the return leg. | 12,311 |
| 100 | The locality list on printed page 1172 includes Kishore Bagh, Virendra Nagar, Maa Bhagwati Colony and Naya Bas Balsamand. The boundary also uses Mandore railway station as a landmark. | 11,736 |

These are extracts. For an address near a road or crossing, use the side of the road and the direction of travel in the Hindi description. A landmark used to trace a boundary need not lie inside the ward.

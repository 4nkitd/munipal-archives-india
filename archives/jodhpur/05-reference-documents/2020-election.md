# The two Jodhpur jurisdictions in the 2020 municipal election

In the Commission's [statistical book for the six ULB general elections of 2020](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/Upload/6-Municipal%20General%20Election%20Book-2020.pdf), see printed page 25, “Number of Polling Stations”, rows 3 and 4.

| Corporation in the report | Wards | Polling stations | In government buildings | In private buildings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Nagar Nigam Jodhpur North | 80 | 355 | 228 | 127 |
| Nagar Nigam Jodhpur South | 80 | 340 | 163 | 177 |

The two totals of 80 describe two electoral jurisdictions in 2020. They are not two zones of today's 100-ward schedule. The report provides no crosswalk to the new numbers.

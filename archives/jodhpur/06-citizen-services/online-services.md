# Where to start a municipal service request

The [Rajasthan Local Self Government online portal](https://lsgonline.rajasthan.gov.in/) has separate **Know Your UDTax** and **Search Property** options. Under **Our Services**, the department lists property tax (UD tax), mutation/name transfer, trade licence, fire NOC, sewer connection, signage licence and street vendor registration. The home page also offers registration and sign-in.

For a problem with the state portal, its published helpline is **1800-180-4872** and its email is **onlineservices.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in**. These are statewide online-service contacts. The page does not identify a Jodhpur municipal switchboard or specify a Jodhpur fee or processing time.

# An election allocation for Jodhpur

On **12 November 2026** the State Election Commission issued [letter 12031 to the district election officers](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/12031.pdf). It sanctioned an additional ₹1,39,47,000 across ten municipal corporation elections under budget head 2217-80-191-09-00-57.

Appendix 1, row 6, identifies **Jodhpur, office ID 20463**, with **721 polling booths**. The allotment is ₹10,28,000 for travel and daily allowances and ₹67,000 for videography, totalling **₹10,95,000**. The letter restricts the money to election expenditure. This is a district election allocation, not expenditure already incurred or Jodhpur Municipal Corporation's annual operating budget.

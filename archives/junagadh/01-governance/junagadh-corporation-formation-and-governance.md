# Junagadh corporation formation and governing bodies

## Municipal status

Junagadh Municipal Corporation says the former Junagadh Municipality became Junagadh Mahanagar Seva Sadan on 15 September 2002. Its profile gives the municipal area as 57.16 square kilometres and reports 15 wards. The [Gujarat corporation directory](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/corporations.php) lists Junagadh in row 8. The municipal area is not the whole of Junagadh district.

## Governing bodies

The corporation identifies the General Board, Standing Committee and Municipal Commissioner as the three principal authorities under the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. It reports 60 councillor seats and 12 Standing Committee members drawn from the councillors. The Municipal Commissioner heads the administration. These figures describe the structure, not who currently holds each seat. For boundary material, see the corporation's [Ward Boundary page](https://jumc.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/wardboundary).

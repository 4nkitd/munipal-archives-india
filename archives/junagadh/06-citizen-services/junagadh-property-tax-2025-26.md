# Junagadh property tax calculation for 2025-26

## General tax

Junagadh Municipal Corporation calculates general tax by multiplying the property's carpet area in square metres by the base rate and four factors: location, building age, usage and occupancy. The rates below are explicitly labelled 2025-26. They are not the total bill or a statement of rates for later fiscal years.

| Property type | Base rate per square metre |
| --- | ---: |
| Residential | ₹26 |
| Commercial | ₹47 |
| Industrial | ₹42 |

The location factors are 1.30 for category A, 1.10 for B, 1.00 for C and 0.90 for D. The owner-occupied factor is 1.00. For tenanted property, the published factor is 1.20 for residential and 1.30 for non-residential property. The page gives a residential minimum charge of ₹300 and shop minimum general tax of ₹800, ₹1,000 or ₹1,200 for shops under 15, 15 to 30, or above 30 square metres, respectively.

## Other charges and online access

The corporation separately lists water and conservancy charges, fire tax at 1% of general tax and residential sanitation tax of ₹200 per property. It dates its door-to-door collection user charge to 1 April 2025. See the source schedule for the other charges, rebates and rates. The corporation's homepage routes property tax to the [JuMC citizen login](https://jumcerp.gipl.in/account/citizenlogin). The login page was reachable; payment was not tested.

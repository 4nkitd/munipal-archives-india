# Junagadh municipal budget publication index

## Fiscal years listed

Junagadh Municipal Corporation lists six budget attachments on its [Budget Details page](https://jumc.gujarat.gov.in/budgetdetails), under Departments → Account. The entries, in the order shown, cover 2025-26, 2024-25, 2023-24, 2022-23, 2021-22 and 2020-21. The page does not list 2026-27. That omission does not show whether the corporation adopted or published a later budget elsewhere.

The fiscal-year labels are not publication dates. This record describes the HTML index, not the content, adoption dates or figures in the individual budget attachments. The corporation lists [annual financial reports](https://jumc.gujarat.gov.in/annualfinancialreport) separately in the same navigation.

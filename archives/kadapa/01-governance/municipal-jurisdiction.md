# Kadapa Municipal Corporation constitution and jurisdiction

## Formation and enlargement

Kadapa city is the district headquarters of YSR Kadapa district. The district's corporation profile gives this chronology for the city government:

| Date | Record or change | Area stated |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1868 | Kadapa constituted as a municipality | 6.84 km² |
| 17 November 2004 | G.O.Ms.No.481 upgraded it to a municipal corporation | 91.05 km² |
| 17 March 2005 and 30 May 2006 | G.O.Ms.No.125 and G.O.Ms.No.89, MA, are cited for the subsequent enlargement by merger of surrounding gram panchayats | 164.08 km² after enlargement |

The district page does not name the merged gram panchayats, give a separate intermediate area for the 2005 order, or reproduce the boundary schedules. The 164.08 km² figure describes the enlargement reported there. It is not a verified measurement of the 2026 electoral area.

## Corporation office

The [district public utility entry](https://kadapa.ap.gov.in/public-utility/kadapa-municipal-corporation/) gives the corporation's address as **Near Traffic Police Station, R.S. Road, Kadapa 516001** and publishes its contact email. Kadapa district is a separate administrative area; the [2026 ward election record](../02-wards-geography/2026-ward-election-record.md) covers the city corporation only.

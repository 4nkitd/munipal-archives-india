# Kadapa electoral wards in the November 2026 corporation election

## Ward numbers and reservations

The Andhra Pradesh State Election Commission's Proforma X, dated 4 November 2026, lists **66 wards numbered 1 through 66** under district `Y.S.R.Kadapa` and urban local body `Kadapa`. These entries occupy PDF pages 112 through 116. The table groups the reservation labels, normalizing the report's variants `SC(WOMEN)` and `SC (Women)` to `SC-Women`.

| Reservation label | Electoral wards |
| --- | --- |
| BC-General | 38, 39, 41, 42, 43, 44, 46, 48, 50, 53, 54, 62 |
| BC-Women | 3, 11, 17, 18, 31, 37, 47, 52, 55, 56, 60, 61 |
| SC-General | 6, 12, 27, 64 |
| SC-Women | 20, 34, 36, 65 |
| ST-Women | 22 |
| Women-General | 2, 9, 13, 14, 23, 28, 29, 30, 32, 33, 35, 51, 57, 58, 59, 63, 66 |
| Un-Reserved | 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 15, 16, 19, 21, 24, 25, 26, 40, 45, 49 |

## Contested and unanimous wards

The return marks **52 wards** as unanimous. It records polls in wards **6, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 24, 33, 45, 46, 51, 52 and 55**. Ward 15 has both 1 and 2 November in the poll-date column. The [repoll order](../04-notifications-orders/2026-ward-15-repoll.md) applies to polling station 15/2 only.

These are election records, not boundary descriptions. The [city map](https://kadapa.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/kadapa/gis-map) is a separate source. Neither a poll result nor a Sachivalayam label establishes which side of a legal ward boundary a property falls on.

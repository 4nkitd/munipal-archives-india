# Kadapa ward 15 repoll order for polling station 15/2

## Station and schedule

Order **1487/SEC-F1/2026-3**, dated **1 November 2026**, declared the poll held that day at **polling station 15/2 in ward 15** void. The Andhra Pradesh State Election Commission cited a report from the Collector and District Election Authority, YSR Kadapa District, dated 1 November, that reported booth capturing at that station.

The order directed that a fresh poll for the offices of mayor and ward member take place at station 15/2 on **2 November 2026 from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.** It did not order a fresh poll across the whole ward or city. The Commission cited Article 243ZA of the Constitution and Section 59A(2)(a) of the Municipal Corporations Act, 1955, and directed use of the 2026 mayor and ward member election rules.

## Local instructions

The District Election Authority and the Municipal Commissioner, acting as Additional Election Authority, were to notify contesting candidates in writing and publicize the order in the polling area and in print and electronic media. The order required indelible ink on the voter's left middle finger. The [ward return](../02-wards-geography/2026-ward-election-record.md) lists both polling dates for ward 15.

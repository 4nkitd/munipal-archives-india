# Kakinada Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## City and district

The [CDMA city profile data](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/69) identifies Kakinada Municipal Corporation in Kakinada district, Andhra Pradesh. It gives 2005 as the establishment year, an area of 31.69 square kilometres and a population of 325,985. The profile does not give a reference year for the population. These are corporation profile figures, not district totals or a gazetted description of the city limits.

The [municipal contact page](https://kakinada.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/kakinada/contact) places the office at Stree Shakthi Bahavan, Mehar Nagar, behind Saradadevi Temple, Kakinada 533003. The city lists toll-free number `1800-4250325`, landline `0884-2357722` and email `kkd_mplcorp@yahoo.com` in its [published contact data](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbcontacts/69).

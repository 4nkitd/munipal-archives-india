# Kakinada ward electoral rolls and election schedule, 2026

## Electoral rolls and ward count

Notification No. 866/SEC-F1/2026 of 17 October 2026 named Kakinada as one of four corporations whose ward photo rolls had not been published with the earlier group. It authorized the municipal commissioner to prepare and publish the rolls with the prescribed Form-I notice on 24 October 2026. The qualifying date was 1 January 2026. The commissioner was to use the latest Assembly rolls as updated with reference to that date, but only for areas within the corporation.

The [CDMA profile](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/69) lists 66 election wards, 30 revenue wards and 101 ward secretariats. These are separate counts. The roll notification does not enumerate the wards or describe their boundaries.

## Election programme

In [Notifications No. 1490/SEC-F1/2026-1 and -2](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/4%20MCs%20Notifications.pdf), dated 12 November 2026, the commission called on Kakinada's registered voters to elect a mayor and ward members. The published programme set polling, if needed, for 26 November from 7 am to 5 pm, any re-poll for 27 November in the same hours, and counting for 30 November from 8 am. These are dates in the notification, not confirmation of polling or results.

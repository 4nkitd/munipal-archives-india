# Kakinada property tax lookup and assessment services

## Find an assessment

Open [Know Your Dues](https://kakinada.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/citizen/search/search-searchForm.action) from the corporation's site. The form says to search by any one of four fields: Assessment Number, Old Assessment Number, Owner Name or Door Number. This is the lookup for an existing assessment. The city lists [Self Assessment](https://kakinada.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/create/createProperty-newForm.action?applicationSource=online) separately.

## Other tax records and requests

The city portal also links to the property tax calculator, revision petitions, mutation, vacancy remission and a proceedings search. The [Kakinada district's K M C page](https://kakinada.ap.gov.in/k-m-c/) separately links to the corporation's vacant land tax balance pending list dated 22 October 2025. That dated list is a historical notice, not a substitute for checking the present dues on an assessment.

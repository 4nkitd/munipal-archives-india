# Kalaburagi City Corporation: office and legal scope

## Municipal office

The district lists Kalaburagi City Corporation at Near Jagat Circle, Main Road, Kalaburagi 585102. The office telephone is 08472-278675; its published email is comm_glb@yahoo.com. The district links the [corporation website](http://www.gulbargacity.mrc.gov.in/). Shahabad City Municipal Council and the town councils at Afzalpur, Aland, Chincholi, Chittapur, Jewargi, Sedam and Wadi have separate district directory entries.

## State law

[India Code](https://indiacode.gov.in/items/a6d7538a-bc38-4496-b2db-aa89d0ad2f03) lists *The Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976* under Karnataka's Urban Development Department. Its long title concerns the establishment of municipal corporations in Karnataka. Although the short title uses 1976, the catalogue identifies the law as Act 14 of 1977, issued on 31 May 1977 and in force from 10 June 1977. Those dates apply to the state Act. The directory identifies the local corporation but does not supply its founding notification or a schedule of its limits.

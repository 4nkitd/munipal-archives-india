# Kalaburagi corporation wards in the 2011 Census

## Reading the ward codes

The [Census workbook](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/6770/download/9847/DDW_PCA2925_2011_MDDS%20with%20UI.xlsx) identifies the town as Gulbarga (M Corp.), code 803207. Its town row gives 100,969 households and 533,587 residents, including 271,660 males and 261,927 females. The catalogue entry dates from 19 January 2021; the figures describe the 2011 Census.

The workbook also gives a combined corporation and outgrowth total of 543,147. The three outgrowth rows, numbered 56 to 58, are outside the corporation subtotal and are excluded below. Ward numbers below identify census units. They do not describe street boundaries or establish today's electoral roll.

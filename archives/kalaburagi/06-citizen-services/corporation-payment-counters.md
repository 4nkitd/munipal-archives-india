# Kalaburagi property tax and water bill counters

## Which document to bring

| Municipal charge | Document to present | What the centre issues |
|---|---|---|
| Property tax on built-up or vacant land | Challan issued by Gulbarga City Corporation | BE1 receipt after a successful payment |
| Monthly corporation water charges | Water bill or receipt | BE1 receipt after the operator records the transaction |

Karnataka One lists cash, cheque and demand draft for both counter services. The corporation issues the property-tax challan; the centre collects the tax shown on it. The water charge is a separate monthly bill. The service guide does not specify property-tax rates or a water tariff.

# Kalyan-Dombivli municipal jurisdiction and office

## District register

Entry 3 in Thane district’s Municipal Corporations register links the [Kalyan Dombivli Municipal Corporation](https://kdmc.gov.in/kdmc/CitizenHome.html). The district lists Mohone under this corporation in its [Aaple Seva Kendra directory](https://thane.nic.in/en/aaple-seva-kendra-2/). The listed centre is on Ambivali Station Road in Ambivali West.

## Correspondence

The district [telephone directory](https://thane.nic.in/en/telephone-directory/) lists `commissioner.kdmc@gmail.com` for the corporation under Corporations & Councils.

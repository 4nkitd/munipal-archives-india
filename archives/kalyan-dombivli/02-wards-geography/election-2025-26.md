# Kalyan-Dombivli wards and seats in the 2025-26 general election

## Declared results

Row 3, labelled “Kalyan-Dombivali”, records 31 electoral wards, 488 contesting candidates and results declared for all 122 seats. The [commission’s publication register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the final party position to 16 January 2026.

| Party or SEC category | Seats won |
|---|---:|
| SS | 53 |
| BJP | 50 |
| SS-UBT | 11 |
| MNS | 5 |
| INC | 2 |
| NCP-SP | 1 |
| **Total** | **122** |

The table reports corporation-wide totals. It does not identify which ward covers a particular address.

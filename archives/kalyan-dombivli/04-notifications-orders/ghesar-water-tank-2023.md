# Ghesar land order for a municipal water tank

## Allotment register, entry 22

Thane district lists Kalyan Dombivali Municipal Corporation as the recipient for Ghesar, survey number 112. The register describes the purpose as the centrally sponsored Amrit Jalkumbh Yojana and the terms as “free of charge”.

The [order linked by the district](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s31afa34a7f984eeabdbb0a7d494132ee5/uploads/2025/05/2025052612.pdf) is listed as **Revenue/K-1/T-7/Ghesar-Kalyan/Advance Possession/Amrut Scheme-Construction of Water Tank/SR-06/2022**, dated 14 February 2023. This is a land order, not a certificate that the tank was built.

# Kalyan-Dombivli panchayat transfers in the 2015-16 district accounts

## Opening balance adjustment

Note 1 below table 4.2 of Thane’s *Socio-Economic Review 2016* (printed page 17) identifies 26 gram panchayats from the Kalyan and Ambarnath blocks that transferred to Kalyan-Dombivli Municipal Corporation. The district excluded their opening balances from the opening balance it reported for Thane Zilla Parishad in 2015-16. The note below table 4.4 repeats the adjustment.

The review provides this explanation for readers comparing accounts across years. The note does not itemise the transferred panchayats, give a transfer date or specify a sum credited to the corporation. The [district publication register](https://thane.nic.in/en/document-category/statistical-report/) dates the review to 27 September 2017.

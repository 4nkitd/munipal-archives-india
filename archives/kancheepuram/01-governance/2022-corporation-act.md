# Kancheepuram City Municipal Corporation Act, 2022

## Establishment and district

Tamil Nadu published Act 2 of 2022 in Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 56, Part IV, Section 2, on 21 January 2022. The Act appears on printed pages 17 to 22. Section 1(3) deems it to have come into force on **21 October 2021**. Section 13 repeals the Kancheepuram City Municipal Corporation Ordinance, 2021, with a saving provision. The [2021 ordinance and explanatory statement](https://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2021/494_Ex_IV_2.pdf) describe upgrading the Kancheepuram Special Grade Municipality with its existing territorial limits.

The [Kancheepuram district administration's local bodies directory](https://kancheepuram.nic.in/about-district/administrative-setup/local-bodies/) identifies Kancheepuram as the district's corporation. The [state Directorate of Municipal Administration website directory](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/list-of-municipality-websites-ulb-locator/) uses the name Kancheepuram City Municipal Corporation.

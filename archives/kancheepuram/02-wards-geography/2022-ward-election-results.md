# Kancheepuram corporation ward election results, 2022

## Election edition and ward coverage

The Tamil Nadu State Election Commission's Kancheepuram corporation result covers wards **1 to 51**. The state set **19 February 2022** as the ordinary urban local body poll date in [Gazette Extraordinary No. 99](https://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2022/99_Ex_II_1.pdf). The results file is marked 2022 and does not give a separate publication day.

| Ward | Electors | Ballots cast | Turnout | Valid | Invalid | Winning votes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5,445 | 3,702 | 67.99% | 3,701 | 1 | 1,750 |
| 2 | 4,795 | 3,086 | 64.36% | 3,086 | 0 | 1,304 |

Ward 1 is marked general (women). The results do not define the boundaries or establish who holds each seat today. The Commission identifies the Returning Officer's Forms 23, 25 and 26 and declaration in Form 27 as final, as applicable.

# Kancheepuram online municipal services

## State portal and corporation office

The city's E-Governance page lists property tax, profession tax, non-tax items, water supply, underground drainage, trade licensing, and birth and death registration. The [Tamil Nadu Directorate of Municipal Administration](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/) lists the [state municipal ePay portal](https://tnurbanepay.tn.gov.in/) for payments and certificate services in corporations outside Chennai.

The state portal shows Quick Payment, Payment Verification Status, Service Request Status Verification and Download Certificate. The [corporation contact page](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/kancheepuram/contact-us-2/) lists the Commissioner Office on Hospital Road, Kancheepuram 631502, including the two office phone channels in this record. No payment or application was submitted.

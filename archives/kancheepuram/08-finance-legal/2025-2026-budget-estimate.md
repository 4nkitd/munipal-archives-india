# Kancheepuram budget estimate, 2025-2026

## Approval and estimates

The [corporation's budget register](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/kancheepuram/budget-details-2/) gives **28 March 2025** as the approval date and **16 April 2025** as the upload date. The linked document is titled *Budget Estimate for the Year 2025-2026*. Its income and expenditure abstract reports the following amounts in rupees.

| Fund | Estimated income | Estimated expenditure |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue | ₹2,53,10,47,500 | ₹2,52,99,31,500 |
| Water Supply | ₹4,07,05,91,900 | ₹4,06,70,75,900 |
| Education | ₹13,04,36,000 | ₹12,65,26,000 |
| Total | ₹6,73,20,75,400 | ₹6,72,35,33,400 |

The PDF cover, budget register and detailed schedules identify FY 2025-2026. The extracted abstract heading reads `2023-2026`; the table reproduces the abstract amounts without treating that heading as a separate fiscal period. The abstract does not report audited outturn or allocate these totals to individual wards.

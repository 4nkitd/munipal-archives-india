# Kannur Municipal Corporation: municipal scope and office

## Corporation, district and cantonment

Kannur district's administrative directory lists one corporation, Kannur. The same site has a [separate directory for municipalities](https://kannur.nic.in/en/muncipalities/). The [State Election Commission's corporation directory for the district](https://sec.kerala.gov.in/public/cnstncy/lb/da39a3ee5e6b4b0d3255bfef95601890afd2/U/C) likewise identifies Kannur as the corporation and links its municipal ward list. The 56 wards in that list belong to the corporation, not to the entire district.

The [Local Self Government Department's civil registration guidance](https://lsgd.kerala.gov.in/en/civil-registrations/birth-and-death-registration/) names the Kannur Cantonment Board separately from Kerala's municipal corporations. For birth or death registration, the local registration unit is the one in whose area the event took place. A shared city name does not make a cantonment record a corporation record.

## Legal basis and office

The [Local Self Government Department's Acts and Rules page](https://lsgd.kerala.gov.in/en/regulations-and-policies/policies-acts-and-rules/) lists the Kerala Municipality Act, 1994. The [State Election Commission's delimitation guidance](https://sec.kerala.gov.in/portal/kc/delimit) cites section 69 of that Act and Article 243R of the Constitution. Councillors are chosen by direct election from territorial wards. A published Gazette order of the Delimitation Commission, rather than a ward name or office address, is the legal reference for a ward boundary.

The [district public utility directory](https://kannur.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/corporation/) gives the corporation address as SPCA Road, Talap, Kannur, Kerala 670001. The [corporation portal](https://kannurcorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en) links to K-SMART for municipal transactions. No change-of-limits or formation Gazette order is reproduced in this record.

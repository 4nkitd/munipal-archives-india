# K-SMART and civil registration in Kannur

## Choose the registration unit

The [Kannur Municipal Corporation website](https://kannurcorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en) directs residents to [K-SMART](https://ksmart.lsgkerala.gov.in/) for certificates, tax payments, applications and tracking. Kerala LSGD's birth and death registration guide says to register with the unit in whose area the event occurred. It lists the Kannur Cantonment Board as a separate local registration unit. For corporation events, LSGD identifies K-SMART, an Akshaya Kendra or the corporation front office as application channels.

| Need | What LSGD says to use |
| --- | --- |
| Report a birth or death | Report within 21 days to the unit responsible for the place of occurrence. |
| Find an existing birth certificate | In K-SMART Certificate Search, give the date of birth and the mother's name or its first three letters in English. |
| Find an existing death certificate | In K-SMART Certificate Search, give the date of death and the name of the deceased. |
| Seek late registration after 30 days and within one year | LSGD identifies the Municipal Secretary as the sanctioning authority for a municipal corporation. For a delay of more than a year, LSGD names the Revenue Divisional Officer. |

## Other municipal transactions

The [state K-SMART service list](https://lsgd.kerala.gov.in/en/services/digital-services-description-add-information-kerala-mission/k-smart/) identifies property tax, building permission, business licensing, digital file management and public grievance redressal among the urban modules. LSGD also links to [public file tracking](https://ksmart.lsgkerala.gov.in/ui/file-management/public/file-tracking). Kannur's municipal home page links to the separate [welfare pension portal](https://welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in/) and the state's local-government project viewer; those are not certificate-search options.

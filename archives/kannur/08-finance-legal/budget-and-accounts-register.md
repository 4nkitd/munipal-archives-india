# Kannur municipal budget and annual accounts register

## Which fiscal years the corporation lists

The corporation's Citizen menu lists Budget Reports and AFS Reports separately. A budget is the place to look for estimates and appropriations. An annual financial statement is the place to look for the accounts for a completed year. The two series overlap, but the labels on the official site are not interchangeable.

| Fiscal year | Budget page | Annual financial statement (AFS) page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2023-24 | Not listed in this menu | [AFS 2023-2024](https://kannurcorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/AFS2023-2024) |
| 2024-25 | [Budget 2024-2025](https://kannurcorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/Budget2024-2025) | [AFS 2024-2025](https://kannurcorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/AFS2024-2025) |
| 2025-26 | [Budget 2025-26](https://kannurcorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/Budget2025-26) | [AFS 2025-2026](https://kannurcorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/AFS2025-2026) |
| 2026-27 | [Budget 2026-27](https://kannurcorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/Budget2026-27) | Not listed in this menu |

## What the public register establishes

The title pages checked show the year and report type, but do not expose amounts, issue dates, adoption resolutions or an audit opinion. The dates in the table are fiscal-year labels. The website also has a separate Beneficiary List category, which should not be read as part of the annual accounts.

For project-specific information, use the [state project details viewer](https://plan.lsgkerala.gov.in/formulation/Public.aspx) linked in the corporation's Services menu and select the relevant local body and plan. That is a separate source from the annual budget or accounts. The district's [public utility directory](https://kannur.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/corporation/) lists the municipal office at SPCA Road, Talap, Kannur 670001 as the place to direct a request for a corporation record.

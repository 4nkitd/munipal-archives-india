# Kanpur municipality, corporation and elected council

## Formation and early public works

Kanpur established its municipality on 22 November 1861 under the chairmanship of the district magistrate. Lala Shiv Prasad was the first vice-chairman of the municipal committee. The corporation dates the city's first drainage system to 1872 and its first protected water supply to 1892. Between those two projects, in 1879, the municipality built the town hall at Cooperganj for approximately ₹2,16,000. Kanpur became a municipal corporation in 1959. Ram Ratan Gupta was its first mayor.

## Which authority does the council represent?

The corporation describes a municipal area of 403 square kilometres divided into 110 electoral wards. Each ward elects one representative; the State Election Commission conducts the elections. These figures describe the corporation, not the whole of Kanpur Nagar district.

The [Kanpur Development Authority](https://www.kdaindia.co.in/en/index.php) publishes planning material, including the draft Kanpur Master Plan 2031 and invitations for objections. That plan is not the corporation's electoral ward map. [Kanpur Cantonment Board](https://kanpur.cantt.gov.in/) publishes separately under the Ministry of Defence; the corporation's councillor directory does not represent the cantonment board.

# Kanpur ward boundaries and reservation notices for 2017

## Choosing the historical notice

| Question about the 2017 wards | Entry on the municipal index |
|---|---|
| Which area did a ward cover? | [परिसीमन अधिसूचना -2017](https://kmc.up.nic.in/parisiman2017/parisimaanAdhisuchna06.09.2017.pdf), delimitation notification |
| Which seats were reserved? | [वार्ड आरक्षण अधिसूचना -2017](https://kmc.up.nic.in/parisiman2017/finalwardrkshaninfo2017.pdf), ward reservation notification |

The page identifies the year of both notices. Its HTML does not list individual neighbourhoods, reservation categories or boundary descriptions. The [mayor and corporators page](https://kmc.up.nic.in/mayor-corporators.html) answers a different question: it lists councillors by ward number, from 1 to 110. Its address column is the councillor's address, not a description of the ward. The directory gives no election year, so it cannot by itself establish whether the 2017 boundaries still apply.

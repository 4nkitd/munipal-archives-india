# Kanpur municipal services and complaint channels

## Match the problem to the municipal function

| Issue | Responsibility on the corporation's duties page |
|---|---|
| Uncollected household waste | Solid waste management covers road sweeping, household waste collection and transport to the dumping yard. |
| Broken street light | Lighting operates and maintains street lights and installs new lighting systems. |
| Damaged road or main drain | Civil engineering constructs and repairs roads. Sewerage and drainage maintains the main sewer and drainage lines under arterial roads. |
| Property assessment or change of owner in tax records | Assessment and collection handles assessment of land and buildings, mutation and property tax collection. |
| Old birth or death registration | Central records keeps old registrations, as well as approved building plans. |

## Where to file

The [municipal homepage](https://kmc.up.nic.in/) gives **18001805124** as the toll-free complaint line. It links the [citizen grievance system](https://online.kanpursmartcity.org/) separately from [Uttar Pradesh Jansunwai (IGRS)](https://jansunwai.up.nic.in/). For a tax payment rather than a complaint, use the homepage's **ऑनलाइन संपत्ति कर** link. For a birth or death registration, the homepage points to the Civil Registration System.

# Kanpur municipal budgets, balance sheets and audit reports

## Which financial document to use

| Question | Municipal register entry |
|---|---|
| What did the corporation originally budget? | The budget section lists an original budget (मूल बजट) for each financial year from 2010-11 through 2025-26. |
| Did the estimates change? | The same section lists revised budgets alongside the originals for 2020-21 through 2024-25. |
| What appears in the audited accounts? | The audit section lists balance sheets. For 2022-23 it distinguishes a consolidated balance sheet from an audited balance sheet. For 2024-25 and 2025-26 it separately labels standalone audited balance sheets. |
| Where are the annual audit reports? | The internal audit section lists annual audit reports for 2014-15, 2015-16, 2019-20 and 2020-21. |

For 2018-19 the budget section also lists a service-level standards declaration and a statement comparing three years of audited or actual figures with estimated income and expenditure for 2018-19. These are separate entries from that year's original budget. The years in this register describe accounting periods; the HTML labels do not give adoption or publication dates.

# Kapurthala municipal election programme, May 2026

## Corporation and timetable

Punjab Gazette Extraordinary notification `SEC/ME/SAM (CME)/2026/832` lists Kapurthala in the municipal corporation part of Annexure I with 50 wards. Sultanpur Lodhi appears separately on the council and nagar panchayat side of the annexure. The orders cover the listed local bodies, not every municipality in Kapurthala district. They do not set the boundaries of the corporation or its wards.

The Commission issued the notification under section 35 of the Punjab State Election Commission Act, 1994, and fixed poll hours under section 57. It made the Model Code of Conduct effective immediately in the jurisdictions holding elections, until completion of the poll.

| Event | Scheduled date and time |
| --- | --- |
| Filing of nominations | 13 to 15 May, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m.; last date 16 May, up to 3 p.m. |
| Scrutiny | 18 May |
| Withdrawal deadline | 19 May, 3 p.m. |
| Poll, where necessary | 26 May, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. |
| Counting | 29 May |
| Completion of the election process | By 1 June |

The programme and annexure appear on printed pages 2331 to 2333 of the gazette dated 13 May 2026.

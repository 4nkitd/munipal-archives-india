# Kapurthala municipal election results, May 2026

## Results for Kapurthala city

The State Election Commission's Municipal Corporation Final Result Sheet lists the corporation of Kapurthala city in row 5. The poll date printed on the sheet is 26 May 2026. The [SEC results index](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/en/result-municipal-election-2026/) dates the sheet to 29 May 2026. All 50 ward results were declared.

| Party or affiliation | Wards won |
| --- | ---: |
| Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) | 11 |
| Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) | 3 |
| Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP) | 0 |
| Indian National Congress (INC) | 31 |
| Independent (IND) | 2 |
| Shiromani Akali Dal (SAD) | 3 |
| Total | 50 |

This is a seat tally for Kapurthala city, not for Phagwara or the whole district. It does not give vote shares or decide who holds the mayor's office. The [June gazette of elected members](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/kapurthala.pdf) identifies ward winners separately.

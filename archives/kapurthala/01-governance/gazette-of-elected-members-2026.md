# Kapurthala elected ward members, June 2026 gazette

## Kapurthala corporation entries

The State Election Commission dated notification `SEC/ME/SAM(CME-RN)/2026/886` to 8 June 2026. The Punjab Gazette Extraordinary published it on 9 June. The notification invokes section 71 of the Punjab State Election Commission Act, 1994, and lists members elected for Kapurthala corporation wards 1 to 50 on PDF pages 1 to 3. PDF pages 4 and 5 contain the separate notification `SEC/ME/SAM(CME-RN)/2026/887` for Municipal Council Sultanpur Lodhi.

| Kapurthala ward | Elected member | Party or affiliation | Category printed in gazette |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Deepak Sulwan | INC | General |
| 2 | Sukhwinder Kaur | INC | Women |
| 3 | Rajbir Singh | INC | General |

The examples above come from the first page. This gazette establishes the declared winners, not their present tenure or boundaries of the wards. Use the ward column, not member addresses, to locate an entry.

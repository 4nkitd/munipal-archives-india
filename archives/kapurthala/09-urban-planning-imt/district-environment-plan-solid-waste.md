# Kapurthala corporation solid waste baseline and targets

## Reported collection and segregation

The District Administration's [District Environment Plan](https://kapurthala.gov.in/district-environment-plan-of-kapurthala/) identifies Municipal Corporation Kapurthala separately from Municipal Corporation Phagwara and the Kapurthala Cantonment Board. In its solid waste table, the city corporation is listed with a population figure of 98,914 and estimated generation of 87 metric tonnes per day. The population figure is from this plan's waste table, not an updated census figure.

| Topic | What the plan reports | Printed page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Segregation | Baseline of 85%. Target of 100% by 31 March 2022, with ward camps, awareness activities and compartmented collection carts. | 14 |
| Door-to-door collection | 50 wards; five tractor trolleys, no Tata Ace vehicles and 125 rickshaw carts in the listed arrangement. | 17 |
| Waste transfer points | Ten available against ten required. | 20 |
| Composting by bulk generators | Eighteen identified, none composting on site at the reported baseline. The plan lists 30 June 2022 for follow-up by the municipal body and the Punjab Pollution Control Board. | 20 |
| Central wet waste treatment | Fifteen compost pits provided against fifteen required; the plan calls for their effective use. | 21 |

The linked copy and its landing page do not state a publication date. The dated targets are part of the historical plan, not proof that the work was completed or that the same facilities operate today.

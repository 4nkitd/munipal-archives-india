# Constitution of Karaikkudi City Municipal Corporation

## Final declaration

Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 257, Part II, Section 2, page 1, contains G.O. Ms. No. 102, notification II(2)/MAWS/743(a-1)/2024. The Governor declared the corporation under section 3(2)(d) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998. The government published the order on 10 August 2024; paragraph 2 brought it into force on 12 August.

## Area at formation

The order included the former Karaikkudi Municipality, Kandanur and Kottaiyur town panchayats, and five village panchayats: Sankarapuram, Kovilur, Iluppakudi, Ariyakudi and Thalakavur (Managiri). All eight local areas entered in full.

The order provides for division into wards for the next ordinary election. It does not number the new corporation wards.

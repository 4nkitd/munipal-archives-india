# Property cards for landowners in Karaikudi

## Scope of the notice

On 18 February 2026, Sivaganga district announced that landowners in Karaikudi Corporation would receive property cards combining their land property details and property tax details. The announcement describes planned issue of the cards; it does not confirm delivery to any individual owner.

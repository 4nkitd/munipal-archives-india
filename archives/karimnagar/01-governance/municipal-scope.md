# Karimnagar Municipal Corporation: municipal history and jurisdiction

## Municipal limits and planning history

Karimnagar became the district headquarters in 1905 when the administration moved from Elgandal. That change concerned the district; the city did not acquire corporation status until 2005. The municipal history records three intervening planning and boundary facts:

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1976 | Approval of the first General Town Plan (GTP), with a planning horizon of 1991. |
| 1987 | Merger of Ramnagar and Rampur gram panchayats into the municipality. |
| 2005 | Upgrade of the municipality to a municipal corporation. |

The plan horizon is not a date of annexation. Nor does the merger identify either former panchayat with a particular electoral ward. The 2026 State Election Commission return records the later corporation election separately from those of other urban bodies in Karimnagar district.

# Karimnagar Municipal Corporation: ward reservations and votes in 2026

## Reading the electoral return

The Telangana State Election Commission's 2026 Proforma X reports each candidate's votes and the reservation and ballot figures for each ward. The Karimnagar Municipal Corporation block occupies PDF pages 130 to 140, serial numbers 1185 to 1250. Use the ward column for the corporation division; the serial number runs across the state. The [gazette notification](https://tsec.gov.in/preview.do?fileName=L1VwbG9hZHMvVFNFQy9wZGYvZ2F6ZXR0ZV9vZl8xMjNfdWxiX3dhcmRfZWxlY3Rpb25zXzUxOC5wZGY=) for these elections is No.150/TGSEC-ULBs/2026, dated 27 January 2026.

The table transcribes the report's reservation code and three ballot columns. W and G are the women's and general categories within the indicated reservation. UR means unreserved. The poll column excludes postal ballots; the valid vote column includes them. These figures concern the 2026 poll, not ward boundaries.

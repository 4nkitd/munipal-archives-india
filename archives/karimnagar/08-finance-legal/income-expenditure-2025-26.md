# Karimnagar Municipal Corporation: income and expenditure, 2025–26

## Accounting period and funds

The Commissioner and Director of Municipal Administration reports income and expenditure for 1 April 2025 through 31 March 2026. The statement separates the Municipal General Fund and Capital Project Fund. The figures below use the combined total column, in rupees. Tax revenue and fees are components of income, not additions to the total.

| Account head or subtotal | Amount (₹) |
|---|---:|
| Tax revenue (110) | 428,461,932.20 |
| Fees and user charges (140) | 289,790,153.28 |
| Total income | 814,594,506.48 |
| Total expenditure before subsequent adjustments | 679,716,448.48 |
| Surplus before depreciation and prior-period items | 134,878,058.00 |
| Depreciation (272) | 186,711,284.00 |
| Prior-period item (280) | 105,300.00 |
| Net balance carried to municipal fund | -62,561,640.00 |

## Reconciliation

The positive operating subtotal did not carry through to the final balance. The statement deducts ₹46,927 in provisions and write-offs, ₹10,576,187 in miscellaneous expenses and ₹186,711,284 in depreciation. A further ₹105,300 prior-period item brings the net deficit to ₹62,561,640. The General Fund ended with a ₹75,900,635.22 surplus and the Capital Project Fund with a ₹138,462,275.22 deficit.

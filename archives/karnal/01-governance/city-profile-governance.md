# City profile and governance of Municipal Corporation Karnal

## Formation and municipal limits

Karnal is the district headquarters of Karnal district, Haryana, on NH-44, the Grand Trunk Road corridor between Delhi and Chandigarh. The Haryana Government constituted Municipal Corporation Karnal on 17 March 2010, as recorded in the [State Election Commission notification on the creation of seven corporations](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2022/07/2022071148.pdf). The corporation operates under the [Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Act).

The [Haryana Department of Urban Local Bodies key figures table](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists a municipal area of 90.92 sq km and a population of 4,08,287. The table cites November 2026 population data from the Citizen Resource Information Department and September 2026 area data from the department's GIS database. These are corporation figures, not a census count or a district total.

The Union government selected Karnal in June 2017 in Round 3 of the [Smart Cities Mission](https://www.mohua.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/smart-cities-czM5cTMtQWa). The [district profile](https://karnal.gov.in/) identifies the city's highway corridor as NH-44.

## Elected council and administration

Karnal has 20 municipal wards, numbered 1 to 20. Voters elected the mayor separately from their ward councillors in the 2 March 2025 general election. Renu Bala Gupta of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won the mayoral election. The State Election Commission published the mayor and ward results in Gazette notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272 on 20 March 2025, the date used for this record. The [elected members directory](https://mckarnal.co.in/elected-members) lists the councillor for each ward.

Saloni Sharma, IAS, is the municipal commissioner. The corporation's [incumbency board](https://mckarnal.co.in/incumbency-board) lists her tenure from 26 June 2026. The corporation's [organizational directory](https://mckarnal.co.in/organizational-structure) and the [Karnal district telephone directory](https://karnal.gov.in/telephone-directory/) also list her as commissioner. The mayor heads the elected council and the commissioner leads the municipal administration.

## Headquarters and public contacts

The corporation's [contact page](https://mckarnal.co.in/contact-us) lists its headquarters as Municipal Corporation Office, Sector 12, Karnal, Haryana 132001, India.

# Municipal Corporation Karnal elected council, March 2025

## Election and mayor

Karnal voted on 2 March 2025, and results were declared on 12 March 2025. The State Election Commission, Haryana notified the elected mayor and ward members on 20 March 2025 in notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272.

Renu Bala Gupta of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won the unreserved mayoral seat with 83,630 votes. Manoj Wadhwa of the Indian National Congress received 58,271 votes, a margin of 25,359.

## Ward results

The BJP won 15 of the 20 ward seats. Sankalp Bhandari in Ward 8 and Sanjeev Kumar Mehta in Ward 11 were elected unopposed. Independents won the other five seats. Names, parties and seat categories below follow the SEC notification and describe the election result, not later party affiliations.

# Karnal wards, sectors and localities

## Delimitation source and limits

The Haryana Gazette of 29 December 2024, No. 211-2024/Ext., publishes the final delimitation of Municipal Corporation Karnal under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994. It refers to the preliminary notification dated 14 December 2024 and lists wards 1 to 20. The DULB wardbandi index lists the final order with an upload date of 30 December 2024. That upload date is not the Gazette date.

The English schedule refers to drawing MC Knl/WB-2024/02 dated 19 December 2024. The Hindi text instead references MC Knl/WB-2024/01 dated 22 February 2024. The two language versions also differ in some locality lists. This record and `../wards.json` use the English schedule for locality allocation. In particular, its English list puts Baljeet Enclave in Ward 2, Sector 32 in Ward 2 and Sector 33 in Ward 3. The Hindi Ward 1 list also names Baljeet Enclave. This discrepancy requires checking the signed plan for an individual address.

The JSON retains the reference archive's ward fields. `zone` and `division` are `null` because the delimitation schedule does not establish an administrative zone/division mapping. Empty locality arrays mean no entry was extracted for that field, not that the ward has no settlements. Sector entries identify sectors named in the area lists, not a guarantee that every plot in a sector belongs to the same ward. No councillor or engineering assignment is inferred.

## Model Town and the Karna Lake/NDRI corridor

Model Town is explicitly in Ward 11. The same area list includes NDRI, IARI, ICAR, DWR, CSSRI, the Kalpana Chawla Medical College campus, Sector 31 and Sector 12 Parts 1 and 2. The Ward 11 perimeter uses the Western Jamuna Canal, NH-44, the Sector 12/13 road, Old GT Road and Mall/Kachawa Road. These are useful address references, but the institutional abbreviations are retained as printed rather than treated as separate municipal authorities.

Haryana Tourism lists Karna Lake and Oasis at Uchana on NH-44. The lake is also commonly written as Karan Lake. This northern tourism corridor should not be treated as a single electoral ward with NDRI. The Gazette names Oasis Resort in Ward 1 and Uchana in Ward 20, while NDRI is in Ward 11. Uchani in Ward 1 and Uchana in Ward 20 are distinct spellings in the area lists and are retained separately.

The English area list does not expressly name the Karna Lake parcel. This archive therefore does not assign the lake itself to a numbered ward. A tourism address at Uchana is not enough to resolve a boundary that follows NH-44 and canal edges. Consult the signed delimitation map for the specific lake/resort parcel.

## Old city and Kunjpura Road

The old city spans several wards. Ward 15 contains the core gate-and-bazaar localities, including Jundla Gate, Karan Gate, Kot Mohalla, Chaura Bazar, Holi Mohalla and Kalandri Gate. Ward 13 includes Old Anaj Mandi, Ibrahim Mandi, Topkhana and Dyalpura Gate. Ward 14 includes New Ramesh Nagar, Janakpuri and Ravi Dass Pura. Ward 7 includes the Subhash Gate and Arjun Gate areas, while Ward 12 covers the Old Tehsil, Char Chaman, Mall Road and Railway Road side.

Kunjpura Road is not a single-ward locality. Near Old Tehsil, the English boundary descriptions for Wards 7 and 12 use the old BSNL office road and Old Suvidha Vali Gali. Farther northeast, ITI Chowk and Kunjpura Road define part of the Ward 9 boundary and the adjoining Ward 2 boundary towards Sector 32. A business advertising a Kunjpura Road address needs a street number, property ID or map location before its ward can be established.

## Regularised colonies and dated approval evidence

Colony regularisation and electoral inclusion are different records. A colony appearing in wardbandi is not proof that its layout or an extension is approved. The following reports are historical evidence, not a current legal approval certificate.

On 10 May 2018, The Tribune reported that 23 Karnal colonies had been regularised, citing the municipal executive officer. The report named Basant Vihar, Lodha Gamdi, Old Pritampura, Krishna Complex, LIC Colony-II, Palm Colony, SP Colony, DC Colony, Bank Colony, Old Durga Colony, Durga Colony, Narendra Colony, Uttam Nagar, Saraswati Colony, MC Colony, Ashok Vihar, Indira Colony, Lovekush Colony, Pal Nagar, Hakikatnagar, Sanjay Colony, Pal Nagar-I and Saidpura Colony. It also reported a condition requiring residents to deposit development charges. Names are retained as reported, since extensions and similarly named colonies need separate checking.

On 15 June 2024, The Tribune reported that eight of 47 colonies proposed by KMC had been approved and 39 remained pending. It named Kanika Vihar Extension, RK Puram Part-II, Sukhbir Colony, RK Puram Extension, Vijay Nagar, Balram Colony Phoosgarh, Shaktipuram Extension and Vikas Colony. The report quoted Commissioner Abhishek Meena and stated that development works worth Rs 9.27 crore were under execution in the eight colonies.

A further report dated 14 February 2025 said approval of the remaining colonies was still pending and referred to nearly 50 patches as well as the 47-colony proposal. These counts describe the reports' dates. They are not verified totals for November 2026. For a transaction or building application, obtain the colony-specific notification and approved boundary/layout from DULB or the municipal Town Planning Branch. Match the exact colony part, plot and property ID.

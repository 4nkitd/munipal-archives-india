# Municipal Corporation Karnal officers and helplines

## Office and public contact

The [municipal contact page](https://mckarnal.co.in/contact-us) publishes the office address as Municipal Corporation, Sector 12, Karnal, Haryana, PIN 132001. Its main telephone is **0184-2254220**, toll-free number is **18001802700**, and email is **cmc-karnal@ulbharyana.gov.in**. The toll-free number is published without operating hours or a specific service scope.

For online complaints, the municipal website links to the [Haryana urban local bodies grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/). It links property-tax services to the [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/).

These are source-verified published contacts, not telephone-tested numbers. The record date is the research date, not an appointment or transfer date. The municipal pages displayed a site-wide update date of 10 November 2026 when retrieved.

## Karnal Smart City Limited

Dr. Anand Kumar Sharma, IAS is identified as Deputy Commissioner-cum-CEO of Karnal Smart City Limited in [The Tribune's report dated 13 November 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/karnal-administration-fixes-december-deadline-for-smart-city-works/). This is a news-source identification of the CEO role, separate from the municipal roster.

The official [district telephone directory](https://karnal.gov.in/telephone-directory/) confirms Dr. Anand Kumar Sharma as Deputy Commissioner and publishes **0184-2267500**, **dckrl@hry.nic.in**, and **First Floor, Mini Secretariat, Karnal**. These are Deputy Commissioner office contacts, not a verified dedicated Smart City helpdesk or CEO mobile. The JSON uses that office contact with the combined designation and separate branch label.

A current dedicated Smart City telephone, email and office address were not independently verified for this record. Older project documents and third-party corporate listings are not treated as current contact evidence.

## Karnal colony notification of 30 April 2018

The Haryana Urban Local Bodies [town planning notifications register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/TownPlanningNotifications) includes "Karnal and Panipat Notification - 30.04.2018", with a 757 KB PDF. The register shows 4 January 2021 as its listing date. That date is not the notification's issue date.

- Official attachment: [Karnal and Panipat notification](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F1086c956-6268-4900-bf43-9b16b5c83130.pdf).
- Secondary corroboration: [The Tribune, 10 May 2018, "Govt regularises 23 Karnal colonies"](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/haryana/govt-regularises-23-karnal-colonies-586757/).

The newspaper names Basant Vihar, Lodha Gamdi, Old Pritampura, Krishna Complex, LIC Colony-II, Palm Colony, SP Colony, DC Colony, Bank Colony, Old Durga Colony, Durga Colony, Narendra Colony, Uttam Nagar, Saraswati Colony, MC Colony, Ashok Vihar, Indira Colony, Lovekush Colony, Pal Nagar, Hakikatnagar, Sanjay Colony, Pal Nagar-I and Saidpura Colony. These names are secondary-source transcriptions, not a verified transcription of the gazette schedule. The report says development charges were a condition.

For a specific property, obtain the notification schedule and approved colony boundary from the town planning branch. This archive does not reproduce historical newspaper charge slabs as a current municipal demand, or infer that every plot bearing a colony name falls inside the notified boundary.

## Later regularized-colony registers

The state [regularized-colony register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) contains notifications extending beyond its page title's 2023 and 2024 date range. It lists schedules dated 25 July 2023, 6 October 2023, 15 December 2023, 1 February 2024, 6 March 2024, 14 November 2024, 18 March 2025 and 22 May 2025. It also lists "Municipalities Status of 836 Colonies Data Notified 2025", published on 13 October 2025.

Those are statewide batch descriptions. Their counts must not be presented as Karnal totals. Karnal entries, colony codes, acreage and notification numbers in those schedules were not independently extracted for this record. Use the state register together with the 2018 Karnal-specific source when checking later inclusion or changes. Ask MC Karnal for the applicable approved map and charge calculation rather than treating a property ID or tax receipt as a substitute for a colony notification.

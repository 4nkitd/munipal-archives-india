# Karnal property tax, NDC, building code and Lal Dora references

## Property tax notifications and current-rate caution

MC Karnal links the Haryana [Property Tax Dues Payment and No Dues Certificate Management System](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) from its official website. The state [property tax notifications register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) distinguishes municipal corporation notifications from those for councils and committees. Karnal is a municipal corporation, so the two classes of notification should not be interchanged.

| State register publication date | Document title | Archival use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-07-31 | Property tax notification 2026 Corporation | Latest corporation notification listed when checked. |
| 2026-05-18 | Notification for one time waiver of interest on the Arrears of property tax pending since year 2010-11 to 2024-2025 | Establishes a listed waiver document, not that its benefit remains open. |
| 2024-10-25 | Compendium of Instructions | Consolidated instructions reference. |
| 2024-03-27 | Regarding Self-Certification of Property Information | Property information verification reference. |
| 2023-09-26 | Standard Operating Procedure for Property Tax Management System; SOP for Creation of Split ID | Administrative process references. |
| 2013-10-11 | Notification Municipal Corporation 11-10-2013 | Historical tax notification, also referenced by MC Karnal's property tax page. |

The [2026 corporation notification PDF](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf) was identified from the official register, but its operative rates, effective date and concessions were not transcribed. No current rupee-per-area tax schedule or waiver deadline is asserted here. The frontmatter date records this register entry's publication date, not a verified commencement date.

## Finding and correcting a Karnal property ID

The state's [NDC portal FAQ](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/FAQ) gives the following process. Register with full name, father or husband name and a ten-digit mobile number, then verify the OTP. After login, open "Search Property" and select the municipality. For a Karnal municipal property, choose Karnal rather than another municipality in the district. Search using the colony, category, plot number, eight-character property ID, old ID, owner or occupier name, or mobile number. "Search on Map" provides a GIS search.

Open "View Details" and check each data section. Select "Yes" and "Verify Details" only when the information is correct. For an error, select "No", choose "Update Details", supply corrected information and supporting documents, and save the draft. The FAQ states that this raises an objection for municipal disposal. Self-certify after the objection is disposed of.

Search existing records before requesting a new ID. The new-ID workflow asks for district, municipality, colony, map location, property details, owner details, floor-wise construction and supporting documents. A subdivision must identify the existing parent ID. The FAQ distinguishes plot subdivision from floor subdivision, and subdivision within a family from subdivision for sale. A tax database change should not be assumed to grant planning permission for subdivision.

## Payments, NDC and published service fees

Under "Make Payment/Generate NDC", enter the property ID, search, choose "Pay All Dues and Generate NDC", verify the demand, choose "Pay Online", verify the OTP and proceed to the payment gateway. The FAQ names property and fire tax, development charges and garbage collection charges in the all-dues workflow. It also permits separate payment of property tax including fire tax, or garbage collection charges. Municipal-office payment is another option.

| New property ID request mode | Published timeline | Fee evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | 10 working days | English FAQ states no charge. |
| Priority | 2 working days from successful receipt of payment | English FAQ and the state's new-PID service sheet state Rs. 1,000; the Hindi FAQ states Rs. 2,500. |

The bilingual fee discrepancy is unresolved. Confirm the payable amount on the current application screen or with MC Karnal before payment. Do not treat either FAQ figure as an independently verified current tariff.

The official [new-PID service sheet](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/f99c58ff-9403-4a82-b14c-c1f7f04aaeef.pdf) identifies the Zonal Taxation Officer for municipal corporations. It lists applicant identity proof, ownership evidence, a location site plan and a building photograph. For Lal Dora properties, its additional evidence list includes old municipal property-register or Panchayat records where owner details are blank, a competent-authority legal-heir certificate in death cases, a court decree registered with revenue authorities, or registry/sale deed, as applicable. An NDC concerns recorded municipal dues; it is not a substitute for ownership documents or building-plan sanction.

## Haryana Building Code 2017

An official ULB-hosted [Haryana Building Code 2017 text](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/Manesar/Images/408719c6-e761-4fdb-a3c1-8caf3787c406.pdf) states that the code applies throughout Haryana from 6 January 2017. This copy includes amendments dated 22 December 2017 and 8 May 2018. Its hosting under a Manesar directory does not limit the statewide extent stated in clause 1.1.

| Provision in the cited edition | Verified subject |
| --- | --- |
| Definition of core area | Includes thickly built-up old town areas and Lal Dora/phirni of villages included in municipalities. |
| Clause 2.1 | Application for erection, re-erection, alteration or demolition, with ownership papers, site and building plans, specifications and applicable structural and service documents. |
| Clause 2.1 | Appointment of an architect or engineer and electronic submission requirements. |
| Clause 4.9 | Damp proof course certification and municipal verification procedure. |
| Clause 4.10 | Occupation certificate application and restriction on occupying new construction without the required certificate, subject to the code's stated provisions. |

The [ULB town planning register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/TownPlanningNotifications) separately lists "Final Haryana Building Code 2017 with letter" and "Adoption of amendments in the Haryana Building Code 2017". Its displayed 4 January 2021 date is a register date, not the code's commencement.

Use the applicable amended text and the site's sanctioned layout, zoning and land-use documents for a building proposal. This archive does not certify that the linked 2018-amended edition contains every later change. It does not publish universal Karnal setbacks, FAR, height limits or current scrutiny fees. Those require the correct plot category and later amendments. Lal Dora's inclusion in the code's core-area definition is not evidence of a blanket exemption from building controls.

## Lal Dora rules and local records

The state property tax register lists "Property Certificate to the occupiers of the properties falling under the Abadi Deh/Lal Dora of Municipal Areas", published 15 March 2024. It also lists the 28 October 2023 instruction concerning anomalies in development charges, agricultural properties, colony masters and Lal Dora areas. The [2024 certificate instruction](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2Feb519b93-6e15-485c-8707-59ad443c6fc9.pdf) is the official policy reference; its detailed eligibility and objection periods were not extracted here.

MC Karnal's [List of Laldora Mukt under Swamitav Yojna of Urban Area](https://mckarnal.co.in/list-laldora-mukt-under-swamitav-yojna-urban-area) provides 25 separate downloads.

| Portal entries | Localities, with portal spellings retained |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 5 | Zarifa Wiran; Uchani; Subhash Gate; Phusgarh; Saidpura |
| 6 to 10 | Mangalpur; Lado Bagri; Kot Mohalla; Kambopura; Kalandri Gate |
| 11 to 15 | Kailash; Jundla Gate; Jhanjhari; Uchana; Holi Mohalla |
| 16 to 20 | Gossi Mohalla; Dholegarh (Uchana); Dayalpura Gate; Daha; Chaura Bajar |
| 21 to 25 | Budhkhera; Banso Gate; Baldi; Arjun Gate; Jatto Gate |

Uchani and Uchana are separate entries and should not be merged. The list mixes villages, gates and urban mohallas. Its existence establishes published locality-level records, not that every occupier has received a final property certificate. Individual property IDs, ownership determinations, areas and certificate status inside these downloads were not transcribed. Check the locality file and municipal property record for the particular property. Do not import Delhi Lal Dora exemptions or certificate procedures into Haryana.

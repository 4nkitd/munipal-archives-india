# Karnal citizen services, RTI and Right to Service timelines

## Service portals and municipal assistance

This is a research compilation dated 22 November 2026, not a new municipal notification. The Department of Urban Local Bodies links to `mckarnal.co.in` as the Karnal corporation website. Its service links identify the following application routes.

| Service | Official application portal | Scope and practical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax and property records | https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/ | Find the property record, check dues and pay property tax. The state RTS schedule also directs new Property ID requests, ownership changes and specified NDC objections here. |
| Birth and death services | https://saralharyana.gov.in/ | MC Karnal lists certificate applications, correction of records, delayed registration and inclusion of a child's name in a birth record. Select the appropriate service rather than treating these as one application. |
| Pet registration | https://saralharyana.gov.in/ | The verified ULB schedule specifically names issue and renewal of dog registration certificates, including dogs brought for show purposes. It does not establish a general registration service for every species of pet. |
| Marriage registration | https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/ | MC Karnal links this portal for marriage registration status and certificates. Consult the portal for the applicable application requirements. |
| RTI application | https://rtiharyana.gov.in/ | The main municipal RTI application card links to Haryana's RTI portal. Confirm the public authority available in the portal before submitting. |
| Municipal grievances | https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/ | State ULB grievance system linked by MC Karnal. |

The municipal office is at Sector 12, Karnal, Haryana 132001. Its published telephone is **0184-2254220**, toll-free number **18001802700**, and email **cmc-karnal@ulbharyana.gov.in**. Keep the application number, payment receipt and any deficiency notice. This compilation does not certify portal transactions, current fees or eligibility checks.

The connection and leakage entries expressly include Karnal among the specified municipal corporations. The schedule directs connections and dog certificates to SARAL, property services to the property portal, solid-waste and streetlight complaints to the Haryana ULB citizen mobile application, and several physical complaints to the municipality.

For delay or refusal, use the designated officer and the two grievance authorities for the particular service. Do not substitute an RTI appeal for an RTS grievance. Birth/death and marriage deadlines are not established by this selected ULB schedule and are not inferred here.

Source: [Department of Urban Local Bodies, Right to Service](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService). Check its current notification and service procedure before relying on a deadline.

## RTI Act, 2005 and appeals

MC Karnal's RTI page identifies access to information held by a public authority, including inspection, notes, extracts, certified copies and certified samples. An RTI request is for existing records, such as a tax assessment, work estimate, payment record or action-taken record. A request to repair a streetlight belongs in the grievance system.

Address a written request to the State Public Information Officer, Municipal Corporation Karnal, at the Sector 12 office, or use the Haryana RTI portal where the authority is available. Specify the records, relevant dates and preferred form of access. The municipal page consulted does not identify a current named SPIO or first appellate authority; confirm the current designation with the office rather than assuming the Commissioner is the SPIO. The municipal footer also points to SARAL for RTI, while its main application card points to `rtiharyana.gov.in`; this inconsistency is retained as a source limitation.

| RTI action | Statutory period |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary decision on a request, section 7(1) | Within 30 days of receipt, subject to statutory provisions |
| Information concerning life or liberty, section 7(1) | Within 48 hours |
| Transfer to the appropriate public authority, section 6(3) | As soon as practicable, no later than 5 days |
| First appeal, section 19(1) | Within 30 days after expiry of the decision period or receipt of the challenged decision |
| Disposal of first appeal, section 19(6) | 30 days, extendable to a total of 45 days with written reasons |
| Second appeal, section 19(3) | Within 90 days of when the decision should have been made or was received |

The first appeal goes to the officer senior in rank to the SPIO within the public authority. A second appeal for this Haryana municipal authority goes to the **State Information Commission, Haryana**, not the Central Information Commission. Delayed appeals may be admitted for sufficient cause. Section 7(6) provides information free of charge where the authority fails to comply with the statutory time limits. Apply Haryana's prescribed fee rules rather than assuming central-government fee amounts.

Sources: [MC Karnal RTI page](https://mckarnal.co.in/rti-act) and [official RTI Act text hosted by the Central Information Commission](https://cic.gov.in/sites/default/files/RTI_English.pdf), sections 6, 7 and 19.

## Grievances and Smart City ICCC contacts

MC Karnal's [public grievances window](https://mckarnal.co.in/public-grievances-window) lists **CM Window**, **CPGRAMS**, the **ULB GRS portal** and **Harpath**. It links to https://cmharyanacell.nic.in/, https://pgportal.gov.in/, https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/ and https://mharisadak.haryana.gov.in/ respectively. Choose the channel appropriate to the responsible department, retain the complaint number, and record the street, nearby landmark and nature of the defect. The official municipal toll-free complaint number is **18001802700**.

Karnal Smart City's Integrated Command and Control Centre, or ICCC, supports traffic and civic-system monitoring. A report dated **17 December 2021** describes integration with police, fire, the municipal corporation and district administration. Reporting on an ICCC inspection dated **7 August 2026** identifies **0184-2200600** as a streetlight complaint helpline and reports instructions to resolve these complaints within **48 hours**. Earlier reporting dated **25 October 2024** also associates that number with Karnal Smart City streetlight complaints and names https://karnallight.in/ and the Karnal Street Light application.

**0184-2200600 is news-reported as a streetlight service contact, not independently verified here as a general ICCC emergency helpline.** Despite some reports calling it toll-free, its format is a Karnal landline and no free-call status is established. The reported 48-hour operational target is separate from the ULB RTS schedule's 10-working-day streetlight replacement entry. No call or complaint submission was made during this research. For immediate life-threatening emergencies, the municipal website directs residents to **112**.

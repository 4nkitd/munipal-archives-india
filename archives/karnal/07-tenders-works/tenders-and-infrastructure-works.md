# Karnal tenders and infrastructure works, 2024-2026

## Scope and evidence

This record covers Municipal Corporation Karnal, also called KMC, and Karnal Smart City Limited, or KSCL. Public Health Engineering Department and HSVP works are identified separately. Newspaper figures below are attributed reports, not certified final expenditure or completion certificates. Tender publication does not establish award or execution, and an official's target date does not establish completion.

The reporting period is 2024-2026. The older LED procurement is retained only as background to the lighting system. No fresh 2024-2026 Karna Lake redevelopment contract value was verified from the sources below.

The 2025 green-belt proposal included bituminous concrete, footpaths, cycle tracks, a central verge, planting, road furniture, thermoplastic markings and zebra crossings. Its route passed Sai Baba Mandir Chowk, Noor Mahal Palace Chowk and Sector 9 Community Centre and market. [S2]

The November 2026 review separately described a concrete road from Meerut Road to Udham Singh Chowk through the Community Centre and Sector 9 market at Rs 16 crore. It also reviewed stormwater reconstruction and modification worth Rs 22.82 crore and underground conversion of overhead 11 kV and LT lines with compact substations at Rs 36 crore. These are figures in that dated review. They must not be treated as a confirmed revision of the 2025 Rs 11.5 crore smart-road estimate without contract-level reconciliation. [S3]

## Integrated Command and Control Centre

The Tribune's 30 March 2024 report located the ICCC on the second floor of the KMC building. It reported 536 CCTV cameras under the Smart City project, including 205 surveillance cameras capturing traffic violations. Red-light violation detection, speed detection and automatic number-plate recognition were described as installed traffic-management functions. These are reported installed assets, not the value of a new 2024 tender. [S4]

On 6 May 2024, Commissioner Abhishek Meena said the ICCC was monitoring traffic and city surveillance, while other services were to be integrated. KMC directed the waste agency to connect vehicle tracking and its waste-management system. The Commissioner also proposed connecting water-supply tubewells and boosting stations through SCADA. That statement establishes an integration plan, not completed commissioning. [S5]

On 11 April 2025, Commissioner Dr Vaishali Sharma again directed complete GPS integration of garbage collection and transport vehicles with ICCC, including services in Assandh, Nilokheri, Taraori and Indri. The renewed direction should not be represented as proof that all integration was already operational. [S6]

## Atal Park and Karna Lake

Haryana eProcurement records an HSVP horticulture tender, reference `HSVP-HORTI-2024-25/94`, tender ID `2024_HBC_369986_1`, published on 16 March 2024. Its title covers green-belt improvements in Sectors 6, 7, 13 and 14 and Atal Park. The specified component is construction of two compost pots at Atal Park in Sector 8 and 9-II, with three years of maintenance and defect liability free of cost. Submission closed on 22 March 2024; bid opening was scheduled for 23 March. The listed work period is 90 days and tender value is `NA`. This is an HSVP tender, not evidence of a KSCL award. [S7]

A 7 July 2024 report documents Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini's inspection of Atal Park and an instruction to provide benches after elderly visitors requested seating. It supplies no verified contract amount for those works. [S8]

For Karna Lake, the earlier KSCL account published on 8 July 2022 identifies development of a recreational zone among Smart City works already constructed. It is historical context, not a new 2024-2026 expenditure figure. [S9] On 28 August 2025, reporting on the Assembly session recorded Karnal MLA Jagmohan Anand seeking beautification of Karna Lake. That demand is not a sanctioned tender or evidence of completed redevelopment. [S10] No lake-upgrade cost is assigned here. Nearby highway bridge and PWD rest-house projects should not be counted as KSCL lake redevelopment.

## Shekhpura Sohana solid-waste plant

On 1 May 2024, The Tribune reported KMC's instruction to Sugam Swachhta to increase processing capacity at Shekhpura Sohana. Deputy Municipal Commissioner Ashok Kumar said the existing four-trommel set processed nearly 250 tonnes in eight hours and the agency had been asked to add another set of four trommels. The article reported nearly 450 tonnes of waste generated collectively by ten urban local bodies and a backlog of 3,800 tonnes. These are dated operational estimates, not an installed design-capacity certificate. [S11]

The ten bodies named were Karnal, Thanesar, Shahabad, Ladwa, Assandh, Gharaunda, Nissing, Taraori, Nilokheri and Indri. The report also cited 83 collection vehicles and a requirement for 114. An instruction to add ten vehicles was pending. Neither a machinery procurement price nor completion of the expansion was established. [S11]

The April 2025 review directed weighbridges at dumping sites and source weighing before transport to Shekhpura Sohana. It also required assessment and processing of legacy waste. These are operational directions, not verified completion or a new plant contract award. [S6]

## Water supply and sewerage

The Tribune reported on 4 December 2024 that PHED had received administrative approval for a Rs 128.21 crore AMRUT-2 project covering eight newly regularised colonies and other KMC urban areas. Technical approval was still awaited, after which tenders would be initiated. [S12]

| Component | Reported approved estimate and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Sewerage | Rs 89.67 crore; 12 km in newly regularised colonies and 48 km in other urban areas |
| Water supply | Rs 38.54 crore; 13 km of pipeline and 20 tubewells, comprising eight for the colonies and 12 for other city areas |

The colonies named were Kanika Vihar Extension, RK Puram Part-2, Sukhbir Colony, RK Puram Extension, Vijay Nagar, Balram Colony in Phoosgarh, Shaktipuram Extension and Vikas Colony on Meerut Road. These approval-stage figures are not expenditure or completion totals. [S12]

A separate PHED rehabilitation programme was reported on 5 April 2024. Hospital Chowk to the Kunjpura Road post office had started at an estimated Rs 7 crore, using desilting, laser profiling and cured-in-place pipe technology. The post office to Maharana Pratap Chowk phase was estimated at Rs 4.5 crore and was still being prepared. For Maharana Pratap Chowk to Namastey Chowk, the tender process was reported complete, but work was expected only after the model code of conduct ended. [S13]

## LED streetlights and procurement checks

The official background procurement is tender `2020_HRY_136808_1`, reference `KSCL/CEO/ RFP/ 2020-21/140`, published on 22 July 2020 under Urban Local Bodies, MC Karnal. It sought replacement or retrofitting of 25,000 street-lighting points with energy-efficient LED luminaires on a CAPEX model for seven years. The listed work period was 180 days; tender value was `NA`. It does not establish that 25,000 lights remained working throughout 2024-2026. [S14]

The November 2026 infrastructure review confirms ongoing work on electrical infrastructure but supplies no reconciled citywide operational LED count. [S3] Applicants should search the Haryana portal using the tender ID, then check corrigenda, bid results, award documents and completion records. Historical closing dates in this archive are not current opportunities. No inferred LED contract value, savings percentage or replacement total is recorded.

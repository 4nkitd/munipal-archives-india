# Karnal municipal budgets and civic financial governance, FY 2024-25 to FY 2026-27

## Annual budget record and evidence limits

The official Accounts Branch page lists the FY 2026-27 budget and FY 2022-23 audit report. It did not list separate FY 2024-25 or FY 2025-26 budget downloads when checked on 22 November 2026. Figures below therefore distinguish official document availability from newspaper accounts of House proceedings.

| Financial year | Verified information | Evidence and qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-25 | Property-tax collection of Rs 11.73 crore against demand of Rs 25 crore | The Tribune, 2 April 2025. This is one revenue head, not the annual budget total. A reliable whole-budget figure was not established in this research. |
| 2025-26 | House-approved expenditure budget of Rs 394.25 crore on 8 April 2025 | The Tribune and Hindustan Times, 9 April 2025. Opening balance and projected receipts must not be mistaken for expenditure. |
| 2026-27 | Unanimously passed budget of Rs 3,47,13,45,000, equivalent to Rs 347.1345 crore | The Tribune, 13 April 2026. An official budget PDF is separately available through the Accounts Branch. |

The official [FY 2026-27 PDF](https://mckarnal.co.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/budget%202026-27%20pdf.pdf) yielded no substantive text using `pdftotext -layout`. [Scanned map/PDF: visual source linked at source_url.] No line-item transcription from that scan is claimed here.

Do not assign the Rs 378 crore figure sometimes mentioned in later reporting to FY 2024-25. Dainik Tribune's report dated 11 October 2023 explicitly identifies Rs 378,58,18,843 as revised proposed income for FY 2023-24, with proposed expenditure of Rs 287,45,54,472. It is both a different fiscal year and a different accounting measure.

## FY 2025-26 receipts and spending proposals

Mayor Renu Bala Gupta chaired the budget meeting. The Tribune identifies Commissioner Dr Vaishali Sharma and senior accounts officer Balwinder Kumar Jaggi at the presentation. It reports no new tax in that budget.

| Budget measure | Rs crore |
| --- | ---: |
| Opening balance | 211.95 |
| Projected income during the year | 312.58 |
| Total estimated funds available | 524.53 |
| Proposed expenditure | 394.25 |
| Projected closing surplus, reported by Hindustan Times | 130.28 |

Reported revenue estimates include property tax of Rs 33.70 crore, rent and lease of Rs 35.11 crore, sales and auctions of Rs 4.75 crore, charges of Rs 19.81 crore, fees of Rs 5.41 crore, fines and compositions of Rs 1.80 crore, interest of Rs 6.20 crore, miscellaneous income of Rs 84.10 crore and loans, advances or deposits of Rs 2.40 crore. The report also identifies government grants as a funding source. This is not a complete reconciliation of receipts.

Reported spending heads include establishment of Rs 80.31 crore, administration of Rs 9.39 crore, sanitation and solid waste management of Rs 54.72 crore, operations and maintenance from own resources of Rs 76.08 crore, other expenditure excluding development works of Rs 7.22 crore and development projects of Rs 137 crore. These rounded heads sum to Rs 364.72 crore, not Rs 394.25 crore. They are a partial press breakdown, not a substitute for the approved expenditure schedule.

## FY 2026-27 priorities, audit and public oversight

The April 2026 budget report identifies drinking water, sanitation, waste management, roads, street lighting, parks, plantations, drainage improvements and digital facilities as priorities. It does not give a verified allocation for each. Mayor Gupta announced ward-level pre-budget meetings for the following financial year to obtain citizen and ward-committee suggestions. This was an announced participation measure, not evidence that such meetings had already occurred.

The municipal portal also links the [FY 2022-23 audit report](https://mckarnal.co.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/Audit%20report%202022-23.pdf). Its listing alone does not establish an audit opinion, clearance of objections or audited FY 2025-26 expenditure.

Residents seeking scrutiny should request the approved budget, House resolution, revised estimates, receipts and expenditure statements, grant utilisation certificates and relevant audit paragraphs from the Accounts Branch. Use the municipality's [RTI page](https://mckarnal.co.in/rti-act) for disclosure procedures. A proposed allocation does not prove that a tender was awarded, money was spent or a work was completed.

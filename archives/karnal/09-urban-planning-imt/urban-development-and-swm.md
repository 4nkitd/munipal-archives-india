# Karnal development planning, sanitation practices and urban water bodies

## Development plan 2031 and 2041: jurisdiction and notification status

The Haryana Town and Country Planning Department's official development-plan index lists Karnal city's Final Development Plan 2025 under notification CCP(NCR)/KNL/FDP/2013/2199 dated 2 September 2013. It lists amendments dated 29 November 2016 and 13 August 2018. The same index does not establish a notified Karnal city Final Development Plan 2031 or 2041.

The Tribune reported on 27 December 2017 that the District Town and Country Planning Department was preparing a Karnal development plan for 2031. District town planner Amrik Singh described a proposed population of nine lakh and a planning area of 7,547 hectares. Reported proposals included 3,060 hectares for housing, 430 hectares for commerce, 535 hectares for industry and 1,345 hectares for open spaces and green belts. These are contemporary planning proposals, not verified notified zoning entitlements. The article's complete land-use breakdown does not reconcile with its stated total, so it should not be used as a statutory area schedule.

| Place in Karnal district | Officially indexed plan | Notification date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Karnal city | Final Development Plan 2025 | 2 September 2013, with later amendments |
| Assandh | Final Development Plan 2031 | 7 August 2019; amended final plan dated 14 January 2021 |
| Indri | Final Development Plan 2031 | 16 September 2019 |
| Nilokheri-Taraori | Final Development Plan 2041 | 1 February 2021 |

Nilokheri-Taraori's 2041 notification is CCP(NCR)/FDP/Nilokheri-Taraori/2041/2021/222. Its geography must not be relabelled as Karnal municipal land. Likewise, the NCR draft regional plan's 2041 horizon is not itself a Karnal city development-plan approval. For a parcel decision, obtain the applicable gazette, amendments, controlled-area boundary, sector plan and land-use permissions from TCP rather than relying on an estate agent's "master plan" label.

## Swachh Survekshan 2024-25 result and sanitation practices

Results announced on 17 July 2025 placed Karnal third nationally in the Swachh Shehar category for cities with populations of 50,000 to three lakh, behind Dewas and Karhad. Contemporary reporting identifies it as Haryana's top-ranked city in that category. It was not an unrestricted third-place ranking across every Indian city and population class.

Commissioner Vaishali Sharma reported 11,067 points out of 12,500, a three-star Garbage-Free City rating and Water+ status. Hindustan Times reported component scores of 9,057/10,000 for the Swachh survey, 800/1,300 for garbage-free city assessment and 1,200/1,200 for open-defecation-free assessment. Water+ recognition does not independently prove the present water quality of every canal or lake.

Reported practices included:

- Expanded door-to-door collection and direct transfer to the waste-processing plant instead of neighbourhood dumping points.
- Night sweeping of commercial areas and litter bins at commercial locations.
- GPS monitoring of collection vehicles and field monitoring of sanitation workers.
- "Meri Beat Meri Pehchan", a competition among sanitation supervisors.
- Source segregation, composting wet waste, recycling dry waste and coordinating scrap dealers through the reduce, reuse and recycle approach.
- Health camps, uniforms and hygiene kits for sanitation workers described as Safai Mitras.

The mayor told Hindustan Times in July 2025 that door-to-door collection had reached 98%, up from 92.5%. This is a dated attributed claim, not a current audited service guarantee.

## Municipal SWM implementation and conflicting published indicators

The official municipal SWM page, checked on 22 November 2026, identifies Sugam Swachhta Private Limited as the designated collection and transportation agency across four municipal zones. Its implementation statement says the system covers collection, transportation and processing and describes an Integrated Solid Waste Management project as a further strengthening measure.

The same page displays collection coverage of 92% and scientific processing of 63%, while July 2025 reporting quotes 98% collection and a commissioner statement of 100% plant processing. These indicators have different dates or unclear denominators. This archive preserves their attribution rather than combining them into a single performance series.

The municipal page also links a document labelled Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026. Its title alone does not establish the legal text, commencement date or applicable penalty schedule. Residents can use the municipal [grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for missed collection or dumping complaints and retain the location, date and complaint number for follow-up.

## Western Yamuna Canal waterfront

In October 2017 the corporation proposed a recreational canal front near Saidpura at the junction described in reporting as the Western Yamuna, Bhakra and SYL canals. Commissioner Priyanka Soni said a request for land transfer and a no-objection certificate had gone to the Additional Chief Secretary, Irrigation and Water Resources Department. Conservation of existing natural areas, flora, fauna and canal embankments formed part of the stated purpose. This report documents a proposal and requested permission, not approval of every component.

On 8 September 2025, The Tribune reported Commissioner Vaishali Sharma's inspection of the Western Yamuna canal front. She directed preparation of a project plan for a street-food hub and light show, uniform vendor stalls, LED lighting, gazebos and selfie points, and instructed officials to engage a consultant. These were project-planning directions. No completion or environmental water-quality finding follows from the report.

Canal-front recreation, irrigation-channel operation and municipal drainage are distinct functions. The 2017 requirement for Irrigation Department permission is relevant when tracing land custody and project approvals.

## Karna Lake and Karna Taal are different sites

Karna Lake near NH-44 must not be confused with the central-city Karna Taal park. A February 2021 report of a chief ministerial review describes a Rs 7 crore Karna Lake tourism proposal, a 1.26 km peripheral development area, paths, lighting and a cycle track. It records an instruction to the Irrigation Department to raise clean-water supply from the Bhakra channel from one cusec to three cusecs. This is an instruction reported at a review meeting, not a measured continuing inflow or proof of completed works.

For Karna Taal, The Tribune's 5 October 2026 report identifies a fresh municipal maintenance and redesign proposal. Commissioner Saloni Sharma said the musical fountain had been made temporarily functional and that reducing the artificial water body's holding capacity was being considered to conserve water. The corporation was exploring an agency for regular maintenance. Reported existing dimensions were a 2,280-square-metre water body, 1.3 metres deep, associated with the earlier redevelopment.

The lake-supply proposal and the Taal redesign address different management problems. Neither report supplies laboratory water tests, sewage interception performance or a complete maintenance contract. Such records should be requested separately before assessing ecological restoration or operational success.

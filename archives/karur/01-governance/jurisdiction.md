# Karur Corporation in the district local bodies register

## Which civic body to contact

The [district register](https://karur.nic.in/about-district/administrative-setup/local-bodies/) lists Karur as the district's only corporation. Kulithalai, Pugalur and Pallapatti have separate municipalities. The district also lists eight town panchayats, including Puliyur and Uppidamangalam.

The district's [Karur Corporation office entry](https://karur.nic.in/public-utility/karur-municipality/) gives the postal address as Commissioner, Municipal Office, Azad Road, Karur 639001. The office telephone is 04324-260341 and the email is commr.karur@tn.gov.in.

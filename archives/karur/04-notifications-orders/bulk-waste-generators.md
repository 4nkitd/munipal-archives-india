# Karur notice on registration of bulk waste generators

## Who the notice covers

The [district's 22 August 2026 release](https://karur.nic.in/karur-corporation-22-08-2026/) carries a Karur Corporation notice on bulk waste generator registration. [Press release 203](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3bbf94b34eb32268ada57a3be5062fe7d/uploads/2026/08/17875548201601.pdf) sets out alternative qualifying criteria. Two of these are a built-up area of at least 20,000 square metres or solid waste generation of at least 100 kilograms per day. Meeting one criterion suffices. The notice also covers eligible generators in the district's municipalities, town panchayats and village panchayats.

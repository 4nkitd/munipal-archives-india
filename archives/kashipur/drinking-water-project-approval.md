# District approval for the Kashipur drinking water project

The district says its Water and Sanitation Committee approved the Kashipur component of a three-town drinking water scheme. The Kashipur plan is ₹261.83 crore and provides for 10 water tanks, 14 tube wells and 457 km of water lines. The district projects 132,143 beneficiaries and says the designs use estimated populations for 2057.

The 32 wards are project coverage, not the total number of corporation wards. The district release does not identify the covered ward numbers. The ₹636.14 crore headline is for Rudrapur, Kashipur and Sitarganj combined, not Kashipur alone. Approval does not show that construction was completed or connections became available.

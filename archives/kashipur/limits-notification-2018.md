# Kashipur municipal limits notification, April 2018

The [state urban local body register](https://udd.uk.gov.in/list-of-ulb/) lists Nagar Nigam Kashipur in Udham Singh Nagar district. The corporation covers the notified urban area, not the entire district or [Kashipur development block](https://usnagar.nic.in/kashipur-block/).

## What the notification changed

Notification 905/IV(3)/2018-1(55)/2010 follows the 9 March 2018 High Court order. It records the cancellation of the 27 November 2017 boundary-extension notice and its 29 December 2017 corrigendum, and cites the provisional notice of 10 March 2018 that invited objections and suggestions again.

Schedule One has 16 numbered village entries. For example, it gives cadastral plot numbers 1 to 398 for कचनालगाजी and 1 to 212 for रम्पुरा. It marks the inclusion at दोहरी परसा as partial, with 26.773 hectares in the parcel column. The schedule is a list of additions to an existing corporation, not its founding instrument or a ward delimitation. The first two pages of the state collection contain the schedule.

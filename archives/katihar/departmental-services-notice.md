# Katihar departmental services: Dak Suchna 22/2025–26

## Notice number and financial year

The corporation issued Dak Suchna No. 22/2025–26 for the list of departmental services for financial year 2026–27. The number refers to the notice series; the services concern the following financial year.

## Publication dates

The [district listing](https://katihar.nic.in/notice/office-municipal-corporation-katihar-12/) gives 3 February 2026 as the start of the notice period and 26 February as the end. It lists 24, 25 and 26 February as the three dates on which the notice would appear. The listing links the 243 KB notice; it does not report an award or receipts.

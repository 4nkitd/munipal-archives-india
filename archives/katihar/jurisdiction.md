# Katihar Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and office

## Municipal limits

The SEC lists Katihar Nagar Nigam in Katihar subdivision and marks the corporation unchanged (अपरिवर्तित). The district publishes separate electoral rolls for Korha, Manihari, Amdabad, Barari, Barsoi, Balrampur and Kursela. [District record](https://katihar.nic.in/nagar-nikay-final-elector-list/).

The [SEC register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0) records 45 wards and a population of 240,838 for the corporation. The [Form 6 schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=10&SID=1&SN=252) identifies this as a 2011 Census population for the territory in the schedule. It is neither a district total nor a current population estimate.

## Municipal office

Nagar Nigam Katihar, Bihar 854105. The district lists comm.katihar@bihar.gov.in for the office. [Official directory](https://katihar.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/).

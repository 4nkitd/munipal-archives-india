# Katni municipal jurisdiction and ward count

## Identifying the corporation

The [City Finance profile](https://www.cityfinance.in/municipal-data/city/murwara---katni-municipal-corporation) reports 45 wards and an area of 68.58 km². The underlying directory identifies the corporation by LGD code 250979 and census code 802351. It attributes the ward count to the urban local body. The population figure of 221,883 is from Census 2011. These figures do not identify the ward for a particular address.

## Local office and jurisdiction

The [district municipal directory](https://katni.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) gives the office address as Nagar Nigam Office, beside Girls College, Katni. The district lists Kemore, Barhi and Vijayraghavgarh as separate Nagar Parishads. Its entry for Nagar Nigam Katni gives the office telephone as +91-7622-230120 and the commissioner’s office email as commkatni@mpurban.gov.in.

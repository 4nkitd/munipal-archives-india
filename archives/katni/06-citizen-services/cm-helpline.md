# Katni public grievances through CM Helpline 181

## Filing a complaint

The district’s CM Helpline entry lists Katni as the city and gives 181 as the telephone number. Callers can request information or raise a complaint. The state helpline routes matters to officials in the responsible departments; the district also links the [CM Helpline portal](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/).

For an enquiry directed to Nagar Nigam Katni itself, the [district municipal directory](https://katni.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) gives +91-7622-230120 at the office beside Girls College. This is the corporation office line; 181 is the state grievance channel.

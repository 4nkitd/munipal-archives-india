# Khammam's 60 wards in the 2021 municipal election

## What the commission reported

Khammam Municipal Corporation is row 2 in the Telangana State Election Commission's Proforma XI report on the 2021 ordinary elections to seven urban local bodies. The report counts 60 wards and 60 elected members. Its heading says the return was to be generated through Te-Poll and sent on 3 May 2021. That is the reporting schedule, not a verified date of online publication.

| Party or status as printed | Elected ward members |
| --- | ---: |
| TRS | 43 |
| INC | 10 |
| CPI | 2 |
| CPI(M) | 2 |
| BJP | 1 |
| Independents | 2 |
| Total | 60 |

## What the count can establish

This is the ward total for the corporation in the 2021 election. The return does not give individual ward numbers, boundaries, polling stations or a current officeholder roster. The corporation's undated [basic-information page](https://khammamcorporation.telangana.gov.in/058/basic-information) reports 50 divisions. Use the commission's 60 for the 2021 election and do not use either count to decide which ward contains an address.

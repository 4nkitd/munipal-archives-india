# Khammam annual accounts for 2025–26

## Income and expenditure

The corporation publishes CDMA's income and expenditure statement under Annual Accounts for 2025–26. It covers 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026 and separates the Municipal General Fund from the Capital Project Fund. Values below are in rupees. The statement does not identify a publication date or provide an audit opinion.

## Other statements for the same year

The [balance sheet](https://khammamcorporation.telangana.gov.in/assets/Accounts/Balance%20Sheet%20Pdf/Khammam_BalanceSheet.pdf) is dated as of 31 March 2026 and shows combined liabilities of ₹6,54,25,66,273.62. The [receipts and payments report](https://khammamcorporation.telangana.gov.in/assets/Accounts/ReceiptMerged/Khammam_ReceiptsAndPayments.pdf) reports opening balances and cash movements by account code. Those cash movements answer a different question than the income and expenditure statement. None of the three documents gives Khammam's adopted budget.

# Public notice for the 2025 Stambhadri draft master plan

## Scope of the draft

The Stambhadri Urban Development Authority (SUDA) issued a Form-1 notice under Rules 8 and 12(4). It says Telangana's Director of Town and Country Planning prepared a draft master plan under AMRUT covering Khammam Municipal Corporation and 45 villages. The notice puts the full planning area at 571.83 sq km. That is the draft SUDA area, not the area of the city corporation or a notice adding 45 villages to it.

## Inspection and representations

The notice says the public could inspect the draft map during office hours at SUDA or the corporation office. It directed written suggestions and objections to the Vice Chairman, SUDA, within 90 days of publication. The notice gives the office as the second floor, Block A, KMC Building, Ghattaiah Center, Khammam, and lists `vcsudakhammam@gmail.com`.

The image gives DIPR release reference `6152-PP/CL/ADVT/1/2025-26` dated 6 August 2025. The corporation homepage update log lists the press note on 8 September 2025. Neither entry is used here as a confirmed date of notice publication, so the closing date is not calculated. This record does not establish that the comment window remains open or that a final plan was approved.

# Khammam city corporation and district municipalities

## The city body and the district

Khammam Municipal Corporation is the urban local body for Khammam city. The [Khammam district directory](https://khammam.telangana.gov.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) gives separate municipal listings for Madhira, Sathupalli and Wyra. Sharing the same district name does not put a property, ward or service account under the city corporation.

## What the municipal profile measures

The corporation's basic-information page gives an area of 93.45 sq km and lists 305,000 residents and 75,000 households under Census 2011. The same page has financial entries from 2012–13 and gives no edition date for the area figure. Those figures describe the published profile, not a new city-limit survey.

The page lists 50 divisions. The [State Election Commission's 2021 abstract](https://tsec.gov.in/preview.do?fileName=L1VwbG9hZHMvVFNFQy9wZGYvU3RhdGlzdGljYWxSZXAvTVBOUC8yMDI0L2Fic3RyYWN0LTdfdWxiX3dpc2VfZWxlY3RlZF93YXJkX21lYmVyc193aXRoX3BhcnR5X2JyZWFrdXBfMjA1MS5wZGY=) instead records 60 electoral wards for Khammam. The numbers should not be combined. The corporation's [ward map landing page](https://khammamcorporation.telangana.gov.in/058/wards-map) was available, but the map file returned 404 when checked. Neither the profile nor the election abstract assigns particular streets or villages to current wards.

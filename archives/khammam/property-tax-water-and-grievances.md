# Khammam property tax, water charges and grievance channels

## Taxes and utility charges

The corporation's Pay Online menu sends residents to the Telangana Commissioner and Director of Municipal Administration (CDMA). Use the following links for municipal accounts belonging to Khammam Municipal Corporation.

| Task | Official page and information shown |
| --- | --- |
| Check house tax, assessment or payment status | [Property tax](https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/cdma_arbs/CDMA_PG/PTMenu). The dues form asks for a 10-digit Property Tax Identification Number (PTIN). The property search lets users select the district and urban local body and search by PTIN, owner name or door number. There are separate options to print receipts and view assessment details. |
| Check water charges | [Water charges](https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/cdma_arbs/CDMA_PG/WTMenu). The payment section asks for the Water CAN number. The water-account search offers district, urban local body, Water Tax NO, owner name and door number fields. |
| Check linked municipal dues | [Bill One](https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/CDMA_ARBS/billone/index). The form accepts a PTIN or registered mobile number and says it verifies a one-time password before displaying dues. |
| Check vacant land tax | [Vacant land tax](https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/cdma_arbs/CDMA_VLT/VLTMenu). The corporation lists this separately from house tax. |

## Complaints to the corporation

The [corporation office contact page](https://khammamcorporation.telangana.gov.in/pages/office-contact) lists `7901298265` and `mckmmts@gmail.com` for grievances. For a billing query, have the assessment or water account number and any receipt reference ready. The service pages were checked without submitting an account lookup, OTP request or payment.

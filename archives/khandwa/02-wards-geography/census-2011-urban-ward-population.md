# Khandwa Municipal Corporation ward population in Census 2011

The Urban Primary Census Abstract in the Khandwa (East Nimar) District Census Handbook, Part XII-B, lists municipal corporation town code `802435`, area 35.77 km², 39,002 households and 200,738 persons, including 24,801 children aged 0–6. The ward rows below come from printed pages 160 and 166 (PDF pages 169 and 175). The town total includes institutional and houseless population.

## How to use these figures

These are census-era counts, not electoral roll totals, ward reservation designations or legal boundary descriptions. The census table labels rows by number rather than by neighbourhood. Its adjacent Chhanera and Omkareshwar rows belong to other urban bodies and are not included above. For the current number and extent of electoral wards, use the relevant state gazette notification under section 10 of the Municipal Corporation Act.

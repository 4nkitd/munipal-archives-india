# Khandwa municipal bills, service complaints and help numbers

## Corporation and state contact routes

| Need | Official route |
|---|---|
| Corporation office or a query about a municipal account | [District corporation entry](https://khandwa.nic.in/en/public-utility/municipal-corporation-khandwa/), 0733-2223523 or commkhandwa@mpurban.gov.in |
| Municipal line in the district's helpline list | [Khandwa district helpline directory](https://khandwa.nic.in/en/helpline/), 0733-2222616 |
| Register or follow up a grievance with the state | [Madhya Pradesh CM Helpline](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/), 181 |
| Fire emergency | 101, listed separately by the district as Fire Brigade |

The corporation entry and the helpline directory publish different municipal numbers. For an issue with a specific service, use the corporation's office contact and identify the address or municipal account concerned. The state CM Helpline homepage links to a [complaint, request or suggestion form](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/Public/VerifyOTPBeforeOnlineComplaint.aspx) and a separate [complaint status page](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/grievance-status.aspx). The status page asks for the complaint number. Keep that number for follow-up. The state grievance line is not a payment channel or an emergency dispatch number.

## Property tax and water charges

The [state Urban Development and Housing Department](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/) links to [e-Nagar Palika](https://enagarpalika.mp.gov.in/home). The state portal's [English service labels](https://enagarpalika.mp.gov.in/assets/i18n/en.json) list property tax payment, water bill payment and new water connection applications. Property tax quick pay asks for a Property ID; water quick pay asks for a Water Connection ID. Use the identifier on the relevant bill and confirm the local body before paying. If the record cannot be found or appears under the wrong body, check the identifier with the corporation office rather than paying against a different account.

The sources establish the published routes and account identifiers. They do not establish a Khandwa tariff, a resolution deadline or that any particular municipal account will accept online payment.

# Khandwa Municipal Corporation: legal scope and municipal duties

## Which local body the Act applies to

The [Khandwa district urban development register](https://khandwa.nic.in/en/urban-development/) lists Khandwa as the district's municipal corporation. It lists Pandhana, Mundi, Omkareshwar and New Harsud (Chhanera) as separate Nagar Panchayats. The [corporation's district directory entry](https://khandwa.nic.in/en/public-utility/municipal-corporation-khandwa/) confirms its official office phone, email and PIN code, 450001. The district register reports a 2011 corporation population of 200,738, not a district-wide figure.

Sections 1(3) and 7 of the Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956 tie the Act's application to the notification of a larger urban area. Under section 7 the Governor specifies the area by public notification, taking into account factors such as population, density, locally generated revenue, non-agricultural employment and economic importance. The corporation is a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal. The same section makes provision for publicly notified industrial townships. The district register and office entry identify the authority; neither is the Khandwa constitution notification or a legal description of city limits.

## Council, wards and municipal work

Section 6 names the Corporation, Mayor-in-Council, Mayor and Commissioner as the municipal authorities charged with executing the Act. Section 9 provides for direct election of the mayor from the municipal area and councillors from the wards. Under section 10 the State Government notifies the number and extent of wards in the official gazette. Each ward elects one councillor, and wards are to be compact and as nearly equal in population as practicable. The government reports completed ward formation to the State Election Commission. Census ward numbers do not substitute for this gazette notification.

Section 66 requires the corporation to make adequate provision for work including street lighting and cleaning, refuse disposal, fire brigade maintenance, public streets and drains, municipal waterworks, and registration of births, marriages and deaths. Section 69 generally vests municipal government in the corporation and places executive power with the Commissioner, subject to approvals and restrictions in the Act. These provisions describe statutory responsibilities, not a current work programme or ward-level service schedule.

The department's [rules and acts index](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=NOWfDSxXXWnABPjhYKFMkQ%3D%3D) also lists a separate 2024 corporation amendment. The 1956 year identifies the Act, not the publication date of the linked compilation or the formation date of Khandwa's corporation.

# Formation of Kochi Municipal Corporation

## Constituent territories in 1967

The Kerala Assembly approved formation on 1 July 1967. The state government then notified the Corporation of Cochin. It came into existence on 1 November with a stated formation area of 83.524 square kilometres.

| Former unit or territory | Incorporated areas |
|---|---|
| Municipalities | Ernakulam, Mattancherry and Fort Cochin |
| Panchayats | Palluruthy, Vennala, Vyttila and Edappally |
| Islands named separately | Willingdon Island, Gundu Deepu and Ramanthuruth |

## Earlier transfers

Ernakulam Municipality had already absorbed Elamkulam Panchayat and the Pachalam and Vaduthala part of Cheranellur Panchayat in 1956. Mattancherry Municipality took in the Mundamveli part of Palluruthy Panchayat in 1962. These transfers explain why a place can fall within the 1967 corporation without appearing as a separate unit in the formation list.

# Kochi: final order for 76 electoral wards

## How the boundaries were settled

G.O.(P) No. 49/2024/LSGD of 10 September 2024 fixed the number of directly elected seats in Kochi at 76. Kerala Extraordinary Gazette No. 2920 published it the following day as S.R.O. No. 813/2024. The Delimitation Commission published its draft division on 18 November 2024 and accepted objections and suggestions until 4 December. After inquiry and hearings, it issued final order SDC/1063/2024/SDC1 on 26 May 2025. The attached Malayalam schedule gives the legal boundary descriptions.

## Finding a ward

The [SEC constituency directory](https://sec.kerala.gov.in/public/cnstncy/wd/d0f41644-754b-4b6e-a8dc-97ecbf5fee16) lists the ward names and codes for local body C07003. The first entry is C07003001, FORT KOCHI; the last is C07003076, FORTKOCHI VELI. Use the directory to identify the ward and the final order to check where its boundary runs. The complete directory is transcribed in [wards.json](../wards.json).

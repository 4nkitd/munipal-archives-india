# Kochi annual financial statements by reporting year

## Accounts available by year

The corporation's Financial Statement page lists an attachment for each reporting year. Choose by the year in the document label: the two most recent files both sit in a September 2026 upload directory.

| Reporting year | Attachment label on the finance page |
|---|---|
| 2020-21 | Financial Statement 2020-2021 |
| 2021-22 | Financial Statement 2021-2022 |
| 2022-23 | Financial Statement 2022-2023 |
| 2023-24 | Financial Statement 2023-2024 |
| 2024-25 | Financial Statement 2024-2025 |
| 2025-26 | Financial Statement 2025-2026_1.pdf |

## Budget or annual statement?

Use the annual statement to look up the accounts for a completed reporting year. For the following year's budget, the corporation posted its [2026-27 budget notice](https://kochicorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/kochi-corporation-budget-2026-27/668) on 10 September 2026. That is the posting date, not a date of budget adoption.

# Kolhapur transport undertaking: Ayodhya Park parking tender

## Site, term and bidding dates

The corporation's tender register lists notice **KMT/ISSUE-CASH/2026/2**, dated 21 November 2026, for operating a "Pay and Park" facility on KMT land near Ayodhya Park in Kolhapur. KMT specified a five-year operating period. The register showed 5 December 2026 as the closing date when checked. That date applies to the bid, not the start of parking operations.

## Distinguishing the Station Road tender

The same register lists a separate ten-year parking operation under **KMT/ISSUE-CASH/2026/1**. That notice covers a mechanical multi-level car park at City Survey No. 507/8 on Station Road near Gokul Hotel, including advertising on the building. Its original entry ran from 27 August to 17 September 2026; the second extension entry ran from 25 September to 5 October. Neither the Station Road extension nor its advertising rights appear in the Ayodhya Park listing.

# Kolhapur municipality, corporation and council chambers

## Three dates in the municipal history

Kolhapur's municipal history distinguishes the foundation of the town municipality on 12 October 1854 from the conversion of the municipal council into a corporation in December 1972. It also records the formation of a local self-government association in March 1941. The first popularly elected corporation followed in August 1978, nearly six years after the change in municipal status.

## Where the corporation met

The east, south and north sections of the municipal building date to 1929, when Kolhapur was still a municipality. A west section completed the four-sided building in 1993. The corporation describes two floors of dressed black stone, with a smaller third storey at the centre of each side. It identifies the old and new halls on the first floor as meeting rooms used by the corporation.

# Who governs the municipal area?

KMC's [municipal history](https://www.kmcgov.in/KMCPortal/jsp/MunicipalHistoryHome.jsp) identifies three authorities under the Calcutta Municipal Corporation Act, 1980: the Corporation, the Mayor-in-Council and the Mayor. The Act took effect in January 1984. Under the earlier 1951 Act the Commissioner had held much of the executive responsibility; the new Act established a full-time political executive.

The city boundary changed over time. Tollygunge merged on 1 April 1953. KMC also records the inclusion of Jadavpur, South Suburban, Garden Reach and Joka. Its account gives the resulting council strength as 144 elected councillors.

The [Kolkata Metropolitan Development Authority](https://kmda.wb.gov.in/) has its own planning directorate and issues its own tenders, including for water supply in South Dumdum and sewerage in Bhatpara. Those projects belong to KMDA; neither municipality is a KMC borough.

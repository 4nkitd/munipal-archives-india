# Wards and boroughs

KMC's [basic statistics](https://www.kmcgov.in/KMCPortal/jsp/BasicStatistics.jsp) give 144 wards, 16 boroughs and a municipal area of 206.08 square kilometres. The ward identifies the electoral constituency. A borough groups wards for administration: for example, its Executive Health Officer handles registration of births occurring in the borough.

Start with [Know Your Ward](https://kmc.wb.gov.in/contact-us/knowYourWard) to identify the ward. KMC's [contact directory](https://www.kmcgov.in/KMCPortal/jsp/KmcContact.jsp) links separately to Councillors, Borough Committee and Borough Offices. For birth registration, its [office list](https://www.kmcgov.in/KMCPortal/jsp/KMCBirth.jsp) places Borough I at 10 B.T. Road, Cossipore, Borough X at 217 Prince Anwar Shah Road and Borough XVI at Diamond Park, Joka.

For a boundary question, the [KMC notice board](https://www.kmcgov.in/KMCPortal/jsp/KMCPortalHome1.jsp) distinguishes the draft Schedule II and invitation for objections from the Gazette publication labelled **1728/UDMA, Amendment Schedule II**. Use the gazetted schedule for the boundary text. The statistics page gives counts only.

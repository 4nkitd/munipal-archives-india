# Electoral roll adoption for KMC

The West Bengal State Election Commission's [Important Notification list](https://wbsec.wb.gov.in/) carries the entry "Order regarding adoption of electoral roll for KMC", dated 12 October 2026. The listing identifies the corporation and the subject. It gives no polling date or election result.

The Commission distinguishes its role in conducting municipal elections from the revision of the underlying electoral roll. On the same page it directs electoral-roll and voter-card queries to the Election Commission of India or the [Chief Electoral Officer, West Bengal](https://ceowestbengal.wb.gov.in/). It specifically identifies the 2026 Special Intensive Revision as an Election Commission of India exercise.

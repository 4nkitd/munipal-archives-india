# Where to register a birth or death

KMC [registers births and deaths](https://www.kmcgov.in/KMCPortal/jsp/KMCBirthDeathHome.jsp) that occur inside its municipal limits. The family address alone does not establish jurisdiction. Since 5 May 2022 certificates have been issued through West Bengal's Janma Mrityu Tathya portal.

| Circumstance | Office or procedure KMC identifies |
| --- | --- |
| Birth in a government hospital | Obtain the certificate through the hospital. |
| Birth in a private hospital with portal access | The hospital sends the record to the borough Executive Health Officer. Parents receive a text link to add the child's name, then a download link. |
| Birth at home | The head of the family reports it to the ward health unit with medical evidence. The local authority verifies the event. |
| Death inside KMC, cremation or burial at a KMC facility | Submit certification of the death on Form 4/4A to the sub-registrar at the facility. |
| Death inside KMC, cremation outside KMC | Apply in the borough where the death occurred with the institution's or physician's death certificate and the original cremation certificate. |

The [borough registration centre list](https://www.kmcgov.in/KMCPortal/jsp/KMCBirth.jsp) gives office addresses. For a birth missing from the register, KMC directs applicants seeking a non-availability certificate to the Health Department, Central Municipal Office Building, 5 S.N. Banerjee Road, with evidence of the parents' residence at the time of birth and documents linking the child to them.

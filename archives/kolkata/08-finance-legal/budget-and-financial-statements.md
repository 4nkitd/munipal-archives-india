# Finding estimates and reported expenditure

On the [KMC budget page](https://www.kmcgov.in/KMCPortal/jsp/KMCBudget.jsp), FY 2026-27 has two entries: **Budget Statement 2026-2027** and **Budget Estimates 2026-2027**. Both also appear for each of the preceding three fiscal years. For FY 2020-21 through FY 2022-23 the budget series lists estimates alone. KMC gives no adoption date in the FY 2026-27 listing.

For actual cash flows, look under the financial statement for the year concerned and choose **Receipt and Payment Statement**. KMC lists income and expenditure and the balance sheet separately. For FY 2021-22 it also lists an audit report under the audited financial statement heading. It labels the next three years, FY 2022-23 through FY 2024-25, **Unaudited/Provisional**. A budget estimate for FY 2026-27 does not establish what KMC ultimately spent.

# Kollam corporation and the other urban local bodies in Kollam district

## Identifying the corporation

The LSGD website directory lists Kollam Corporation under local body code C020100. The [SEC corporation register](https://sec.kerala.gov.in/public/cnstncy/lb/da39a3ee5e6b4b0d3255bfef95601890af13/U/C) lists Kollam as C02002 with 56 wards. Use the former to find the municipal website and council decisions, and the latter for election records.

## Other urban local bodies

The [SEC municipal register](https://sec.kerala.gov.in/public/cnstncy/lb/da39a3ee5e6b4b0d3255bfef95601890af13/U/M) lists four other urban bodies in Kollam district. Each has its own ward register; their wards are outside this archive.

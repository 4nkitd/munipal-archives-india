# Kollam civil certificates, property tax and complaints

## Request a certificate

Kollam's municipal portal lists three K-SMART quick certificate searches. Choose the entry for the registered event:

| Certificate | Official search |
| --- | --- |
| Birth | [Birth certificate](https://ksmart.lsgkerala.gov.in/ui/cr/public/quick-certificate-birth) |
| Death | [Death certificate](https://ksmart.lsgkerala.gov.in/ui/cr/public/quick-certificate-death) |
| Marriage | [Marriage certificate](https://ksmart.lsgkerala.gov.in/ui/cr/public/quick-certificate-marriage) |

## Other municipal services

For property tax, use the Property Tax entry under Quick Payment. The Services section directs building permit, licence and public grievance requests to K-SMART. For a welfare pension enquiry the corporation instead links to the [state welfare pension portal](https://welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in/index.aspx). The municipal office lists 0474-2742192 for enquiries.

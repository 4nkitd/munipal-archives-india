# Korba Municipal Corporation office and jurisdiction

## Municipal office

The district lists Korba Municipal Corporation at Saket Bhawan, ITI Chowk, Rampur, Korba. The office email is corporationkorba@gmail.com.

## Other urban local bodies in the district

Bankimongra, Dipka and Katghora have separate municipal councils. Chhurikala and Pali are Nagar Panchayats.

# Korba second tender for printing municipal photo electoral rolls

## Procurement and notice period

The Collector’s local election office sought printing of electoral rolls with photographs for the Municipal Corporation General Election 2024. The [first notice](https://korba.gov.in/en/notice/office-collector-local-election-korba-2/) ran from 5 to 26 September 2024. The second notice ran from 27 September to 9 October 2024. Neither listing identifies an awardee or contract value.

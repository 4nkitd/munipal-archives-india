# Municipal Corporation Kota and its North and South predecessors

## Reorganisation order

In [notification DLB/25/9661 dated 27 March 2025](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/order/detail/228720), Rajasthan's Local Self Government Department exercised powers under sections 3, 5, 6 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009. The notification combines the areas of Municipal Corporation Kota North and Municipal Corporation Kota South and reconstitutes them as one Nagar Nigam Kota. It takes effect on completion of the two boards' then-current terms. The notification itself does not give a calendar date for that trigger or set out the transfer of staff, assets or liabilities.

## Current corporation

Rajasthan's [Local Self Government Department list of local bodies](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) records one Nagar Nigam named Kota in Kota district (row 212), with 100 wards and the official directory email `nnks.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in`. The list separately identifies Itawa, Ramganj Mandi, Sangod, Suket and Sultanpur as Nagar Palikas. The Kota corporation covers the city municipal area, not the whole district.

The [Rajasthan Gazette of 23 September 2025](https://kotamc.org/PDF/New_Ward_Delimitation_Kota.pdf) publishes one ward schedule for Nagar Nigam Kota. The State Election Commission also used that single corporation name in its [2026 election programme appendix](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/List_merged.pdf) and its [17 September 2026 order](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/8986.pdf).

## Historical corporations

The March 2025 order cites notification **DLB/19/11001–11260 dated 18 October 2019** as the instrument that split the earlier Kota corporation into North and South. The State Election Commission's [5 March 2020 election preparation letter](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/25570001.pdf) identifies the two newly created corporations separately. The municipal website still hosts North-era material, including a budget variance report and old delimitation links.

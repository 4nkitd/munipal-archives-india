# 2025 Gazette schedule for Kota's 100 wards

## What the state approved

Notification **DLB/25/19661** is dated **20 September 2025**. The Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary, Part 6(क), published it on **23 September 2025**. It cites sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994. After considering objections to the draft ward formation order, the state accepted the Kota Collector's recommendation and approved the proposed order.

Form A describes the limits of wards 1 to 100 in Hindi and gives a population for each. The [state local-body register](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371474/2380) also lists 100 wards for Kota and links to [the same Gazette on the state file server](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files/Content/UploadFolder/DepartmentMaster/215/2026/Apr/30409/215b447631e-5f21-4fbc-b8cf-7a97b673bbf1.pdf).

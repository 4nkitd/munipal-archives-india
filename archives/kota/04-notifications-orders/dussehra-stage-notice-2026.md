# October 2026 revised Dussehra fair stage notice

## Historical public notice

For the 2026 National Dussehra Fair, the municipal Fair Cell invited applications for cultural programmes on the Kisan Rangmanch stage. The revised notice, RajKaj reference **25021273**, carries an electronic approval dated **13 October 2026**. It refers to the original order **15125-37** dated **7 October 2026**.

| Item | Original date | Revised date |
|---|---|---|
| Last day to submit an application | 13 October 2026 | 16 October 2026 |
| Audition | 15 October 2026 | 17 October 2026, 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. |

The notice left the other conditions unchanged. It was issued by the Fair Cell, Nagar Nigam Kota, at Rajiv Gandhi Bhawan, Dussehra Ground. These dates have passed.

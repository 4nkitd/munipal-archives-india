# September 2026 Kota corporation office elections rescheduled

## Historical election notice

The State Election Commission's [14 September 2026 order 8780](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/8780.pdf) recorded EVM technical problems that prevented results for some booths from being released in Kota and three other local bodies. It postponed the elections for the chairperson and vice-chairperson until further orders.

In [order 8986 of 17 September 2026](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/8986.pdf), the commission issued a replacement schedule. For the chairperson, it set 18 September for public notice, 19 September for the nomination deadline and 25 September for polling from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., with counting immediately afterward. For the vice-chairperson, it set the meeting for 26 September at 10 a.m., with polling if necessary from 2:30 p.m. to 5 p.m. and counting immediately afterward.

Both orders refer to one Nagar Nigam Kota. These notices establish the schedule, not who took office or the outcome of any poll.

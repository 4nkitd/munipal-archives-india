# Municipal service applications and complaints in Kota

## Applications

Use the [Rajasthan Local Self Government online services directory](https://lsg.rajasthan.gov.in/lsg/pages/sm/department-page/371475/2390) and select the service before signing in. It has separate entries for **Urban Development Tax**, **Property ID Creation**, **Online Building Plan Approval System**, and **Trade License and Renewal**. The building-plan section links a checklist, applicant manual, fee and timeline. The trade-licence section links the SSO application, a checklist and mandatory documents. The tax and property-ID entries are separate services.

## Complaints and municipal contact

The [corporation website](https://kotamc.org/) links to [Rajasthan Sampark](https://sampark.rajasthan.gov.in/), the state's platform for routing public grievances to departments. The [municipal contact page](https://kotamc.org/ContactUs.aspx) lists **0744-250-1903** and the office opposite Dussehra Ground, C.A.D. Circle, Shakti Nagar, Dadabari, Kota 324009. For an older bill bearing a North or South label, ask the municipal office which record to use rather than assuming the old ward number still applies.

These are published entry points. No payment, application or grievance was submitted to test the workflow.

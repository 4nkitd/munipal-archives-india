# Kotdwar corporation jurisdiction and the Kanvnagri name

The [state urban local body register](https://udd.uk.gov.in/list-of-ulb/) lists Nagar Nigam Kotdwar in Pauri district. Notification 939 of 6 April 2018 defines the corporation's territorial jurisdiction as the whole area of the predecessor Nagar Palika Parishad Kotdwar, following its boundary expansion under notification 920 of 5 April 2018. The corporation does not cover all of Pauri Garhwal district. The 6 April notice recounts the cancellation of the 2017 extension, upgrade and administrator notices after the High Court's 9 March 2018 order.

## Official usage

The English cover of the [FY 2022–23 financial statement](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s38208974663db80265e9bfe7b222dcb18/uploads/2025/05/20250530633051061.pdf#page=2) reads “Nagar Nigam Kotdwar.” The corporation seal on that same cover reads “नगर निगम कण्वनगरी कोटद्वार,” rendered here as “Nagar Nigam Kanvnagri, Kotdwar.” The district's tender notice page also uses the English spelling “Kotdwara.” The seal verifies official usage of Kanvnagri; it does not establish a legal renaming or a second corporation.

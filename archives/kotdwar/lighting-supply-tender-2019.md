# Kotdwar tender notice for sensor lights and pipe clamps

The corporation issued notice 813 on 18 September 2019. Its signed attachment says tender information would be available on the state procurement portal from 20 September 2019.

| Supply item | Quantity | Earnest money | Tender document price |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 70/72-watt LED light with sensor | 2,000 | ₹1,20,000 | ₹4,000 plus GST |
| Class B GI pipe clamp assembly with nuts and bolts | 2,000 | ₹24,000 | ₹1,000 plus GST |

The notice gives 45 days of bid validity and 20 days for completion for each item. The district listing displays 18 October 2019 as its end date. This archival copy does not establish the submission cutoff, an award or delivery.

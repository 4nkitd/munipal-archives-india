# Kotdwar in the state's legacy waste remediation update

The Urban Development Department's Swachh Bharat Mission dashboard reports funding received for remediation of eight dumpsites across seven urban local bodies, including Kotdwar. Haridwar accounts for two sites in that group. The same section reports approval by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs of 21 detailed project reports across 20 urban local bodies under SBM 2.0.

Those are state-level counts, not Kotdwar totals. The funding entry does not give a Kotdwar site address, the local allocation or a completion date. It does not show that the dumpsite was remediated.

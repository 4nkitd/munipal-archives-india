# Formation of Kothagudem Municipal Corporation

## Statutory entry and commencement

The Governor assented to Act 17 of 2025 on 9 April 2025. The Telangana Gazette published it on 15 April. Section 4(iv) of the amendment omitted Kothagudem and Palvancha, entries 27 and 28, from the Bhadradri Kothagudem district list in Schedule I of the Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019. Section 5(ii), on page 6, placed Kothagudem in Schedule II as corporation entry 15 with 60 wards.

[G.O.Ms.No.103](https://tggazette.cgg.gov.in/viewDocument/1752576956769), dated 28 May and published in Gazette No. 106-F on 29 May 2025, appointed **29 May 2025** as the commencement date of the Act. The corporation's [history page](https://kothagudemmunicipality.telangana.gov.in/pages/about-us) cites 28 May as the date of the order, not the date on which the Act came into force.

## Areas named in the merger

The new entry combines Kothagudem and Palvancha municipalities with the Komatipalli, Laxmidevipalli, Nimmalagudem, Mangapeta, Nayakulagudem, Sujathanagar and Narasimhasagar gram panchayats. The Act lists the merging bodies and fixes the number of wards. It does not assign these places to individual wards. The corporation's history page also mentions 33 election wards in the former Kothagudem body, which is not a count for the post-merger corporation.

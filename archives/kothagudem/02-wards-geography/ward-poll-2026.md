# Kothagudem ward reservations and votes in 2026

## Election coverage

The State Election Commission's [27 January 2026 election notification](https://tsec.gov.in/preview.do?fileName=L1VwbG9hZHMvVFNFQy9wZGYvZ2F6ZXR0ZV9vZl8xMjNfdWxiX3dhcmRfZWxlY3Rpb25zXzUxOC5wZGY=#page=12), No. 150/TGSEC-ULBs/2026, lists 60 ward-member seats for Kothagudem. Page 2 scheduled polling for 11 February and counting for 13 February 2026.

The corporation's Proforma X return runs from serial 1410 to 1469 on PDF pages 156 to 165. These serials are statewide report indexes, not ward numbers. The return identifies reservations and votes for this election; it is not a delimitation schedule or address-to-ward lookup.

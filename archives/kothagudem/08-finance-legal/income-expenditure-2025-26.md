# Kothagudem income and expenditure statement, 2025–26

## Reported figures

The corporation portal posts this CDMA statement for 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. These amounts in rupees come from the statement's Total column, which combines the Municipal General Fund and Capital Project Fund.

| Account head or statement line | Amount (₹) |
|---|---:|
| Tax revenue (110) | 397,540,745.61 |
| Fees and user charges (140) | 111,704,265.67 |
| Total income | 525,035,557.08 |
| Establishment expenses (210) | 77,053,048.00 |
| Operations and maintenance (230) | 77,072,882.00 |
| Total expenditure | 180,937,746.42 |
| Surplus before depreciation and prior-period items | 344,097,810.66 |
| Miscellaneous expenses (271) | 563,760.00 |
| Depreciation (272) | 23,705,867.00 |
| Net surplus carried to the Municipal Fund | 319,828,183.66 |

## Reading the statement

The Municipal General Fund shows a net surplus of ₹342,012,528.44; the Capital Project Fund shows a net deficit of ₹22,184,344.78. The combined figure is the accounting balance carried forward, not the amount of cash on hand.

The reporting period begins before the corporation's 29 May 2025 commencement. The statement gives no separate before-and-after figures for the merger and shows no publication date or audit opinion.

# Kozhikode Municipal Corporation: municipal scope and office

## Corporation and district

The corporation's history page dates its establishment to 1962 and names H. Manjunatha Rao as its first mayor. The [district administration's corporation page](https://kozhikode.nic.in/en/about-district/adminstration/muncipal-corporation/) confirms both facts and identifies the corporation as the local government for the city of Kozhikode. The district administration lists [other municipalities](https://kozhikode.nic.in/en/about-district/adminstration/muncipality/) separately. A Kozhikode district postal address does not by itself make a property or event part of the corporation.

The city history page mentions Kozhikode North, Kozhikode South, Beypore and Elathur as legislative constituencies. These are not council wards. The history page also describes an older 75-ward arrangement. Use the [State Election Commission constituency directory](https://sec.kerala.gov.in/public/cnstncy/wd/b5fc772c-e554-490f-b077-a8ef99885d25) for the 76 ward codes and names listed at verification.

## Legal basis and office

The [Local Self Government Department's Acts and Rules page](https://lsgd.kerala.gov.in/en/regulations-and-policies/policies-acts-and-rules/) lists the Kerala Municipality Act, 1994. The [State Election Commission's delimitation guidance](https://sec.kerala.gov.in/portal/kc/delimit) cites section 69 of that Act and Article 243R of the Constitution for municipal wards. It explains that councillors are directly elected from territorial wards and that Delimitation Commission orders published in the Gazette have the force of law. Neither the history page nor the election directory is an address-level boundary schedule.

The [corporation's address page](https://kozhikodecorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/address) gives the headquarters as Calicut Beach, near Akashvani, Kozhikode 673032. It publishes 0495-2365040 and secretarykkd@gmail.com as corporation office channels.

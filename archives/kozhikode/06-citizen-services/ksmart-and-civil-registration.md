# K-SMART and civil registration in Kozhikode

## Register an event or find a certificate

Kerala LSGD says a birth or death must be registered with the local unit where it occurred. The family's home address does not determine the registrar. For an event within Kozhikode Municipal Corporation, the state guide identifies K-SMART as the municipal system and also lists Akshaya Kendras and the local body's front office as application channels. It says to report the event within 21 days. For a delay beyond 30 days and up to one year, the guide identifies the Municipal Secretary as the sanctioning authority in a corporation; for later cases it identifies the Revenue Divisional Officer.

For an existing birth record, the guide says to use Certificate Search in the [K-SMART citizen portal](https://ksmart.lsgkerala.gov.in/) with the date of birth and the mother's name or its first three letters in English. For a death certificate, it lists the date of death and the deceased person's name. Searching for an existing certificate is separate from registering an event or correcting a record.

## Tax, permits and grievances

The [state K-SMART service list](https://lsgd.kerala.gov.in/en/services/digital-services-description-add-information-kerala-mission/k-smart/) lists property tax, building permission, trade and industry licensing, and public grievance redressal among the modules in use in urban local bodies. The [Kozhikode corporation home page](https://kozhikodecorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en) still displays older links to Sevana, property tax e-payment, Suvega and Sanketham. Those links do not establish which system takes a new application. The state guidance is the basis for the K-SMART route described here.

# Kozhikode annual financial statement, 2020-21

## Statement and accounting period

Kozhikode Municipal Corporation's entry for the 2020-21 annual financial statement says it was posted on 5 November 2021. Its attachment labels identify 1 April 2020 through 31 March 2021 as the reporting period for income and expenditure, the ledger trial balance, and receipts and payments. The balance sheet and its schedule are dated 31 March 2021. These dates identify the accounting period and page posting, not the date of an audit opinion.

| Material named in the municipal entry | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Income and expenditure and I&E schedules | Annual statement and supporting schedules for the reporting period. The page lists two income-and-expenditure files without explaining the difference. |
| Balance sheet, balance-sheet schedule and B1 schedule | Year-end statements or schedules dated 31 March 2021. |
| RP and RP schedules | Receipts-and-payments files dated 1 April 2020 to 31 March 2021. |
| Ledger trial balance, cash flow and key ratios | Separately listed files; the trial-balance label gives the reporting period and the ratios file is dated 31 March 2021. |

## Find other finance publications

The corporation's [financial statement register](https://kozhikodecorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/financial-statement) groups the annual accounts. The [budget register](https://kozhikodecorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/budget) is separate. For example, the corporation posted its [2023-24 budget page](https://kozhikodecorporation.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/node/757) on 31 May 2024. The budget period and the web posting date are not the same thing, and the budget is not evidence of actual receipts or expenditure. This record preserves the public filing details, not figures from the attachments.

# Establishment of Kumbakonam City Municipal Corporation

## Succession and city limits

Tamil Nadu Extraordinary Gazette No. 602, Part IV, Section 2, published the ordinance on the day the Governor promulgated it. Section 1 brought it into force at once. Section 3 made the area of the former Kumbakonam Municipality the city and abolished the former municipality. The corporation could hold and dispose of property, enter into contracts and sue or be sued in its own name. Subsequent changes to the city limits required a government notification, prior publication and consultation with the corporation.

## Authorities and council

Section 4 named the Mayor, council, standing committee, wards committee and Commissioner. Section 5 authorised the government to fix the number of elected councillors, subject to a ceiling of 72. Members of Parliament and the Legislative Assembly meeting the qualifications in section 5(2) could participate in council proceedings without voting. The subsequent Act 6 of 2022 retained 20 December 2021 as the commencement date.

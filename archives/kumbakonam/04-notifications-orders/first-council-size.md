# Notification fixing 48 elected seats for Kumbakonam

## Size of the elected council

G.O. Ms. No. 136 of 24 December 2021, notification II(2)/MAWS/925(c)/2021, exercised the power in section 5(1) of Ordinance 7 of 2021. It fixed the number of councillors to be elected at 48, effective from the next ordinary election. The order gives a seat total, not a list of ward boundaries.

## Authority for divisions and reservations

On 29 December, [G.O. (D) No. 140](https://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2021/621_Ex_II_2.pdf), notification II(2)/MAWS/957(a)/2021, made the Commissioner competent to exercise the government’s powers under sections 5(4) to (6) of the ordinance and section 51 of the Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation Act, 1981, for Kumbakonam. The gazette concerns delimitation and reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and women. It delegates authority without assigning particular wards to categories.

# Transfer of Kumbakonam municipal taxes, property and liabilities

## Financial continuity under section 9

Act 6 of 2022 received assent on 21 January 2022. It appears at printed pages 41 to 48 of the combined gazette. Section 1(3) gives it effect from 20 December 2021. For a bill, debt or claim dating from the municipal council period, the relevant transitional provisions are on printed pages 44 and 45.

| Provision | Effect at conversion |
|---|---|
| 9(1) | Council property, rights, interests and legally subsisting liabilities vested in the corporation, subject to government directions. |
| 9(2) | The corporation could recover arrears due to the council, including taxes, payments in composition of tax, expenses and compensation. |
| 9(3) | Existing municipal taxes, fees and duties continued until revised, cancelled or superseded. |
| 9(4) | Proceedings by or against the former council or authority could continue by or against its successor. |

These provisions explain the transfer of obligations; section 9 does not prescribe a present-day rate of tax.

# Kurnool Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and official portals

## Which local body this is

Kurnool district administration lists Kurnool Municipal Corporation separately from **Adoni Municipality**, **Yemmiganur Municipality** and **Gudur Nagarapanchayat**. In the State Election Commission's November 2026 report, both the district and the urban local body columns read **Kurnool** for the corporation's ward elections. The first label is the district; the second identifies the city body. The [district corporation listing](https://kurnool.ap.gov.in/public-utility/kurnol-municipal-corporation/) points to the Kurnool-specific municipal portal.

## Which record to use

For a municipal property assessment, use the [Kurnool corporation's service links](https://kurnool.cdma.ap.gov.in/) and match the municipal body named on the assessment. For an electoral ward number in the November 2026 edition, use the [ward election record](../02-wards-geography/2026-ward-election-record.md). The district's municipal directory does not give a corporation perimeter or a ward-to-locality schedule. A Kurnool district address is not by itself evidence that Kurnool Municipal Corporation assesses the property.

The district corporation listing gives the location only as Kurnool. This entry does not supply a street address, a contact from a different office, or a formation date without a supporting source.

# Kurnool electoral wards in the November 2026 corporation election

## Ward numbers and reservations

The Andhra Pradesh State Election Commission's Proforma X, dated 4 November 2026, lists **68 wards numbered 1 through 68** under Kurnool as both district and urban local body. The city entries occupy PDF pages 96 through 108. The reservation labels apply to this election edition.

## Poll and map limits

The return marks ward **53** as unanimous and gives 1 November 2026 as the poll date for the other wards. The source records the electorate, invalid and valid votes and candidate-level results for contested wards. The separate [CDMA first-meeting order](../04-notifications-orders/2026-first-corporation-meeting.md) sets the date when the elected ward members were to take office.

The report does not give boundary lines or a ward-to-locality list. The [Kurnool city map](https://kurnool.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/kurnool/gis-map) is a separate lookup; it is not the statutory delimitation schedule.

# CDMA order setting Kurnool's first corporation meeting after the 2026 election

## First meeting and term

The Commissioner and Director of Municipal Administration, acting as Election Authority, issued proceedings **Roc.No.13021/471/2026/H1** dated **8 November 2026**. The State Election Commission portal lists the order on **22 November 2026**. It appoints **23 November 2026** as the date of the first meeting for eleven named municipal corporations, including Kurnool. The proceedings state that elected ward members take office from that date.

The order cites **Section 7(1)(a) of the Andhra Pradesh Municipal Corporations Act, 1994**: except as the Act expressly provides otherwise, the term of elected members is five years from the date the Election Authority appoints for the first meeting. It also cites **Section 673-A of the Municipal Corporations Act, 1955** for the oath procedure.

## Deputy mayor election

The order cites **Section 90(2) of the Municipal Corporations Act, 1955**, under which the deputy mayor election is to take place at the first meeting or a subsequent meeting within two weeks. It records a State Election Commission schedule for that election on 23 November for the eleven corporations. It is a scheduling order, not meeting minutes or an election result.

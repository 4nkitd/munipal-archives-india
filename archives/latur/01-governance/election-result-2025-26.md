# Latur: wards and seats in the 2025-26 general election

## Final result

Row 22 of the Commission’s final party table gives separate figures for electoral wards (18), council seats (70) and contesting candidates (359). Results were declared for all 70 seats.

INC won 43 seats, BJP 22 and NCP one. The Commission identifies the four seats in its unrecognized-party column as Vanchit Bahujan Aghadi.

The [Commission’s publication register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the table to 16 January 2026.

# Latur: final ward schedule and locality index

## Legal extent

Notification SEC/MC-2025/CR-31/D5 of 8 October 2025 follows the draft of 3 September. Under section 5(3), the final schedule sets the boundaries for the next municipal general election. Ward 1, for example, has a scheduled population of 20,807 and four seats. Its northern boundary reaches the municipal limit near Hanmatwadi Road; its eastern boundary runs from Anna Bhau Sathe Chowk towards Hanuman Temple.

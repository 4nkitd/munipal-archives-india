# Latur city budget estimates for 2025-26

## Original estimates and opening balance

The Accounts Department statement dated 27 March 2025 presents revised estimates for 2024-25 and original estimates for 2025-26 under sections 95 and 95A of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. The opening statement gives the following figures for 2025-26.

| Budget line | Estimated amount (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|
| Opening balance | 140.44 |
| Revenue income | 200.63 |
| Capital receipts | 224.35 |
| Central government grant | 285.10 |
| Total available including opening balance | 1,064.07 |
| Revenue expenditure | 236.24 |
| Capital expenditure | 284.64 |
| Total expenditure across all heads | 1,063.90 |

The opening balance is money carried forward, not revenue raised in the year. After all projected spending, the statement estimates a closing balance of ₹17 lakh. These figures are budget estimates rather than audited outturns.

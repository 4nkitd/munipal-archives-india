# Lucknow Nagar Nigam and its municipal jurisdiction

## Corporation and district

The [Lucknow District Administration's public-utility listing](https://lucknow.nic.in/public-utility/nagar-nigam-lucknow/) identifies Nagar Nigam Lucknow at Lalbagh and links to the corporation's website. The corporation's [Contact Us page](https://lmc.up.nic.in/helpline.aspx) gives the office address as Lucknow Nagar Nigam, Trilokinath Road, Lalbagh, Lucknow-226001, Uttar Pradesh. It lists the control room toll-free number as `1533` and the municipal email as `nnlko@nic.in`.

The Uttar Pradesh [ward delimitation notification of 31 October 2022](https://lmc.up.nic.in/pdf/DelimiationfinalNotification31_10_2022HINDI.pdf) divides the corporation's urban local body area into 110 wards. It does not divide the whole district. The district's [separate tehsil directory](https://lucknow.nic.in/tehsil%e2%80%8c-name/) lists five tehsils. A tehsil entry is not evidence that an address lies within a Nagar Nigam ward. Use the municipal notification's locality and boundary columns together when checking a ward.

## Lucknow Cantonment

The Ministry of Defence's [Directorate General Defence Estates](https://dgde.gov.in/en/cantonments/#Central-Command) lists Lucknow Cantonment separately under Central Command and states that Cantonment Boards carry out municipal administration in notified cantonments. For civic administration at an address within the cantonment, consult the [Lucknow Cantonment Board](https://lucknow.cantt.gov.in/) rather than the corporation.

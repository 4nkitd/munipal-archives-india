# Final delimitation of 110 Lucknow Nagar Nigam wards, 31 October 2022

## Notification and schedule

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The Uttar Pradesh Government's Urban Development Section 1 issued notification **3052/9-1-2022-45परि०/22** at Lucknow on **31 October 2022**. It cites section 32-B (धारा 32 ख) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959 (Uttar Pradesh Act No. 2 of 1959). The notification says the Governor considered objections received after the order published in the gazette under subsection (2), notification **1730/9-1-2022-45परि०/22** of **12 July 2022**.

The schedule gives a ward number, ward name, locality extent and boundary description. The notification fixes each ward's extent by reference to columns 3 and 4 together. The last row is ward 110. The figures below cover selected rows, not all 110 wards. Read the scan's full row for a particular address, especially where a locality is marked `आंशिक` (part only).

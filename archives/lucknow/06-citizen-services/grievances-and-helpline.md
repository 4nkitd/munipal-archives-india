# Lucknow municipal complaints and control room

## Municipal routes

The corporation's [Contact Us page](https://lmc.up.nic.in/helpline.aspx) lists these contacts:

| Route | Published contact |
| --- | --- |
| Toll-free control room | `1533` |
| WhatsApp and calling | `9219902911`, `9219902912`, `9219902913`, `9219902914` |
| Municipal email | `nnlko@nic.in` |
| Office | Lucknow Nagar Nigam, Trilokinath Road, Lalbagh, Lucknow-226001, Uttar Pradesh |

On the [corporation homepage](https://lmc.up.nic.in/), use `शिकायत दर्ज़करे` to file a municipal complaint or `शिकायत की स्थिति जाने` to look up its status. These menu links lead to an external complaint platform. Start from the corporation homepage so it supplies the links. The same page separately tells residents to call the mayor's office at `6389200005` if someone demands additional money for waste collection after the resident has already paid online. That announcement identifies a specific use for the number; the control-room number is the general municipal contact.

## Uttar Pradesh grievance portal

[Jansunwai–Samadhan](https://jansunwai.up.nic.in/) is the Uttar Pradesh Government's separate grievance portal. It describes complaint registration using a mobile OTP, status tracking, a reminder when no action has been taken within the prescribed time, and feedback on the disposition. The state portal excludes Right to Information requests, matters pending before a court, suggestions, requests for financial assistance or employment, and government employee service matters where internal options have not been used. A Jansunwai reference is for the state system, not the corporation's complaint tracker.

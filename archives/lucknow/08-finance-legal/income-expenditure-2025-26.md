# Lucknow Nagar Nigam income and expenditure to 31 March 2026

## Statement and comparative figures

The [Accounts Department](https://lmc.up.nic.in/lekhavibhag.aspx) publishes the corporation's [income and expenditure statement for the period ended 31 March 2026](https://lmc.up.nic.in/pdf/IncomeandExpenditureStatementforthePeriodEndedon31stMarch2026.pdf#page=1). The amounts below are in **rupees**, not lakh. The source's comparative column is for 2024–25.

| Item or head of account | 2025–26 (₹) | 2024–25 (₹) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Tax Revenue | 6,49,04,55,839.96 | 5,42,17,17,269.00 |
| Fees & User Charges | 94,37,59,272.89 | 76,37,08,435.71 |
| Revenue Grants, Contributions & Subsidies | 14,95,06,60,273.00 | 12,57,93,00,261.00 |
| Total income | 25,69,72,44,318.60 | 21,20,89,00,274.51 |
| Establishment Expenses | 6,20,79,15,205.00 | 6,14,57,83,865.00 |
| Operations and Maintenance | 13,73,25,78,692.23 | 10,35,33,62,647.07 |
| Total expenditure | 24,46,54,44,236.91 | 20,52,02,00,682.76 |
| Surplus before prior-period items | 1,23,18,00,081.69 | 68,86,99,591.75 |
| Net surplus carried over to Municipal Fund | 1,23,18,00,081.69 | 46,00,21,595.75 |

The statement shows a negative prior-period adjustment of **₹22,86,77,996.00** for 2024–25. The 2025–26 adjustment cell is blank and the reported surplus is the same before and after prior-period items. The [balance sheet on page 2](https://lmc.up.nic.in/pdf/IncomeandExpenditureStatementforthePeriodEndedon31stMarch2026.pdf#page=2) reports Municipal (General) Fund balances of **₹32,47,45,23,050.63** as of 31 March 2026 and **₹31,24,27,22,968.92** as of 31 March 2025. These balances are not the annual surplus or the next year's budget.

# Lucknow Nagar Nigam main budget for 2026–27

## Resolution and estimates

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The [Accounts Department](https://lmc.up.nic.in/lekhavibhag.aspx) lists this scan as "Main Budget-26-27". The [summary sheet](https://lmc.up.nic.in/pdf/MainBadjut202627.pdf#page=3) says the executive committee passed the main budget unchanged at its special meeting on **22 February 2026**, by resolution **396**. It also says the budget was presented to the municipal House for approval under section 146 of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959. The committee resolution is not a publication date or a separate record of the House vote.

The table transcribes the 2026–27 original provisions in **₹ lakh**, the unit used by the source.

## Reading the comparison columns

The summary also shows actual income and expenditure for 2024–25, revised provisions for 2025–26, and 2025–26 actuals only for **1 April 2025 through 31 December 2025**. The revised and partial-year columns are not the full-year accounts. The separately published [income and expenditure statement to 31 March 2026](income-expenditure-2025-26.md) reports full-year accounting figures in rupees.

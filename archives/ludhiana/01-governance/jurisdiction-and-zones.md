# Ludhiana municipal jurisdiction and zonal offices

## City and district

Municipal Corporation Ludhiana is the local body for Ludhiana city in Ludhiana district, Punjab. Its [About Us page](https://mcludhiana.gov.in/About.aspx) reports a city area of 159.37 square kilometres. This is the corporation's published figure, not a legal boundary survey. The [district administration](https://ludhiana.nic.in/administration/municipal-councils-nagar-panchayats/) lists Jagraon, Khanna, Doraha and other towns as separate local bodies.

The Punjab State Election Commission's [district-wise ward-count list](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/CamScanner_08-01-2023_13.08.52_3.pdf) records 95 for Municipal Corporation Ludhiana on page 2. The [Commission's index](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/en/municipal-election/district-wise-numbers-of-wards-of-municipalities/) dates the list entry 14 August 2023. The document gives a count, not a ward boundary schedule or a ward-to-zone crosswalk.

## Zonal offices

The corporation's [Contact Us page](https://mcludhiana.gov.in/ContactUs.aspx) and the district's [MC Zones directory](https://ludhiana.nic.in/public-utility-category/mc-zones/) list four zones:

| Zone | Municipal office location | Office PBX |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Mata Rani Chowk | 0161-2917009 |
| B | Behind Shingar Cinema | 0161-2917010 |
| C | Gill Chowk | 0161-2917078 |
| D | Saraba Nagar | 0161-2917004 |

The corporation lists its Commissioner Office separately at Zone D, Saraba Nagar, with PBX numbers 0161-2917005 and 0161-2917006. The About Us page does not give a publication date.

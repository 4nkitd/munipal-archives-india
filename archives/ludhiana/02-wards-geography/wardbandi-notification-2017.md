# Ludhiana draft wardbandi notification, December 2017

## Municipal notice and scan

The corporation's [Important Updates & Notifications](https://mcludhiana.gov.in/Notifications.aspx) lists serial 119 as "Notification Wardbandi (SOB) MC Ludhiana-2017", dated 22 December 2017 in the portal. Select page 6 and use Download beside serial 119. The portal returns a 27-page Punjabi scan named `1330596679.pdf` through the same URL.

The first page is dated 21 December 2017 and identifies the issuer as the Punjab Department of Local Government. It cites section 8 of the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976 and clause 7 of the Delimitation of Wards of Municipal Corporations Order, 1995. It proposes dividing the city into 95 wards and gives seven days from publication for objections and suggestions. The proposed boundary descriptions follow in a schedule. The date on this archive record is the portal listing date; the Gazette publication date was not verified.

## What this record does not establish

The scan has no usable text layer. This record describes the draft from its first page; it does not transcribe the boundary schedule or treat the proposal as a final notification. The [State Election Commission's ward-count list](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/en/municipal-election/district-wise-numbers-of-wards-of-municipalities/) from 2023 establishes a later count, not the validity of the proposed 2017 ward lines.

# Ludhiana bulk water supply resettlement plan notice

## Disclosure and underlying report

The corporation's [notifications index](https://mcludhiana.gov.in/Notifications.aspx) dates "Resettlement Action Plan (RAP) Report- LBWSS Project" to 20 November 2026. The [linked report](https://mcladmin.mcludhiana.gov.in/Imp_Upload/Imp_File//Resettlement_Action_Plan_(RAP)_Report-_LBWSS_Project_18.12.2025_Clean_File_07.01.2026_134396329519143921.pdf) identifies itself as December 2025, Revision 1. The date on this archive record is the notice listing date, not the report's revision date.

## Pipeline works and affected vendors

The report describes a proposed 580 million litres per day water treatment plant at Bilga, drawing from the Sirhind Canal. It gives a proposed bulk transmission network of 164.928 kilometres and 70 new overhead service reservoirs. The plan anticipates that excavation and pipe laying may temporarily disrupt roadside vendors on some stretches, even though it says transmission lines would not require land acquisition. It records a 29 October 2025 public notice of the eligibility cut-off and says the socioeconomic survey began on 3 November 2025.

The notice does not establish that construction is complete or that households have switched to treated canal water.

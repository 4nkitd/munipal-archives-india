# Applying for a Ludhiana water or sewerage connection

## New connection documents

The [Operation and Maintenance Branch guide](https://mcludhiana.gov.in/SelfHelpServiceDetail.aspx?ServiceId=UHS4kt3GLM9xxNaE9xi%2bXg%3d%3d) says the property must already have a municipal house number. Use the corporation's Form A, with separate forms for water and sewerage. A plumber approved by the corporation must verify the completed form. Attach a verified self-declaration, a completed agreement form and property evidence. The guide lists copies of TS-1, the current year's property tax return and an electricity bill.

The page links to application, self-declaration, plumber technical and agreement forms. It also points residents to the corporation website for road-cutting charges. The guide does not state a single new-connection fee.

## Billing corrections are a separate request

For a correction to a water or sewerage bill, the same guide calls for Annexure A, clearance of outstanding dues, a verified self-declaration and evidence for the change, such as a copy of the property registry and the current year's property tax return. It lists a ₹50 correction fee. This is the amount stated in the undated guide, not a verified current tariff. No application was submitted.

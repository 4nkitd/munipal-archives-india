# Filing and paying Ludhiana property tax

## Property tax returns and payments

The corporation's [Property Tax Payment guide](https://mcludhiana.gov.in/SelfHelpServiceDetail.aspx?ServiceId=5Uyn39EsyvIMvRPHlKP4Fg%3d%3d) sends residents to [eSeva](https://eseva.mcludhiana.gov.in/) to self-assess their property, calculate tax, print the statement or challan and pay. It lists credit card, debit card and net banking as online payment options. The guide says to map the UID number to the property tax return to use services through a single UID.

Residents can also file a property tax return at a Sewa Kendra or a zonal Suwidha Centre. The guide lists Suwidha centres as an in-person payment option. Its designated helpline is 84375-35700.

The guide is undated. Its instructions and the eSeva landing page were checked, but no return was filed and no payment was made. This record does not establish any current tax rate, rebate or due date.

# Ludhiana proposed municipal budget for 2019–20

## Proposed totals

The corporation's [Municipal Budget index](https://mcludhiana.gov.in/Budget.aspx) labels this file "Municipal Budget 2019-20". Page 1 of the [scan](https://mcladmin.mcludhiana.gov.in/Content/Budget/Budget%202019-20.pdf) describes it as a proposed budget and shows ₹94,965 lakh in both proposed income and proposed expenditure. The income schedule on page 2 gives these selected estimates:

| Income head | 2019–20 estimate, ₹ lakh |
| --- | ---: |
| Share of GST | 57,000 |
| House tax and property tax | 10,000 |
| Income of the Operation and Maintenance Cell | 7,500 |
| Municipal tax on electricity | 4,500 |
| Additional excise duty | 4,000 |
| Total across all income heads | 94,965 |

The PDF is a scan. Figures were read using OCR on the first two pages and the income column was checked visually. These figures are estimates, not actual collections or audited expenditure. The index and the checked budget pages give a financial year but do not establish a publication date or an adoption date.

# Machilipatnam municipal jurisdiction and city figures

## Municipal jurisdiction

Machilipatnam is the headquarters of Krishna district. The state profile lists 2019 as the corporation establishment year. Krishna district lists its municipal office at Srinivas Nagar Colony, Machilipatnam, Andhra Pradesh 521001. Gudivada, Pedana and Vuyyuru have their own municipal listings. See the [district municipal directory](https://krishna.ap.gov.in/public-utility-category/municipality/).

## Figures in the state municipal profile

| Measure | Reported figure |
| --- | ---: |
| Municipal area | 26.67 km² |
| Population | 169,892 |
| Households | 57,494 |
| Election wards | 60 |
| Revenue wards | 32 |
| Ward secretariats | 51 |

The [state directory](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/ulbdetails/ulbprofiles) classifies urban local body 81 as a corporation. The population and household fields in the city profile have no reference year. They describe the city profile, not the district census.

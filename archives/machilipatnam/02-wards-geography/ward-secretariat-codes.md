# Machilipatnam electoral wards and ward secretariat codes

## Electoral, revenue and service divisions

The [city profile](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/81) distinguishes 60 election wards, 32 revenue wards and 51 ward sachivalayams (secretariats). The state directory identifies the secretariats by name and an eight-digit office code. A suffix such as “01” belongs to the office name; neither the suffix nor the code gives an electoral ward number.

# Machilipatnam property tax assessment and proceedings

## Choosing the property tax service

On the Machilipatnam municipal home page, open Municipal Services and choose an entry under Online Services. “Know Your Dues” is the balance enquiry. “Property Tax” opens the calculator, while “Self Assessment” links to a new property assessment form. A calculated amount is distinct from an existing demand against a property.

| What the resident needs | City portal entry |
| --- | --- |
| Ask for review of an assessment | Revision Petition |
| Record a registered transfer | Mutation |
| Apply for relief on grounds of vacancy | Vacancy Remission |
| Search for the proceedings on a property | Download Proceedings |

The city links to Machilipatnam-specific eMunicipal pages. Its service menu gives no fee, decision deadline or tax rate for these applications.

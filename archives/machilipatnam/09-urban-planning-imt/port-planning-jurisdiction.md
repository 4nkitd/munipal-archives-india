# Machilipatnam port planning jurisdiction and land acquisition

## Corporation and regional planning area

Krishna district describes a proposed 426.16 km² area for the Machilipatnam Urban Development Authority (MUDA). It includes Machilipatnam Municipal Corporation and 28 revenue villages, and concerns land use near the port, described as a 10 to 30 km radius. The 426.16 km² figure refers to the proposed planning region. It is not the area of the corporation or a list of villages brought into city wards.

## Planning powers and land for the port

The district cites the Andhra Pradesh Metropolitan Region and Urban Development Act, 2016 for master and zonal plans, land acquisition and disposal, inspections and penalties. It lists both a land pooling scheme and a land purchase scheme for acquiring land for port construction. The authority draws on Development Fund grants and revolving funds of the Municipal Administration and Urban Development Department. The district description is not a parcel-specific acquisition notice.

# Madhubani Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## Legal class and geographic scope

The district's [public utility entry](https://madhubani.nic.in/public-utility/nagar-parishad/) is titled Nagar Nigam, although the web address retains `nagar-parishad`. The title identifies the city office as a corporation. The Bihar State Election Commission independently records MADHUBANI as a नगर निगम (municipal corporation) in Madhubani Sadar subdivision at serial 194 of the [final ward formation register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0). The status column says उत्क्रमित (upgraded) and the register gives 45 wards. That column does not establish the date or terms of the original upgrade notification.

The municipal corporation covers the city area described in its ward documents. It is not a district-wide body. In the district's [urban local body directory](https://madhubani.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/), Ghoghardiha, Jainagar, Benipatti and Phulparas appear under Nagar Panchayat entries, and Jhanjharpur appears as a Nagar Parishad. Those entries are separate from the corporation. Shared use of the district name or a nearby postal address does not put a place inside Madhubani Nagar Nigam.

## Office and boundary references

The district lists the corporation office at Madhubani, PIN 847211, with the landline 06276-221125. It supplies neither a street address nor office hours in the entry. The [SEC Form 6](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=5&SID=4&SN=1549) is the ward-level reference for the edition spanning wards 1 to 45. Its total of 164,396 is drawn from Census 2011 figures attributed to the wards, not a new headcount at the time of upgrade.

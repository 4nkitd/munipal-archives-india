# Madhubani wards in the Bihar SEC final Form 6

## Ward counts and census basis

The [Bihar SEC Form 6](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=5&SID=4&SN=1549) is headed Madhubani district, Madhubani Sadar subdivision and Madhubani Nagar Nigam. It gives wards 1 through 45, each with a Census 2011 population breakdown, boundary text in Hindi and a ward map link. The body is listed as upgraded in the [parent register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0).

Ward 6 has the smallest recorded total and ward 39 the largest. The figures count residents on the stated census basis, not electors or votes. The six individual rows illustrate the format; the source gives figures for all wards.

## Boundary landmarks

Ward 2 has the Kosi Canal on the west and a northern segment from the Kosi canal bridge to the Laheriyaganj turn at Marar Gate. Ward 20's eastern description mentions a road and the Kamla River, while ward 22's eastern text runs from the railway station to the southern wall of Watson School. Ward 39's row names the Kamla River to the north and the Kosi Canal to the west. These markers help locate the relevant row; they are not a complete delineation of the ward. Read all four sides and use its linked map for an address near an edge. The district's [Municipal Election page](https://madhubani.nic.in/municipal-election/) also indexes election materials, but it groups municipal entries across the district; confirm the named body before applying a list to Madhubani Nagar Nigam.

# Madhubani Nagar Nigam sixth-phase teacher recruitment notices

## Notice sequence

Madhubani District Administration preserved several records of teacher recruitment under the city Nagar Nigam. The notices distinguish classes 1 to 5 and 6 to 8, and identify the recruitment cycle as 2019-2020, sixth phase. The dates in the right column are the district portal's Start Date and End Date fields. They are not the effective dates of every appointment or posting.

## Using the archive

For a retrospective appointment query, look for the city recruitment unit and the class band first, then choose the notice type. A vacancy list records posts; it does not show who received an appointment letter. An announcement about distributing appointment letters does not establish that any named candidate took up a post. The district's notices link separate attachments for these stages. This record indexes them without reproducing candidate information, and none of the display windows above is a current application period.

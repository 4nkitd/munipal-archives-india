## Reading the accounts

These are ledger figures for the reporting period, not individual tax rates, budget appropriations or cash collection totals. The PDF also contains the Revenue Fund income and expenditure statement and balance sheet, and separate Water Supply and Drainage Fund statements. The [municipal accounts page](https://maduraicorporation.co.in/aboutus/budget-accounts/) labels this link “2024 - 2025”; it does not label that entry as audited or show its publication date.

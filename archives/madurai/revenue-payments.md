# Madurai Corporation online revenue payments

## Which charges the department lists

The Revenue Department lists property tax, water tax and underground drainage payments. Its payment link goes to [Tamil Nadu Urban ePay](https://tnurbanepay.tn.gov.in/), the state Commissionerate of Municipal Administration's portal.

## Assessment details and receipt

The portal's [tax payment manual](https://tnurbanepay.tn.gov.in/UserManual-converted.pdf) describes signing in, choosing the tax in the Make Payment menu and entering or searching for the assessment number. After a successful payment, it directs the user to select the receipt number for the printable receipt. The corporation's Revenue page does not specify a rate, deadline or waiver for any of these charges.

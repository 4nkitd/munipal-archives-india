# Madurai Corporation ward election results, 2022

## Ward coverage and reservation

The Tamil Nadu State Election Commission's district-specific return lists Madurai Corporation wards 1 through 100. Counting the labels printed next to the ward numbers gives 47 general, 46 general (women), three Scheduled Caste (general), and four Scheduled Caste (women) wards for this election.

## Poll totals as reported

| Ward | Electors | Votes polled | Turnout | Valid votes | Invalid votes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 9,639 | 4,885 | 50.68% | 4,884 | 1 |
| 2 | 11,375 | 5,048 | 44.38% | 5,048 | 0 |

The PDF includes candidate-level returns. For corrections, the commission refers readers to the local Returning Officer and says the applicable entries in Forms 23, 25 and 26 and the result declared in Form 27 are final. The return does not identify ward boundaries or establish who holds office today. The year in the title identifies the election, not a publication date.

## Routing RTI requests

In each zone, the directory lists the zone Administrative Officer as Public Information Officer and the zone Assistant Commissioner as appellate authority. At the central office it lists the Assistant Commissioner (Personnel) for central administration and for council resolutions and elections, the Assistant Commissioner (Accounts) for central accounts, the Assistant Commissioner (Revenue) for central revenue, and the City Health Officer for the Health Section. The contact page does not assign wards to these offices.

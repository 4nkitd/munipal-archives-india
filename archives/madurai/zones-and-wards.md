The “Zone-wise distribution” table is the source for this assignment. The historical narrative elsewhere on the page separately describes a four-zone arrangement at expansion. The [corporation's office directory](https://maduraicorporation.co.in/contact-us/) lists five zone offices. The allocation table does not trace individual ward boundaries or date the change to five zones.

## Municipal history

The corporation dates Madurai's formation as a municipality to 1 November 1866 under the Town Improvement Act of 1865 and its upgrade to a corporation to 1 May 1971. The page reports that expansion added three municipalities, three town panchayats and eleven village panchayats to the corporation area, raising the number of wards from 72 to 100.

# Formation of Mahabubnagar Municipal Corporation

## Statutory entry and commencement

The Governor assented to Act 4 of 2025 on 3 January 2025. The Telangana Gazette published it on 4 January. Section 6(xvi)(a) omitted entry 110 from Schedule I of the Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019. Section 7(iii), page 11, inserted Mahabubnagar as entry 13 in Schedule II, with 60 wards. The entry combines Mahabubnagar Municipality with Divitipally and Zainallipur gram panchayats.

[G.O.Ms.No.14](https://tggazette.cgg.gov.in/viewDocument/1738650747527), published on 25 January 2025 in Gazette No. 20, assigns different dates to different provisions. Paragraph (iii) appoints **27 January 2025** for both the Schedule I deletion and the Schedule II insertion affecting Mahabubnagar.

## Name and jurisdiction

The Act spells the corporation's name “Mahabubnagar”. The State Election Commission uses “Mahaboobnagar” in its January 2026 election notification. The statutory entry names the bodies merged into the corporation but does not give ward-by-ward boundaries.

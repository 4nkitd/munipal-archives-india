# Mahabubnagar ward reservations and votes in 2026

## Election coverage

Page 12 of the [27 January 2026 election notification](https://tsec.gov.in/preview.do?fileName=L1VwbG9hZHMvVFNFQy9wZGYvZ2F6ZXR0ZV9vZl8xMjNfdWxiX3dhcmRfZWxlY3Rpb25zXzUxOC5wZGY=#page=12), No. 150/TGSEC-ULBs/2026, lists 60 ward-member seats under “Mahaboobnagar”. Page 2 scheduled polling for 11 February and counting for 13 February 2026.

The corporation's Proforma X return covers serials 1596 to 1655 on PDF pages 179 to 188. The serials run across the statewide document; the local ward column runs from 1 to 60. These are 2026 election reservations, not descriptions of the area covered by each ward.

## Reading the statement

The reported net balance includes a deficit of ₹56,658,593.00 in the Municipal General Fund and a deficit of ₹171,136,347.00 in the Capital Project Fund. Depreciation and miscellaneous expenses appear below the Total Expenditure line, so the preliminary surplus and final deficit measure different things. The report gives no publication date or audit opinion.

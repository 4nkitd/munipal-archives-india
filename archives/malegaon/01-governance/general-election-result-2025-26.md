# Malegaon municipal general election result, 2025–26

## Final party-wise return

Row 11 of Maharashtra's final party-wise return records **21 electoral wards**, **84 councillor seats**, and **300 contesting candidates** for Malegaon. The commission reports results for all 84 seats. The election cycle is printed in the table title; the report does not print a publication date.

| SEC party or reporting category | Seats won |
| --- | ---: |
| Unrecognized parties registered with the SEC | 35 |
| Parties registered with the SEC and recognized as a state party outside Maharashtra | 26 |
| Shiv Sena (SS) | 18 |
| Indian National Congress (INC) | 3 |
| Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) | 2 |
| Total | 84 |

The 35- and 26-seat entries preserve the commission's reporting categories. The state table does not assign individual party names to those two numbers in Malegaon's row, and it reports no independent winners there.

## Checking a seat or a boundary

The corporation's [election page](https://malegaoncorporation.org/mmc/general-election-2025) links a separate [corporators' circular](https://malegaoncorporation.org/file/Corp_Gazzete.pdf), the candidate list and the final ward materials. Consult the circular for individual seats and the final ward gazette for geographic extent. This statewide table does not identify who won a particular seat or track changes after the return.

# Malegaon final electoral ward formation for the 2025–26 general election

## Gazette notification

Maharashtra Government Gazette, Extraordinary Part One-A, Nashik Divisional Supplement, issue 19, published the final Malegaon ward formation on **13 October 2025**. The English Annexure 15 gives the reference **SEC/MNV-2025/CR-19/DO-5**, cites Section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949, and says the notification takes effect for the next general election. It fixes **21 geographic wards**, each electing four councillors, for **84 seats**. The four municipal ward offices listed on the corporation's portal are not the election divisions.

The 21 schedule rows sum to 590,998. The schedule also gives Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe figures and directional boundary descriptions. Ward 1 is Bhaygaon and Malegaon Camp; its northern line follows the Mosam River along specified group numbers. Ward 11 is Soygaon and references localities including Mothabhau Nagar and Jaldhara Colony. These examples are not complete boundary descriptions. The corporation posts [separate final maps for all 21 wards](https://malegaoncorporation.org/mmc/wardwise-final-formation) and the combined city map under its [election materials](https://malegaoncorporation.org/mmc/general-election-2025).

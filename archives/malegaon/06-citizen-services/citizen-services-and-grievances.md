# Malegaon access to municipal forms, payments and grievances

## Choosing an application channel

Malegaon's [application-download page](https://malegaoncorporation.org/mmc/application-download) groups forms under births and deaths, fire brigade, market trade, marriage, no-objection certificates, property tax, public works, town planning and water. The [birth and death forms page](https://malegaoncorporation.org/mmc/bnd) identifies a birth certificate application, a declaration to register a child's name and Sample No. 1 for statistical information on a birth report. These are different forms. The corporation links the [Civil Registration System](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/web/index.php/auth/login) separately.

| --- | --- |
| Pay municipal property tax | [Property search and payment](https://nagarkaryavali.com/ANCL_OnlinePG/PropSearch.aspx?@=931A18BC9DEA25A966379EFA803788F9BDC85F9AE13CB52C08A09C9172479E06) |

| Look up notified Right to Service applications | [Municipal service catalogue](https://nagarkaryavali.com/ANCL_RTS/App/FrmDeptServices.aspx?@=A0FA08A6B82A741A3979161F891FCAB2) |

## Office and complaint lines

The [corporation contact page](https://malegaoncorporation.org/contact) lists head-office telephone numbers 02554-231800 and 02554-231801, and the office address opposite Bhoikot Fort, near Sabzi Market and Ram Setu Pool, Sangmeshwar 423203. The [Nashik district corporation directory](https://nashik.gov.in/en/corpocouncil/) publishes 1800-833-1500 for Malegaon municipal complaints and 9130000660 for complaint SMS. The city portal also points to the state's [Aaple Sarkar portal](https://aaplesarkar.maharashtra.gov.in/) as a separate grievance route.

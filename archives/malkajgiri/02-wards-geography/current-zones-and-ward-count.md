# Malkajgiri municipal zones, circles and wards

## Current jurisdiction

Malkajgiri Municipal Corporation covers Malkajgiri, Uppal and L.B. Nagar zones, with 14 circles and 74 wards. The constituting [G.O.Ms.No. 55 dated 11 February 2026](../../hyderabad/01-governance/2026-reorganisation-order.md) defines the corporation by the areas constituted by those three zones in the December 2025 reorganisation. Its jurisdiction is not the district boundary or the area of the Malkajgiri Zone alone.

The corporation publishes a [zone, circle and ward map](https://mmc.telangana.gov.in/img_NewDesign/MMCMap.jpeg). This record preserves the published totals and map link, not a ward-number range or boundary transcription. The 300 wards mentioned in the corporation's history belonged to the former expanded GHMC.

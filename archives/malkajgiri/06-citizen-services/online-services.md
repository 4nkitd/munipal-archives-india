# Malkajgiri online tax, certificate and grievance services

## Public entry points

| Task | Link published by Malkajgiri |
|---|---|
| Pay property tax | [Online payments](https://onlinepayments.ghmc.gov.in/) |
| Pay vacant land tax | [Vacant land tax payments](https://onlinepayments.ghmc.gov.in/VLT/ShowVLT_Main) |
| Verify a municipal birth certificate | [Birth certificate verification](https://bnd.ghmc.gov.in/Birth_Certificate.aspx) |
| Verify a municipal death certificate | [Death certificate verification](https://bnd.ghmc.gov.in/Death_Certificate.aspx) |
| Lodge a civic complaint | [Grievance portal](https://igs.ghmc.gov.in/operator/send_otp_mobile) |

For local office routing, use the [Malkajgiri contact directory](https://mmc.telangana.gov.in/KeyContacts.aspx). The shared `ghmc.gov.in` services are linked by Malkajgiri itself. They do not make Malkajgiri a branch of the present GHMC.

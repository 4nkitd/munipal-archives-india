# Malkajgiri tender notices register

## What the register shows

The public register lists tender type, work name, downloads, start and end dates, submission deadline and bid-opening date fields. Examples from the November 2026 listings:

| Work | Scope | Listed bid submission deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Detailed project report for sewerage and storm-water drainage | Bandlaguda T-Junction to Ahmedguda Y-Junction corridor | 16 November 2026, 4 pm |
| Construction and demolition waste processing plant, third call | 200 tonnes per day at Survey No. 226, Rampally, Keesara Mandal, Ghatkeesar Circle; SBM(U) 2.0 funding and PPP build-operate-transfer model | 4 December 2026, 3 pm |

The third-call listing shows a bid start of 26 November 2026 at 2 pm, after the archive's check. The dates above are timetable entries, not publication dates or awards. Consult the live register for later changes.

# Formation of Mancherial Municipal Corporation

## Statutory entry and commencement

The Governor assented to Act 4 of 2025 on 3 January 2025. The Telangana Gazette published it on 4 January. Section 6(x) omitted former Mancherial and Naspur municipality entries 36 and 39 from Schedule I of the Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019. Section 7(iii), pages 11 and 12, added Mancherial as Schedule II entry 14 with 60 wards.

[G.O.Ms.No.14](https://tggazette.cgg.gov.in/viewDocument/1738650747527), dated and published on 25 January in Gazette No. 20, brought Section 6(x) and Section 7 into force on **27 January 2025**. The 25 January commencement date for some other provisions does not apply to these entries.

## Areas named in the merger

The new corporation combines Mancherial Municipality and Naspur Municipality with the Pochampad, Gudipet, Narsingapur, Namnur, Chandanapur, Mulkala, Kothapalli and Vempally gram panchayats. The Kothapalli in this entry is named among Mancherial's merging gram panchayats. It should not be confused with Kothapally Municipality, which the same amendment lists in the Karimnagar entry. The Act does not provide individual ward boundaries.

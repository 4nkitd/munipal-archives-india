# Mancherial ward reservations and votes in 2026

## Election coverage

The State Election Commission's [27 January 2026 election notification](https://tsec.gov.in/preview.do?fileName=L1VwbG9hZHMvVFNFQy9wZGYvZ2F6ZXR0ZV9vZl8xMjNfdWxiX3dhcmRfZWxlY3Rpb25zXzUxOC5wZGY=#page=12), No. 150/TGSEC-ULBs/2026, lists 60 ward-member seats for Mancherial Municipal Corporation. Page 2 scheduled polling for 11 February and counting for 13 February 2026.

The corporation's Proforma X return occupies PDF pages 189 to 196, statewide serials 1671 to 1730. Other entries in Mancherial district, such as Bellampally and Chennur, are separate election returns. The corporation return lists votes and reservations, not ward boundary lines.

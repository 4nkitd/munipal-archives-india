## Reading the statement

Operations and maintenance consists of ₹167,549,296.00 in the Municipal General Fund and ₹38,406,284.00 in the Capital Project Fund. The accounts show costs by head and fund, without a ward or former-municipality breakdown. The report has no stated publication date or audit opinion.

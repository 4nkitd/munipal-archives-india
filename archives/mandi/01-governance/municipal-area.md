# Mandi municipal area and corporation upgrade

Entry 36 of the [district-wise urban local body register](https://ud.hp.gov.in/storage/media/Municipalities%20at%20a%20glance/idt8VksfK499MixaXlK0D4jadbpoA7tCKJRqoGAw.pdf), as at 31 March 2026, lists Municipal Corporation Mandi in Mandi district. It gives 1950 for the earlier municipality, 28 October 2020 for the upgrade, 28.66 square kilometres for the area and 41,375 for the 2011 population.

The same register lists Sundernagar and Nerchowk as separate municipal councils. The [district directory](https://hpmandi.nic.in/public-utility/municipal-council-mandi/) identifies the city corporation at Mandi 175001; its URL retains the older council designation.

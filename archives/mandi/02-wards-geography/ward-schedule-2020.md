# Mandi's 15 wards in the November 2020 reservation order

The district's [delimitation order 37242-47](https://hpmandi.nic.in/notice/order-37242-47-delimitations-of-wards-municipal-corporation-mandi/) dates from 21 November 2020. Four days later, the Deputy Commissioner issued a [reservation order](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s308c5433a60135c32e34f46a71175850c/uploads/2020/11/2020112642-1.pdf) for the forthcoming corporation election. Its first page lists these wards:

| Ward | Name | Ward | Name |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Khaliyar | 9 | Palace Colony-II |
| 2 | Purani mandi | 10 | Suhra |
| 3 | Paddal | 11 | Samkhetar |
| 4 | Nela | 12 | Bhagwahan |
| 5 | Mangwain | 13 | Thanehra |
| 6 | Sanyahrad | 14 | Behna |
| 7 | Talyahar | 15 | Doundhi |
| 8 | Palace Colony-I | | |

The election order identifies the seats by number and name. It does not establish a street address's ward or the reservation categories for a later election.

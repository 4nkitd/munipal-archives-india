# Mandi ward delimitation proposal and orders, October to November 2020

The Mandi district notice register separates three stages of the corporation's ward delimitation:

| District listing start date | Instrument |
|---|---|
| 29 October 2020 | [Proposal 34756-62](https://hpmandi.nic.in/notice/notice-proposal-34756-62-delimitations-of-wards-municipal-corporation-mandi/) |
| 9 November 2020 | [Order 35929-34](https://hpmandi.nic.in/notice/order-35929-34-delimitations-of-wards-municipal-corporation-mandi/) |
| 21 November 2020 | [Order 37242-47](https://hpmandi.nic.in/notice/order-37242-47-delimitations-of-wards-municipal-corporation-mandi/) |

The [21 November instrument](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s308c5433a60135c32e34f46a71175850c/uploads/2020/11/2020112642.pdf) refers back to the October proposal. The district site's common listing end date of 31 March 2021 is not evidence that the boundaries expired on that date.

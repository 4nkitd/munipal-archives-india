# Mandi corporation election liquor restrictions, May 2026

[Notification DC-ADC-ULB-2026-1626-1632](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s308c5433a60135c32e34f46a71175850c/uploads/2026/05/17786494034605.pdf) barred the sale, service, gift or distribution of liquor in public and private premises within the polling area of Municipal Corporation Mandi. The restriction covered the 48 hours ending at 3 pm on 17 May 2026 and the counting day, 31 May 2026. The notification identifies the corporation polling area rather than the entire district.

# 2022 notification altering the boundaries of Municipal Corporation Manesar

## Notification and legal authority

[Scanned map/PDF: visual source linked at source_url]

The Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department issued notice **No. 20/57/2022-6CI** on **16 December 2022**. Under section 3(3) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 (Haryana Act 16 of 1994), the Governor of Haryana altered and defined Manesar's municipal boundaries after consulting Municipal Corporation Manesar. The notice supersedes [No. S.O. 58/H.A. 16/1994/S. 3/2020 of 24 December 2020](/doc/manesar/mcm-gov-formation-2020).

Arun Gupta, Additional Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana, Urban Local Bodies Department, is named as signatory. The scan includes the English and Hindi versions and an endorsement dated 16 December 2022 requesting publication in both languages in the Haryana Government Gazette (Extraordinary). The scan does not give a gazette issue number.

## Revised boundary from Dhankot to Shikohpur

The notification directs readers to use the written schedule with points A to M on drawing **MCM/TP/2022/01**, dated **30 November 2022**, attached at the end of the scanned PDF.

| Points | Route in the notification |
| --- | --- |
| A to B | Start where Dhankot's northern revenue boundary meets the Gurugram-Farrukhnagar-Jhajjar Road. Follow that road east to B, where it meets the road dividing Sectors 99 and 99A. |
| B to C | Go south to C, where the sector-dividing road meets the existing Northern Peripheral Road (NPR). |
| C to D | Follow the NPR south to D, where it meets the Delhi-Jaipur Road (NH-48). |
| D to E | Follow NH-48 northeast to E, where it meets the road dividing Sectors 75A and 76. |
| E to F | Follow the Sector 75A/76 dividing road southeast to F, the junction with the road dividing Sectors 75 and 75A. |
| F to G to H | Go southwest to G on Shikohpur's revenue boundary, then southeast to H, where the Shikohpur and Kherki Daula revenue boundaries meet. |

## Boundary from Shikohpur through Mewat and back to Dhankot

| Points | Route in the notification |
| --- | --- |
| H to I | Go south along the common revenue boundaries of Shikohpur and Sakatpur, Naurangpur and Gairatpur Bas, and Bargujjar and Gairatpur Bas to I on the District Mewat boundary. |
| I to J | Follow the district boundary southwest to J, where the western revenue boundary of Nainwal meets it. |
| J to K | Go southwest along the western revenue boundaries of Nainwal and Manesar and the southeastern revenue boundary of Kukrola at K. |
| K to L | Follow Fazalwas's southeastern and southern revenue boundaries to L, where its southwestern revenue boundary meets the northeastern revenue boundary of Binola. |
| L to M | Go northwest along the common revenue boundaries of Fazalwas and Fakharpur, Kukrola and Fakharpur, Kasan and Mokalwas, Kasan and Kharkhari, and Kasan and Baslambi to M on the southeastern revenue boundary of Khawaspur. |
| M to A | Go northeast along the western revenue boundaries of Kasan, Dhana, Basharia, Jhund Sarai (Abad), Jhund Sarai (Viran), Bhangrola, Dhorka, Wazirpur, Garhi Harsaru and Gopalpur to return to A. |

## Dwarka Expressway and additions reported by the corporation

[Manesar City Info](https://mcmanesar.in/manesar-city-info) describes Dwarka Expressway as the division between the Manesar and Gurugram municipal corporations after the amendment. In the notification, the road is called the Northern Peripheral Road, and the segment used for the boundary is C to D. The portal's [About Us page](https://mcmanesar.in/about-us) and City Info page name Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A and the villages of Harsaru and Sihi as additions to Manesar's limits. This is the portal's description, not a list of additions in the notification's written schedule. Garhi Harsaru is separately named in the western leg of the schedule. Use the drawing, not the place names alone, to judge whether a specific location lies within the boundary.

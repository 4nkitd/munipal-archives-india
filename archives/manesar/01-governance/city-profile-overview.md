# Manesar city profile and municipal overview

## Formation and municipal services

The Government of Haryana formed Municipal Corporation Manesar on 24 December 2020 to oversee development and provide basic amenities in the new sectors and villages of New Gurugram. Manesar is in Gurugram district. The Haryana State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation, or HSIIDC, developed Manesar as an industrial hub. The municipal portal describes the Industrial Model Township, or IMT Manesar, as home to manufacturing industries that employ thousands of people.

The corporation's duties include water supply, sanitation, drainage, road works and urban development in its jurisdiction. The City Info page states that the corporation floated tenders for several works, but does not identify the tenders or give completion dates.

The date of this record is the date the pages were checked, not a publication or census date.

## Boundary change and map

The City Info page states that Haryana amended the boundaries of the Manesar and Gurugram municipal corporations on 16 December 2022. It identifies Dwarka Expressway as their dividing road and names Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A and the villages of Harsaru and Sihi as additions to Manesar's limits. For the exact route use the [2022 boundary notification](/doc/manesar/mcm-gov-boundary-amendment-2022). The [MCM GIS map](https://onemapggm.gmda.gov.in/mcm_boundary/) is linked from About Us.

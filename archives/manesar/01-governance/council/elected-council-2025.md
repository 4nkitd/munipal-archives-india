# Elected council of Municipal Corporation Manesar, 2025

## Council leadership

| Name as listed by MC Manesar | Role | Constituency | March 2025 election affiliation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dr. Inderjeet Kaur Yadav | Mayor | Corporation-wide | Independent |
| Sh. Parveen Kumar Yadav | Senior Deputy Mayor | Ward 12 councillor | Independent |
| Smt. Reema Chauhan | Deputy Mayor | Ward 2 councillor | Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) |

The mayor was directly elected for an unreserved seat. The House elected Parveen Kumar Yadav and Reema Chauhan [unopposed on 5 August 2025](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/bjpbacked-camp-takes-control-of-manesar-civic-body-101754379265534.html). Both had BJP backing in that election. The deputy posts are held by serving councillors and do not add two ward seats. The record date is the date of the deputy elections, not a publication date for the municipal directory.

## Reported affiliation changes after polling

The Times of India reported that Independent winners in Wards 7, 8, 11, 12, 14, 15 and 16 [joined the BJP in June 2025](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/upper-hand-for-bjp-in-mcm-as-7-independent-councillors-switch-sides/articleshow/121580351.cms) and identified Ward 17's Suman Kumari as [an Independent who had joined the BJP by August 2025](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/bjp-secures-key-posts-in-finance-panels-of-municipal-corporations-of-gurgaon-and-manesar/articleshow/123244553.cms). It also reported that [the mayor and eight councillors formally joined the BJP at an event on 29 April 2026](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/manesar-mayor-8-councillors-join-bjp-saffron-sweep-of-corporations-now-complete/articleshow/130617498.cms). That group included the Independent winners in Wards 1, 10, 13, 18 and 19. The paper also included Wards 8, 15 and 20 in the April group, but Wards 8 and 15 were already named in earlier reporting and Ward 20 won on a BJP ticket.

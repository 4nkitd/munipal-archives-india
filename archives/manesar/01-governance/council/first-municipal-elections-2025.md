# Manesar's first municipal corporation elections, 2025

## Election programme

Municipal Corporation Manesar was formed in December 2020. Its first general election covered a directly elected mayor and one councillor for each of its 20 wards. State Election Commission Haryana [notification SEC/1ME/2025/530](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/02/20250205588926428.pdf) set the programme. The [municipal Elections notification page](https://mcmanesar.in/notification) displayed a heading but no notices when checked. The [SEC's municipal orders and notifications index](https://secharyana.gov.in/orders-notifications-related-to-municipal-elections-4/orders-notifications-related-to-municipal-elections/) carries the programme and the gazetted results.

## How to consult the 2025 voter list

1. Open the [MC Manesar Voter List](https://mcmanesar.in/mc-manesar-voter-list) and select **Click here** beside **MC Manesar Final Wardwise Voter list**, dated 6 January 2025.
2. On the [District Gurugram voter-list page](https://gurugram.gov.in/voter-list-for-mc-election-manesar-2025/), find the ward and booth number, then open **View File** for that booth. The district page lists 96 booths across 20 wards. Booth 50 in Ward 10 and Booth 55 in Ward 12 each have a separate **Supplementary** file.
3. This is the roll for the 2025 election. For that election, the SEC directed voters with an Electoral Photo Identity Card (EPIC) number recorded in the municipal roll to show the card, or use one of the alternate photo IDs listed in the election programme if the card was unavailable.

The [SEC's 14 February 2025 electoral-roll instructions](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/02/20250214751954736.pdf) reproduce Rules 14 and 14A. Under Rule 14, requests to include, correct or move an entry to another ward went to the Deputy Commissioner on Form A; objections and deletion requests used Form B. Once the election programme was issued, Rule 14A allowed eligible people already on the relevant Assembly roll to apply to the returning officer to add a missing municipal entry through the nomination deadline. These were procedures for the published roll and election period, not current filing dates.

## Turnout and results

The district administration's figures, [reported by the Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/rural-areas-drive-voter-participation-in-first-manesar-corporation-poll/articleshow/118668108.cms), were 65,292 voters out of 97,073 registered, or 67.3%. [The Tribune's election-night report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/manesar-debuts-with-flying-colours-in-mc-poll-records-64-8-voter-turnout/) gave an interim figure of 64.8% and said the tally was expected to rise.

Independent candidate Dr. Inderjeet Kaur Yadav won the mayoral race with 26,393 votes. BJP candidate Sunder Lal Yadav received 24,100, a margin of 2,293 votes. [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/independents-sweep-manesar-civic-polls-bjp-to-introspect-loss-101741801812529.html) reported the counts. The SEC result records 13 Independent and seven BJP ward winners. No other party won a ward seat. Ten ward seats were unreserved and seven of the ten reserved ward seats were designated for women. The [municipal roster](https://mcmanesar.in/elected-members) lists all 20 members; the SEC result notice gives each seat category.

## Formation of the first elected House

The first mayor and newly elected councillors took their oaths in Panchkula on 25 March 2025 at the [state oath-taking ceremony](https://haryanacmoffice.gov.in/25-march-2025-0), with Manesar's delegation [reported by Dainik Bhaskar](https://www.bhaskar.com/local/haryana/gurugram/news/mayor-councilors-oath-ceremony-gurugram-update-news-134705182.html). The mayor [assumed office on 26 March](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/inderjeet-kaur-yadav-takes-charge-as-1st-manesar-mayor/articleshow/119556088.cms). On [5 August](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/bjpbacked-camp-takes-control-of-manesar-civic-body-101754379265534.html) the House elected Ward 12 councillor Parveen Kumar as Senior Deputy Mayor and Ward 2 councillor Reema Chauhan as Deputy Mayor, both unopposed.

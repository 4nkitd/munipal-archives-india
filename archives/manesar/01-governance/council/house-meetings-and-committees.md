# Manesar House meetings, committees and citizen participation

## House proceedings in 2025

This chronology uses contemporary reporting for House and committee proceedings and the municipal order dated 19 January 2026 for Area Sabha. It does not substitute for signed minutes.

| Date | Reported proceedings |
| --- | --- |
| 25 March 2025 | The newly elected mayor and councillors took their oaths in Panchkula. [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/others/manesar-municipal-body-yet-to-elect-deputy-senior-deputy-mayors-101750098777577.html) described this as Manesar's first official House meeting. |
| 7 April 2025 | The House approved its [first budget proposal considered by elected councillors](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/mcm-downsizes-budget-focus-shifts-to-sanitation-waste-management/articleshow/120074451.cms). The FY 2025-26 proposal estimated ₹418 crore in expenditure and ₹356 crore in income. It allocated ₹115 crore to sanitation and solid waste management and ₹90 crore to new roads and bridges. The corporation was to send the proposal to the Urban Local Bodies Department for approval. |
| 10 June 2025 | The House [passed 11 of 15 items, including ₹29 crore in development proposals](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/infrastructure-revamp-to-safai-reforms-mcm-house-nod-to-29cr-projects/articleshow/121786994.cms). Items included citizen facilitation centres in villages, land for sub-health centres in Shikohpur and Kukdola, and water and sewer works. The House backed a colony regularisation proposal for Dhana, Bas Hariya and Bas Kusla, and terminated the street vending zone agency's contract. It also [unanimously urged the Gurugram Divisional Commissioner to schedule the deputy mayor elections](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/others/manesar-municipal-body-yet-to-elect-deputy-senior-deputy-mayors-101750098777577.html). |
| 5 August 2025 | The House [elected Parveen Kumar of Ward 12 and Reema Chauhan of Ward 2 unopposed](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/bjpbacked-camp-takes-control-of-manesar-civic-body-101754379265534.html) as Senior Deputy Mayor and Deputy Mayor, respectively. |
| 24 September 2025 | [Punjab Kesari](https://haryana.punjabkesari.in/haryana/news/manesar-municipal-house-meeting-held-and-60-problem-discussed-2218081) reported agreement on 60 agenda items and about ₹171 crore in works. Items included 10,000 streetlights, road sweeping, sewer and drainage works, citizen facilitation centres, and a proposal to give 3,452 square metres in Manesar and Sehrawan to NCRTC. The commissioner said the five-year road sweeping proposal would be sent to headquarters. |

## House proceedings in 2026

On [16 March 2026](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcm-clears-51-proposals-passes-678-crore-budget-for-civic-works-in-manesar-101773684576551.html), the House approved 51 of 54 agenda items and unanimously passed a ₹678 crore budget. It earmarked ₹480 crore for development and ₹86 crore for sanitation and waste management. The House approved village roads, sewer and water lines, community centres where land was available, micro sewage-treatment plants, libraries and sanitation equipment. It resolved to send a proposal on regularising unauthorised colonies to Haryana's government and decided that contract extensions should not be granted without consulting the mayor and affected councillor.

On [4 August 2026](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/manesar-civic-meeting-ends-in-chaos-cross-complaints-filed-with-police-101785990600186.html), a scheduled House meeting was adjourned after a dispute over whether to take up committee formation before listed business. The report did not identify any resolutions passed at that sitting.

## Finance and Contracts Committee

Sections 40(3) and 40(4) of the [Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/2d98f87d-c6f6-44c2-920e-8fb13fede849.pdf) provide for a six-member Finance and Contracts Committee (F&CC). It has the mayor as ex-officio chair, the two deputy mayors, the municipal commissioner and two members elected by the councillors from among themselves. The Act gives the committee the corporation's powers concerning contracts and purchases. On [11 August 2025](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/bjp-secures-key-posts-in-finance-panels-of-municipal-corporations-of-gurgaon-and-manesar/articleshow/123244553.cms), Ward 10 councillor Ram Parkash and Ward 17 councillor Suman Kumari won the two elected seats with eight and seven votes, respectively.

- **October 2025.** An F&CC sitting with roughly ₹64 crore in works on the agenda [was postponed](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/after-a-spat-over-meet-manesars-mayor-says-work-dont-do-politics/articleshow/125023421.cms) after members disputed road estimates. An agenda value is not an approval.
- **December 2025.** [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/water-infra-streetlights-waste-pickup-manesar-municipal-corporation-nod-to-37-projects/articleshow/126064415.cms) and [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/manesar-soon-to-get-lake-view-park-in-kasan-village-101766081609226.html) reported 37 approved works worth about ₹132 crore, including water and sewer lines, drains, roads, and community toilet maintenance. Officials cited 16 December for the meeting in [later reporting](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/civic-projects-stalled-over-delayed-finance-meet-in-manesar-101789063956005.html).
- **March 2026.** [Hindustan Times reported approval of 36 works worth ₹174 crore](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/mcm-clears-174-crore-for-36-development-works-in-manesar-101774403654240.html). These included a ₹3 crore convention hall at Harsaru's panchayat building and over ₹5 crore for an indoor sports complex in Shikohpur. That report places the meeting on 23 March; a [later Times of India report](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/manesar-mayor-inspects-civic-works-amid-stalled-approvals/articleshow/134320748.cms) gives 21 March for a March F&CC meeting. No signed minutes were located to reconcile the dates.
- **Autumn 2026.** [Hindustan Times reported ₹390 crore in approvals](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/gurugram-news/390-crore-development-plan-cleared-for-manesar-civic-areas-101790879358188.html), including sports facilities in Naurangpur and Fazilwas and a sewage-treatment plant in Sikanderpur. It attributed the decisions to a meeting on 30 September. [The Times of India later reported](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/no-finance-panel-meeting-yet-municipal-corporation-of-manesar-clears-rs-390cr-projects/articleshow/134969121.cms) that committee members signed a circulated list without meeting. The approval was reported by both outlets; the procedure is disputed.

## Citizen participation and notices

Manesar issued its [Area Sabha Constitution Order](https://mcmanesar.in/area-sabha-constitution-order) on 19 January 2026. The [Haryana Municipal Citizens' Participation Act, 2008](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/192e29e4-796b-4dae-b459-ca2eddc1961a.pdf) defines an Area Sabha as the people on the electoral rolls for the polling booths in that area. Section 8 allows Area Sabhas to propose and prioritise works, forward them to the ward committee or municipality, help identify eligible beneficiaries for schemes, and recommend fixes or locations for public amenities.

The order lists each Area Sabha by ward and booth, with a municipal employee as member secretary. Residents can look up their area and secretary in the order, then check the municipal [Area Sabha Notice of 19 January](https://mcmanesar.in/area-sabha-notice) and [meeting notices posted on 21 January](https://mcmanesar.in/notices-regarding-area-sabha-meeting). The schedules are historical. The municipal site's [service notification index](https://mcmanesar.in/services/notification) holds these three entries, separate from the empty election notification landing page.

For a ward issue, consult the [elected members directory](https://mcmanesar.in/elected-members) or submit a complaint through Haryana's [public grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/). For municipal records, use Manesar's [Right to Information page](https://mcmanesar.in/right-information-rti).

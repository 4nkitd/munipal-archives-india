# 2020 notification forming Municipal Corporation Manesar

## Gazette and legal authority

Haryana Government Gazette (Extraordinary), issue **No. 192–2020/Ext.**, was published at Chandigarh on Thursday, 24 December 2020 (Pausha 3, 1942 Saka). The notification appears in Part III, pages 327 to 329. Page 328 gives the authorised English translation. Page 329 contains the boundary drawing.

In notification **No. S.O. 58/H.A. 16/1994/S. 3/2020**, the Governor of Haryana exercised the powers under section 3(2) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 (Haryana Act 16 of 1994) to declare Manesar and the area specified in the schedule a municipal corporation known as Municipal Corporation, Manesar. S. N. Roy, Additional Chief Secretary to Government Haryana, Urban Local Bodies Department, is named as signatory.

## Original schedule of boundaries

The gazette directs readers to use the schedule with points A to G on drawing **MCG/TP/2020/61**, dated **1 December 2020**.

| Side | Points | Route in the authorised English schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North | A to B | Start at A, the tri-junction of the revenue boundaries of Gopalpur, Dhankot and Chandu. Follow the common Gopalpur and Dhankot revenue boundary southeast to B on the existing Municipal Corporation Gurugram boundary. |
| East | B to C | Follow the existing western boundary of Municipal Corporation Gurugram southwest and southeast to C, where the southeastern revenue boundary of Shikohpur meets it. |
| East | C to D to E | Go south along the common revenue boundaries of Shikohpur and Sakatpur, Naurangpur and Gairatpur Bas, and Bargujjar and Gairatpur Bas to D on the District Mewat boundary. Follow the district boundary southwest to E, where it meets the western revenue boundary of Nainwal. |
| South | E to F | Go southwest along the western revenue boundaries of Nainwal and Manesar, the southeastern revenue boundary of Kukrola and the southeastern and southern boundaries of Fazalwas. F is the junction of the southwestern Fazalwas and northeastern Binola revenue boundaries. |
| South | F to G | Go northwest along the common revenue boundaries of Fazalwas and Fakharpur, Kukrola and Fakharpur, Kasan and Mokalwas, Kasan and Kharkhari, and Kasan and Baslambi. G is where this line meets the southeastern revenue boundary of Khawaspur. |
| West | G to A | Go northeast along the western revenue boundaries of Kasan, Dhana, Basharia, Jhund Sarai (Abad), Jhund Sarai (Viran), Bhangrola, Dhorka, Wazirpur, Garhi Harsaru and Gopalpur back to A. |

## Villages shown on the original drawing

The gazette sets a perimeter, not a numbered schedule of constituent villages. The following names are legible in the area shaded for Manesar on drawing MCG/TP/2020/61. They are map labels, not a substitute for the legal boundary or a claim that every revenue estate shown was included in full.

| Part of drawing | Place names within the shaded area |
| --- | --- |
| North | Gopalpur, Garhi Harsaru, Wazirpur, Hayatpur |
| West and centre | Jhund Sarai (Viran), Jhund Sarai (Abad), Dhorka, Meoka, Bhangrola, Basharia, Dhana, Baskusla, Kankrola, Kasan |
| Centre and east | Badha, Sikanderpur Badha, Nawada Fatehpur, Naharpur Kasan, Lakhnola, Rampur, Shikohpur, Khoh, Manesar |
| South | Naurangpur, Bargujjar (labelled Bar gujar on the drawing), Sehrawan, Kukrola, Nainwal, Fazalwas |

The schedule also names places used as boundary references, including Chandu, Dhankot, Sakatpur, Gairatpur Bas, Binola, Fakharpur, Mokalwas, Kharkhari, Baslambi and Khawaspur. A boundary reference is not proof that the village fell inside Manesar. The drawing shows Garhi Harsaru within the shaded area and Harsaru as a separate place outside it.

The [16 December 2022 notification](/doc/manesar/mcm-gov-boundary-amendment-2022) superseded the 2020 boundary schedule. Both files are listed under [General Documents](https://mcmanesar.in/general-documents) on the municipal portal.

# Municipal leadership messages for Manesar

## Mayor: Dr Inderjeet Kaur Yadav

The [Mayor's profile](https://mcmanesar.in/mayor-municipal-corporation-manesar) lists roads, water supply, sanitation, streetlighting and waste management as basic services to improve. Dr Yadav says the corporation is promoting digital governance to make services more convenient and transparent. She also identifies parks, community spaces and recreational facilities as priorities. She asks residents to keep their surroundings clean, conserve water, take part in civic initiatives and give feedback.

## Senior Deputy Mayor: Sh. Parveen Kumar

The [Senior Deputy Mayor's profile](https://mcmanesar.in/senior-deputy-mayor-municipal-corporation-manesar) describes priorities for planned development: stronger civic infrastructure, better delivery of municipal services, cleanliness and public safety. Kumar calls for transparency, accountability and effective use of municipal resources. He asks residents to participate in civic work and share responsibility for it.

## Deputy Mayor: Smt. Reema Chauhan

The [Deputy Mayor's profile](https://mcmanesar.in/deputy-mayor-municipal-corporation-manesar) states that Chauhan aims to support the corporation's day-to-day functioning and the timely execution of development works. She names policy implementation, monitoring of civic services and coordination between departments as areas of work. The message singles out sanitation, waste management, water supply, streetlighting and maintenance of public infrastructure. It calls for transparent and responsive service and asks residents to share feedback and support ongoing works.

## Commissioner: Sh. Pradeep Singh, IAS

The [Commissioner's profile](https://mcmanesar.in/commissioner-municipal-corporation-manesar) identifies the Administrative Department. Singh describes the focus of ongoing projects as:

- Upgrading roads, drainage and streetlighting for connectivity and safety.
- Expanding access to safe drinking water and modern sewerage.
- Improving waste management, with an emphasis on recycling and cleanliness.
- Promoting digital services for easier access and greater transparency.
- Developing parks, green belts and community spaces.

He asks residents to participate in and support the work.

## Source dates and scope

The profiles did not state publication dates or provide individual phone numbers or email addresses. This record is dated to the portal check on 22 November 2026. The messages describe priorities; they are not appointment orders, completion reports or project schedules.

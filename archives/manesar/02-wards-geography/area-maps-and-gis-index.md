# Manesar area maps, ward maps and GIS portal

## Area maps and the Dwarka Expressway divide

| Municipal download | What the sheet shows |
| --- | --- |
| [Manesar Area Map.pdf](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/Manesar%20Area%20Map.pdf) | The [area map page](https://mcmanesar.in/area-map) calls this "Area Map of MC Manesar(Amended)". The first sheet reads "MC MANESAR" and keys the MCM boundary and MCM zone boundary to 2022. Other layers include district and GMDA boundaries, village boundaries, sectors, the railway, national highway, major roads and Kundli Manesar Expressway. The PDF has two pages; the second is blank. The first is A1 landscape. The file metadata gives 13 March 2023 as the modification date. |
| [MCG and MCM Map.pdf](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/MCG%20and%20MCM%20Map.pdf) | The portal calls this "Area Map of MC Manesar". The sheet title is "MC GURUGRAM & MC MANESAR". Its legend expressly dates **both municipal boundaries to 2020**. It shows four MCG zones and seven MCM zones, village abadis, roads, railway and district/state boundaries. One landscape sheet, 2808 × 1656 points; Esri ArcMap 10.8, creation date 24 February 2021. |

According to the [municipal city profile](https://mcmanesar.in/manesar-city-info), Haryana amended the two limits on **16 December 2022**. Dwarka Expressway divides the corporations; the profile names Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A and the villages of Harsaru and Sihi. The zone table specifies **parts** of the latter two revenue estates. Use the [amended area notification](https://mcmanesar.in/general-documents) and the amended map for this change. The older combined map predates it. HARSAC's note on the amended sheet says the data came from MCM and that the map is for viewing, not for a dispute in court.

## Wardbandi maps

| Stage on the [wards page](https://mcmanesar.in/wards) | PDF | Date printed on sheet | Portal date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preliminary | [w4.pdf](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/w4.pdf) | 25 July 2023 | 23 August 2023 |
| Final | [w2.pdf](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/w2.pdf) | 10 October 2023 | 3 November 2023 |

Both are single A1 portrait sheets created in Esri ArcMap 10.9.1. Both retain the heading "PROPOSED WARD MUNICIPAL CORPORATION MANESAR". Use the portal's stage and the sheet date to tell the versions apart. The legend distinguishes wards 1 to 20 by colour, and marks assembly, village, sector, LC and municipal boundaries, KMP Expressway, national highway, major roads, railway lines and stations. Peripheral labels include places outside MCM.

The [preliminary notification](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/w3.pdf) and [final order](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf) are both 19-page scans. Under rule 9 the preliminary notice allowed ten days from Gazette publication for objections and suggestions through the Deputy Commissioner, Gurugram. The final rule 10 schedule gives the route around each ward, including the coordinates that the map labels omit.

## GIS portal

[Municipal Corporation Manesar GIS](https://gis.mcmanesar.in) is the interactive mapping reference. For a plot on a dividing road, use the road, railway, society wall or revenue boundary in the November schedule to identify the relevant side. A sector label locates the label point, not every property in that sector.

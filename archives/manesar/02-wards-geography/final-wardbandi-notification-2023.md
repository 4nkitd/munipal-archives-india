# Final wardbandi order of 3 November 2023: all 20 Manesar ward boundaries

## Order and publication record

Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department Order **19/44/2023-6CI**, dated **3 November 2023**, publishes the boundaries of the 20 wards of Municipal Corporation Manesar, District Gurugram. The Governor acted under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994, following the [rule 9 draft of 23 August 2023](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/w3.pdf). Vikas Gupta, Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Urban Local Bodies Department, is named at the end of the schedule.

The [municipal scan](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf) and [state department copy](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2F01e80552-1227-4f87-a366-9e20441b6b10.pdf) are identical. PDF pages 1 to 9 carry the English schedule; page 10 forwards the order to the Controller, Printing & Stationary Department, Chandigarh, for publication in the Haryana Government Extraordinary Gazette and requests 25 printed copies each for the Commissioner and Secretary and the Director. Pages 11 to 19 contain the Hindi text. The scan does not show a November Gazette issue number or printed Gazette page range.

## Reading the schedule

The routes below restate the English schedule in boundary order. Hyphenated village pairs denote revenue boundaries; three names denote junctions. Nearby villages named in a route need not belong to that ward. Sector lists are label locations read against the colours of the [final ward map](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/w2.pdf), not a statement that every plot of a sector lies inside the ward. Colony names are locality cross-references from the [municipal colony maps](https://mcmanesar.in/regularized-colonies) and ward-specific public works, not a colony schedule in the order.

## Population and reservation

The final boundary order gives no ward population totals or seat reservations. A separate [Haryana Government Gazette (Extraordinary), No. 228-2023/Ext, 28 December 2023, pp. 4353-4354](https://egazette.servantsofknowledge.in/details/in.gazette.haryana.2023-12-28.Extra-Ordinary_19-44-2023-6CI/) cites this order and section 11(8) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. That December notification reserved Wards 15, 16 and 20 for Scheduled Castes, including Ward 15 for Scheduled Castes women; Ward 9 for Backward Classes 'A' women; and Wards 2, 3, 14, 18 and 19 for general women. These are the allocations in that dated notification.

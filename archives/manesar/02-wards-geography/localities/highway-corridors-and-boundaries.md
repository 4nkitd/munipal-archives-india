# Highway corridors and municipal boundaries of Manesar

## Area, formation and controlling boundary documents

The official [city profile](https://mcmanesar.in/manesar-city-info) reports a municipal area of **137.89 sq km**, with **30 + 4 partial villages**, **63 sectors**, **20 wards**, **seven zones** and **two divisions**. This is the corporation's published area, not a fresh GIS measurement.

MCM was created on **24 December 2020** under notification **S.O. 58/H.A. 16/1994/S. 3/2020**, invoking section 3(2) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. The original schedule referred to points A to G on drawing **MCG/TP/2020/61 dated 1 December 2020**.

The **16 December 2022** notification, invoking section 3(3), supersedes the earlier boundary definition and refers to points **A to M** on drawing **MCM/TP/2022/01 dated 30 November 2022**. The scanned notification bears handwritten reference **20/57/2022-6CI** and is signed by **Arun Gupta, Additional Chief Secretary to Government Haryana, Urban Local Bodies Department**.

The expressways do not form a complete polygon around MCM. The legal boundary combines roads, sector-dividing roads, revenue-estate boundaries and the district boundary.

## Major transport corridors

| Corridor | Search names | Municipal relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delhi-Jaipur highway | NH-48, former NH-8, NH8 | Main Manesar/IMT access corridor; a specific segment is part of the amended boundary |
| Dwarka Expressway | Northern Peripheral Road, NPR | Main MCM/MCG dividing feature in the December 2022 amendment; only the scheduled segment has that legal role |
| Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway | KMP, Western Peripheral Expressway | Regional freight and bypass access near Manesar; not described as the whole municipal edge |
| Gurugram-Pataudi-Rewari corridor | Pataudi Road, Rewari Expressway | Western/southwestern regional access; the city profile lists Rewari Expressway among major roads, but the notification does not make the entire route a municipal boundary |
| Gurugram-Farrukhnagar-Jhajjar Road | Farrukhnagar/Jhajjar road | Explicit northern starting segment in the 2022 schedule |

Road names in postal addresses can identify a corridor without establishing municipal jurisdiction. "Rewari Expressway" and "Pataudi Road" are retained as corridor search terms; no claim is made that every alignment or service road carrying either name is identical.

## Dwarka Expressway and the MCM/MCG interface

The official city profile says the 2022 amendment made Dwarka Expressway the dividing line between Manesar and Gurugram corporations. It specifically names **Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A**, and **Harsaru and Sihi**, as additions.

For the New Gurugram stretch, MCM is generally on the western side and MCG on the eastern side. That is an orientation aid, not a rule for every point along the full Delhi-Gurugram expressway. The actual schedule starts north of the NPR segment and turns along NH-48 and sector roads before continuing along village boundaries. Check the notification drawing where a society, plot or village overlaps an administrative edge.

The current zone directory explicitly treats **Dhankot, Harsaru, Sihi and Kherki Daula as partial revenue estates**. It lists Sector 99A but not all of Sector 99. The boundary's mention of the Sector 99/99A dividing road does not place both sectors wholly in MCM.

## December 2022 boundary schedule, roads and northern/eastern turns

The following is a structured transcription of the scanned English schedule. Point labels have meaning only with the attached drawing.

| Segment | Boundary description |
| --- | --- |
| A to B | Start at the intersection of Dhankot's northern revenue boundary with Gurugram-Farrukhnagar-Jhajjar Road; follow that road east to its meeting with the dividing road of Sectors 99 and 99A |
| B to C | Move south to the existing Northern Peripheral Road |
| C to D | Move south along NPR to its meeting with Delhi-Jaipur Road, NH-48 |
| D to E | Follow NH-48 northeast to the dividing road of Sectors 75A and 76 |
| E to F | Follow the Sector 75A/76 dividing road southeast to the meeting of the dividing roads of 75/75A and 75A/76 |
| F to G | Move southwest to the revenue boundary of Shikohpur |
| G to H | Move southeast to the meeting of the revenue boundaries of Shikohpur and Kherki Daula |
| H to I | Move south along the common boundaries Shikohpur-Sakatpur, Naurangpur-Gairatpur Bas and Bargujjar-Gairatpur Bas, to the boundary of district Mewat |
| I to J | Follow the district Mewat boundary southwest to the western revenue boundary of Nainwal |

The source uses the historical district name **Mewat**. Its printed sector connector appears as "expand" in places; this atlas reads the paired sector numbers as the dividing road between those sectors rather than inventing a new sector label.

## December 2022 boundary schedule, southern and western closure

| Segment | Boundary description |
| --- | --- |
| J toward K | Move southwest along the western revenue boundaries of Nainwal and Manesar and the southeastern revenue boundary of Kukrola |
| K toward L | Continue along the southeastern and southern revenue boundaries of Fazalwas to its junction with Binola's northeastern revenue boundary |
| L to M | Move northwest along common boundaries Fazalwas-Fakharpur, Kukrola-Fakharpur, Kasan-Mokalwas, Kasan-Kharkhari and Kasan-Baslambi to the southeastern revenue boundary of Khawaspur |
| M to A | Move northeast along the western revenue boundaries of Kasan, Dhana, Basharia, Jhund Sarai Abad, Jhund Sarai Viran, Bhangrola, Dhorka, Wazirpur, Garhi Harsaru and Gopalpur, returning to A |

Neighbouring villages such as Binola, Fakharpur, Mokalwas, Kharkhari, Baslambi, Khawaspur, Sakatpur and Gairatpur Bas occur as boundary references. Their appearance in a boundary description is not proof of inclusion in MCM.

The southern/western schedule follows revenue boundaries rather than simply KMP or NH-48. Claims that "everything between KMP and Dwarka Expressway is MCM" are therefore too broad.

## Checking a property or road complaint

1. Identify the property ID, revenue village, khasra and sector/sub-sector. Preserve prefixes such as IMT/M and P.
2. Locate the property against drawing MCM/TP/2022/01 and the official area map. For a partial village, check the parcel rather than assuming the whole village is in one corporation.
3. Use the ward map separately for electoral ward identification. Do not turn a zone number into a ward number.
4. Establish the agency responsible for the asset. Municipal jurisdiction does not automatically make MCM responsible for a national-highway carriageway, HSIIDC estate asset, GMDA trunk network or private internal road.
5. For a reservation or setback question, check the development plan and approved layout. A highway-facing property can still be affected by a proposed road, green belt or restricted belt.

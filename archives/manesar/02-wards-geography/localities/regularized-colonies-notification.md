# Regularized colonies notification and approved locality maps

## Notification and legal scope

The Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department issued notification **No. 04/42/2023-2CI on 25 July 2023**, published in Haryana Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 131-2023/Ext. The notification invokes section 3 of the Haryana Management of Civic Amenities and Infrastructure Deficient Municipal Areas (Special Provisions) Act, 2016, Act 14 of 2016. Its operative declaration is that the scheduled areas are "civic amenities and infrastructure deficient areas".

The statewide schedule contains 131 entries. Only entries **70, 71 and 72**, on printed Gazette page **1968**, belong to Municipal Corporation Manesar, district Gurugram. There are no additional MC Manesar colonies in this particular notification. Colonies elsewhere in the statewide schedule must not be presented as Manesar colonies. The notification is signed by **Vikas Gupta, Commissioner and Secretary to Government Haryana, Urban Local Bodies Department**.

The corporation's [Regularized Colonies page](https://mcmanesar.in/regularized-colonies) lists this notification and exactly three map PDFs. The September 2025 URL folder is an upload location, not the notification date.

## Exclusions and what regularization does not do

The final notification page, printed Gazette page **1975**, states that colony boundaries are those in approved layouts submitted by municipalities and recommended by the concerned Divisional Commissioners and District Municipal Commissioners.

It excludes plots affected by the influence zone of high-tension lines or gas/petroleum pipelines, proposed-road alignment, and green or restricted belts shown in the layout and Development Plan, subject to the notification's removal/revision conditions. It also states that commercial, industrial, institutional and other non-residential uses are not considered under the notification until permission for residential use is sought.

The inclusion of a colony name is therefore not blanket approval for every plot, building, land use or title. `min` denotes a part entry in the printed revenue schedule. Do not treat it as approval of an entire khasra.

## Civic amenities, development charges and building plans

The declaration brings the scheduled residential areas within the statutory framework for addressing deficient civic infrastructure. It is relevant when seeking roads, drainage, sewerage, water supply and sanitation. It does not certify that these facilities have already been installed. The scanned layouts contain historical infrastructure survey fields, not a present-day service audit.

The July 2023 notification itself supplies **no per-square-yard development-charge rate, payment deadline, construction fee or building-plan sanction**. Ask MCM for a property-specific assessment tied to the colony ID and approved boundary. Check previously paid charges and exemptions before paying a fresh demand.

ULB's memo **DULB/DMC-HQ/2023, dated 28 October 2023**, on property-data anomalies directs municipalities to update already-paid development charges and states that residential properties within Lal Dora/Abadi Deh do not attract development charges. It also identifies exemptions for HSVP, HSIIDC, planned/licensed colonies, CLU-granted properties and agricultural properties. These are classification-dependent rules, not automatic exemptions for all properties in the three colonies.

A building plan remains a separate approval. The [ULB Right to Service schedule](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService) includes sanction of eligible building plans in regularized/notified colonies through [Haryana OBPAS](https://www.haryanaobpas.gov.in/). Check the applicable service category, land use, road reservation and current fee schedule. Colony regularization does not retrospectively sanction an existing unauthorized structure.

For plot verification, carry the property ID, title documents, khasra details, colony ID, location plan and earlier payment receipts. MCM's published helpdesk is **0124-2012232**, email **cmc-manesar@ulbharyana.gov.in**.

# New Gurugram and IMT Manesar sectors atlas

## Reported total and the published list discrepancy

The official city profile gives a total of **63 sectors**. The official zone list actually names **62 distinct sector labels**, counting every listed subdivision separately: **34 New Gurugram numeric-sector labels, 21 M-series labels and seven P-series labels**. Sector 97 is expressly listed as **part**. The sources do not identify the additional label needed to reconcile the reported total. No missing sector has been invented.

This is a count of listed labels, not 63 whole, identically classified residential sectors. A suffix such as 79A or 1B is a separate listed label. The zone list includes sectors beyond the often-used shorthand "76 to 95B". It also includes Sector 96, part Sector 97, Sector 98 and Sector 99A. **Sector 99 without A is not listed** and must not be substituted for 99A.

The record date is the **16 December 2022 boundary amendment**. The directory was checked on the verification date. Zones are service/engineering groupings; they are not the corporation's 20 electoral wards.

New Gurugram includes residential developments, but the municipal zone table is not a land-use schedule. A sector can contain village habitation, licensed housing, roads, commercial sites and reserved land. Check the sanctioned sector/development plan and the property's licence or CLU rather than treating the entire sector as residential.

The frequently requested core **IMT Sectors 1 to 8** are therefore split across Zones 5 and 6. MCM's own office address is **CP-09, 3rd Floor, Cyberwalk, Sector 8, IMT Manesar, Gurugram**. The city profile identifies IMT as an industrial hub developed by **HSIIDC**, with manufacturing employment.

## December 2022 additions and boundary qualifications

The [official city profile](https://mcmanesar.in/manesar-city-info) says the **16 December 2022** amendment made **Dwarka Expressway** the divider between MCM and MCG and added **Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A**, plus Harsaru and Sihi.

The legal schedule is more precise than that shorthand. It follows the northern Dhankot/Gurugram-Farrukhnagar-Jhajjar Road interface, the dividing road of Sectors **99 and 99A**, the **Northern Peripheral Road**, then **NH-48** and dividing roads around Sectors **75A and 76**, before following revenue boundaries southward. Merely being near Dwarka Expressway is not proof that a whole society, sector or revenue estate is in MCM. The current zone table explicitly marks Sector 97 as partial and Harsaru, Sihi, Dhankot and Kherki Daula as partial estates.

## Civic services and overlapping authorities

MCM's published duties include water supply, sanitation, drainage and roads within its jurisdiction. Municipal inclusion does not, by itself, transfer every developer-owned internal road, HSIIDC asset, national-highway carriageway or regional trunk network to MCM.

For a complaint, identify the asset and its responsible agency as well as the sector:

| Asset or question | First verification |
| --- | --- |
| Municipal sanitation, local road or drain | MCM zone/engineering responsibility and actual asset ownership |
| HSIIDC industrial plot, estate conditions or estate infrastructure | HSIIDC allotment/layout and handover status |
| Major regional water, sewer or arterial-road infrastructure | GMDA project/asset record and connection responsibility |
| Licensed colony internal infrastructure | Developer or maintenance agency and documented handover |
| NH-48 or expressway carriageway | Highway authority and road-concession/project responsibility |
| Building permission or land use | Competent authority, licence/CLU and approved layout |

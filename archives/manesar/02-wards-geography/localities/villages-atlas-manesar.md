# Manesar villages atlas and partial revenue estates

## Coverage and the village-count discrepancy

The official [city profile](https://mcmanesar.in/manesar-city-info) reports **30 + 4 partial villages**, **137.89 sq km**, **63 sectors**, **20 wards**, **seven zones** and **two divisions**. Its older narrative still says 29 villages. The [zone directory](https://mcmanesar.in/zones) names **29 villages without a partial qualifier and four partial revenue estates**. The [taxation document index](https://mcmanesar.in/taxation-documents) additionally names **Bamroli**, producing 30 distinct full-village candidates across the two official sources.

This atlas preserves that discrepancy rather than assigning Bamroli an invented zone or silently merging village names. Bamroli's exact municipal extent and zone need confirmation against the notified boundary drawing and revenue records. The four expressly partial estates are **Dhankot, Harsaru, Sihi and Kherki Daula**. Jhund Sarai Abad and Jhund Sarai Viran are separate entries, not a duplicate.

MCM was constituted on **24 December 2020**. The **16 December 2022** amendment changed its boundary with Gurugram. The city profile specifically identifies **Harsaru and Sihi** as additions in that amendment. Current membership in a zone is not itself proof of an individual village's full extent at formation. The directory below labels those two additions separately and does not manufacture an original 2020 annexure.

## Lal Dora, property IDs and civic jurisdiction

Lal Dora/Abadi Deh is the recognized village habitation area. It is not synonymous with the whole revenue estate, the whole municipal village, an adjoining licensed sector or a regularized colony. MCM's village-named taxation PDFs are useful search aids for locating property entries, but inclusion in a filename alone does not prove a particular property's title, building permission or exemption.

The [ULB memo of 28 October 2023](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2F818c97e7-1645-4ec0-aec7-a694f399949d.pdf) states that development charges do not apply to residential properties situated in Lal Dora/Abadi Deh. This is not a general property-tax exemption. For a disputed demand, ask MCM to check the property ID against the actual habitation boundary and relevant assessment classification.

For partial villages, establish the parcel's position against the amended municipal boundary before choosing MCM or MCG for a civic complaint. Use the official ward map for councillor/ward identification. A seven-zone engineering/service group is not a ward number.

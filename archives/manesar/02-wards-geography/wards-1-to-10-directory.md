# Manesar wards 1 to 10: villages, sectors and boundary landmarks

## Finding an address

The sectors below locate labels on the [final ward map](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/w2.pdf), listed on 3 November 2023. A label does not establish a whole sector's ward. Use the route for the side of a dividing road. Names joined by hyphens in a route refer to boundary lines or junctions.

The [service-zone table](https://mcmanesar.in/zones) lists places by zone. Engineering divisions follow the [civil executive engineer ward groups](https://mcmanesar.in/officers-directory) and [ward-specific works](https://asiantender.com/location/haryana/manesar-tenders). Colony references come from the [municipal colony map index](https://mcmanesar.in/regularized-colonies) and a [public work in BPL/SC Colony, Harsaru](https://tender.zoltwic.com/company/MANISHA%20CONSTRUCTIONS). The order has no comprehensive colony or house-number register.

## Ward 1

Village or estate areas: Garhi Harsaru, Gopalpur. Final map sector labels: Sector 99A.

Service areas: Zone 1. Civil engineering: Division 1. The Gopalpur-Dhankot line is an outer boundary. Zone 1 includes only part of the Dhankot estate.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 1](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=1): From the northwest corner of Grace Centre, follow the northern municipal limit east along Gurugram-Farukhnagar Road to Pakora Wala Shop (28.472272, 76.949746). Go southeast along the limit and the Sector 99/99A road to the Gurugram-Rewari railway. Follow the railway southwest to the Wazirpur-Dhanawas boundary at the municipal limit. Go north along the limit via the Wazirpur-Hamirpur line, the Wazirpur-Garhi Harsaru-Sadhrana junction and the Garhi Harsaru-Sadhrana line to the Garhi Harsaru-Sadhrana-Chandu junction. Follow the municipal limit northeast along Gopalpur-Dhankot to the starting road.

## Ward 2

Village or estate areas: Harsaru. Final map sector labels: Sector 88A, Sector 88B, Sector 89A, Sector 89B. Colony reference: BPL/SC Colony, Harsaru.

Service areas: Zone 1, Zone 2. Civil engineering: Division 1. Garhi Harsaru Junction railway station is marked on the final sheet. Harsaru and Garhi Harsaru are separate entries in the zone table.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 1-2](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=1): From the Gurugram-Rewari railway at Dwarka Expressway, go south along the expressway and municipal limit to Harsaru-Bamroli at CPR Road. Follow Bamroli-Harsaru northwest to the Sector 88/89 road; go north to Pataudi Road, then east to (28.428282, 76.954525). Go north to (28.433191, 76.954565), west to (28.433068, 76.945248), south to (28.431596, 76.94521), west to (28.431914, 76.942301), and south to (28.428637, 76.939335), on the west side of Vatika Seven Elements Society. Continue south to Hayatpur-Harsaru. Go northwest along Hayatpur-Harsaru, Wazirpur-Harsaru and Wazirpur-Garhi Harsaru via the Hayatpur-Harsaru-Wazirpur junction to the railway. Follow it northeast to the expressway.

## Ward 3

Service areas: Zone 1, Zone 2. Civil engineering: Division 1. The order prints different coordinates on the two sides of the Ward 2/3 description. The figures here follow the Ward 3 entry.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 2](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=2): From Sihi-Hayatpur at Dwarka Expressway go southwest along Hayatpur-Bamroli-Sikanderpur Badha to the Sihi-Hayatpur-Bamroli-Badha intersection. Go northwest along Bada-Hayatpur-Bamroli to the Sector 89/93 road. Go southwest on Hayatpur-Badha across the Sector 90/93 road via Hayatpur-Badha-Nawada Fatehpur; continue on Hayatpur-Nawada Fatehpur to Nawada Fatehpur-Meoka-Hayatpur. Go north on Hayatpur-Wazirpur across the Sector 90/93 road and Pataudi Road to Wazirpur-Hayatpur-Harsaru. Turn southeast along Hayatpur-Harsaru to the southwest corner of Vatika Seven Elements Society. Follow its west wall north to (28.431914, 76.942301); go east to (28.431596, 76.94521), north to (28.433068, 76.94548), east along the north side of Godrej Icon Society to (28.433151, 76.954565), then south to (28.428282, 76.954535) on Pataudi Road. Go west to the Sector 88/89 road, south to Hayatpur-Harsaru and southeast along that boundary to Hayatpur-Sihi at Dwarka Expressway.

## Ward 4

Village or estate areas: Wazirpur, Meoka, Dhorka. Final map sector labels: Sector 91, Sector 92, Sector 95, Sector 95A, Sector 95B. Colony reference: B-44 Wazirpur Enclave.

Service areas: Zone 1. Civil engineering: Division 1. The boundary runs through Dhorka; the map places the Dhorka village label on the Ward 6 side.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 2-3](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=2): Start at Wazirpur-Garhi Harsaru on the Gurgaon-Rewari railway. Go southeast via Wazirpur-Garhi Harsaru-Harsaru, then along Wazirpur-Harsaru to Wazirpur-Harsaru-Hayatpur. Follow Wazirpur-Hayatpur south across the Sector 90/93 road via Wazirpur-Hayatpur-Meoka and Hayatpur-Nawada Fatehpur-Meoka. Follow Meoka-Nawada Fatehpur to Kankrola; go west on Meoka-Kankrola to the Bhangrola junction, then along Meoka-Bhangrola to the Sector 91/92 road. Go northwest through Dhorka to the Sector 92/95 road. Follow that road northeast to Wazirpur, then go north on Wazirpur-Dhorka across Pataudi Road via Dhorka-Wazirpur-Dhanawas to the railway. Follow it northeast to the start.

## Ward 5

Village or estate areas: Kankrola. Final map sector labels: Sector 87, IMT Sector 15.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 3](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=3): From Kankrola-Bhangrola-Meoka follow Kankrola-Meoka east to Nawada Fatehpur. Follow Kankrola-Nawada Fatehpur southeast to Naharpur Kasan; go southwest on Kankrola-Naharpur Kasan to Kasan. Go northwest via Kankrola-Kasan and Kankrola-Kasan-Baskushla to Kankrola-Bhangrola-Baskushla. Turn north on Kankrola-Bhangrola to the starting junction.

## Ward 6

Village or estate areas: Bhangrola, Dhorka, Jhund Sarai (Viran). Final map sector labels: IMT Sector 10, IMT Sector 12, IMT Sector 14. Colony reference: B7 Bhangrola.

Service areas: Zone 1, Zone 4. Civil engineering: Division 1. Dhorka is in Zone 1; Bhangrola and Jhund Sarai (Viran) are in Zone 4.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 3-4](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=3): From Bhangrola-Kankrola-Meoka follow Bhangrola-Kankrola south to Baskushla. Go southwest along Bhangrola-Baskushla via the Basharia junction, Bhangrola-Basharia and the Jhund Sarai Abad junction. Follow Bhangrola-Jhund Sarai Abad to Jhund Sarai Viran. Go northwest on the Abad-Viran boundary to (28.399444, 76.875972), then southwest to (28.395358, 76.87335) at the municipal limit. Follow the limit north along Jhund Sarai Viran-Patli Hajipur, crossing Pataudi Road to Wazirpur-Dhorka-Dhanawas. Go southeast on Dhorka-Wazirpur to the Sector 92/95 road; follow it southwest to (28.40755, 76.898578). Go southeast by road to the junction of the Sector 91-Bhangrola line with Meoka-Bhangrola, then northeast along Meoka-Bhangrola to the starting junction.

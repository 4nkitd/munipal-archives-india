# Manesar wards 11 to 20: villages, sectors and boundary landmarks

## Finding an address

The sectors below locate labels on the [final ward map](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/w2.pdf), listed on 3 November 2023. A label does not establish a whole sector's ward. Use the route for the side of a dividing road. Names joined by hyphens in a route refer to boundary lines or junctions.

The [service-zone table](https://mcmanesar.in/zones) lists places by zone. Engineering divisions follow the [civil executive engineer ward groups](https://mcmanesar.in/officers-directory) and [ward-specific works](https://asiantender.com/location/haryana/manesar-tenders). Colony references come from the [municipal colony map index](https://mcmanesar.in/regularized-colonies) and a [public work in BPL/SC Colony, Harsaru](https://tender.zoltwic.com/company/MANISHA%20CONSTRUCTIONS). The order has no comprehensive colony or house-number register.

## Ward 11

Village or estate areas: Manesar. Final map sector labels: IMT Sector 1B.

Service areas: Zone 6. Civil engineering: Division 2. The order includes the NSG Campus area in Ward 10. The campus wall is a Ward 11 boundary.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 6](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=6): From (28.355718, 76.938035) on the Delhi-Jaipur National Highway take the street northeast to (28.356989, 76.939295). Go south along the street to (28.356337, 76.939276), east to (28.356303, 76.941624), northeast by road to (28.358018, 76.942813), then east by street to (28.358065, 76.945027). Go south along the M1A/M1B road to (28.352603, 76.945115), then east to (28.353032, 76.954761). Follow the M1B boundary and NSG Campus wall southwest to the highway, then follow the highway north past the ESIC Dispensary and Aravali Hospital to the start.

## Ward 12

Village or estate areas: Manesar. Final map sector labels: IMT Sector 2.

Service areas: Zone 6. Civil engineering: Division 2. Manesar spans Wards 10, 11 and 12. The school and IMT Chowk identify the Ward 12 highway segment.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 6](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=6): From Manesar-Naharpur Kasan-Lakhnaula follow Manesar-Nakhdaula east to the Delhi-Jaipur National Highway. Follow the highway south past IMT Chowk and Government Senior Secondary School, Manesar to Manesar-Sehrawan. Go north along that boundary via Khoh-Manesar-Sehrawan, then along Khoh-Manesar to the starting junction.

## Ward 13

Village or estate areas: Khoh. Final map sector labels: IMT Sector 5.

Service areas: Zone 5, Zone 6. Civil engineering: Division 2. Khoh is in Zone 6; IMT Sector 5 is in Zone 5.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 6](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=6): From Khoh-Kasan-Naharpur Kasan go southeast along Naharpur Kasan-Khoh to Khoh-Naharpur Kasan-Manesar. Go south along Manesar-Khoh to Manesar-Khoh-Sehrawan, west along Khoh-Sehrawan to Khoh-Sehrawan-Kasan and north along Khoh-Kasan to the starting junction.

## Ward 14

Village or estate areas: Naharpur Kasan. Final map sector labels: IMT Sector 6.

Service areas: Zone 4, Zone 5. Civil engineering: Division 2. Naharpur Kasan is in Zone 4; IMT Sector 6 is in Zone 5. Naharpur Kasan and Kasan are different villages.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 6-7](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=6): From Naharpur Kasan-Kankrola-Nawada Fatehpur go southeast on Naharpur Kasan-Nawada Fatehpur via the Lakhnaula junction. Continue along Lakhnaula-Naharpur Kasan to Naharpur Kasan-Manesar-Lakhnaula. Go south to Khoh-Manesar-Naharpur Kasan, then northwest via Khoh-Kasan-Naharpur Kasan and Kasan-Naharpur Kasan to Naharpur Kasan-Kankrola-Kasan. Go north along Naharpur Kasan-Kankrola to the start.

## Ward 15

Village or estate areas: Lakhnola. Final map sector labels: Sector 81.

Service areas: Zone 2. Civil engineering: Division 2. The zone table spells the village Lakhnola; the schedule uses Lakhnaula and Nakhdaula in this area. Works listings also use Nakhdola.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 7](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=7): From Lakhnaula-Nawada Fatehpur-Bada go east on Lakhnaula-Bada to circle 04, Jaisingh Chowk. Go southeast by road past Sector 81 and D.L.F. Altima Society to Rampura Chowk on the Delhi-Jaipur National Highway. Follow the highway southwest to Lakhnaula-Manesar, then go north to Lakhnaula-Manesar-Naharpur Kasan. Go northwest along Lakhnaula-Naharpur Kasan to Lakhnaula-Nawada Fatehpur-Naharpur Kasan, then north along Nawada Fatehpur-Lakhnaula to the start.

## Ward 16

Village or estate areas: Naurangpur, Bar Gujar, Nainwal. Final map sector labels: Sector 79, Sector 79B, Sector 80, IMT Sector 1D.

Service areas: Zone 3, Zone 6, Zone 7. Civil engineering: Division 2. Bar Gujar also appears as Bar-gujar, Bargurjar and Badgurjar. Nainwal is in Zone 7, Naurangpur and Bar Gujar in Zone 3, and IMT Sector 1D in Zone 6.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 7-8](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=7): From Naurangpur-Shikohpur-Lakhnaula on the Delhi-Jaipur National Highway follow Naurangpur-Shikohpur southeast to the municipal limit. Follow the limit south to Naurangpur-Bargurjar-Gairatpur Bas and southwest to Nainwal-Manesar. Go northeast on Nainwal-Manesar to Nainwal-Manesar-Badgurjar; follow Badgurjar-Manesar north via Manesar-Badgurjar-Naurangpur and Manesar-Naurangpur to the Lakhnaula junction. Go northwest along Manesar-Nakhdaula to the highway, then northeast along the highway to the start.

## Ward 17

Village or estate areas: Shikohpur. Final map sector labels: Sector 76, Sector 77, Sector 78, Sector 79A.

Service areas: Zone 3. Civil engineering: Division 1. The map has a joint label "Shikohpur & Rampura". The Rampura Chowk circuit is separately defined in Ward 18. The zone table spells Shikohpur "Sikohpur".

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 8](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=8): From Naurangpur-Shikohpur-Lakhnaula on the Delhi-Jaipur National Highway go north across Kherki Daula toll to the municipal limit. Go southeast along the limit and Sector 75/76 road to the Shikohpur boundary. Continue south to Hasanpur-Kherki Daula-Shikohpur and south along the limit to Shikohpur-Naurangpur-Sakatpur. Follow Naurangpur-Shikohpur northwest to the highway at the starting junction.

## Ward 18

Village or estate areas: Rampura. Final map sector labels: Sector 82, Sector 82A.

Service areas: Zone 2. Civil engineering: Division 1. The zone table calls the village Rampur; the order and ward-specific works call it Rampura.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 8](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=8): From Rampura Chowk on the Delhi-Jaipur National Highway go northwest by road via Sectors 82 and 82A to circle 4, Jaisingh Chowk. Follow the road northeast to Sheetla Mata Mandir Chowk, then Vatika Road southeast via the Rampura revenue boundary to the highway. Go southwest along the highway via Vatika Chowk to the start.

## Ward 19

Village or estate areas: Sikanderpur Badha, Sihi. Final map sector labels: Sector 83, Sector 84, Sector 85.

Service areas: Zone 2. Civil engineering: Division 2. Zone 2 includes only part of Sihi. The map places the "Badha" label on the Ward 19 side; the schedule follows the Badha-Sikanderpur Badha line and municipal works place Badha village works in Ward 20.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 8](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=8): From Sihi-Hayatpur at Dwarka Expressway go southeast along the Sector 36A/84 road to the Delhi-Jaipur National Highway. Follow it southwest to Kherki Daula-Shikohpur-Rampura. Go south along that line to Kherki Daula-Shikohpur-Sihi and south along Sihi-Shikohpur to the Sector 82/83 road. Go northwest along it to Sheetla Mata Mandir Chowk, southwest on the Sector 82/85 road to Jaisingh Chowk, northwest on the Sector 85/86 road to Dadi Sati Chowk, then northeast on the Sector 85/89 road to Hayatpur-Bada. Follow Bada-Hayatpur southeast to Bada-Hayatpur-Sihi, then Sihi-Hayatpur north to Sihi-Hayatpur-Harsaru at Dwarka Expressway.

## Ward 20

Village or estate areas: Nawada Fatehpur, Badha. Final map sector labels: Sector 86, Sector 90.

Service areas: Zone 1, Zone 2. Civil engineering: Division 2. The Sector 90 label lies in Ward 20. The zone table puts Sector 90 in Zone 1 and Nawada Fatehpur, Badha and Sector 86 in Zone 2.

[Boundary schedule, PDF pp. 9](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf#page=9): From Dadi Sati Chowk go southeast along the Sector 85/86 road to Badha-Sikanderpur Badha; go south along that boundary to Lakhnaula. Go northwest along Lakhnaula-Bada to Badha-Lakhnaula-Nawada Fatehpur, then south along Nawada Fatehpur-Nakhdaula to Naharpur Kasan. Go north along Nawada Fatehpur-Naharpur Kasan via Kankrola, then along Nawada Fatehpur-Kankrola to Meoka. Go northeast to Nawada Fatehpur-Meoka-Hayatpur, southeast to (28.402252, 76.930724), then northeast via Hayatpur-Nawada Fatehpur and the Bada junction. Continue on Bada-Hayatpur to the Sector 85/89 road and follow it to Dadi Sati Chowk.

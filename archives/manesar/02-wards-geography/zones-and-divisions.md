# Service zones and engineering divisions of Municipal Corporation Manesar

The M-prefix denotes Manesar sectors, shown here as IMT sectors; P-prefixes remain distinct. The portal uses the spellings "Nawada Fathehpur" and "Bar-gujar".

## Sector lookups without a confirmed ward

Five service-zone entries have no confirmed ward label in this index. Sector 81A belongs to Zone 2; Sector 97 (part), Sector 98 and IMT Sector 13 (M-13) to Zone 4; IMT Sector 6A (M-6A) to Zone 6. On the final ward sheet the printed Sector 97 and Sector 98 labels sit outside the ward colours. A label outside a ward does not rule out a partial overlap. For these five sectors, compare the property location to the [final ward map](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/w2.pdf) and [boundary schedule](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/W1_0.pdf) before assigning a ward.

## Engineering divisions

The [city profile](https://mcmanesar.in/manesar-city-info) records two divisions. The [civil executive engineer roster](https://mcmanesar.in/officers-directory) groups responsibilities by electoral ward. Ward-specific works identify the two groups as follows:

| Engineering division | Civil executive engineer's wards |
| --- | --- |
| Division 1 | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 17, 18 |
| Division 2 | 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 19, 20 |

For example, [reported finance committee approvals of 10 March 2026](https://hindustanera.in/rs-47-crore-development-works-approved-by-finance-contract-committee-in-manesar/) place Hayatpur (Ward 3), Bhangrola (Wards 6 and 7) and Shikohpur works (Wards 17 and 18) in Division 1. Haryana Engineering Works Portal tender **2026_HRY_504505_1** names Ward 9, Division II, for tubewells in Sehrawan dhani and Kasan. [Works listings](https://asiantender.com/location/haryana/manesar-tenders) also identify Ward 8 in Kasan and Ward 14 in Naharpur Kasan as Division II.

The zone page has no division column. An electoral ward may span zones: in Ward 16, the zone table puts Naurangpur in Zone 3 and Nainwal in Zone 7, while the civil works group is Division 2. For an address, look up its village or sector in the zone table and its ward in the engineering roster separately.

The record date is the municipal website footer update, not the date of a zone order.

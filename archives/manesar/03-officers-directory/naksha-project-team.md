# NAKSHA urban land survey project team in MC Manesar

## Project and source context

NAKSHA means National Geospatial Knowledge-based Land Survey of Urban Habitations. The urban land survey project concerns geospatial surveying and urban land records. MC Manesar publishes a local NAKSHA team directory and links to the [Department of Land Resources NAKSHA portal](https://naksha.dolr.gov.in/NakshaPortal/).

The [Department of Land Resources programme description](https://www.dolr.gov.in/offerings/naksha) places NAKSHA under the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme. It describes aerial and field surveys used to build a GIS-integrated database of urban land parcels. This is national programme context, not evidence of a particular survey method, completion date or coverage area in Manesar.

The [municipal team page](https://mcmanesar.in/naksha-team), fetched on 22 November 2026, lists 36 people, all designated Surveyor. It publishes their names, serial numbers, telephone numbers and photographs. It does not publish individual emails, ward allocations, survey schedules, reporting hierarchy or parcel-level responsibilities. No such assignments are inferred here.

The record date is the retrieval date, not the project's launch date or a staff appointment date. The common website footer says last updated 11/11/2026. Names and phone formatting below preserve the source, with HTML spacing cleaned. This NAKSHA roster is separate from the 53-row municipal officers roster in `officers.json`.

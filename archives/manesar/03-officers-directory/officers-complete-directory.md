# Complete officers directory of Municipal Corporation Manesar

## Scope and source

This snapshot preserves all 53 rows of the [official officers directory](https://mcmanesar.in/officers-directory), fetched on 22 November 2026. The record date is the retrieval date, not an appointment date. The portal footer says last updated 11/11/2026, but does not give a separate roster effective date.

Names, designations, serial numbers and phone formatting follow the published table. HTML spacing is cleaned. "Not published" means an empty email cell; "Not Available" preserves that explicit source label. Neither means the officer has no email account.

Department headings are archival groupings of the published designations, not an official organization chart. Ward numbers are explicit assignments. No zone or division is stated in the roster, so none is inferred from email addresses. The companion `officers.json` preserves 53 rows, including repeated names and distinct responsibilities. Its unavailable emails are empty strings and its `wards` arrays contain only explicit published ward numbers.

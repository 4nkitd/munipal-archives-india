# MC Manesar offices, helplines and contact directory

## Headquarters and office telephone

Municipal Corporation Manesar headquarters: **CP-09, 3rd Floor, Cyberwalk Building, Sector-8, IMT Manesar, Gurugram-122052**.

The live [Contact Us page](https://mcmanesar.in/contact-us) gives CP-09, 3rd Floor, Cyberwalk, Sector-8, IMT Manesar, Gurugram, helpdesk **0124-2012232** and toll-free **18001801817**. The postal code 122052 and "Cyberwalk Building" wording appear in the older ULB Haryana contact listing.

General municipal email: **cmc-manesar@ulbharyana.gov.in**. The Commissioner has a separate published office telephone, **0124-2012230**. Office opening hours are not stated on the fetched contact page.

The [legacy ULB Haryana Contact Us record](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Manesar/10669) is search-indexed with last updated date 18-03-2025. During retrieval on 22 November 2026, this URL redirected to `https://mcmanesar.in/`, rather than returning the older contact page. The WhatsApp, sanitation WhatsApp, missed-call and additional sidebar numbers above are preserved from the indexed official record, not represented as newly displayed on the live contact page.

The [GMDA official homepage](https://gmda.gov.in/) also lists 18001801817, WhatsApp 7840001817 and missed call 9873353224 for its integrated grievance system. A 22 July 2024 [Tribune report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/gurugram-residents-can-now-register-civic-complaints-on-whatsapp-642076/) describes this system as covering GMDA, Gurugram MC and Manesar MC, accepting complaint photographs on WhatsApp and returning missed calls to register complaints. No telephone or message was placed to test any number.

## Emergency and public helplines

The [municipal homepage](https://mcmanesar.in/) groups the following six numbers under "Emergency Helpline Numbers" and describes that group as round-the-clock. This archives the website's wording; individual service hours were not independently tested.

| Number | Homepage label | Published purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1098 | Child Helpline Number | 24/7 Child Protection Services |
| 1091 | Women Helpline Number | Women Safety & Support |
| 1064 | Anti-Corruption Helpline | Report Corruption Issues |
| 112 | City Hospital Helpline | Medical Emergency Services |
| 1950 | Voter Helpline Number | Election Related Queries |
| 102 | Ambulance Service | Medical Emergency Transport |

For life-threatening emergencies, the homepage explicitly says to dial **112** for immediate assistance. The unusual homepage label "City Hospital Helpline" is retained above as source wording, not a claim that 112 is a particular hospital's direct telephone.

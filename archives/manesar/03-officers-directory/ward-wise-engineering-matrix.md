# Ward-wise engineering responsibility matrix for MC Manesar Wards 1 to 20

## Reading this lookup

Source: the [official officers directory](https://mcmanesar.in/officers-directory), fetched on 22 November 2026. EE means Executive Engineer, AE means Assistant Engineer, and JE means Junior Engineer. The record date is the retrieval date, not the date an assignment began. The common portal footer says last updated 11/11/2026.

Civil assignments below are taken directly from ward numbers in the published designations. There is one published civil EE and AE for every ward. Wards 16, 17 and 18 each have two published civil JE entries. No primary JE or division between these two is stated.

"Not published" means the email cell is blank; "Not Available" is the explicit source label. Both become empty email strings in `officers.json`. Zone and division are not published in this roster. They cannot be derived reliably from mailbox names.

Electrical, horticulture, SBM and specialist enforcement JE entries have no ward allocations in the source. Their full phone and email directory follows the ward sections. These are departmental contacts for enquiries, not verified ward-specific appointments.

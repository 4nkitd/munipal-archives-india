# Manesar Area Sabha constitution order and January 2026 meeting notices

## Constitution and statutory duties

Municipal Corporation Manesar issued `MCM/ZTO/2026/2955` on 19 January 2026, signed by the Deputy Municipal Commissioner, Joint Commissioner-II and Commissioner. The three-page order constitutes 76 Area Sabhas in the 20 wards under the [Haryana Municipal Citizens' Participation Act, 2008](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/192e29e4-796b-4dae-b459-ca2eddc1961a.pdf). It lists every voter on the applicable roll as a member of the Area Sabha. The totals printed in the order are population 134,460 and registered voters 79,200; the voter figure is a snapshot, not the current roll.

Paragraph 2 reproduces Section 8(a) to (g). The Sabha proposes and prioritizes development schemes for the ward committee, or the municipality where there is no ward committee. It verifies eligible beneficiaries using government criteria, ranks them and forwards the list for inclusion in development plans. It proposes locations for streets, lights, community taps, wells and public sanitation units; identifies deficiencies and recommends remedies; assists public health centres in disease prevention and family welfare and arranges reporting of epidemics and natural calamities; mobilizes voluntary labour, cash and contributions in kind and oversees work through volunteer teams; and supports tax mapping and reminds residents to pay municipal taxes and user charges.

Section 5 ordinarily calls for public meetings at least twice each year, 15 May to 15 June and 15 November to 15 December, with at least 10 percent of registered voters present. The notices below are the separate January meetings, not those statutory date windows.

## Meeting notices and agenda

[Area Sabha Baithak Notice](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/AreaSabha%20Baithak%20Notice.pdf), one page, is letter `MCM/DMC/2026/2956` dated 19 January 2026. It invites all voters for ward 1, Sabha 1/1, to the local chaupal or community centre on 22 January, 10:00 to 12:00. The issuing office is on the third floor, Cyberwalk Building, CP-09, IMT Sector 8, Manesar 122052. That is the office address, not the meeting venue.

The [compiled meeting notices](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/Notices%20Regarding%20Area%20Sabha%20Meeting.pdf) comprise 68 scanned pages and were listed on 21 January 2026. The venue wording is the area's own chaupal or community centre (चौपाल / कम्युनिटी सेंटर); the notices do not generally give a named building, street address or map. The notices require participation by 10 percent of the Sabha's registered voters. Agenda:

1. Verify old-age pension beneficiaries (वृद्धावस्था पेंशन).
2. Verify widow pension beneficiaries (विधवा पेंशन).
3. Verify beneficiaries of Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana (दीन दयाल लाडो लक्ष्मी योजना).
4. Consider other business with the Area Sabha's permission.

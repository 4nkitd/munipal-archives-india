# GRAP Stages I and II in Delhi-NCR, October 2025

## Sources and dates

Manesar posted both two-page circulars on 27 October 2025. The upload date is not the date when restrictions took effect. CAQM invoked Stage I (Poor, Delhi AQI 201 to 300) on 14 October, when the Delhi daily average reached 211. On 19 October, CAQM order `120017/27/GRAP/2021/CAQM` invoked Stage II (Very Poor, Delhi AQI 301 to 400) immediately throughout NCR, on top of Stage I. Delhi AQI reached 296 at 4 p.m. and 302 at 7 p.m. on 19 October, with further deterioration forecast by IMD/IITM. The underlying GRAP schedule was revised on 13 December 2024.

## Stage I: Construction, road dust and waste

Sources: [Manesar's Stage I PDF](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-10/Implementation%20of%20GRAP%201%20in%20Delhi%C2%A0NCR.pdf) and the [Government of India release of 14 October 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2179193&lang=2&reg=3), which sets out the wider 27-point regional plan.

Haryana memo `SNT/DULB/2025/SBM-299`, dated 17 October 2025 and signed by the Joint Director (Administration) for the Director General, cites CAQM order `120017/27/GRAP/2021/CAQM-1421DT`. It instructs DMCs and CMCs to implement, monitor and enforce Stage I in urban local bodies under their jurisdiction, keep daily enforcement records and send weekly compliance status to the Directorate (paragraphs 4 to 6). Its 500-square-metre stop-work instruction specifically names the Haryana State Pollution Control Board Dust Pollution Control Self-Assessment portal. The memo also requires C&D waste to be processed or recycled at the processing facility, calls for a "heavy fine" for open burning without stating an amount, and names e-Samadhan and SAMEER as reporting channels. In the memo, periodic mechanical sweeping and water sprinkling are joined by "and/or"; the regional action plan calls for both.

- Enforce dust mitigation rules for construction and demolition (C&D) and sound environmental management of C&D waste. Under CAQM Direction Nos. 11 to 18 dated 11 June 2021, do not allow C&D on a project plot of 500 square metres or more if it is not registered on the applicable state dust-monitoring portal or otherwise fails the statutory requirements.
- Contain and cover C&D materials and waste on site. Use covered vehicles for their transport. Use anti-smog guns in proportion to the built-up area under construction. Intensify anti-smog gun deployment, sprinkling and dust suppression on road works, widening, repairs and maintenance.
- Sweep roads mechanically and sprinkle water periodically. Dispose of swept dust scientifically at designated sites or landfills. Lift municipal solid waste (MSW), C&D waste and hazardous waste regularly from dedicated dump sites. Prohibit illegal dumping on open land, and watch landfills and dump sites for fires.
- Enforce the ban on open biomass and MSW burning. The plan calls for maximum environmental compensation under the National Green Tribunal orders of 4 December 2014 and 28 April 2015 in OA 21/2014. It does not specify a new flat municipal fine.

### Other Stage I actions in the regional plan

- Deploy traffic police at congested corridors and intersections, enforce pollution under control (PUC) requirements, and impound visibly polluting vehicles and/or impose the maximum applicable fine. Divert non-Delhi-bound trucks via the Eastern and Western Peripheral Expressways and enforce applicable orders on end-of-life vehicles.
- Take legal or penal action against illegal and non-compliant industrial units. Enforce emission and approved-fuel requirements at industries, brick kilns and hot-mix plants; close units that violate the approved-fuel requirement. Enforce emissions requirements at thermal power plants, enforce firecracker restrictions, and arrange proper collection and disposal of industrial waste.
- Minimize electricity supply interruptions; do not use diesel generators as a regular power source. Enforce the ban on coal and firewood in tandoors and require electricity, gas or clean-fuel appliances in hotels, restaurants and open eateries.
- Publish pollution alerts and control room contacts, respond promptly to reports through channels such as the 311, Green Delhi and SAMEER apps, and encourage collective office commutes.

## Stage II: Haryana municipal circular of 21 October

Memo `SNT/DULB/2025/SBM-303` (paragraphs 2 to 6), signed by the Joint Director (Administration) for the Director General, Urban Local Bodies, Haryana, directs all District Municipal Commissioners (DMCs) and Commissioners of Municipal Corporations (CMCs) in Haryana's NCR to operationalize and rigorously enforce both stages:

| Memo clause | Municipal action |
| --- | --- |
| 5(i) | Sweep roads daily by mechanical or vacuum-based means. |
| 5(ii) | Sprinkle water with dust suppressants at least every alternate day, especially at hot spots, heavy traffic corridors and vulnerable areas before peak hours. Dispose of dust at designated sites or landfills. |
| 5(iii) | Inspect C&D sites regularly and strictly enforce dust controls. |
| 5(iv) to (v) | Do not allow coal or firewood, including in tandoors. Require electricity or clean fuel gas-based appliances at hotels, restaurants and open eateries. |
| 5(vi) | Stop use of diesel generators except for emergent and essential services. The only service listed in this Haryana municipal memo is a sewage treatment plant. |
| 5(vii) to (ix) | Use newspapers, television and radio for pollution advice; raise parking fees to discourage private transport; require resident welfare associations to provide electric heaters to security staff in winter to avoid burning biomass and MSW. |
| 5(x) | Urge citizens to avoid dust-generating construction from October to January. |
| 6 | Monitor and report enforcement daily, provide weekly compliance status to the Directorate, and ensure strict on-ground enforcement. |

The [CAQM Stage II invocation](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Home/News/Invocation%20of%20GRAP%20order-%20Stage-%20II%20191020257fe54de8-42f2-456b-b1b7-75ec39e2bb14.pdf) directs all implementing agencies to maintain vigilance and enforce both stages. The Haryana memo specifies the municipal DG-set exception noted above; it is not a consolidated NCR-wide list of exceptions for all sectors.

### Wider NCR Stage II measures and DG categories

The [Government of India's 12-point account of the CAQM action plan](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2180933&lang=2&reg=48) calls for daily mechanical or vacuum sweeping and sprinkling on identified roads, longer sweeping deployment, and daily sprinkling with dust suppressants, preferably before peaks. This differs from the Haryana memo's stated minimum of alternate days for sprinkling with suppressants. The regional measures also call for targeted action in hot spots, uninterrupted electricity, traffic personnel at congestion points, more frequent CNG and electric buses and metro services, and differential fares for off-peak travel. Interstate buses from NCR states entering Delhi must be electric, CNG or BS-VI diesel, apart from buses and tempo travellers on All India Tourist Permits. The pollution boards of NCR states and the Delhi Pollution Control Committee are among the implementing agencies.

The release summarizes CAQM Direction No. 76 of 29 September 2023 for operations in all sectors:

| Generator category | Regional conditions |
| --- | --- |
| LPG, natural gas, biogas, propane or butane, all capacities | Unrestricted without a separate emissions control system. |
| Up to 800 kW (1,000 kVA) conforming to MoEFCC G.S.R. 804(E), 3 November 2022 | Unrestricted without a separate system. |
| Older sets 800 kW (1,000 kVA) and above | Unrestricted with an emissions control system meeting Direction 76. |
| Older sets 41 kW (51 kVA) to under 800 kW | Unrestricted in dual-fuel mode or with an emissions control device retrofitted by a certified agency. |
| Older sets 19 kW (23 kVA) to under 41 kW | Unrestricted in dual-fuel mode. If unavailable only for lack of gas infrastructure and supply, use limited to prescribed emergency services. |
| Portable sets below 19 kW (23 kVA) | Not generally permitted; prescribed emergency services only. |

The regional emergency list covers operation of elevators, escalators and travelators; healthcare; rail and metro; airports and interstate bus terminals; sewage treatment plants and water pumping stations; national security, defence and national-importance projects; and telecom and IT/data services. Haryana's separate municipal memo lists only sewage treatment plants under its DG instruction.

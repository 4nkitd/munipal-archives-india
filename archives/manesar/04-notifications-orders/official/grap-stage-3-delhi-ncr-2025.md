# GRAP Stage III invocation in Delhi-NCR, 11 November 2025

## Invocation and enforcement

In the eight-page CAQM order `120017/27/GRAP/2021/CAQM-1444-DT`, paragraphs 1 to 6 invoke all actions under Stage III (Severe, Delhi AQI 401 to 450) of the schedule revised on 13 December 2024. Stage I and Stage II, invoked on 14 October and 19 October 2025, remained in force. Delhi AQI rose from 362 on 10 November to 425 at 9 a.m. on 11 November. The Sub-Committee cited calm winds, a stable atmosphere and unfavourable meteorological conditions and reviewed the IMD and IITM forecasts.

The order required implementing agencies across NCR to enforce, monitor and review all three stages, keep strict vigil and intensify measures. The Sub-Committee would review Delhi air quality and IMD/IITM forecasts. Residents were asked to follow the Stage III citizen charter. Recipients included Haryana's Chief Secretary, Environment, Transport and Urban Local Bodies departments, police and traffic police, Haryana State Pollution Control Board, Gurugram Deputy Commissioner, Municipal Corporation Gurugram and Gurugram Metropolitan Development Authority. The file is the CAQM order circulated by Manesar's portal, not a separate Manesar municipal order. It does not prescribe a separate fine or document inspections undertaken by Manesar.

## Construction, demolition and dust restrictions

The invocation PDF does not reproduce the schedule. The measures below are from the [Government of India's contemporaneous nine-point summary](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2188873&lang=2&reg=48).

| Restricted activity across NCR | Scope or stated exception |
| --- | --- |
| Earthwork, excavation and filling, including boring and drilling; piling; all demolition | Covered by the restrictions on dust-generating activity |
| Sewer, water, drainage and electric cabling | Restricted when using an open trench |
| Brick and masonry work; ready-mix concrete batching plants | Restricted on site |
| Major welding and gas cutting | Minor welding for mechanical, electrical and plumbing works permitted |
| Painting, polishing and varnishing; cement, plaster and other coatings; cutting, grinding and fixing tiles, stone and other flooring | Minor indoor repairs or maintenance excepted from the coating and flooring restrictions |
| Road construction and major repairs | Restricted |
| Loading, unloading or transferring dusty materials | Includes cement, fly ash, bricks, sand, murram, pebbles and crushed stone within or outside the site |
| Construction materials moved by vehicles on unpaved roads; any transport of demolition waste | Restricted |

Less polluting construction not on the restricted list could proceed only in strict compliance with C&D Waste Management Rules, dust prevention and control norms, and CAQM directions. That compliance condition also applied to otherwise restricted work on the exempt projects: railway and metro works and stations; airports and interstate bus terminals; defence, national security and national-importance projects; hospitals and health facilities; linear public projects such as highways, roads, flyovers, overbridges, power transmission and distribution, pipelines and telecom; sewage treatment and water supply projects; and activities specifically ancillary to those projects.

## Vehicles, stone crushers and other measures

- NCR states and the Government of NCT Delhi were to strictly restrict BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel four-wheel light motor vehicles in Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad and Gautam Budh Nagar. Vehicles specifically adapted for personal use by a person with a disability were excepted.
- Delhi was to restrict Delhi-registered diesel medium goods vehicles of BS-IV or lower and deny entry to BS-IV diesel light commercial goods vehicles registered outside Delhi, except vehicles carrying essential commodities or providing essential services.
- Close stone crushers and all mining and associated activities across NCR. The December Stage III list does not impose a separate blanket brick-kiln closure. Its ban on brick or masonry work concerns construction sites; existing industrial fuel restrictions were not displaced.
- Conduct classes through Class V in hybrid mode where online teaching is feasible in Delhi and the four named districts. The option to attend online belongs to students and guardians. NCR states may consider hybrid classes elsewhere.
- Stagger public office and municipal body hours in Delhi and the four named districts. NCR states may extend that measure elsewhere, and the Central Government may stagger its own office hours in Delhi-NCR.

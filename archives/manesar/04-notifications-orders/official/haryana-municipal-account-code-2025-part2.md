# Haryana Municipal Account Code, 2025: Part II, forms and schedules

## Source, commencement and scope

The Manesar English Part II PDF is a 255-page extract of the Haryana Government Gazette dated 27 May 2025 (Jystha 6, 1947 Saka), printed pages 2684 to 2938. It accompanies notification `S.O. 45/H.A.24/1973/S.257/2025` dated 20 May 2025. Under Part I paragraph 1.3, the Code commenced on Gazette publication, 27 May 2025. The portal listed the file on 25 September 2025. Part II opens with the register for removal of unauthorized advertisements under paragraph 2.3(e) and continues with proformas, not a separate sequence of substantive chapters. Clause citations below are to [Part I](haryana-municipal-account-code-2025-part1.md) unless stated otherwise. The PDF uses custom font encoding, so the form titles and fields were read from the printed page images.

## Expenditure controls and payment trail

Paragraphs 17.1 to 17.4 require municipal acceptance of a charge against the fund, subject to the stated exception for direct debits by Government or its authorized agencies. Claimants must submit a written claim and supporting invoice or other documents to the Chief Municipal Officer or Accounts In-Charge; the designated officer checks and attaches them to the bill. No payment may be made from cash collected for the fund. Draw cheques only for immediate disbursement or to replenish a permanent advance.

The competent authority normally issues a written order only after checking municipal sanction and the budget allotment under the relevant head. Emergency payments without municipal sanction must be reported at the next municipal meeting. Paragraph 17.6 permits sums below ₹5,000 in emergent cases to be paid from the permanent advance. Paragraph 17.8.2 requires joint signatures on cheques or RTGS/NEFT advice. For a municipal corporation, the signatories are the officer in charge of Accounts and the Commissioner or an authorized officer, subject to the vacancy arrangements in that paragraph. Procurement follows GeM or another mode prescribed by the State.

## Contractor billing and works

Under paragraph 19.2.6, repairs and maintenance require budget provision and technical sanction, without administrative approval for those works. Paragraph 19.3 calls works costing up to ₹5 lakh petty works and those above ₹5 lakh but below ₹25 lakh minor works; it describes major works as above the minor-work limit. Paragraph 19.16.3 requires the Accounts Officer to verify the budget sanction before tender approval.

Paragraphs 19.22.10 to 19.22.13 prohibit removing pages or erasing entries from the Measurement Book. Strike through corrections with one line, date and initial them; cancel and attest blank pages, keep an up-to-date index and have the accountant or clerk initial checked amounts. Paragraph 19.37.1 gives a journal example for a ₹1,000 contractor deduction for liquidated damages, debiting contractor creditors and crediting Deposit Works. Paragraphs 19.38 and 19.39 offset completed project costs against deposits and refund unutilized deposit balances.

## Fixed assets, stores and inventory

Paragraphs 6.6 and 6.7 direct the municipality to keep asset records, including infrastructure register AR-1 at the asset custodian level, GEN-30 for immovable property and the separate movable property register. Paragraph 2.17(d) expenses assets costing less than ₹5,000. Paragraphs 6.12 and 6.13 prescribe written-down value or straight-line depreciation as specified, exclude land, and exclude heritage assets carried at ₹1. Apply the selected method consistently and quantify and disclose the effect of a change. Capital work in progress transfers to the relevant asset account on completion. Part II opening-balance schedule B-11 (p. 2926) shows gross block cost, accumulated depreciation and net block.

Paragraphs 30.4 to 30.7 require the stores unit to document procurement, receipt, issue and disposal. Use ST-1 Material Receipt Note on delivery, post receipt to ST-2 Store Ledger, check the supplier invoice against ST-1, then enter the bill in GEN-13. Accrue accepted goods without an invoice at the cutoff using the purchase order. Reconcile physical and book stock at financial year-end and record differences in the closing stock statement. Value stores at FIFO cost; value finished goods and work in progress manufactured for sale at cost or market value, whichever is lower. Recognize disposal revenue on actual receipt.

Part II prints the ST-2 ledger at p. 2932, ST-3A closing stock statement at p. 2933 and G-29 Stock Register at p. 2934. ST-3A captures the store ledger reference, quantity, rate and amount; G-29 records receipts, issues, balances and signatures. Part II also supplies B-14 Stock in Hand (Inventories) at p. 2927.

## Audit, reconciliation and financial reporting

Part I paragraph 23.1.8 requires a special municipal meeting within **one month** of receiving the audit note, and communication of its decision to the Director, Local Audit, Haryana within **three months** of receipt. Paragraphs 23.1 to 23.3 ask auditors to test proper books, authorization, legal compliance, complete recording of receipts, classification, physical verification of stores, internal controls and bank reconciliations. Chapter 29 assigns reconciliation to Accounts, Audit and relevant branches, covering bank and inter-unit balances, deposits, tax receivables and collections, advances, loans, contractor payables and government balances.

Paragraphs 28.2 to 28.6 require an annual report with balance sheet, income and expenditure statement, cash flow statement, detailed receipts and payments account, notes and financial performance indicators. Include the Municipal Auditor's report, the Chief Municipal Officer's response and the action taken report. At quarter-end, prepare the four statements; flow statements show quarter, year-to-date and prior-year comparable figures. The income and expenditure statement also shows the annual budget figure. The cash flow statement distinguishes operating, investing and financing flows. Part II schedules support the statements and opening balances:

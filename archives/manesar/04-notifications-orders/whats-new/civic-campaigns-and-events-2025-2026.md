# Manesar civic campaigns and events, 2025 to 2026

## 24 Hours Challenge: You Spot It, We Fix It

The [MCM announcement](https://mcmanesar.in/24-hours-challange-you-spot-it-we-fix-it), listed under [News & Events](https://mcmanesar.in/news-events), is dated 5 November 2026. The [complaint portal](http://complaint.mcmanesar.in/) says residents can lodge one complaint at a time for listed problems within MCM limits. Choose the issue, attach at least one clear photo (up to three), pin the actual location on the map or use GPS, and give the address, name and mobile number. The form asks for either sector or village, or ward. Keep the Complaint ID to track it. The terms say complaints are routed by category and location and are closed only after the resolution is verified with a photo and approved.

| Issue | Target after registration |
| --- | --- |
| Big garbage heap | 24 hours |
| Tree pruning | 48 hours |
| Major pothole, sewer overflow or non-working streetlight | 72 hours |

The portal terms call these time limits targets, not guarantees. If a complaint misses its target and a review finds employee negligence, the competent authority may approve ₹5,000 compensation. Filing does not itself create an entitlement.

## Cleaning and monsoon campaigns

[Cleaning Services Provided by MC Manesar](https://mcmanesar.in/cleaning-services-provided-mc-manesar) has five posters. The gallery identifies **7428860890** as the number to call for garbage and cleaning issues. This is the campaign's advertised telephone number; the challenge uses the separate complaint portal.

[Preventions for rainy seasons](https://mcmanesar.in/preventions-rainy-seasons) displays a 1 August 2025 posting date. Its 11 images cover rain precautions, conjunctivitis prevention, mosquito characteristics, malaria symptoms and mosquito prevention. The "Monsoon Pool Party" and quiz graphics sit alongside the public health materials. The gallery gives no separate schedule or venue for those graphics.

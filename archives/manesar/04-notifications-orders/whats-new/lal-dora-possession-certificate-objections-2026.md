### Property ID structure and redacted example

List 1 has 84 eight-character `1L` IDs (including one printed with a lower-case `l`), 14 eight-character `1C` IDs, and one seven-character `1L` ID. All three List 2 IDs and all 14 List 3 IDs are eight-character `1L` IDs. The prefix is followed by letters and digits. Copy the ID from the relevant official row; do not pad the shorter value or infer missing characters.

The PDFs can contain resident names and other personal details; the archive does not reproduce them.

## Checking a Property ID and asking about an objection

The [Verify Lal Dora Property ID Details entry](https://mcmanesar.in/node/126) shows **16 September 2025** as its creation date. That entry links to [Taxation Documents](https://mcmanesar.in/taxation-documents), where the "Lal Dora Property ID details" table links to files for 26 villages. Select the village, open its file, and find the Property ID column. If the PID is not known, use the property address and owner or occupier details to identify the entry. Then compare the ID and colony with the relevant 2026 possession list. Check the source page for any later list or correction.

The verification entry separately attaches [12.pdf](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-10/12.pdf), stored under an October 2025 upload path. It is actually Haryana Government Gazette No. 32-2021 of 10 August 2021, containing a Public Works Department notification on the Haryana Schedule of Rates. It is not the verification notice and contains no Lal Dora property register.

None of the three PDFs or their portal entries states a filing window, deadline, dedicated objection office or submission channel. The News & Events expiration fields are blank. For the applicable instructions, ask MCM's general helpdesk at **0124-2012232**, toll-free **18001801817**, or **cmc-manesar@ulbharyana.gov.in**. Its published office address is **CP-09, 3rd Floor, Cyberwalk, Sector 8, IMT Manesar, Gurugram**. These are general contacts, not a verified filing route. Have the list number, row number and PID ready when asking MCM how to raise a discrepancy.

# Haryana tentative seniority and gradation list for municipal engineers (civil), July 2025

## Circular and objection hearing

Haryana's Urban Local Bodies Department, Committee-II Branch, issued circular **No. 2/29/2023-2CII** at Chandigarh on **21 July 2025**. Vinod Singh, Deputy Superintendent, signed for the Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Urban Local Bodies Department. MCM published the nine-page scan in [What's new](https://mcmanesar.in/services/whats-new) on **30 July 2025**.

The circular says the government prepared the municipal engineer seniority and gradation list and would finalise it after hearing any objections. The Secretary asked the named engineers to appear with their objections, if any, at **10:30 a.m. on 30 July 2025** at the **Directorate of Urban Local Bodies Department, Bays 11 to 14, Sector 4, Panchkula**. The circular gives no separate written deadline, online submission channel, phone number or email. This was a personnel hearing, not a public recruitment round.

## List coverage and Manesar entries

The seven annexure pages contain **54 numbered entries** as at **1 July 2025**. The annexure heading names municipal councils and committees, but the covering letter also lists engineers at municipal corporations, including Manesar, and the Directorate of Urban Local Bodies. Across the annexure, 52 appointments are marked "By Promotion" and two "Direct". Several entries distinguish actual promotion from its notional effective date.

# Public notice for up to two restaurants in residential Sector 63, Gurugram

## Issuing authority and zoning basis

The Director General, Urban Local Bodies Department, Haryana, signed the notice at Panchkula on **12 July 2025**. MCM posted it on [What's new](https://mcmanesar.in/services/whats-new) on **30 July 2025**. The source is a one-page scan.

The Department of Town & Country Planning, Haryana, notified its **10 November 2017** policy for permissions capped by Net Planned Area (NPA). The policy provides guidelines for categories limited by area or number. The notice directs readers to [the Town & Country Planning Department](https://tcpharyana.gov.in) for the policy. It also cites the policy dated **30 June 2022**, read with the 2017 policy.

The department framed a zoning regulation for restaurants in residential and commercial sectors. The notice says the policy allows a maximum of two new restaurant units in a residential or commercial sector, **in addition to existing units**. This invitation applies only to **Sector 63 (Residential Zone), Gurugram**, under GMUC-2031 A.D.

## Applications and deadline

| Item | Published condition |
| --- | --- |
| Who could apply | Interested persons, companies, firms, trusts and similar applicants seeking restaurant permission. |
| Application office | Office of the Director, Urban Local Bodies, Haryana. |
| Submission method | Online through the [CLU portal](https://ulbcluhry.org). |
| Filing window | Two months from publication of the notice. Applications received after that period would not be considered. |
| Consideration | Online applications would be considered only after the two-month period. |
| Enquiries during working hours | Department landline **0172-2570110** or District Town Planner **Sunil Verma**, **9888977318**. |

The notice does not print a calendar closing date, fee, required document checklist or selection method. The 30 July date is the MCM posting date; the notice measures the window from publication, not from its signature date.

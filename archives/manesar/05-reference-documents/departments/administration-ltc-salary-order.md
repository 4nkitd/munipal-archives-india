# One month's salary in lieu of LTC for Haryana urban local body employees

## Memo and eligibility

Memo No. **DULB/Estt./3E/2022/18082**, dated **28 April 2022**, is from the Principal Secretary to Government, Haryana, Urban Local Bodies Department, Chandigarh. The Additional Director (Administration) signed for the Principal Secretary. The [Municipal Corporation Manesar Administration documents index](https://mcmanesar.in/administration-documents) lists 2 May 2022; the date printed on the signed order is 28 April.

| Provision | What the memo says |
| --- | --- |
| Benefit | Encashment of one month's salary in lieu of Leave Travel Concession (LTC) for the block years 2020 to 2023. |
| Eligible employees | Employees of Haryana urban local bodies (ULBs), subject to individual eligibility and admissibility of LTC. The index description does not override this condition. |
| Funding | Payment is to come from the concerned ULB's own financial resources. |
| Unutilized LTC outside the Treasury System | The Haryana Finance Department clarified that the State Government had not stopped payment in lieu of unutilized LTC to employees outside the Treasury System. The Administrative Department could act at its own level after checking admissibility for municipal employees. |

The memo extends a benefit given to the Health, Medical Education and Police departments. It gives recognition of ULB staff working during the COVID-19 response as the reason for the extension. It does not specify a salary calculation, employee classes, a claim form, a claim deadline or a benefit for later blocks.

## Distribution and Finance Department references

The order went to all Municipal Corporation Commissioners and District Municipal Commissioners in Haryana and to the Executive Officers and Secretaries of Municipal Councils and Municipal Committees.

The memo refers to Finance Department letter No. **4/12/2020-2FR/8664**, dated **4 June 2021**, and to clarifications requested by municipalities. Paragraph 3 cites Finance Department U.O. No. **04/12/2020-5FR/6231**, dated **21 April 2022**. The forwarding endorsement prints the same reference with **2FR** rather than **5FR**. Both readings are retained as printed.

Endorsement No. **DULB/Estt./3E/2022/18083**, also dated **28 April 2022**, forwarded the memo to the Additional Chief Secretary (Finance), all Deputy Commissioners in Haryana and the Director, Local Audit, Haryana, Panchkula. The copy list names PS/ULBM, PS/PSULB, PA/DULB, CAO (Headquarters) and Section Officer (Administration), Headquarters.

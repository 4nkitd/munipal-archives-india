# Contractor enlistment checklist

## Source and scope

The [Municipal Corporation Manesar Engineering documents index](https://mcmanesar.in/engineering-documents) labels this PDF "Contractor Enlistment Checklist" and dates the listing 19 April 2022. The one-page PDF itself is headed "Checklist". It has no printed date, order number, signature or issuing memo. The same index links the [Haryana Engineering Works Portal (HEWP)](https://works.haryana.gov.in/HEWP_Login/login.aspx) for contractor enlistment.

This is a contractor qualification checklist, not a civil works order or specification. It does not prescribe material or execution standards, tender rules, HSR rates, or works classifications beyond the Class I staffing requirement below. "Amendment to HSR rates 2021" is a separate index entry and is not part of this PDF. The checklist refers to Annexures B and C but does not reproduce them.

## Application, registration and finances

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| (i) | Address the enlistment application to the Chief Engineer. |
| (ii) | Submit the Labour Department registration certificate. |
| (iii) | Submit an affidavit as per Annexure B. |
| (iv) | Submit a certificate showing immovable property and its market value, duly attested. The PDF prints the attesting authority as "Collector of First Class Magistrate". Confirm the accepted attesting authority with the department. |
| (v) | Obtain a solvency certificate from a scheduled bank showing the financial position, including cash in hand, bank funds or credit limits, as per Annexure B. |
| (vi) | For a firm or company, submit the registered copy of the deed or Articles of Association. |
| (viii) | Where applicable, furnish the registered partnership deed, name the person holding power of attorney and enclose an attested copy of the power of attorney. |
| (xiv) | Submit the latest income tax clearance certificate and PAN number from the competent authority, in original or as an attested copy. |

## Work history, equipment and technical staff

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| (vii) | List works executed during the last three financial years and the amount of work done. A Gazetted Officer must attest the list. |
| (ix) | Provide details of machinery, tools and plant, including numbers of trucks, rigs and compressors. |
| (x) | List permanently employed technical staff and specify each employee's technical qualifications and experience. |
| (xi) | For Class I enlistment, employ a degree-qualified engineer for technical guidance and enclose proof of employment. The checklist also accepts equivalent qualifications. |
| (xii) | List previous enlistments with Haryana PWD B&R, the Irrigation Branch, HUDA, HSAMB, U.T. Chandigarh Administration or any other enlistment agency. |
| (xiii) | Enclose a Superintending Engineer's or Executive Engineer's certificate of satisfactory completion for works executed. |

## Identity and verification

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| (xv) | Produce original documents for verification if required after the application is found in order. |
| (xvi) | Provide the PAN number. The checklist repeats PAN separately from item (xiv). |
| (xvii) | Provide proof of identity and address. |
| (xviii) | Fill the identity card as per Annexure C. The department issues the card at the time of enlistment. |

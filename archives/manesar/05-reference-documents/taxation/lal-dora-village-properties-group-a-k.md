# Lal Dora village property registers: Badha to Kukrola

## How to verify a Lal Dora property

The municipal index links village property registers. Each register lists serial number, property ID (PID), owner or occupier, address, mobile number, property type, area and unit. Counts below use the PID-bearing rows, not the last printed serial. Each observed PID has eight uppercase letters and digits in total. The prefix is the first two characters; it is not a unique village code. These are January 2025 PDF metadata dates, not dates of survey or certificate issue.

For a Lal Dora property certificate enquiry, find the village PDF and compare the PID, locality, area and unit with your property papers. A register entry does not itself issue a certificate or establish title. Ask the Taxation Branch which certificate process and supporting papers apply. To correct the municipal assessment, search by PID on the [Haryana Property Tax and No Dues Certificate portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/), choose View Details/Self Certify and check each field. For an incorrect field select No, supply the correction and supporting evidence, then preview and submit the objection. The [Citizen Corner](https://mcmanesar.in/services/citizen-corner) links the illustrated instructions. The registers give no objection deadline or certificate fee.

# Property tax assessment of petrol pumps: clarification of 20 April 2022

## Which area does a fuel station pay tax on?

Memo DULB/Tax/2022/16976 of 20 April 2022 answers three questions from the Commissioner, Municipal Corporation Yamunanagar (letter 3405/ADA of 25 March 2022). For a petrol pump on a plot **up to and including 1,000 square yards**, assess the plot area, including open land. For a plot **over 1,000 square yards**, assess it as commercial space on carpet area. Once the plot crosses that threshold, the separate commercial-space threshold is measured in **square feet of carpet area**.

The pump rate is 50% of the applicable clause 2B commercial rate for the respective city category. The table shows the resulting annual pump rates before other adjustments. They are calculated from the gazette rates, not separately notified amounts.

| Pump assessment basis | Unit per year | A1, ₹ | A2, ₹ |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Plot up to 50 sq yd | Per sq yd | 12 | 9 |
| Plot 51 to 100 sq yd | Per sq yd | 18 | 13.50 |
| Plot 101 to 500 sq yd | Per sq yd | 24 | 18 |
| Plot 501 to 1,000 sq yd | Per sq yd | 30 | 22.50 |
| Plot over 1,000 sq yd; carpet area up to 1,000 sq ft | Per sq ft | 6 | 4.50 |
| Plot over 1,000 sq yd; carpet area over 1,000 sq ft | Per sq ft | 7.50 | 5.625 |

The memo reproduces the shop floor rules: where one owner owns and occupies the entire building, the first-floor rebate is 40%, the second and higher floors and basement 50%. If different owners hold floors, each attracts the ground-floor rate. Basements used exclusively for parking are exempt. For shops and commercial space alike, the rent or lease multiplier is 1.25 on the rented or leased area only.

## Which assessment years does the clarification cover?

The memo addresses the choice of the old policy (applicable before 1 April 2010) or the policy of 11 October 2013 for the period before publication. It refers to the new policy for FY 2013-14 onward. Its conclusion on retrospective application is specific: the 50% commercial rate applies **from FY 2010-11 where the owner opts for the new policy**.

The 3 March 2014 notification, S.O. 38/H.A.16/1994/S.87/2014, added paragraph 2F(xii) on petrol pumps for both A1 and A2 cities. The 21 January 2016 notification, 14/3/2016-3C-II, removed the words "with immediate effect" from the first paragraph of the March amendment. The gazette sheet also carries the corresponding change to S.O.39 under the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973. This removal is the reason the department gives for using the half rate from FY 2010-11.

The Additional Director (Administration) signed for the Principal Secretary, Urban Local Bodies. Endorsement DULB/Tax/2022/16977 of the same date circulated the instructions to all municipal corporation commissioners and district municipal commissioners in Haryana. The 14-page PDF contains the three-page memo, the [2013 notification](property-tax-rates-notification-2013.md), the English and Hindi 2014 amendment, and the 2016 gazette page.

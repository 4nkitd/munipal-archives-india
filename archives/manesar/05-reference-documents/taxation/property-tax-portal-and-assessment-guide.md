# Manesar property tax portal and assessment guide

## Find a property ID and verify the assessment

The Property ID (PID) identifies the property record used for municipal dues. The [Haryana Property Tax Dues Payment and No Dues Certificate (NDC) portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) offers citizen login by mobile number, email ID or PID, new registration, payment, help manuals and application status. Select Manesar when a municipality is requested. A No Dues Certificate concerns outstanding dues; a Lal Dora village register is a list of property records.

After login, properties linked to the registered mobile number or email appear. If yours is missing, search by owner name, mobile number, plot or unit number, or PID. Select **View Details/Self Certify**. Check property details, mobile number, owner details and outstanding dues. Dues include property and fire tax and development charges; the latter also shows whether the property falls in an authorised or unauthorised colony.

If every section is correct, select Yes in each. Enter your Family ID (Parivar Pehchan Patra) and verify the OTP sent to its registered mobile. The [self-certification manual](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/How%20to%20Self%20certify%20Property.pdf) provides an Aadhaar-linked OTP route if there is no Family ID. If neither ID is held, declare that fact and verify the OTP on the property's registered mobile. The manual uses an older portal address; the link above is the one on the municipal page.

## When the assessment is wrong

Select No against the incorrect section. Enter the proposed correction, attach the evidence requested, save the draft and compare the existing and proposed details. For dues, distinguish a wrong charge from a payment that has not appeared; both can be raised together. The manual covers property and fire tax, development charges and colony status, and garbage collection charges. Use **Final Preview and Submit Objection**, choose the application type and complete OTP verification. A saved draft is not a submitted objection.

The [objection manual](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/Verify_Property_Details_and_Submit_Objection.pdf), listed on Citizen Corner on 4 August 2025, gives normal processing as 10 working days without a fee and Tatkaal as 2 working days for ₹2,500. Check the terms displayed at submission. Use the state's [application status page](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ApplicationStatus) to follow the request.

## Rate tables and rebates

The Manesar page describes property tax as municipal revenue payable on residential, commercial, industrial, institutional and vacant property. It shows ₹1, ₹4, ₹6 and ₹7 per sq yd for residential plots up to 300, 301 to 500, 501 to 1,000 and 1,001 sq yd to 2 acres respectively. For offices and multiplexes it prints ₹12 per sq ft up to 1,000 sq ft, but switches to square yards for the ₹15 row. The [2013 gazette](property-tax-rates-notification-2013.md) uses square feet of **carpet area** for both commercial-space bands. It also includes a residential band over 2 acres omitted from the page.

The original notification allowed a 10% rebate for full payment by 31 July of the assessment year. Its arrears relief and the later Lal Dora rebate had historical deadlines. Self-certification verifies the property record; it does not by itself establish eligibility for a particular rebate. For current liability and relief, the [state calculator](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/taxScreen.html) links the corporation policy dated 30 July 2026 and treats the 2013 assessment as the old demand.

## What the ULB Manesar PDF contains

The [ULB-hosted PDF](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/Manesar/Images/e97ba978-236e-40b3-a4d7-560f7ebc4310.pdf) is entry 9 on the municipal taxation index. Its heading reads "Village Lal Dora Harsaru". Created 13 January 2025, it has 16 pages and 566 rows with distinct eight-character PIDs: 565 begin with `1C`, one with `1L`. It lists property and occupancy particulars, not instructions on tax rates, rebates or self-certification. Harsaru and Garhi Harsaru have separate registers.

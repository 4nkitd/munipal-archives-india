# Haryana property tax rates notification of 11 October 2013

## Scope of the notification

Haryana Gazette Extraordinary, 11 October 2013, pages 1841 to 1848, authorised English translation. Notification S.O. 85/H.A.16/1994/S.87/2013 invokes sections 87(3) and 149(1) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 and supersedes S.O. 14/H.A.16/1994/S.87/2013 of 28 January 2013. P. Raghavendra Rao signed for the Urban Local Bodies Department.

Clause 1 names Gurgaon and Faridabad as A1; Ambala, Panchkula, Karnal, Panipat, Rohtak, Hisar and Yamunanagar as A2. Manesar publishes this notification but is not named in either list. Its [property tax overview](https://mcmanesar.in/propertytax) quotes selected A1 figures. For a present assessment, the [state calculator](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/taxScreen.html) identifies this schedule and its amendments as the **old** annual demand and links the corporation policy dated 30 July 2026.

## Residential houses and flats: clause 2A

| Property and area | Annual unit | A1, ₹ | A2, ₹ |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| House plot up to 300 sq yd | Per sq yd, ground floor | 1 | 0.75 |
| 301 to 500 sq yd | Per sq yd | 4 | 3 |
| 501 to 1,000 sq yd | Per sq yd | 6 | 4.50 |
| 1,001 sq yd to 2 acres | Per sq yd | 7 | 5.25 |
| More than 2 acres | Per sq yd | 10 | 7.50 |
| Flat carpet area up to 2,000 sq ft | Per sq ft | 1 | 0.75 |
| 2,001 to 5,000 sq ft | Per sq ft | 1.20 | 0.90 |
| More than 5,000 sq ft | Per sq ft | 1.50 | 1.125 |

For houses under single ownership where the whole building is owned and occupied under that ownership, the first-floor rebate is 40% of the ground-floor rate; the second and higher floors and the basement receive 50%. The printed cross-reference specifies 2A(a)(i) to (iv); it does not mention slab (v). If different owners hold the floors, each floor attracts the ground-floor rate. Basements used exclusively for parking are exempt. For flats, assess common facilities and buildings by area under each use.

## Shops and commercial space: clause 2B

| Area | Annual unit | A1, ₹ | A2, ₹ |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Shop plot up to 50 sq yd | Per sq yd, ground floor | 24 | 18 |
| 51 to 100 sq yd | Per sq yd | 36 | 27 |
| 101 to 500 sq yd | Per sq yd | 48 | 36 |
| 501 to 1,000 sq yd | Per sq yd | 60 | 45 |
| Commercial space up to 1,000 sq ft carpet area | Per sq ft | 12 | 9 |
| Commercial space over 1,000 sq ft carpet area | Per sq ft | 15 | 11.25 |

The shop schedule gives a 40% first-floor rebate and a 50% rebate on second and higher floors and on the basement where the whole building is owned and occupied under single ownership. Separately owned floors each attract the ground-floor rate. Basements used exclusively for parking are exempt. Commercial plots over 1,000 sq yd are assessed as commercial space. Shopping malls, multiplexes and commercial offices are examples of commercial space. For a shop or commercial space rented or leased in whole or part, apply 1.25 times the rate to the rented or leased area.

## Rebates and religious-property exemptions: clauses 3 and 4(v)

An owner may choose one admissible clause 3 rebate.

| Rebate | Eligible property and conditions |
|---|---|
| 100% | Religious properties, orphanages, almshouses, municipal buildings, cremation or burial grounds, dharamshalas, and central or state government educational institutions and government hospitals. |
| 100% | Self-occupied house up to 300 sq yd owned by serving defence or paramilitary personnel, ex-service or paramilitary personnel or their spouse, or families of deceased soldiers, ex-servicemen or former central paramilitary personnel. They must have no other residential house in Haryana, live in the house and let no part of it. The letting condition does not apply to those receiving a pension of ₹1,275 per month or less. |
| 100% | Self-occupied house owned by a freedom fighter or spouse, or a war widow, where they have no other residential house in Haryana, live in it and let no portion. |
| 100% | Vacant plots of at least 1 acre used for horticulture or agriculture. |
| 50% | State government buildings, excluding buildings of boards, corporations, undertakings and autonomous bodies. |

Buildings and land attached to religious properties, including mosques, temples, churches and gurudwaras, qualify for exemption where the institution serves the community at large free of charge and uses all income for religious causes. It may not use income for private religious purposes or for a particular caste or group. A portion used for a non-religious purpose attracts the normal applicable rate.

## Assessment years, arrears and penalties: clauses 4 and 5

The system applied from FY 2010-11. For the period before publication owners could choose the old or new policy. As originally printed, clearing all dues and arrears through FY 2012-13 within 45 days of notification earned a one-time 30% rebate. Excess tax already paid was credited against future liabilities without interest. Paying the full assessment-year tax by 31 July earned a 10% rebate; the original FY 2013-14 window was 30 days from publication.

For previous years' pending arrears, dues or disputes, including court cases, an owner could pay under the system in vogue, whether or not a bill or notice had issued. Payment settled those disputes, dues and arrears without penalty or interest. Otherwise, a wrong declaration attracted a penalty equal to the tax evaded. Late payment attracted 1.5% interest per month or part of a month. A detected wrong declaration attracted that interest in addition to the penalty; penal interest could not exceed the initial liability.

## Amendments enclosed with the 2022 clarification

The [petrol pump clarification](property-tax-petrol-pumps-clarification.md) includes the 3 March 2014 gazette. It added petrol pumps at 50% of the commercial property rate and IT parks, cyber cities and cyber parks at 50% of the commercial-space rate. It added a 100% rebate through 31 March 2016 for Lal Dora properties in villages brought within corporation limits on or after 1 April 2010, and a 50% rebate on flats up to 2,000 sq ft. For clauses 4(ii) and 4(iv) it substituted 31 March 2014 for 10 December 2013. These historical deadlines are not current rebate offers.

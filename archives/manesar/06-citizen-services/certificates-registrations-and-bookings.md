# Manesar certificates, registrations, bookings and permits

## Choose the service before uploading documents

Use the current checklist for your exact service and jurisdiction. The documents below are items to have handy if the portal asks for them, not a substitute for that checklist. For fees, limits, inspections and timelines, follow the instructions on the selected application. Avoid filing under a different city or agency merely because a portal accepts the form.

| Service | Official portal | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birth or death registration and certificate | [Antyodaya-SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Search for the appropriate Birth or Death service. Distinguish reporting an unregistered event, obtaining a copy of an existing registration, and correcting a record. Confirm the local registrar for the place of occurrence. Use the hospital or event record if available, or the registration number and event details for an existing entry. Take any late registration or missing entry to the registrar if the normal workflow does not cover it. Track the application and download the certificate once issued. |
| Marriage registration | [Haryana Marriage Registration](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) | Sign in or register on the dedicated state portal. Follow the form to select the applicable Registrar and jurisdiction, enter the event and both spouses' details, and supply the proof and witness information it requests. Save the acknowledgement; attend any appointment shown, and retrieve the certificate after approval. |
| Dog or pet registration | [Antyodaya-SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Sign in and search for Dog or Pet Registration. Select the competent local body for the owner's address. Prepare owner and pet details and the current veterinary vaccination record where required. Submit, save the reference and use the issued registration or renewal instructions. |
| Community centre booking | [Manesar booking portal](https://booking.mcmanesar.in/) | Select the community centre and date or date range, then choose **Check Availability**. The landing page listed **Naurangpur Vatika** when checked. Review the terms and charges, follow the steps for documents and payment, and keep the confirmation and receipt. Use [Manage Booking](https://booking.mcmanesar.in/manage) to follow an existing booking. Availability alone is not a reservation. |
| Building plan approval | [Invest Haryana](https://investharyana.in/#/) | Follow the Building Plan Approval entry point linked by Manesar. Confirm whether the site falls within the corporation's planning jurisdiction. Choose the correct building use and plot size, prepare the ownership, site and plan documents required for that case, and answer scrutiny remarks against the same application. Approval of a plan is not an occupation certificate. |
| Outdoor media or advertisement permission | [Antyodaya-SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Search for Outdoor Media Management or advertisement rights. Check who controls the proposed site, whether that type of display is permitted, and which scheme or permission applies. Prepare location, dimensions and site consent as required. Obtain the permission before installing the display. |
| Mobile tower approval | [Invest Haryana](https://investharyana.in/) | Select the applicable tower or communication infrastructure service for the site and applicant. Confirm which local or road authority must consent. Prepare site rights, drawings and any structural or safety documents requested. Use the application reference for scrutiny and approval. Road cutting is a separate notified service in the RTS PDF. |
| Water or sewerage connection | [Antyodaya-SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Confirm which agency operates the local line, then search for a new water or sewerage connection and select the responsible provider and correct service. Prepare proof for the premises and any site or service records listed. Keep inspection, payment and approval references. Manesar's footer links water connection to SARAL; the historical RTS PDF's seven-day water/sewerage row names only Gurgaon and Faridabad, so do not assume that row applies to Manesar. |

## Track applications and ask for help

For SARAL services, register or sign in, find the relevant service, complete the requested fields and uploads, then keep the SARAL ID and payment receipt. Use [Track Application](https://status.saralharyana.nic.in/) or the SARAL dashboard to see an application, certificate or appeal. The SARAL helpline is **0172-3968400**, Monday to Saturday, 7 AM to 8 PM, excluding government holidays. For marriage, booking and Invest Haryana, use the status or account area of the portal where you filed.

For a Manesar routing question, call **0124-2012232** or **18001801817**. If an application is refused or late, first check whether a [notified Right to Service entry](right-to-service-timelines.md) covers the exact service. For other unresolved municipal issues, use the [Public Grievances Window](https://mcmanesar.in/node/103).

The link index comes from the [Manesar homepage](https://mcmanesar.in/), including the water connection link in its footer. Portal landing pages were retrieved, not signed-in workflows. The service index is undated and the record date is a verification-year placeholder.

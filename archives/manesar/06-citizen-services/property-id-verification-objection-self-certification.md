# Find, correct and self-certify a Manesar Property ID

## Sign in and identify the right property

1. Go to the [Haryana Property Tax Dues Payment and No Dues Certificate portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). If needed, choose **New User Registration** or **NRI / Foreign National Registration**. The guides mention `ulbhryndc.org` for login, but start at the current government portal linked by Manesar.
2. Sign in with your registered mobile number, email ID or PID as offered by the portal. Enter the OTP sent to your registered contact and select **Login**. Never send an OTP, full Aadhaar number or identity document over a complaint chat.
3. Look at the properties linked to the login. If none are linked, choose **Search Property** or click the search link in the no-property message. Search by owner or occupier name, mobile number, plot or unit number, or PID.
4. For **Search on Map**, select the municipality and colony. The manual shows the colony boundary in orange on satellite imagery. Search by owner or occupier, PID or mobile number. If that does not find the plot, zoom to level **20**, select **Show all Property Markers** and select the marker at the actual address. Compare the property photo and details and note the PID.
5. Open **View Details/Self Certify**. Check each of the four sections: property details, mobile number, owner details and outstanding dues. The dues section covers property and fire tax, development charges and the authorised or unauthorised colony status. Garbage collection dues appear only in some municipalities.

## Correct the record before certifying

Mark **Yes** against correct data and **No** against each item that needs a change. **No** reveals **Update Details**. Use **Save Draft** in each editor. The system returns to **View Details** and puts the existing record beside the proposed change. A saved draft is not a filed objection.

| What is wrong? | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Property details | Choose **No** against **Property Details is Correct?**, open **Update Details**, and enter the correct values. For a floor correction, the manual's screen shows deleting the incorrect row and using **Add Floor** for the corrected entry. Choose whether to update property images; in **Upload Documents related to your objection**, select the applicable document type and choose the file, then save. |
| Mobile number, PPP route | Choose **No** against **Mobile Number is Correct?** and open **Update Details**. Choose the Family ID or Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) route, enter the Family ID, choose **Proceed**, select the family member, choose **Send OTP**, and submit the OTP sent to the PPP mobile. The guide says this updates instantly if the owner names in both records match. |
| Mobile number, municipal scrutiny | Choose to update through a municipal objection. Enter the correct number, attach identification proof, enter remarks and save. The municipality reviews this route. |
| Owner details | Choose **No** against **Owner Details is correct** if the name is wrong or missing. Open **Update Details**, choose the ownership type and **Death Case** or **Non Death Case** as applicable. Use **Add Owner** to enter the correct details; the illustrated editor instructs deleting an incorrect row before adding the corrected owner. Attach the requested proof, add remarks and save. |
| Property tax and fire tax | Select **No** and open **Update Details**. Choose incorrect charges, payment already made but missing from the record, or both. Enter the proposed amounts, attach applicable evidence such as a payment receipt if requested, and save. |
| Development charges or garbage collection dues | Use the corresponding **Update Details** control. With development charges, check and correct the authorised or unauthorised status too. Garbage collection charges are shown only for some municipalities. |

Use the portal's [List of Documents](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments) and the requirements shown for the fields you change. Upload readable proof to the relevant field, verify that the draft shows the attachment and proposed correction, and keep your own copies.

## Submit the objection and track the decision

1. Choose **Final Preview and Submit Objection** after saving all corrections. Compare every proposed value with your records.
2. The published guide offers **Objection (Normal)** at no fee with a **10-working-day** resolution timeline, and **Objection under Tatkaal** at **Rs 2,500** with a **2-working-day** timeline. Check the portal's current fee and timeline before choosing or paying.
3. When asked for a PPP ID, select **Yes** if you have one, enter it, select the family member and verify the registered-mobile OTP. If you have no PPP, select **No** and answer the Aadhaar question. If you have Aadhaar, enter the number and verify the OTP sent to its registered mobile. If you have neither, select **No** and certify that you have neither.
4. Select **Submit Objection** and complete the final OTP sent to the registered mobile or the new number submitted for update. The objection then goes to the department for correction. Keep the application reference, PID, submission date, and any Tatkaal receipt.
5. Use [Check Application Status](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ApplicationStatus) or look for **My Objections** after sign-in, as shown in the manual. Check the outcome and any remarks. If the objection is approved, reopen the PID and confirm the corrected values before using the record or self-certifying. If it does not appear or a discrepancy persists, follow up with the Manesar taxation branch using the application reference and PID.

## Self-certify only when all details are correct

Log in, find the property and select **View Details/Self Certify**. Review property, mobile and owner details, tax and other dues, and colony authorisation. Choose **Yes** for each correct section or line. If any item is wrong, use the objection process instead of declaring it correct.

1. If you have a Family ID, enter it, use the OTP sent to the mobile registered with that ID and select **Verify Details**.
2. If you do not have a Family ID but have Aadhaar, enter the Aadhaar number, verify the OTP sent to the Aadhaar-linked mobile and select **Verify Details**.
3. If you have neither, select **No** and certify that you have neither. Enter the OTP sent to the mobile registered with the property, then select **Verify Details**.

The manual says successful verification self-certifies the property. Reopen the PID to check the result. Self-certification is distinct from municipal review of an objection and from issuance of a No Dues Certificate.

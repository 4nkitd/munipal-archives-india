# Public grievances and the 24 Hours Challenge

## Make a report that can be acted on

Provide the ward if known, sector or village, exact address, landmark, date first noticed, and one clear photo where appropriate. Give a reachable phone number. Specify what needs fixing, such as a blocked sewer outside a named building or a missed collection at a particular pickup point. Save the complaint ID, submission date, status, and closure message. Keep one ID per issue rather than opening duplicates.

Call Manesar's toll-free **18001801817**, call the helpdesk at **0124-2012232**, or email **cmc-manesar@ulbharyana.gov.in**. The [Haryana ULB Manesar contact page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Manesar/10669) also lists general WhatsApp **7840001817**, missed-call callback **9873353224**, and sanitation WhatsApp **9773797487**. Ask for a reference number when reporting by phone or message. For an in-person follow-up, the [municipal contact page](https://mcmanesar.in/contact-us) lists CP-09, 3rd Floor, Cyberwalk, Sector 8, IMT Manesar, Gurugram.

## File and track through Haryana GRS

1. Open the [Grievance Redressal System](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) and use the **Citizen** tab. Sign in with your mobile and OTP, or use [Create an account](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/SignIn/Register).
2. Select the concerned city/body and municipal service, such as sanitation or streetlights. Describe the issue and its exact location, attach evidence if the form offers it, and submit.
3. Keep the grievance ID. Use [Check Grievance Status](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/Login/checkstatus) to see what action the department reports. For help, the state GRS lists **1800-8900-929**, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. The citizen app is called **eSamadhan DULB Haryana Citizen**.

## Use the 24 Hours Challenge for a listed issue

The [Citizen Corner](https://mcmanesar.in/services/citizen-corner) lists the campaign on 5 November 2026. The [secure complaint form](https://complaint.mcmanesar.in/) is for listed issues within Manesar municipal limits. Choose one problem, attach at least one clear photo (maximum three), and pin the exact spot with GPS or the map. Fill in the address and at least a sector/village or ward, check any values filled from the pin, and add your name and 10-digit mobile number. The landmark and extra description are optional. Read and accept the terms, then submit. Keep the complaint number and due time. The same page has **Track your complaint**.

| Issue | Target from registration |
| --- | --- |
| Big garbage heap | 24 hours |
| Tree pruning | 48 hours |
| Major pothole, sewer overflow, or non-working streetlight | 72 hours |

The portal routes the report by category and location. Its terms say closure requires photo verification and approval, and an unapproved closure may be reopened. Reports outside Manesar may be forwarded. A late resolution does not automatically entitle the complainant to Rs 5,000. The campaign terms require a review finding employee negligence and approval by the competent authority. The targets are separate from statutory Right to Service timelines.

## Escalate using the original complaint ID

Check the portal for action notes and closure evidence. If the work is incomplete or the stated time has passed, contact the municipal helpdesk or the complaint channel with the existing ID, date, location, and a current photo. Ask which agency has the case and request a review of the closure if the issue remains. If you need to use a separate portal, cite the old ID and explain why you are escalating.

| Portal listed by the Public Grievances Window | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [CM Window](https://cmharyanacell.nic.in/) | Haryana Chief Minister's Public Grievance Redressal Cell. An option for an unresolved state or municipal grievance. |
| [CPGRAMS](https://pgportal.gov.in/) | Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System. Use for a grievance within the relevant participating government's jurisdiction. |
| [MC Manesar ICCC Call Center](https://services.gmda.gov.in/) | Integrated Command and Control Center grievance services linked to GMDA. Useful when routing between Manesar and a GMDA service. |
| [Haryana ULB GRS](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | State urban local body complaint filing and tracking. |
| [Harpath](https://mharisadak.haryana.gov.in/) | Haryana Road Complaint & Feedback Portal, for agency routing of road issues. |
| [Nagar Darshan](https://nagardarshan.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Haryana citizen grievances and civic services portal. |

For a refusal or delay on a notified service, use the [Right to Service appeal route](right-to-service-timelines.md) rather than assuming a general grievance preserves the appeal deadline. Use [RTI](right-to-information-rti-guide.md) to obtain records, not to order a repair. The record date is the Citizen Corner campaign listing date. No test complaint was submitted.

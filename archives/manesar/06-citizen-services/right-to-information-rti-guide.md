# Right to Information in Manesar: request records, fees and appeals

## Find the officer before filing

The Manesar RTI page links the Act, a Haryana fee amendment and an extract on paying fees. **None of the three PDFs identifies a Manesar SPIO, Assistant SPIO or First Appellate Authority by name, department or designation.** The [Haryana Directorate of Urban Local Bodies PIO list](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList) names the Joint Commissioner as SPIO and Commissioner as First Appellate Authority for several corporations, but does not list Manesar in that table. The [Manesar officers' directory](https://mcmanesar.in/officers-directory) is a general directory, not an RTI appointment order. Do not use it to infer which officer holds an RTI appointment.

| Role | Who handles what |
| --- | --- |
| State Public Information Officer (SPIO), Section 5(1) | Receives and decides information requests for the concerned public authority. Must give reasonable assistance to applicants. |
| State Assistant Public Information Officer (SAPIO or APIO), Section 5(2) | Receives applications or appeals at the designated level and forwards them promptly. Add five days when computing the Section 7(1) response period for an application lodged through an Assistant PIO. |
| First Appellate Authority (FAA), Section 19(1) | The officer senior in rank to the SPIO in the same public authority, designated to hear first appeals. |

Ask the helpdesk at **0124-2012232** or **cmc-manesar@ulbharyana.gov.in** for the current designation order, the officer for your subject and postal details. Address a paper request to the **State Public Information Officer, Municipal Corporation Manesar**, CP-09, 3rd Floor, Cyberwalk, Sector 8, IMT Manesar, Gurugram **122052**, subject to confirmation of the receiving desk. Keep the inward number or delivery proof. An email asking the helpdesk for routing is not evidence that an RTI application was formally received.

## Ask for specific records

Use [Haryana RTI Online](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/) if it accepts the relevant authority. The portal says to choose the related headquarters or directorate for divisional or district-level information. If you cannot identify the correct option, confirm it with the department or file on paper. The [State Information Commission forms page](https://cicharyana.gov.in/forms) has Model Form A and the Haryana Rules and amendments.

Write in English, Hindi or the official language of the area. State the agency, location, document and period and ask for a copy, certified copy or inspection. For example: "Please provide certified copies of the work order, measurement-book entries and completion certificate for [road and work], [date range]. If the records are held by another public authority, please transfer the relevant part under Section 6(3) and inform me." An applicant need not give a reason or unnecessary personal details. An SPIO must assist a person who cannot make the request in writing. Transfer to another public authority under Section 6(3) is due as soon as practicable and no later than five days after receipt.

## Haryana application fee and copying charges

Haryana's 18 March 2016 amendment, Notification No. **5/4/2008-1AR**, substituted Rule 5 of the Haryana Right to Information Rules, 2009. A request ordinarily should not exceed **500 words**, excluding annexures and the applicant's and SPIO's addresses. It cannot be rejected solely for exceeding that count.

| Item | Charge in the published Haryana fee sources |
| --- | --- |
| Section 6(1) application | Rs 10 |
| Created or copied A4 or A3 page | Rs 2 per page |
| Larger paper | Actual cost of the paper |
| Information on floppy or diskette | Rs 50, as specified for each of those legacy media |
| Priced printed publication | Fixed publication price; Rs 2 per page for a requested extract or page |
| Inspection | First hour free; Rs 5 for each subsequent hour or fraction |

The municipal fee extract lists payment to the SPIO by cash against receipt, bank draft, Indian Postal Order or treasury challan. Treasury head: **0070 Other Administrative Services / 60 Other Services / 800 Other Receipts / 86 Fee under the Right to Information Act, 2005**. Corporations may use their own accounts, so confirm the payee with Manesar before dispatching a draft or postal order. Use the official payment gateway for an online filing. Keep the receipt.

Under Section 7(3), the SPIO must send the calculation of any additional fee and review details. The fee extract specifies Model Form B for this notice. The time between dispatch of the notice and payment is excluded from the Section 7(1) calculation. Applicants determined to be below the poverty line are exempt under Section 7(5); supply the evidence asked for by the official filing channel. Section 7(6) requires information to be provided free if the authority misses the applicable statutory time limit.

## Deadlines, appeals and complaints

1. **Initial decision (Section 7):** ordinarily within **30 days** of receipt. A request involving a person's life or liberty is due within **48 hours**. Where Section 11 third-party consultation applies, the officer must make a decision within **40 days** of receipt. Silence beyond the applicable period is deemed refusal. A rejection must state the reasons, appeal period and appellate authority particulars.
2. **First appeal (Section 19(1)):** file with the FAA within **30 days** after the response period expires or after receiving the decision. Include the application, proof of receipt and fee, any response, the reason for appeal and the relief sought. Use Haryana RTI Online for the supported online first-appeal workflow. The FAA should decide in **30 days**, extendable to **45 days** in total for reasons recorded in writing. The FAA can accept a late filing for sufficient cause.
3. **Second appeal (Section 19(3)):** file with the State Information Commission, Haryana, within **90 days** of when the first appeal should have been decided or when the decision was received. The [state filing portal](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/) directs applicants to submit second appeals by hand or post, in triplicate, under Rule 6. Include copies of the RTI request, ID proof, fee details, first appeal, proof of filing, and replies or orders if received. The Commission may admit a late appeal for sufficient cause.
4. **Section 18 complaint:** the Commission can inquire where no officer has been appointed, an officer refuses to accept a request or appeal for forwarding, access is denied, no response arrives, the fee is unreasonable, or information is incomplete, misleading or false. If you seek disclosure of the records themselves, follow the Section 19 appeal route. Manesar's portal points to the SIC; consult the [Commission website](https://cicharyana.gov.in/) for its current complaint instructions rather than assuming the state RTI application portal accepts second appeals.

The Commission's [contact page](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/Contactus.aspx) lists **Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Suchana Bhawan, IP-8, Sector 3, Panchkula, Haryana 134112**, and **cic@hry.nic.in**.

## Section 4: what the municipality should publish without a request

Section 4(1)(a) requires the authority to catalogue and index records for access and, within a reasonable time and subject to resources, computerise appropriate records. Section 4(1)(b) required publication within 120 days of enactment and annual updates. Its disclosure subjects are:

| Clauses | What should be published |
| --- | --- |
| (i) to (iv) | Organisation, functions and duties; powers and duties of staff; decision process and supervision and accountability channels; norms for discharging functions. |
| (v) to (viii) | Rules, instructions, regulations, manuals and records used; document categories; arrangements for public consultation or representation; boards, councils, committees and whether their meetings or minutes are public. |
| (ix) to (xiii) | Officer and staff directory; monthly remuneration and compensation system; budgets, plans, proposed spending and disbursements; subsidy administration, allocations and beneficiaries; recipients of concessions, permits and authorisations. |
| (xiv) to (xvii) | Information held electronically; public access facilities and reading-room or library hours where maintained; names, designations and other PIO details; other prescribed information. |

Section 4(1)(c) requires relevant facts to be published when formulating important policies or announcing decisions affecting the public. Section 4(1)(d) requires reasons for administrative or quasi-judicial decisions to be given to affected persons. Sections 4(2) to (4) call for regular proactive disclosure, including online, in an accessible, cost-effective manner that considers local language. Check the municipal notices, budgets, bye-laws, officer directory and procedure pages first. If a required item is not posted, ask for the latest disclosure or the underlying record by name. This list describes statutory duties; it does not certify that Manesar has published all 17 items.

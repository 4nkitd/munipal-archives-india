# Haryana Right to Service timelines and appeals for municipal services

## When the clock starts and how to appeal

[RTS 1](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/RTS%201.pdf) is Haryana Act No. 4 of 2014, Notification **Leg. 7/2014**, assented to on **22 March 2014** and published in the Extraordinary Gazette on **26 March 2014**. The posted text incorporates amendments, including Haryana Act No. 23 of 2016 effective 16 September 2016. Section 3 provides for notifying services, timelines and officers. Section 2(b) defines the days referred to as the time limit as working days.

1. **Apply to the Designated Officer.** Under Sections 4 and 5, the clock starts when the officer or authorised receiver gets a requisite complete application. Get an acknowledgement. If they refuse to receive it or issue the acknowledgement, submit it with an explanation to the First Grievance Redressal Authority (FGRA). In that case the clock starts when the FGRA receives the complete application. Rejection reasons must be recorded and communicated.
2. **First appeal, Section 6.** File with the FGRA within **30 days** of rejection or expiry of the notified time limit. The FGRA has **30 days** from receipt to dispose of the appeal. It can direct the officer to deliver the service within seven working days or another period it specifies.
3. **Second appeal, Section 7.** File with the Second Grievance Redressal Authority (SGRA) within **60 days** of rejection by the FGRA or expiry of the time given in an order accepting the first appeal. The SGRA should decide within **30 days** of receipt as far as possible, and may direct provision within seven working days or another specified period. Either appellate authority may admit a late appeal for sufficient cause. Under Section 9 the SGRA can impose penalties and may order compensation of up to **Rs 1,000** to the appellant.
4. **Revision, Section 10.** If aggrieved by the SGRA order, apply to the [Haryana Right to Service Commission](https://haryana-rtsc.gov.in/) within **90 days** from the order. The Commission may entertain a late revision for reasonable cause; the posted law gives it **30 days** from receipt to decide. Attach the application, acknowledgement, relevant service entry, rejection or delay details and appeal orders.

[SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) describes automatic escalation for covered applications, first to the FGRA after breach of the notified timeline, then to the SGRA and the Commission if the respective authority has not made a final decision in 30 working days. Check your appeal status rather than assuming automation applies to a paper or other non-integrated application.

## Haryana statewide ULB schedule in RTS 2

[RTS 2](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/RTS%202.pdf) is a 14-page ULB schedule extract, not the Act or necessarily today's complete statewide list. The municipality labels both PDFs **26 March 2014**, but the schedule itself contains a **27 July 2018** omission note. Numbers below are the ULB service numbers in the PDF; its overall serial numbers are 60 through 90. EO means Executive Officer, DMC means Deputy Municipal Commissioner, DC means Deputy Commissioner, SDM means Sub-Divisional Magistrate, SDO(C) means Sub-Divisional Officer (Civil), ULB means Urban Local Bodies and NBC means National Building Code.

# Sanitation, household waste collection and the live sweeping feed

## Door-to-door waste collection

Ask the ward sanitation team or [Sanitation WhatsApp](https://wa.me/919773797487) at **9773797487** for the designated collector and pickup schedule if your address is new or collection has changed. Keep wet and dry waste separate and ask how to hand over sanitary, special-care, bulky or construction waste. The published sweeping page does not provide doorstep pickup routes, fees, or schedules. A sweeper passing your road does not confirm a household pickup.

For a missed collection, give the date and usual pickup time, ward, sector or village, exact address, and a landmark. Say whether one address, an entire lane, or a housing society is affected. If the issue is instead waste dumped on a street, report where the heap is and how much of the road it blocks. Save the complaint ID. The [24 Hours Challenge](https://complaint.mcmanesar.in/) lists a 24-hour target for a **big garbage heap**, not every missed doorstep collection. The [state Right to Service extract](right-to-service-timelines.md) separately lists two working days for removal of solid waste from streets or roads, with the Chief Sanitary Inspector as the corporation's designated officer.

Bulk waste generators have separate duties. See Manesar's [bulk waste management empanelment notice](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/MCM_RFP_Bulk_Waste_Generators.pdf) before assuming ordinary household arrangements apply to a housing society or other large generator.

## Mechanical road sweeping

1. Open the municipality's [Live Sweeping Machine Feed](https://mcmanesar.in/live-sweeping-machine-feed-0) page. It publishes login details for the viewer. Use the details on the official page rather than an old copy.
2. Follow **Click here to view live sweeping machine feed** to the [TMYV login screen](https://www.tmyv.in/SiteLogin.aspx?ReturnUrl=%2f). The municipal page and destination login screen were retrieved. No login or sweep coverage was tested.
3. If a road was missed, note the affected stretch, direction and landmarks, date and time, and any machine identifier shown. Report the street itself to sanitation. If a vendor screen is unavailable, use Haryana [GRS](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) or the municipal helpdesk.

## Use the GIS map to identify the location

The municipality's [GIS Portal](https://gis.mcmanesar.in/) is listed on the [municipal homepage](https://mcmanesar.in/). Its landing page offers **View on Map** and lists assets such as roads, drains, manholes, streetlights, public toilets, community centres, water supply lines, storm-water drains and construction-and-demolition waste sites. Use the map location or asset ID, when available, in your complaint. The GIS portal is separate from the sweeping feed and complaint tracking; a map entry is not a service request or confirmation of responsibility.

The sweeping page is undated. The record date uses the verification year as a schema placeholder. Sanitation WhatsApp is from the [official Haryana ULB Manesar contact page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Manesar/10669); the named officers are from the [Manesar officers' directory](https://mcmanesar.in/officers-directory).

# HEWP contractor registration and e-tendering in Manesar

## Where to register and where to submit the bid

The [Haryana Engineering Works Portal](https://works.haryana.gov.in/) (HEWP) issues contractor IDs and handles contractor registration. The [NIC Haryana eProcurement portal](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app) is where bidders obtain the bid documents and submit bids. The [Manesar tenders page](https://mcmanesar.in/tenders-0) links directly to NIC. HEWP says notices that it processes appear on its [live tenders page](https://works.haryana.gov.in/LiveTender.aspx/1000) the day after publication on NIC.

1. Create a contractor login through [HEWP New User Sign-up](https://works.haryana.gov.in/frmRegistration.aspx). The form asks for the agency's constitution and addresses, PAN and GST details, and the applicant's contact details. The portal sends an OTP to the stated mobile number. Creating a login is free, according to the [HEWP FAQ](https://works.haryana.gov.in/FAQs.aspx).
2. Apply for contractor registration in HEWP if claiming the earnest money deposit (EMD) exemption. A contractor ID alone is not registration approval or an exemption. Check the registration and enlistment rules listed in [HEWP Downloads](https://works.haryana.gov.in/HEWP_Login/downloads.aspx) for the applicable contractor class, documents and deposits. The portal lists the 2024 registration-cum-enlistment rules.
3. Use Online Bidder Enrollment and the [digital signature certificate instructions](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app?page=DSCInfo&service=page) on NIC. Search for the tender by ID or under Haryana Government, Urban Local Bodies, MC Manesar. Download the Detailed Notice Inviting Tender (DNIT), bidding document, bill of quantities (BOQ) and any corrigenda for that call.
4. Submit the technical and price parts online in the covers specified by the notice. The example DNIT does not accept physical submissions. Follow its fee and EMD instructions, use the published BOQ, and confirm receipt before the closing time.

## Earnest money, fees and bid validity

The [23 October 2026 Bhangrola pond DNIT](https://www.tenderfiles.com/tenderdocuments/102026/23/94d91c17-53cc-4219-a0f3-1849f3e140e2?FileName=Tendernotice_1.pdf), NIT `20261023-09-20-434532989`, illustrates the process. The [Haryana Common Bidding Document](https://works.haryana.gov.in/home/docs/Common_Bid_Document.pdf) also describes the declaration procedure in clause 16. Always use the requirements for the specific call.

| Bidder or payment | What the October DNIT says |
| --- | --- |
| Contractor ID, not registered | Pay the EMD online and submit the mandatory documents in clause 3.3. |
| Registered contractor claiming exemption | Clause 16.1 requires a contractor ID, HEWP registration and the one-time deposit. Generate the bid-specific Earnest Money Declaration Form in HEWP and upload it with the bid as provided in section 7. |
| Unregistered contractor or society | The DNIT specifies ₹1,64,331 for contractors and ₹82,165.50 for societies, a 50% concession on this tender. The [HEWP FAQ](https://works.haryana.gov.in/FAQs.aspx) also cautions that a contractor registered in a lower class who is technically eligible to bid in a higher class pays EMD as an unregistered contractor. |
| Tender document fee | ₹10,000, non-refundable, paid online. The DNIT describes a document-fee exception for eligible repeat bidders when the tender is recalled because only one bid was received or qualified on the first call. |
| E-service fee | The DNIT states ₹1,180 online in favour of the Society for IT initiative fund for e-Governance, payable at Chandigarh. This is separate from the document fee and EMD. |
| Bid validity | The DNIT key dates and notice state 270 days from bid closing, with a listed expiry of 31 July 2027. The standard instruction in clause 15.1 of the same PDF states a 120-day minimum. Other Manesar tenders list different validity periods. |

The [eProcurement listing copy](https://govttenderhub.com/tender/466178-rejuvenation-of-pond-in-bhangrola-village-near-shiv-mandir-in-ward-no) for `2026_HRY_556023_1` shows ₹1,000 processing and ₹1,71,704 EMD, different from the DNIT figures above. These are separate source readings, not amounts to add together. If the live NIC record, bidding document and corrigenda do not agree, ask the authority named in the tender for clarification before paying or submitting.

The DNIT says the authority checks the document fee and EMD or declaration first. It then opens the technical bids online. Only offers from bidders meeting the qualification criteria proceed to the financial opening, whose date is announced separately.

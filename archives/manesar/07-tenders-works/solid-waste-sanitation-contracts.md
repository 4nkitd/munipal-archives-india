# Manesar solid waste and sanitation procurement, 2024 to 2026

## October 2026 bulk waste generator empanelment

The [municipal website](https://mcmanesar.in/rfp-solid-waster-management-services-bulk-waste-generators-mcm) calls the document an RFP and lists it on [What's New](https://mcmanesar.in/services/whats-new) on 14 October 2026. The [municipal PDF](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/MCM_RFP_Bulk_Waste_Generators.pdf) calls it an Expression of Interest (EOI). The [eProcurement listing copy](https://www.tendershark.com/details/haryana-tender/haryana-government/95171b01-21fd-431d-bd04-dc27fe530407) records tender ID `2026_HRY_555512_1`, reference `BWG`, publication at 17:00 on 30 October 2026, and bid closing at 11:00 on 16 November 2026. It lists a ₹1,000 tender fee, ₹1 fixed EMD and 270-day bid validity. These bid-stage amounts are separate from the EOI's security and empanelment fee.

The EOI allows agencies to apply for one or more of seven categories: collection and transportation, wet waste, dry waste, material recovery facility operation, sanitary and special-care waste, horticulture waste, or integrated management. It calls for segregated handling, authorised downstream facilities and digital records. The eligibility section sets a minimum average annual turnover of ₹10 lakh over the last three financial years, certified by a chartered accountant, and requires applicable authorisations, capacity and experience.

The initial empanelment runs three years from the empanelment letter. A successful agency owes a ₹1 lakh non-refundable empanelment fee and must provide ₹5 lakh in bank guarantee or security under sections 21 and 22. Empanelment is not an award of municipal work and guarantees no payment, waste volume or clients. A bulk waste generator and its chosen agency sign their own written agreement before service begins. The agency submits a copy to the corporation within 15 days.

### Bulk waste generator criteria in the EOI

The PDF cites the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 and lists three alternative thresholds. It also describes registration, source segregation, decentralised processing of wet or horticultural waste as applicable, annual returns and Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility documentation.

| Criterion | Threshold in the October EOI |
| --- | --- |
| Building floor area | 20,000 square metres or more |
| Water consumption | 40,000 litres per day or more |
| Solid waste generation | 100 kg per day or more |

For earlier records, the indexed [Haryana ULB SBM Documents page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Manesar/10996) lists a 20 June 2024 BWG empanelment notice and a 23 January 2025 list of empanelled agencies. The index link returned an error on direct access during verification. Haryana ULB also hosts [earlier draft terms](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/c32b2352-fb41-4af9-b325-58669d1ae28b.pdf) that use the 2016 rules. Those records are not proof of empanelment under the October 2026 EOI.

## Door-to-door collection and road sweeping

These procurements were for municipal operations, separate from the EOI for services contracted directly by bulk waste generators. The short Zone 1 machinery tender covered 80 days and included waste lifting at garbage-vulnerable points. The citywide July 2025 notice covered door-to-door collection, segregation and transport to the processing site for 1,825 days. The October 2025 sweeping notice covered both mechanized and manual work; its official listing has a cancellation corrigendum.

## Legacy waste and construction debris

Legacy municipal waste removal, construction and demolition (C&D) waste transport, and civil works at waste sites are distinct scopes. The Nainwal notice covers preparing a processing site, including its perimeter and ground. The C&D transport RFP covers collecting and delivering material to a processing site. Neither notice by itself shows that the facility was operational. [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/mcm-clears-1-1-lakh-tonnes-of-legacy-waste-reclaims-5-acres/articleshow/126457131.cms) identifies the Sector 8 dumping site as IMT Sector 8.

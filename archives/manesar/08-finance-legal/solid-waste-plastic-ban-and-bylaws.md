# Solid waste, plastic restrictions and civic compliance in Manesar

## Legal timeline and source segregation

The Solid Waste Management Rules are national rules applicable in Haryana, not a separate enactment called the Haryana Solid Waste Management Rules. The Union environment ministry notified replacement Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 on 27 January 2026, effective 1 April 2026. Its parliamentary reply of 30 March 2026 confirms that these supersede the 2016 rules.

| Period | Segregation framework |
| --- | --- |
| 2016 framework | Three streams: biodegradable/wet, non-biodegradable/dry and domestic hazardous waste |
| From 1 April 2026 | Four streams: wet, dry, sanitary and special-care waste |

Wet waste includes food and biodegradable kitchen material. Dry waste includes recyclable paper, plastic, metal and glass. Sanitary waste includes used diapers and sanitary pads, securely wrapped before handover. Special-care waste includes items requiring separate handling, such as expired medicines, CFLs, paint containers and batteries. Follow the applicable specialist rules and authorized collection arrangements for excluded or separately regulated streams. Industrial process waste, biomedical waste and e-waste must not simply be mixed into municipal bins.

No waste generator should burn, bury or dump waste in roads, drains, water bodies or open spaces. Keep horticultural material and construction debris separate. Segregation at source remains the generator's responsibility even when a contractor collects the waste.

## Bulk waste generators and societies

Under the 2016 definition, bulk waste generators included specified government, commercial and institutional premises producing an average exceeding 100 kg/day. Rule 4 also imposed duties on RWAs/market associations, hotels/restaurants and gated communities/institutions above 5,000 square metres, including segregated collection, authorized recycling and processing biodegradable waste within premises as far as possible. The 100 kg threshold was not a reason for a smaller hotel to ignore its separate Rule 4 duties.

The 2026 rules introduce Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility. A PIB implementation briefing identifies criteria including floor area of at least 20,000 square metres, daily water consumption of 40,000 litres or generation of at least 100 kg solid waste/day. The historical phrase "exceeding 100 kg" should not be substituted for the replacement threshold. Obtain the current Gazette text and MCM identification/registration notice for a particular establishment.

MCM's homepage lists an RFP titled "RFP for Solid waster management services to Bulk waste generators in MCM", posted 14 October 2026. This verifies procurement activity, not that a bidder has been appointed or all BWGs are compliant. The 2017 MoHUA guidance hosted by GMDA remains useful historical implementation guidance, but its model notices, blank penalties and indicative categories are not a Manesar notification.

## User charges and enforcement amounts

The 2016 rules required generators to pay user fees specified in local-body bye-laws. A lawful demand needs the applicable local schedule, category, billing period and authorized collecting agency. No authenticated current MCM household/BWG tariff table was obtained for this record. It therefore gives no flat monthly charge and makes no blanket claim that payment to a builder either cancels or duplicates a municipal liability.

An HSPCB status report filed before the NGT reproduces Haryana-wide directions prohibiting unauthorized dumping. It states environmental compensation of Rs 5,000 for a first non-bulk incident and Rs 10,000 for a further incident; for bulk dumping, Rs 25,000 first and Rs 50,000 thereafter. These are the cited dumping directions, not an SUP fine table or the total possible liability under every law. The report reproduces a public notice dated 4 November 2024 and attributes the quoted directions to the NGT order of 26 July 2024 in O.A. 458/2023. Check later orders and the actual demand before relying on those historical amounts.

## Single-use plastic restrictions

The national prohibition effective 1 July 2022 covers identified low-utility, high-littering items, including plastic-stem ear buds, balloon sticks, flags, candy/ice-cream sticks, thermocol decorations, plates, cups, glasses, listed cutlery, straws, trays and stirrers. It also includes specified wrapping films and plastic/PVC banners below 100 microns. Plastic carry bags below 120 microns have been prohibited since 31 December 2022. This is not a claim that every plastic product is banned.

ULBs enforce waste-generator/retail compliance, while pollution-control authorities have their prescribed manufacturing, registration and waste-processing roles. HSPCB lists a 1 July 2024 order adopting CPCB environmental-compensation guidance for Plastic Waste Management Rules violations. Seizure, local fines and environmental compensation arise under their respective instruments. No verified MCM-specific SUP penalty schedule was retrieved, so this archive does not repeat an assumed Rs 500 or Rs 5,000 spot fine, or an outdated generic imprisonment maximum.

## Construction waste, pets, cattle and encroachments

Construction debris requires separate storage and authorized transport/processing. Do not leave malba on footpaths, green belts, roads or in drains. The Environment (Construction and Demolition) Waste Management Rules, 2025, notified as G.S.R. 219(E) on 2 April 2025, replace the 2016 C&D framework from 1 April 2026. They add registration, waste-management planning and producer-responsibility mechanisms. Confirm the authorized facility and collection arrangement with MCM or the asset-owning authority before booking disposal; this record does not identify an unverified local dumping site.

MCM's official homepage provides Dog/Pet Registration through SARAL Haryana. That verifies a service route, not a specific fee, breed restriction or renewal period. Seek the operative licence/registration conditions before applying. Report stray-cattle hazards and insanitary animal-waste conditions to the municipal helpdesk at 0124-2012232; no local impounding or release-fee schedule was established.

Street vending, occupation of public land and obstruction are separate civic matters. Waste disposal does not confer permission to occupy a footpath. Encroachment action depends on land ownership, statutory powers and due process. Provide a location, photograph and nearby landmark with a complaint, without assuming every IMT road or green belt belongs to MCM.

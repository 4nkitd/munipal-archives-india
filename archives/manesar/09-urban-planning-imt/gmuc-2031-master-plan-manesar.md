# GMUC Final Development Plan 2031 and the Manesar planning area

## Plan identity and authoritative references

The Final Development Plan 2031 AD for Gurgaon-Manesar Urban Complex was notified by Haryana's Town and Country Planning Department as No. CCP(NCR)/FDP-2031/GGN/2012/3541 dated 15 November 2012. The official GMDA metropolitan-area notification dated 12 August 2017 expressly cites that plan and notification number in Schedule II. This official cross-reference is the frontmatter source; its own date is 2017, while the record date identifies the original 2031 plan.

The 2031 horizon is a planning year, not a promise that every road, sewer, water line or housing sector would be complete by then. "Gurgaon" remains in historical notification titles even though current usage is Gurugram. The urban complex is larger than Municipal Corporation Manesar. Neither its plan boundary nor a numbered sector automatically establishes MCM ward jurisdiction or infrastructure ownership.

For legal use, obtain the original final notification, drawing and zoning regulations from DTCP, followed by all applicable final amendments and the relevant sector plan. The original final-plan drawing is identified as DTP(G) 2104/2012 dated 9 November 2012 in the reproduced notification text.

## Original land-use allocation

The original 2012 plan's area schedule is reproduced in GMDA's IIT Roorkee infrastructure report of March 2024. It gives the following values in hectares:

| --- | --- |
| Residential | 16,021 |
| Commercial | 1,616 |
| Industrial | 4,613 |
| Transport and communication | 4,428 |
| Public utilities | 608 |
| Public and semi-public | 2,027 |
| Open spaces | 2,928 |
| Special zone | 114 |
| Defence land | 633 |
| Total of above headings | 32,988 |
| Existing town | 406 |
| Village abadies | 478 |
| Grand total in schedule | 33,872 |

These are historical plan allocations, not surveyed current developed areas. The same infrastructure report has an inconsistent total in a separate summary table; this archive uses the explicit land-use schedule, not that conflicting summary cell. The report and GMDA's revision notice describe a projected population of 42.5 lakh for the complex, not Manesar's census population.

Residential, commercial and industrial zones have different permitted uses. Public/semi-public land covers institutional functions, while public utilities accommodate infrastructure. Open spaces and green belts are not a reserve of freely buildable land. Agriculture and natural-conservation controls also require attention in the zoning regulations, even where not included as separate urban land-use totals in this table.

## Manesar, New Gurugram sectors and transport corridors

For inquiries across the New Gurugram belt commonly described as Sectors 76 to 95B, locate the exact sector and any letter suffix on the official drawing and then obtain the detailed sector plan. "76 to 95B" is a geographic search shorthand, not a claim that every intervening number has the same use or municipal status. Sector 95, 95A and 95B are not interchangeable. IMT numbering must also be distinguished from Gurugram's city-sector numbering.

The complex includes Manesar's industrial and adjoining residential development. Sector-level layouts may contain several uses, roads, village abadi pockets and reserved strips. The plan is not proof that a builder holds a licence or that an industrial plot permits residential conversion.

The Northern Peripheral Road corridor is associated with the Dwarka Expressway; the Southern Peripheral Road is a distinct southern connection. KMP forms a separate western regional expressway corridor. Plan alignments, transport reservations and green belts must be distinguished from the as-built highway and its access-control limits. Do not infer permission to open a driveway merely because a site fronts an expressway.

## Amendments and the 2025 draft

GMDA's official revision public notice describes proposals involving Sectors 36, 36B and 37B for the Global City mixed-use project, transport/open-space interchange, and correction of an IMT Sector M3 pocket. That document invites public comments on proposed revisions; it is not by itself proof that every proposal became operative.

A Haryana Gazette notification dated 13 June 2025, No. 06/35/2025-4CI, publishes a draft further amendment to the 2012 final plan. It proposes replacing the drawing reference with DTP(G) 2710/2025 dated 3 January 2025 and changing the area schedule, among other matters. The draft's industrial allocation of 4,202 hectares must not silently replace the original 4,613-hectare figure in an account of the 2012 plan.

The draft allows thirty days for objections/suggestions. It directs municipal-limit representations to the Director General, Urban Local Bodies, and those outside municipal limits to GMDA's Chief Executive Officer. This was the draft consultation process, not a currently open submission deadline. No final disposal of that draft was established in this review.

## How to check a site

Ask the MCM planning branch, whose official directory lists Sh. Rajender Sharma, for the competent authority and operative plan extracts. Supply village, khasra number, sector, site coordinates and any colony licence or CLU number. Request the zoning/sector plan, approved layout, licence conditions, building sanction and relevant final amendment. Property-tax payment is not a substitute for those permissions.

If a map is unclear, obtain a certified extract rather than tracing a broker's image. Neither the historical plan nor this summary confirms title, acquisition status, services handover or permission for a particular building.

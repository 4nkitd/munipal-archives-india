# Manesar jurisdiction guide: MCM, HSIIDC, GMDA and planning authorities

## Read jurisdiction at asset level

Being inside MCM does not make every road, pipeline, industrial plot or builder society a municipal asset. Three questions must be answered separately: is the site within the notified municipal boundary, which body regulates the activity, and who owns or has taken over maintenance of the asset? A sector name alone does not settle all three.

MCM's General Documents page lists formation on 24 December 2020 and an amended-area notification of 16 December 2022. Contemporary reporting describes 30 original villages and later additions including Sectors 83, 84, 88 and 88A. MCM's City Info page instead says 29 villages. Consequently this archive does not certify the requested shorthand "30+4 villages". Four added sectors are not four verified added villages. The Gazette schedules and ward map, rather than arithmetic on website summaries, control any boundary decision.

This is a 2026 reference compilation; the frontmatter date is a year-only placeholder, not an agency-transfer order date.

## Municipal and industrial-estate responsibilities

MCM supplies municipal services within its notified area, including property-tax administration, sanitation and municipal water/drainage/road functions. Its departments also handle municipal streetlights, parks, planning and enforcement. For regularized colonies and New Gurugram handovers, confirm the notification and dated takeover list before saying a specific internal road or sewer is MCM-maintained. Regularization, inclusion in municipal limits and physical asset takeover are different events.

HSIIDC develops industrial estates and IMTs and manages industrial-plot allotment and estate-management matters. The official 2014 NCRPB water-supply environmental report hosted by ADB identifies HSIIDC as the IMT Manesar water-system operator. It distinguishes residential Sector 1 from industrial, institutional, commercial and technology-park uses elsewhere. Calling all of IMT Sectors 1 to 8 exclusively industrial would be inaccurate.

Times of India reported on 16 November 2025 that MCM and HSIIDC had signed an agreement assigning sewer, water, roads, streetlights and green-belt maintenance in IMT Sectors 1 to 8 to HSIIDC. This is reported handover evidence, not a reproduced signed agreement. An official Haryana eTender published on 8 January 2026 independently identifies HSIIDC as the procuring body for sewage-line maintenance in Sectors 5, 6 and 7, Tender ID 2026_HBC_495815_1. Route estate infrastructure complaints to HSIIDC first and ask for the applicable asset schedule when disputed.

## Metropolitan, planning and highway responsibilities

GMDA's official Infrastructure page identifies its two infrastructure divisions as responsible for dry infrastructure and wet infrastructure, including roads, water supply, sewerage and drainage. Its metropolitan work includes master water transmission, trunk sewer/drainage networks and designated master roads. Mobility and smart-city projects include metropolitan bus coordination and CCTV infrastructure. Local distribution pipes, internal colony drains and individual streetlights are not automatically GMDA assets.

HSVP handles its allotted plots and assets retained in its developed sectors. DTCP administers planning, colony licensing and related controls under the relevant State laws; developers remain responsible for services they have not lawfully handed over. MCM's planning branch handles matters within its delegated municipal remit. An official GMDA CLU rejection dated 14 January 2021 for a Sector 90 site specifically cites its inclusion in MCM under the 24 December 2020 notification as the reason GMDA could not process that case. Do not use metropolitan infrastructure coverage as proof that GMDA is the competent CLU authority everywhere.

NHAI is the appropriate starting point for NH-48 and the Dwarka Expressway national-highway assets, subject to package boundaries and concession arrangements. KMP is a separate Haryana/HSIIDC expressway project, not a road to assign automatically to NHAI. Official environmental and NCRPB project records identify HSIIDC as the KMP project agency.

## For this issue, contact this authority

| Issue | First authority or service route | Evidence to include or check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property ID, municipal property tax | MCM taxation branch; Haryana property portal | PID, address, demand and disputed entry |
| Missed municipal waste pickup or public dumping | MCM sanitation branch | Exact pickup point, route/date, location photograph |
| IMT plot allotment, transfer or estate dues | HSIIDC estate office | Plot number, allotment letter, estate demand |
| IMT Sectors 1 to 8 road, water, sewer, light or green belt | HSIIDC maintenance/estate office first | Asset location and relevant handover schedule |
| Internal road, park or light in a municipal village/regularized colony | MCM engineering branch if vested/taken over | Ward, street and takeover record |
| Internal services in a builder society not handed over | Developer/maintenance entity; DTCP for licence obligations | Colony licence, service agreement and complaint record |
| Master water pipeline, trunk sewer, master drain or designated sector road | GMDA relevant infrastructure division | Pipeline/road identifier, sector junction and asset owner |
| HSVP plot account or retained HSVP infrastructure | HSVP estate/engineering office | Allotment and takeover status |
| Building plan, CLU or zoning question | MCM planning branch or DTCP/other competent authority, according to site and delegation | Khasra, municipal boundary, licence/CLU and site plan |
| City-bus service or metropolitan mobility | Bus operator/GMCBL and GMDA mobility | Route number, stop, date/time |
| Public CCTV infrastructure fault | GMDA smart-city project channel; police for crime | Camera location; dial 112 for emergency |
| NH-48 or Dwarka Expressway carriageway defect | NHAI/project concessionaire | Highway, direction, chainage or nearest interchange |
| KMP carriageway/toll/maintenance issue | HSIIDC KMP project office/operator | Toll plaza, direction, chainage and receipt |
| Electricity supply fault | DHBVN, not municipal streetlight maintenance | Consumer number and outage location |

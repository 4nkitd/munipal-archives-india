# G.O.Ms.No. 19 constituting Mangalagiri Tadepalli Municipal Corporation

## Areas declared and effective date

On 23 March 2021, the Andhra Pradesh Municipal Administration and Urban Development Department issued G.O.Ms.No. 19. Its notification declares, with immediate effect, the areas covered by Mangalagiri Municipality and Tadepalli Municipality as a larger urban area named Mangalagiri Tadepalli Municipal Corporation. It cites section 3(1) of the Andhra Pradesh Municipal Corporations Act, 1994, read with rule 2 of the 1994 rules on specifying larger urban areas. It directs publication in an extraordinary issue of the Andhra Pradesh Gazette dated 23 March 2021.

The order cites Ordinance No. 17 of 2020, dated 31 December 2020, for prior inclusion of 11 gram panchayats in Mangalagiri Municipality and 10 in Tadepalli Municipality. G.O.Ms.No. 19 does not name those panchayats or reproduce a village boundary schedule. The notification refers to the existing municipal areas, not the entire mandals bearing those names.

## Municipal corporation and capital development authority

MTMC is a civic body. Its [municipal portal](https://mangalagiri.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/mangalagiri/home) handles municipal services such as property tax, while the [Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority](https://crda.ap.gov.in/apcrdav2/views/home.aspx) separately publishes the Amaravati master plan, capital-city development permissions and land-pooling information. An APCRDA planning or land-pooling record does not establish municipal jurisdiction or a ward boundary. The municipal office is beside the RTC Bus Stand, Mangalagiri, according to the corporation's contact page.

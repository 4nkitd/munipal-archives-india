# Mangalagiri Tadepalli ward reservations for the 2026 municipal election

## Seat reservation schedule

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The Guntur District Collector published the ward reservations for MTMC in District Extraordinary Gazette No. 184 on 31 October 2026. The [APSEC phase II statistics](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/Vital%20Statistics%20-%20Phase%20II%20Elections%20-%2023%20ULBs.pdf#page=4) independently confirm 62 wards and list MTMC as a corporation in Guntur district.

| Category in the gazette | Seats | Example ward |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Scheduled Tribes, women and general combined | 2 | 18 |
| Scheduled Castes, women and general combined | 13 | 1 |
| Backward Classes, women and general combined | 16 | 46 |
| Women, general | 16 | 56 |
| Unreserved | 15 | 62 |

The gazette records reservations, not ward boundary descriptions. An entry in the corporation's [City Map](https://mangalagiri.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/mangalagiri/gis-map) is a ward secretariat record and cannot substitute for delimitation.

# Administrative sanction for the Weavers Bazar municipal shopping complex

## Work and source of funds

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

G.O.Rt.No. 588 dated 25 October 2021 grants administrative sanction of Rs. 390.00 lakh for the municipal shopping complex, called Weavers Bazar in the abstract and Chenetha Bazar in the order. It places the project at survey number 273, Old Bus Stand, in Ward 28 as identified in 2021. It cites municipal council resolution No. 98 dated 23 June 2021.

The order names Municipal General Funds as the source and requires plan approval from the competent authority. It directs the Commissioner and Director of Municipal Administration to take further action. The sanction does not establish that the work was tendered, awarded or completed, and the site's historic ward number is not a crosswalk to the 2026 election wards.

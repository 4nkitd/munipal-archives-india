# Mangaluru corporation and the other urban bodies of Dakshina Kannada

## Corporation scope

The district's Municipal Administration page lists one city corporation, two city municipal councils, three town municipal councils and eight town panchayats. Mangaluru became a city corporation in 1980. The district describes it as having 60 wards and more than six lakh residents, without identifying a population reference year. The count applies to the city corporation; the other urban local bodies in the district have separate administrations.

| Type in the district directory | Local bodies |
| --- | --- |
| City corporation | Mangaluru |
| City municipal councils | Ullala, Puttur |
| Town municipal councils | Moodabidri, Bantwal, Someshwara |
| Town panchayats | Mulki, Kotekar, Vittla, Belthangady, Sullia, Kadaba, Bajpe, Kinnigoli |

The district publishes the corporation-wide ward count, but does not give individual ward names, numbers or boundaries on this page.

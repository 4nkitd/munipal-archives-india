# Mangaluru municipal venue booking notice

## Where to request a booking

The city's integrated payment portal advertises venue booking alongside property tax, water charges, trade licences and municipal shop rent. A notice on the same portal gives a specific instruction for Ambedkar Bhavan, Town Hall and open grounds: visit the Revenue Section at the Mangalore City Corporation head office, Lalbhag. The contact block spells the locality “Lalbagh” and gives MG Road, Mangaluru, Karnataka 575003.

For these three venue types the notice gives an office destination. It does not state the rental tariff, deposit, documents required, cancellation terms or an effective date. A property-tax receipt or municipal shop-rent receipt belongs to a different service on the portal and does not confirm a venue reservation.

# Mangaluru property tax self-assessment and payment manual

## Search and self-assessment

The manual asks the taxpayer to enter a 10-digit mobile number and verify a six-digit code sent by SMS. Select the ward name and search by door number. The matching property record shows the property ID, owner, address and assessment-year payment history. New properties can be submitted online, but the manual requires review and approval by MCC staff.

The calculation form distinguishes converted and non-converted land. If land was surrendered for road widening under a relinquishment deed, the form asks for the area surrendered. It also records tenure, floor details, khata number and survey details. The self-assessment summary can be downloaded before payment.

## Payment evidence

Choose online payment or print a challan and pay at the bank using the account details on it. The manual lists cards, net banking, wallets, UPI and bank transfer among its online options. The downloadable property tax receipt appears after successful payment; the self-assessment form and unpaid challan are distinct documents.

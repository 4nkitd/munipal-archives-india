# Mango Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and office

## Municipal area

The state describes Mango as a suburb connected to Jamshedpur by two bridges across the Subarnarekha. The postal address uses Jamshedpur. [UDHD notification 5842 of 28 December 2023](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/Other/Notifications.aspx) concerns the declaration of Jamshedpur as an industrial township; it is not a notification constituting Mango.

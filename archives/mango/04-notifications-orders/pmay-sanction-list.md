# Mango PMAY(U) sanctioned beneficiary list, notice 1154

## Housing notice register

Mango lists notice 1154, dated 9 July 2021, under the title “Sanctioned list of PMAY(U) Beneficiary of Mango Municipal Corporation”. A separate entry, notice 1582 of 1 July 2021, concerns the newly sanctioned Phase 1 detailed project report for Vertical 4 in 2021-22. The register does not state that payments were made.

# Project Aamdani food-cart expression of interest for Mango and neighbouring bodies

## District notice

The District Planning Office listed 16 July 2025 as the start date and 25 July 2025 as the end date. The project concerned food carts for transgender people and women. The notice names JNAC, Mango Nagar Nigam and Jugsalai Nagar Parishad separately.

# Formation and original boundary of Municipal Corporation Mathura-Vrindavan

## State notification

Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Section 7 issued notification `1799/9-7-17-8(SeemaVistar)/2016` at Lucknow on 12 May 2017. It cites Article 243-Q(2) of the Constitution and section 3(1) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959. The Governor specified the scheduled smaller urban areas of Municipal Council Mathura and Municipal Council Vrindavan as one larger urban area and provided that the corporation for that area would be known as Municipal Corporation Mathura-Vrindavan.

The corporation's [history page](https://nnmvonline.in/history.aspx) dates the earlier Cabinet decision to 9 May 2017. The date used here is the one printed on the formation notification, not the Cabinet date or an independently verified Gazette publication date.

## Reading the original boundary schedule

The [state order index](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/GO_main_menu-2017.html) links the scanned notification. The English translation begins on PDF page 10.

| Part | What the schedule contains |
|---|---|
| 1 | Mathura-side revenue villages and land parcels, listed by khasra number or range, with parcel counts, hectares and remarks. |
| 2 | Vrindavan-side boundary text, including a reference to the 22 December 1956 notification and a description using boundary pillars and landmarks. |
| 3 | The combined outer boundary, described in four directions. The closing paragraph says it includes the full areas of the then-existing Mathura and Vrindavan municipal councils. |

Use the schedule, not the town names or a district map, to read the original limits. The [district's urban local body list](https://mathura.nic.in/municipal-corporation/) identifies Kosi Kalan, Barsana and Govardhan as separate local bodies. The formation order is not a ward delimitation or proof of the present-day boundary after any later amendments.

# State Election Commission ward list for Mathura-Vrindavan

## What the commission publishes

From the linked Urban Local Body/Ward List page, select the ward details view (`वार्डों का विवरण`) and use **Export to Excel**. In the export, the Mathura district entries for the municipal corporation labelled `2-वृन्दावन-मथुरा` run consecutively from ward 1 to ward 70. The leading `2` in the body label is not a ward number. The commission's [district summary](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/summary.aspx) separately reports one Nagar Nigam and 70 Nagar Nigam wards for Mathura district.

The export does not state the effective date or provide street limits, polygons, zone assignments or councillor names. The year in the portal route is not used as a publication date. The corporation's “Wards & Zones” PDF link returned 404. Its [ward-wise door-to-door collection list](https://nnmvonline.in/Forms/DoortoDoorWardWiseDriverDetails.pdf) is an operational roster, not a complete ward register or a delimitation notification.

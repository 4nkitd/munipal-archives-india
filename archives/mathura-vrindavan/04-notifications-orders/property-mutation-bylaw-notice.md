# Public notice inviting objections to the property mutation bylaw

## Inspection and objections

The Additional Municipal Commissioner's notice is dated 27 October 2025 on the signed scan. The municipal notice page separately gives 28 October 2025 as its website publication date. The notice cites section 213 and clauses (42) and (49) of section 541 of the 1959 Act.

The notice gave 30 days from publication to submit written objections or suggestions on working days, with late submissions inadmissible. It named the corporation website, the notice boards of the four zone offices and the Tax Department office as places to inspect the text. A copy could be obtained on payment of the prescribed fee at the Tax Department office in the Bhuteshwar zone.

This is a consultation notice. It does not establish a current fee, a final bylaw publication date or an effective date. No final Gazette text was verified.

# Online services and municipal control room

## Where to start

| Service | Official entry point |
|---|---|
| House, water and sewer tax | The corporation's property- and water-tax links both lead to the [municipal billing portal](https://mathura.upptax.org/), which lists these three taxes and provides a Pay Tax menu. |
| Property mutation and assessment | The same tax portal provides Apply for Mutation, Fill Self Assessment, Re-Assessment and Track Application. The city links directly to the [mutation login](https://mathura.upptax.org/public/login?t=am). |
| Birth and death certificates | The separate certificate links in the municipal service menu redirect to the [corporation citizen login](https://nnmvonline.in/CitizenLogin.aspx). |
| Complaints | The municipal service menu lists complaint filing and complaint status. The filing link redirects to the corporation citizen login. |

The [corporation contact page](https://nnmvonline.in/contactus.aspx) gives the Bhuteshwar Jalkal Compound office address and office mailbox. The [district directory](https://mathura.nic.in/directory/nagar-nigam-animal-control-room/) independently labels the same landline `0565-2503632` as the Nagar Nigam control room. No payment or application was submitted in checking these links.

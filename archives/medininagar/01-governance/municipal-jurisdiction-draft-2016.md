# Medininagar draft corporation jurisdiction, May 2016

## The proposal and the objection period

Notification 2262, issued on 22 April 2016, appeared in Jharkhand Gazette extraordinary issue 289 on 5 May 2016. It proposed making the existing Class B Medininagar Nagar Parishad and 15 surrounding revenue villages into a larger urban area under the Jharkhand Municipal Act, 2011. It gave affected people 30 days from gazette publication to submit objections or suggestions to the Palamu Deputy Commissioner's office. An [earlier draft, notification 1961](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/Handlers/NoticesHdler.ashx?id=13052016060522AM.pdf), dated 29 May 2015 and gazetted on 15 July 2015, instead described nine village or area entries.

## What the later district sources establish

The [Palamu urban local body directory](https://palamu.nic.in/urban-local-body/) identifies Medininagar as the district's only municipal corporation and lists Bishrampur, Hussainabad, Chhatarpur and Hariharganj as separate bodies. The [2026 district election index](https://palamu.nic.in/municipal-election-2020-3/) also treats the five bodies separately. Neither source supplies a final revenue-village schedule. Do not use the draft list to determine whether a particular property is within the corporation. The [municipal contact page](https://udhd.jharkhand.gov.in/ULB/Medininagar/ContactUs.aspx) places the office near Katchari, Daltonganj, Jharkhand.

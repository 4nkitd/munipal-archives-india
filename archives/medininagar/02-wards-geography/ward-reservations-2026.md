# Medininagar ward seat reservations for the 2026 municipal election

## Form 3 schedule

Extraordinary Palamu District Gazette issue 93/25–26, dated 16 December 2025, published the reserved and unreserved territorial constituencies under Rule 9 of the Jharkhand Municipal Election and Election Petition Rules, 2012. The schedule identifies the body as II Palamu / 01 Medininagar Nagar Nigam (Class B). It covers councillor seats in wards 1 to 35 and separately marks each seat as women or other.

| Seat category | Women (महिला) | Other (अन्य) |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled Caste | 1, 4 | 2, 3 |
| Scheduled Tribe | None | 5 |
| Extremely Backward Class I | 20, 26, 32, 33 | 11, 19, 27, 29 |
| Backward Class II | 34, 35 | 9, 10 |
| Unreserved | 6, 7, 8, 14, 16, 17, 22, 25, 31 | 12, 13, 15, 18, 21, 23, 24, 28, 30 |

Seventeen seats are marked in the women column. The [district's final voter list](https://palamu.nic.in/final-voter-list/) independently indexes wards 1 to 35 and their booths. Its [final candidate index](https://palamu.nic.in/final-candidate-list-ward-commissioner/) separately labels Medininagar wards 5, 23 and 29 as uncontested. The corporation homepage's figure of 26 wards does not match these district election sources. Form 3 is a record of seat reservations, not ward boundaries, election results or the mayoral reservation.

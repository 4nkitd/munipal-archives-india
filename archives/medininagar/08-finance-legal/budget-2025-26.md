# Medininagar Municipal Corporation budget estimates for 2025–26

## Major account heads

The BUD-4 estimation sheet compares 2025–26 estimates with 2023–24 actuals, 2024–25 actuals through December 2024 and the 2024–25 budget estimates. These are the 2025–26 estimate-column figures as printed.

| Line in BUD-4 | Printed estimate |
|---|---:|
| Revenue receipts | 13,64,47,708.00 |
| Capital receipts | 54,43,25,000.00 |
| Total receipts | 68,07,72,708.00 |
| Revenue expenditure | 16,12,54,028.65 |
| Capital expenditure | 47,55,25,000.00 |
| Total expenditure | 63,67,79,028.65 |

The detailed BUD-1 schedule identifies holding tax as the only tax line with a 2025–26 estimate, printed as 7,00,00,000.00. It also gives estimates for rental income and service charges. Those figures are budgeted collections, not the fees payable by an individual resident.

The BUD-4 header reads "Amt. In Lakhs," but the portal's Amount field is 636779028.00, numerically close to the expenditure total as printed, without multiplying it by 100,000. This record preserves the printed numbers without converting them and does not reconcile the 0.65 difference. The portal entry does not show a publication date.

# Meerut Nagar Nigam jurisdiction and municipal office

## Corporation, district and cantonment

The [UP Directorate of Local Bodies](https://localbodies.up.nic.in/listnn.htm) lists Meerut as a Nagar Nigam in Meerut district. The corporation identifies the U.P. Nagar Nigam Act, 1959 as the basis for its duties. It lists public street and drain cleaning, drain maintenance, public toilet cleaning, garbage removal and park maintenance among those duties.

The [district administrative setup](https://meerut.nic.in/administrative-setup/) lists three tehsils: Meerut, Mawana and Sardhana. These are not corporation wards. The [district municipal directory](https://meerut.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) identifies Nagar Palika Parishad Sardhana and Nagar Palika Parishad Mawana separately. The [UP State Election Commission turnout table](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ULBPolling2023.aspx) likewise separates Meerut Nagar Nigam from the district's other urban local bodies. [Meerut Cantonment Board](https://meerut.cantt.gov.in/) is a separate civic authority under the Ministry of Defence.

## Main office

The [municipal contact page](https://meerutnagarnigam.com/contact-us.htm) gives the address as near Ghanta Ghar, Kaiser Ganj Road, Meerut 250002. It lists 0121-2515133, nnmee@nic.in and customer care at 1800-180-3090.

These sources identify the authority but do not establish a parcel-level boundary, current corporate area or founding notification. District area and population should not be used as corporation figures.

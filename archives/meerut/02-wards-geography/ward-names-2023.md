# Meerut corporation ward names in the 2023 election

## Where to find the list

In the State Election Commission's Winner Result List 2023, select `नगर निगम पार्षद` for the post and `मेरठ` for the district. The resulting 90 ward rows all identify the local body as `मेरठ`. The year in this record is the election year, not an inferred publication date. The [commission's turnout table](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ULBPolling2023.aspx) records Meerut Nagar Nigam in phase 2 with 45.68% total turnout.

The full set of number/name pairs is in `archives/meerut/wards.json`. Examples below show why phase and directional qualifiers matter.

## What the list does not establish

The results establish election-time ward names and winners, not current officeholders, legal boundaries, or the ward of a particular house. No final delimitation map or later revision was verified. Ward names are not treated as exhaustive lists of included colonies, villages or sectors.

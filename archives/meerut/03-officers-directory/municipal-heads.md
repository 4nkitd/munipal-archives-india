# Meerut Nagar Nigam municipal heads and office contacts

## Published municipal leadership

The first page of the corporation's officer list and its [homepage](https://meerutnagarnigam.com/) agree on these five entries. Neither source establishes appointment dates or ward assignments.

| Office | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Municipal Commissioner | Saurabh Gangwar |
| Additional Municipal Commissioner | Lavi Tripathi |
| Additional Municipal Commissioner | Pankaj Kumar |
| Additional Municipal Commissioner | Pramod Kumar |
| Assistant Municipal Commissioner | Sharad Kumar Pal |

The homepage names Harikant Ahluwalia as mayor. The [UP State Election Commission](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/NewWinnerList2023.aspx) records him as the 2023 Meerut mayoral winner under `नगर निगम महापौर`. The [UP Directorate of Local Bodies](https://localbodies.up.nic.in/listnn.htm) also lists him in its 2023 elected mayors table, but that table names Amit Pal Sharma as commissioner. The corporation's own two listings take precedence here as a published municipal snapshot, not as a verified appointment order.

## Public office channels

For official correspondence, the [corporation contact page](https://meerutnagarnigam.com/contact-us.htm) lists 0121-2515133 and nnmee@nic.in. Individual mobile numbers are not reproduced. The municipal Mayor / Parshads' Profile page does not provide a usable current councillor directory.

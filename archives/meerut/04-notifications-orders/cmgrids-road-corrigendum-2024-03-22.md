# Gandhi Ashram to Tejgarhi road tender corrigendum

## Revised tender dates

The [corporation homepage](https://meerutnagarnigam.com/) identifies the work as beautification and line shifting on the road from Gandhi Ashram Chauraha to Tejgarhi Chauraha. The Office of the Chief Engineer's corrigendum, dated 22 March 2024, changes the schedule of the CMGRIDS tender notice published on 16 March 2024.

| Event | Revised date and time |
| --- | --- |
| Tender documents available on the e-tendering portal | 30 April 2024, noon |
| Pre-bid meeting | 10 May 2024, 3:00 pm at the URIDA office, Lucknow, or online |
| Deadline for uploading tender documents | 10 June 2024, 3:00 pm |
| Technical bids open | 10 June 2024, 4:00 pm |

The corrigendum directs readers to the municipality and UP e-Tendering portal for terms and later amendments. These dates are historical. No later corrigendum, award or completion was verified.

# Meerut property tax, certificates and municipal complaints

## Published service routes

| Service | Where the official sources direct residents |
| --- | --- |
| Property tax, new property registration and mutation | The corporation labels these as separate links to the same property-tax host. Its [Property Tax Department page](https://meerutnagarnigam.com/property-tax-department.htm) lists assessment, tax collection, name changes and property transfer among its duties. The tax host returned HTTP 403 during verification, so no payment or application route is confirmed. |
| Birth and death registration | The municipal registration menu points to the Civil Registration System. Its [login link](https://crsorgi.gov.in/web/index.php/auth/login) redirected to the Civil Registration System login on `dc.crsorgi.gov.in`. The Directorate of Local Bodies separately links to birth and death certificate verification on [its homepage](https://localbodies.up.nic.in/). |
| Licences and water connections | The corporation points both services to Uttar Pradesh's [e-NagarSewa portal](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/home). No eligibility rules, fees or submission were verified. |

## Grievances

The [corporation grievance page](https://meerutnagarnigam.com/online-public-grievances-redressal.htm) describes registration through e-NagarSewa, but the old direct form returned HTTP 404 when checked over HTTPS. The [municipal contact page](https://meerutnagarnigam.com/contact-us.htm) lists customer care at 1800-180-3090. The state's [Jansunwai portal](https://jansunwai.up.nic.in/), also linked by the [district election office portal](https://meerut.nic.in/deo-meerut/), provides complaint registration and tracking. No complaint was filed to test routing or response time.

# Meerut Nagar Nigam FY 2023-24 budget at a glance

## Proposed receipts and expenditure

Page 2 of the scanned budget is headed `मूल आय-व्ययक 2023-2024 एक दृष्टि में`. The figures below are the FY 2023-24 proposal columns, not audited outturns. Figures are in ₹ lakh.

| Account | Proposed receipts | Proposed expenditure |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Revenue | 40,616.30 | 42,376.00 |
| Capital | 18,150.00 | 22,690.00 |
| Suspense | 810.00 | 920.00 |
| Subtotal as printed | 59,576.30 | 65,991.00 |

The printed opening balance is ₹38,620.66 lakh; the proposed closing balance is ₹32,205.96 lakh. The document prints ₹98,196.96 lakh as the grand total on each side after including those balances. The three expenditure lines add to ₹65,986.00 lakh, ₹5.00 lakh less than the printed subtotal. The table above preserves the source rather than correcting it.

## Date and scope

The same page distinguishes FY 2021-22 actuals, FY 2022-23 revised proposals, and actuals from 1 April 2022 through 31 January 2023. FY 2023-24 is the budget period; no publication date was verified. The [municipal homepage](https://meerutnagarnigam.com/) separately lists an audited balance sheet for FY 2022-23. This record does not establish the latest approved budget.

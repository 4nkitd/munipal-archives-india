# September 2025 additions to Mahesana city limits

## Schedule to GV-352 of 2025

The department issued notification GV-352 on 25 September 2025. The Gazette published it on 29 September in Part IV-A, Extra No. 134, pages 134-2 and 134-3. The preceding schedule in the same issue concerns Gandhidham.

Mahesana entries 1 to 10 are Tavdia, Dela, Ucharpi, Virampura, Detrojpura, Rampura, Kukas, Rupal (Kukas), Hebuva and Shobhasan. Entry 11 includes **all revenue survey numbers of Lakhvad**, including the numbers already in the January schedule. The notification states that government land within the scheduled areas does not vest in the corporation.

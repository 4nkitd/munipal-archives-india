# Solid waste management text in Mahesana's 2026 public health notices

## What the corporation posted

The [notifications index](https://mahesanacity.in/notifications) labels the entry Public Health Bylaws 2026 in Gujarati. The attachment itself is titled *The Solid Waste Management Bye-Laws, 2026 for Mahesana Municipal Corporation*. Chapter 1 covers waste generators, public premises and contractors engaged in collection, transport, processing or disposal of municipal solid waste.

## Adoption and charges

Page 2 instructs urban local bodies to adopt schedules for user charges, environmental compensation, delegated authorities, forms and notices by resolution before notification. The linked listing does not supply an adoption resolution or commencement date. It therefore does not establish that a particular charge is payable.

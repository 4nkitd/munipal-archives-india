# Birth and death registration and certificate copies in Mahesana

## Registration and certificate fees

The department says registration within 21 days of the event is free. Forms and guidance are available at the head office, ward offices and civic centres. The department directs online certificate applications to Gujarat's eOlakh portal.

| Certificate request | Published charge |
|---|---:|
| First copy within one year | Free |
| Additional certified copy within one year | ₹70, including ₹20 administration charge |
| Copy after one year | ₹90, including ₹20 administration and ₹20 computer charges |

Certificates are available in Gujarati and English. The department distinguishes these copy charges from fees for late registration.

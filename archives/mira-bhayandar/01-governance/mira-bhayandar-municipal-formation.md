# Formation and municipal scope of Mira Bhayandar

## Municipal formation

The municipal history records three milestones:

| Date | Change |
| --- | --- |
| 12 June 1985 | Five gram panchayats were combined to form the Mira Bhayandar municipality. |
| 23 January 1990 | The municipality added the Raimurdhe, Dongri, Uttan and Versova gram panchayats. |
| 28 February 2002 | The municipality became a municipal corporation. |

The same page reports an area of 79.40 km² and a 2011 Census population of 809,378. These are municipal profile figures, not a legal boundary description or a current population estimate. The predecessor municipality's founding date is distinct from the corporation's formation date.

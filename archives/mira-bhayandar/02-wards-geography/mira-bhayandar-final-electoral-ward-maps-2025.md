# Mira Bhayandar final electoral ward maps for the 2025-26 election

## Final maps and the election count

The municipal index has a separate final map link for every numbered ward from 1 through 24. The [Election Department](https://mbmc.gov.in/mbmc/election-department) lists both draft and final ward formation; use the final index and [final notification](https://www.mbmc.gov.in/assets/uploads/file-9488.pdf) for this election, not the draft maps or the 2017 election section. The final notification did not yield searchable text; this record does not transcribe its boundaries or assign a publication day to the index.

The [Maharashtra State Election Commission's final results table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) records 24 wards, 95 seats, 434 candidates and results for all 95 seats in the 2025-26 general election.

## Electoral wards versus municipal offices

The numbered electoral wards are not the same as MBMC's six numbered ward committees. The [ward office directory](https://mbmc.gov.in/en/mbmc/ward-offices) gives office addresses, such as Ward Committee No. 2 at Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Bhavan in Bhayandar West. An office address is not a boundary or proof that an address belongs to an electoral ward.

# Mira Bhayandar property tax lookup and payment

## Look up a bill

Search with the property number and sub-code or use the owner-name search. The form has a ward committee filter and a CAPTCHA. Check the property details on the resulting bill before choosing Pay Now. The portal lists net banking, debit or credit cards and UPI. Its FAQ says a mobile number and email address are required at payment.

The portal cites Chapter VIII, Rule 30 of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act and states that property tax is payable in advance in two half-yearly instalments, on 1 April and 1 October. Use the bill for the actual amount payable; the portal notes that revised assessment, changes of use or added construction may generate a supplementary bill.

## Corrections and objections

The [Property Tax Department](https://mbmc.gov.in/mbmc/property-tax) handles assessment objections, changes in use or area, and property transfer requests. Those requests change the assessment or account; paying the existing bill does not make the change.

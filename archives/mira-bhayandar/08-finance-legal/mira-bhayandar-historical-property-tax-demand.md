# Mira Bhayandar property tax demand and collection, 2018-19 to 2021-22

## Historical series

These are the figures in the Tax Department's total demand and collection table. All amounts are in ₹ crore. The table heading refers to 2018 to 2021, but the table also includes a row for 2021-22.

| Financial year | Demand | Collection | Arrears |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2018-19 | 203.11 | 113.07 | 90.04 |
| 2019-20 | 246.16 | 127.75 | 118.41 |
| 2020-21 | 232.01 | 174.94 | 57.07 |
| 2021-22 | 274.00 | 160.01 | 113.99 |

The page contains separate tables for entertainment tax and open-land tax. The figures above come from the multi-year demand and collection table, not those tables, and are not current arrears or an individual property's balance. MBMC also keeps a separate [budget archive](https://mbmc.gov.in/mbmc/d-budget), with main, fire and transport budget entries for 2025-26. A budget entry is not a collection total.

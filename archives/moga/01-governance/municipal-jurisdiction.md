# Moga corporation and the district’s other local bodies

## Which body covers the city?

The [district directory](https://moga.nic.in/public-utility-category/corporation-municipalities-nagar-panchayat/) lists Municipal Corporation Moga and Improvement Trust Moga separately. It also lists Badhni Kalan, Fatehgarh Panjtur, Kot Ise Khan and Nihal Singh Wala as nagar panchayats. Moga town is the district headquarters. The district formation date of 24 November 1995 in the municipal portal’s introduction refers to the district, not the corporation.

The [May 2026 gazette](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Gazette_Notification_of_Election.pdf) names Moga in the municipal corporation column of Annexure I, printed page 2333. The election schedule identifies the body but does not describe its perimeter.

# Mohali elected ward members gazetted in June 2026

## Election and source scope

Notification No. **SEC/ME/SAM(CME-RN)/2026/881**, published on 6 June 2026 in the Punjab Government Gazette Extraordinary, pp. 2627–2630, notifies the elected members for Municipal Corporation Mohali wards 1 to 50 under section 71 of the Punjab State Election Commission Act, 1994. The ward return is signed by the Deputy Commissioner-cum-District Election Officer, S.A.S. Nagar, on 29 May. The [13 May election gazette](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Gazette_Notification_of_Election.pdf), Annexure I, lists the same corporation as “SAS Nagar” with 50 wards. It set the poll for 26 May, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., and counting for 29 May.

The [SEC final result sheet](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Municipal_Corporation_Final_Result_Sheet.pdf) shows all 50 Mohali results declared: AAP 27, INC 12, SAD 4, independents 4, BJP 3 and BSP 0. The ward table below preserves the gazette spellings with capitalization normalized. It groups independent candidate labels, including their symbols, under “Independent,” and uses “Women” for the source's Woman/Women variants. The scanned corporation schedule was read visually. The district PDF contains separate municipal notices after the corporation pages; those are not part of the table. Residential addresses are omitted, and the record does not establish ward boundaries or subsequent changes in office.

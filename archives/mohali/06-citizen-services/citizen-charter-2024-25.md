# Mohali citizen charter 2024–25: services and complaints

## Service standards

The charter names the municipal office as Municipal Bhawan, Sector 68, S.A.S. Nagar. The cover is marked **2024–25** but does not give an exact publication date. Section 2.3 expresses the standards in working days. These are entries in that edition, not measurements of municipal performance.

| Service | Working days |
| --- | ---: |
| New water or sewerage connection | 7 |
| Change of title in water or sewerage bill | 7 |
| Approval of water or sewerage disconnection | 7 |
| Street light not working, service standards table | 10 |
| Residential or commercial building-plan sanction | 30 |
| Sanction of change of land use | 60 |
| Collection and disposal of solid waste | 2 |
| Issue of a current-year birth or death certificate | 2 |
| Fire safety NOC | 30 |
| Property ID assignment, assessment or collection | 12 |
| Issuance or renewal of trade licence, section 343 | 12 |

The table identifies Junior Engineer, A.C.E. and C.E. roles for water and sewerage. It names the local registrar and Joint Commissioner roles for current-year birth and death certificates. For the property-tax entries it names Clerk, Inspector and Superintendent.

## Filing and following up on complaints

The charter lists the Sector 68 Complaint Centre, the municipal line **1800-137-0007**, the state Local Government line **1800-1800-0172**, and the MC CRAMAT app. Section 3.1 gives office grievance hours of 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. except public holidays. Section 3.2 separately gives the phone centre's working-day hours as 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. It says the centre sends a complaint ID by SMS and allows callers to check status using that ID. The corporation homepage also links Punjab's [Public Grievance Redressal System](https://connect.punjab.gov.in/service/grievance/GR1).

Section 3.3 gives complaint redress norms of one day for sanitation, three for water supply and sewerage, and two each for horticulture, street lighting and encroachment. The two-day street-light grievance norm is from a different table than the ten-working-day street-light service standard. No hours or service channels were tested by phone or by submitting a ticket.

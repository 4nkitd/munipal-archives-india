# Moradabad's 70-ward delimitation of 5 September 2017

## Notification and jurisdiction

The Uttar Pradesh government issued notification **4137/नौ-1-2017-333 परि/2017** at Lucknow on 5 September 2017 under sections 32 and 33 of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporations Act, 1959. It records that the state considered objections following notification 3782 of 16 August 2017. The September order divides the **municipal corporation area**, rather than the whole district, into 70 wards.

## Reading the attached schedule

Page 2 gives the first ward entries. Column 3 describes sides of roads and other boundaries; column 4 names localities. Examples from that page:

| 2017 ward | Name as printed | Selected references in the schedule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | शाहपुर तिगरी | Column 4 lists Poonam Vihar, Vivek Vihar and Siddharth Nagar. The boundary description also refers to Gautam Buddh Park. |

The linked scan is the 2017 schedule. A current ward number for an address requires a check for subsequent delimitation; a locality name by itself does not establish which side of a boundary a property occupies.

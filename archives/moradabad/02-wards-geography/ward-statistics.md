# Moradabad municipal ward population, voter and household figures

## Coverage and date

The sheet reports 887,871 residents and a municipal area of 75 square kilometres. Its totals row gives 618,384 voters and 129,924 households. The sheet does not give a census year, electoral roll revision or household survey date. The columns therefore cannot establish current population or turnout. For example, the source prints 9,561 voters and 3,234 residents for ward 8.

## Ward figures as printed

The letter codes are the sheet's area labels. The 70 household entries sum to **129,928**, four more than the printed total of **129,924**. The voter and population columns sum to their printed totals.

# Find a Moradabad house or water tax account

## Search the house details register

On the [corporation homepage](https://www.nagarnigammoradabad.in/), “Pay House & Water Tax” opens the House Details form. It identifies the PIN as the number printed on the delivered bill. If the bill is unavailable, the page also offers a search by name, house number or latest receipt number and displays a CAPTCHA.

| Task | Published municipal link |
|---|---|
| Look up the property | [Pay Tax Online](https://tax.onlinemnn.com/SearchHouseDetailsF.aspx) |
| Retrieve a payment receipt | [View Receipt](https://tax.onlinemnn.com/SearchPaymentDeposite.aspx) |
| Prepare a challan | [Create Challan](https://tax.onlinemnn.com/createvoucherhdfc.aspx) |
| Open the bill lookup | [View Bill](https://tax.onlinemnn.com/propertyNoWiseBillCreation_public.aspx) |

The form displays ward options 01 through 16. It gives no mapping to the corporation's 70 electoral wards. For a mismatch, the corporation publishes its Civil Lines office number, **+91 9105900538**. The service page is undated.

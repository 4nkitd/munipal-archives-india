# Moradabad original municipal budget for 2025-26

## Budget passed at the executive committee meeting

The [budget register](https://www.nagarnigammoradabad.in/Budget.php) lists the five-page scan as Report 2025-26. Its title is *मूल बजट 2025-26*. The final column on page 1 says the executive committee passed the original budget at its meeting on **24 March 2025**. Neither the scan nor the register gives a publication date.

| Original budget item | FY 2025-26, ₹ lakh | PDF page |
|---|---:|---:|
| Opening balance | 29,173.97 | 1 |
| Building tax | 4,000.00 | 1 |
| Water tax | 2,200.00 | 1 |
| Share of 2% stamp duty | 800.00 | 1 |
| Sewer tax | 50.00 | 1 |
| Total annual income, excluding opening balance | 65,339.00 | 2 |
| Total expenditure | 64,789.50 | 5 |
| Closing balance | 29,723.47 | 5 |
| Grand total, including closing balance | 94,512.97 | 5 |

The four tax lines are selected income estimates. On [page 2](https://www.nagarnigammoradabad.in/assets/files/2025-26.pdf#page=2), annual income plus the opening balance gives ₹94,512.97 lakh. Page 5 reaches the same total by adding expenditure and the closing balance. Separate columns give 2023-24 actuals, the 2024-25 original and revised estimates, and actuals through January 2025.

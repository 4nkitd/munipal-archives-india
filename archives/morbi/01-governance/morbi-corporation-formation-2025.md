# Constitution of Morbi Municipal Corporation

## Effective date and legal basis

Notification `KV-08 of 2025-UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/5932/P Section` constituted the Municipal Corporation of the City of Morbi on 1 January 2025. The Gujarat Government Gazette published it on that date in Extraordinary Part I-A, Extra No. 5. It cites Article 243Q of the Constitution and section 5(1) of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. The remaining provisions of the Act came into operation in Morbi on the same date under section 1(3).

## Transition from Morbi Nagarpalika

The order refers to the predecessor as Morbi Municipality. The [corporation's About page](https://mmcgujarat.in/about) calls it Morbi Nagarpalika and confirms the 1 January 2025 upgrade. The [1951 Census handbook for Madhya Saurashtra](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/29499) already lists Morvi as a municipality in Table A-V. The original municipal founding date is unverified.

Gujarat appointed Kiran B. Jhaveri, IAS, as administrator for one year from 1 January 2025 or until councillors were elected at the first corporation election, whichever came first. This is a historical appointment. The order also says that state government land within the predecessor municipality and the scheduled gram panchayats would not vest in the corporation.

# Initial larger urban area of Morbi

## January 2025 schedule

Notification `GV-07 of 2025-UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/5932/P Section` designated Morbi Municipality and the areas in the following schedule as a larger urban area under Article 243Q(2) of the Constitution. The Gujarat Government Gazette published the notification on 1 January 2025 in Extraordinary Part IV-A, Extra No. 3.

| Schedule entry | Name in the gazette |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Shaktasanala |
| 2 | Ravapara |
| 3 | Lilapar |
| 4 | Amreli |
| 5 | Nani Vavdi |
| 6 | Bhadiyad (Jawahar) |
| 7 | Trajpar (Malia Vanaliya) |
| 8 | Mahendranagar (Indiranagar) |
| 9 | Madhapar/Vajepar O.G. |

The table preserves the gazette's combined wording for the last row. The entries identify the founding area and do not assign ward numbers.

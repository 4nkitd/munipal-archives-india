# Amreli outgrowth areas in the former Morbi Municipality

## Predecessor municipality boundary changes

Gujarat's Urban Development and Urban Housing Department issued this notification on 1 September 2022, the date used in its [M Branch publication index](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/notification_m.php). Under Article 243P(d) of the Constitution it added the scheduled areas to Morbi Municipality. It states that government land would not vest in the municipality.

The order recalls an area notification for Morbi Municipality dated 5 February 2015, the exclusion of the Amreli entry and related survey numbers on 29 May 2018, and a 26 February 2019 notification specifying Amreli Village Panchayat. It cites Amreli Gram Panchayat resolution No. 3 of 30 June 2020 and Morbi Municipality resolution No. 17 of 27 August 2020 in support of inclusion.

The 2015 date is evidence of the older municipality's existence, not its founding date. The 2022 order predates the corporation. Use the signed source schedule rather than this summary for individual survey numbers.

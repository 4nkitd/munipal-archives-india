# July 2025 determination of Morbi wards and seats

## Ward and seat totals

Notification `KV-238 of 2025 UDUHD/MSM/e-file/18/2025/0418/P Section`, dated 9 July 2025, cites section 5 of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949 and the 2011 Census. The order specifies 13 wards and 52 councillor seats. The [corporation portal](https://mmcgujarat.in/) also lists 13 wards.

| Reservation | Seats | Of which for women in that category |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled Castes | 4 | 2 |
| Backward Classes | 14 | 7 |
| Women overall | 26 | Includes the women already counted in the Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes rows |

[Scanned PDF, visually checked.] These are corporation-wide totals. The notice does not draw ward boundaries or allocate seats to specific wards.

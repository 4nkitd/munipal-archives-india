# Morbi online municipal services and complaint contacts

## Where to start

| Service | Link on the corporation portal |
| --- | --- |
| Property tax, professional tax, shop and establishment, and complaint registration | [Morbi citizen login](https://erp.mmcgujarat.in/account/citizenlogin) |
| Marriage certificate | [Gujarat eNagar](https://enagar.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) |
| Birth and death certificates | [Gujarat eOlakh](https://eolakh.gujarat.gov.in/) |

The corporation lists `02822-220551` for registering complaints and `02822-220552` as its control number. It lists `079-23238887` for online tax and grievance technical issues only, with hours of 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; it does not state the days of operation.

The municipal office is at 15, Vasant Plot Main Road, Sardar Nagar, Morbi 363641. The municipality's portal lists `commi-mmc-mor@gujarat.gov.in` as its office email. This is a guide to the published routes; it does not establish fees, processing times or successful submission.

# Morena AMRUT projects reported as grounded, March 2026

## Reported counts and costs

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' table lists Morena under Madhya Pradesh at serial 198. It describes the entries as projects grounded under AMRUT and gives 2 March 2026 as the date of the state update.

| Sector | Projects | Cost, ₹ crore |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Sewerage and septage management | 1 | 138.16 |
| Parks | 2 | 2.00 |
| Urban transport | 1 | 1.10 |
| Total of reported cells | 4 | 141.26 |

The largest entry by cost is the one sewerage and septage management project. The water supply and drainage cells are blank. They are not evidence that Morena has no other work in those sectors. The table does not identify project locations, beneficiaries, contractors, tender awards or disbursements.

# Morena municipal ward population in Census 2011

## The census municipality

The Primary Census Abstract workbook lists the town as `Morena (M)` under code `802083`, with 33,104 households and 200,482 people. Ward records 1 through 39 sum to those totals. The four rows below include the first and last wards and the wards with the smallest and largest population.

| Census ward | Households | Population |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1,104 | 6,495 |
| 6 | 1,833 | 11,063 |
| 20 | 376 | 1,998 |
| 39 | 1,342 | 8,310 |

The catalogue entry was created on 19 January 2021 for Census 2011 data. The later [district containment order of 22 May 2020](https://morena.nic.in/document/%e0%a4%95%e0%a4%a8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%9f%e0%a5%87%e0%a4%a8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%ae%e0%a5%87%e0%a4%a8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%9f-%e0%a4%95%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%b7%e0%a5%87%e0%a4%a4%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%b0-%e0%a4%86%e0%a4%a6%e0%a5%87-3/) explicitly refers to Morena Municipal Corporation Ward 47. It establishes that the 2011 list cannot be reused as a later corporation register. The order does not establish the full ward count or boundaries.

# Morena municipal general election and corporation term, 2022 to 2027

## Official calendar

The Madhya Pradesh State Election Commission's urban programme index records the statewide general-election programme under letter `F-53NN-01/2022/5/445`, dated 1 June 2022. Serial 343 of the commission's [term calendar](https://www.mplocalelection.mp.gov.in/Data/rightpanel/UploadCalendar_2024.pdf#page=11) identifies Morena Municipal Corporation. The [election overview](https://www.mplocalelection.mp.gov.in/Data/rightpanel/EkNagarUpdated2025.pdf#page=1) gives the separate counting and result-announcement date for that poll phase.

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| General-election poll, second phase | 13 July 2022 |
| Second-phase counting and result announcement | 20 July 2022 |
| First corporation meeting | 5 August 2022 |
| Projected end of term | 4 August 2027 |

## Corporation election, not all district elections

The [Morena District Local Election 2022 register](https://morena.nic.in/en/document-category/local-election-2022/) also contains separate orders for bodies such as Sabalgarh, Ambah and Porsa. Those orders are not the Morena corporation's ward schedules. Sections 9 and 14 of the [Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/Uploaded%20Document/act%20%26%20rules/MunicipalCorporationACT1956%2024.pdf) distinguish the citywide mayoral contest from ward contests for councillors and put both under the State Election Commission.

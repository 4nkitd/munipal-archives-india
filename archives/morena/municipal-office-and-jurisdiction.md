# Morena municipal office and jurisdiction

## Office and local-body identifiers

The district directory locates the corporation office on M.S. Road. It prints the telephone as `917532226387`; the [state directory of urban bodies](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=7HRZoU6F4ZQQb0k3xdV0HA%3D%3D) formats the same line as `07532-226387` and gives the institutional mailbox `cmomorena@mpurban.gov.in`. The district directory does not give a separate corporation website. The state's [MP e-Nagar Palika](https://enagarpalika.mp.gov.in/home) is linked by the Urban Development and Housing Department for municipal services.

The [state division-wise list](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=hxIqP77vI9ypoSI%2B5%2FYrMg%3D%3D) classifies Morena city as a Nagar Nigam in Chambal division. Ambah, Porsa, Sabalgarh and the other urban bodies shown beneath it have separate entries. The Morena row gives a Census 2011 population of 200,482. That is the figure in this roster, not a current estimate for the city or district. [LGD](https://lgdirectory.gov.in/) code `250677` identifies the corporation; `802083` is the Census town code.

## Limits of the listing

Under section 7 of the [Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/Uploaded%20Document/act%20%26%20rules/MunicipalCorporationACT1956%2024.pdf), larger urban areas are specified by public notification. The district and state office entries identify the body but do not reproduce that notification or define its ward boundaries.

# Motihari Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and classification

## Municipal jurisdiction

In its final Phase II ward-formation register, the Bihar State Election Commission lists Motihari at serial 33 as नगर निगम, in Motihari Sadar subdivision of East Champaran district. The register marks the body as newly constituted (नवगठित). The 46 wards are those of the corporation, not the whole subdivision or district.

## District evidence

East Champaran district identifies the appointing body as Nagar Nigam Motihari in its final merit-list notice for sixth-phase higher secondary teacher appointments for 2019-20. The district displayed the notice from 9 to 31 July 2022. Its urban utilities directory still uses the older Municipal Council label. [District record](https://eastchamparan.nic.in/notice/information-regarding-final-merit-list-for-the-appointment-of-teachers-in-the-sixth-phase-of-higher-secondary-schools-under-nagar-nigam-motihari-in-the-year-2019-20/).

# Motihari final ward schedule: boundaries and census population

## Reading Form 6

The SEC’s Form 6 lists wards 1–46, their four boundaries and their populations on the 2011 Census basis. Form 6 describes the Khadwa River as the western boundary of Ward 1. At the other end of the schedule, the Dhanauti River marks the southern boundary of Ward 46. The full Hindi boundary descriptions are transcribed in `wards.json`; the SEC links a map alongside each ward. These figures are residents counted on the stated census basis, not electoral-roll totals.

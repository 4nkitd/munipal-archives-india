# Motihari higher secondary teacher recruitment: final merit list

## Recruitment cycle and notice dates

The appointing jurisdiction in the district notice is **Nagar Nigam Motihari**. The notice concerns the final merit list for appointments to higher secondary schools in the sixth phase of the **2019-20** recruitment cycle. It is a merit list, not an appointment order.

The district records **9 July 2022** as the listing start and **31 July 2022** as the end. The cycle year refers to the recruitment exercise; it is not the year in which the list appeared on the district website. The district links the [390 KB merit-list attachment](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3bac9162b47c56fc8a4d2a519803d51b3/uploads/2022/07/2022070966.pdf).

# Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation profile

## Which authority this archive covers

Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) is also known as the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM). Section 1 of the Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act, 1888 limits the Act's extent to Brihan Mumbai. Both the [Mumbai City Collectorate](https://mumbaicity.gov.in/en/public-utility/municipal-corporation-of-greater-mumbai/) and [Mumbai Suburban District](https://mumbaisuburban.gov.in/en/public-utility/municipal-corporation-of-greater-mumbai/) list the same municipal corporation and its headquarters at Mumbai C.S.T. 400001. The district collectorates are separate authorities. Navi Mumbai and Thane have separate municipal corporations.

## How wards are counted

The State Election Commission's [final 2025–26 results table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) lists 227 electoral wards, 227 seats and results for all 227 seats for Brihanmumbai. BMC's [Disaster Management Department](https://dm.mcgm.gov.in/ward-maps) has a separate map directory for 24 lettered administrative wards. Use the administrative ward to find a civic ward office and the numbered electoral ward to look up the relevant council seat.

## Where to start

Call [BMC's Central Complaint Registration System](https://dm.mcgm.gov.in/central-complaint-registration-system) at 1916 for civic complaints or disaster reporting. The separate [property tax](https://ptaxportal.mcgm.gov.in/CitizenPortal/) and [water billing](https://aquaptax.mcgm.gov.in/aqua/CitizenHome.html) portals take residents directly to the respective services.

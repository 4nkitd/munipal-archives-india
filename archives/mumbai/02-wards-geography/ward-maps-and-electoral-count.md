# Mumbai ward maps and the 2025–26 electoral ward count

## Administrative ward maps

The [BMC Disaster Management Department map directory](https://dm.mcgm.gov.in/ward-maps) lists 24 administrative wards: A, B, C, D, E, F North, F South, G North, G South, H East, H West, K East, K West, L, M East, M West, N, P North, P South, R North, R South, R Central, S and T. The department has a separate [ward directory](https://dm.mcgm.gov.in/ward-directory). These are for municipal administration and disaster management. They do not identify the electoral ward for a property.

## Electoral map edition used for the 2026 election

The [State Election Commission polling notice](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Upload/PDF/SEC%20140126%20Ready%20to%20Poll.pdf#page=2) says Brihanmumbai had 227 electoral wards, with one elected member per ward. The [final election results table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf#page=1) confirms 227 wards, 227 seats, and results declared for all 227 seats. BMC's [combined final electoral map for the 2025 general election](https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/MCGM%20Department%20List/Assessment%20And%20Collection%20%28Election%29/Docs/Election%20Department/BMC%20General%20Election%202025/Ward%20Formation/Final/Final%20Prabhag%20Maps%202025/BMC%20All%20Final%20Prabhag%20in%20One%20Map_2025.pdf) returned a portal error when checked. The archive does not reproduce its boundaries.

## Why older materials give 236

The 236-ward figure belongs to the earlier 2022 election preparation. Section 5(1)(a), footnote 9, in the [state's consolidated Act](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/09/20250929999585550.pdf#page=60) records that section 2 of Maharashtra Act 43 of 2022 replaced "two hundred and thirty-six" with "two hundred and twenty-seven." The official [27 January 2022 order](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Upload/PDF/bmc_order_dated_2712022.pdf) is a historical instruction on publication of draft boundaries, objections and the reservation process. Neither that order nor a 236-ward map is evidence of the boundaries used in January 2026.

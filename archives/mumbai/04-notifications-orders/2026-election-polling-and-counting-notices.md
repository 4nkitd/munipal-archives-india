# January 2026 BMC election polling and counting notices

## Polling schedule

The State Election Commission's [14 January 2026 polling notice](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Upload/PDF/SEC%20140126%20Ready%20to%20Poll.pdf) covers general elections to 29 municipal corporations, including Brihanmumbai. The notice says the Commission announced the election programme on 15 December 2025. Polling was scheduled for 15 January 2026, 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., and counting for 16 January 2026, starting at 10 a.m. Brihanmumbai used single-member wards, so each voter had one municipal vote. The notice distinguishes this from the multi-member system in the other 28 corporations.

## Brihanmumbai counting clarification

A [separate Commission notice of the same date](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Upload/PDF/SEC%20140126%20PADU.pdf) says a Printing Auxiliary Display Unit was only to be used in exceptional cases involving a technical issue during the count, not as a routine step. The notice says 140 units were made available for Brihanmumbai. That figure describes availability, not actual use.

These are historical notices. The [final results table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) is a separate record of the outcome.

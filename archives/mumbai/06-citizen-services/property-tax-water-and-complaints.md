# BMC property tax, water bills and civic complaints

## Property tax

Open the [BMC Property Tax Citizen Portal](https://ptaxportal.mcgm.gov.in/CitizenPortal/) for online payment or a bill report. The portal also lists transfer of property, changes to the billing name and address, reassessment, and new property assessment. Select the service you want rather than treating a payment as a request to change the underlying property record.

## Water bills and receipts

Open the [BMC water billing portal](https://aquaptax.mcgm.gov.in/aqua/CitizenHome.html) and keep the Consumer Number (CCN) from the bill handy. The portal offers Water Bill Payment, Download Bills/Receipts and Supplementary Bill under Pay/View Bills & Download Receipts. For online payment, the published instructions say to choose Water Bill Payment, enter the CCN, proceed, enter the payment amount and choose the online payment mode before selecting a payment gateway.

## Civic complaints

The [BMC Disaster Management Department](https://dm.mcgm.gov.in/central-complaint-registration-system) identifies 1916 as both a disaster helpline and a civic complaints number. The department's [ward directory](https://dm.mcgm.gov.in/ward-directory) is organized by lettered administrative ward. The 227 numbered electoral wards are not the ward office directory.

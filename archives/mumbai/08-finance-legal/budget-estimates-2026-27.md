# BMC budget estimates for 2026–27

## Consolidated estimate

BMC tabled its fiscal year 2026–27 budget on 25 February 2026. Its [Budget Estimate 2026–2027 page](https://www.mcgm.gov.in/irj/portal/anonymous/qlBEst2627) returned a portal error when checked. [The Indian Express's contemporaneous account of the presentation](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/mumbai/mega-mumbai-makeover-bmc-unveils-massive-rs-80952-crore-budget-10551528/) reports a proposed total of ₹80,952.56 crore and estimated capital expenditure of ₹48,164.28 crore. Those consolidated figures are from the press account, not a verified municipal budget book. The tabled estimates are not evidence of actual spending or of the final adopted total.

## Education estimate

[Akashvani's News On AIR report of 25 February 2026](https://newsonair.gov.in/municipal-corporation-of-greater-mumbai-proposes-education-budget-for-2026-27-of-over-4200-crore/) gives the proposed education budget as ₹4,248.08 crore. It reports estimated revenue generation of over ₹3,758 crore and capital expenditure of about ₹490 crore. The proposal earmarked ₹340 crore for school infrastructure repairs, upgrades and reconstruction, and ₹187.57 crore for free scholastic materials for primary and secondary students.

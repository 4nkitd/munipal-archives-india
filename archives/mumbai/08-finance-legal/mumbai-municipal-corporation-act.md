# Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act, 1888: jurisdiction and duties

## Territorial extent and municipal authorities

Maharashtra Act III of 1888 is dated 14 September 1888. The [Law and Judiciary Department's reprint](https://lj.maharashtra.gov.in/en/document/the-mumbai-municipal-corporation-act-text-as-on-24th-june-2025-2/) consolidates the Act as at 24 June 2025. Section 1 says the Act extends only to Brihan Mumbai. Section 4 lists the corporation, Mayor, Municipal Commissioner and municipal committees among the authorities that carry out the Act. Section 5 gives the corporation its legal identity and distinguishes directly elected councillors from nominated councillors.

Greater Mumbai is the corporation's area, not all of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. The [State Election Commission's results](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) list Brihanmumbai separately from Thane and Navi Mumbai.

## What the corporation must and may provide

[Section 61](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/09/20250929999585550.pdf#page=108) requires adequate provision for matters including water supply, drainage, refuse removal, registration of births and deaths, public hospitals and dispensaries, fire protection and public street works. [Section 63](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/09/20250929999585550.pdf#page=111) lists discretionary matters, including slum improvement, libraries, and public parks and recreation grounds.

The reprint date is the cutoff for its text. Check later gazette amendments for questions about the law after that date.

# Munger district gazette ward map of 17 March 2012

## Map and commission listing

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The one-page scan bears the heading "District Gazette, Munger, 17 March 2012." It shows a north arrow, numbered areas and ward boundary lines. The footer credits publication to the Collector, Munger, and printing to the District Council Press, Munger.

At serial 46 in the [State Election Commission's unchanged-ward list](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC), the commission links that scan and lists 45 wards for Munger Municipal Corporation in Munger Sadar. The row gives a total population of 213,303: 14,562 Scheduled Caste, 406 Scheduled Tribe and 198,335 other residents. Those figures come from the commission table, not the older map. No street-level boundary has been transcribed from the scan.

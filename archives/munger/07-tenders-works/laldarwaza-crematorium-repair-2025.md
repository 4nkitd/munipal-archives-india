# Munger tender 09/2025-26: Laldarwaza electric crematorium repairs

## Repair scope and bidding schedule

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

The Municipal Commissioner issued short-term e-tender 09/2025-26, letter 3116, on 22 December 2025. The notice said the electric crematorium at Laldarwaza in ward 1 was closed because of equipment failure. It sought repair work and supplies. The first page lists 12 heating elements, one temperature controller, two fabricated stainless-steel flue duct sets and 85 heating element bricks, among other items.

| Step | Date and time |
| --- | --- |
| Tender documents available for download | 9 January to 3 p.m. on 15 January 2026 |
| Pre-bid meeting at Munger Municipal Corporation | 10 January 2026, 11 a.m. |
| Online bid deadline | 16 January 2026, 3 p.m. |
| Hard-copy documents due | 17 January 2026, 3 p.m. |
| Scheduled opening | 17 January 2026, 3:30 p.m. |

The [district notice page](https://munger.nic.in/notice/short-term-tender-notice-nagar-nigam-office-munger-22-12-2025/) lists 16 January as its end date; the attached tender sets separate hard-copy and opening times. The tender establishes the facility's location for this notice, not the boundary of ward 1. It does not show that the crematorium reopened.

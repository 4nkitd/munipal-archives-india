# Bihar Municipal (Amendment) Act, 2022: Munger corporation and elections

## Statutory scope

Bihar Act 06 of 2022 amends the Bihar Municipal Act, 2007 (Bihar Act 11 of 2007). It received the Governor's assent on 1 April 2022 and took effect on publication in the Bihar Gazette Extraordinary, Patna No. 176, on 2 April. The authorized English text begins on page 4.

Under amended section 11(1), the elected membership of a municipality includes its chief councillor, deputy chief councillor and the councillors for the number of wards determined under section 13. Under amended section 23(1), voters enrolled in the municipality elect the two leadership posts directly under the direction, control and supervision of the State Election Commission.

## Application to Munger

The [Commission's unchanged-ward schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC) names Munger as a Nagar Nigam (municipal corporation) at serial 46 in Munger Sadar. The [district directory](https://munger.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) identifies Munger Nagar Nigam separately from Jamalpur municipality. The amendment sets statewide rules, not Munger's formation date or territorial limits.

# City and district jurisdiction in Muzaffarpur

## Identifying the urban local body

| Field | City Finance listing |
|---|---|
| Urban local body | Muzaffarpur Municipal Corporation |
| State and district | Bihar, Muzaffarpur |
| Local Government Directory code | 249603 |
| Census town code | 801324 |
| Municipal area | 26.88 sq km |
| Population, 2011 Census | 3,54,462 |

The [City Finance urban local body directory](https://www.cityfinance.in/api/v1/ulb) supplies the two codes and identifies the population figure as a 2011 Census count. The [district's administrative summary](https://muzaffarpur.nic.in/about-district/at-a-glance/) counts one municipal corporation, three Nagar Parishads and seven Nagar Panchayats separately. Its district area of 3,122.56 sq km and its 1875 formation date describe the district, not the corporation. Neither directory establishes whether a particular address lies inside city limits.

## Municipal office contact

The [district telephone directory](https://muzaffarpur.nic.in/telephone-directory/) lists `mmc-muz-bih@nic.in` and `muzaffarpur.ulb@gmail.com` under the Municipal Commissioner. Both are office email addresses. The directory entry does not give a municipal office landline.

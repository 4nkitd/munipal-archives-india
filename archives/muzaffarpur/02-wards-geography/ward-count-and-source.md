# Muzaffarpur ward count and delimitation source

## What the sources establish

City Finance records 49 wards for Muzaffarpur Municipal Corporation. Its [underlying urban local body directory](https://www.cityfinance.in/api/v1/ulb) attributes the count to the corporation and does not give an effective delimitation date, ward names or street boundaries.

The Bihar State Election Commission's [draft municipal ward publication form](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx) is Form 6 under Rule 29(1). It provides selectors for district, subdivision and municipality. The table is structured to show ward population by 2011 Census category, boundary descriptions and a map column. The commission also provides a separate [municipal electoral roll search](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/SearchElectroralRoll2025P1.aspx).

No completed delimitation schedule for the corporation was verified. The ward count is not a boundary list, and a draft ward report or an electoral roll is not a substitute for a final notification when checking whether a property is inside a ward.

# Birth and death certificate service

## Registration and certificate access

The district's “Birth & Death Certificate” service page directs users to the national Civil Registration System (CRS). The listed address, `crsorgi.gov.in`, redirects to `dc.crsorgi.gov.in`.

[CRS guidance](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/about) says births and deaths are registered where the event occurred, not where the family lives. The normal reporting period is 21 days from the event. This is not a certificate delivery deadline.

The district service page does not identify the local registrar or give a city-specific fee or document checklist. No application or certificate lookup was submitted to verify a transaction.

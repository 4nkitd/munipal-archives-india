# Urgent notice for public settlement tender 02/GS/MMC/2025-2026

The district portal lists an “Urgent Notice regarding Public Settlement Tender” under Muzaffarpur Municipal Corporation. The notice refers to tender **02/GS/MMC/2025-2026**. The portal labels 30 April 2025 as the start date and 4 May 2025 as the end date. It does not give a separate issue date.

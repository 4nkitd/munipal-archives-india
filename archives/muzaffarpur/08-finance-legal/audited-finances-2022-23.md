# Muzaffarpur audited financial indicators for 2022-23

## City Finance dashboard figures

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' City Finance dashboard attributes these figures to audited financial statements for 2022-23. The panel displays a last-updated date of 4 July 2025, not an audit signature date or accounts publication date.

| Dashboard indicator | Amount in rupees |
|---|---:|
| Total tax revenue | 13,94,80,075 |
| Total own revenue | 24,00,96,276 |
| Total grants | 40,11,48,773 |
| Total revenue | 77,40,21,361 |
| Total expenditure | 1,14,79,26,287 |
| Total balance sheet size | 2,90,50,69,442 |

The dashboard also offers financial-year selections for 2019-20, 2020-21 and 2021-22. The figures above are historical accounting indicators, not budget estimates. The dashboard's published own revenue and grants do not add up to its published total revenue. Each is transcribed as shown; this record does not assign the difference to any account.

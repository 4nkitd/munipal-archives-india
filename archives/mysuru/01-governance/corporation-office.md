# Mysuru civic administration and corporation office

## City office and jurisdiction

The district public-utilities directory classifies this office as a corporation and gives its postal address as Mysuru City Corporation, New Sayyaji Rao Road, Mysuru 570024. The [corporation's city summary](https://mysurucitycorporation.co.in/WebSiteMysuru/Home.aspx) identifies the corporation as responsible for civic administration of Mysuru city. Mysuru is also the district and divisional headquarters; those are different administrative jurisdictions.

The municipal website locates the office next to Banumaiah College on Sayyaji Rao Road. Its public menu directs property owners to the tax search and businesses to a separate trade licence search.

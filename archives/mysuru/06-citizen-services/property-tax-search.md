# Mysuru property tax search and payment acknowledgement

## Find the assessment and obtain the receipt

Use “Search by PID” when the property identifier is known. Otherwise choose “Search by Criteria”. The manual instructions list layout, site number, khata holder name or mobile number as ways to find the record. Search first, then use “View” on the matching khata to see the complete property tax details. The search results alone are not a payment acknowledgement.

On the property details page the corporation identifies two actions: “Get Form-1” and “View Tax & Pay”. Use the payment route for the selected assessment, and download or print the acknowledgement after a successful online payment. The page marks mandatory fields with an asterisk and directs objections to the Revenue Department. It does not state a tax rate, rebate period or processing deadline.

# Constitution of the Municipal Corporation of the City of Nadiad

Notification KV-23 of 2025, Gujarat Gazette Part I-A, Extra No. 15, took effect on 1 January 2025. It constituted a corporation covering the former Nadiad Municipality and the gram panchayats specified in the companion declaration GV-22. KV-23 does not reproduce that schedule.

The state appointed Amit Prakash Yadav as administrator for one year from 1 January or until the first councillors were elected, whichever came first. It excluded state land in the constituent areas from vesting in the new corporation.

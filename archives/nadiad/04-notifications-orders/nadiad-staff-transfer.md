# Transfer of Nadiad municipal and panchayat staff

Gujarat Gazette Part I-A, Extra No. 16, notification KV-24 covers officers and servants employed before 1 January 2025 by Nadiad Municipality or the gram panchayats specified in GV-22. Under paragraph 5(c) of Part I of Appendix IV to the 1949 Act, they became employees of the corporation.

Until other provisions were made under the Act, they were to receive salaries and allowances, including pension, according to the rules. The order lists no employees, pay scales or sanctioned post counts.

# Nagercoil ward delimitation annexures for wards 1 to 52

## Finding a ward in the published schedule

| City page label | File linked by the corporation | Coverage stated on the page |
|---|---|---|
| Annexure I(d) | `ward_delimit_1d.pdf` | No ward range in label |
| Annexure II(d), first part | `ward_delimit_2d_1.pdf` | Wards 1 to 26 |
| Annexure II(d), second part | `ward_delimit_2d_2.pdf` | Wards 27 to 52 |

Ward 26 ends the first file; ward 27 begins the second. These ranges identify which annexure to consult for this edition. The page gives no notification date or effective date. It lists reservation of seats separately under Gazette Notifications; a reservation category does not describe the physical extent of a ward.

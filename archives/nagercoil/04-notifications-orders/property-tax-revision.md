# Nagercoil property tax revision documents for 2022

## Which document to consult

| Document | Date | Use in the revision file |
|---|---|---|
| G.O.(Ms.) No. 52 | 30 March 2022 | Government order for the general revision of property tax |
| Circular No. 40032/2012/R1 | 1 April 2022 | Instructions published with the revision order |
| Nagercoil Revision Self Assessment Form | 2022 | City form for the owner's declaration |

The `2012` in the circular reference is part of its file number. The city explicitly dates the circular to 2022. For an enquiry about an assessment made under this revision, distinguish the state order from the city form. Neither document label establishes the amount due on an individual assessment.

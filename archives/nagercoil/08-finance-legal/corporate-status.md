# Nagercoil's transition from municipality to city corporation

## Two bodies in the archive

The [city's history](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/nagercoil/) dates the extension of the Tamil Nadu District Municipalities Act, 1920 to Nagercoil to 1 April 1959. First-grade status followed on 1 April 1961, selection grade on 12 October 1978 and special grade on 30 May 1988. These dates describe the former municipality.

Section 4(1-A)(xiv) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998 identifies the later body as the Municipal Corporation of Nagercoil, constituted under Tamil Nadu Act 11 of 2019. Section 4 treats it as incorporated under the common Act in its own name and area. The [district local bodies register](https://kanniyakumari.nic.in/localbodies/) lists Nagercoil as the district's corporation and distinguishes it from its five municipalities.

# Nagpur Municipal Corporation and city services

## Formation and jurisdiction

Nagpur Municipal Corporation (NMC) dates the establishment of its predecessor municipal council to 1864 and the start of the corporation to March 1951. Its institutional history says the City of Nagpur Corporation Act, 1948 was repealed in 2012 and the corporation now operates under the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949.

NMC is the city authority for municipal water supply, sewerage, waste management, internal roads, street lighting and parks. It also lists slum improvement and primary health and education among its city responsibilities. NMC headquarters are in the Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Administrative Building, Mahanagar Palika Marg, Civil Lines, Nagpur 440 001. The [Nagpur Collectorate](https://nagpur.gov.in/collectrate/) is a separate district office.

## City leadership

The Municipal Commissioner is the administrative head. On the verification date the [commissioner's directory](https://nmcnagpur.gov.in/honorable-commissioner-and-additional-municipal-commissioner) listed Dr. Vipin, IAS, as Commissioner. The [mayor's directory](https://nmcnagpur.gov.in/honorable-mayor-amp-honorable-deputy-mayor) listed Neeta Thakre as Mayor and Leela Hathibed as Deputy Mayor. These are directory listings, not appointment dates.

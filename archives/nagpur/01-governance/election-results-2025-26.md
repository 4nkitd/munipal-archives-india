# Nagpur Municipal Corporation election results, 2025–26

## Citywide results

Row 26 of the Maharashtra State Election Commission's final table gives Nagpur 38 wards, 151 seats, 993 contesting candidates and results declared for all 151 seats.

| Party or category in the SEC table | Seats |
| --- | ---: |
| BJP | 102 |
| INC | 34 |
| Unrecognized parties registered with SEC | 10 |
| SS - UBT | 2 |
| BSP | 1 |
| NCP | 1 |
| SS | 1 |
| **Total** | **151** |

All other columns, including independents, are zero in the Nagpur row. These are election results, not a count of subsequent officeholders or changes in party affiliation.

## Ward and seat results

For the members elected to a particular ward, see [Gazette Notification 2027-NV-Manapa-2025-2026](https://election.nagpurnmc.in/storage/uploads/1768895174_document%286%29.pdf), published 19 January 2026 in the Maharashtra Government Gazette Extraordinary, Part I-A, Nagpur Divisional Supplement. The Gazette cites the SEC election programme of 15 December 2025. The SEC summary table does not show its own publication date.

# Nagpur final ward structure for the 2025–26 municipal election

## Notification and seat count

The final schedule invokes section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949 and refers to the State Election Commission's order dated 30 September 2025. The subsequent [final ward reservation gazette](https://election.nagpurnmc.in/storage/uploads/1764739672_%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%AD%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AF%20%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%AE%20%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%A3%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%9A%E0%A5%80%20%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%9A%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%2002-12-2025.aspx.pdf), dated 2 December 2025, identifies the final delimitation as Gazette Notification No. 550 of 4 October 2025. These are different notices: one fixes ward boundaries and the other assigns seat reservations.

## Reading the schedule

The schedule gives localities, boundary directions, population figures and the number of councillors to elect. The following entries are examples, not full boundary descriptions.

| Ward | Example localities named in the schedule | Population in the schedule | Seats |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | Nara Gaon, Kasturba Nagar, Jaripatka and Old Jaripatka | 69,790 | 4 |
| 38 | Takli Sim, Hingna Naka Area, Jayatala Settlement and Shivan Gaon | 47,216 | 3 |

The [NMC election index](https://election.nagpurnmc.in/nmc_yearwise_news/1) lists both draft and final maps, including a combined final map and individual maps for Wards 1 through 38. Use the final schedule and final map together for a specific address near a boundary. Electoral wards are not the same as the corporation's administrative service zones.

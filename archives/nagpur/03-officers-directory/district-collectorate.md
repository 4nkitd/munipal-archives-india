# Nagpur District Collectorate and the municipal boundary of responsibility

## District offices and city offices

The district's [administrative setup](https://nagpur.gov.in/administrative-setup/) groups the Collectorate with tahsils, subdivisions and blocks, and constituencies. Its [tahsil directory](https://nagpur.gov.in/tahsil/) lists Nagpur City and Nagpur Rural as separate tahsil offices. The Nagpur City Tahsildar is part of the district administration; the title does not refer to the NMC Commissioner or to an NMC zone office.

The Collectorate lists separate posts for revenue, Nazul, land acquisition, district planning and district election administration. Use the district directory for those offices. Start with NMC for city property tax or city complaints about garbage, drains and streetlights. For municipal corporation election records, use the Maharashtra State Election Commission and the NMC election portal rather than treating the district's other election listings as the city's ward schedule.

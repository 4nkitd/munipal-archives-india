# Nagpur right-to-public-services timelines and appeals

## Selected services

| Service | Time limit | Charge shown | Designated officer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Birth certificate | 1 day urgent; 3 days normal | ₹100 urgent; ₹20 normal | Zonal Officer |
| Death certificate | 1 day urgent; 3 days normal | ₹100 urgent; ₹20 normal | Zonal Officer |
| Property tax no-dues certificate | 3 days | No amount shown | Tax Inspector |
| New property tax assessment | 15 days | No amount shown | Tax Inspector |
| Creation of a property tax demand bill | 15 days | ₹5 | Tax Inspector |

The certificate entries list the Statistical Officer for the first appeal and the Health Officer (M) for the second appeal. The property tax entries list the Tax Superintendent and the Assistant Commissioner of the zone, respectively. "No amount shown" records a blank or null charge field, not a promise that there is no fee.

## Applying or appealing

Use the [NMC Right to Services portal](https://nmcnagpur.gov.in/RTSservices/ws/user/login.do) to sign in or register. The table marked these entries as "Integration in Progress" in its Aaple Sarkar column. For a tax payment rather than an assessment or certificate request, use [NMC's property tax payment page](https://nmcnagpur.gov.in/pay-property-tax).

NMC's [Right to Public Services guidance](https://nmcnagpur.gov.in/rtsinformation) says the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 has been in force since 28 April 2015. An eligible applicant can appeal if a notified service is late or refused without proper grounds. The first two appeals go to the designated appellate authorities; a third appeal may go to the Maharashtra State Commission for Right to Public Service.

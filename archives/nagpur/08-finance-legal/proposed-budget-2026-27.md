# Nagpur Municipal Corporation proposed budget for 2026–27

## Proposed receipts and spending

The corporation's financial statement index labels this book "NMC BUDGET - 2026-27." The Standing Committee chair's budget speech describes it as the revised 2025–26 and proposed 2026–27 budget presented to the corporation. The book also has a separate transport budget section.

| Item in the 2026–27 budget speech | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Estimated opening balance | ₹512.61 crore |
| Total expected receipts including opening balance | ₹6,202.99 crore |
| Estimated spending | ₹6,202.48 crore |
| GST assistance grant | ₹1,913.68 crore |
| Property tax and other collections under that heading | ₹359.02 crore |
| Proposed water-charge receipts | ₹285 crore |

Amounts are as printed in the speech, at the document's rounding. Use the [financial statement index](https://nmcnagpur.gov.in/financial-statement) to distinguish budget estimates from annual accounts. The fiscal year in the title and the month in the file path do not establish a publication date.

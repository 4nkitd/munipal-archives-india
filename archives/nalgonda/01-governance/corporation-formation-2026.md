# Formation of Nalgonda Municipal Corporation

## Statutory entry and commencement

The Governor assented to the Telangana Municipalities (Third Amendment) Act, 2026, on 11 January 2026, and the Gazette published it that day as Act 5 of 2026. Section 2 omitted Nalgonda's entry, serial 86, from Schedule I of the Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019. Section 3 inserted Schedule II entry 16 for Nalgonda, with 48 wards. Both changes are on page 2 of the Act.

[G.O.Ms.No.13](https://tggazette.cgg.gov.in/viewDocument/1770791115586), published in Gazette No. 14-A, appointed **13 January 2026** as the commencement date. The same issue carries G.O.Ms.No.12 for the separate Second Amendment Act. G.O.Ms.No.13 is the order for Nalgonda's upgrade.

## Scope of the entry

The new entry names Nalgonda and gives its ward count. It does not list merging gram panchayats, give survey numbers or describe ward boundaries. Entry 16 is a schedule serial number, not a count of active corporations. The election gazette of 27 January 2026 confirms 48 ward-member seats for Nalgonda.

# Nalgonda ward reservations and votes in 2026

## Election coverage

The State Election Commission's [27 January 2026 election notification](https://tsec.gov.in/preview.do?fileName=L1VwbG9hZHMvVFNFQy9wZGYvZ2F6ZXR0ZV9vZl8xMjNfdWxiX3dhcmRfZWxlY3Rpb25zXzUxOC5wZGY=#page=12), No. 150/TGSEC-ULBs/2026, lists 48 ward-member seats for Nalgonda. Page 2 scheduled polling for 11 February and counting for 13 February 2026.

The corporation's Proforma X return occupies PDF pages 222 to 228, statewide serials 1971 to 2019. Page 223 prints Ward 12 under both serials 1982 and 1983 with the same reservation and ballot totals. The table counts the ward once. This return is a record of the election, not a ward-boundary schedule.

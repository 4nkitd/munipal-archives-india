# Nalgonda income and expenditure statement, 2025–26

## Reported figures

Nalgonda's portal posts this CDMA statement for 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. Amounts are in rupees and use the statement's Total column for the Municipal General Fund and Capital Project Fund together.

| Account head or statement line | Amount (₹) |
|---|---:|
| Tax revenue (110) | 149,962,011.50 |
| Fees and user charges (140) | 135,627,603.45 |
| Total income | 321,746,070.95 |
| Establishment expenses (210) | 207,919,796.00 |
| Operations and maintenance (230) | 190,060,237.00 |
| Total expenditure | 437,316,368.21 |
| Balance before depreciation and prior-period items | −115,570,297.26 |
| Miscellaneous expenses (271) | 86,917.00 |
| Depreciation (272) | 134,626,968.00 |
| Net balance carried to the Municipal Fund | −250,284,182.26 |

## Reading the statement

The reported Municipal General Fund deficit is ₹86,622,370.26 and the Capital Project Fund deficit is ₹163,661,812.00. The period includes time both before and after the 13 January 2026 upgrade. The report gives one set of figures for the year with no split at that date. It is not a corporation-only budget, and no publication date or audit opinion appears on the statement.

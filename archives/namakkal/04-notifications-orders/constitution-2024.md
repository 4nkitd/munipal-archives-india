# Constitution of Namakkal City Municipal Corporation

## Final declaration

Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 257, Part II, Section 2, page 4, contains G.O. Ms. No. 105, notification II(2)/MAWS/743(a-4)/2024. The Governor declared the corporation under section 3(2)(d) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998. The government published the order on 10 August 2024; paragraph 2 brought it into force on 12 August.

## Area at formation

The order included the former Namakkal Municipality and the entire areas of 12 village panchayats: Vagurampatti, Vallipuram, Reddippatti, Veesanam, Marurpatti, Pappinaickenpatti, Siluvampatti, Thottipatti, Vasanthapuram, Vettampatti, Lathuvadi and Kadappalli.

The order provides for division into wards for the next ordinary election. It does not number the new corporation wards.

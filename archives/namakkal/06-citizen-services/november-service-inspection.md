# Inspection of Namakkal’s breakfast kitchen and new bus stand

## Corporation facilities

On 17 November 2026 the Collector visited the corporation kitchen at Mudalaipatti that prepared breakfast for government and aided schools. At the new bus stand the inspection covered two-wheeler parking charges, shop leases, toilets, drinking water and the timetable board. The Collector also met traders at the corporation’s daily vegetable market.

The district [posted the release on 18 November](https://namakkal.nic.in/the-district-collector-inspected-the-areas-under-namakkal-corporation-and-puduchattaram-union-under-the-ungalai-thedi-ungal-ooril-scheme/). Its later account of visits to Puduchattaram Panchayat Union concerns a separate local body.

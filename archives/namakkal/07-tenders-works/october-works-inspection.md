# Inspection of sewerage and zonal office works in Namakkal

## Projects inspected

The Collector inspected the ₹3.95 crore zonal office under construction at Mudalaipatti in Ward 2. The ₹211.83 crore sewerage project included an 11.13 million litre per day treatment plant at Rajiv Gandhi Nagar and a pumping station at Chinnamudalaipatti. The Collector also inspected the ₹3.10 crore Mudhalvar Padaippagam built with education funds.

Press release 784 is dated 22 October 2026. The [district posted it on 27 October](https://namakkal.nic.in/district-collector-inspected-ongoing-development-projects-areas-under-the-namakkal-municipal-corporation/).

# Nanded: wards and seats in the 2025-26 general election

## Final result

Row 21 of the Commission’s final party table gives separate figures for electoral wards (20), council seats (81) and contesting candidates (491). Results were declared for all 81 seats.

BJP won 45 seats and INC won 10. A footnote identifies the 14 seats in column 16 as AIMIM. The remaining seats went to NCP (2), Shiv Sena (4), unrecognized parties registered with the Commission (5) and an independent (1).

The [Commission’s publication register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the table to 16 January 2026.

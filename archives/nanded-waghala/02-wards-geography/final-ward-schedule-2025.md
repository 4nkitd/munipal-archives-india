# Nanded Waghala: final ward names and extent

## Notification and jurisdiction

Notification SEC/MC-2025/C.R.30/Desk-5 follows draft notification 128 of 3 September 2025. Under section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, the final schedule provides for 19 four-member wards and one five-member ward. It applies to the next general election.

Ward 1 covers Taroda Kd. Old Village and adjoining localities including Rajeshnagar and Belanagar. Its northern line starts at the northwest corner of Survey No. 19. In the south of the corporation, Ward 19 includes Mouje Fattejangpur, Asarjan, Kautha and Rahimpur. Ward 20 includes Mouje Asadwan, Waghala and part of CIDCO. The notification gives directional boundaries as well as place names.

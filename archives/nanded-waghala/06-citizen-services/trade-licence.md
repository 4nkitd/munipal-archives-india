# Nanded Waghala: applying for a new trade licence

## Information requested

The new trade licence form distinguishes rented, owned and leased premises. Select the firm type: proprietary, partnership, private limited, public limited or other. The applicant section asks for a name, father’s name, mobile number, ward, email and postal address. The business section separately asks for the owner’s name and the business ward.

The ward choices on this service form include codes such as 22A, 22B and 22C. These are form choices, not evidence of electoral wards. Use the [trade licence tracking page](https://nwcmc.gov.in/web/citizen/trade/trackstatus.php?uid=NDI2&id=ENG&cmd=Clear) for an existing application.

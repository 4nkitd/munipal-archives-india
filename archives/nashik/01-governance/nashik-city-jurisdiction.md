# Nashik city jurisdiction and service divisions

## City and municipal offices

The [Nashik district corporation and council directory](https://nashik.gov.in/en/corpocouncil/) distinguishes Nashik's city corporation from other urban bodies in the district. NMC's city profile gives a land area of about 259.13 square kilometres and describes the Godavari flowing through the old residential settlement. The city profile does not provide a legal boundary schedule for deciding whether an address lies in NMC.

NMC's headquarters is at Rajiv Gandhi Bhavan, Sharanpur Road, Nashik. The [municipal office directory](https://nmc.gov.in/home/getfrontpage/7/191/E) lists six administrative divisions: Nashik East, Nashik West, Nashik Road, Panchavati, Satpur and CIDCO, also called New Nashik in the directory. The same directory lists departmental offices by function, including water supply distribution, public works and town planning. These service divisions are separate from the numbered electoral wards in the [2025-26 final ward formation files](https://nmc.gov.in/home/getfrontpage/244/204/M).

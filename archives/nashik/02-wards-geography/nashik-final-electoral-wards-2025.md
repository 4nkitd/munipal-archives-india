# Nashik final electoral ward formation for the 2025-26 election

## Electoral layout

Marathi Appendix 14 sets 31 electoral wards under section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. The Maharashtra Government Gazette published it on 6 October 2025 in Extraordinary Part Two, Extraordinary No. 177. It sets 29 four-member wards and two three-member wards, making 122 seats. The notice cites draft notification No. 117 of 22 August 2025 and says that objections and suggestions were considered. The [Maharashtra State Election Commission's final 2025-26 results table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf), row 10, confirms the 31 wards and 122 seats.

## Find the final schedule or map

The [NMC election page](https://nmc.gov.in/home/getfrontpage/244/204/M) lists the gazette, a [separate copy of the notification](https://nmc.gov.in/assets/admin/upload/download/nmcelection2025-26_final/NMC_election_2025_adhisuchana.pdf), the [combined final map](https://nmc.gov.in/assets/admin/upload/download/nmcelection2025-26_final/NASHIK_2025_ALL_MAP_PDF_final.pdf) and maps for wards 1 to 31 under the final ward formation heading. Use that set for boundaries. The draft and final voter rolls and the later gazette naming elected members are separate documents. A municipal service division is not an electoral ward.

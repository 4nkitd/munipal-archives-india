# Nashik municipal water shutdown scheduled for 24 October 2026

## Scheduled work

The [NMC news listing](https://nmc.gov.in/home/news) identifies Saturday, 24 October 2026 as the shutdown date for repairing major pipeline leaks and carrying out urgent water-supply work in the city. The scan's first page groups works under Nashik East, Nashik West, Nashik Road and Panchavati. This list describes how that page groups the works; it is not a complete outage-area or electoral-ward list.

24 October is the scheduled work date, not a verified publication date. This record does not establish whether the work was completed or when normal supply resumed. Check the municipal news listing for newer water notices.

# Nashik civic applications, tax payments and complaints

## Applications and payments

The civic services catalogue distinguishes original birth and death certificates from duplicates and requests for minor corrections. Choose the matching service before opening its application. The catalogue also lists marriage certificates and, separately, property name transfer, nominee transfer, an assessment-register extract, self-assessment and a property-tax no-dues certificate.

Pay an existing bill through the [property tax portal](https://propertytax.nmctax.in/) or [water tax portal](https://watertax.nmctax.in/). A new water connection and a water-connection name transfer are applications, not payment items. NMC lists them under Water Supply Distribution on the [city site](https://nmc.gov.in/). The city site also routes building permission and completion certificates to the state's [MahaVastu portal](https://mahavastu.maharashtra.gov.in/).

## Complaints

NMC links its [grievance login](https://grievance.nmcutilities.in/login) from the city site and publishes 7030300300 as its complaint helpline. Its separate toll-free line is 1800 233 1982.

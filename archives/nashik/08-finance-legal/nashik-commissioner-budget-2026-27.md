# Nashik commissioner's 2026-27 budget and 2025-26 revised estimates

## Read the correct budget edition

The book identifies itself as the commissioner's estimates of receipts and expenditure under section 95 of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act. It presents 2026-27 estimates alongside revised 2025-26 estimates. The [Accounts Department](https://nmc.gov.in/home/getfrontpage/97/11/E) lists commissioner, Standing Committee and General Body budgets separately in its archive. This record covers the commissioner book, not later approved figures or audit results.

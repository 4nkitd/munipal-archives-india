# Navi Mumbai Municipal Corporation and CIDCO responsibilities

## Identify the issuing body

Thane district's directory gives Navi Mumbai Municipal Corporation its own entry and links to [NMMC](https://www.nmmc.gov.in/). Thane Municipal Corporation is a separate entry. NMMC's published municipal services include property-tax payment, water bills and birth and death certificates. Its head office is at Palm Beach Junction in CBD Belapur.

## What the 1970 date refers to

[CIDCO's formation record](https://cidco.maharashtra.gov.in/Page?Token=613552F54) dates the incorporation of the City and Industrial Development Corporation of Maharashtra Limited to **17 March 1970**, under the Companies Act, 1956. CIDCO records that the state issued its resolution the next day and gave the company new-town development authority status. This is the history of the development authority, not NMMC's incorporation date.

The [CIDCO portal](https://cidco.maharashtra.gov.in/) publishes its own plot-allotment notices, development permissions and payment services. A CIDCO lease or plot notice concerning Navi Mumbai therefore remains a CIDCO record. For a municipal tax bill, check the issuer on the bill before choosing a payment portal.

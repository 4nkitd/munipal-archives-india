# Navi Mumbai's 28 electoral wards and 111 seats in 2025-26

## Corporation result

The [SEC news register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the final party position to 16 January 2026. In row 4 of the result sheet, the commission counts 28 wards, 111 seats and 500 contesting candidates for Navi Mumbai. Results were declared for all 111 seats.

| SEC party or category with at least one seat | Seats won |
|---|---:|
| BJP | 65 |
| SS | 42 |
| SS - UBT | 2 |
| MNS | 1 |
| Independent | 1 |
| Total declared | 111 |

The table separates Navi Mumbai from Thane in row 2 and Panvel in row 9. The 111 figure counts council seats, not wards. This return gives no map, numbered ward boundaries or list of sectors against individual seats.

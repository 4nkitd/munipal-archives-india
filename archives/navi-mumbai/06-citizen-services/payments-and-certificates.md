# Navi Mumbai municipal bills, certificates and helplines

## Choose a municipal service

| Service listed by NMMC | What the portal offers |
|---|---|
| Property tax | Pay an NMMC property-tax bill online. The [property-tax page](https://www.nmmc.gov.in/property-tax) distinguishes the ledger from the bill. |
| Water bill | Pay a municipal water bill online. Check the supplier on the bill: CIDCO also publishes a separate water-charge payment service. |
| Birth certificate | Apply for or download a birth certificate. |
| Death certificate | Apply for or download a death certificate. |

## Contact the head office

NMMC lists **022-27567070** as its helpline and **1800 222 309** and **1800 222 310** as toll-free lines. It lists English and Marathi for all three. The published office address is NMMC Head Office, Plot No. 1, near Kille Gaothan, Palm Beach Junction, Sector 15 A, CBD Belapur, Navi Mumbai 400614. The sector identifies the office location, not the coverage of a municipal ward.

# Constitution and jurisdiction of the City of Navsari

The [larger urban area notification GV-01](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/P/Notification/Notification%20Related%20inclusion%20of%20Gram%20Panchayat%20for%20Formation%20of%20Making%20of%20Municipal%20Corporation%20for%20Navsari.pdf) names **Navsari-Vijalpor Municipality** and four gram panchayats: Dantej, Dharagiri, Eroo and Hansapore. The same day's [notification KV-02](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/P/Notification/Notification%20Related%20to%20Formation%20of%20Navsari%20Municipal%20Corporation.pdf) constituted the **Municipal Corporation of the City of Navsari** for that area with effect from 1 January 2025. It expressly excluded government land within the area from vesting in the corporation.

The [state notification index](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/notification_p.php) uses the shorter name *Navsari Municipal Corporation*. The [district office directory](https://navsari.nic.in/public-utility/navsari-nagarpalika/) uses *Navsari Mahanagarpalika*. The archive spelling `navsari-vijalpore` refers to the predecessor place name; the formation order spells it *Navsari-Vijalpor*.

# Transfer of municipal and gram panchayat employees to Navsari corporation

[Notification KV-03 of 1 January 2025](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/P/Notification/Notification%20Related%20to%20Absorption%20of%20Employee%20in%20Navsari%20Municipal%20Corporation.pdf) covers officers and staff employed before that date by Navsari-Vijalpor Municipality or by a gram panchayat listed in notification GV-01. They became employees of the Municipal Corporation of the City of Navsari.

Page 2 provides for their salaries and allowances, including pension, under the applicable rules until other provisions are made under the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949.

# Navsari ward and council seat determination

On 9 July 2025 Gujarat issued [notification KV-240](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/pdf/P/Notification/KV-240-Navsari.pdf). Page 2 determines that the City of Navsari shall have **13 wards and 52 councillors**.

| Category in the order | Seats reserved | Of these, seats reserved for women |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Scheduled Castes | 3 | 1 |
| Scheduled Tribes | 8 | 4 |
| Backward Classes | 14 | 7 |

The order reserves 26 of the 52 seats for women overall, including the women's seats in those three categories. It gives citywide totals but does not describe individual ward boundaries.

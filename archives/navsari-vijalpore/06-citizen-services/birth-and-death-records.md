# Birth and death records: Navsari district guidance and city enquiries

The [district service page](https://navsari.nic.in/service/birth-death-certificate/), last updated 20 February 2023, directs residents to [Gujarat's eOlakh portal](https://eolakh.gujarat.gov.in/). It says births and deaths must be reported to the registrar and are registered only at the place of occurrence.

The page predates the corporation and lists gram panchayat locations. For an enquiry about a record within the city, the [district's Navsari Mahanagarpalika directory entry](https://navsari.nic.in/public-utility/navsari-nagarpalika/) gives the general municipal office at Dudhiya Talav Ring Road, Asha Nagar, Navsari 396445, phone 02637250253 and email mc-nmc@gujarat.gov.in. It does not identify a dedicated civil-registration counter.

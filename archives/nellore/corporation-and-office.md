# Nellore Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## Municipal jurisdiction

The [state directory](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/open/ulbs) classifies Nellore as a corporation, ULB ID 64, in Andhra Pradesh. The municipal profile dates the municipality to 1866 and the corporation to 2004. The profile places the city on the Penna River and identifies water pipelines, sewerage treatment and park development as local AMRUT work. It also lists door-to-door waste collection and segregation at source. The corporation is one urban body within the larger Nellore district.

## Where to address a municipal enquiry

The district directory lists the office at Dargamitta, Nellore 524003 and gives 0861-2316777. The municipal contact record uses Ambedkar Nagar, Nellore 524003, and gives 1800 425 1113 as the toll-free number. Both entries give the Commissioner’s office mailbox commissioner_nellore@yahoo.co.in. The [municipal contact entry](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbcontacts/64) lists mc.nellore@cdma.gov.in. The [district directory entry](https://spsnellore.ap.gov.in/public-utility/nellore-municipal-corporation/) provides a separate check on the location.

# Nellore election wards, revenue wards and sachivalayams

## Counts in the municipal profile

The Andhra Pradesh municipal profile for Nellore carries an update timestamp of 14 October 2026. Its area field gives 149 square kilometres. The following counts refer to different units in that record.

| Profile field | Value |
|---|---:|
| Election wards | 72 |
| Revenue wards | 0 |
| Ward sachivalayams | 167 |

## Which figure to use

The revenue-ward field contains zero. The profile gives no accompanying schedule to show whether zero means no separate revenue wards or missing data. The 167 sachivalayams are service offices, not 167 electoral divisions. The [municipal About page](https://nellore.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/nellore/about) draws on this profile. It does not provide street limits, a ward-by-ward schedule or parcel assignments.

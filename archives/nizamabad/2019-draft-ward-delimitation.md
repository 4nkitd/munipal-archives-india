# Nizamabad's December 2019 draft delimitation of 60 wards

## Notice and public comment period

Nizamabad Municipal Corporation issued draft notification `NMC/Elections/28/2019` on 3 December 2019. It cited the Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019, Act No. 11 of 2019, and the Delimitation of Wards Rules in MA&UD G.O.Ms.No.78 of 29 June 2019. The notice invited views and suggestions from 3 December through 5 p.m. on 9 December 2019, the schedule cited as G.O.Rt.No.795 of 3 December 2019. This was a past comment period.

## What the draft records

The schedule lists each proposed ward's voter strength, localities and descriptions for its north, east, south and west edges. It uses survey numbers, roads, railway lines and landmarks. Examples below preserve the source's locality spellings and count units.

| Proposed ward | Localities shown | Voter strength |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | Kaloor, Khanapoor | 5,303 |
| 2 | Manikbhandar, Borgam(K) | 5,040 |
| 3 | Part of Manikbhandar, Gupanpally | 4,019 |
| 59 | Quilla Road, Ahmedpura | 4,920 |
| 60 | Hashmi Colony, Ahmedpura Colony, Malapally, Shanthi Nagar | 4,811 |

These are draft entries, not a present-day ward lookup. The [State Election Commission 2026 ward report](https://tsec.gov.in/preview.do?fileName=L1VwbG9hZHMvVFNFQy9wZGYvY2lyY3VsYXJfZ29zL25ld19maW5hbF9udWxsLnBkZg==#page=245) separately identifies wards 1–60 for the corporation, but does not establish that their boundaries match this draft. Armoor, Bodhan and Bheemgal have separate listings in the [district's municipal directory](https://nizamabad.telangana.gov.in/public-utility-category/municipality/). The district's [older town profile](https://nizamabad.telangana.gov.in/about-district/municipalities/) lists 50 wards; it is not the basis for the 2026 ward count.

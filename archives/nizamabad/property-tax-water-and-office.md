# Nizamabad property tax, water charges and office contacts

## Look up a bill

Use the [CDMA property tax page](https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/cdma_arbs/CDMA_PG/PTMenu) for house tax. The dues form asks for the 10-digit Property Tax Identification Number (PTIN). The search form also accepts an owner name or door number after selection of the district and urban local body. Select Nizamabad Municipal Corporation for a corporation property. The same menu has separate actions for assessment details, payment status and receipt printing. The corporation links [vacant land tax](https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/cdma_arbs/CDMA_VLT/VLTMenu) separately.

The [water charges page](https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/cdma_arbs/CDMA_PG/WTMenu) asks for a Water CAN number. Its account search also offers a Water Tax NO, owner name and door number. [Bill One](https://cdma.cgg.gov.in/CDMA_ARBS/billone/index) offers a linked-dues check by PTIN or registered mobile number; it requires one-time-password verification before displaying dues. No account lookup, OTP request or payment was submitted for this record.

## Corporation office and sanitation complaints

The [office contact page](https://nizamabadcorporation.telangana.gov.in/pages/office-contact) publishes `08462-221001` and `nmc.nzb1@gmail.com` or `nmc.nzb@gmail.com` for the corporation. It lists `14420` specifically for sanitation grievances. The postal address is opposite the NTR statue, Yellamma Gutta, Nizamabad, Telangana 503001.

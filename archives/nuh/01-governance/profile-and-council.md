# Municipal Council Nuh: profile and elected council

## Municipal status and geography

Nuh is the headquarters of Nuh district, formerly Mewat, in Haryana. Nuh was a Municipal Committee before the state provided for council status at district headquarters regardless of population. The [Haryana Municipal (Amendment) Act, 2020](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=CONTENTFILES%5Cc00d50eb-ad3e-4bca-8654-39d889a42a1d.pdf), Haryana Act No. 18 of 2020, amended section 2A(1) of the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973. The state published the Act on 19 September 2020. The [bill's statement of objects and reasons](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_states/haryana/2020/Bill%20No.%2024%20of%202020%20Haryana.pdf) identifies Nuh as the reason for the change. The [state municipal directory](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists Nuh as a council under Mewat, with an area of 7.21 sq km, a population figure of 25,956 and 9,571 properties. The directory does not give a reference year for those figures.

Nuh town lies in the Mewat Aravalli region. [National Highways Authority of India route records](https://nhai.gov.in/nhai/sites/default/files/StateWiseLengthNHsIndia.pdf) identify NH-248A through Nuh and Sohna, and NH-919 through Taoru and Sohna. The [Manesar formation notification of 24 December 2020](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/Municipal%20Corporation%20Manesar%20Formation%20Notification.pdf) places part of Manesar's edge along the Mewat district boundary. That is a boundary with Nuh district, not evidence of a shared boundary with Nuh Municipal Council.

## Latest verified municipal election and leadership

The Haryana State Election Commission's [2022 election programme and press note](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2022/06/2022060961.pdf) list **13 wards** for Municipal Council Nuh. The [Local Government Directory ward data as reproduced by Bharat Survey](https://bharatsurvey.org/urban-local-bodies/haryana/nuh) also show 13 wards for local body code 248581 as of 25 September 2026. No official notification supporting a count of 23 wards was found.

The latest Nuh council general election verified here was held on **19 June 2022**, with counting on **22 June 2022**. The [gazetted result, SEC/1ME/2022/6120, dated 4 July 2022](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2023/11/20231109536788481.pdf), pp. 2172 and 2173, records **Sanjay Kumar**, known in local reporting as **Sanjay Manocha**, as the directly elected president. He won the unreserved seat for the Jannayak Janta Party (JJP). [Dainik Bhaskar](https://www.bhaskar.com/local/haryana/news/haryana-municipal-election-2022-result-185-candidates-for-the-post-of-chairman-129965567.html) reported 2,704 votes for Manocha and 2,467 for independent runner-up Vishnu Singla, a margin of 237. [Amar Ujala identified Manocha as Nuh chairperson on 16 September 2026](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/palwal/waterlogging-everywhere-in-nuh-palwal-news-c-25-1-mwt1001-115559-2026-09-16), and [Hindusthan Samachar referred to him by his first name and title on 31 October 2026](https://www.hindusthansamachar.in/Encyc/2026/10/31/Sardar-Patel-laid-the-foundati.php). A current vice-president was not verified.

The [March 2025 municipal election programme](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/02/202502221607687499.pdf) listed Taoru Municipal Committee in Nuh district and a presidential by-election for Sohna Municipal Council in Gurugram district. Neither was a poll for Nuh Municipal Council. The [May 2026 municipal election programme](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2026/04/20260413724367870.pdf) did not list Municipal Council Nuh.

## Municipal office

The [Nuh district public utility directory](https://nuh.gov.in/public-utility/municipal-committee-nuh/) lists the municipal office at Civil Lines and the email `secymc.nuh@hry.nic.in`. It still uses the former label "Municipal Committee Nuh." For municipal services online, the district links to the [Haryana Urban Local Bodies portal](https://online.ulbharyana.gov.in/).

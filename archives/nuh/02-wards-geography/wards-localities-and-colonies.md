# Nuh wards, town localities and notified colonies

## Ward register and evidence limits

The district administration's MC Nuh final electoral-roll archive lists Wards 1 through 13, dated 24 March 2022. The first page contains Wards 4 through 13 and the second page contains the remaining ward records. These records establish an archived ward series, not a newly verified 2026 delimitation. No authenticated perimeter schedule or current ward-to-colony map was extracted for this record. The companion `wards.json` retains all 13 numbers and leaves unverified village, sector and colony assignments empty. Empty arrays mean unknown, not that a ward contains no colonies.

The Haryana ULB municipality figures page classifies Nuh as a council and reports 7.21 square kilometres. The district contact page still uses "Municipal Committee Nuh" and gives Civil Lines as its office address. These are different source labels, not evidence of two separate Nuh urban bodies.

Use the latest wardbandi notification and municipal GIS for a property's ward. Assembly polling areas, district village lists and an enterprise registration under "Nuh-MC" do not prove inclusion in the municipal boundary.

## Town mohallas, colonies and roads

The NIC Haryana Har Udhyam enterprise report records the following address evidence. Entries are applicant-supplied addresses and portal locality categories, not a sanctioned layout or legal ward schedule. The report also contains rural and industrial addresses outside Nuh town, so its administrative grouping must not be treated as a municipal map.

| Locality or landmark | Evidence and qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Hamid Colony | Multiple address entries explicitly say Ward 1, including an address near its mosque. |
| Saddik Nagar, also written Saddiq Nagar or Saddik Colony | A Ward 2 address appears in the report; other entries place the locality on Delhi-Alwar Road or near the HAFED godown. |
| Hidayat Colony | Addresses mention Wards 4 and 8. Other category combinations occur. Do not assign the entire locality to either ward. |
| Jatiya Mohalla | An explicit Ward 4 address names Madarsa Jamiya Siddiqia. |
| Meoli Road and Meoli Mor | Ward 4 appears in business addresses. This does not establish that Meoli village lies inside that ward. |
| Mahavar Mohalla, also written Mahawar | Registry category spelled "Mahavar Nohalla"; Gonda Ram Chowk entries use both Wards 10 and 11. Mapping remains unresolved. |
| Baghel Mohalla | Registry category occurs with Ward 5 and Ward 12 entries. No exclusive ward assignment is supported. |
| Purana Gurudwara and Punjabi Mohalla | Registry category and addresses use differing ward numbers, including 2, 4 and 13. |
| Aravali Colony | Address text names Tauru Road and the vicinity of Balaji petrol pump. No verified ward assignment. |
| Aazad Colony, Madina Colony, Freedom Colony, Javahar Colony, Dhimar Colony and Ekta Colony | Names occur in report categories. Their legal status and extents are not established by those entries. |
| Balmiki Mohalla, Old Nalhar Road | A tender aggregator's notice published 9 May 2025 describes an aspirational public toilet in Ward 11. Treat this as a works-location cross-reference, not a delimitation. |

Old and new bus stands, Delhi-Alwar Road, Civil Lines Road, Nalhar Road and Nangli Road are useful address search terms. Naming a locality here does not declare it regularized. Housing Board Colony also appears in enterprise address text; a Housing Board address is not proof of an HSVP sector.

## Colonies notified during the 2023 to 2026 research window

The controlling document located for Nuh is Haryana Government notification No. **09/47/2025-4C-II**, dated **22 May 2025**, issued under section 3 of the Haryana Management of Civic Amenities and Infrastructure Deficient Municipal Areas, Special Provisions, Act, 2016. Its English schedule names Municipal Council Nuh, district Nuh, for these two entries.

| Schedule serial | Colony and identifier | Acres | Revenue estate and khasra numbers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | Jitender Kumar and others colony, 219 | 18.37 | Nuh: 1//23min; 4//3, 4, 7, 8, 9min, 12min, 13/1, 13/2, 14, 17/1, 17/2, 17/3, 18, 19/1, 22, 23, 24; 7//1/2, 1/3, 2/1, 2/2, 3/1, 3/2, 4 |
| 12 | Vishnu Colony, 170 | 5 | Nuh: 50//13, 14/2, 14/3, 17, 18, 23, 24 |

These total 23.37 acres. The ULB consolidated list uploaded on 13 October 2025 repeats them at serials 504 and 505. That upload date is not their notification date. The portal heading "List Of Regularized Colony 2023 & 2024" also contains 2025 notifications.

The legal declaration is of civic amenities and infrastructure deficient areas. The notification ties boundaries to approved layouts and excludes specified restricted areas and uses subject to its conditions. It is not unconditional title validation or permission for every parcel advertised under the colony name. Ward assignments for both colonies remain unverified.

No separate Nuh-specific 2023, 2024 or 2026 colony declaration was verified in this research. This is not a finding that none exists. Obtain the approved layout, relevant khasra coverage and any later amendments before relying on a property's status.

## HSVP sectors and the Taoru corridor

Secondary scheme articles advertise "Nuh Sectors 1, 2 and 9", but no matching official Nuh-town layout or allotment schedule was verified. Those numbers are deliberately excluded from the sector arrays. A development-plan sector, an HSVP urban estate and an industrial sector are different geographical and administrative units.

There is official evidence for **Sector 11 Taoru**, in Nuh district. Haryana RERA's promoter submission for "Development of Pocket at Sector 11 Taoru Distt Nuh", temporary project ID RERA-PKL-PROJ-937-2021, identifies HSVP Faridabad and a 31.37-acre project land area. Its submission date is 27 June 2022. It describes Taoru, not a sector assigned to Nuh Municipal Council.

The ULB consolidated notified-colony list separately places **Mewat Green Udyog & Mehar Chand & Other Colony, Part-1 & Part-2**, 7.16 acres, under **Taoru**, district Nuh, at serial 579. It must not be merged into Nuh town's two-colony table. Taoru and Tauru are spelling variants used by the cited portals.

## IMT Sohna and the direction toward Manesar

Rojka Meo, also written Roz Ka Meo, is part of the industrial corridor toward Sohna. Haryana Water Resources Authority's Nuh industry compilation records addresses in the Rozka Meo industrial area and **Sector 20, IMT Sohna**, including plot EP-1. The district's village list separately records Rojka Meo. Neither source is a Nuh ward annexation notification.

For regional searches, distinguish the Nuh-to-Sohna route from the Taoru-side connection toward the Kundli-Manesar-Palwal expressway and Manesar. This is corridor context, not a continuous municipal boundary. IMT Sohna, Taoru, Sohna municipality and Municipal Corporation Manesar must retain separate jurisdiction records. An industrial estate address ending in "Nuh" may identify the district rather than Nuh town.

No cadastral boundary between these jurisdictions was verified here. A parcel inquiry needs its revenue estate, khasra number, approved development or estate layout, and the latest municipal-limit notification.

# Municipal Council Nuh officers and published contacts

## Office address and institutional contact

The Nuh district administration publishes the municipal office address as **Civil Lines, Municipal Committee Nuh**, and the email **secymc.nuh@hry.nic.in**. Its public-utility page has a displayed update date of 10 March 2018 and still uses the committee label. The Haryana Urban Local Bodies letter dated 25 June 2025 explicitly calls the body Municipal Council Nuh.

The Haryana eSamikSha nodal-officer listing, indexed as on 28 April 2025, describes the office as **Near Old DC Residence, Municipal Council Nuh**. A CAQM direction dated 29 September 2026 addresses the Executive Officer at **Near Sabzi Mandi, Civil Lines, Nuh, Haryana 122107**, using the committee label. These are source-specific address descriptions, not evidence of separate offices.

No Nuh municipal office landline or direct officer mobile was verified in the sources below. The email is an institutional contact, not a verified personal mailbox for any current incumbent.

# Nuh official notices, notified colonies and GRAP orders

## Scope and source dates

This is an archival index compiled on 22 November 2026, not a municipal order issued on that date. The primary source is the ULB [Civic Amenities and Infrastructure Deficient Areas register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure). Its Nuh rows use district "MEWAT", municipality "Nuh" and revenue estate "Nuh". These labels are preserved as source metadata, not treated as evidence that the district's current name is Mewat.

The separate [regularized-colony document index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) contains historical notifications and a 2025 status compilation. A website upload date is not necessarily the date on which a colony was notified. District-wide counts must not be assumed to be counts for Nuh town; Taoru and other municipalities have separate jurisdictions.

## Historical notification index and other official notices

The ULB [regularized-colony index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) lists "Municipalities Status of 836 Colonies Data Notified 2025", uploaded 13 October 2025, with this [status PDF](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2F012fa553-5f2b-4575-866d-034a892c23ac.pdf). It also lists "List of Regularized Colony 2023 & 2024", uploaded 2 February 2024, and notification batches dated 17 April, 25 July, 17 August, 6 October and 15 December 2023, plus later 2024 and 2025 batches.

Those index titles identify statewide document batches. They do not establish how many colonies within each batch belong to Nuh municipality. The 13 October 2025 status upload also predates the 11 December 2025 notification cited in the current Nuh civic-amenities rows. Do not merge the two source dates into a single regularization event.

The [ULB notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Notification) lists the Haryana Management of Civic Amenities and Infrastructure Deficient Municipal Areas, Special Provisions, Amendment Act, 2025, Haryana Act No. 26 of 2025, published 15 December 2025. This statewide legal listing is not a substitute for the individual Nuh colony notification.

Another official [town-planning notification dated 30 July 2026](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2Fbd3ef882-e9ce-44e2-ae6b-799af043d27c.pdf), No. `09/58/2026-4CII`, publishes directions for residential plotted-housing town-planning schemes where controlled-area provisions do not apply. Annexure A includes Municipal Council Nuh. It is a planning-scheme instrument, not a declaration that every existing unauthorized colony in Nuh has been regularized.

## GRAP framework applicable to Nuh

The Commission for Air Quality Management issued a [revised GRAP schedule on 29 September 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/fa4070ca-5b45-4161-b220-0871227ae9ea.pdf), under Section 12 of the CAQM Act, 2021, file `A-110018/01/2021-CAQM`. It applies to NCR agencies within their jurisdictions when the relevant stage is invoked. Its distribution list expressly includes the Deputy Commissioner, Nuh, Second Floor, Mini Secretariat, and the Executive Officer, Municipal Committee Nuh, Near Sabzi Mandi, Civil Lines, PIN 122107.

| Stage | Category | Delhi AQI range in the schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Poor | 201 to 300 |
| II | Very Poor | 301 to 400 |
| III | Severe | 401 to 450 |
| IV | Severe+ | Above 450 |

The schedule permits advance invocation based on forecasts. Higher-stage measures are additional to the preceding stages. The table is not a rule that residents can determine the operative legal stage merely by reading a local AQI value.

Municipal duties in the schedule include waste lifting, preventing illegal dumping and open burning, road sweeping, dust disposal and water sprinkling. Stage II intensifies road-dust control and inspections. Stage III contains restrictions on listed dust-generating construction and demolition activities, with defined exemptions and conditions. Consult the full schedule and invocation orders before deciding whether a particular project can continue.

## Dated GRAP order and limits on current-status claims

The [CAQM order dated 17 November 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/b91570de-8cc1-4735-8879-28f2bc1550f2.pdf) revoked Stage III measures invoked on 6 November 2026. It stated that Stage I and Stage II measures remained in force under the schedule revised on 29 September 2026. The order's distribution list includes the Nuh Deputy Commissioner and municipal Executive Officer.

The order also stated that a separate CAQM order of 30 October 2026 continued to govern specified construction and office-attendance measures for the stated period from 5 to 20 November. That period must not be silently extended beyond 20 November. It further specified that sites subject to individual closure orders could not resume without a specific reopening order.

This is a historical description of the 17 November order, not certification of the operative GRAP stage on 22 November 2026 or on a reader's later visit. Subsequent CAQM orders may invoke, amend or revoke measures. Read the latest official order and any project-specific closure direction before acting.

## Verification notes

Colony names, acreages, codes, khasra numbers and the cited 11 December 2025 notification date were read from the official HTML register. Layout URLs are preserved for inspection, but the maps and their boundaries were not independently verified. The linked PDFs are not stored in this archive.

The root Nuh URL currently renders the statewide ULB website and includes notices for other towns. Such notices are not automatically Nuh municipal notices. Only records with an explicit Nuh entry, an applicable statewide scope or an NCR-wide scope are identified above.

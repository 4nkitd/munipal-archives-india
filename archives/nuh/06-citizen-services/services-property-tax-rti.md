# Nuh citizen services: property tax, certificates, RTI and grievances

## Scope and municipal office

This is a service-source compilation dated 22 November 2026, not a new notification. The district directory calls the office "Municipal Committee Nuh" and gives Civil Lines as its address, with email secymc.nuh@hry.nic.in. Its displayed update date is 10 March 2018, so the email is an officially published contact, not a confirmed current officer appointment. A 30 July 2026 ULB town-planning notification lists "Municipal Council Nuh" in Annexure A. Older service pages and even some newer circulation lists retain the committee designation.

The ULB toll-free number 1800-8900-929 is for departmental IT support and grievance registration, available Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 6 PM. It is not a dedicated Nuh municipal telephone number. Do not send identity documents to an unverified personal contact.

## Property tax, Property ID and No Dues Certificate

Use the [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for property records and the services linked in the ULB Right to Service schedule. Select the appropriate Nuh municipal record before making a payment or requesting a correction. Keep the Property ID, application acknowledgement and payment receipt.

The department's instruction dated 27 March 2024 explains self-certification. A citizen locates or links the property, selects "View Details/Self Certify", then reviews property details, mobile number, owner details and outstanding dues. Incorrect information should be flagged rather than certified as correct. The instruction explains OTP verification routes with and without Family ID or Aadhaar. It explicitly states that property-tax information is not ownership proof.

The state schedule sends applications for a new Property ID, owner/occupier changes and objections on the No Dues Certificate portal to the property portal. Objections include dues, size, floor details, authorized or unauthorized status and property category. A tax payment or an NDC is not itself proof that a colony or building has planning approval.

The [property-tax notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists a councils-and-committees notification published on 31 July 2026 and an interest-waiver notification published on 18 May 2026. This guide does not reproduce their rates or assume that a rebate remains available. Check the actual applicable notification and assessment year. Rebates described in the March 2024 self-certification letter are historical.

## Birth and death certificates

The [Nuh district birth-certificate page](https://nuh.gov.in/service/birth-certificate/) directs citizens to inform the jurisdictional registrar within 21 days and identifies Municipal Committee Nuh, PIN 122107. The [Haryana Health Department civil-registration page](https://haryanahealth.gov.in/service/civil-registration-system/) confirms that registration is at the place where the event occurred, not automatically at the applicant's present residence.

Use [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) for the relevant service. The Health Department's registration material identifies separate applications for a birth/death certificate or non-availability certificate, correction of an entry, inclusion of a child's name and delayed registration. Choose the matching service rather than submitting a second registration for an event already recorded.

For hospital events, establish which registrar holds the record. Nalhar medical college has its own officially published birth-and-death registration department; a birth in Nuh district is not necessarily registered in the Nuh municipal register. Delayed registration, corrections and additional copies can have different documents and charges. Confirm the current checklist and fee shown for the selected application.

## Marriage registration

The official Nuh service entry is titled [Application for Marriage Registration](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Nuh/2604?DynamicTypeIdView=7333). It remains discoverable in the search index, but legacy Nuh detail routes may return an error or redirect after the state website redesign. The statewide ULB citizen-service menu also offers marriage registration and application tracking.

Start from [SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) or the current [ULB homepage](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/) and choose the marriage-registration service appropriate to the applicant and jurisdiction. Retain the application number and follow the official document, attendance and fee instructions. This archive does not verify a Nuh-specific fee, processing period or current registrar appointment. Registration of an existing marriage and solemnization under the Special Marriage Act are different procedures.

For missed service limits, use the designated service grievance authorities and preserve the application date and acknowledgement. For IT failures or grievance registration, use the departmental toll-free number above. The ULB website says Harpath is available through the Jan Sahayak mobile application, in a notice published 13 December 2024. Entries expressly limited to water/sewer services in Gurugram, Faridabad, Karnal and Sonepat must not be presented as Nuh service guarantees.

## RTI requests and appeals

The [Nuh district RTI page](https://nuh.gov.in/right-to-information/) explains that Indian citizens can request information from a public authority and that the ordinary response period is 30 days. RTI is a route to existing records, such as an assessment order, application movement record or certified municipal document. It is not a substitute for submitting a tax correction or certificate application.

The [Haryana RTI portal](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/) accepts online RTI applications and first appeals for Haryana public authorities, with an online payment gateway. Its instructions say that information from a divisional or district-level office should be routed by selecting the related headquarters/directorate from the authority list. Clearly identify Municipal Council Nuh and the records requested. Check the public-authority and SPIO/FAA directories before submission; no current Nuh PIO name or specific Haryana RTI fee is asserted here.

The portal recommends a computer or laptop rather than a mobile interface. It states that second appeals are not available online and should be filed manually by post or hand in triplicate under Rule 6 of the Haryana Right to Information Rules, 2009, with the specified supporting papers. Check that instruction again before filing. RTI appeals and Right to Service grievances are separate remedies.

# Nuh tenders, budgets and infrastructure, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and evidence

This record concerns Nuh town, not every project in Nuh district. Municipal procurement appears under the organisation chain Haryana Government, Urban Local Bodies, MC Nuh. Public Health Engineering Department procurement for Nuh town is recorded separately below. Works in Tauru, Punhana, Firozpur Jhirka and rural blocks are not treated as Municipal Council Nuh projects.

The official Haryana Engineering Works Portal, HEWP, provides links to [live tenders](https://works.haryana.gov.in/LiveTender.aspx) and [ongoing works in your area](https://works.haryana.gov.in/Works_Under_Village.aspx). Its public homepage describes estimate preparation, contractor registration, work assessment and payments. These links are official access points, not evidence that a particular Nuh work has been completed.

The tender tables below reproduce publicly accessible tender-index records. They are secondary transcriptions of Haryana eProcurement information, not independently inspected signed notices or measurement books. Rounded amounts retain the precision published by the index. Award reports do not establish expenditure, physical completion, commissioning or current service quality. The official external-detail link inspected during research returned only the portal heading, so no unavailable official tender document is presented as reviewed.

The frontmatter date is the compilation date. Individual publication and award dates appear below.

## Documents needed to establish delivery

For a work-specific enquiry, cite the tender ID and request the signed NIT, sanctioned estimate, administrative and technical approvals, award letter, agreement, measurement-book entries, running and final bills, and completion certificate. For water and sewerage, seek PHED commissioning records and service or laboratory data separately. For annual finances, request the council-approved budget and subsequent revised estimates, grant-release orders and utilisation certificates.

These records answer different questions. An advertised estimate is not a contract price, an award is not a paid bill, and a completed civil structure is not proof of a functioning public service.

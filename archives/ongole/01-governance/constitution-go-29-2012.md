# G.O.Ms.No. 29 constituting Ongole Municipal Corporation

## Original corporation area

G.O.Ms.No. 29 is dated 21 January 2012 and directs publication of the notification in the Andhra Pradesh Extraordinary Gazette dated 25 January 2012. The notification specifies Ongole Municipality in Prakasam district and eight adjacent gram panchayats as a larger urban area under section 2(d) of the Andhra Pradesh Municipal Corporations Act, 1994. It names the area Ongole Municipal Corporation, with immediate effect. The [corporation's civic history](https://ongole.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/ongole/about) dates the upgrade to 25 January 2012.

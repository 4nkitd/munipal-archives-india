# G.O.Ms.No. 416 approving Ongole's 50-ward delimitation in 2012

## Historical delimitation approval

The state government approved delimitation of Ongole Municipal Corporation into 50 wards on 3 November 2012 under rule 11 of the Andhra Pradesh Municipal Corporations (Delimitation of Wards) Rules, 1996. The order says the draft was published for suggestions and objections and that some boundaries were revised in response. It directs the municipal authorities to publish the approved delimitation in Form VI in the Andhra Pradesh Gazette.

The linked order refers to an annexed schedule, but the copy available from the Government Orders Issue Register contains only the two-page order. It does not support an address-level boundary transcription. The 50-ward count belongs to the 2012 edition. For the 2026 election edition, see the [Ongole ward reservation schedule](ward-reservations-2026.md), which lists 62 wards.

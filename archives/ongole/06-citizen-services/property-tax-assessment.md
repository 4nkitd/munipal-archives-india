# Find an Ongole property tax assessment

## Search the municipal register

The [Ongole municipal home page](https://ongole.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/ongole/home) links to the corporation's property tax lookup under **Know Your Dues**. The search form offers Assessment Number, Old Assessment Number, Owner Name and Door Number. If you have a municipal notice or tax receipt, use its assessment number and confirm that the property details in the result match.

If a record needs a change rather than a lookup, return to the municipal portal. It lists separate services for self-assessment, revision petition, mutation, vacancy remission and downloading proceedings. This guide records the available form, not a payment or an assessment determination.

# Historical election record: Pali Municipal Council in 2019

## November 2019 poll

The State Election Commission labels Pali a **Nagar Parishad** in its report on the 2019 municipal general election. The Pali city row (36) records 65 wards and 163,236 electors. Its turnout table shows 116,470 votes polled and 71.35% turnout. Sumerpur appears separately in the next row; its totals do not form part of Pali city's figures.

The report's preface is dated 20 January 2020. Its electoral rolls and council classification describe the 2019 poll, before the 2024 upgrade. The matching count of 65 wards in the later corporation schedule does not establish that any particular boundary stayed the same.

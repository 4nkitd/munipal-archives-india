# Pali city corporation in the state local bodies directory

## Distinguishing Pali city from Pali district

Row 238 lists **Pali** as both district and urban local body, classifies the body as a Nagar Nigam and gives 65 wards. Its institutional email is `mcpali.lsg@rajasthan.gov.in`. The row links directly to the corporation's ward boundary schedule.

Marwar Junction (row 237), Rani Khurd (239) and Sumerpur (243) are separate Nagar Palikas in the same district. Each has its own ward schedule and municipal contact.

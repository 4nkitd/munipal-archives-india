# Pali ward boundaries approved in September 2025

## Reading Form A

In Part 6(क) of the 20 September 2025 Extraordinary Gazette the state approved Pali's ward formation after considering objections forwarded through the Collector. Notification DLB/25/17861 cites sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994.

Ward 1 starts on PDF page 1 at the Jawai canal crossing opposite the CMHO office on Jodhpur Road. The schedule gives a population of 3,838. Ward 65 appears on page 21, lists 3,995 and includes the RTO office and Bangar Stadium in its boundary description. These are schedule populations.

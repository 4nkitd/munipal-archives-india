# Pali Municipal Council upgraded to a municipal corporation

## Change in legal status

The department lists notification **2741**, issued on **2 September 2024**, for the upgrade of Nagar Parishad Pali in Pali district to Nagar Nigam Pali. The Municipal Council named in the 2019 election report is the predecessor body. The Rajasthan Gazette of 20 September 2025 uses the corporation name in its approval of Pali's ward boundaries.

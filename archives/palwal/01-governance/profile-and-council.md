# Municipal Council Palwal: Profile and elected council

## Jurisdiction and regional links

Municipal Council Palwal is the urban local body for Palwal city, the district headquarters in southern Haryana's National Capital Region. Its 31 wards are within the municipality, not the district as a whole. The [Haryana Urban Local Bodies key figures](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) list Palwal as a council with 59.17 sq km, a population figure of 2,32,301 and 99,330 properties. The table does not state a population reference year, and the property total is not a household count. The older [2011 Census handbook](https://censusindia.mha.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/461) counted 1,31,926 people for Palwal Municipal Council plus its then-listed outgrowth. The figures describe different geographic and reporting bases.

The 1983 Gurgaon District Gazetteer's [chapter on local government](https://docslib.org/doc/4406752/a-progressive-account-of-the-development-of-local) traces Palwal's municipal history to a Class III municipality constituted in 1864. That date refers to the historical municipality, not the creation of Palwal district, which the [district history](https://palwal.gov.in/history/) dates to August 2008.

Palwal is at the southern end of the Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway, also known as the Western Peripheral Expressway. The Union Ministry of Road Transport and Highways [inaugurated the 52.33 km Manesar-Palwal section on 5 April 2016](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=138596). It intersects NH 919, the separate Palwal-Sohna-Rewari road corridor, and connects to the Delhi-Mathura highway near Palwal. These are regional road links, not extensions of the council's jurisdiction. The municipal office is at Committee Chowk. The [district directory entry](https://palwal.gov.in/public-utility/municipal-council-palwal/) gives `01275-252526` and `secymc.palwal@hry.nic.in`.

## Elected leadership and ward representation

The Haryana Municipal Act, 1973 provides for direct election of the President and ward members; the council elects its Vice-President from among its elected members. See [sections 9 and 18 as amended in 2019](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=CONTENTFILES%5C5376aac8-45d2-4eb0-8e2c-3a9fcededafa.pdf).

## Election status

The latest verified general election for Municipal Council Palwal took place on 19 June 2022. Results were declared on 22 June. [The Tribune's count report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/bjp-rebel-bags-top-post-in-hodal-civic-body-elections-406164/) gives Yashpal 23,423 votes and runner-up Dheeraj Kumar, an Independent, 20,667, a margin of 2,756. The July gazette recorded the elected President and ward members.

The State Election Commission's [municipal notification index](https://secharyana.gov.in/orders-notifications-related-to-municipal-elections-4/orders-notifications-related-to-municipal-elections/) did not show a later Palwal council general election. Its by-election result notices `SEC/1ME/2025/4353` and `SEC/1ME/2026/2384` do not list Municipal Council Palwal. [Punjab Kesari reported on 6 August 2026](https://haryana.punjabkesari.in/haryana/news/preparations-for-local-body-elections-begin-in-66-cities-of-haryana-2365826) that Palwal was among the councils included in ward delimitation preparations for the expected 2027 cycle. As of 22 November 2026, no Palwal polling date or final replacement ward notification was verified. Consult the Commission for the election schedule and Haryana Urban Local Bodies for final ward boundaries.

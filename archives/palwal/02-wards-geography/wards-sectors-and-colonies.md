# Palwal wards, sectors, villages and regularized colonies

## The 31-ward directory and its limits

The LGD-derived directory for Municipal Council Palwal, local body code 248586, lists Ward 1 through Ward 31 as of 25 September 2026. It does not supply locality names for those wards. The district's official documents index separately provides two Ward 22 electoral-roll files dated 16 March 2022. The State Election Commission's notification dated 4 July 2022 records results of the municipal election held on 19 June 2022.

The accompanying `../wards.json` preserves all 31 ward numbers. Empty locality arrays mean that no reliable ward allocation was established during this research, not that the ward has no villages or colonies. Null zone and division fields mean that no verified administrative assignment was found. In particular, a shop address using a ward number is not enough to assign an entire colony to that ward.

For a property-specific answer, obtain the latest final wardbandi notification and map, electoral-roll part, and property identification record from the council. Haryana's official GIS portal describes ward, colony and municipal boundary layers, but this archive has not verified Palwal's individual polygons. Neither Sector 2's layout nor a colony regularization notification substitutes for a ward boundary.

## Sector 2 and the established urban localities

| Place | Evidence and geographic use | Ward assignment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HSVP Sector 2, also called HUDA Sector 2 | HSVP hosts a residential part-demarcation plan, drawing DTP(PAL) 55/14 dated 18 March 2014, in its January 2026 auction directory. The plan marks parks, a primary school site, shopping centre and 9 m and 18 m roads. | Not verified |
| Sector 2 shopping centre | A separate HSVP revised layout-cum-demarcation plan identifies the shopping centre, booths, SCO sites and public-use sites. This is planning evidence, not proof that every shown facility has been built. | Not verified |
| Old GT Road | A 4 July 2025 Jagran report places a PWD drain-clearance operation between Committee Chowk and Minar Gate, with earlier work toward HUDA Chowk. Bhawan Kund is another named point on this urban corridor. | Not verified |
| Camp Colony and Camp Market | Locality references identify Camp Colony among Palwal's established urban neighbourhoods. Colony and market names should be retained as address terms, not treated as statutory boundaries. | Not verified |
| New Colony | The locality guide identifies New Colony as a residential and commercial area associated with the highway and bypass road. Its historic NH-2 wording is retained only as source context. | Not verified |
| Krishna Colony | Independently listed as a Palwal locality. No colony-wide ward assignment is established here. | Not verified |
| Agra Chowk | Identified in the locality guide as a commercial junction and market area. It is a landmark, not a ward or a notified colony polygon. | Not verified |

The commercial locality guide is secondary evidence for names only. Its promotional descriptions, distances and road classifications are not adopted as official geography. Sector 2 is the normalized sector label in frontmatter; HSVP is the current authority name, while HUDA remains common in older plans and addresses.

## Merged villages and revenue-estate names

A September 2020 Repco News report, quoting a political representative, names Alapur, Aghwanpur, Naya Gaon or Fazalpur, and Firozpur as villages previously included in Palwal's municipal area. A November 2024 Mazdoor Morcha report dates the Firozpur-Aghwanpur inclusion to 2009. These are reported historical inclusions, not a verified exhaustive merger schedule. The original municipal-limit gazette and its cadastral map are still needed to establish inclusion dates and the extent of each estate.

Stronger primary evidence exists for specific colony sites. The 14 November 2024 gazette places municipal colony entries in Dholagarh, Khursrupur, Palwal and Fazalpur revenue estates. It does not say that each entire estate was merged through that notification. Preserve its English spelling "Khursrupur" until reconciled with revenue records.

The contemporary Amar Ujala report instead calls the estate for colony 263 "Kuslipur". That conflicts with the gazette and is not silently substituted here. Bedha Patti and Garhi Patti extension reports concern Municipal Council Hodal, in Palwal district, not Municipal Council Palwal. Likewise, controlled-area declarations around the Yamuna and Jewar airport corridor are planning controls, not municipal-merger orders.

## Seven colonies notified on 14 November 2024

The Haryana Government Gazette, Extraordinary, 14 November 2024, schedule entries 26 through 32, lists the following under Municipal Council Palwal. English schedule pages 3698 and 3699 provide the revenue estates and khasra descriptions. The official October 2025 status compilation repeats these seven colony IDs and acreages.

| Gazette serial | Colony ID | Revenue estate in English gazette | Area in acres |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 26 | 464 | Dholagarh | 2.45 |
| 27 | 263 | Khursrupur | 5.03 |
| 28 | 289 | Palwal | 1.05 |
| 29 | 463 | Dholagarh | 11.70 |
| 30 | 343 | Palwal | 37.57 |
| 31 | 369 | Palwal | 3.07 |
| 32 | 328 | Fazalpur | 15.23 |

The total is 76.10 acres. These are colony IDs, not ward numbers. Examples of cadastral references are `158//20/3, 20/4, 21/1` for colony 289 and `330//6min, 7min, 8min, 12/2min, 13, 14, 15min, 18/2min` for colony 369. Use the complete gazette schedule and notified polygon for title or eligibility checks.

The 21 November 2024 Amar Ujala report transposes the 463 and 464 acreages, prints 284 rather than 289, describes colony 263 as Kuslipur, and gives 15.27 acres for colony 328. This archive follows the primary gazette, supported by the later official status compilation. Regularization does not establish that roads, sewerage or water supply were already delivered. The Tribune reported on 2 December 2024 that development work in the seven colonies had not yet begun.

## Reading the 2023 to 2026 record

The DULB notification index contains 2023 and 2024 gazettes and later 2025 additions. Its upload dates must not be mistaken for notification dates. For example, the 14 November 2024 notification is displayed with a 26 September 2025 portal date. The 836-colony municipal status compilation is listed on 13 October 2025.

Statewide or district-wide announcements have a different scope. A February 2024 announcement reported nine ULB colonies in Palwal and 44 Town and Country Planning colonies in Palwal district. June 2024 reporting describes the 44 as rural colonies outside municipal limits, divided among Prithla, Hathin, Hodal and Palwal revenue areas. Those figures cannot be added to the seven November entries to produce a verified city total without checking IDs, polygons and possible overlap.

No additional Palwal-specific regularization or final ward-boundary amendment for 2026 was verified in this research. The 2026 HSVP auction-directory plans confirm the availability of Sector 2 planning documents, not a new municipal ward scheme. This record therefore separates verified gazette entries from reported village history and unresolved ward geography.

## Executive Officer uncertainty

The available sources do not establish the current EO consistently. News coverage names Sanjay Kumar at the September 2025 NDMC event. Official ULB Palwal pages indexed with an update date of 19 January 2026 name Sanjay Kumar as a website nodal officer, which is not itself an appointment order. A DAY-NULM Haryana SUDA social post dated 30 June 2026 refers to "EO Maninder Singh". The social post is insufficient to replace an official appointment record.

For that reason, `officers.json` retains Sanjay Kumar only as a dated historical identification and states that the current EO is unresolved. Ask the municipal office to confirm the incumbent before addressing a personal representation. The email secymc.palwal@gmail.com occurs in the 2025 municipal order and eSamikSha listing. The district directory instead publishes secymc.palwal@hry.nic.in. Both are source-specific office addresses; neither is represented here as an individual officer's personal email.

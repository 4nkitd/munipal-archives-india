# Palwal official notices, notified colonies and GRAP orders

## Scope and source limits

This is an index of official records relevant to Municipal Council Palwal, not a new notification or a certificate for any plot. The colony data below are transcribed from DULB's [Civic Amenities and Infrastructure Deficient Areas register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure), rows 509 through 531. Hodal entries under the same district heading are excluded because Hodal is a different municipality.

The register gives colony identifiers, acreage, revenue estates, khasra schedules, layout links and notification references. A colony identifier is not a ward number or Property ID. Inclusion of a colony does not establish that every parcel bearing its informal name is within the notified boundary. Match the notification, khasra schedule and linked layout before relying on a property's status.

The frontmatter date refers to the CAQM order of 17 November 2026 summarized below. The verification date records review of the public sources, not a certification of current GRAP stage or land title.

## GRAP directions applicable to Palwal

CAQM's [direction dated 29 September 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/fa4070ca-5b45-4161-b220-0871227ae9ea.pdf), file `A-110018/01/2021-CAQM`, issues the revised GRAP schedule under section 12 of the CAQM Act, 2021. Its circulation includes the Deputy Commissioner, Palwal and a Municipal Council Palwal addressee.

The schedule uses Delhi AQI as the trigger framework across NCR, subject to invocation orders. It defines Stage I at AQI 201 to 300, Stage II at 301 to 400, Stage III at 401 to 450 and Stage IV above 450. Higher stages add to lower-stage measures. The schedule alone does not mean every stage is continuously in force.

Municipal duties include covered storage and transport of construction material and waste, lifting municipal and construction waste, preventing illegal dumping, road sweeping and water sprinkling, and enforcing prohibitions on open burning of biomass and municipal solid waste. Stage II adds daily mechanical/vacuum sweeping and sprinkling on identified roads, hotspot action and stricter inspection of construction-site dust controls.

| --- | --- |
| 2 November 2026 | [CAQM Stage II invocation](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/011a2816-839f-43e5-99df-e5a4019b6bb2.pdf) invokes Stage II throughout NCR and continues Stage I. |
| 17 November 2026 | [CAQM Stage III revocation](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/b91570de-8cc1-4735-8879-28f2bc1550f2.pdf) revokes the Stage III invocation of 6 November and continues Stages I and II. |

The 17 November order separately preserves the 30 October 2026 precautionary order for its stated 5 to 20 November period. That order restricted dust-generating demolition and outdoor construction across NCR, with specified exemptions. Its 50 percent office-attendance provision names Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat, Ghaziabad and Gautam Buddha Nagar, not Palwal. Do not extend that geographically limited office rule to Palwal merely because Palwal is in NCR.

The revocation also says sites individually closed for violations cannot resume without specific permission. This archive records the instruments reviewed; it does not establish whether a subsequent order changed the stage after 17 November 2026. Check the current CAQM orders before starting restricted work.

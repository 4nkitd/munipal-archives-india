# Municipal Council Palwal citizen services, property tax and RTI

## Scope and municipal contact

The District Palwal municipal-services page lists birth and death registration, marriage certificates, property tax, building permissions, occupation certificates and other municipal services. Its page footer records an update on 4 September 2023. Some links are older service addresses, so this guide distinguishes that district directory from the current state service links.

The district's [Municipal Council Palwal contact entry](https://palwal.gov.in/public-utility/municipal-council-palwal/) gives Committee Chowk, Palwal, telephone 01275-252526 and email secymc.palwal@hry.nic.in. This is an office contact, not a verified individual PIO appointment.

The frontmatter date is the publication date of the state's 2026 property-tax notification for councils and committees. This record combines several service sources and is not itself a municipal notification. Public information pages were checked on 22 November 2026; no application or payment was submitted.

## Property tax, Property ID and NDC

Use the [Haryana property-tax and No Dues Certificate portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). Its public landing page provides new-user registration, NRI/foreign-national registration, application-status checking and a tax calculator labelled for the policy dated 30 July 2026. The [public property-search page](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/propertylist/propertysearch) is a separate entry point for locating a property.

Select the Palwal municipal record and check its Property ID and recorded particulars before payment or a correction request. Retain the application number, tax receipt and generated certificate. A payment receipt and an NDC are different records; this guide does not treat one as proof that the other has been issued.

The [DULB property-tax instruction index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists:

| Publication date | Official entry |
| --- | --- |
| 31 July 2026 | Property tax notification 2026 Councils and Committees |
| 18 May 2026 | One-time waiver of interest on property-tax arrears for 2010-11 to 2024-2025 |
| 27 March 2024 | Self-certification of property information |
| 15 March 2024 | Property certificates for occupiers within Abadi Deh/Lal Dora of municipal areas |
| 26 September 2023 | Property Tax Management System SOP and SOP for creation of Split ID |

These are index descriptions. This record does not reproduce tax slabs, waiver deadlines or eligibility without reading the applicable instrument. In particular, an archived waiver entry does not establish that a waiver remains available.

## Birth, death and marriage registration

The [District Palwal municipal-services directory](https://palwal.gov.in/municipal-council/) links birth and death registration to the Civil Registration System at `http://crsorgi.gov.in/web/index.php/auth/login`. The current DULB citizen-service menu describes applications for certificates, correction of birth/death records, delayed registration and inclusion of a child's name in a birth record.

For an existing record, distinguish a certificate request from correction or delayed registration. Ask the local registrar which application applies and preserve the registration or application number. The sources checked here do not establish a Palwal-specific fee schedule, document checklist or guaranteed completion period for these civil-registration services.

For marriage, the district directory links to [DULB's marriage-certificate service address](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/marriage-certificate.html). DULB's service menu describes marriage registration and application tracking. If an older link opens a general landing page, use the current marriage-service menu or ask the municipal office for the active application route. The [SARAL portal](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) is also the state application route listed for several municipal services in the RTS schedule. This guide does not assume every marriage application follows the same enactment or checklist.

## RTI applications and appeals

The [Haryana RTI portal](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/) accepts RTI applications and first appeals from Indian citizens for Haryana government public authorities. It provides electronic fee-payment options. Its instructions say that information sought from divisional or district-level offices should be routed through the relevant headquarters/directorate in the public-authority list, rather than selecting a Commissioner/District office merely because of location.

For council records, identify Municipal Council Palwal explicitly and specify the records, period and application or Property ID where relevant. Check the available public-authority list and the [DULB PIO list](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList) before choosing the authority. No current Palwal-specific PIO or first appellate officer was verified in the sources used here.

The portal states that second appeals must presently be filed manually by post or by hand in triplicate under rule 6 of the Haryana Right to Information Rules, 2009. Follow its current instructions for the required papers. The [District Palwal DC-office RTI page](https://palwal.gov.in/dc-office/) concerns the Deputy Commissioner's establishment and specified district SPIOs; its appellate appointments must not be assumed to cover the municipal council.

## Grievances and service follow-up

DULB publishes 1800-8900-929 for IT-services support and grievance registration, available Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 6 PM. Its RTS page links the Haryana municipal citizen mobile application for solid-waste complaints and street-light replacement. The [latest-news index](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/LatestNewsAndEvents) also lists eSamadhan DULB Haryana Citizen and records on 13 December 2024 that Harpath is available through the Jan Sahayak mobile application.

Record the complaint number, exact location, date and service category. Use the relevant RTS grievance authority when a notified service is delayed. An RTI request seeks existing records; it is not a replacement for the original certificate application, tax objection or sanitation complaint.

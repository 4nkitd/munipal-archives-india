# Palwal tenders, budgets and infrastructure, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and procurement portals

This is a selected source register, not a complete works ledger. Haryana's Engineering Works Portal states that tenders floated through HEWP are published on the NIC Haryana eProcurement website for bidder participation. HEWP's live-tender list is updated with those tenders the following day. Search the NIC portal using the tender IDs below and check corrigenda, tender status and award documents before relying on an old deadline. [S1]

The municipal tenders below identify the organisation chain as Haryana Government, Urban Local Bodies, MC Palwal. A tender value is the advertised value, not proof of the accepted bid, payment or completion. The published period of work is a contract parameter, not a verified completion date. No award or completion is asserted here.

The district administration lists Municipal Council Palwal at Committee Chowk, telephone 01275-252526 and email secymc.palwal@hry.nic.in. [S2] Ward numbers in the body reproduce tender titles. The frontmatter uses an empty ward array because this is a municipality-wide compilation.

## Annual budgets and reported provisions

Amar Ujala reported on 11 March 2025 that the council passed its 2025-26 budget at a meeting chaired by Dr Yashpal. These figures are verified against that news report, not against a signed municipal budget book. The report gives the previous financial year's budget as Rs 83 crore 92 lakh 50 thousand, equivalent to Rs 83.925 crore, and the 2025-26 total as Rs 133.61 crore. [S7]

## Water supply, sewerage and stormwater infrastructure

The Haryana State Pollution Control Board's monthly progress report for September 2025, published through the National Mission for Clean Ganga, lists Palwal's existing sewage-treatment capacity as 27.5 MLD and utilised capacity as 27.5 MLD. It records the operational status as operational and says three STPs are complying. This is a dated regulatory report, not a present-day inspection or proof that every sewer connection functions. [S8]

A PTI report dated 6 June 2025 records the Chief Minister's announcement of Rs 50 crore for basic infrastructure in Sector 21, Transport Nagar, including parking, rainwater drainage, sewage systems and water supply. This is an announced allocation, not evidence of municipal expenditure, a tender award or completed infrastructure. The location-specific announcement should not be added to the municipal annual budget as if the two figures were a single approved account. [S9]

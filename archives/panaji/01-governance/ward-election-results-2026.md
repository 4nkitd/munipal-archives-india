# Panaji corporation election results by ward, 2026

## Poll and count

The [State Election Commission's Form I schedule](https://sec.goa.gov.in/assets/media/docs/general-election-to-corporation-of-city-of-panaji-2026/election-schdule.pdf) set polling for 11 March 2026 from 08:00 to 17:00 and counting for 13 March 2026 from 08:00. The Commission's [ward-wise end-of-poll report](https://sec.goa.gov.in/assets/media/docs/general-election-to-corporation-of-city-of-panaji-2026/ward-wise-ccp-endof-poll.pdf) reports 22,444 votes polled out of 32,496 registered voters, or 69.07 percent.

The winners list has no printed publication date. The [consolidated result sheet](https://sec.goa.gov.in/assets/media/docs/general-election-to-corporation-of-city-of-panaji-2026/result-sheet-ccp.pdf) gives votes by candidate. This is a record of the ward election, not a roster of nominated members, subsequent appointments or present officeholders.

# Panaji municipal ward delimitation, February 2026

## What the notification defines

The Department of Urban Development published notification No. 10/988/2025-DMA/Election/3750 in the *Official Gazette*, Series II No. 44, Extraordinary No. 4, on 2 February 2026. With state approval, the Director of Municipal Administration issued it under section 9(2) of the City of Panaji Corporation Act, 2002, for the election of councillors. The annexure spans printed pages 1457 to 1542 and numbers the wards I through XXX.

For each ward, the annexure lists Assembly constituency parts and local sections within those parts, voter serial numbers or ranges, voter counts and house-number descriptions. Assembly rolls organize the entries; they are not a statement that a whole Assembly constituency belongs to the corporation.

## How to read the annexure

| Municipal ward | Example of an entry in the Gazette |
|---|---|
| I | Part 15 of 12-Taleigao includes an entry for La Marvel Colony, Dona Paula (p. 1457). |
| XXX | Part 2 of 11-Panaji includes an entry for Rebeiro Grande, Fondvem, Ribandar (p. 1541). |

The entries above are examples, not ward names or complete boundaries. For a particular house, check the local section and house number against the full annexure, including any continuation page. For seat reservations, use the [separate 10 February 2026 State Election Commission notification](https://sec.goa.gov.in/assets/media/docs/general-election-to-corporation-of-city-of-panaji-2026/2526-45-SII-EOG-3-01d24.pdf). That notification gives reservation categories, not boundary descriptions.

# House tax, civil registration and grievances in Panaji

## House tax: payment and new assessment

The [annual house tax guide](https://ccpgoa.com/2021/01/11/payments-of-house-tax-yearly/) tells taxpayers to bring the bill or previous tax receipt to the Taxation counter. The counter issues a receipt after payment. The guide describes an April to May payment window and late fees after the specified period. Check the bill and any notice for the financial year before relying on that older guide for a due date.

On [Goa's house tax payment page](https://goaulbservice.gov.in/PayHouseTax.aspx), choose **Corporation of the City of Panaji** under Municipal Council and enter the house number and verification code shown on the form. This is the payment route for an existing record. The corporation's [new assessment guide](https://ccpgoa.com/2021/01/11/house-tax-assessment/) instead asks for an occupancy certificate and completed assessment form. It describes a notice inviting objections to the valuation for 30 days, followed by a bill payable within 15 days of issue. The same guide lists separate paperwork for changes in ownership.

## Registration, record search and complaints

The corporation's [birth and death certificate guide](https://ccpgoa.com/2021/01/02/birth-death-certificate/) directs applicants to the Registration of Births and Deaths Section. It describes submitting the applicable form to the section for hospital or residential events, using Form 1 for births and Form 2 for deaths. The [state birth search form](https://goaulbservice.gov.in/Birth.aspx) lists the corporation and asks for the date of birth. A lookup is separate from registering an event or requesting a certificate. The corporation also links the state's [public grievances form](https://goaulbservice.gov.in/Complaints.aspx), which lists the corporation as an option.

The office is on Dr Pandurang Pissurlekar Road, Altinho, Panaji, Goa 403001. Use the corporation's [office and service directory](https://ccpgoa.com/ccp-at-your-disposal/) for the relevant section. This record describes the published routes. No payments, record searches or complaints were submitted.

# Panaji corporation notice seeking ISO 9001:2015 consultancy, 2023

## What the corporation sought

The [municipal Tender Notice page](https://ccpgoa.com/tender-notice/) lists this invitation for expressions of interest. The corporation sought a consultant to help implement its quality management system and prepare for assessment by a certification body. The scope included comparing existing documentation with the standard, preparing procedures, training staff and internal auditors, and coordinating the certification process. The proposal called for implementation across the corporation within six months of award or earlier.

The notice states that the corporation would open the financial proposals only for consultants scoring above 70 percent in the technical evaluation and would select the lowest cost quoted from those proposals. The invitation describes a procurement process; it does not establish that a contract was awarded or certification obtained.

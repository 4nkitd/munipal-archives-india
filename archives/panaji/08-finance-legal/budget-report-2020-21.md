# Panaji corporation budget report for 2020-21

## Figures in the budget report

The corporation's [Public Disclosure page](https://ccpgoa.com/public-notice/) labels this file "Budget 2020-21." The document itself says "Financial year: 2020" and gives no separate publication date. The file lists account codes and amounts under the headings Estimated Amt, Actual Amt and Available Amt. The entries below reproduce the first two amount columns for selected codes. They are account lines, not totals or tax rates.

## Other published financial records

The same city page lists budgets for 2018-19 and 2019-20, plus separate tabs for balance sheets, income and expenditure, receipts and payments, and audit reports. The audit link labelled "CAG Audit Report 2020-21" opens an [audit inspection report for 1 April 2020 to 31 March 2022](https://ccpgoa.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/CAG-AUDIT-REPORT-2020-2022.pdf). Use the period on that report's title page when citing it. It describes a test-check of accounts and transactions and records that the audit took place at the corporation office in November 2022. The budget report and audit inspection report are separate documents; their figures should not be treated as interchangeable.

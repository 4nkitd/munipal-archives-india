# City profile and governance of Municipal Corporation Panchkula

## Formation and jurisdiction

Haryana notified the formation of Municipal Corporation Panchkula on 17 March 2010. The original corporation combined the Panchkula Municipal Council, the Kalka and Pinjore municipal committees and adjoining rural areas. On 6 July 2020, the Haryana Cabinet approved separating Kalka and Pinjore into a common Municipal Council, Kalka. The state directory now lists the two municipal bodies separately.

| Measure | Municipal Corporation Panchkula |
| --- | --- |
| Area | 96.21 sq km |
| Population | 2,83,786 |
| Municipal wards | 20 |

The area and population are the corporation figures in Haryana's Municipalities Key Figures table. The table does not specify a population reference year. These are not district totals or figures for the original 2010 boundaries.

Haryana's Urban Local Bodies Department issued the final delimitation order for wards 1 to 20 on 30 December 2025, under order No. 04/53/2025-4C1. The department's index lists it on 31 December 2025. Use the order's boundary schedules and drawing No. 17647/MCPKL, dated 9 December 2025, to check which ward covers an address.

## Mayor, council and commissioner

Kulbhushan Goyal won the December 2020 mayoral election. The elected body's term expired on 4 January 2026. Haryana appointed the municipal commissioner as administrator from that date until the first meeting of the newly elected members.

Panchkula voted on 10 May 2026 and results were counted on 13 May. Sham Lal Bansal of the Bharatiya Janata Party succeeded Goyal as mayor. The BJP won 17 of the 20 ward seats, the Indian National Congress won one and independents won two. The State Election Commission notified the elected mayor and members on 21 May 2026 in notification No. SEC/1ME/2026/2368. That is the date used for this profile.

The corporation lists Vinay Kumar, an Indian Railway Personnel Service (IRPS) officer, as municipal commissioner. He replaced Ram Kumar Singh, IAS, in January 2026. The mayor is elected; the commissioner is appointed.

Sources: [State Election Commission notification index for the 2020 and 2026 elections](https://secharyana.gov.in/orders-notifications-related-to-municipal-elections-4/orders-notifications-related-to-municipal-elections/), [Haryana administrator order](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2Fb70f4512-27ba-4d6c-88b1-093494406b66.pdf), [2026 results and ward seat totals](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/bjp-sweeps-panchkula-civic-polls-bansal-new-mayor/articleshow/131077297.cms), [corporation leadership listing](https://mcpanchkula.in/) and [January 2026 commissioner appointment report](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/panchkula-mc-gets-new-commissioner/articleshow/127648816.cms).

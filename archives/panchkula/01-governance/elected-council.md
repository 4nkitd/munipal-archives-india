# Elected council of Municipal Corporation Panchkula

## May 2026 general election

Panchkula directly elected its Mayor and 20 ward councillors on 10 May 2026. Votes were counted on 13 May, and the State Election Commission, Haryana, notified the elected candidates under Section 14(1) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994, on 21 May in [notification SEC/1ME/2026/2368][sec2026]. BJP won 17 ward seats, Congress won Ward 18, and Independents won Wards 17 and 20. Party labels in this record are those at election.

Sham Lal Bansal of the BJP won the mayoral election with 68,544 votes against 32,292 for Sudha Bhardwaj of Congress, a margin of 36,252 votes, [The Times of India reported][result2026]. The Mayor and councillors [took their oaths on 28 May][oath2026]. Bansal [assumed charge on 30 May][charge2026]. The [corporation portal][mc] lists him as Mayor and gives 9216505596 as his official contact number.

## Senior Deputy Mayor and Deputy Mayor

Under [Section 36(2) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994][act], the corporation elects both deputies from among its elected members. The election scheduled for 4 November 2024 was [postponed on polling day][deferred2024]. The corporation did not elect either deputy during Kulbhushan Goyal's term. Both posts [remained vacant on 21 September 2026][september2026]. No later election result or order naming either officeholder was verified as of 22 November 2026.

## House meetings

[Section 52(1) of the Act][act] says the corporation should ordinarily meet at least once a month to transact business. It also requires an additional meeting of at least three days every six months.

| Date | What the record shows |
| --- | --- |
| January 2025 | [The Times of India][gap2025] reported that the House had not held a general meeting since February 2024. |
| 10 March 2025 | Goyal chaired the [House meeting that approved the 2025-26 budget estimates][budget2025]. The Tribune reported projected income of Rs 300.23 crore and expenditure of Rs 284.40 crore. |
| 16 June 2026 | Bansal chaired the [first general House meeting of the new term][june2026]. The House discussed a proposed Rs 97 crore, five-year waste collection, transport and processing plan. |
| 21 September 2026 | [Hindustan Times][september2026] reported that no second general House meeting had taken place since 16 June. |
| 28 October 2026 | [Amar Ujala][october2026] reported that the corporation postponed the meeting scheduled for that day until after Diwali. The proposed agenda had 45 items. |
| 21 November 2026 | [ThePrint][november2026] reported that a councillor expected a meeting by month-end. This was a forecast, not confirmation that the House met. |

This is not a complete minutes register. Committee meetings, informal meetings with councillors, and Area Sabha meetings are separate from general House meetings.

## December 2020 election and the end of the term

Polling took place on 27 December 2020 and results were declared on 30 December. BJP's Kulbhushan Goyal defeated Congress candidate Upinder Kaur Ahluwalia by 2,057 votes, [The Tribune reported][result2020]. The SEC's [notification SEC/1ME/2021/90][sec2020], dated 1 January 2021, lists nine BJP ward winners, seven Congress winners, two Jannayak Janta Party winners, and two Independents.

The [Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department order of 31 December 2025][administrator] gives 4 January 2026 as the expiry date of the elected body. Under Section 400(2)(b), the order appointed the Municipal Commissioner as Administrator from expiry until the first meeting of the newly elected members.

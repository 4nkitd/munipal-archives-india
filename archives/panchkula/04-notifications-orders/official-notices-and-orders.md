## Pet dogs and stray cattle

The official [Haryana Right to Service list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService), described as updated through 4 November 2025, provides these municipal corporation service standards:

| Service | Time limit | Designated officer | First and second grievance authorities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Issue dog registration certificate, including dogs brought for show | 15 working days | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Municipal Commissioner; Commissioner |
| Renew dog registration certificate | 7 working days | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Municipal Commissioner; Commissioner |
| Remove stray cattle | 2 working days | Senior Sanitary Inspector | Deputy Municipal Commissioner; Commissioner |

Dog registration applications are linked to [SARAL Haryana](https://saralharyana.gov.in/). The cattle service directs applicants to the concerned municipality. These are official service standards, not evidence that a particular complaint has been resolved.

A [Hindustan Times report published 7 September 2025](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/chandigarh-news/panchkula-mc-steps-up-efforts-for-pet-dog-registration-101757191700563.html) describes an MC chief sanitary inspector's letter to RWAs citing section 311 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 and the Registration and Proper Control of Dogs Bye-Laws, 2008. It reports mandatory registration, vaccination certification, a collar token, leashing and cleanliness directions. The reported fees were Rs 500 for registration and Rs 250 for annual renewal. It also reports an offline submission arrangement at the Sector 4 MC office because of SARAL technical problems.

That newspaper account is a secondary source, not an inspected signed order or a current fee notification. Its Sector 4 submission address should not be substituted for the separate Sector 14 directory address without confirmation. No authenticated Panchkula breed-ban order or cattle impounding fee schedule was retrieved.

## Parking decisions and their limits

A [9 March 2022 Hindustan Times report](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/chandigarh-news/panchkula-municipal-corporationwaives-parking-fee-for-15-days-101646774585187.html) records a temporary 15-day parking-fee waiver in Sectors 8, 9, 10, 14 and 20 and letters to the deputy commissioner and contractor. This is a historical temporary measure, not a continuing exemption.

[Khaskhabar's report of 6 October 2025](https://www.khaskhabar.com/local/chandigarh-ut/chandigarh-ut-news/news-paid-parking-ends-in-panchkula-parking-will-now-be-free-several-important-decisions-taken-at-the-municipal-corporation-meeting-news-hindi-1-758481-KKN.html) says the House decided to end paid parking in Sectors 8, 9 and 10 after the contractor's agreement ended on 10 November. A [News 7 World archive entry dated 11 November 2025](https://news7world.com/tag/panchkula-municipal-corporation/page/3/) reports implementation in those three sectors.

Neither account supplies an inspected municipal order attachment here. They do not establish the current tariff or free-parking status of every municipal or privately managed parking site.

## Regularized colony notifications

The official [DULB regularized colony register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) lists notification batches dated 17 April 2023, 25 July 2023, 6 October 2023, 15 December 2023, 1 February 2024, 6 March 2024, 14 November 2024, 18 March 2025 and 22 May 2025. Its separate status compilation for 836 colonies is listed on 13 October 2025. These are state registers. Their presence does not establish that a particular Panchkula colony was notified.

Under the official [Property Tax Management System SOP](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/86e89b8a-23f1-47e2-8162-2cb9adb054e5.pdf), correction of a property's authorized or unauthorized colony status requires municipal recommendation and headquarters Town Planning Cell approval. For regularized colonies, the maker must supply the notified colony name, notification copy and signed full-size layout plan.

No Panchkula-specific colony schedule or boundary polygon was authenticated in this record. A reported council proposal, property ID or payment receipt must not be presented as a regularization notification.

# Panchkula property tax, NDC and building planning reference

## Property tax notifications and assessment records

Panchkula is a municipal corporation. The relevant state tax notification category is therefore municipal corporations, not municipal councils and committees. The official [DULB property-tax register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists:

| Publication date | Official document title | Archival qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-07-31 | Property tax notification 2026 Corporation | Latest corporation notification listed when researched; operative rates and commencement provisions were not extracted |
| 2026-05-18 | Notification for one time waiver of interest on the Arrears of property tax pending since year 2010-11 to 2024-2025 | A separate concession instrument; title alone does not establish an open application window |
| 2024-03-27 | Regarding Self-Certification of Property Information | Reference for property information verification |
| 2023-09-26 | Standard Operating Procedure for Property Tax Management System | Register publication date, distinct from the SOP effective date |
| 2023-09-26 | SOP for Creation of Split ID | Separate subdivision and record-processing reference |
| 2023-05-23 | Property Tax Policies and Amendments | Historical policy compilation |

The [2026 corporation notification attachment](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf) is linked for checking the actual instrument. No current residential or commercial slab table is reproduced because its operative text was not verified. Older schedules and expired rebate announcements should not be used as a current tax calculation.

Use the [Property Tax and No Dues Certificate portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for the municipal property record, dues and payment workflow. Check the municipality, owner details, area, floor details, use category, colony status and past payment adjustment before accepting a demand.

## NDC, corrections and property ID documentation

The official [proactive service delivery SOP](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/86e89b8a-23f1-47e2-8162-2cb9adb054e5.pdf), SOP 01, version 01, took effect on 25 May 2023. That effective date is the frontmatter date for this compiled reference.

It covers owner-name corrections, property-detail corrections, authorized or unauthorized colony status, mobile-number updates, dues updates, new property IDs, split IDs, NDC generation and payment of municipal dues. The dues fields include property tax, fire tax, development charges and garbage collection charges where the municipality has enabled that charge.

For an incorrect charge, upload the relevant notification, order or policy. For a payment that has not been adjusted, upload its receipt. The SOP gives dues correction a normal timeline of 10 working days and a Tatkal timeline of 2 working days. These are the 2023 SOP standards, not confirmation that a Tatkal option is currently offered for every application.

New property ID documentation includes identity proof, ownership proof, site map, undertaking and building photograph. Before creating a new ID, municipal staff must check that one does not already exist. Ownership proof examples include sale, conveyance or lease deed, transfer or relinquishment documents, jamabandi, government allotment letter and qualifying court decree documentation. The official portal also publishes a [service-wise document list](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments).

The SOP states that development charges are not to be levied in specified approved categories, including HSVP colonies, TCP licensed colonies, residential units in Lal Dora, Improvement Trust colonies, HSIIDC colonies, rehabilitation schemes and Housing Board colonies. Apply that instruction to the verified category of the particular property. It is not a blanket exemption from property tax or every municipal charge.

The [Right to Service list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService), updated through 4 November 2025, gives 10 working days for clearance of NDC objections concerning dues, size, floors, authorization status and property category, and 10 working days for a new property ID. For corporations, the designated officer is the Zonal Taxation Officer, with Deputy Municipal Commissioner and Commissioner as first and second grievance authorities.

## Haryana Building Code 2017

The official [Building Code copy with amendments dated 22 December 2017 and 8 May 2018](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/Manesar/Images/408719c6-e761-4fdb-a3c1-8caf3787c406.pdf) states in clause 1.1 that the Haryana Building Code 2017 applies throughout Haryana from 6 January 2017. Its location in another municipality's official document store does not make the Code Manesar-specific.

Clause 2.1 requires notice to the competent authority in Form BR-I for erection, re-erection, alteration or demolition. The listed supporting materials include ownership documents, site plan, building drawings, specifications in BR-II, structural documents and applicable fire-safety and public-health plans. The cited edition prescribes a non-refundable scrutiny fee of Rs 10 per square metre of covered area.

Clause 6.1 ties site use and building character to the development plan, approved layout, sector plan or zoning plan. Applicable architectural control sheets take precedence as specified in the Code. Where the Code is silent, the National Building Code provisions apply as stated there.

The [DULB building-plan checklist](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ApprovalRevisionOfBuildingPlan) also lists the property-tax receipt, site report, applicable CLU permission and location-dependent NOCs. These may include fire, pollution control, access, airport, monument, environmental, water and sewerage permissions. A property ID, NDC or tax receipt is not a substitute for building-plan permission.

## Current portal and amendment checks

The official [Unified ULB OBPAS portal](https://obpas.ulbharyana.gov.in/) announces operation from 8 May 2026 and requires compliance with the Haryana Building Code 2017 as amended. Historical instructions link to Haryana OBPAS/HOBPAS, so use the current ULB portal entry point and confirm the competent authority for the plot.

The [DULB homepage](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/) lists a notice published 29 July 2026 concerning keeping approval of S+4 floors on residential plots on hold while online approval and self-certification modalities are finalized. The notice title alone does not establish its application to an individual plot or any later change. Do not infer a current entitlement to a particular floor count, FAR, setback or stilt arrangement from the older Code copy.

For Panchkula, first identify whether the plot is administered by MC, HSVP or another competent planning authority. Check the latest amendment, approved layout and zoning controls before submitting drawings or beginning work.

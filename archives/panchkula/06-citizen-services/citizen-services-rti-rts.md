# Panchkula citizen services, RTI and Right to Service

## Scope and date context

This guide compiles official service information checked on 22 November 2026. The record date is the compilation date, not the commencement date of a law. The Urban Local Bodies Right to Service page labels its consolidated list as updated through 4 November 2025 and links the notification dated 16 August 2022 and amendments dated 11 March 2025 and 27 October 2025.

The municipal homepage was available through official search-index results, but a direct text fetch returned HTTP 999. Homepage contacts below are therefore published contact information, not confirmation that a telephone call or WhatsApp exchange succeeded. No application, payment or booking was submitted during research.

## Property tax and property records

Use the Department of Urban Local Bodies [Property Tax Dues Payment and No Dues Certificate Management System](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). The portal provides citizen registration, login using mobile number, email or PID, municipality selection, application-status checking, document lists and help manuals. Select Panchkula and check that the property identifier and property particulars match before paying.

The same portal is the official application route for changes of owner or occupier, new Property IDs and objections concerning dues, size, floor details, authorized or unauthorized status and property category. These are separate services with separate deadlines. Keep the application acknowledgement and payment receipt; a payment receipt does not itself establish that a correction has been approved.

At the time of checking, the portal linked a tax calculator for the new policy dated 30 July 2026. This guide does not reproduce tax slabs or temporary rebate offers. Check the applicable policy, assessment and outstanding balance on the official portal before payment.

## Birth and death certificates

The [SARAL portal](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) lists birth-certificate issuance, death-certificate issuance, delayed registration, correction of birth or death records and inclusion of a child's name in a birth record. Choose the service that matches the request. Certificate issuance from an existing entry is not the same as delayed registration or correction.

The Haryana Health Department states that births and deaths must be reported within 21 days and registered at the place of occurrence. Its [Civil Registration System guidance](https://haryanahealth.gov.in/service/civil-registration-system/) identifies the head of household as the reporting person for a domiciliary event and the medical institution's in-charge for a hospital event. The [Panchkula district certificate page](https://panchkula.nic.in/service/birth-death-certificate/) also directs citizens to the national Civil Registration System.

Apply through the relevant official service and retain the SARAL Application Reference ID. The portal supports application tracking and SMS updates. Assisted applications are available at the SARAL Kendra on the ground floor of the Mini Secretariat, Panchkula, according to the [district SARAL directory](https://panchkula.nic.in/service/antyodaya-saral/). This record does not assign the ULB dog-registration or document-copy deadline to birth and death services; confirm the deadline displayed for the specific Health Services application.

## Dog registration and community-centre booking

The ULB Right to Service list directs applications for a dog-registration certificate and renewal to [SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/). Its wording is "Dog Registration/brought for show purpose"; it does not establish a general registration scheme for every species of pet. The listed limits are 15 working days for issuance and 7 working days for renewal.

Community-centre booking is also listed on SARAL, with a 15-working-day limit. For Municipal Corporations, the designated officer for these services is the Zonal Taxation Officer, the first grievance authority is the Joint Municipal Commissioner and the second is the Commissioner. Select the relevant municipality and service before applying.

Confirm the particular centre's availability, charges, deposit, cancellation conditions and permitted use with MC Panchkula before arranging an event. The statutory processing limit is not a guarantee that a requested venue or date is available. No current Panchkula venue inventory, tariff or deposit schedule was verified for this record, so none is reproduced.

## Marriage registration

Use [Shaadi Haryana](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) for the state marriage-registration route. The [National Government Services Portal](https://services.india.gov.in/service/detail/apply-for-marriage-registration-haryana) identifies urban-local-body registration under the Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Act, 2008 and Rules, 2008.

Prepare the bride's and bridegroom's particulars, identity, address and date-of-birth evidence, marriage evidence and photographs as required by the current application checklist. Follow the portal's instructions for affidavits, witnesses, verification and any appointment. The MC homepage indexes a "Checklist For Marriage Registration" dated 18 November 2024.

The Shaadi website returned no readable text in this research session. Current fees, appointment requirements and a marriage-specific RTS deadline were not independently verified and are not asserted here. Do not substitute the community-centre or dog-registration deadline for marriage registration.

## Right to Information Act, 2005

RTI is for access to existing records, inspection and copies. Use the complaints route to seek repair or service delivery; use RTI to request the relevant inspection report, work order, payment record, file noting or recorded action taken. Specify the records, subject and date range, rather than asking the authority to create an explanation.

Address municipal-record requests to the State Public Information Officer of Municipal Corporation Panchkula. The [district's municipal directory](https://panchkula.nic.in/public-utility/municipal-corporation-panchkula-2/) publishes SCO No. 15-16, Sector 14, near Rally Chowk, Panchkula, Haryana 134112, telephone 0172-2583794 and email mcpanchkula@gmail.com. Confirm the current receiving office, SPIO allocation, first appellate authority and Haryana-prescribed fee before filing. This record does not infer a named SPIO from the Commissioner's title or assume that sending an ordinary email completes a valid application.

Under section 6, an Indian citizen may apply in writing or electronically in English, Hindi or the area's official language. No reason for seeking information is required. The [India Code text of the Act](https://indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2065/1/aa2005.pdf) provides these periods:

| RTI step | Statutory period |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary decision under section 7(1) | Within 30 days of receipt |
| Information concerning a person's life or liberty | Within 48 hours |
| Transfer to the appropriate public authority under section 6(3) | As soon as practicable, no later than 5 days |
| Receipt through an Assistant Public Information Officer | 5 days added for calculating the response period |
| Decision where section 11 third-party procedure applies | Within 40 days |
| First appeal under section 19(1) | Within 30 days of the response deadline expiring or receipt of the decision |
| Disposal of first appeal | 30 days, extendable to a total of 45 days with written reasons |
| Second appeal to State Information Commission Haryana | Within 90 days of when the first-appeal decision should have been made or was received |

The Act permits admission of delayed appeals for sufficient cause. Section 7(3) excludes the interval between dispatch of an additional-fee intimation and payment from the ordinary response calculation. Section 7(6) requires information free of charge when the authority fails to comply with the specified response limit. Disclosure remains subject to the Act's exemptions.

Give the location, sector, street or property identifier, problem description and earlier complaint number. Preserve the acknowledgement. A complaint number confirms registration, not resolution.

For unresolved Haryana-government grievances, [CM Window](https://www.haryana.gov.in/cm-window/) provides registration through district e-Disha and sub-division counters, followed by SMS acknowledgement and online tracking. The [CM Grievance Cell](https://cmharyanacell.nic.in/cm_window_index) identifies DC-office and SDM-office counters and publishes 0172-3322500 for the grievance cell. A service complaint, an RTS grievance and an RTI appeal are different procedures; filing one does not automatically complete another.

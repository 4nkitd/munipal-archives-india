# Panchkula municipal budgets and civic finance, FY 2024-25 to FY 2026-27

## Scope and evidence status

This record covers financial years ending March 2025, March 2026 and March 2027. The date above is the research cutoff, not a budget adoption date. Figures below distinguish House proposals, later reported approved estimates, financial-statement results and state grant estimates. None is proof of payment to a contractor or completion of a work.

The official MC website is indexed with “Panchkula AFS 2025-26”, “Panchkula AFS 2024-25 Final” and “Panchkula AFS 2023-24 Final”, each dated 14 August 2026. Direct retrieval of the homepage returned HTTP 999 during this research. The underlying statements were not inspected. Budget figures therefore rely on the dated reporting identified below, except for the state Finance Commission allocation table.

## Annual budget register

| Financial year | Evidence and stage | Income or outlay | Expenditure and development provision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-25 | House meeting of 29 February 2024, reported by Hindustan Times on 1 March | Proposed income about Rs 255 crore | Expenditure about Rs 251 crore; development works Rs 129.77 crore; proposal to go to Haryana Urban Local Bodies for final approval |
| 2024-25 | Previous-year approved comparator in The Tribune, 11 March 2025 | Rs 262.94 crore | Rs 251.35 crore |
| 2025-26 | House approval on 10 March 2025, reported by The Tribune on 11 March | Estimated income Rs 300.23 crore | Estimated expenditure Rs 284.40 crore |
| 2025-26 | Same meeting, Hindustan Times, 11 March 2025 | Rounded budget Rs 300 crore | Development works Rs 152.70 crore |
| 2026-27 | Research cutoff 22 November 2026 | MC-wide adopted budget total not verified | Do not substitute the PMDA budget for this missing MC figure |

The February 2024 proposal and March 2025 retrospective comparator are different figures at different reporting stages. No official revision order was retrieved to reconcile them. Amar Ujala's account of the March 2025 meeting gives expenditure of Rs 299 crore, unlike The Tribune's Rs 284.40 crore. The original signed budget schedules and approval order are needed to settle that discrepancy.

## FY 2025-26 revenue assumptions and governance

The Tribune reports projected stamp-duty income of Rs 60 crore, property tax of Rs 28 crore and municipal electricity tax of Rs 10 crore. Hindustan Times adds Rs 11 crore under land sale and acquisition, Rs 25 crore expected from HSVP and Rs 5 crore from advertising permissions. These are forecasts, not receipts.

Hindustan Times reports that an agreement required HSVP to share 75% of revenue from building-plan approvals and plot sales, with no amount received under that component at the time of the meeting. The agreement itself was not retrieved, so the report should not be treated as a complete statement of its legal scope. The same account records Rs 50 lakh for stray-dog control and Rs 2 crore for vehicles and photocopy machines.

The February 2024 House meeting also waived residential garbage collection charges for 2023-24. That historical decision does not establish a permanent exemption or the charges applicable in 2026.

## Reported results versus budget estimates

The Times of India reported on 23 August 2026 that MC annual financial statements showed the following results. These are secondary-source transcriptions pending inspection of the official statements.

| Measure, Rs crore | FY 2024-25 | FY 2025-26 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Revenue | 307.34 | 167.65 |
| Expenditure | 168.60 | 190.50 |
| Reported surplus or deficit | 138.74 surplus | 22.84 deficit |
| Tax revenue | 159.82 | 40.54 |
| Establishment expenditure | 45.13 | 50.83 |
| Operations and maintenance | 113.94 | 129.95 |

The small difference between the rounded revenue/expenditure subtraction and the reported deficit should not be “corrected” without the statement. These accounting results are not directly interchangeable with the gross budget totals above.

## FY 2026-27 official allocations and institutional separation

Haryana's Finance Department publication, *Budgetary transfers to local bodies 2026-27*, page 6, gives Panchkula MC allocations under scheme 2217-80-191-97-51:

| Column in state document | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| FY 2024-25 actuals | Rs 3,83,07,000 |
| FY 2025-26 budget estimates | Rs 14,83,30,000 |
| FY 2025-26 revised estimates | Rs 27,98,12,748 |
| FY 2026-27 budget estimates | Rs 31,41,98,859 |

This is a Finance Commission grant line, not Panchkula MC's total annual budget. A budget estimate also does not establish release or utilisation.

Separately, Haryana's official press release of 18 September 2026 announces a PMDA budget of Rs 829.23 crore for FY 2026-27. PMDA is a metropolitan infrastructure authority, not the elected municipal corporation. Its allocation must not be added to MC income without checking transfers and possible double counting.

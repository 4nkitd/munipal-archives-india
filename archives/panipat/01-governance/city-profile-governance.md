# Municipal Corporation Panipat: city and governance profile

## Formation and municipal area

Municipal Corporation Panipat (MCP) was constituted on 17 March 2010. The [corporation's history](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/) traces the local body to a municipal committee formed in 1867. It became a municipal council in 1994 and was upgraded to a corporation in 2010 when adjoining village areas were brought within the municipal limits.

Panipat is an industrial city on [NH-44](https://panipat.gov.in/how-to-reach/), about 90 km north of Delhi. Panipat district is part of Haryana's [National Capital Region](https://ncrpb.nic.in/ncrconstituent.html). The city is known for [handloom weaving, carpets, blankets and recycled textile yarn](https://panipat.gov.in/industries/).

The [Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department's municipal key figures](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) list the corporation at 60.47 sq km and 655,896 residents. The table gives no population reference year. The [corporation's own profile](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/about-panipat/) gives 62 sq km and 623,571 residents, labeling the population as Census 2011, while its homepage gives an approximate area of 56 sq km. The figures are reported as published; the pages do not reconcile the differences. The district's provisional 2011 [urban agglomeration figure](https://panipat.gov.in/fact-file/) of 442,277 describes a separately defined area and should not be substituted for the corporation figure.

## Council and administration

The corporation has 26 wards, numbered 1 through 26, each with an elected councillor. The [Haryana State Election Commission's result notification](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/202507081506973353.pdf), No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272 dated 20 March 2025, records that Komal Saini of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) was elected mayor at the municipal election held on 9 March 2025. The same notification lists 23 BJP and three independent winners for the 26 ward seats. The corporation publishes a separate [ward councillor directory](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/municipal-councillors/).

Ankita Choudhary, IAS, is the Municipal Commissioner as listed on the [corporation's contact page](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/contact/). The [10 November 2026 Haryana government reshuffle](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/14-ias-hcs-officers-transferred/) posted her to Panipat and transferred the former commissioner, Pankaj, IAS, to Gurugram. The mayor is elected; the commissioner is appointed by the state government and heads the municipal administration.

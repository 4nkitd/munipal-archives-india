# Panipat Municipal Corporation election results, March 2025

## Mayoral result

Panipat voted on 9 March 2025. The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) candidate, Komal Saini, won the unreserved mayoral seat. In its 12 March report on the count, [The Public World](https://thepublicworld.com/archives/105461) reported that Saini received 162,075 votes and Indian National Congress candidate Savita Sanjay Garg received 38,905. The margin was 123,170 votes.

The State Election Commission, Haryana, notified the elected mayor and ward members on 20 March 2025 under section 14(1) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. The result notification is No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272.

## Ward winners

The BJP won 23 seats. Independents won wards 4, 8 and 18.

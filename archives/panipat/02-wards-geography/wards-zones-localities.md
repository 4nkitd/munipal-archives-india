# Panipat wards, sectors and locality reference

## Scope and ward count

The municipal [city profile](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/about-panipat/) states that Panipat has 26 wards. The [municipal councillor directory](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/municipal-councillors/) lists Ward 1 through Ward 26. The State Election Commission's [notification dated 20 March 2025](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/202507081506973353.pdf), No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272, records elected members for these 26 wards following Panipat's election on 9 March 2025.

A December 2024 news report described a proposal for 28 wards. That proposal is not the ward count used here because the later election notification and municipal directory record 26.

This record's date is the compilation date, not the date of a delimitation notification. The final legal boundary schedule and associated ward map were not verified during this compilation. No numbered municipal zones or engineering divisions have been assigned. In `../wards.json`, `null` means an unverified zone or division; empty geographical arrays mean no legal ward-to-locality allocation was established, not that a ward lacks settlements.

## HSVP sectors and industrial areas

HSVP's [community-centre register](https://communitycentre.hsvphry.org.in/frmListOfProperties.aspx) lists Panipat facilities in Sector 6, Sector 18 and Sector 11-12. Its [layout-plan index](https://www.hsvphry.org.in/documents/home/news_updates/newsUpdate_202408161539_638594195624775879.pdf) includes Sector 13-17, Sector 13-17 Part II, Sector 18 Part II, Sector 24, Sector 25 Part I and Sector 25 Part II. These are estate-planning references, not municipal electoral maps.

| Sector 24 | Listed in HSVP's layout-plan index |
| Sector 25 | Industrial area, with Part I and Part II identified separately in official industrial references |
| Sector 29 | Industrial area, with Part I and Part II; textile and dyeing activities are documented in the official water-reuse report |

The Government of India [MSME industrial profile](https://dcmsme.gov.in/dips/har_panipat.pdf) names HUDA Sector 25 phases I and II and Sector 29 phases I and II. It is a historical profile, so its plot counts and rates should not be used as current figures. The [November 2023 Panipat treated-water reuse report](https://nmcg.nic.in/writereaddata/fileupload/12_City%20SRTW%20_Solution%20Document%20Panipat.pdf) separately documents the Sector 29 industrial cluster and its effluent-treatment infrastructure.

For a property query, retain the sector number, part or phase, plot number and property ID. The relevant estate plan determines the plot location. Municipal ward eligibility requires the applicable ward map or electoral roll.

## Model Town, Tehsil Camp and Old City

Model Town is a locality name rather than a single verified electoral ward. The municipal service-centre directory associates one Model Town address with Ward 19. The Haryana Police [Model Town station area list](https://panipat.haryanapolice.gov.in/PS_Model) separately names Model Town, New Model Town, Azad Nagar, Shanti Nagar, Virat Nagar phases 1 and 2, Friends Colony and other localities. A police jurisdiction is not a municipal ward or zone.

Tehsil Camp appears explicitly in the Ward 4 centre address with Patel Nagar and Skylark Road. [Haryana Tourism's Devi Temple entry](https://haryanatourism.gov.in/places_of_interest/devi-temple/) gives its location as Devi Mandir Area, Tehsil Camp. These sources establish place-name usage, not the extent of Tehsil Camp or its complete ward allocation.

For Old City searches, the Haryana Police [City station list](https://panipat.haryanapolice.gov.in/PS_City) supplies useful locality spellings including Insar Bazar, Pachranga Bazar, Halwai Hatta, Jain Mohalla, Salarganj Gate, Palika Bazar and Railway Road. The district administration's [Bab-i-Faiz Gate entry](https://panipat.gov.in/tourist-place/bab-i-faiz-gate/) identifies Bab-i-Faiz as Salarganj Gate and describes it as an entrance to the historic town. "Old City" is used here as a geographical search label, not as a notified municipal zone.

The table preserves source spellings and does not assign ward numbers. The 836 figure is statewide, not Panipat's colony count; these 11 rows are the Panipat entries in this particular consolidated list, not a claim that only 11 Panipat colonies have ever been regularized.

For a specific plot, consult the [civic amenities and infrastructure deficient areas register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure). Match the municipality, revenue estate, colony ID or layout drawing, notified area and khasra numbers. A colony's familiar name alone cannot prove that a plot lies inside its notified polygon. The consolidated list's upload date does not establish the original legal notification date of each colony.

## Boundary verification

Request the final Panipat wardbandi notification and its attached drawing from the municipal election or town-planning branch before using this index for elections, land transactions or legal claims. Compare the exact address and property ID with that map and the applicable electoral roll. For estate plots, also check HSVP's demarcation plan and any relevant service-transfer order. Neither an HSVP sector nor a police-station jurisdiction substitutes for a ward.

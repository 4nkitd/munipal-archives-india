# Official notices and orders for Municipal Corporation Panipat

## Scope and municipal publication

This record combines notices issued or hosted by Municipal Corporation Panipat with state and NCR orders relevant to its jurisdiction. The frontmatter date is the date of the revised CAQM GRAP schedule, not a common issue date for all documents.

The corporation's [Public Notices page](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/public-notices/) links a document titled [1845 Publication](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/1845-Publication.pdf). The page identifies the file but does not explain its subject in readable text. Retrieval of the PDF returned HTTP 406 during this research. Its notification number, issue date, purpose and objection deadline have therefore not been inferred from the filename.

The municipal contact address is Tau Devi Lal Complex, Railway Road and Palika Bazar office, Panipat. The contact number is 0180-4059837. The generic toll-free number in the website header is not used here because its placeholder-like format does not establish an operational helpline.

## GRAP orders applicable to Panipat

The district administration's [Notices page](https://panipat.gov.in/notices/) lists an [Order for Graded Response Action Plan](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s338b3eff8baf56627478ec76a704e9b52/uploads/2023/11/2023111452.pdf). This is an archival link, not evidence that a particular GRAP stage remains active.

CAQM issued a [revised GRAP schedule dated 29 September 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/fa4070ca-5b45-4161-b220-0871227ae9ea.pdf), file number A-110018/01/2021-CAQM, under section 12 of the CAQM Act, 2021. Its distribution includes the Deputy Commissioner, Panipat, and Commissioner, Municipal Corporation Panipat, at Tau Devi Lal Complex, Railway Road, Panipat 132103.

| Stage | Delhi AQI category and range |
| --- | --- |
| I | Poor, 201 to 300 |
| II | Very Poor, 301 to 400 |
| III | Severe, 401 to 450 |
| IV | Severe+, above 450 |

The schedule says stage measures apply when invoked by an appropriate order. Higher stages add to lower-stage actions. An AQI reading alone is not an invocation order. Individual measures may also have narrower territorial coverage than the whole NCR.

A [CAQM order dated 17 November 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/b91570de-8cc1-4735-8879-28f2bc1550f2.pdf) revoked the Stage III invocation of 6 November 2026 and retained Stage I and II actions. It also retained the separately ordered construction restrictions for their stated period of 5 to 20 November 2026. This records the order's contents, not a certification of the active stage on 22 November. Check subsequent CAQM orders before starting restricted work. The [HSPCB GRAP index](https://hspcb.org.in/page/grap-orders) preserves Haryana implementation and revocation orders.

The [town-planning notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/TownPlanningNotifications) also lists [Karnal and Panipat Notification, 30 April 2018](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F1086c956-6268-4900-bf43-9b16b5c83130.pdf). The portal upload date, 4 January 2021, is not the gazette date. This combined file includes Karnal entries, which must not be relabelled as Panipat colonies.

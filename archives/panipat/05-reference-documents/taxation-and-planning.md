# Property tax, NDC, building code and Lal Dora references for Panipat

## Property tax and the 2026 policy reference

Municipal Corporation Panipat's [official website](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/) directs both Search & Pay Property Tax and Apply for No Dues Certificate to the Haryana [Property Tax Dues Payment & No Dues Certificate Management System](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). The state portal provides citizen registration, OTP login, application-status checking, help manuals and a tax calculator explicitly labelled for the new policy dated 30 July 2026. That policy-reference date is used in the frontmatter.

The [DULB property-tax library](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) separately lists the following documents. These are portal publication dates unless the title states otherwise.

| Published date | Official reference |
| --- | --- |
| 31 July 2026 | [Property tax notification 2026 Corporation](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf) |
| 18 May 2026 | One-time interest-waiver notification for property-tax arrears pending since 2010-11 to 2024-2025 |
| 25 October 2024 | Compendium of Instructions |
| 27 March 2024 | Regarding Self-Certification of Property Information |
| 28 October 2023 | Removal of property-data anomalies concerning development charges, agricultural properties, colony masters and Lal Dora areas |
| 26 September 2023 | SOP for Property Tax Management System; SOP for Creation of Split ID |
| 11 October 2013 | Notification Municipal Corporation |

Panipat is a municipal corporation. The separately listed councils-and-committees notification should not be substituted for the corporation notification. The [current calculator](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/taxScreen.html) and applicable gazette should be checked before quoting a payable amount. This record does not reproduce an unverified 2026 rate table or assume an interest-waiver deadline.

The municipal [Property Tax page](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/property-tax/) contains historical narrative referring to rates effective from April 2010 and identifies Panipat among A-2 cities. It also quotes A-1 residential rates. Those A-1 figures must not be presented as a current Panipat tariff.

## No Dues Certificate and property records

Use the state tax/NDC portal linked by the corporation. Check the municipality and PID, property area, usage, ownership/occupation information and recorded dues before submitting a request. Preserve the tax receipt and application reference for any correction or follow-up.

The municipal website links a [No-dues-certificate manual](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/No-dues-certificate-manual.pdf). Its separate [NDC information page](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/no-dues-certificate/) lists ownership proof, buyer and seller affidavits, registry chain where applicable, location map, tax bill or receipt, titama, development-charge receipts, approved building-plan letter for a constructed site, and earlier NOC or court decree where applicable. That page also refers to a Gurgaon facilitation centre, a clear reason not to treat every sentence as a Panipat-specific current procedure. Confirm the live portal checklist with MC Panipat.

Tax payment, a PID, colony notification, an NDC and building approval are different records. Do not describe one as automatic evidence that the others have been granted.

## Haryana Building Code 2017 and building-plan approval

The Panipat homepage labels building-bye-law references for 2016 and 2017 and links HOBPAS for building plans. Its 2017 link points to an old designer-domain URL ending in `.pdff`. That link is not a reliable substitute for an authoritative consolidated code.

The [DULB town-planning notification library](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/TownPlanningNotifications) lists "Final Haryana Building Code 2017 with letter" with no downloadable document shown. It does provide [Adoption of amendments in the Haryana Building Code 2017](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F455f005a-8818-4499-bca7-e903adace365.pdf), indexed 4 January 2021, and instructions regarding Haryana Online Building Plan Approval System, indexed 7 January 2021. These index dates are not established commencement dates.

The municipal [Building Applicatoins page](https://nagarnigampanipat.in/building-applicatoins/) still refers to the 1982 bye-laws and 2006/2007 policy parameters. It lists BR forms, ownership records, fire NOC and tax/development-charge receipts. Its fee schedule and physical-verification instructions are historical web guidance, not a verified current HOBPAS checklist.

Consult the current code and amendments for plot-specific setbacks, FAR, height, parking and occupation-certificate requirements. The [DULB What's New page](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/WhatsNew) lists an instruction published 29 July 2026 about keeping approval of S4 floors in residential plots on hold pending finalization of modalities. Do not infer a present entitlement to additional floors from a 2017 reference alone.

## Municipal Lal Dora certification rules

The [state property-tax library](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists "Property Certificate to the occupiers of the properties falling under the Abadi Deh/Lal Dora of Municipal Areas", published 15 March 2024. An [official indexed copy of the policy and SOP](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES+%2F%2F9bb08af2-072a-4fff-a20f-a8e54dd3e36e.pdf) carries a forwarding memo dated 12 March 2024. It invokes section 250 of the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973, and section 398 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994.

The policy covers old Abadi Deh/Lal Dora properties of erstwhile villages where revenue records of rights were not traditionally maintained. It provides for property certification using existing PIDs, acceptable ownership documents or evidence of possession for the preceding ten years.

| Requirement or remedy | Policy provision |
| --- | --- |
| Mandatory evidence | Claimant affidavit attested by the revenue authority stating ownership/possession in Abadi Deh/Lal Dora |
| Possession evidence | Any two listed categories establishing ten years of possession, including electricity bills, water bills, addressed government documents, property-tax receipts or constructed-structure proof |
| Additional available records | Sale/conveyance deed, transfer/relinquishment/release deed, jamabandi/farad or court decree registered with revenue authorities |
| Original owner deceased | Legal-heir certificate from competent revenue authority or civil court, with the supporting documents |
| Claims and objections | Within 30 days of publication of the relevant notice |
| Appeal | To the ward committee within 30 days of publication of the final property-possession list |
| Appeal decision | Speaking order within 15 days of filing the appeal |

The municipality must publish lists, hear objections, resolve appeals, obtain the House or Administrator's resolution and update the tax/NDC portal before distributing certificates. Public streets, wells, common grounds, burial grounds and drains remain municipal property under the policy. Certificates are subject to the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, and court decisions. The general policy periods above do not establish that a particular Panipat objection window is open now.

# Panipat citizen services, RTI and Right to Service

## Service portals and application records

This guide records sources checked on 22 November 2026, not a new municipal notification. Select Municipal Corporation Panipat wherever a portal asks for the local body. Keep the application reference, receipt, uploaded documents and any deficiency notice. A payment receipt, an application acknowledgement and an issued certificate are different records.

| Service | Official route | What to retain or check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax, property ID and no-dues certificate | [ULB property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Select the correct municipality and property ID. Check ownership, address, area, use and outstanding dues before payment. Retain the receipt and correction reference. |
| Birth and death services | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) and the local Registrar of Births and Deaths | Search the service catalogue for the appropriate certificate, registration or correction service. An already registered event and delayed registration are different applications. |
| Pet registration | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | The ULB schedule specifically names dog registration and renewal. Check the live form for documents and fees rather than assuming every pet species is covered. |
| Marriage registration | [Haryana marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) | SARAL links this portal and provides a marriage certificate appointment rescheduling link. Follow the applicable registrar's verification and appointment instructions. |
| Application or appeal status | [SARAL status portal](https://status.saralharyana.nic.in/) | Use the application reference issued on submission. |
| Service-query ticket | [SARAL e-ticket tracking](https://eticketing.saralharyana.nic.in/CitizenViewComplaints) | Retain the separate ticket number. |

The property portal provides document lists, manuals, application-status checks and municipal support contacts. At verification it advertised a tax calculator for the policy dated 30 July 2026. This guide does not reproduce tax rates or promise a rebate. Check the applicable policy and current demand.

SARAL's FAQ directs users to "Apply for Services > View all available services" after login. Assisted applications are available through Antyodaya/SARAL Kendras and CSCs. Its published helpline hours are 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, excluding government holidays. No application or payment was submitted while preparing this record.

## Birth, death and marriage guidance limitations

Panipat's municipal birth/death page states that births must ordinarily be registered within 21 days. It names the Citizen Facilitation Centre at the municipal office as a submission point. The municipal contact address is Tau Devi Lal Complex, Railway Road and Palika Bazar office, Panipat.

The municipal website contains conflicting legacy certificate guidance. Its birth/death page lists Rs 25 per copy, while a separate FAQ lists a free informant copy, Rs 12 for a first copy and Rs 10 for additional copies. That FAQ also mentions three working days for already registered events. These figures should not be treated as a verified current fee schedule or statutory RTS deadline. Confirm the live service charge and receipt with SARAL or the Registrar.

An older official ULB testing page, labelled updated through 24 February 2023, lists certified birth/death copies for municipal corporations at 14 working days for the current year and 30 for previous years. It identifies the Local Registrar, SDM and Deputy Commissioner as the designated officer and successive grievance authorities. The current ULB schedule cited below does not reproduce that entry. Confirm the latest applicable birth/death notification before relying on those historical timelines.

The marriage portal returned no readable page text during verification. Its official status is cross-checked through SARAL's link, but its current fees, document checklist and completion deadline were not independently verified. Marriage registration should not be confused with eligibility for a marriage assistance scheme.

## Haryana Right to Service deadlines

The Haryana Right to Service Act, 2014 applies to notified services. SARAL states that the clock starts when the designated officer or authorised receiving official receives the requisite complete application. Rejection must include written reasons. The following are working-day limits from the ULB consolidated page labelled updated through 4 November 2025.

| Notified municipal service | Working days | Designated officer for a corporation | First grievance authority | Second grievance authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Change of owner/occupier in property tax register, except death case | 15 | Secretary/Executive Officer/Zonal Taxation Officer, as listed | Joint Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Change of owner/occupier in death case | 45 | Secretary/Executive Officer/Zonal Taxation Officer, as listed | Joint Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Clearance of NDC objections about dues, size, floor details, authorised status or property category | 10 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Deputy Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| New property ID | 10 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Deputy Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Issue of dog registration/show-purpose certificate | 15 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Renewal of dog registration/show-purpose certificate | 7 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Request for copy of documents | 15 | Zonal Taxation Officer | Joint Municipal Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Removal of solid waste from streets, roads, green belts or open spaces | 2 | Senior Sanitary Inspector | Joint Commissioner | Commissioner |
| Replacement of street lights | 10 | Assistant Engineer | Executive Engineer | Superintending Engineer/Chief Engineer |
| Repair of road potholes | 5 | Assistant Engineer | Executive Engineer | Commissioner |

These are service-specific authorities, not a verified roster of current named officers. The document-copy service is not the same procedure as an RTI application. The ULB schedule restricts its water/sewer connection and leakage entries to named corporations that do not include Panipat, so those limits are not applied to Panipat here.

SARAL describes automatic appeals for integrated notified services when deadlines are breached. It states that unresolved appeals escalate after 30 working days at each of the first and second grievance levels. Check the application's appeal status or contact the SARAL helpline. Do not assume that a separate municipal complaint has automatically entered this system.

## RTI Act, 2005

Use RTI to request existing municipal records, such as a tax assessment, complaint action-taken record, work order, expenditure statement or certified file copy. Ask for identifiable records and a date range. A request to repair a street or approve an application belongs in the service or grievance process.

Address a paper application to the State Public Information Officer, Municipal Corporation Panipat. The current named municipal SPIO and first appellate officer were not verified for this guide. Confirm their designations with the municipal office. The [Haryana RTI portal](https://rtiharyana.gov.in/) accepts online RTI applications and first appeals for Haryana public authorities. Follow its authority-selection instructions, including its guidance for district-level information. Do not use the central-government RTI portal for a Haryana municipal request.

| RTI step | Statutory rule |
| --- | --- |
| Application | Section 6 permits written or electronic requests in English, Hindi or the area's official language. No reason for seeking information is required. |
| Transfer to another public authority | Section 6(3) requires transfer as soon as practicable and no later than five days, with notice to the applicant. |
| Ordinary decision | Section 7(1) provides 30 days from receipt, subject to statutory adjustments. |
| Life or liberty | Section 7(1) provides 48 hours. |
| Delayed information | Section 7(6) provides information free of charge when the authority misses the applicable statutory limit. |
| First appeal | Section 19(1) ordinarily allows 30 days from the decision or expiry of the response period. |
| First appeal disposal | Section 19(6) provides 30 days, extendable to a total of 45 days with written reasons. |
| Second appeal | Section 19(3) ordinarily allows 90 days to the State Information Commission. |

Additional-fee intervals, APIO receipt and third-party procedures can affect the calculation. Applicable exemptions still govern disclosure. Verify Haryana's prescribed application and copying fees rather than applying central-government fees. The Haryana portal's published notice says second appeals are currently filed manually by post or by hand in triplicate with supporting records.

## Grievances and Samadhan camps

For a municipal complaint, identify the location, landmark, property ID if relevant, service category and earlier complaint number. Submit photographs where useful and request an acknowledgement. ULB's published IT support and grievance-registration number is 1800-8900-929, available 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

The district administration lists Municipal Corporation Panipat at 0180-2645800 and nagarnigampanipat132103@gmail.com. The municipal website separately lists 0180-4059837. Its generic header also contains a template-style toll-free number, which is not used as a verified contact here.

Samadhan Shivir is a public grievance hearing route, not a replacement for an RTI appeal or an RTS application. A local reproduction of a Panipat DIPRO release dated 19 November 2024 reported municipal and block-office camps on working days from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM. Later district reporting in The Tribune on 9 August 2025 stated that the district programme moved to Mondays and Thursdays from April 2025. These reports concern different dates and administrative coverage. Neither establishes the current municipal camp timetable.

Confirm the next camp's venue, day and hours with the municipal office before travelling. Carry a written complaint, relevant application receipts, earlier correspondence and PPP details if requested. Ask for the complaint reference and responsible department. A camp submission does not itself extend an RTI appeal deadline.

# Panipat municipal budgets and civic financial governance, FY 2024-25 to FY 2026-27

## Annual budget evidence

The period covered is 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2027. The amounts below come from dated reporting on Municipal Corporation Panipat, not Samalkha Municipal Council or another council within Panipat district. Signed annual budget books, complete demand schedules and audited year-end accounts were not retrieved. Figures described as approved are newspaper accounts of council proceedings, not independently authenticated copies of resolutions.

| Financial year | Verified information | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-25 | Property-tax recovery target of Rs 34 crore; approximately Rs 5 crore collected by early January 2025 | Interim revenue evidence only. No verified total annual municipal budget located |
| 2025-26 | Approximately Rs 390 crore budget passed unanimously at the first house meeting on 11 April 2025; approximately Rs 170 crore allocated to development works | Council approval reported by Amar Ujala on 12 April 2025 |
| 2026-27 | Approximately Rs 394 crore initially proposed; approximately Rs 460 crore reported passed on 28 February 2026; revenue target approximately Rs 386 crore | Proposal and approved headline must remain separate; full reconciliation requires the adopted budget |

The 1 March 2026 report says Rs 70 crore expected for an auditorium in Sectors 11 and 12 and other major projects was included. Rounded figures of Rs 394 crore, Rs 70 crore and Rs 460 crore do not reconcile exactly. Do not derive an exact funding gap or revised total from them. Nor should the Rs 386 crore revenue target be described as money already received. [F1, F2, F3]

## Property tax and collection context

The Tribune's 4 January 2025 report identified 440 defaulters, including government departments, with cumulative dues exceeding Rs 66.21 crore. It reported approximately 1.89 lakh property units in residential, commercial, industrial, mixed-use, educational and government categories. These were the figures reported then, not a current certified property register.

For FY 2024-25, the same report gave a Rs 34 crore recovery target and approximately Rs 5 crore collected so far. This was an interim collection figure, not the final annual outturn. Historical comparisons in the report were Rs 29.72 crore collected in FY 2023-24 and Rs 15.27 crore in FY 2022-23 against a Rs 40 crore target. Arrears, current demand, interest, rebates and cash collected are different measures and should not be combined without the assessment and collection registers. [F4]

Property tax recovery was an explicit priority in the April 2025 budget meeting report. For an exact financial comparison, obtain the original and revised estimates, receipts and payments statement, income and expenditure account, balance sheet, grant register and audit observations for each year. Budget provisions alone do not establish expenditure, completion of works or service quality.

## Legal and accounting reference points

The Department of Urban Local Bodies, Haryana provides the Acts library and the Haryana Municipal Account Code 2025 portal. Its official listing identifies the final Account Code notification as dated 20 May 2025, with English and Hindi parts published on the portal on 6 June 2025. The listing establishes publication metadata; it does not prove how far Panipat implemented the code or whether a particular account was audited. [F5]

Use the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 and applicable rules for statutory financial powers and procedures. This compilation does not reproduce unverified section numbers. A council-approved budget, administrative approval, technical sanction, procurement award, contractor payment and audited expenditure are distinct records.

The municipal office contact above is confirmed by the Panipat district administration directory. For missing schedules, request the adopted FY 2024-25, FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27 budgets, approval orders, council minutes, supplementary appropriations and audit reports from the corporation. The directory is a contact source, not a source for budget amounts. [F6]

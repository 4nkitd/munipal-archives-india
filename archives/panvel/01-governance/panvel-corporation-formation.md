# Formation and municipal scope of Panvel

## Council and corporation

The corporation's history dates the Panvel Municipal Council to 25 August 1852. It says a 1991 proposal to convert the council into a corporation was not finalised. Panvel Municipal Corporation came into existence on 1 October 2016 and was the first municipal corporation in Raigad district.

The municipal history describes a corporation covering 29 revenue villages in Panvel taluka and names the CIDCO colonies of Taloja, Kharghar, Kalamboli, Kamothe and New Panvel. This is a description of municipal scope, not a cadastral schedule. The corporation is distinct from Panvel taluka and from CIDCO. Do not use the named colonies alone to decide whether a parcel falls inside the municipal limits.

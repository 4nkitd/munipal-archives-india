# Panvel electoral wards and seats in the 2025-26 election

## Ward and seat counts

The corporation's list of newly elected councillors is arranged by numbered ward and lettered seat. Wards 11 and 20 each have three seats in that list. Wards 1 through 10 and 12 through 19 each have four. The [Maharashtra State Election Commission's final general election table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) independently reports 20 wards, 78 seats, 246 candidates and results declared for all 78 seats.

The list establishes seat coverage for the 2025-26 election, not ward boundaries or current appointments. Its publication date is not stated in the list.

## Maps and municipal office labels

The corporation's [Election page](https://panvelcorporation.maharashtra.gov.in/election) separates its 2017 and 2025 material. The 2025 section labels its ward formation notification as a draft. It is not cited here as a final delimitation order.

The municipal [Ward Office directory](https://panvelcorporation.maharashtra.gov.in/Zone_Office) has four lettered office entries, A through D. Those are not the 20 numbered electoral wards. The office and area labels for A and B are inconsistent on that page, so confirm directions with the head office rather than inferring a ward or office location from the labels.

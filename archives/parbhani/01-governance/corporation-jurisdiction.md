# Parbhani: municipal status and city limits

## Municipal area

The City Secretary’s summary gives 2011 as the year of formation, classifies the corporation as Class D and records 65 elected members. It says the corporation’s limits had not been extended since formation. It discusses possible inclusion of a village and part of another village’s land in conditional terms; it does not record their annexation.

The secretary’s summary gives the area as 57.60 km²; the narrative on the same page gives 57.61 km². Both refer to the city, which the page identifies as district headquarters. The [district municipalities directory](https://parbhani.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) separately lists councils including Gangakhed, Jintur, Purna and Selu.

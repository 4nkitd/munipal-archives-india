# Parbhani: wards and seats in the 2025-26 general election

## Final result

Row 23 of the Commission’s final party table gives separate figures for electoral wards (16), council seats (65) and contesting candidates (411). Results were declared for all 65 seats.

Shiv Sena (UBT) won 25 seats. BJP and INC each won 12, NCP won 11, independents won four and an unrecognized party registered with the Commission won one.

The [Commission’s publication register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the table to 16 January 2026.

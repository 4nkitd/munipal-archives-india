# Parbhani: ward maps and final electoral rolls

## Finding the ward record

For the 2025-26 municipal election, the corporation lists final electoral rolls separately for wards 1 through 16. The postal voting section follows the same numbering. The last table lists each prabhag and then a citywide “Mahanagarpalika Prabhag Rachna Map” at serial number 17. That seventeenth entry is the composite map, not a seventeenth ward.

The page also links the final ward reservation gazette (Appendix 12) and the gazette naming the newly elected candidates. Use the map for geography, the final roll for electoral registration and the candidate gazette for representation.

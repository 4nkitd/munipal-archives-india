# Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi municipal profile and elected council

## Formation and boundaries

Municipal Council Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi is in Gurugram district, Haryana, west of Municipal Corporation Manesar. On 13 August 2022 the Haryana Urban Local Bodies Department [constituted the council](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F9d58798e-ce28-426f-afaf-cc3159341306.pdf) under sections 2A and 3 of the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973. The notification combined Municipal Committee Pataudi and Municipal Committee Haily Mandi, which it identifies with the Jatauli revenue estate. Jatauli, also written Jatoli, was not a separate municipal committee in the merger.

The 2022 notification named ten villages: Nerhera, Janola, Rampur, Chhawan, Milakpur, Mirzapur, Mubarkpur, Devlawas, Heraheri and Khanpur. On 18 September 2023, [notification No. 9/32/2023-4CII](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F54d088cd-f36a-4247-98ff-91e9e18cb3a8.pdf) excluded part of Janaula, the spelling used there for Janola, and set out the revised boundary on drawing MCP/2023/1504. The founding order calls the council Pataudi-Mandi. The State Election Commission calls it Pataudi Jatauli Mandi.

The [Haryana municipal directory](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists 59.61 square kilometres, a population of 66,013 and 32,735 properties. The [Gurugram district ward and booth index](https://gurugram.gov.in/voter-list-for-mc-election-pataudi-jatauli-mandi-2025/) covers wards 1 through 22. The landline in the [Haryana property-tax helpdesk directory](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Contact) is 0124-2672869.

## March 2025 election

Voting took place on 2 March 2025 and results were announced on 12 March. The [State Election Commission's notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4291](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/20250708663947525.pdf), dated 20 March 2025, lists **Parveen Kumar (BJP)** as the elected **president**, also called chairperson. The post was reserved for a Scheduled Caste candidate. [Local reporting](https://thepublicworld.com/archives/105461) names him **Praveen Thakaria** and reports 12,356 votes against 10,465 for Rajrani Sudhir Chaudhary of the Indian National Congress, a margin of 1,891 votes.

The BJP won eight of the 22 ward seats and independents won fourteen. The table records the names and affiliations at the election, not later party changes or nominations. The president was elected separately and is not counted as a ward member.

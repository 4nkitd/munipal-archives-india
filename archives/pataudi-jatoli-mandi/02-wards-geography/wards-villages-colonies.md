# Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi wards, villages and regularized colonies

## The 2024 wardbandi and its evidence limits

The Haryana Urban Local Bodies wardbandi index lists "Regarding delimitation of wards MC Pataudi Jatauli Mandi", a 9,145 KB PDF, dated 19 November 2024. The frontmatter date records that portal entry, not a verified date printed inside the notification. The same index lists the preliminary notification for Municipal Council Pataudi Mandi on 27 February 2024.

[Scanned map/PDF - visual source linked at source_url]

Direct text extraction of the November document returned no readable text. Its individual boundary descriptions, ward populations, notification number and signing date have therefore not been transcribed here. The accompanying `wards.json` contains Ward 1 through Ward 22, but does not guess which villages, streets or colonies belong to each ward. Empty locality arrays mean unverified, not that a ward has no settlements. Empty zone and division strings mean no verified administrative allocation.

The 5 January 2024 seat-fixation notification, No. 09/06/2024/4CII, established 22 seats. A 30 January 2024 Hindustan report described the ad hoc committee agreeing to a 22-ward draft and sending it to Chandigarh for approval. Its reported average population of 2,790 was a draft planning figure, not a verified population for each final ward.

The later 24 December 2024 notification, No. 9/153/2024-4CII, lists Pataudi Jatauli Mandi with 22 seats and supersedes the January seat-fixation notification. Its schedule gives a population basis of 56,782 and a Census 2011 population of 53,795. Seat-fixation and reservation schedules do not by themselves establish street boundaries.

## Merged towns and villages

The constitution notification dated 13 August 2022, No. 8/28/2022-4CII, merged Municipal Committee Pataudi and Municipal Committee Haily Mandi, identified as Revenue Estate Jatauli, with ten named villages. Haily Mandi and Jatoli should not be counted as two separate former municipal committees. Jatoli and Jatauli are spelling variants of the revenue-estate name; this record uses Jatauli where the official English notification does.

| Settlement | Verified role in the 2022 notification |
| --- | --- |
| Pataudi | Former municipal committee |
| Haily Mandi, revenue estate Jatauli | Former municipal committee |
| Narhera | Included village, printed Nerhera in the introductory list |
| Janaula | Included as Janola; a later notification excludes part |
| Rampur | Included village |
| Chhawan | Included village; boundary schedule also uses Chawan |
| Milakpur | Included village |
| Mirzapur | Included village |
| Mubarkpur | Included village |
| Devlawas | Included village |
| Heraheri | Included village; also written Hera Heri |
| Khanpur | Included village |

This is a formation history, not a statement that all 2022 land remains within the council. Notification No. 9/32/2023-4CII dated 18 September 2023 excludes Janaula village in part and replaces the outer-boundary schedule. It refers to drawing No. MCP/2023/1504 dated 3 May 2023, marked A to T. Do not describe the whole of Janaula as either included or excluded without consulting that drawing.

## Outer boundary and the surrounding rural belt

The following names come from the revised 18 September 2023 boundary schedule. They identify adjacent revenue limits and junctions, not automatic municipal membership or ward allocation.

| Boundary side | Revenue estates and junctions named in the revised schedule |
| --- | --- |
| North | Jatauli/Haily Mandi adjoining Lohari in Jhajjar district, Faridpur, Mehchana and Khandewala; Rampur adjoining Jataula, Jori and Janaula; Narhera adjoining Janaula and Ghoshgarh |
| East | Narhera adjoining Bhora Kalan and Uncha Majra; Bas Padmaka and Bid Khurd; Pataudi adjoining Bas Padmaka and Mumtazpur |
| South | Mumtazpur, Safedaar Nagar, Heraheri and Hansaka junctions; Khanpur adjoining Nanukalan and Khor |
| West | Heraheri, Pataudi and Khor; Chawan, Milakpur and Ransika; Haily Mandi adjoining Shahpur Jat, Rajpura and Lohari |

For research toward Manesar and Bilaspur, begin with the eastern names Narhera, Bhora Kalan, Uncha Majra and Bas Padmaka. For the Rewari-facing rural belt, retain the southern and southwestern names Mumtazpur, Hansaka, Nanukalan, Khanpur and Khor. These are directional research groupings, not declarations of a direct common boundary with Municipal Corporation Manesar, Bilaspur village or Rewari district. The notifications reviewed here do not establish those three direct adjacencies. A shared road corridor or nearby village does not establish municipal jurisdiction.

For a disputed property, match its revenue estate and khasra number against the revised municipal-limit drawing, then the final ward schedule. Postal descriptions such as Pataudi Road or Bilaspur Road are not substitutes for either document.

## Regularized colonies

The official ULB consolidated colony lists identify three entries under Municipal Council Pataudi Mandi. The detailed list includes revenue estates and khasra schedules. Its serial numbers differ from the shorter summary list, so colony identifiers and names are the safer cross-reference.

| Colony | Identifier | Revenue estate | Notified area in acres |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unnamed Colony B-22 | 7994 | Pataudi | 19.36 |
| Anandpur Ashram Colony | 7997 | Pataudi | 2.16 |
| Unnamed K-64 | K-64 | Jatauli | 3.18 |

The detailed list places Unnamed K-64 within parts of rectangles 142, 143 and 144 of Jatauli. It lists Anandpur Ashram Colony within parts of rectangles 37 and 38 of Pataudi. For precise parcel coverage, use the full khasra schedule and approved colony plan rather than treating an entire rectangle as regularized.

A 2 February 2024 IANS report also records three Pataudi Mandi colonies among 44 regularized colonies in Gurugram district. This is corroboration, not a substitute for the colony-specific notification. The consolidated lists do not provide a verified link between these colonies and any of the 22 ward numbers, so the JSON does not assign them to a ward.

Do not import nearby Farukhnagar or Sohna entries into Pataudi. In particular, Sherawali Colony, Balaji Nagar and Nikhar Colony appear under Farukhnagar, while Amtek Colony and Amtek Colony Extension appear under Sohna.

# Municipal Council Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi officers and contacts

## Helpdesk and office location

The official Haryana property-tax contact page lists the council as **COUNCIL: PATAUDI MANDI**.

| Service | Published contact |
| --- | --- |
| Property-tax helpdesk official | 8607195238 |
| Additional property-tax helpdesk official | 9992456004 |
| Helpline or landline | 0124-2672869 |

The portal prints the landline as `1242672869`. This directory adds the domestic trunk prefix and separators only. The two mobile numbers are helpdesk contacts, not verified personal numbers for the EO, Secretary, Engineer or Sanitary Inspector. No municipal email address or general sanitation helpline was verified.

The official eProcurement record for tender `2024_HRY_410156_1`, supply and commissioning of a DG set for the Mandi office, gives the authority address and bid-opening place as **Municipal Council Pataudi-Mandi**, with work location **Haily Mandi, PIN 122503**. This is an office-level reference, not a verified street-level headquarters address. Confirm the correct Pataudi or Mandi counter and visiting address by telephone before travelling. Do not use the state directorate's Panchkula address as the municipal headquarters.

## SDM Pataudi and oversight

The Gurugram district telephone directory lists **Sh. Dinesh, HCS**, SDM Pataudi, at **SDM Office Pataudi**, telephone **0124-2672900**, email **sdm.pataudi-hry@nic.in**. The page displays a last-updated date of 5 May 2026.

The Haryana ULB PIO list identifies the **Secretary, Municipal Committee, Pataudi** as State Public Information Officer and the **Sub Division Magistrate, Pataudi** as First Appellate Authority. That list retains the older committee name. It supports the published RTI appellate relationship, but does not establish that the SDM currently holds municipal administrator or EO charge. Confirm the council's current PIO and appellate designation before filing an appeal.

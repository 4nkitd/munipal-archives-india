## GRAP scope and municipal responsibilities

Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi is in Gurugram district in the National Capital Region. CAQM's [GRAP schedule revised on 21 November 2025](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Schedule/GRAP%20Schedule7e3bd726-7308-484f-995f-7cb3fa705b8d.pdf) covers the entire NCR, subject to the territorial wording of individual measures.

| Stage | Delhi AQI classification in the cited schedule |
| --- | --- |
| I | Poor, 201 to 300 |
| II | Very Poor, 301 to 400 |
| III | Severe, 401 to 450 |
| IV | Severe+, above 450 |

CAQM invokes stages by order and may act in advance on forecasts. Higher stages add restrictions to the lower-stage measures. The AQI table is therefore not an automatic determination of which restrictions are presently in force.

The cited schedule assigns urban local bodies responsibilities for road-dust control, mechanized sweeping, water sprinkling and construction-site compliance. Stage III restricts specified dust-generating construction and demolition activities throughout NCR, with stated project exemptions. Stage IV extends construction restrictions to specified linear public projects. Read the complete schedule and the applicable invocation order before deciding whether a particular work can proceed.

## Historical GRAP orders and current-status caution

CAQM's [order dated 17 January 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Home/News/Invocation%20of%20GRAP%20order-%20Stage-%20IV%2017012026eb1a5169-52ec-4ebb-8381-3ee680d6f70f.pdf) invoked Stage IV in addition to Stages I, II and III. It also clarified restrictions on truck entry into Delhi. Those Delhi-entry provisions are not a blanket description of every local road restriction in Pataudi.

This is a historical example, not confirmation that Stage IV remains active on 22 November 2026. Check the [CAQM GRAP orders register](https://caqm.nic.in/landingpage/pages/grap/grap-orders) for later invocation, modification and revocation orders and consult district implementation directions. No separately issued Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi GRAP order was verified for this record.

The cited CAQM revocation material also warns that a construction site specifically closed for non-compliance cannot resume solely because a GRAP stage is revoked. Such sites need the specific permission required by the closure order.

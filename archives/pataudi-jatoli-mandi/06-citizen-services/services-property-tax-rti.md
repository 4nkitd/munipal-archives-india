# Property tax, certificates, RTI and citizen services in Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi

## Property tax and no dues certificate

Use the Haryana [property tax and NDC portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for municipal property records. NDC means no dues certificate. The portal provides registration, property search and application-status tools. Select the correct municipality and check the Property ID, location, ownership and recorded property details before making a payment or requesting a correction. Preserve the application reference and payment receipt.

Haryana's [Right to Service list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService) explicitly directs requests for new Property IDs, property-data objections and changes of owner or occupier to this portal. A tax-register change is a municipal record service, not a substitute for the underlying ownership documents.

Consult the [property tax notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) for the applicable council and committee notification. The index lists "Property tax notification 2026 Councils and Committees", published on 31 July 2026, and a one-time interest-waiver notification published on 18 May 2026 concerning arrears from 2010-11 to 2024-2025. These titles alone do not establish an applicant's eligibility or a currently available rebate. Read the notification conditions and check the portal assessment before paying. This record does not reproduce unverified tax slabs or waiver deadlines.

## Birth, death and marriage certificates

The Gurugram district [e-services directory](https://gurugram.gov.in/service/e-services/) directs birth and death certificate procedures to [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) and links the civil registration system. Search SARAL for the relevant certificate service and follow its current document checklist. Registration of an event, correction of an entry and obtaining a certificate copy are different requests. Confirm the registering authority for the place where the event occurred.

SARAL supports online applications, status tracking and certificate download. Its helpdesk can also explain the service route. The district's [SARAL centre list](https://gurugram.gov.in/service/antyodaya-saral/) identifies Antyodaya Saral Kendra, Pataudi, at the SDM Office, Haily Mandi Road, Pataudi-122503.

For marriage registration, the SARAL homepage links [Haryana's marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) and the [marriage appointment rescheduling page](https://edisha.gov.in/eForms/MarriageScheduleAppointment). Confirm the registrar and jurisdiction before booking. The district e-services page contains a broad urban-area link to Municipal Corporation Gurugram; it should not be treated as proof that MCG is the registrar for every Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi application. No local marriage fee, appointment interval or certificate deadline was verified for this record.

## Right to Service deadlines

The DULB page describes its consolidated list as updated through 4 November 2025 and links amendments dated 11 March and 27 October 2025. Its table labels time limits as working days.

| Service | Published limit |
| --- | --- |
| Change of owner or occupier in property tax register, except death cases | 15 working days |
| Change of owner or occupier in death cases | 45 working days |
| Clearance of NDC objections concerning dues, size, floors, authorized status or category | 10 working days |
| New Property ID | 10 working days |
| Removal of solid waste from streets, roads, green belts or open spaces | 2 working days |
| Replacement of street lights | 10 working days |
| Repair of road potholes | 5 working days |
| Request for copy of documents | 15 working days |

For NDC objections and new Property IDs in municipal councils, the table names the Superintendent/Secretary as designated officer, the Executive Officer as first grievance authority and the District Municipal Commissioner as second authority. For owner-change cases, it names the Sub-Divisional Officer, Civil, as first grievance authority and the District Municipal Commissioner as second authority.

SARAL states that the notified period begins when a complete application reaches the designated officer or authorized recipient. Keep the acknowledgement. Its auto-appeal system handles breaches of notified deadlines for integrated services. The DULB list's water-connection and sewer-overflow entries expressly concern specified municipal corporations; their limits must not be copied as Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi service commitments.

## RTI and access to municipal records

Use an RTI request for existing municipal records, such as assessment orders, work files, sanctioned expenditure, meeting proceedings or a colony notification. Describe the record, date range and requested copy or inspection clearly. RTI is not the route for paying tax or resolving a service application.

The official [PIO directory](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList) still lists Pataudi and Haily Mandi separately as municipal committees. Its Pataudi entry names the Secretary, Municipal Committee, Pataudi, as State Public Information Officer and the Sub-Division Magistrate, Pataudi, as first appellate authority. The Haily Mandi entry is also under the former committee structure. These are legacy entries, not verified appointments for the merged municipal council. Confirm the current SPIO and appellate designation with the council before filing or appealing. No current named Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi PIO, RTI payment instructions or local disclosure inventory was verified.

## Grievances and tracking

For municipal IT support and grievance registration, DULB publishes 1800-8900-929, available Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 6 PM. For SARAL service applications, call 0172-3968400 during its published hours or use the [ticket tracker](https://eticketing.saralharyana.nic.in/CitizenViewComplaints). Use the [application and appeal tracker](https://status.saralharyana.nic.in/) for SARAL reference numbers.

Give the Property ID or application reference, exact location, date and a concise description of the unresolved issue. Keep the complaint number separate from the original service application number. DULB's Right to Service page links its municipal citizen mobile app for solid-waste and street-light complaints. The department also announced on 13 December 2024 that Harpath is available through Jan Sahayak. Portal availability and publication of a contact are not proof that an individual application has been completed.

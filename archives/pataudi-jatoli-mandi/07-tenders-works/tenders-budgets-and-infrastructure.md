# Pataudi-Jatoli Mandi tenders, budgets and infrastructure, 2024 to 2026

## Procurement portals and evidence

The Haryana Engineering Works Portal states that tenders floated through HEWP are published on Haryana's NIC e-Procurement portal for bidder participation. HEWP's live tender list is scheduled to show them on the following day. Its table provides the tender reference, e-Tender ID, department and division, submission deadline, estimated amount, earnest money deposit, tender notice and bill of quantities. [S1]

The municipal procurement organisation appears as `Haryana Government > Urban Local Bodies > MC Pataudi Mandi`. Sources also spell the municipality as Pataudi Jatauli Mandi. The entries below preserve identifiable tender IDs rather than relying on name spelling alone.

The official external detail link tested during this compilation returned only the portal heading and copyright, not the tender fields. Tender-specific facts below therefore identify the secondary notice reproductions actually consulted. They are not represented as independently inspected municipal contracts. An invitation or estimate does not establish award, expenditure or completion. No completion certificate or physical inspection was obtained.

## Water supply and sewerage

A borewell near the government school in Heraheri, ward 20, appears under municipal tender `2026_HRY_513461_1`. The notice reproduction dates publication to 8 April 2026, bid closing to 13 April 2026 and the work period to 120 days. TenderDetail's result reproduction gives a tender value of ₹3,36,660 and contract value of ₹3,69,989, with contract date 27 April 2026. These are reported procurement figures, not confirmation that the borewell is operating or that its water is potable. [S5]

Sector 1 also has infrastructure procurement by Haryana Urban Development Authority, now HSVP, rather than the municipal council. A tender index records a package for special repair of a 12-metre-wide road, strengthening of a 24-metre-wide road, and provision of water supply, sewerage and stormwater drainage in front of plots 1145P to 622P on Rampura Road. It lists an estimated ₹7.52 crore and closing date 30 September 2025. The same index lists water supply, sewerage and stormwater maintenance in Sector 1 with a value of ₹84,627 and closing date 10 February 2025. These index entries do not establish municipal expenditure or scheme completion. [S6]

## Budget allocations, not the council's full budget

Haryana Finance Department's *Budgetary transfers to local bodies* documents provide a separate row for `PATAUDI-MANDI` under Gurugram district. The following figures concern Central Finance Commission grant-in-aid, scheme `2217-80-192-93-51`. They are not the council's consolidated receipts, own-source revenue or annual works budget.

| State budget publication | Pataudi-Mandi figures as published | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget 2024-25 [S10] | 2023-24 budget estimate ₹4,41,03,000; 2023-24 revised estimate ₹4,41,03,000; 2024-25 budget estimate ₹4,62,36,000 | Printed page 8, row 16 |
| Budget 2025-26 [S11] | 2023-24 actual ₹1,33,05,000; 2024-25 budget estimate ₹4,67,40,000; 2024-25 revised estimate ₹4,67,40,000; 2025-26 budget estimate ₹4,77,36,000 | Printed page 8, row 15 |
| Budget 2026-27 [S12] | 2024-25 actual ₹1,24,55,000; 2025-26 budget estimate ₹4,77,36,000; 2025-26 revised estimate ₹9,45,62,597; 2026-27 budget estimate ₹11,34,76,735 | Printed page 7, row 15 |

The two publications give different 2024-25 budget-estimate figures. Both are retained with their publication context rather than silently reconciled. The 2026-27 document identifies its new-year estimates with the 16th Central Finance Commission and earlier years with the 15th Commission. [S12]

No authenticated council-approved consolidated budget for 2024-25, 2025-26 or 2026-27 was established in this research. These grant allocations must not be described as the total municipal budget or as money spent.

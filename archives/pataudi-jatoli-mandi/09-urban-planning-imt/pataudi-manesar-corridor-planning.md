# Pataudi-Manesar corridor planning and regional transport interfaces

## Scope and statutory planning baseline

This is a compiled planning reference dated 22 November 2026, not a new development-plan notification or a parcel-level land-use certificate. The source documents use the historical planning name Pataudi-Haily Mandi. That planning area should not be equated with the present Pataudi-Jatoli-Mandi municipal boundary.

The Haryana Government's Gurugram Metropolitan Area notification of 12 August 2017, number T&CP/GMDA/PF-89-III/2017/19918, identifies the Pataudi-Haily Mandi Development Plan notification as CCP(NCR)/GGN/FDP-2031/PTD-HMD/2013/308, dated 28 January 2013. It brings specified revenue estates from that development-plan area into the metropolitan area, rather than declaring the whole Pataudi-Haily Mandi planning area to be a single municipality.

The listed estates include Jatola, Tajnagar, Khawaspur, Jamalpur, Pathreri, Chandla Dungarwas, Baghanki, Kherki, Langra, Udeypur, Binola, Bilaspur and Sanpka. Mokalwas, Fazilpur Badli, Babra Bakipur, Dhana and Kasan are listed only in part. Jatola in this schedule must not be silently substituted for Jatoli or Jatauli near Haily Mandi.

## Gurugram-Pataudi-Rewari highway

The official NHAI procurement record identifies the road as NH-352W and describes it as a feeder route constructed on Hybrid Annuity Mode. Tender ID 2020_NHAI_57181_1, published on 9 September 2020, gives a design length of 46.110 km and a 730-day work period. Although commonly described as an expressway corridor, the procurement title calls it a national-highway feeder route. It is distinct from the KMP and Dwarka expressways.

IRCON Gurgaon Rewari Highway Limited's 2025-26 annual report records a concession agreement signed on 20 January 2021 and an appointed date of 24 November 2021. It reports 74.913% progress through March 2026 and a revised timeline of 30 September 2026 for the balance work in km 0 to km 6. It also states that NHAI delinked the 2.240 km six-lane section between Hero Honda Chowk and Umang Bhardwaj Chowk.

These are dated project disclosures, not evidence that every section opened by the revised deadline. This archive does not certify full-corridor commissioning as of November 2026. The highway and Pataudi bypass affect through-traffic planning, but roadside development still requires confirmation of land use, highway access permission and the applicable road reservation.

## Bilaspur, Manesar and the logistics planning area

The 17 May 2012 draft development-plan explanatory note identifies Bilaspur's industrial setting near KMP as part of the regional economic context. It proposes a 934-hectare logistics zone and a separate 66-hectare industrial area along KMP. Those figures belong to the draft proposal, not an inventory of operational warehouses or proof of final land-use permissions.

A publicly available digitised Pataudi-Haily Mandi plan drawing, number DTP(G) 2109/2012 dated 21 November 2012, includes land-use code 480 for a logistics zone and code 410 for railway yards, stations and sidings. The copy warns users to cross-check the notified scanned plan. Its transport reservations and logistics designation explain the planning relationship between the Pataudi area, Bilaspur and the Manesar industrial belt. They do not establish that a warehouse on a particular khasra has permission.

For a proposed warehouse, verify the notified land-use drawing and amendments, revenue estate and khasra, change-of-land-use or licence conditions, approved building plan, fire requirements, drainage arrangements and lawful truck access. A highway frontage or proximity to IMT Manesar is not a substitute for these records. No privately marketed logistics park is treated here as a government-notified hub.

## Pataudi Road, WDFC and the orbital rail corridor

Pataudi Road station, code PTRD, serves the Haily Mandi area on the conventional Delhi-Rewari railway. It should not be labelled a Western Dedicated Freight Corridor interchange solely because it is near the regional freight network.

DFCCIL's Western Corridor description states that the Rewari-Dadri section follows a new alignment and lists Rewari and Pirthala Road among the junctions with the existing railway system. Its progress table records inauguration of the 127 km Dadri-Rewari section on 25 January 2024. Neither source establishes a WDFC terminal or junction at Pataudi Road.

HRIDC's July 2024 newsletter explains the more specific regional connections. HORC Part A includes links to the existing railway at Patli and Sultanpur and an additional Dhulawat-New Tauru connection to WDFC. It records work on New Patli-Patli and New Patli-Sultanpur connections intended to permit trains from Farrukhnagar towards Rewari without reversal. These are separate stations and projects, not alternative names for PTRD. The July 2024 construction account is not evidence of their current commissioning status.

## Manesar and Farrukhnagar boundary interface

| Boundary or responsibility | What the cited records establish | What still needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pataudi-Haily Mandi development-plan area | A statutory planning geography referenced in the 2017 metropolitan notification | Current plan amendments and parcel land use |
| Gurugram Metropolitan Area | Specified parts of the Pataudi plan and Farrukhnagar plan, plus Municipal Committee Farrukhnagar, were included in 2017 | Later metropolitan-boundary amendments |
| MC Manesar interface | The 2017 notification predates MC Manesar and cannot establish its present limits | Current municipal constitution and boundary notifications |
| Pataudi-Jatoli-Mandi interface | This archive concerns the municipality, but regional roads and railway links extend beyond it | Current municipal schedule, revenue boundaries and ward map |

The metropolitan notification lists the Gurgaon-Manesar Urban Complex Development Plan 2031 separately from the Pataudi-Haily Mandi and Farrukhnagar planning areas. Shared roads and rail connections therefore do not make their statutory boundaries interchangeable. For a disputed site or proposed infrastructure connection, obtain the current municipal boundary schedule and revenue map before assigning responsibility for local roads, drains, property tax or building approvals.

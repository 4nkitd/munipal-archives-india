# From municipal council to corporation

The Punjab Government Gazette Extraordinary of 26 July 2011, printed page 367, records two legal steps. First, the Department of Local Government designated the area comprising Municipal Council Pathankot as a large urban area under section 3(1) of the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976. The gazette identifies that instrument as S.O.67/P.A.42/1976/S.3/2011, also dated 26 July.

In [S.O.70/P.A.42/1976/Ss. 3 and 4/2011](https://enigambackuprestore.blob.core.windows.net/securefilestructure/MC/Pathankot/MCSubMenu/pdf/linkpdf_9_25_2024_59_670.NOTIFICATION%20%2026_7_2011%20MUNICIPAL%20CORPORATION%20PATHANKOT.pdf), the Governor exercised the powers under sections 3(2) and 4(1) to constitute the “Municipal Corporation of Pathankot” for that area. The territory named in this instrument is the former council area. The notification does not give its acreage, enumerate streets or describe the present municipal perimeter.

# Pathankot corporation election, May 2026

Punjab's [election programme](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Gazette_Notification_of_Election.pdf), notification SEC/ME/SAM (CME)/2026/832 of 13 May, scheduled nominations for 13–16 May, scrutiny for 18 May and withdrawals until 3 p.m. on 19 May. Polling, where necessary, was set for 26 May from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.; counting was scheduled for 29 May.

Row 7 of the [corporation final result sheet](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Municipal_Corporation_Final_Result_Sheet.pdf) identifies both the district and local body as Pathankot. The commission reports 50 wards and 50 results declared.

| Party or category as printed | Seats won |
|---|---:|
| AAP | 10 |
| BJP | 22 |
| BSP | 0 |
| INC | 18 |
| IND | 0 |
| SAD | 0 |

The record date is the poll date printed on the result sheet. The sheet gives seats won, not votes cast or ward-level winners. Its footnote counts four unopposed winners across the entire sheet without assigning them to a corporation.

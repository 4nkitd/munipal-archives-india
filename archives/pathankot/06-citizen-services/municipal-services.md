# Which service link to use

The [corporation's Utility Services menu](https://mcpathankot.punjab.gov.in/Home) directs residents to Punjab service portals. For an existing water or sewerage bill, both “Pay Water Bill” and “Pay Sewerage Bill” point to the [same billing page](https://stvending.punjab.gov.in/wsbills/). For a property without a connection, “Apply New Water Sewer Connection” points to the [Local Government department](https://lgpunjab.gov.in/index.php#openModal).

| Request | Link listed by Pathankot |
|---|---|
| Pay property tax | [mSeva](https://mseva.lgpunjab.gov.in/) |
| Apply for a trade licence | [mSeva common screen](https://mseva.lgpunjab.gov.in/common-screen) |
| Building plan | [E Municipal Naksha](https://enaksha.lgpunjab.gov.in/) |
| Apply for a fire no-objection certificate | [Fire NOC](https://firenoc.lgpunjab.gov.in/) |

For historical property-tax arrears, the notice board separately lists a [one-time settlement notification dated 1 August 2025](https://enigambackuprestore.blob.core.windows.net/securefilestructure/MC/Pathankot/FlashNews/FlashNews_8_4_2025_1_135.01.08.2025%20OTS%20Scheme.pdf), posted 4 August. The listing does not establish that relief remains available. The service menu itself has no publication date; these are published routes, and no transactions were tested.

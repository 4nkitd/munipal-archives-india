# Approved budget for 2026–27

The [approved budget](https://enigambackuprestore.blob.core.windows.net/securefilestructure/MC/Pathankot/MCSubMenu/pdf/linkpdf_10_27_2026_53_932.Approved%20Budget%202026-27.pdf) identifies Pathankot as the urban local body. Dispatch 20262710838 is dated 16 October 2026. Page 1 gives the following budget estimates in rupees:

| Budget head | Estimate (₹) |
|---|---:|
| Revenue receipts | 504,499,000 |
| Revenue expenses | 451,828,000 |
| Capital receipts | 0 |
| Capital expenditure | 55,000,000 |
| Extraordinary receipts | 5,150,000 |
| Extraordinary payments | 0 |

The largest revenue receipt line on page 1 is the Punjab Municipal Fund (account 120-20-02), at ₹340,000,000. Property tax is budgeted at ₹70,400,000 and plan sanction fees at ₹35,000,000. The budget keeps water charges (₹10,000,000) separate from sewerage charges (₹9,600,000).

These are estimates for 2026–27. For prior-year outturn, the corporation's [Account Statements menu](https://mcpathankot.punjab.gov.in/Home) separately lists audited financial statements, a balance sheet and a cash flow statement for 2025–26.

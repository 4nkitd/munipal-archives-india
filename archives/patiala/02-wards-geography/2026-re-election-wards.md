# Patiala re-election notification for 30 wards

## Which wards the notice covers

Notification No. **SEC/ME/SAM (CME)/2026/1100**, published on 13 November 2026 in the Punjab Government Gazette Extraordinary, pp. 4403–4405, cites the Supreme Court order of 28 September 2026 in SLP No. 3894 of 2026 (CWP 388/2025), *Seema Sharma & Ors. v. State of Punjab & Ors.* It also cites Department of Local Government notifications No. `03/01/Elections(S-2)/MEB/2026/176` and `177` dated 23 October 2026 and `03/01/Elections(S-2)/MEB/2026/178` dated 2 November 2026. The election programme invokes section 35 of the Punjab State Election Commission Act, 1994; the poll hours invoke section 57.

| Patiala entry, Annexure A | Ward numbers |
| --- | --- |
| 1–30 range | 1, 4, 6, 12, 14, 15, 16, 22, 26, 28 |
| 31–45 range | 32, 33, 34, 36, 37, 38, 40, 41, 43, 44, 45 |
| 46–60 range | 46, 48, 50, 51, 52, 53, 55, 57, 58 |

The notice covers 52 wards across three municipalities, but only these 30 are in Patiala. Its separate Bagha Purana and Dharamkot entries are for municipal councils in Moga district. The [SEC district-wise ward list](https://sec.punjab.gov.in/en/municipal-election/district-wise-numbers-of-wards-of-municipalities/), indexed 14 August 2023, gives Patiala 60 wards at row 148, printed page 3. The [corporation's homepage](https://mcpatiala.punjab.gov.in/Home) also gives 60 elected seats in its description. This re-election notice is neither delimitation nor a complete ward roster.

## Election programme as notified

| Event | Date and time in the gazette |
| --- | --- |
| Nomination filing | 13, 17 and 18 November 2026, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m.; last day 19 November, up to 3 p.m. |
| Days on which nominations were not accepted | 14 November (bank holiday), 15 November (Sunday), 16 November (gazetted holiday) |
| Scrutiny | 20 November 2026 |
| Last day to withdraw | 21 November 2026, by 3 p.m. |
| Poll if necessary | 28 November 2026, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. |

The gazette left counting instructions for later and applied the Model Code of Conduct immediately to the whole jurisdiction of the respective municipalities until completion of the poll. The 30-ward list identifies where re-election was ordered, not where the code applied. The scheduled poll was still in the future when this record was checked. The notification supplies no result or ward boundaries.

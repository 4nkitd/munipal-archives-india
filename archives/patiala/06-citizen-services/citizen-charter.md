# Patiala citizen charter: service standards and complaints

## Service standards

Section 2.3 of the charter gives the following working-day targets. They are published service standards, not observed processing times. The charter lists the first issue of a birth or death certificate separately from corrections and delayed registration.

| Service | Working days |
| --- | ---: |
| New water or sewer connection | 9 |
| Change of title in water or sewer bill | 9 |
| Water disconnection approval or reconnection | 7 |
| Street light not working, service standards table | 2 |
| Residential building-plan sanction | 30 |
| Commercial building-plan sanction | 60 |
| First copy of a birth or death certificate | 4 |
| Correction to a birth or death certificate, within one year / after one year | 22 / 43 |
| Delayed registration of a birth or death | 45 |
| Fire safety NOC | 30 |
| Assignment of a property ID | 12 |
| Trade licence issuance or renewal, section 343 | 12 |

## Filing and following up on complaints

Section 3 identifies the Complaint Centre at the corporation office in Moti Bagh, Patiala 147001. It lists working-day hours of 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. and the Patiala toll-free number, **1800-180-2808**. It also lists Punjab's M-SEWA app, the Department of Local Government line **1800-1800-0172**, and the [Punjab Public Grievance Redressal System](https://connect.punjab.gov.in/service/grievance/GR1). The portal is linked by the corporation; no application was submitted for this archive.

The charter says the centre generates a complaint ID, sends it by SMS and uses it for status checks on the toll-free line. The grievance table in section 3.3 separately gives four days for street lighting and water supply/sewerage and 13 days for sanitation and horticulture. The four-day street-light grievance norm is separate from the two-day entry in the service standards table. Section 3.4 lists escalation for water, sewerage and street lighting from Junior Engineer through A.C.E. and C.E. to Commissioner.

# Patiala House proceedings on the FY 2026–27 budget

## Meeting and resolution

Patiala's Digital MC House proceedings identify the meeting as **2 March 2026 at 2:30 p.m.**, chaired by Mayor Kundan Gogia. The decision column for **Resolution 126** records that the General House discussed the office's proposed budget and unanimously approved it. This is the meeting date; the PDF gives no separate publication date. The council's decision is distinct from the figures in the [October approved-budget dispatch](approved-budget-2026-27.md).

The report says it used FY 2025–26 income and expenditure as its starting point and cites Local Government accounts-branch budget instructions of 20 December 2025. It records approved FY 2025–26 income of ₹13,906.20 lakh, receipts through 31 December 2025 of ₹9,372.42 lakh, and revised estimated year-end income of ₹15,418.65 lakh. The December figure is a receipt to date, not a forecast or an audited year-end result.

The report puts fuel, stationery and telephone expenses under contingency. It describes development works as including roads, water supply lines, sewerage, park maintenance and street-light stores. It separately discusses approximately ₹15,000 lakh of expected grant-supported development works. The ₹17,900 lakh expenditure total in the resolution's closing table sums the three expenditure rows shown above, without adding that separate grant figure.

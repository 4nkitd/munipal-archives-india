# Patiala approved budget for FY 2026–27

## Budget totals

The corporation's [Account Statements menu](https://mcpatiala.punjab.gov.in/Home) labels this three-page account-wise report “Approved Budget 2026-2027.” The report names Patiala as the ULB and gives dispatch No. **20262710977**, dated **13 October 2026**. The amount column is labelled “BE By HOD.” These are estimates, not audited receipts or payments.

| Budget category | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Revenue receipts | ₹1,439,200,000 |
| Revenue expenses | ₹1,557,816,000 |
| Capital receipts | ₹0 |
| Capital expenditure | ₹0 |
| Extraordinary receipts | ₹0 |
| Extraordinary payments | ₹0 |

Revenue expenses exceed revenue receipts by ₹118,616,000 on the face of the report. The report does not state how that difference would be financed. Works can appear under revenue expenses even where the summary shows no capital expenditure.

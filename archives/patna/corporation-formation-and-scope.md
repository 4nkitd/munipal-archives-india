# Formation and municipal jurisdiction of Patna Nagar Nigam

## The 1952 corporation

The corporation's history traces its establishment to the Patna Municipal Corporation Act, 1951. It cites State Government notification L.S.G. 1088/52 Leg-538 of 26 July 1952 and Bihar Gazette Extraordinary No. 249 of 30 July 1952 for the Act. The corporation dates its establishment and the Act's commencement to 15 August 1952, citing notification No. 6613 of 9 August 1952, published in Bihar Gazette Extraordinary No. 95 on 11 August 1952.

The corporation replaced Patna City Municipality, the Patna Administrative Committee and the Patna Bankipur Joint Waterworks Committee. Its history distinguishes the first election, completed in March 1954, from the date the councillors took office, 1 February 1955. These are events cited by the history page, not dates of publication for that page.

## The state municipal framework

Section 488 of the [2007 municipal text posted by the Bihar State Election Commission](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/old-sec/download/The%20Bihar%20Municipal%20Act-2007%28%20English%29.pdf) provides for repeal of the 1951 Patna Act and contains saving provisions. The commission labels the English link as the Act, but the document is headed “Bill”; consult the enacted Gazette and later amendments for authoritative legal wording.

Patna Municipal Corporation describes an elected corporation headed by a mayor and administration under a Municipal Commissioner. Its six administrative circles are New Capital, Patliputra, Kankarbagh, Bankipur, Azimabad and Patna City. These are municipal operating areas, not separate urban local bodies. The corporation's account does not establish the present outer boundary or include every urban local body in Patna district. Consult applicable Gazette notifications for municipal limits and Bihar SEC Form 6 for the boundaries of individual electoral wards in that edition.

# Patna electoral wards in Bihar SEC Form 6

## The final electoral ward list

The [Bihar SEC unchanged-wards register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=NC) lists Patna Nagar Nigam under Patna Sadar at row 25 and marks it `अपरिवर्तित` (unchanged). Its Form 6, prescribed under rule 29(1), lists electoral wards 1 through 75. Each row gives the ward number, population columns based on the 2011 Census, a written boundary description and a map link. The totals are 1,687,753 people: 151,957 Scheduled Castes, 5,157 Scheduled Tribes and 1,530,639 in the Other column. These are the populations adopted for the report, not current population estimates.

| 72 | 23,180 | The Ganga is to the north; the description cites the outer limit of the corporation to the east and south. |
| 73 | 22,421 | The Ganga is to the north. The southern description refers to the railway line, Ashok Rajpath, Kurji bridge and a drain. |
| 75 | 21,742 | The eastern description runs along Dr. Upendra Nath Lane from Ashok Rajpath to Nehru Nagar Nala. |

## Electoral and administrative labels

The [municipal area table](https://pmc.bihar.gov.in/wardwisearea.aspx) also totals 75 wards but lists 1–72 plus 22A, 22B and 22C. The [corporation's circle table](https://pmc.bihar.gov.in/about.aspx) places these three lettered labels under Patliputra Circle. The sources do not establish an equivalence between those labels and SEC wards 73–75. Use the SEC report and its maps for the electoral edition and check the corporation's directories for service routing.

# Patna property tax services and door-to-door waste charges

## Assessment, payment and changes to a holding

The Revenue Department distinguishes new assessment and generation of a Property ID from demand generation and online payment for existing assessments. It lists mutation and reassessment for changes to the property record. The corporation links a [citizen tax portal](https://pmcptax.bihar.gov.in/pmc/public) for online services. Its main website separately lists self assessment, online mutation and a property or solid waste tax grievance channel. The general corporation office is on the second floor, Block C, Maurya Lok Complex, Budhha Marg, Patna 800001, according to the Revenue Department page footer.

The same page lists 252,445 assets across six circles: Patna City 63,487; New Capital 45,003; Kankarbagh 41,708; Patliputra 40,007; Bankipur 39,372; and Azimabad 22,868. The table has no stated reference year. These are asset counts, not numbers of taxpayers, households or bills paid.

## Separate monthly waste collection charges

The page also publishes a schedule for door-to-door garbage service. Selected entries are below. The charges are not a property tax calculation or a statement of what a particular holding owes. The page does not date the schedule; check the latest municipal demand before paying.

| Category | Monthly charge |
| --- | ---: |
| Hut or kutcha dwelling in a slum settlement | ₹0 |
| Pucca dwelling in a slum settlement | ₹20 |
| Residential | ₹30 |
| Street vendor | ₹0 |
| Shop up to 200 square feet | ₹75 |
| Shop over 200 square feet | ₹100 |
| Restaurant, guest house, dharamshala or hotel | ₹500 |
| Star hotel | ₹5,000 |
| Religious site | ₹0 |

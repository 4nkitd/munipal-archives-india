# Phagwara municipal jurisdiction and leadership

The [corporation’s city profile](https://mcphagwara.punjab.gov.in/Home) places Phagwara on National Highway 44, about 40 km from Kapurthala, the district headquarters. It gives the municipal area as approximately 20 km². Its 2011 figures distinguish the city (97,864 residents) from the larger urban agglomeration (117,954, provisional).

The municipal leadership page identifies Ram Pal Uppal as mayor and Randeep Singh Heer, PCS, as commissioner. The Digital-MC House directory labels the house “Second” and lists 50 councillors.

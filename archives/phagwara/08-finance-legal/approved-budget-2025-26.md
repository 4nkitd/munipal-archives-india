# Phagwara approved budget, FY 2025–26

The [approved budget](https://enigambackuprestore.blob.core.windows.net/securefilestructure/MC/Phagwara/MCSubMenu/pdf/linkpdf_6_12_2026_0_259.Sanction%20Phagwara%202025-26_260612_093847.pdf) for FY 2025–26, dispatch 20252610522, carries the dispatch date 2025-08-27. Its summary sets out the following estimates in rupees:

| Budget head | Estimate (₹) |
| --- | ---: |
| Revenue receipts | 45,62,10,000 |
| Revenue expenses | 39,74,60,000 |
| Capital expenditure | 3,70,00,000 |
| Capital receipts | 0 |

The ₹3.70 crore capital estimate consists of ₹2 crore for cement-concrete street flooring, ₹1 crore for public lighting, ₹60 lakh for office and other equipment, and ₹10 lakh for sewerage and drainage. These are budget estimates, not actual collections or payments.

# Pimpri-Chinchwad city jurisdiction and corporation headquarters

## City and district

[Pune district's corporations directory](https://pune.gov.in/en/muncipal-corporations/) lists Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation separately from Pune Municipal Corporation. The two corporations are distinct city authorities in the same district. PCMC's city profile gives its establishment date as 11 October 1982, records a city area of 181 square kilometres and gives a 2011 Census population of 1,729,359. The area figure describes the city; it does not establish whether a particular property sits inside municipal limits.

## Where to go

PCMC lists its central office on Mumbai-Pune Road, Pimpri 411018. The [zonal office directory](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/ward_info) is for municipal administration. Use the [election page](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/electionNew) and its final maps for electoral wards instead of treating a zonal office as evidence of an electoral boundary.

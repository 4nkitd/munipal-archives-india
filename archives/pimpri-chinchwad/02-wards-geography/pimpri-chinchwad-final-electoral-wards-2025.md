# PCMC final electoral ward formation for the 2025-26 election

## Electoral layout

The English Appendix 15, issued under section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949, sets 32 electoral wards, each electing four councillors, for a total of 128 seats. It cites the draft published as Extraordinary No. 10 on 23 August 2025 and states that the final schedule applies for the next general election. The [Maharashtra State Election Commission's final 2025-26 results table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf), row 16, confirms the 32 wards and 128 seats.

## Find a boundary

PCMC's [election page](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/electionNew) groups the [Marathi Appendix 14](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/marathi/pdf/Finalantimmap2025WardMap/parishistha14.pdf), English Appendix 15, the [combined final map](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/marathi/pdf/Finalantimmap2025WardMap/Final_PCMC_ALL_WARD-28-SEP-2025.pdf) and individual maps for wards 1 to 32 under its final ward formation heading. The schedule gives the extent by compass direction. For example, it lists the Indrayani River as Ward 1's northern edge and names only part of Chikhali Gaothan and part of Morewasti in the ward's locality description. A locality name by itself therefore cannot place every address in the same ward. Polling-station lists, reservation sheets and zonal office listings answer different questions.

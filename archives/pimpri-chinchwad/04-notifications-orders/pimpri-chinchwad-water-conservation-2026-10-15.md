# PCMC water conservation measures announced on 15 October 2026

## Supply planning and requested changes

PCMC reported that it had begun planning a 10% reduction in supply following state directions. The announced planning goal was to stretch available water stocks through 31 August 2027. The corporation said it had assigned ward-level inspection teams to check misuse. The announcement does not give a street-by-street supply timetable.

PCMC asked residents not to use drinking water to wash vehicles, terraces or courtyards and to report leaks from street pipes to the Water Supply Department. It asked housing societies to use treated water for lawns and gardens and construction firms to use treated water from municipal sewage treatment plants rather than borewell water for construction.

The corporation said that it would seize electric pumps found drawing water directly from municipal connections and take penal action. It also said it would not grant new connections to vehicle-washing centres and other large commercial projects. These are the measures described in the dated municipal announcement, not a reproduction of a separate state order.

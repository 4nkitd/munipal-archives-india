# PCMC property tax, certificates and grievance services

## Choose the municipal channel

| Task | Where PCMC directs residents |
| --- | --- |
| Property tax | [Property tax portal](https://publicptaxpcmc.in), linked by PCMC |
| Water bill | [Water bill payment page](https://water.pcmcindia.gov.in/online_payment/waterbillpayment.html), separate from property tax |
| Birth or death certificate | [Municipal birth and death information](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/bnd_info) |
| Register a municipal complaint | [Citizen grievance portal](https://grievance.pcmcindia.gov.in:8443/Grievance/citizen/) or the Sarathi helpline at 8888006666 |
| Find an in-person service counter | [Citizen Facilitation Centre directory](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/cfc_info), which lists the centre codes and locations |

## Notified services

The [PCMC Right to Service page](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/rts_e) cites the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 and links to the notified service list and appellate officer information. For a delay or refusal on a notified service, use that directory to check the procedure for the specific service. A general civic complaint and a statutory service appeal are different routes.

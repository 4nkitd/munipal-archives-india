# PCMC 2026-27 original budget and 2025-26 revised estimates

## Revenue book

The [PCMC budget index](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/budget_L2_E) publishes revenue and capital books separately. Both identify 2025-26 as revised and 2026-27 as original. In the revenue book, printed page 1 is the consolidated summary; pages 3–6 show departmental revenue receipts and expenditure, and pages 7 and 8 give the receipts and expenditure summaries. The consolidated summary includes central government schemes, but its municipal-budget subsection is distinct. That subsection shows ₹5,704.38 crore in revenue receipts, ₹3,583.72 crore in revenue expenditure and ₹1,147.34 crore in capital expenditure for the original 2026-27 estimate.

## Capital book

The [capital book](https://www.pcmcindia.gov.in/pdf/capital2627.pdf) lists departmental consolidated capital expenditure on printed pages 2–4. The civil works, gardens, sports and environment project lists under budget head A are on pages 23–143. The water supply, sewerage and environment capital expenditure summary under head K is on pages 399–400. A budget provision is an estimate, not proof that a project was awarded or paid for.

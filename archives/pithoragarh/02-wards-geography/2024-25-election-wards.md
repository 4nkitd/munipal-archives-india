# Pithoragarh corporation wards and first board after the 2024-25 election

## Corporation status

The Uttarakhand Urban Development Department [ULB roster](https://udd.uk.gov.in/list-of-ulb/) lists Nagar Nigam Pithoragarh at serial 60. The department's elected-representative register, pages 18–19, independently records an operating corporation: it names Kalpana Devalal (कल्पना देवलाल) as mayor and gives 6 February 2025 for both her oath and the first board. The register records the same date for most ward members but leaves both date columns blank for wards 23, 26 and 29. The district's utility directory still uses the former Nagar Palika name. The original constitution notification and legal commencement date were not located.

## Ward lookup scope

Serial 60 in the election register lists wards 1 through 40. The archive's ward directory uses the Hindi ward labels from this edition. For example, ward 1 is बजेटी, ward 2 is न्यू बजेटी, ward 24 is पुराना बाजार, ward 25 is नया बाजार and ward 40 is विवेकानन्द. These labels are not street-by-street boundaries. They do not identify engineering assignments, revenue villages or eligibility for a parcel-level municipal service.

The department's web listing dates this register to 2 September 2026. That is the listed publication date, not the date of the election or first board.

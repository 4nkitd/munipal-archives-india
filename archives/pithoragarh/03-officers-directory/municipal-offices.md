# Pithoragarh corporation officers and published municipal contacts

## Officers named by the state

The Urban Development Department's officer list, dated 7 September 2026 in the document and posted in its portal index on 21 September, lists these posts at serial 63 for Nagar Nigam Pithoragarh:

| Officer | Posting listed |
| --- | --- |
| Deepak Saini (दीपक सैनी) | Municipal Commissioner and Chief Development Officer, additional charge |
| Rajdev Jayasi (राजदेव जायसी) | Tax and Revenue Superintendent and acting Assistant Municipal Commissioner |
| Deepa Parihar (दीपा परिहार) | Nagar Panchayat Executive Officer and acting Assistant Municipal Commissioner |

The list identifies postings as of its edition. It does not assign any of these officers to specific wards.

## Office contacts and older listings

The department's [5 August 2026 ULB email list](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s38208974663db80265e9bfe7b222dcb18/uploads/2026/08/202608051632633803.pdf) lists `npppithoragarh@gmail.com` in the corporation's row 63. The [district utility directory](https://pithoragarh.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists Nagar Palika Chowk and landline 05964-225231 under Nagar Palika Pithoragarh. The [district email list](https://pithoragarh.nic.in/e-mail-directory/) still lists `eopit-mb-uk@nic.in` under former council or municipal board entries. Those are directory listings, not evidence that the council remains the governing body. The landline was not called and neither inbox was tested.

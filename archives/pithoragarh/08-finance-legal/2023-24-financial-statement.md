# Pithoragarh predecessor council financial statements for FY 2023-24

## Entity and period

The package published by Uttarakhand's Urban Development Department contains a balance sheet at 31 March 2024, a statement of profit and loss and a cash flow statement. The compilation letter is addressed to the Executive Officer, Nagar Palika Parishad Pithoragarh. This is evidence of the name used for the predecessor council's accounts, not a present-day classification of the local body.

## Selected balance sheet figures

| Line item | At 31 March 2024 (INR) |
| --- | ---: |
| Total assets | 884,028,789.63 |
| Fixed assets, net block | 716,245,579.00 |
| Grants and contributions for specific purposes | 155,073,186.16 |
| Current liabilities and provisions | 18,627,682.63 |

The accountant describes a compilation under SRS 4410 (Revised), using the Uttarakhand Municipal Accounting Manual 2021. The letter expressly does not give an independent audit opinion or review conclusion. The balance sheet date is an accounting date; no publication date was established for this specific file.

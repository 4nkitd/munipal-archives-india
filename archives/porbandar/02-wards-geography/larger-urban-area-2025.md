# Porbandar larger urban area and included gram panchayats

## Schedule at formation

Under Article 243Q(2) of the Constitution, the Gujarat government designated the former Porbandar-Chhaya Municipality and the following gram panchayat areas as a larger urban area. The notification cites population, density and economic importance as grounds for constituting the corporation.

| Schedule entry | Gram panchayat |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Vanana (Virpur) |
| 2 | Digvijaygadh |
| 3 | Ratanpar |
| 4 | Javar |

This is the schedule in notification GV-25 of 2025, file UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/6142/P Section, 1 January 2025, *Gujarat Government Gazette Extraordinary*, Part IV-A, Extra No. 9, pages 9-1 and 9-2. Its entry numbers are not ward numbers. The separate [constitution notification KV-26](../01-governance/corporation-formation-2025.md) used this larger urban area as the corporation's jurisdiction.

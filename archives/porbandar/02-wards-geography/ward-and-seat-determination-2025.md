# Porbandar corporation ward and seat determination, July 2025

## Ward count and seat reservations

Notification KV-241 of 2025, file UDUHD/MSM/e-file/18/2025/0418/P Section, set 13 electoral wards and 52 councillor seats for the Municipal Corporation of the City of Porbandar. Issued on 9 July 2025 under section 5 of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949, the notification refers to 2011 Census population figures and the corporation's forthcoming general election.

| Reservation | Seats | Women included in the category |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Scheduled Castes | 3 | 1 |
| Scheduled Tribes | 1 | Not separately specified |
| Backward Classes | 14 | 7 |
| Women across categories | 26 | Includes the women already counted in the Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes rows |

The notice gives totals for the city. It does not draw ward boundaries, assign reservations to individual wards, or list elected councillors. Figures were read from page 2 of the scanned notification.

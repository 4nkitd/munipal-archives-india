# Transfer of predecessor staff to Porbandar Municipal Corporation

## Staff covered by the notification

Gujarat government notification KV-27 of 2025 covers officers and staff employed by Porbandar-Chhaya Municipality and the gram panchayats in [the GV-25 schedule](../02-wards-geography/larger-urban-area-2025.md) before 1 January 2025. The notification made those staff employees of the new Municipal Corporation of the City of Porbandar. It provides for salaries, allowances and pension under the applicable rules until other provisions are made under the Act.

The state issued the notification under clause (c) of paragraph 5 of Part I of Appendix IV under section 493 of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. It appears in the *Gujarat Government Gazette Extraordinary*, 1 January 2025, Part I-A, Extra No. 18, pages 18-1 and 18-2, file UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/6142/P Section. This notice defines the transfer rule and does not give staff names or branch postings.

# Prayagraj Municipal Corporation and other local bodies in the district

## Which body covers the address?

The district administration lists Prayagraj as a municipal corporation. Its directory, last updated on 9 June 2022, lists Sirsa, Lalgopalganj, Phulpur, Shankargarh, Koraon, Handia, Bharatganj and Mauaima as separate town areas. A Prayagraj district address alone does not establish that a property lies in a corporation ward.

The [corporation](https://www.prayagrajmc.up.gov.in/) gives its office address as 1, Sarojini Naidu Marg, Civil Lines, Prayagraj (Allahabad), 211001. Allahabad is the former city name, not another municipal body in this directory.

## Kumbh records

The corporation links separately to the Prayagraj Mela Authority. The [district portal](https://prayagraj.nic.in/about-district/) lists land and facility allotment under Maha Kumbh 2025. A Mela allotment identifies an event site; it does not identify a municipal ward or establish municipal property ownership.

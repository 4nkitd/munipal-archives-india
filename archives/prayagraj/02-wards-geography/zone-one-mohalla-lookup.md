# Selected Zone 1 ward and mohalla entries in Prayagraj

## Reading the municipal lookup

The corporation's “Know Your Zone Ward” table gives each mohalla a separate row. The following entries show where a familiar name can match more than one ward.

| Ward | Ward name in the lookup | Examples of listed mohallas |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 28 | Jhuley Lal Nagar | Bhusauli Tola, Khusrubagh Road, Leader Road |
| 44 | Chakiya | Beniganj, Bhawapur, Himmatganj, Kasari Masari |
| 53 | Chak Niratul | Chak Niratul, Jai Ram Pur, Karbala |
| 54 | Rajrooppur | Kalindi Puram, Kasari Masari, Om Prakash Sabhasad Nagar |
| 71 | Om Prakash Sabhasad Nagar | Kalindipuram, Kasari Masari, Om Prakash Sabhasad Nagar |
| 74 | Beniganj | Beniganj, Bhawapur, Kasari Masari |

The [zone office directory](https://www.prayagrajmc.up.gov.in/SectionInformation.html?editForm&rowId=354&page=) locates the Zone 1 office at Sabji Mandi (Akbarpur), Khuldabad. The citywide [councillor directory](https://www.prayagrajmc.up.gov.in/SectionInformation.html?editForm&rowId=261&page=) lists 100 numbered wards. Neither directory supplies the legal boundary of a particular property.

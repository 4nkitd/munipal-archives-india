# Prayagraj property tax registration, changes and mutation

## Choose the application

| What the applicant needs | Option under Property Tax Related Services |
| --- | --- |
| Register a property for self-assessment | Self Assessment (New Registration) |
| Change the ownership recorded for municipal tax | Mutation (Change Of Ownership) |
| Amend an existing self-assessment | Self Assessment (Change) |

For a bill on an existing account, the [municipal home page](https://www.prayagrajmc.up.gov.in/) has separate property and water bill lookup options. A new water connection, water disconnection or plumber licence belongs under [Water Tax Related Services](https://www.prayagrajmc.up.gov.in/SectionInformation.html?editForm&rowId=365&page=). The property application menu does not state a fee or disposal time.

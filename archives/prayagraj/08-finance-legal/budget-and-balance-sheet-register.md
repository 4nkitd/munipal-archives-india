# Prayagraj original budgets, revised budgets and balance sheets

## Financial years represented in the municipal register

The portal labels an original budget “MOOL Budget”. The table distinguishes those entries from revisions in the same register.

| Financial year | Budget entry or entries | Balance sheet listed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2019-20 | Original and revised | No entry in this list |
| 2020-21 | Original and revised | Yes |
| 2021-22 | Original and revised | Yes |
| 2022-23 | Revised | Yes |
| 2023-24 | Revised | Yes |
| 2024-25 | Original | Yes |

For 2021-22, for example, the two budget entries permit comparison of the original estimates with the revised estimates. The balance sheet for the same year is a separate accounting document. These are financial-year labels; the register does not give a budget adoption date, a publication date or an expenditure figure alongside these links.

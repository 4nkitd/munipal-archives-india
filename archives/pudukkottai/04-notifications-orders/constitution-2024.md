# Constitution of Pudukkottai City Municipal Corporation

## Final declaration

Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 257, Part II, Section 2, page 3, contains G.O. Ms. No. 104, notification II(2)/MAWS/743(a-3)/2024. The Governor declared the corporation under section 3(2)(d) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998. The government published the order on 10 August 2024; paragraph 2 brought it into force on 12 August.

## Area at formation

The former Pudukkottai Municipality, Kaspa Forest (West) and eight village panchayats entered in full: Thirukkattalai, Thirumalairaya Samuthiram, Kavinadu East, Kavinadu West, 9-A Natham Pannai, 9-B Natham Pannai, Vagavasal and Mullur.

Three village panchayats entered only in part. The ward numbers below belong to the predecessor panchayats.

| Predecessor | Area included | Former wards |
|---|---|---|
| Thekkattur | Melathemuthupatti, Keelathemuthupatti, Mallukkanpatti and Sivapuram villages | 1, 2, 3 |
| Vellanur | SIPCOT Industrial Estate | 7, 8, 9 |
| Thiruvengaivasal | SIPCOT Industrial Estate | 3, 4 |

The order provides for division into wards for the next ordinary election. It does not number the new corporation wards.

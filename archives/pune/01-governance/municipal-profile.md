# Pune Municipal Corporation and its main office

## Office and jurisdiction

The district directory gives the address as PMC Main Building, Congress House Road, near Mangla Theatre, Shivajinagar, Pune, Maharashtra 411005. It lists the local telephone number as 25501000.

The [district's municipal corporation list](https://pune.gov.in/en/muncipal-corporations/) has two entries: Pune and Pimpri Chinchwad. The wider [metropolitan region](https://www.pmrda.gov.in/en/about-us/) includes both corporations and three cantonment boards. A Pune postal address alone does not identify which local body handles a property.

# PMC notice on applications for compounded structure status

## Resolution and construction cutoff

The notice on PMC's building-permission portal cites Maharashtra Government Resolution **TPS-1814/CR-82/14/RULES/UD-13**, dated **7 October 2017**, and the Maharashtra Town Planning (Compounded Structures) Rules, 2017. The date belongs to the state resolution; the municipal portal does not date its own notice.

PMC describes unauthorized developments carried out **before 31 December 2015** within its jurisdiction as eligible to apply. Filing an application did not itself confer status. The notice says that after PMC approval the development qualifies as a "compounded structure" under section 2, clause (5A), of the Maharashtra Regional and Town Planning Act, 1966. The portal lists a compounding form under **Formats**. The notice gives no closing date or decision on any individual property.

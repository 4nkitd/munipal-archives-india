# Property tax assessment, bills and objections in Pune

## First assessment

PMC uses carpet area rather than built-up area. It also considers property use, the ready reckoner and the type of construction. Owners should notify PMC within 15 days of completion of construction or actual occupation. The FAQ says there is no assessment fee.

| Evidence | Documents listed by PMC |
| --- | --- |
| Ownership | Sale deed, Index II, 7/12 extract or city survey extract |
| Construction | Building permission or occupancy certificate, completion certificate and approved plan |

The department gives an assessment time of 21 days without an objection and 45 days if it receives one. For a dispute over an assessment or bill, it names the Peth Inspector and Divisional Inspector for the property's location.

## Bill and payment receipt

Enter the property ID under **Tax Bill** to retrieve the bill, or ask the ward office where the property lies. For a receipt, select **Tax Receipt**, enter the property ID, open the paid details and choose the challan number for the payment.

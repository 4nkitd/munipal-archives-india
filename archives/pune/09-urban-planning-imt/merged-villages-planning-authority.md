# Planning permission in 23 villages added to PMC

## Municipal limits and planning powers

PMRDA's Development Permission Department identifies 697 villages in its Special Planning Authority area, including 23 added to PMC. For those 23, it identifies the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations, 2020. For the PMRDA region it also lists its Development Control and Promotion Regulations, 2018. The figure of 697 counts villages in the planning authority's area, not villages in PMC.

## Which permissions the department handles

PMRDA lists layout and building permissions, revised permissions, occupancy certificates, amalgamation and subdivision, and transfer of development rights. It directs project promoters to the Building Plan Management System (BPMS) for scrutiny of proposals. Municipal inclusion alone is therefore insufficient to select PMC's building-permission portal for a plot in a merged village. The department overview gives no plot boundaries or electoral ward numbers.

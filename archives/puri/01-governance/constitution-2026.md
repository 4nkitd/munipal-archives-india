# Constitution of Puri Municipal Corporation

## Effective date and jurisdiction

S.R.O. 243/2026, department notification 7365, constitutes a municipal corporation under section 3(2) of the Odisha Municipal Corporation Act, 2003. It applies to the whole of the former Puri Municipality, specified as a Larger Urban Area by notification 7362. The operative clause takes effect “from the date of publication of this notification in the Odisha Gazette.” The masthead dates issue 1094 to 10 March 2026.

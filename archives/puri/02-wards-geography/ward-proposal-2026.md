# Draft division of Puri into 39 electoral wards

## What the draft proposed

Odisha Gazette Extraordinary No. 1241 published S.R.O. 257/2026 under section 60(1) of the Odisha Municipal Corporation Act, 2003. Statement I describes proposed wards and their four-sided boundaries. Statement II assigns proposed reservations under section 7. The department allowed ten days from publication for objections or suggestions.

For example, the draft places the Musa River on the southern boundary of proposed wards 5 and 6. This describes the March proposal only; it is not evidence of the final boundaries or ward count.

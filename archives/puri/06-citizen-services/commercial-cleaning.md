# Express cleaning for commercial premises in Puri

## Express cleaning service

The municipal public works page dates the start of express cleaning to 15 August 2019, under the predecessor municipality. It describes paid collection of segregated waste for business establishments, including hotels and lodgings. Under “Express Cleaning service” the city lists its notification, procedure, application for occasional service and application for lifting solid waste.

## How collected waste is handled

The same page describes aerobic composting of wet waste from households and businesses into manure sold as Mo Khatta. Material recovery facilities sort dry waste; registered vendors buy recyclables and non-recyclables go to a cement factory for co-processing.

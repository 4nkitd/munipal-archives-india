# Purnia municipal employment unit: provisional instructor merit list

## Post and employing unit

The Physical Education and Health Instructor recruitment notice names Nagar Nigam, Purnia as the employing unit. Its table has one entry, serial 1, for that unit. Candidates looking for a council recruitment list elsewhere in Purnea district should check the employing unit before using this list.

## Publication window

The district lists 4 July 2026 as the start date and 31 July 2026 as the end date. The [notice](https://purnea.nic.in/notice/office-municipal-corporation-purnia-employment-unit-municipal-corporation-purnia-provisional-merit-list-for-appointment-to-the-post-of-physical-education-and-health-instructor-year-2026/) links the municipal list and a 343 KB attachment. It labels the list provisional; it does not announce final appointments.

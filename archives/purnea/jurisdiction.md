# Purnea Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and office

## Municipal limits

The SEC lists PURNIA in Purnea Sadar subdivision. Its ward-formation register marks the corporation unchanged (अपरिवर्तित); Kasba and Banmankhi have separate municipal administrations. [District record](https://purnea.nic.in/?s=municipal).

The [SEC register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0) records 46 wards and a population of 282,248 for the corporation. The [Form 6 schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=9&SID=4&SN=305) identifies this as a 2011 Census population for the territory in the schedule. It is neither a district total nor a current population estimate.

## Municipal office

Near Bahumanzila Market, Purnea, PIN 854301. [Official directory](https://purnea.nic.in/public-utility-category/municipality/).

# Final notification upgrading Raichur City Municipal Council

## Which issue establishes corporation status?

The state published two Extraordinary Gazette entries on the change. Both appear under Urban Development, Municipal Administration (One and Three). The subject of the 16 October 2024 issue describes the upgrade of Raichur City Municipal Council. The 4 December 2024 issue adds “ಅಂತಿಮ ಅಧಿಸೂಚನೆ”, meaning final notification. Its full register title is “ರಾಯಚೂರು ನಗರಸಭೆಯನ್ನು ಮಹಾನಗರ ಪಾಲಿಕೆಯನ್ನಾಗಿ ಮೇಲ್ದರ್ಜೆಗೇರಿಸುವ ಬಗ್ಗೆ- ಅಂತಿಮ ಅಧಿಸೂಚನೆ.”

| Gazette date | How to identify the record in department search |
|---|---|
| 16 October 2024 | Earlier upgrade entry; file listed at 1405.54 Kb |
| 4 December 2024 | Final notification; file listed at 1310.21 Kb |

The successor is Raichur City Corporation. The council name remains useful when locating earlier municipal records and the district's entry for the Office of the Municipal Commissioner. Raichur Urban Development Authority appears separately in the register as a publisher of master-plan notices. Its planning notices do not constitute the city corporation.

## Operative declaration and territory

Notification **UDD 139 MLR 2022(e)**, Extraordinary Part III No. 591, records that no objections or suggestions arrived within the thirty days allowed under the earlier notice (Part III No. 466). The Governor exercised powers under sections 3, 4 and 503 of the Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976 to declare the larger urban area and specify it as “The Corporation of the City of Raichur.”

Schedule A lists the **Raichur City Municipal Council area, 43.85 km²**, as the area included. Schedule B starts the boundary at the southwest corner of Raichur village and traces it by survey numbers, PWD Road and the railway line. These schedules describe the outside boundary, not wards.

# Raichur City Corporation final notice on solid waste user charges

## Municipal charge and legal reference

The Karnataka Gazette attributes this notice to **City Corporation Raichur** under Urban Development. The Kannada subject identifies a final notification to implement SWM user charges for solid waste disposal under the Karnataka Model Solid Waste Management Bye-laws, 2018. The 2018 date refers to the model bye-laws. The local corporation's notice appeared in the **daily issue of 31 May 2025**, after the 4 December 2024 corporation upgrade.

The municipal notice is dated **29 May 2025** and appears in Volume 160, Issue 26, Part 6-C, starting at printed page 959. It cites government order UDD/90/CSS 2018 of 10 July 2018. Part I, printed pages 960 and 961, applies to generators other than bulk waste generators. The table below reproduces selected entries from the population band of at least one lakh and below ten lakhs.

| Waste generator or event | Scheduled amount (₹) |
|---|---:|
| House up to 50 m² built-up area | 40 |
| House over 50 m² and up to 300 m² | 80 |
| House over 300 m² | 160 |
| Small shop or eating place under 200 m² | 400 |
| Restaurant seating up to 50 | 1,500 |
| Restaurant seating more than 50 | 2,000 |
| Event or gathering in a public place: refundable cleanliness deposit, one time | 30,000 |
| Event or gathering in a public place: collection, transport and processing fee, one time | 15,000 |

The refundable event deposit and the waste collection fee are separate schedule entries. For construction and demolition waste, the schedule refers to the C&D Rules rather than giving a flat charge.

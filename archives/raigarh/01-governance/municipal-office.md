# Raigarh Municipal Corporation office and jurisdiction

## Municipal office

The district lists Raigarh Municipal Corporation at Municipal Corporation Office, Raigarh, Chhattisgarh 496001.

## Other urban local bodies in the district

Kharsia has its own municipal council. Dharamjaigarh, Gharghoda, Kirodimal Nagar, Lailunga and Pussore are separate Nagar Panchayats.

# Raigarh ward count in the State Election Commission register

## District-wise municipal corporation table

The Chhattisgarh State Election Commission lists Raigarh in Bilaspur division. Its row in the municipal corporation table reads:

| District | Corporation | Wards | Population (2011 census) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Raigarh | Raigarh Municipal Corporation | 48 | 166,034 |

The population figure is for the corporation entry, not the district. The ward column gives a total; the register does not identify individual wards.

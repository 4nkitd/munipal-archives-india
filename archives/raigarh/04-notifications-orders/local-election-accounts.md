# Raigarh urban election accounts and nomination notices, 2025

## Election accounts and nomination publications

The district lists 16 March 2025 as the last date for submission of accounts for the 2025 urban body election. Under “Publication for nomination,” entry 01 links the Raigarh Municipal Corporation document. Separate entries cover Kharsia Municipal Council and the Nagar Panchayats of Kirodimal Nagar, Pusaur, Gharghoda and Dharamjaigarh. The district lists the three-tier panchayat election material separately.

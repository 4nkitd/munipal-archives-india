# Raipur candidate expenditure filings for the 2025 municipal election

## Locate a filing

In the district table, select the **Final Expenditure** link on the row for the candidate. The Raipur block begins with Deepti Pramod Dubey (32) and Meenal Choubey (33) and ends with Shobha (46) and Smt. Shushma Anand Agrawal (47). Row 31 belongs to Gobra Nawapara; row 48 begins Samoda. The table links individual filings and does not give a rupee total or election result.

For elected-office and vote returns rather than candidate expenses, see the [State Election Commission’s 2025 municipal election index](https://cgsec.gov.in/hin_2025chunavnagarpalika.html).

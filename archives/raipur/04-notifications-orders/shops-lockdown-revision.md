# Raipur lockdown notice: revised list of shops

## Scope of the notice

The notice names the Raipur Municipal Corporation area. It does not extend its list to the separate Birgaon corporation. The district gives 22 May 2020 as the date of revision and lists the notice from 22 May to 22 July 2020. The latter is the portal’s end date; the page does not identify it as the legal expiry date. The HTML entry links the list but does not reproduce individual categories of shops.

# Raipur municipal tax: refund or adjustment of excess payment

## Request a refund

The policy covers an amount entered too high, multiple payments for the same property, and technical or transaction errors. Apply in writing to the zone commissioner where the property is registered. The commissioner verifies the payment and forwards the file to the head-office Finance Department. After approval, the authorized bank or payment system initiates the refund.

## Carry the excess forward

To request adjustment in the next financial year, write to the same zone commissioner and specify adjustment. The commissioner forwards this request to the revenue coordinator. After verification, the excess is recorded against the next financial year in the municipal tax system. The policy gives no fixed processing deadline.

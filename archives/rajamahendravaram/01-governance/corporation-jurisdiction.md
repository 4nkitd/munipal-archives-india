# Rajamahendravaram Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## City and district

The [CDMA city profile data](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/74) identifies Rajamahendravaram Municipal Corporation in East Godavari district, Andhra Pradesh. It lists 1994 as the establishment year and reports an area of 238.5 square kilometres and a population of 579,802, without a population reference year. The area figure is not a boundary schedule.

The [East Godavari district directory](https://eastgodavari.ap.gov.in/public-utility/rajamahendravaram-municipal-corporation/) uses the name Rajamahendravaram Municipal Corporation and links to the Rajahmundry CDMA hostname. The district entry describes the location as near Godavari Railway Station. The [city's contact data](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbcontacts/74) gives the more specific office address as near Gokavaram Bus Stand, Sheshyametta, Rajamahendravaram, and gives the landline `08832-479993`.

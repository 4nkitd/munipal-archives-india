# Rajamahendravaram ward electoral rolls and election schedule, 2026

## Electoral rolls and ward count

Notification No. 866/SEC-F1/2026 of 17 October 2026 names Rajamahendravaram in East Godavari as one of four corporations whose ward photo rolls had not been published with the earlier group. It directed the municipal commissioner to publish the rolls with a Form-I notice on 24 October 2026, using 1 January 2026 as the qualifying date. The Assembly rolls were to be used only for the portions within the municipal corporation.

The [CDMA city profile](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/74) and [ULB directory data](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/ulbdetails/ulbprofiles) both report 72 election wards. The city profile lists 133 ward secretariats, while the directory total is 98. Neither figure is an election ward count. The roll notification does not provide a ward-by-ward boundary schedule.

## Election programme

In [Notifications No. 1490/SEC-F1/2026-1 and -2](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/4%20MCs%20Notifications.pdf), dated 12 November 2026, the commission called on Rajamahendravaram's voters to elect a mayor and ward members. The published programme schedules polling, if needed, for 26 November from 7 am to 5 pm, any re-poll for 27 November in the same hours, and counting for 30 November from 8 am. The notification records the schedule, not the outcome.

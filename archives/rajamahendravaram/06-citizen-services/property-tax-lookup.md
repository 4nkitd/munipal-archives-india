# Rajamahendravaram property tax lookup and assessment services

## Find an assessment

Use [Know Your Dues](https://rajahmundry.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/citizen/search/search-searchForm.action) for a property already on the tax roll. The corporation's municipal website and eMunicipal tax service both use the Rajahmundry hostname. The form asks for any one of four fields: Assessment Number, Old Assessment Number, Owner Name or Door Number.

## Choose the route for the request

The city portal lists [Revision Petition](https://rajahmundry.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/search/searchproperty-revisionpetition.action?applicationSource=online) and [Mutation](https://rajahmundry.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/search/searchproperty-registeredtransfer.action?applicationSource=online) separately from the dues lookup. It also links to self assessment, vacancy remission and [Download Proceedings](https://rajahmundry.emunicipal.ap.gov.in/ptis/reports/citizen-searchnotices.action). To ask the municipality about these services, use `08832-479993`, the landline in the [city contact data](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbcontacts/74).

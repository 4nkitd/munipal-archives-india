# Rajkot Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and contact

## Municipal scope

Rajkot Municipal Corporation (RMC) is the local government for Rajkot city in Rajkot district, Gujarat. It does not cover all of Rajkot district. The district administration lists Bhayavadar, Dhoraji, Gondal, Jasdan, Jetpur-Navagadh and Upleta as separate municipalities. The district directory's displayed update date is 21 November 2026; that is not the corporation's formation date or the effective date of a boundary change.

The [RMC website](https://www.rmc.gov.in/) lists municipal responsibilities including water supply, sewerage, street maintenance, solid waste, sanitation and urban health, primary education, and fire and emergency services. The [RMC City GIS](https://gis.rmc.gov.in/rajkotcitygis) offers a searchable map for wards, property and public utilities. This record does not reproduce a statutory municipal boundary.

## Corporation office

The RMC website lists the corporation at Dhebarbhai Main Road, Rajkot, Gujarat 360001. The [district administration's corporation entry](https://rajkot.nic.in/public-utility/rajkot-municipal-corporation/) gives Dhebar Road, Rajkot and the same institutional email, `mc_rmc@rmc.gov.in`.

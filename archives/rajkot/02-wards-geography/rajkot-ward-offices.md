# Rajkot ward numbers and ward offices

## Numbered wards and service locations

RMC's [Elected Body page](https://www.rmc.gov.in/ElectedBody) labels wards `Ward-1` through `Ward-18`. The office directory independently lists office locations under those 18 ward numbers. Letter suffixes on the office labels distinguish multiple service locations; they are not extra numbered wards. The addresses below are office locations, not locality assignments or ward boundaries. The office directory does not display a publication date.

## Zone offices and map lookup

RMC lists the Central Zone at Dr. Ambedkar Bhavan on Dhebar Road, the West Zone at the Harisinhji Gohil divisional office behind Big Bazaar on 150 Feet Ring Road, and the East Zone at Zaverchand Meghani Bhavan. The same directory has separate tabs for civic centres, urban health centres, libraries and Aadhaar centres. Use the [City GIS map](https://gis.rmc.gov.in/rajkotcitygis/map/all) for ward and property lookup. No ward-to-zone mapping has been inferred from office addresses.

# Rajkot municipal complaint registration and status

## Register a complaint

RMC lists two options: call its 24-hour call centre at `155304` or use its online complaint form. The form asks the resident to select a department, complaint type and area. Listed departments include Solid Waste Management, Water Works (Outdoor), Drainage Maintenance, Drainage Chockup/Overflow, Tax Branch and Online Payment Issue. Area labels in the form assist complaint routing; they are not a ward delimitation schedule.

## Check complaint status

Use the separate [Complaint Status page](https://www.rmc.gov.in/ComplaintStatus) and enter the complaint number. The registration page does not state a publication date or promise a resolution deadline. No complaint was submitted to verify the service.

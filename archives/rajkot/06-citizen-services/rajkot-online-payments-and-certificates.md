# Rajkot online tax, water and certificate links

## Tax and water charges

The [RMC online payments page](https://egov.rmc.gov.in/onlinepaymentservices) groups property tax and water charges separately from profession tax. The main RMC site lists these routes:

| Service | Links published by RMC |
| --- | --- |
| Property tax | [Payment](https://www.rmcegov.gov.in/payonline/), [view](https://www.rmc.gov.in/TaxReceipt), [bills and receipts](https://www.rmc.gov.in/TaxBill), [assessment application](https://www.rmc.gov.in/AssessmentApplication) |
| Water charges | [Payment](https://www.rmcegov.gov.in/payonline/watertax.php), [view](https://www.rmc.gov.in/WaterTax), [bills and receipts from 2005 onwards](https://www.rmc.gov.in/WaterTaxBillReceipt) |
| Profession tax | [EC payment](https://www.rmcegov.gov.in/payonline/proftax.php), [RC payment](https://www.rmc.gov.in/ProfessionTaxRCPayment), [payment history](https://www.rmc.gov.in/ProfessionalTaxHistory) |

These are the routes published on the portal, not a statement of current rates, deadlines or proof that a payment completed.

## Birth and death registration lookup

The [birth lookup](https://www.rmc.gov.in/BirthCertificate) and [death lookup](https://www.rmc.gov.in/DeathCertificate) ask for the full registration number. Each page says its lookup applies to births or deaths from 1 January 1990 onwards and, in the portal's wording, to a "notation date" up to 30 September 2020. Those are lookup limits, not limits on civil registration itself. RMC also links to the [Civil Registration System](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/). The service menu does not display a publication date.

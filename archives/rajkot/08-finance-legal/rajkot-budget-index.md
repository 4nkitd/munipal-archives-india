# Rajkot budget and accounts publication index

## What the Account Section lists

The Account Section's Budget tab lists 17 annual budgets, from Budget 2010–2011 through Budget 2026–2027. The 2026–2027 entry is first in the list, followed by 2025–2026 and 2024–2025.

| Portal tab | Selected entries as listed |
| --- | --- |
| Financial Statement and Audit Reports | Audited Financial Statement for year ended on 31-03-2026; separate audited statements for year-ends from 31-03-2018 through 31-03-2025 |
| Internal Audit Report | Internal Audit Report Annual FY 2022-23; Internal Audit Report Annual FY 2021-22 |
| Provisional Accounts | Provisional Receipt and Payment 01-04-2026 to 30-09-2026; Provisional Receipt and Payment 01-04-2026 to 30-06-2026; Receipt and Payment 01-04-2025 to 31-03-2026 |
| Local Fund Account Audit Report | Local Fund Account Audit Report FY 2017-18; Local Fund Account Audit Report FY 2016-17 |

## What the index does not establish

The financial years and period-end dates identify what the documents cover, not when RMC published or adopted them. The index does not display a publication date. This record describes the HTML listings only and does not state revenue, expenditure, tax rates, approval dates or audit findings from the linked files.

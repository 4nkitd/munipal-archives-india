# Rajnandgaon birth certificate application points

## Where to apply

For a birth certificate the district identifies the Lok Seva Kendra at the Collectorate, G. E. Road, Rajnandgaon 491441. The district service page also links to the state e-District service list. Use the district page for that link: its destination includes a session token. The Collectorate counter is distinct from Nagar Palik Nigam, which has its own office telephone in the district public-utilities directory.

## Municipal office enquiries

The [district municipal directory](https://rajnandgaon.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) lists Nagar Palik Nigam, Rajnandgaon. Office telephone: 07744-404893.

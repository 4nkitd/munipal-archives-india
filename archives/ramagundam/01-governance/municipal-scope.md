# Ramagundam Municipal Corporation: city and industrial townships

## Geography and the four urban centres

The corporation describes Ramagundam as a city of 104.34 km² on the Godavari in Peddapalli district, about 250 km northeast of Hyderabad. The municipal profile places it at 18.8° N, 79.45° E and gives its average elevation as 179 metres. It distinguishes four centres within the urban area:

| Centre | Local landmark or activity identified by the corporation |
|---|---|
| Godavarikhani | Commercial centre and coal mining hub; the municipal office is near its bus stand. |
| NTPC (Jyothi Nagar) | Power generation township. |
| Ramagundam | Historic town centre and railway gateway. |
| Eight Incline Colony | Township associated with Singareni Collieries Company Limited. |

These place names describe the city. The corporation does not present them as four electoral divisions; the 2026 election report numbers individual wards separately.

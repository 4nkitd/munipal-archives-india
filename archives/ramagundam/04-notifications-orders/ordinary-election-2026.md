# Ramagundam: ordinary municipal election notification, 27 January 2026

## Notification and seats

The Telangana Gazette, Part VII Extraordinary, No. 07, published Notification No.150/TGSEC-ULBs/2026 on 27 January 2026. The corporation schedule on page 12 lists Ramagundam Municipal Corporation under Peddapalli district with 60 ward members. The seven corporations listed account for 414 seats; the municipalities appear in a separate schedule. The notification concerns the direct election of ward members.

## Deadlines in Annexure I

| Stage | Date and time in the notification |
|---|---|
| Returning officer to issue notice and display ward rolls | 28 January 2026; nominations accepted from 10:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. |
| Last nominations | 30 January, 5 p.m. |
| Scrutiny and list of valid nominations | 31 January, scrutiny from 11 a.m.; list immediately afterwards |
| Appeal against rejection; disposal | Appeal by 5 p.m. on 1 February; disposal before 5 p.m. on 2 February |
| Withdrawal and final list | 3 February, withdrawal by 3 p.m.; final list after 3 p.m. |
| Scheduled poll | 11 February, 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. |
| Re-poll, if required | 12 February |
| Counting and declaration | 13 February, counting from 8 a.m.; declaration after counting |

The dates above are the notified programme. The separate SEC ward return records votes and uncontested seats.

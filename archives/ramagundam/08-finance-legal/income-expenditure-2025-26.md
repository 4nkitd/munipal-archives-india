# Ramagundam Municipal Corporation: income and expenditure, 2025–26

## Accounting period and funds

The Commissioner and Director of Municipal Administration reports income and expenditure for 1 April 2025 through 31 March 2026. The statement separates the Municipal General Fund and Capital Project Fund. The figures below use the combined total column, in rupees. Tax revenue and fees are components of income, not additions to the total.

| Account head or subtotal | Amount (₹) |
|---|---:|
| Tax revenue (110) | 132,307,286.13 |
| Fees and user charges (140) | 264,290,345.87 |
| Total income | 417,332,756.00 |
| Total expenditure before subsequent adjustments | 322,755,506.00 |
| Surplus before depreciation and prior-period items | 94,577,250.00 |
| Depreciation (272) | 68,949,581.00 |
| Prior-period item (280) | 0.00 |
| Net balance carried to municipal fund | 21,135,927.00 |

## Reconciliation

After ₹4,491,742 in miscellaneous expenses and ₹68,949,581 in depreciation, ₹21,135,927 remained. The statement shows no prior-period item or transfer to reserves. The net amount combines a ₹124,753,256 surplus for the General Fund and a ₹103,617,329 deficit for the Capital Project Fund.

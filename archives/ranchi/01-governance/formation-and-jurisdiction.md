# Formation and jurisdiction of Ranchi Municipal Corporation

## Formation

Ranchi Municipal Corporation gives 15 September 1979 as its establishment date under Government Notification No. 1406. The corporation combined the former Ranchi Municipality, Doranda Municipality and Ranchi Doranda Joint Water Board. The municipal overview identifies the Jharkhand Municipal Act, 2011 as the governing law. Its summary of Section 70 includes regulation of land use, local roads and bridges, water supply, sanitation and solid waste management, and registration of births and deaths.

## Which authority covers the city

The corporation covers the Ranchi municipal area, not Ranchi district as a whole. The [district election notice](https://ranchi.nic.in/notice/municipal-general-election-2026-2/) provides separate booth lists for Ranchi Nagar Nigam and Bundu Nagar Panchayat. The founding history does not establish whether a particular property is inside recent city or ward limits.

The [district municipal office listing](https://ranchi.nic.in/public-utility/ranchi-municipal-corporation/) gives the office location near Kutchary, Ranchi 834001, and the public office number +91-651-2211215. The corporation uses the address Kutchery Road, Ranchi 834001.

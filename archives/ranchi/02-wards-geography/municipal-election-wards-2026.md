# Ranchi municipal election booth lists and wards, 2026

## How to locate the corporation's list

Schedule II of Jharkhand Extraordinary Gazette No. 22, dated 27 January 2026, lists Ranchi Nagar Nigam wards 1 to 53 at serial numbers 234 through 286. The schedule set polling for 23 February 2026. Schedule I separately lists the mayoral election and records the mayoral seat as reserved for Scheduled Tribes for that election; ward reservations have their own entries in Schedule II.

The [Ranchi district notice](https://ranchi.nic.in/notice/municipal-general-election-2026-2/) has a start date of 28 January 2026. It links a folder with Form 5, Form 4 voter lists and first pages, the list of returning officers and assistant returning officers approved by the State Election Commission, and the booth lists. The district-linked folder labels two corporation files “Booth List (RMC) Ward-1-53” and “Booth List (RMC) Ward-1-53 With Relocation.” The Bundu Nagar Panchayat booth list is a separate file and does not extend Ranchi's ward numbering.

The [earlier district notice](https://ranchi.nic.in/notice/municipal-general-election-2026/) has a start date of 5 January 2026 and links Form 3, the Reservation Gazette, separately from the voter list and returning officer materials.

## Why the published ward counts differ

The [corporation's undated overview](https://www.ranchimunicipal.com/AboutUs.aspx?uid=1) still says 55 administrative wards. Its website also labels a 55-ward area document “Wards Old.” This record uses the 53 wards in the district's 2026 election materials. The booth index does not establish the legal boundary of any ward, the date any boundary changed, or the ward assignment of a given address.

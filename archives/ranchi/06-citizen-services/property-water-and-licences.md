# Ranchi property tax, water charges and municipal licences

## Property tax and assessment

Use the [Ranchi property tax portal](https://municipalservices.jharkhand.gov.in/CitizenProperty/home) for the corporation's advertised dues lookup, property details, tax collector lookup and property tax payment. The [self-assessment page](https://municipalservices.jharkhand.gov.in/CitizenSaf2/manuall) and the [assessment status search](https://municipalservices.jharkhand.gov.in/CitizenSaf/searchApplication) are separate routes. A payment lookup does not itself file an assessment or reassessment.

## Water, licences and help

The corporation links the [state municipal services home page](https://municipalservices.jharkhand.gov.in/) for water bill viewing, payment and payment details. That portal also advertises an application route for a new water connection. The [municipal licence page](https://municipalservices.jharkhand.gov.in/Home/tradeList) is the same destination the corporation links for applying, renewing and searching for a municipal licence.

For public grievances, the state portal links [PGMS](https://pgms.dmajharkhand.in/index.aspx) and separately labels a property, water and trade grievance route. Ranchi Municipal Corporation publishes its general support number and mailbox above. This record describes the published routes, not a completed payment, application or grievance filing.

# Ratlam city corporation and council offices

## Jurisdiction and representation

The corporation identifies the Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956 as the law governing its municipal services. Its [city portal](https://rmcratlam.in/) reports 49 wards in Ratlam city. The corporation directory distinguishes the municipal mayor, municipal president and municipal commissioner from the councillors listed under “Parshad”.

## Office correspondence and council records

The mayor and president entries give the same institutional mailbox, nn.ratlam@mpurban.gov.in. The commissioner's entry instead gives commratlam@mpurban.gov.in and the municipal office address at 1 College Road, Ratlam.

Under “जनप्रतिनिधियों की सूची”, the corporation lists a PDF of elected representatives with an upload date of 17 March 2023. That date describes the register on the website, not the term of a councillor or the date of an election.

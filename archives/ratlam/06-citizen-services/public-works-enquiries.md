# Ratlam municipal sanitation, street repairs and building enquiries

## Municipal responsibilities

Ratlam Nagar Nigam lists cleaning public streets, drains, toilets and urinals among its duties. It also lists drain repairs, removal of garbage dumps and debris, solid waste management, disposal of animal carcasses, maintenance of parks and protection of trees in public places.

## Requests and documents

| Request | What the city's service page lists |
| --- | --- |
| Street light | Applicant's name and mobile number; the Street Light link points to the state's new-grievance service |
| Road service | Applicant's name and mobile number |
| Building permission | Photo identification, address proof and a building map |

The service enquiry number on the page is 07412-270555, at Shastri Nagar, Ratlam. The building permission entry does not specify a fee, decision deadline or format for the map.

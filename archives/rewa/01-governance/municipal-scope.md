# Rewa Nagar Nigam status and office

## Which local body this record covers

The district's urban-local-body directory calls the corporation **Nagar Palik Nigam Rewa**. Its [public-utility directory](https://rewa.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) calls it **Nagar Nigam Rewa** and gives its office PIN as 486001. These are two labels for the same city corporation in the district directory. This archive covers the corporation's municipal area, not Rewa district or Rewa division. The directory treats bodies such as Nagar Parishad Gurh and Nagar Parishad Semaria as separate entries. The [state urban local body map index](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=SF2Fyv4OVHlghtRd63MGiQ%3D%3D) also classifies the Rewa city row as नगर निगम, with Rewa in both the division and district columns.

## Office contact

Use the published corporation office line, **07662-242305**, or **commrewa@mpurban.gov.in** for a city municipal enquiry. The public-utility directory links a separate city domain, but that domain returned an error page when checked. The archive links to the district's working municipal directory instead. Neither directory sets out the legal boundary or supplies a formation notification; use the applicable gazette or delimitation order when a particular plot or locality is in question.

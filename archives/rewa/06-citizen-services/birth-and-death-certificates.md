# Birth and death certificate guidance for Rewa

## Birth certificate

The district's birth-certificate page, marked last updated **16 July 2019**, lists hospital proof showing the place of birth and the parent's identity proof. It marks the parent's marriage certificate optional. It identifies the Nagar Nigam office in Rewa, PIN 486001, and links to an older version of MP e-Nagar Palika. The document list describes the district's published guidance, not a complete set of rules for home births, corrections, or delayed registrations.

## Death certificate

The [district death-certificate page](https://rewa.nic.in/en/service/death-certificate/), also marked last updated **16 July 2019**, says applicants can visit a Lok Seva Kendra in Rewa district and submit the form in person. It names the Nagar Nigam office as its city contact point. The [district municipal directory](https://rewa.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) gives **07662-242305** and **commrewa@mpurban.gov.in**.

The state Urban Development and Housing Department now links the [MP e-Nagar Palika homepage](https://enagarpalika.mp.gov.in/home). The newer landing page was reachable, but no application was filed and no fee, deadline, or current application workflow was verified.

# Rewa Nagar Nigam in the state audited-accounts index

## Find the corporation row

On the department's audited-accounts pages, find the row with **रीवा** in the division, district and ULB columns and **नगर निगम** in the ULB Type column. This is the corporation entry. Nearby district rows for bodies such as Gurh and Semaria do not refer to the corporation.

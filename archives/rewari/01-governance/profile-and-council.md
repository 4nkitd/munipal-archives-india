# Municipal Council Rewari: Profile and elected council

## City and jurisdiction

Rewari is the headquarters of Rewari district in Haryana, southwest of Manesar. The city is linked to the Delhi to Jaipur corridor via NH-48 near Dharuhera and Bawal. NH-352W is the separate Gurugram to Pataudi to Rewari route, commonly called the Rewari Expressway. The [National Highways Authority of India route inventory](https://nhai.gov.in/nhai/sites/default/files/StateWiseLengthNHsIndia.pdf) identifies NH-352W as linking NH-352 near Vijay Nagar in Rewari with NH-48 near Shaktinagar in Gurugram.

The [council's city profile](https://mcrewari.in/about-us/about-rewari-city) dates the municipal office to 1885 and gives a 2011 Census city population of 143,021 and a city area of 24.93 sq km. The [Haryana Urban Local Bodies key figures](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) list 25.78 sq km for the municipal council and a population of 150,276 without a reference year in the table. The archive uses the state figure for council area and keeps the council's dated census figure for city population.

The municipal office is on Bharawas Road near Agarsen Chowk, Rewari, Haryana 123401. The [district directory](https://rewari.gov.in/public-utility/municipal-council/) gives the office phone as 01274-225263. The [council contact page](https://mcrewari.in/contact-us) lists eomcrwr1@gmail.com.

## Ward count and election history

Rewari had 31 wards at the 27 December 2020 general election. The [State Election Commission's 1 January 2021 notification](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2022/06/2022061047.pdf) names Poonam Yadav of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) as the elected president. The council's older city profile still shows the 31-ward count.

Haryana Urban Local Bodies order No. 9/124/2025-4CII, dated 24 December 2025, set out boundaries for Wards 1 through 32. The [council election page](https://mcrewari.in/election) links the final delimitation order and its map. The May 2026 general election used the 32-ward schedule.

## Chairperson and vice-chairperson

The May general election took place on 10 May 2026. Votes were counted on 13 May, and the [State Election Commission published notification SEC/1ME/2026/2378](https://secharyana.gov.in/document-category/municipal-elections-may-2026/) on 21 May under section 24(2) of the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973.

Vinita Pippal of the BJP won the directly elected president's post, which the council also calls chairperson. The post was reserved for Scheduled Castes (women). [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/bjp-bags-sampla-civic-body-presidency/) reported 44,521 votes for Pippal and 23,066 for Congress candidate Neharika Chaudhary, a margin of 21,455. The [council homepage](https://mcrewari.in/) lists Pippal as chairperson.

On 8 October 2026 the council elected its vice-chairperson. Ward 12 councillor Nikhil Yadav, identified as Nikhil Mandhaiya in [Amar Ujala's report](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/rewari/nikhil-mandhaiya-of-the-rao-camp-becomes-city-council-vice-chairman-wins-election-by-5-votes-rewari-news-c-198-1-rew1001-246843-2026-10-09), defeated Ward 7 councillor Chetram Saini by 19 votes to 14.

## Ward councillors elected in May 2026

The May result for the 32 ward seats was 19 independents, 12 BJP and one Indian National Congress (INC). The chairperson was elected separately. Names follow the [council directory](https://mcrewari.in/electedmember) and the election record, with spelling cross-checked against the [published ward results](https://www.indiatvnews.com/haryana/rewari-municipal-council-election-results-2026-complete-list-of-ward-and-party-wise-winners-latest-updates-2026-05-13-1040939).

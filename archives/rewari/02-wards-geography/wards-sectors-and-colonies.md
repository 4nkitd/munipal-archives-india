# Rewari wards, sectors and colonies

## The 31-ward index is historical

The council's [About Rewari City](https://mcrewari.in/about-us/about-rewari-city) page says Rewari has 31 wards. It also gives the 2011 Census population as 143,021 and the city area as 24.93 square kilometres. These figures belong to that city profile, not necessarily to the latest municipal boundary.

The final delimitation order dated 24 December 2025, No. 9/124/2025-4CII, contains **32 wards**, including a separate Ward 32. It refers to the preliminary notification dated 10 December 2025 and drawing No. 2171/Drawing/MCR dated 18 December 2025. The written schedule must be read with that drawing. The [council election page](https://mcrewari.in/election) links both the final notification and [final map](https://mcrewari.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/Wardbandi.pdf). The state wardbandi index lists the final notification on 29 December 2025. That is a publication-list date, not the order date.

The companion `wards.json` retains exactly Ward 1 through Ward 31 to satisfy the historical archive scope. Its empty locality arrays mean unverified, not uninhabited. No verified historical boundary schedule or old-to-new crosswalk was recovered for this record. Zones, engineering divisions and councillors are consequently not assigned. Do not use that JSON as the 2026 electoral map or silently omit Ward 32 from a current ward directory.

## Old town, markets and road corridors

Locality names, market names, police jurisdictions and municipal wards describe different things. Model Town should not be labelled an old-town mohalla simply because it appears in the same city index.

| Place or name | Evidence and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Gokul Gate, also Gokal Gate | Rewari Police uses the spelling Gokal Gate for a police post. A report dated 8 October 2026 connects the gate's public toilet with Punjabi Market, Kewal Bazaar, Kath Mandi, Railway Road and Moti Chowk. These are locality and market references, not a ward allocation. |
| Gurjarwada, Baluwada and Vaidhwada | These old-town mohalla names occur in the December 2025 Ward 15 boundary description, together with Jeewali Bazaar, Kanod Gate, Agrasen Chowk and Nai Wali Chowk. Boundary references do not establish that each entire mohalla lies within that ward. |
| Model Town | The December 2025 schedule uses Model Town addresses and its police station as boundary landmarks, notably in Wards 11, 19 and 20. It cannot be assigned to one historical ward from those references. |
| Brass Market | The secondary area-map catalogue lists a HUDA Brass and Allied Market plan separately from the sector plans. Preserve the commercial name. Do not treat it as proof that all historic brass-working lanes belong to that planned market. |
| Bawal Road and Bawal Chowk | A road corridor and junction within Rewari's geography. The December 2025 Ward 19 and Ward 23 descriptions use Bawal Chowk. Bawal municipality is a separate local body and its colony notifications must not be merged into Rewari's. |

The [Rewari Police account](https://rewari.haryanapolice.gov.in/about-rewari-police) also names Bharawas Gate, Kanode Gate and Jagan Gate posts. These support place-name indexing only. Police-post boundaries are not ward boundaries.

## HSVP sectors and planned areas

HUDA is the older name found on many plans. Current authority references use Haryana Shehri Vikas Pradhikaran, or HSVP. A sector number is not a ward number, and nearby private townships are not automatically HSVP-developed land.

| Sector or planned area | Source-backed detail | Limit of this record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sector 1 | The area-map catalogue calls it HUDA Sector 1, N.M.T., Rewari and lists a shopping-centre plan. | Secondary plan index only. Historical ward allocation unverified. |
| Sector 3 | HSVP's 20 September 2024 notice names Sector 3 Part I, Rewari in an additional-price recovery notice. The secondary catalogue separately lists Parts 1 and 2 and Housing Board plans. | Part-specific notices should not be generalized to the entire sector. |
| Sector 4 | HSVP's January 2026 demarcation-plan index lists residential and commercial plans for Sector 4, Rewari. The December 2025 ward schedule uses Sector 4 addresses in Wards 11 and 20. | References show that one sector need not equal one ward. Consult the full drawing for parcel placement. |
| Sector 18 | HSVP's 20 September 2024 notice names Sectors 18 and 19, Rewari together for additional-price recovery. | No verified 31-ward allocation. |
| Sector 19 | The same recovery notice and a 29 October 2024 HSVP site-allotment advertisement identify Sector 19, Rewari. The December 2025 Ward 11 description references an Ansal Town address near Sector 19. | Ansal Town and HSVP plans must be checked separately. |
| Other sector references | The secondary catalogue also indexes Sector 5 shopping centre, Suncity Sector 6, Sector 7 and Sectors 20 and 24. | Discovery references only, not a verified inventory of developed sectors or municipal inclusion. |

Keep Rewari city, Dharuhera, Bawal and Kosli distinct when searching plan catalogues. The same catalogue contains plans from all of them, including similarly numbered sectors.

## Regularized colonies, 2023 to 2026

The [DULB regularized-colony index](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) is the primary discovery source. Despite its 2023 and 2024 title, it includes 2025 documents, including a municipality-wise status list of 836 colonies dated 13 October 2025. Several older notifications were uploaded again on 26 September 2025. Do not mistake the upload date for a new legal notification.

| Date and evidence | Rewari city names or status | Archival qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 21 December 2023, Amar Ujala report | Uday Nagar; Buddh Vihar; Khati Colony; Shiv Nagar Part 3; Laxmi Nagar Phase 2. | Reported as five newly regularized Rewari colonies. Exact polygons and notification entries have not been independently transcribed here. The same report's Nehchana Road Part 2, Hanuman Colony and Sant Kabir Colony belong to Bawal and are excluded. |
| 26 August 2024, Amar Ujala report | Sajjan Lok Colony; Thathera Colony Gokalgarh; Gokul Dham Colony; Shiv Nagar Part A; Ram Vihar Colony; Shastri Nagar Qutubpur Mola Colony; Sajjan Society Colony. | Reported as seven colonies still awaiting regularization at that date. These are not recorded as approved. Transliteration follows the report's Hindi names. |
| 2025, state portal | State notification batches dated 18 March and 22 May 2025 and a status document listed on 13 October 2025. | Portal availability alone does not establish which additional Rewari colonies were approved. No new Rewari name is asserted from these lists without inspecting the relevant entry and plan. |
| 2026 review | No additional colony-specific Rewari approval was verified in the sources consulted for this record. | This is a research gap, not a statement that no approval occurred. |

The 2023 report also mentions 14 colonies regularized earlier that year, but does not provide a complete name schedule. No names have been invented to fill that gap. The 2024 report distinguishes colonies outside the city area from colonies within council jurisdiction. District-level totals must not be substituted for Municipal Council Rewari totals.

Regularization evidence must be matched to the exact part, phase and notified polygon. Shiv Nagar Part 3 and Shiv Nagar Part A are not interchangeable. Nor does a regularization report establish title, sanctioned building plans or completion of sewer and water works for an individual property.

## Sources and verification route

1. [Municipal Council Rewari city profile](https://mcrewari.in/about-us/about-rewari-city), the historical 31-ward statement.
2. [Council election documents](https://mcrewari.in/election), final order dated 24 December 2025 and final ward map.
3. [DULB wardbandi index](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/WardBandiNewNotification), final Rewari notification listed 29 December 2025.
4. [DULB colony notifications and status lists](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024), notification-level discovery source.
5. [HSVP 2024 notices](https://www.hsvphry.org.in/Home/Notices?branch=All&type=All&year=2024), sector-specific recovery and allotment references.
6. [HSVP demarcation-plan index](https://hsvphry.org.in/documents/home/news_updates/newsUpdate_202601271259_639051155993331731.pdf), January 2026 index containing Sector 4 Rewari.
7. [Rewari Properties area maps](https://www.rewariproperties.com/rewari/area-maps/), secondary map catalogue, not a statutory boundary source.
8. [Amar Ujala, 21 December 2023](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/rewari/5-colonies-of-city-and-3-colonies-of-bawal-regularized-rewari-news-c-198-1-rew1001-7696-2023-12-21), five Rewari and three Bawal colonies.
9. [Amar Ujala, 26 August 2024](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/rewari/seven-colonies-not-legalized-lack-of-facilities-rewari-news-c-198-1-rew1001-208532-2024-08-26), seven pending city colonies.
10. [Amar Ujala, 8 October 2026](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/rewari/demand-for-early-completion-of-the-public-toilet-at-gokal-gate-rewari-news-c-198-1-rew1001-246772-2026-10-08), Gokal Gate and adjoining market names.

For an address lookup, first identify the election year. Match the property and street against that year's final ward drawing and voter list. For colony status, obtain the notification entry and polygon from the town-planning branch before using a locality name as evidence of approval.

## Executive Officer, sanitation and taxation gaps

The current officers table names Abhe Singh as Deputy Municipal Commissioner, not Executive Officer. A search-index result for the former [Executive Officer page](https://mcrewari.in/executive-officer) names Arun Nandal, but that URL returned HTTP 404 when fetched on 22 November 2026. It is not evidence of a current appointment. No current Executive Officer is therefore asserted in this roster.

The official [organizational chart](https://mcrewari.in/about-us/organizational-chart) lists Executive Officer, Chief Sanitary Inspector and Sanitary Inspector posts. A named current sanitary inspector, sanitation telephone number and taxation officer were not verified from the checked official directory. Accounts staff listed above must not be assumed to hold taxation responsibility.

For property-tax services, the council links to the [Haryana property tax portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). For sanitation complaints, the council links to the [ULB grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/). These are official service routes, not direct officer contacts. Ask the municipal office to confirm the responsible branch and current officer before addressing a formal representation.

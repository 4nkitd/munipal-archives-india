## GRAP directions relevant to Rewari

CAQM's [direction dated 29 September 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/fa4070ca-5b45-4161-b220-0871227ae9ea.pdf), file A-110018/01/2021-CAQM, issues a revised GRAP schedule under section 12 of the CAQM Act, 2021. It requires Urban Local Bodies and other agencies to enforce applicable measures within their jurisdictions when the relevant stage is invoked. This is an NCR direction relevant to Rewari, not an order issued by Rewari Council.

| Stage | Delhi AQI range in the revised schedule |
| --- | --- |
| I, Poor | 201 to 300 |
| II, Very Poor | 301 to 400 |
| III, Severe | 401 to 450 |
| IV, Severe+ | Above 450 |

Stages may be invoked in advance on forecasts, and higher stages add to lower-stage measures. The schedule alone is not evidence that a particular stage is currently active.

Official order references include:

- [2 November 2026 Stage II invocation](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/011a2816-839f-43e5-99df-e5a4019b6bb2.pdf), applying across the entire NCR and retaining Stage I measures.
- [30 October 2026 precautionary order](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/22e7a559-e78f-49b0-b3f6-3f4e93cfa80f.pdf), specifying restrictions for 5 to 20 November 2026. Its outdoor construction/demolition restriction covers the entire NCR subject to stated exemptions. Its 50 percent office-attendance provision names Delhi and selected districts, not Rewari.
- [17 November 2026 Stage III revocation](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/b91570de-8cc1-4735-8879-28f2bc1550f2.pdf), revoking the 6 November invocation while retaining Stages I and II and the separate time-limited precautionary order.

These are dated archival references, not a live enforcement-status declaration for 22 November 2026. Check the [CAQM GRAP orders index](https://caqm.nic.in/landingpage/pages/grap/grap-orders) for later invocation, revocation, extension and project-specific closure orders. No Rewari-issued GRAP order was identified on the municipal notification page reviewed for this record.

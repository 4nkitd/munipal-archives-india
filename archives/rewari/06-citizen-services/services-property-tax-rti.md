# Rewari citizen services: property tax, certificates, RTI and grievances

## Official service routes

The Municipal Council Rewari website links the following services. These are verified published links, not a record of a completed application or payment. This guide's date is the compilation date, not the date of a new municipal notification.

| Service | Official destination linked by Rewari | Published purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax | [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Find property details and pay property tax |
| Birth and death registration | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Registration status and certificate services |
| Marriage registration | [Haryana marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) | Marriage registration status and certificates |
| RTI application | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Request information under the RTI Act, 2005 |
| Public grievance | [DULB grievance system](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Lodge and track a municipal complaint |
| Building plan approval | [ULB OBPAS](https://obpas.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Online building plan application |

The municipal office address published on the website is Bharawas Road, near Agarsen Chowk, Bhajan Ka Bagh, Raam Bagh Mohalla, Rewari, Haryana 123401, plus code 5JV6+HCJ.

The certificate links do not establish a uniform fee, document checklist or completion period. Select the relevant service in the destination portal and use its current requirements. Birth/death and marriage services are separate from the property portal. The historical Rewari DULB page also lists instructions on ORGI/CRS birth and death registration software, including an instruction dated 12 October 2015, but its displayed archival validity ended in 2019. It should not replace current application instructions.

## Property tax, Property ID and NDC

The [DULB Right to Service schedule](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService) directs property register changes, new Property IDs and objections on the No Dues Certificate portal to the Haryana property portal. NDC objections expressly cover property dues, size, floor details, authorized or unauthorized status, and property category.

Use the property's existing record when seeking correction. Keep the application number and payment receipt so a later complaint identifies the transaction. This archive does not treat a tax payment or Property ID as proof of title or colony regularization.

| Service | Published limit in working days | Municipal Council responsibility and redress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change of owner/occupier, other than death cases | 15 | Schedule lists Secretary/Executive Officer/Zonal Taxation Officer across ULB classes; first authority for Councils is the concerned Sub Divisional Officer, Civil; second is District Municipal Commissioner |
| Change of owner/occupier following death | 45 | Same published property-register authority and Council redress route |
| NDC objections concerning dues or property particulars | 10 | Superintendent/Secretary; first authority Executive Officer; second District Municipal Commissioner |
| New Property ID | 10 | Superintendent/Secretary; first authority Executive Officer; second District Municipal Commissioner |
| Copy of documents | 15 | Superintendent/Secretary; first authority Executive Officer; second District Municipal Commissioner; apply through SARAL |

The schedule identifies itself as consolidated through 4 November 2025 and links the notification dated 16 August 2022 and amendments dated 11 March and 27 October 2025. These are published service limits, not observed processing times.

The [property tax instruction index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists a Councils and Committees notification published on 31 July 2026 and an interest-waiver notification published on 18 May 2026 for arrears from 2010-11 to 2024-25. Their titles alone do not establish eligibility or an open rebate deadline. Check the full applicable notification before calculating dues.

## RTI and access to municipal records

The [DULB PIO list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList), Municipal Councils row 18, identifies the Executive Officer, Municipal Council Rewari, as State Public Information Officer and the City Magistrate, Rewari, as First Appellate Authority. This records the published designations rather than an assumed current officer name.

Rewari's "Apply RTI" control points to SARAL. Request identifiable existing records, such as an assessment entry, municipal order, expenditure record or notification copy, and specify the period and subject. Retain the acknowledgement and any transfer or reply. A complaint seeking a service correction is different from a request for information.

The PIO page contains entries reflecting older municipal classifications elsewhere in the state. Confirm the receiving office if SARAL presents a different authority. No Rewari-specific RTI fee or newly issued PIO appointment order was verified for this record.

## Complaints and Right to Service redress

The [DULB grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) offers registration and sign-in. It also identifies the "eSAMADHAN DULB HARYANA" mobile app and toll-free 1800-8900-929, available 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday. Rewari separately links CM Window at [cmharyanacell.nic.in](https://cmharyanacell.nic.in/).

Selected municipal service commitments in the DULB schedule are:

| Complaint/service | Working-day limit | Council designated officer | First / second redress authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Removal of solid waste from streets, roads, green belts and open spaces | 2 | Chief Sanitary Inspector | Executive Officer / District Municipal Commissioner |
| Replacement of streetlights | 10 | Junior Engineer | Municipal Engineer / District Municipal Commissioner |
| Removal of stray cattle | 2 | Chief Sanitary Inspector | Executive Officer / District Municipal Commissioner |
| Installation of drain covers | 2 | Chief Sanitary Inspector | Executive Officer / District Municipal Commissioner |
| Coverage of sewerage and storm-water manholes | 2 | Chief Sanitary Inspector | Executive Officer / District Municipal Commissioner |
| Repair of road potholes | 5 | Municipal Engineer | Executive Engineer / District Municipal Commissioner |

The same state schedule limits its water-supply connection and water-leakage/sewer-overflow entries to specified Municipal Corporations. Those entries are not Rewari Council service guarantees. Preserve the acknowledgement date when pursuing the notified first and second grievance authorities.

# Rewari tenders, budgets and civic infrastructure, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and procurement records

This record covers Municipal Council Rewari works and related infrastructure delivered by other agencies. A tender value is an estimate, not proof of an award, payment or completed work. Dates below are the published schedules, subject to corrigenda.

The [HEWP live-tender page](https://works.haryana.gov.in/LiveTender.aspx) states that tenders floated through the Haryana Engineering Works Portal are published on NIC Haryana eProcurement for bidder participation. They appear in the HEWP live list the next day. The [HEWP FAQ](https://works.haryana.gov.in/HEWP_Login/FAQs.aspx) distinguishes creation of a contractor ID from contractor registration and departmental enlistment. EMD exemption depends on the applicable registration and performance conditions, not merely having an account.

Search [Haryana eProcurement](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app) using the tender IDs below and the organisation chain `Haryana Government || Urban Local Bodies || MC Rewari`. Use the portal's archive, tender-status, corrigendum and cancelled/retendered sections for historical records. For non-municipal works, use the agency identified below. The [municipal website](https://mcrewari.in/) is an additional official reference, but its homepage is not an annual budget statement or a works-completion register.

## Roads, water supply and sewerage

The [official HSVP tender 2024_HBC_383985_1](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app?component=%24DirectLink&page=FrontEndViewTender&service=direct&sp=SAkrJTTlJeZjfYzPrWdFckw%3D%3D), reference `38/2024/EE/HSVP/REWARI`, was published on 27 June 2024. Its value is Rs 4,90,000. The portal title combines roads, water supply, sewerage and storm-water drainage in Sector 7 with construction of 12-metre roads near Plot 1016 P in Sector 18. Preserve that wording when locating the DNIT rather than assuming the amount represents an entire sector infrastructure scheme. The issuing body is HSVP Division Rewari, not the municipal council.

For municipal market roads, [Amar Ujala, 2 May 2025](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/rewari/the-condition-of-the-main-roads-of-the-city-will-improve-with-rs-363-crore-rewari-news-c-198-1-rew1001-218798-2025-05-02), reported Rs 3.63 crore of tendered repairs covering Aggarsain Chowk to Jhajjar Chowk, Railway Chowk to Gokal Gate, and the old vegetable market to Barahazari Chowk. The report says the council chose cement-concrete roads after objections to tiled roads. This is a reported tender estimate, not a measured completion value.

Do not treat storm-water desilting as sewerage network renewal. No verified municipality-wide sewer replacement allocation or completed water-treatment capacity was found in the sources used here.

## AIIMS Majra-Rewari corridor

AIIMS at Majra and its access infrastructure are district and state-agency projects. Their costs must not be presented as expenditure from the Rewari municipal budget.

The [official PHED tender 2025_HRY_469496_1](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app?component=%24DirectLink&page=FrontEndLatestActiveTenders&service=direct&sp=SudIVUB7r%2Bt%2FlNRU0dVnZWg%3D%3D) is for annual operation and maintenance of the village water-supply scheme at Majra Mustil Bhalkhi during 2025-26. Issued by Bawal PHED on 2 September 2025, it has a value of Rs 1,95,710 and a 365-day work period. It is not described as construction of the dedicated AIIMS waterworks.

[The Tribune, 6 April 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/26-8-crore-waterworks-unit-for-aiims-rewari/), quoted Health Minister Arti Singh Rao announcing in-principle approval for a dedicated canal-based AIIMS waterworks estimated at Rs 26.83 crore, with the state bearing the cost. In-principle approval does not establish a construction award or commissioning.

For access from NH 11, [TenderShark's listing of 2026_HRY_561405_1](https://www.tendershark.com/details/haryana-tender/government-of-haryana/3499b789-5f59-4fb6-9507-d5388223f332), corroborated by [IndianTenders](https://www.indiantenders.in/tenderdetail/const-rob-over-rewari-narnaul-railway-line-ir-km-20880-924-and-rewari-narnaul-dfccil-ohe-no-1316-conn-063ca19/), records a PWD EPC tender published on 19 November 2026. The proposed work is a road overbridge across the Rewari-Narnaul railway and DFCCIL corridor, with a multi-loop trumpet interchange connecting AIIMS Majra to Rewari-Narnaul Road, NH 11. The secondary listings report approximately Rs 169.31 crore and a bid deadline of 10 December 2026.

This corridor entry is secondary-source tender metadata. The official DNIT, subsequent corrigenda and award were not retrieved. As of the verification date, the listed deadline is still in the future; no completed bridge, interchange or uninterrupted operational access is claimed.

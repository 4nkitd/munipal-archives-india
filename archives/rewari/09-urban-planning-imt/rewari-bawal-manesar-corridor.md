# Rewari development plan and the Bawal-Manesar transport corridor

## Rewari's notified development plan

Haryana published the Final Development Plan 2031 AD for Rewari through notification **CCP(NCR)/RE/FDP/2020/492 dated 3 June 2020**, in Haryana Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 76-2020/Ext. The notification invokes section 5(7) of the Punjab Scheduled Roads and Controlled Areas Restriction of Unregulated Development Act, 1963 and section 203C(7) of the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973. It follows the draft notification CCP(NCR)/RE/DDP/2019/1675 dated 22 August 2019. The final drawing is DTP(RE)650/2020 dated 25 February 2020. [1]

The plan covers the controlled areas around Rewari, including Controlled Area II in part and the controlled area around the primary school at Khaliawas. This planning geography is not identical to the municipal boundary. The plan's population projection for 2031 is 704,062, approximately seven lakh. It is a planning assumption, not a census count.

| Proposed land use | Area in hectares |
| --- | ---: |
| Residential | 2,148 |
| Commercial | 404 |
| Industrial | 333 |
| Transport and communication | 612 |
| Public utilities | 197 |
| Public and semi-public uses | 341 |
| Open spaces | 758 |
| Total in the land-use statement | 4,793 |

The statement excludes the 787-hectare existing town area from its residential proposal. The industrial allocation is in Sectors 11 part, 11A, 12 part and 40. The explanatory note relates this allocation to nearby industrial development at Dharuhera and Bawal and the Manesar-Bawal Investment Region. Transport and communication reservations include Sectors 28 and 39. These are allocations and proposals, not evidence that every sector or facility has been built. [1, pp. 849-851]

## What 2041 means in this record

The official 2041 document located for this research is the National Capital Region Planning Board's **Draft Regional Plan 2041**, dated December 2021. It is a multi-state regional document, not a document titled Rewari Final Development Plan 2041. No separate notified Rewari city development plan for 2041 was verified in the sources reviewed. This record therefore uses the notified Rewari 2031 plan for local land-use facts. [1, 2]

Section 3.2.6 of the NCR draft records Haryana's controlled-area coverage along the Gurugram-Manesar-Bawal-Dharuhera-Rewari highway corridor. That is a statement about planning control over a regional corridor, not a single municipal jurisdiction or a blanket industrial land-use permission. The same draft discusses highway corridor zones and transit-oriented development at the regional scale.

The December 2021 draft should not be treated as proof of the final notification status of Regional Plan 2041 in November 2026. A buyer or applicant must obtain the current regional notification and the applicable Haryana development plan, zoning regulations and sector layout. Proximity to NH-48, a freight alignment or IMT Bawal does not itself establish development rights. The Manesar-Bawal Investment Region mentioned in Rewari's explanatory note is also distinct from the individual HSIIDC industrial estates.

## HSIIDC IMT Bawal and the industrial link

HSIIDC's official IMT Bawal profile places the township about **14 km from Rewari**, **60 km from Gurgaon** and **100 km from Delhi**. Its location paragraph retains the former highway number NH-8, while its nearest-highway field identifies **NH-48, Delhi-Jaipur**. These refer to the same highway corridor under old and current numbering. HSIIDC identifies Rewari as the nearest railway station. These are estate-profile distances, not measured journey times. [3]

| IMT Bawal phase | Establishment year |
| --- | --- |
| Phase I | 1995 |
| Phase II | 2006 |
| Phase III | 2008 |
| Phase IV | 2010 |

The profile describes all four phases as planned and developed. It lists auto parts, zippers and medical equipment among the estate's products. Named units include Becton Dickinson India, YKK India, Technico Industries, Keihin FIE and India Japan Lighting. Listed infrastructure includes estate roads, water supply through overhead service reservoirs, a common effluent treatment plant, sewerage, power distribution, a police station building and a fire station. These are HSIIDC's published descriptions, not an independent inspection of service performance.

IMT Bawal and IMT Manesar are separate industrial townships. NH-48 provides the regional road connection through the industrial belt toward Manesar and Gurugram, while Rewari has its own Bawal road connection. HSIIDC estate permissions, allotment conditions and layouts must be checked separately from Rewari municipal records. The estate profile directs applicants to Estate Management Procedures 2015 for possession, project completion, transfer, renting and other allotment matters. [3]

## New Rewari freight junction and Kathuwas

The Ministry of Railways records the dedication of the **306 km Rewari-Madar section of the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor on 7 January 2021**. Approximately 79 km of that section lies in Haryana's Rewari and Mahendragarh districts and 227 km in Rajasthan. Of its nine new DFC stations, three are junction stations: **New Rewari, New Ateli and New Phulera**. The other six are crossing stations. [4]

New Rewari is the freight-corridor junction name. It must not be silently substituted for the conventional Rewari Junction passenger and railway-network station. The two belong to the wider Rewari rail geography but are not interchangeable station references. The 2031 development plan's transport reservations and proposed railway facilities likewise do not prove that a particular freight terminal, siding or passenger facility is operational.

The Railways release explicitly identifies industries in the **Rewari-Manesar** area among the beneficiaries of the opened section. It also identifies CONCOR's container depot at **Kathuwas** as a facility brought onto the DFC map, with expected faster throughput. This is the documented basis for describing the Rewari area as a freight connection for the industrial corridor. It does not establish a dedicated rail siding into every Bawal or Manesar factory.

The release describes the wider western corridor as connecting Dadri with Jawaharlal Nehru Port and names Gujarat ports among the expected beneficiaries. Its figures and completion statements are dated January 2021. They should not be read as a current claim that every part of the wider corridor or every port connection was complete on that date. [4]

## NH-48 and Gurugram-Pataudi-Rewari NH-352W

The two roads have different functions. **NH-48** is the Delhi-Jaipur highway serving the Manesar, Dharuhera and Bawal industrial belt. **NH-352W** is the Gurugram-Pataudi-Rewari connection, providing a separate approach between Rewari and Gurugram through Pataudi. NH-352W is not the same road as NH-352, which also appears in Rewari's development plan.

The 2031 plan reserves existing NH-48 width with a 60-metre green belt on either side. It gives the Rewari-Pataudi NH-352W road the existing width with 60-metre green belts on either side. Rewari-Bawal Road has a planned 30-metre road reservation with 30-metre green belts on either side. These are statutory planning reservations, not measurements of today's paved carriageway. [1, p. 851]

On 14 July 2020, the Union government announced the foundation stone for the **46 km, four-lane Gurugram-Pataudi-Rewari section of NH-352W**, with a stated project cost of **Rs 1,524 crore**. [5] IRCON Gurgaon Rewari Highway Limited's 2024-25 annual report identifies the concession's design length as **46.11 km**, its bid project cost as **Rs 900 crore including GST**, the concession agreement date as **20 January 2021**, and the appointed date as **24 November 2021**. The announcement cost and concession bid cost are different reported cost measures and should not be merged. [6]

The annual report records **63.68% physical progress at 31 March 2025** and an expected completion date of 31 December 2025. A forecast is not a completion certificate. This record does not verify full opening of the upgraded highway as of November 2026. Access to individual IMT Manesar sectors also depends on connecting roads and estate entrances, rather than NH-352W alone. [6]

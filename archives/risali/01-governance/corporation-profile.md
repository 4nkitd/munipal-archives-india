# Municipal Corporation Risali profile

## Corporation and jurisdiction

The Chhattisgarh State Election Commission lists Risali as a separate corporation in Durg district and records its formation on 26 December 2019. The [Durg district register](https://durg.gov.in/en/urban-local-body/) gives Risali 40 wards. An [older tender from January 2019](https://durg.gov.in/en/notice/manual-tenders-are-invited-from-registered-contractors-in-the-competent-category-under-integrated-registration-system-on-behalf-of-commissioner-municipal-corporation-corporation-bhilai-zone-6-risali-b/) describes the area as Bhilai Zone 6 and names Municipal Corporation Bhilai as the issuing office. That tender predates the formation date and does not establish present-day jurisdiction.

## Office

The [district directory entry](https://durg.gov.in/en/public-utility/nagar-nigam-risali/) lists Nagar Nigam Risali, PIN 490006, phone 0788-4049469 and email `nagarnigamrisali@gmail.com`. It does not give a separate municipal website.

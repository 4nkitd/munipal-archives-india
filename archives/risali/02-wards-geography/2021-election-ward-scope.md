# Risali ward scope in the 2021 municipal election

## Where to find Risali in the results

The State Election Commission's councillor list uses municipal body entry 8 for Risali on PDF pages 3–4. The block starts after Bhilai ward 70 and ends before Bhilai-Charoda ward 1. The rows give a ward number, name, elected councillor and affiliation.

| Ward | Name in the 2021 result |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Talpuri |
| 2–4 | Ruabandha North, South and East |
| 7–8 | Risali Sector East and West |
| 11–12 | Maroda Sector East and West |
| 33–34 | Newai Basti East and West |
| 40 | Puraina Basti |

These are examples of election labels, not boundary lines. The named sectors and bastis appear as ward labels in that edition; the result does not establish that every address with a similar locality name belongs to a particular ward.

# Risali 40-ward draft boundary publication notice

## What the signed notice says

[Scanned map/PDF — visual source linked at source_url]

On 7 February 2020 the Collector, Durg, signed a Rule 7 form notifying residents that proposals had been prepared to divide Municipal Corporation Risali into 40 wards and define their boundaries. The proposals were open for inspection at the Risali corporation office during office hours. The written notice allowed suggestions or objections within seven days of its date, to the Collector, District Durg, in Room 21 of the District Urban Development Agency.

## Notice date and portal display dates

The district portal lists 8–22 February 2020 as the page's display period. That does not replace the seven-day instruction in the signed notice. The attachment is the publication notice itself, not the draft boundary schedule or final delimitation.

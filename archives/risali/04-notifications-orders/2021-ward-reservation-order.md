# Risali ward reservation schedule for January 2021

## Risali session

The Collector and District Election Officer signed order no. 22 on 15 January 2021. It scheduled reservation proceedings for Municipal Corporation Risali at 11 a.m. on 23 January in the Collectorate meeting room, Durg. The order lists 40 Risali wards and says elected representatives could attend. It refers to Urban Administration and Development Department letter F 1–2/2018/18 of 23 December 2020 and the applicable municipal statutes.

Bhilai-Charoda had a separate session at 2 p.m. on 22 January and Jamul Municipal Council a session at 11 a.m. on that day. The district portal's display start date, 18 January, is later than the signed date. The order gives the schedule only, not individual ward reservations or the final outcome of proceedings.

# Risali building permission portal and office channel

## Selecting the local body

The Chhattisgarh Building Permission Management System lists Municipal Corporation Risali in the corporation selector. It also lists Bhilai and Durg separately. Use the corporation that governs the property, rather than the postal town or an older Bhilai Zone 6 reference. The portal describes a document upload and approval process; this record confirms the Risali option, not successful submission, the fee on an individual application or approval eligibility.

For a property where municipal jurisdiction is unclear, the [district's Risali directory page](https://durg.gov.in/en/public-utility/nagar-nigam-risali/) gives the municipal office at PIN 490006, 0788-4049469 and `nagarnigamrisali@gmail.com`.

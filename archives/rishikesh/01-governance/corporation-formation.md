# Rishikesh municipal boundary expansion and corporation formation

The corporation’s history distinguishes two actions. On **6 November 2017**, the municipality expanded to include Gram Sabha Rishikesh and Virpur Khurd (वीरपुर खुर्द). On **8 December 2017**, the expanded municipality became a Nagar Nigam. The first mayor, Anita Mamgain, and the first elected councillors took their oaths on 2 December 2018. Shambhu Paswan took office as the second mayor on 7 February 2025.

The [state register](https://udd.uk.gov.in/list-of-ulb/) places the corporation in Dehradun district. It lists Muni-ki-reti in Tehri and Swargashram Jaunk in Pauri as separate local bodies.

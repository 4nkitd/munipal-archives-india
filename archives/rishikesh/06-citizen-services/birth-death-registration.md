# Registering a birth or death in Rishikesh

A family member or close relative can report the event to the municipal birth and death registrar. Use **Form 1** for a birth and **Form 2** for a death. The department says that reporting within **21 days of the event** qualifies for a free certificate.

The department’s “Links & Downloads” section has separate applications for births and deaths. For a late report, contact the [municipal office](https://nagarnigamrishikesh.com/contact/) at 0135-2430015; the cited guidance describes the within-21-days procedure.

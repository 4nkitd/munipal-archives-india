# Municipal Corporation Rohtak: City and governance profile

## Formation, area and population

Haryana constituted Municipal Corporation Rohtak on 17 March 2010, upgrading the former municipal council under the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. The [State Election Commission's March 2010 notification](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2022/07/2022071148.pdf) confirms the date. Rohtak city is the headquarters of Rohtak district, part of the [Haryana subregion of the National Capital Region](https://ncrpb.nic.in/ncrconstituent.html).

The [Haryana Urban Local Bodies key figures](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) list the corporation at **139.29 sq km** with a population of **4,58,163**. The table does not state a population reference year. The [corporation's city profile](https://www.mcrohtak.in/about-rohtak-city) separately reports **139.26 sq km** and quotes **4,17,355** for 2011. The [2011 District Census Handbook](https://censusindia.mha.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/464/download/1504/DH_2011_0614_PART_A_DCHB_ROHTAK.pdf) lists **3,74,292** for the census town entry labelled Rohtak (Municipal Council). These figures should not be treated as counts for the same boundary and date.

## Wards and leadership

The corporation has **22 wards**, numbered 1 to 22. The [elected members directory](https://www.mcrohtak.in/elected-members-0) lists the councillor for each ward and identifies **Ram Avtar Valmiki** as mayor.

Rohtak voted on 2 March 2025. Ram Avtar Valmiki of the Bharatiya Janata Party won the mayoral election, with the result [declared on 12 March 2025](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/chandigarh-news/setback-for-hooda-on-home-turf-as-bjp-s-valmiki-wins-rohtak-mayoral-election-101741771021529.html). He received 1,02,269 votes, winning by 45,198 over Suraj Mal Kiloi. The State Election Commission's [result notification of 20 March 2025, No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/202507081506973353.pdf), records the winner as Ram Avtar.

The [municipal officer directory](https://www.mcrohtak.in/officer-directory) lists **Satyender Duhan, IAS** as Municipal Commissioner. An [October 2026 Haryana government release](https://prms.prharyana.gov.in/press-release/9698) also identifies Duhan as commissioner and Valmiki as mayor.

# Rohtak Municipal Corporation elected council, March 2025

## Election and mayor

Rohtak voted on 2 March 2025. Results were declared on 12 March 2025. The State Election Commission, Haryana notified the elected mayor and ward members on 20 March 2025 in Notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272.

Ram Avtar Valmiki of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won the mayoral seat, which was reserved for a Scheduled Caste candidate. He received 102,269 votes. His nearest rival, Suraj Mal Kiloi of the Indian National Congress (INC), received 57,071 votes. The margin was 45,198 votes. The SEC notification lists the winner as Ram Avtar; the municipal directory gives his full name as Ram Avtar Valmiki.

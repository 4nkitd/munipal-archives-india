# Rohtak wards, sectors and locality geography

## Delimitation and limits of this index

The Haryana Government Gazette of 14 December 2024 publishes 22 wards for Municipal Corporation Rohtak under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994. Its English text prints notification number 04/07/2024-4CI and refers to the earlier notification dated 17 February 2024. The archive catalogue renders the number as 04/07/2024-4CII. Preserve this transcription difference when retrieving the original.

The schedule must be read with drawing MCR/TP/2024/3870 dated 4 December 2024. The DULB notification index lists the final Rohtak wardbandi on 16 December 2024. That is the portal listing date, not the gazette publication date.

The companion `../wards.json` contains one record for each ward. Locality and sector arrays identify names encountered along the scheduled boundary. They are not complete lists of inhabitants, plots or colonies inside a ward. A boundary can run through a colony or along a sector road. Empty arrays mean no allocation was established here, not that the ward has no such places. `zone` and `division` are null because the researched sources do not establish a municipal administrative-zone or engineering-division crosswalk.

The municipal website separates its 2018 election maps from its 2024 wardbandi page. Do not apply the 2018 map numbers automatically to the December 2024 schedule. For a property-level answer, obtain the notified drawing and match the property ID and road side against the current electoral roll.

## HSVP sectors and ward numbers

Sector numbers identify planning areas. Ward numbers identify electoral units. Ward 14, for example, does not establish an allocation for Sector 14.

The official Haryana e-procurement record for tender 2026_HRY_527727_1, published 9 June 2026, names a municipal rainwater-disposal pipeline in HSVP sectors 1, 2, 3 and 4. This verifies those sector names in an MC Rohtak works context but supplies no electoral crosswalk. HSVP's published layout-plan index also names Rohtak sectors 5, 6 Block A and B, 21, 25, 26, 27 and 28, and a Sector 3 shopping centre. Planning records and individual layout sheets should be consulted for plot boundaries.

The final ward schedule directly names sectors 3, 4, 4 Extension and 6 along Ward 9; sectors 3, 4, 4 Extension, 5 and 36 along Ward 10; Sector 3 along wards 12 and 13; and Sector 20A along Ward 22. These references can occur on shared boundaries. This archive does not assign all of sectors 1 or 2 to a ward.

Sector 14 was included in the research scope, but the sources reviewed do not establish its Rohtak layout or ward allocation. It is deliberately excluded from the verified sector array. Avoid importing Sector 14 information from Gurgaon, Hisar or Faridabad, which appear in statewide HSVP indexes.

## Old city, Model Town and Civil Lines

The old-city search area is best approached through named roads and mohallas rather than a single ward. The schedule describes Bara Bazar, Pili Kothi, the old vegetable market, Killa road, Lal Masjid, Mata Darwaja, Pithwara Mohalla, Para Mohalla, Salara Mohalla and Khokhra Kot across the boundaries of wards 3 through 7. Spellings vary between the Hindi schedule and English transcription. Khokra Kot and Khokhra Kot, and Silara and Salara, should be treated as search variants pending a map-level match.

Model Town occurs in both the Ward 13 and Ward 14 descriptions. The Ward 14 route names Khurana Eye Hospital road, Jain Mandir chowk and Chhotu Ram Polytechnic Society road; Ward 13 names the Model Town approach to Kamal Colony and Bharat Colony. Assigning all Model Town addresses to one ward would therefore be unsafe.

For Civil Lines and the administrative area, use the schedule's Civil road, Canal Rest House, Zila Vikas Bhawan, Zila Sainik Board, Mini Secretariat and court chowk references. They occur in the Ward 16 and Ward 17 schedules and neighboring boundary descriptions. The gazette does not provide a complete Civil Lines colony-to-ward list. A police-station jurisdiction is not a substitute for an electoral boundary.

## PGIMS and MDU institutional belt

The PGIMS area has particularly detailed boundary landmarks in wards 13 and 14. Ward 13 names the mortuary, Director office, Dean Park and Ranbir Singh OPD. Ward 14 names the PGI wall, Civil Surgeon office, medical campus gate, main PGI chowk, auditorium and pharmacy hostel before reaching the Delhi railway. Adarsh Nagar, D-Park and Neki Ram College chowk are nearby references in these routes.

Do not reduce the medical and university belt to a single ward. Maharshi Dayanand University and PGIMS are separate institutions. MDU's official telephone directory provides institutional context, but it is not a municipal delimitation document. The reviewed schedule does not explicitly assign the whole MDU campus to a ward. Campus addresses, hostels, adjoining colonies and land south of the railway need separate checks against the notified drawing.

## Bohar, Sunaria and the IMT corridor

Ward 10's route explicitly reaches Shiv Mandir at Bohar on Sonipat road and then follows segments beside IMT Sector 30 and IMT Sector 31B. It returns through Nandal Bhawan road, Sonipat road and the JLN canal area. This is direct evidence that the IMT corridor appears in the delimitation, not evidence that the whole industrial township belongs to Ward 10.

Ward 11 uses Sonipat road, the outer municipal limit, Karor-Kheri Sadh road, Delhi railway and JLN canal. The schedule does not enumerate every revenue village enclosed by that route. Bohar, Garhi Bohar and Kheri Sadh should not be treated as interchangeable village names.

Sunaria road, Sunaria chowk, Balchand chowk, Jhajjar road and the old sugar mill are reference points for wards 20, 21 and 22. Ward 22 also follows municipal-limit segments beside Drain 8 and Sector 20A. Sunaria Kalan and Sunaria Khurd are distinct revenue-estate names in colony records. A road called Sunaria road is not proof that either entire village is inside the ward described.

## Regularized colonies and revenue estates

DULB maintains a regularized-colony notification index and a Civic Amenities and Infrastructure Deficient Areas register. Colony regularization concerns a notified polygon and its revenue-estate and khasra details. It is not a new ward, a guarantee for every similarly named extension, or evidence of title to an individual plot.

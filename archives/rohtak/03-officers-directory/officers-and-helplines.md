# Municipal Corporation Rohtak officers and helplines

## Sources and currency

The [official officer directory](https://www.mcrohtak.in/officer-directory) supplies the officer names, designations and telephone numbers below. The [official contact page](https://www.mcrohtak.in/contact-us) supplies the office address and complaint helplines. Both pages display a site footer update date of 17 September 2026. This is a website update label, not an officer appointment date. The pages were checked on 22 November 2026. Telephone numbers are verified as published, not by placing calls.

The accompanying `officers.json` contains a selected administrative and citizen-facing roster, not every employee on the source page. Its serial numbers are local record identifiers. Empty email, ward, zone and division fields mean the directory does not supply that information; they do not establish a vacancy or a ward allocation.

# Rohtak official notices, GRAP orders and colony notifications

## Municipal notices and publication dates

The municipal website publishes an [Area sabha order, municipal corporation Rohtak](https://mcrohtak.in/area-sabha-order-municipal-corporation-rohtak), with a created date of 19 January 2026 and attachment [MC rohtak Area.pdf](https://mcrohtak.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/MC%20rohtak%20Area.pdf). The frontmatter date records this page date, not an independently established signing date.

The companion [Area sabha wardwise agenda](https://mcrohtak.in/area-sabha-wardwise-agenda) page was created on 20 January 2026. Its attachment is [area sabha wardwise agenda.pdf](https://mcrohtak.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/area%20sabha%20wardwise%20agenda.pdf). These are distinct records. The landing pages do not reproduce meeting venues, ward schedules, order numbers or the operative text, so those details are not transcribed here.

The separate [Notification](https://mcrohtak.in/notification) page, under the website's MC Election 2024 navigation, links [Notification wardbandhi.pdf](https://mcrohtak.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/Notification%20wardbandhi.pdf). The upload directory is not evidence of the notification's issue date. Consult the attachment for legally operative boundaries rather than treating this general notice index as a ward map.

The municipal contact address is Church Road, Near Ambedkar Chowk, Mahavir Colony, Company Bagh, Rohtak, Haryana 124001. The website footer displays an email naming another municipality; it is deliberately not reproduced as a verified Rohtak email.

## GRAP order applicable to the NCR

CAQM's [19 October 2025 Stage-II invocation](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Home/News/Invocation%20of%20GRAP%20order-%20Stage-%20II%20191020257fe54de8-42f2-456b-b1b7-75ec39e2bb14.pdf), number 120017/27/GRAP/2021/CAQM, invoked Stage II, "Very Poor" air quality, AQI 301 to 400, throughout the NCR with immediate effect. It retained Stage-I actions already invoked on 14 October 2025. The order refers to the GRAP schedule then issued on 13 December 2024.

The distribution list explicitly names the Deputy Commissioner, Rohtak, and Commissioner, Municipal Corporation Rohtak. It directs implementing agencies to monitor compliance, intensify measures and pay particular attention to dust mitigation. This establishes a direct Rohtak administrative connection rather than relying only on a generic Delhi pollution report.

This is a historical invocation, not verification of the GRAP stage in force on 22 November 2026. Later schedule revisions, invocation orders and revocations must be checked at [CAQM](https://caqm.nic.in/) before deciding whether a particular construction activity is prohibited. Do not apply geographically limited vehicle restrictions to Rohtak without checking the actual order.

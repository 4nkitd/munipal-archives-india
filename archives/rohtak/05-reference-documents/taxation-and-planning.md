# Rohtak property tax, NDC, building code and Lal Dora reference

## Rohtak property tax and no-dues certificate

MC Rohtak's [Property Tax & NDC page](https://mcrohtak.in/services/property-tax-ndc) lists "Generate No-Dues Certificate & Make Payment", dated 29 July 2025, and directs residents to the [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). The frontmatter date records this service listing.

The official [portal FAQ](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/FAQ) describes registration using full name, father or husband name and the applicant's own 10-digit mobile number, followed by OTP verification. After login, select the municipality as Rohtak. Search using the property ID, old property ID, colony, plot number, owner or occupier name, mobile number, or the GIS map.

Open "View Details" to inspect the record. Self-certification requires confirming the correctness of the individual data sections and selecting "Verify Details" when all data is correct. Incorrect data should not be confirmed merely to proceed with a payment.

Under "Make Payment/Generate NDC", enter the PID, search, and select the appropriate payment option. The [official payment manual dated 8 July 2024](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Content/UserManuals/How_to_Make_Payment_of_Property_Tax_Dues_08.07.2024.pdf) distinguishes these choices:

| Portal option | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Pay Property Tax | Payment only for property tax |
| Pay Garbage Collection Charges | Payment only for garbage collection |
| Pay all Dues and Generate NDC | All pending dues, including property tax, garbage collection and development charges |

The FAQ also includes fire tax within property-tax dues. Paying a single component is not the same workflow as clearing all dues and generating the NDC. The FAQ permits payment at the municipal office as well.

## Tax notification reference and limits

The [DULB property-tax notification register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists a "Property tax notification 2026 Corporation", published 31 July 2026, with [official attachment](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf). It separately lists a notification for councils and committees. Rohtak is a municipal corporation, so those two classes should not be interchanged.

The same register lists the corporation notification of 11 October 2013, subsequent amendments, and a one-time interest-waiver notification published 18 May 2026 concerning arrears from 2010-11 through 2024-25. These are verified index entries. This record does not transcribe the 2026 PDF's rate schedule, effective dates or waiver conditions and does not claim a waiver remains available.

Use the applicable notification and the assessed property's category, area, use and relevant financial year to establish liability. No current rupee-per-square-yard table is asserted here because the latest operative schedule has not been extracted and reconciled with its amendments.

## Lal Dora and Abadi Deh instructions

DULB's [28 October 2023 instruction](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F818c97e7-1645-4ec0-aec7-a694f399949d.pdf) addresses anomalies in municipal property data, development charges, agricultural properties and Lal Dora or Abadi Deh areas.

It directs municipalities to keep properties in Lal Dora or Abadi Deh areas marked approved. Development charges are not applicable to residential properties situated in those areas. This is a development-charge instruction, not a blanket exemption from every municipal tax or building requirement.

The instruction also says municipalities should update development charges already paid using municipal admin or checker accounts rather than forcing owners to raise objections for that purpose. It identifies statutory procedures under section 145 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 where an owner contests development charges on the ground of earlier payment.

The [DULB register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) separately lists "Property Certificate to the occupiers of the properties falling under the Abadi Deh/Lal Dora of Municipal Areas", published 15 March 2024, and its [attachment](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2Feb519b93-6e15-485c-8707-59ad443c6fc9.pdf). The listing does not itself prove an individual occupant's eligibility or title.

The official [new PID service document](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DirectorateofUrbanLocalBodies/Images/f99c58ff-9403-4a82-b14c-c1f7f04aaeef.pdf) names the Zonal Taxation Officer for municipal corporations. It lists identity proof, ownership proof, a location site plan and a building photograph. For Lal Dora cases it additionally lists relevant old ULB or Panchayat records where owner details are null or NA, legal-heir evidence for death cases, a revenue-registered court decree, or registry/sale deed. The document gives normal service as 10 working days and Tatkal as two working days, with Rs. 1,000 charged for Tatkal. These are the source document's published terms, not a measured Rohtak processing time.

## Haryana Building Code, 2017

The official [Haryana Building Code, 2017 reference copy](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/Manesar/Images/408719c6-e761-4fdb-a3c1-8caf3787c406.pdf) states statewide applicability from 6 January 2017 and includes amendments dated 22 December 2017 and 8 May 2018. Although hosted in another municipality's directory, it is a Haryana-wide code, not a Rohtak-specific permission or a consolidated statement of all later amendments.

The code defines "core area" to include thickly built-up old-town areas and Lal Dora or phirni of villages included in municipalities. That classification should not be confused with an exemption from obtaining a required building sanction.

Code 2.1 requires a person proposing erection, re-erection, alteration or demolition to notify the competent authority in Form BR-I. The listed accompanying material includes ownership documents, site and building plans, specifications in BR-II, structural drawings and certificates, public-health plans, applicable fire-safety design and prescribed scrutiny fees. The reference edition specifies a scrutiny fee of Rs. 10 per square metre of achieved covered area; confirm later amendments before payment.

Code 6.1 ties building use and character to the development plan, approved layout, sector plan or zoning plan. Property ID creation, property-tax payment, NDC generation and colony notification are not substitutes for building-plan approval. Confirm the site's competent authority, current code amendments and applicable planning controls before designing or starting work.

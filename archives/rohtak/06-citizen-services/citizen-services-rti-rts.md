# Rohtak citizen services, RTI and Right to Service

## Scope and service portals

This is a research compilation dated 22 November 2026, not a new municipal notification. Municipal Corporation Rohtak's e-services list and contact-page service links identify the following routes. Portal links and published guidance were checked; no application, payment or certificate download was submitted.

| Citizen need | Official route | What the municipal source lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax and property records | [Property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Payment of property tax dues, generation of No Dues Certificate, change of owner or occupier, and creation of new Property ID |
| Birth and death services | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Certified copies; birth, death and non-availability certificates; correction; delayed registration; inclusion of child's name |
| Pet registration | [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Certificate of dog registration; the RTS list separately includes renewal |
| Marriage registration | [Shaadi Haryana](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) | The current municipal contact-page footer links this address; the e-services table retains the older `shaadi.edisha.gov.in` address |
| Municipal grievances | [ULB grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Current municipal footer's public-grievance link |

The verified pet service is dog registration. These sources do not establish a general registration scheme for every species of pet. SARAL offers online applications and assisted access through SARAL Kendras and Common Service Centres. Its FAQ instructs applicants to log in, choose "Apply for Services", then "View all available services" and search for the relevant service.

## Property tax and Property ID

Use the property portal for Rohtak municipal property-tax dues and record corrections. Check that the selected municipal body and property record are correct before paying or raising an objection. Preserve the Property ID, payment receipt and application acknowledgement for follow-up.

The portal's [List of Documents](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments) distinguishes name changes in death and non-death cases, corrections to property details, dues updates, creation of a new Property ID and splitting an existing ID. For a new ID it lists applicant identity proof, ownership proof, a site plan showing the location and a building photograph, with additional documents for specified cases. A dues-update request for an unadjusted payment requires the payment receipt.

The document list states that there is no fee for updating the owner's name. This is not a statement that all property services or tax liabilities are free. Death-related name changes have a separate evidence checklist, including the death certificate and succession-related documents. Follow that checklist rather than using a non-death application. No tax rebate, amnesty period or current rate is asserted in this guide.

## Birth, death, dog and marriage registration

For birth and death records, select the precise SARAL service. A certified copy of an existing entry, a correction, delayed registration and inclusion of a child's name are different requests. Retain the Application Reference ID. SARAL provides application and appeal tracking at [status.saralharyana.nic.in](https://status.saralharyana.nic.in/) and a certificate-download link on its home page. The portal states that status messages are sent after application steps.

For dog registration, use the SARAL service named in the municipal list. The ULB RTS schedule distinguishes an initial certificate from renewal, with different time limits. It does not establish a deadline for registering other pets. Obtain the current service checklist and fee from the selected application before submitting.

For marriage registration, use the Shaadi Haryana link published by the municipal contact page and SARAL. Haryana's official national service listing identifies urban registration under the Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Act, 2008 and its rules. Select the applicable marriage-registration service and jurisdiction. Current documents, payment requirements and appointments must be checked on the official application. The public Shaadi root returned no readable text during this research, so this guide does not claim to verify its logged-in workflow or current fee.

SARAL states that the notified period starts when the designated officer or authorized subordinate receives the complete application, which must be acknowledged. Rejection must be reasoned and communicated. Preserve the acknowledgement and any deficiency notice. The portal describes automatic first appeals for breached notified timelines and escalation where the first and second authorities do not decide within 30 working days respectively. It also permits citizens to seek appeal support through the portal or helpline.

The ULB schedule's water-connection and leakage or sewer-overflow entries expressly name Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat and Karnal. Do not apply those particular deadlines to Rohtak merely because they appear on the statewide page. Birth, death and marriage deadlines are not established by the ULB table reproduced here; check the current department-specific SARAL notification.

## RTI Act, 2005

Use RTI to seek existing municipal records, such as certified copies of orders, tax-assessment records, work files or action-taken records. Use the service application or grievance route to request the underlying civic work. Address a written request to the State Public Information Officer, Municipal Corporation Rohtak, and describe the records and period sought. The municipal office is on Church Road, near Ambedkar Chowk, Mahavir Colony, Company Bagh, Rohtak, Haryana 124001.

The [RTI Act on India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2065?locale=en) provides:

| Provision | Statutory period or rule |
| --- | --- |
| Section 6(3), transfer to another public authority | As soon as practicable, no later than 5 days; applicant must be informed |
| Section 7(1), ordinary response | Within 30 days of receipt, subject to statutory qualifications |
| Section 7(1), life or liberty | Within 48 hours |
| Section 19(1), first appeal | Within 30 days of the response deadline expiring or receiving the decision; delayed filing may be admitted for sufficient cause |
| Section 19(6), first-appeal decision | Within 30 days, extendable to a total of 45 days with written reasons |
| Section 19(3), second appeal | Within 90 days of when the decision should have been made or was received; to the State Information Commission for this state public authority |

Section 6(2) does not require reasons for seeking information. Section 7(5) exempts qualifying below-poverty-line applicants from fees; section 7(6) provides information free where the authority misses the applicable statutory time limit.

The municipal [General Branch page](https://www.mcrohtak.in/general-branch-1) describes receipt and registration of RTI requests, a Rs. 50 receipt and dispatch of replies within 30 days. It also gives appeal periods inconsistent with section 19. Treat that workflow as a municipal publication, not an amendment to the Act. Confirm the current Haryana-prescribed fee before payment and use the statutory appeal periods above. This research did not establish a current named SPIO appointment.

## Grievances and Samadhan camps

For sanitation, roads, toilets and street lights, the municipal e-services table links the Haryana ULB citizen Android app, package `in.gov.ulbharyana.cms.citizen`. The current municipal footer also links the ULB grievance portal. Record the complaint number, precise location, issue and earlier application references.

The [municipal contact page](https://www.mcrohtak.in/contact-us) publishes:

| Contact | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Municipal toll-free number | 1800-180-5007 |
| Control room | 82959-00992 |
| Street-light complaints | 70278-45050 |
| Safai Suraksha Mitra helpline | 14420 |
| Municipal office | 01262-258675 |

DULB lists IT support and grievance registration at 1800-8900-929, 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday. SARAL lists 0172-3968400, 7 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday excluding government holidays, for application, grievance and appeal support.

A [Haryana government press release dated 15 October 2026](https://prms.prharyana.gov.in/press-release/9261) records Samadhan camps every Monday and Thursday, 10 AM to noon, at district and subdivision headquarters. It identifies the auditorium at Rohtak Mini Secretariat as the district venue and also records camps at Sampla and Meham. A [22 October 2026 release](https://prms.prharyana.gov.in/press-release/9733) specifically reports Property ID and municipal complaints among the matters heard.

Bring the application acknowledgement, Property ID if relevant, payment receipts and earlier complaint details. Confirm the next camp with the administration before travelling. Camp attendance does not replace a statutory RTI or RTS appeal, and the releases do not promise immediate disposal of every case. Matters needing field verification or departmental processing require further action.

# Rohtak tenders and infrastructure works, 2024 to 2026

## Procurement portals and scope

The Haryana Engineering Works Portal, HEWP, states that tenders floated through it are published on the NIC Haryana eTendering website for bidder participation. HEWP says these notices appear in its live-tender list the next day. Search the NIC portal under `Haryana Government > Urban Local Bodies > MC Rohtak`, using the tender IDs below. PHED and PWD projects must be searched under their respective departments, not assumed to be MC contracts. [S1]

This record covers notices and project updates dated 2024 to 2026. Its frontmatter date is the compilation date, not a common sanction or tender date. The linked notices are historical records unless explicitly described otherwise. Bid deadlines are the dates shown in the cited record and may have been superseded by corrigenda.

An estimated tender value is not expenditure or an awarded contract price. EMD is bid security, not project cost. A cancellation or retender entry does not establish that the work was awarded. The sources below do not establish completion of all listed works.

## Sunaria solid-waste plant and collection procurement

### Legacy-waste remediation, 2024

The official NIC record identifies MC Rohtak tender `2024_HRY_408039_1`, reference `MCR/LegacyWaste/10/2024`, for bio-remediation of legacy waste at the dump site in the Municipal Solid Waste Management Plant at Sunaria. It was published on 29 October 2024 at 18:00. The displayed submission deadline was 13 November 2024 at 11:00, with opening at 13:00. The stated work period was 180 days and bid validity 120 days. ### Collection and daily-waste processing, 2025

The official collection RFP `2025_HRY_455492_1`, reference `MCR/DoortoDoor/30/06/2025`, covered door-to-door collection, segregation of municipal solid waste from each establishment, and transportation to the processing site. It was published on 1 July 2025 at 09:00. The displayed closing date was 23 July 2025 at 11:00 and opening at 14:00. A pre-bid meeting was listed for 7 July at 11:00 at the Executive Engineer's office. [S3]

The notice shows EMD of Rs 1,75,12,900, tender fee of Rs 25,000 and processing fee of Rs 1,180. Its tender-value and work-duration fields show NA. The listed documents are Tendernotice_1.pdf and BOQ_533165.xls, with a technical-bid corrigendum. These figures must not be substituted for the processing-plant contract.

A separate daily-waste processing RFP is indexed by two procurement services as `2025_HRY_455594_1`, reference `MCR/processing/07/2025`, for ten years. Both identify a 23 July 2025 deadline. The official external link returned only a portal shell during verification, so this entry is retained as a secondary-source tender locator. Its reported award amount and contractor are not adopted as verified official contract particulars here. [S4]

Sunaria, Sunariya and Sunaria Jail Road occur in source descriptions. This record preserves the spelling Sunaria for the SWM tender. The sewage treatment works discussed below are a separate asset class from municipal solid-waste processing.

## Water supply and sewerage

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs AMRUT 2.0 portal lists the following approved Rohtak projects. Each displayed milestone was "Contract Awarded" when checked. These are portal project costs, not independently checked final payments or completion certificates. The page does not provide an award date for each row. [S5]

| Project | Portal project cost | Displayed milestone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upgrade existing 40 MLD UASB sewage treatment plant to 60 MLD with biological nutrient removal, clarifiers, tertiary disc filtration and chlorination | Rs 54.67 crore | Contract Awarded |
| Strengthen and replace pumping machinery at existing disposal and main pumping stations, and provide sewer line | Rs 10.02 crore | Contract Awarded |
| Strengthen and replace old pumping machinery and lay distribution pipeline | Rs 10.59 crore | Contract Awarded |
| Raw-water pumping station at JLN canal and upgradation of HSIIDC water infrastructure, as described on the portal | Rs 12.37 crore | Contract Awarded |

The STP scope includes five years of operation and maintenance, excluding the trial-run period and including one year of defect liability. The portal wording is the source for this duration; it does not prove the upgraded plant is operational.

On 11 September 2025, The Tribune reported Deputy Commissioner Sachin Gupta's statement that three drinking-water projects had been approved, with individual amounts of Rs 210 crore, Rs 16.35 crore and Rs 26 crore. The first concerned waterworks serving Paharawar, Kanheli, Baliyana, Bohar, Sunaria Khurd, Sunaria Kalan and Kheri Sadh, including a direct JLN canal connection. The second concerned replacement of damaged water pipelines. The third concerned a rising main between the Sonepat Road waterworks and JLN canal, and an SS tank. The article described work as due to begin, not completed. These reported approvals are not added to the AMRUT table because overlap has not been resolved. [S6]

### Maintenance and monsoon arrangements

On 22 May 2025, The Tribune reported that MC Rohtak had identified 16 waterlogging-prone locations, assigned junior engineers as nodal officers, and instructed sanitation inspectors to complete drain cleaning by 15 June, followed by weekly time-bound cleaning. Listed locations included Canal Rest House to Chhotu Ram Chowk, Gohana Adda, Sukhpura Chowk, Hisar Road, Kath Mandi flyover to the railway station, Mal Godam Road, Bohar Road drain and Mata Darwaza. These were reported operational instructions, not a quantified capital tender. [S9]

The HEWP live list checked on 22 November 2026 separately displayed PHED Rohtak No. 3 tender `2026_HRY_560763_1` for repair and rewinding of a sewer submersible pump, estimated at Rs 1,26,500 with Rs 2,530 EMD. It also displayed `2026_HRY_560766_1` for pump repair, rewinding and sluice-valve work at Rahar Johar and Kabir Colony disposal, estimated at Rs 3,13,444 with Rs 6,270 EMD. Both listed 23 November 2026 at 09:00 as the submission deadline. These are PHED maintenance notices, not evidence of citywide sewer rehabilitation. [S1]

## Elevated railway corridor and road works

A Haryana government press release dated 16 September 2026 describes the road along the elevated railway track from Chinyot Colony to Sector 6. It gives a length of approximately 3.790 km, width of 5.50 m and administrative approval of approximately Rs 21.27 crore. The scope includes pavement layers, a toe wall on the railway-structure side, side drains and improvements at major road junctions. It states a target of completion before Diwali, not a certified completion date. [S10]

The release separately describes approximately Rs 34 crore of proposed additional works, including green belts beneath the elevated railway, footpaths, colony connections and parking. It explicitly states that the Haryana government and Northern Railway MoU process for these additional works was pending. That proposed amount is not treated as an awarded extension of the road contract.

For the earlier chronology, The Tribune reported on 12 December 2024 that removal of the old ground-level railway lines had restarted after extraction began in June and remained incomplete. This is preparatory work for the road, not construction of a new elevated railway during the archive period. [S11]

The existing elevated road through the old city and the elevated railway corridor are distinct projects. A 6 October 2026 Tribune report described another elevated road, from Ambedkar Chowk to Delhi Bypass, as a proposal estimated at Rs 842.30 crore. The article attributed its status to former minister Manish Kumar Grover and said the state government was considering it. It is not recorded as an approved or awarded MC construction tender. [S12]

# Rohtak municipal budgets and civic financial governance, FY 2024-2027

## Coverage and evidence status

This record covers the three financial years beginning on 1 April 2024 and ending on 31 March 2027. The date in the frontmatter is the reported FY 2026-27 budget meeting date, not the publication date of an official consolidated budget.

The corporation's [Budget & Income page](https://mcrohtak.in/budget-income-municipal-corporation-rohtak) was accessible on 22 November 2026. Its annual budget links stop at FY 2023-24. It also lists older audited accounts, grant expenditure statements and utilisation certificates. These historical documents do not establish approved figures for FY 2024-25 onward. The current-year figures below therefore retain their newspaper attribution. No signed budget schedule, sanction letter or audited annual statement for these three years was verified in this research.

The [official house proceedings index](https://mcrohtak.in/house-meeting-proceedings) lists proceedings dated 9 April 2025 and subsequent meetings through 19 December 2025. The linked [9 April 2025 proceedings PDF](https://mcrohtak.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/Proceeding%2009.04.25_0.pdf) is a primary-source retrieval lead. Its budget tables were not text-verified here.

The [4 April 2025 report](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/rohtak/municipal-corporation-headquarters-sent-back-the-budget-of-rs-299-crore-now-it-will-have-to-be-passed-by-the-house-rohtak-news-c-17-roh1019-628806-2025-04-04) attributes the return to the absence of house approval when officials sent the proposal. It reports sanitation provision falling from Rs 60 crore to Rs 54 crore and the property-tax target falling from Rs 35 crore to Rs 28 crore in the proposal. These are proposal-stage comparisons. They should not be presented as the final adopted head-wise allocations without the approved schedule.

The [10 April 2025 report](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/rohtak/municipal-corporation-presented-a-budget-of-rs-296-crore-rohtak-news-c-17-roh1019-631627-2025-04-10) supplies the later Rs 296 crore and Rs 273 crore figures. The [30 March 2026 meeting report](https://www.bhaskar.com/g/local/haryana/rohtak/news/rohtak-kila-road-toilet-scam-allegations-councillor-314cr-budget-137569614.html) quotes the mayor as saying Rs 273 crore had been spent out of the previous Rs 296 crore budget. That retrospective statement is separate from the Rs 273 crore expenditure estimate reported in April 2025.

## Revenue recovery and conflicting totals

[The Tribune, 2 April 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/rohtak-municipal-corporation-collects-rs-32-cr-against-target-of-rs-28-crore-as-property-tax/) reports approximately Rs 32 crore property-tax collection for FY 2025-26 against a Rs 28 crore target. Municipal Commissioner Dr Anand Kumar Sharma also reported about Rs 2 crore fire tax and over Rs 3 crore user charges. These are revenue-head collections, not the corporation's total annual receipts.

A [Haribhoomi report dated 10 September 2026](https://www.haribhoomi.com/state-local/haryana/news/protest-by-bjp-councilors-rohtak-municipal-corporation-112403) quotes Councillor Kapil Nagpal as referring to a Rs 325 crore budget passed in a March finance committee meeting. This differs from the Rs 314 crore house budget and Rs 316 crore collection target in the March meeting report. The committee reference, possible revisions and accounting basis were not reconciled from official documents. The Rs 325 crore figure must not silently replace the house-report figures.

## Civic financial accountability

Budget passage, administrative sanction, technical sanction, procurement, payment and audit are different records. The 2025 return from headquarters illustrates why a draft sent by officials should not be labelled a house-approved budget.

To establish the final position for a particular work or financial year, request the following from the Accounts Branch and the branch executing the work:

- The signed adopted budget, receipt and expenditure schedules, house resolution number and approval correspondence.
- Any supplementary or revised budget, including the finance committee recommendation and later house decision.
- Head-wise actual receipts and payments, opening and closing balances, grant sanctions and utilisation certificates.
- Work-specific estimate, sanction, tender award, agreement, measurement book, bill and completion record.
- Audited accounts and the response to outstanding audit objections.

Use the official budget and proceedings pages above as the starting index. Where documents are absent, a request through the corporation's information-access process should specify the financial year and document name. Neither a newspaper total nor a proposed tender proves that a work was completed or its full allocation spent.

# Roorkee municipal financial statements for 2023–24

## Scope of the accounts

The compilation report on page 1 addresses the Commissioner of Nagar Nigam Roorkee. It covers the balance sheet at 31 March 2024, the profit and loss statement and cash flow statement for the year then ended, and the accounting notes. The report identifies the Uttarakhand Municipal Accounting Manual, 2021 as the basis of preparation.

## Status of the report

RR Bajaj & Associates compiled information supplied by the corporation under SRS 4410 (Revised). The accountants expressly give neither an audit opinion nor a review conclusion. The [state finance index](https://udd.uk.gov.in/document-category/balance-sheet-budget023-24/) files the statements under 2023–24; 31 March is the reporting date.

# Roorkee invitation to operate the municipal gaushala

## Operation of the cattle shelter

The district published the corporation’s invitation for expressions of interest to run the Nagar Nigam Roorkee gaushala (cattle shelter). The notice title dates the invitation to 7 August 2026. The [attached invitation](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s33416a75f4cea9109507cacd8e2f2aefc/uploads/2026/08/17860942232150.pdf) is the source for the application terms. The listing does not identify an appointed operator.

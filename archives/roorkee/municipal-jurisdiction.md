# Roorkee municipal corporation and contact

## Corporation and jurisdiction

The state lists Nagar Nigam Roorkee at serial 33 under Haridwar district. Dhandera and Padligurjar are separate Nagar Panchayats. Haridwar and Roorkee have separate corporations.

## Municipal correspondence

The [district municipal directory](https://haridwar.nic.in/public-utility/nagar-nigam-roorkee/) publishes `npproorkee@gmail.com` for Nagar Nigam Roorkee.

# Roorkee ward names in the 2024–25 municipal election

## Election edition

The district’s पार्षद (councillor) table numbers 40 wards. The [state register of elected representatives](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s38208974663db80265e9bfe7b222dcb18/uploads/2026/06/202609031174499502.pdf), PDF page 7, confirms the numbered seats and records 7 February 2025 as the first board meeting. The names below follow the district table. Its candidate links do not establish who won.

# Rourkela Municipal Corporation jurisdiction in Sundargarh

## Local body and district

The Odisha Housing and Urban Development Department lists **Raurkela Municipal Corporation** under **Sundaragada** in its [district-wise urban local body register](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/en/more/district-wise-ULBs-and-population?field_district_target_id=4012), with 40 wards and a Census 2011 population of 309,689. These are the register's spellings for Rourkela and Sundargarh. The same table lists Birmitrapur, Rajgangpur and Sundaragada municipalities separately. Those municipalities are not wards of Rourkela.

## Corporation status and planning

The [2015 Service Level Improvement Plan](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-05/Rourkela.pdf) says Rourkela converted to corporation status in 2014 with additional area included. The State Election Commission's [2022 election information book](https://sec.odisha.gov.in/Landing/ULBGEto2022), introduction, gives 14 November 2014 as the constitution date. Neither source is the instrument defining the legal boundary.

The urban department lists [Rourkela Municipal Corporation](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/en/urbanattachedoffices/rourkela-municipal-corporation) and the [Rourkela Development Authority](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/en/urbanattachedoffices/rourkela-development-authority) separately. Its corporation entry links to `rmc.nic.in`; the municipal site did not respond when checked.

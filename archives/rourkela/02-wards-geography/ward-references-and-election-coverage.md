# Rourkela ward references in the 2015 drainage survey

## Historical ward references in the drainage survey

The [2015 Service Level Improvement Plan](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-05/Rourkela.pdf), storm-water drainage section 4.1, identifies stretches prone to blocked drains. Selected references follow.

| Ward | Locations named in the survey |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Panposh Basti, Tunguri Tola, Jagannath Colony |
| 9 | Panposh, PHD Colony, Government College, Area 7 and 8, Mantola Basti |
| 17 | Basanty Colony |
| 19 | Malgodam Jhumpudi and Khotal Basti, among other locations |
| 21 | Madhusudan Pali and Railway Colony |
| 23 | Timber Colony, in section 2.3 on mixed drainage |
| 25 | Rear of the new bus stand, Bisra Road area, Gurudwara Road area and Traffic Gate |

These references identify locations in the 2015 survey. They do not show a whole ward, give its legal boundary or establish the present ward for an address.

## What the election book records

The [state urban department's Rourkela entry](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/en/node/191016) lists 40 wards. The State Election Commission says in the introduction to its [2022 information book](https://sec.odisha.gov.in/Landing/ULBGEto2022) that Rourkela Municipal Corporation did not vote in that round because disputes over delimitation and seat reservation were before the Orissa High Court. This describes March 2022, not the status of later litigation or elections.

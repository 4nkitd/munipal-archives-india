# Regional urban transport notification for Rourkela and neighboring planning areas

## Notice and area

The Odisha Housing and Urban Development Department lists notification no. 17384 of 2022, dated 17 October 2022, in Gazette no. 2964 of the same date. The portal describes it as extending the area of operation of capital region urban transport to the urban cluster covering the Rourkela, Biramitrapur and Rajgangpur master-plan areas.

## Scope

The notice describes a transport operating area across three master-plan areas. It does not identify municipal ward boundaries or publish a timetable, fare or route list. The [state district-wise local-body register](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/en/more/district-wise-ULBs-and-population?field_district_target_id=4012) treats Rourkela, Birmitrapur and Rajgangpur as separate urban local bodies.

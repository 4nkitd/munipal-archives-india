# Rourkela water supply baseline in the 2015 AMRUT plan

## Measured gaps

The Service Level Improvement Plan records 77.8 million litres per day of water-treatment capacity, 6.8 million litres of elevated service reservoir capacity and a 173 km distribution network. The plan names the Brahmani and Koel rivers as sources. The table compares what the plan reported with the Union urban ministry's benchmarks at the time.

| Indicator | Reported | Benchmark |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Coverage of water-supply connections | 36% | 100% |
| Metered water connections | 0% | 100% |
| Non-revenue water | 61% | 20% |
| Water-service cost recovery | 20% | 100% |
| Collection efficiency for water charges | 60% | 90% |

## Areas treated separately

The household table excludes railway colony and NIT households with their own water supply. The associated note cites wards 22, 37, 38 and 40 without assigning the two providers to individual wards. The plan also says an existing project would not cover newly added areas in wards 1, 2, 3, 8 and 36. It identifies new treatment, feeder mains, storage and distribution as needs for those additional areas. These are baseline figures and proposed works, not current service commitments.

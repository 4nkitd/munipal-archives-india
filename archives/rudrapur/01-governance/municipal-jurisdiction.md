# Nagar Nigam Rudrapur: municipal status and jurisdiction

## City corporation

Nagar Nigam Rudrapur, also called Municipal Corporation Rudrapur, is the civic body for the city of Rudrapur, the headquarters of Udham Singh Nagar district in Uttarakhand. The municipal profile says Rudrapur has had corporation status since 2013. It reports 40 wards and gives a municipal area of 27.65 square kilometres in 2001 and 55.22 square kilometres after delimitation in 2018. The city page does not reconcile those figures with the areas in the [state's 2018 expansion schedule](../02-wards-geography/territorial-expansion-2018.md). Neither source gives a road-by-road ward schedule.

The profile describes municipal responsibilities for sanitation, solid waste, public health, street lighting and civic infrastructure. The state [urban local body roster](https://udd.uk.gov.in/list-of-ulb/) lists Rudrapur at entry 98. It lists Kashipur, Kichha, Dineshpur, Nagla and Lalpur as separate local bodies in the same district. The city corporation is not a district-wide authority.

## Municipal helpline

The [corporation homepage](https://www.nagarnigamrudrapur.com/) labels 05944-242400 as the Nagar Nigam helpline. The city's [October 2026 tender letterhead](https://www.nagarnigamrudrapur.com/tender-2/tenders/tender_notice_76_construction_works_2026_27.pdf) confirms that landline and supplies the municipal office email. A separate Contact Us page shows a Dehradun address and email; neither is used here.

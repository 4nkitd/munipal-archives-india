# 2018 Rudrapur municipal boundary expansion

## Notification and additions

The state Urban Development Section issued notification no. 923/IV(3)/2018-1(53)/2010 on 5 April 2018. It recounts cancellation of the 20 November 2017 expansion notice after the Uttarakhand High Court's 9 March 2018 order, followed by a fresh provisional notification on 10 March 2018 for objections and suggestions.

Schedule One, on PDF page 23, lists 11 revenue villages for inclusion in Nagar Nigam Rudrapur, Udham Singh Nagar district. The notification gives 4,716.7952 hectares and a population of 34,879 for the schedule. These are the notification's figures for the listed areas, not a measurement of the entire corporation.

## Perimeter, not wards

PDF page 24 describes the resulting perimeter. It references revenue villages to the east and west, Phoolbagh forest land to the north, and land in Rampur district, Uttar Pradesh, to the south. This is a territorial expansion notice; the schedule's serial numbers do not identify electoral wards.

The scanned [state compilation](https://udd.uk.gov.in/document-category/nikay-adhisuchna/) preserves the issued notification, which directs extraordinary gazette publication. The gazette publication date itself was not verified.

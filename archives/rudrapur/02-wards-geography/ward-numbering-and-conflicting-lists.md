# Rudrapur's 40 wards and conflicting municipal ward lists

## Consistent ward count

The corporation's Ward Details Parsiman page lists ward numbers 1 through 40. Its [Ward Member List](https://www.nagarnigamrudrapur.com/ward-member-list/) also enumerates 40 numbered wards and gives nominated members in a separate table. The [city profile](https://www.nagarnigamrudrapur.com/nagar-nigam-rudrapur/) describes a 2018 delimitation and reports the same count. The web tables do not give a delimitation notification number or an edition date.

| 38 | Kichha Road Area | आवास विकास पश्चिमी |
| 40 | Pantnagar Border Area | सिडकुल |

The lists broadly agree for wards 1 through 19, with spelling and language differences, but give different names for wards 20 through 40. In particular, the Parsiman page labels ward 35 Lalpur, while the state [local body roster](https://udd.uk.gov.in/list-of-ulb/) separately lists Nagar Panchayat Lalpur. A reused name does not establish that the panchayat is inside the corporation. Neither municipal page is a substitute for the signed delimitation schedule or an official map. No ward names from the conflicting range are treated as definitive here.

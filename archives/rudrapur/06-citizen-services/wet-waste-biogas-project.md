# Rudrapur municipal wet-waste biogas plant

## Capacity and reported throughput

In its case study dated 1 August 2024, the Uttarakhand Urban Development Department describes a municipal project to treat biodegradable solid waste and produce compressed biogas. The project description lists a design capacity of 50 tonnes per day and reports processing 20 to 35 tonnes of wet waste daily. The higher figure is the design capacity, not the reported throughput. The page attributes execution to Rudrapur Green Energy Pvt. Ltd. and describes a public-private partnership.

The department lists organic waste collection, collection and purification of biogas, electricity generation and compressed-biogas production and sale as parts of the project. Its case study identifies mixed or poorly segregated wet waste as a problem the plant was intended to address. It does not give an audited operating series, an actual daily gas yield or a current household pickup schedule.

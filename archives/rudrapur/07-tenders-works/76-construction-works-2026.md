# Rudrapur tender notice for 76 municipal construction works

## Tender schedule

The corporation issued notice no. 1820 on 14 October 2026 for 76 construction works in its municipal area. Both the [municipal tender index](https://www.nagarnigamrudrapur.com/tender-2/) and page 1 of the scan give this schedule:

| Step | Schedule in the notice |
| --- | --- |
| Buy documents | 16 October to 6 November 2026, 10 am to 4 pm on working days, Construction Section, room 23 |
| Submit sealed bids | By 1 pm on 7 November 2026, in the tender box at the Nagar Nigam office |
| Scheduled opening | 11 am on 12 November 2026 |

Page 2 requires proof of city contractor registration for FY 2026–27 and a self-attested copy of the GST registration certificate. It also asks for the specified earnest-money instrument separately for each work and a notarized affidavit on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper. The archive records the original notice and does not establish any award or later amendment.

The scan requests newspaper publication on 15 October 2026, but does not confirm that it ran on that date. The municipal index gives the issue date, not a separate posting date.

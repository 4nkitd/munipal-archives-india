# Rudrapur municipal financial statements for 2024–25

## Balance sheet as of 31 March 2025

The Urban Development Department lists the municipal accounts under Balance Sheet and Budget 2024–25. Its document page shows 2 April 2026. The scanned [financial statements](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s38208974663db80265e9bfe7b222dcb18/uploads/2026/04/202604291773914058.pdf) use the accrual basis and the Uttarakhand Municipal Accounting Manual 2021. Page 3 contains the comparative balance sheet. The figures below are in rupees as printed.

| Account | Current year | Previous year |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Total own fund reserves and surplus | 947,639,713.79 | 742,005,197.96 |
| Current liabilities and provisions | 260,638,327.11 | 278,286,996.41 |
| Cash and bank balances | 143,529,492.59 | 187,244,260.41 |
| Total assets | 1,296,759,516.46 | 1,174,954,851.93 |

The portal category includes the word budget, but the cited table is a statement of balances, not a set of spending authorizations. The 31 March 2025 date is the accounting period end, not the portal listing date.

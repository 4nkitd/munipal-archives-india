# Sagar AMRUT projects reported as grounded, March 2026

## Reported counts and costs

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' table lists Sagar under Madhya Pradesh at serial 206. It records city-level figures for projects grounded under AMRUT, based on state updates as of 2 March 2026.

| Sector | Projects | Cost, ₹ crore |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Sewerage and septage management | 1 | 299.10 |
| Parks | 2 | 4.50 |
| Total of reported cells | 3 | 303.60 |

The row leaves water supply, drainage and urban transport blank. The total adds only the two populated sectors. The costs are project figures, not bills paid by the corporation. The report does not identify the two parks, the sewerage project location, individual contracts, completion status or covered wards.

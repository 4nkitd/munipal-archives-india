# Sagar municipal ward population in Census 2011

## Corporation and outgrowth are different totals

Under town code `802159`, the district Primary Census Abstract gives `Sagar (M Corp.)` 52,573 households and 273,296 people. Its 48 municipal ward rows sum to those totals. The separately labelled Gaur Nagar outgrowth has 260 households and 1,260 people. The workbook labels the outgrowth `WARD NO.-0049` and includes it in the `Sagar (M Corp. + OG)` total of 52,833 households and 274,556 people. It is not one of the 48 corporation ward rows in this edition.

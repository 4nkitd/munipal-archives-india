# Sagar municipal general election and corporation term, 2022 to 2027

## Official calendar

The Madhya Pradesh State Election Commission's urban programme index records the 1 June 2022 statewide election programme under letter `F-53NN-01/2022/5/445`. Serial 208 in the commission's [term calendar](https://www.mplocalelection.mp.gov.in/Data/rightpanel/UploadCalendar_2024.pdf#page=7) is Sagar Municipal Corporation. The [election overview](https://www.mplocalelection.mp.gov.in/Data/rightpanel/EkNagarUpdated2025.pdf#page=1) supplies the first-phase counting and result-announcement date.

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| General-election poll, first phase | 6 July 2022 |
| First-phase counting and result announcement | 17 July 2022 |
| First corporation meeting | 6 August 2022 |
| Projected end of term | 5 August 2027 |

## How to read the calendar

Sagar city has its own row, separate from other urban local bodies in the district. The first-meeting date is not the polling date or the date the city became a corporation. Under sections 9 and 14 of the [Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/Uploaded%20Document/act%20%26%20rules/MunicipalCorporationACT1956%2024.pdf), the municipal area elects the mayor and individual wards elect councillors under the supervision of the State Election Commission. The term calendar is not a candidate result sheet or a ward delimitation notification.

# Sagar municipal office and jurisdiction

## Office contact and address discrepancy

The municipal contact page lists the office opposite Excellence School, Court Road, Sagar 470001, with `07582-229454` and `commsagar@mpurban.gov.in`. The [district public-utility entry](https://sagar.nic.in/en/public-utility/sagar-municipal-corporation/) links that same corporation portal and prints the same number, but gives Bus Stand Road, Sagar 470002. The [district government email directory](https://sagar.nic.in/en/government-email-directory/) also assigns `commsagar@mpurban.gov.in` to the corporation commissioner. The sources do not explain the two addresses. Call the office before travelling or sending papers.

## Municipal area and classification

The [district local-body register](https://sagar.nic.in/en/administrative-setup/local-bodies-administration/) lists Sagar under local-body type 4, Municipal Corporations, with LGD code `250764` and Census town code `802159`. It lists Makronia Buzurg, Khurai and Bina separately. The [corporation's home page](http://www.sagarmunicipalcorporation.com/) dates the corporation's establishment to 1987. That statement is not a copy of the formation notification.

Section 7 of the [Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/Uploaded%20Document/act%20%26%20rules/MunicipalCorporationACT1956%2024.pdf) provides for corporations in publicly notified larger urban areas. An office address or an LGD record does not locate the legal municipal boundary.

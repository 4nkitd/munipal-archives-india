# Saharanpur city and the limits of the district directory

The district administration places Saharanpur Nagar Nigam in Saharanpur tehsil. Even within that tehsil, Sultanpur Chilkana has its own Nagar Panchayat. Deoband, Nakur, Sarsawan and Gangoh each have a separate Nagar Palika Parishad. The district list identifies governing bodies; it does not define street-level boundaries.

The [municipal budget preface, printed page 3](https://www.saharanpurnagarnigam.com/pdf/MoolBudjet2026-2027.pdf) dates the earlier municipality to 21 December 1867 and its upgrade to a municipal corporation to 1 October 2009. The [district administration](https://saharanpur.nic.in/) also gives 1 October 2009 for corporation status.

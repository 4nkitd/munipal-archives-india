# Where to report sanitation, drinking water and streetlight problems

For a सफाई (cleanliness), पेयजल (drinking water) or पथ प्रकाश (street lighting) problem in the corporation area, call **0132-2648112**. The Hindi service notice on the municipal home page identifies all three issues.

The [district office listing](https://saharanpur.nic.in/public-utility-category/nagar-nigam/) confirms the same landline and lists the corporation office at Gurudwara Road, Saharanpur 247001. Its general email is nagarnigamsaharanpur@gmail.com.

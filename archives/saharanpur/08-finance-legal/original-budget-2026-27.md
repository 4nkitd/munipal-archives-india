# Saharanpur original municipal budget for 2026–27

The [budget cover](https://www.saharanpurnagarnigam.com/pdf/MoolBudjet2026-2027.pdf#page=1) lists executive committee resolution 77 dated 6 February 2026 and council resolution 61 dated 29 March 2026. The Accounts Department index gives no publication date.

## Citywide budget estimates

The rightmost column of the [signed summary on page 7](https://www.saharanpurnagarnigam.com/pdf/MoolBudjet2026-2027.pdf#page=7) gives the original estimates for 2026–27.

| Budget item | 2026–27 estimate (₹ lakh) |
|---|---:|
| Opening balance | 19,586.13 |
| Income for the year | 55,690.24 |
| Total available, including the opening balance | 75,276.37 |
| Expenditure for the year | 68,472.12 |
| Closing balance | 6,804.25 |

## Estimated tax receipts

Printed page 8 gives the following amounts in lakh rupees. These figures come from the rightmost column, मूल प्राविधान 2026–27 (original provision), rather than the adjacent actual-receipts column.

| Receipt | 2026–27 estimate (₹ lakh) |
|---|---:|
| House tax, including service charges | 3,126.00 |
| Water tax | 2,129.20 |
| Sewer tax | 380.00 |
| Auditorium tax (show tax) | 4.00 |
| Total tax receipts | 5,639.20 |

The table below that subtotal begins non-tax receipts, which are outside this figure. The [Accounts Department index](https://www.saharanpurnagarnigam.com/budgetandbalancesheet.php) lists balance sheets and revised budgets separately.

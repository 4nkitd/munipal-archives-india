# Saharsa draft voter roll: claims and objections for 46 wards

## Which electoral roll the notice covers

The District Election Office published its claims and objections notice on 5 April 2023. It identifies wards 01 to 46 of Nagar Nigam Saharsa and ward 03 of Nagar Panchayat Bangaon. Bangaon ward 03 belongs to the panchayat, even though the district published both entries in one notice.

## Document status

The [459 KB attachment on the district page](https://saharsa.nic.in/document/district-election-office-saharsa-regarding-claim-objection-related-to-draft-voter-list-of-nagar-nigam-saharsa-ward-no-01-46-and-nagar-panchayat-bangaon-ward-no-03/) concerns the draft voter list. For the boundaries of a corporation ward, consult the [SEC Form 6 schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=12&SID=1&SN=1524); the draft-roll notice identifies the electoral units but does not define their limits.

# Saharsa Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and office

## Municipal limits

The District General Section published a notice of the conversion of Saharsa Municipal Council into a municipal corporation on 15 July 2022. The SEC marks Saharsa as upgraded (उत्क्रमित). Bangaon, Nauhatta, Sonbarsa and Saurabazar have separate Nagar Panchayats. [District record](https://saharsa.nic.in/document/district-general-section-notification-regarding-transformation-of-municipal-council-saharsa-into-municipal-corporation/).

The [SEC register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0) records 46 wards and a population of 216,491 for the corporation. The [Form 6 schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=12&SID=1&SN=1524) identifies this as a 2011 Census population for the territory in the schedule. It is neither a district total nor a current population estimate.

## Municipal office

The district directory lists 9031071867 for the Municipal Commissioner, Nagar Nigam, Saharsa. [Official directory](https://saharsa.nic.in/whos-who/).

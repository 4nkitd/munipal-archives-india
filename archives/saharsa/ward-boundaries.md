# Saharsa territorial wards: Form 6 boundary schedule

## How to read Form 6

The Bihar State Election Commission publishes the territorial constituencies of Saharsa Nagar Nigam in Form 6 under Rule 29(1). Population is from the 2011 Census. The boundary descriptions below reproduce the Hindi entries, including their place-name spellings. The directional labels are east (पू), west (प), north (उ) and south (द). The [official schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=12&SID=1&SN=1524) includes ward map links.

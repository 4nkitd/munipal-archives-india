# Salem municipal limits and formation of the corporation

## Municipal limits and dates

Salem became a special grade municipality on 1 April 1979. Its limits expanded on 1 April 1994 to include Suramangalam Municipality, the town panchayats of Jarikondalampatty and Kannankurichi, and 21 village panchayats. The corporation gives the expanded area as 91.34 square kilometres. Corporation status took effect two months later, on 1 June 1994.

## Zonal administration

The [corporation profile](https://www.salemcorporation.gov.in/about-corporation.php) reports 60 wards under the Suramangalam, Hasthampatty, Ammapet and Kondalampatty zonal offices. Each has a zonal chairman and an assistant commissioner. The [main office](https://www.salemcorporation.gov.in/) is on Fort Main Road; the portal lists 0427-2777888 for support and commr.salem@tn.gov.in for correspondence.

# Salem corporation ward turnout in the 2022 urban local elections

## Reading the return

The [Tamil Nadu State Election Commission return](https://tnsec.tn.gov.in/tnsec_static/election_results/ulbe2022/corp_slm.pdf) lists 60 numbered wards for the 2022 election. Each ward heading precedes its electorate, turnout and candidate totals. These are electoral records for that edition; the return does not describe street boundaries. The Commission identifies the returning officer's declaration in Form 27 as final.

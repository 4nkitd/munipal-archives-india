# Salem 2026-27 budget and council approval dates

## Council approval and publication

The [annual budget register](https://www.salemcorporation.gov.in/budget.php) distinguishes council approval from the date a budget appeared online. These dates coincided for the [2026-27 budget](https://www.salemcorporation.gov.in/download/2026-27%20budget.pdf) but differed for the two preceding budgets.

| Financial year | Council approved | Updated | Published |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-25 | 21 February 2024 | 25 April 2024 | 2 May 2024 |
| 2025-26 | 25 February 2025 | 27 March 2025 | 27 March 2025 |
| 2026-27 | 5 March 2026 | 5 March 2026 | 5 March 2026 |

# Samastipur Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## City corporation and other district bodies

The district's [Administrative Setup](https://samastipur.nic.in/about-district/administrative-setup/) identifies Samastipur as its Nagar Nigam. It lists Rosera, Dalsinghsarai, Tajpur and Shahpur Patori separately as Nagar Parishads, and Musrigharari, Sarairanjan and Singhiya as Nagar Panchayats. The district date of 14 November 1972 refers to its separation from Darbhanga district, not the creation of Samastipur Municipal Corporation.

The Bihar State Election Commission puts Samastipur's city body in Samastipur subdivision and identifies it as an upgraded Nagar Nigam at serial 231 of the [final ward formation register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0). The SEC's [Form 6](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=19&SID=1&SN=191) gives 47 numbered wards and their boundary descriptions. The 253,136 total is on its 2011 Census basis, not an estimate of the city's present population.

## Office and planning notice

The district's [public utility page](https://samastipur.nic.in/public-utility/city-council-samastipur/) has the older title "City council, samastipur" and gives the address opposite the bus stand, near Thaneshwar Temple, Samastipur 848101. The corporation's [Animal Birth Control tender landing page and attachment](https://samastipur.nic.in/notice/office-of-the-municipal-corporation-samastipur/) use the corporation name and specify the office near the temple and its landline, 06274-225010.

A separate [district notice](https://samastipur.nic.in/notice/municipal-corporation-samastipur/) sought suggestions and objections from stakeholders and the public on a draft proposed Samastipur planning area. Its portal display dates were 5 through 31 July 2024. The consultation notice does not by itself turn the proposed planning area into the corporation's electoral or service area.

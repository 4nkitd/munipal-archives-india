# Samastipur wards in the Bihar SEC final Form 6

## Wards in the second delimitation phase

The Bihar State Election Commission's [second-phase final ward formation register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=2) lists Samastipur as an upgraded Nagar Nigam with 47 wards. Its [Form 6 schedule](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarNikayPopulationReport.aspx?DID=19&SID=1&SN=191) uses Samastipur for both district and subdivision. Wards 1 to 47 each have a Census 2011 population breakdown, four-sided Hindi boundary text and a map link. A delimitation phase is not an election polling phase.

Ward 6 has the smallest total in this edition and ward 11 the largest. The table gives selected wards and the corporation-wide sum, not a voter roll or a statement of present-day population.

## Using the boundary text

Ward 2 is described with the Gandak embankment to the east, the road to Pokhraira to the west, Pokhraira to the north and Pusa Road to the south. Ward 45 uses the Jamuari River to the north and refers to Mohanpur ward 43 to the west. Ward 47 uses the road to Bejhadih as its western limit and identifies Maheshpatti panchayat on the east. A panchayat or neighbouring ward used as a boundary marker is not thereby part of the ward. For an address close to the boundary, use the corresponding Form 6 row and its map; a road or locality name alone may appear in more than one ward.

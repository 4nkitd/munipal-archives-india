# Samastipur animal birth control agency retender, 2026

## Procuring body and RFP dates

The [Samastipur district notice](https://samastipur.nic.in/notice/office-of-the-municipal-corporation-samastipur/) calls this a tender for the Animal Birth Control Centre. The attachment identifies it as RFP No. 04/NNS-2026-27 (Re Tender), dated 30 October 2026, for selecting an agency to implement the Animal Birth Control programme in Samastipur district. Samastipur Municipal Corporation is the district urban local body headquarters named in the Notice Inviting Tender, near Thaneshwar Temple, Samastipur, Bihar 848101.

| Event | Date listed by the source |
| --- | --- |
| Date printed on the RFP notice | 30 October 2026 |
| Pre-bid meeting in the RFP | 5 November 2026, 12 noon |
| District notice display period | 7 to 30 November 2026 |
| Hard-copy submission deadline in the RFP | 19 November 2026, 5 p.m. |

The district site's 30 November End Date is not the bid deadline shown in the RFP. The source establishes no award, contract start or current availability of an animal collection service.

## Submission method

The first page of Section I instructs bidders to submit hard copies at the respective district urban local body headquarters. The technical and financial bids go in separate sealed envelopes inside one outer envelope. It marks online bid submission as not applicable. Firms applying for more than one district must file separate bids and submit separate tender fees and bid securities for each district. The district coverage in the RFP describes the procurement; it does not change the corporation's ward boundaries.

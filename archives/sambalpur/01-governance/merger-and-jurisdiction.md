# Sambalpur Municipal Corporation merger and jurisdiction

## Municipal area

The corporation profile describes a merger of the old Sambalpur Municipality, Burla Notified Area Council, Hirakud Notified Area Council, seven gram panchayats in Dhankauda block and five in Maneswar block. It gives the municipal area as 303 square kilometres, including 33.66 square kilometres of the former Sambalpur municipal area, 87.30 of the former Burla NAC and 49.02 of the former Hirakud area. The gram panchayats round out the corporation, not the whole of either block.

The [state urban department's corporation entry](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/en/node/191010) lists 41 wards and a Census 2011 population of 335,761. The corporation's [public contact page](https://smcsambalpur.nic.in/contact-us/) gives its office address as Durgapali, Sambalpur 768006.

## Dates in the official sources

The corporation profile cites Housing and Urban Development Department notification no. 32943/HUD dated 15 November 2013 when describing the merger. The introduction to the [State Election Commission's 2022 election information book](https://sec.odisha.gov.in/Landing/ULBGEto2022) gives 21 November 2014 as the corporation's constitution date. These attributions are recorded separately. Neither source supplies the gazetted parcel schedule.

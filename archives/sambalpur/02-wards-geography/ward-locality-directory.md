# Sambalpur wards and selected localities in the municipal directory

## Reading the ward directory

The corporation's table has an "Area included" column for each numbered ward. The following entries are selected examples, not a complete transcription. The table marks some places `(P)` for part. For example, it lists part of Balerpada in both wards 3 and 4, and part of Dhipapada in wards 6 and 7. A locality name alone may not establish the ward for a specific address.

| Ward | Selected areas named by the corporation |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Bagbira, Tihikipali, Senhapali, Jharapali, Mudoghat and Potapali |
| 2 | Sansinghari, Baijamunda, Raghunathpali and A. Katapali |
| 3 | Hirakud railway station, Sambalpur University campus, MCL Jagruti Bihar and part of Balerpada |
| 4 | Part of Balerpada, Post Office area, Daily Market, Pucca Market and Gopabandhu Chowk |
| 6 | Old Burla, VSS Medical College area, Shakti Nagar and part of Dhipapada |
| 7 | VSSUT area, NAC College area, Mahanadipada and part of Dhipapada |
| 20 | Kainsir, Densargi, Singhpali and Ranibandha |
| 41 | Gunderpur, Chhatabar, Golabandh, Gulunda, Gurundipali and Kateipali |

## Directory versus electoral record

The [state urban department's register](https://urban.odisha.gov.in/en/node/191010) lists 41 wards. The [State Election Commission's 2022 election book](https://sec.odisha.gov.in/Landing/ULBGEto2022) says Sambalpur Municipal Corporation did not vote in March 2022 because disputes over delimitation and seat reservation were before the Orissa High Court at the time. The corporation's undated locality directory is neither a gazetted delimitation schedule nor a record of elected corporators.

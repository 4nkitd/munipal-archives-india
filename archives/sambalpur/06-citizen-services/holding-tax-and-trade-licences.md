# Sambalpur holding tax, trade licences and municipal fees

## Tax and Revenue responsibilities

The corporation's Tax and Revenue page assigns the section responsibility for assessing and collecting holding tax, also called property tax. The same section issues and renews trade licences and collects the related licence fees. It also collects market fees, advertisement fees and solid-waste management user fees. The page does not publish a rate table or say that all five payments can be made online.

## Online holding tax and office help

The [corporation portal](https://smcsambalpur.nic.in/) points to Odisha SUJOG for online holding-tax payments. Use the [SUJOG home page](https://sujog.odisha.gov.in/) if the corporation menu's `/home` link does not open directly. For questions about an assessment, licence or fee, the [corporation office](https://smcsambalpur.nic.in/contact-us/) is at Durgapali, Sambalpur 768006, and lists 0663-3500390 as its public contact number.

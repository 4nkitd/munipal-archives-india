# Sambalpur waste collection and municipal sanitation complaints

## What the corporation lists

The Health and Sanitation page lists segregation at source, house-to-house waste collection, road cleaning and transport to disposal sites. It describes night cleaning on busy roads and in market areas and fixed collection times for individual lanes. It also lists non-retention of waste at a particular site for more than 24 hours as a service practice. That statement is not a published deadline for resolving a complaint.

## What to report

The municipal page identifies sweeping, drain cleaning, garbage lifting and animal carcass removal as services handled through its ward sanitation directory. Give the ward if known, the lane and a nearby landmark so the corporation can route a complaint. The [corporation contact page](https://smcsambalpur.nic.in/contact-us/) lists 1800-345-6791 for grievance registration. The undated sanitation directory's service zones should not be used to infer ward boundaries.

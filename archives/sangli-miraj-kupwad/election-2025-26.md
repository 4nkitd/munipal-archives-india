# Sangli-Miraj-Kupwad municipal general election 2025-26: final seat totals

## One return for three cities

Row 19 in the final party position is headed "Sangli-Miraj-Kupwad". The SEC recorded 381 contesting candidates for 78 councillor seats across 20 electoral wards. These totals cover the three cities together. The [SEC news register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) lists the report on 16 January 2026.

| Party with a seat total in row 19 | Seats won |
|---|---:|
| Bharatiya Janata Party | 39 |
| Indian National Congress | 18 |
| Nationalist Congress Party | 16 |
| Nationalist Congress Party (Sharadchandra Pawar) | 3 |
| Shiv Sena | 2 |
| Total declared | 78 |

BJP's 39 seats accounted for exactly half the council. The SEC gives separate columns for the two Nationalist Congress parties. It leaves several other party columns blank in this row; the five printed party figures account for all 78 declared results.

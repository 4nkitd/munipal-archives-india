# Formation and area of the Sangli, Miraj and Kupwad joint corporation

## Municipal jurisdiction

The municipal profile names Sangli, Miraj and Kupwad as three connected cities administered by one corporation in Sangli district, Maharashtra. It gives **9 February 1998** as the date of establishment. Its area figure of **118.18 square kilometres** refers to the joint corporation. Applying that figure to Sangli alone would omit the other two cities; applying it to Sangli district would extend it beyond the municipal area.

## Population reference

The profile attributes **502,793 residents** to the 2011 Census. That count and the area are corporation-wide figures. The page gives no separate population totals for Miraj or Kupwad. It also prints two inconsistent lakh figures for a later estimate, without a census reference year; the census count is the dated population figure retained here.

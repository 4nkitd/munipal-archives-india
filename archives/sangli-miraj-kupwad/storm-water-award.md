# Sangli-Miraj-Kupwad storm-water works contract award

## Scope and selection

The corporation's Public Works Department identifies the contract as **IN-SMKMC-493093-CW-RFB**. The works cover construction of storm-water drains and flood-resilient structures within the municipal project area. The final contract price was ₹5,08,77,66,694.62, for a duration of 30 months.

The department used a request for bids with rated criteria. Ajwani Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. received a combined score of 87.31, followed by Konkan Railway Corporation Ltd. at 87.18. Konkan Railway's technical score was higher, but Ajwani received the higher financial score, 50.00 against 37.18. The evaluation names Ajwani Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. as the most advantageous bidder; the successful-bidder line renders the name as "Ajwani Infra India Ltd".

## Dated evidence

The Public Works Department dated the award notice **17 August 2026**, handwritten at the top of page 1. The attached disclosure of 5 August refers to an earlier letter of acceptance dated 3 August 2026. The 30-month duration does not by itself establish a completion date.

# Sasaram municipal wards: boundaries and census population

## Municipal jurisdiction

The State Election Commission classifies Sasaram as an upgraded municipal corporation. The Rohtas district directory lists the corporation at Sasaram, PIN 821115. The [commission’s municipal register](https://sec.bihar.gov.in/ForPublic/NagarPalikaEntryReport.aspx?ID=0) lists 48 wards. Form 6, prescribed under rule 29(1), describes each ward separately. The population column uses the 2011 Census.

The Hindi boundary descriptions follow the published entries. The directional labels पू, प, उ and द indicate east, west, north and south.

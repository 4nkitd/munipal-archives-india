# Sasaram daily-wage computer operator and amin recruitment notice

## Vacancies and pay basis

The Municipal Commissioner cited item 5, paragraph 5 of the empowered standing committee meeting held on 28 November 2024. The decision authorised engagement of five computer operators and one amin (land surveyor) on daily remuneration at rates fixed by the Labour Department. No monthly salary was specified.

## Application and publication

Applicants had to submit their application by 13 May 2025 and attach certificates of qualification and experience. Memo 1367, dated 7 May 2025, directed the corporation's office to post the notice on its noticeboard and requested the District Informatics Officer, Rohtas, to upload it to the district website. The letter records the invitation, not appointments.

# Sasaram municipal market and transport fee auction for 2025–26

## Open auction

Sasaram Municipal Corporation set the first open auction for 11:00 am on 26 March 2025, with 27 and 29 March as successive dates if bidding did not take place. The rights offered included the fish market, small-animal slaughterhouse and Beda bus stand. Page 1 separates the fees chargeable to users from the reserve and security amounts for bidders.

| Revenue right | Daily fee specified | Reserve amount | Security |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Fish market | ₹15 per 20 kg basket; ₹50 per pickup jeep; ₹100 per large 407 vehicle | ₹12,01,491 | ₹1,20,149 |
| Small-animal slaughterhouse | ₹15 per animal | ₹2,76,038 | ₹27,603 |
| Rickshaws, carts, bullock carts and horse carts | ₹30 each | ₹1,84,026 | ₹18,402 |
| Beda bus stand and designated collection points | ₹50 per bus; ₹15 per jeep or tempo; ₹10 per listed kiosk, cart or e-rickshaw | ₹45,17,151 | ₹4,51,715 |

These were terms offered for one financial year, not a report of receipts.

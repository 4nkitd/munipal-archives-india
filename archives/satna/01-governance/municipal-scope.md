# Satna Nagar Nigam status and office

## Corporation and district

The district's Local Bodies page lists one municipal corporation, **Satna Nagar Nigam**, and separately lists eight town panchayats: Birsinghpur, Chitrakoot, Jaitwara, Kotar, Kothi, Nagod, Rampur Baghelan and Unchehra. The corporation entry covers Satna's municipal area, not all of Satna district. The [state urban local body map index](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=SF2Fyv4OVHlghtRd63MGiQ%3D%3D) places the Satna corporation in Satna district, Rewa division, under the category नगर निगम. The state index shows no map link in the Satna corporation row checked; the entry does not establish city limits.

## Municipal office

The [district birth-certificate page](https://satna.nic.in/en/service/birth-certificate/) gives the municipal office at Swami Vivekanand Marg, opposite Gayatri Temple, Diversion Road, near Circuit House, Satna 485001. It publishes **commsatna@mpurban.gov.in** as the office email. The district's [death-certificate page](https://satna.nic.in/en/service/apply-for-death-certificate/) points to the same office. These service addresses identify where to enquire, not the boundary of the registration area.

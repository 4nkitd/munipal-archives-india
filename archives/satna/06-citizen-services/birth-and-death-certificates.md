# Birth and death certificate guidance for Satna

## Official directions

Satna District Administration's [birth-certificate page](https://satna.nic.in/en/service/birth-certificate/) is marked last updated **23 July 2019**; its [death-certificate page](https://satna.nic.in/en/service/apply-for-death-certificate/) is marked last updated **31 July 2019**. Both point to the Civil Registration System login and identify the Satna Municipal Corporation office for in-person enquiries. The address is **Swami Vivekanand Marg, opposite Gayatri Temple, Diversion Road, near Circuit House, Satna 485001**. The birth-certificate page publishes the office email **commsatna@mpurban.gov.in**.

## What the district pages do not specify

The pages do not give fees, processing times, or a document checklist, and they do not describe delayed registration or certificate corrections. The old login did not render during verification, so this archive does not present it as a tested application route. Use the published municipal office email or address to confirm how to apply and whether the event falls within Satna city's registration area.

# Shahjahanpur municipal office and citizen service routes

## Local office

The Uttar Pradesh Directorate of Local Bodies lists `05842-223282` and `nagarnigamshahjahanpur@gmail.com` under municipal commissioner contact details for Shahjahanpur. The [district's public utility page](https://shahjahanpur.nic.in/public-utility/nagar-palika-parishad-shahjahanpur/) identifies the body as Nagar Nigam Shahjahanpur despite the older municipal council wording in its URL. The corporation website listed by the state directory did not respond during verification. The office contact is published in the directory; it was not tested by phone or email.

## State service routes

The [e-NagarSewa citizen login](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/onlinepay) says users must register with a specific city or urban local body and says that available online services depend on that registration. It lists property tax deposit, property mutation and public grievances. The [state portal](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/home) publishes `1533` for registering grievances. The [birth and death registration notice](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/birthDeathDisclaimer) says e-NagarSewa stopped providing registration and redirects users to the Civil Registration System.

These are published routes. No Shahjahanpur account, tax payment, grievance or civil registration was tested. Use the municipal office to check local availability rather than assuming every state portal menu item works for the city.

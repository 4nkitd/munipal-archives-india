# Shahjahanpur municipal election results and turnout in 2023

## Seats and winner

The State Election Commission's 2023 results dashboard lists one mayoral post and 60 councillor posts for the corporation, with results reported for all 61. The same district row lists other urban local bodies separately. The [Directorate of Local Bodies' elected-mayor list](https://localbodies.up.nic.in/listnn.htm) names Archana Singh as Shahjahanpur's 2023 winner. [Reservation notification 974](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/data/Govt_Orders/974.pdf), dated 9 April 2023, reserved the mayoral seat for a woman from the backward classes. These sources describe that election, not current officeholders.

## Phase and turnout

[Notification 972](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/data/Govt_Orders/972.pdf), dated 9 April 2023, set the statewide municipal corporation election period as 9 April to 13 May 2023. The Commission's [turnout table](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ULBPolling2023.aspx) identifies Nagar Nigam Shahjahanpur as a phase 2 body and reports total turnout of 50.78%. It is row 695, in Shahjahanpur district, not the similarly named Nagar Panchayat in Meerut district. The results dashboard does not give a publication day.

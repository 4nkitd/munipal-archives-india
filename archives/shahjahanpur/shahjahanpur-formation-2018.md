# Formation and original jurisdiction of Municipal Corporation Shahjahanpur

## What the state constituted

Notification `425/नौ-7-2018-01(सी०वि०)शाहजहाँपुर/2018`, dated 25 April 2018, designated a larger urban area under section 3(1) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959, read with Article 243-Q(2) of the Constitution. It provides for a corporation named Nagar Nigam Shahjahanpur.

Schedule 1 lists revenue-village lands and plots and the transitional area of Nagar Panchayat Roza. Schedule 2 describes the former Shahjahanpur municipality's smaller urban area. Schedule 3 describes the resulting corporation perimeter, using landmarks that include railway lines, the Pilibhit road and cantonment boundary pillars. For the original legal perimeter, consult the schedules in the [scanned notification](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/data/GO-2017/NV-7-425%5B25-04-18%5D.pdf).

The [department's order index](https://urbandevelopment.up.nic.in/GO_main_menu-2017.html) separately lists notification `503`, dated 26 April 2018, for dissolution of the former Shahjahanpur Nagar Palika Parishad and Roza Nagar Panchayat.

## City, district and division

The [Uttar Pradesh district directory](https://up.gov.in/en/page/districts) places Shahjahanpur district in Bareilly division. The [district's local-body list](https://shahjahanpur.nic.in/npp-panchayats/) lists Nagar Nigam Shahjahanpur separately from the Tilhar and Powayan municipal councils and other urban bodies in the district. The corporation is not a districtwide authority. This record does not establish whether any individual parcel falls inside the current city limits or whether later orders changed the perimeter.

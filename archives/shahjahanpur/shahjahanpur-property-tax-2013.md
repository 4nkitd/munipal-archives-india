# Shahjahanpur predecessor municipality property tax rules in the 2013 gazette

## What the district link contains

The district's [Information Related to Nagar Nigam](https://shahjahanpur.nic.in/service/nigam/) page links a scan labelled `नगर निगम की संपत्ति टैक्स लिस्ट एवं नियमावली`. The Shahjahanpur material actually comes from the predecessor Nagar Palika Parishad. Its assessment rules start on printed page 79 of the 16 March 2013 Uttar Pradesh Gazette, Part 8, on page 3 of the PDF. The initial PDF pages concern a different notice for Orai, Jalaun.

The municipal notice is dated 11 March 2013 and cites section 128(1) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipalities Act, 1916. It says the rules take effect on publication in the gazette. The scan includes self-assessment forms and tables of minimum monthly rental rates for residential buildings or land by locality.

Neither the district page label nor the PDF filename establishes that the rates apply after the municipal corporation was constituted in 2018. Check the municipal office for the applicable assessment and use the state's [e-NagarSewa](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/home) service menu for the published bill and receipt routes.

# Shahjahanpur's 60 wards and councillor seat reservations in 2023

## What the notice covers

Notification `956/9-1-2023-15आर/22`, dated 9 April 2023, sets councillor seat reservations after considering objections and suggestions on draft notification `785`, dated 30 March 2023. It cites section 7(1), (3) and (4) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959, and the 1994 reservation and allotment rules as amended. Its distribution list includes the Bareilly divisional commissioner.

The table transcribes the ward names from pages 2 and 3 of the scanned notice. SC means Scheduled Caste; OBC translates `पिछड़ा वर्ग`. These are the 2023 seat categories, not a current reservation list or a ward boundary map.

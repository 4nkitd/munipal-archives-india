# Shimla municipal councillor results notified in May 2023

## What the notification establishes

On 6 May 2023 the [Himachal Pradesh State Election Commission](https://sechimachal.hp.gov.in/assets/files/Notification-%20result%20mc%20shimla%20english.pdf) published the elected councillors for Shimla under section 13 of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. The notification cites the election programme of 3 April 2023 and receipt of Form 44 returns for all 34 Shimla wards under rule 80(6) of the 2012 election rules. Its final page also includes a separate by-election result for Palampur's ward 2. That result is outside Shimla and is not counted here. The 6 May date is the notification date, not a polling date.

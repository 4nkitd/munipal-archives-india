# Municipal authority and public offices in Shimla

## Which body runs the city?

Municipal Corporation Shimla is the urban local body for the municipal area. The [district administration's account](https://hpshimla.nic.in/mc-shimla/) dates the original Municipal Committee to December 1851 under Act XXVI of 1850. The [corporation's history](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/News/Index/33) links the later conversion to a corporation to the Capital of Himachal Pradesh (Development and Regulations) Act, 1968, and says municipal elections resumed in 1986. It does not give an exact conversion date. The corporation's [Acts and Rules library](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/ActsRules/Index/23) lists the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. That municipal copy is labelled updated through September 2013, so check subsequent amendments before relying on a provision.

The corporation's [governance profile](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/News/Index/32) distinguishes the House, House Committees and Municipal Commissioner. Councillors form the elected part of the House; the Mayor heads it with the Deputy Mayor. The committees carry out assigned House business. The state appoints the Commissioner, who handles administrative and executive work and implements corporation decisions. The corporation is separate from the district administration, whose Deputy Commissioner and subdivision offices have district and revenue responsibilities.

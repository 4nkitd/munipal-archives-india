# Shimla's 34 municipal wards and the district boundary

## Which delimitation applies?

The [State Election Commission notification of 15 February 2023](https://sechimachal.hp.gov.in/assets/files/MC_shimla/delimitationwardsMCShimla2023.pdf), No. SEC (F) 2-3/2020-1-193, republished the 34-ward delimitation of 6 December 2016 as Annexure I for the 2023 general election. It explicitly superseded the notification of 10 March 2022. The Commission said the corporation's territorial limits had not changed between the 2016 notification and its 2023 decision. That statement is not an assurance that all city boundaries remain unchanged indefinitely. The 2023 notified results and the corporation's profile corroborate the count of 34 elected wards.

Annexure I gives English and Hindi descriptions of each ward and lists landmarks. Names here follow the English headings rather than the spellings in the results or the live councillor directory.

## Where the ward and district maps differ

Annexure I describes the edge of Bharari using the municipal boundary near Jangal Pagog, Kelti and Green Gate, as well as the nallah below Snowdon Hospital on its southern side. It treats Upper Dhalli and Lower Dhalli as separate wards. Lower Dhalli adjoins Sanjauli to the west and Seepur to the east; its landmarks include Dhalli Sabzi Mandi and Mashobra Bazaar. This is why a locality or postal label by itself is not enough to assign a property to a ward. The archive's ward summaries are pointers to the notification, not parcel determinations.

Shimla town is the district headquarters, not the full district. The district's [administrative directory](https://hpshimla.nic.in/subdivision-blocks/) distinguishes Shimla Urban and Shimla Rural subdivisions and lists other subdivisions, including Rampur and Rohru. The [district map](https://hpshimla.nic.in/map/) is for the district boundary. The [district disaster management plan](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3b534ba68236ba543ae44b22bd110a1d6/uploads/2021/08/2021082322.pdf), section 2.1, describes neighbouring Mandi and Kullu to the north, Kinnaur to the east, Uttarakhand to the southeast, Solan to the southwest and Sirmaur to the south. These are district neighbours, not municipal ward boundaries.

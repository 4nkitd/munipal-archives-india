# 2026 local holidays within Shimla municipal limits

## Dates and jurisdiction

The Himachal Pradesh General Administration Department, Section A, issued [notification No. GAD-A(F)10-8/2025](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3b534ba68236ba543ae44b22bd110a1d6/uploads/2026/02/17714822638867.pdf) on 18 February 2026. It continues an earlier notification of the same number dated 23 October 2025. The 2026 order declares two local holidays:

| Holiday | Date |
|---|---|
| Maha Ashtami | Monday, 19 October 2026 |
| Govardhan Pooja | Monday, 9 November 2026 |

The order applies to employees of government offices, boards, corporations and educational institutions stationed **within Shimla municipal limits only**. It does not apply to daily-wage employees and does not declare holidays for purposes of section 25 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881. It is not a closure order for all of Shimla district or for all businesses in the city.

## Order date versus notice-board dates

The [district notice listing](https://hpshimla.nic.in/notice/local-holidays-in-shimla-within-municipal-limits-only/) shows 19 February 2026 as its start date and 31 December 2026 as its end date. Those are listing dates. The linked signed notification supplies the 18 February issue date and the two October and November holiday dates. The district administration hosts the notice; the state department issued it.

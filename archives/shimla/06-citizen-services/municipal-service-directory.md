# Where to apply for municipal services in Shimla

## Bills, applications and complaints

The [municipal utility directory](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/Utility/Index) is the starting point for Shimla municipal services. Several entries hand off to state portals or separate billing sites. The links below identify where to start; no payment or application was submitted to test them.

| Need | Published route |
|---|---|
| Pay municipal property tax | The state Directorate of Urban Development's [Citizen Seva property-tax payment page](https://citizenseva.hp.gov.in/hp-udd/bill-payment/quick-bill-payment/property). For assessment questions rather than payment, use the [municipal Tax Department charter](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/CitizenCharter/Index/19). |
| Garbage bill or collection | The utility directory has a Garbage Bill Payment link. The [municipal Health Branch charter](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/CitizenCharter/Index/17) assigns the branch door-to-door collection, street sweeping and public toilet maintenance. For a collection issue rather than a bill, use the municipal office to reach the branch. |
| Water or sewerage | The utility directory lists separate water and sewerage connection links and a separate Water Bill Payment entry. The [municipal contacts directory](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/Contact/Index/14) identifies the Water and Sewerage contact group as Shimla Jal Prabandhan Nigam Limited (SJPNL). |
| Building plan, electricity NOC or debris dumping | The directory lists Building Planning Approval, NOC for Electricity Connection and Permission for Muck/Debris Dumping separately. The former eDistrict links can redirect to the [state HimSeva portal](https://himseva.hp.gov.in/). |
| Street light fault | The [municipal homepage](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/) publishes **1800-180-3580** specifically for street light complaints. |

Other services in the utility directory include street-vendor registration, canopy and commercial-space permission, non-PFA trade licensing and lease rent. The directory has no usable record-level publication date.

## Birth and death certificates

The [Health Branch charter](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/CitizenCharter/Index/17) assigns certificate issuance to the branch. It links a [birth and death procedure](https://mcslogin.hp.gov.in/SecureFileStructure/DepartmentDetails/Doc/1_7_23_2021_7_12.pdf) with a specimen application addressed to the Registrar, Birth and Death, Municipal Corporation Shimla. Registration of an event and obtaining a copy of an existing entry are separate steps. The copy-request specimen asks for the person's name and the place and date of the event. Birth and death reporting forms are also available on the [municipal download page](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/Download/DownloadForm). Confirm current fees, evidence and office hours with the Registrar rather than relying on the older procedure for those details.

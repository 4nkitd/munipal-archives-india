# Shimla municipal budget estimates for 2026–27

## Publication and accounting periods

The corporation's [notice board](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/Notices/AllNotices) lists **Budget Estimates 2026-27** on 10 March 2026. That is the portal posting date. The [budget book](https://mcslogin.hp.gov.in/SecureFileStructure/DepartmentDetails/Doc/Budget%20Estimates%202026-27_3_10_2026_39_348.pdf) pairs revised estimates for 2025–26 with budget estimates for 2026–27. Its cover refers to the special budget meeting of 20 February 2026. The columns are estimates, not year-end actuals, audited accounts or evidence that a grant or loan was received.

## Selected budget heads

The income comparison on printed page 88 and expenditure comparison on printed page 90 give the following amounts in **₹ lakh**:

| Head | 2025–26 revised | 2026–27 estimate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tax revenue (110) | 2,003.00 | 2,103.80 |
| Assigned revenues and compensation (120) | 3,332.00 | 3,750.00 |
| Revenue income subtotal | 9,387.87 | 9,934.64 |
| Grants and contributions for specific purposes (320) | 4,962.32 | 38,867.75 |
| Secured loans (330) | 0.00 | 20,000.00 |
| Capital income subtotal | 4,962.32 | 58,867.75 |
| Total income | 14,350.19 | 68,802.39 |
| Establishment expenses (210) | 5,331.83 | 6,195.13 |
| Fixed assets (410) | 5,824.50 | 7,856.00 |
| Capital work in progress (412) | 1,006.00 | 33,506.00 |

The difference between the two income totals comes mainly from the capital side, especially specific-purpose grants and the secured-loan estimate. The full book has separate detailed schedules for revenue receipts, capital receipts, revenue expenditure and capital expenditure. The corporation also lists **Special Budget House** and **Supplementary Budget Session** on its [House Business portal](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/HouseBusiness/Index). Use those records to trace later decisions; the March posting establishes the estimates in this book.

## Separate municipal bond procurement notice

The [municipal notice board](https://shimlamc.hp.gov.in/Notices/AllNotices) posted [Corrigendum-8](https://mcslogin.hp.gov.in/SecureFileStructure/DepartmentDetails/Doc/Acounts%20tender_9_19_2026_32_706.pdf) on 19 September 2026. The notice itself is dated 18 September. It concerns a request for proposals to appoint a transaction adviser and merchant banker for municipal bond fundraising. It states a revised submission deadline of 15 December 2026 at 5 p.m., with technical and financial bids scheduled to open on 16 and 31 December respectively, both at 11 a.m. It is a procurement timetable, not a notice of bond issuance or a commitment that any part of the budgeted loan was raised through bonds. Check the official board for subsequent changes before relying on these dates.

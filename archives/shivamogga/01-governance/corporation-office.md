# Shivamogga corporation office and district municipal directory

## District directory entry

The district's public-utilities entry gives the address as City Corporation, Durgigudi, Shivamogga, Karnataka 577201. Its [municipality directory](https://shivamogga.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality-3/) separately lists City Municipal Council Bhadravati at Hosamane, Bhadravati 577301 and the town municipal office at Shikaripura. Those offices serve different local bodies; sharing a district does not put them under the Shivamogga corporation office.

The [district helpline directory](https://shivamogga.nic.in/en/helpline/) separates the city corporation, Shivamogga taluk and the Deputy Commissioner's office in its COVID-19 control-room table. The number beside the corporation there is specific to that table. The Durgigudi public-utility entry itself gives a postal address without a general telephone number or email.

# Shimoga municipal wards in the 2011 District Census Handbook

## Municipal area in the census

The Karnataka handbook, Series 30, Part XII-B, identifies Shimoga as a city municipal council (CMC) in the 2011 census. The town summary on printed page 24 assigns it code 803137 and records 76.71 square kilometres, 76,009 households and 322,650 residents. Of these residents, 162,018 were male and 160,632 female. The next town, Bhadravati (CMC), has its own code, 803138, and a separate population total.

## Ward and enumeration-block identifiers

Under town code 803137 the enumeration-block listing uses ward labels `WARD No.-0001` through `WARD No.-0035`. Each ward can have several EB or sub-EB entries; counting those entries as additional wards would overstate the total. These are historical census units for Shimoga (CMC), not a delimitation notification for today's corporation. The [district census catalogue](https://shivamogga.nic.in/en/document-category/census/) dates the published entry 16 June 2014.

# Shivamogga monthly municipal water bills at Shimoga One

## Paying a municipal water demand

On the ShimogaOne Services page, the relevant entry is “Shimoga City Corporation Water Charges”. The state describes a counter service for viewing and paying monthly water bills. Bring the bill or receipt so the operator can identify the demand and enter the transaction. The guide lists cash, cheque and demand draft as payment modes and says the operator issues a receipt after successful payment.

This is the municipal water account. The next entry concerns electricity and has its own account-number rules; those instructions do not establish the identifiers needed for a water connection. The water entry describes collection of an existing demand. It does not list a tariff, a new-connection procedure or a deadline for resolving a supply complaint. The page uses the spelling “Shimoga One” for the service centre.

# Silchar corporation and its 42-ward jurisdiction

## Board and corporation ward counts

Silchar's municipal history dates the one-member-per-ward system to the 1966 municipal election, when there were 25 wards. The board had 28 wards in 1979. The city cites Assam notification E-228921/508 of 28 November 2022 as constituting Silchar Municipal Corporation from the board with **42 wards**. The earlier ward counts are not the corporation count.

## Corporation in office

The city lists Srishti Singh as first municipal commissioner, from 16 June 2025 to 16 March 2026. The [State Election Commission](https://sec.assam.gov.in/press-release/press-release-regarding-party-wise-result-of-smc-election2026) published its party-wise result on 7 October 2026. The municipal history records 42 elected councillors and the selection on 5 November 2026 of Amarendra Paul as first mayor and Bijendra Prasad Singh as first deputy mayor.

# Silchar registration notice for unauthorised hoardings, unipoles and kiosks

## Structures covered and deadline

Entry 24 in Silchar's notice register, dated 16 July 2026, required registration by 31 July 2026 of hoardings, unipoles and kiosks installed without permission within Silchar Municipal Corporation limits. The register files it under Trade License. This was a dated registration deadline, not a standing annual renewal date.

## Related entry

The register separately lists a 30 June 2026 notice for banners, hoardings, festoons and welcome gates under Trade License (entry 22). The index does not state a registration fee or penalty for the July notice.

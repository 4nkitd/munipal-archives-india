# Fifteenth Finance Commission sanctions recorded by Silchar

## Reading the sanction register

The register separates the financial year a grant was for from the year against which Silchar records the instalment. Each entry identifies a sanction letter and an amount. These amounts represent sanctions, not verified expenditure or completed works.

| Letter reference | Letter date | For year | During year | Sanctioned amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| FS/MDB-2021-22(1)_82 | 22 September 2021 | 2020–21 | 2021–22 | ₹4,58,68,000 |
| FS/MDB_2023-24(I)_86 | 16 December 2023 | 2022–23 | 2023–24 | ₹6,48,67,000 |
| FS/MDB_2024-25(I)_01 | 12 April 2024 | 2022–23 | 2024–25 | ₹6,48,67,000 |
| FS/MDB_2024-25(I)_02 | 12 April 2024 | 2023–24 | 2024–25 | ₹3,99,74,000 |

The last two letters have the same date but concern different grant years.

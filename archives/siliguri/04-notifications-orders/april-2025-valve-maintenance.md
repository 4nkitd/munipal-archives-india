# Water supply during valve maintenance at Jhanker pumping station

## Households and shifts covered

The corporation reported that the Public Health Engineering department needed urgent valve maintenance for Collegepara and Rathkhola at the Jhanker intermediate pumping station. The [attached municipal notice](https://www.siligurismc.in/userfiles/file/Noticephe.pdf) identified Wednesday, 23 April 2025, and warned that supply could be affected in both the morning and evening shifts. It did not announce a complete shutdown in every listed ward.

| Area | Coverage in the notice |
|---|---|
| Wards 15 through 24 | Named for the two shifts |
| Ward 29 | Part of the ward only |

The corporation said it would deploy additional water tankers in the affected areas. The notice gives no tanker stops, dispatch times or restoration hour. The publication date here is the municipal news page’s explicit “Posted on” date, Monday, 21 April 2025. The scheduled maintenance date comes from the body of the attached notice; the two dates serve different purposes.

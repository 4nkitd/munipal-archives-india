# Siliguri: corporation limits and electoral wards

## Municipal territory

Siliguri became a corporation in 1994. Its 47 wards extend across Darjeeling and Jalpaiguri districts; the corporation says 14 are in Jalpaiguri. The state directory records five boroughs and 41.90 km². A district address for headquarters does not describe the whole municipal territory. See the [state corporation directory](https://udma.wb.gov.in/modal-content/4) and [local authority record](https://www.siligurismc.in/about-us.php).

## Which record defines the boundary?

Section 1(5) specifically allows notified exceptions and modifications in Darjeeling district and preserves the powers of the autonomous hill council for the specified hill areas. A district name alone does not establish municipal jurisdiction. Section 403(d) names the Siliguri Municipal Corporation Act, 1990 and preserves qualifying earlier sanctions and permissions.

The Kolkata Gazette Extraordinary of 29 May 2008 published West Bengal Act XXXIX of 2006 under notification 957-L. Chapter II distinguishes the declaration of intention, its publication, consideration of objections and constitution of the corporation area (sections 3–6). Section 7 concerns the number of wards; section 8 deals with abolishing or altering the limits of a corporation area. The publication date is that of the Act, not a redrawing of Siliguri’s wards.

The [SEC Municipal Election Manual 2015](https://wbsec.wb.gov.in/writereaddata/Upload/Portal/Publication/Municipal_Election_Manual_201520241203083924.pdf), printed page 19, reproduces section 3 of the West Bengal Municipal Elections Act, 1994. The State Government determines the ward count; the Commission divides the area by notification, considering population, dwelling pattern, geography and economic conditions. For an existing municipality it considers the municipality’s views. Each ward is a single-member constituency. To establish which side of a street was in a ward at a particular election, use the delimitation notification for that election. Neither the ward count nor this Act supplies that street schedule.

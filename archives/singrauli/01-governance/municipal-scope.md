# Singrauli Nagar Nigam status and office

## Corporation and district

The district public-utility directory lists **Nagar Palik Nigam Singrauli** at Majan Mod. The [state urban local body map index](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=SF2Fyv4OVHlghtRd63MGiQ%3D%3D) records Singrauli corporation under Singrauli district, Rewa division, and labels it नगर निगम. The [state audit index](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=xB4B9%2BRBeVZsSiC8ZMDlxw%3D%3D&leftid=FFjp7b7TC4MpRynyv7jRfw%3D%3D) lists Bargawa separately as a Nagar Panchayat. An address in Singrauli district is not by itself evidence that the address falls inside the city corporation.

## Municipal office

The [district birth-certificate page](https://singrauli.nic.in/en/service/birth-certificate/) identifies the Nagar Nigam office at **Majan Mod, Waidhan, Singrauli 486886**. The public-utility entry gives the location without an office phone or email. The map index links a base map for Singrauli, but its index row is not a ward delimitation schedule. This record does not assign villages, industrial sites or specific streets to corporation wards.

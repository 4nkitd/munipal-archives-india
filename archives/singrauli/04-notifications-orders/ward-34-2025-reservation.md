# Singrauli ward 34 councillor by-election reservation entry, December 2025

## What the election index records

The Madhya Pradesh State Election Commission's urban-local-body programme index lists a communication dated **11 December 2025** about reservation status for the councillor post in **ward 34, Nagar Palika Nigam Singrauli**, for the second-half 2025 by-election cycle. The index row has ID **11568**, reference **एफ-53NN 02/2025/ पांच /1567** and attachment label `1567.pdf`. Find it by its title beginning `नगरपालिका निगम सिंगरौली के वार्ड क्रमांक - 34`.

This is a dated reference to a specific ward and councillor post, not a list of city wards. The HTML row does not state the reservation category, polling date, candidates, or result. Those details are not transcribed; the attachment was not opened. Ward 34 also appears in the Census 2011 inventory, but the two sources do not establish that its boundaries stayed the same.

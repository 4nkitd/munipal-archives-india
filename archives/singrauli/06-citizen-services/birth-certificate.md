# Birth certificate guidance for Singrauli

## City office and online reference

The district birth-certificate service page is marked last updated **27 March 2023**. It gives the Nagar Nigam office as **Majan Mod, Waidhan, Singrauli 486886** and points applicants to the Civil Registration System login. The [district's corporation office entry](https://singrauli.nic.in/en/public-utility/nagar-palik-nigam-singrauli/) independently lists the corporation at Majan Mod.

## Before applying

The page does not say which papers to bring, whether fees apply, or how to correct an existing record. It gives neither a municipal office phone number nor an email. The Civil Registration System link did not render during verification; no application was submitted. The published municipal office address is the verified enquiry point for Singrauli city, and the district page does not establish that the same office registers births elsewhere in Singrauli district.

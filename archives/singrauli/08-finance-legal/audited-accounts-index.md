# Singrauli Nagar Nigam in the state audited-accounts index

## Identify the accounts entry

The Madhya Pradesh Urban Development and Housing Department lists the city corporation as **सिंगरौली** under Rewa division, Singrauli district, and local-body type **नगर निगम**. In the FY 2024–25 index, the separate **बरगवां** row is a Nagar Panchayat even though its district column also says Singrauli.

| [2024–25](https://www.mpurban.gov.in/?page=xB4B9%2BRBeVZsSiC8ZMDlxw%3D%3D&leftid=FFjp7b7TC4MpRynyv7jRfw%3D%3D) | View link, file label `NN Singrauli_compressed.pdf` |

The apparent spelling difference is in the attachment label, not the Hindi ULB name. The fiscal years identify the index editions; they are not audit sign-off dates. No balances, audit findings or completion status are inferred from the index.

# Sitamarhi Municipal Corporation: jurisdiction and classification

## Municipal jurisdiction

In its final Phase II ward-formation register, the Bihar State Election Commission lists Sitamarhi at serial 77 as नगर निगम, in Sitamarhi Sadar subdivision of Sitamarhi district. The register marks the body as expanded (विस्तारित). The 46 wards are those of the corporation, not the whole subdivision or district.

## District evidence

Sitamarhi district published a quotation invitation from नगर निगम सीतामढ़ी under memo 1652 dated 9 September 2026. Its general utilities listing still uses the older Nagarparishad label.

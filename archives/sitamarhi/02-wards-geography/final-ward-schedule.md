# Sitamarhi final ward schedule: boundaries and census population

## Reading Form 6

The SEC’s Form 6 lists wards 1–46, their four boundaries and their populations on the 2011 Census basis. Ward 1 runs between the Lakhandai River on the north and the railway line on the south. Its western limit is Kapraul Road; its eastern limit follows the Mohanpur and Mehsaul revenue boundary. The full Hindi boundary descriptions are transcribed in `wards.json`; the SEC links a map alongside each ward. These figures are residents counted on the stated census basis, not electoral-roll totals.

# Birth and death certificates for Sivakasi residents

## Which records are online

The Tamil Nadu Civil Registration System says the public can download certificates free of charge for births and deaths registered from 1 January 2018. For records before that date, the portal directs applicants to the respective local body. These instructions concern obtaining a certificate for a record that already exists. They do not describe how to register a new event, correct an entry or handle a delayed registration.

## Older Sivakasi records

Use the local body responsible for the original registration when asking about a pre-2018 record. The state's cutoff refers to registration, and the source does not identify which office now holds individual legacy registers or list a Sivakasi-specific counter or fee.

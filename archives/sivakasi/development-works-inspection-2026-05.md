# Virudhunagar district press release on Sivakasi municipal works, May 2026

## Field inspection

Virudhunagar district published this item on 21 May 2026. Its [Tamil press release no. 306](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3c86a7ee3d8ef0b551ed58e354a836f2b/uploads/2026/03/17793415385487.pdf) records that the District Collector visited on 19 May 2026. It reports the launch of a joint cleaning drive in the Thiruthangal area and a visit to the public restroom and sanitation complex at Sivakasi bus stand. The release says the Collector instructed officials to clean public sanitation facilities at set intervals.

The release also reports a site check confirming removal of a TASMAC retail outlet near the bus stand. The item records the inspection and instructions; it does not publish a project estimate, tender award or ward boundaries.

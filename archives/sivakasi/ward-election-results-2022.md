# Sivakasi Municipal Corporation ward election results, 2022

## Ward coverage and reservation

The Tamil Nadu State Election Commission files Sivakasi's corporation return under Virudhunagar district. It lists wards 1 through 48. Counting the labels printed next to the ward numbers gives 21 general, 20 general (women), three Scheduled Caste (general), and four Scheduled Caste (women) wards for this election.

## Poll totals as reported

| Ward | Electors | Votes polled | Turnout | Valid votes | Invalid votes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 2,652 | 1,863 | 70.25% | 1,862 | 1 |
| 2 | 2,326 | 1,625 | 69.86% | 1,625 | 0 |

The PDF contains candidate-level returns. The commission says the applicable entries in Forms 23, 25 and 26 and the result declared in Form 27 by the Returning Officer are final. The return is not an Assembly election result, a ward boundary map or a current officeholder roster. The year in the title identifies the election, not a publication date.

# Municipal Council Sohna: profile and elected council

## Municipal status and area

Sohna is a municipal council in Gurugram district, Haryana, southeast of Manesar and Gurugram. The Sohna Elevated Road connects it to Gurugram, and the Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway passes near Sohna. The [Gurugram district administrative directory](https://gurugram.gov.in/about-district/administrative-setup/) lists Sohna separately from the municipal corporations of Gurugram and Manesar.

Haryana upgraded Sohna from a Municipal Committee to a Municipal Council in September 2014. The [DULB old-notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/WebCMS/Start/?DynamicTypeIdView=12739&Id=10363) lists the upgrade notification dated 5 September 2014, and [Press Trust of India reported the upgrade on 11 September](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/sohna-municipal-committee-becomes-municipal-council-114091101015_1.html). The upgrade was not Sohna's first municipal institution. The [Gurgaon District Gazetteer's local government chapter, reproduced by DocsLib](https://docslib.org/doc/4406752/a-progressive-account-of-the-development-of-local), records a Class III municipal committee in 1879 and again in 1955, with changes in status in between.

## President and election dates

- **19 June 2022:** Anju Bala, also known as Anju Devi, won the general election for President on a BJP ticket. Results were declared on 22 June. The [State Election Commission removal order dated 28 November 2022](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2022/12/2022120979.pdf) disqualified her and declared the seat vacant. [Hindustan reported](https://www.livehindustan.com/ncr/gurgaon/story-high-court-rejects-sohna-chairperson-anju-devi-s-petition-over-fake-educational-documents-201734459553810.html) that she took the oath on 30 January 2023 while the order was stayed. The [Punjab and Haryana High Court dismissed her challenge](https://www.livelaw.in/pdf_upload/cwp28460202216122024finalorder-578121.pdf) on 16 December 2024.
- **2 March 2025:** Sohna voted in a bye-election for President only. On 12 March, Preeti of the BJP [was declared the winner](https://thepublicworld.com/archives/105452) with 11,191 votes to Congress candidate Lalita's 6,687, a margin of 4,504. The [State Election Commission's 20 March notification, SEC/1ME/2025/4353](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/202507081560191790.pdf), lists the winner as **Preeti** and specifies that she serves the remainder of her predecessor's term. [Reporting on her taking charge](https://dailypioneer.com/india/newly-elected-gurugram-mayor--sohna-chairperson-take-charge) and [coverage of the October 2026 council meeting](https://www.amarujala.com/delhi-ncr/gurgaon/councilors-raise-objection-to-dumping-of-waste-from-gurugram-area-in-sohna-gurgaon-news-c-24-1-fbd1020-105169-2026-10-23) identify her as Preeti Bagri, not Baghel. No Sohna ward seats were contested in March 2025.

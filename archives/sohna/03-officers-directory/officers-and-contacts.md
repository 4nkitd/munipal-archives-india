## Office address and published locations

The GeM bid GEM/2026/B/8242959 dated 5 November 2026 publishes the consignee location as Municipal Council Sohna, near Government Senior Secondary Girls School, Ward No. 14, Sohna, Haryana 122103 [3]. This is a procurement delivery address; confirm the appropriate public counter before visiting.

The ULB e-auction advertisement separately publishes "Near Fauwara Chowk, Municipal Council Sohna, Gurugram, Haryana-122103", email mcsohna@gmail.com, phone 8178453647 and mobile 9466676699 against the person designation Executive Officer [7]. These are advertisement contact fields, not independently verified current personal EO numbers. The source gives no clear posting date. The two location descriptions are preserved separately rather than assumed to be identical.

For property tax queries, use the dedicated Sohna helpdesk number 9213887631 [6]. For other branches, request the current charge holder through the institutional council mailbox or EO office. No separate Secretary, Building Inspector, ME or sanitation telephone has been inferred from an email pattern or another municipality.

## SDM oversight and RTI

DULB's published PIO list identifies the Executive Officer, Municipal Council Sohna as State Public Information Officer and the Sub-Division Magistrate, Sohna as First Appellate Authority [8]. This establishes an RTI appeal relationship, not that the SDM is a municipal employee or that every municipal service complaint is an RTI appeal.

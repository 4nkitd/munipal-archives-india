# Sohna notices, regularized colonies and GRAP order sources

## Official notified-colony list

DULB's [regularized-colony index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) contains "Municipalities Status of 836 Colonies Data Notified 2025", published 13 October 2025. The linked [official status PDF](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2F012fa553-5f2b-4575-866d-034a892c23ac.pdf) groups the following five entries under Municipal Council Sohna, Gurugram district.

| Serial in 2025 status list | Colony identifier | Colony name | Area in acres |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 210 | 8015 | Unnamed Colony C-4 | 10.48 |
| 211 | 8035 | Hari Nagar Colony | 3.82 |
| 212 | 8039 | Unnamed Colony C-13 | 16.17 |
| 213 | 8041 | Amtek Colony Extension | 7.66 |
| 214 | 8120 | Amtek Colony | 31.33 |

The preceding entry, Unnamed K-64, belongs to Pataudi Mandi, not Sohna. Municipality labels occupy merged cells in the PDF. Reading the names as a flat list can misassign this entry.

This is an official status-list extract, not a plot-level certificate or a claim that these are every colony ever notified in Sohna. The frontmatter date is the status-list publication date, not the individual colonies' notification dates. Payment of property tax or appearance in a property survey does not by itself establish inclusion in the notified colony boundary.

## Boundary evidence and earlier list

The same index links [List of Regularized Colony 2023 & 2024](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2Fa691a824-0aaf-4fe3-ae0f-3ab8487a9414.pdf), published 2 February 2024. Its text gives municipality, colony area and revenue descriptions. Serial numbers differ from the later status list.

| Colony | Serial in earlier list | Revenue locality wording in source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8015, Unnamed Colony C-4 | 209 | Dhunela |
| 8035, Hari Nagar Colony | 210 | Sohna |
| 8041, Amtek Colony Extension | 211 | Zakupur, revenue reference 189 |
| 8120, Amtek Colony | 212 | Jakhopur and Sohna |
| 8039, Unnamed Colony C-13 | 507 | Sohna |

"Zakupur" and "Jakhopur" reproduce different source spellings. This guide does not assert that they are separate revenue villages or silently combine them. The earlier list includes khasra references with partial-parcel markers such as "min". A buyer or applicant should compare the exact parcel against the gazette schedule and approved boundary map, not infer eligibility from a colony's popular name or total acreage.

The index separately lists notification batches dated 25 July 2023, 6 October 2023, 15 December 2023, 1 February 2024, 6 March 2024 and later dates. Their portal upload dates can differ from the dates in their titles. No individual notification number or batch date is assigned to a Sohna colony here without a verified match to its gazette entry.

## GRAP instructions involving Sohna

An [official Gurugram district air-pollution implementation letter hosted by DULB](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Website/DynamicUploadContent/CONTENTFILES/8a1161dd-7e64-429a-85f7-48ae1283f4dc.pdf) includes the Sub-Divisional Magistrate, Sohna and Executive Officer, Municipal Council Sohna among its addressees. It refers to a meeting held on 3 October 2022 under the Deputy Commissioner regarding CAQM directions and revised GRAP.

The letter directs departments to nominate nodal officers with mobile numbers and email addresses and submit daily action-taken reports by 3 PM to HSPCB Gurugram Region South. HSPCB is to compile the information and send it to the District Public Relations Officer by 3:30 PM. It requires prompt handling of Sameer app complaints and immediate fire response.

For construction and demolition violations, it allocates enforcement below 20,000 square metres to the agencies responsible for their respective jurisdictions and larger sites to HSPCB or DTCP. It also allocates diesel-generator enforcement among jurisdictional agencies, HSPCB and the Industries Department. These are historical administrative instructions, not proof that a particular GRAP stage remains active today. The handwritten date and memo number are not transcribed here because they are not reliably legible in the indexed text.

## Later orders and checking current restrictions

The [HSPCB GRAP and CAQM directions index](https://www.hspcb.org.in/page/grap-orders) lists implementation and revocation orders. Examples include Stage I implementation dated 14 October 2024, Stage II implementation dated 21 October 2024 and an implementation direction dated 25 November 2024.

A later [DULB Stage II circular hosted by Municipal Corporation Manesar](https://mcmanesar.in/sites/default/files/2025-10/Implementation%20of%20GRAP%202%20in%20Delhi%C2%A0NCR_1.pdf), memo SNT/DULB/2025/SBM-303 dated 21 October 2025, addresses all District Municipal Commissioners and corporation commissioners in Haryana's NCR region. It cites CAQM order 120017/27/GRAP/2021/CAQM dated 19 October 2025. It directs enforcement of Stage I and II, daily mechanical or vacuum road sweeping, water sprinkling with dust suppressants at least every alternate day, construction-site dust inspections and restrictions on coal or firewood in eateries. It asks for daily monitoring and weekly compliance reporting.

That circular is a regional instruction, not a Sohna-issued notice. Before starting work or assessing a violation, check the latest CAQM invocation, revision and revocation orders and the applicable exemptions. Neither the 2022 letter nor the 2025 circular establishes the GRAP stage active on 22 November 2026.

## Public-notice coverage limits

The legacy Sohna town-planning notification URL, `https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Sohna/7790`, returned a portal 404 page during verification. The `/Sohna` landing route also displayed department-wide content, including notices for other municipalities. Those items must not be relabelled as Sohna notices merely because they appear under that route.

Use the [DULB notification index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/Notification) and the colony index above to retrieve original documents. Request the signed notification, boundary schedule and map from Municipal Council Sohna when a parcel-level decision is required. This record archives the verified official references rather than claiming a complete municipal notice register.

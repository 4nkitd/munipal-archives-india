# Sohna citizen services, property tax, NDC and RTI

## Property tax and No Dues Certificate

Use the [Haryana property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) for property tax and property-record services. Select the correct municipality, Municipal Council Sohna, rather than Municipal Corporation Gurugram. The DULB Right to Service schedule expressly directs property-register ownership changes, new property IDs and No Dues Certificate portal objections to this address.

Check the property ID, owner or occupier, area, floors, use category and recorded dues before paying. Preserve the payment receipt and application reference. If the data is wrong, use the relevant correction or objection service instead of treating payment as a correction request. NDC objections cover dues, size, floor details, authorized or unauthorized status and property category. A tax receipt, property ID or NDC should not be treated as a substitute for a registered title document or a building permission.

The [DULB property tax index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) lists "Property tax notification 2026 Councils and Committees", published 31 July 2026, separately from the corporation notification. It also lists a one-time interest-waiver notification published 18 May 2026 for arrears from 2010-11 to 2024-2025. These are document publication dates. This guide does not reproduce rates, eligibility conditions or a rebate deadline without extracting the applicable notification.

## Right to Service limits

The [official consolidated ULB schedule](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService) states that it is updated through 4 November 2025. Its column specifies working days. The following entries are relevant to a municipal council.

| Service | Published limit | Council-level designated officer | First authority | Second authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Change owner or occupier, except death cases | 15 working days | Property-register entry groups Secretary, Executive Officer and Zonal Taxation Officer across ULB types | Sub-Divisional Officer Civil | District Municipal Commissioner |
| Change owner or occupier after death | 45 working days | Secretary or Executive Officer for council or committee cases in the combined entry | Sub-Divisional Officer Civil | District Municipal Commissioner |
| Clear NDC portal objections | 10 working days | Superintendent or Secretary | Executive Officer | District Municipal Commissioner |
| Issue new property ID | 10 working days | Superintendent or Secretary | Executive Officer | District Municipal Commissioner |
| Issue copy of documents | 15 working days | Superintendent or Secretary | Executive Officer | District Municipal Commissioner |
| Remove solid waste from streets, roads, green belts or open spaces | 2 working days | Chief Sanitary Inspector | Executive Officer | District Municipal Commissioner |
| Replace street lights | 10 working days | Junior Engineer | Municipal Engineer | District Municipal Commissioner |

The ownership-change entries use combined and inconsistently ordered officer descriptions on the source page. Confirm the receiving officer on the application acknowledgement rather than assigning a named Sohna official from that wording.

SARAL explains that the notified clock starts when the designated office receives a complete application. Its Auto Appeal System provides escalation for integrated services when deadlines are breached. Keep the acknowledgement and check whether an appeal has been generated; do not assume every municipal complaint is automatically enrolled.

## Birth, death and marriage certificates

Start birth and death certificate requests through [Antyodaya SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/). Search for the exact service after login. DULB distinguishes certificate applications, correction of birth or death records, delayed registration and inclusion of a child's name in a birth record. These are different requests and may require different supporting documents. The SARAL catalogue or service form, not the old municipal citizen charter, should be checked for current requirements and fees.

For marriage registration, DULB states that urban local body registration follows the Haryana Compulsory Registration of Marriage Act, 2008 and its 2008 Rules. Use the [official Haryana marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) linked from SARAL. SARAL also links an [appointment rescheduling service](https://edisha.gov.in/eForms/MarriageScheduleAppointment). Retain the application reference and attend any verification appointment specified by the registrar. Marriage registration is unrelated to planning permission for a marriage palace.

SARAL offers application tracking at [status.saralharyana.nic.in](https://status.saralharyana.nic.in/) and assisted access through SARAL Kendras and CSC centres. No birth, death or marriage processing deadline is asserted here because the ULB schedule cited above does not supply those service-specific entries.

## RTI requests and appeals

The [DULB PIO list](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PIOList), municipal councils table, serial 15, identifies the Executive Officer, Municipal Council Sohna as State Public Information Officer and the Sub-Divisional Magistrate, Sohna as First Appellate Authority. The directory supplies designations, not verified current personal names.

Address an RTI request for council-held records to that SPIO. Identify the records and period clearly, for example a property assessment entry, sanctioned work estimate, council resolution or colony notification. RTI seeks existing information; a request to repair a street light or change a tax entry belongs in the service or grievance process. Check current Haryana RTI filing instructions and fees before submission. This record does not verify a Sohna-specific online RTI integration.

## Public grievances and source limits

DULB publishes 1800-8900-929 for IT support and grievance registration, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. For SARAL service queries, grievances and appeals, call 0172-3968400 during the hours in the frontmatter or use its [ticket tracking service](https://eticketing.saralharyana.nic.in/CitizenViewComplaints). Record the location, property ID where relevant, application or complaint number, date and requested action.

The ULB schedule links its citizen complaint application for waste removal and street lights. Escalation roles above are service-specific. Do not import the schedule's water-connection or sewer-overflow entries into Sohna: those entries expressly name only Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat and Karnal municipal corporations.

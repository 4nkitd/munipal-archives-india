# Sohna tenders, budgets and civic infrastructure, 2024 to 2026

## Scope and procurement portals

This record separates Municipal Council Sohna procurement from Public Health Engineering Department works in Sohna. It also separates official tender metadata from newspaper accounts of council budgets and proposed projects. The date in the frontmatter is the compilation date, not the publication date of an individual tender.

The official [HEWP live-tender page](https://works.haryana.gov.in/LiveTender.aspx) states that tenders floated through HEWP are published on the NIC Haryana eTendering website for bidder participation. HEWP says its live-tender listing displays these tenders on the day after NIC publication. Use the tender ID and reference number to locate historical notices at [Haryana eProcurement](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app). A live listing is not a complete historical register.

Amounts below retain their source meaning. A tender value is the advertised estimate, an EMD is bid security, and a budget provision is a planned amount. None establishes the final payment. Work periods are advertised contract periods, not verified completion times. Check corrigenda, cancellations and award documents before relying on an old notice.

## Shiv Kund access and Damdama-area civic amenities

[Daily Jagran, 17 November 2025](https://www.thedailyjagran.com/haryana/sohna-municipal-council-to-develop-eight-key-model-roads-across-city-focus-on-drainage-footpaths-40041636), reported a proposed ₹15 crore programme for eight model roads with drainage, footpaths and lighting. The listed routes include Fawwara Chowk to Shiv Kund, the hot-spring access corridor. It quoted Executive Engineer Ajay Panghal and described consultant appointment and DPR preparation. This is a reported planning-stage programme, not evidence of an awarded hot-spring redevelopment contract. No separate verified 2024 to 2026 municipal hot-spring capital allocation was found.

- [Sigma Tenders](https://sigmatenders.com/tender/7qJaHy2TKAxKxcbxgWbhMK) indexes `2024_HRY_406155_1`, published 23 October 2024, for completion work in the cremation ground on Damdama Road, ward 3, under D Plan 2024-25. Reference `2024F7E2B6B6 AC47 4F64 931F 5E5E65EF15AA325ULB`. Its [official notice locator](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app?page=FrontEndTenderDetailsExternal&service=page&tnid=482625) is retained for verification.

# Sohna Development Plan 2031, IMT Sohna and the Manesar corridor

## Notification and planning extent

The Town and Country Planning Department notified the Final Development Plan of Sohna 2031 A.D. on 15 November 2012 through notification No. CCP(NCR)/FDP-2031/GGN-SHN/2012/3543. It followed the draft notification dated 25 July 2012 and invokes section 5(7) of the Punjab Scheduled Roads and Controlled Areas Restriction of Unregulated Development Act, 1963. The notification identifies final-plan drawing DTP(G) 2103/2012 dated 9 November 2012. Its explanatory note and Annexure B zoning regulations must be read together. [1]

The plan covers 45 controlled areas totalling 45,687 hectares and provides for a projected population of 640,000 in 2031. This is a planning projection, not a census count. The notification gives a 2011 planning-area population of approximately 67,785, including urban villages. Neither the controlled-area boundary nor the industrial estate boundary should be treated as the municipal boundary of Sohna.

| Planning component | Area in hectares |
| --- | ---: |
| Proposed urbanization | 5,600 |
| Agriculture zone, including forest land, village abadis and existing structures | 34,793 |
| Water recharge zone | 340 |
| Natural Conservation Zone | 4,563 |
| Existing town and village abadi within proposed urbanization | 391 |
| Total development-plan extent | 45,687 |

Within the 5,600-hectare proposed urbanization, the allocation is 1,719 hectares residential, 255 commercial, 1,236 industrial, 627 transport and communication, 241 public utilities, 573 public and semi-public uses, and 949 open spaces and green belts. These are notified allocations, not statements that all land has been acquired, serviced or built out. [1]

## IMT Sohna and the acreage distinction

HSIIDC's Industrial Model Township at Sohna is a separate estate from IMT Manesar. Its location near the intersection of NH-248A and the Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway places it on the regional industrial corridor south of Gurugram. A June 2022 report records an approximately 1,500-acre township and a proposed 500-acre Electronics Manufacturing Cluster, following the Centre's October 2021 selection of HSIIDC as project implementation agency. [3]

Official historical acreage supports this scale. The Haryana Investors' Handbook lists “Sohana IMT” in its Mewat district table at 1,501.61 acres for financial year 2017-18. That table is historical and its land stock and prices are not current allotment information. [2] A Haryana government release dated 28 September 2026 again describes Sohna IMT as spread over 1,500 acres, alongside Manesar IMT at approximately 4,000 acres. It separately reports a proposed Sohna expansion of approximately 1,500 acres. The proposed expansion must not be added to the existing estate as completed development. [4]

The 2012 development-plan explanatory note says HSIIDC had acquired 607 hectares along KMP for IMT development. This is about 1,500 acres, not 607 acres. Its broader industrial allocation of 1,236 hectares covers planned industrial land uses and is not the same measurement as the acquired estate. The note also discusses approximately 600 hectares for further expansion as an expectation at that time. [1]

The plan records the existing Rozka Meo industrial area and Textile Valley in Sector 15, approximately 89 hectares, on the Sohna-Nuh road. It proposes worker housing adjoining industrial sectors in Sectors 17 and 25. Crucially, the acquired IMT land is described as low-lying and flood-prone, requiring special measures by HSIIDC. Industrial zoning does not remove drainage or flood-risk obligations. [1]

## Opened highways and the Manesar connection

| Link | Sourced status and relevance |
| --- | --- |
| Gurugram-Sohna highway, commonly called Sohna Elevated Road | Opened through to traffic on 11 July 2022. The approximately 22-km project is a six-lane access-controlled corridor with about 7 km of elevated sections, not a continuously elevated 22-km structure. The reported capital investment was about Rs 2,000 crore. |
| Alipur interchange | The July 2022 account identifies Alipur as the Sohna road connection to the Delhi-Vadodara section of the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway. |
| Delhi-Dausa-Lalsot expressway section | The Prime Minister dedicated the 246-km section on 12 February 2023. PIB records a cost exceeding Rs 12,150 crore. This milestone does not establish completion of the entire Delhi-Mumbai Expressway. |
| KMP connection to the expressway | PIB's April 2023 Bharatmala factsheet identifies the cloverleaf interchange at Khalilpur village in Nuh district. This is distinct from the Alipur connection. |
| Manesar and NH-48 | KMP provides the regional east-west connection toward the Manesar side and NH-48. Estate access still depends on the relevant interchange and local approach roads. |

The Sohna road report distinguishes the 8.94-km first package, opened on 11 July 2022, from the 12.72-km Badshahpur-Sohna package, opened on 1 April 2022. It reports three-lane service roads on both sides for local traffic. [5] These roads connect Sohna with Gurugram's employment areas, while KMP links the southern industrial belt toward Manesar. They do not merge Sohna IMT into IMT Manesar or create a single planning jurisdiction.

The dedicated freight corridor and the proposed orbital rail corridor are separate transport projects. Their proximity was cited in the 2022 industrial-township report, but this record does not establish that IMT Sohna has an operational rail siding, freight terminal or passenger station. [3] No fixed journey time between the two IMTs is asserted.

## Aravalli, forest and recharge-zone restrictions

The notified plan designates 4,563 hectares of the Aravalli ranges as Natural Conservation Zone. It permits agriculture, horticulture, pisciculture, social forestry and afforestation. Regional recreational activities have a construction ceiling of 0.5% of the area and require competent-authority permission under Annexure B. The explanatory note excludes forest areas from this permissive description. The ceiling is not a general entitlement to build a house, factory or commercial resort. [1]

The plan expressly applies the Ministry of Environment and Forests notification dated 7 May 1992 for Aravalli protection. It also refers to environmental clearance under the notification dated 14 September 2006. A project's actual clearance obligations must be checked against applicable law, project category and subsequent amendments rather than inferred from a land-use colour on the plan.

The notification treats land under sections 4 and/or 5 of the Punjab Land Preservation Act, 1900, and Aravalli plantation areas as forest in view of Supreme Court orders, and states that no urbanization shall be allowed there. It records 10,553 hectares of forest within the agriculture-zone discussion and provides that further forest areas identified by the Forest Department are deemed incorporated in the plan. Forest land and NCZ are therefore not interchangeable categories. [1]

The 340-hectare water recharge zone has a stronger express construction prohibition. Annexure B, clause XXII, states: “No construction of any type shall be permitted in water recharge zone.” Proposed roads through Aravalli hills require competent-authority permission. The plan also retains the Sohna, Mohmmadpur and Ghamroj bundhs and Mehandwara drainage alignments as flood-management and recharge features. Highway proximity does not override these protections. [1]

## How to use this record

For a particular parcel, obtain the notified plan, current sector or estate layout, revenue identification and applicable amendments from DTCP and HSIIDC. Check municipal jurisdiction separately. Confirm forest classification, PLPA restrictions, NCZ and recharge-zone boundaries with the competent authorities before treating land as developable.

It is not a consolidated register of every later amendment or a parcel-level legal clearance. The notification linked below is a third-party reproduction of an official document.

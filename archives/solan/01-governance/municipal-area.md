# Solan municipal area and corporation upgrade

In its [register as at 31 March 2026](https://ud.hp.gov.in/storage/media/Municipalities%20at%20a%20glance/idt8VksfK499MixaXlK0D4jadbpoA7tCKJRqoGAw.pdf), the Urban Development Department lists Municipal Corporation Solan as entry 62. The row gives 1950 for the earlier municipality and 28 October 2020 for the upgrade. It records an area of 11.62 square kilometres and a 2011 population of 47,418.

The [corporation's history page](https://mcsolan.in/Web/history) gives the area to three decimal places, 11.624 square kilometres, and reports 17 wards. The [district administration](https://hpsolan.nic.in/municipal-corporation/) lists Baddi and Solan separately. Baddi's municipal area therefore falls outside this profile.

# Solan electoral roll revision schedule for wards 1 to 17

The [instruction of 19 March 2026](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3e2230b853516e7b05d79744fbd4c9c13/uploads/2026/03/17739926607420.pdf) directed the corporation's revising authority to revise the electoral rolls for ward numbers 1 through 17. It fixed 1 April 2026 as the qualifying date for elector eligibility.

| Step | Deadline in 2026 |
|---|---|
| File claims and objections | 28 March |
| Decide claims and objections | 2 April |
| File appeals | 10 April |
| Decide appeals | 17 April |
| Publish supplementary electoral rolls | On or before 20 April |

These dates describe the programme. The letter does not certify that publication took place.

# Solan parking auction notice for Sunny Side and the municipal office

[Short-term auction notice 870-72](https://mcsolan.in/assets/web/file/AuctionNotice_160125.pdf) invited bids at the municipal office at 2 pm on 29 January 2025. It identifies Sunny Side parking in ward 12 and the parking area behind the municipal office in ward 7.

The notice required an earnest-money demand draft payable to the commissioner before participation. It specified a two-year agreement, municipal parking fees and a seven-day deadline for the highest bidder to enter into the agreement. It does not identify a winning bidder.

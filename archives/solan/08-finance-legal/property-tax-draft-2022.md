# Solan draft property taxation bye-laws, 2022

The [corporation's notice index](https://mcsolan.in/) invited objections and suggestions before 26 March 2022. Page 1 of the [draft](https://mcsolan.in/assets/web/file/BYE_LAWs_2022_Property_taxation.pdf) defines the assessment year as 1 April through 31 March. Clause 3 directs the commissioner to keep an assessment list in Form A.

Clause 1 ties commencement to publication in the Himachal Pradesh Rajpatra (e-gazette). The invitation establishes the consultation deadline, not a commencement date or a tax rate in force.

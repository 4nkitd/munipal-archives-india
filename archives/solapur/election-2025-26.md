# Solapur municipal general election 2025-26: final seat totals

## Solapur result, row 17

The SEC's [news register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates its final party position to 16 January 2026. Row 17 records 26 wards, 102 councillor seats and 564 contesting candidates for Solapur city. All 102 seats had declared results.

| Party or grouping in the return | Seats won |
|---|---:|
| Bharatiya Janata Party | 87 |
| SEC-registered parties recognized as state parties outside Maharashtra | 8 |
| Shiv Sena | 4 |
| Indian National Congress | 2 |
| Nationalist Congress Party | 1 |
| Total declared | 102 |

Column 16 groups eight seats by party registration status; the SEC gives no Solapur-specific footnote naming a party for those seats. The columns for independents and unrecognized SEC-registered parties both show zero. The 102 results count councillors rather than separately numbered wards.

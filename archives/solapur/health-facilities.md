# Solapur municipal primary health centres and maternity homes

The municipal directory lists 9:45 a.m. to 6:15 p.m. for the following urban primary health centres (UPHCs). Localities follow the facility entries; the directory does not identify electoral ward boundaries.

## Maternity homes with similar names

The Sabale Maternity Home entry gives "Near Kasturba Water Tank" and lists 24-hour operation. The Sabale UPHC entry instead gives Hanuman Nagar and the daytime hours above. The directory also lists Ahilyabai Holkar Maternity Home at Duffrin Chowk, Darasha Maternity Home at Lashkar, Bhavanarushi Maternity Home at Ashok Chowk and Chakote Maternity Home at Jodhbhavi Peth. Each of these four maternity-home entries gives 9:45 a.m. to 6:15 p.m.

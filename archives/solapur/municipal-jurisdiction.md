# Solapur municipal area, headquarters and responsibilities

## City jurisdiction and headquarters

The corporation gives **178.57 square kilometres** as its area. Its [municipal history](https://www.solapurcorporation.gov.in/smc/history_marathi.aspx) distinguishes the establishment of the municipality in 1860 from the grant of corporation status in 1963. These dates refer to the civic body for Solapur city, not the district administration.

The profile locates the headquarters at Indrabhavan, Dr Babasaheb Ambedkar Chowk, Railway Lines, Solapur 413001, and gives the main office number as 0217-2740300.

## Municipal responsibilities

The profile assigns city planning, roads and transport infrastructure, water supply, sanitation and solid waste management to the corporation. Its published committee list includes standing, transport, city improvement, medical assistance and health, law, and women and child welfare committees. The citizen services listed alongside them include property-tax payment, birth and death registration, marriage registration and the water-supply schedule.

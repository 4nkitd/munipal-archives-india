# Municipal Corporation Sonipat city and governance profile

## Formation and jurisdiction

Haryana constituted Municipal Corporation Sonipat (MCS) on 6 July 2015. The [corporation's history](https://mcsonipat.in/about-us) traces it back to the municipal committee established in 1933 and its upgrade to a municipal council in 1993. The corporation is in Sonipat district in Haryana's part of the National Capital Region (NCR). Some state records use the spelling Sonepat.

## Wards and leadership

Sonipat has 22 wards. Haryana's Urban Local Bodies Department published the [final ward delimitation order No. 6/58/2025-4CI](https://mcsonipat.in/sites/default/files/2026-04/Final%20order%20of%20wardbandi%20of%20Municipal%20Corporation%2C%20Sonepat.pdf), dated 26 December 2025. The [10 May 2026 general election results](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2026/05/20260521432943498.pdf) confirm that all 22 wards were in use by that election. The [27 December 2020 general election](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2022/06/2022061018.pdf) used 20 wards. See the [ward record](../02-wards-geography/wards-zones-localities.md) for the official drawing and the limits of the Gazette and staffing evidence.

Rajeev Jain of the Bharatiya Janata Party won the mayoral by-election held on 2 March 2025 to serve the remainder of his predecessor's term. The [State Election Commission's notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4264](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/2025070813232246.pdf), dated 20 March 2025, spells his name Rajiv Jain. He won again at the 10 May 2026 general election; the commission notified the result on 21 May 2026 under No. SEC/1ME/2026/2368. As checked on 22 November 2026, the [municipal officers directory](https://mcsonipat.in/officers-directory) lists Rajeev Jain as mayor, Harshit Kumar, IAS, as municipal commissioner, and Meetu Dhankhar, HCS, as joint commissioner.

## NCR links to Manesar

The Kundli-Manesar-Palwal (KMP) Expressway provides a road link from the Sonipat area at Kundli to Manesar. The [Haryana Orbital Rail Corridor project](https://www.hridc.co.in/haryana-orbital-rail-corridor.php) is planned from Palwal to Sonipat via Sohna, Manesar and Kharkhoda, with a connection to the existing railway at Harsana Kalan near Sonipat. [The project company](https://www.horcl.co.in/introduction.php) says much of the rail alignment follows the KMP Expressway. The rail route is a project, not an end-to-end passenger service between Sonipat and Manesar.

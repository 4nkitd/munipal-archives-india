# Sonipat elected council: December 2020 election and March 2025 mayoral by-election

## December 2020 general election

Sonipat voted for its first directly elected Mayor and 20 ward councillors on 27 December 2020. Results were declared on 30 December. The [State Election Commission result notification](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2022/06/2022061018.pdf), No. SEC/1ME/2021/90 dated 1 January 2021, lists Nikhil Madaan of the Indian National Congress as Mayor. The BJP won 10 ward seats, Congress won nine, and an independent won one. The mayoral seat is separate from those 20 ward seats.

[Punjab Kesari reported in May 2025](https://haryana.punjabkesari.in/haryana/news/sonipat-congress-councillors-joined-bjp-senior-and-deputy-mayor-in-minority-2153587) that Nayyar and Naveen, whom it names as Naveen Tanwar, had joined the BJP. It reported that the Ward 6 councillor, whom it names as Renu Saini, had switched earlier.

## Senior Deputy Mayor and Deputy Mayor

The House elected Ward 9 councillor Rajeev Kumar Saroha as Senior Deputy Mayor and Ward 18 councillor Manjeet as Deputy Mayor on 27 February 2023. [Total TV reported](https://www.totaltv.in/after-the-post-of-mayor-the-posts-of-senior-deputy-mayor-and-deputy-mayor-are-also-in-the-account-of-congress/) that both Congress candidates received 11 votes each. The election notification lists the Ward 18 councillor as Manjeet; [coverage of the April 2025 budget meeting](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/sonipat/municipal-corporations-budget-of-rs-578-crore-passed-debate-took-place-between-officers-and-councillors-sonipat-news-c-197-1-snp1003-134913-2025-04-08) names him as Manjeet Gahlawat.

Under [sections 36 and 37C of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/02/202502111747569454.pdf), the corporation elects both deputies from among its elected members. During a mayoral vacancy the Senior Deputy Mayor acts as Mayor, or the Deputy Mayor acts in the Senior Deputy Mayor's absence. [Dainik Bhaskar reported in February 2025](https://www.bhaskar.com/local/haryana/sonipat/news/sonipat-updates-mayor-by-election-2nd-march-voting-results-nagar-nigam-nagar-palika-134415045.html) that the Senior Deputy Mayor had been given acting charge pending the by-election.

## March 2025 mayoral by-election

Madaan ceased to be Mayor effective 8 October 2024, when he was declared elected to the Haryana Legislative Assembly. The State Election Commission recorded that date in [notification No. SEC/1ME/2024/1710](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2024/12/20241204589691325.pdf), issued on 2 December 2024.

Sonipat voted on 2 March 2025 to fill the mayoral vacancy. In the [count declared on 12 March](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/rajiv-is-sonepat-mayor/), Rajeev Jain of the BJP received 57,858 votes against 23,109 for Kamal Diwan of the Indian National Congress, a margin of 34,749. The commission spells the winner's name **Rajiv Jain** in [notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4264](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/2025070813232246.pdf), dated 20 March 2025. Jain [took the oath on 25 March](https://www.dainiktribuneonline.com/news/rohtak/rajiv-jain-took-oath-as-mayor-will-assume-charge-in-sonipat-today/). The by-election filled only the Mayor's office for the remainder of the term and did not elect new councillors.

## House meetings

Section 52 of the [Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/02/202502111747569454.pdf) calls for at least one ordinary meeting each month and an additional meeting of at least three days every six months. Section 64 covers the keeping and inspection of minutes. These reports document particular meetings, not a complete calendar or a substitute for signed minutes.

## The successor House

This is a historical 20-ward roster. Sonipat voted again on 10 May 2026, this time for a Mayor and 22 ward councillors. The [21 May 2026 result notification, No. SEC/1ME/2026/2368](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2026/05/20260521432943498.pdf) records Jain's re-election as Mayor and names the successor councillors. Consult the [corporation's elected members page](https://mcsonipat.in/elected-members) for the new roster. The [notice for the 16 July 2026 House meeting](https://mcsonipat.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/General%20House%20meeting%2016.07.2026.pdf) lists the formation of ward committees for all 22 wards as an agenda item. The notice does not establish that the House approved it.

# Sonipat wards, sectors, villages and locality geography

## Ward count and the historical index

Haryana's final order No. 6/58/2025-4CI, dated 26 December 2025 and issued under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994, lists Wards 1 through 22. It refers to the preliminary notification dated 11 December 2025 and requires the boundary schedule to be read with drawing MCS/2025/SPL-1 dated 23 December 2025. The final drawing appears on PDF page 44. Page 21 forwards the order for publication in the Haryana Government Extraordinary Gazette, but this archive has not verified the issue number or publication date. The State Election Commission's results show the 22 wards were in use by 10 May 2026.

A secondary directory derived from Local Government Directory data still reports 20 wards under local body code 248529. That directory is evidence of a conflicting directory snapshot, not authority to override the final government order.

The companion [wards.json](../wards.json) records the 22 numbered wards and gives boundary landmarks with page references. These summaries do not assign entire localities to wards. Empty locality arrays and null zones and divisions mean unverified, not absent. The separate [historical 20-ward index](../wards-legacy-20.json) belongs with the December 2020 election era. No crosswalk was verified: a ward number in that index cannot be used to identify the same territory in the current schedule. [Officer assignments](../03-officers-directory/officers-and-helplines.md#sanitation-responsibility) are not established for the 22-ward layout.

## Localities and road corridors

Sonipat's locality names describe different kinds of geography. Old Sonipat refers broadly to the older built-up city and its bazaar and mohalla network, not a single electoral ward. Model Town is a locality name. Atlas Road is an address and transport corridor through the city. None is a substitute for a dated ward map.

| Area or corridor | Verified reference and use |
| --- | --- |
| Old city and Murthal Adda | The final Ward 1 boundary names Murthal Adda, Jain Mandir Gali, Lal Darwaza and Kami Road. These are boundary landmarks, not a declaration that all Old Sonipat belongs to one ward. |
| Model Town and Atlas Road | A bank's branch directory gives Plot 215, Atlas Road, Model Town, Sonipat. UHBVN separately lists its Model Town subdivision office at Kothi 181-D, Sector 14 main market. Utility service areas are not electoral wards. |
| Murthal Road | The final schedule uses Murthal Road, Aggarsain Chowk and the Sector 14/15 dividing road in Wards 3 and 4. The outer corridor also appears in Wards 5 and 6. |
| Bahalgarh Road and Delhi Road | The final schedule uses these names for stretches of the southeast approach, including Vivekanand Chowk, Maharana Pratap Chowk, ECE Factory and the Fazilpur approach. A road name does not prove that the whole village of Bahalgarh lies inside a particular ward. |
| Gohana, Mehlana and Kakroi roads | These roads, Sector 23 roads and the railway appear repeatedly in the western ward boundaries. The final Wards 21 and 22 must also be consulted for this side of the city. |

## HSVP sectors and boundary references

Sector numbers are planning identifiers. They are not municipal ward numbers, and a dividing road or outer road may mark a boundary without putting an entire sector inside that ward. The following references concern the 2025 final 22-ward schedule only.

| Sector | Evidence in the official schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Sector 12 | Ward 4 cites house 1234, Sector 12, and the outer road. Ward 6 cites the Sector 12/15 outer road and the Fazilpur approach. |
| Sector 14 | Wards 3 and 4 use the Sector 14/15 dividing road. The Civil Hospital and Bharat Hospital lane are additional Ward 4 landmarks. |
| Sector 15 | Ward 4 names houses 64 and 1170 and the sector's outer road. Ward 6 shares several of these boundary references. |
| Sectors 8 and 19 | Wards 6 and 7 refer to the Sector 8/19 turn or T-point on GT Road and the road toward Garh Shahjanpur. |
| Sector 10 | Ward 7 names the road from Garh Shahjanpur and Sector 10 toward GT Road. |
| Sector 23 | Ward 19 describes Sector 23 roads, market road, chowki road, Tirth Chowk and the outer road. Wards 20 and 21 use adjoining roads. This is not an exclusive whole-sector assignment. |

Housing Board Haryana independently lists housing estates in Sectors 15 and 23, with totals of 1,358 and 690 dwellings respectively. These estate records confirm housing geography, not electoral boundaries or present occupancy. Confirm whether an individual issue belongs to HSVP, Housing Board or the corporation using the property's scheme and transfer records.

## Villages, revenue estates and merger history

The 2011 District Census Handbook distinguishes municipal merger from census outgrowth status. It records Jamalpur Kalan, Sultanpur, Ahmedpur and Sonipat Rural as fully merged in Sonipat municipality. It describes Lahrara, Shadipur, Kabirpur and Bandepur as outgrowths in its jurisdictional history. These are historical census classifications, not a complete list of villages incorporated into the corporation constituted on 6 July 2015.

The 2025 final ward schedule supplies more recent revenue-estate references. Ward 1 uses Devru revenue boundaries. Wards 6 and 7 name Fazilpur, Garh Shahjanpur and the Kumaspur revenue boundary. Ward 17 reaches the Lahrara municipal edge. Ward 22 refers to development in Tharru revenue estate and the Sonipat-Jind and Delhi-Ambala railways. A village mentioned on a perimeter can be partly inside, adjacent to, or used to define the corporation boundary. Read the full schedule and drawing before assigning its entire revenue estate.

A June 2015 report about 28 villages opposing proposed inclusion along GT Road between Murthal and Rai records a proposal and objections. It does not establish that all 28 were ultimately merged. This archive does not turn that proposed list into a confirmed annexation register.

## Regularized colonies and address aliases

Haryana ULB publishes both a regularized-colony notification index and a civic-amenities register with municipality, revenue estate, colony ID, layout drawing, acreage and regularized khasra numbers. These records should establish the exact notified portion of a colony. Ward inclusion, a property ID, a tax payment or a road tender alone does not establish regularization.

Amar Ujala reported on 6 February 2024 that 21 colonies in Sonipat corporation had been declared regularized after a survey of 63 colonies. A separate statewide report gives 41 colonies for Sonipat district. Those counts have different geographic scopes and must not be combined or substituted for each other. Colony names and ward allocations are not supplied here as confirmed regularized entries because the corresponding notification schedules and layouts were not verified.

For address searches, retain historical names. A 28 May 2025 report says Khan Colony, adjoining Sector 12, was renamed Swatantra Nagar and Madina Masjid Colony near Patel Nagar was renamed Shanti Vihar following a municipal house resolution on 6 May. This is a reported name change, not evidence of regularization or a verified ward reassignment.

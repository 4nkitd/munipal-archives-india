# Municipal Corporation Sonipat officers and helplines

## Office and citizen contact

The municipal office is at Municipal Corporation, Sonipat, Railway Road, Opp. PwD Rest House, Sonipat. The official [contact page](https://mcsonipat.in/contact-us) publishes telephone **0130-2243032** and email **cmc-sonipat@ulbharyana.gov.in**. These are office contacts, not direct personal numbers for the Commissioner.

The [officers directory](https://mcsonipat.in/officers-directory) lists Sh. Alok, Clerk, Complaint Branch, at **9050573629**. The corporation links to the [Haryana ULB public grievance portal](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/). No separate dedicated municipal toll-free helpline or service hours were established from these sources.

The directory and contact pages display a site footer last-updated date of 16 November 2026. This record uses that date as page context, not as an appointment or transfer date. Contacts were checked against the published pages on 22 November 2026; telephone connectivity was not tested.

## GRAP directions applicable to Sonipat

CAQM issued [Direction F. No. A-110018/01/2021-CAQM dated 29 September 2026](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/Advisories%20and%20Direction/Directions/fa4070ca-5b45-4161-b220-0871227ae9ea.pdf) under section 12 of the CAQM Act, 2021. It issues the revised GRAP schedule with immediate effect. Individual stage measures apply when that stage is invoked through an appropriate order. The distribution includes the Deputy Commissioner, Sonepat, and Commissioner, Municipal Corporation Sonepat.

| Stage | Delhi AQI band in the schedule | Sonipat relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 201 to 300, Poor | NCR-wide actions relevant to municipal dust, waste and transport enforcement. |
| II | 301 to 400, Very Poor | Additional measures apply upon invocation. |
| III | 401 to 450, Severe | Restrictions on specified dust-generating construction and demolition activities across NCR. Sonipat is expressly included among districts subject to BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel or below-standard four-wheeler LMV restrictions. |
| IV | Above 450, Severe+ | Additional restrictions operate with earlier-stage measures. Territorial wording must be checked because some measures concern entry into Delhi. |

The [standalone revised schedule](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Schedule/01fcdebf-83e1-4cfe-bc0c-d0b1470a4d4e.pdf) permits forecast-based advance invocation and makes higher-stage actions cumulative. Stage III includes an exception for specifically adapted vehicles used personally by persons with disabilities. It allows pothole repairs and identifies exempt project categories subject to pollution-control compliance.

A separate [CAQM precautionary order](https://caqm.nic.in/FileUploadDomain/WebsiteDocument/GRAP/GRAP%20Orders/22e7a559-e78f-49b0-b3f6-3f4e93cfa80f.pdf) specifies measures for **5 to 20 November 2026**, including outdoor construction restrictions and office attendance measures covering Sonipat. That stated period has ended by this record's verification date. Neither that order nor the standing schedule proves which GRAP stage is currently active. Check subsequent CAQM invocation and revocation orders before treating a restriction as operative.

## Regularized-colony notifications

The [DULB regularized-colony index](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) publishes notifications and consolidated lists. Its title refers to 2023 and 2024, but the index also contains 2025 material. For example, it lists notification batches dated 25 July 2023, 6 October 2023, 15 December 2023, 6 March 2024, 14 November 2024, 18 March 2025 and 22 May 2025. Several were uploaded on 26 September 2025. Upload dates must not replace notification dates.

The official [Municipalities Status of 836 Colonies Data Notified 2025](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%2F%2F012fa553-5f2b-4575-866d-034a892c23ac.pdf), listed on 13 October 2025, includes the following Sonipat entries. Use the [civic amenities and infrastructure deficient areas register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure) to match municipality, colony drawing number, revenue estate, notified khasra numbers and layout plan. A colony name alone does not establish that every adjoining plot lies inside the notified boundary. Do not include Gohana, Gannaur, Kundli or Kharkhoda entries merely because they are in Sonipat district.

## Town-planning notification register

The [Sonipat town-planning notifications page](https://mcsonipat.in/town-planning/notifications) links amendments to the Haryana Municipal and Municipal Corporation Communication and Connectivity Infrastructure Bye Laws, 2013, construction-time extensions for Improvement Trust plots, the illegal plot-subdivision policy and government-land data instructions.

The linked [subdivision amendment](https://mcsonipat.in/sites/default/files/2026-04/Policy%20for%20regularization%20of%20illegal%20of%20Sub-division%20plots..pdf) is notification **9/60/2022-4CII dated 3 January 2023**, amending policy **9/32/2022-4C-II dated 22 July 2022**. Its 2026 upload folder is not its legal issue date. This plot-subdivision policy is distinct from a notification declaring an entire colony eligible for civic amenities.

# Sonipat property tax, NDC, building code and Lal Dora references

## Property tax sources and date context

Sonipat Municipal Corporation directs property owners to the Haryana [Property Tax Dues Payment & No Dues Certificate Management System](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). The live portal links a tax calculator expressly described as following the **new policy dated 30 July 2026**. That policy date is the frontmatter date for this multi-source reference, not the issue date of every document below.

| Official reference | Date context | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Property tax notification 2026 Corporation](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%5Ca320f610-b161-4530-8f69-d823dd21ad66.pdf) | Published on the DULB index 31 July 2026 | Corporation-specific notification to consult before applying current rates or exemptions. |
| [Tax calculator](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/taxScreen.html) | Portal labels policy date 30 July 2026 | Calculate using the applicable property details and policy. |
| [Property-tax instructions index](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/PropertyTax) | Contains notices through July 2026 | Earlier notifications, SOPs, self-certification and Lal Dora documentation. |
| [Property Tax TriFold Brochure Haryana](https://mcsonipat.in/sites/default/files/2026-08/Property%20Tax%20TriFold%20Brochure%20Haryana.pdf) | Sonipat portal publication 26 August 2026 | Municipal-hosted explanatory reference. |

This record does not reproduce unverified 2026 rate slabs. Do not apply a historical rate table or a councils-and-committees notification to Sonipat merely because it appears beside the corporation notification.

The DULB index lists a one-time interest-waiver notification for arrears from **2010-11 to 2024-25**, published 18 May 2026. Sonipat's homepage separately lists an ongoing **75% waiver of arrear interest** order on 31 August 2026. These headings do not establish a permanent exemption or a waiver of principal. Read the attachments for payment windows, conditions and any later extension.

## Property ID, corrections and NDC

The official [portal FAQ](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/FAQ) describes municipality selection as mandatory for a property search. Select Sonipat and search using an eight-character Property ID, old ID, plot number, colony, owner or occupier name, mobile number, or the GIS map.

After opening "View Details", self-certify only correct data. Where information is wrong, select "No", use "Update Details", attach supporting documents and save the request. The municipality addresses the objection. The FAQ then instructs the applicant to verify details after disposal.

For all dues, use "Make Payment/Generate NDC", search the PID, select "Pay All Dues and Generate NDC", verify the assessment and proceed through OTP and payment. The FAQ identifies property and fire tax, development charges and garbage collection charges as dues handled by the system. It also permits separate payment of property tax or garbage charges. Paying one component must not be confused with clearing every component needed for an NDC.

| PID service | Published FAQ information |
| --- | --- |
| Normal new PID request | No charge; 10 working days. |
| Priority request | 2 working days from successful receipt of payment. |
| Priority fee | English FAQ states INR 1,000; Hindi FAQ states INR 2,500. This is an unresolved official-page inconsistency. Confirm the current fee before payment. |
| Split PID | Horizontal plot subdivision or vertical floor subdivision, using the parent PID and supporting documents. |

The [document checklist](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments) specifies identity and ownership evidence, and additional papers for particular changes. A PID split is a database service, not evidence that a planning subdivision or construction has been approved.

## Haryana Building Code 2017 and subdivision controls

The [DULB town-planning notifications register](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/TownPlanningNotifications) lists "Final Haryana Building Code 2017 with letter" and adoption of amendments. Some older rows have no downloadable document. The displayed 4 January 2021 listing date is not proof of the code's original notification date.

Sonipat's own [subdivision policy attachment](https://mcsonipat.in/sites/default/files/2026-04/Policy%20for%20regularization%20of%20illegal%20of%20Sub-division%20plots..pdf) provides a direct verified reference to the code. Notification **9/60/2022-4CII dated 3 January 2023** amends the policy issued through **9/32/2022-4C-II dated 22 July 2022**, concerning residential plots in Town Planning, Rehabilitation and Improvement Trust schemes in municipal areas.

| Amended provision | Verified requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Clause 4(b) | Minimum original plot size eligible for regularization and new subdivision is 100 square yards and above. |
| Clause 4(c) | Resulting subdivided plot must not be less than 50 square yards. |
| Clause 5(i) | Eligibility threshold changes from 200 to 100 square yards. |
| Clause 5(ii) | Application goes to the competent authority, the Commissioner for a Municipal Corporation. |
| Clause 5iii(vi) | Building plan must comply with Haryana Building Code 2017; the front setback of the subdivided plot is as per the original plot. |

These thresholds are not a universal permission to divide every plot in Sonipat. The underlying scheme, policy conditions and subsequent amendments still require examination. Sonipat links [subdivision applications](https://subdivision.ulbharyana.gov.in/), [building-plan application status](https://online.ulbharyana.gov.in/OnlineServices/BuildingPlanApplication) and [CLU permission within controlled areas](https://ulbcluhry.org/) as separate services. Tax payment and an NDC do not replace these approvals.

## Lal Dora and Abadi Deh references

The [notification dated 16 March 2018, No. 14/10/2017-3C-II](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2F95536765-717c-4552-b197-100c1d22afae.pdf) specifically distinguishes Sonipat. It provides a **100% rebate until 31 March 2019 for Municipal Corporation Sonepat**, compared with 31 March 2018 for other corporations, for residential properties within the Lal Dora of villages brought into corporation limits on or after **1 April 2010**. This is a historical, time-limited provision, not evidence of a current blanket tax exemption.

The DULB property-tax index also lists:

| Publication date | Reference |
| --- | --- |
| 2024-03-15 | [Property Certificate to occupiers of properties falling under Abadi Deh/Lal Dora of Municipal Areas](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/AdminDashboard/ViewUploadFile?fileName=%5CCONTENTFILES%20%2F%2Feb519b93-6e15-485c-8707-59ad443c6fc9.pdf). |
| 2023-10-28 | Instructions on anomalies involving development charges, agricultural properties, colony masters and areas under Lal Dora. |
| 2023-09-26 | Property Tax Management System SOP and separate split-ID SOP. |

The [Lal Dora possession-certificate notice search](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/PropertyList/SearchProperty) is separate from the general tax login. The current document checklist asks for additional Lal Dora evidence according to service and circumstances, including a registry or sale deed, a court decree registered with revenue authorities, or a competent revenue authority or civil court legal-heir certificate in death cases.

No Sonipat village-wise Lal Dora PID schedule or universal building-plan exemption was verified for this record. Check the property's revenue boundary, service-specific evidence and applicable planning provisions. A historical tax rebate, possession-certificate process, PID and permission to build are different matters.

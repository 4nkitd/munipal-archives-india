# Sonipat citizen services, RTI and Right to Service

## Scope and municipal contact

This guide records links published by Municipal Corporation Sonipat and the Department of Urban Local Bodies Haryana. The record date is the compilation date, not a service notification date. The state Right to Service page identifies its consolidated schedule as updated through 4 November 2025, with notifications dated 16 August 2022 and 16 November 2022 and amendments dated 11 March 2025 and 27 October 2025.

The municipal office is at Railway Road, opposite PWD Rest House, Sonipat. Its website publishes telephone **0130-2243032** and email **cmc-sonipat@ulbharyana.gov.in**. Ask the relevant branch for current document requirements and charges before submitting an application. Portal links were checked as published service routes; no authenticated application, payment or certificate download was performed for this archive.

## Property tax and property records

The corporation directs property-tax users to [Haryana's property portal](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/). Use the Sonipat municipal entry and the relevant property record to check details and pay property tax. Retain the property ID, demand details and payment receipt for later enquiries.

The state RTS schedule routes changes of owner or occupier, property-data objections and new property IDs to the same portal. A tax payment and a correction request are different transactions. If the owner, area, floor details, property category or dues are disputed, retain the objection acknowledgement as well as any payment receipt.

The notified municipal-corporation officer for these property services is the Zonal Taxation Officer. Owner-change cases go first to the Joint Commissioner for grievance redressal and then to the Commissioner. For new property IDs and the listed No Dues Certificate portal objections, the first authority is the Deputy Municipal Commissioner and the second is the Commissioner. These are designated posts from the schedule, not an assertion about an individual officer's current charge.

No current tax rebate, interest-waiver expiry date or tax rate is inferred from an undated portal link. Check the applicable notification and assessment before payment.

The [district birth/death service page](https://sonipat.gov.in/service/apply-for-death-certificate/) states that births must be reported and registered within 21 days at the place of occurrence. That reporting period is not a promise to issue a certificate within 21 days. Late registration and corrections require the applicable procedure; this guide does not substitute a generic checklist for those cases.

The reviewed municipal RTS schedule lists dog-certificate issue and renewal deadlines below. It does not provide a birth, death or marriage certificate deadline in the extracted municipal entries, so none is assigned here. Confirm the selected service's current documents, fee and notified deadline in SARAL or the marriage portal.

RTS service redressal is separate from an RTI appeal. The 15-working-day copy-of-documents service is not the statutory RTI response period.

## RTI Act 2005 and appeals

The municipal [Acts page](https://mcsonipat.in/acts) links an RTI Act document. Under the [Right to Information Act, 2005, Act 22 of 2005](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2065?locale=en), an Indian citizen can request existing information held by a public authority. For municipal records, address the request to the State Public Information Officer, Municipal Corporation Sonipat, at the municipal office address. Confirm the current designated SPIO and first appellate authority with the corporation; the sources reviewed do not establish a current named municipal SPIO.

Specify the records and period sought, such as an assessment entry, sanctioned work estimate, payment record or complaint action-taken report. Section 6 permits requests in English, Hindi or the official language of the area and does not require reasons for seeking information. Applicable Haryana rules govern fees and payment methods. This guide does not assume the Central Government's application-fee amount applies to Haryana.

| RTI stage | Statutory rule |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary decision on request | Within 30 days of receipt under section 7(1), subject to statutory qualifications |
| Information concerning life or liberty | Within 48 hours under section 7(1) |
| Transfer to another public authority | As soon as practicable, no later than 5 days under section 6(3) |
| Request received through an Assistant PIO | 5 days added when computing the response period under section 5(2) |
| First appeal | Within 30 days of the decision or expiry of the response period, under section 19(1) |
| Disposal of first appeal | 30 days, extendable to a total of 45 days with written reasons, under section 19(6) |
| Second appeal | Within 90 days to the State Information Commission, under section 19(3) |

Section 7(6) provides information free of charge when the authority fails to comply with the specified time limits. Statutory exemptions, third-party procedures and fee-intimation rules can affect a particular request. The municipal footer's "RTI Portal" link points to `#dummy` on the reviewed Acts page, so it is not treated as a functioning online filing route.

## Grievances and Samadhan camps

MC Sonipat links [ULB grievance registration](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) from its citizen-services menu. For sanitation, street-light or infrastructure complaints, give the precise location, ward if known, photographs where useful and any earlier complaint reference. Preserve the acknowledgement. The state ULB site also publishes **1800-8900-929** for IT support and grievance registration, available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Samadhan Shivir is a separate public-grievance hearing channel. Haryana's Chief Secretary Office lists an advisory dated **18 July 2024**, number **5/47/2024-3MC(SP)**, requiring district and subdivision camps to be conducted and designated officers to attend. This establishes the programme, not a current Sonipat municipal camp timetable.

Contemporary reporting on the state municipal rollout says camps started on **22 October 2024**, with officers available from **9 AM to 11 AM**, including for property-ID issues. That is a historical rollout schedule, not a verified standing appointment schedule for November 2026. Confirm the current Sonipat venue, days and hours with the municipal office or district administration before travelling. Take the application or complaint reference, a written account and copies of relevant records, and ask for a receipt or tracking reference.

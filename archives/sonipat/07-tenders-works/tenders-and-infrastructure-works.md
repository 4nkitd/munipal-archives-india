# Sonipat tenders and infrastructure works, 2024 to 2026

## Procurement portals and interpretation

The [Municipal Corporation Sonipat tender register](https://mcsonipat.in/tenders) publishes work titles, tender amounts, submission dates and linked notices. The [Haryana Engineering Works Portal live-tender page](https://works.haryana.gov.in/HEWP_Login/LiveTender.aspx) states that HEWP tenders are published on the NIC Haryana eTendering website for bidder participation. HEWP says these tenders appear in its live-tender list on the day after publication on NIC. Search the [NIC portal](https://etenders.hry.nic.in/nicgep/app) using the exact tender ID and the organisation chain Haryana Government, Urban Local Bodies, MC Sonepat. The portal uses the spelling "Sonepat".

Amounts below are advertised tender values, not expenditure or certified final bills. A submission deadline, an "Open" or "Closed" label, and even an award do not establish physical completion. Check corrigenda, the award of contract, work order and completion certificate separately. The MC's [development-works page](https://mcsonipat.in/engineering/development-works) contains reporting formats and civil-estimate guidance, rather than a completed-project register.

## JBM Murthal waste-to-energy plant and the regional cluster

The Murthal facility is a shared Sonipat-Panipat cluster installation, not a Sonipat-only plant. The [HSPCB action-taken report dated 2 May 2024 in NGT OA 576/2023](https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/Action%20Taken%20Report%20by%20HSPCB%20in%20OA%20No.%20576%20of%202023%20%28Rajesh%20Kumar%20Vs%20State%20of%20Haryana%20%29.pdf) identifies its operator as JBM Environment Management Pvt. Ltd. It places the inspected facility at village Tajpur, Sonipat. Attached permissions identify the address near the 132 kV HVPNL Tajpur substation on Tajpur-Murthal Road, Murthal village. The report states that it receives municipal solid waste from Sonipat, Panipat, Samalkha and Ganaur.

HSPCB inspected the facility on 15 March 2024. Its report describes 10.0 MW electricity generation. The attached SWM authorization dated 17 October 2023 records 750 tonnes/day of authorized waste processing and 10 MW under waste-to-energy technology. That authorization ran only to 30 September 2025. This historical document does not establish renewal or consent validity in 2026.

Capacity descriptions differ across official documents. Haryana's [March 2025 river-rejuvenation progress report](https://nmcg.nic.in/writereaddata/fileupload/ngtmpr/9_Haryana%20-%20MPR%20March%202025.pdf) describes the Sonipat-Panipat plant as 700 tonnes/day, commissioned on 16 August 2021, with approximately 6.77 MW being generated. The attached older environmental clearance in the NGT filing describes a proposed 8 MW plant. These are source-specific authorization, progress-report and design figures. They should not be merged into a single current measured capacity.

## Treated-water reuse and environmental works

The same [2 May 2024 HSPCB report](https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/Action%20Taken%20Report%20by%20HSPCB%20in%20OA%20No.%20576%20of%202023%20%28Rajesh%20Kumar%20Vs%20State%20of%20Haryana%20%29.pdf) states that MC Sonipat supplied 90 to 100 kilolitres/day of treated effluent from the 30 MLD Rathdhana sewage-treatment plant for industrial use at the waste-to-energy facility. Four tankers transported it, with transfer logbooks maintained at both sites. The report identifies cooling-tower makeup and boiler use, while treated leachate was used for ash quenching and green-belt maintenance.

HSPCB reported that boiler-stack samples and leachate-treatment effluent samples met prescribed limits in analysis reports dated 19 and 21 March 2024 respectively. Soil samples from three locations were reported uncontaminated in reports dated 3 April 2024. These findings apply to those samples and dates, not continuous compliance.

The report also describes JBM biomining legacy waste near Gohana bypass in Sonipat and near Nimiri village in Panipat district, with completion then expected by 31 December 2024. That was a forecast. No completion certificate is established here. The complaint's allegation of an accumulated waste quantity is not treated as a verified stock measurement.

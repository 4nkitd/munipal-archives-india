# Sonipat municipal budgets and civic finance, FY 2024-25 to FY 2026-27

## Coverage and source status

This record covers the three financial years from 1 April 2024 through 31 March 2027. The frontmatter date is a year-only placeholder for the latest budget year, not a verified council approval date. The official Municipal Corporation Sonipat budget page lists "Budget FY 2026-27" and links the nine-page file [Budget 2026-27-1 (1).pdf](https://mcsonipat.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/Budget%202026-27-1%20%281%29.pdf).

## FY 2024-25 reported municipal allocations

A [report published on 18 February 2024](https://jantaserishta.com/local/haryana/mc-passes-rs-550-crore-budget-for-2024-25-3122385) records unanimous passage of a Rs 550 crore budget, compared with Rs 516 crore in the preceding year. Mayor Nikhil Madan chaired the meeting. Municipal Commissioner Vishram Kumar Meena and Joint Commissioner Ankita Verma attended.

| Reported expenditure provision | Rs crore |
| --- | ---: |
| Development works, overall reported allocation | 299 |
| Roads and streets | 120 |
| Sewerage and drinking water | 47 |
| Residential parks | 26 |
| CCTV, streetlights and traffic lights | 28 |
| Entrance beautification | 10 |
| Village pond cleaning and rejuvenation | 10 |

These are selected reported heads, not an independently reconciled account. Do not add them to the overall development allocation as separate expenditure.

The report quotes a Rs 310.39 crore receipts target. Named sources include property tax Rs 40 crore, stamp duty Rs 40 crore, electricity-related municipal tax Rs 14 crore, fire tax Rs 2 crore, municipal rents Rs 2.40 crore, water and sewerage bills Rs 5 crore and development charges Rs 20 crore. It also names central grants of Rs 18 crore and state grants of Rs 77 crore. Although the article calls receipts "own sources", grants are transfers, not own-source tax revenue. The signed budget is needed to resolve that classification.

## FY 2025-26 approval and estimates

[Amar Ujala, 8 April 2025](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/sonipat/municipal-corporations-budget-of-rs-578-crore-passed-debate-took-place-between-officers-and-councillors-sonipat-news-c-197-1-snp1003-134913-2025-04-08), reports that the house approved Rs 578 crore on 7 April. An initial Rs 564 crore proposal increased after additional development suggestions. Mayor Rajiv Jain and Commissioner Harshit Kumar presented the budget.

The report gives receipts of Rs 345,67,93,000, an opening balance of about Rs 311 crore and an estimated closing balance of Rs 68.36 crore at 31 March 2026. It quotes government grants of Rs 95 crore, property sales of Rs 100 crore, salary and allowances expenditure of Rs 62 crore, advertising receipts of Rs 2.50 crore, trade licence fees of Rs 1 crore and Swachh Bharat Mission receipts of Rs 20 crore.

The report's rounded balance figures and final house total should not be treated as a reconciled cash-flow statement. Property sales are not recurring tax income; opening balances and fixed deposits are not new annual revenue. Obtain the approved head-wise schedules and revisions before comparing spending capacity across years.

## Separate SMDA budgets and civic accountability

The Sonipat Metropolitan Development Authority is a separate statutory body. Its budget must not be relabelled as the Municipal Corporation budget or added to it without checking project overlaps and inter-agency transfers.

| Authority | Financial year | Reported budget | Source |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| SMDA | 2024-25 | Rs 363 crore | [The Tribune, 12 July 2024](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/sonepat-authority-approves-rs-363-cr-budget-for-2024-25-638956/) |
| SMDA | 2025-26 | Rs 349.8 crore | [Dainik Bhaskar, 9 June 2025](https://www.bhaskar.com/local/haryana/sonipat/gohana/news/sonipat-municipal-corporationbudget-approved-349-crore-development-plan-135201385.html) |

Section 39 of the [Sonipat Metropolitan Development Authority Act, 2023](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/haryana/2023/ActNo-28of2023-Haryana.pdf) requires the CEO to submit the next financial year's estimated receipts and disbursements. The authority approves the budget, forwards it to the state government and publishes it on its website after laying before the state legislature. Section 18 separately addresses the annual infrastructure action plan and funding estimates.

For municipal scrutiny, request the approved original and revised budgets, house resolution, head-wise actual receipts and expenditure, grant utilization certificates, outstanding liabilities and audit observations from Municipal Corporation Sonipat. Use the [official budget page](https://mcsonipat.in/budget) as the document entry point. Neither an election manifesto nor a regional project announcement proves municipal budget sanction or payment.

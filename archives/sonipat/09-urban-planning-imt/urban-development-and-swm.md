# Sonipat metropolitan planning, industrial hubs, orbital connectivity and waste services

## SMDA and municipal jurisdiction

The Sonipat Metropolitan Development Authority Act, 2023 is Haryana Act No. 28 of 2023. The Governor assented on 20 September 2023; notification No. Leg. 30/2023 published it on 3 October 2023. The [Act text hosted by PRS](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/haryana/2023/ActNo-28of2023-Haryana.pdf) provides for coordinated metropolitan infrastructure, urban amenities, mobility and environmental management. Sections 3 and 4 require state notifications for the metropolitan area and establishment of the authority. The Act alone does not prove the boundary of every notified area.

The [SMDA portal](https://smda.org.in/) identifies the Chief Minister as chairman and describes a 17-member authority. It publishes infrastructure-planning material and a grievance contact, 0130-4053761. SMDA does not replace every municipal or industrial-estate function.

| Agency | Relevant responsibility or record |
| --- | --- |
| Municipal Corporation Sonipat | Municipal budgets, local civic services, sanitation complaints and municipal works |
| SMDA | Metropolitan infrastructure planning and coordinated projects under its Act |
| HSIIDC | Industrial estates, industrial plot allotment and estate infrastructure |
| HRIDC and HORCL | Haryana Orbital Rail Corridor implementation and project records |
| HSPCB | Pollution-control consents, compliance and industrial-effluent oversight |

Kundli, Rai and Kharkhoda are regional industrial locations in Sonipat district. Inclusion in this record does not place every estate or project inside Municipal Corporation Sonipat. Confirm the parcel's municipality, estate boundaries and responsible asset-owning agency before applying for permissions.

## IMT Kharkhoda and the Maruti Suzuki plant

Maruti Suzuki's [13 May 2022 release](https://www.marutisuzuki.com/corporate/media/press-releases/2022/may/maruti-suzuki-finalises-land-site-for-new-manufacturing-plant-in-haryana) records completion of allotment of an 800-acre site at IMT Kharkhoda, Sonipat district, through HSIIDC. It described first-phase investment of over Rs 11,000 crore and planned initial annual capacity of 250,000 vehicles. These were announcement-stage figures.

The company's [25 February 2025 release](https://www.marutisuzuki.com/corporate/media/press-releases/2025/february/maruti-suzuki-begins-commercial-production-at-kharkhoda) confirms commercial production began that day, initially with annual capacity of 250,000 vehicles and the Brezza model.

A later [company release dated 2 July 2026](https://www.marutisuzuki.com/corporate/media/press-releases/2026/july/maruti-suzuki-s-kharkhoda-vehicle-manufacturing-facility-inaugurated-by-hon-ble-prime-minister-shri-narendra-modi-and-prime-minister-of-japan-h-e-ms-sanae-takaichi) states current capacity of 0.5 million vehicles annually, an integrated supplier park and planned scale-up to 1 million vehicles with total investment of Rs 35,000 crore. The future capacity is a plan, not the initial commissioned output.

The [Haryana RERA project filing](https://haryanarera.gov.in/view_project/project_preview_open/1827), submitted on 29 December 2021, identifies HSIIDC as promoter of Industrial Model Township Kharkhoda and lists project land of 3,217.19 acres. This is the filed township project area, not the 800-acre Maruti allotment, and should not be assumed to describe every later expansion.

## Kundli and Rai industrial hubs

HSIIDC's [auction portal](https://hsiidc.bidx.in/app) lists industrial plots at IE Kundli, IE Rai, Rai Phase II Mega Food Park and Rai TP. These estate names distinguish the established Kundli and Rai hubs from the automobile-led IMT Kharkhoda development. Auction notices demonstrate an allotment process, not that every listed plot is available, sold or occupied.

The [Sonipat district HSIIDC directory](https://sonipat.gov.in/department/hsidc/) lists the Kundli estate office and the email estatekundli@hsiidc.org.in. Use the current HSIIDC directory for estate-management questions and the specific auction notice for deadlines, reserve prices and conditions. Old directory numbers should be reconfirmed before relying on them.

Industrial-estate infrastructure and municipal services can meet at boundary roads, storm drains and outfalls. A plot allotment or building approval does not substitute for pollution-control permission or lawful effluent disposal. The distinction matters particularly for the Kundli and Nathupur industrial cluster, where official inter-agency minutes identify unresolved sewer connectivity and treatment-site issues.

## KMP Expressway links Sonipat district to Manesar by road

The Kundli-Manesar-Palwal Expressway, also called the Western Peripheral Expressway, provides the road connection around Delhi between the Kundli side of Sonipat district and Manesar. [PIB records the Kundli-Manesar section's inauguration on 19 November 2018](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1553220). The [associated address](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1553231&lang=2&reg=48) describes an approximately 83 km Kundli-Manesar section and about 135 km for the complete KMP.

The [52.33 km Manesar-Palwal section opened on 5 April 2016](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=138596). These are historical opening records, not a live inspection of road conditions.

For industrial movement, the KMP gives the Sonipat-Kundli-Kharkhoda region access toward the Manesar industrial area without a route through central Delhi. Local approach roads and designated interchanges still matter. The main expressway is not a gate-to-gate connection between every factory, and this record does not establish current tolls, truck restrictions or travel time.

## Haryana Orbital Rail Corridor and the planned Manesar connection

The [HRIDC October 2025 equity-participation memorandum](https://www.hridc.co.in/pdf/HORC%20EOI%2006102025%20%282%29.pdf) describes HORC as an approximately 126 km electrified broad-gauge corridor from Palwal to Sonipat via Sohna, Manesar and Kharkhoda, designed for freight and passenger trains. The project received CCEA approval on 15 September 2020 at an estimated Rs 5,618 crore. Much of its alignment follows the KMP corridor.

The Sonipat end connects toward the Delhi-Ambala railway through New Harsana Kalan. Planned junction links include Patli, Sultanpur and Asaudah, with Dedicated Freight Corridor connections at New Tauru and New Prithala. This creates a planned direct orbital rail connection between the Sonipat-Kharkhoda region and Manesar, rather than a route through Delhi.

| Section or milestone | Status in the October 2025 HRIDC memorandum |
| --- | --- |
| Patli-Manesar-MSIL freight connectivity | Commissioned on 17 June 2025 |
| Maruti Manesar cargo terminal | Four lines and two loading platforms; reported cost Rs 162 crore |
| Mandothi-Kharkhoda | Target commissioning December 2028 |
| Kharkhoda-New Harsana Kalan | Target commissioning April 2029 |

Those target dates are the memorandum's schedule, not confirmation that works have since met it. The operational Manesar terminal does not establish an operational through rail route from Sonipat to Manesar. No end-to-end passenger service or fully commissioned Sonipat section was verified for this record. Older HORCL pages give different route lengths and schedules; the dated 2025 memorandum is used here rather than combining incompatible versions.

## Drainage, sewage and solid waste

Reports of SMDA's meeting on 9 June 2025 distinguish its Rs 349.8 crore FY 2025-26 budget from the municipal budget. [Jagran's account](https://www.jagran.com/haryana/sonipat-ncr-sonipat-news-smda-approves-development-projects-in-sonipat-23960349.html) lists approvals, not completed assets:

| Approved proposal | Reported estimate |
| --- | ---: |
| Expand 3 MLD STP behind DCRUST to 7.5 MLD | Rs 15.75 crore |
| Bega-Ganaur 10 MLD Ranney-well water project | Rs 35.50 crore |
| Sector 4 Waste to Wonder theme park | Rs 26.38 crore |
| Kundli stormwater drainage toward Drain No. 6 | Rs 28.15 crore |
| Kharkhoda stormwater drains | Rs 42.37 crore |
| Two new Ranney wells for Sonipat | Rs 47.54 crore |

The proposed park's use of scrap is not evidence of municipal waste-processing capacity. Sewage treatment, stormwater drainage and solid waste require separate performance records.

[HSPCB minutes of the 9 July 2026 Yamuna Action Plan review](https://nmcg.nic.in/writereaddata/fileupload/rrcgb/28_Haryana%20-%2009.07.2026.pdf), circulated on 6 August 2026, state that a proposed Sonipat STP was being increased from 30 MLD to 90 MLD and its revised DPR was under preparation. They also record a 7.5 MLD Kundli STP tender under approval, with construction targeted by 31 December 2027. These are different dated project descriptions; do not silently merge the Kundli STP with the DCRUST expansion.

The same minutes identify a proposed 6 MLD CETP for the Kundli/Nathupur CLU area. Its selected site was 10 to 12 km from the industrial cluster, with no connecting sewerage system. SMDA, HSIIDC and the Deputy Commissioner were directed to meet on site selection and related issues.

A [Haryana government release dated 7 October 2026](https://prms.prharyana.gov.in/press-release/8644) records directions to municipal officers to prioritize cleaning dumping areas around the city. It says a DPR for Bahalgarh Road waterlogging and drainage had been prepared and that Aterna STP commissioning required further review. It does not certify completion. The [municipal portal](https://mcsonipat.in/) links the [state SWM portal](https://swm.ulbharyana.gov.in/) and [ULB grievance system](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/). No verified daily waste tonnage, processing efficiency or current collection-contractor performance is asserted here.

# Srikakulam municipal jurisdiction and city figures

## Municipal jurisdiction

Srikakulam, formerly Chicacole, became a municipality in 1856. The city profile dates its upgrade to a municipal corporation to 9 December 2015. The city lies on the Nagavali River. The district directory lists Amudalavalasa, Ichchapuram and Palasa-Kasibugga separately; their municipal records do not belong to the Srikakulam city corporation. See the [district municipal directory](https://srikakulam.ap.gov.in/public-utility-category/municipality/).

The [state directory](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/ulbdetails/ulbprofiles) classifies urban local body 102 as a corporation. The population and household fields in the city profile have no reference year. They describe the city profile, not the district census.

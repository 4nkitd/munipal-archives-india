# Srikakulam riverfront, park and street-light works

## Works named in the city profile

| Location | Work described by the corporation |
| --- | --- |
| Nagavali riverfront | Riverfront rejuvenation project |
| Kargil Park | Development of the park |
| Ramalaxmana Junction to Pedapadu Junction | Central lighting on 10-metre galvanised iron octagonal poles, with underground cable and 150-watt LED streetlights |
| Junctions and central dividers | Sculptures and fencing as part of beautification works |

The street-lighting entry identifies both ends of the corridor and gives the pole height, cable arrangement and lamp rating. The junction beautification entry gives no individual sites. The corporation lists these under ongoing programmes; it supplies no tender numbers, contract values, award dates or completion certificates.

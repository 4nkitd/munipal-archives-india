# Srinagar Garhwal corporation wards and first board after the 2024-25 election

## Corporation status

The Uttarakhand Urban Development Department [ULB roster](https://udd.uk.gov.in/list-of-ulb/) lists Nagar Nigam Srinagar at serial 54 in Pauri district, separately from Nagar Nigam Kotdwar at serial 58. The department's elected-representative register, pages 17–18, records Aarti Bhandari (आरती भण्डारी) as mayor and gives 7 February 2025 for both her oath and the first board. It records the same dates for all but ward 31. That ward's oath is dated 15 February 2025, with a dash in the first-board column. The [district election page](https://pauri.nic.in/panchsthaneey-election-2024-2025/) separately lists mayoral candidacies and wards 1–40 under NAGAR NIGAM SRINAGAR, but that page is not an election-results list.

## Ward lookup scope

Serial 54 lists 40 wards. The archive's ward directory transcribes the Hindi labels in that register. Ward 4 is मेडिकल कॉलेज कॉरीडोर रोड, ward 5 is बेस हॉस्पिटल and wards 32–36 are five numbered ऐठाणा डांग wards. These are election labels, not legal boundary descriptions or complete lists of localities. This record concerns Srinagar Garhwal in Uttarakhand, not Srinagar in Jammu and Kashmir.

The state portal dates the elected-representative register to 2 September 2026. The February 2025 entries are board and oath dates, not publication dates.

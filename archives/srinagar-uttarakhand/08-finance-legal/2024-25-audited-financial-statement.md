# Srinagar Garhwal corporation audited accounts for FY 2024-25

## Entity and period

The audit report is addressed to the Mayor, Nagar Nigam Srinagar, District Pauri, Uttarakhand. Ved Vrat & Associates dated its report 29 December 2025. The audit covered the balance sheet at 31 March 2025, the income and expenditure account, and the receipts and payments account for the year then ended. This is a second official record of the corporation in operation, separate from the election register.

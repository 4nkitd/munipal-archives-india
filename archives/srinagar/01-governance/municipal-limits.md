# Srinagar Municipal Corporation: status and municipal limits

## Corporation and office

The [corporation's history](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/Home/AboutUS) dates the municipal department to 1886, the change from committee to council to 1956, and the upgrade to a municipal corporation to 2003. The head office is on Sheikh-ul-Alam Road, Karan Nagar. The [commissioner's office](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/Home/Current) identifies Dr Basharat Qayoom, IAS, as Administrator/Commissioner. The office telephone is 0194-2474499 and its email is commissioner-smc@jk.gov.in.

## What the notifications cover

[SRO-72, 2 March 2016](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/files/SRO-72.pdf), issued under section 4(2) of the Jammu and Kashmir Municipal Corporation Act, 2000, replaced Annexure A of SRO-288 of 4 September 2012. Its electoral ward schedule lists mohallas and directional boundaries and ends at ward 73, Chatterhama.

[SRO-219, 29 June 2016](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/files/SRO%20219.pdf), issued under section 3(3), brought specified khasra parcels in Shankarpora and Bagh-i-Mehtab inside the municipal limits. It neither numbers nor delimits ward 74. SMC's jurisdiction page associates the addition of Baagh-i-Mahtaab with the 74th ward. The [SMC electoral map index](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/Home/WarsMaps) labels ward 74 “Bagh-e-Mehtaab.”

SMC's jurisdiction page describes the resulting area as 227.34 square kilometres, with 74 wards. Its geographic reference points include Pandach to Nagbal on the north, Harwan Bridge on the northeast, Baagh-i-Mahtaab on the south and Mujgund to Abdullahpora on the northwest. These reference points are not survey lines. For a municipal limit certificate, SMC directs applicants to [JanSugam](https://jansugam.jk.gov.in/).

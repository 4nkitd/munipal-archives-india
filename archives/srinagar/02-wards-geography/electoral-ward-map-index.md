# Srinagar's 74 electoral ward maps and the administrative ward directory

## Which ward number to use

SMC's electoral map index numbers Harwan as ward 1 and Lal Chowk as ward 6. The separate [administrative ward directory](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/Home/KnowYourWard) lists Brein, Nishat as administrative ward 1 and Lal Chowk as administrative ward 2, with its office at Sher-i-Kashmir Park. Use that directory to locate a service office. Its 35 offices do not form a second list of electoral constituencies. The map index below preserves SMC's electoral names and links; a point location for an office does not establish where an electoral boundary runs.

For written demarcations, [SRO-72](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/files/SRO-72.pdf) lists directional boundary references and identifies some localities as only partly included. For example, the Harwan entry lists parts of Banigam, Pazwalpora and Harwan. A locality name alone may not identify the correct ward for an address.

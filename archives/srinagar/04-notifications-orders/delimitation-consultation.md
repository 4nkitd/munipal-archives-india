# Public consultation on delimitation of Srinagar electoral wards, February 2024

## Who could respond and when

The Deputy Commissioner notified residents within Srinagar Municipal Corporation limits that electoral ward delimitation was under way under section 6 of the J&K Municipal Corporation Act, 2000. The [signed public notice](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3f4b9ec30ad9f68f89b29639786cb62ef/uploads/2024/02/2024021214.pdf) cites notification S.O. 600 of 30 November 2023. It invited representations, comments and suggestions within seven days of publication and expressly excluded late submissions.

The district website lists 12 February 2024 as the start date and 29 February as the end date for the listing. The latter was not the comment deadline in the notice. Written representations could be delivered to the Office of the Joint Commissioner (S) & Secretary at SMC's central office in Karan Nagar. The notice also accepted submissions by email.

## What the notice establishes

The notice records a stage in the delimitation process. It contains no adopted ward schedule, boundary descriptions or replacement map. SMC's separately published [ward map index](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/Home/WarsMaps) lists 74 numbered maps.

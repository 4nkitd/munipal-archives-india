# SMC order for a 24-hour snow clearance control room, November 2026

## Public helplines and reporting

The commissioner signed the [order](https://apismc.easio.in/Files/PageContent/427c41df-5a7e-4fca-a604-3fd1d09c2e4f.pdf) on 12 November 2026, reference SMC/128/2026-02 (C.No. 8014367). The [SMC orders page](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/Home/Orders) lists its public posting on 13 November 2026. The order directs the Joint Commissioner (Administration) to arrange a 24-hour duty roster, maintain contact with the divisional and district control rooms and keep **0194-2470465** and **0194-2474499** operational. The Joint Commissioner (Works) is to refer grievances to the executive engineers and obtain hourly reports on clearance.

## Equipment and ward responsibilities

The mechanical wing is to prepare 43 tractors with ploughs, 10 JCBs/loaders and two skid steers. The order assigns the drainage wing responsibility for 104 permanent dewatering stations and 281 mobile pump units. It requires diesel stocks for pumping during snowfall. These are preparedness instructions, not a count of machines deployed on a particular day.

All 35 administrative ward officers must report daily on roads, lanes, water and electricity supply stations, ration ghats and hospitals in their area. Each ward is to receive its clearance route plan, with a paper copy available to the zone head.

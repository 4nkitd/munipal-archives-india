# Srinagar municipal certificates, permissions, payments and grievances

## Birth and death records

The [SMC registration page](https://smcsrinagar.jk.gov.in/Home/Birth) distinguishes two applications. For a birth or death already registered with the corporation, follow **Old Birth & Death Registration** to [JanSugam](https://jansugam.jk.gov.in/) to search, verify or request a certified copy. For a new event, **New Birth & Death Registration** links to the [Civil Registration System](https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/) to submit event details and supporting documents. The distinction is whether a registration already exists.

## Other municipal transactions

| Request | Destination SMC lists |
| --- | --- |
| Municipal limit certificate, vending licence, road cut, trade fair or signage permission | [JanSugam](https://jansugam.jk.gov.in/) |
| Building plan approval | [Online Building Permission System](https://obps.jk.gov.in/BPAMSClient/Home.aspx) |
| Trade tax details, payment and receipts | [SMC trade tax](https://smctradetax.jk.gov.in/) |
| Municipal rent | [SMC rents](https://smcrents.jk.gov.in/) |
| Municipal user charges and payment history | [SMC user charges](https://smciswm.jk.gov.in/) |
| Lodge and track a civic grievance | [J&K Samadhan](https://samadhan.jk.gov.in/login) |

SMC lists grievances about sanitation, roads, street lighting and water supply among the issues that can be reported through Samadhan. For help identifying the responsible municipal office, use the Karan Nagar office channel above.

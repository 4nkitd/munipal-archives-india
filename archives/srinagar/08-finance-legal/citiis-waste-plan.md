# Srinagar's ₹67 crore waste management plan in the J&K budget speech, 2026–27

## What the ₹67 crore covers

In paragraph 140, printed page 61, the Chief Minister and Minister of Finance reported that Srinagar City had been selected for integrated waste management under CITIIS 2.0. The speech gives a combined action-plan value of ₹67 crore. It specifies:

| Planned component | Capacity or coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Waste pre-processing unit | 700 tonnes per day |
| Leachate treatment plant | 150 kilolitres per day |
| Briquetting plant | 300 tonnes per day |
| RFID tagging | 2.40 lakh households and 80,000 commercial units |

## How to read the amount

The ₹67 crore is a plan value. The speech does not identify an SMC appropriation, municipal funding share, actual expenditure or commissioning date for the plan. The household figure is a tagging target, not a census count.

Paragraph 140 also reports ₹358 crore for projects to reduce pollution in water bodies and add 28 million litres per day of sewerage treatment capacity. It does not attribute that amount specifically to Dal Lake. The speech separately identifies ₹306 crore for a 30-million-litre-per-day sewage treatment plant serving uncovered areas of Dal Lake. Neither figure is part of the ₹67 crore waste plan.

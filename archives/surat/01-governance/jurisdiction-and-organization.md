# Surat Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and organization

## Formation and municipal limits

Surat Borough Municipality became Surat Municipal Corporation on **1 October 1966**, according to SMC's city-limit history and past-election register. SMC's [City Introduction](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/TheCity/Introduction) dates the earlier municipality to 1852. The formation date here refers to the corporation, not to the older municipality or the date of the statute.

The [city-limit history](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/TheCity/City/Stml1) records the municipal area as 21.95 sq km at conversion. It lists later changes, including Rander and Adajan in 1970 and extensions in February and July 2006. Its 2011 census row gives 326.515 sq km and 44,66,826 residents. For the extension dated **18 June 2020**, SMC cites Gujarat Government Notification **KV/46 of 2020/SMN902019/9933/P**. The table lists places including Sachin, Kansad, Gothan, Bhatha, Kathor, Laskana and Saroli. Use the source table for the complete list; this is not a property-level boundary determination.

The City Introduction and [Demographics](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/TheCity/Demographics) pages both give **461.60 sq km** and **46,45,384 people** on a Census 2011 basis after the 2020 extension. This is not a 2020 headcount. The City Introduction separately labels **86,02,322** as its 2025 population estimate. SMC's detailed area tables differ from the overview: the city-limit history ends with 462.149 sq km, while the [zonewise table](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/TheCity/City/Stml3) gives 462.145 sq km. The archive profile uses the overview value and does not treat it as a survey measurement.

## Elected and administrative wings

SMC's [corporation introduction](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Corporation/Introduction) cites section 4 of the 1949 municipal corporation statute as the basis for three authorities: the General Board, Standing Committee and Municipal Commissioner.

| Authority | Role described by SMC |
| --- | --- |
| General Board | Comprised of elected councillors. SMC describes four seats in each of 30 wards, 120 seats in total and a five-year term. Councillors elect the Mayor and Deputy Mayor from their own membership. |
| Standing Committee | Consists of 12 councillors appointed by the corporation. It exercises financial and contracting powers and meets weekly, according to the elected-wing description. |
| Municipal Commissioner | Heads the administrative wing. The elected-wing page says the Commissioner may participate in General Board discussions with the presiding authority's permission, but cannot vote or make a proposition there. |

SMC's [elected-wing page](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Corporation/ElectedWings) describes the constitution and meeting arrangements; its [committees page](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Corporation/Committees) lists bodies for health, public works, water, town planning and other functions. The [administrative organogram](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Content/Documents/Corporation/organogram.pdf) is the separate chart for the administrative side. Municipal headquarters are at Shri Tapi Bhawan, Surat Mahanagar Seva Sadan, Gordhandas Chokhawala Marg, Shri Tapipura, Surat 395003.

## Scope of this record

The jurisdiction here is the municipal corporation and its city limits. It is not the Surat Urban Development Authority planning area or the entirety of Surat district.

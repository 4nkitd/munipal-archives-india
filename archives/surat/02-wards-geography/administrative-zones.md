# Surat's nine municipal service zones

## Zone office directory and map

SMC's [zone functions page](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Zones/Functions) says the corporation uses nine zones to deliver municipal services. These are administrative divisions, not the 30 wards that elect councillors. The following are office locations from SMC's [Zone Officials directory](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Home/ZoneOfficials), not descriptions of the territory each office covers.

The [City Introduction](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/TheCity/Introduction) links the [Administrative Zones Map](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Content/Documents/TheCity/ADMINISTRATIVE%20ZONES%20Map.pdf), which supplies the areas above and shows the SMC boundary separately from village and zone boundaries. Its total is 461.60 sq km. SMC's [City Civic Center directory](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Services/CityCivicCenterListOfCenters) groups counters by zone. A civic centre may be at a different address from the zone office. Use the Zone Officials directory for office contacts and the civic-centre directory for counter addresses.

## Which work goes to zone and ward offices

SMC places property assessment and recovery, including property and education taxes, under zone offices. Their health work includes sanitation, vaccination and birth and death registration. The engineering list covers local roads and footpaths, municipal buildings, some water connections and pipeline repairs, storm drainage, and action on encroachments and unauthorized connections. The functions page describes the administrative division of work; it does not give a schedule of fees or approvals for individual applications.

The same source lists frontline sanitation tasks for ward offices: local cleaning, pest control, collection and removal of solid waste, clearing choked gully traps and inspecting certain food premises. The portal's administrative ward-office lists should not be used to assign electoral wards to zones. The source pages verified here do not provide a complete electoral ward-to-zone crosswalk.

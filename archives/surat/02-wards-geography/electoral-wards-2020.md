# Surat electoral wards under the 2020 delimitation

## Notification and map

SMC's [Election and Census page](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Departments/ElectionCensus/ElectionCensusHome) attributes the 30-ward, 120-seat arrangement to a Gujarat Urban Development and Urban Housing Department notification dated **3 September 2020**. Separately, the Gujarati annotation on SMC's election map cites a **3 October 2020** final order of the State Election Commission for the ward layout. The archive dates this record to the final order cited on the map. The next corporation election in SMC's [past-election table](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Departments/ElectionCensus/ElectionCensusPastMunicipalElections) was held on **21 February 2021**.

The [Election Map Year 2020](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Content/Documents/Departments/ElectionAndCensus/SuratCityElectionMapYear2020.pdf) is SMC's drawing for that election geography. The map distinguishes the city limit, election ward lines and zone lines. Its English legend labels the ward lines as proposed, although the Gujarati annotation references the final order. Ward labels in the following table come from SMC's [Wardwise Allotment of Seats](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Departments/ElectionCensus/ElectionCensusWardwiseAllotmentSeats), including that page's spelling. The names are labels, not a list of every locality inside each ward. SMC's separate [corporator list](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Corporation/Corp_list) uses spelling variants for some wards.

## Later orders and limits of this record

The State Election Commission, Gujarat, indexes Surat in its [2020 municipal corporation delimitation archive](https://sec.gujarat.gov.in/mnp-2020.htm) and its [2026 municipal corporation rotation index](https://sec.gujarat.gov.in/mp-rotation-2026.htm). Surat does not appear on the Commission's separate [2026 delimitation index](https://sec.gujarat.gov.in/mp-delimination-2026.htm) as accessed. Rotation and delimitation are different records. This table does not apply 2020-era reservations to 2026, reproduce the legal boundary descriptions, or assign the electoral wards to service zones.

# Surat civic centres, online services and complaint channels

## Civic counters and bills

SMC's [City Civic Center service description](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Services/CityCivicCenterIntroduction) lists property and professional tax collection, birth and death certificates, shops and establishment registration, complaints, municipal tenement and EWS housing instalments, and distribution of forms. It also lists part plans of town-planning maps and receipt of applications. Use the [zonewise counter directory](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Services/CityCivicCenterListOfCenters) to find an address or the phone listed for a particular counter. The service introduction gives conflicting centre counts, so use the directory rather than either count.

The [Virtual Civic Center](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/OnlineServices/) lists property tax, professional tax, water meter charges, shops and establishment applications, and municipal venue bookings. The [property tax lookup](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/OnlineServices/PropertyTax/Enroll/) asks for the 14-character tenement number, which the form illustrates with a letter in the second segment. It provides links to bill viewing, transaction status and receipt reprinting, as well as separate routes for transfer and assessment applications. The lookup page states a daily maintenance window of 23:30 to 00:30.

## Birth and death certificates

The [birth](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/OnlineServices/BirthCertificate/Enroll/) and [death](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/OnlineServices/DeathCertificate/Enroll/) certificate searches show the same cutoff. SMC says it began using Gujarat's eOlakh for these registrations on **1 April 2020**. Certificates for events registered on or after that date are not available in these SMC searches. The cutoff concerns the registration date, not necessarily the event date. The SMC forms offer either a date-based search or a search using the certificate number's zone, registration year and serial number.

For questions about registration within SMC limits or obtaining forms in person, see the [municipal Birth and Death Registration page](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Departments/BirthDeathRegistration). It names civic centres, zone offices and municipal headquarters as form sources.

## Complaint registration

| Channel | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [SMC complaint form](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/OnlineServices/complaint/New) | SMC redirects to the Virtual Civic Center login before lodging a complaint. The portal also lists My Complaints. |
| [Toll-free complaint registration](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Home/TollFreeNumbers) | Call 1800-123-8000. The same official page lists 1800-212-2829 for construction and demolition waste, and 1800-233-3002 for plastic waste. |
| [SMC WhatsApp chatbot](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Home/WhatsAppChatbotService) | Send `Hi` to +916359930020 and choose complaint registration or tracking. The menu also lists property-tax and water-meter bill details and civic facility information. |

SMC says complaint registration issues a ticket for tracking. Keep the ticket number. The public entry points were checked; no account was accessed, payment made or complaint submitted.

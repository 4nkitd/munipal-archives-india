# Surat General Board municipal budget for 2026-27

## What the Board approved

The [General Board budget book](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Content/Documents/Departments/Accounts/2026-27/BudgetBookGeneralBoard_2026_27.pdf) opens with a resolution passed when the adjourned ordinary meeting of **16 February 2026** resumed on **17 February 2026**. It cites Standing Committee Resolution **321/2026**. The Board approved a revised estimate for 2025-26 and approved the 2026-27 income and expenditure estimates and tax and rate proposals described in the resolution.

The resolution lists ten budget units: headquarters and the nine municipal service zones. Headquarters is an accounting unit, not a tenth zone. The book contains citywide summaries and more detailed zone schedules. The [Accounts budget index](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Departments/Accounts/Budget) lists this as the General Board book alongside books for earlier years.

## Selected amounts from Schedule 1

Schedule 1, printed page I, calls these budget figures and gives the unit as **rupees in lakhs**. The table below uses the total column across funds, not just the revenue or capital fund columns. These amounts are estimates, not actual collections or spending.

| Schedule 1 line | Total (₹ lakh) |
| --- | ---: |
| Opening balance | 3,48,458.73 |
| Revenue receipts | 6,37,211.10 |
| Capital receipts | 3,64,605.29 |
| Revenue expenditure | 5,57,272.15 |
| Capital expenditure | 5,72,901.84 |
| Closing balance | 2,23,096.34 |

The closing balance follows the book's adjustments for receivables, expenditure without a cash effect and transfers between funds. It is not calculated by subtracting the two excerpted spending lines from the two excerpted receipt lines. Use SMC's separate [Revenue-Capital Budget Summary](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Departments/Accounts/CapitalRevenueBudgetSummary) for the reporting view, which distinguishes original and revised budgets from tentative actuals and uses rupees in thousands rather than lakhs.

## Separate invitation for 2027-28 suggestions

SMC's [Gujarati public notice](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Content/Documents/Downloads/BudgetSuggetion_BY2027_28.pdf) invites suggestions on the **draft** 2027-28 budget. It allows online or written submissions within seven days of the advertisement. Written submissions are addressed to the Municipal Commissioner, Room 75, Central Office, Surat Municipal Corporation. The notice copy does not show a legible advertisement date, so this archive does not calculate a deadline.

The [online suggestion form](https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/Others/SuggestionForBudget) lists water supply, sewerage, roads, street lighting, health, public parks and gardens, accounts and budget, housing, bridges and other topics. It limits the suggestion text to 500 characters. Access to the public form was verified without submitting it; form visibility does not establish that the submission window is still open.

# Constitution of Surendranagar Municipal Corporation

## Effective date and jurisdiction

Gujarat constituted the Municipal Corporation of the City of Surendranagar on 1 January 2025 under Article 243Q of the Constitution and section 5(1) of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. Notification KV-20 of 2025, file UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/5957/P Section, appears in the *Gujarat Government Gazette Extraordinary*, Part I-A, Extra No. 13, pages 13-1 and 13-2. The remaining provisions of the Act came into operation in the corporation on the same date.

The corporation comprised the larger urban area in [GV-19 of 2025](../02-wards-geography/larger-urban-area-2025.md). KV-20 specifies that government land within the included areas does not vest in the corporation.

## Transitional administration

The notification appointed K. C. Sampat, IAS, as administrator for one year starting 1 January 2025 or until councillors were elected in the first corporation election, whichever came first. This was the initial appointment, not a current officer listing.

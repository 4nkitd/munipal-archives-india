# Surendranagar larger urban area and included gram panchayats

## Schedule at formation

Under Article 243Q(2) of the Constitution, the Gujarat government designated the former Surendranagar-Dudhrej-Wadhwan Municipality and the following gram panchayat areas as a larger urban area. The [corporation website](https://snmcgujarat.com/) describes the predecessor municipality as including Surendranagar, Dudhrej, Joravarnagar, Ratanpar and Wadhwan.

| Schedule entry | Gram panchayat |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Mulchand |
| 2 | Chamaraj |
| 3 | Khamisana |
| 4 | Kherali |
| 5 | Malod |

The list uses the spellings in notification GV-19 of 2025, file UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/5957/P Section, 1 January 2025, *Gujarat Government Gazette Extraordinary*, Part IV-A, Extra No. 7, pages 7-1 and 7-2. The schedule numbers are not ward numbers. The separate [constitution notification KV-20](../01-governance/corporation-formation-2025.md) used this larger urban area as the corporation's jurisdiction, not the whole district.

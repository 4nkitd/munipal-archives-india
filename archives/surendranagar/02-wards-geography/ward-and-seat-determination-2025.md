# Surendranagar corporation ward and seat determination, July 2025

## Ward count and seat reservations

Notification KV-242 of 2025, file UDUHD/MSM/e-file/18/2025/0418/P Section, set 13 electoral wards and 52 councillor seats for the Municipal Corporation of the City of Surendranagar. Issued on 9 July 2025 under section 5 of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949, the notification refers to 2011 Census population figures and the corporation's forthcoming general election.

| Reservation | Seats | Women included in the category |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Scheduled Castes | 5 | 2 |
| Backward Classes | 14 | 7 |
| Women across categories | 26 | Includes the women already counted in the Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes rows |

Page 2 of the scanned notification lists these citywide totals. It does not draw ward boundaries or assign reservations to individual wards. The [corporation's own site](https://snmcgujarat.com/) separately describes 13 administrative wards in North and South zones on either side of the Bhogavo River. Its [Zones / Wards page](https://snmcgujarat.com/zones_wards) lists branches for each zone, but does not match individual electoral wards to the zones.

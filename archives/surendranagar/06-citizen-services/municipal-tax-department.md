# Surendranagar municipal tax department and online property tax

## Property tax

The Property Tax Branch maintains property registers, issues and dispatches bills, and collects property tax. The corporation's [Property Tax link](https://app.snmcgujarat.com/#/paypropertytax) points to its separate payment portal. The department page does not give tax rates, penalties or deadlines.

## Business taxes and shop licensing

The Business and Other Tax Branch maintains a register of shops and businesses within the municipal area, and collects fees and taxes under the applicable rules. The Shops and Establishment Branch issues shop licences and keeps a register of shops and businesses. The corporation links [Gujarat e-Nagar](https://enagar.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) for professional tax and water tax, rather than the property tax payment link above.

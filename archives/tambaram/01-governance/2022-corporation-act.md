# Tambaram City Municipal Corporation Act, 2022

## Establishment and district

Tamil Nadu published Act 5 of 2022 in Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 56, Part IV, Section 2, on 21 January 2022. The Act appears on printed pages 35 to 40. Section 1(3) deems it to have come into force on **3 November 2021**. Section 13 repeals the Tambaram City Municipal Corporation Ordinance, 2021, with a saving provision.

The [Chengalpattu district administration's local bodies directory](https://chengalpattu.nic.in/administrative-setup/local-bodies/) lists Tambaram under Corporation. The [corporation home page](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/tambaram/) reports 87.64 sq km and a 2011 Census population of 723,017 for the corporation. Its [About Us page](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/tambaram/about-us-2/) instead describes the earlier Tambaram municipality and gives 20.72 sq km and 164,830 people. Those older figures do not describe the corporation.

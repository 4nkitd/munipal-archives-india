# Tambaram ward committee zones, January 2022

## Ward committee composition

Notification III in Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 19 records the creation of five ward committees under G.O. Ms. No. 3 of 6 January 2022. The gazette reference is II(2)/MAWS/29(e)/2022. It cites section 5-A(1) of the Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation Act, 1981, read with section 8 of the Tambaram City Municipal Corporation Ordinance, 2021.

| Ward committee | Wards in the notification |
|---|---|
| Zone 1 | 1 to 8, 10 to 12, 29 to 31 |
| Zone 2 | 9, 13 to 21, 24, 26 to 28 |
| Zone 3 | 22, 23, 25, 34 to 44 |
| Zone 4 | 32, 33, 49 to 61 |
| Zone 5 | 45 to 48, 62 to 70 |

This January 2022 schedule assigns ward numbers to committees. It does not describe street-level ward boundaries or establish whether a later notification changed the composition.

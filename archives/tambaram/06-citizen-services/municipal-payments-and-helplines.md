# Tambaram online payments and municipal helplines

## Payment portal and office channels

The corporation publishes [Tamil Nadu Urban ePay](https://tnurbanepay.tn.gov.in/) as its online payment route and names TN Urban Esevai as the mobile app. Its ePay panel lists property tax, drinking water and sewerage among the payment categories. The state portal shows Quick Payment, Payment Verification Status, Service Request Status Verification and Download Certificate.

The city lists separate helplines for Tambaram and Pallavapuram. Its Commissioner Office is at 28 Muthurenga Street, West Tambaram, Chennai 600045. The Chennai postal address does not make Greater Chennai Corporation the billing authority for a Tambaram assessment. These are published contact routes; no payment or call was made.

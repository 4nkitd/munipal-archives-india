# Tambaram detailed budget, 2025-2026

## Approval and document dates

The corporation's [Budget Details register](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/tambaram/budget-details-2/) lists council approval on **6 March 2025** and publication on **3 April 2025**. The linked detailed report itself is marked **printed 7 April 2025**. The archive uses month precision because the register's publication day precedes the print date on the linked copy.

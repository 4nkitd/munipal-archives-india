# Thane city corporation and the district municipal directory

## Which corporation issued the record?

Thane district lists Thane Municipal Corporation first and Navi Mumbai Municipal Corporation second, each with a different website. Its other entries are Kalyan Dombivli, Bhiwandi Nizampur, Ulhasnagar and Mira Bhayander. A district address alone therefore does not identify a TMC tax account. Use the corporation named on the assessment.

## Committee names on Thane tax records

TMC's [property-tax search](https://propertytax.thanecity.gov.in/) offers these nine Prabhag Samiti filters: Diva, Kalwa, Lokmanya-Savarkar, Majiwada-Manpada, Mumbra, Naupada-Kopri, Uthalasar, Vartak Nagar and Wagale. Its FAQ directs taxpayers seeking a change of name to the nearest TMC Prabhag Samiti office. These names identify administrative committees on the tax form; the electoral ward count comes from the State Election Commission.

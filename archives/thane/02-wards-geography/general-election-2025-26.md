# Thane's 33 electoral wards and 131 seats in 2025-26

## Results declared

The [SEC news register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the final party position to 16 January 2026. Row 2 of the result sheet gives 33 wards and 131 seats for Thane. The 648 contesting candidates are a count across the corporation. The result sheet does not assign streets or committee offices to ward numbers.

| SEC party or category with at least one seat | Seats won |
|---|---:|
| SS | 75 |
| BJP | 28 |
| NCP-SP | 12 |
| NCP | 9 |
| SEC-registered parties recognized as state parties outside Maharashtra | 5 |
| SS - UBT | 1 |
| Independent | 1 |
| Total declared | 131 |

Navi Mumbai has a separate return in row 4. Neither its wards nor its seats enter the Thane total.

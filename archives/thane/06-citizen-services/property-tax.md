# Thane property-tax payment, receipts and name changes

## Find and pay a bill

Enter the property number and sub-code, then choose **Search**. The form also offers an owner-name search with a Prabhag Samiti filter. Check the property details before selecting **Pay Now**. TMC lists net banking, debit or credit card and BHIM UPI. The payment requires a mobile number and email address.

## Receipts and corrections

According to the TMC FAQ, the payer receives a confirmation on the mobile number and the receipt at the registered email address. For another copy, the FAQ directs users to `myptax.thanecity.gov.in`. Sign in with the registered mobile number or register the property, display its details and select the receipt download. This also applies to payments made at an office.

For a missing assessment on the portal, the property-tax helpdesk publishes daily hours of 9 am to 8 pm. For a name change, visit the nearest TMC Prabhag Samiti office. The FAQ gives corporation office hours as Monday to Friday, 10 am to 6 pm.

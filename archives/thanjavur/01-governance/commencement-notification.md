# Commencement of the Thanjavur City Municipal Corporation Act

## Notification and effective date

The Governor exercised the commencement power in section 1(3) of Tamil Nadu Act 24 of 2013. The department issued G.O. Ms. No. 23 on 17 February 2014 and appointed **19 February 2014** as the day the Act came into force. The notification appears in Part II, Section 2 of Extraordinary Gazette No. 29 as II(2)/MAWS/145(a-1)/2014. The second notification on the same page concerns Dindigul under a separate Act.

The [district administration’s corporation profile](https://thanjavur.nic.in/municipal-corporation-thanjavur/) gives the same commencement date. Its introductory figures describe a 36.33-square-kilometre municipal area and 222,619 residents at the 2011 Census. It reports a larger area separately in discussing a proposed expansion.

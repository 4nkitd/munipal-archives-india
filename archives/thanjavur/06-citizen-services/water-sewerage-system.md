# Thanjavur water supply and sewerage infrastructure

## Water supply

The district profile puts daily demand at 30.05 million litres. It attributes 20 million litres per day to the Colleron river source and 7.50 million to local borewells. The Thirumanur to Vennar pumping main ran 19 km. The distribution network covered 334.70 km, with 19 overhead tanks holding 114.75 lakh litres and 29,200 house service connections. These are figures from the district report, rather than a live supply schedule.

## Sewerage scheme

Municipal Administration and Water Supply G.O. Ms. No. 720 of 30 December 2000 sanctioned the scheme. The district says TWAD maintained it through March 2015 and handed it to the corporation in April 2015. The treatment plant at Samuthiram Eri had a design capacity of 28.05 million litres per day. The report lists five pumping stations, 12 lifting stations, 10,059 manholes and 258.79 km of sewer lines. It records 27,889 connections against 30,000 sanctioned.

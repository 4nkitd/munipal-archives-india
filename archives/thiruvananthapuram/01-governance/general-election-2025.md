# Thiruvananthapuram: December 2025 local election timetable

## Schedule announced on 10 November 2025

The Kerala State Election Commission scheduled polling in Thiruvananthapuram district for Tuesday, 9 December, from 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. The second phase on 11 December covered other districts. The timetable on the last page of the announcement gives these dates:

| Stage | Date in 2025 |
|---|---|
| Notification and returning officer's public notice | 14 November |
| Last date for nominations | 21 November |
| Scrutiny | 22 November |
| Last date for withdrawal | 24 November |
| District polling day | 9 December |
| Counting begins | 13 December, 8 a.m. |
| Deadline to complete election | 18 December |

The timetable records the scheduled poll. It does not certify an individual ward result. For the 101 wards in this election cycle, see the [final delimitation order](https://wardmap.ksmart.live/FinalN/notification/FinalN_Thiruvanathapuram_Thiruvananthapuram.pdf).

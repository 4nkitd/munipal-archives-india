# Thiruvananthapuram corporation taluk and village reference

## Revenue geography on the corporation portal

The corporation groups the following names under Thiruvananthapuram taluk. Spellings and order follow its English-language “Taluks and Villages” page.

| Entries | Village names |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | Thycadu, Sasthamangalam, Vattiyurkavu |
| 4 to 6 | Kowdiar, Peroorkada, Kudappanakunnu |
| 7 to 9 | Pattom, Ulloor, Cheruvaikal |
| 10 to 12 | Attipra, Kadakampalli, Pettah |
| 13 to 15 | Vanchiyur, Muttathara, Manacaud |
| 16 to 18 | Thiruvallam, Nemom, Thirumala |

## Municipal and electoral jurisdiction

The [corporation profile](https://tmc.lsgkerala.gov.in/en/general-information) places the municipal office at Vikas Bhavan Post, PIN 695033. Its figure of 100 wards predates the [final order for 101 wards](https://wardmap.ksmart.live/FinalN/notification/FinalN_Thiruvanathapuram_Thiruvananthapuram.pdf). The village list provides no ward numbers or parcel boundaries; a village name alone does not identify an electoral ward.

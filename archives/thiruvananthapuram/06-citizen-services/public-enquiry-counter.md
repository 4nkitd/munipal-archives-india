# Thiruvananthapuram public enquiry and receipt counter

## Where to submit an application

The corporation places the Janasevanakendram and information centre on the ground floor, on the right at the front of the main municipal office. The office address is Vikas Bhavan Post, Thiruvananthapuram 695033. The counter gives municipal information and accepts applications and incoming correspondence. It issues an acknowledgement on receipt.

| Request | Function listed for the counter |
|---|---|
| Taxes, rent, licence fees and other service charges | Receive sums and cheques payable to the corporation |
| Birth, death and marriage certificates | Receive applications and distribute certificates |
| Municipal enquiry | Provide information on corporation services |

## Online certificate route

The [corporation home page](https://tmc.lsgkerala.gov.in/en) directs residents to [K-SMART](https://ksmart.lsgkerala.gov.in/ui/web-portal) for birth, death and marriage certificates. The enquiry page also lists prices for individual forms. These are form prices and do not establish the fee for registering an event or issuing a certificate.

# Thoothukudi's four administrative zones and 60 wards

## Ward assignments in the corporation's published table

| Zone | Ward numbers | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| North | 1 to 14, 20 | 15 |
| West | 15 to 19, 30 to 37, 42, 44 | 15 |
| East | 21 to 29, 38 to 41, 46, 47 | 15 |
| South | 43, 45, 48 to 60 | 15 |

The numbers alternate between West and South at wards 42 to 45. Use the number itself when matching a ward to a zonal service office. The undated corporation table identifies administrative assignments; it does not specify streets, survey numbers or the effective date of a delimitation.

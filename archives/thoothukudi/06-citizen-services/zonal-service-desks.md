# Thoothukudi zonal desks for tax, water and building enquiries

## Office numbers and section codes

| Zonal office | Office landline | Property tax | Water supply | Professional tax |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North, State Bank Colony | 0461-2347120 | A1 | A2 | A3 |
| West, Millerpuram | 0461-2332365 | A1 | A1 | A2 |
| East | 0461-2324901 | A2 | E2 | A2 |
| South | 0461-2356996 | A2 | E2 | A2 |

Section F1 handles building licences and encroachment in all four zones. For right-to-information enquiries, the table gives E1 in North and A2 in West, East and South. A resident with a water enquiry in South would ask for E2, whereas the same enquiry in North goes to A2. The phone numbers belong to the zonal offices.

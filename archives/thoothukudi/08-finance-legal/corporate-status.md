# Thoothukudi's incorporation and the five adjoining panchayats

## Legal identity and former areas

Section 4(1-A)(x) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998 identifies the Municipal Corporation of Thoothukudi as the corporation constituted under Tamil Nadu Act 27 of 2008. It continues as a corporation under the common Act in its own name and area.

The [corporation history](https://thoothukudicorporation.com/history-of-thoothukudi-corporation) attributes the expanded area to G.O.(Ms) No. 13 of 29 July 2008. Its study table distinguishes the existing town from Thoothukudi Rural, Meelavitan, Muthiapuram, Athimarapatti and Sankaraperi. The table's ward counts refer to those component areas; the corporation describes the combined city as divided into 60 wards. An old panchayat ward number alone cannot identify the corresponding city ward.

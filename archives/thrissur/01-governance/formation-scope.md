# Formation and territorial scope of Thrissur Municipal Corporation

## From municipality to corporation

Thrissur Municipality existed from 1 July 1942. On 2 October 2000 the municipal area and adjoining panchayat areas became Thrissur Municipal Corporation. The corporation records a total area of 101.42 square kilometres at formation.

| Extent incorporated | Area named in the municipal history |
| --- | --- |
| Existing urban body | Thrissur Municipality |
| Adjoining panchayats | Ayyanthole, Ollukkara, Koorkanchery, Ollur and Vilvattom |
| Partial inclusion | Parts of Nadathara panchayat |

The corporation reports 317,474 residents in 2001, including 154,188 males and 163,286 females. These figures describe the early corporation, not the present district.

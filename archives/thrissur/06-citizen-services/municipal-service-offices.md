# Thrissur municipal service offices and electricity enquiries

## Municipal office and zonal counters

The main office is on Municipal Office Road, Thrissur, Kerala 680001. Its listed lines include 0487-2422020 and 0487-2428550. For a zonal office, use the number below.

## Electricity and social security pension

The electricity department lists 0487-2441188 and 0487-2423613, and accepts email at astced.lsgd@kerala.gov.in. For social security pension enquiries, the corporation lists the toll-free number 1800 425 5150.

# Tiruchirappalli council and zonal administration

## Council and executive

The [corporation profile](https://www.trichycorporation.gov.in/about) describes a council with a Mayor and 65 ward councillors, one per ward. It says the Mayor conducts a council meeting at least once a month. The Commissioner is the executive authority. The Deputy Commissioner, City Engineer, City Health Officer, Executive Engineers for East and West, and Assistant Commissioners for Personnel, Revenue and Accounts assist the Commissioner.

## Zonal offices and General Department

The five zones are Srirangam, Ariyamangalam, Tiruverumbur, Ponmalai and K. Abishekapuram, numbered 1 to 5 respectively. Each zonal Assistant Commissioner has an Assistant Executive Engineer, Administrative Officer, Assistant Revenue Officer and Sanitary Officer. The General Department handles appointments, transfers, employee pay, correspondence with government, public grievances, lawsuits and requests for information under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

# Applying for a domestic water connection in Tiruchirappalli

## Application and charges

The corporation accepts applications for buildings assessed for property tax and buildings the council has exempted. Clear any outstanding property tax before applying. The owner or an authorised representative submits Form A, the registration fee and the supporting documents to the Commissioner. The municipal office maintains a seniority list by the date of payment of the registration fee.

| Stage | Procedure on the corporation page |
|---|---|
| Estimate | Within three days of receipt, the office sends notice of the caution deposit and estimated cost. |
| Payment | The applicant has two days to remit the deposit and estimate and obtain the work order before starting work. |
| Connection | The page cites MA&WS G.O. No. 97 of 12 July 2007 for a seven-day connection timeline in registration order. |

The estimate includes a supervision charge of 10% of its net value. Road reconstruction charges and the penalty fixed for unauthorised buildings also apply where relevant. The cited order date is not a publication date for the service page.

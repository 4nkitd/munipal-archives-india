# Tirunelveli water connections and published charges

## Deposit and tariff in the municipal inventory

| Connection class | House service deposit | Monthly tariff |
|---|---:|---:|
| Domestic | ₹6,500 | ₹100 |
| Non-domestic | ₹12,500 | ₹200 |
| Industrial | ₹12,500 | ₹200 |

The city lists the deposit separately from recurring charges. It gives no tariff order or effective date, so these amounts describe the published schedule rather than the amount payable on a new application.

## Service coverage in the same return

The inventory identifies the Tamirabarani River as the source and gives 1956 as the year of commissioning. It records four distribution zones, 610.16 km of distribution mains, 1,754 public fountains and 76,532 domestic house connections. Its stated daily frequency is a system-level figure; the return does not give household delivery times.

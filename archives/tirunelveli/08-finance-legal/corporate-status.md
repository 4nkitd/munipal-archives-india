# Tirunelveli's incorporation under the common municipal Act

## Which corporation the Act covers

Section 4(1-A)(v) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998 names the Municipal Corporation of Tirunelveli and identifies its founding statute as the Tirunelveli City Municipal Corporation Act, 1994, Tamil Nadu Act 28 of 1994. Section 4 deems Tirunelveli incorporated under the common Act in its own name and area. The provision concerns the urban corporation, not all of Tirunelveli district.

## What incorporation permits

Under section 3(3), the corporation has perpetual succession and a common seal. It can enter contracts, hold or transfer property, and sue or be sued in its corporate name, subject to statutory restrictions. This identifies the legal party to a municipal contract even when the individual commissioner changes.

# Tirupati Municipal Corporation jurisdiction and office

## Municipal jurisdiction

The [state directory](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/open/ulbs) classifies Tirupati as a corporation, ULB ID 20, in Andhra Pradesh. The municipal profile dates the municipality to 1886 and the corporation to 2007. The milestones give 1 April 1886 for the third-grade municipality, 7 October 1998 for selection grade and 2 March 2007 for corporation status. The profile records the first mayoral election in March 2021. [Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams](https://www.tirumala.org/) publishes temple and pilgrim information, including darshan. Municipal property assessments and civic works belong in the corporation records.

## Where to address a municipal enquiry

The municipal contact record gives Alipiri Road, opposite SPJNM High School Ground. The district directory lists a corporation office on Tilak Road, Kotramangalam, PIN 517501. These are different published locations. Confirm the destination with the Commissioner’s office before sending physical papers. The [municipal contact entry](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbcontacts/20) lists commissioner.mct@gmail.com. The [district directory entry](https://tirupati.ap.gov.in/public-utility/tirupati-municipal-corporation-office/) provides a separate check on the location.

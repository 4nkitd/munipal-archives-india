# Tirupati election wards, revenue wards and sachivalayams

## Counts in the municipal profile

The Andhra Pradesh municipal profile for Tirupati carries an update timestamp of 14 October 2026. Its area field gives 27.44 square kilometres. The following counts refer to different units in that record.

| Profile field | Value |
|---|---:|
| Election wards | 66 |
| Revenue wards | 23 |
| Ward sachivalayams | 102 |

## Which figure to use

The historical background describes 50 election wards and 20 revenue wards. The structured fields give 66 and 23 respectively. The profile does not date the transition or supply a notification. Its milestones also describe an October 2025 council proposal involving 63 villages; a proposal does not establish which villages entered the municipal limits. The [municipal About page](https://tirupati.cdma.ap.gov.in/ulbprofile/tirupati/about) draws on this profile. It does not provide street limits, a ward-by-ward schedule or parcel assignments.

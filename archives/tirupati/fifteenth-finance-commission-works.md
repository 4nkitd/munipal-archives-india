# Tirupati Fifteenth Finance Commission road and drain works

## Period and reference

The corporation published reference 359/TPT/23-24/15th FC/TO on 29 January 2026. It covers progress of Fifteenth Finance Commission works from the first instalment of 2020-21 through the second instalment of 2023-24. The listing describes works rather than inviting bids.

## Locations in the tender-register description

| Location | Work recorded |
|---|---|
| SGS Arts College to Tiruchanoor Main Road | Drain along APSPDCL Office Road |
| Kumarimitta Street and Besta Veedhi | Concrete road at door numbers 3-8-308 to 3-8-330 and 3-7-195 to 3-7-250, respectively |
| Gidangi Street crossroads | Concrete road from SV Cafe to Bala Vinayaka Swamy Temple |
| Mitta Street, ward 28 | Concrete road between door numbers 7-6-263 and 7-6-244 |
| Kothapalli, ward 1 | Concrete road from 22-5-145/B to Sri Venkateswara Engineering Works (Masthan Naidu flats) |

The [linked progress document](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/docs/APCMS/ulb_tnders/doc_1769666015631.pdf) accompanies the listing. The published description gives no rupee expenditure or completion certificate.

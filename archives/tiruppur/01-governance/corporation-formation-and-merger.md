# Tiruppur corporation formation and 2011 jurisdiction expansion

## Formation and merger

Tiruppur's municipal portal dates the upgrade to a City Municipal Corporation to **1 January 2008**. It cites Municipal Administration and Water Supply Department **G.O.Ms.No. 11 of 6 January 2011** and **G.O.Ms.No. 89 of 28 June 2011**, the latter from its Election branch, for the merger effective **25 October 2011**. The two municipalities were 15-Velampalayam and Nallur. The eight village panchayats were Andipalayam, Chettipalayam, Mannarai, Muthanampalayam, Murugampalayam, Veerapandi, Neruperichal and Thottipalayam. Those names identify the bodies cited by the corporation, not the boundaries of any individual ward.

## Area and population

The corporation reports an area of **159.35 sq km**, **60 wards** and **877,778 residents** on a 2011 Census basis. The [district corporation table](https://tiruppur.nic.in/corporation/) also gives 60 wards and 877,778 people; the population is not a current estimate. The [district local bodies directory](https://tiruppur.nic.in/administrative-setup/local-bodies/) lists the corporation separately from the district's municipalities and town panchayats. The corporation office is on Mangalam Road, Tiruppur 641604, according to its [contact page](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/tiruppur/contact-us-2/).

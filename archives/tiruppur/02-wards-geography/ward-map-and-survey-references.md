# Tiruppur municipal ward map and land survey references

## Municipal map and zones

The corporation publishes a [visual ward map](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/tiruppur/wp-content/uploads/sites/145/2019/12/WARD-MAP.pdf) under its Ward Map page. The [district corporation table](https://tiruppur.nic.in/corporation/) gives **60 municipal wards**. The corporation's [office directory](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/tiruppur/contact-us-2/) has separate entries for **Zone 1, Zone 2, Zone 3 and Zone 4**. It does not assign wards to zones. Use the published map for visual reference; this record does not infer a ward-to-zone table from office listings.

## Land survey cross-reference

The district [Town Re-survey Correlation Statement](https://tiruppur.nic.in/town-re-survey-correlation-statement/) has a separate set of lettered survey wards. It lists Tiruppur North's new survey wards A through H and Tiruppur South's A through G. For example, North new ward G corresponds to old ward J, while South new ward A corresponds to old ward L. Those letters identify records for town survey correlation. They are not electoral ward numbers or zone identifiers.

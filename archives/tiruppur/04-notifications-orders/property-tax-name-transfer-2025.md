# Tiruppur circular on property tax name-transfer fees

## Fees and authority

The corporation's central office circular **7750/2025/மை.அ/அ1**, dated **29 December 2025**, directs collection of the revised fee for changing the name in a property tax assessment. The circular cites Municipal Administration and Water Supply Department **G.O. No. 480** and Tamil Nadu Gazette Extraordinary **No. 836**, both dated **16 December 2025**. It also cites the Directorate of Municipal Administration's circular dated 24 December 2025 and refers to rule 256(3) and (4) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Rules, 2023.

| Building use | Name-transfer fee |
| --- | ---: |
| Residential | ₹500 |
| Non-residential | ₹1,000 |

## Linked utility records

The circular says not to charge a name-transfer fee on the linked water-charge assessment. It directs the corporation to update the linked water-charge and underground sewer connection records to the owner's name on the basis of the same property tax application. It also says the municipal software supports paying the property tax name-transfer fee online. The circular concerns the corporation's assessment records, not registration of a sale deed.

The [corporation homepage](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/tiruppur/) lists the scanned circular as "Property Tax Name Transfer – Application Fee Revision Notification". The date above is printed on the Tamil circular, not taken from the January 2026 upload path.

# Tiruppur citizen charter for streetlight repairs and civil registration

## Streetlight repairs

In its roads and streetlights section, the charter lists **two days** for repairing a non-burning light on main roads or streets and **three days** for repairing a light on an inner street. These are the targets printed in the online charter, not evidence of how long an individual repair took. Use the [corporation's office directory](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/tiruppur/contact-us-2/) to check the current reporting route.

## Registration and certificate extracts

The birth and death section says the Commissioner is the city Registrar and sanitary inspectors act as registrars for assigned registration units. The charter lists **16 units** and says the event is registered at the place where it occurred. The unit for a hospital birth is therefore determined by the hospital location, not by the family's current address. The page distinguishes initial registration, the later addition of a child's name, and the issue of extracts.

The charter has no confirmed publication date for the online edition. Its introduction cites **G.O.Ms.No. 58 of 16 April 1998** on the introduction of citizen charters across Tamil Nadu. That date belongs to the government order, not this edition of Tiruppur's charter. Confirm current registration requirements before applying.

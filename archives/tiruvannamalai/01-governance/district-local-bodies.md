# Tiruvannamalai in the district local-body register

## District classification

The district lists Tiruvannamalai as its only municipal corporation. Arni, Thiruvathipuram, Vandavasi, Polur and Chengam are separate municipalities. The district lists eight town panchayats in a third table.

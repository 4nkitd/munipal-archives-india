# Constitution of Tiruvannamalai City Municipal Corporation

## Final declaration

Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 257, Part II, Section 2, page 2, contains G.O. Ms. No. 103, notification II(2)/MAWS/743(a-2)/2024. The Governor declared the corporation under section 3(2)(d) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998. The government published the order on 10 August 2024; paragraph 2 brought it into force on 12 August.

## Area at formation

The order included the former Tiruvannamalai Municipality and Adiannamalai Reserve Forest. It also included the full areas of 18 village panchayats: Vengikkal, Chinnakangiyanur, So.Kilnatchipattu, Nochimalai, Endal, Thenmattur, Kilkatchirapattu, Melathikkan, Savalpoondi, Nallavanpalayam, Kanathampoondi, Anaipiranthan, Athiyanthal, Adiannamalai, Devanandhal, Adaiyur, Durgainammiyandal and Malappambadi.

The order provides for division into wards for the next ordinary election. It does not number the new corporation wards.

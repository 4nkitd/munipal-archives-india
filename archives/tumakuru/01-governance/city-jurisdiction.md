# Tumakuru city jurisdiction and corporation office

## Municipal area and responsibilities

The corporation's [City Summary](http://www.tumakurucity.mrc.gov.in/en/city-summary) reports 64.00 km² and 35 wards. It does not give the legal description of the boundary. The [district municipal directory](https://tumkur.nic.in/en/public-utility-category/municipality/) calls this body Tumkur City Corporation and lists municipalities such as Sira and Tiptur separately. The corporation does not administer the whole district.

The corporation's [Functions page](http://www.tumakurucity.mrc.gov.in/en/functions) lists public street maintenance, lighting, drains, sewage and rubbish disposal, municipal waterworks and public markets among its obligatory functions.

## Office

The [corporation contact page](http://www.tumakurucity.mrc.gov.in/en/contact-us) lists Townhall, Bhagwan Mahaveer Road, near the railway station, Gandhinagar, Tumakuru 572102, with 0816-2272200 and `ka.tumakuru.cc@gmail.com`. The district directory puts 572102 in its street address but 572101 in a separate postcode field. This record follows the corporation's own contact page for the mailing address.

# Tumakuru online municipal payments and grievances

## Municipal payments

The [corporation home page](http://www.tumakurucity.mrc.gov.in/en/home) advertises a single [property search link](https://tumakurucitycorp.org/WebSite/Citizen/PropertySearch.aspx) for property tax, water and underground drainage (UGD) charges. The form shows two options: search by property ID or search by criteria. For people without a property ID, the form instructions name a ward number and an old or new assessment number. No property search or payment was submitted for this record.

The separate [Citizen Services directory](http://www.tumakurucity.mrc.gov.in/en/citizen-services) lists [E-Aasthi](https://eaasthi.karnataka.gov.in/) for property records, [Jalanidhi](http://www.mrc.gov.in/jalanidhi/) for tap and UGD connections, and [eJanMa](https://ejanma.karnataka.gov.in/frmLogin.aspx) for birth and death registration.

## Grievances

The home page directs people to [Karnataka iPGRS](https://ipgrs.karnataka.gov.in/) or 1902 to register a public grievance. It labels 155304 as toll-free and 9449872599 as a public WhatsApp number. The [contact page](http://www.tumakurucity.mrc.gov.in/en/contact-us) also lists the corporation's main telephone, 0816-2272200, and `ka.tumakuru.cc@gmail.com`.

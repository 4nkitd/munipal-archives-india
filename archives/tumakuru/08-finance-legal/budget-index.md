# Tumakuru municipal budget and audit index

## Budget and audit coverage

The corporation's [Budget page](http://www.tumakurucity.mrc.gov.in/en/budget) lists seven fiscal years, 2020-21 through 2026-27, in descending order. It describes each budget as covering revenue receipts and payments, capital receipts and payments, and extraordinary receipts and payments. Some downloads are hosted on the corporation's portal and others are linked through Google Drive.

The separate [FSA Audit page](http://www.tumakurucity.mrc.gov.in/en/fsa-audit) lists FSA Audit Reports (CA) for seven years, 2018-19 through 2024-25. The two indexes do not show approved budget totals, actual receipts or expenditure, or audit findings. The linked attachments were not used to extract figures.

# Udaipur municipal election results for November 2019

## Polling and seats

The Rajasthan State Election Commission's calendar dates the ward poll to 16 November 2019 and the count to 19 November 2019. Its Udaipur Nagar Nigam polling table lists 346,501 registered electors and 200,315 votes recorded by the final reporting interval, or 57.81%. The report lists Kanod under a separate local body.

| Party or affiliation | Udaipur seats |
|---|---:|
| Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) | 44 |
| Indian National Congress (INC) | 20 |
| Independents | 5 |
| Communist Party of India (Marxist), CPI(M) | 1 |
| Total | 70 |

## Leadership election and source reading

The report records Govind Tank as the elected chairperson and Paras Singhavi, as spelled in the report, as deputy chairperson. The election calendar lists 26 and 27 November 2019 for those contests, respectively.

The archive date is the report's signed preface date, 20 January 2020. In the printed pagination, see page 1 for the election calendar, page 32 for the polling table, page 48 for party seats, pages 175–176 for ward-level results, and pages 178 and 182 for the elected chairperson and deputy. The results describe the 2019 contest, not the officeholders or ward boundaries in force today.

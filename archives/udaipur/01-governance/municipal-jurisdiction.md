# Udaipur municipal office and jurisdiction

## Municipal limits and history

Udaipur Municipal Corporation, also called Nagar Nigam Udaipur, is the city local body in Udaipur district, Rajasthan. A district address alone does not establish that an address falls inside municipal limits. The [Rajasthan State Election Commission's report on the 2019 election](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/Upload/ULB_Report_2019.pdf#page=12) lists Udaipur as a Nagar Nigam and lists Kanod separately as a Nagarpalika in the same district, on printed pages 7–8.

The [District Collector's 27 March 2025 notice](https://udaipurmc.org/uploads/1743257048-4388.pdf) cites the state Local Self Government Department's 26 December 2024 notification on the inclusion of new gram panchayat or revenue-village areas within corporation limits. The March notice invited objections to proposed ward boundaries. The [final Rajasthan Gazette notification DLB/25/19001](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files/Content/UploadFolder/DepartmentMaster/215/2026/Apr/30409/2158fa11fb5-68d6-4192-9164-f152a35a0f69.pdf), dated 1 September and published on 20 September 2025, approves the 80-ward schedule.

The corporation's [institutional history](https://udaipurmc.org/pages/aboutumc) dates the first municipal board to December 1922, the formation of a corporation to July 1948, and the constitution of a municipal council to 13 October 1959. These dates come from the corporation's historical account, not from the original instruments.

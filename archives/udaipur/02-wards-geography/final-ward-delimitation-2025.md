# Udaipur's final 80-ward delimitation in the September 2025 Gazette

## Approval and publication

The Rajasthan Local Self Government Department's notification **DLB/25/19001** is dated **1 September 2025**. The *Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary*, Part 6(क), published it on **20 September 2025**, the archive date. It cites sections 3 and 10 of the Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 2009 and rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Election) Rules, 1994. After considering objections and the District Collector's recommendations, the state approved the ward formation order for Nagar Nigam Udaipur.

The approved Form A schedule numbers wards **1–80**. The [State Election Commission's 19 August 2026 election programme](https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/List_merged.pdf#page=7) independently lists Udaipur Municipal Corporation with 80 wards in its second-phase appendix. This is separate from the 70 wards in the November 2019 contest and from the March 2025 proposal.

## Reading the boundaries

Ward 1 starts at Badgaon khasra no. 1 and describes the Badgaon police outpost and a crossing of the Madar Canal. Ward 80 starts on Gogunda Road in Badgaon and its description refers to revenue plots in Bedla Khurd and the Ayad River. These are landmarks in the legal descriptions, not ward names or assignments of whole localities. The archive does not infer address assignments, polygons, councillors, or a crosswalk to the 2019 wards.

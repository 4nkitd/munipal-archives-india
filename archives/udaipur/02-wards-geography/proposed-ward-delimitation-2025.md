# Udaipur's proposed 80 wards and March 2025 objections notice

## What the notice proposed

The Office of the District Collector, Udaipur, issued notice `न.नि.उ./2024-25/276` on 27 March 2025. It records 70 previously constituted wards and 80 in the new delimitation proposal. It cites the Rajasthan Local Self Government Department's municipal inclusion notification dated 26 December 2024 and its order no. 1624 of 13 February 2025. The notice refers to newly included gram panchayat or revenue-village areas.

The public could inspect the proposal in the corporation's mini meeting hall, room 21, or room 251 at the District Collector's office on working days. Written objections could be submitted to the Additional District Collector (Administration) and the Udaipur Municipal Commissioner through 17 April 2025. That deadline has passed.

## Map and ward descriptions

The linked scan contains the notice, a map and a Form A schedule of proposed boundaries based on 2011 population. The corporation's [notices page](https://udaipurmc.org/downloads?page=notices) also links a [standalone map](https://udaipurmc.org/uploads/1743315319-7885.pdf). Its printed heading calls the 80 wards *proposed*, even though the download listing describes the map as post-delimitation. The schedule uses plot numbers and landmarks, such as the Badgaon police outpost in the description of proposed Ward 1.

This is the historical objections notice, not the approved boundary schedule. The state subsequently issued [DLB/25/19001](https://jankalyanfile.rajasthan.gov.in/Files/Content/UploadFolder/DepartmentMaster/215/2026/Apr/30409/2158fa11fb5-68d6-4192-9164-f152a35a0f69.pdf), dated 1 September 2025 and published on 20 September 2025. Use that Gazette schedule, rather than the draft map, for the approved ward descriptions.

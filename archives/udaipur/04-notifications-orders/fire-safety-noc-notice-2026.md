# Udaipur fire safety and fire NOC public notice of 12 June 2026

## Application and renewal

The corporation's Fire Section notice, no. `अग्नि/ननिउ/2026-27/201` and RajKaj reference **22785023**, addresses fire safety at premises such as hotels, guest houses, restaurants, marriage gardens, multiplexes and coaching institutions. It cites the National Building Code 2016, Part 4, and directs covered premises to use **SSO ID Login → LSG Online Service → Fire NOC**.

The notice calls for an online renewal application 15 days before the certificate expires, followed by a site inspection. It specifies a ₹5,000 penalty for failures including not renewing on time. A fire NOC does not by itself establish ownership, regularise a building, or substitute for building, occupancy, completion or land-use approvals.

## Scope

The source is a scanned notice, read visually. It describes circumstances where the competent authority may also issue a fire NOC for covered premises in the Udaipur Development Authority area and notified urban industrial areas after checking fire equipment. That service provision is not a statement that those areas all lie within the municipal boundary. This archive does not establish whether an individual building needs a fire NOC or whether a later order supersedes the notice.

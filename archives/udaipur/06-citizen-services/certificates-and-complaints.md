# Udaipur certificate and sanitation complaint channels

## Birth, death and marriage certificates

The corporation links its birth and death certificate entry to the state [Pehchan homepage](https://pehchan.raj.nic.in/pehchan3/Mainpage.aspx). Its separate marriage certificate entry links to [Pehchan's marriage registration page](https://pehchan.raj.nic.in/pehchan3/P_MarriageRegistration.aspx). These are the links the corporation publishes, not verified application or certificate issuance instructions. The Pehchan host did not resolve during collection.

## Sanitation complaints

The corporation describes the **Udaipur City App** as a way to register sanitation complaints and check their status and history. The Citizen Services menu links to the [Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.udaipurmc.app). The site also links the state [Rajasthan Sampark](https://sampark.rajasthan.gov.in/) grievance portal and publishes **0294-2426262** as its own helpline. No complaint was submitted.

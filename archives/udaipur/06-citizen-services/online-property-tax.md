# Udaipur property tax search and payment portal

## Find a property account

The municipal [Citizen Services menu](https://udaipurmc.org/) links **Property Tax Online** to Raj Municipal's Search Property page. Select **Nagar Nigam Udaipur** under Urban Local Bodies. The form has a ward selector and search fields for service number, acknowledgement number, mobile number and owner name.

The portal links **View Payment Details** and **Pay Online** to the same property search. Use the ward options the payment portal supplies, rather than treating the 2025 draft delimitation map as a tax-account ward directory. The account search and payment were not tested with a resident's data. If you cannot identify the account, the corporation lists **0294-2426262** as its municipal helpline.

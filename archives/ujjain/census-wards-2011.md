# Ujjain municipal wards in the 2011 Primary Census Abstract

## Edition and coverage

Census table `PC11_PCA-TV-2318` identifies Ujjain by town code `802230`. The catalog record was created on 19 January 2021; the figures refer to 2011. The [City Finance register](https://www.cityfinance.in/api/v1/ulb) separately reports 54 wards for LGD `250851`. Numbering in the census is not evidence of present electoral boundaries.

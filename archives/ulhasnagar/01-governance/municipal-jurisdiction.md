# Ulhasnagar municipal jurisdiction and office

## District register

Entry 5 in Thane district’s Municipal Corporations register links the [Ulhasnagar Municipal Corporation](https://www.umc.gov.in/). The corporation’s [location table](https://umc.gov.in/api/api/location-info?lang=en) gives an area of 13.50 square kilometres and a 2011 census population of 506,098.

## Correspondence

The district [telephone directory](https://thane.nic.in/en/telephone-directory/) lists `commissionerulhasnagar@gmail.com` for the corporation under Corporations & Councils.

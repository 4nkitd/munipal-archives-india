# Ulhasnagar wards and seats in the 2025-26 general election

## Declared results

Row 5, labelled “Ulhasnagar”, records 20 electoral wards, 432 contesting candidates and results declared for all 78 seats. The [commission’s publication register](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/ViewAllNews) dates the final party position to 16 January 2026.

| Party or SEC category | Seats won |
|---|---:|
| BJP | 37 |
| SS | 36 |
| INC | 1 |
| Unrecognized parties registered with SEC | 3 |
| Independent | 1 |
| **Total** | **78** |

The table reports corporation-wide totals. It does not identify which ward covers a particular address.

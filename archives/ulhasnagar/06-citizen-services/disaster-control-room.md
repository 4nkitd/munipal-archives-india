# Ulhasnagar municipal disaster control room

## Municipal control room

For a disaster-management incident in Ulhasnagar, Thane district lists **0251-2720143** against the corporation in row 11 of its control-room directory.

| Level | Office | Control room |
|---|---|---|
| Municipal | Ulhasnagar Municipal Corporation | 0251-2720143 |
| Divisional | Commissioner Office, Konkan Division | 022-27571516 |
| National | National Disaster Management Authority | 1078 |

The divisional and national numbers are separate entries in the same directory.

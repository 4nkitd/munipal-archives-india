# Ulhasnagar expression of interest for a municipal solid waste facility

## Municipal notice 144

The English news register lists “Expression of Interest for Setting up of Municipal Solid Waste Processing & Disposal Facility.” Its issue date is 31 May 2016. The entry links the [original notice](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1C7ai7eHJ2G97uVD6ngH25CrxWS5xbcpK/view).

The notice identifies both processing and disposal as the intended functions. The register entry does not give a project cost, plant capacity, work order or awardee.

# Ulhasnagar budget publication for 2025-26

## Budget register entry 34

On its [Budget page](https://www.umc.gov.in/budget), the corporation publishes an entry titled “Budget Details 2025-26”. The underlying English register identifies the year as 2025-2026, gives the issue date as 16 July 2025 and links the [budget document](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1GyoAi2mvP7PEI8f-NZiT0qx6M-EtKVC9/view?usp=sharing).

The register also links separate documents for 2018-19 and 2019-20. Its rows for 2014-15, 2015-16 and 2017-18 have no document target. The register itself gives no budget totals or approval date.

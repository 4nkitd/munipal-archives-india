# Formation of Municipal Corporation Una

## Effective date

The Governor declared Municipal Council Una, District Una, to be a corporation named Municipal Corporation Una under section 3(2) of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1994 (State Act No. 12 of 1994). Notification No. UD-A(1)-3/2022 is dated 23 December 2024. It expressly takes effect on publication, which occurred on 26 December 2024. The authoritative English text is on printed page 11000 of that issue of the Rajpatra.

## Municipal jurisdiction

The declaration concerns the municipal body for Una city, not all of Una district. A separate notification in the same issue, beginning on printed page 10624, includes scheduled areas in the former council's limits. Its parcel schedule supplies the boundary additions; the corporation declaration does not assign parcels to wards.

# Inclusion of scheduled Jalgran parcels in Municipal Corporation Una

## Which land was included

Notification No. UD-A(1)-3/2022-Part-1, dated 10 March 2026, includes the scheduled land under section 3(3) of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. It took effect on publication on 11 March 2026. The authoritative English text begins on printed page 12836 of the Rajpatra.

The schedule marks Jalgran as partial. It identifies the patwar circle as Bharolian Kalan and the panchayat as Tabba. The listed khasras include 425, 426, 1699/427 and 1700/427. These are examples; inclusion depends on the full schedule, not the mohal or panchayat name alone.

## Exemption and customary rights

The notification exempts land and buildings in the schedule from property tax for three years from 11 March 2026 under sections 3(4) and 143(1). It preserves residents' customary rights as provided in the Wazib-Ul-Urj record of rights. This is a separate exemption from the one for areas added in December 2024.

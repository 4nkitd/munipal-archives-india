# Una municipal boundary additions and property-tax exemption

## Which properties were added

Notification No. UD-A(1)-3/2022, signed 23 December 2024 and published 26 December 2024, includes the listed areas in Municipal Council Una under section 5(3) of the Himachal Pradesh Municipal Act, 1994. This is a separate instrument from the corporation declaration published in the same issue. Its English text begins on printed page 10624 and the parcel schedule starts on page 10625.

The schedule identifies areas by patwar circle, revenue village and khasra number. It marks Jhalera in Takka-II patwar circle, and Ajnoli in Kotla patwar circle, as complete. It marks two Rainsary entries as partial. These are examples from the schedule, not a full list. Use the gazette schedule to check individual parcels.

## Property-tax treatment

Under section 5(4) read with section 80(1), the notification exempts land and buildings in the schedule from property tax for three years from publication on 26 December 2024. It also preserves residents' customary rights as provided in the Wazib-Ul-Urj record of rights. The exemption applies to the scheduled areas, not to every property in Una.

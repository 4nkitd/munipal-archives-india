# Una property taxation bye-laws, 2026

## Final publication and assessment method

Municipal Corporation Una issued No. MCU/P.Tax bye Laws/Comm/2026-1099 on 30 April 2026, after publishing the draft on 28 March 2026 for objections and suggestions. The final bye-laws took effect when published on 6 May 2026. They begin on printed page 2361 of the Rajpatra.

The bye-laws define the unit area in square metres. Their assessment provisions distinguish Zone A, the old municipal area, and Zone B, the areas merged under the December 2024 inclusion notification. The five factors for rateable value are location, structure, building age, occupancy and use. Placement in a tax zone does not itself establish whether a property is still covered by a separate notified exemption.

## Checking or disputing an assessment

The Commissioner must publish a notice stating where the assessment list can be inspected. For an amendment to the assessment list, a person served with notice may object within 30 days of receipt. The bye-laws provide for payment at the corporation's head office or another notified location and describe payment methods. This record does not establish the amount payable for a particular property.

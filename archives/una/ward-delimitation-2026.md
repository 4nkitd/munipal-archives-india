# Una corporation ward delimitation, April 2026

## Notification and scope

The State Election Commission issued Notification No. SEC-HP-F002(01)/1/2021-II-8953 on 8 April 2026 after receiving the Deputy Commissioner's final delimitation order. It was published in the Rajpatra on 9 April 2026, beginning on printed page 699. The Una section of Annexure A lists 17 wards. A separate Bir Nagar Panchayat schedule in the same notification is outside Una corporation.

Annexure A says all of revenue mohal Lalsingi falls in ward 1. It identifies both parts of Rainsary and all of Jhalera in the description of ward 2. Use the annexure's full descriptions for address-level checks rather than assuming ward names and revenue boundaries match.

The [10 April 2026 electoral roll programme](https://sechimachal.hp.gov.in/assets/files/DraftElectoralProgrammeforMCUnaandNPBir.pdf) set 1 April 2026 as the qualifying date and scheduled final roll publication by 3 May 2026. The [Commission's 2026 general election report](https://sechimachal.hp.gov.in/assets/files/GeneralElectionReportToLocalBodies2026.pdf) notes that Una corporation elections were not held in the round covered by that report. Neither the delimitation nor the roll programme establishes who represents a ward.

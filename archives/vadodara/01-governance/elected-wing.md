# Who governs Vadodara Municipal Corporation

## Representation and voting

VMC identifies three statutory authorities under section 4 of the 1949 provincial municipal corporations law: the General Board, the Standing Committee and the Municipal Commissioner. All 76 elected councillors sit on the General Board. Each of the 19 electoral wards elects four councillors for five years. Half the seats are reserved for women.

The Commissioner sets the first meeting after a general election. The Board elects the Mayor and Deputy Mayor from among its members and appoints 12 councillors to the Standing Committee. The Mayor sets subsequent Board meetings; the Board must meet at least monthly. The Commissioner can explain facts and, with permission, join the discussion, but cannot vote or move a proposition.

## Works contracts

The Standing Committee meets weekly. It has financial powers and can authorize the Commissioner to enter into works contracts. VMC also lists nine special committees for particular functions.

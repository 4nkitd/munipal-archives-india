# Vadodara administrative ward offices by zone

## Where to take a ward issue

For revenue and sanitation work, VMC directs residents to the ward officer or the zone's Assistant Municipal Commissioner. For engineering work, contact the Deputy Executive Engineer or the zone's Executive Engineer.

## Office locations

VMC's [Wardwise Area table](https://vmc.gov.in/pdf/administation%20ward%20Final.pdf) pairs each administrative ward from 1 to 19 with the electoral ward of the same number. The PDF was created on 1 April 2022; that is not a legal commencement date. The table establishes the numbering correspondence, not the current electoral boundary of a particular property.

The landmarks below locate administrative offices. For Ward 19, the [geolocated office list](https://vmc.gov.in/pdf/2025/Ward%20Office%20Address28102025.pdf) confirms the direction in the Gujarati directory entry.

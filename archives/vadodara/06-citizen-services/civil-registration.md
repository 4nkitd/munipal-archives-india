# Birth, death and marriage registration in Vadodara

## Registration and certificates

The registration office at Manjalpur Sports Complex handles births, deaths and marriages. The department issues certificates after checking documents. Its English guidance describes online and urban primary health centre registration for births and deaths from 22 May 2015 to December 2020 and from 1 January 2021 onward.

## Requesting a certificate copy

The Gujarati notice says copies for births and deaths from 2 May 2015 through 31 December 2020 are available at the nearest urban health centre. The [online copy application](https://vmc.gov.in/birthdeathapp/AppOnline.aspx) offers certified or PDF copies for births and deaths. Its event-date choices are “on or before 31 December 2020” and “after 31 December 2020 to 31 August 2025.” The form has no option for a later event or a marriage certificate. For a later event, ask the registration office which service to use.

## Cash counter and enquiries

The cash counter operates from 10:30 am to 3:30 pm, with lunch from 2:00 to 2:30 pm. The Gujarati office notice lists closures on Sundays, second and fourth Saturdays, and public holidays. The office enquiry number during working hours is 0265-2417422.

## Registration totals

The department reports 29,456 birth registrations, 21,016 death registrations and 11,137 marriage registrations for 2024. These are entries in the registers, not a city population estimate.

# Vadodara municipal payment and revenue audit

## Who checks a payment

The Audit Department says all payments undergo pre-audit. Its expenditure checks cover public works, salaries, pensions and pay fixation. It lists the following delegation by payment amount:

| Payment amount | Pre-audit authority |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹1,00,000 | Auditor |
| ₹1,00,001 to ₹5,00,000 | Senior Auditor |
| ₹5,00,001 and above | Chief Auditor |

## Revenue and reporting

The department checks revenue after the transaction. The review covers ward, store, department and zone accounts, including cash books and bank books. The Corporation appoints the Chief Auditor. The department cites sections 47, 105 and 106 of the Gujarat provincial municipal corporations law of 1949 for Standing Committee supervision and submits its annual audit report to that committee.

The department separately describes the Local Fund audit under section 108A, carried out in consultation with the Comptroller and Auditor General. That report goes before the Assembly.

# How to find Vadodara's budget, fee schedules and accounts

## FY 2026-27 budget documents

For the municipal budget, select the FY 2026-27 Budget Book under **Budget**. VMC lists the Municipal Commissioner's speech and budget presentation separately. The numerical annexures appear as *Parishisht 1 to 6*. For fees and charges, use *Lagat (Parishisht-10)*. The tax-rate document is a separate entry under *Kar-Dar*. Other FY 2026-27 entries include the Work List and the Establishment Schedule.

## Accounts and debt

For accounts rather than estimates, the department indexes annual reports and audit reports, including FY 2025-26. Its financial statements distinguish revenue income and expenditure, own-source capital income and expenditure, all-source capital expenditure, and borrowings. For municipal bonds and green municipal bonds, the index lists separate placement memoranda and repayment schedules. The budget index itself gives no rupee totals.

# Constitution of the Municipal Corporation of the City of Vapi

## What the notification did

Gujarat Gazette notification KV-11 of 2025, file UDUHD/COC/e-file/18/2024/6141/P Section, constituted the corporation from 1 January 2025 under Article 243Q and section 5(1) of the Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949. Clause (ii) brought the remaining provisions of the Act into force in the city on the same day.

## Territory at formation

The corporation covers the larger urban area declared by companion notification GV-10 of 2025. The [corporation](https://vapicity.in/) identifies the former municipality and 12 surrounding villages: Namdha, Chandor, Balitha, Chhiri, Charwada, Salvav, Chanod, Vatar, Kunta, Tarakpardi, Morai, Karvad.

# Vapi elected-wing ward index

## Finding a ward entry

The corporation's Elected Wing page offers a name search and a ward selector. Choose a numbered ward rather than “All Wards” to narrow the directory. The selector lists Ward 1 through Ward 13. These numbers identify electoral wards; the directory does not describe their boundaries.

The [state notification index](https://udd.gujarat.gov.in/notification_p.php) separately lists the Vapi determination of wards and seats dated 9 July 2025.

# Vapi Urban Development Authority draft plan 2045: publication and maps

## Reading the publication

The plan page identifies the publication under section 13(1) of the Gujarat Town Planning and Urban Development Act, 1976. The first item is the publication notice; the next two are the existing land-use map and the proposed land-use map for the whole development authority area. Proposed uses are part of the draft.

## Finding the map sheet

The six detail sheets are A1, A2, B1, B2, B3 and C2. For example, the index lists Vapi, Namdha and Chandor on B1; Karvad appears on B2 and B3; Chanod and Dungra appear on B2 and C2. These are planning grid references, not electoral wards.

# Vapi property tax lookup by property number

## Looking up the bill

Open Property Tax under Citizen Quick Services on the [corporation homepage](https://vapicity.in/). The payment page asks for the **Property Number**. Enter the number and choose **Show Payable Amount**. The same page offers a **Property Tax Receipt/Bill List**. The corporation lists “Property Tax Village” separately on its homepage and directs that option to the citizen login.

# Vapi revenue expenditure estimates for 2025-26

## Selected revenue expenditure heads

The [Budget Details index](https://vapicity.in/budgetdetails) links the 2025-26 budget separately from the five preceding financial years. The expenditure schedule's final column gives the estimate for 2025-26. The figures below are rupees.

| Revenue head | Estimated total 2024-25 | Estimate 2025-26 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Public safety and firefighting | 1,553,261 | 6,000,000 |
| Water supply | 130,555,262 | 178,600,000 |
| Sanitation | 140,766,470 | 405,200,000 |

The 2024-25 column combines expenditure through December with an estimate for the remaining quarter. Neither column is an audited full-year actual.

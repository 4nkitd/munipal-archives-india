# Varanasi Nagar Nigam and the extension of its municipal limits

## What the notification covers

The Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Department's December 2019 notification invokes Article 243-Q(2) of the Constitution and Section 3(2) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959. It specifies the area in the schedule as a larger urban area known as Nagar Nigam, Varanasi, with effect from publication in the Gazette. The municipal site labels the attachment “Gazette 84 Villages,” but the schedule is parcel-specific. It does not say that every named revenue village was included in full.

| Schedule example | What the scan specifies |
| --- | --- |
| Bhagwanpur, Kashi Vidyapeeth block | Whole revenue village, plots 1 to 554, 138.000 hectares |
| Daaphee | Part of the village, with individual chak numbers listed |
| Domri, Kashi Vidyapeeth block | Whole revenue village, plots 1 to 315, 386.070 hectares |
| Sujabad, Kashi Vidyapeeth block | Whole revenue village, plots 1 to 229, 79.119 hectares |
| Chandpur | Part of the village, with listed gata numbers and 67.000 hectares |

The first PDF page shows Bhagwanpur and starts the Daaphee entry. The final page of the scan shows the Hindi entries for Domri, Sujabad and Chandpur. For a parcel-level decision, check the plot number in the original schedule and any later changes. The date of the notification and the date of Gazette publication are not interchangeable.

## Which body is responsible

The corporation's [About VNN page](https://nnvns.org.in/nnvns/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=3&Itemid=121&lang=en) dates its establishment as a Nagar Mahapalika to 24 January 1959 and its change to Nagar Nigam to 1994. It describes an elected council headed by the mayor and lists municipal services such as roads, solid waste, water and sewerage. Its figure of 90 wards does not match the [2023 State Election Commission dashboard](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ResultDashboard2023.aspx), which reports 100 corporation councillor seats for Varanasi district. The dashboard and [turnout table](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ULBPolling2023.aspx) distinguish the corporation from Gangapur Nagar Panchayat. The corporation's final ward schedule also names Ramnagar wards within the Nigam.

The [district directory](https://varanasi.nic.in/public-utility/nagar-nigam-varanasi/) identifies the corporation's municipal office at Sigra. [Varanasi Smart City Limited](https://varanasismartcity.gov.in/) identifies itself as the special-purpose vehicle for implementing Smart City Mission projects. Its project boundaries and publications do not establish municipal limits or substitute for Nagar Nigam records.

# Final delimitation of Varanasi Nagar Nigam's 100 wards

## The order and its schedule

The Uttar Pradesh Urban Development Department issued final notification `3474/9-1-2022-55Pari/22` on 10 November 2022 under Section 32-B of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959. The order says the Governor considered objections following preliminary notification `2885/9-1-2022-55Pari/2022` of 26 September 2022, then divided the municipal area into 100 wards. The Hindi forwarding instruction on PDF page 16 directs publication in the Extraordinary Gazette of 10 November 2022; the English translation begins on PDF page 17 and gives the same date.

The schedule's four columns give the ward number, ward name, directional boundaries and extent, including the names of constituent mohallas. Some locality entries include house-number ranges, portions of a named locality, or revenue villages.

These examples come from PDF pages 1, 2 and 32. Use the complete row in the linked schedule for a particular address instead of relying on a locality name or an isolated boundary landmark. The [State Election Commission's 2023 results dashboard](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ResultDashboard2023.aspx) independently reports 100 corporation councillor posts and 100 results for Varanasi. It confirms the election count but is not a boundary description.

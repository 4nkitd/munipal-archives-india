# Final reservation of Varanasi councillor seats for the 2023 election

## Notification and selected entries

Notification `961/9-1-2023-17आर/22`, dated 9 April 2023, is the final order for the councillor seats of Varanasi Nagar Nigam. It cites Section 7(1), (3) and (4) of the Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959 and the amended 1994 rules on reservation and allotment of seats and offices. It records consideration of objections and suggestions to notification `790/नौ-1-2023-17आर/22` of 30 March 2023. The forwarding instruction specifies the Extraordinary Gazette of 9 April 2023, legislative supplement, Part 4, Section (b).

The schedule is organized by ward number, ward name and reservation. The table below is a sample from PDF pages 2 and 4; it does not show all 100 entries.

The categories shown here are Scheduled Caste woman, Scheduled Caste, Backward Class woman, unreserved, woman and Backward Class. The reservation schedule concerns eligibility for councillor seats in that election. It neither sets ward boundaries nor identifies the winning councillors or current officeholders. For the election outcome, use the [Uttar Pradesh State Election Commission's 2023 results](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ULBElection2023.html). In its [polling table](https://sec.up.nic.in/site/ULBPolling2023.aspx), row 333 records Varanasi Nagar Nigam in phase 1 with final turnout of 40.42%.

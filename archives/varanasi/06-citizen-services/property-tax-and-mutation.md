# Property tax bills, assessment objections and mutation in Varanasi

## Find the right municipal form

| Task | Official page | What the page asks for or provides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pay property, water or sewage tax | [Municipal payment page](https://nnvns.org.in/PropertyPayment/Mutation_Citizen/ViewPropInvoice.aspx) | Offers searches by house number, 17-digit unique number or MISID, or WS consumer number. |
| Look up a bill | [View Property Tax Bill](https://nnvns.org.in/PropertyPayment/Mutation_Citizen/ViewBill.aspx) | Requests the house number rather than a full address and gives `CY.8/91` as an example. |
| Read a GIS demand notice | [View Demand Notice](https://nnvns.org.in/PropertyPayment/Mutation_Citizen/ViewProGISInvoice.aspx) | Offers house number and House Unique Number lookup. |
| Self-assess or object to an assessment or GIS demand | [Municipal citizen login](https://nnvns.org.in/VNNEXISTINGDATA/VNNLIVE/Mutation_citizen/Login.aspx) | The municipal E-Services menu routes all three actions to this mobile-number and password login. |
| Check how tax is calculated | [Property Tax Calculation Method](https://nnvns.org.in/nnvns/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=194&Itemid=398&lang=en) | Links government orders and rules, ward-wise rates, the self-assessment form and the calculation method. |

The bill and demand notice pages say that their older House Unique ID format expects 10 characters. Their instructions say to append two hyphens to an eight-character ID or one hyphen to a nine-character ID. The 17-digit search option is on the payment page, not those older lookup forms.

If a completed payment leaves a nonzero outstanding amount or the receipt does not download, the payment page tells users not to pay again. It directs them to email transaction screenshots to `nnvns.mpos@gmail.com` and use Payment History with the registered mobile number to retrieve the receipt. This is the portal's instruction, not a guarantee of when an individual payment will clear.

## Mutation of the municipal property record

The corporation links to the state [e-NagarSewa citizen portal](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/onlinepay/) for online mutation. The state portal says registration is tied to a specific city or urban local body, so choose the corporation for a Varanasi Nagar Nigam property. The municipal menu separately links [mutation instructions](https://nnvns.org.in/nnvns/attachments/article/9/Mut_Instruction.pdf), [required documents](https://nnvns.org.in/nnvns/attachments/article/9/NN_Mutation_Documents.pdf), the [fee schedule](https://nnvns.org.in/nnvns/attachments/article/214/mutation_fee_structure.pdf) and [application tracking](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/ulbappsmain/mutationtrack). The mutation workflow is separate from paying a bill or objecting to an assessment.

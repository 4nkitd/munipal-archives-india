# New water and sewer connections in Varanasi

## Apply and check the dashboard

The corporation's SOP places new water and sewer connection applications on the Government of Uttar Pradesh [e-NagarSewa portal](https://e-nagarsewaup.gov.in/onlinepay/). The state portal lists the service as “New Water / Sewerage Connection” and says a citizen account is registered against a specific urban local body.

1. Use Register if you are a new applicant. If already registered, sign in using your mobile number and password.
2. Open Water Connection from the citizen menu. Read the application-flow pop-up and select Proceed.
3. Complete the form and upload the requested identity and property documents. The SOP lists a voter ID, driving licence, PAN card or passport as examples of identity proof. For the property document it names the first and last pages of a lease agreement, rent agreement or registered deed, in PDF form no larger than 200 KB.
4. Submit the application. After a satisfactory departmental inspection report, the SOP says an online payment link appears in the applicant's dashboard.
5. Pay through that link. Return to the dashboard to check further departmental action. The SOP does not give a fixed fee or installation deadline.

## Connection applications and cleaning requests use different channels

The [corporation homepage](https://nnvns.org.in/) publishes 1533 for municipal complaints and separately lists 14420 for sewer and septic tank cleaning. A cleaning request is not an application for a new connection. The homepage also links the [Jal Kal website](https://www.jalkalvaranasi.org/) under JalKal Vibhag. The connection process above comes from the corporation's own published SOP, rather than a Smart City project page.

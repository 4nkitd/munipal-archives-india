# Varanasi Nagar Nigam's proposed original budget for 2026-27

## Income, spending and balances

The corporation's *Mul Budget 2026-27* labels its two summary columns “Revised Budget 2025-26” and “Proposed Original Budget 2026-27.” The following figures are in ₹ lakh, as printed on pages 1 and 2 of the budget (PDF pages 2 and 3).

| Budget line | 2025-26 revised | 2026-27 proposed original |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Revenue income | 68,140.40 | 107,574.40 |
| Capital income | 51,326.78 | 110,185.00 |
| Total revenue, capital and reserve fund income | 125,351.18 | 217,993.40 |
| Opening balance | 7,095.25 | 8,248.75 |
| Grand total | 132,446.43 | 226,242.15 |
| Revenue expenses | 56,112.40 | 88,826.40 |
| Capital expenses | 66,770.28 | 128,353.00 |
| Net revenue and capital expenses, including other current assets line | 124,197.68 | 218,494.40 |
| Closing balance | 8,248.75 | 7,747.75 |

The grand total includes the opening balance on the income side and the closing balance on the expense side. It is not an expenditure-only figure. The proposed capital income includes ₹68,925.00 lakh in central government grants for specific purposes and ₹41,260.00 lakh in state grants. The expense schedule separately provides ₹4,525.00 lakh for projects under the municipal bond.

## Property tax and audited accounts

Printed page 3 distinguishes revised estimates from collections. It lists actual property tax income of ₹9,502.49 lakh for 2024-25 and ₹4,723.21 lakh for April through December 2025. Neither is the 2025-26 revised estimate of ₹12,500.00 lakh. The proposed original property tax estimate for 2026-27 is ₹15,000.00 lakh.

The [municipal financial statements index](https://nnvns.org.in/nnvns/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=68:budget-and-balance-sheets&lang=en&catid=17&Itemid=254) separately lists the [audited balance sheet as at 31 March 2026](https://nnvns.org.in/nnvns/attachments/article/68/25-26%20Audited%20Balance%20Sheet.pdf), its audit report and a Jalkal financial data section. Those are distinct records. The proposed budget is not an audited outturn, and the Nagar Nigam figures are not Smart City company accounts.

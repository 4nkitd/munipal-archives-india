# Formation and municipal scope of Vasai Virar

## Municipal and planning areas

VVCMC's corporation profile dates its formation to 3 July 2009 and describes the original combination as four municipal councils and 53 villages. It reports a municipal area of 311 km² and a 2011 Census population of 12.22 lakh. The historical village count describes formation, not a schedule of today's municipal limits.

The profile also reports a special planning authority for 21 villages and an area of 380 km² when that jurisdiction is included. The larger planning figure is not the municipal area. VVCMC's [city profile](https://vvcmc.in/en/about-us/) locates the city in Palghar district, north of Vasai Creek and Mumbai.

## Administrative wards and head office

The corporation profile identifies nine administrative wards, A through I. Its reference to 115 electoral wards conflicts with the final delimitation: the October 2025 Maharashtra Gazette specifies 115 councillors across 29 electoral wards.

The [municipal contact page](https://vvcmc.in/en/contacts/) lists the head office at Virat Nagar, near MHADA Colony, Virar West, Maharashtra 401303. Use that municipal listing for office directions; the [district directory](https://palghar.gov.in/en/public-utility/vasai-virar-city-municipal-corporation/) gives a different head office address in Virar East.

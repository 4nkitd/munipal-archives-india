# Vasai Virar final ward delimitation for the 2025-26 election

## Gazette and seat allocation

The Maharashtra Government Gazette, Extraordinary Part Two, No. 178, published the final notification on 6 October 2025. The notification is dated 3 October 2025, cites section 5(3) of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949, and refers to objections and suggestions on draft notification No. 64/2025-26 dated 22 August 2025. It applies to the next general election following the notification.

The schedule sets 28 four-member wards and one three-member ward, Ward 29, for 115 seats across 29 wards. The [state election table](https://mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/PDF/Municipal%20Corporations%20final%20party%20wise%20position.pdf) confirms those ward and seat totals. The [separate results gazette](https://vvcmc.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/GazetteSearch-5.pdf) states that the results were declared on 16 January 2026 and publishes them on 19 January 2026.

## Find a boundary

The English schedule starts on page 32. It lists localities, four directional boundaries, population and seat counts for each ward. For example, Ward 1 includes Chandip, Chandansar and Shirsad, and the schedule cites Vaitarna Creek on its northern side. Ward 29 includes Kolivada, Pachubandar and Killa Bandar and has Vasai Creek to the south and the Arabian Sea to the west.

Use the [final ward map index](https://vvcmc.in/election/) for a particular property or street. The gazette says the mapped boundary prevails if the Marathi and English descriptions differ and that a map is available for inspection at the corporation office during office hours.

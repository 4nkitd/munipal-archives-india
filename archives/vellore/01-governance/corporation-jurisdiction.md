# Vellore corporation jurisdiction and the statewide municipal law

## Founding law and statutory continuation

Tamil Nadu Act 26 of 2008, the Vellore City Municipal Corporation Act, constituted the corporation. The Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies (Amendment) Act, 2022, published in Gazette Extraordinary issue 352 on 20 June 2022, identifies the corporation under amended section 4(1-A)(ix) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998. That provision deems the listed corporations constituted and incorporated under the statewide Act in their respective areas. The amendment also added the 2008 Vellore Act to the repeal list in section 200(1). The [commencement order](https://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2023/123_Ex_II_2_2023.pdf) put the amendment into force on 13 April 2023.

## Corporation limits and the planning area

The [district planning office's account](https://vellore.nic.in/district-town-and-country-planning-vellore-district/) identifies the old Vellore corporation area plus former local bodies, including Sathuvachari, Dharapadavedu and Katpadi, as now within the corporation. It cites Municipal Administration and Water Supply (Elections) G.O. (Ms) No. 221 dated 28 September 2010. A separate [gazette proposal dated 18 October 2010](https://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2010/316A-Ex-II-2.pdf), G.O. (D) No. 247, is a proposal and is not treated here as the operative order. The planning office also lists areas from nearby panchayat unions for the wider planning area. Inclusion in that planning area alone does not establish corporation jurisdiction.

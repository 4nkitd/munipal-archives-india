# Vellore corporation wards in the 2022 urban local election

## Ward numbers and seat categories

The Tamil Nadu State Election Commission records 60 ward-member races for Vellore City Municipal Corporation in the 2022 urban local election. The ward headings identify the seat categories. The result tables give elector and ballot counts and each candidate's result. The seat categories below apply to that election.

| Seat category | Wards |
| --- | --- |
| General | 1, 2, 6, 8, 10–12, 21–27, 30, 34–36, 39, 47, 49, 51, 52, 54, 55, 59 |
| General (women) | 4, 7, 9, 13, 14, 18, 19, 28, 29, 31, 32, 38, 40–46, 48, 50, 53, 56, 57, 60 |
| Scheduled Castes (general) | 3, 15, 33, 58 |
| Scheduled Castes (women) | 5, 16, 17, 20, 37 |

## How to use the return

On the [state results page](https://tnsec.tn.gov.in/tnsec_static/election_results/ulbe2022/ulbe2022.php), select Vellore district and the corporation ward-member results. The municipal council and town panchayat results for the district are published separately. The corporation return has no street schedules or ward boundaries and does not establish later seat reservations or present representation.

# Katpadi land withdrawn from Vellore corporation control in July 2025

## Order and land schedules

On 9 July 2025 the state published G.O. Ms. No. 146 in Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary issue 367, Part II, Section 2. The four notifications, II(2)/MAWS/637(s-1)/2025 through II(2)/MAWS/637(s-4)/2025, invoke section 118(2) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998. They withdraw the scheduled extents from Vellore City Municipal Corporation's control as of gazette publication. All four schedules identify Katpadi Village, Katpadi Taluk, Vellore District.

| Notification suffix | Survey number before subdivision | Survey numbers after subdivision | Government land classification | Extent withdrawn (gazette notation, ha) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| s-1 | 680/1A | 680/3 to 680/47 | Poramboke, Mayanam | 0.30.42 |
| s-2 | 17/1 | 17/4 to 17/11 | Poramboke, Pattai | 0.06.19 |
| s-3 | 69 | 69/2 to 69/5 | Poramboke, Pattai | 0.03.20 |
| s-4 | 205 | 205/4 to 205/18 | Poramboke, Pattai | 0.09.02 |

## What the order changes

The order concerns control of the scheduled land, not corporation or ward boundaries. The gazette gives the adjacent survey numbers on each side of each extent. It does not list individual title grants or ward numbers.

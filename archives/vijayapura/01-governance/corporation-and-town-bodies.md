# Vijayapura City Corporation and the district's other town bodies

## Which Vijayapura?

The district's [overview](https://vijayapura.nic.in/en/about-district/) identifies Bijapur as the earlier name of Vijayapura, its district headquarters. The district municipal directory lists Vijayapura City Corporation on its own, apart from town municipal councils such as Indi, Muddebihal, Sindagi and Talikote and town panchayats such as Babaleshwar and Tikota. The city corporation is Local Government Directory body **251993**. The separate town body named Vijayapura in Bengaluru Rural has code **251842** and does not belong to this archive.

## Upgrade and corporation office

The corporation's [gazette register](http://www.vijayapuracity.mrc.gov.in/en/gazette-notification) lists an upgradation gazette, No. UDD 133 MLR 2013, with 20 December 2013 as its publication date. The linked scan has no usable text layer. The register date is not taken here as a separately verified effective date.

The [district's public utility entry](https://vijayapura.nic.in/en/public-utility/vijayapura-city-corporation/) gives the address as Bagalkot Road, Vijayapura 586101 and the office telephone as 08352-278539. The [municipal contact page](http://www.vijayapuracity.mrc.gov.in/en/contact-us) specifies Jal Nagar and publishes `ka.vijapura.cc@gmail.com`.

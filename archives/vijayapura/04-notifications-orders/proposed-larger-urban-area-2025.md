# Vijayapura draft larger urban area notification of November 2025

## Proposed area and boundaries

The [municipal gazette register](http://www.vijayapuracity.mrc.gov.in/en/gazette-notification) lists No. UDD 348 MLR 2024 with publication on 27 November 2025. The English notification in the attachment cites sections 3 and 4 of the Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976. Schedule A describes a proposed municipal area of **159.45 square kilometres**, including the existing corporation and revenue areas listed in the schedule. Schedule B gives north, east, south and west boundary descriptions using revenue survey numbers. It references landmarks such as the Hubli-Sholapur road and a railway crossing on the eastern side. The schedule gives city limits, not numbered ward boundaries.

## Objections and legal status

The notification asked for written objections or suggestions, with reasons, to the Director, Directorate of Municipal Administration, 9th Floor, V.V. Tower, Bengaluru, within 30 days of publication in the official gazette. It says the notification would be taken up for final consideration after that period. The 159.45 figure in this record is proposed area, not a verified current area. No final expansion order was verified.

# Vijayapura property tax calculator and property search

## Choose the right local body

The [municipal citizen-services page](http://www.vijayapuracity.mrc.gov.in/en/citizen-services) links the **Property Tax Calculator** on its own domain. That page offers a tax calculator, search by PID, a broader **Search Property** form and a separate challan-status link.

The broader [Property Search form](http://www.vijayapuracity.mrc.gov.in/ptaxcalculator/propSearchDetails.htm) presents separate district and urban local body (ULB) selectors. It lists Vijayapura among the districts. The criteria displayed include owner first name, tax-arrears range, khata number, property ID and survey number. Select the city corporation as the ULB for a city assessment. A district selection alone does not identify the proper tax authority.

The same citizen-services page has a standalone **Search Property** tile that points to Ballari. The links above use the Vijayapura host. The calculator and search forms were inspected without sending personal data, testing a payment or confirming rates or deadlines.

# Vijayawada Municipal Corporation and the capital region

## From Bezawada municipality to VMC

The [corporation's history](http://www.ourvmc.org/general/aboutvmc.htm) dates the establishment of Vijayawada (Bezawada) Municipality to 1 April 1888. It became a selection-grade municipality in 1960 and a corporation in 1981. In 1985 the corporation absorbed the village panchayats of Gunadala, Patamata and Bhavanipuram and the villages of Payakapuram and Kundavari Kandrika. The history distinguishes the elected body headed by the mayor from the commissioner, who directs day-to-day operations.

## Which office covers which area?

[NTR district's municipal directory](https://ntr.ap.gov.in/public-utility/vijayawada-municipal-corporation/) gives VMC's address as Canal Road, Krishnalanka, Vijayawada 520001. The [state municipal profile](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/86) identifies VMC as ULB 86 and gives its area as 61.88 square kilometres.

The [Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority's regional statistics](https://crda.ap.gov.in/APCRDAV2/views/DynamicHorizontalTabNew.aspx?CapitalType=1) cover several urban local bodies and villages. Its [planning portal](https://crda.ap.gov.in/) also covers Amaravati land pooling and development permissions. A capital-region planning zone is not a VMC ward; use the municipal authority for the property's own urban local body when checking tax or water dues.

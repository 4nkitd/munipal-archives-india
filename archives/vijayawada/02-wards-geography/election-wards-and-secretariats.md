# Vijayawada's 86 election wards and 286 ward secretariats

## Which count to use

| Source | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| [CDMA municipal profile](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/86), updated 14 October 2026 | 86 election wards; 286 ward secretariats, reported separately. |
| [City Finance municipal profile](https://www.cityfinance.in/municipal-data/city/vijayawada) | 64 wards in the profile accompanying the financial series. |
| [VMC's older city history](http://www.ourvmc.org/general/aboutvmc.htm) | 59 political wards; elsewhere on the same page VMC describes 34 sanitary divisions for street cleaning. |

Use the CDMA election-ward field for the 2026 profile. Neither the number of secretariats nor the number of sanitary divisions measures seats on the council.

## Ward reservations

The [ward-reservation gazette](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/Res/MC_Vijayawada.pdf), Roc. No. M-1-115516/2026 dated 13 October 2026, concerns ST, SC, BC, women and unreserved seats. The signatory is the District Election Authority and Collector, NTR. The gazette concerns seat categories, not street boundaries. A ward number in an older service directory should not be assigned a 2026 reservation without matching the ward edition.

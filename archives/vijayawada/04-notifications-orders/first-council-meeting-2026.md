# Instructions for Vijayawada's first council meeting after the 2026 election

## What the commissioners were asked to do

In [letter Roc.No.13021/471/2026/H1](https://sec.ap.gov.in/Uploads/Doc26/GVMC%20%26%20VMC%20-%20First%20Meeting%20on%2023.11.2026%20-%20Intimnation.pdf), the state municipal administration asked the commissioners of Vijayawada and Greater Visakhapatnam to fix their corporations' first meetings in line with the date set for the deputy-mayor elections. The letter cites section 88(b): the first meeting must take place within a month of publication of the declaration under section 66, at a time and place fixed by the commissioner.

## Three dates in the record

The letter says State Election Commission notifications of **4 November 2026** superseded earlier notifications and set the deputy-mayor elections for **23 November 2026**. The letter itself is dated **8 November**. The [SEC election register](https://sec.ap.gov.in/LocalBodiesSection?type=second) lists the intimation on 22 November. It is a scheduling instruction; it does not record attendance, proceedings or an election result.

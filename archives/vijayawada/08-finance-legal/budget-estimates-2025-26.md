# Vijayawada municipal budget estimates for 2025-26

## Reading the budget abstract

Page iv of the [VMC budget book](http://www.ourvmc.org/accounts/VMC%20BUDGET%202025-26.pdf) puts 2023-24 actuals beside the original and revised estimates for 2024-25 and the estimates for 2025-26. The figures below use only the final column, converted to crore and rounded to two decimal places.

| 2025-26 estimate | ₹ crore |
| --- | ---: |
| Opening balance | 349.63 |
| Revenue income | 808.33 |
| Capital income | 454.15 |
| Deposits and advances received | 47.05 |
| Total inflow | 1,309.52 |
| Revenue expenses | 705.95 |
| Capital expenses | 691.78 |
| Loan repayments | 9.80 |
| Deposits and advances paid | 47.05 |
| Total outflow | 1,454.56 |
| Closing balance | 204.59 |

VMC estimates an excess of outflows over inflows of ₹145.04 crore. Page 1 budgets ₹220 crore for property tax, separate from ₹24.66 crore for vacant-land tax. For actual collections, consult the financial statements in the [municipal accounts register](http://www.ourvmc.org/accounts/acmenu.htm), which lists budget books, audited statements and unaudited submissions separately.

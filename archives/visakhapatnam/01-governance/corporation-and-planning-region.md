# How Greater Visakhapatnam was constituted

## The 2005 merger

The [corporation's history](https://gvmc.gov.in/wss/static_content/Introduction.jsp) dates the municipal association to 1858 and the conversion to a municipal corporation to 1979. It dates the government order creating Greater Visakhapatnam to 21 November 2005. That expansion combined the existing corporation, Gajuwaka Municipality and 32 villages. GVMC describes the change at the time as an increase from 111 to 640 square kilometres.

## Municipal limits and regional planning

GVMC lists water supply, street lighting, sanitation, solid waste management and municipal revenue collection among its duties. The [state municipal profile](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/113) identifies the corporation as ULB 113 in Visakhapatnam district and records 681.96 square kilometres. The 640-square-kilometre figure describes the earlier expansion.

The [Visakhapatnam Metropolitan Region Development Authority](https://vmrda.ap.gov.in/department/planning) prepares regional master plans and zonal development plans. Its [village maps](https://vmrda.ap.gov.in/villagemaps) concern planning jurisdiction. A village shown there does not, on that evidence alone, belong to a GVMC election ward.

# GVMC's preliminary 120-ward schedule, May 2026

## Notice and objection period

Form I, Rc.No.00165/2026/TP Section/G1/G2, is dated 17 May 2026. GVMC [listed the signed English notice](https://gvmc.gov.in/wss/) on 18 May. It invokes Rule 8 of the Andhra Pradesh Municipal Corporation (Delimitation of Wards) Rules, 1996, and gives residents seven days from publication to send suggestions or objections to the Commissioner.

## Reading the descriptions

| Proposed ward | Landmarks in the [signed schedule](https://gvmc.gov.in/wss/WhatsNew/Whats_New_DocMay_909.pdf) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The eastern description begins at Gudivada Bridge near Kondapeta Colony and follows the Gosthani River. The southern description uses survey numbers 74 and 73 of Chittivalasa; the western description follows National Highway 16. |
| 2 | The eastern description starts at Chittivalasa Peddacheruvu, follows survey and road boundaries and reaches Dekkatipalem Junction. The southern description follows Mamidipalem Road and Kummaripalem Road towards the municipal boundary. |

The later [CDMA profile](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/all/ulbprofiles/113) records 120 election wards and 578 ward secretariats. It confirms the count, not that every boundary in the preliminary schedule took effect unchanged.

# Public consultation on the draft Visakhapatnam Green City Action Plan

## What GVMC put out for comment

The [GVMC What's New register](https://gvmc.gov.in/wss/) dates the entry “Green City Action Plan (GCAP)” to 19 October 2024. It credits the International Finance Corporation with preparing the report and asks readers to submit suggestions on or before 22 October 2024.

The [attached draft](https://gvmc.gov.in/wss/WhatsNew/Whats_New_DocOct_658.pdf) describes the city's environmental priorities and proposed actions under Eco-Vizag. Its introductory note identifies the assessment method as Advanced Practices for Environmental Excellence in Cities and acknowledges European Union co-funding. The register records a call for comments. Neither the closing date nor the attachment establishes that GVMC adopted the recommendations or funded a particular project.

# How to register and track a GVMC complaint

## By telephone

The [GVMC grievance guide](https://gvmc.gov.in/wss/static_content/Grievances.jsp) gives **1800-4250-0009**, 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. Select **1** to record a complaint. After the beep, give the complaint, name, address and telephone number. Keep the complaint number. The guide says the operator transcribes the message, the responsible official receives the complaint by SMS, and the complainant receives a further SMS when the case is marked resolved.

Select **2** for a water tanker: the operator records the details and the system generates a code for the filling station. Select **3** for tax information.

## In person or online

GVMC lists a public grievance session at its premises on Mondays from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. It also lists its online grievance redressal system and the Smart Vizag app. The guide does not give a guaranteed resolution time.

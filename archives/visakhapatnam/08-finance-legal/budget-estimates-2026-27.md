# GVMC budget estimates for 2026-27

## Consolidated estimates

The first numbered page of the [budget book](https://gvmc.gov.in/wss/image_uploads/bdgt2627.pdf) gives five totals. The following abstract labels the unit as rupees in lakh.

| Item | ₹ lakh |
| --- | ---: |
| Opening balance | 36,596.57 |
| Receipts under all heads | 381,441.00 |
| Receipts including opening balance | 418,037.57 |
| Expenditure under all heads | 404,712.00 |
| Closing balance | 13,325.57 |

Estimated spending exceeds new receipts by ₹23,271 lakh. The budget draws on the opening balance to cover the difference. The functional abstract puts engineering expenditure at ₹102,818 lakh, split between ₹29,258 lakh revenue expenditure and ₹73,560 lakh capital expenditure.

The [finance department's budget series](https://gvmc.gov.in/wss/static_content/html/3%20Budget.html) lists annual books from 2012-13 through 2026-27. These figures are estimates, not an audit of the year.

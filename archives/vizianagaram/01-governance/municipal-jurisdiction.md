# Vizianagaram municipal jurisdiction and city figures

## Municipal jurisdiction

The state profile gives 2019 as the establishment year for the corporation. This date concerns the civic body: the same profile dates the founding of the city to 1713 and the formation of the district to 1979. Bobbili and Nellimarla appear as separate municipal administrations in the district directory. See the [district municipal directory](https://vizianagaram.ap.gov.in/public-utility-category/municipality/).

## Figures in the state municipal profile

| Measure | Reported figure |
| --- | ---: |
| Municipal area | 53.01 km² |
| Population | 244,598 |
| Households | 79,911 |
| Election wards | 62 |
| Revenue wards | 27 |
| Ward secretariats | 61 |

The [state directory](https://apcmms.ap.gov.in/apiv1/public/web/ulbdetails/ulbprofiles) classifies urban local body 110 as a corporation. The population and household fields in the city profile have no reference year. They describe the city profile, not the district census.

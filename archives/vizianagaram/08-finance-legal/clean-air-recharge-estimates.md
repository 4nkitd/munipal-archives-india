# Vizianagaram clean-air and groundwater works estimates

## Clean-air works planned for 2025–26

The corporation reports a total estimate of about ₹440.51 lakh under the National Clean Air Programme for parks and bituminous roads in 2025–26. It gives one estimate for the programme, without allocating a sum to each park, road or ward. The figure records planned works, not expenditure paid to contractors.

## Water conservation and municipal energy use

Under AMRUT 2.0 the corporation reports taking up soak pits and rainwater harvesting pits at an estimated cost of about ₹40 lakh. This is a separate project from the clean-air works. The profile also records a 55 kW solar power installation and second prize in the 2024 Andhra Pradesh State Energy Conservation Award. It gives no capital cost or annual energy savings for the installation.

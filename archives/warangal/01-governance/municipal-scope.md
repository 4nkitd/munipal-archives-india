# Greater Warangal Municipal Corporation: municipal history and jurisdiction

## Changes to the municipality

Warangal held its first adult-franchise election in 1952 under the Hyderabad Municipal and Town Committees Act, 1951. It became a special-grade municipality in July 1959 and a selection-grade municipality in July 1960. The declaration as a municipal corporation followed on 18 August 1994.

| Date | Government action recorded by GWMC |
|---|---|
| 19 March 2013 | G.O.Ms.No.99 brought areas of 42 surrounding gram panchayats into the corporation. |
| 17 May 2014 | G.O.Ms.No.122, MA & UD (Elec II), set the sanctioned strength at 58 ward members. GWMC gives 407.77 km² for this edition. |
| 28 January 2015 | G.O.Ms.No.40 declared Greater Warangal Municipal Corporation. |

The 58 seats refer to the 2014 arrangement. The District Collector's April 2021 election schedule records 66.

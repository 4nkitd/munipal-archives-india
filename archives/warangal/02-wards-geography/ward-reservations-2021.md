# Greater Warangal: reservation schedule for 66 wards, 2021

## Authority and edition

On 15 April 2021 the Collector and District Election Authority, Warangal Urban, issued the reservation schedule for Greater Warangal Municipal Corporation. It cites section 7 of the Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019, and delegation under G.O.Ms.No.219, MA & UD Department, dated 2 August 2019. The Warangal schedule is page 1 of the SEC compilation; the following pages cover other local bodies.

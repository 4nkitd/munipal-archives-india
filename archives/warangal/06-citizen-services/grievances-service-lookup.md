# Greater Warangal: register a grievance or find the local service person

## Register a complaint

Open **Grievance Registration** under General Info on the [GWMC portal](https://gwmc.gov.in/). The first screen asks for the applicant's mobile number. The corporation explicitly requires the applicant's own number for one-time password validation. The separate **Grievance Report** link leads to the [applications received report](https://gwmc.gov.in/grievance/applreceivedreportapp.aspx); it is not the entry form.

## Identify the local service contact

The [election ward enquiry](https://gwmc.gov.in/KnowUrAreaServicePersoneward.aspx) asks for the election ward and the displayed captcha. Under Enquiry, GWMC also lists **Know Your Service Person with House No.** for residents starting with a house number. These lookups identify service personnel. The corporators directory is a separate link under Contacts.

For help from the municipal office, call the GWMC call centre at 0870-2500781. The [contact page](https://gwmc.gov.in/ContactUs_New.aspx) gives the office address as beside MGM Hospital, Warangal 506002.

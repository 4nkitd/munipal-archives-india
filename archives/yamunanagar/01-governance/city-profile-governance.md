# Municipal Corporation Yamunanagar-Jagadhri: city and governance profile

## Formation and municipal figures

Haryana constituted the Municipal Corporation Yamunanagar-Jagadhri (MCYJ) on 17 March 2010 to govern the twin cities of Yamunanagar and Jagadhri in Yamunanagar district. The [State Election Commission's notification of 25 March 2010](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2022/07/2022071148.pdf), No. SEC/4E-III/2010/827, identifies Yamuna Nagar as one of seven corporations constituted by the state's notification of 17 March.

The [corporation's profile](https://www.mcynr.com/about-us) gives an area of 185.45 sq km and reports 549,622 residents under Census 2011. Haryana's [Urban Local Bodies Department table](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/StateMunicipalities) lists 111.87 sq km and 532,066 residents for the corporation, without a population reference year. The two sources do not explain the difference. The archive metadata uses the corporation's figures as published, not as a current population estimate.

## Council and administration

The corporation has 22 wards, numbered 1 through 22, with one councillor per ward. In [notification No. SEC/1ME/2025/4272 dated 20 March 2025](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31c6a0198177bfcc9bd93f6aab94aad3c/uploads/2025/07/202507081506973353.pdf), the State Election Commission named Suman Bahmani of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) as the elected mayor after the 2 March 2025 municipal election. The corporation's [elected members directory](https://www.mcynr.com/elected-members-0) lists the mayor and the ward councillors.

The mayor is elected. The state appoints the municipal commissioner to head the administration. The [commissioner's page](https://www.mcynr.com/commissioner) lists Mahabir Parsad, IAS, in that role. [Local reporting on 18 November 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/yamunanagar-mc-launches-cleanliness-drive-after-chhath-puja/) also identifies him as commissioner.

# Yamunanagar-Jagadhri Municipal Corporation elected council, March 2025

Polling took place on 2 March 2025, and the results were declared on 12 March 2025. The State Election Commission, Haryana, notified the winners on 20 March 2025 in notification `SEC/1ME/2025/4272` under section 14(1) of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Act, 1994. The notification lists the corporation as Yamuna Nagar.

## Mayor

Suman Bahmani of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won the mayoral seat, which was reserved for Scheduled Caste women. She received 126,749 votes, defeating the Indian National Congress runner-up, named Kirna Devi by *The Tribune*, who received 53,430 votes. The margin was 73,319 votes.

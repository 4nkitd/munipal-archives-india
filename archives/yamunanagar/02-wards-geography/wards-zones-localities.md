# Yamunanagar-Jagadhri wards, sectors and localities

## Delimitation and source scope

The corporation covers Yamunanagar and Jagadhri through 22 numbered wards. Haryana's final wardbandi notification, No. 9/144/2024-4CII dated 29 December 2024, publishes their boundaries under rule 10 of the Haryana Municipal Corporation Delimitation of Ward Rules, 1994. The municipal wardbandi page links both the 2018 and 2024 notifications and a 2024 boundary map. Use the 2024 material for this index, not the older 20-ward schedule.

The companion `../wards.json` contains one record for each ward. Colony lists are selected municipal directory entries, not exhaustive lists or cadastral boundaries. A place appearing in several wards retains those shared assignments. A road or village used as a boundary landmark does not establish that the entire road frontage or village falls inside that ward.

The gazette's Hindi schedule cites drawing MCY/2024/12331 dated 24 December 2024; the English text rendering cites the same drawing number with 7 February 2024. Preserve that discrepancy rather than silently choosing a map date. The OCR also contains malformed coordinates. Boundary summaries here use named landmarks and are not survey instructions.

## HSVP sectors and industrial geography

The municipal locality directory places Sector 15 and Sector 15 Phase I and II in Ward 11. Sector 17 Part A-I and A-II have joint Ward 6 and 7 entries; Part B is in Ward 7 and Part II is in Ward 6. Sector 18, Sector 18A and Sector 18 Part II-A are indexed jointly in Wards 7 and 22. Sector 18 Part II-B is in Ward 22. The Sector 18 road also appears in Ward 1's boundary description, which does not override the directory's sector assignments. A councillor's residential address is not proof of a sector's ward.

Model Town is in Ward 8, while Model Colony is in Ward 9. Gulab Nagar village is listed in Ward 1, but Gulab Nagar colony and its extensions include Ward 17 entries. Such repeated names require the full locality description.

Industrial areas do not form a single municipal ward. HSIIDC Industrial Area Manakpur and its Phase II entries are in Ward 3. Old Industrial Area and Industrial Estate Phase I and II are in Ward 15. The directory's "Industrial Area Radour Road" entry spans Wards 13 and 18. Railway Workshop is in Ward 19; nearby Workshop Road residential colonies occur in Wards 16 and 20.

Jagadhri's metal-market area should not be assigned wholesale to one ward without a street address. The old-city directory identifies Khera Bazar, Pansari Bazar, Railway Bazar Road and Ganga Kasera Street in Ward 2, and Buria Gate, Pattri Mohalla and Swran Mohalla in Ward 5. These are verified street or locality entries, not an official polygon named "Jagadhri Metal Market".

## Zones, divisions and property checks

The municipal [Zones page](https://www.mcynr.com/zones) presents property counts under rows labelled Ward No. 1 through Ward No. 22. It does not provide a separate numbered administrative-zone or engineering-division crosswalk. Consequently `zone` and `division` are `null` in the JSON rather than inferred from the twin-city names.

The locality directory includes split entries, spelling variants and historical ward numbers embedded within colony names. For example, "Amar Puri, Ward No. 19" is assigned to Ward 20 in the actual ward column. The assignment column takes precedence over numbers retained in the name. Mandebar has an outside-Lal-Dora entry in Ward 13 but village and Lal Dora entries in Ward 18; Mandebari entries are in Ward 19. Confirm the individual property against the official map and municipal property record before using these names for an application.

## Regularized colonies and approval limits

Appearance in a ward list does not establish that a colony or every plot within it is regularized. Haryana ULB maintains a [regularized-colony notification index](https://www.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfRegularizedColony2023And2024) and a [colony layout and khasra register](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/CivicAmenitiesAndInfrastructure). The index includes a notification titled "17-04-2023 35 Yamuna Nagar Colonies Gazette" and another dated 17 August 2023 covering Yamunanagar, Hisar and Sirsa. The number 45 in the latter title is not a verified Yamunanagar-only total.

The municipal list contains entries such as Green Vihar Colony_2004, Green Vihar Colony_2023, extensions, outside-Lal-Dora areas and abandoned town-planning schemes. These labels alone do not establish the legal status of a specific parcel. Match the colony name, revenue estate, notification, layout polygon and regularized khasra numbers.

The Tribune reported on 28 August 2026 that the commissioner described 76 colonies under the 1996 notification, with 12 regularized earlier and 64 receiving approval while the final government notification remained pending. Surveys were still defining some boundaries. This is a dated status report, not evidence that all 64 had completed final notification by this archive's verification date.

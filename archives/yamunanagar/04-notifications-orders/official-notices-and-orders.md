The August title covers Yamunanagar, Hisar and Sirsa together. Its count of 45 must not be described as 45 Yamunanagar colonies. The same index republishes the April and August batches on 26 September 2025 and includes a municipality-wise status document for 836 colonies notified in 2025, published 13 October 2025. Those portal dates are not replacement gazette dates.

## Limits of this index

Colony names, notification numbers, polygon boundaries and individual plot eligibility have not been transcribed from the gazette attachments. No property is certified as regularized by this record. For a transaction or planning application, obtain the relevant gazette schedule and notified boundary from the corporation's planning branch. A ward-list entry, property ID or tax payment alone does not establish inclusion in a notified colony.

The older "List of Colonies in Yamuna Nagar" entry in the town planning index carries a portal date of 4 January 2021 but no downloadable document. It is not used here as evidence for a colony count or regularization date.

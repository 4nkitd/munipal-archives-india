# Citizen services, RTI and Right to Service in Yamunanagar-Jagadhri

## Service portals and scope

This guide covers the Municipal Corporation of Yamunanagar-Jagadhri. The date above is the compilation date, not the issue date of a single municipal order. The corporation's official website links citizens to the following state portals. Links were checked against published official pages; no application, payment or certificate issuance was tested.

| Service | Official route | Citizen use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property tax and property records | [Property Tax Management System](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Check property particulars, pay dues and submit eligible property-record corrections |
| Birth and death certificates | [Antyodaya-SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Find the applicable certificate or registration service and track the application |
| Pet dog registration and renewal | [Antyodaya-SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | Choose the dog registration service for municipal corporations, not councils or committees |
| Marriage registration | [Haryana marriage portal](https://shaadi.haryana.gov.in/) | Follow the registration and certificate process linked by the corporation |
| RTI application | [Antyodaya-SARAL](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) | The corporation's published online RTI link |
| Municipal complaints | [ULB grievance system](https://grs.ulbharyana.gov.in/) | Lodge a civic complaint and retain its reference number |
| Office appointment | [Municipal e-token booking](https://etoken.mcynr.com/citizen/booking) | Book a visit through the corporation's linked e-token system |

The municipal helpdesk is **7082410824**. The published office locations are near Kanhaiya Sahib Chowk, Yamunanagar, and near Jandha Chowk, Jagadhri. ULB's state IT support and grievance number is **1800-8900-929**, published as available Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm.

## Property tax, Property ID and corrections

Use the property portal for tax payment and the municipal property record. Check the municipality and Property ID before payment, then retain the receipt. A request to correct ownership, area, floor details, property category or dues is a separate transaction from paying the displayed bill.

The portal's [list of required documents](https://property.ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/ListOfDocuments) distinguishes ordinary ownership changes from death cases. For ordinary changes, it lists ownership evidence such as a sale or conveyance deed, transfer or relinquishment deed, allotment letter or court decree, together with an accepted identity document. It states that name updates following sale deeds executed through WebHALRIS are automatic and that there is no fee for name updation. This is not a statement that property tax or every other property service is free.

Death cases require additional evidence. The list includes the death certificate, evidence of the deceased's ownership, succession or will-related documents and a notice with a 30-day period published in two national newspapers. Consult the current service checklist for the documents applicable to the particular case.

Where a previous payment has not been adjusted against outstanding dues, upload its receipt with the dues-correction request. Keep the application number and any deficiency notice so that a later complaint identifies the actual pending transaction.

## Birth, death, pet and marriage registration

The corporation directs birth and death certificate users to SARAL. The Haryana Health Department's [civil registration guidance](https://haryanahealth.gov.in/service/civil-registration-system/) states that registration is compulsory under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969, and the Haryana Registration of Births and Deaths Rules, 2025. Events must be reported within **21 days** and registered at the **place of occurrence**. The head of household reports a home event, while the institution's person in charge reports an event occurring in a hospital.

Distinguish a certificate for an already registered event from delayed registration, correction of an entry or inclusion of a child's name. These are different services. The 21-day reporting requirement is not a municipal certificate-processing deadline.

For pet services, the verified ULB schedule specifically covers **dogs**, including dogs brought for show purposes. Apply through SARAL using the corporation service. The notified limits are 15 working days for initial registration and seven working days for renewal. This record does not establish a general registration system for every species of pet or a current local fee schedule.

The corporation links marriage registration to **shaadi.haryana.gov.in**. Follow the portal's applicable registration category and current document, appointment and payment instructions. A current Yamunanagar-specific marriage fee or processing deadline was not verified in the sources used here; none is inferred from the dog-registration or property-service schedule.

The [state ULB consolidated schedule](https://ulbharyana.gov.in/Home/RightToService) labels itself updated through **4 November 2025** and links amendments dated **11 March 2025** and **27 October 2025**, alongside the notifications of **16 August 2022** and **16 November 2022**.

The schedule's seven-day water or sewer connection entry and two-day water-leakage or sewer-overflow entry expressly apply only to Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat and Karnal corporations. They must not be presented as Yamunanagar municipal RTS guarantees.

## RTI Act, 2005

Use RTI to obtain existing municipal records, such as a work order, recorded inspection report, expenditure statement or action-taken record. A demand to repair a road or remove waste belongs in the grievance system, even if an RTI request separately seeks records about that complaint.

The corporation links its RTI application service to SARAL. For a written request, address the State Public Information Officer of Municipal Corporation Yamunanagar-Jagadhri and obtain proof of receipt. The reviewed pages did not establish the current named municipal PIO, first appellate authority or Haryana-specific application fee. Confirm these before filing rather than assuming the central government's fee applies.

The [Right to Information Act, 2005, on India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2065?locale=en) provides these rules:

| Provision | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Section 6(2) | No reason for seeking information is required |
| Section 6(3) | Transfer to another public authority, where applicable, within five days |
| Section 7(1) | Ordinary decision within 30 days of receipt; information concerning life or liberty within 48 hours |
| Section 7(6) | Information is free where the authority fails to comply with the applicable section 7(1) time limit |
| Section 19(1) | First appeal ordinarily within 30 days of the decision or expiry of the response period |
| Section 19(6) | First appeal disposal within 30 days, extendable to a total of 45 days with recorded reasons |
| Section 19(3) | Second appeal to the State Information Commission ordinarily within 90 days of when the appellate decision was due or received |

These are statutory periods, not the working-day RTS limits above. APIO receipt, additional-fee notices and third-party procedures can affect the applicable calculation. Do not use the [central RTI Online portal](https://rtionline.gov.in/) for this Haryana state public authority; that portal expressly excludes state-government authorities.

## Grievances and Samadhan camps

Use the municipal website's ULB grievance link or call the municipal helpdesk for civic complaints. State the location, ward if known, relevant Property ID or service application number, dates and requested action. Preserve the complaint reference and any closure response. A generic grievance does not replace a service-specific RTS escalation or an RTI appeal.

Samadhan Shivirs have also operated at the Yamunanagar and Jagadhri municipal offices. A **5 January 2025** Tribune report records that 363 complaints were received between **22 October 2024 and 3 January 2025**, with 314 resolved and 49 pending. It quotes the then Municipal Commissioner as saying that camps were held on working days at both offices.

These figures and arrangements are historical, not a verified November 2026 timetable. Confirm the current camp date, venue and hours with the municipal helpdesk before travelling. Carry the original application acknowledgement, relevant receipts and earlier complaint references. For an overdue notified service, identify the designated and grievance authorities in the RTS schedule rather than relying only on an informal camp assurance.

# Yamunanagar-Jagadhri tenders and infrastructure works, 2024-2026

## Official tender access and scope

The Haryana Engineering Works Portal states that tenders floated through HEWP are published on the Haryana NIC e-Tendering website for bidder participation. They appear in HEWP's live-tender list on the next day after publication on NIC. Use the organisation chain Haryana Government, Urban Local Bodies, MC Yamunanagar to locate municipal procurement. PHED sewerage projects are separate departmental works and should not be treated as municipal corporation contracts. [1]

The corporation's own tender page returned "No Tenders Found" when checked for this record. This describes that page, not the absence of municipal procurement. The NIC portal and HEWP should also be checked. Tender identifiers, closing dates, corrigenda, financial evaluations and award documents must be matched to the individual procurement before bidding or treating an estimate as an awarded contract. [1][2]

This is a selected historical record, not a list of currently open bids. The frontmatter date is the latest dated project report used below, 7 February 2026. Figures retain their source dates. No completion certificate or current award status is inferred from a tender notice or newspaper announcement.

## Kail solid waste management plant

On 11 December 2024, Aaj Samaaj reported a solid waste review chaired by Municipal Commissioner Ayush Sinha. Municipal officers reported that the collection agency had deployed 117 tippers. The commissioner directed the agency to add 10 more, and the agency representative promised to do so within 10 days. The report attributed processing of more than 300 tonnes daily and operation of one trommel for eight hours daily to the agency representative. These are meeting statements, not independently measured plant capacity or proof of later delivery. [3]

The same report described slow processing and accumulation of waste at Kail. It recorded instructions to create working space by moving accumulated material with a Poclain or JCB and to accelerate processing. It also reported a penalty of Rs 5,000 per day when a collection tipper did not arrive. This is a reported enforcement instruction, not a reproduced contract clause. [3]

On 22 January 2026, The Tribune reported Mayor Suman Bahamani's announcement of a Rs 7.24 crore shed project at the Kail plant. The scope included paving the ground beneath the shed and covering the processing area to protect machinery and reduce monsoon disruption. Additional Municipal Commissioner Dheeraj Kumar said preparatory work was complete and construction would begin shortly. The report does not establish that the shed was completed. [4]

A 7 February 2026 report of the Haryana Vidhan Sabha Environment Pollution Subject Committee's inspection described proposed or ongoing upgrades at Kail, including sheds, ground concretisation, machinery-based processing and refuse-derived fuel generation. It supplied no verified plant-wide processing capacity or completed shed measurement. [5]

## Yamuna, Ditch Drain and sewerage treatment works

HSPCB's March 2024 monthly progress report, forwarded on 31 May 2024, recorded Yamunanagar's existing STP capacity as 65 million litres per day, with 43.70 MLD utilised. Jagadhri was listed separately at 24 MLD installed and 18.5 MLD utilised. These are town-wise figures for that reporting period, not a combined municipal total. [6]

The report's plant annexure identified the following PHED facilities. All five were recorded as complying on samples dated 15 March 2024. [6]

| Facility | Capacity |
| --- | --- |
| Radaur Road, Yamunanagar | 20 MLD |
| Radaur Road, Yamunanagar | 25 MLD |
| Baddimajra, Yamunanagar | 10 MLD |
| Baddimajra, Yamunanagar | 10 MLD |
| Parwaloo, Jagadhri | 24 MLD |

HSPCB's February 2025 report, forwarded on 27 May 2025, listed a proposed 77 MLD PHED STP at Radaur Road as "Under allotment", with a likely completion date of 31 August 2026. Its September 2025 report, forwarded on 10 December 2025, instead placed the 77 MLD Yamuna Nagar plant in the under-construction table at 2% physical progress, with a completion timeline of 31 March 2027. The later timeline supersedes the earlier forecast for this archive; neither date is evidence of commissioning. [7][8]

The September report also stated that ULB had started bio/phyto-remediation works in drains in Municipal Corporation Yamuna Nagar-Jagadhri as a pilot project. It did not identify a municipal tender value for that pilot. [8]

On 22 January 2025, The Tribune reported HSPCB notices to 220 industrial units over alleged untreated discharge into the Dhanaura escape through the Ditch Drain. The report quoted the regional officer confirming the notices and a local MLA referring to a planned STP. This establishes pollution-control context, not proof that a sewer interception scheme had been completed. [9]

On 7 February 2026, The Times of India reported the Assembly committee's inspection of an under-construction 77 MLD STP at Jammu Colony. It confirms the reported construction status at that time, but gives no commissioning date or updated percentage. The official progress reports describe the project as Radaur Road or Yamuna Nagar; the press report uses Jammu Colony. No additional capacity is added for this naming difference. [5]

## Streets, roads and drainage procurement

Amar Ujala's 28 May 2025 report described Rs 76.62 lakh of approved works for which municipal e-tendering had begun. The following amounts are reported estimates, not final bills or verified awards. Ward numbers reproduce the contemporary report and are not a current ward-boundary crosswalk. [10]

| Reported location | Work | Reported estimate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tejli, ward 11 | Streets and drains from Shiv Mandir to Gold Fitness Gym, and from Kandela's house to Mohan Lal's house | Rs 42.35 lakh |
| Raipur, ward 12 | Valmiki chaupal | Rs 26.69 lakh |
| Buria, ward 4 | Toilet at the waterworks | Rs 7.58 lakh |

The same report described another Rs 46.37 lakh package in the e-tendering process. Its street and drainage components included a Rs 14.10 lakh drainage pipeline from Shri Lal Dwara Mandir to the old-age home in Lal Dwara Colony, ward 9; Rs 7.91 lakh for streets and drains in Mamidi, ward 13; Rs 5.93 lakh for a street from Hanuman Gate to the large drain, ward 15; and Rs 2.93 lakh for a covered drain on Chhoti Line, ward 15. The package also included Rs 15.45 lakh for park entrance gates. The article's package total and component estimates are reproduced without treating them as audited expenditure. [10]

These examples document street and drainage procurement. They do not establish resurfaced road lengths, pavement specifications, contractor performance or completion. No city-wide road expenditure total is inferred.

## LED streetlights and central monitoring

The Tribune's 25 November 2024 report stated that ULB had approved 41,011 LED lights for Yamunanagar and Jagadhri and that the corporation had issued a tender. Municipal Commissioner Ayush Sinha described replacement of sodium lamps, CFLs and damaged tube lights, as well as new lighting points. The reported installation period was 18 months. [11]

The proposed common control and monitoring system, CCMS, was to operate from the corporation office. Its described functions included identifying faulty lights by pole and remotely switching lights on and off. The survey figures in the report were approximately 28,000 new points and 13,000 existing points. These rounded survey figures should not replace the precise approval quantity of 41,011 lights. [11]

The source gives Rs 17.12 crore as the rounded project cost, but uses both Rs 1,711.90 lakh and Rs 1,711.95 lakh in its text. This archive retains Rs 17.12 crore as the reported approximate figure and flags the inconsistency rather than selecting an unsupported exact value. The report verifies approval and tender issuance, not installation of all approved lights, a functioning CCMS or project completion. [11]

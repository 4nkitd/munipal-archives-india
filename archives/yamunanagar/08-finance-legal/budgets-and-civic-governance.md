# Yamunanagar-Jagadhri municipal budgets and civic financial governance, FY 2024-25 to FY 2026-27

## Scope and evidence

This record covers the Municipal Corporation Yamunanagar-Jagadhri, abbreviated MCYJ. Financial years run from 1 April to 31 March. FY 2026-27 therefore ends on 31 March 2027. No FY 2027-28 budget is asserted here.

The corporation's official audited-account index lists statements for 2015-16, 2016-17, 2017-18, 2018-19, 2019-20, 2020-21 and 2023-24. It also lists a combined 2015-16 and 2016-17 document. At verification, that index did not list FY 2024-25 or FY 2025-26 audited accounts. Its 2023-24 entry is an account-statement link, not evidence that the later budgets were audited. [1]

The budget amounts below come from named contemporary news reports describing corporation meetings. Signed budget books, approval resolutions and complete revised-estimate schedules were not retrieved. These amounts should not be presented as independently reconciled audited accounts. The frontmatter date refers to the reported FY 2026-27 budget meeting.

For FY 2024-25, the April 2025 report gives receipts of Rs 250,86,26,489 and expenditure of Rs 139,90,46,359. These are reported prior-year figures, not figures independently checked against the corporation's audited ledger. [2]

For FY 2025-26, receipts up to January 2026 were reported as approximately Rs 214.92 crore, with expenditure of approximately Rs 161.65 crore. January totals are partial-year figures and must not be labelled final annual actuals. The FY 2026-27 estimates exceed the preceding year's income estimate by Rs 10.43 crore and expenditure estimate by Rs 10.20 crore. [3][4]

## FY 2026-27 allocations and revenue

The Tribune and Times of India agree that Rs 124.37 crore was allocated collectively to development works, cleanliness and waste management. The detailed report divides this into Rs 90.35 crore for development and Rs 34.02 crore for sanitation and waste management. These are components of the overall expenditure budget, not additional spending outside it. [3][4]

| Head | Reported FY 2026-27 allocation |
| --- | ---: |
| Development works, including streets, community centres and municipal buildings | 90.35 |
| Sanitation and solid waste management | 34.02 |
| Salaries, pensions and other associated expenditure | 108.25 |
| Administrative expenditure | 14.60 |
| Operations and maintenance | 20.32 |
| Advance loans | 2.15 |
| Other expenditure | 3.22 |

The sanitation allocation includes door-to-door collection, municipal vehicle and machinery repairs and maintenance, and material recovery facility centres. The seven expenditure heads printed by Times of India sum to Rs 272.91 crore, Rs 0.01 crore below its stated total. Retain the reported total of Rs 272.92 crore and request the signed schedule for reconciliation. [4]

Reported income targets include Rs 37 crore from property, fire and municipal taxes; Rs 21 crore from stamp duty and labour cess; Rs 3.23 crore from rent, lease and tehbazari; and Rs 141.41 crore from State Finance Commission and Central Finance Commission grants. The same report gives development and other charges as Rs 22.28 crore in its narrative but Rs 22.20 crore in its list. Neither version is silently substituted for an official schedule. [4]

## Civic financial accountability

The February 2026 meeting report names Deputy Commissioner and Municipal Commissioner Preeti, Additional Municipal Commissioner Dheeraj Kumar and the senior accounts officer as participants. These are historical meeting roles, not a current officer directory. News reports spell the accounts officer's name differently, as Krishan Jain and Krishna Jain. [3][4]

To check a particular civic work against these allocations, obtain the budget-head code, administrative approval, technical sanction, tender and work-order number, payment vouchers and completion status. A budget allocation alone does not establish that a work was tendered, completed or paid for.

The official corporation portal links property-tax payment to the Haryana ULB property portal and grievances to the Haryana ULB grievance system. It also links an RTI application route through SARAL. Use an information request to seek the signed FY 2024-25, FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27 budgets, revised estimates, resolution numbers, grant releases and audit observations. [5]
